Job Description



We are seeking a Change Manager to lead and administer project change control processes for pharmaceutical capital projects, facility upgrades, and manufacturing expansions. The role will ensure all project changes are properly assessed, documented, approved, and implemented while maintaining alignment with project governance, schedule, cost, risk, and compliance requirements.



Key Responsibilities



Establish and manage the project change control process and governance framework.
Facilitate change review meetings and maintain a comprehensive change register.
Coordinate impact assessments covering scope, cost, schedule, risk, contracts, resources, and forecasts.
Ensure supporting documentation, approvals, and audit trails are properly maintained.
Monitor change progression, support prioritisation and escalation of critical changes.
Ensure approved changes are incorporated into project baselines, budgets, schedules, and forecasts.
Produce change reports, trend analysis, KPIs, and management dashboards.
Collaborate with Project Management, Engineering, Quality, Validation, Procurement, and Finance teams to support decision-making.
Provide visibility of cumulative change impacts and project exposure.


Qualifications


Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Project Management, Life Sciences, or related discipline.
5+ years of experience in Change Management, Project Controls, or Project Management within the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, or life sciences sector.
Strong understanding of project controls, cost management, scheduling, risk management, and change governance.
Experience supporting GMP-regulated projects and capital investment programmes.
Strong stakeholder engagement, analytical, and communication skills.
