We are currently seeking an experienced professional to join our team in the role of Senior Consultant Specialist

In This Role, You Will


Deploy and maintain critical applications on cloud-native microservices architecture
Implement automation, effective monitoring, and infrastructure-as-code
Deploy and maintain CI/CD pipelines across multiple environments
Support and work alongside a cross-functional engineering team on the latest technologies
Iterate on best practices to increase the quality & velocity of deployments
Sustain and improve the process of knowledge sharing throughout the engineering team. Follow standards and controls to ensure quality of outcome
Support production deployment and post development implementation activities
Perform application support as necessary; participate and drive the Crisis Calls for production incidents
Identify and work on system performance improvement


To Be Successful, You Will


Hands-on Unix/Linux Administration. Expertise with GCP. Experience with configuration management tools (e.g. Ansible)
Experience with infrastructure-as-code (e.g. Terraform, Cloudformation). Version control system experience (e.g. Git)
Practical experience with Docker containerization and orchestration (Kubernetes). Hands-on experience with administration and setup of Jenkins CI/CD pipelines, alerts and workflow setup
A drive towards automating repetitive tasks (e.g. scripting via Bash, Python etc). Experience with administration and maintenance with GKE backup and blue green deployment strategies
Working knowledge on GCP products like GKE, GCE, Cloud Logging, Cloud Run Functions, Cloud Composer, Dataflow, VPC, NAT Gateways and subnets, Big query, IAM. is mandatory
Fair knowledge and experience of DevOps & Agile discipline. Confluence, JIRA, Unix / Linux. Strong interpersonal and communications skills
Be a team player - someone who can work with our other engineers to get things done. We are looking for someone who takes pride in the quality of their work, and who cares about how their product is used
Strong unit testing and debugging skills
