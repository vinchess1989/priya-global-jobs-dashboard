Our commitment to our employees & clients is at the heart of everything we do.  

About The Role

Rabobank’s Technology team within COO Domain delivers the secure, reliable & scalable technology that keeps our people & customers connected.

We are currently hiring for a Senior Quality Engineer - Mobile to join our Mobile Banking Squad.

In this role you will help shape the quality of mobile banking experiences used by customers across Australia & New Zealand by delivering secure, reliable & accessible mobile banking experiences, working across native iOS & Android applications, APIs & integrated services.

Top Role Responsibilities & Accountabilities:


Design & execute risk-based testing across iOS, Android, APIs & integrated customer journeys
Own, maintain & improve the mobile test automation framework.
Build reliable automated tests & integrate quality controls into CI/CD pipelines
Lead testing across functional, integration, regression, accessibility & production verification testing
Identify quality risks early & provide clear recommendations on release readiness
Lead defect triage & support severity assessment, prioritisation & resolution
Coach Engineers & help embed strong quality engineering practices across the Mobile Banking Squad
Support production releases, incident investigation & root cause analysis
Maintain test environments, devices, test data & automation assets required for delivery
Contribute to continuous improvement across testing, automation & production assurance


To be successful you will have:


A background in software testing or quality engineering with experience testing native iOS & Android applications, APIs & integrated services
Proven experience designing, maintaining & improving reusable mobile automation frameworks
Experience integrating automated tests into CI/CD pipelines & release processes
Experience with risk-based testing, defect management, release assurance & production verification
Experience with API & web automation tools such as Postman, REST Assured, Selenium or Playwright
Experience using modern AI coding tools, such as GitHub Copilot or equivalent
Understanding of modern AI engineering concepts, including LLMs, prompt engineering, RAG, agentic workflows, evaluation & responsible AI
Excellent verbal & written communication skills
Experience guiding quality engineers & working within Agile delivery teams
Experience in financial services & mobile banking highly regarded


Location & Working Arrangements:

This role must be based in Sydney & offers a flexible hybrid working model with both in office & at home working.
