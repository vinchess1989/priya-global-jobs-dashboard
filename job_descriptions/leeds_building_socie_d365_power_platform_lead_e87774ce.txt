How You'll Help Us Live Our Purpose

We've been helping our members save for their future and buy their own home since 1875. Join us and you'll play a big role in helping us to put home ownership within reach of more people, generation after generation.

It's a purpose that drives everything we do. And you can play your part too – join our team as a Power Platform Lead

How You'll Make a Difference

You'll lead the day-to-day operation, administration and governance of our Power Platform and Dynamics 365 services, making sure they remain secure, compliant, scalable and ready to meet evolving business needs.

As our technical subject matter expert, you'll guide design decisions, manage environments, and releases, monitor platform health, resolve incidents and drive continual improvement. Working across IT, business teams and suppliers, you'll bridge the gap between technology and the business while enabling innovation and supporting the colleagues who run the platform.

What will you bring to the role?


Strong experience in Power Platform, Dynamics 365 CE or Microsoft 365, with strong knowledge of Power Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse, Admin Centre and plugins
Expert knowledge of Power Platform and Dynamics 365 CE governance, administration and operational support, including environments, tenant settings, access, licences and Dataverse capacity
Practical experience of security and RBAC, compliance, audit, DLP, connector and maker governance controls
Experience operating application lifecycle management and release controls across development, test, UAT and production environments using Azure DevOps
Strong ITIL-aligned service management experience, including incident and problem management, service transition, continual improvement and performance reporting
Major incident management, including coordination, communication, escalation and post-incident review.
Supplier and third-party vendor management, ensuring services are delivered in line with agreed expectations and contractual obligations.
We're building capability in this space, so you'll need line management and stakeholder management experience, with confidence guiding delivery teams, suppliers and citizen developers and challenging design decisions
