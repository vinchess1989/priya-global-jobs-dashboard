Job Description


Key Responsibilities

Drive the development and maintenance of the DevOps best practice and capability across SNE
Provide mentoring and guidance to the Ops and Engineering teams
Develop, implement and maintain software automation to improve products and services
Prepare clear and concise process documentation describing how networks, infrastructure, products and services operate
Carry out in-depth and critical analysis of vendor/open source technologies and provide contingency options to ensure a streamlined integration/delivery
Make technology investment and business asset recommendations and assessments based on extensive knowledge of new and emerging technologies
Review and provide input into the 'product infrastructure' plans and directions (roadmap)

Skills and experience:

Excellent knowledge of Linux based environments
Must have hands-on experience on Redhat Openshift installation, configuration and maintenance.
Automation skills are a must using python, shell scripting etc.
Excellent knowledge of working with DevOps tools such as – Bamboo, Jira, BitBucket/GitLab, SonarQube, Coverity, Artifactory/Nexus etc.
Good knowledge on document-oriented databases like Elasticsearch, Couchbase etc.
Knowledge on security protocols like TLS, HTTPS, LDAPS is preferred.
Knowledge of infrastructure automation tools such as Ansible, Chef, and Puppet etc. will also be looked upon favourably.
Knowledge of

Big Data technologies is preferred but not mandatory.

Excellent time management skills to meet the required project timeframes
Able to manage and prioritise workload to optimise business results
Able to work as part of a tight-knit team, taking on feedback
Able to think strategically while developing technology solutions
Demonstrated technology subject matter expertise.
Familiarity with technology hands on configuration.
Able to communicate effectively with stakeholders.
Able to comprehend and deliver to a schedule.
Ability to work under pressure to deliver to near-term deadlines.
