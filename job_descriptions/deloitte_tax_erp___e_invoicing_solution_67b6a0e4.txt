Why you’ll enjoy this new opportunity

At Deloitte, you’ll join a growing, multidisciplinary Tax Transformation Consulting (TTC) team where tax, data and automation specialists work together to help shape the future of tax. As a Tax ERP & E-Invoicing Solutions Manager, you’ll help lead the design, development and implementation of indirect tax digital reporting solutions, turning clients’ tax, legal and business requirements into reliable technical integrations.

You’ll work across tax, finance and technology, connecting ERP systems with e-invoicing providers and helping clients adapt to evolving requirements. No two days will look the same. Working across industries and services, you’ll broaden your expertise, build your career in Ireland and make a tangible difference for clients.

Key skills and prior experience that align well to this position



Experience integrating ERP systems with finance, invoicing or e-invoicing solutions, including third-party solutions
Experience designing system interfaces and data mappings using APIs or file-based integrations, and troubleshooting data flows across multiple systems
Experience designing and delivering system interfaces and data mappings through APIs or file-based integrations, and troubleshooting data flows across multiple systems
Understanding of the end-to-end technology supporting AR and AP processes, with the ability to explain the advantages and limitations of different solutions in the context of a client’s entity footprint
Familiarity with e-invoicing standards and formats, such as PEPPOL, UBL and EDIFACT, as well as document validation, transaction-status workflows and relevant regional regulations
Strong technical documentation skills and the ability to communicate complex challenges and solutions clearly, working closely with functional consultants and external vendors
A proactive and collaborative approach, with the ability to prioritise competing demands and deliver high-quality work across multiple projects in a fast paced environment
Integration of middleware and production-support processes (desirable)
Familiarity with programming languages such as Java, Python, or C#. Ability to understand code, as well as ability to analyse rules, conditions, and data mappings (desirable)
API management tools and platforms (e.g., Postman) (desirable)



What type of work will I be doing?

Your responsibilities will include:



Assessing invoice data, source systems and existing integration architecture across EPM environments
Translating functional requirements into technical specifications, data mappings, and interface designs
Leading solution design with clients and vendors, including documenting configuration and implementation requirements
Collaborating with client and vendor teams on integrations between ERP systems and e-invoicing platforms, including data exchange, status updates, and error handling
Mapping client invoice data to the structures required by e-invoicing providers and identifying data-quality, reconciliation, monitoring and operational-support requirements
Overseeing configuration and development, leading end-to-end integration testing, and coordinating timely defect resolution
Troubleshooting integration issues and supporting deployment, technical documentation and handover to client teams
Strong communication and stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to explain technical matters to varied audiences
Providing technical guidance and training to clients and internal teams as needed
Leading and developing diverse teams, fostering an inclusive culture that values collaboration and recognises individual contributions
Building trusted relationships with clients, colleagues across Deloitte and other stakeholders managing risk, and supporting business development and go-to-market activities
Staying informed about developments in e-invoicing requirements and technology including API integration



Who is the Hiring Manager?

The hiring manager is an experienced senior leader within Deloitte’s Tax Transformation team who fosters an inclusive, collaborative, and high-performing working environment. They empower team members to take ownership, contribute ideas, and build their capabilities, while recognising and rewarding collaboration, commitment, and individual contribution. Their leadership balances a strong focus on client outcomes and quality with a genuine commitment to teamwork, innovation, professional development, and operational integrity.

Where is this role based?

This role is based in our Dublin office. Our team operates a hybrid working model under our Deloitte Works policy, with team members balancing time between Deloitte offices, home, and client sites in line with business, team, and client requirements.
