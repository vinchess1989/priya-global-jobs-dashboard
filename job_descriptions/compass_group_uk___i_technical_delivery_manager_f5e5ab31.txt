Technical Delivery Lead - Birmingham (Flexible hybrid working) | Full-Time / Permanent

Up to £75,000 + bonus and excellent benefits including healthcare, wellbeing support, 27 days' annual leave plus bank holidays, life insurance and more.

We're looking for a detail orientated Technical Delivery Lead to drive the delivery of critical infrastructure and platform transformation programmes across Compass Group UK&I. This is an exciting opportunity to lead large-scale technology initiatives spanning cloud, identity, security, networking and infrastructure modernisation, ensuring solutions are delivered successfully and create measurable business value.

If you're passionate about technology transformation and delivering outcomes in complex environments, we'd love to hear from you.

Ready to lead the technology foundations that power one of the world's largest hospitality businesses? Apply today.

Compass Digital & Technology is where ideas turn into possibilities.

We’re the technology team behind Compass Group UK&I, part of Compass Group, one of the world’s largest hospitality businesses. We’re shaping the next generation of products, platforms and experiences for Compass Group, harnessing AI to create competitive advantage and accelerate growth.

We create, manage and deploy technology which supports our operators, clients and guests. From enterprise-scale cloud engineering, cybersecurity, mobile ordering and guest-facing experiences to everyday IT support. Our work reaches across our business and all walks of life, from schools to banks and into the daily lives of millions of people.

But D&T is about more than technology. It’s a place to be curious, challenge what’s possible and turn great ideas into real outcomes. A place where talented people collaborate, experiment, learn and make an impact at scale.

We own it from idea to outcome. We challenge what’s possible. And together, we’re creating better solutions, every day.

Technical Delivery Lead – The role

Coordinate engineers, architects, cyber security teams and delivery partners to achieve programme objectives.
Lead the end-to-end delivery of infrastructure and platform projects, from design and build through migration, cutover and transition into live service.
Drive the successful delivery of strategic infrastructure initiatives including integration middleware, identity transformation and legacy technology retirement.
Lead the migration to Microsoft Entra, including identity management, single sign-on and application onboarding activities.
Own technical risk, dependencies and delivery planning across cloud, infrastructure, security, network and identity domains.
Challenge suppliers and technical partners to ensure high-quality, timely delivery and successful business outcomes.
Build and maintain delivery plans, budgets, forecasts and governance processes that enable effective programme execution.
Collaborate with stakeholders across Digital & Technology and the wider business to ensure change is successfully adopted with minimal operational disruption.
Measure and track delivery benefits including cost reduction, risk mitigation, service improvement and platform stability.
Support the ongoing evolution of Compass's Modern Foundations programme and technology transformation roadmap.

What we're looking for

Experience delivering infrastructure, cloud, platform or technology transformation projects within complex organisations.
Strong technical knowledge across areas such as AWS, Microsoft Entra ID, Microsoft 365, Azure, networking, infrastructure or security technologies.
Experience leading migration, transformation and decommissioning initiatives while maintaining business continuity.
Ability to manage third-party suppliers, delivery partners and managed service providers effectively.
Strong project planning, governance, budget management and risk management capabilities.
Excellent communication skills with the ability to engage both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Experience working across infrastructure, cloud, cyber security and architecture teams to achieve shared outcomes.
A hands-on, outcome-focused approach with the confidence to make decisions and remove delivery blockers.
Relevant project delivery or technical certifications would be advantageous.

What you'll get in return

Competitive salary with bonus and full company benefits
27 days' annual leave plus bank holidays, and a holiday purchase scheme
Healthcare & wellbeing: Aviva Digicare, Medicash (dental, optical, therapy treatments)
Mental health support: 24/7 Employee Assistance Programme
Family benefits: Enhanced maternity, paternity, and adoption leave. 2 days’ additional leave after returning from maternity leave, day off for your baby’s first birthday, enhanced family leave
Perks & discounts: Shopping, entertainment, and travel discounts, 20% off Nuffield Health and 10% off PureGym memberships
Financial wellbeing: Pension scheme, Life Assurance, preferred rates on salary finance products
Development opportunities: Professional subscriptions, ongoing training and structured career pathways

Compass Group is committed to creating a diverse and inclusive workplace where everyone can thrive.
