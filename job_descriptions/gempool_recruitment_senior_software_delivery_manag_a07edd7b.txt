GemPool's client is a leading management and technology consultancy with strong European roots and a growing presence in Ireland. They offer the chance to work on large-scale digital transformation programmes where delivery quality, client impact and technical excellence really matter.




I'm looking for a Senior Software Delivery Manager who enjoys leading software programmes, delivering multiple complex projects, working closely with clients, and leading delivery across hybrid teams.




The ideal person will have delivered software programmes/projects at scale, worked with cross-functional teams, and will excel at managing delivery across multiple workstreams. You will be client-facing, so strong stakeholder management and the ability to build trust with senior stakeholders will be important. Depending on the client/programme, the role will involve managing teams of around 30 people across multiple projects, including internal delivery teams and, in some cases, client-side resources.




This position would suit someone who brings a strong mix of three key areas:

Project management
A technical background or strong understanding of software engineering
Agile delivery, with the flexibility to use waterfall approaches where needed




You will be involved in:

Leading software delivery across complex programmes
Managing delivery teams across agile and hybrid environments
Working closely with clients and senior stakeholders
Overseeing governance, reporting, risks, budgets and resourcing
Supporting delivery quality and continuous improvement
Collaborating with technical teams to ensure solutions are delivered effectively
Helping teams stay focused, organised and moving in the right direction




The ideal background:

At least 5 years of experience in a senior leadership or programme management capacity
15+ years of experience in software delivery or programme delivery
Experience managing large-scale technology projects
A good understanding of modern software engineering practices
Agile delivery experience, with a pragmatic approach to methodology
Strong project management and stakeholder management skills
Experience managing hybrid or multi-disciplinary teams
Consulting or management consultancy experience would be a strong advantage
Product delivery experience would also be very relevant




This position is ideal for someone who enjoys being directly involved in delivery, engaging with clients, and understanding the technology behind the work. It offers a great opportunity for individuals seeking to take responsibility for significant software delivery tasks within a consulting setting, with clear opportunities for growth and involvement in large-scale transformation initiatives.




Apply now or message cathal.mcaliskey@gempool.ie if you’d like to hear more.
