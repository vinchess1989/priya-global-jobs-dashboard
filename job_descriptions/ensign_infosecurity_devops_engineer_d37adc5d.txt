Responsibilities:

Configure, maintain, and support operations of multi-cloud infrastructure
Support DevOps operations by maintaining and enhancing existing DevOps workflow in Cloud/on-premises
Create and write playbooks in Ansible and shell scripts to automate incidents, service requests, deployments, etc.
Proficient in CI concepts and implementing and improving CI in existing environment
Maintain project’s internal Gitlab repository.
Automate routine system tasks: cooking up recipes with Ansible, and deploying environments including test automation
Develop, configure and administer overall DevOps and IT automation solutions
Define and setup process for managing Ops developed playbooks
Administer Ansible infrastructure, perform maintenance and configuration and provide SME level support




Requirements:

Minimum of 2 years of experience in cloud-related technologies (preferably with AWS CloudOps Engineer Associate)
Minimum of 2 years of experience developing coding/scripting to build required automation and tools using Ansible, YAML, JSON, Python, Bash, Azure CLI, PowerShell, Java, JavaScript, etc.
Minimum of 2 years of experience on RHEL (RHCSA/RCHE certified preferred)
Diploma or degree in a related field or equivalent knowledge is required
Windows /Unix / Linux / Bash knowledge
Good understanding of cloud computing (e.g. Technologies, Deployment, Costing, HA/DR, etc.)
Good understanding of cloud-related technologies (e.g. Docker, Ansible, IaC tools, RA, deployment tools, etc.)
Some Experience with different cloud providers (e.g. AWS, Google Cloud, Internal Cloud (e.g. Openstack, VMWare), Alicloud, etc.).
Good understanding of infrastructure services such as DNS, LDAP, DHCP, TCP/IP Networking and integrating them to Cloud platform.
Familiar with security fundamentals at the infrastructure and operating systems, including Cloud environments
Ability to demonstrate automation of daily tasks, testing and integration experience on projects
Ability to adapt to a changing and dynamic environment
