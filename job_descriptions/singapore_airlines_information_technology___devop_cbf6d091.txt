Job Description

Enable secure, reliable delivery using GitLab CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code, and governed AI-assisted SDLC. Partner with Development, QA, CloudOps, InfoSec, and AI teams with human-in-the-loop for security and production changes.

Key Responsibilities


Standardize GitLab pipelines/templates; manage runners, protected branches/environments, promotions, approvals, and rollbacks.
Automate cloud infra and app deployments with Terraform/Ansible; ensure reproducible environments and drift detection.
Embed DevSecOps controls: SAST/DAST, SCA, secrets, container/IaC scans, and API auth checks with risk-based gates and tracked exceptions.
Secure supply chain: curate dependencies and AI assets; enforce SBOMs, signing/provenance, least-privilege identities, and build-once/promote.
Govern AISDLC and agents: approved MCP integrations, reusable prompts/skills, safe agent permissions, and auditable workflows; human review of AI output.
Deliver AI workloads with versioned prompts/models/datasets, pipeline evaluations, rollout controls, and fallbacks.
Operability and risk: metrics/logs/traces/dashboards, runbooks and incident response; track versions/EOL and plan upgrades or retirement.


Requirements


Degree in IT/Computer Science or related.
2–5 years in automation and CI/CD/cloud (Ansible or similar).
Linux, Bash/shell, Python; API-based tooling integration.
GitLab CI/CD hands-on (MRs, runners, troubleshooting, artifacts, controlled releases).
AWS, Docker, Kubernetes; familiarity with IAM, networking, secrets; OpenShift/ROSA a plus.
IaC/config management; quality gates; interpret SonarQube/security findings with AppSec.
Observability with Grafana, Prometheus/PromQL, and logs/traces.
Safe use of AI coding assistants; understanding of AISDLC, LLM risks, prompt injection, and least-privilege agents.
Working knowledge of Java and Node.js; strong problem-solving, communication, and collaboration.


Preferred


RAG, evaluation pipelines, AI gateways, secure agent/MCP integrations; AI deployment/monitoring.
OpenTelemetry, distributed tracing, SLOs; cloud/AI cost optimization.


Note: You may be posted to any subsidiary within SIA Group. Only shortlisted candidates will be notified.
