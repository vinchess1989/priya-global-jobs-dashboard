Job Title: Senior DevOps Engineer

Department: DevOps

Location: Bengaluru/ Coimbatore

Experience: 5-10 Years

About AppViewX:

AppViewX is the only machine and agent identity security company built for the AI and quantum era, bringing together discovery, automation, control, and intelligence. The AVX Platform helps enterprises reduce risk by providing complete visibility and governance over every machine and AI agent identity, automating their lifecycle, controlling their access, and proving compliance at the speed business demands. Trusted by global enterprises across financial services, healthcare, and technology, AppViewX is recognized as a leader in the IDC 2026 MarketScape for Certificate Lifecycle Management and KuppingerCole’s 2025 Non-Human Identity Management Leadership Compass. For more information, users can visit appviewx.com.

Role Overview

Technical and Platform Responsibilities


Lead collaboration with Product Engineering teams to architect and deliver scalable, secure, and reliable SaaS platform solutions.
Own end-to-end DevOps and platform engineering initiatives from design through production readiness.
Design and lead AI-driven automation for cloud-native infrastructure and DevOps workflows.
Lead AIOps capabilities for proactive monitoring, incident detection, root cause analysis, and automated remediation.
Architect self-healing infrastructure and validated automated recovery workflows.
Establish AI-assisted CI/CD practices for faster, secure, traceable, and reliable delivery.
Drive Kubernetes reliability, predictive scaling, capacity planning, and intelligent resource management.
Define automation patterns and operational standards across AWS, Azure, and GCP environments.
Set and improve platform reliability, scalability, performance, security, and cost-efficiency standards.
Evaluate emerging DevOps and AI-driven operational technologies and guide production adoption based on measurable value and risk.


Ownership and Collaboration Responsibilities


Provide technical leadership for the design and delivery of key platform components and DevOps initiatives.
Partner with architects, engineering leaders, senior engineers, and tech leads to deliver high-impact projects.
Plan, prioritize, and deliver strategic projects with clear ownership, milestones, risk management, and accountability.
Drive cross-functional alignment across Product, Engineering, Security, SRE, and customer-facing teams.
Translate business and operational challenges into scalable platform designs, standards, and implementation roadmaps.
Define and uphold high standards for architecture, engineering quality, security, reliability, and operational excellence.
Take full ownership of outcomes, including technical decisions, delivery quality, production readiness, and continuous improvement.


What You Bring/Qualifications:


Strong Linux/Unix administration skills, preferably Red Hat or Ubuntu.
Strong programming skills in Python, with the ability to design maintainable automation frameworks and review production-quality code.
Hands-on experience with AWS, Azure, and/or GCP.
Deep understanding of container platforms and Kubernetes architecture, operations, networking, storage, and security.
Proven hands-on experience designing and operating Kubernetes platforms in SaaS or production environments.
Advanced experience with Helm, Terraform, and reusable Infrastructure as Code patterns.
Hands-on experience implementing GitOps using Argo CD, Flux CD, or equivalent tools.
Strong experience designing and governing CI/CD platforms using Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, or equivalent tools.
Experience implementing secure automation, quality controls, policy enforcement, and release governance within CI/CD workflows.
Strong understanding of microservices, distributed systems, failure modes, and resilient design patterns.
Strong experience with observability platforms and standards, including Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, and OpenTelemetry.
Advanced troubleshooting and root cause analysis skills across distributed, cloud-native environments.
Proven experience architecting or operating highly available, resilient, and recoverable systems.
Working knowledge of AI/ML platforms, Generative AI infrastructure, model observability, and safe operational integration.
Strong knowledge of DevSecOps, supply chain security, secrets management, vulnerability management, and security automation frameworks.
