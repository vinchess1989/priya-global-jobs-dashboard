Knowledge Specialist – Knowledge & Document Services

Position Overview

Ecolab Global Business Solutions (GBS+) supports the business through globally connected service delivery. The Knowledge Specialist – Knowledge & Document Services is an operations-focused role responsible for creating, formatting, maintaining, publishing and improving knowledge and document assets used by GBS+ teams and their internal clients.

The role combines hands-on Knowledge Management with Document-as-a-Service support. The specialist partners with subject matter experts and service teams to turn business content into clear, consistent, user-friendly and professionally formatted knowledge assets. This position is designed as an individual contributor role with limited people-management responsibilities and a strong focus on execution, service quality and day-to-day delivery.

Main Responsibilities

Knowledge Operations


Create, update, quality-check, publish and maintain knowledge content and templates.
Partner with SMEs to capture clear, user-friendly knowledge.
Apply knowledge standards, naming, templates, taxonomy, metadata and repository requirements.
Review, validate, revise, version, archive and retire content.
Keep repository and self-service content accurate, searchable and accessible.
Identify gaps, duplicates, outdated content and formatting issues; coordinate fixes with owners.


Document-as-a-Service Support


Produce and format Word and PowerPoint SOPs/DTPs, job aids, presentations, templates and business communications.
Refine documents while preserving approved meaning, sequence and source information.
Apply consistent layouts, formatting and visuals to approved brand and document standards.
Develop reusable documents and presentation templates for consistency and efficiency.
Check formatting, readability, links, images, completeness, spelling, templates, versions and naming before publishing.
Convert and remediate legacy documents into approved templates and publishing formats.
Improve visual hierarchy and usability without altering approved content or adding unsupported information.


Governance, Quality & Collaboration


Follow GBS+ governance, document, access and content ownership standards.
Ensure accuracy, confidentiality and proper information handling.
Support peer reviews, quality calibration and standard-work updates.
Guide requestors on templates, formatting and publishing requirements.
Collaborate across global teams without direct people-management responsibility.


Basic Qualifications


Relevant bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience, subject to local hiring requirements.
Minimum 6 to 8 years of relevant work experience
Experience with SharePoint or similar knowledge repositories.
Experience in knowledge, document or content operations, or business/shared services.
Strong Word and PowerPoint formatting skills.
Experience producing, maintaining or publishing SOPs and knowledge content.
Strong writing, proofreading, organization and attention to detail.
Ability to manage requests, meet deadlines, follow standards and collaborate.


Preferred Qualifications


Experience in GBS, ServiceNow or shared services.
Experience in document governance, taxonomy, metadata and content lifecycle management.
Advanced Word/PowerPoint formatting or document-production experience.
Experience supporting process documentation, transformation, standardization or transitions.
Exposure to AI-assisted content tools, source-quality checks and human validation.
Excel skills for tracking, reporting or basic analysis.
