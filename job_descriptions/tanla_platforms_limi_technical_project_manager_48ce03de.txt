Role Summary

We are looking for a Technical Project Manager to own delivery of complex software products across multiple teams. You will combine program governance (scope, schedule, risk, stakeholders) with hands-on agile leadership (ceremonies, team health, continuous improvement). You will translate business goals into executable plans, surface risks early, and give leadership reliable data on progress and predictability.

Key Responsibilities

Project and Program Delivery

• Own end-to-end delivery of assigned projects, from initiation and planning through release and post-release stabilization.

• Build and maintain project plans, roadmaps, milestones, and release calendars. Track progress against commitments.

• Identify, track, and close cross-team and external dependencies before they become blockers.

• Maintain the risk, issue, assumption, and change registers, each with a named owner, mitigation, and due date.

• Manage scope through formal change control and assess the impact of changes on timeline, cost, and quality.

• Define acceptance criteria, definition of done, and release exit gates with Product, Engineering, and QA.

• Lead release readiness reviews, including go/no-go decisions, rollback planning, and post-release reviews.

Agile Leadership and Scrum Facilitation

• Facilitate sprint planning, daily stand-ups, backlog refinement, sprint reviews, and retrospectives.

• Coach teams, product owners, and leads on agile principles and practices. Fix ineffective ceremonies rather than adding more of them.

• Protect team focus by removing impediments and shielding the team from scope churn.

• Drive backlog quality: well-written stories, clear priorities, and right-sized work.

• Track and improve team health and delivery metrics (velocity, sprint predictability, cycle time, lead time, defect leakage).

• Support scaled agile ways of working, including quarterly or release-level planning across teams.

Technical Engagement

• Understand system architecture and technical design well enough to challenge estimates, spot integration and migration risks, and flag testing and environment bottlenecks.

• Work with architects and engineering leads to plan non-functional requirements such as performance, scalability, security, reliability, and disaster recovery.

• Ensure quality practices (code review, test automation, CI/CD discipline, release hygiene) are planned into delivery, not bolted on at the end.

Stakeholder Management and Reporting

• Be the single point of accountability for status, risks, and decisions across Product, Engineering, QA, Operations, Support, and business stakeholders.

• Publish regular status reports (RAG, milestones, risks, decisions needed) and prepare concise executive updates.

• Escalate early with options and recommendations, not just problems.

• Run governance forums such as steering committees, program reviews, and dependency syncs.

Process and Continuous Improvement

• Define and improve delivery processes, templates, and standards.

• Keep project tracking data accurate and consistent so dashboards can be trusted.

• Mentor junior project managers, Scrum Masters, and coordinators.

Required Qualifications

• Experience: 8–12 years in software delivery, with at least 4 years as a Project Manager, Technical Program Manager, or Scrum Master on multi-team product development.

• Proven record delivering complex, large-scale software products on time, with measurable results.

• Agile and delivery frameworks: Strong working knowledge of Scrum, Kanban, and scaled agile frameworks, plus traditional planning methods for hybrid delivery.

• Solid technical grounding in the software development lifecycle, architecture concepts, APIs, testing, and release management.

• Demonstrated skill in risk management, dependency management, estimation, and scope control.

• Experience with project tracking, documentation, and reporting tools, including building dashboards and metrics.

• Excellent written and verbal communication. Able to brief an executive in five slides and an engineer in five lines.

• Strong facilitation, negotiation, and conflict-resolution skills, with the ability to influence without direct authority.

• Education: Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.

Preferred Qualifications

• Certifications such as CSM/PSM, SAFe (SPC/RTE), PMP, or PMI-ACP.

• Experience managing distributed or multi-location teams and vendor or partner delivery.

• Exposure to regulated or high-availability environments with strict quality and SLA expectations.

• Familiarity with AI/ML or data-driven product delivery.

• Experience with scripting or data analysis for self-serve reporting.

• Master's degree or MBA.

Key Competencies

• Ownership: Treats delivery outcomes as personally accountable.

• Analytical thinking: Decides with data and quantifies risk.

• Pragmatism: Applies process where it adds value and drops it where it doesn't.

• Technical credibility: Earns the respect of engineers through informed questions.

• Leadership: Builds team trust and drives alignment across functions.
