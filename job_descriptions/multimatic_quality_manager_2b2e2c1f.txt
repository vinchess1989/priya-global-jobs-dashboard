About Us

Multimatic is a global enterprise supplying engineered components, systems and services to the automotive industry. The company’s success has been fueled by its ability to attract and motivate a team of highly skilled and passionate individuals who want to make a difference. With expanding facilities around the world, including in Canada, the US, the UK, Germany, Mexico, Japan and China, there is always room in Multimatic’s growing team for people who share a dedication to technical excellence, quality and enthusiasm.

Scope of Position

The Quality Manager is responsible for the overall leadership, development, implementation, and continuous improvement of the Company's Quality Management System. This position ensures that all products, processes, and manufacturing operations consistently meet customer-specific requirements, applicable industry standards, regulatory requirements, and internal quality objectives.

Operating within a Tier 1 automotive manufacturing and assembly environment, the Quality Assurance Manager oversees quality performance for the Company's product lines, including automotive hinges and Ford running boards/steps. This role works closely with Operations, Engineering, Materials, Maintenance, and external suppliers to ensure product conformity, process capability, defect prevention, and customer satisfaction.

The Quality Manager provides strategic direction and hands-on leadership to the Quality Department, establishes departmental objectives, develops team capabilities, and promotes a culture of accountability, continuous improvement, and quality ownership throughout the organization.

Key Responsibilities And Authorities


Quality Management System and Compliance
Lead the development, implementation, maintenance, and continuous improvement of the Company's Quality Management System in accordance with IATF 16949, ISO 14001, customer-specific requirements, and applicable regulatory standards.
Ensure departmental processes, procedures, work instructions, and documentation remain current, effective, and compliant.
Lead internal and external quality audits, customer audits, certification audits, and follow-up activities.
Ensure timely completion and closure of audit findings, non-conformances, and corrective actions.
Monitor changes to applicable quality standards, customer requirements, and industry regulations and ensure appropriate implementation.
Act as the Quality Management Representative, as required, and fulfill associated responsibilities in their absence.
Ensure compliance with applicable product certification requirements, including CQC/CCC requirements, where applicable.
Maintain oversight of product certification validity, proper use of certification marks, and product conformity to applicable standards.
Customer Quality and Satisfaction
Serve as the primary point of contact for customer quality concerns, escalations, and technical inquiries.
Maintain strong working relationships with customer quality representatives and participate in customer meetings, visits, and quality reviews.
Monitor and analyze customer scorecards, portals, quality KPIs, QOS reporting, and performance metrics.
Lead the investigation, containment, root cause analysis, and resolution of customer complaints and quality concerns.
Ensure timely and effective submission of customer corrective action reports, including 8D reports and other required problem-solving documentation.
Oversee customer-specific requirements, including PPAP submissions, product approvals, and engineering or process changes.
Monitor customer performance trends and proactively implement improvement initiatives to prevent repeat concerns.
Coordinate customer containment activities, sorting, rework, and recovery plans when required.
Ensure customer concerns are communicated effectively across all relevant departments.
Supplier Quality Management
Oversee supplier quality performance and ensure suppliers meet established quality expectations and customer requirements.
Review supplier performance scorecards and quality KPIs on a monthly basis.
Lead supplier corrective action requests, investigations, and follow-up activities.
Ensure appropriate containment and corrective actions are implemented for supplier-related quality issues.
Work collaboratively with Purchasing, Materials, and Operations to address supplier performance concerns.
Participate in supplier assessments, audits, and approval activities as required.
Monitor supplier trends and identify opportunities to improve incoming material quality and supplier performance.
Production and Process Quality
Provide leadership and direction to ensure quality standards are maintained throughout all manufacturing and assembly operations.
Ensure effective implementation and adherence to Control Plans, PFMEAs, Job Instructions, inspection standards, and process documentation.
Review and approve applicable Job Instructions, Control Plans, and quality-related documentation.
Oversee the disposition of quarantined, suspect, and non-conforming material.
Ensure effective identification, segregation, and control of non-conforming product.
Monitor production quality performance, scrap, rework, internal rejects, and first-time-through performance.
Lead the investigation of recurring production issues and drive permanent corrective actions.
Work closely with Operations and Engineering to identify process risks, improve process capability, and prevent defects.
Participate in new program launches, engineering changes, process trials, and production readiness reviews.
Ensure appropriate inspection methods, measurement systems, and testing procedures are established and maintained.
Coordinate external testing and laboratory services when required.
Oversee calibration and verification of inspection, measurement, and testing equipment.
Quality Performance, Reporting and Continuous Improvement
Establish, monitor, and report departmental quality objectives and key performance indicators (KPIs).
Maintain and update Quality Fast Response Boards and actively participate in daily Fast Response Meetings.
Analyze quality trends, customer complaints, scrap, rework, internal rejects, and supplier performance to identify areas requiring improvement.
Develop and implement action plans to achieve established quality objectives and improve overall manufacturing performance.
Lead and participate in cross-functional problem-solving activities using recognized methodologies, including 8D, 5 Why, Fishbone Analysis, and other root cause analysis tools.
Ensure corrective and preventive actions are effective, sustainable, and implemented within established timelines.
Promote defect prevention, process standardization, and continuous improvement throughout the organization.
Participate in and support Kaizen initiatives, GEAR activities, and other corporate improvement programs.
Encourage employee involvement in identifying opportunities to improve product quality, process efficiency, and operational performance.
Prepare and present quality reports, departmental performance updates, and recommendations to Senior Management.
Departmental Leadership and Development
Provide leadership, direction, and supervision to all Quality Department personnel.
Establish departmental objectives, priorities, and performance expectations aligned with overall business objectives.
Ensure adequate staffing, resources, and technical capabilities are maintained within the department.
Conduct annual performance reviews for departmental employees and provide ongoing coaching, feedback, and development.
Identify employee training requirements and submit departmental training needs to Human Resources.
Develop employee competencies through technical training, cross-training, mentoring, and succession planning.
Promote accountability, teamwork, communication, and employee engagement within the Quality Department.
Ensure employees understand and consistently follow established quality procedures and requirements.
Manage departmental budgets, including staffing, equipment, training, calibration, and external testing expenses.
Identify opportunities to improve departmental effectiveness, efficiency, and cost management.
Health and Safety Responsibilities
Ensure all Quality Department personnel perform their duties in accordance with Company health and safety policies, procedures, and applicable legislation.
Promote and maintain a safe working environment through regular workplace inspections and proactive hazard identification.
Participate in health and safety meetings, training sessions, and continuous improvement initiatives.
Investigate incidents, employee complaints, and work refusals as required.
Provide employees with the necessary information, instruction, and supervision to perform their work safely.
Take every reasonable precaution to protect the health and safety of employees.
Identify and ensure timely correction of unsafe conditions, practices, and regulatory non-compliance.
Recognize and reinforce positive health and safety behaviours.
Ensure competent supervision and appropriate employee training within the department.
Support the Company's Health and Safety and Return to Work initiatives.
General Responsibilities
Maintain effective working relationships with all departments, including Operations, Engineering, Materials, Maintenance, Purchasing, and Human Resources.
Participate in departmental and cross-functional meetings, special projects, and business improvement initiatives.
Support the Company's strategic objectives and corporate initiatives.
Ensure all activities are conducted in compliance with applicable Company policies, procedures, customer requirements, and regulatory standards.
Maintain confidentiality of Company, customer, supplier, and employee information.
Perform other related duties and special projects as assigned by Management.
Maintain a professional, collaborative, and results-oriented approach at all times.


Basic Requirements And Competencies

Education and Experience


Post-secondary education and degree in Quality Management, Engineering, Manufacturing Technology, or a related discipline, or an equivalent combination of education and experience.
Minimum of five years of experience in quality within an automotive manufacturing environment.
Minimum of three years of experience in a Quality Management or equivalent leadership position.
Previous experience within a Tier 1 automotive manufacturing and assembly environment is required.
Experience supporting automotive manufacturing processes, preferably metal stamping, forming, welding, assembly, and related production processes.
Strong knowledge and practical experience with IATF 16949 and ISO 14001 requirements.
Experience managing customer quality relationships, including direct communication, customer visits, audits, and escalations.
Demonstrated experience with PPAP, APQP, PFMEA, Control Plans, MSA, SPC, and other core automotive quality tools.
Proven experience leading root cause analysis, corrective action implementation, and cross-functional problem-solving initiatives.
Experience managing supplier quality performance and supplier corrective actions.
Experience preparing departmental budgets, monitoring KPIs, and managing team performance.


Knowledge, Skills And Competencies


Strong leadership, management, and supervisory capabilities, with the ability to develop and motivate a team.
Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and decision-making skills.
Strong understanding of automotive manufacturing processes, production controls, and quality assurance practices.
Ability to interpret engineering drawings, specifications, GD&T, and customer requirements.
Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced manufacturing environment.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to effectively communicate with all levels of the organization and external customers.
Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to work effectively in a multicultural environment.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, Word, and Outlook.
Ability to analyze data, identify trends, and develop actionable improvement plans.
Strong sense of accountability, ownership, urgency, and attention to detail.
Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively with cross-functional teams.
Ability to lead change, influence others, and promote a culture of continuous improvement.
