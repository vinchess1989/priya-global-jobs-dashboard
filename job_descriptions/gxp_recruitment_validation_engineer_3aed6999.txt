Therese Golden

Our client is urgently looking for a skilled Validation Engineer to assist in the development of new manufacturing and test equipment and systems used in the development and production of medical devices. Independently or as member of a team; develops specifications, designs, plans and develops complex manufacturing and test equipment.




Prepare validation protocols for Automated Assembly Equipment and Testing Equipment execute these protocols and ensure sign-off is obtained at each stage.
Ensure software is controlled and stored as per validation procedure guidelines.
Work with Quality department to ensure equipment validations are in line with plant validation standards.
Ensure regulatory compliance with European and US software validation requirements.
Take responsibility for correctly carrying out and maintaining all validation and associated documentation for equipment.
Provides software validation/compliance guidance and training to staff across various functions as required.
Develops and enhances cross-functional team relationships and works cross-functionally to identify and resolve technical and organisational issues.
Creates and executes IV/OT documents and reports, reviews FS's and provides direction and support on validation plans.




Qualifications:

BSc/MSc degree or equivalent in Computer Engineering or related discipline.
Minimum of 4+ years in software development with experience and a knowledge in the following: VB.Net, C#, VB 6, SQL Server, software testing, version and change management software.
Experience in a regulated environment with 21CFR Part 11 experience an advantage.
