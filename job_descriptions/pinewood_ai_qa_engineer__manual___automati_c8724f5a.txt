Katelyn Govender
Pinewood.AI is seeking a technically strong, hands-on QA Engineer to deliver our quality strategy for our Automotive Intelligence Platform.
We are seeking a QA Engineer with a strong manual testing mindset who has some experience with automation testing and takes ownership of product quality and user experience. This role focuses on thoughtful, risk‑based testing, clear communication, and collaboration.
You'll work closely with engineering and product teams to ensure features are fit for purpose, reliable, and releasable, while contributing to continuous improvement of our QA approach.
Key Responsibilities:

Own manual testing for assigned features from refinement to release
Design and execute meaningful test scenarios, not just scripted cases
Perform functional, regression, exploratory, and integration testing
Validate requirements and acceptance criteria
Identify risk, edge cases, and user‑impacting defects early
Provide clear go/no‑go quality assessments for releases
Raise clear, actionable defects with strong reproduction steps
Assess severity, priority, and customer impact
Work closely with developers to support root‑cause analysis
Retest fixes thoroughly and verify acceptance
Engage actively in backlog refinement, planning, and retros
Challenge unclear or incomplete requirements constructively
Communicate quality status, risks, and confidence clearly
Contribute ideas to improve QA practices and consistency
Support regression and release testing activities
Responsible for contributing towards the organisation's information security strategy, ensuring the protection of sensitive data and compliance with relevant regulations, while managing risk and adhering to all established security measures
Work in a hybrid model (approx. 2/3 days onsite)

Requirements

Strong manual testing experience in Agile environments
Experience with at least one automation tool or framework (e.g. Playwright, Cypress, Selenium)
Solid understanding of testing principles and techniques
Ability to test across browsers, devices, and environments
Experience with defect tracking tools (e.g. Jira, Azure DevOps)
API testing basics (e.g. Postman)
Comfortable validating data (basic SQL or equivalent)
Familiarity with version control (Git) is a plus
Thinks like a user, not just a tester
Focuses on risk and impact, not box‑ticking
Cares deeply about shipped quality
Shift left experience in the development cycle

Desirable Experience:

Testing dealer management systems or automotive retail software.
Familiarity with service scheduling, parts inventory, warranty workflows and aftersales processes.
Experience integrating Playwright or similar tools into Microsoft tech stacks and cloud CI pipelines.

Why Join Pinewood.AI?
This is a rare opportunity to help shape the QA function in a fast-growing, innovative automotive software company. You'll work with modern tools and approaches, have a direct impact on release quality, and play a pivotal role in helping us deliver outstanding products to automotive retailers and OEMs across the globe. With a culture that values collaboration, continuous improvement, and technical excellence, Pinewood.AI is the place to make your mark.
About Us:
Our story began more than 20 years ago, but right from the start, it has been rooted in the specific needs of the automotive industry. As automotive professionals as well as technologists, we wanted to build practical technology solutions that were designed around how automotive businesses work, recognising what makes them different. Pinewood.AI is an unparalleled Automotive Intelligence Platform that enables automotive retail customers and OEMs to drive growth and profitability throughout every aspect of their business.
Pinewood's cloud-based, secure end-to-end ecosystem unlocks the value of every customer. Our vision is to be the full-service technology partner that helps automotive retailers and OEMs run more efficiently and increase revenue by making better commercial and business decisions more easily.
