Duties & Responsibilities
Job Purpose
The Intermediate DevOps Engineer is responsible for building, maintaining and supporting automation, CI/CD pipelines and cloud infrastructure to enable product teams to deliver software quickly, safely and reliably. The role involves managing platform tasks, resolving operational issues and contributing to the quality, security, availability and cost efficiency of cloud environments.

Key Responsibilities
Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines for application and infrastructure code.
Automate build, testing, security scanning and deployment processes, and troubleshoot pipeline failures.
Implement and support release strategies, including blue-green deployments, canary releases and automated rollbacks.
Provision and manage cloud infrastructure across AWS, Azure or Google Cloud using Terraform or equivalent tools.
Manage configuration using Ansible, Puppet or Chef and maintain consistent, reproducible environments.
Build and maintain Docker images and support Kubernetes clusters, including networking, scaling and upgrades.
Create and maintain Helm charts or equivalent deployment manifests.
Implement and maintain monitoring, logging, metrics, tracing and alerting across platforms and services.
Participate in on-call support, resolve incidents and escalate major incidents where required.
Contribute to post-incident reviews, backup testing, disaster recovery and capacity planning.
Apply DevSecOps practices, including secrets management, vulnerability scanning, policy as code and access controls.
Support audit, risk and regulatory requirements, including PCI DSS and POPIA where applicable.
Monitor cloud expenditure and assist with resource optimisation, tagging and auto-scaling.
Take ownership of assigned tasks and smaller projects within DevOps and cloud initiatives.
Participate in peer reviews, support junior engineers and share technical knowledge.
Collaborate with development, QA, operations and security teams to improve delivery processes.
Maintain technical documentation, runbooks and standard operating procedures.
Evaluate tools and recommend improvements to the DevOps and Cloud environment.
Minimum Requirements
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
Minimum of 3 years' experience in DevOps, cloud, site reliability or infrastructure engineering.
Hands-on experience delivering CI/CD pipelines and cloud infrastructure in production environments.
Experience working in Agile teams.
Experience in fintech, payments or telecommunications will be advantageous.
Technical Skills
Good working knowledge of CI/CD tools such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps or Jenkins.
Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes, including Helm and managed services such as EKS, AKS or GKE.
Infrastructure as Code experience using Terraform or equivalent tools.
Working experience with at least one major cloud platform: AWS, Azure or Google Cloud.
Configuration management experience with Ansible, Puppet or Chef.
Scripting experience in Python, Bash or PowerShell. Go will be advantageous.
Knowledge of monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, ELK or Datadog.
Understanding of DevSecOps practices, including secrets management, vulnerability scanning and policy as code.
Good Linux administration, networking and Git workflow knowledge.
Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to solve problems calmly under pressure.
Professional Certifications
At least one relevant certification, such as:

AWS Certified SysOps Administrator or Developer – Associate.
Microsoft Certified: Azure Administrator Associate (AZ-104).
Google Associate Cloud Engineer.
The following certifications will be advantageous:

Microsoft Certified: Azure DevOps Engineer Expert.
Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA).
HashiCorp Terraform Associate.
Key Competencies
Analytical thinking and problem-solving.
Assertiveness, confidence and adaptability.
Accountability and a strong focus on quality.
Teamwork and collaboration.
Clear communication and relationship-building.
Initiative and a willingness to learn.
Planning, organisation and time management.
Resilience and the ability to work under pressure.
Customer focus and a good understanding of business needs.
Automation, scripting and cloud platform administration.
Incident management and technical documentation.
Only candidates who meet the essential requirements will be considered.
