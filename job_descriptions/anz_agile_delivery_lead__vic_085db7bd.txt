About Us

At ANZ, we're shaping a world where people and communities thrive, driven by a common goal: to improve the financial wellbeing and sustainability of our millions of customers.

About The Role

As an Agile Delivery Lead in ANZ's Digital Channel team, you'll lead the end-to-end delivery of initiatives across multiple squads using agile frameworks. You'll enable teams to deliver business value, achieve high performance and continuously improve across people, processes and technology.

Role Type: Fixed - Term (12 Months)

Role Location: 839 Collins Street, Docklands VIC 3008

Work Hours: 40 hours/week

What will your day look like?

As an Agile Delivery Lead, you are accountable for:


Lead end-to-end delivery of agile initiatives, ensuring business value is delivered within agreed scope, time and quality.
Define, manage and communicate delivery plans, milestones, risks, issues, dependencies and progress to stakeholders.
Shape and mobilise work by clarifying scope, outcomes, design, dependencies, estimates, test approach and delivery milestones.
Remove impediments, resolve issues and maintain momentum across squads and contributing teams.
Partner with Product Owners to prioritise backlogs, align delivery to strategic objectives and identify measures of value.
Maintain delivery transparency and governance through clear decisions, actions, Jira standards, deployment planning and production readiness.



What will you bring?


Demonstrated experience leading end-to-end delivery of initiatives using agile frameworks across one or more teams.
Strong delivery planning, risk, issue, dependency and stakeholder management skills.
Practical experience facilitating agile ceremonies, coaching teams and improving agile maturity.
Ability to work across business and technical teams, remove blockers and maintain focus on outcomes.
Experience using Jira and delivery metrics to create transparency and support continuous improvement.
Sound understanding of delivery governance, risk management, compliance and production readiness.
