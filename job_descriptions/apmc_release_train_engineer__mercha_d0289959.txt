Introduction

We're accepting applications for the position of Release Train Engineer: Merchant Delivery. The Business Payments Agile Release Train delivers multiple ways for the businesses to accept payments across channels whilst offering a range of attractive value-added services that go well beyond just payments. The company offers a Smartphone Application across Android and iOS, with global payment capabilities which includes international remittance, and business tools serving merchants and agents supporting the company's vision. The role translates agreed product priorities into an integrated delivery plan, with clear milestones, dependencies, resource requirements and release commitments. It manages execution from discovery and planning through development, integration, testing, market readiness, launch and post-launch stabilisation.

Working within the scaled agile framework (SAFe), you will perform all aspects of the Release Train Engineer role. This includes being accountable for and facilitating the execution of ART processes. Your role will be to manage impediments, escalating, when necessary, to manage risk, to help ensure value delivery and help drive relentless improvement within your Agile Release Train and across your teams. You will also have an opportunity to directly support Lean-Agile Transformation to the Target Operating model, helping to coach leaders, teams and Scrum Masters and develop the “agile mindset”. Success means delivering reliable merchant capabilities at pace, with the technical, operational and market readiness needed to support adoption and business value.

This is a 12 month contract position in Cape Town.
