About The Role

We are seeking a Senior DevOps Engineer to join our Release Management team in

Montreal. In this role, you will serve as the dedicated point of coverage for managing and

coordinating production releases during EST business hours, ensuring timely release

execution and operational stability across our environments.

You will play a central role in release governance — planning, coordinating, and executing

production deployments while working closely with development, QA, and infrastructure

teams to minimize delivery risk and keep production activities running smoothly.

Key Responsibilities


Manage and coordinate production releases end to end, including scheduling,


execution, validation, and rollback planning


Provide dedicated release coverage during EST business hours, acting as the


primary point of contact for release activities in the region


Maintain and enforce release governance processes, ensuring changes move to


production in a controlled, auditable manner


Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines to support reliable, repeatable


deployments


Automate build, deployment, and operational tasks using Python and shell scripting
Support and troubleshoot containerized workloads running on Docker and


Kubernetes


Apply SRE and production engineering practices: monitoring, alerting, incident


response, and post-incident review


Diagnose and resolve issues spanning application, network, and Linux


infrastructure layers


Collaborate with development and QA teams to improve deployment frequency,


reduce lead time, and lower change failure rates


Document release procedures, runbooks, and operational processes


Required Qualifications


4+ years of hands-on experience in DevOps, SRE, or production engineering roles
Strong experience managing or coordinating production releases in an enterprise


environment


Proficiency with CI/CD tooling and pipeline design; hands-on experience with


GitHub and GitHub Actions (or comparable platforms)


Solid experience with Docker and Kubernetes in production environments
Strong scripting skills in Python and shell (Bash)
Strong Linux administration and troubleshooting skills
Working knowledge of networking fundamentals (DNS, load balancing, firewalls,


TCP/IP)


Experience with monitoring, logging, and observability tooling
Ability to work on-site in Montreal 3 days per week and provide coverage during EST


business hours

Nice to Have


Experience with infrastructure as code (Terraform, Ansible, or similar)
Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
Experience in regulated or change-controlled environments
