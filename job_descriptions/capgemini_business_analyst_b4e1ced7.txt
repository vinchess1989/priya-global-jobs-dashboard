About Capgemini

Capgemini is an AI-powered global business and technology transformation partner, delivering tangible business value. We imagine the future of organizations and make it real with AI, technology and people. With our strong heritage of nearly 60 years, we are a responsible and diverse group of over 420,000 team members in more than 50 countries. We deliver end-to-end services and solutions with our deep industry expertise and strong partner ecosystem, leveraging our capabilities across strategy, technology, design, engineering and business operations. The Group reported 2025 global revenues of €22.5 billion.




Job Responsibilities

Engage stakeholders to gather, analyse, and document functional and non-functional requirements
Identify system enhancement opportunities and translating business needs into business requirement documents
Review implementation plans, manage application product backlogs, and collaborate with business stakeholders, external parties, and ICT providers for operational, maintenance, and enhancement activities
Design, develop, and execute automated test scripts such as using Tricentis Tosca for functional and regression testing across frontend, backend, and integration workflows
Validate system enhancements against business expectations, track and manage defects from creation to closure, and ensure project delivery compliance with relevant policies and guidelines; and
Provide regular updates on testing/work progress, backlog status, system enhancements, defects, and operational issues to project stakeholder.




Job Requirements

At least 3 years of experience as business analyst or equivalent position, preferably in Agile projects
Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Information Technology, Business/Data Analytics or related discipline is preferred
Good planning, communication and stakeholder management skills to effectively engage internal and external stakeholders to gather and provide project updates across technical and business users
Performing tasks including project coordination & monitoring, asset management, resource tracking, meeting logistics, service/change request processing, records/document management, etc, for projects with vendors and internal teams
Perform requirement gathering, requirement analysis, business process mapping, design thinking, prepare requirement specifications, flow charts, logical diagrams, initiate process improvements and testing
Experienced in driving product development through Agile methodologies, leveraging Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and testing practices to iteratively deliver, validate and enhance product outcomes
Experienced in using automation tools for designing, developing and executing automated test scripts across functional and regression testing
