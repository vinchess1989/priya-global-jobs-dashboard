Help take a next-generation class III medical device to the US.




Our client is designing and manufacturing a Class III medical device, and their latest device is generating real excitement among leading surgeons, has strong clinical momentum behind it, and is now moving towards the US market.




As they prepare for FDA, they're growing their quality systems team and adding a Senior Quality Engineer.




The role




You'll take ownership of significant parts of the ISO 13485 quality management system in a small, growing team, where you'll have a real say in how quality is done. Your focus areas will include:




Supplier management
Equipment validation
Document control
Validating the business and QMS software the company uses
Supporting FDA readiness across the business




What you'll bring




Strong hands-on experience with ISO 13485, either across several quality engineering roles or by owning large parts of a QMS
Proven experience in supplier management and equipment validation
A background working in an electronic QMS
An all-rounder mindset: you're happy to turn your hand to whatever the business needs




What would set you apart




Experience at an FDA-registered manufacturer
Software or computer system validation




Why this role

An innovative implant with a clear route to the US market
A visible role where your work directly shapes the company's FDA readiness
The chance to see an FDA journey first-hand.
