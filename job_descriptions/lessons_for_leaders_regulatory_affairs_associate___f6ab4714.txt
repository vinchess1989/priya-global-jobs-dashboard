Role Description

We are seeking detail-oriented, analytical, and highly organized professionals to join our Regulatory Affairs and Compliance team. This role focuses on ensuring organizational compliance with applicable regulatory requirements, coordinating regulatory submissions, maintaining accurate compliance documentation, and supporting regulatory approval processes. The successful candidate will collaborate with quality assurance, legal, research and development, product management, and business operations teams to ensure products, services, and business activities meet relevant regulatory standards and industry requirements.

Key Responsibilities:

Support regulatory affairs activities, including regulatory documentation, compliance monitoring, and submission coordination.
Prepare, review, and maintain regulatory applications, technical documentation, registration dossiers, and supporting compliance records.
Monitor changes in relevant laws, industry regulations, regulatory guidelines, and compliance requirements.
Collaborate with internal departments to ensure products, processes, and business activities comply with applicable regulatory standards.
Coordinate regulatory submissions, product registrations, license applications, renewals, and approval processes.
Communicate with regulatory authorities, external consultants, and relevant stakeholders regarding regulatory requirements and submission status.
Review product information, technical specifications, labeling, and promotional materials for regulatory compliance.
Maintain accurate regulatory databases, approval records, submission schedules, and compliance documentation.
Conduct regulatory research and assess the potential impact of new regulations on business operations and product development.
Support internal regulatory audits, compliance assessments, and regulatory inspections.
Assist in identifying regulatory risks and developing appropriate corrective and preventive action plans.
Provide regulatory guidance to product development, quality assurance, marketing, and operational teams.
Coordinate with cross-functional stakeholders to ensure timely completion of regulatory submissions and compliance-related projects.
Prepare regulatory reports, compliance updates, and management presentations regarding regulatory developments.
Support the implementation and improvement of regulatory procedures, internal controls, and compliance management systems.
Monitor regulatory approval timelines, submission requirements, and documentation accuracy to ensure efficient regulatory processes.
Assist in developing regulatory strategies for new product launches, market expansion, and changes to existing products.
Maintain professional relationships with regulatory agencies, certification bodies, and external compliance partners.
For managerial positions, oversee regulatory affairs operations, develop regulatory strategies, supervise team members, and ensure effective management of regulatory submissions and compliance activities.
Qualifications
Diploma or Bachelor's degree in Life Sciences, Pharmaceutical Sciences, Biomedical Sciences, Chemistry, Biotechnology, Business Administration, Regulatory Affairs, or a related discipline.
Relevant professional experience in regulatory affairs, regulatory compliance, quality assurance, product registration, or related regulatory functions.
Strong understanding of regulatory frameworks, industry standards, compliance procedures, and regulatory submission requirements.
Familiarity with regulatory agencies and frameworks relevant to the industry, such as Singapore's Health Sciences Authority (HSA), the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), or the European Medicines Agency (EMA), where applicable.
Experience preparing regulatory submissions, registration documents, compliance reports, and technical documentation.
Good knowledge of regulatory approval processes, product registration procedures, and applicable licensing requirements.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel, Word, PowerPoint, and Outlook.
Strong analytical and research skills with the ability to interpret regulatory guidelines, technical documents, and compliance requirements.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to communicate effectively with regulatory authorities and internal stakeholders.
Strong organizational and time management abilities with the capacity to manage multiple regulatory submissions and project deadlines.
High attention to detail when reviewing technical documentation, regulatory applications, and compliance records.
Familiarity with quality management systems (QMS), standard operating procedures (SOPs), and document control processes.
Knowledge of applicable international standards, such as ISO 13485, ISO 9001, or Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP), depending on the relevant industry.
Strong problem-solving abilities with the capacity to identify regulatory risks and recommend appropriate compliance solutions.
Ability to collaborate effectively with research and development, quality assurance, legal, manufacturing, and commercial teams.
Experience supporting regulatory audits, inspections, compliance assessments, or certification processes is an advantage.
Understanding of regulatory intelligence, risk assessment, change control, and regulatory impact analysis.
Familiarity with electronic document management systems, regulatory information management systems (RIMS), or compliance management platforms is preferred.
Ability to work independently while maintaining professional communication with cross-functional stakeholders.
Strong adaptability and willingness to stay informed about evolving regulatory requirements and industry developments.
Professional certifications in Regulatory Affairs, such as Regulatory Affairs Certification (RAC), are an advantage.
For managerial positions, demonstrated experience in regulatory strategy development, regulatory submission management, team leadership, stakeholder coordination, and compliance risk management is preferred.
