Lead the design and delivery of Salesforce solutions that drive business transformation & customer engagement.

Tō kamupene hou | Your new company


Join a well-established organisation embarking on an exciting phase of digital transformation and customer experience enhancement. With Salesforce at the heart of its technology strategy, the organisation is investing in modern platforms, process optimisation, and data-driven decision-making to better serve its customers and stakeholders.



Tō tūranga hou | Your new role


As the Salesforce Functional Lead, you will act as the bridge between business stakeholders and technical delivery teams, leading the design and optimisation of Salesforce solutions across Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, and related platform capabilities. You will drive requirements gathering, solution design, stakeholder engagement, and functional leadership throughout the project life cycle, ensuring business outcomes are translated into scalable and effective Salesforce solutions.



Ngā uara me ngā pūkenga e angitu ai koe | What you'll need to succeed


Proven experience as a Salesforce Functional Lead or Lead Consultant for enterprise Salesforce programmes.
Strong knowledge of Salesforce capabilities including Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Experience Cloud, or related products.
Ability to facilitate workshops, gather requirements, and translate business needs into functional solutions.
Strong stakeholder management skills with experience engaging business leaders, end users, and technical teams.
Experience working within Agile delivery environments and collaborating with Salesforce developers, architects, and implementation partners.
Salesforce certifications such as Salesforce Administrator, Business Analyst, Consultant, or similar would be highly regarded.
