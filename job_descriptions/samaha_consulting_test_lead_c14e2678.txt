Introduction

The Mid-Level Test Lead will be responsible for leading and managing end-to-end testing and quality engineering activities across complex telecommunications IT environments. The role requires developing test strategies, test plans, quality standards and release-readiness criteria while coordinating functional, API, integration, regression and non-functional testing. The candidate should drive test automation, continuous testing and CI/CD integration, implement Agile and DevOps practices such as TDD, BDD and shift-left testing, and manage defect tracking, test coverage, quality metrics and risk assessments. The ideal candidate must have a minimum of 5 years of software testing and quality engineering experience, preferably within telecommunications environments such as OSS/BSS, B2B or wholesale telecom, along with knowledge of TM Forum frameworks, eTOM, SID and TMF Open APIs. ISTQB certification or equivalent is desirable.

Duties & Responsibilities

Define and manage overall test strategies, master test plans and detailed test plans.

Establish quality gates, testing standards and release-readiness criteria.

Lead and coordinate testing activities across multiple teams and integrated environments.

Develop test automation approaches and integrate automated testing into CI/CD pipelines.

Implement Agile and DevOps testing practices, including TDD, BDD, shift-left and continuous testing.

Oversee functional, API, integration, regression and non-functional testing.

Ensure end-to-end requirements traceability through requirements traceability matrices (RTM).

Manage test data, test environments and test coverage.

Lead defect identification, tracking, triage, prioritisation and resolution.

Monitor testing progress and prepare quality dashboards and reports.

Analyse test results, identify quality risks and recommend corrective actions.

Provide evidence-based recommendations on release readiness.

Coordinate with business stakeholders, developers, architects, security and infrastructure teams.

Prepare test completion reports, risk assessments and governance documentation.

Maintain testing documentation, conduct knowledge-transfer sessions and prepare handover packs.

Ensure compliance with delivery standards, security requirements and governance processes.

Desired Experience & Qualification

Minimum 5 years of experience in software testing and quality engineering.

Experience leading or coordinating testing activities in complex IT delivery environments.

Strong experience in telecommunications IT environments, preferably OSS/BSS, wholesale or enterprise telecom programmes.

Experience developing test strategies, master test plans and detailed test plans.

Hands-on exposure to test automation frameworks and automated regression testing.

Experience integrating automated tests into CI/CD pipelines.

Strong understanding of Agile and DevOps testing methodologies.

Experience with:

Functional Testing

API Testing

Integration Testing

Regression Testing

Non-Functional Testing

Risk-Based Testing

Continuous Testing

Test Automation

Knowledge of TDD (Test-Driven Development) and BDD (Behaviour-Driven Development).

Experience with defect management, test metrics, quality gates and release management.

Understanding of requirements traceability, test data management and test environment readiness.

Working knowledge of TM Forum frameworks, including:

TM Forum Frameworx

eTOM

SID

TMF Open APIs

Understanding of telecom business processes such as order-to-activate, service provisioning, service assurance and billing.

Experience working with B2B and wholesale telecommunications customers and partners.
