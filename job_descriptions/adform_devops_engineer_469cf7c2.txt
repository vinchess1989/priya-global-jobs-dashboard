About the Role and Team:

Adform is a leading global advertising technology provider. As a company, we are at the forefront of the industry and, through the combination of human competence and outstanding software, aspire to outperform any other platform and deliver unparalleled returns for our clients. We balance the pursuit of extraordinary results with care for each other.

Our ambition in India means that we are building a strong, high-performing DevOps Services and Platform Engineering presence to supplement our great DEV&IT organization.

This team is responsible for owning, maintaining, and driving code and deployment platforms to enable our development teams to release and deploy their code efficiently and to the highest quality standards.

We hope you are ready to change the game! Be ready to:


Maintain internal GitHub Enterprise and Artifactory instances
Manage and automate on-premises and cloud-based GitHub Actions infrastructure
Abstract Kubernetes deployments via internal Helm charts
Act as owners of and advisors on best CI/CD practices and approaches for the development teams
Hands-on contributions to CI/CD pipelines of Adform products
Collaborate with Adform Security team on code vulnerability scanning
Provide internal tooling related to change management and deployment gating
Introduce tools to improve overall code quality and drive best practices
Write and maintain internal automation tooling within the team services scope
Build agents and automations that improve team workflows, productivity, and overall efficiency


Attitude First. Everything else will follow. We can grow together faster if you have:


A minimum of 5 years’ experience in the DevOps field
Working experience managing GitHub Enterprise and Artifactory instances
Hands-on experience writing GitHub Actions workflows
Strong understanding of CI/CD workflows and practices
Hands-on experience with Linux and Windows operating systems
Experience working with configuration-as-code and infrastructure-as-code systems
Experience writing Helm charts
Ability to use AI tools effectively for coding and investigations while critically validating outputs and avoiding blind reliance on generated results
Ability to read and understand code in Python or Go languages
Advanced experience in working with Docker


Stand out by having:


Experience working with Puppet and Terraform
Experience maintaining code quality tooling (e.g., Snyk)
Experience deploying and managing Kubernetes applications
Knowledge of the agile software development cycle


Our promise to you:


You will have the opportunity to learn and grow daily and gain exposure to modern technologies
Opportunities to learn and work with experts from different countries, with offices around the world
You will work with highly professional and knowledgeable colleagues who share local and global objectives
Informal style of leadership and communication – but with the highest expectations of performance and professionalism
You will be given challenging and unique tasks that require a creative approach to problem-solving
You will be surrounded by a dynamic, inspiring, and international environment that also provides security
Opportunity to take an international business trip once a year
Brand new office
Premium health insurance with ₹10,00,000 coverage for you and your family
24 paid vacation days to enjoy life outside of work
Paid maternity (6 months) and paternity (2 weeks)
Annual learning budget to help you grow your skills and support continuous learning
Rewarding referral program for helping us grow
Global perks such as birthday gifts, work anniversaries, and company events to connect with colleagues and have fun
And much more – join us to explore the full experience
