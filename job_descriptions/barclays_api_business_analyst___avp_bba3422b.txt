Join us as a API Business Analyst - AVP at Barclays, where you'll spearhead the evolution of our digital landscape, driving innovation and excellence. You will be working as Business Analyst to bridge the gap between business stakeholders and software engineering teams within a banking environment. The successful candidate will be responsible for understanding business needs, translating them into detailed technical requirements, supporting agile delivery teams, and ensuring that technology solutions align with business objectives, regulatory requirements, and customer expectations. To support the organisation, achieve its strategic objectives by the identification of business requirements and solutions that address business problems and opportunities.

To be a successful as API Business Analyst - AVP, you should have experience with:



Analyse banking products and processes including:
Retail and Digital Banking
Payments and Cards Processing
Lending
Savings & Current Accounts
Customer Onboarding and KYC
Ensure solutions comply with banking regulations, risk controls, security requirements, and internal governance standards.Work closely with software engineers throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
Participate in Agile ceremonies including sprint planning, backlog refinement, reviews, and retrospectives.
Prioritize and manage product backlogs in collaboration with Product Owners.
Support development teams with requirement clarifications and business context.
Perform data analysis and validation using SQL and reporting tools.
Define data requirements and support data migration initiatives.
Assist in creating business reports, dashboards, and KPIs.



Some Other Highly Valued Skills May Include



Strong understanding of SDLC, Agile, Scrum, and DevOps practices.
Understanding of software engineering concepts and system architecture
SQL and data analysis
API and integration understanding (REST, JSON, XML)
Process Mapping
Gap Analysis
Stakeholder Management
Experience with Agile tools such as Jira, AWS, DevOps, or Confluence
Retail and Digital Banking
Payments and Card Processing
KYC / AML Compliance
Core Banking Systems
Financial Products and Services
Risk and Regulatory Requirements
Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities
Excellent communication and presentation skills
Ability to work with both technical and non-technical stakeholders
Strong facilitation and negotiation skills
Attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities
Proven ability to read and translate Open API/Swagger Specs and UCRs to defi ne and document API requirements (user stories, acceptance criteria, data models)
Experience delivering API/integration change across the full lifecycle, working with engineering, architecture, and 3rd parties. 



You may be assessed on key critical skills relevant for success in role, such as risk and controls, change and transformation, business acumen, strategic thinking and digital and technology, as well as job-specific technical skills.

This role is based in Bengaluru.

Purpose of the role

To support the organisation, achieve its strategic objectives by the identification of business requirements and solutions that address business problems and opportunities.

Accountabilities



Identification and analysis of business problems and client requirements that require change within the organisation.
Development of business requirements that will address business problems and opportunities.
Collaboration with stakeholders to ensure that proposed solutions meet their needs and expectations.
Support the creation of business cases that justify investment in proposed solutions.
Conduct feasibility studies to determine the viability of proposed solutions.
Support the creation of reports on project progress to ensure proposed solutions are delivered on time and within budget.
Creation of operational design and process design to ensure that proposed solutions are delivered within the agreed scope.
Support to change management activities, including development of a traceability matrix to ensure proposed solutions are successfully implemented and embedded in the organisation.
