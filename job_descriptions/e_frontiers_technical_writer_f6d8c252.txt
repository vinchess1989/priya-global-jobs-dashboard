Job Description for Technical Writer (Maintenance & Transition) We’re seeking a highly qualified Technical Writer to support a critical infrastructure transition. This role combines technical documentation with maintenance process mapping, ensuring core digital public infrastructure is fully documented for handover to a dedicated Maintenance Team. The ideal candidate will translate complex software engineering concepts into clear runbooks, incident playbooks, and user guides. You will extract knowledge from subject matter experts (SMEs) across agile delivery squads and build a centralized documentation library within Azure DevOps, ensuring long-term system stability, security, and efficient maintenance. Objectives of this Role ● Lead the documentation strategy to ensure the successful transition of digital services from development to stable maintenance. ● Develop Maintenance Runbooks, Incident Response Playbooks, and system architecture maps for the incoming Maintenance Team. ● Identify knowledge silos and documentation gaps across agile squads, capturing critical system information before project close-outs. ● Build strong relationships with developers, architects, and product owners as a trusted knowledge capture specialist. ● Manage the documentation lifecycle from drafting to publication, aligning engineering realities with maintenance needs. ● Drive continuous improvement in DSU documentation standards, establishing reusable templates and style guides. Responsibilities ● Lead knowledge extraction and produce Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), system recovery guides, and L2/L3 support manuals. ● Facilitate stakeholder workshops and SME interviews to ensure technical accuracy and completeness. ● Coordinate documentation activities with Project Managers, Tech Leads, Engineers, and external partners. ● Oversee the DSU documentation repository in Azure DevOps, ensuring proper structure, version control, and access. ● Prepare and publish user-friendly integration guides and training manuals for external line departments and vendors. Required Skills and Qualifications ● 5+ years of experience in technical writing or knowledge management within complex IT/software engineering environments. ● Demonstrated experience delivering large-scale system runbooks, API documentation, and architecture guides. ● Strong proficiency in Azure DevOps for repository governance, document management, and version control (Key Requirement). ● Solid understanding of DevOps practices, Agile methodologies, cloud infrastructure, and modern SDLC. ● Excellent written communication, interviewing, and editing skills with precise attention to clarity and detail. Preferred Skills and Qualifications ● Professional certification in Technical Writing or Information Design. ● Experience documenting Microservices architectures, APIs, and cloud-native platforms. ● Experience working within the public service or managing documentation across multi-vendor delivery environments.
