CPB Contractors is Australia’s leading contractor and the largest infrastructure construction company in our region. Part of the CIMIC Group, we are proud to be constructing Australia’s future and do so collaboratively with our partners and clients.

We hire for careers, not project life. CPB Contractors has more than 50 major infrastructure projects in delivery, across all key sectors of the construction industry, including roads and bridges, rail, tunnelling, defence, airports, buildings and resources, providing you with career opportunities for years to come.

The Project

CPB is working on a large infrastructure project as part of building Queensland’s future. We are currently seeking an experienced Quality Engineer to join our project delivery team on a project based in initially Brisbane until you transfer on to the project.

Your Opportunity

As a Quality Engineer, reporting directly to the Quality Manager, you will be responsible for the management and facilitation of all site-specific quality assurance requirements and providing day-to-day support to project team members to ensure that specified project quality requirements are met.

You will be responsible for:

Monitoring and coordinate closure of project delivery records and ensure completeness of records to Client requirements, including Lots and ITPs.
Working with Engineers to coordinate inspections, identifying and investigating any quality issues.
Assisting in monitoring and maintaining the requirements of the Project Quality Management System and systems for data, document and record management.
Maintaining quality registers and activities across the sites.

Minimum requirements for this role include the following:

Bachelor’s degree in Engineering or similar (desirable). 
Quality Management experience on large infrastructure projects.
Strong understanding of relevant Australian and International standards, both technical and those related management systems.
Internal Auditor and/or Lead Auditor qualifications highly desirable.
