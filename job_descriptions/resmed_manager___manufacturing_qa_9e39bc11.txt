As the leader of the Quality Assurance Product Focus Team (PFT) in Tuas, you’ll drive Manufacturing Quality, Availability and Value across Resmed’s operations. Your work helps protect patient safety while improving performance and innovation in how our products are made.

You’ll lead a team that uses risk-based and data-driven quality methods to strengthen manufacturing systems and deliver consistent, reliable outcomes. Working closely with engineering, operations and digital teams, you’ll embed quality into every stage of production to uphold Resmed’s reputation for trusted, high-quality healthcare technology.

What You’ll Do


Lead the QA PFT team to achieve Manufacturing Quality, Availability and Value objectives through risk-based Quality Engineering strategies that safeguard patients, customers and performance.
Drive Quality by Design and manufacturing readiness for new products, processes and technologies, ensuring robust quality controls through all cross-functional deliverables and phase transitions.
Establish and refine Manufacturing QA standards, methods and tools to enhance consistency, scalability and engineering capability across global manufacturing teams.
Provide quality leadership for major strategic initiatives, including automation, MES adoption, site expansion and new production lines, ensuring quality readiness and risk mitigation.
Use manufacturing data and leading indicators to anticipate risks, eliminate recurrences, strengthen Right First Time performance and drive continuous improvement.
Advance QA transformation toward preventive and system-enforced quality controls using digital and data-driven technologies.
Lead quality governance, risk management and compliance with Resmed QMS and relevant regulatory standards.
Build collaborative partnerships across engineering, operations and digital functions, embedding quality in manufacturing design and execution.
Coach, develop and empower a high-performing QA PFT team with a focus on accountability and continuous improvement.



What You Bring


Bachelor's degree in Engineering or Science with at least 8 years’ experience in regulated volume manufacturing, including Quality Engineering leadership.
Deep understanding of Manufacturing Quality, Quality Systems, risk management, process validation and Quality by Design principles.
Proven success leading complex quality initiatives, managing risks and driving systemic improvements.
Experience in readiness planning and risk-based assurance for manufacturing technologies and processes.
Strong analytical skills with the ability to use data, trends and leading indicators for preventive decision making.
Effective leadership, coaching and collaboration skills within cross-functional and matrixed environments.
Demonstrated ability to deliver strategic priorities and outcomes at scale.



Nice to Have



Experience in Quality and/or Regulatory leadership in medical device manufacturing.
Familiarity with automation, MES, digital quality, CSV/CSA and advanced manufacturing technologies.
Exposure to AI, analytics and data-driven technologies for improving quality performance.
Track record of developing QA capabilities and leading organizational change.



What Sets You Apart


Strategic Quality Leadership - You inspire teams to connect compliance with business excellence.
Preventive Mindset - You naturally look upstream to mitigate risks before they occur.
Data-Driven Thinking - You translate metrics and signals into action that brings measurable improvement.
Collaborative Partnership - You thrive in cross-functional settings and build strong working relationships.
Continuous Improvement Orientation - You’re motivated by making good processes even better.
Coaching Focus - You enable growth and accountability within your team.



Why Join Resmed Resmed exists to pioneer innovative solutions that help millions breathe, sleep and live healthier lives. In our manufacturing environment, your leadership will directly enhance product quality and reliability that patients depend on every day. You’ll work with diverse global teams committed to continuous improvement, innovative technology and operational excellence—each contributing to better healthcare outcomes around the world.

Joining us is more than saying “yes” to making the world a healthier place. It’s discovering a career that’s challenging, supportive and inspiring. Where a culture driven by excellence helps you not only meet your goals, but also create new ones. We focus on creating a diverse and inclusive culture, encouraging individual expression in the workplace and thrive on the innovative ideas this generates.
