Job Title: Platform Reliability and Release Manager

Jisc Grade: TGE3

Salary: c £50,000 per annum (negotiable based on experience) + comprehensive benefits package

Job Type: 12 month Fixed Term Contract

Hours: 35 per week

Reports into: Head of DXP Operations

Working style: Hybrid - A blend of working from home and your nominated hub office. We have hubs located in London, Bristol, Manchester and Oxford.




About Jisc:

At Jisc, we're driven by a simple ambition: to use technology to transform education, research and innovation.

For more than 30 years, we've been the digital partner for the UK's education and research sectors, providing world-class networks, innovative digital services and expert guidance that help universities, colleges and research institutions thrive.




Our work supports millions of learners, educators and researchers every day, enabling everything from groundbreaking discoveries and life-changing research to exceptional digital learning experiences. While many people may not realise it, the technology and services we provide help power some of the UK's most important educational and research achievements.




Join Jisc and you'll be part of a purpose-driven organisation where your ideas can have a real impact. Together, we're shaping the future of learning, research and innovation for the benefit of society.




Shape the stability, scalability and future of Jisc's Digital Experience Platform

At Jisc, we're on a mission to empower education, research and innovation through world-class digital services. Behind every seamless customer experience is a reliable, secure and high-performing platform. We're looking for a Platform Reliability & Release Manager to take ownership of that mission and play a pivotal role in ensuring our Digital Experience Platform operates at its very best.

This is an exciting opportunity for someone who thrives at the intersection of platform engineering, release management and operational excellence. You'll lead the strategy, processes and technologies that keep our critical platforms running smoothly while enabling the safe and rapid delivery of new features and improvements.




Why this role matters

As our Platform Reliability & Release Manager, you'll be responsible for the resilience, availability and continuous evolution of Jisc's Digital Experience Platform (DXP), built primarily on Salesforce, MuleSoft and Copado.




You'll be the driving force behind reliable platform operations, high-quality releases and continuous improvement. Whether it's preventing service disruption, automating operational processes, improving release pipelines or leading platform upgrades, you'll help ensure that thousands of users benefit from secure, stable and scalable digital services.




This is more than a technical leadership role. It's an opportunity to influence how Jisc delivers change, manages risk and improves the experience of our members, customers and colleagues.




What you'll be doing




Own platform reliability and performance

Lead the day-to-day operational integrity of the Digital Experience Platform.
Drive platform stability, resilience, scalability and service availability.
Champion a proactive approach to monitoring, incident prevention and continuous improvement.
Identify opportunities to improve performance, reduce risk and optimise costs.

Lead enterprise release management

Define and own the release and environment management strategy across Salesforce, MuleSoft and related technologies.
Oversee the full release lifecycle, ensuring high-quality, low-risk deployments.
Coordinate complex releases across multiple environments and stakeholder groups.
Establish release readiness criteria, manage dependencies and minimise deployment risks.

Accelerate automation and DevOps practices

Leverage software engineering principles to automate operational activities and improve platform efficiency.
Enhance CI/CD capabilities and deployment processes.
Drive the adoption of DevOps and DevSecOps best practices across the platform landscape.

Manage environments and platform tooling

Lead platform upgrades, maintenance activities and security patching.
Oversee sandbox management, environment synchronisation and data refresh strategies.
Manage associated tooling including Copado and related release management technologies.
Ensure teams are equipped with the processes, tooling and guidance required for successful delivery.

Collaborate and influence

Partner closely with development teams, security specialists, architects and operational stakeholders.
Lead release communications and change governance activities, including CAB engagement.
Act as a trusted technical leader, influencing platform strategy and championing best practice.




What we’re looking for

You’ll be someone who enjoys solving complex operational challenges and creating structure in fast-moving technology environments. You’ll combine technical expertise with leadership, communication and planning skills to deliver reliable outcomes.




You'll bring experience in:

Platform operations, reliability engineering or release management.
Managing multiple environments across development, testing and production.
Salesforce, MuleSoft, Copado or similar enterprise platforms.
Release management, CI/CD pipelines and DevOps practices.
Automation and process improvement initiatives that drive tangible business value.
Incident management, troubleshooting and root cause analysis.
Change management and governance processes.
Working across technical and non-technical stakeholder groups.
Leading initiatives, mentoring others and driving continuous improvement.
Experience with technologies and tools such as Git, GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Confluence, Power Platform, Power Automate, Tableau, Power BI and cloud-based platforms would be highly beneficial.




What success looks like

In this role you'll:

Improve platform reliability and service uptime
Deliver smooth, predictable and successful releases
Increase automation and operational efficiency
Reduce platform risk and technical debt
Help teams deliver change faster and more confidently
Create a culture of continuous improvement and operational excellence




Professional development

We're looking for professionals who are committed to continual learning and growth.




Relevant certifications include:

Salesforce Certified Administrator
Salesforce Certified Platform Developer I
Copado Certified Fundamentals I & II - Metadata Pipeline

If you're passionate about building reliable platforms, delivering change at scale and helping shape the future of digital services, we'd love to hear from you.
