As an Application Specialist III – Pharmacy Retail Systems, you’ll play a senior role in managing and evolving the critical applications that support the Group’s Pharmacy Retail environment.

Operating with a high level of independence, you’ll take ownership of complex, business-critical applications and lead technical initiatives across application support, system enhancements, releases and implementations. Working closely with business stakeholders, project teams, functional teams and vendors, you’ll help ensure systems remain reliable, scalable and aligned with evolving business needs.

You’ll bring deep application and technical expertise to influence system architecture, guide solution design and support the implementation of new technologies. Beyond keeping critical systems running, you’ll proactively identify opportunities to improve performance, processes and user experience, helping shape the future application landscape for Pharmacy Retail.
