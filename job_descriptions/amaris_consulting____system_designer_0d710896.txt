We are seeking a System Designer to lead assigned system design activities within New Product Introduction (NPI) projects for computer-assisted surgical and robotic systems. In this role, you will own technical deliverables across system requirements, design inputs, architecture support, risk management, verification and validation, usability engineering, design changes, and Design History File (DHF) documentation.

You will work independently to drive product features, subsystems, or projects forward, while taking part in hands-on robotic testing and integration activities and collaborating closely with multidisciplinary project teams.




Location: Montreal, Quebec

Work Mode: Hybrid (minimum three days per week in office)

Travel: Up to 25% is required







Key Responsibilities

Lead assigned system design activities within NPI projects for computer-assisted surgical and robotic systems
Define and maintain system requirements and design inputs, ensuring they are clear, testable, and traceable
Support system architecture development and decisions
Draft and maintain DHF content, including user needs, system requirements, design inputs, traceability matrices, and risk documentation
Support risk management activities, including risk analysis (e.g., uFMEA), and contribute to design mitigations
Contribute to usability planning and usability engineering activities
Support the development of verification and validation (V&V) protocols in collaboration with the Product Development team
Manage and document design changes in accordance with applicable procedures
Participate in hands-on robotic testing and integration activities
Independently drive technical deliverables for assigned product features, subsystems, or projects
Collaborate with R&D, QA, clinical, and regulatory teams, translating technical information clearly for diverse stakeholders
Engage effectively with both internal and external collaborators







Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in engineering, science, or a related technical field (e.g., biomedical, computer, mechanical, or electrical engineering)
A degree in biomedical engineering is an asset
5+ years of experience in system design within a multidisciplinary organization
Experience working in a regulatory environment with strict compliance requirements
Experience drafting and maintaining DHF content, including user needs, system requirements, design inputs, traceability matrices, and risk documentation
Experience with Codebeamer, Windchill, and/or JIRA, or similar tools
English required




Knowledge and Skills

Familiarity with ISO 14971 (required)
Familiarity with IEC 62366 (preferred)
Ability to support risk analysis (e.g., uFMEA) and contribute to usability planning and design mitigations
Ability to define testable requirements, support V&V protocol development, and ensure traceability
Strong analytical skills and sound judgment
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Strong collaboration skills across R&D, QA, clinical, and regulatory functions
Ability to deal effectively with internal and external collaborators







Why choose us

An international community bringing together more than 110 different nationalities
An environment where trust is central: 70% of our leaders started their careers at the entry level
A strong training system with our internal Academy and more than 250 modules available
A dynamic work environment that frequently comes together for internal events (afterworks, team buildings, etc.)
