This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, which manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer II based in Ireland.

As a Senior DevOps Engineer II, you will take ownership of critical infrastructure domains and drive complex engineering initiatives within a growing, international technology organization. You will play a key role in designing, scaling, and maintaining secure, resilient, and high-performing cloud platforms. Working primarily with AWS and modern Infrastructure-as-Code technologies, you will lead projects that strengthen platform reliability, automation, security, and operational efficiency. You will collaborate closely with engineering leadership, software developers, security specialists, and infrastructure teams to translate complex challenges into scalable technical solutions. Beyond hands-on engineering, you will influence architectural decisions, establish best practices, and mentor other engineers. This role offers significant technical ownership and the opportunity to shape infrastructure strategy while driving measurable improvements across multiple teams.

Accountabilities


Take ownership of the technical health, scalability, reliability, and long-term sustainability of a defined infrastructure domain or platform area.
Lead complex, multi-phase infrastructure initiatives from initial problem definition and architectural design through implementation, delivery, and stabilization.
Design and implement robust, scalable, and resilient infrastructure architectures that reduce operational risks and improve platform performance.
Translate ambiguous technical challenges into clear strategies, defining implementation plans, evaluating trade-offs, and communicating recommendations to technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Collaborate with Engineering Managers, Product teams, software engineers, and cross-functional stakeholders to align infrastructure initiatives with business objectives and engineering roadmaps.
Identify and address systemic risks, technical debt, performance bottlenecks, and architectural limitations before they affect production environments or customers.
Contribute hands-on to infrastructure development and configuration, producing secure, maintainable, and high-quality solutions using Terraform, CI/CD pipelines, and platform automation.
Drive improvements in CI/CD processes, Infrastructure-as-Code practices, reusable modules, and automation standards across the wider engineering organization.
Apply and promote cloud infrastructure best practices, particularly within AWS, focusing on security, cost optimization, scalability, compliance, and operational excellence.
Work closely with software engineering, security, and QA teams to integrate infrastructure and security considerations early into development workflows and architectural decisions.
Strengthen the organization's security posture by leveraging cloud security and vulnerability management tools, such as Aikido, and implementing proactive risk identification and remediation practices.
Improve monitoring, observability, alerting, and incident response processes to enhance service availability, detection capabilities, and root cause analysis.
Participate in production troubleshooting, debugging, incident management, and long-term remediation activities to maintain platform stability.
Establish and promote engineering standards through code reviews, architectural discussions, technical documentation, and knowledge sharing.
Mentor mid-level and senior engineers, helping them develop stronger technical expertise and take greater ownership of infrastructure initiatives.
Act as a technical reference point within the infrastructure domain, promoting alignment, collaboration, and consistency across engineering teams.
Identify and apply opportunities to leverage AI-powered tools to optimize infrastructure workflows, improve engineering productivity, and deliver measurable efficiency gains.


Requirements


At least 9 years of professional experience in DevOps, Infrastructure Engineering, or Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), with demonstrated ownership of complex production systems or infrastructure domains.
Proven experience designing, deploying, and operating large-scale cloud applications, primarily on AWS. Experience with GCP or Azure is an advantage.
Strong expertise in Infrastructure as Code, particularly Terraform, including reusable modules, state management, and governance practices.
Demonstrated experience designing and maintaining secure, scalable CI/CD pipelines and automated release processes for production environments, using tools such as GitHub Actions.
Experience leading complex technical initiatives, platform migrations, infrastructure transformations, or large-scale refactoring projects across multiple teams.
Deep understanding of cloud-native architectures and compute services, including ECS, EKS, Fargate, Lambda, or equivalent technologies.
Strong Linux administration fundamentals, networking knowledge, and proficiency in scripting languages such as Bash, Ruby, Python, or Go.
Experience managing PostgreSQL databases and familiarity with caching and messaging systems such as Redis or Sidekiq.
Strong knowledge of observability and monitoring tools, including Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, OpenTelemetry, and Datadog.
Practical experience defining and implementing Service Level Objectives (SLOs), incident response procedures, and reliability engineering practices.
Proven ability to implement zero-downtime deployments, autoscaling strategies, and capacity planning in production environments.
Solid understanding of cloud security best practices, including IAM, secrets management, network segmentation, access control, and vulnerability management.
Hands-on experience with cloud security and vulnerability management platforms such as Aikido, Snyk, or Wiz, including vulnerability assessment, remediation workflows, and CI/CD integration.
Ability to identify, analyze, and resolve performance bottlenecks across compute, storage, and networking layers.
Familiarity with modern AI/ML services or data-intensive workloads is considered an advantage.
AWS Certified DevOps Engineer – Professional certification, or an equivalent qualification, is a strong plus.
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, with the capacity to navigate complex and evolving technical environments.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, particularly in distributed and remote-first teams.
Strong leadership and mentoring capabilities, with the ability to influence technical decisions and promote engineering excellence without relying on formal management authority.
Proactive, collaborative mindset, combined with a strong sense of ownership and accountability for production systems.


Benefits


Fully remote working arrangement, with the option to work from home or use a funded local co-working space.
Monthly remote work allowance to help cover home internet and electricity expenses.
Home office equipment package provided at the beginning of employment, including a laptop, monitor, keyboard, and other essential equipment.
Additional home office support after onboarding, including furniture, accessories, or a local co-working membership.
Flexible working hours designed to support work-life balance.
25 days of annual leave, in addition to applicable national public holidays.
Sick leave and parental leave benefits.
Personal development budget for courses, books, conferences, and professional learning resources.
Opportunities to participate in an annual international team-building trip.
Regular monthly and quarterly virtual team events and social activities.
Collaborative, international team environment with a strong culture of knowledge sharing and shared ownership.
Opportunity to contribute to major infrastructure initiatives and influence technical direction within a growing technology organization.
