About the role:

As the Senior Document Controller, you will act as a key Document Control and Information Management focal point, responsible for the effective control, coordination and lifecycle management of engineering, operational and business documentation.

This is a hands-on role suited to an experienced Document Controller who is confident managing controlled documentation throughout its lifecycle, while also supporting the administration and effective use of Information Management systems.

You will work closely with engineering, operational and business stakeholders to ensure information remains accurate, current, secure, traceable and readily accessible, while maintaining strong document control standards, system integrity and data quality.

The role will be based in Barrow-in-Furness, with an expected working pattern of four days per week in the office and one day working from home.

As the Senior Document Controller, you will:


Manage and maintain controlled document repositories, ensuring engineering, operational and business records are accurate, current, secure and readily accessible
Manage documentation throughout its full lifecycle, including registration, review, approval, publication, distribution, revision control, retention and archiving
Maintain robust version control, metadata and audit trails to ensure document integrity, traceability and compliance with agreed standards and processes
Coordinate document review and approval workflows, working closely with engineering, operational and business stakeholders
Provide document retrieval, archive search and records management support for operational, maintenance and project activities
Support the administration of Information Management systems and associated document repositories
Manage user accounts, permissions, access controls and engineering subscriptions across relevant Information Management platforms
Monitor system performance, data quality and user adoption, proactively identifying and resolving issues to maintain effective system utilisation
Carry out document and data quality checks, identifying discrepancies and ensuring information is appropriately controlled
Maintain accurate document registers, revision histories, metadata and associated records
Provide user support and troubleshooting across Information Management systems and document control processes
Deliver guidance, training and onboarding to users, helping ensure the consistent application of Information Management processes and standards
Work proactively with stakeholders to resolve document control and Information Management issues and maintain effective information flows
Identify opportunities to improve document control processes, system utilisation, data quality and overall consistency


Requirements


Strong experience in Document Control, Information Management or a related discipline within energy, oil & gas, engineering or a similarly complex technical environment
Good working knowledge of Electronic Document Management Systems (EDMS) and Information Management platforms
Experience administering or supporting document and Information Management systems, including user accounts, permissions and access controls
Experience managing engineering, operational or business documentation throughout its lifecycle, including registration, review, approval, revision, publication, retention and archiving
Strong understanding of document control principles, including version control, metadata, audit trails, document integrity and traceability
Experience maintaining controlled document repositories and carrying out document and data quality checks
Experience supporting document retrieval, archive searches and records management activities
Experience providing system and process support to users, including troubleshooting, training and onboarding
Experience with systems such as PIMS, SolidWorks, Viewport, BMS, FTP, EWB and SharePoint would be advantageous
Good understanding of engineering and technical documentation
Strong attention to detail with a structured, methodical and organised approach
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills
Proactive and self-motivated, with the ability to take ownership, work independently and use initiative
