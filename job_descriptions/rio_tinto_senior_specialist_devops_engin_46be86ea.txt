We’re Finding Better Ways™ to provide materials the world needs, now and in the future. Our values – care, courage and curiosity – guide how we work and how we treat each other. 

About The Role 

We are looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer to design, build and operate secure, scalable and reliable platform capabilities that help Rio Tinto teams deliver data, analytics and AI solutions efficiently. You will join an open, dynamic platform engineering environment where innovation, automation, and practical problem-solving are central to how the team works. 

With a particular focus on data and AI infrastructure, you will improve developer experience and deployment performance through reusable engineering patterns, self-service capabilities, and resilient cloud services. You will work across IS&T, Enterprise Architecture, Cloud, Cyber Security and digital teams in our Product Groups and Functions, helping teams adopt the platform capabilities you build and continuously improving how they are consumed. 

What you’ll be doing 

Working in an open, collaborative and innovative platform engineering team within IS&T, you will be  


Designing, building and operating secure, scalable platform services that enable data, analytics and AI workloads across cloud environments
Building and maintaining Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD pipelines and release automation using tools such as Terraform and GitHub
Creating reusable, self-service platform capabilities that make infrastructure easier for engineering teams to consume and adopt
Improving platform reliability, observability, deployment performance and operational efficiency through Site Reliability Engineering practices, monitoring and automation
Leading the investigation and resolution of complex platform incidents and service issues, including opportunities to introduce intelligent and self-healing capabilities
Embedding secure-by-design controls, identity and access management, governance standards and maintainable engineering patterns across platform services
Partnering with Enterprise Architecture, Cloud, Cyber Security, Data Science, Data Engineering, Product Groups, Functions, delivery partners and vendors to deliver platform outcomes and guide effective adoption
Identifying opportunities to improve reliability, security, cost optimisation and engineering quality as Rio Tinto evolves its Platform Engineering, DevOps and AI Enablement capabilities


About You 

We’re looking for someone whose experience aligns with the key requirements below. You do not need to meet every “helpful for success” item to apply, if you bring relevant experience and are excited by the opportunity, we encourage you to submit an application. 

Required for Success 


A commitment to the safety of yourself and your team. 
Strong platform engineering and DevOps capability, including Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD, release automation and modern cloud solution design
Demonstrated experience designing, operating and supporting enterprise-scale cloud platforms and production environments in Azure or AWS
Experience supporting data, analytics or AI platforms, with capability to improve platform reliability, automation and developer experience
Hands-on experience with Terraform or comparable Infrastructure as Code tooling, source control such as GitHub, containerisation, monitoring and observability tooling, and security and identity controls
Experience translating complex platform needs into practical outcomes and working across technical and business teams to build adoption, documentation and service excellence


Helpful for Success 


Experience with Databricks, Lakehouse platforms, machine learning infrastructure or AI/ML platform operations
Experience with Foundry or related enterprise data platform environments
A degree in computer science, engineering or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience
Professional cloud certification in Azure, AWS or Google Cloud

Where you’ll be working 

Information Systems and Technology (IS&T) is a global function delivering integrated and critical IT services and support to Rio Tinto’s mines, refineries, smelters, remote operations centres and corporate offices. IS&T’s mission is to power Rio Tinto’s pioneers with data and technology, enhancing safety, productivity, and value for the business. Through our collaborative partnerships and a deep understanding of our mining and metals business, IS&T carefully balances the implementation of ‘here and now’ solutions with emerging technologies that will help to create the digitally-optimised Rio Tinto of tomorrow.    

About Rio Tinto

Rio Tinto is a leading global mining and materials company. We operate in 35 countries where we produce iron ore, copper, aluminium, critical minerals, and other materials needed for the global energy transition and for people, communities, and nations to thrive.

We have been mining for 150 years and operate with knowledge built up across generations and continents. Our purpose is finding better ways to provide the materials the world needs – striving for innovation and continuous improvement to produce materials with low emissions and to the right environmental, social and governance standards. But we can’t do it on our own, so we’re focused on creating partnerships to solve problems, create win-win situations and meet opportunities.

Respect and Inclusion

At Rio Tinto, we particularly welcome and encourage applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people, women, the LGBTQ+ community, mature workers, people with disabilities and people from different cultural backgrounds.

We are committed to an inclusive environment where people feel comfortable to be themselves. We want our people to feel that all voices are heard, all cultures respected and that a variety of perspectives are not only welcome – they are essential to our success. We treat each other fairly and with dignity regardless of race, gender, nationality, ethnic origin, religion, age, sexual orientation or anything else that makes us different.
