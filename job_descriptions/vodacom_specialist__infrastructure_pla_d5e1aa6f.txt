Role Purpose/Business Unit

To design, operate and continuously improve secure, resilient and automated Kubernetes/OpenShift platforms for containerised and virtualised workloads through infrastructure automation, observability and Zero Touch Operations practices.

Your Responsibilities Will Include


Platform Operations and Lifecycle Management: Deploy, operate, scale, upgrade and maintain Kubernetes/OpenShift platforms across production and non-production environments, ensuring platform availability, performance, capacity, resilience and operational stability.
Infrastructure Automation and Zero Touch Operations: Develop and maintain infrastructure automation, orchestration and run book automation using Ansible Automation Platform, Terraform, GitOps and approved automation frameworks to standardise provisioning, configuration, patching, remediation, self-service enablement and repeatable operational workflows.
Platform Security, Compliance and Governance: Implement and maintain platform security controls, RBAC, vulnerability management, image governance, compliance monitoring, hardening and configuration consistency across Kubernetes/OpenShift environments.
Observability, Reliability and Service Continuity: Maintain monitoring, alerting, logging, dashboarding, backup and recovery capabilities that provide visibility into platform health, service continuity and operational risk, enabling proactive detection, incident prevention and event-driven remediation.
Continuous Improvement and Technology Enablement: Work with infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud and application teams to improve platform services, support CI/CD and approved deployment practices, enable self-service consumption patterns and support responsible use of emerging technology and AI-enabled operational improvements.



The Ideal Candidate For This Role Will Have


Grade 12 is essential.
A 3 year bachelor's degree/diploma in Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related technology field is essential.
3-5 years’ relevant experience with Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform, Kubernetes and OpenShift Virtualization.
Experience with infrastructure automation using Ansible Automation Platform, Terraform, GitOps or similar tooling.



Preferred Certifications


Red Hat Certified Specialist in OpenShift Administration or Red Hat Certified OpenShift Administrator (EX280).
Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA).



Core Competencies, Knowledge, And Experience


Advanced Kubernetes knowledge (OpenShift preferred), including architecture, lifecycle management, virtualisation, networking, storage, scheduling, troubleshooting and platform operations.
Strong infrastructure automation and orchestration experience using Ansible Automation Platform, Terraform, GitOps and scripting to support provisioning, configuration, patching, remediation and self-service workflows.
Experience managing platform reliability, availability, performance, capacity, resilience, observability, backup and disaster recovery in enterprise production environments.
Strong understanding of Linux, containers, registries, CI/CD practices, platform security, RBAC, vulnerability management, hardening and configuration governance.
AI-Enabled Leadership and Delivery advantageous.
Demonstrates the ability to identify, evaluate and implement technology, automation and AI opportunities that improve team performance, operational efficiency and business outcomes.
Creates an environment that encourages innovation, experimentation, continuous learning and responsible adoption of emerging technologies.
Leads teams through evolving operating models and technology-enabled transformation, building capability and readiness for future workforce requirements.
Tracks and delivers measurable business outcomes from digital, automation and AI initiatives through effective prioritisation, governance and benefits realisation.
Demonstrates AI fluency and literacy through the ability to effectively use, evaluate and govern AI-enabled tools and solutions, applying them responsibly to improve business outcomes, productivity and decision-making.
