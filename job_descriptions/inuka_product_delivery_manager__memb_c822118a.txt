Introduction

Do you know what it takes to keep a consumer app reliable, well designed and delivered on time? INUKA is looking for an experienced delivery leader to own the roadmap delivery and day-to-day running of its Member App, working hand in hand with an external product development partner.

About INUKA

INUKA is a proudly South African, Member-based direct-selling business that develops and supplies fragrances, cosmetics and personal-care products. The business is growing at around 50% a year and serves its Members locally and across borders. The Member App is INUKA's primary channel for communicating with those Members, so this role has a direct impact on how thousands of people experience the business every day.

About the job

Reporting to the Applications Manager in INUKA's TECH team, and working closely with the Member App Product Owner and the Member Relations operations team, you will own delivery governance for the Member App on INUKA's side. The heart of the job is delivery: keeping INUKA and its external product development partner in step on a large roadmap of about 16 to 18 months, from backlogs and release plans to budget and governance. You are also the go-to person for how the app is running day to day. The role suits a seasoned software delivery professional, ideally with a mobile or e-commerce background, who sets the rhythm for a team. For the right person, the role can grow into a Product Owner role.

Duties & Responsibilities

1. Roadmap, release and governance

Deliver the approved roadmap phase by phase with the partner, tracking scope, milestones and dependencies
Own intake and triage, the delivery framework and PMO gates
Own release planning and the readiness decision: Definition of Ready to Deploy, operational readiness and CAB approval, and coordinate UAT and the Go/No-Go gate
Review the partner's UX/UI designs, prototypes and releases for usability and accessibility before build and release
Track adoption, usage and app store ratings after each release and feed the findings back into the backlog

2. Partner, budget and knowledge

Manage the partner's performance against service levels and DORA metrics, and act as the level 1 escalation point
Track budget burn, quotes and purchase orders against the approved budget and forecast
Keep runbooks, release history and Service Desk knowledge articles current, and train Member Relations users on admin tools and new features

3. Running the Member App day to day

Monitor app availability, login, registration and transaction success, notification delivery and the integration with Odoo
Act as INUKA's L2 for incidents: triage, confirm severity (P1 to P4) and escalate to the partner with full evidence
Coordinate P1 and P2 incidents with the partner and IT Service Management, including after hours, and track root cause analyses and preventive actions to closure
Prepare the app for business events such as month end, promotions and campaigns, including release freezes, peak-load readiness and the downtime and recovery plan
Report weekly to Member Relations and monthly to the Applications Manager on performance, service and compliance, and run the monthly service review with the partner
Desired Experience & Qualification

Minimum Requirements

Grade 12 (National Senior Certificate)
Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Business or a related field
Agile delivery certification (for example Professional Scrum Master, Certified ScrumMaster, Kanban Management Professional, SAFe, Professional Scrum Product Owner or PMI-ACP)
10+ years' experience in digital product and software delivery, of which at least 5 years delivering consumer-facing mobile and web apps in a competitive market
Has delivered consumer apps across the full lifecycle, from discovery and design to build, release and continuous improvement
Hands-on Agile (Scrum and Kanban) and DevOps experience, including CI/CD, test automation and measuring delivery with DORA metrics
A strong UX/UI track record: user research, usability testing and working with designers on journeys, prototypes and design systems
Technically fluent enough to challenge estimates, architecture trade-offs and quality on native mobile and web apps
Experience managing external development partners, including service levels and budgets
Experience running production support and incident coordination for a live consumer product
A record of releases shipped on committed dates within formal governance (PMO gates, CAB and ITSM)

Advantageous

Product management, UX (e.g. Nielsen Norman Group), DevOps, PMP, PRINCE2 or ITIL V4 Foundation certification
A major app transformation or re-platforming
Experience in member, loyalty or communication platforms, e-commerce or fintech, and in multi-country or multi-language products
Product analytics and app store ratings used to measure the impact of releases
Tools such as Azure DevOps, GitHub, Figma and New Relic; working knowledge of POPIA

Location & Employment Type

Permanent, full-time position on-site at INUKA's Tyger Valley office in Bellville, Cape Town, working day to day with the Member Relations operations team. Some travel may be required between INUKA's locations in Bellville. Standby is required for P1/P2 incidents, releases and major business events, including after hours.

Package & Remuneration

A competitive, market-related salary will be offered in line with experience and the level of responsibility.

This is an Employment Equity position. Preference will be given to candidates from designated groups in line with INUKA's Employment Equity plan.
