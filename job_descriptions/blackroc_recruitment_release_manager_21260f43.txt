BLACKROC is currently partnering with a large enterprise environment in Brisbane to find an experienced Release Manager to join their technology delivery team on a contract through to August 2027, with potential for extension.




This is a great opportunity for someone who enjoys being hands-on with release planning and coordination, working across multiple projects and bringing together technical teams, business stakeholders and vendors to ensure smooth and successful deployments. You'll be joining a busy technology portfolio delivering a range of system enhancements and changes across complex enterprise applications.




What you'll be doing

Managing and coordinating end-to-end release activities across multiple projects and environments.
Developing and maintaining detailed release schedules, deployment plans, cutover runbooks and rollback strategies.
Coordinating SIT, UAT and production releases, ensuring teams and environments are ready for deployment.
Working closely with project teams, technical resources, business stakeholders and external vendors.
Managing release risks, dependencies and issues to keep activities on track.
Supporting release governance, including CAB meetings, change requests and approvals.
Maintaining release reporting, schedules and documentation using tools such as Microsoft Project, Jira and ServiceNow.




What we're looking for

At least 6 years' experience leading deployments and release activities across large, complex ICT projects.
Strong hands-on Microsoft Project experience, particularly managing detailed schedules across multiple teams and workstreams.
Demonstrated experience managing complex releases across SIT, UAT and production environments.
Experience developing deployment runbooks, cutover plans and rollback strategies.
A good understanding of release governance, change management and ITIL processes.
Strong communication and stakeholder engagement skills, with the ability to coordinate both technical and non-technical teams.
Someone who is organised, proactive and comfortable managing competing priorities in a busy delivery environment.
Experience with Agile delivery, Jira, ServiceNow or enterprise platforms such as Microsoft Dynamics 365 would be highly regarded.




As part of the role, some after-hours or weekend availability will be required to support planned deployments.
