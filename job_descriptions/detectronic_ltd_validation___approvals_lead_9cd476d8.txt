Job Description

The Head of Validation & Approvals is responsible for leading the global validation, certification, regulatory compliance, and product approvals strategy for the company's portfolio of IoT devices
ensures all products meet quality, safety, radio, environmental, cybersecurity, and market-specific regulatory requirements before commercial release.
establish and lead a robust validation and approvals function, working closely with Product Management, Hardware, Firmware, Operations, Manufacturing, Quality, and external certification bodies to enable successful product launches across global markets.
reducing compliance risk, accelerating time-to-market, and ensuring products meet customer, regulatory, and industry standards.




Qualifications

Essential

10+ years experience in product validation, regulatory compliance, or certification leadership.
5+ years leading global engineering or compliance teams.
Proven experience delivering approvals for connected IoT devices.
Strong understanding of RF technologies including: (LoRaWAN, NB-IoT, LTE-M, Cellular, Bluetooth)
Experience working with certification laboratories and notified bodies.
Strong understanding of global market access requirements.
Experience with complex hardware and embedded systems products.




Desirable

Experience with wastewater, utility, environmental monitoring, industrial IoT, or smart infrastructure solutions.
Experience managing products deployed internationally.
Background in product quality or reliability engineering.
Familiarity with cybersecurity regulations for connected devices.
