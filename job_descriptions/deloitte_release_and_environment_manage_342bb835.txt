Tackle big issues like cyber, trust, resilience and digital transformation
Mentoring, coaching and leadership programs to help you make an impact that matters


Step into a Manager role in Brisbane where you'll help clients deliver stable, well-governed technology releases that keep major programs moving with confidence.

What will your typical day look like?

You'll lead release planning across programs and projects, bringing structure to milestones, dependencies, approvals, risks and implementation activity. Working across delivery teams, Test Managers and technology stakeholders, you'll keep releases coordinated, controlled and aligned to quality gates and readiness criteria.

A big part of the role is making sure environments are available, fit for purpose and ready when teams need them. That includes coordinating provisioning, access, test data, integrations, refreshes and demand across multiple environments, while resolving conflicts and keeping a clear view of what could impact delivery.

Just as importantly, you'll be the person who brings clarity to complex delivery landscapes. From governance forums and readiness reporting through to cutover, rollback and post-release validation, you'll help clients make informed decisions and navigate change with less risk and more confidence.

About The Team

Deloitte's Technology Consulting practice helps organisations solve complex technology challenges and deliver transformation that sticks. Within Quality Engineering, you'll work with teams focused on quality, delivery control and operational readiness across major programs, using practical delivery disciplines, modern tooling and adaptable methods to support better business outcomes for clients.

Enough About Us, Let's Talk About You

You may have all or some of the following skills/experiences:


Experience leading release and environment management across medium to large-scale, multi-vendor programs
Strong knowledge of end-to-end release lifecycles across Waterfall, Agile and hybrid delivery models
Hands-on experience managing test environments, including scheduling, provisioning, access, test data and issue resolution
Confidence leading governance forums, readiness assessments, implementation planning, cutover and post-release validation
Experience using tools such as Jira Releases and/or Azure DevOps to track and report on releases, environments and testing activity
A solid grounding in Test Management, including planning, defect management, reporting and coordination across stakeholders
Strong communication, stakeholder management and negotiation skills in complex delivery environments
Nice to have: exposure to CI/CD, deployment strategies, Non-Functional Testing, Systems Monitoring, or using data and AI to improve delivery outcomes
