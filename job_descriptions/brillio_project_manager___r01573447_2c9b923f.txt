Job requirements


Experience Range: With at least 5 to 8 years of hands-on experience in Agile program and release train management, including direct involvement in CI/CD pipeline maintenance and SDLC release processes. Key Responsibilities: 1. Coordinate and execute end-to-end release management activities to ensure successful code deployment across lower and production environments. 2. Maintain and optimize automated CI/CD pipelines to streamline deployment workflows, enhance delivery speed, and minimize manual errors. 3. Enforce established SDLC release processes, governance models, and deployment protocols across internal teams and stakeholders. 4. Collaborate actively with development, engineering, and product teams to track dependencies and ensure timely delivery of release artifacts. 5. Diagnose and troubleshoot complex CI/CD pipeline and deployment failures, implementing permanent technical fixes to ensure operational stability. 6. Provide clear, transparent updates regarding release schedules, risks, and deployment statuses to stakeholders. Required Skills: 1. Hands-on experience building, scaling, and maintaining CI/CD pipelines using GitLab and GitHub 2. Expertise in release management methodologies within Agile environments 3. Strong technical troubleshooting skills for CI/CD pipeline and deployment failures 4. Experience with code deployments for Snowflake, Apache Airflow, and DBT 5. Deep understanding of SDLC and Agile release processes Preferred Skills: 1. Experience working within cloud data warehouse environments or data engineering release tracks 2. Certification in GitLab, GitHub Actions, or relevant cloud/DevOps practices 3. Experience managing releases in multi-tenant or complex enterprise client ecosystems Desired Qualifications: 1. Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a closely related discipline 2. Certified Scrum Master (CSM) or equivalent Agile certification 3. Relevant certification in CI/CD tools or cloud/DevOps practices (e.g., GitLab Certified Associate, GitHub Actions Certification) Additional Information: Immediate joiner required
