Job Description

Senior DevOps Engineer

Are you excited about solving technical issues? You feel a need to automate everything?

Do you enjoy collaborating with developers, engineers, architects, impacting products touching life online for billions of users?

Join our team!

Our DevOps team consists of collaborative, inclusive, and creative individuals spread across the globe. We are dedicated to building and operating high-reliability, massive-scale, cloud-native services. Our team is at the forefront of developing a cloud-agnostic platform. engineered to operate seamlessly across multi-cloud environments, ensuring safety, performance and stability.

Partner with the best

You will build and operate infrastructure powering our security product line. Join a cross-functional team designing, developing, and maintaining cloud-native services for thousands of customers, delivering secure, scalable solutions across multi-cloud environments.

As a Senior DevOps Engineer, you will be responsible for:


Contributing to the design and implementation of highly available, scalable, and high-performance solutions
Designing and developing DevOps tools to ensure seamless software delivery
Creating and implementing procedures to automate operational tasks, moving towards a zero-touch operations model
Developing processes and tools to monitor, analyze, maintain, and improve distributed systems
Collaborating with security teams to integrate automated security checks (DevSecOps) into our delivery pipelines, ensuring compliance and vulnerability management.


Do What You Love

To be successful in this role you will:


5+ years of relevant experience and a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or its equivalent
Have proven experience in building cloud-agnostic infrastructure and managing multi-cloud environments (e.g., AWS, GCP, Linode, ).
Have hands-on experience building Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and managing cloud infrastructure using Terraform.
Show hands-on experience in setting up and managing large-scale container environments using Docker and Kubernetes.
Have proficiency in cloud security governance, zero-trust frameworks, and PoLP access models.
Demonstrate proficiency in CI/CD (GitOps) and robust scripting skills in Linux Shell and Python.
Have an understanding of distributed systems and Linux networking (including TCP/IP, SSH, SSL, HTTP).
