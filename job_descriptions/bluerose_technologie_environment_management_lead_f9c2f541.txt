Job Description – Environment Management Lead

Experience: 12+ Years

Role: Environment Management Lead

Domain: Banking / Financial Services

Key Responsibilities & Must-Have Skills

Lead end-to-end environment implementation and management across banking delivery programs, ensuring stability, availability and readiness for testing and releases.
Manage environment requirements, dependencies, allocation, availability, planned downtime and resource conflicts across multiple teams.
Maintain a central environment repository covering application versions, infrastructure, connectivity, data refresh status and third-party integrations.
Coordinate with application, testing, infrastructure, cloud, database, network, security and release management teams.
Govern deployment schedules, release windows, environment changes, freeze periods and data refresh activities.
Drive DevOps practices, including CI/CD implementation, automated deployments, configuration management and environment provisioning.
Collaborate with platform and release engineering teams to automate environment setup, deployment, validation and monitoring.
Resolve environment-related incidents, defects, blockers and conflicts to ensure smooth delivery operations.
Ensure compliance with banking controls, change governance, audit requirements, data protection, access management and data masking.
Drive continuous improvement through automation, standardisation, self-service capabilities and effective stakeholder management.

Nice-to-Have Skills

ITIL certification or practical experience.
DevOps and CI/CD experience.
Cloud-related certifications or hands-on cloud experience.

Mandatory Requirements

Minimum 12+ years of overall IT experience.
Strong experience in Environment Management across banking or financial services programs.
Hands-on understanding of DevOps, CI/CD and deployment automation.
Strong stakeholder coordination and environment governance experience.
