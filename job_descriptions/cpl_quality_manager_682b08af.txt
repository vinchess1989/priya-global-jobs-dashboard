Quality Engineer

Our client, a global manufacturer operating within a highly regulated industry, is seeking a Quality Engineer to join their growing quality team.

This position will support quality systems, compliance, validation, supplier quality, auditing and continuous improvement initiatives while collaborating closely with manufacturing and operational teams.

Responsibilities


Drive Quality Management System improvements.
Lead CAPA and investigation activities.
Support validation programmes.
Conduct internal audits.
Ensure compliance with quality and regulatory requirements.
Support supplier quality initiatives.
Participate in risk management activities.
Assist with new product introduction and transfer projects.
Drive continuous improvement programmes.


Requirements


Degree qualified in Engineering or Science.
3+ years Quality Engineering experience.
Experience within a regulated manufacturing environment.
Knowledge of ISO standards and quality systems.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.


Please send your updated CV to morgan.danaher@cpl.ie if interested in hearing more.
