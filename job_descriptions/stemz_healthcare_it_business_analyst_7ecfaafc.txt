Permanent Role

Primary Responsibility

As a Business Analyst, you will be responsible for defining and documenting functional requirements and risk analysis using solution management tools and processes, and define testing strategy for solution outcomes using manual, exploratory and automated test methodologies.

Additionally, you will have the opportunity to lead and own discussions between development teams, subject matter experts, internal users or client-facing teams to clarify user stories, track issues or resolve problems.

Reporting Structure

This role will report to the Head - IT and will have an indirect reporting to the Strategy team.

Job Description

Analyze User requirements and enhancement requests.

Creating tests at various levels, including unit, functional, integration, smoke, stress, and performance (added advantage); understand the strengths and weaknesses of each level

Prepare Test Data and maintain existing test artifacts.

Document and produce daily activities report

Coordinate and liaise with relevant parties for project related items

Support in the smooth rollout of initiatives through pre and post-implementation activities

Performance Metrics

Metrics & Organization Management:

TAT

Customer Satisfaction

Working on FD s based requirement understanding

Documentation Management

Qualifications, Experience And Education Requirements

Bachelor s/ Master s Degree in Business Management in Healthcare with minimum of 3 years experience in a similar function

Sound functional knowledge of Healthcare domain

Mandatory Experience in a similar role preferably in Hospitals/ Large Clinics

Proficiency in MS office

Mandatory Skills

Fluency in written and spoken English

Strong communication, teamwork, and time management skills.

Strong Problem solving and decision-making skills.

Client interaction and achieving desired targets.

Understanding and analyzing requirements.

QA Testing of the products.

Able to Coordinate with the development team

Mandatory Experience in healthcare clinics

Ability to handle growing business
