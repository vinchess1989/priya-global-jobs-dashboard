Job Description:




Strong software engineering background with proven ownership of complex technology delivery end-to-end. • Demonstrated experience leading engineering teams across one or more squads, workstreams or technology domains. • Experience managing delivery plans, risks, dependencies, technical decisions, releases and production readiness. • Ability to remain technically credible and sufficiently hands-on to review designs, guide implementation and challenge delivery approaches. • Strong stakeholder management, communication and influencing skills, including presenting risks and recommendations to senior stakeholders. • Experience working across distributed, cross-functional or onshore and offshore teams. • Experience delivering business-critical applications in Agile environments; SAFe and PI Planning exposure are desirable.




About Happiest Minds Technologies:

Happiest Minds Technologies is an AI-first, customer-centric digital engineering and IT services company focused on helping enterprises accelerate their digital transformation journey. Headquartered in Bengaluru, the company delivers innovative solutions across digital engineering, cloud, data analytics, cybersecurity, automation, and Generative AI. Guided by its philosophy of "Happiest People. Happiest Customers.", Happiest Minds serves global clients across diverse industries through a culture of innovation, agility, and excellence.
