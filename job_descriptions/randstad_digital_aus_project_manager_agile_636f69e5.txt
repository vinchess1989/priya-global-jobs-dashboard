We are seeking an experienced Agile Project Manager for a 12-month fixed-term assignment with a global consultancy practice to manage multiple platform deliveries across D365, ServiceNow, Databricks, Power BI and integration. Key Responsibilities & Capabilities1


Lead Agile, Scrum, Kanban, and hybrid delivery models across multiple simultaneous workstreams.
Coordinate cross-functional activities across business, architecture, development, testing, and deployment teams.
Manage application dependencies and mitigate integration risks across interconnected enterprise platforms (D365, ServiceNow, Databricks, Power BI, and integration services).
Sequence releases across platforms and ensure environment readiness across all staging levels.
Track dependencies, maintain backlog hygiene, enforce traceability from requirement to deployment, and oversee Azure DevOps pipelines and release management.


To be successful in this position you will be a highly experienced Project Manager with experience delivering integrated enterprise solutions across Dynamics 365, ServiceNow, Databricks, Power BI. You will also be experienced with working in an Agile environment, and have Advanced Jira administration and hands-on Azure DevOps (boards, pipelines, release management) oversight. Excellent stakeholder engagement skills are a must. Apply using the link or email Leonie.woodfine@randstad.com.au
