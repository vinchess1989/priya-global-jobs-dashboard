Title: Master Data Team Leader

Reports to: Engineering Lead

Location: Droitwich

Imagine working at the forefront of innovation in fluid-flow technology, with over 1,400 colleagues across the globe, and contributing to a legacy of excellence that spans eight manufacturing facilities on four continents. Armstrong Fluid Technology is more than just a leader in our industry; we are a community of the brightest and most creative minds, driven by a shared mission to engineer the future and safeguard our planet.

As part of our team, you'll be immersed in an environment that fosters growth, creativity, and collaboration. Here, you'll have the opportunity to push boundaries, tackle exciting challenges, and develop cutting-edge solutions that promote energy efficiency and reduce environmental impact. Every day offers the chance to make a meaningful contribution to a more sustainable future, driving innovations that help lower global carbon footprints. Together, we’re creating a legacy that goes beyond business—one that’s changing the world for the better.

As the Master Data Team Leader, you will govern and improve Armstrong’s Engineering Item Master and BOM structures, ensuring accurate, consistent data across PLM, ERP, CAD, procurement, manufacturing, and quality systems.

As the BOM and item master subject matter expert, the role leads data standards, engineering change control, product governance, and process improvement to support product development, manufacturing, supply chain, and project delivery.

The role drives data quality, supports PLM and ERP development, establishes best practices, and works across functions to maintain a single source of truth for product data.

Key Accountabilities

BOM Governance & Management


Own and govern engineering BOM structures throughout the product lifecycle.
Establish and maintain BOM standards, templates, procedures, and best practices.
Ensure BOMs are accurate, complete, structured correctly, and released in accordance with company procedures.
Manage version control, revision status, and configuration management processes.
Lead BOM reviews and audits to identify and eliminate inconsistencies or errors.


Item Master Data Ownership


Own and govern engineering BOM structures throughout the product lifecycle.
Establish and maintain BOM standards, templates, procedures, and best practices.
Ensure BOMs are accurate, complete, structured correctly, and released in accordance with company procedures.
Manage version control, revision status, and configuration management processes.
Lead BOM reviews and audits to identify and eliminate inconsistencies or errors.


Engineering Change Control


Support and coordinate Engineering Change Requests (ECRs), Engineering Change Orders (ECOs), and Engineering Change Notices (ECNs).
Ensure changes are accurately reflected within all affected BOMs and item master records.
Drive timely implementation of engineering changes to support production, procurement, and project delivery requirements.
Support impact assessments relating to engineering changes, obsolescence, and supplier changes.


Data Quality & Compliance


Develop and implement data quality metrics, dashboards, and reporting.
Conduct routine audits of BOMs, part records, and product structures.
Identify recurring data quality issues and implement corrective and preventive measures.
Ensure compliance with company quality management systems and engineering procedures.


Cross-Functional Coordination


Serve as the primary point of contact for BOM and item master data queries.
Work closely with Engineering, Procurement, Manufacturing, Planning, Supply Chain, Quality, and Operations teams.
Facilitate cross-functional reviews of product structures and master data requirements.
Coordinate assignment and control of engineering part numbers and product configurations.


PLM, ERP & Digital Systems Support


Act as a key user and subject matter expert for PLM, ERP, and related engineering systems.
Support PLM implementation, enhancement projects, data migrations, and process development initiatives.
Define business requirements for BOM and master data improvements.
Assist with testing, validation, and user acceptance activities related to system changes.


Reporting & Continuous Improvement


Generate and maintain KPI reports relating to BOM completion, accuracy, data quality, and change management performance.
Support Engineering Leadership with Engineering Timing Release Structure (ETRS) reporting.
Identify opportunities to improve engineering data management processes and system efficiency.
Lead continuous improvement initiatives focused on data governance and product information management.


Leadership & Training


Provide guidance and support to BOM Coordinators and Engineering teams regarding data management best practice.
Deliver training on BOM processes, item master standards, PLM workflows, and engineering change procedures.
Promote a culture of data ownership, accountability, and continuous improvement throughout the organisation.


What We’re Looking For

To Thrive In This Role, You Should Bring

Education and Experience


Experience working within engineering, manufacturing, or product development environments.
Experience with ERP, PLM, PDM, CAD, and product data management systems.


Soft Skills


Excellent attention to detail with a strong focus on data accuracy and governance.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to identify root causes and implement corrective actions.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
Ability to manage multiple priorities within a fast-paced engineering environment.


Technical Skills


Strong understanding of engineering BOM structures, product configuration management, and item master data management.
Ability to interpret engineering drawings, product assemblies, and technical documentation.
Strong knowledge of engineering change control processes, including ECR, ECO, and ECN workflows.
Advanced Microsoft Excel and reporting skills.


Desirable


Experience supporting PLM implementation or digital transformation projects.
Working knowledge of SolidWorks PDM, PLM systems, and manufacturing ERP systems.
Understanding of configuration management and product lifecycle management principles.
Experience developing engineering standards, procedures, or governance frameworks.
Lean, Six Sigma, or Continuous Improvement experience.
