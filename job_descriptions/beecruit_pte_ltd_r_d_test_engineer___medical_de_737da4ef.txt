Engineering & Technical Headhunter | MedTech, Medical Device & Advanced Manufacturing | MSc Management

Fongs is seeking an R&D Test Engineer to support product testing, design verification and validation, working closely with Development, Quality and Regulatory teams.




Hands-on experience in medical device product testing and validation is essential.







Key Responsibilities




• Develop and execute test plans and protocols for design verification, process validation and product performance.

• Design and implement manual and automated test procedures.

• Conduct feasibility, functional, performance and benchmark testing.

• Analyse test results and prepare technical reports.

• Develop and validate test equipment and fixtures.

• Manage laboratory equipment, calibration and qualification activities.

• Support compliance with applicable regulatory requirements and standards.

• Coordinate with external test laboratories and equipment vendors.







Candidate Requirements




• Three to five years of relevant experience, including hands-on medical device product testing and validation.

• Degree or Master’s in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Bioengineering or a related discipline.

• Strong knowledge of verification and validation methodologies, regulatory requirements and technical documentation.

• Understanding of Design of Experiments and proficiency in Minitab or similar statistical analysis software.

• Proficiency in test automation tools and scripting languages.

• Understanding of cybersecurity requirements for medical device testing.

• Strong problem-solving and stakeholder communication skills.







Interested candidates are invited to send their CV via LinkedIn direct message, highlighting their medical device testing and validation experience.
