Siemens Mobility is dedicated to pioneering the future of transportation. Our dynamic team is always exploring new ways to drive innovation and efficiency in rail. We are searching for forward-thinkers who are eager to challenge themselves and make a tangible impact. If you are passionate about creating sustainable and intelligent transport solutions, we want you on our team.

We are looking for a Document Controller to join our Glasgow office as part of our Rail Infrastructure business. The role will assist the engineering design team with the design process to ensure that the maximum output is optimised for producing detailed signalling design.

You’ll make a difference by


Providing full document control support for specific projects – including document control, Collate and submit design/documents on NR platforms for approval and follow the document cycle within that process.
Assist in the collation of project H&S Files.
Assist with production of Design Specifications and associated Project Documentation.
Production of site documentation from Production Office Copies.
Assist with scanned documentation to produce sets of design documentation required to be transferred between different engineering sites.
Produce documentation for Issue Control Logs.
Assist with production and upkeep of the Project Document Register and related correspondence and engineering documentation.
Assist with production and upkeep of the Design Metric spreadsheets.
Produce transmittal documentation.


Your success will be grounded by


Essential: Word, Excel.
Useful: Knowledge of Design Control Process, ProjectWise, (Training will be given)
