NTT DATA strives to hire exceptional, innovative and passionate individuals who want to grow with us. If you want to be part of an inclusive, adaptable, and forward-thinking organization, apply now.

We are currently seeking a Systems Integration Senior Specialist to join our team in Noida/ Bangalore, Uttar Pradesh (IN-UP), India (IN).


Strong experience as a Technical Author/Technical Writer, preferably focused on technology governance, SDLC, or engineering standards.
Strong understanding of the end-to-end Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
Experience writing policies, standards, procedures, controls, guidelines, and technical documentation.
Good understanding of Agile, DevOps, DevSecOps, CI/CD, software testing, release management, and change management.
Understanding of secure software development principles and application security controls.
Ability to interpret complex technical and governance requirements and translate them into concise, structured documentation.
Strong document-management and version-control skills.
Excellent written English, editing, proofreading, and information-structuring skills.
Strong stakeholder-management skills with the ability to work with technical SMEs, senior management, risk, compliance, and audit teams.
