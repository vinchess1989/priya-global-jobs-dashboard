Job Description

A Quality Engineer is required to join our client’s team at their electronics manufacturing and design facility in Cork. Permanent site-based role.

Role


Support Continuous Improvement of Quality & Quality Systems.
Review of customer requirements, identification and ensuring compliance to quality related requirement.
Review and approval of Engineering Change Requests.
Lead and support AS9145 (APQP) activities like Product Specific Quality Plans, FMEA, Control plans, MSA, First Article Inspection Reports and PPAP.
Lead and support 8D investigations of customer claims, provide reports and communication with customers as required.
Monitoring production quality yields and drive improvements by using Lean and Six Sigma methods – such as 5 Whys, FMEA, 8D to create robust permanent corrective actions.
Collaborate with production, engineering and project teams to resolve quality issues.
Provide technical quality support to supplier quality engineering and quality inspectors in incoming inspection.
Provide technical quality support to quality inspectors and quality technicians on the production floor.


Requirements


Honours Bachelor Degree / Level 8 qualification in relevant engineering or science discipline.
Working experience as a quality engineer within an electronics manufacturing environment is an advantage.
Six Sigma Green or Black Belt certified.
Good knowledge of industry standards, such as ISO 9001, EN/AS9100, IPC-WHMA-A-620, IPC-A-610 and J-STD-001 is an advantage.
Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings and specifications.
Good knowledge of Lean Manufacturing, Six Sigma, Sampling Plans, Statistics, PFMEA and 8D Problem-Solving.
