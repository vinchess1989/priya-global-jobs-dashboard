FRAMECAD Frontier Team




The AI frontier is moving every week. Work at it, not near it.

AI is changing how software gets written and how decisions get made. The next step is AI that acts in the physical world, where an agent's output turns into steel on a building site. At FRAMECAD, you'll make sure every AI output we rely on is proven correct and safe before it reaches a customer, a fabricator or a building site. The agent that does the work never grades its own homework.




Why now, why here

Large organisations are still deciding how to use this technology. A small founding team with the right tools can move faster. At FRAMECAD you're a short step from the engineers who design the product, the machines that make it and the customers who use it. Few AI teams have that access to the physical end of their work. Founding members set the foundations: the problems we choose, how we work and who joins after.




Why this team

You've probably seen how often an impressive AI output turns out to be quietly wrong. This role makes catching that the whole job. You'll be the control point of the agentic loop, deciding what gets accepted, retried or stopped. The Frontier team is small, flat and reports to the AI Principal Engineer, based in Auckland.




Tools. The latest frontier models, agents, frameworks, and the freedom to choose and combine them.
Tokens. The token budget to run real experiments at real scale, not rationed access.
Time. Space to be ambitious: explore, prototype and go deep, away from a packed sprint calendar.
Taste. You can tell when a model's output is wrong, and you can explain why.
Training. Support to stay at the leading edge, including courses, conferences and research.




What you'll do

Set the verification strategy for AI agents across all our software platforms ,and internal automation.
Design and run evaluation frameworks: test sets, scoring, regression suites and benchmarks that track agent quality over time.
Write verification contracts with product and engineering, setting the bar an agent capability must clear before release.
Build independent checking into delivery pipelines, separate from the agent that did the work, with automated checks first and human sign-off where the stakes are high.
Define review patterns for agent work, such as independent reviewer agents, cross-model review and test-first loops, and decide when each applies.
Work with engineering certification to verify AI-produced work against the relevant codes




What you bring

Senior experience in software quality, test engineering or ML evaluation, and a record of shipping complex systems.
Hands-on experience evaluating LLMs or agents, including both hosted and open-weight models.
Rigour, with the judgement to know where automated checks are enough and where a human needs to sign off.
Current, practical experience building with frontier AI, in a day job, a side project or open source.
A wish to build something from the start, with real ownership.




Nice to have

Experience with structural or engineering design codes
Knowledge of the construction industry, especially cold-formed steel framing or engineering software.




Who We Are – FRAMECAD

We are a technology company that has been changing how the world builds for 38 years. We're a global business operating on six continents, and our solutions are proven and trusted by industry-leading builders in over 110 countries.




We do what few companies can: we cover the whole journey from idea to building.




Design software that connects the designer's vision to the factory floor.
Manufacturing equipment of the highest quality, which turns those designs into precise components on site or in the factory.
Cloud business systems that connect the whole operation, from quote to production to delivery.
Custom-designed components built for the job at hand.
Hands-on servicing, training and support, so our customers succeed long after the machine is installed.




Together, these power faster, more cost-efficient and more consistent building than traditional approaches. Our solutions are behind homes, commercial buildings, military facilities and government projects around the world, and many of the industry's leading companies choose FRAMECAD as their partner. We believe our technology can revolutionise construction, and we prove it every day.




What we offer:

As well as the opportunity to work on the future, we offer a great modern office environment, working flexibility, an onsite gym, competitive pay, catering and refreshments regularly, and all of the support you will need to be successful in this role.
