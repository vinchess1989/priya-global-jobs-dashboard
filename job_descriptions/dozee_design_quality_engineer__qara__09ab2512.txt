Overview

Dozee Health AI is a pioneer in Contactless Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM), proven to drive transformation at scale. Headquartered in Bengaluru, India, Dozee has emerged as a leader in this category. We are seeking talented individuals to help us in this very exciting journey. Candidates will be working with top healthcare providers in the country and leverage RPM's innovation to tackle healthcare's biggest challenges - from resource constraints to quality outcomes, to new care models.

As our Design Quality Engineer, you will be part of Dozee’s QARA – Quality Assurance & Regulatory Affairs team, embedded with the design team and working with Product, Engineering and Data Science on design controls, verification and validation, and product risk management. It is a high-ownership role for someone who brings structure, quality and pace to everything they deliver, and who wants to help improve patient care through connected health

Responsibilities


Play a key role in Dozee’s Quality and Regulatory function, ensuring design quality across software and hardware medical devices.
Perform design assurance activities and verification and validation reviews, and support product risk management, in line with US FDA requirements and applicable medical device standards.
Work closely with Product, Engineering, Data Science, and Regulatory teams to ensure product development meets quality and compliance expectations.
Participate in design and development meetings, providing Quality input on user needs, design inputs and outputs, risk, and verification and validation.
Review software and hardware Verification & Validation (V&V) protocols and reports, ensuring requirements traceability and compliance with applicable standards, including IEC 62304 for software.
Facilitate and review product risk management files in line with ISO 14971, including software hazard analysis and risk mitigation.
Support usability engineering activities, including usability assessments and documentation in accordance with IEC 62366.
Ensure design documentation, risk files, and validation records are accurate, complete, and audit-ready.
Partner with Engineering and Product teams to identify design quality gaps, resolve issues, and drive continuous improvement.
Support design reviews, quality audits, CAPA activities, and regulatory readiness.


Requirements


Minimum 8 years of medical device industry experience.
Bachelor's degree in Engineering or any relevant field is required.
At least 3 years of experience working with US FDA requirements.
Hands-on Design Quality experience with both software and hardware medical devices.
Experience reviewing V&V protocols and reports, risk management documentation, and design control records.
Working knowledge of IEC 62304, ISO 14971, and IEC 62366.
Strong understanding of US FDA regulations (21 CFR 820 / ISO 13485)and medical device design quality requirements.
Ability to review technical documentation and identify design quality and compliance gaps.
Ability to work effectively with Product, Engineering, Data Science, and Regulatory teams.
Strong documentation, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
High ownership, attention to detail, and commitment to maintaining product quality and regulatory compliance.


Skills That Set You Up To Win


Strong understanding of US FDA regulations, medical device quality systems, and design assurance requirements.
Ability to translate regulatory and standards requirements into practical guidance for design and engineering teams..
Strong risk management, problem-solving, and quality remediation capabilities.
Ability to influence and collaborate with, Product, Engineering, Data Science, Regulatory, and Manufacturing teams.
Strong documentation and audit readiness skills.
High ownership, attention to detail, and ability to drive quality standards in a fast-paced product development environment.
