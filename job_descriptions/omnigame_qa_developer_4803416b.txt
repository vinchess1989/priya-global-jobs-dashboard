About The Job

Omnigame is a rapidly growing Danish company headquartered in central Copenhagen. We develop and operate fun and different online casino games on our own platform and casino site pip.dk. We benefit from an international work environment with 60+ employees in Copenhagen and colleagues from over ten different countries.

We are well established on the Danish market and are looking for new colleagues to help us fulfill our international ambitions. Omnigame consists of dedicated people - all with an entrepreneurial drive. We take pride in having a healthy work environment which is fun to be part of and where creativity can flourish.

We offer an exciting job with great opportunities for professional and personal development in an inspiring environment that prioritizes a culture of people empowerment where your impact will be welcomed. We value initiative, responsibility and the right balance between creativity and quality in all solutions.

The position

You will be joining the Games team alongside other game frontend, backend and math developers. We are looking for a skilled QA developer to test and raise the quality of games and internal tools, refine the architecture and processes, and collaborate with game creatives to develop state-of-the-art online slot games.

Work tasks


Own and evolve the test strategy
Perform comprehensive testing to ensure software system integrity
Configure test environments and support CI/CD pipelines
Participate in requirement analysis and user story refinement
Complete regression testing for software improvements
Document and report testing outcomes effectively
Support troubleshooting, bug triage, prioritization, and defect management
Build and maintain dashboards, metrics, and KPIs to measure quality and release health


Your Qualifications


Over 2 years of experience in iGaming QA for slot games
Deep understanding of QA methodologies: Unit, API, UI, Integration, Performance, E2E
Experience with Docker as GitHub CI/CD
Comfortable working with tools such as DevTools, cURL, or command - line utilities for debugging and testing
Proven experience designing and executing full-stack test strategies
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a strong sense of ownership, responsibility, and pride in delivering quality solutions
Ability to meet deadlines and maintain a positive attitude with a great sense of humor
Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail
Strong oral and written communication skills in English


Bonus Skills


Experience with Java, Kotlin and JavaScript
Broader knowledge of CI/CD tools


At Omnigame, you get the opportunity:

To work in a fantastic, social, and informal environment at full speed and lots of flexibility. We have a strong focus on personal development and favor an open and flat structure.

Additionally, you are offered pension, health insurance and a delicious lunch – including breakfast – and an incredible amount of cake. In short, you get the best conditions for having a great workday. Both in terms of tasks, but also when it comes to good and spirited colleagues as well as being part of a strong unity.

Location

Omnigame offers flexible work options, including on-site roles in Copenhagen or Malta, as well as hybrid and remote setups.
