Well, this may be the role for you. Ready to make your mark?

ADP is seeking a Senior Knowledge Content Developer to lead the creation, governance, maintenance, and continuous improvement of knowledge content that supports both associates and clients across ADP Canada's products and services. This senior-level individual contributor serves as a trusted advisor to business, product, and operational stakeholders, ensuring knowledge assets are accurate, accessible, scalable, and optimized for associate enablement, client self-service, and AI-powered experiences.

The role is responsible for managing the full knowledge lifecycle, from content strategy and requirements gathering through authoring, publication, maintenance, optimization, and retirement. This role works closely with Service, Implementation, Operations, and Technology teams to ensure knowledge content remains aligned with evolving business processes, product functionality, regulatory requirements, and client needs.

What you’ll do: Responsibilities

Knowledge Content Creation & Lifecycle Management


Develop, maintain, and optimize high-quality knowledge content supporting associates and clients across ADP Canada's products and services.
Create and manage multiple content types, including knowledge articles and in-product help and contextual guidance.
Translate business processes, product functionality, and operational requirements into clear, concise, and user-focused content.
Ensure content aligns with established writing standards, information architecture, metadata, taxonomy, accessibility, and compliance requirements.
Apply structured authoring principles to improve content consistency, reusability, discoverability, and maintainability.
Establish and execute content review and maintenance plans to ensure information remains current, accurate, and relevant.
Manage the complete content lifecycle, including content planning, creation, validation, publishing, monitoring, updating, archiving, and retirement.
Contribute to the evolution of content governance standards, templates, and reusable content models.


AI and Knowledge Optimization


Ensure knowledge assets are optimized for search, self-service, digital support channels, and generative AI consumption.
Apply content design best practices that improve findability, semantic clarity, and content usability across channels.
Partner with Knowledge, Product, and Technology teams to prepare knowledge assets for evolving AI and automation capabilities.


Business and Product Stakeholder Relationship & Collaboration


Build trusted relationships with product managers, subject matter experts, and operational teams and stakeholders.
Facilitate content discovery, requirements gathering, and knowledge transfer sessions to identify content needs and priorities.
Coordinate content reviews and approvals with subject matter experts and business stakeholders.
Ensure alignment between operational procedures, product functionality and knowledge content.
Contribute to enterprise knowledge governance programs and standards development initiatives.


Team Support, Continuous Improvement and Standards Development


Support team members in content development practices, writing techniques, and knowledge management principles.
Identify opportunities to improve content workflows, authoring processes, tools, and team effectiveness.
Recommend and implement best practices that improve content quality, consistency, scalability, and operational efficiency.
Stay current with emerging trends and best practices in knowledge management, content strategy, AI-enabled knowledge experiences, content operations, and digital support technologies.


TO SUCCEED IN THIS ROLE: Requirements


7+ years of experience in Knowledge Management, Content Strategy, Technical Writing, Content Development, or a related discipline.
Strong technical writing and content design expertise.
Strong knowledge and experience with structured authoring, DITA, XML, or component-based content development.
Experience working with Salesforce Knowledge, IXIASOFT, or similar platforms.
Experience with content governance, taxonomy, metadata management, and information architecture principles.
Experience supporting AI-powered search, knowledge retrieval, or generative AI initiatives.
Experience managing content through its full lifecycle, including governance, maintenance, and optimization.
Experience using knowledge management platforms, content management systems (CMS), or customer support content platforms.
Experience interpreting business requirements and translating complex concepts into user-friendly content.
Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
Quebec candidates: While French is required for Quebec-based roles, use of the English language is also required due to the global nature of the business and the need to interact with ADP’s headquarters and international sites.


Bonus points for these: Preferred Qualifications


Demonstrated experience creating and maintaining knowledge content supporting software, SaaS solutions, HR, payroll, workforce management, or complex business operations.
AI-powered search, knowledge retrieval, or generative AI initiatives.
Experience operating within Agile product development environments.
Knowledge of Canadian payroll, HR, workforce management, time and attendance, benefits, or HR technology solutions (Optional)
