Job Summary

About the Role

McKesson is seeking a Sr. Cloud Engineer to lead the design, implementation, support, and continuous improvement of enterprise cloud and commerce platform infrastructure. This role partners with Product, Architecture, Engineering, and Operations teams to deliver scalable, secure, highly available digital platforms supporting critical business capabilities. The ideal candidate brings deep expertise in cloud-native architecture, DevSecOps, platform engineering, incident management, automation, and modern commerce technologies.

What You'll Do


Lead the architecture, engineering, and operational support of cloud platforms across Azure and AWS environments.
Design and implement Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Bicep, CloudFormation) to standardize provisioning, governance, and scalability.
Support enterprise commerce platforms including SAP Commerce (Hybris), Adobe Commerce, Shopify, and Salesforce Commerce Cloud.
Build, maintain, and optimize CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, and Jenkins.
Drive containerization and orchestration strategies using Docker and Kubernetes.
Lead incident triage, major incident response, root cause analysis (RCA), and problem management activities.
Implement observability, monitoring, and alerting solutions using Dynatrace, Splunk, AppDynamics, and Azure Monitor.
Partner with cross-functional teams to drive cloud modernization, platform reliability, security, automation, and operational excellence.



Basic Requirements


7+ years of experience in Cloud Engineering, Infrastructure Engineering, Platform Engineering, Site Reliability Engineering, DevOps, or related technical disciplines.
Hands-on experience with Azure and/or AWS cloud platforms.
Experience designing and supporting cloud-native applications, microservices, and API-based architectures.
Experience with Infrastructure as Code using Terraform, Bicep, or CloudFormation.
Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, or Jenkins.
Experience with Kubernetes and Docker in enterprise environments.
Experience supporting production applications and managing incidents, outages, RCA, and problem management processes.
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or equivalent combination of education and experience.



Preferred Skills/Experience


Experience supporting SAP Commerce (Hybris), Adobe Commerce, Shopify, or Salesforce Commerce Cloud platforms.
Experience with event-driven architectures utilizing Kafka, Redis, messaging technologies, or streaming platforms.
Experience with SQL and NoSQL databases.
Strong understanding of DevSecOps practices, cloud security controls, RBAC, and compliance requirements.
Experience implementing observability and monitoring strategies using Splunk, Dynatrace, AppDynamics, or Azure Monitor.
Proficiency in PowerShell, Python, Shell scripting, or automation development.
Experience with release management, environment management, and deployment automation.
Experience within healthcare, pharmaceutical, retail, e-commerce, or other regulated industries.
Proven ability to influence engineering standards and lead technical initiatives across multiple teams.



Travel / Work Environment / Physical Requirements


Occasional travel may be required for team meetings, planning events, or business needs.
Position may operate in a remote, hybrid, or office-based environment based on business requirements.
Ability to work extended hours when supporting critical incidents or major production events.
Ability to remain engaged in computer-based work for extended periods.plus.
