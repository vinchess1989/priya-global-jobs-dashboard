Role Overview

Eutelsat is seeking a System Engineer be responsible for all the system requirements for both our own next generation satellite system and for the IRIS2 satellite system

Working within the wider System Engineering team, this role involves talking to the commercial side of the company to understand in detail what they want from our next generation system and then working with the internal engineering teams and external suppliers for the various components to work out what is achievable.

In the case of IRIS2, the main stakeholder is the European Commission.




Key Responsibilities

IRIS2 and OW Next System Requirements
Own the System Requirements document for both systems
Ensure traceability so it’s clear to what extent these fulfil the stakeholders’ requirements
System Architecture
Contribute to the top-level design of the system to understand how these requirements will be met.
Help determine and document the functional breakdown of the systems. Arbitrate trade-offs between various functional decomposition choices.
Verification and Validation
Work with the Integration, Verification and Validation to figure out how we are going to verify that the design and ultimately the system meets the requirements at the different stages of the program.




Required Experience

Requirements Management
Experience using at least one formal requirements management tool (e.g. DOORs / Jama or other) on a large project.
Communications Systems
Experience either with a satellite or a terrestrial communications system
Qualifications
A degree in telecommunications, electrical engineering, computer science, or a related discipline.




Desirable Experience

Experience with 5G systems and familiarity with 3GPP / 5G jargon.
Customer facing experience selling connectivity services.
Engineer facing experience building a connectivity system.




Candidate Profile

A System Engineer who can write clear, concise and unambiguous documents. You should be willing to take the time with stakeholders to make sure that you understand exactly what someone is asking for and that it can be expressed in a crystal-clear fashion. In some cases – this will require explaining to the stakeholders how our communications systems work, in other cases, you need to understand how the customer intends to use them so you need to be able to both absorb and disseminate information effectively.

You should also understand the appropriate level of detail to apply at the system level to allow component designers / vendors to use their domain expertise to make the best choices.
