Job Title

Product Definition Analyst

Summary of the Role

Join the Travel Distribution (TRD) Artificial Intelligence (AI) organization and help shape the next generation of AI-powered travel distribution products.

As a Product Definition Analyst (PDA), you’ll connect business and technical stakeholders, understand user and business needs, and translate them into clear product capabilities. You’ll work closely with Product Management, Architecture, Engineering, and AI specialists to develop solutions that are valuable for users, aligned with business needs, and sustainable over time.

In This Role You’ll / Your Main Responsibilities


Understand user and business needs: Collaborate with customers, business stakeholders, and product teams to understand user journeys, processes, challenges, and opportunities, and translate these insights into clear functional requirements and expected outcomes.
Define product capabilities and experiences: Create user stories, business rules, workflows, acceptance criteria, and functional specifications, while designing end-to-end experiences that are understandable, testable, and maintainable.
Contribute to AI-enabled solutions: Explore how technologies such as Generative Artificial Intelligence (Generative AI), AI agents, and intelligent automation can address user and business needs, and define the functional requirements that support their responsible integration into products.
Support delivery and validation: Work with Agile software development teams throughout the product lifecycle to clarify requirements, support implementation, validate delivered functionality against agreed expectations, and contribute to resolving functional issues.
Connect teams and share knowledge: Work across Product Management, Architecture, Engineering, Operations, and business teams to maintain a shared understanding of product goals, provide functional expertise, maintain relevant documentation, and contribute to continuous product improvement.


About The Ideal Candidate

You’ll be a good fit for this role if you bring relevant experience and enjoy working across business, technology, and user needs. We encourage you to apply even if your experience does not match every aspect listed below.


Experience in Product Definition, Functional Analysis, Business Analysis, or a related role, including translating business needs into clear product requirements or specifications.
Experience working with Agile software development teams, with knowledge of areas such as requirements elicitation, user stories, functional specifications, business process modelling, functional design, Scrum methodologies, or product lifecycle management.
Understanding of technical concepts relevant to digital products, including Application Programming Interfaces (APIs) and data integration, with an interest in developing your understanding of AI-enabled products and workflows.
Effective problem-solving, critical-thinking, communication, and facilitation skills, including the ability to simplify complex topics, organize information clearly, and work effectively with business and technical stakeholders.
Ability to understand customer needs and collaborate across teams, together with an interest in learning and applying new approaches. Experience in the travel search or shopping domain can help you get started in the role, but candidates with relevant experience from other domains are also encouraged to apply.
