OCU Group is a global infrastructure and energy services business with more than 5,000 people delivering projects that power communities and support the energy transition.

Our Australian and New Zealand operations are growing fast, bringing together great businesses including All Energy Contracting (AEC), Bam Bam, Pilecom and Volta under one group.

All Energy Contracting (AEC) is a leading multidisciplinary construction contractor specialising in renewables and energy infrastructure projects. We deliver projects across solar, wind, battery energy storage systems (BESS), high voltage connections, substations, balance of plant and associated civil, electrical and mechanical works.

About the opportunity

Every successful project runs on accurate, accessible information. As a Document Controller in our Project Management Office (PMO), you’ll make sure the right people have the right documents at the right time, across a portfolio of projects delivered by AEC and the wider OCU ANZ Group.

Reporting to our Senior Document Controller, you’ll manage the control, distribution and archiving of project documentation across the full project lifecycle. You’ll work closely with engineering, procurement, construction, commissioning and commercial teams, and you’ll be the person they rely on to keep information flowing and documentation compliant.

This role suits someone with a few years of document control experience who is ready to broaden their exposure across multiple projects and grow within a supportive PMO team.

What you’ll do

Document control

Manage document control systems, registers and logs, making sure numbering, metadata and revisions are accurate and up to date
Issue and track transmittals, and coordinate review, approval and distribution workflows
Keep documentation organised so project teams can find what they need quickly

Engineering and project documentation

Process engineering drawings, specifications and technical documents
Maintain contractual deliverable registers and track subcontractor submissions
Help compile commissioning records, O&M manuals and as-built documentation for project handover

Quality and compliance

Uphold PMO document governance standards across the portfolio
Support internal and external quality audits
Spot document control issues or delays early and escalate them so they get resolved

What you’ll bring

2–5 years’ document control experience in construction, infrastructure or renewables
Hands-on experience with Aconex, SharePoint, TeamBinder or a similar CDE platform
An understanding of how projects run and why governance matters
Strong Microsoft Office skills
A sharp eye for detail and the ability to juggle competing priorities
Confidence communicating with people at all levels, from site teams to senior stakeholders

What we offer
