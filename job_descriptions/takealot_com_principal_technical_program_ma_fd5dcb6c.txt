Purpose of Role

The Principal Technical Program Manager owns the integrated delivery plan and cross-programme view across Takealot Group's largest parallel engineering programmes. These multi-team initiatives, spanning app integrations, core platform migrations, and logistics re-architecture, cross numerous teams, systems, and business units while drawing on the same shared pool of senior engineers.

This role involves building bottom-up delivery plans grounded in real scope and real team throughput, identifying critical paths, and escalating cross-programme trade-offs early to the right decision-makers with clear options and the cost of delay for each. While each individual programme retains its own delivery lead for day-to-day execution, the Principal Technical Program Manager plays a pivotal role in orchestrating cross-programme alignment, capacity visibility, and executive decision-making across the Group Engineering function.

Key Responsibilities

Integrated Programme Planning & Delivery


Own the end-to-end integrated delivery plan across major parallel engineering programmes, and answer for committed delivery dates.
Build delivery plans bottom-up from actual Jira scope and real team throughput, challenging any estimate that cannot be traced to either.
Identify critical paths and re-sequence work where necessary to shorten delivery timelines.


Dependency & Capacity Management


Map dependencies across internal teams, system components, and external parties (including app store reviews, contractors, and vendors), assigning a clear owner and need-by date to every dependency.
Maintain a single, consolidated view of shared senior engineering capacity across programmes.
Escalate capacity conflicts and resource trade-offs early to the Group CTO and relevant decision forums, backed by clear options and the quantified cost of delay for each.


Governance, Cadence & Executive Reporting


Lead the programme governance rhythm, including weekly delivery reviews and consolidated risk and issue registers.
Provide structured programme inputs to steering committees, executive gates, and weekly/monthly business reviews.
Write clear, data-led narratives and decision papers that enable the executive team to take immediate action without requiring follow-up status meetings.


Operational Readiness & Performance Measurement


Define and track programme Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) in partnership with Product and Engineering leads.
Drive end-to-end operational readiness for every launch, ensuring rollout plans, runbooks, monitoring, support structures, and communications are fully in place prior to go-live.


Minimum Required Qualification and Experience


Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a related technical field.
Programme or project management certification (PMP, PgMP, MSP, or equivalent) (Preferred).
8+ years of technical programme management experience working directly with software engineering teams.
3+ years of experience as a software engineer, or equivalent hands-on technical depth.
Proven track record managing at least one portfolio comprising several multi-team programmes with hard external dependencies.
Proven track record driving delivery across multiple engineering and product teams without direct line-reporting authority.


Output


Creating: Building bottom-up, evidence-based integrated delivery plans, cross-team dependency registers, and clear executive decision papers from scratch across complex parallel initiatives.
Maintaining: Continuously updating consolidated senior engineering capacity views, weekly programme risk/issue registers, and programme KPI tracking across active portfolios.
Managing: Orchestrating cross-programme delivery cadences, tracking external vendor/contractor and app store dependencies to closure, and driving launch operational readiness (runbooks, monitoring, rollout, support).
Planning: Mapping critical paths, aligning multi-team schedules to real throughput and Jira scope, and sequencing complex engineering work to optimise delivery speed.
Strategy: Framing resource conflicts and portfolio trade-offs with quantified cost-of-delay analyses to guide Group CTO and executive-level strategic decisions.
Learning: Maintaining deep technical fluency in distributed systems, mobile release processes, and logistics/e-commerce architectures to accurately interrogate engineering estimates.
Teaching: Raising the bar for programme execution across Group Engineering by modelling evidence-based estimation, rigorous dependency tracking, and concise executive writing.
Collaboration: Partnering across Engineering, Product, business units, individual programme delivery leads, and external contractors/vendors to align execution without direct line authority.


Minimum Required Competencies (Skills, Knowledge & Abilities)


Proven track record elevating complex resource and timeline trade-offs to executive leadership and securing timely decisions.
Mastered foundational and advanced technical programme management skills, with the technical depth to read architecture designs, trace service dependency graphs, and distinguish realistic estimates from optimistic ones.
Complete autonomy in structuring complex multi-programme delivery plans, identifying critical paths, and re-sequencing work to accelerate outcomes.
Decisions and escalations directly impact department- and group-wide engineering delivery across major strategic initiatives.
Proactively identifies and raises risks early, supported by hard data, quantitative impact, and actionable recommendations.
Earned trust and respect of senior engineering, product, and business peers; leads effectively through influence across cross-functional teams.
Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, capable of distilling complex technical dependencies into brief, actionable executive narratives.
Proficient in Jira and enterprise portfolio planning tools.
Solutions- and results-oriented with a rigorous, evidence-based approach to estimation and scheduling.
Strong analytical and critical thinking skills, using throughput metrics and cost-of-delay data to inform executive trade-offs.
Proven ability to build productive working relationships and manage delivery across external contractors and vendor teams (Advantageous).
Domain experience in logistics, supply chain, fulfilment, or on-demand delivery (Advantageous).
Experience managing mobile app delivery, including Apple App Store and Google Play Store release processes (Advantageous).
Familiarity with distributed systems or event-driven architectures (Advantageous).
