About the Role

Glen Fisk Recruitment is seeking an experienced Release Coordinator / Stream Lead to support the delivery of enterprise-level ICT integration solutions within a large and complex environment.

In this role, you will lead and coordinate multiple Agile delivery teams, overseeing the development, testing and release of software integration solutions. You will work closely with technical teams, Product Owners and key stakeholders to ensure successful project delivery while managing risks, dependencies and competing priorities.

This is an excellent opportunity for an experienced Agile delivery professional with a strong technical background to contribute to significant ICT initiatives and drive continuous improvement across software delivery processes.

Key Responsibilities

Lead and coordinate multiple Agile teams delivering enterprise-level integration solutions.
Facilitate Scrum ceremonies, including daily stand-ups, sprint planning, reviews and retrospectives.
Identify and remove impediments to maintain team momentum and delivery efficiency.
Support Product Owners in refining, prioritising and maintaining product backlogs.
Oversee the end-to-end delivery of software integration solutions, ensuring alignment with business and technical requirements.
Coordinate the verification, testing and release of software packages developed by delivery teams.
Ensure all deliverables comply with relevant policies, procedures and quality standards.
Monitor team performance, delivery progress and project milestones.
Identify, manage and escalate technical dependencies, risks and issues.
Provide regular reporting on delivery progress, Agile metrics, risks and issues to key stakeholders.
Collaborate with developers, automation testers, technical business analysts and senior technical leaders to support successful delivery.
Essential
Demonstrated experience leading multiple Agile teams within a large-scale enterprise environment.
Extensive experience managing the end-to-end delivery of enterprise-level software solutions.
Strong understanding of Agile methodologies, Scrum practices and software development lifecycles.
Proven ability to facilitate Agile ceremonies and manage team delivery activities.
Experience using Agile Application Lifecycle Management (ALM) tools, particularly Azure DevOps.
Strong technical understanding of software development practices, integration solutions and release management.
Demonstrated ability to identify and manage technical risks, dependencies and delivery challenges.
Excellent stakeholder engagement, communication and relationship management skills.
Strong organisational, problem-solving and leadership capabilities.
Ability to manage competing priorities while ensuring delivery outcomes are achieved.
Desirable
Previous experience coordinating software releases within complex enterprise ICT environments.
Experience delivering enterprise integration solutions across multiple systems and platforms.
Knowledge of software testing, automation testing and release verification processes.
Experience working with blended teams of contractors and permanent employees.
Familiarity with continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) practices.
Experience working closely with Product Owners, technical specialists and senior leadership teams.
Ability to drive continuous improvement across Agile delivery processes and team performance.
