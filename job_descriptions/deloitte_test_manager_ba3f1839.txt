Job Description

Your role as a leader

This is an exciting opportunity for an experienced Test Manager to join our Wellington team. In this role, you will help shape the future of testing and quality engineering, driving innovation and supporting digital transformation across a range of complex client environments.

You will play a key role in delivering quality outcomes across client engagements while also contributing to the growth of our Quality Engineering (QE) practice. This includes developing high-performing teams, strengthening client relationships, and supporting the pursuit of new opportunities.

The impact you’ll make


Build and maintain strong, trusted relationships with clients and vendors through delivery excellence, thought leadership, and market presence
Manage project delivery, including planning, budgets, risks, and reporting
Lead, coach, and develop high-performing teams, driving a culture of continuous improvement and quality
Translate complex testing data into meaningful insights, providing clear recommendations and articulating risks and value to senior stakeholders
Facilitate client workshops and stakeholder engagements, helping teams navigate complex challenges and align to programme goals
Apply Deloitte and industry-leading Quality Engineering frameworks to uplift testing capability and delivery standards
Solve complex business challenges by designing fit-for-purpose testing solutions
Drive continuous learning and adoption of emerging tools, practices, and methodologies
Manage QE resources across local and global delivery teams
Provide testing advisory services, including process optimisation and quality uplift across programmes


Qualifications

Your expertise


Proven Test Manager with 6+ years’ experience delivering and scaling enterprise testing capability across Large ERP Transformation Programmes particularly in government or enterprise environments
Expertise in end-to-end test management, including strategy, planning, execution, governance, and executive reporting
Hands-on leader with experience building and leading QA and technical test teams
Experience across the full testing lifecycle: unit, system, integration, UAT, regression, performance, API, and data validation
Proven delivery across Agile, hybrid, and traditional methodologies, including multi-vendor environments
Experience supporting cloud transformations, including cutover, data migration validation, and business readiness
Familiarity with tools such as JIRA, ALM, and enterprise test management solutions, including environment and defect management
Strong capability in integration and API testing across complex system landscapes
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to engage confidently at all levels
Commercially aware, with experience working in consulting or client-facing environments
Relevant tertiary qualification (or equivalent experience) in IT, Computer Science, Information Systems, or a related discipline
