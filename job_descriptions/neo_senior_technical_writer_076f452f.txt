About the role

Neo is seeking a Senior Technical Writer to join our Product Documentation team and take ownership of how Neo's products explain themselves - through feature docs, user guides, knowledge base articles, FAQs, release notes, and any other technical writing we publish for the outside world.

At Neo, documentation isn't a support function bolted onto the product - it's a product surface in its own right, and a community asset that accelerates adoption by helping people learn and trust Neo. We run docs-as-code and docs-as-a-product: writing lives in Git, ships through review like any other product change, and is held to a real quality bar, built in close partnership with Product and Engineering.

You read specs closely enough to catch what they leave out, understand what a user actually needs to know, and write it clearly. You don't stop at drafting one page at a time, you build the systems behind it: the skill files and prompt templates that let AI tools generate consistent first drafts from PRDs and tickets, grounded in a tone and style you've codified so every draft, human or AI, sounds like Neo.

This is a hands-on, build-the-system individual contributor role that calls for a combination of strong editorial judgment and an automation mindset.




Responsibilities




Write & organize

Read PRDs and engineering specs closely, and translate them into clear, concise documentation - user guides, knowledge base articles - organized with clean, findable information architecture

Set the standard

Define and maintain Neo's documentation style guide, voice, and tone; and hold every contributor to it
Own end-to-end documentation quality (completeness, accuracy, consistency, tone) across every Neo product, and report on documentation health (coverage, freshness, usage) on an ongoing basis

Partner & advocate

Flag issues with the underlying feature, not just the docs, when something won't serve users well or a spec lacks the detail needed for accurate documentation; partner closely with PMs and engineers to close those gaps and stay aligned with the release roadmap

Build the system

Set direction for Neo's AI-powered documentation automation - skill files, prompt templates, scripts - deciding what to automate and how it should scale; maintain and evolve these systems as products and doc formats change




Skills

Technical fluency to read, question, and explain PRDs and API specifications directly with engineers and PMs, understand and document Neo's APIs and MCP tools, and ensure the accuracy of every customer-facing doc that depends on them
Strong written communication - this is a writing-first role requiring the editorial judgment to set the bar for others, plus experience defining or maintaining a style guide, voice/tone standard, or review process, and the instinct to know when something's ready to publish
Strong project-management instincts - able to plan, prioritize, and carry a complex documentation project to an accurate, polished finish, with working knowledge of tools like Jira, Linear, or Notion
Hands-on experience with AI - not just prompting in the moment, but building the reusable system behind it (skill files, prompt templates, context files) so AI can produce consistent, on-brand first drafts without constant re-prompting
Systems-thinking mindset - with automation instincts, able to design processes that scale as the team and product surface grow




Experience

5+ years writing technical documentation, with a solid understanding of version control and experience managing documentation systems
1–2+ years owning editorial standards or leading a documentation function for a team - ideally at a B2B SaaS or product company
Track record partnering with product and engineering teams to close documentation gaps and improve PRD quality
Computer science/ background
Nice to have: experience contributing to or maintaining an open source project
Nice to have: a public writing track record on technical or documentation topics - a blog, newsletter, or bylined guest posts
