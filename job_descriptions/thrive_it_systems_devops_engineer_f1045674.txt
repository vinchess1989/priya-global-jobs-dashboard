Job Summary:




Seeking a Specialist with 5 to 7 years of experience in DevOps CICD to design automate and manage containerized infrastructure using Ansible Docker Terraform Shell Scripting and Kubernetes




Job Description:




Design deploy and maintain scalable Kubernetes clusters to support application lifecycle and business requirements
Develop and maintain infrastructure as code using Terraform for consistent environment provisioning
Create and optimize automation scripts with Ansible and Shell Scripting to streamline configuration management and operational tasks
Manage container environments using Docker to ensure efficient application packaging and deployment
Collaborate with development and operations teams to implement and enhance CICD pipelines ensuring seamless application delivery
Monitor infrastructure and cluster performance resource utilization and availability to ensure high reliability and scalability
Troubleshoot and resolve issues related to container orchestration infrastructure automation and deployment processes
Stay current with industry best practices and emerging technologies in Kubernetes automation and DevOps methodologies
Utilize Python scripting skills to enhance automation and operational efficiency where applicable




Roles and Responsibilities:




Lead the design and implementation of Kubernetes based infrastructure solutions aligned with organizational goals
Automate routine operational and deployment tasks using Ansible playbooks Shell scripts Terraform modules and Docker workflows
Ensure adherence to security best practices throughout container orchestration and infrastructure automation
Collaborate with cross functional teams to gather requirements and deliver robust scalable and secure infrastructure solutions
Participate in capacity planning disaster recovery and backup strategy formulation for Kubernetes environments
Mentor and provide technical guidance to junior team members to foster knowledge sharing and skill development
Document infrastructure configurations automation workflows and troubleshooting procedures to maintain a comprehensive knowledge base
Drive continuous improvement initiatives within the DevOps CICD skill cluster
