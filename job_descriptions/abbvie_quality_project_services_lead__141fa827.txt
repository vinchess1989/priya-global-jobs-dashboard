Job Description

We are now hiring a Quality Projects Services Lead on our Ballytivnan site in Sligo. The Quality Project Services Lead will manage a team of quality team members, develops and maintains quality systems and procedures that ensure products meet quality standards, and leads NPI product transfer quality at AbbVie, Ballytivnan.

People Leadership


Manage a team of individual contributors, setting clear goals and objectives in line with site priorities
Conduct career development conversations, regular coaching and performance feedback, and establishing development plans for team members
Builds a team culture with active participation from team members


Quality Systems


Develop and implement quality systems and procedures to ensure products meet quality standards and regulatory requirements
Lead key quality systems, including training, metrics and reporting, management review and change management.
Maintain and continually improve the AbbVie Ballytivnan Quality Management System, with continued oversight of Regulatory Compliance/ISO requirements
Ensure site activities comply with applicable regulations and internal quality requirement.
Lead Notified Body Audits, Audit Readiness, execution and follow up items.
Maintain and Manage the Quality Plan and Quality Manual for AbbVie Medical Devices.
Ownership of Change Management Process, CRB and Change Coordination as required.
Report Quality Metrics for input into Global Metrics Management and Quality Index.


NPI and Product Transfer


Act as QA lead for NPI Product Transfer projects, when a design transfer plan is transferred to site
Execute project quality plans and provide quality governance for project deliverables.


Audits and Inspection Readiness


Manage external audits, including preparation, response and follow-up of findings
Own the internal audit program, including scheduling, execution, reporting and follow-up of corrective actions.
Lead inspection readiness activities and take ownership of the area during regulatory inspections and corporate audits


Cross-Functional Leadership


Lead or participate in cross-functional investigations, improvement initiatives and business-critical projects
Communicate quality risks, performance, priorities and required actions to site leadership and relevant functions


Qualifications

Essential Requirements


Third level qualification in a science/engineering discipline with at least 5 years’ experience in the healthcare/pharmaceutical/medical device industry.
Practical understanding of GMP and regulated Quality environments
Working knowledge of ISO13485 / 9001 or other related standards supporting medical device compliance
Demonstration of supervisor and leadership capability


Desired Requirements


Project Management qualification(s)
Lean / Six Sigma certification(s)
Auditor certification (s)
NPI/design-transfer experience
Experience with systems such as SAP and TrackWise
