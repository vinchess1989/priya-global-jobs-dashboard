Role Overview:




We are seeking a DevOps Engineer with 5-12 years of experience in building and managing CI/CD pipelines, container platforms, cloud infrastructure, and deployment automation. The role requires strong hands-on experience with Jenkins, GitLab, Git, Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, OpenShift, and Azure to support enterprise application delivery, platform reliability, operational excellence, and cloud-native transformation initiatives.




Key Responsibilities:




Design, implement, and maintain CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins and GitLab CI/CD.
Manage Git repositories, branching strategies, merge requests, release workflows, and deployment automation.
Build, deploy, and support containerized applications using Docker.
Administer, monitor, and troubleshoot Kubernetes and OpenShift platforms across non-production and production environments.
Create, maintain, and optimize Helm charts for repeatable and controlled application deployments.
Deploy and manage Azure cloud resources with focus on availability, scalability, security, and performance.
Troubleshoot deployment and platform issues, perform Root Cause Analysis (RCA), automate recurring tasks, and collaborate with development, QA, security, and infrastructure teams.




Required Skills:




5-10 years of experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering, Cloud Engineering, or Infrastructure Automation.
Strong hands-on experience with Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, Git, Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, and OpenShift.
Experience with Microsoft Azure services and cloud-native application architectures.
Strong understanding of CI/CD, release management, deployment automation, and Infrastructure as Code principles.
Experience with Linux/Unix administration and troubleshooting.
Hands-on scripting experience using Shell Scripting (Bash) and PowerShell.
Strong troubleshooting, analytical, communication, stakeholder management, and Agile/Scrum collaboration skills.




Good to Have:




Experience with Terraform, Ansible, Azure DevOps, or GitHub Actions.
Experience and understanding of Airflow, Databricks, & Snowflake.
Exposure to monitoring and observability tools such as Splunk, Grafana, Prometheus, Dynatrace, ELK, or AppDynamics.
Knowledge of DevSecOps, container security, and vulnerability management; Azure, Kubernetes, OpenShift, or DevOps certifications are an advantage.
