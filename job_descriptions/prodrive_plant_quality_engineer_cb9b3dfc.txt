At Prodrive we believe there's no limit to what can be achieved when the brightest minds come together. Joining one focused and driven team, you will challenge boundaries in order to change the game for our customers.

We are looking for a Quality Engineer to be responsible for ensuring that vehicles, components and manufacturing processes consistently meet engineering specifications, customer requirements, applicable standards and internal quality expectations within our new Birmingham-based Car Build facility. You will have responsibility for developing and maintaining quality controls throughout the vehicle build process, from incoming vehicle and component inspection through to final vehicle inspection and release.

Key Responsibilities

Problem Resolution (Plant/Customer):



Lead investigations using appropriate tools to establish clear root cause(s), and coach team members where appropriate.
Implement and validate robust permanent corrective actions.
Co-ordinate thorough containment of any non-conforming material when concerns arise, eliminating potential escape routes and ensuring manufacturing and production streams have validated stock available for use.



Vehicle Quality & Release



Provide technical quality support for incoming vehicle inspections and develop processes for recording vehicle condition and specification.
Conduct in process inspections at appropriate quality gates to ensure process outputs are conforming and consistent.



Continuous Improvement:



Maintain core tools documents: PPAP, control plans
Use KPI data to identify trends and developing improvement plans where targets are not met.
Where applicable, maintain the integrity of the integrated Quality and Environmental Management System to support continued certification.



Who are we looking for?



Proven experience in a Quality Engineering role within an automotive, aerospace, motorsport, or another regulated engineering and manufacturing environment.
Strong understanding of APQP, PPAP, FMEA, SPC and other core quality tools.
Hands-on experience supporting prototype, pre-series, and low-volume production environments.
Excellent analytical skills with a data-driven mindset.
Commercially aware and able to balance quality requirements with production, cost and delivery considerations.
Able to interpret complex engineering drawings, CAD, and GD&T.
Strong communication skills with the ability to influence at all levels internally, and external at customers and suppliers.
Experience leading root cause investigations and delivering sustainable improvements.
Comfortable working in a fast-paced, low-volume/high-mix engineering environment.
