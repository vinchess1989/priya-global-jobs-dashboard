Job Description

Permanent | Gauteng | Banking


In banking, the difference between a minor incident and a headline is usually measured in credentials. This permanent role puts you at the controls of CyberArk Credential Provider, Central Credential Provider and Conjur—designing, implementing and governing the secret management layer that keeps a major financial institution’s machine identities and privileged access airtight.


You will own the policies, the pipelines, the integrations and the audits. The work is precise, high-stakes and entirely practical. If you already live in CyberArk secret management and Azure DevOps YAML, this is the seat where that expertise actually matters.




Key Responsibilities:


•        Design, implement, and manage Secret Management solutions using CyberArk Credential Provider, Central Credential Provider and CyberArk Conjur.


•        Develop and enforce policies and procedures for secure handling of secrets and credentials.


•        Monitor and audit secret management systems to ensure compliance with security standards.


•        Collaborate with IT and security teams to integrate secret management solutions with existing infrastructure.


•        Provide training and support to team members on best practices for secret management using machine identities.


•        Stay updated with the latest trends and technologies in secret management and cybersecurity.
