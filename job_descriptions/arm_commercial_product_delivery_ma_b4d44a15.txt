Job Overview

Assist the product owner in defining data, lifecycle, and operating changes to establish and embed the commercial product team as recognised function owners within Arm.

Lead the delivery of the commercial product change roadmap by using in-depth knowledge of Arm commercial products and transaction flows. Ensure initiatives are carried out efficiently and lead to clear improvements in product readiness, quality, and operational performance.

Ensure timely set up and readiness of commercial products to meet the needs of Arm Product Managers, Licensing and delivery teams and in alignment with team targets.

Responsibilities


Deliver operational Change
Support Product Owner in rolling out and embedding Commercial Product definition and operating model
Translate agreed lifecycle changes into actionable Commercial Product requirements and delivery plans, working with relevant teams to support effective execution.
Embed Commercial Product processes within the Concept to Withdraw lifecycle, ensuring creation, readiness, change and withdrawal activities are implemented at the appropriate lifecycle points.
Requirements & Change Definition
Support definition of detailed requirements and acceptance criteria for roadmap initiatives, working with Product Owner, commercial product team and collaborators
Ensure delivery requirements remain clear, structured, and traceable throughout implementation
Understand that the Arm environment is changing and needs may shift during delivery. Lead this well to keep alignment with the target business outcomes set by the Product Owner.
Delivery Management & Change Implementation
Ensure alignment across teams involved in delivering change, including IT and PPP
Proactively drive progress across multiple initiatives
Lead efficient delivery planning and tracking efforts
Identify risks, dependencies, and blockers early and bring them up to the Product Owner
Ensure consistent and effective use of Jira across the team
Assist the Product Owner in change initiatives to guarantee successful adoption by the organisation
Assistance with Commercial Product Setup and Readiness
Perform commercial product setup tasks according to established processes and standards
Support Product Managers in understanding product setup requirements and processes
Support timely completion of activities required to achieve commercial readiness
Identify and bring up issues, risks, or missing information that could impact readiness timelines


Necessary Skills And Experience


Working knowledge of SAP or similar ERP systems
Understanding of product lifecycle and commercial readiness concepts
Strong experience in requirement elicitation and generation from all parts of the business
Experience translating business requirements into change proposals and actionable delivery plans
Comfortable operating in ambiguity and driving clarity across delivery activities
Project management / scrum delivery experience, including sprint planning and backlog management
Experience in cross-team change delivery, transformation, or programme coordination


Preferred Skills And Experience


Awareness of sales order and product transacting processes
Solid understanding of Arm’s products
Experience in configuring Jira, maintaining it, and tracking delivery
