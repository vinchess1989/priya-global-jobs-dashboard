Feroze Malik

GxPeople is partnering with a fast-growing UK MedTech company that makes orthopaedic devices and surgical solutions. The business is moving into new products and global markets, including a full range of CE-marked implantable devices. It needs a hands-on QA/RA Specialist to help drive the next phase of growth.




This role has real ownership. You’ll work from product development through to market release and help shape how the quality system scales.




What you’ll do

Maintain and secure ISO 13485, CE and EU MDR approvals across the device portfolio
Support regulatory work for new implantable products
Manage internal and external audits
Own the CAPA process
Bring manufacturing process control into new product development, including additive manufacturing
Write and improve procedures, work instructions, plans and reports
Build a strong culture of quality and best practice




What you’ll bring

A degree in a scientific, engineering, regulatory, quality or legal discipline
3–5 years’ QA/RA experience within surgical or orthopaedic medical devices
Practical knowledge of ISO 13485, ISO 14971, CE marking, EU MDR and FDA 510(k)
Experience with QMS software
Clear technical writing and strong problem-solving skills




Nice to have

IEC 62304 software validation or SaMD experience
Additive manufacturing (3D printing) process control
A second language




What’s on offer

£45,000–£60,000 base salary, plus pension and benefits
Fully remote with flexible hours
Patient-specific orthopaedic technology that changes lives
Visible impact in a growing company
Clear routes for professional development
