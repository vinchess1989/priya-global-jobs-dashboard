Azenta Life Sciences are seeking a Quality and Regulatory Manager to lead and inspire a high-performing quality team to deliver exceptional product standards and customer satisfaction. Drive continuous improvement through data-driven insights, advanced quality systems, and collaboration across all functions. Champion compliance, sustainability, and operational excellence in a fast-paced, global environment.




Full time, Permanent

Onsite: Wotton, Surrey




What You'll Be Doing




Ensure a safe, compliant workplace aligned with Health, Safety, Environmental, and regulatory standards.
Own and evolve the site’s Quality Management System (QMS) to maintain ISO9001:2015 & ISO13485 certifications and readiness for future standards.
Lead initiatives to reduce defects and customer complaints through root cause analysis, predictive analytics, and proactive prevention strategies.
Partner with operations and engineering to embed ‘Right First Time’ principles and digital quality tools.
Drive continuous improvement using Lean, Six Sigma, and automation to optimize processes and enhance customer experience.
Oversee audits, compliance programs, and corrective actions with rigor and transparency.
Coach and develop team members, fostering a culture of accountability, innovation, and collaboration.




Metrics:

Customer Complaint Reduction

Defect Rate Improvement

On-Time Delivery Performance

Compliance Audit Scores

Cost of Poor Quality (PoNC)




What You'll Bring




Wealth of quality leadership within manufacturing or regulated environments.
Strong knowledge of ISO standards, QMS platforms, and data analytics tools.
Proven ability to lead cross-functional teams and manage change effectively.
Experience with Lean/Six Sigma methodologies and digital transformation initiatives.




Core Competencies:




Digital Fluency: Comfortable using ERP, QMS, and data analytics platforms to drive decisions.

Customer-Centric Mindset: Prioritizes customer experience and quality outcomes in every process.

Change Leadership: Skilled at leading teams through transformation and continuous improvement initiatives.

Risk Management: Proactively identifies and mitigates operational and compliance risks.

Data-Driven Decision Making: Uses metrics, trend analysis, and predictive tools to guide quality strategies.

Collaboration & Influence: Builds strong relationships across functions and geographies to achieve shared goals.

Innovation Orientation: Seeks out new technologies and methods to enhance quality and efficiency.

Sustainability Awareness: Integrates environmental and social responsibility into quality practices.
