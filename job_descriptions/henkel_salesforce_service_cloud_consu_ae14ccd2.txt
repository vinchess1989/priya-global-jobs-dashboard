About this Position:

Manage the Salesforce Service Cloud application, including incident and change management, user support, development team coordination, enhancements, releases, security and compliance, while ensuring system performance and SLA adherence

What you’ll do:


Responsible for the incident and changing management process
Act as application manager Service cloud
Responsible for Level1 user support; escalation to L2
Handle service requests and lead and manage the Henkel Salesforce Service cloud Product (Developers) team
Implement enhancements and process changes and act as release/deployment manager
Responsible for the IT security and compliance (Access control concept, back up strategy, etc)
Ensure service level agreements (SLAs) are met and monitor performance metrics


What makes you good fit:


Bachelor’s / master’s degree in computer science, IT or a related field
Minimum 3+ years of proven experience managing Salesforce applications in a global business (user) environment and 3+ years of professional experience as Salesforce Developer
Experience as (Developer) Team Manager
Strong understanding of application life-cycle management, release management, and IT service management processes. Experience with Azure DevOps for backlog management, release planning, and Agile delivery processes
Managed global user community, fluent in English, global mindset


Some perks of joining Henkel:


Flexible work scheme with flexible hours, hybrid work model, and work from anywhere policy for up to 30 days per year
Diverse national and international growth opportunities
Globally wellbeing standards with health and preventive care programs Gender-neutral parental leave for a minimum of 8 weeks Employee Share Plan with voluntary investment and Henkel matching shares
Best-in-class Group Medical Insurance policy covering employee, spouse and up to 2 children
Competitive accident and term life policies for up to 3 times annual gross salary
Progressive OPD policy of INR 30,000 for employee, spouse and up to 2 children
