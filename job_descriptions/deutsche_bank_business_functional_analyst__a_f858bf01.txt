Position Overview

Job Title: Business Functional Analyst, AVP

Location: Pune, India

Role Description


Deutsche Bank is actively renewing its digital channels and has started many strategic and challenging projects. Next to some major technological changes Deutsche Bank has decided to step into the Agile software development methodology in order to improve its agility and reduce time to market without scarifying quality.
The team is on a growth path focused on internalization derived from increasing demand by internal stakeholders. Therefore the team is looking for enthusiastic team players with a passion to deliver change to make the bank achieve program goals, more stable, but also more efficient and effective.
The Business Functional Analyst has to support wide range of solutions for projects for meeting the business expectations from application. The typical roles include working as part of broader project teams with personal ownership for key deliverables.

Your Key Responsibilities


Play critical role of SME/L3 support in gathering, clarifying, documenting requirements and perform test/ production validations, provide sign-off
Understanding, Challenging, capturing, and articulating requirements according to the quality standards
Participate and help development and testing teams in strategic projects and DR exercises
Managing the requirement priorities transparently which are agreed in stakeholders
Supports preparation, pre-requisites, feasibility studies towards creation of business cases outlining value drivers of proposed solutions for business clients in crucial projects
Identify system defects and proactively recommend solutions/resolutions
Collaborate with developers and subject matter experts, Project Managers to establish the technical vision and analyze trade-offs between usability and performance needs
Identify areas for improvement and delivery capabilities
Have demonstrated proficiency in working with global and local teams, managing vendors and/or 3rd parties
Problem solving ability managing unexpected events



Your Skills And Experience

As BFA you will bring:


At least 9+ years of experience in role for Business Functional Analyst
Strong analysis skills and should be able to demonstrate expertise in impact analysis, business processes, writing/simplifying requirements and use cases
Previous experience in Banking/ Financial Domain, accounts, transaction processing for private/corporate customers will be a plus
Strong experience in driving business discussions, business workshops, processes, meetings and translate into requirement documents
Should understand definition of technical requirements for transaction representation
Experience to deliver artefacts as needed: Business Requirements Document, Functional Specification Document, PRD, Use Cases, estimates
conceptualize complex process flows and business flows
Hands-on experience in End to End business processes, requirements engineering for financial institutions, process management tools and techniques
Should be able to design E2E services from functional processing aspects in mind
Experience in stakeholder management like business units/users, infrastructure teams, audit, management, DEV/Testing teams
Should have good understanding of LPT, smoke, E-2-E tests and performance issues
Experience working in entire project lifecycle from pre kick-off until post production
Should be fluent in clarifying, breaking down requirements with development & testing teams, program management
Should be comfortable with agile/waterfall model, create user stories where applicable
Experience in delivering projects in a challenging environment with tight timelines
Awareness/experience how cloud technology works in the background
Hands-on experience on tools like HP ALM, Jira, ServiceNow, Confluence
Awareness of Quality Management tools, techniques, and matrices
Proven experience in handling multiple simultaneous projects and meeting timelines
Pragmatic yet showing a high attention to details



Skills that bring added advantage:


Understanding of financial products and high level risk management principles
Beneficial to have experience working on projects requiring large data validation
Knowledge of how IT application architecture and development works - desirable but not must
Basic understanding of Technology



Soft skills:


Must have - excellent communication, strong coordination skills & interpersonal skills
Should possess strategic thinking, passion for driving solutions
Self-motivated, proactive with ability to work in fast paced environment and under pressure
Should be able to challenge/convince/adoptive in approach, if required
Good team player over different geographies



Proven ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimise workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs.
