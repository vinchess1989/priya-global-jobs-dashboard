Job Summary

We are looking for a skilled DevOps Engineer to design, build, and maintain highly reliable CI/CD pipelines and cloud infrastructure. You will work closely with the engineering & product team to ensure seamless deployments, high availability, and secure, scalable systems across our AWS and on-premises based platforms.

Key Responsibilities


Design, implement, and manage robust CI/CD pipelines for building, testing, deploying, and releasing software products.
Automate infrastructure provisioning and configuration using Infrastructure-as-Code tools.
Manage and optimize cloud infrastructure and on-premises for performance, security, scalability, and cost.
Deploy, monitor, and maintain containerized applications using Docker, Kubernetes, EKS, ECS, and Fargate.
Configure and manage web servers and process managers such as Nginx and Supervisor.
Handle SSL/TLS certificate configuration and renewals.
Monitor system health, troubleshoot production issues, and perform root cause analysis.
Collaborate with development teams to improve deployment strategies, reliability, and observability.
Ensure best practices around security, logging, monitoring, and disaster recovery.
Strong Linux administration: networking, systemd, performance tuning, troubleshooting under pressure.


Mandatory Skills & Experience


5+ years of proven, hands-on experience as a DevOps Engineer, primarily in cloud environments.
Strong experience building and maintaining complex CI/CD platforms.
5+ years of scripting experience using Python, Shell, or Go.
Proficiency with Git or similar source control systems.
Solid understanding of SSL/TLS certificate fundamentals.
Hands-on experience with Nginx and Supervisor for web server configuration and operations.
Excellent troubleshooting and debugging skills.
Strong experience with AWS services, including but not limited to:
EC2, RDS, EKS, ECS, VPC, ELB, S3
CloudFormation, CloudTrail, Route 53, SQS
Good understanding of AWS best practices, with strong hands-on expertise in CloudFormation and Terraform.
Proficiency with the AWS CLI.
Strong knowledge of AWS compute and networking concepts.
Extensive experience with Docker containers, Kubernetes, EKS, and Fargate.
Experience in trading, broking, fintech, or other low-latency or high-availability environments.


Nice to Have


Experience with monitoring and logging tools (CloudWatch, Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, etc.).
Exposure to security best practices and DevSecOps workflows.
Experience supporting high-traffic, production-grade systems.
Secrets management tools (Infisical, Vault) and SSO solutions like Keycloak.
