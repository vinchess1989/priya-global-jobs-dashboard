At Alcon, we're passionate about enhancing sight and helping people see brilliantly. With more than 25,000 associates, we innovate fearlessly, champion progress, and act swiftly to impact global eye health. We foster an inclusive culture, recognizing your contributions and offering opportunities to grow your career like never before. Together, we make a difference in the lives of our patients and customers. Are you ready to join us?

This role is part of Alcon's Quality & Regulatory Affairs function, a team that ensures our products are in compliance with global, local, and internal regulations and meet the strictest standards of quality as we help people see brilliantly. The Manager, Regulatory Operations (Professional Path) is primarily responsible for acting as a key regulatory partner, facilitating operations to align new and existing products with global strategies, and ensuring compliance with regional regulations. You will build strong relationships with regional Regulatory Heads and manage product registrations and licenses.

In this role, a typical day will include:


Manage regulatory data activities across Veeva RIM, EUDAMED, GUDID, and eSTAR, ensuring accurate and timely regulatory information.
Oversee Veeva RIM data stewardship, including registration, license, product, and regulatory hierarchy data.
Ensure regulatory data quality, completeness, consistency, and traceability through validation, reconciliation, and ongoing maintenance.
Manage regulatory data required for device registrations, listings, licenses, renewals, and submissions.
Coordinate EUDAMED and GUDID data preparation, maintenance, and updates, including device and UDI-related regulatory data.
Support accurate and consistent regulatory data used in eSTAR submissions.
Identify and remediate regulatory data gaps, discrepancies, and duplicate or outdated records.
Monitor data quality KPIs and dashboards and drive improvements based on identified trends.
Partner with Regulatory Affairs and system/IT teams on data requirements and system enhancements while maintaining Regulatory Operations ownership of the regulatory business data.


Preferred Qualifications / Skills / Experience:


Experience with Veeva RIM regulatory data management and data stewardship.
Working knowledge of EUDAMED, GUDID, and FDA eSTAR.
Experience with regulatory registration, license, product, UDI, and submission data.
Experience with data validation, reconciliation, remediation, and data-quality monitoring.
Experience developing regulatory data KPIs, dashboards, and reporting, preferably using Power BI or Tableau.
6 to 8 years of experience
