A Day in Your Life at MKS:

As a Quality Engineer at MKS Mass Spectrometry Solutions (MSS) in our company based in Crewe (UK), you will partner with Engineering, Operations, Service, Supply Chain, and Quality teams to improve product quality, support new product introduction activities, drive continuous improvement initiatives, and ensure compliance with customer, regulatory, and business quality requirements. In this role, you will report to the Quality Engineering Manager, Sophia

You Will Make an Impact By:


Develops, evaluates, revises, and applies technical quality assurance protocols/methods to inspect and test in-process raw materials, production equipment, and finished products
Ensures activities and items are in compliance with both company quality assurance standards and applicable government regulations
Performs analysis and identifies trends in the inspection of finished products, in-process materials and bulk raw materials, and recommends corrective actions when necessary
Ensures that established manufacturing inspection, sampling and statistical process control procedures are followed
May assure compliance to in-house and/or external specifications and standards, such as GMPs and ISO regulations
Plans, performs, and supports internal ISO 9001 and process audits, including identification of improvement opportunities, verification of corrective actions, and support of external audits
Evaluates and analyzes the efforts in organizing, documenting, and interpreting inspection support documents and records
Supports quality investigations, root cause analysis, CAPA activities, and continuous improvement initiatives using quality tools and methodologies including 8D, 5 Why, Ishikawa, statistical analysis, and structured problem-solving techniques


Skills You Bring:


Bachelor of Science in Engineering degree or equivalent experience
Experience leading quality investigations, root cause analysis, and CAPA activities using methodologies such as 8D, 5 Why, Ishikawa, statistical analysis, and other structured problem-solving techniques to drive continuous improvement
Experience conducting internal, supplier, or process audits and supporting compliance with quality management systems
Experience supporting First Article Inspection (FAI), product qualification, and manufacturing readiness activities to ensure successful introduction of new products into production
Working knowledge of ISO 9001 requirements, auditing principles, FMEA, control plans, and quality system requirements


Preferred Skills:


Internal Auditor or Lead Auditor training in ISO 9001 or equivalent quality management systems. Perhaps
Experience supporting Measurement System Analysis (MSA), Gauge R&R studies, statistical analysis, or process capability improvement activities
Experience within semiconductor, instrumentation, electronics, vacuum technology, or high-technology manufacturing environments
Experience supporting supplier quality, incoming inspection, or manufacturing quality activities
Knowledge of Lean Manufacturing and continuous improvement methodologies
