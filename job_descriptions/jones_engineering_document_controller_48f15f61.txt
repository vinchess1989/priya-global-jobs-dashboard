Position: Document Controller

Location: Bagenalstown Office, Co. Carlow




Company Description

We are a leading engineering services company with a high-quality track record, focused on delivering projects cantered on customer demands for over 130 years. Our main divisions include Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Fire Protection (Sprinklers). We are currently expanding our Fire Protection team based in our Valencia office to provide centralized design support for highly complex international projects.

We offer competitive salaries, financial support for continuing education and Continuing Professional Development (CPD), and the ability to advance your career with a world-class engineering company.




About Jones Engineering Manufacturing and This Opportunity

At Jones Engineering Manufacturing Ltd., a division of Jones Engineering, we are pioneers in industrial Off-Site Manufacturing (OSM). Our facility is powered by highly experienced mechanical and electrical engineers, alongside seasoned construction personnel who have successfully transitioned into the OSM space. As we continue to scale, we recognise that true OSM requires more than building off-site; it requires a fundamental shift from a "construction project" mindset to a "manufacturing production" mindset. Jones Engineering has a requirement for a Document Controller within the design department, to join our Design team as part of Jones Engineering Manufacturing Ltd in Carlow (Centre of Off-Site Construction Excellence).




Job Description

The Document Controller will act as point of contact for all design projects in Jones Engineering Manufacturing, ensuring all data exchanges comply with ISO standards. The role will ensure critical path of technical submittals and RFIs, ensuring that information bottlenecks never delay project delivery.




Role and Responsibilities:

1. Information Governance & CDE Management

Document control on several design projects simultaneously (small to large scale)
Integrating data into electronic systems
Managing the transmittal process/collaboration process
Generating periodic reports on project
Project filing/document handover process
Project filing/archiving process
Providing general department administration support
Awareness & understanding of applicable company procedures




2. Information Delivery & Stakeholder Coordination

Actively "chase" and coordinate with discipline leads to extract forecasted submittal dates, ensuring technical reviews are completed on time, and the design process stays on track.




3. Workflow Monitoring & RFI Management

Status Reporting: Contribute to the information status section of weekly project meetings. You will provide clear metrics on submittal status, identify "stale" RFIs to superiors etc.




Skills and Requirements:

A formal qualification in Information Management, Quality Documentation, or GMP/Data Integrity is essential.
Minimum 2+ years in a high-compliance engineering environment (Pharma, MedTech, or Large-scale Infrastructure).
Practical knowledge Information Management cycles, including the transition of information from Work-in-Progress (WIP) to Shared and Published states.
Desired:Knowledge of BIM360 / ACC and advanced Excel/Google Sheets for data tracking and reporting.
Highly organized with an extreme attention to detail and the assertiveness to manage multiple tasks to strict deadlines.
