DevOps Engineer
MSP Staffing Pty Ltd
I'm interested

Our client is seeking talented DevOps professionals who go beyond the conventional and bring a strong problem-solving mindset to modern technology environments. If you thrive on automation, cloud technologies, continuous improvement and building reliable platforms, this could be an opportunity to make your mark.

 

Key Requirements

Bachelors degree – relevant to skills
4+ experience in a DevOps, Cloud, Platform Engineering or similar role.
Strong understanding of CI/CD principles and automation.
Experience with cloud platforms and infrastructure technologies.
Familiarity with scripting, version control and infrastructure-as-code concepts.
Strong troubleshooting and analytical abilities.
A passion for technology and a willingness to explore unconventional solutions.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills.

 

Should you meet the requirements for this position, please email your CV to it.jobs@mspstaffing.co.za . You can also contact the IT team on 031 350 3607 or visit our website at https://mspstaffingza.co/  NOTE:  When replying to the advert, also include the reference number in the subject line. Correspondence will only be conducted with short listed candidates. Should you not hear from us within 3 days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
