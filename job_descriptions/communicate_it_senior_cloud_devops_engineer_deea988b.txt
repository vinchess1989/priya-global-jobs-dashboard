Do you get more excited about building deployment pipelines than deploying code manually? Does scaling infrastructure, automating everything possible, and keeping production environments running smoothly sound like a good day at the office?
 
My client is looking for a Senior Cloud DevOps Engineer to join a growing engineering team building a large-scale SaaS platform. This opportunity is ideal for someone who enjoys owning cloud infrastructure, improving system reliability, driving automation, and working closely with software developers to deliver high-quality solutions.
 
For professionals searching for IT jobs, jobs in information technology, developer jobs, jobs in IT, or senior cloud engineering opportunities, this role offers significant technical ownership and the chance to influence infrastructure strategy from the ground up.
Duties & Responsibilities

Job Description:
 
As a Senior Cloud DevOps Engineer, you will be responsible for designing, building, automating, securing, and optimizing cloud infrastructure that supports business-critical applications.
You will work closely with engineering and data teams to deliver scalable, reliable, and secure environments while helping improve developer productivity and deployment efficiency.
 
Responsibilities include:

Designing and managing scalable cloud infrastructure
Building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines
Managing containerized environments using Docker and Kubernetes
Deploying and supporting cloud-based applications
Implementing monitoring, logging, alerting, and observability solutions
Automating operational processes and infrastructure provisioning
Driving cloud security best practices and system hardening initiatives
Supporting high-availability production environments
Managing backup and disaster recovery strategies
Assisting with database infrastructure and optimization
Troubleshooting production issues alongside development teams
Identifying opportunities for cloud cost optimization
Maintaining documentation of infrastructure, processes, and environments

Strong communication and collaboration skills
