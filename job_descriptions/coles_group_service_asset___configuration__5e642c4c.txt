About The Team

Our Technology team is the backbone of the business, building and maintaining the information technology infrastructure that support Coles Group. As a part of the Technology team, you get the opportunity to work through complex, challenging, meaningful problems using large data sets and new technologies to expand your skills and experience.

Coles Technology is focused on delivering reliable, secure and effective technology services that support our teams and customers. Within Service Management Enablement, we are building a new Service Asset and Configuration Management capability to strengthen visibility and governance across our technology environment.

You will work closely with Technology Asset owners, Service Management, ITSM tooling, Infrastructure and Cloud, Architecture, Finance and Procurement, Application Engineering and Product teams, as well as managed service providers and vendors.

About The Role

As the Service Asset & Configuration Manager, you will establish, govern and continuously improve the Service Asset and Configuration Management (SACM) practice across Coles Technology.

This is an opportunity to shape a new enterprise capability, defining the strategy, operating model and controls required to provide trusted visibility of technology assets and configuration items, and their relationships to business and technology services.

You will lead the design and implementation of the SACM capability, including the CMDB architecture and data modelling, service modelling, asset lifecycle governance, data quality and ownership standards across a complex, multi-vendor technology environment.

In this role, you will define and implement the SACM strategy, roadmap and operating model aligned to ITIL practices, while establishing the governance frameworks, processes and standards required for effective asset and configuration management.

You will design and govern the end-to-end CMDB architecture and data model, including CI classes, relationships, ownership and service modelling standards aligned to ServiceNow Common Service Data Model (CSDM) principles.

You will closely work with the ITSM Tooling team to implement integration across ServiceNow, discovery and observability tools, cloud platforms, Finance and Procurement systems and establish lifecycle management for hardware, software, cloud and digital assets, and support licensing, contractual and financial compliance.

Partnering with Procurement, Finance, technology teams and vendors, you will improve asset data, utilisation, cost and risk outcomes, govern outsourced partner delivery against SLAs, KPIs and contractual obligations, and drive consistent processes and data standards.

You will also define and track CMDB and asset management KPIs, use automation and analytics to drive continuous improvement, and provide executive reporting and insights to key stakeholders.

About You And Your Skills

You bring deep experience in IT Service Asset and Configuration Management and have a track record of building or maturing CMDB and Asset Management capabilities. You are comfortable operating across technical, commercial and supplier environments, and can influence senior stakeholders to establish strong governance and sustainable ways of working.

You will ideally bring


10+ years of experience in ITIL Service Asset and Configuration Management practices, with retail domain experience preferred.
Demonstrated ability to establish data ownership and governance across federated technology teams, managed service providers and other third parties.
Proven experience establishing, transforming or maturing enterprise SACM and CMDB capabilities.
Experience working in outsourced or multi-vendor environments.
Excellent stakeholder management and influencing skills, with a strong analytical and governance mindset.
ITIL 4, IT Asset Management or relevant ServiceNow certification would be highly regarded.
Relevant ServiceNow and/or Asset Management certifications.
Strong knowledge of ServiceNow CMDB, CSDM, Discovery and service modelling, with an understanding of Hardware Asset Management (HAM) and Software Asset Management (SAM) capabilities.
A degree in Information Technology, Business or a related discipline.


If you’re passionate about building robust service asset and configuration management capabilities and want to make a meaningful impact across a large, complex technology environment, we’d love to hear from you.
