About The Role

We are seeking a Mid DevOps Engineer to join our growing team and drive the design, implementation, and maintenance of our infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines. You will play a key role in automating processes, enhancing system reliability, and ensuring smooth software delivery in a fast-paced environment.

Key Responsibilities


Design, implement, and manage CI/CD pipelines using tools like Jenkins, GitLab CI, CircleCI, etc.
Automate infrastructure provisioning and configuration using Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) tools such as Terraform, Ansible, or CloudFormation.
Monitor and support cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, or GCP) ensuring high availability and performance.
Collaborate with development, QA, and operations teams to streamline the software release process.
Manage container orchestration tools like Docker and Kubernetes.
Enhance system observability with monitoring/logging tools like Prometheus, Grafana, ELK/EFK Stack, or Datadog.
Implement security best practices across CI/CD and infrastructure layers.
Lead incident management and root cause analysis of production issues.


Requirements

Required Skills and Qualifications:


Bachelor’s/Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
4+ years of hands-on experience in DevOps, system administration, or infrastructure engineering.
Strong knowledge of at least one major cloud platform (AWS, Azure, or GCP).
Expertise in CI/CD tools, infrastructure automation, and scripting languages (Bash, Python, etc.).
Deep understanding of Docker, Kubernetes, and containerized application deployment.
Familiarity with configuration management tools like Ansible, Puppet, or Chef.
Experience with version control systems (e.g., Git) and agile development practices.
Solid understanding of networking, system security, and performance tuning.


Preferred Qualifications


Certifications such as AWS Certified DevOps Engineer, Azure DevOps Expert, or Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA).
Experience with service mesh, secrets management, or zero-downtime deployments.
