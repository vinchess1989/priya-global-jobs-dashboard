Position Title: Manager, Quality Assurance

Employment Type: Full-Time Staff | Non-Union

Annual Salary Range: $80,000.00 - $92,000.00

Division: Product and Technology

Location: 2180 Yonge Street, Toronto, ON

TVO Media Education Group (TVO) is devoted to inspiring learning that changes lives and enriches communities. We are an internationally recognized digital learning organization that strives to engage, inform, and educate Ontarians of all ages.

Who We Are

We are an agency of the Ontario Ministry of Education and a registered charity that contributes to learning, civic engagement, and public service media across Ontario. We provide free, trusted, and accessible educational content that can support Ontarians of all ages, regardless of background, location, or circumstance. To fulfill our mandate, TVO delivers curriculum-connected resources through TVO Learn and TVOkids, offers Ontario’s largest online high school through TVO ILC, and produces journalism, documentaries, current affairs programming, and podcasts with TVO Today that help Ontarians understand the issues shaping their communities.

Explore how donor support helps make more possible for people across Ontario with our 2025-26 Impact Report.

What We’re Looking For

Bring your passion for quality and people leadership to TVO. As Manager, Quality Assurance, you’ll shape testing strategy, advance automation, and use quality insights to guide release readiness. You’ll nurture your team’s growth and partner with product and development colleagues to manage risks and continuously improve software quality.

Strategy & Planning

How you will make an impact


Lead the development and implementation of QA strategies, testing standards, and methodologies across software development, application integration, and product release lifecycles.
Define quality metrics and reporting processes, using data analysis, data verification, to assess product quality and identify opportunities for improvement.
Optimize QA processes and change control practices that support project delivery and align testing priorities with organizational goals and objectives.
Ensure QA standards and testing practices reflect applicable regulatory requirements, industry standards and established best practices.
Guide the analysis of test results to identify defects, configuration issues, and interoperability risks, and prioritize their resolution with product and development teams.
Ability to apply and guide the team on AI usage in QA lifecycle
Review requirements and technical specifications to clarify intended functionality, identify quality risks, and define appropriate test coverage.


Acquisition & Deployment


Evaluate, guide, selection and use of automated testing frameworks, supporting coding languages, and data collection tools to meet testing needs.
Review test plans and scripts for application integrations and product releases, ensuring comprehensive coverage, clear defect tracking, and verification of fixes.
Knowledge of AI agents, AI-assisted test & code review, good understanding of cloud infrastructure and AWS
Use testing metrics and verified quality data to assess release readiness, identify outstanding risks, and communicate recommendations to product and development managers.
Translate test progress, results, and quality trends into clear reports and presentations for technical teams, project stakeholders, and executive management.


People Management


Manage QA team priorities, workloads, and delivery commitments in collaboration with product and development managers, balancing competing demands within the software development framework.
Train, coach, and mentor QA team members in strengthening their testing, automation, analytical, and problem-solving capabilities.
Foster a collaborative, detail-oriented QA culture through active listening, knowledge sharing, and clear communication of testing best practices to technical and non-technical colleagues.


What You Will Bring To TVO


College diploma or university degree in software engineering, computer science, or business administration and/or 3 - 5 years equivalent work experience.
3 years direct experience in overseeing the design, development, and implementation of quality assurance standards for software testing.
3- 5 years direct experience as a QA Manager/ QA Lead for major application integration and/or major application product release and direct experience managing all aspects of a QA team.
Experience establishing and maintaining test infrastructure including CI/CD Integration and cloud infrastructure
Knowledge of non-functional testing/scalability and tools like K6 Grafana, security and vulnerability scanning tools, OWASP and WCAG Standards
Experience in reporting test metrics, best practices, and methodologies.
Experience building and maintaining scalable automation frameworks using Javascript/typescript, cypress, playwright, etc. , test data management and automated reporting
Proven data analysis, data verification, and problem-solving abilities.
Good project management skills and/or substantial exposure to project-based work structures.
Excellent understanding of the organization’s goals and objectives.
Ability to prioritize based on business risk and impact and execute in a high-pressure environment.
Ability to communicate ideas in both technical and user-friendly language - both oral as well as written
Excellent listening, coaching, presentation, problem-solving and negotiating skills.
Excellent team player with an attitude to break silos and knowing when to step in


What We Offer

Working at TVO, you’ll have the opportunity to do meaningful work that impacts millions of Ontarians. You’ll belong to a diverse, tight-knit community of talented colleagues who share a passion for education. With learning at the centre of everything we do, we invest in developing our people and promoting a culture of continuous learning. You might even get to meet Polkaroo!

We hope you’ll consider joining us.
