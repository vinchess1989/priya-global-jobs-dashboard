About The Opportunity

An exciting opportunity exists for an experienced Release Train Engineer (RTE) to support the delivery of strategic merchant payment and business solutions within a large-scale, enterprise digital payments environment.

The successful candidate will operate within a SAFe Agile Release Train responsible for delivering merchant services, digital payment capabilities, mobile applications, value-added financial services, and enterprise solutions.

This role requires a strong blend of Agile delivery leadership and technical expertise, as the successful consultant will not only facilitate ART delivery processes but will also contribute directly to technical and delivery-related initiatives.

The ideal candidate will possess strong stakeholder management capabilities and be able to coordinate delivery activities across Product, Engineering, Architecture, Operations, Business and Vendor teams.

Role Purpose

The Release Train Engineer is responsible for translating product priorities into integrated delivery plans, ensuring alignment between strategy and execution, and driving successful delivery through the Agile Release Train.

The role oversees execution from discovery and planning through development, integration, testing, deployment, launch and post-launch stabilisation.

Working within a SAFe environment, the RTE will facilitate ART ceremonies, manage delivery risks and dependencies, improve delivery flow, coach teams and leaders, and help drive continuous improvement across the organisation.

Success in this role is measured by the ability to deliver quality merchant capabilities at pace while ensuring technical readiness, operational readiness and business value realisation.
