Job Description

All of our engineering is AI-assisted by default — and we're looking for a DevOps Engineer to build the guardrails that decide what AI-generated code is allowed to do in production.

The role at a glance


Title: DevOps Engineer
Location: Southampton, UK — hybrid, minimum three days per week in the office, up to two days remote
Compensation: £57,000 – £62,000 per year
Contract: Full-time, permanent — 40 hours per week
Team: Product Engineering — DevOps. Five engineers including this role, reporting to the Senior DevOps Architect


This is a hands-on DevOps role on our Product Engineering team, and it's built around two things.

First - Alliants engineering is AI-assisted by default, and the function is shifting from doing the work directly to building the controls that let the wider organisation ship safely. You'll build and maintain the automated validation, testing, standards enforcement and risk detection that stand between generated code and production. Your work decides what everyone else can safely build.

Second - Ownership. You'll deploy, maintain and optimise the AWS infrastructure behind our proprietary products and client solutions and hold it to 99.9%+ availability, security compliance, and no single points of failure from network through to database.

Role Focus & Operating Context

All code in this role is produced with AI coding agents. A thorough understanding of infrastructure-as-code and automation is essential. The skill we hire for is directing, constraining, reviewing and verifying output to a high standard.

Standards, architectural decisions and runbooks are treated as machine-readable inputs to agents, not just documents for humans. They have to stay accurate and enforceable as the volume of change keeps rising: every control must scale without a human in the loop for the routine case, while reliably stopping the dangerous one.

You'll work across proprietary products and client hosting environments, under real SLA and support commitments, alongside four other DevOps engineers and the software engineering teams who consume what you build.

What You'll Work On And Own


The guardrails — policy-as-code, protected environments, scoped IAM, blast-radius limits and promotion paths that make the dangerous change impossible rather than discouraged
The automated quality layer — static analysis, schema and migration safety checks, dependency and licence scanning, automated code review and integration testing
The infrastructure — AWS environments defined in OpenTofu/Terraform, for Alliants products and for clients
The pipelines — fast, parallel, self-verifying CI/CD with automatic failure detection and rollback to last known-good
The environments assigned to you — availability, security posture, cost, and what happens at 3am when something breaks
The templates and standards that make the safe route the easiest route for everyone else


Job Requirements

What you'll bring


Hands-on experience managing and configuring AWS environments — ECS (Fargate), RDS, VPC, S3, API Gateway, Lambda, CloudFront, IAM
Hands-on Infrastructure-as-Code experience with Terraform or OpenTofu, including established module patterns, state management, and safe handling of destructive changes
Containerisation experience with Docker, including image optimisation and secure runtime configuration
Demonstrated experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation, including automated testing and rollback
Strong command-line, scripting and programming ability — Linux, Bash, Git, plus Python, TypeScript or JavaScript — for diagnostics and automation
Solid grounding in information security best practice, secrets management, system documentation, and operating resilient systems with no single points of failure
Commercial experience supporting multiple environments under SLA and support commitments
Practical, current experience using AI coding agents (Claude Code or equivalent) as the primary means of producing code, and the judgement to verify their output rather than trust it


To thrive in this role, you'll be someone who:


Asks "how do I make this impossible to get wrong" rather than "how do I fix this instance"
Takes proactive initiative on environment health and automation without waiting for direction
Gets satisfaction from other people shipping safely at speed, and builds controls that unblock rather than obstruct
Uses AI aggressively while assuming its output is wrong until verified, and encodes that assumption into the checks
Explains technical issues clearly to non-technical colleagues and, where needed, client contacts
Stays analytical during high-severity production incidents


Job Responsibilities


Implement and maintain the guardrails — designed with the Senior DevOps Architect — that let engineers and non-engineers safely ship AI-generated application and infrastructure code: policy-as-code, protected environments, scoped IAM and blast-radius limits, mandatory automated gates, plan review, drift detection, and clear sandbox-to-production promotion paths.
Build out and maintain the automated quality control layer to agreed standards, covering static analysis, schema and migration safety checks, dependency and licence scanning, automated code review and integration testing, so unsafe or substandard changes are caught by the system rather than by a person.
Deploy, maintain and optimise cloud infrastructure, primarily AWS, for Alliants products and clients — delivering 99.9%+ availability, security compliance, and no single points of failure from network through to database. All infrastructure is defined as code in OpenTofu/Terraform.
Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines that are fast, parallel and self-verifying, with automatic detection of failed deployments and rollback to the last known-good version, reducing release cycle time while driving deployment-related incidents towards zero.
Implement instrumentation, alerting, dashboards and performance reporting to identify bottlenecks and regressions before they become incidents.
Apply InfoSec and regulatory standards, including WAF coverage, centrally managed secrets and least-privilege access.
Manage, troubleshoot and resolve service desk escalations and production incidents, with root-cause analysis and automated prevention of recurrence rather than repeat manual fixes.
Contribute to cloud cost optimisation, and provide the usage data behind accurate reporting.
Support internal and client technical teams with hosting and environment queries, communicating performance and incident reports clearly.
Maintain the templates and standards that make the safe route the easiest route for everyone else.
Take part in the out-of-hours on-call rota as first responder, currently one week in six.
