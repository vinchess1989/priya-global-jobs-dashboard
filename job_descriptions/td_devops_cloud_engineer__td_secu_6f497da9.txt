Job Description:

Department Overview

TD Securities offers a wide range of capital markets products and services to corporate, government, and institutional clients who choose us for our innovation, execution, and experience.

With more than 6,500 professionals operating out of 40 cities across the globe, we help clients meet their needs today and prepare for tomorrow. Our services include underwriting and distributing new issues, providing trusted advice and industry-leading insight, extending access to global markets, and delivering integrated transaction banking solutions.

We are growth-oriented, people-focused, and community-minded. As a team, we work to deliver value to our clients every day.

Job Description

We are seeking an experienced, hands-on Cloud Engineer to help implement TD Securities’ next-generation Risk Modernization platform as part of our innovative, transformational initiatives. This role offers the opportunity to use cutting-edge technologies to address scale and speed challenges. You will design and implement the platform’s infrastructure on TD’s public cloud, using Azure services for scalable distributed computing and big data.

Responsibilities


Partner with the development team to design and implement platform infrastructure.
Oversee infrastructure resources, including hardware, software, and storage.
Set up the CI/CD pipeline.
Explore emerging capabilities and technologies to foster innovation.
Collaborate closely with development and production support teams to support the platform.


Desired Skillsets:


Cloud DevOps engineering skills; TD Cloud onboarding & EDP experience is an asset
Proficiency in Unix shell scripting and Python for automation
Skills in networking and Linux administration
Experience with monitoring and logging tools, such as Dynatrace and Datadog
Experience with Azure SQL Database and Databricks
Strong communication and practical problem-solving skills
Strong ownership, accountability, and teamwork
Experience with agentic coding tools, such as GitHub Copilot


Technical Skills


5+ years of cloud DevOps engineering experience, preferably with Azure.
Experience designing, implementing, and maintaining large-scale platforms.
Strong experience administering Linux systems.
Strong experience using Kubernetes in production environments.
Experience managing web and service/API platforms.
Experience using messaging technologies such as Kafka.
RDBMS experience and demonstrated SQL proficiency; Azure SQL Database preferred.
5+ years of experience managing Git, CI/CD tools (Maven), and Jenkins.
Experience implementing and managing system monitoring tools (Dynatrace, Datadog).
Experience designing and maintaining highly scalable, low-latency systems.


Business Skills


Effective communication skills and experience collaborating efficiently across a global team.
Proven experience working in a fast-paced environment with engineers, BSA/QA, PMs, and business sponsors.
Risk management knowledge is an asset.
