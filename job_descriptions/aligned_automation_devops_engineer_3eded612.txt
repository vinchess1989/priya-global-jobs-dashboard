DevOps Engineer


Location: Limerick


Experience: 3–5 years





Role Overview


We are looking for a motivated and hands-on DevOps Engineer to build, automate, and support the CI/CD pipelines, environments, and deployment tooling underpinning our Manufacturing Execution System (MES) programme. Working as part of the DevOps team alongside the DevOps Lead, you will help enable fast, reliable, and compliant delivery of MES capabilities — bridging development, operations, and quality in a regulated manufacturing environment. Candidates with hands-on experience deploying, configuring, or maintaining off-the-shelf MES platforms such as Syncade are strongly preferred.


Key Skills


Azure DevOps Jenkins GitHub Actions Terraform Ansible PowerShell Python Bash Syncade MES GxP CSV GAMP 5 Docker Kubernetes CI/CD Infrastructure-as-Code


Key Responsibilities


• Build, maintain, and troubleshoot CI/CD pipelines that support reliable and repeatable MES deployments across development, test, and production environments.


• Maintain the DevOps toolchain — source control, build automation, release management, environment provisioning, monitoring, and observability.


• Apply branching strategies, code review processes, and release management standards appropriate to a regulated manufacturing environment.


• Work with MES vendors, system integrators, and internal teams to understand platform architecture and translate it into maintainable infrastructure-as-code and deployment automation.


• Deploy, configure, and support environments for MES platforms including Syncade, ensuring they are correctly configured, version-controlled, and fit for purpose across all lifecycle stages.


• Implement and maintain monitoring, alerting, and logging solutions that provide operational visibility of MES platform health and performance.


• Embed automated testing into pipelines in collaboration with the testing team, and maintain robust quality gates in line with shift-left practices.


• Support computer system validation (CSV) activities by maintaining audit trails, deployment records, and change control documentation in compliance with GxP requirements.


• Provision and maintain infrastructure supporting MES workloads, applying security, resilience, and scalability best practices.


• Identify and resolve bottlenecks in the delivery pipeline, helping to improve deployment frequency, lead time, and recovery metrics (DORA).


• Provide technical support and troubleshooting for infrastructure and deployment issues, escalating to the DevOps Lead where required.


• Contribute to disaster recovery and business continuity planning and testing for MES platforms.


• Create and maintain runbooks, technical documentation, and standard operating procedures for pipelines and environments.


Essential Requirements


• 3–5 years of experience in a DevOps Engineer, Release Engineer, or similar role on enterprise software programmes.


• Hands-on experience with CI/CD tooling — Azure DevOps, Jenkins, GitHub Actions, or equivalent.


• Working knowledge of infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Ansible, or similar) and configuration management practices.


• Experience supporting environments for enterprise applications, including environment promotion and configuration management.


• Proficiency in scripting languages (PowerShell, Bash, Python, or similar) for automation and tooling.


• Good understanding of the software delivery lifecycle, release management, and change control in enterprise settings.


• Experience of, or exposure to, regulated industries (pharmaceutical, life sciences, food & beverage, or similar) with awareness of compliance and validation requirements.


• Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to diagnose and resolve infrastructure and deployment issues under pressure.


Desirable Requirements


• Direct experience deploying, maintaining, or supporting off-the-shelf MES platforms — ideally Syncade (Emerson) — including patch management, configuration, and upgrade activities.


• Familiarity with GxP computer system validation (CSV), GAMP 5 lifecycle, and associated documentation requirements (IQ, OQ, PQ).


• Knowledge of containerisation technologies (Docker, Kubernetes) and their applicability within regulated manufacturing environments.


• Understanding of MES integration patterns — ERP (SAP), LIMS, historian, and shop-floor device connectivity.


• Experience with security hardening, vulnerability management, and operational security in manufacturing environments.


• Familiarity with OT/IT convergence and the specific constraints of deploying technology close to manufacturing operations.


• Windows Server and SQL Server administration experience.


Personal Attributes


• Technically hands-on and solutions-oriented, with a drive to build things that are simple, reliable, and maintainable.


• Clear communicator — able to explain technical topics to non-technical stakeholders including QA and operations.


• Collaborative team player who works effectively with vendors, system integrators, and cross-functional internal teams.


• Detail-oriented, with a disciplined approach to documentation and change control in a regulated environment.


• Comfortable operating with a degree of ambiguity in a programme environment, and keen to learn as understanding develops.


• Committed to continuous improvement — staying current with DevOps practices and identifying opportunities to improve delivery performance.
