Creospan is a growing tech collective of makers, shakers, and problem solvers, offering solutions today that will propel businesses into a better tomorrow. “Tomorrow’s ideas, built today!” In addition to being able to work alongside equally brilliant and motivated developers, our consultants appreciate the opportunity to learn and apply new skills and methodologies to different clients and industries.




ROLE SUMMARY

The Release Manager will own end-to-end release planning and coordination for NT application teams, ensuring smooth, controlled, and well-governed deployments across environments within a large enterprise financial services setting.




KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Own and maintain the release calendar, coordinating release schedules across multiple application and development teams.
Plan, sequence, and manage releases across Development, QA, UAT, and Production environments.
Drive release readiness reviews, facilitate go/no-go decisions, and coordinate post-release validation.
Work closely with the Environment Manager, DevOps, QA, infrastructure, and application teams to manage dependencies, scheduling conflicts, and release impacts.
Identify and manage release risks; ensure rollback plans are prepared and aligned with change management processes, CAB, and ITSM requirements.
Track and communicate release status, issues, risks, and performance metrics to stakeholders and senior management.
Ensure adherence to client release governance, audit requirements, and change-control standards.
Coordinate with CI/CD teams on deployment pipelines and support release automation initiatives.




REQUIRED SKILLS & EXPERIENCE

12–15 years of overall IT experience, with significant experience in Release Management.
Demonstrated experience managing releases in large, complex enterprise environments; financial services experience is strongly preferred.
Strong understanding of SDLC, Agile/Scrum methodologies, and DevOps practices.
Hands-on familiarity with release and change management tools such as ServiceNow, JIRA, and Azure DevOps.
Experience coordinating cross-functional teams across development, QA, infrastructure, and environment management.
Strong stakeholder management, communication, and reporting skills, including the ability to present release status to senior management.
Exposure to CI/CD pipelines and release automation is an advantage.




PREFERRED DOMAIN EXPERIENCE

Exposure to banking, capital markets, or asset servicing environments is preferred.
