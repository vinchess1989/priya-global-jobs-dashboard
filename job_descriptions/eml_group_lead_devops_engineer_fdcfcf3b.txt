Make a real difference in people's lives with EML

As we continue to expand, our Technology team is growing rapidly, driving innovation in AI for Workers Compensation. We're transforming the industry through technology that enhances claims management, improves outcomes and streamlines processes.

We're seeking an experienced DevOps Lead to join our supportive, purpose-driven team in Sydney, in a full-time, permanent position.

You'll play a key role in strengthening our DevOps capability, driving automation and ensuring our infrastructure is secure, scalable and reliable across on-premises environments.

What You'll Be Doing

Lead and develop the DevOps team, providing technical direction, coaching, setting engineering standards and building a high-performing team.
Drive CI/CD and automation, designing and improving pipelines to deliver faster, more reliable and secure software releases across cloud and on-premises environments.
Manage hybrid infrastructure, designing and optimising solutions across AWS, , VMware and on-premises systems using Terraform and Ansible.
Oversee containerisation and orchestration, managing self-hosted Kubernetes environments to ensure scalability, availability and resilience.
Strengthen reliability and security, improving monitoring, observability, incident management, disaster recovery and compliance with APRA CPS 234 and ISO 27001.
Collaborate and innovate, partnering with AI development, infrastructure and security teams to improve developer experience and support emerging AI capabilities.


What We're Looking For

At least 7 years' IT experience, including 5 years in DevOps, SRE or cloud infrastructure, and 3 years leading or mentoring technical teams.
Strong hands-on experience across AWS, VMware and on-premises infrastructure, including managing and supporting hybrid environments. On-premises experience is essential.
Advanced knowledge of Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform and Ansible, alongside Linux administration and scripting.
Proven experience with Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD pipelines, GitHub Actions (or similar), GitOps and modern software delivery practices.
A strong understanding of infrastructure security, monitoring, disaster recovery and operational resilience, ideally within regulated environments.
Excellent leadership, communication and problem-solving skills, with basic AI/LLM knowledge and the ability to engage technical and senior stakeholders..
