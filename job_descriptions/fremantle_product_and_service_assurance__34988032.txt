Job Description

Department/Area of Business: Global Media Supply Chain & Operations

Corporate/Group/Region/Segment/Business: Commercial & International

Reports to: Global Head of Media Supply Chain & Operations

Overall Purpose of the Role

The Product and Service Assurance Manager is responsible for the product and operational health of Fremantle's Media Supply Chain. Focusing on the MAM and related integration layers and the vendors who build, configure and support these. This role owns the operational management of live services, including incident, change and release management.

The role sits between Media Operations and Media Technology, translating operational challenges into clear, prioritised, actionable work. This role is expected to be hands-on, getting into support queues, data and high level technical troubleshooting directly. The role holder will run work on a milestone and priority-led basis, track all work through appropriate tools such as Jira across internal and external teams, and take an ‘automate, don't own’ approach to repetitive tasks, using AI where appropriate.

Main Objectives of the Role


Own the delivery performance of vendors across the Media Supply Chain, tracking deliverables against contracted SLAs.
Escalate and resolve vendor underperformance, feeding outcomes into renewal and commercial decisions.
Coordinates incidents, releases, testing and acceptance, backlog prioritisation and lifecycle, dependency and decommissioning decisions.
Act as the central point of coordination and escalation for significant service incidents, ensuring issues are triaged, progressed and communicated through to resolution.
Coordinate release planning, testing and go-live activity across multiple vendors and internal teams, ensuring changes are sequenced and communicated without disrupting BAU Operations.
Act as the primary interface between Media Operations and Technology (internal and external), collating and converting user requests and workarounds into clear, actionable, prioritised work.
Own and maintain the operational Jira estate, internal and vendor-facing, ensuring all work is tracked, visible and accountable.
Run a milestone- and priority-led approach to managing the flow of work.
Get hands-on with troubleshooting, data investigation when required, rather than only coordinating others to do so.
Identify repetitive or manual work and build or commission automation/AI-driven solutions to remove it, rather than absorbing it as ongoing manual effort.
Contribute to regular vendor governance and review forums.
Identify solutions, drawing on broader technical and media industry knowledge and that of SMEs within teams.


Qualifications

Knowledge and Skills


5+ years' experience in senior media technology or content supply chain roles.
Highly experienced in managing third-party vendor deliverables and SLAs, including commercial and delivery performance tracking.
Proven experience tracking and managing projects/tasks, across both internal teams and external vendors.
Comfortable running a milestone- and priority-driven delivery approach
Demonstrated ability to act as the interface between Operations and Technology teams (internal and external), converting user requirements, service issues and operational pain points into clear, actionable, prioritised work.
High degree of technical literacy.
Practical and hands-on, with the ability to investigate support tickets, data issues and media-technical problems directly.
AI-literate, with a ‘build to automate’ mentality — actively looks to remove repetitive or manual work through automation rather than absorbing it personally.
Experience with SaaS platforms and integrations between systems, ideally within a media/content supply chain (MAM, CRM or similar).
Experience with service/run operations: incident management, release management and QA practices.
Strong stakeholder management skills, confident working with vendors, internal technical teams and business/operations stakeholders alike.
Solution-oriented, with high attention to detail, accuracy and a bias to action under tight deadlines.
Commercial acumen — comfortable reading and negotiating around vendor contracts and SLAs.
Experience with a programme management framework applied pragmatically rather than dogmatically.


Additional Information

Responsibility for Resources


SaaS technology estate and vendor relationships
Ticketing estate — internal and vendor-facing.
Service, release and QA governance.
