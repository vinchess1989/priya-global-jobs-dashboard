Job Responsibilities


Creates new manual and automated test cases or scripts, likely with guidance from more experienced Quality Engineers
Produces or collects all required metrics, reports, and audits for relevant work products according to process
Maintains test cases or scripts and seeks to continuously improve product coverage
Executes all assigned automated testing according to the schedule and productivity targets
Escalates concerns about project/product/process quality in clear, timely, collaborative, constructive way
Assists project manager in identifying data or metrics to maintain and to analyze and report against
Leads root cause analysis identification and resolution
Assists with change management and communicate the plans and results effectively to drive change
Evaluates processes and products against applicable company and industry standards, and procedures
Performs peer review, code review and sign-off of others work
AI Skill – Demonstrates willingness and openness to learn and work with AI tools; actively participates in AI learning opportunities and experiments with new ways of applying AI to day-to-day work.
AI Adoption – Applies AI tools with growing skill and confidence in practical, work-relevant ways; uses sound judgement about when and how to rely on AI outputs and shares learnings with others.


Work Arrangements


Standard work week or as defined by assignment requirements
Primarily works in standard office environment or remotely
May require after-hours, on-call support and/or holidays
On-call and after hours work during peak times including end of month/quarter/year; during this time PTO is limited to meet business needs


Work Experience

4 - 7 years relevant work experience; 4-5 years at the Senior level or equivalent experience
