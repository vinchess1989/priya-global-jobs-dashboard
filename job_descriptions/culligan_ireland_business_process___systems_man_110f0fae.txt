About Us

At Culligan, we believe that cleaner, healthier water can help contribute to improving the lives of people around the world and is a key driver towards solving some of the biggest challenges that lie in our future.

Our goal is to do everything we can in the areas where we are best equipped to truly effect change. To that end, we've developed a core set of commitments that align with the UN Sustainable Development Goals and serve as the focus of our work to build a better world.

We're hiring for a Business Process & Systems Manager to be responsible for the optimization and ongoing support of CRM, finance and other key business systems across Culligan Water Ireland (B2B) and Culligan Home Ireland (B2C). The role combines business process analysis, systems support and the coordination of controlled changes to workflows and connected systems. The role will ensure that each system is effectively embedded, aligned to business needs, and fully leveraged to drive process efficiency, data accuracy, and commercial insight.

Key areas of responsibilities:

Team Leadership


Lead and coordinate the day-to-day activities of the Business Systems team
Translate business priorities into clear work plans and delivery schedules
Monitor workloads, remove blockers and ensure effective resource allocation
Support knowledge sharing, documentation and business continuity


Systems Management & Support


Oversee the configuration, maintenance and performance of core business systems, including CRM and finance platforms
Manage escalated support issues and coordinate resolutions with internal and external partners
Ensure system security, documentation and governance standards are maintained
Build strong relationships with software vendors and service providers


Business Process Improvement


Identify opportunities to streamline processes and improve operational efficiency
Analyze current ways of working and recommend automation solutions where appropriate
Develop dashboards, workflows and reporting tools to improve visibility and decision making
Lead continuous improvement initiatives across multiple business functions


Change & Project Management


Manage system enhancements, upgrades, integrations and new functionality rollouts
Coordinate business requirements, testing, training and implementation activities
Assess business impacts, dependencies and risks before changes are introduced
Maintain clear governance and change control processes


Training & Stakeholder Engagement


Deliver system training, user guides and support materials
Act as the primary contact for system users across the business
Promote adoption of best practice processes and system usage
Build strong relationships across Finance, Operations, Sales, HR and Leadership Teams


Data Quality & Governance


Ensure the integrity, accuracy and consistency of business data
Support compliance with GDPR, audit requirements and internal controls
Use data and reporting insights to help drive informed business decisions


Requirements

Essential:


Experience in implementing and/or supporting CRM and finance systems (e.g., Dynamics, Salesforce, HubSpot, Sage, NetSuite, etc.)
Excellent stakeholder management across technical and non-technical audiences
Strong business analysis and problem-solving skills
Solid understanding of data management, integrations, and reporting
Proven ability to direct and coordinate a systems team's workload across multiple locations, including allocating tasks, agreeing delivery dates, reviewing progress and resolving competing priorities, while managing multiple projects or systems concurrently


Desirable:


Experience supporting both B2B and B2C operational models
Project management experience (Agile, PRINCE2, or similar)
Strong Excel and reporting capability (e.g., Power BI, SQL familiarity)
Experience in utilities, field service or service-based industries
