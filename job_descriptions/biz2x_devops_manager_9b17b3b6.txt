As a technical DevOps Engineering manager at Biz2x,

You would be responsible for facilitating various sprint ceremonies like daily standups, sprint planning, sprint retrospective, release retrospective, aligning team for sprint goal, grooming sessions, sprint demo’s etc.
You would facilitate implementation of fully automated Continuous Integration, Continuous Delivery, Continuous Deployment pipelines and DevOps processes for Agile projects.
You would be responsible for orchestrating, leading, and executing multiple projects by utilizing standard of agile methodologies and tools.
You would be responsible for leading a team of 8-10 people and focus on their growth path.
You would be responsible for driving reusable terraform based cloud platform for different cloud providers like GCP, AWS, Azure.
You would be responsible for supporting the Head of IT Operations in setting up cloud center of excellence and FinOps best practices across the organizations.
You would be responsible for maintaining the Burn down charts, Velocity Chart using JIRA and Confluence of documentation.
You would be Involved in driving the adoption of DevOps best practices across different projects in an organization.
You should be tracking Service Incidents/ Problem tickets and documenting RCA for cloud infrastructure incidents.
You would be point of contact for external communications (e.g. from customers or stakeholders)
You would ensure deliverables are up to quality standards at the end of each sprint.




Experience- 10 to 14 years
