The Quality Department plays a very important role in maintaining the quality management system and driving quality improvements to deliver high quality products for our customers.




Responsibilities

Support and maintain the Quality Management System (QMS) in accordance with ISO 13485 requirements.
Perform Incoming Quality Control (IQC), In-Process Quality Control (IPQC), and Final Quality Control (FQC) activities.
Review and interpret engineering drawings, specifications and inspection requirements.
Conduct inspections and measurements using common inspection equipment.
Investigate material, process and product quality issues and support corrective actions.
Handle non-conforming materials and products.
Support CAPA, NCR, customer complaints and supplier quality issues.
Participate in supplier audits and supplier quality improvement activities.
Support internal and external audits.
Prepare and maintain quality records, inspection reports, and related documentation.
Work closely with Engineering, Production, Procurement, and Warehouse teams to resolve quality issues.
Support continuous improvement projects and quality initiatives.
Perform other quality-related duties as assigned.




Minimum Qualifications

Diploma or Degree in Engineering, Quality, Manufacturing, Biomedical Engineering, or related discipline.
2 to 4 years of experience in Quality Engineering, Quality Assurance, or Manufacturing Quality.
Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings and specifications.
Familiarity with basic inspection and measurement equipment.
Good problem-solving and analytical skills.
Good communication and interpersonal skills.
Proficient in Microsoft Office applications.
Hands-on and willing to work on the production floor when required.




Preferred Qualifications

Experience in Medical Device, Precision Engineering, Electronics, or regulated manufacturing industries.
Knowledge of ISO 9001, 13485, 14001 Quality Management Systems added advantage.
Experience in IQC, IPQC, and PQC activities.
Experience in handling CAPA, NCR, customer complaints, or supplier quality issues.
Familiarity with quality tools such as 5 Why, Fishbone Diagram and 8D.
Exposure to supplier audits or internal audits.




What We Are Looking For

Hands-on and willing to support day-to-day quality operations.
Able to work independently and take ownership of quality issues.
Positive attitude and willingness to learn.
Good teamwork and communication skills.
