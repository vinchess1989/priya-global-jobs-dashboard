“Design global labelling that transforms complexity into clear and compliant messaging, creating confidence and elevating the product experience.”

That’s the mission of the ResMed Labelling Centre of Excellence.

We partner with teams across ResMed to translate regulatory, product, usability, manufacturing and market inputs into accurate, controlled and customer-focused labelling. In a highly regulated environment, attention to detail, traceability and sound judgement are essential to supporting product safety, quality and timely delivery.

As a Labelling Specialist, you will be a hands-on technical specialist responsible for translating product, regulatory, usability, manufacturing and market requirements into accurate, compliant and customer-focused medical device labelling across the product lifecycle. You will develop and maintain user guides, clinical guides, labels and other controlled content while applying technical judgement to achieve clear, verifiable and traceable outcomes.

You will own labelling outcomes for assigned projects and changes, engaging directly with cross-functional partners to clarify requirements, assess impacts, determine appropriate labelling responses and manage activities through delivery. You will lead or contribute to monitoring and assessing global regulations and standards, understanding their impact on labelling requirements, including regressions and change-impact assessments. You will participate in and/or lead technical reviews and verification activities, identify and resolve issues within your scope, and escalate matters requiring broader technical expertise or higher-level decisions.

You will use and help improve labelling systems, governance and structured-content practices, identify opportunities to improve quality and efficiency, and contribute to digital and continuous-improvement initiatives. You will share knowledge, exercise initiative and remain accountable for your technical decisions and deliverables.

You lift the people around you. You build trust and contribute to a culture of trust and accountability, where people feel confident to contribute, challenge, learn and grow.

Let’s Talk About What You’ll Do

Translate requirements: Interpret upstream regulatory, product, usability, manufacturing and other approved source inputs and translate them into clear, verifiable labelling outcomes.

Deliver controlled labelling outcomes: Own the development, review, proofing, approval and release of labelling deliverables, using approved tools and processes and ensuring content is technically accurate, usable, compliant and aligned to applicable requirements.

Assess change impact: Lead or contribute to regression and change-impact assessments as standards, approved source information, products or labelling requirements change. Determine and document impacts, recommend appropriate labelling actions and escalate complex or ambiguous matters when needed.

Manage controlled labelling content: Create, maintain and manage content in applicable legacy and enterprise systems, applying structured-content principles in ELS/CCMS and maintaining appropriate control, reuse, traceability and change history.

Apply labelling governance: Apply quality, regulatory and content governance requirements consistently; identify gaps or inconsistencies and work with relevant stakeholders to strengthen compliance, traceability and audit readiness.

Ensure quality and verification: Lead or contribute to technical reviews, change documentation, verification and validation activities, resolve findings within scope and ensure that labelling outcomes are complete, accurate and aligned with approved requirements.

Own labelling projects and deliverables: Act as the Labelling representative on assigned projects, understand the context and business requirements, establish and manage labelling deliverables, plan work, coordinate stakeholders and drive activities through to completion.

Manage issues, risks and dependencies: Proactively investigate and resolve labelling issues within scope, evaluate options, recommend solutions, manage dependencies and communicate risks or decisions early to stakeholders; escalate when appropriate.

Support global labelling: Coordinate with external partners to support localisation and translation workflows in accordance with applicable procedures and market requirements.

Improve how we work: Identify and contribute to implementing practical improvements to labelling requirements, processes, content quality, tools and workflows that improve consistency, efficiency, control and cycle time.

Develop labelling systems capability: Use applicable labelling systems and structured-authoring tools, investigate or troubleshoot content and system issues, and contribute to migration, implementation and system improvement activities.

Push the edge: Evaluate and use approved digital, automation and AI-enabled approaches that may improve content quality, consistency or efficiency, within applicable quality, regulatory, privacy, security and governance controls.

Build technical capability: Continue developing technical expertise and judgement, share knowledge and effective practices with colleagues, seek and provide constructive feedback, and contribute to an accountable and inclusive team culture.

Communicate and collaborate effectively: Engage cross-functionally to clarify technical inputs, challenge incomplete or inconsistent information, present recommendations and risks clearly, and support timely, informed decisions.

You’ll bring:


A bachelor’s degree in a relevant science, engineering, communications, regulatory or technical discipline, or equivalent relevant experience.
Relevant experience in labelling, regulatory, quality, technical documentation, technical operations or another discipline involving interpretation of controlled technical requirements.
Strong critical thinking and problem-solving skills, with the ability to analyse information, question assumptions, identify gaps and apply sound judgement to develop practical solutions.
A systems mindset, with the ability to understand how requirements, products, processes and labelling interact.
Experience taking ownership of technical documentation or labelling deliverables through review, change control and release, with an understanding of traceability.
Working knowledge of quality-system principles relevant to controlled documentation, change control, technical review and audit readiness.
Exceptional attention to detail, with a keen eye for inconsistencies, conflicting information and potential quality or compliance risks.
Ability to independently plan and manage assigned work, coordinate stakeholders and dependencies, and identify and communicate delivery risks.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with a collaborative style that builds effective working relationships across functions.



Preferred

It would be beneficial if you also bring:


Experience in medical devices, healthcare, electronics or another regulated industry.
Familiarity with medical device labelling regulations, standards and requirements across the product lifecycle.
Experience using technical authoring, desktop publishing or controlled-content systems, including Adobe Suite or similar tools.
Familiarity with structured authoring and enterprise content management platforms such as ELS/CCMS.
Experience leading or contributing to technical reviews, regression and change-impact assessments, verification or controlled-document changes.
Experience supporting translation or localisation activities across global markets.
Experience contributing to process improvements, content migration, digital tools or automation initiatives.
Familiarity with approved AI-enabled tools used to support technical-content workflows.
