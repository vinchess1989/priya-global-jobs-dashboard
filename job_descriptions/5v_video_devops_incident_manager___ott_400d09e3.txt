DevOps Incident Manager

UK (Fully Remote)
Full-Time Permanent
Salary: Up to £65,000 (DOE)
Total Comp: 10% Bonus, Standard Benefits




An established video technology company delivering platforms and services to media and entertainment customers. With the business growing quickly alongside its customers, they’re investing in how their platforms are run and building a more proactive, customer-focused approach to reliability.




They’re looking for a Technical Incident Manager to join their Cloud Reliability function. As the independent guardian of platform quality, you’ll hold engineering teams to high standards, make sure changes reach customers safely, and turn every incident into a lesson that sticks.




Key Responsibilities:

Leading detailed post-incident reviews as an independent voice, establishing what went wrong, why, and what needs to change
Building and owning a change management process, ensuring every engineering change has proper monitoring and testing plans before it reaches customers
Proactively reviewing the platform, even when no alerts are firing, to spot risks and process gaps before customers do
Deep-diving into operational data and trends, questioning anything that doesn’t add up and turning findings into improvements
Driving improvements to core KPIs and operational SLA targets
Translating technical issues into clear business terms for senior stakeholders and customers
Providing governance and consistent standards across support, SRE and testing teams




Ideal Profile:

A technical background, ideally in DevOps, engineering or application development
Experience working with cloud technology at scale
Strong hands-on knowledge of AWS
A solid understanding of how web and mobile applications work
Proven experience in change management
A track record of improving core KPIs or operational SLA targets
A curious, hands-on character who asks why and is confident holding engineers to account on their own ground
Experience in OTT or broadcast is a strong plus
This is a great opportunity to play a central role in shaping how a growing video technology business runs its platforms, working fully remote from anywhere in the UK.
