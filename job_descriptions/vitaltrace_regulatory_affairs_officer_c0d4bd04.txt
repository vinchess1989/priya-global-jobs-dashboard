About VitalTrace

VitalTrace Pty Ltd is a multi-award-winning, VC-funded Australian-based Med-tech start-up company aiming to improve outcomes for mothers and children during the childbirth process. We are doing this by developing novel cutting-edge biosensor technologies for fetal monitoring during childbirth. We believe that mothers and babies deserve the highest standard of care, and that childbirth monitoring should be accurate and allow clinicians to make informed and timely decisions. We have a vibrant culture with a diverse, high-performing multidisciplinary team of clinicians, researchers, engineers, chemists, software developers, and data scientists all working towards the same goal




The role
We are looking for a Regulatory Affairs Officer to join our Quality & Regulatory department at our Malaga office. You’ll be responsible for regulatory strategy and submissions for our device portfolio. You’ll keep the business compliant as global regulations change, and you’ll be our main contact with regulators and notified bodies.

Key tasks and duties

Develop regulatory pathways and submission strategies for new and existing medical devices, and manage timelines to meet project and launch milestones
Prepare, assemble and maintain regulatory dossiers and technical documentation for submissions to the FDA (510(k), PMA), the TGA and EU Notified Bodies (CE Marking)
Act as the company’s primary contact with regulatory authorities, notified bodies and in-country representatives, coordinating responses to queries, deficiency letters and requests for further information
Represent Regulatory Affairs on cross-functional project teams from concept to market, so regulatory requirements are built into design and development plans
Engage and coordinate external regulatory consultants where required
Support the business to maintain compliance with its quality management system, including design controls, risk management and post-market surveillance
Support audit and inspection readiness, and contribute to post-market activities such as vigilance reporting and field actions
Track changes to international medical device regulations, standards and guidance, assess the impact on company processes and products, and advise management on required actions
Work with the Clinical Affairs team so study designs meet regulatory expectations, and give regulatory input across the planning, conduct and reporting of clinical investigations
Develop and maintain internal regulatory procedures, records and reporting


Bachelor’s degree in Life Sciences, Biomedical Engineering, Regulatory Affairs or a related discipline (Master’s degree preferred)
Minimum 2–4 years’ regulatory affairs experience in the medical device industry
Demonstrated experience preparing regulatory submissions for Class II/III medical devices
Direct experience dealing with regulatory bodies such as the FDA, TGA or EU Notified Bodies
Sound working knowledge of FDA 21 CFR Part 820, EU MDR, ISO 13485 and other relevant international requirements
Working knowledge of clinical investigation requirements, including GCP and ISO 14155
Strong analytical, organisational and problem-solving skills, with close attention to detail and accuracy
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Ability to manage multiple projects and competing priorities in a fast-paced environment
