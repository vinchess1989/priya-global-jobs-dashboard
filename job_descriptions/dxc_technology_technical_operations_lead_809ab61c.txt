Job Description

DXC Technology (NYSE: DXC) is a leading enterprise technology and innovation partner delivering software, services, and solutions to global enterprises and public sector organisations — helping them harness AI to drive outcomes at a time of exponential change with speed. With deep expertise in Managed Infrastructure Services, Application Modernisation, and Industry-Specific Software Solutions, DXC modernises, secures, and operates some of the world’s most complex technology estates. Learn more on dxc.com

We're looking for a Technical Operations Lead to own the operational stability, release readiness and technical governance of our Assure platform and associated insurance technology services. You'll be the connective tissue between delivery, engineering, testing, infrastructure, security and service operations — making sure changes land safely, production risks are actively managed, and regulatory requirements are baked into everything we do.

What You'll Bring


Strong background in software delivery lifecycle, production acceptance and release governance
Hands-on experience with release, change, incident and problem management
Familiarity with DevSecOps, CI/CD, cloud and enterprise application operations
Excellent cross-functional leadership and stakeholder management
Sound judgement in high-risk operational and release decisions
Ability to translate technical issues into clear business impact
Experience with Jira or equivalent delivery tooling


Qualifications


Relevant degree or equivalent experience in technology, engineering, service management or delivery
Significant experience leading complex application delivery or technology operations
ITIL, DevOps, cloud, security or project management certification (preferred)
Experience in enterprise insurance, regulated environments or mission-critical platforms (highly regarded)
