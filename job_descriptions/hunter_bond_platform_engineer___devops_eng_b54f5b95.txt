Job Title: Platform Engineer / DevOps Engineer Project & Integration Lead

Client: Quant Firm

Salary: $150,000-$300,000 SGD + Bonus

Location: Singapore

Sells: Project Coordination & Management, Stakeholder Management, DevOps / SRE, Implementation, Integration




The role:




My client, an established Quant Trading firm are seeking an individual with a broad skillset across both Projects and Platforms to join their team.




The role sits between Project Management and Engineering, and encompasses elements of both. It would suit an experienced Platform Engineer who has transitioned into a role where they are managing / running projects, whilst still being in touch with the core Engineering / Tech side.




Responsibilities:




Leading end to end delivery of cross-team technical initiatives.
Working from initial scoping through to stakeholder management, and production rollout
Acting as a core anchor point to keep complex projects moving
Structuring work, managing dependencies, dealing with blockers, and driving cross-team projects forwards
Working closely with DevOps, SRE’s and leadership teams
Utilising previous Platform / DevOps related experience to give credibility and context to drive delivery




Experience required:




Experience coordinating cross-functional technical projects in enterprise environments
Proven experience driving delivery, managing stakeholders, dependencies and timelines
Breadth of technical experience across DevOps/Platform/SRE, comfortable contributing to conversation around CI/CD, Kubernetes, Docker, Observability, Middleware, Automation and associated technology
You should be competent with Linux, and delivery tools such as Jira, Confluence and Git




Please apply ASAP for more information.
