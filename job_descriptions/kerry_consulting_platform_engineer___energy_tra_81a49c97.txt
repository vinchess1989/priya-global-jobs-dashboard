Our client is a global energy trading organisation seeking a Platform Engineer to join its engineering team in Singapore. Combining hands-on development with operational ownership, this role focuses on the shared platforms, automation and delivery tools that enable software teams across the business. You will help improve how applications are built, deployed and supported across AWS and on-premises environments, while contributing to the direction of the organisation's platform capabilities.

Role

You will build and enhance reusable infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, deployment templates and internal tooling, with a focus on improving developer experience and reducing manual effort. Alongside engineering new capabilities, you will take responsibility for platform reliability, incident resolution, upgrades and lifecycle management. You will work closely with development teams to address delivery challenges and partner with cyber security colleagues on access controls, secrets management, vulnerability remediation and hardening. Within a globally distributed team, you will contribute to platform roadmaps, engineering standards, knowledge sharing and follow-the-sun support.

Requirements

You should bring at least three years' experience across CI/CD engineering, enterprise platform operations and AWS-based cloud infrastructure, including support for .NET and/or Python application environments. Experience across Windows and Linux is required, together with scripting capability in Python, Bash or PowerShell. You should have hands-on experience with infrastructure as code, source control and container tooling, using technologies such as Terraform, Jenkins, GitLab/GitHub and AWS ECS. Experience operating shared enterprise platforms, such as Kafka, Redis, Elastic, Datadog or workflow orchestration tools, is also required. Clear communication, independent problem-solving and an interest in applying AI to engineering workflows are important. A software development background, commodities or trading experience, contributions to enterprise CI/CD strategy, or practical experience with AI-assisted development and internal AI tooling would be advantageous. Exposure to security platforms such as HashiCorp Vault, or additional cloud environments such as GCP or Azure, would also be useful.
