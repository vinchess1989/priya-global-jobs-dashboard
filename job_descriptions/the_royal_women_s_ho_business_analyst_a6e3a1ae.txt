About The Women's

The Royal Women's Hospital is one of Australia's oldest and most distinguished tertiary hospitals, dedicated to improving the health and wellbeing of women and newborns and providing specialised services in maternity, neonatal and women's health. Our goal is to be the best place to work, learn and contribute, and to provide exceptional experiences for our patients and consumers.

About The Team (Digital Innovation)

Digital Innovation provides a unified digital and ICT capability across the Parkville health-service environment, delivering shared infrastructure, systems and innovation services that support safe, reliable and connected care. The Project Delivery and Transformation team leads complex digital initiatives and works closely with health-service leaders, operational teams, vendors and specialist partners to deliver sustainable technology-enabled change.

About The Rostering Project

The Rostering Project is a major workforce transformation initiative modernising rostering, scheduling, leave management, temporary staffing and agency workforce management. The project is implementing the RLDatix rostering platform, including Optima, Loop and BankStaff, and spans business process change, technology and integration, system configuration, payroll assurance, testing, training, staged deployment, hypercare and transition to business-as-usual.

About The Role

As an Business Analyst you will work across all project streams to understand operational needs and translate them into clear, validated and traceable requirements. You will lead detailed business discovery, process analysis, requirements elicitation, business-rule clarification and acceptance criteria, ensuring business and operational needs remain connected to solution design, reporting, testing, training and implementation.

A Regular Day Will See You


Plan and facilitate discovery sessions, process walkthroughs, requirements workshops, playbacks and validation discussions with business and project stakeholders.
Document current-state and future-state processes, business requirements, rules, acceptance criteria, assumptions, decisions and operational variations.
Maintain end-to-end traceability from requirements through configuration, integration, reporting, testing, training, readiness and acceptance.
Analyse gaps, competing requirements and process issues, and provide clear options, impacts and recommendations to support timely decisions.


About You

You are curious, structured and analytical, with the ability to listen carefully, challenge constructively and turn complex operational information into clear and practical requirements. You build trust quickly and are comfortable working across business, technical and clinical or operational stakeholder groups.

You'll Bring With You


Demonstrated experience in business analysis, process design, requirements management or business transformation within complex system implementations.
Strong workshop facilitation skills and experience eliciting, analysing, documenting, validating and prioritising business and operational requirements.
Demonstrated capability in current-state and future-state process mapping, gap analysis, options analysis and development of acceptance criteria.
Experience maintaining requirements traceability across configuration, technical delivery, reporting, testing, training and business acceptance.
Excellent analytical, problem-solving, written communication, documentation and stakeholder-engagement skills with strong attention to detail.
Experience in health, workforce management, rostering, payroll, HRIS, ERP or another complex public-sector environment will be highly regarded.
