The Role:
The role is site-based at a port facility in Port Hedland, on a 4:3 roster, working 12-hour shifts.
The successful candidate will be supporting multiple engineering projects. At this stage, the engagement is expected to run for approximately three to six months, with the potential for extension depending on project requirements and performance. As such, we are ideally looking for applicants who are available for the full duration and are interested in a longer-term opportunity should it be extended.

Job Overview:
We are seeking a skilled and detail-oriented Fixed Plant Technical Document Writer to support the creation and maintenance of work instructions and Production Resource Tools (PRTs) for newly installed capital projects. This role will ensure compliance with statutory obligations, update existing documentation to align with new templates, and capture any changes related to upgrades, replacement of components, or strategy adjustments. The ideal candidate will have experience in technical writing within an industrial or fixed plant environment and a solid understanding of equipment and operational procedures.

Key Responsibilities:
Create New Documentation


Develop clear, concise, and accurate work instructions for newly installed capital equipment
Produce PRTs and other technical documentation in line with project requirements and industry standards
Ensure all newly created documents meet statutory and safety regulations
Update Existing Documents

Review and update existing technical documents to reflect new templates, formatting, and company guidelines
Amend documents to reflect component replacements, upgrades, or changes to operational strategies
Ensure that all revisions undergo thorough review and approval processes
Compliance and Statutory Obligations

Stay informed on relevant statutory requirements and regulations that affect documentation
Ensure all technical documents are compliant with health, safety, and environmental standards
Collaboration and Stakeholder Engagement

Work closely with engineering, maintenance, and operations teams to gather accurate and up-to-date information
Engage with project managers and compliance officers to ensure timely document creation and updates
Facilitate the document review and approval process with all relevant stakeholders
Quality Control and Document Management

Maintain consistency in technical writing style and terminology across all documents
Ensure all documents are stored, indexed, and accessible in the document management system
Conduct regular reviews of document archives to identify outdated or obsolete information
Qualifications:

Proven experience in technical writing, preferably in a fixed plant or industrial environment
Strong understanding of equipment, work processes, and compliance requirements within capital projects
Familiarity with statutory obligations related to plant operations and equipment maintenance
Excellent communication and collaboration skills to liaise with cross-functional teams
Proficiency in document management systems and MS Office Suite
SAP experience advantageous
Strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple tasks simultaneously
Preferred:

Engineering or technical background
Experience with Production Resource Tools (PRTs) and work instructions development
Knowledge of safety, environmental, and statutory regulations for industrial or mining operations
