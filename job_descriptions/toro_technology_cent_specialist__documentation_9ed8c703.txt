Job Title – Specialist, Documentation Company: Toro Technology Center India

Who Are We?

The Toro Company is a homegrown, Minnesota-based company that has been in business since 1914. We pride ourselves on providing world-class equipment to help maintain the environment that we love, while putting an emphasis on giving back to the communities that surround us. From residential lawns and gardens to Golf Clubs and sports grounds we are a company with a global footprint and a passion for helping people beautify whatever landscape they may be in. With 100+ years of operation under our belt and an average employee tenure of 15 years, come find out what makes The Toro Company the best place to build a career.

Responsibilities

What Will You Do? In order to grow and build a successful career with The Toro Company, you will be responsible for:


Basic DITA conversion of legacy pubs
Complete publications database and scheduling tasks
Provide translation reviews
Publish analytics dashboards
Provide multimedia project tracking support
Convert process and training documents into Tech Pubs Training Portal
Convert service content into Help Centers
Adding cross references to legacy publications
Other duties, as assigned


What Do You Need?

To be considered for this role, an individual should meet the following minimal requirements:


Bachelor’s degree or equivalent from an accredited college in Technical Communications, Business Analyst, English, or related engineering field or applicable experience as a writer/communications specialist.
0-5 plus years’ experience in documentation or business analyst field.
Experience with XML; database publishing is desirable.
Experience with Arbortext Editor or similar application is desirable.
Proficiency with MS software (Excel, Word, and PowerPoint) is desirable.
Other Job-Related Components: Author instructional documentation that complies with the Toro Style Guide for Instructional Documentation.
Work closely with the team lead to ensure that all projects have the proper priorities and support to meet plant production and on-line requirements.
Communicate cross functionally with SME (subject matter experts), Engineering, Product Evaluation, Service, Legal, and Marketing.
