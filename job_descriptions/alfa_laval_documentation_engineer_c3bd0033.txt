Vacancy for Documentation Engineer

The purpose of this job is to ensure that the local PES site has a well-defined documentation delivery process and to improve documentation procedures at every step to achieve the set performance targets on quality. Position shall also contribute in the development of technology know-how.

About The Company

In Alfa Laval, we are every single day contributing to a more sustainable future for our planet through engineering innovation. Our global team designs and implements the refining, recycling and purifying process that allow our customers to be better themselves. You will find our work in leading applications for water purification, clean energy, food production, waste processing, pharmaceutical development and much more. The people of Alfa Laval enjoy the freedom to pursue ideas and the resources to see those ideas to become reality, in an environment that welcomes drive, courage and diversity. The result is a company where we all can be very proud of what we do.

Our core competencies are in the areas of heat transfer separation, and fluid handling. We are dedicated to optimizing the performance of our customers' processes. our world-class technologies, our systems, equipment and services must create solutions that help our customers stay ahead. Please feel free to peruse our website www.alfalaval.com.

The Position / About The Job

In this job the Documentation engineer would be responsible for:

Duties

supporting a project team in all areas of a project’s activities in accordance with Alfa Laval’s project methodology to reach the desired outcome. To achieve the same typical administrative duties can be elements such as :


Experience in having in-depth knowledge about using Adobe Acrobat Professional software, MS Office tools.
Good at reading and interpreting technical documents, drawings and specifications
In charge of receiving and redistributing all Documents
Maintain document control registers for incoming and outgoing project documents, by using defined database for tracking and recording project document.
Ensure that the quality of the documentation package is maintained at a desired level for all projects assigned by our internal customer from different sites of Alfa Laval. To achieve the same typical administrative duties can be elements such as :
Work with quality assurance of documents and tasks and follow given quality procedures
Follow the machinery directives (table of contents) while working on technical manuals
Work with product experts and project teams in setting and improving standards for Alfa Laval’s product documentation
Capable of understanding various types of process components from other suppliers
Capable of understanding and analyzing the contents of technical documents
Compilation of Documents using Adobe Acrobat professional Software
Handling DCC (Document Controller Activities) for various applications
Cross-cultural awareness
Should be well conversant with providing PDF interactive output and should have basic knowledge about Indesign and Frame maker software
Providing suggestions and ideas for improving documentation and implementing these in daily work procedures
Delivering and driving an improvement of current solutions and/ or developing new solutions to ensure our competitive advantage.
Carry out root cause analysis & suggest corrective actions for observed non-conformances
Support the site Best practice quality manager in maintaining and developing the Quality Management System for PES
Contribute in QMS audits


Who You Are

Experience and Education:


Mechanical / Industrial Production / Chemical graduate or Diploma engineer having relevant experience
Minimum 4-5 years of experience in relevant function within project industry. Preferably in handling international projects and/ or Indian projects of MNCs.


Knowledge


Good knowledge in writing the Technical Manuals
Should have in depth knowledge about Adobe Acrobat Professional software
Knowledge and experience in QMS like ISO 9001.


Skills


Fluent written and spoken English.
Experience in having in-depth knowledge about using Adobe Acrobat Professional software, MS tools and Oxygen software's
Experience with quality systems and management.
Good at reading and interpreting technical documents, drawings and specifications
Good knowledge on writing Technical Manuals (like IOM’s)
Fluent written and spoken English


Attitude


Commitment to deliverables and deadlines
Good team player and customer-oriented with good communication skills
Eager to learn
Eager to teach others based on experience
Willing and able to work independently and persistently.
Structured and analytical approach to assignments.


Motivation


Is driven by a wish to improve quality aspects of the work and processes in the organization.
Eagar to contact internal customers and work with new assignments

Exciting place to build a global network with different nationalities to mingle and to learn.

Your work will have a true impact on Alfa Laval’s future success, you will be learning new things every day.
