The EA Tool Business Analyst will support the design, development, testing, release and adoption of the Bank's Enterprise Architecture (EA) Tool. The role acts as a bridge between Enterprise Architecture stakeholders, the Bank's application teams, and the delivery team developing and testing the EA Tool, ensuring that business needs are understood, translated into actionable backlog items and delivered with appropriate quality and control.

The successful candidate will be hands-on, organised and comfortable operating as part of multidisciplined software product team. The role requires strong analytical and communication skills, attention to detail, disciplined follow-through and the ability to provide clear progress updates to multiple stakeholders.

Key Responsibilities


Product design and requirements: Participate actively in EA Tool product design discussions, workshops and backlog refinement sessions. Understand the problem domain of user needs, business processes and governance requirements, and translate them into clear functional requirements, design documentation, process flows, acceptance criteria and well-structured user stories
User stories and backlog readiness: Create, refine and maintain user stories throughout the product lifecycle. Ensure scope, dependencies, assumptions, business rules and expected outcomes are documented and aligned with relevant stakeholders before development
Prototype and UI design: Develop practical prototypes, wireframes and screen concepts using AI-assisted tools, based on agreed design discussions. Use prototypes to clarify requirements, validate workflows and accelerate stakeholder feedback
Test planning and execution: Prepare test scenarios and detailed test cases that provide traceability to requirements and acceptance criteria. Coordinate and participate in functional testing, regression testing and user acceptance testing, and support defect triage and retesting
Release management and live verification: Follow release plans closely and coordinate with product, engineering, testing and support teams to confirm release readiness. Participate in production live verification, document outcomes and track any observations, defects or follow-up actions to closure
Stakeholder coordination: Work with Enterprise Architecture team, technology stakeholders, product owner, developers, testers and user representatives. Facilitate alignment, resolve requirement gaps and keep the team informed of progress, risks, dependencies and decisions
Progress reporting and governance: Maintain accurate delivery status and supporting artefacts. Support weekly reviews, prepare concise progress updates and present deliverable status, upcoming milestones, issues, risks and required decisions to stakeholders
Continuous improvement: Identify opportunities to improve EA Tool workflows, usability, documentation, test coverage and product delivery practices. Contribute to reusable templates, standards and ways of working that strengthen product quality and delivery consistency


Requirements

Skills and Work Experience


3 to 5 years of relevant experience in a product development lifecycle, business analysis, software product operations, or technology delivery role
Experience in requirements gathering, process analysis, user story creation, acceptance criteria definition and backlog refinement
Strong proficiency in Object-Oriented Analysis and Design (OOAD), creating and maintaining conceptual data models, class diagrams, and ERDs from ambiguous business requirements
Practical experience creating test scenarios and test cases, supporting user acceptance testing, tracking defects and validating fixes
Good understanding of release management activities, including release readiness, deployment coordination, live verification and post-release follow-up
Ability to translate design discussions into clear prototypes or wireframes using Figma, AI-assisted prototyping tools or similar design platforms
Strong stakeholder management skills, with the ability to work effectively across business, architecture, product, engineering, testing and support teams
Clear written and verbal communication skills, including the ability and willingness to write good techno-functional user stories, design documentation, and test cases
Good organisational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to manage multiple priorities, dependencies and deliverables in a fast-paced environment
Understanding Enterprise Architecture concepts, governance processes and tools is an advantage
Experience in the banking or financial services industry is an advantage


Tool Skills


Requirements Management: Jira, Azure DevOps (ADO), Productboard / Aha!
Testing: Zephyr, BDD (Behavior-Driven Development) frameworks like Cucumber / Gherkin
Creating and communicating Design: Figma, Excel, Powerpoint, Visio, Lucidchart, Miro, Mural, Draw.io
Making UI prototypes: Gemini, Claude, Figma Make


Measures of Success


User stories and acceptance criteria are clear, complete and ready for delivery
Test coverage is traceable to requirements, and defects are tracked through closure
Releases are supported with disciplined readiness checks and timely live verification
UI Prototypes enable faster alignment and reduce ambiguity during design and development
Stakeholders receive accurate, concise and timely progress updates, including risks and dependencies
