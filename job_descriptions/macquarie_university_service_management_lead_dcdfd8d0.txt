The Service Management Lead shapes user-centred knowledge and information environment and frameworks across Student Shared Services (service catalogue and knowledge ecosystem), with a focus on improving self-service and increasing its use and consistency of content and enabling automation/AI pathways in partnership with relevant functions.

Salary $147K - $155K per annum (HEW Level 9), plus 17% superannuation
2 Year Full-time fixed term appointment
Macquarie University, Wallumattagal Campus, North Ryde


Shape a knowledge and self-service ecosystem that makes everyday work simpler for thousands of students and staff


Every day, students across Macquarie reach out for help and the quality of that experience depends on how well our knowledge, content, service catalogue, and AI-enablement are designed. This is a rare opportunity to own that service end to end, setting the strategy, standards and frameworks that make information easy to find, easy to use and easy to trust.


As Service Management Lead (Student Shared Services), you will be the senior specialist voice on service management across Student Shared Services. You will reposition self-service as a genuine first-choice channel, uplift the consistency and quality of content, and open the way for automation and AI-enabled service delivery through structured, reusable knowledge assets.


Working alongside MQIT and other enabling functions, you will help shape enterprise platforms such as Salesforce and translate user insights and behavioural data into initiatives that noticeably improve the student experience.


About the Role


Reporting to the Head of Enabling Services, you will design and govern the knowledge and service catalogue environment across Student Shared Services. Key accountabilities include:




Make self-service a first-choice channel through better discoverability, experience design and knowledge embedded in service journeys.
Shape the knowledge management strategy, frameworks, governance and operating model.
Partner with MQIT and enabling functions to optimise Salesforce and AI-enabled service delivery.
Establish organisation-wide standards for knowledge and service catalogue design to eliminate duplicated student service content across platforms and channels, improving consistency, usability and efficiency.
Monitor and optimise knowledge and chatbot performance using data, user insights and behavioural analytics.
Turn systemic insights across content, information architecture and service design into prioritised initiatives.
Advise senior stakeholders on service design, policy and continuous improvement.
Manage review cycles, quality reviews and adherence checks with Head of Student Services and Shared Services leaders.
Build capability through mentoring, coaching and training.


About Us


Macquarie University is a place of pioneering minds, with a reputation for research excellence, outstanding teaching and a collegiate culture that welcomes fresh thinking. Set on a green, connected campus in North Ryde, we bring together more than 44,000 students and over 3,000 staff.


This position sits within Enabling Services in the People and Operations portfolio (DVCPO), the team that lifts the quality, consistency and efficiency of services across the University. Enabling Services brings together specialist expertise in service management, process design, data and insights, governance, and capability and adoption, all pointed at one outcome: service experiences our community values.


About You (Selection Criteria)


You are an experienced service management or knowledge management professional who understands what good looks like in a complex, customer-focused environment. You bring frameworks and discipline, you make decisions based on evidence, and you communicate with clarity and credibility at senior levels.




Tertiary qualifications or certification in knowledge management or a related discipline, and/or equivalent experience in knowledge management and service catalogue functions within complex, customer-focused environments.
Demonstrated ability to design, implement and govern enterprise knowledge management and service catalogue frameworks that are scalable and aligned to organisational strategy.
Proven expertise in digital service delivery and service management practices (e.g. ITIL), and the ability to translate these into knowledge, self-service and automation strategies.
Demonstrated success in standardising processes and delivering measurable gains in student experience and operational efficiency.
Proven ability to influence across functions and stakeholder groups, including senior leaders, to deliver integrated knowledge and service design outcomes.
Demonstrated experience building capability through coaching, training and best-practice approaches that encourage ownership and continuous improvement.
Experience using data, user insights and analytics to improve content quality, self-service outcomes and AI-enabled service channels.
