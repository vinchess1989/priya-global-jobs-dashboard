Shape the engineering delivery tools and platforms used by thousands of CommBank engineers to plan, build, test and release software.
Lead a large, multidisciplinary engineering crew across Australia and India, with the opportunity to influence engineering strategy and the developer experience at Group scale.
Drive the next generation of AI-powered engineering, automation and delivery tooling as CommBank builds simpler, faster and safer ways to deliver technology.


Do Work That Matters

Technology is central to how CommBank is building tomorrow’s bank today. Across the Group, we’re focused on making delivery simpler, faster and more consistent, supported by modern platforms, better engineering practices and AI embedded into how we build and operate technology.

As Crew Lead for Engineering Delivery Tooling, you’ll lead the crew responsible for the engineering tools thousands of our engineers rely on every day, spanning planning and collaboration, continuous testing and engineering quality. You’ll set the strategy for these platforms and lead their evolution as AI and agentic workflows become an increasingly important part of how software is delivered. What you do will directly influence how quickly, safely and effectively our engineers can turn ideas into customer outcomes.

See yourself in our team

Engineering Platform sits within our Chief Technology Office, supporting teams across CommBank with the platforms, tools and engineering capabilities they need to deliver secure, reliable and innovative technology.

Reporting to the Technology Lead, Engineering Platform, you’ll join the Engineering Platform Leadership Team and work closely with leaders across Engineering Pathways and Engineering Data to create a connected, frictionless engineering delivery experience.

In this role, you will:


Own the strategy and roadmap for engineering delivery tooling across planning, collaboration, continuous testing and engineering quality.
Modernise our enterprise Atlassian ecosystem, including Jira and Confluence, simplifying workflows, configuration and integrations at scale.
Embed AI and agentic workflows into software delivery and turn innovation into measurable improvements in engineering productivity and experience.
Scale continuous test automation and quality practices, strengthening how automated testing and quality gates are built into delivery.
Set effective governance across tooling standards, guardrails, access, licensing, technology risk and software delivery controls.
Use engineering data to understand adoption, usage and value, improving the engineer experience while optimising tooling and licence costs.
Be accountable for the availability, resilience, security and cost of the crew’s platforms, alongside its budget, vendors and workforce plan.
Lead, coach and develop a multidisciplinary crew of around 60–70 people across Australia and India, creating an inclusive, high-performing culture through change.


We’re interested in hearing from people who:

You’ll bring a strong engineering foundation together with strategic leadership, commercial judgement and the ability to lead technology change at enterprise scale. You’ll be comfortable moving between engineering detail and executive conversations, while creating clarity and empowering your teams to deliver.

We’re particularly interested in experience across:


Leading large, multidisciplinary engineering, platform or technology teams, including geographically distributed teams.
Owning enterprise engineering platforms or developer tooling at scale, ideally including Jira, Confluence or the broader Atlassian ecosystem.
Applying AI, intelligent automation or agentic workflows to engineering and demonstrating measurable improvements in productivity, quality or delivery.
Modern software delivery practices, including continuous testing, CI/CD, DevSecOps, automation and engineering quality.
Technology risk, platform governance, controls, security and resilience within a complex or regulated environment.
Using engineering data and platform metrics to understand adoption, value, cost and developer experience.
Leading complex tooling modernisation, migration or consolidation programs and driving adoption through organisational change.
Managing budgets, vendors and workforce planning while influencing senior leaders across a large, matrix organisation.


Working with us:

Whether you're passionate about customer service, driven by data, or called by creativity, a career with CommBank is for you.

Our people bring their diverse backgrounds and unique perspectives to build a respectful, inclusive, and flexible workplace with flexible work locations. One where we're driven by our values, and supported to share ideas, initiatives, and energy. One where making a positive impact for customers, communities and each other is part of our every day.
