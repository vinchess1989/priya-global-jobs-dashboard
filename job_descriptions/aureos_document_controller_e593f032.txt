If you are looking to work for a growing business who truly value and care for their employees, then look no further!


Maintain and administer project document control systems (Aconex, SharePoint, ProjectWise, Asite, Viewpoint & ACC).
Register, track, distribute and archive all incoming and outgoing project documentation.
Ensure documents are correctly numbered, version controlled and approved in line with project procedures.
Manage document transmittals and workflows between clients, contractors, subcontractors and design teams.
Monitor document review and approval processes to ensure deadlines are achieved.
Support project teams in meeting contractual document control requirements.
Produce document control reports, registers and KPI dashboards for project management.
Coordinate the preparation, submission and tracking of project deliverables.
Manage project handover documentation, including Operations & Maintenance (O&M) manuals.
Ensure compliance with company quality management systems and client requirements.
Carry out regular audits of project documentation and records.
Maintain confidentiality, security and integrity of project information.
Liaise with engineers, planners, commercial teams, site management and client representatives.
Provide guidance and training to project teams on document control procedures and systems.
Support project meetings by issuing controlled documents and recording actions where required.
Maintain accurate and up-to-date document registers.
Ensure timely distribution and retrieval of project information.
Monitor document status and report on overdue submissions and approvals.
Support successful project close-out and document handover activities.
Ensure compliance with ISO 9001 and project governance requirements.
Manage documentation for major energy projects including substations, transmission and distribution networks, HV cable projects, renewable energy and grid infrastructure works.
Work effectively under pressure, managing multiple priorities and deadlines.
Demonstrate strong attention to detail, organisational skills and stakeholder management abilities.
Use Microsoft Office and document management platforms proficiently.
Support continuous improvement initiatives in document control processes and reporting.
