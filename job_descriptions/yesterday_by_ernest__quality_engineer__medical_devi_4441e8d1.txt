Quality Engineer (Medical Devices)
£40,000 - £43,000 + Bonus + Sick Pay + Life Assurance + 33 Days Holiday
Andover, Hampshire

Are you a Quality Engineer from a medical devices background, looking to utilise your knowledge and experience within a rapidly expanding manufacturer, working within a best-in-class manufacturing facility?

Are you looking to be recognised as a go-to expert in a niche field, where you can influence change and drive quality standards throughout a company you will be proud to represent?

In this role you will be helping to maintain and develop the QMS in a high-end cleanroom environment, supported by a team of 6. You will ensure compliance with ISO 9001/13485, along with AS9100, with training given if needed.

Founded nearly 40 years ago, this manufacturer supplies their specialist products into industries such as aerospace, automotive, and medical, with trusted clients across the globe. They have experienced unprecedented growth over recent years and have a clear direction for the future with a new site opened recently.

This role would suit a Quality Engineer from a medical devices or similar background, looking for an exciting career within a company who will develop them for the future.

The Role:

Cleanroom focused, helping to develop and maintaining the QMS
Remaining compliant to ISO 9001, ISO 13485, and AS9100, with training given
Using CAPA, 8D's, 5 Whys
Monday to Thursday, 8:30am - 5pm, finish at 3:30pm on Friday

The Person:

Quality Engineer
Medical devices or similar background
