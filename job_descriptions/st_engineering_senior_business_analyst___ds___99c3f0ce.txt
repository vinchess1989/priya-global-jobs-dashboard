About ST Engineering

ST Engineering is a global technology, defence, and engineering group with offices across Asia, Europe, the Middle East, and the U.S., serving customers in more than 100 countries. The Group uses technology and innovation to solve real-world problems and improve lives through its diverse portfolio of businesses across the aerospace, smart city, defence, and public security segments. Headquartered in Singapore, ST Engineering ranks among the largest companies listed on the Singapore Exchange.




Our history spans more than 50 years, and our strategy is underpinned by our core values – Integrity, Value Creation, Courage, Commitment and Compassion. These 5 core values guide every aspect of our business and are embedded in our ST Engineering culture – from the people we hire, to working with each other, to our partners and customers.




About our Line of Business – Mission Software & Services

Our Mission Software & Services business provides leading-edge mission critical command, control, and communications (C3) systems with secured IT infrastructure and managed services. We support our client’s innovation journey through design thinking, analytics, and AI-enabled decision support with our full suite of cloud computing solutions. We provide intelligent, actionable insights and sustainable solutions to our valued partners in diverse industries including defence, government, and commercial sectors.




Together, We Can Make A Significant Impact

Join our aviation systems team as a Senior Business Analyst supporting human machine interface development and customization. You will connect operational stakeholders and engineering teams, translating user needs into clear, testable requirements and coordinating delivery across multiple Agile squads.




Be Part of Our Success

Facilitate workshops with operational users and engineering stakeholders to clarify human machine interface workflows, priorities and acceptance criteria.
Translate business and operational needs into epics, features and user stories; maintain traceability through verification, validation and acceptance.
Coordinate 2–3 Agile squads, facilitating sprint planning, backlog refinement, reviews and retrospectives while managing dependencies and impediments.
Work with software, DevSecOps and V&V (Verification & Validation) teams to resolve interface and data-flow questions, assess change impacts and align delivery commitments.
Communicate progress, risks and decisions clearly, supporting stakeholder reviews and readiness for acceptance.




Qualities We Value

5–10 years of experience in Business Analysis, Scrum Master or Agile delivery roles for large-scale software/system integration projects.
Proven experience leading or facilitating minimum 2–3 Agile squads concurrently, including sprint planning, backlog refinement, reviews, retrospectives, dependency management and impediment resolution.
Strong experience translating business/operational needs into epics, features, user stories, acceptance criteria and testable requirements, with requirements traceability through V&V and acceptance.
Experience leading Agile delivery involving full-stack software, distributed/event-driven architectures, system integration and DevOps/CI/CD, working closely with software, DevSecOps and V&V teams.
Good technical understanding of APIs, data flows, system interfaces, containerised/Kubernetes environments and messaging/event-streaming concepts; able to facilitate technical discussions without being the solution architect.
Strong stakeholder and workshop facilitation skills, with experience managing cross-squad dependencies, competing priorities and technical/operational stakeholders.
