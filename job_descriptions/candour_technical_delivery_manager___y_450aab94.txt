Role overview:




We are working with a global financial services player seeking a Technical Delivery Manager to turn business priorities into well-defined, deliverable work across its data and reporting products. Working with the Data Product Manager and multidisciplinary teams, you will support the product roadmap and take day-to-day ownership of backlog quality, delivery planning, dependencies, risks and release readiness. You will coordinate delivery from discovery through release and live service, keeping work transparent, controlled and focused on outcomes.




You will bring proven experience as a Delivery Manager or in a comparable product delivery role, ideally in a complex organisation. Strong Agile delivery, backlog management and stakeholder skills are essential, alongside the technical understanding to identify constraints and discuss trade-offs across areas such as architecture, integrations, security, data governance and non-functional requirements.




Key Skills & experience:




Proven Delivery Manager or comparable product delivery experience
Complex-organisation delivery ownership
Backlog management, epics, user stories and acceptance criteria
Agile planning, estimation, dependency, risk and issue management
End-to-end delivery: discovery through release and live service
Understanding of architecture, integrations, environments, security, data governance and non-functional requirements
Strong stakeholder management, facilitation and communication skills
Ability to balance business value, risk, delivery constraints and technical sustainability
Experience influencing across organisational boundaries and resolving complex dependencies




Nice-to-haves:




Product, Scrum, Agile delivery or project delivery accreditation
Jira, Confluence or similar tools
Data platforms, reporting and analytics product experience
Data governance, data quality, supplier management or BAU product operations experience
