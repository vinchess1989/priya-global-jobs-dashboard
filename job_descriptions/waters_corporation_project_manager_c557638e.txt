Overview

Waters Reagent Kitting buisness provides key IVD and RUO medical devices to our customers. We are looking for a Project Manager to deliver key initiatives to improve our Manufacturing products and processes.This is as exciting opportunity to join a fast growing team.

Responsibilities


Lead and manage projects from concept to implementation on time and within budget for new and existing products and processes.
Own and manage Engineering Change Requests (ECRs) and Change Controls for the Reagent Kitting Department, ensuring timely implementation and compliance with quality and regulatory requirements.
Lead Continuous Improvement projects aimed at reducing Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) through material optimisation, process improvements, and productivity enhancements.
Collaborate with multi-functional teams to deliver projects and support strategic revenue-generating opportunities.
Manage projects via Waterfall or Agile methodologies, provide Gantt charts, project timelines and action owners to ensure successful completion.
Perform additional tasks as directed.
Ensure all experimental data, validation documentation, and quality records are accurately maintained and completed in accordance with company procedures, regulatory requirements, and compliance standards.
Lead or support investigations, root cause analyses, and corrective actions as required, initiating and implementing appropriate controlled changes to ensure effective resolution.
Conduct all project activities in accordance with appropriate standards (21CFR820, ISO9001, ISO13485, GMP).
Identify, assess, and manage project risks, issues, dependencies, and resource requirements to ensure successful project delivery.



Qualifications

Education:


BSc. or higher in chemistry or relevant science discipline.
Formal Project Management qualification (PMP, PRINCE2, Agile) or equivalent project delivery experience.



Experience:


Experience in IVD, clinical diagnostics, laboratory systems, or life sciences preferred.
Experience working in an R&D environment (pharmaceutical or medical device) would be advantageous.
Experience managing stakeholders across multiple functional groups and levels within an organisation.
Demonstrated experience in structured project planning, execution, and delivery.



Competencies:


Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to make data-driven decisions.
Proven report writing and presentation skills.
Good interpersonal and communication skills.
Good time management and attention to detail.
