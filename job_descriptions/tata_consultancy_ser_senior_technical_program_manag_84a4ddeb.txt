Job Description:

12+ years of technology/program delivery experience, with experience leading large, complex enterprise programs.
Proven experience taking at least one mission-critical technology program from inception through production.
Demonstrated experience working directly with C-level executives.
Strong understanding of modern technology environments including cloud, APIs/microservices, enterprise integration, data, DevSecOps, cybersecurity, SRE and production operations.
Strong executive communication, stakeholder management, problem-solving and decision-making skills.
Ability to operate effectively between CXO-level conversations and deep technical/engineering discussions.
Experience in Financial Services, FinTech, Payments or other high availability/regulated environments is preferred.
Own a mission-critical technology program end to end, from strategy and planning through production rollout and stabilization.
Partner with CIO/CTO/COO and senior executives, providing concise updates on progress, risks, decisions and business impact.
Drive execution across product, architecture, engineering, cloud/infrastructure, security, data, operations and vendor teams.
