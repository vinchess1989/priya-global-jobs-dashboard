About The Role

We are looking for a highly skilled Senior DevOps Engineer to join our engineering team. You will be responsible for designing, implementing, and maintaining scalable, secure, and reliable infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines. This role requires deep expertise in Kubernetes ecosystems, GitOps practices, and cloud-native technologies.

Key Responsibilities


Design, build, and manage scalable infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code (IaC) practices
Implement and manage Kubernetes clusters and associated tooling in production environments
Deploy and manage microservices using Helm charts
Implement and maintain GitOps workflows using ArgoCD
Configure and manage service mesh (Istio) for traffic management, observability, and security
Automate CI/CD pipelines to improve developer productivity and system reliability
Collaborate with development teams to streamline application deployment and operations
Monitor system performance, troubleshoot issues, and ensure high availability
Enforce security best practices across infrastructure and deployment pipelines


Requirements

6-10 years of hands-on DevOps experience


Strong expertise in Kubernetes (production-grade environments)
Hands-on experience with Istio service mesh
Proficiency in Helm charts for application deployment
Experience implementing GitOps workflows using ArgoCD
Strong experience with Terraform for infrastructure provisioning
Solid hands-on experience with either AWS or GCP
Experience working with managed Kubernetes services such as EKS or GKE
Strong understanding of CI/CD principles and tools
Experience with SAST/DAST implementation
Good scripting skills (Bash, Python, or similar)


Benefits


A meaningful role at the heart of a high-growth cybersecurity company
Direct visibility with leadership and real influence over people decisions
Competitive compensation with performance-linked incentives
Flexible working arrangements and a culture of ownership and trust
