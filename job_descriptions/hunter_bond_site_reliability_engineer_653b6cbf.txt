Job Title: Site Reliability Engineer (Bare Metal Infrastructure)

Client: Elite FinTech Firm

Salary: Up to $1.2m HKD Base + Bonus + Package

Location: Hong Kong

Experience Level: 2 - 20 years

Sells: Cutting-edge tech, ownership of multiple greenfield projects, no red tape, a friendly/collaborative environment, beautiful offices, personal projects on Fridays!




An Elite FinTech Firm is looking for a highly talented SRE to join a team working on mission critical infrastructure at a scale thought impossible!




This team have an unlimited tech budget, promote a great culture and are made up of fantastic like-minded individuals.




Role:

Working as part of a team of engineers involved in building out observability infrastructure from scratch
Automating anything and everything
Working across a range of infrastructure areas including HPC, Scheduling, Storage, Networks etc.
R&D work on best possible solutions going forward within the SRE space including the use of LLMs




Skills:

Any years experience working as either an SRE or within Application Support/Linux Systems and wanting to learn SRE!
You must have experience with some monitoring technologies e.g. Prometheus, Victoria Metrics, ELK
Knowledge of programming (ideally Python) - Scripting is also fine.
Knowledge of CI/CD
A background in Linux Engineering/Application Support is a huge plus
An interest in the ML space is a cool addition!
