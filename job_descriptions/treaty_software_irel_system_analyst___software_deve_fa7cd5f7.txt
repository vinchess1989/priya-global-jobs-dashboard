System Analyst - Software Development (Limerick Hybrid)

Treaty Software is recruiting a System Analyst on behalf of one of our Limerick based customers.




The ideal candidate will work within a Product Team supporting a Product Owner working with both internal & external stakeholders to translate business needs & requirements into user stories ready for development. 

You will also work with Project Management to help analyse & streamline the team’s Agile workflows to strengthen the team’s processes.




Key Responsibilities:

·       Understand user needs & translate them into detailed requirements for the development team.

·       Concisely document clinical workflows, product specifications, acceptance criteria, design details, & user stories & ensure each feature has a clear definition of done

·       Communicate product vision to developers & testers

·       In partnership with the PO, maintain a deep understanding of the business processes and requirements to ensure that the development backlog priorities are aligned with business needs

·       Track product metrics & build reports to measure progress

·       Support the R&D team as well as the wider team to increase overall understanding of the product during & after development

·       Help manage dependencies across other development teams, working closely with their PO’s, peer BAs & the Product Manager

·       Apply business process improvement methods for optimization

·       Assist in customer research to guide optimal workflows & designs

·       Subject matter expert in Agile processes in the field of product analysis

·       Support &/or facilitate system demos via inspect & adapt workshops as needed




Qualifications:

- Bachelor’s degree in computer science, business information system, Healthcare informatics systems

- Minimum 3 years+ experience

- Previous experience as a Business Analyst or similar role in technology/med tech

- Proven knowledge & experience of understanding standard practices, technology & supporting industry principles

- Proficiency with Microsoft 365 tools, CoPilot, experience with Excel, Visio, PowerPoint, Azure Devops or similar.

- Experience in Scrum / SAFe Agile methodologies




Required Knowledge, Skills, and Attributes:

·       Strong communication skills, both written & oral

·       Knowledge of best practices for Agile software development

·       Strong problem-solving skills

·       Self-motivated to drive progress




Nice-to-Have:

- Knowledge of human clinical microbiology or hospital IT systems

- Experience in an FDA/ISO regulated environment

- Knowledge of AI tools

- Experience with large scale, enterprise applications
