Quality Engineer (ISO 9001 | Advanced Manufacturing) - Bristol (Hybrid)




We’re working with a fast-growing advanced manufacturing business developing complex, next-generation technology systems. As they continue to scale, they are looking to bring in a Quality Engineer to take ownership of quality and compliance across the organisation.




This is a broad, high-impact role focused on ISO 9001 compliance, customer quality, and supplier quality, with real scope to shape how quality is managed as the business grows.




What you’ll be doing:

Own and manage quality & compliance processes across the business
Ensure alignment with ISO 9001 standards and broader regulatory requirements
Manage customer quality, ensuring products meet industry-specific standards (e.g. aerospace, automotive, defence)
Oversee supplier quality, including assessments, audits, and compliance checks
Track and manage non-conformances, complaints, and corrective actions
Support inspection processes and quality reporting
Work closely with engineering and technical teams to ensure compliance is embedded in product development
Maintain and update quality documentation and compliance packs
Monitor regulatory changes and ensure ongoing compliance




What we’re looking for:

Experience in a Quality Engineer / Quality Assurance role within engineering or manufacturing
Strong understanding of ISO 9001 and quality management systems
Experience working with customer-driven quality standards (e.g. aerospace, automotive, defence)
Exposure to supplier quality, audits, or compliance processes
Comfortable working in a fast-paced, evolving environment
Proactive, pragmatic mindset able to balance structure with adaptability




Nice to have:

Experience with aerospace or automotive quality standards
Audit training or experience managing internal/external audits
Exposure to scaling or start-up environments
Understanding of technical documentation or compliance packs




This is a great opportunity to join a business scaling internationally, where you’ll have real ownership and the chance to shape quality processes as the company grows.
