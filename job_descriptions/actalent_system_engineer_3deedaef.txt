Senior Systems Engineer – Medical Devices

Location - Dublin

Contract length - 12 months




We are looking for an experienced Senior Systems Engineer to support the development and lifecycle management of medical devices. This position will take ownership of system-level requirements, architecture and technical decision-making while working closely with cross-functional engineering, quality, regulatory and clinical teams.




Key Responsibilities

Understand project objectives, technical constraints and user needs throughout the full product lifecycle.
Develop a strong understanding of existing medical device performance and behaviour to support lifecycle management and new product development.
Translate user and stakeholder needs into clear system-level requirements and design inputs.
Define and assess system architecture and system-level solutions alongside cross-functional teams.
Decompose system requirements into subsystem requirements, ensuring functional coverage and traceability.
Lead technical problem-solving using a structured, data-driven systems engineering approach.
Act as a system owner / subject matter expert, taking responsibility for decisions impacting the overall device.
Support Verification & Validation, Quality and Regulatory activities.
Work within established medical device design controls and Quality Management Systems (QMS).
Support project planning and technical milestones alongside Program and Project Management.
Lead and facilitate architectural and design decisions to support successful project delivery.
Identify and escalate technical risks and challenges appropriately.
Collaborate with Verification, Validation, Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Clinical, Commercial and Engineering teams.
Clearly communicate technical project status, risks and decisions to key stakeholders.




Experience & Skills

Strong Systems Engineering experience within medical devices / MedTech.
Experience across the medical device product development lifecycle.
Strong experience with system requirements, requirements decomposition and traceability.
Experience translating user needs into design inputs and technical requirements.
Knowledge of system architecture, system integration and system-level design.
Experience working within medical device design controls and a regulated QMS.
Experience supporting Verification & Validation and risk management activities.
Knowledge of ISO 13485 and ISO 14971 would be advantageous.
Strong technical problem-solving and decision-making capabilities.
Experience working across multidisciplinary engineering, quality, clinical and regulatory teams.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.




Ideal Profile

You will be an experienced Medical Device Systems Engineer who can take a holistic view of a product, understand how individual subsystems interact and take ownership of system-level technical decisions.

Experience across requirements engineering, system architecture, design controls, V&V, risk management and regulated medical device development would be particularly valuable.
