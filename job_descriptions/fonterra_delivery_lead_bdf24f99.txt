Position Description

Imagine Tomorrow with Us | Ata whakaarohia tō āpōpō

At Fonterra Te Mātāpuna, we want everyone to feel they can be themselves.

Whether you're early in your career or ready for your next step, Fonterra offers a unique opportunity to grow with a global dairy co-operative that values care, collaboration, and contribution.

Owned by New Zealand dairy farmers and supported by a dedicated team, we are all working together to create a better future. Imagine tomorrow with us, where your career can grow, and you can create positive change.

About us | He kupu mō mātou

We’re on a transformation journey within Fonterra IT, shifting our focus to delivering exceptional customer experiences and measurable business value.

A core enabler of this transformation is the adoption of a Products and Platforms operating model. We are maturing our ways of working to be strongly outcome‑driven, with a clear emphasis on accelerating speed‑to‑value.

Our IT operating model is built around enduring, business‑aligned teams that own the full product lifecycle design, build, run, and continuously improve leveraging automation wherever possible. Supporting this, our IT Practices operate across business units to establish consistent ways of working, enable flexible resource planning, and align on standards and best practices, fostering resilience, consistency, and quality at scale.

About the Opportunity | Mō tēnei tūranga

As a Delivery Lead, You Will Enable One Or More Product Or Platform Teams To Deliver Outcomes Predictably And Sustainably. You Will


Coordinate delivery from backlog through to production.
Improve flow of work, throughput and work-in-progress metrics.
Facilitate cross-team planning, synchronisation and Inspect and Adapt sessions.
Identify and resolve risks, dependencies and systemic impediments.
Align delivery with product roadmaps, OKRs and business priorities.
Provide clear visibility of progress, outcomes, risks and trade-offs.
Coach teams in Agile, Lean and promote DevSecOps practices.
Support release readiness, governance and continuous improvement.


About You | Mōu

You Are An Experienced Agile Delivery Leader Who Brings


5+ years’ experience leading delivery across multiple cross-functional teams.
Proven delivery coordination from backlog through to production.
Strong knowledge of Agile and Lean, applied pragmatically rather than dogmatically.
Experience facilitating scaled planning events and cross-team ceremonies.
Confidence managing dependencies, delivery risks and release coordination.
Hands-on use of delivery and flow metrics to improve predictability and quality.
Strong stakeholder management and the ability to influence without authority.
Experience coaching Scrum Masters, Product Owners and delivery teams.
Working knowledge of DevSecOps, CI/CD and modern delivery practices.
Proficiency with tools such as Jira, Azure DevOps, Confluence or Planview.
Clear communication across technical teams, business stakeholders and senior leaders.
A recognised Agile certification; a relevant tertiary qualification is preferred.
