At CogniVeu, we empower businesses to unlock the full potential of Cloud, Kubernetes, and DevOps technologies. We work with enterprises and startups globally, helping them build scalable, secure, and high-performing infrastructure. We’re looking for a DevOps Engineer who is passionate about automation, cloud-native technologies, and building reliable systems. If you thrive in solving complex challenges, love working with cutting-edge tools, and enjoy collaborating in a fast-paced environment - we’d love to talk to you.

What You’ll Do


Design, build, and manage scalable cloud infrastructure (AWS, GCP, Azure).
Implement and optimize CI/CD pipelines using GitOps practices (ArgoCD, GitLab CI/CD, GitHub Actions).
Manage Kubernetes clusters (GKE, EKS, AKS) including upgrades, scaling, and security hardening.
Deploy and manage service mesh (Istio, Linkerd) for secure service-to-service communication.
Set up and maintain monitoring, logging, and alerting with Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, and OpenTelemetry.
Automate infrastructure using Terraform, Terragrunt, and Ansible.
Optimize cloud costs, performance, and reliability across multi-cloud environments.
Collaborate with developers to improve deployment workflows and platform reliability.


What We’re Looking For


3+ years of hands-on experience in DevOps or Site Reliability Engineering.
Strong experience with Kubernetes and containerization (Docker).
Proficiency with Terraform (or similar IaC tools).
Experience with cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure).
Knowledge of CI/CD pipelines and GitOps workflows.
Familiarity with monitoring tools (Prometheus, Grafana) and logging/alerting systems.
Programming experience in Go or Python.


Benefits


Innovation-Driven and Global Projects
Remote-First Culture – Work from anywhere with flexible hours.
Continuous Learning – Access to certifications, conferences, and training programs.
Impactful Work – Your contributions will directly help businesses scale smarter and faster
