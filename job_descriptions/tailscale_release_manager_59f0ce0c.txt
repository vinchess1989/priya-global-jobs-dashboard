About The Role

We’re looking for a Release Manager to own how Tailscale ships our product, both internally and to customers. You’ll build and run our release coordination process and create the operational practices that coordinate product and engineering.

You’ll work closely with engineering leads, product managers, TPMs and support. At first you’ll focus on building release management from the ground up: setting the release cadence, defining roles, building tooling, and agreeing on norms with the teams involved in releases. Later, it will grow into creating processes around other parts of how product and engineering collaborate, like operational metrics.

You should understand how software gets shipped, including branching strategies, build pipelines, feature flags, and rollout mechanics. Your technical aptitude will allow you to design release processes that engineers trust and use.

Tailscale moves fast and your processes should help us maintain our speed. When a process works well, it is nearly invisible - it keeps the right people informed, stays out of engineers’ way, and gives the team a plan.

Key Responsibilities


Design, implement, and continuously improve Tailscale’s release management process: including release cadence, readiness criteria, rollout coordination, and go/no-go decisions
Build and maintain the tooling, templates, runbooks, and documentation that make release processes repeatable and low-friction
Partner with engineering leads and product managers to surface risk early and drive resolution before it becomes a crisis
Define and track operational metrics that reflect the health of our release practices (e.g., release frequency, change failure rate)
Keep releases on schedule by facilitating cross-team planning rituals that keep engineering and product coordinated
Identify and drive systemic improvements to how we operate, using data and judgment to prioritize what actually matters


What We Are Looking For


5+ years in engineering operations, release management, or a closely related role at a software company
Direct experience building or significantly improving release management processes
Technical fluency: you understand software development deeply enough to have credible conversations with engineers about build systems, deployment pipelines, branching strategies, and production operations
Strong instincts for what process is worth having and what’s just overhead, and optimizing process for trust and adoption
Comfort with ambiguity and the ability to build structure from scratch


Nice to Have


Experience with both client and server software release processes
Experience at a developer tools, infrastructure, or security-focused SaaS company
Familiarity with DORA metrics and how to use them to drive meaningful improvement
Experience with feature flag systems, progressive rollouts, or canary deployments as release tools
Experience scaling operational practices through periods of rapid team or product growth
Hands-on experience with Tailscale at work or home
