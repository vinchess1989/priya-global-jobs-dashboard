Job Description



Role Purpose

Manage all project information, documentation, correspondence, and records to ensure accurate document control, governance compliance, and traceability throughout the laboratory fit-out project lifecycle.



Key Responsibilities



Establish and administer the project's document management system, workflows, registers, and approval processes.
Maintain control of drawings, specifications, technical submittals, RFIs, laboratory equipment schedules, commissioning records, and close-out documentation.
Coordinate document reviews, approvals, transmittals, and distribution to project stakeholders.
Ensure revision control and document traceability across all project phases.
Support governance reporting, audit readiness, and quality management requirements.
Compile and manage handover documentation, O&M manuals, commissioning records, validation support documents, and project close-out deliverables.
Facilitate secure management of controlled documents in accordance with client requirements and project protocols.


Qualifications


Experience supporting laboratory, pharmaceutical, healthcare, life science, or other regulated environments is highly desirable.
Familiarity with documentation requirements associated with commissioning, qualification, validation, and compliance-driven projects.
