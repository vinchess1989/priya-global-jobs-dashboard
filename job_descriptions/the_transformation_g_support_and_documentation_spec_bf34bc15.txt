Support & Documentation Specialist


Sydney | Hybrid (2 days - Monday and Thursday per week in office) | Up to $110,000 + Super

Are you the type of person who spots a broken process, builds a better framework, and documents it so nobody has to ask the same question twice?

We are partnering with one of Australia's leading HR technology businesses, helping organisations streamline pre-employment screening through automated reference checks, background screening, ID verification, right-to-work checks and qualification verification solutions. As the business continues to grow and invest heavily in AI-powered support, we're looking for a Support & Documentation Specialist to take ownership of our knowledge ecosystem and help shape the future of customer support.

This isn't a traditional support position and it isn't a pure technical writing role.

You'll sit at the intersection of Customer Support, Product, Engineering and Knowledge Management, owning the documentation and knowledge base that powers both customer self-service and AI-driven support experiences.

What You'll Be Doing
Own the Knowledge Base

Maintain and continuously improve customer-facing and internal documentation
Create and update user guides, support resources, process documentation and knowledge articles
Identify documentation gaps and proactively prioritise improvements
Ensure knowledge content is structured for both human users and AI systems
Drive documentation governance, consistency and quality standards across the business
Provide Level 2 Support

Act as the first human escalation point when AI support cannot resolve an issue
Investigate and resolve more complex customer enquiries
Support customers through email and chat channels
Partner with the wider Support team to ensure excellent customer outcomes
Surface recurring customer pain points and turn insights into better documentation
Work Across the Business

Collaborate closely with Product and Engineering teams on new releases and changes
Develop training materials and onboarding resources
Support continuous improvement initiatives across support and documentation functions
Help improve the effectiveness of AI-powered support by maintaining high-quality knowledge resources
What We're Looking For


You'll likely come from a SaaS support, customer enablement, application support or knowledge management background and enjoy turning complexity into clarity.

We're particularly interested in people who have:

Experience creating customer-facing documentation, knowledge articles or support content
SaaS or software support experience
Excellent written communication skills
The ability to simplify technical concepts for different audiences
Strong organisational and time-management skills
Experience working independently and prioritising their own workload
High attention to detail and a continuous improvement mindset
Confidence using modern AI tools to improve productivity and content quality
Experience with platforms such as Freshdesk, Zendesk or similar support tools
Why Join?
Genuine Ownership


You'll own a critical function rather than simply execute tasks. This role is ideal for someone who enjoys building structure, improving systems and influencing how knowledge is managed across a growing technology business.

AI-First Environment


Work with tools including Claude, AI-powered support solutions and Synthesia to create scalable, high-quality customer experiences.

Product Exposure


Partner closely with Product and Engineering teams, gaining visibility into new releases, roadmap initiatives and strategic business projects.

Strong Foundation


You'll inherit a mature documentation framework and build upon it, rather than starting from scratch. Approximately 90% of the documentation already exists, creating a great platform for continuous improvement.

Flexible Hybrid Working


Enjoy a hybrid working model with just two days onsite per week in the office.

The Ideal Person


Success in this role comes down to ownership. You're someone who naturally identifies opportunities for improvement, creates frameworks where needed, communicates proactively, and enjoys operating with a high degree of autonomy. Rather than waiting to be told what to update, you're already thinking about what could be better and how to make it happen.

If you're excited by the idea of combining support expertise, knowledge management and AI-powered innovation in a growing SaaS business, we'd love to hear from you.
