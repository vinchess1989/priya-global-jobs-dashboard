Quality Engineer




Athenry, Galway




Growing Company | Excellent Career Progression | Fully On-Site




This is a fantastic opportunity for a Quality Engineer or Senior Quality Engineer to join a collaborative, down-to-earth team in a business that has experienced significant growth in recent years.




With a strong culture of promoting from within, the company offers excellent long-term stability and genuine career progression.




This role will suit a Quality Engineer or Senior Quality Engineer that is looking for a hands-on varied role offering a mix of office-based work and hands-on involvement on the production floor.




The role

The ideal candidate will be approachable, practical and genuinely collaborative who enjoys rolling up their sleeves, collaborating with colleagues and adding value..
Friendly, supportive and collaborative working environment.
Varied, hands-on role where your contribution will make a difference.




Key responsibilities

Supporting CAPA, non-conformance investigations and root cause analysis.
Working closely with production teams to troubleshoot quality issues and drive improvements.
Supporting QMS improvements and ensuring regulatory compliance.
Participating in internal and external audits.
Supporting validation, supplier quality and risk management activities.
Providing quality support for new product introductions and transfers.




Requirements

Previous experience in the medical device industry is essential.
Qualification in Science, Engineering or a related discipline.
Ideally 3–4 years' Quality Engineering experience.
Knowledge of ISO 13485
Strong problem-solving, communication and organisational skills.
Experience in auditing, Lean or new product introductions would be advantageous but is not essential.




Please contact me for a confidential discussion or to discuss further.
