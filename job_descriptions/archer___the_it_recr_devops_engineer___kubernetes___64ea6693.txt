Docker, Kubernetes & Infrastructure
End-to-End Exposure Across DevOps, SRE & Operations
Broad Exposure & Real Responsibility | Automation & Technical Variety




Not every DevOps role gives you visibility across the entire technology stack. This opportunity puts you close to the platforms behind software delivery, giving you exposure to everything from containerised infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines to databases, monitoring, networking and system reliability.




You’ll join an established telecommunications organisation investing in its technology platforms and software delivery capabilities. You’ll collaborate with DevOps Engineers, Developers and QA specialists, contributing to the reliability, performance and continuous improvement of business-critical systems.




This is a broad, hands-on position where there are no rigid boundaries between infrastructure, development, QA and operations. You’ll have the opportunity to understand how the different components connect, investigate issues from end to end and introduce automation and improvements that make a real difference. If you enjoy troubleshooting, learning new technologies and having genuine visibility across a technical environment, this is an opportunity worth exploring.




You will:

Maintain and optimise Docker and Kubernetes environments, ensuring platform availability, stability and performance.
Monitor systems, investigate incidents and troubleshoot infrastructure, application and connectivity issues.
Maintain databases including MariaDB, MySQL, Oracle and PostgreSQL, alongside load balancers and SSL certificates.
Support and improve CI/CD processes using GitLab CI/CD, collaborating with Development and QA teams.
Use monitoring tools such as Grafana and Prometheus, while introducing automation through Python and Bash.




Skills / Experience:

3+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, Systems Engineering or a similar operational role.
Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD and Linux-based infrastructure.
Good understanding of infrastructure operations, troubleshooting and networking. Database administration, monitoring, load balancing and scripting experience will be highly advantageous.




Salary / Remuneration: 45,000€ - 50,000€ + benefits
