Quality & Regulatory Manager (QA/RA) | Medical Devices

📍 London (Office-Based) | Monday-Friday

💰 Competitive Salary + Benefits




Are you an experienced Quality & Regulatory professional looking for an opportunity to lead regulatory strategy and quality excellence within an innovative medical device business?

We are seeking a Quality & Regulatory Manager (QA/RA) to take ownership of our Quality Management System and Regulatory Affairs activities, ensuring compliance with global medical device regulations while supporting ambitious growth plans across international markets.




Reporting directly to the CFO, this is a highly visible leadership role where you'll act as the company's subject matter expert for quality and regulatory compliance, driving continuous improvement and playing a key role in bringing innovative products to patients worldwide.




What You'll Be Doing

✅ Act as the Person Responsible for Regulatory Compliance (PRRC) under EU MDR 2017/745.

✅ Lead the company's transition from Class I to Class IIa medical devices, ensuring successful MDR compliance.

✅ Maintain and continually improve the ISO 13485 Quality Management System.

✅ Manage technical documentation, product registrations and regulatory submissions across the UK, EU, US and Canada.

✅ Lead internal audits, CAPA activities, complaint handling, vigilance reporting and post-market surveillance programmes.

✅ Analyse quality metrics and trends to drive continuous improvement initiatives.

✅ Develop, mentor and lead the QA/RA team while managing departmental priorities and resources.




What We're Looking For

✔ Degree in Life Sciences, Biomedical Science, Engineering or a related discipline.

✔ Minimum 5 years' management experience within a quality and/or regulatory role in the medical device industry.

✔ Strong knowledge of EU MDR, UK MDR, FDA and Health Canada regulatory frameworks.

✔ Experience managing ISO 13485-compliant QMS environments.

✔ Proven track record in regulatory submissions, technical documentation, audits, CAPA and post-market surveillance.

✔ Previous leadership experience with the ability to build and develop high-performing teams.

Desirable Experience

➕ Dermatology, wound care or skin-health products.

➕ Microbiology qualifications or practical microbiology experience.




Why Join?

This is an opportunity to make a genuine impact within a growing medical device company at a pivotal stage of its journey. You'll have the autonomy to shape regulatory strategy, influence business decisions and lead critical projects that support both compliance and international expansion.

If you're looking for a role where your expertise will directly influence the success of innovative healthcare products, we'd love to hear from you.
