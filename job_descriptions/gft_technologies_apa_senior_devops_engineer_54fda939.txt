About GFT

 

GFT Technologies is an AI-centric global digital transformation company. We design advanced data and AI transformation solutions, modernize technology architectures and develop next-generation core systems for industry leaders in Banking, Insurance, Manufacturing and Robotics. Partnering closely with our clients, we push boundaries to unlock their full potential. With deep industry expertise, cutting-edge technology, and a strong partner ecosystem, GFT delivers responsible AI-centric solutions that combine engineering excellence, high-performance delivery, and cost efficiency. Our team of 12,000+ technology experts operate in 20+ countries worldwide offering career opportunities at the forefront of software innovation.

 

 

Overview 

 

We are seeking an experienced Senior DevOps / Production Operations Engineer to support the production operations and operational readiness of a next-generation Stablecoin and Digital Assets Platform. 

 

This role is focused on ensuring the platform operates securely, reliably, and efficiently in production. The successful candidate will work closely with product, engineering, infrastructure, security, compliance, and operations teams to support production environments, improve operational processes, manage incidents, and drive platform reliability. 

 

The role is based in Hong Kong and will work directly with the CTO and key business stakeholders. 

 

Key Responsibilities 

 

Production Operations 

Own day-to-day production support activities across platform environments. 
Monitor platform health, performance, availability, and operational KPIs. 
Coordinate incident management, root cause analysis, and resolution activities. 
Ensure timely communication and escalation of production issues. 

 

Operational Readiness 

Establish and improve production support processes, runbooks, operational procedures, and support models. 
Drive release readiness, change management, and deployment coordination. 
Ensure support teams have appropriate documentation and operational knowledge. 

 

Platform Reliability & Monitoring 

Implement and maintain monitoring, alerting, logging, and observability capabilities. 
Identify reliability risks and proactively drive remediation activities. 
Improve platform resilience, availability, recovery, and operational maturity. 

 

Infrastructure & Cloud Operations 

Support cloud infrastructure and platform environments. 
Manage deployment pipelines, environment configuration, and infrastructure automation. 
Work closely with engineering teams to improve operational stability. 

 

Security & Compliance 

Support operational controls required for a regulated financial platform. 
Participate in audits, security reviews, disaster recovery testing, and operational risk assessments. 
Ensure operational processes align with security and compliance requirements. 

 

Stakeholder Coordination 

Act as a bridge between Engineering, Product, Operations, Compliance, Risk, and external partners. 
Coordinate operational activities across multiple teams. 
Provide clear operational reporting and status updates to management.

Profile description:



 Candidate Profile 

The ideal candidate: 

Is operationally minded rather than purely development focused. 
Enjoys running and improving production platforms. 
Is comfortable handling incidents and production issues. 
Takes ownership and drives issues to resolution. 
Can coordinate across technology, product, operations, compliance, and risk teams. 
Thinks proactively about reliability, resilience, and operational excellence. 

 

Required Experience 

7+ years of experience in DevOps, Production Support, SRE, Platform Operations, or Infrastructure Engineering. 
Experience supporting critical production systems in financial services, fintech, payments, trading, digital banking, or regulated environments. 
Experience with cloud platforms (AWS preferred). 
Strong experience in monitoring, observability, incident management, and operational support. 
Experience with CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure automation. 
Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving capabilities. 

 

Preferred Experience 

Experience supporting blockchain, digital asset, stablecoin, payments, or financial market infrastructure platforms. 
Experience with Kubernetes, containerized environments, and cloud-native architectures. 
Experience with Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practices. 
Experience with security operations, audit readiness, and regulated environments. 
Familiarity with operational processes supporting 24x7 services.
