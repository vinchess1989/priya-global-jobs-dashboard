Senior Recruitment Consultant - Life Sciences - Quality Assurance (Contract)

Documentation and Compliance Specialist - 12 Month Contract - Fully Onsite




Are you a quality-minded professional looking to drive compliance and standardisation in life sciences manufacturing?




We are seeking a Documentation and Compliance Specialist to oversee critical documentation for site technology transfers and New Product Introduction (NPI) projects. In this role, you will take full ownership of the technical document ecosystem, ensure robust lifecycle traceability, and serve as a central reference point for audit readiness across complex tech transfer streams.




Core Responsibilities

Document Lifecycle Ownership: Oversee the creation, review, and archiving of technology transfer and NPI records (including transfer strategies, validation protocols, and summary reports) in strict alignment with site templates and document control standards.
Change Management & Routing: Lead change control workflows, track document approval pathways, and maintain end-to-end traceability against baseline source technical data.
Gate Review Support: Drive documentation completeness and accuracy checks ahead of formal project phase-gate reviews and technology handovers.
Status & Metrics Reporting: Maintain transfer logs, execution trackers, and compliance dashboards to provide clear visibility for program leadership.
Audit & Inspection Readiness: Function as the key documentation point of contact during internal QA reviews and external regulatory inspections.

Candidate Profile & Qualifications

Industry Experience: 3+ years in documentation control, technical writing, or quality assurance within a regulated pharmaceutical, biopharmaceutical, or medical device environment.
Technical Writing & Precision: Demonstrated ability to draft, edit, and review complex technical records with strong attention to regulatory detail.
QMS Expertise: Hands-on experience navigating Quality Management Systems, electronic document control platforms (eDMS), and change control procedures.
Stakeholder Coordination: Strong organizational and interpersonal skills to keep cross-functional teams aligned on documentation timelines and deliverables.




Preferred Qualifications

Direct experience supporting tech transfers, NPI, or equipment/process validation campaigns.
Knowledge of sterile manufacturing standards, including EU GMP Annex 1 principles.
Experience establishing or scaling documentation templates and workflows for newly established site functions.
