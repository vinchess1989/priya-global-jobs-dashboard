Position Summary

The Quality Systems Technician is responsible for the execution of a variety of tasks with a primary responsibility for management of Documentation Compliance Manager (DCM) and archiving in relation to QMS systems. This role is key in maintaining and updating the documentation system activities throughout the site. While ensuring management’s awareness of their performance. The execution of all applicable requirements related to QMS, Corporate requirements, Regulatory compliance, and applicable Grifols Policies or Procedures

What You Will Do


Management of Documentation Compliance Manager (DCM) system
Provide Reports to site in relation to Quality Management System (QMS) as required.
Management of Documentation Archive for QMS documentation.
Provide regular reports on site’s metrics for periodic review to appropriate management
Management and ownership of
Documentation Compliance Manager (DCM)
Applicable training requirements (i.e. GMP introductory and refresher training)
Communicate with cross functional departments sites’ compliance and documentation systems issues
Supporting the coordination of self-inspection system in GWWO, where applicable
Provide support ensuring external audit readiness and during external audits and corporate internal audits
Identifies and shares "Best Practices"
Participate in investigations of quality system issues and drives CAPA implementation to ensure CAPA findings, risks, recommendations, and outcomes are appropriate and are clearly documented and communicated


Responsibilities include managing and coordinating


Documentation Compliance Manager (DCM)
Other activities related to QMS.



Other additional duties may be assigned by the Quality Systems & Compliance Management, if required

What You Will Bring


Bachelor’s degree (B.S.) or equivalent in pharmaceutical or science-based equivalent
1-2+ years’ experience in the pharmaceutical and/or biotechnology-based industry
Experience supporting quality systems activities such as document control, change control, deviations, CAPA, training administration, audits, and quality records management.
Knowledge of cGMP requirements, quality management systems, and applicable regulatory standards.
Good working knowledge of GMP regulations and site SOPs/protocols.
Highly organized, detail-oriented, and results-driven professional with strong technical writing, analytical, and communication skills. Demonstrates expertise in process improvement and procedure development, proficiency in Microsoft Office applications (Word, Excel, and PowerPoint), and the ability to work proactively both independently and collaboratively.
Flexible and adaptable, with the capacity to manage changing priorities and perform effectively in a fast-paced GMP environment.
