Business Analyst – AI Use Cases & QA Validation

Experience: 4+ Years

Job Role: Business Analyst

Focus Areas: Business Analysis, AI Use Case Development, QA & Testing, UAT

Job Description

We are looking for an experienced Business Analyst with strong business analysis capabilities, hands-on QA/testing exposure, and experience in defining and validating AI-driven use cases. The ideal candidate will bridge business requirements, AI solution design, and quality assurance to ensure that AI and automation solutions meet business objectives and functional expectations.

Key Responsibilities

Business Requirements & Analysis: Gather, analyze, and document business requirements; translate them into functional specifications, user stories, use cases, and clearly defined acceptance criteria.
AI Use Case Definition: Identify business opportunities for AI and automation, develop relevant AI use cases, define expected outcomes, and establish validation criteria aligned with business requirements.
QA & Functional Testing: Leverage QA experience to prepare test scenarios and test cases, perform functional and integration testing, identify defects, and validate solution behavior against business expectations.
AI Solution Validation: Evaluate AI-generated responses for accuracy, relevance, consistency, completeness, and alignment with defined business requirements and expected outcomes.
User Acceptance Testing (UAT): Coordinate UAT activities with business stakeholders, validate end-to-end workflows, track issues, and support defect resolution and business sign-off.
Documentation & Governance: Maintain business requirements documents, functional specifications, test documentation, and validation reports while ensuring adherence to platform governance standards.
Stakeholder Collaboration: Work closely with business teams, developers, QA engineers, and AI/automation teams to ensure successful implementation and adoption of solutions.

Required Skills & Experience

5–8+ years of experience in Business Analysis, with exposure to QA, functional testing, or software quality assurance.
Experience writing business use cases, user stories, functional requirements, and acceptance criteria.
Understanding of AI-driven solutions and the ability to define, assess, and validate AI use cases.
Hands-on experience in functional testing, integration testing, test case preparation, defect tracking, and UAT coordination.
Ability to assess AI outputs against business rules, expected results, and defined acceptance criteria.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, documentation, and stakeholder management skills.
Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments is preferred.

Key Competencies

Business Analysis | AI Use Case Development | QA & Functional Testing | AI Response Validation | Requirements Management | User Stories & Acceptance Criteria | UAT | Test Documentation | Stakeholder Management
