Job Description

DePuy Synthes is recruiting for a Technology Manager, MAKE MES-IoT Systems

Locations: Raritan, New Jersey, West Chester, Pennsylvania, Palm Beach Gardens, Florida, Raynham, Massachusetts, Warsaw, Indiana, Loughbeg, Ringaskiddy, Ireland; Leeds, Beeston, England.

Job Overview

The Technology Lead, MAKE MES-IoT Systems is a hands-on functional engineering role supporting the design, implementation, adoption, and continuous improvement of MES, IoT, and related digital manufacturing solutions. Working directly with manufacturing and engineering teams, the role connects shop-floor processes and user requirements with fit-for-purpose technology solutions.

The role applies a process- and product-oriented mindset to maximize adoption of technology solutions and ensure digital capabilities are usable, sustainable, and connected to the needs of manufacturing operations.

Key Responsibilities


Partner directly with manufacturing and engineering teams to understand shop-floor processes, operational requirements, pain points, and opportunities for digital enablement.
Translate manufacturing and user requirements into functional designs and requirements for MES, IoT, and associated manufacturing technology solutions.
Support the integrated build and implementation of solutions so that end-to-end functionality works as designed and aligns with applicable requirements.
Liaise cross-functionally to identify and develop Functional Engineering solutions that address specific manufacturing and business needs.
Act as a Functional Engineering domain resource for technology management capabilities, scope definition, solution extensions, and enablement through final delivery.
Support configuration, testing, release planning, deployment, stabilization, and continuous improvement of manufacturing technology solutions.
Help maximize user adoption by working closely with shop-floor users, incorporating feedback, resolving practical implementation issues, and improving the usability of technology-enabled processes.
Support MES capabilities such as electronic manufacturing records, traceability, manufacturing workflow execution, manufacturing data collection, and process controls.
Support IoT and connected-manufacturing capabilities including equipment connectivity, data collection, equipment monitoring, and manufacturing visibility.
Partner with Manufacturing, Engineering, Quality, Automation/OT, Supply Chain, and Technology stakeholders to ensure solutions meet operational needs.
Apply a continuous-improvement mindset to identify opportunities to simplify, standardize, and improve manufacturing processes through technology.
Communicate appropriate Quality, Safety, Engineering, and compliance expectations during solution development and implementation.
Create and maintain functional and technical documentation supporting solutions, testing, implementation, and ongoing operation.
Coach and support more junior colleagues in relevant Functional Engineering techniques, processes, and responsibilities.


Qualifications

Education


Required: Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline.
Preferred: Education or advanced training in process engineering, industrial engineering, manufacturing engineering, or a closely related discipline.


Required

Experience and Skills


5+ years of experience in manufacturing, process engineering, industrial engineering, manufacturing technology, or a related discipline.
Familiarity with manufacturing/MAKE processes and shop-floor operations.
Experience partnering with engineers, manufacturing users, and cross-functional stakeholders to define and implement solutions.
Understanding of MES, manufacturing systems, digital manufacturing, or comparable production technology.
Ability to translate business and manufacturing needs into functional requirements and practical solution designs.
Experience supporting solution testing, implementation, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder-collaboration skills.
Demonstrated focus on technology adoption and effective integration of digital solutions into operational processes.


Preferred


Experience with MES capabilities such as electronic manufacturing records, production traceability, manufacturing workflows, or manufacturing data collection.
Familiarity with Industrial IoT, equipment connectivity, real-time manufacturing data, historians, or manufacturing analytics.
Experience in medical device, pharmaceutical, life sciences, or another regulated manufacturing environment.
Experience with Lean Manufacturing and/or Six Sigma methodologies.
Experience supporting smart manufacturing, connected-factory, or digital manufacturing initiatives.


Other


Travel: Up to 10%.
Language: English proficiency required.
Certifications: Lean and/or Six Sigma certification preferred.
