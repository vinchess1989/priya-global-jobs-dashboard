We are looking for a Senior Veeva Consultant with extensive experience in Veeva Vault and Regulated Information Management (RIM). You will play a crucial role in managing system changes and validation documentation within a GxP-regulated environment. This remote position offers the opportunity to collaborate with business stakeholders and Digital QA teams to ensure compliance with governance and quality standards. If you are passionate about contributing to the pharmaceutical industry and have a proactive, structured approach to problem-solving, this opportunity may be the right fit for you.

Your tasks


Managing configuration and validation documentation for system changes in RIM
Conducting impact assessments and change analysis to ensure compliance with GxP regulations
Gathering requirements and aligning with stakeholders to design scalable system solutions
Overseeing the end-to-end management of system changes, from solution design to implementation and validation
Collaborating with Digital QA and Digital Enablement teams to translate requirements into effective system solutions
Maintaining familiarity with Veeva General Releases and handling release impact assessments
Driving tasks independently while adhering to governance and quality standards
Ensuring the integrity and compliance of validated systems in a regulated environment


Requirements


Minimum 4 years of experience in system analysis or consulting within the pharmaceutical industry
Proven experience with Veeva RIM
Strong understanding of GxP regulations and experience working in a GxP-regulated environment
Veeva Certified Administrator certification is highly desirable
Solid background in impact assessments and change management processes
Fluent English


Nice to have


Experience with Jira Xray for digital validation
Familiarity with Veeva Quality and Veeva General Releases
