Job Description:

The Specialist/Senior Analyst - Solution Engineering supports the analysis and translation of business requirements into technical specifications. This role collaborates with Engineering and Product teams to develop and implement solutions that meet user needs. The role contributes to project planning and execution, ensuring deliverables align with project timelines and objectives.

You will work on a daily basis with the solution architects, development, QA, PM and SM team members to solution products that customers love. You will also have to coordinate core solutions with the Product and Technology Teams.




Duties & Responsibilities:

Complete High-Level Analysis of the CRs submitted by the airline
Submit CR to the Product Intake process for approval
BRD and functional user stories creation, in case of Product acceptance, and creation of AHA item to add it to Product backlog
BRD, functional/development user stories, and solution approach documentation in case of customer specific (no core)
Work at the same time on projects related to three releases:
Support airline with current release in UAT
Support Accelya development and QA Team with current release in implementation phase (SIT)
Create BRDs and functional user stories for the projects targeted in the upcoming release before the last sprint of the current release
Support airline with any inquiry related to future CRs (Pre-CR inquiries)
Handover BRD and user stories to Accelya development and QA teams
Initial handover for development and QA estimations for release planning
Second handover to QA after Business analyst sanity test, once implemented
Training/Demo UAT release deliverables to the airline QA.
Internal Release Demo to Accelya team.
In cases of airlines with solutions with third party vendors, BA must participate in sessions to align the solution with the other parties
Support PMs and SMs in the release and sprint planning
Follow up with Product Owners regarding delivered solutions from Product
Support PreSales team by answering questions about functionality.
Work with NDC Team to update schemas, as needed.
Address queries from Onboarding and Service Management teams.




Knowledge, Experience & Skills:

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Excellent communication and facilitation
Understanding of software development lifecycle (SDLC)
Basic technical knowledge (APIs, databases, system flows)
Stakeholder management
AWS platform is preferred.
Practical AI fluency: can use AI as a thinking partner to accelerate requirement elicitation, analysis mapping, and documentation, while maintaining strong human judgment over final outcomes. Ability to iterate on prompts, validate outputs (accuracy, completeness, assumptions), and clearly communicate AI‑assisted insights to stakeholders.
