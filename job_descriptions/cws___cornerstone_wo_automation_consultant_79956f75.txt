Role: Automation Consultant




Location: Singapore




Experience: 10 yrs+




About the job

We are hiring an Automation Consultant (Technical Lead, Automation Track) for a financial project in Singapore. In the role, you will lead the automation track of a multi-year patch management program, using Ansible Automation Platform (AAP) as the orchestration layer. It decides what gets patched, in what order and under which change record, and it proves that the patching happened. You will design the architecture, lead the engineers building it, and hand a working framework to the client’s own team.




Overview: The role is for a self-motivated technical leader with deep Ansible Automation Platform expertise and experience delivering enterprise automation in regulated environments. You will be extensively involved in architecture, design documentation, build leadership, testing and knowledge transfer. You must be able to work with a multi-disciplinary team and adapt to changing priorities.




What you’ll do:

Own the Ansible framework architecture: one workflow (discover, prioritize, approve, sequence, execute, verify) and one evidence trail across every platform
Author framework sections of the high-level and low-level designs, and support the Principal Automation Consultant at the Architecture Review Board
Shape the intended-state approach for agentless targets (NetBox or policy-as-code)
Lead the Senior Automation Engineer, Automation Engineer and ServiceNow Engineer
Deliver workflow templates for the ServiceNow change gate, owner approvals, five-ring promotion logic and evidence to Splunk
Set standards for execution environments, collections and content-as-code in GitHub Enterprise and JFrog, aligned to client coding standards
Drive the first agentless canaries: ESXi host evacuation and patch, and a network platform firmware update
Build Robot Framework test coverage, and own component, integration and security testing for automation content
Coordinate with the Tanium security team on integration points and shared approval paths
Work with platform owners on cluster-aware sequencing, rollback and validation criteria
Mentor client automation engineers until they author playbooks in the framework unaided, and contribute to SOPs, recorded training and supervised knowledge transfer
Build reusable patterns for later tiers: databases, middleware, storage, firmware, AIX and Solaris




Technical requirements:

Area Requirements

1. Framework architecture- Design a single orchestration workflow (discover, prioritize, approve, sequence, execute, verify) with one audit-ready evidence trail across all platforms. Author HLD/LLD sections suitable for architecture and change review boards.

2. AAP platform- Workflow templates, execution environments, automation mesh across segmented networks, RBAC and credential plugins.

3. ServiceNow integration- AAP integration with ServiceNow Change and CMDB. Build the change gate, owner approvals and five-ring promotion logic, with evidence sent to Splunk.

4. Agentless patching- Intended-state approach (NetBox or policy-as-code). First canaries: ESXi host evacuation and patch, and a network platform firmware update. Reusable patterns for later tiers (databases, middleware, storage, firmware, AIX, Solaris).

5. Patch orchestration at scale- Rolling and cluster-aware sequencing, maintenance windows, rollback and validation criteria, agreed with platform owners. Integration points and shared approval paths with Tanium.

6. Content and testing- Content-as-code in GitHub Enterprise and JFrog, aligned to client coding standards. Robot Framework coverage plus component, integration and security testing.

7. Handover- Mentor client engineers to author playbooks unaided. Contribute to SOPs, recorded training and supervised knowledge transfer.




What you’ll need:

10+ years in infrastructure automation, with 8+ years of hands-on Ansible in production
Deep Ansible Automation Platform expertise: workflow templates, execution environments, automation mesh across segmented networks, RBAC and credential plugins
Prior technical lead or architect role on an enterprise automation program
Python, Git, REST APIs and CI pipelines
Experience integrating AAP with ServiceNow Change and CMDB
Patch or change orchestration at scale: rolling and cluster-aware sequencing, maintenance windows, rollback
Delivery in a regulated industry (financial services preferred): change control, separation of duties, audit-ready evidence
Clear written design documentation suitable for architecture and change review boards
Preferred: Red Hat certifications (RHCE, EX374, EX467)
Preferred: Agentless patching for VMware ESXi and vCenter, Nutanix and network operating systems (Cisco, F5, Palo Alto, Juniper)
Preferred: CyberArk, PingID, Splunk and Datadog
Preferred: NetBox or Nautobot as a source of truth
