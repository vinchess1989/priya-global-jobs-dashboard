DevSecOps Engineer – Salesforce CRM Platform, Release & Security Engineering

Role Purpose

To manage secure release engineering, environment controls, deployment processes, and DevSecOps practices for Salesforce CRM and its supporting components, ensuring stable and secure change delivery.

Key Responsibilities


Establish and maintain deployment, release, and environment management practices for Salesforce CRM.
Support CI/CD or equivalent release controls for configuration, code, and integration changes.
Embed security checks and controls into the application delivery lifecycle.
Support secure handling of secrets, configuration, access controls, and release approvals.
Work with engineers and architects to ensure that platform and integration changes meet security and operational requirements.
Support remediation tracking, vulnerability handling, evidence preparation, and audit/security review readiness.
Maintain deployment runbooks, rollback plans, and release procedures.
Assist with incident analysis where release, security, or configuration issues may be contributing factors.


Requirements


3–5 years of relevant experience in DevSecOps, release engineering, environment management, or secure delivery practices.
Experience in DevOps / DevSecOps / release engineering / platform operations.
Good understanding of secure SDLC, deployment controls, and environment governance.
Familiarity with SaaS platforms and integration-driven enterprise systems.
Strong awareness of security and operational control principles.


Preferred


Experience with Salesforce deployment processes and supporting cloud components.
Experience in regulated or government environments.
Relevant cloud or security certifications.
