Job Title: Quality Control Engineer (EN ISO 13485)

Location: Pune

Experience: 3–5 years

Industry Preference: Medical Devices/ Equipment manufacturer




Role Overview

We are looking for a Quality Assurance Engineer with hands-on experience in EN ISO 13485 standards to strengthen our quality systems in manufacturing. The ideal candidate should have practical exposure to audits, documentation, and process compliance in a regulated environment.




Key Responsibilities

Implement, maintain, and improve the Quality Management System (QMS) as per EN ISO 13485
Ensure compliance across manufacturing, incoming, in-process, and final inspection stages
Prepare and manage quality documentation: SOPs, work instructions, records, validation protocols
Handle internal and external audits, including EN ISO 13485 certification audits
Drive CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Actions) and root cause analysis
Coordinate with production, and supply chain teams for quality improvements
Monitor and analyze quality KPIs (rejections, defects, complaints)
Ensure traceability and regulatory compliance for products
Support new product development from a quality perspective




Required Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in engineering (Biomedical Engineering/Mechanical / Production / Industrial)
4-5 years of experience in Quality Assurance
Minimum 2 years continuous experience in EN ISO 13485 environment
Hands-on experience in ISO audits (internal/external)




Preferred Skills

Strong knowledge of QMS documentation and implementation
Experience in medical devices / healthcare products manufacturing
Familiarity with tools like FMEA, Control Plans, SPC, 7 QC tools
Good understanding of regulatory requirements and compliance for EN ISO 13485
Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
