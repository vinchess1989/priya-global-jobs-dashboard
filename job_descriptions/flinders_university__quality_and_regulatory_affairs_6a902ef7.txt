About the Role

Join FORGE Medical Devices at Flinders University and play an important role in supporting the development of innovative medical technologies, helping turn research and ideas into market-ready solutions. Working across FORGE and its industry client projects, you will contribute to the effective management and continuous improvement of quality systems, while providing practical regulatory and technical support to ensure medical devices meet relevant standards and regulatory requirements.

In this role, you will:

Support the implementation and continuous improvement of FORGE's Quality Management System, including quality documentation, audits, risk management and corrective actions.
Research and interpret medical device regulations, standards and requirements, contributing to regulatory strategies, submissions and technical documentation.
Work with FORGE clients to develop fit-for-purpose quality and regulatory documentation, supporting medical device development and pathways towards commercialisation.
Collaborate with researchers, engineers, industry partners and other stakeholders to strengthen quality and regulatory practices and support the successful delivery of client projects.

This position makes a significant contribution to the University's strategic plan to changing 10 million lives, Every One Matters, by 2035 directly contributing to Research that matters through supporting the translation of innovative medical device research into safe, compliant and commercially viable solutions.

About You

You are a detail-oriented and solutions-focused professional with an interest in medical device quality and regulatory affairs. You enjoy working with technical information, interpreting regulatory requirements and developing clear, accurate documentation. With strong organisational and communication skills, you are comfortable managing competing priorities, working with diverse stakeholders and contributing to continuous improvement.

You will bring:

Relevant qualifications and experience in quality management, regulatory affairs, medical devices or a related technical or scientific discipline, with knowledge of standards such as ISO 13485 and ISO 14971.
Experience preparing, reviewing or maintaining quality, regulatory, scientific or technical documentation, with the ability to interpret and apply relevant standards and requirements.
Strong analytical, problem-solving and organisational skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities, meet deadlines and exercise sound professional judgement.
Well-developed communication and relationship-building skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with technical and non-technical stakeholders and contribute to continuous improvement in a multidisciplinary environment.

FORGE Medical Devices

FORGE Medical Devices is a Flinders University initiative helping transform promising medical device ideas into real-world solutions. Building on nearly two decades of experience through the Medical Device Partnering Program (MDPP), FORGE brings together researchers, clinicians, industry, manufacturers and end-users to accelerate innovation and support the development of technologies with commercial and clinical potential.

Delivered through Flinders Factory of the Future, FORGE provides a connected pathway for innovators and emerging companies-from early-stage concept validation through to product development, manufacturing readiness, quality systems, regulatory planning and trial-ready preparation. Through its programs, FORGE is helping great ideas become products that improve lives and deliver meaningful impact.
