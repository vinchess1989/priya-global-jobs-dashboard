Here’s What We Do And Why It Matters

We’ve been problem-solvers from the start. Founded on the simple idea that there had to be an easier way to manage human resources, PurelyHR is a small but outstanding team working to empower HR decision-makers all over the world with an HR platform designed to provide their talent with the best HR experience possible. Used by over 2,000 companies in more than 65 countries, PurelyHR, our Human Resource Management platform, is transforming the way major companies manage their teams.

Find out how we do that here: www.purelyhr.com/about/

Here's what your day-to-day will look like in this role

Functional analysis (60–70%)


Own the functional detail of requirements from discovery through release: workflows, business rules, functional specifications and acceptance criteria.
Challenge requests and assumptions rather than just documenting them. Ask what problem we're solving, for whom, and whether there's a simpler way.
Tell customer-specific requests apart from broader product requirements, and recommend whether something belongs in the product, in configuration, or nowhere.
Own the functional definition of workflows, business rules, data mappings, integrations and API behaviour.
Write, refine and break down user stories with clear, complete and testable acceptance criteria.
Work with the Product Owner, developers and stakeholders to clarify scope, dependencies, edge cases and expected behaviour.
Keep functional documentation current in Confluence so it stays the single source of truth for how the product works.


Quality and validation (30–40%)


Build test strategies, plans, scenarios and test data based on requirements and product risk.
Test complete user workflows, including negative, boundary, permission-based and error-handling scenarios, plus API behaviour.
Log defects with clear reproduction steps, evidence, and severity and priority recommendations. Retest fixes and check for regressions.
Support UAT and release validation, and give the Product Owner a clear readiness recommendation for each release.
Track quality indicators (defect trends, escaped defects, regression results, test coverage) and help set QA standards and quality gates.


Here's what you need to have and master to get the job


A college diploma or university degree in technology, or equivalent professional experience.
At least 5 years in functional, business or product analysis, including hands-on QA.
HRIS/HCM, payroll or payroll-adjacent domain experience (strongly preferred).
Proven experience owning requirements end to end, not just documenting them.
Strong experience with workflows, business rules, data mappings, integrations and REST APIs.
Hands-on testing of SaaS web applications, APIs, databases and end-to-end business workflows.
Working knowledge of SQL, Swagger, Scalar, Confluence, GitHub, Azure DevOps, and bug or test management tools.
An understanding of Agile delivery, risk-based testing and release validation.
Strong analytical and communication skills, the confidence to push back, and excellent written and spoken English.


Nice to have


Experience with AI automations or simple AI agents.
Experience integrating automated tests into a CI/CD pipeline.
Knowledge of object-oriented concepts and service-oriented architecture.
