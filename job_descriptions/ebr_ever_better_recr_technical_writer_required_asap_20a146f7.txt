An exciting opportunity has risen for a talented and dedicated Technical writer to join one of Australia’s rapidly growing and certified Information Technology and Services company that is dedicated to providing highly secure cloud services to its varied clients.

The successful candidate will join a highly collaborative team of talented and passionate technical professionals on a 3-month contract with a possibility of extension subject to business requirements and will be reporting directly to the scrum master/project manager.

What you will be doing:

Creating a brochure for one of the business products as well as producing engaging and memorable content explaining the differentiating factors of the company’s offerings from other cloud providers
Producing high-quality documentation that meets applicable standards and is appropriate for intended audience
Completing the company’s Operations as well as R&D documentation
Translating and developing highly technical language into a voice for business audiences
Creating and maintaining the information architecture to enhance customer understanding of the company’s products and systems


What you’ll need:

University degree in Computer Science, Engineering or equivalent preferred
Proven working experience in technical writing of software documentation
Excellent written skills in English
Strong working knowledge of Microsoft Office
Ability to deliver high quality documentation paying attention to detail
Must be based in either Sydney or Canberra
Ability to quickly grasp complex technical concepts and make them easily understandable in text and pictures
