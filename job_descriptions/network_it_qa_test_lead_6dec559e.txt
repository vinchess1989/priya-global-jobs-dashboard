Our client is seeking an experienced QA Test Lead to join their growing technology team. This is a permanent opportunity for a quality-focused professional who is passionate about software testing, test automation, and driving quality assurance best practices across the software development lifecycle. The successful candidate will lead testing initiatives, mentor QA team members, and work closely with developers, business stakeholders, and product teams to ensure the delivery of high-quality software solutions.

The successful candidate will play a key role in defining and implementing testing strategies, improving quality processes, and ensuring that applications meet both functional and non-functional requirements. This role offers exposure to modern testing frameworks, Agile methodologies, automation practices, and DevOps-driven delivery environments.
Duties & Responsibilities
Requirements:
Relevant Diploma or Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Quality Assurance, or a related field.
5+ years of software testing experience, including experience in a QA leadership or senior testing role.
Proven experience leading testing teams and managing testing activities across multiple projects.
Strong hands-on experience with:
Manual Testing
Test Planning and Test Strategy Development
Test Case Creation and Execution
Defect Management and Root Cause Analysis
API Testing
SQL and Database Testing
Test Automation Frameworks
Regression, Integration, System, and User Acceptance Testing
Agile and Scrum Methodologies
Azure DevOps, Jira, or Similar Testing and Defect Tracking Tools
Strong understanding of software development lifecycles and quality assurance methodologies.
Experience designing and implementing test processes, standards, and best practices.
Excellent analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
Ability to mentor, coach, and develop QA team members.
Advantageous Experience:
Experience working within DevOps environments.
Exposure to CI/CD pipelines and automated testing integration.
Hands-on experience with Azure DevOps.
Experience with test automation tools such as Selenium, Playwright, Cypress, or similar technologies.
Knowledge of performance and load testing tools.
Experience testing RESTful APIs and microservices-based architectures.
Exposure to cloud platforms such as Microsoft Azure.
Knowledge of containerisation technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
Experience within enterprise software environments.
Relevant certifications such as ISTQB Foundation, Advanced Test Manager, or similar quality assurance qualifications.
Responsibilities:
Lead testing activities across the full software development lifecycle.
Develop and implement testing strategies, frameworks, and quality standards.
Manage and coordinate test planning, estimation, execution, and reporting activities.
Lead defect management processes and ensure timely resolution of identified issues.
Oversee functional, integration, regression, system, and user acceptance testing efforts.
Drive test automation initiatives and improve overall testing efficiency.
Collaborate with developers, business analysts, product owners, and stakeholders to ensure high-quality product delivery.
Review requirements and specifications to identify quality risks and testing requirements.
Monitor quality metrics and provide regular reporting on testing progress and product quality.
Support CI/CD and DevOps initiatives by integrating testing practices into delivery pipelines.
Mentor and support QA team members through coaching, training, and knowledge sharing.
Continuously evaluate and recommend improvements to testing processes, tools, and methodologies.
