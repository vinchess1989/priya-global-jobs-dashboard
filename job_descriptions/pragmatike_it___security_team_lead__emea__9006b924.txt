About The Opportunity

Pragmatike is hiring an IT Manager to support the company's day-to-day IT operations and ensure employees have the equipment, accounts, and technical resources they need to work efficiently.

This is a hands-on operational role focused on equipment preparation, device configuration, user account management, access permissions, and basic IT support.

You'll be responsible for managing practical IT needs across the organization, supporting employee onboarding and offboarding, and ensuring that internal systems and workplace tools are properly configured and accessible.

What You’ll Do


IT Operations: Own the administration and day-to-day management of the company's SaaS ecosystem, including Google Workspace, messaging, collaboration tools, GitHub, password managers, cloud platforms, and HR and finance systems.
Device Management: Manage the lifecycle of company devices across macOS, Windows, and Linux, including procurement, configuration, deployment, monitoring, and offboarding.
IT Asset Management: Maintain an inventory of company software, hardware, licences, subscriptions, costs, and renewal dates, ensuring critical tools remain operational and properly maintained.
Identity & Access Management: Own employee provisioning and deprovisioning, SSO configuration, authentication policies, access permissions, and regular access reviews across company systems.
Network Security: Manage secure remote access to internal systems and infrastructure through VPNs, zero-trust access solutions, and appropriate device and network security controls.
Security Policies & Procedures: Develop, implement, and maintain company-wide security policies covering access control, acceptable use, data handling, incident response, and vendor risk management.
Security Monitoring & Incident Response: Establish security monitoring and alerting across identity, endpoints, and SaaS applications, and coordinate incident response when security issues arise.
Security Awareness: Implement security awareness initiatives, including onboarding training, phishing simulations, and best practices around password management and data protection.
Compliance & Audits: Drive the company's compliance roadmap, supporting frameworks such as ISO 27001, SOC 2, and NIS2. Coordinate internal controls, documentation, evidence collection, and audit preparation.
Customer Security Requirements: Prepare and maintain security documentation, policies, technical overviews, and reusable responses to enterprise customer security questionnaires and assessments.
Vendor Risk Management: Evaluate the security practices of external software providers and IT vendors, ensuring they meet the company's operational and security requirements.
Internal IT Support: Act as the primary point of contact for internal IT issues, troubleshooting technical problems and coordinating with external providers when necessary.


What We’re Looking For


5+ years of experience in IT administration, IT operations, information security, or a related field.
At least 2 years of experience owning IT operations and security responsibilities within a company of approximately 50–500 employees, ideally in a cloud, SaaS, or infrastructure environment.
Strong hands-on experience administering Google Workspace, including SSO, SAML/OIDC, two-factor authentication, groups, organizational units, and data protection controls.
Experience managing devices across macOS, Windows, and Linux using MDM or endpoint management solutions such as Jamf, Kandji, Microsoft Intune, or similar.
Strong understanding of identity and access management, including SCIM provisioning, least-privilege access, access reviews, and password management.
Experience securing remote workforce access through VPNs, zero-trust network access solutions, DNS, and related technologies.
Practical experience implementing or maintaining at least one security and compliance framework, such as ISO 27001, SOC 2, or NIS2.
Understanding of endpoint security, identity monitoring, security alerting, and incident response processes.
Ability to automate IT administration and operational tasks using scripting languages such as Python, Bash, or PowerShell.
Strong documentation and communication skills, with the ability to write clear security policies, operational procedures, and customer-facing security responses.
Comfortable taking ownership of an IT and security function, establishing processes, and working independently in a fast-paced environment.
Fluent English.


Nice to Have


Certifications such as CISSP, CISM, or ISO 27001 Lead Implementer.
Experience securing production environments, bare-metal infrastructure, or GPU fleets within a cloud or hosting provider.
Experience handling enterprise and public-sector security reviews, security questionnaires, and third-party risk assessments.
Familiarity with security requirements and standards such as SecNumCloud and HDS.
Experience with EDR, SIEM, and security monitoring platforms.
Knowledge of confidential computing, tenant isolation, AMD SEV-SNP, Intel TDX, or hardware-based attestation.
Experience building IT and security processes within a startup or scale-up experiencing rapid growth.
Strong familiarity with AI tools and their practical application in IT administration and security operations.


Why Join Us


100% remote with the flexibility to work from anywhere in the EMEA region.
Hands-on ownership of day-to-day IT operations and employee technical support.
International environment working with a distributed team across different locations.
Varied responsibilities across equipment management, IT administration, and user support.
Immediate impact by ensuring employees have the tools and access they need to work effectively.
Six-month contract with the opportunity to contribute to the company's operational setup.
