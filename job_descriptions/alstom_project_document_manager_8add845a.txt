Your future role

The Project Document Manager will be based in the Brisbane CBD office supporting a high criticality / major infrastructure project,. You will be responsible for defining, implementing and continuously improving the document and information management strategy for a high-criticality project. We offer hybrid working arrangements with 3 days in the office and 2 days from home.

This is a hands-on document management leadership role, requiring strong process capability and practical knowledge of InEight and other documentation management tools. The successful candidate will develop and implement the project's document management strategy, processes, governance and controls across the full project lifecycle. A key focus will be establishing a robust subcontractor document management framework, ensuring subcontractors operate consistently with project, contractual and customer requirements. The role will define how subcontractor documentation is planned, created, reviewed, transmitted, controlled, accepted and handed over through InEight.

Working closely with project management, engineering, commercial, quality, customer-facing teams and subcontractors, the Document Management Manager will ensure documentation is accurate, traceable, contractually compliant and available when required to support project milestones and successful delivery. The position builds on the existing high-criticality Documentation Manager profile, which places the role at the centre of the project documentation lifecycle, EDMS deployment, customer interface, contractual deliverables, process compliance and document management performance.

You’ll work with sharp, committed teams across Alstom — solving complex challenges that shape the future of mobility.

Key Responsibilities

Document Management Strategy & Governance


Develop and own the Project Document Management Strategy and Documentation Management Plan.
Translate contractual, customer and project requirements into clear document management processes, procedures, workflows and working instructions.
Establish the document governance framework covering document identification, numbering, metadata, review and approval, revision control, transmittals, distribution, acceptance, superseding and archiving.
Establish clear document ownership, accountabilities and interfaces across engineering, project management, commercial, procurement, construction, testing and commissioning, quality and subcontractors.
Develop fit-for-purpose controls appropriate for a high-criticality project, with particular emphasis on document quality, traceability, contractual compliance and timely delivery.
Drive a strong document management culture across the project rather than operating document control as an administrative function.


The corporate role similarly requires creation and updating of the DMP, deployment of numbering systems and repositories, management of internal and external documentation requirements, and assurance that project personnel understand and apply the DMP.

InEight / EDMS Leadership


Act as the project's Document Management process owner and key functional lead for InEight.
Define how InEight will be used to support the project's document management strategy, contractual requirements and project delivery model.
Establish and maintain appropriate document structures, metadata, naming and numbering conventions, workflows, permissions, review cycles, distribution requirements and document status controls within InEight.
Ensure the system configuration and processes support effective control of both internal and subcontractor documentation.
Develop practical InEight working instructions and provide coaching and support to project personnel and subcontractors.
Identify process or system weaknesses and lead improvements to prevent duplication, uncontrolled documentation, incorrect revisions and inefficient document workflows.
Work with relevant system support teams where configuration or technical changes are required.
Train users, manage access rights, provide first-level support and contribute to configuration and change reviews.


Subcontractor Document Management


Develop the subcontractor document management strategy and framework, setting clear minimum requirements that can be incorporated into packages, scopes and subcontractor mobilisation.
Establish requirements for subcontractor Document Management Plans, document registers, numbering, metadata, review and approval workflows, transmittals, revision management, deliverables and final handover.
Define how subcontractors interface withInEight, including document submission, review, comment resolution, acceptance and controlled distribution.
Establish document requirements early during subcontractor mobilisation to avoid downstream document recovery and handover issues.
Review and challenge subcontractor document management arrangements to ensure they are suitable for the scale, complexity and risk of each package.
Monitor subcontractor compliance and document performance, escalating recurring issues or risks to the relevant project and commercial stakeholders.


Customer & Contractual Deliverables


Act as a key document management interface with the customer.
Understand customer documentation requirements and translate them into effective internal and subcontractor processes.
Ensure contractual documentation is appropriately reviewed, approved and controlled before formal submission.
Maintain visibility of contractual document deliverables and identify emerging risks to required submission dates.
Work closely with the project team to resolve documentation issues that could affect customer acceptance, project milestones or commercial outcomes.
Provide clear reporting on document status, overdue reviews, submissions, acceptances, outstanding comments and critical deliverables.


What You Bring


Proven document management experience on large, complex or high-criticality infrastructure, rail, construction or engineering projects.
Strong practical knowledge of InEight, with the ability to establish, implement and improve document management processes and workflows.
Demonstrated ability to develop document management strategies, processes and governance frameworks aligned with project and contractual requirements.
Experience establishing and managing subcontractor document management requirements, including deliverables, reviews, submissions and handover.
Strong understanding of document lifecycle, revision control, contractual deliverables and customer-facing document management.
Strong stakeholder and communication skills, with the ability to drive process compliance and influence project teams and subcontractors.
