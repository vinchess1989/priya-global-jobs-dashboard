Position Overview




A detail-oriented Quality Assurance Officer (QMS) is sought to support quality management operations within the pharmaceutical industry. The role involves maintaining the Quality Management System (QMS), ensuring GMP compliance, and overseeing quality documentation and regulatory procedures.




Key Responsibilities

The selected candidate will manage document control, change control, and quality records, ensuring compliance with internal procedures and regulatory standards.

Responsibilities include reviewing SOPs, batch documentation, deviations, CAPAs, complaints, and Product Quality Reviews (PQRs), while maintaining accurate quality records.

The role also involves coordinating GMP training, maintaining supplier and product documentation, and supporting equipment qualification and audit activities.

Additional duties include monitoring temperature mapping activities, reporting non-conformances, and supporting Quality Assurance management in maintaining high standards of product quality and safety.




Requirements

Candidates should possess a Bachelor's degree in Pharmacy (B.Pharma), a B.Sc. degree, or a diploma in a scientific field, or have 2 years of experience in a Quality Assurance department.

A minimum of 2 years of working experience in a GMP environment is required.

The ideal candidate should demonstrate strong communication and interpersonal skills, attention to detail, and good organisational abilities, with the ability to maintain accurate documentation and follow established quality procedures.




What's Offered

The position offers a professional and supportive working environment, opportunities to gain broad experience in pharmaceutical quality assurance and quality management systems, and prospects for professional development and career growth.
