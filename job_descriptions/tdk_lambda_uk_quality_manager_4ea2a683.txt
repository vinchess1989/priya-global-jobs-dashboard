Human Resources Manager at TDK-Lambda UK

TDK is a supplier to some of the most diverse, technology based and original equipment manufacturers worldwide including global organisations within the Industrial, Medical and Test & Measurement markets.




TDK-Lambda UK are seeking an experienced and proactive Quality Manager to lead our Quality Assurance and Quality Control team and activities to ensure that our market-leading power supplies meet the highest standards of reliability, safety, and performance.




This role is pivotal in maintaining TDK-Lambda’s reputation for excellence, ensuring that every product, from standard AC-DC power supplies to complex medical-grade converters, exceeds customer expectations.




The main duties and responsibilities of this role are:

Lead, manage and develop the Quality Assurance and Quality Control teams.
Accountability for the Quality Management System (QMS), driving continuous improvement initiatives, and ensuring compliance with international standards (ISO 9001, ISO 13485, etc.) that are fundamental to our business.
Define the local quality strategy in alignment with TDK-Lambda’s global quality vision.
Oversee quality control across the full manufacturing lifecycle, including SMT (Surface Mount Technology), manual assembly, and final test/burn-in.
To oversee quality control across the full manufacturing lifecycle, including SMT (Surface Mount Technology), manual assembly, and final test
Oversee Supplier Quality Management
Establish metrics and KPIs for organisational culture, identifying gaps and opportunities to improve.
At all times demonstrate world class quality culture standards and support the company in culture development activities focussed on safety, people, quality, cost and performance.
Apply and role model good Lean Manufacturing problem-solving techniques to deliver quality improvements.
Use tools such as 5S, value-stream / process mapping, 5 Whys, Fishbone and root-cause analysis.




Required Skills and Experience:

Proficiency in Quality tools including Lean Six Sigma (Green/Black Belt preferred), APQP, PPAP, FMEA, and MSA.
Strong understanding of IPC standards (e.g., IPC-A-610) for electronic assemblies.
Proven experience managing ISO 9001; experience with ISO 13485 (Medical) is desirable.
Strong planning and organisational skills, ability to manage workload, resources, risks and deadlines
Min. five years’ experience leading and managing a team, or equivalent substantial people-management experience within the Manufacturing Industry.




Working week: Monday – Thursday 07:30 – 16:30 / Fridays 07:30 – 12:00.




Qualifications required: Degree level Qualification in Electrical/Electronic Engineering, Quality Management, or a related technical discipline Six Sigma Green Belt or equivalent continuous improvement qualification (desirable).




Salary: Up to £65k dependent on experience




Please forward a letter of application and CV stating relevant experience to tlu.personnel@tdk.com by Friday 6th November 2026.
