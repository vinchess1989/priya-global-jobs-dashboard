Job Description - Delivery Lead

We are seeking an experienced Integration Delivery Lead to take ownership of end-to-end integration delivery across multiple workstreams and releases. The successful candidate will lead delivery from planning and estimation through development, testing, deployment, stabilisation, and warranty support.




This role requires a strong combination of delivery leadership, integration knowledge, financial awareness, governance, stakeholder management, and Agile delivery experience.




Key responsibilities

Lead delivery from planning and estimation through build, testing, deployment and stabilisation.
Manage delivery plans, timelines, dependencies, RAID, scope and resources.
Forecast capacity, demand, delivery costs and resource requirements.
Coordinate across BA, Development, QA, Architecture and programme teams.
Provide clear delivery reporting and communicate risks, issues and decisions to senior stakeholders.
Ensure architecture, security, release and delivery governance requirements are met.
Coach delivery team members and work effectively with internal teams and external vendors.




What we’re looking for

Strong end-to-end technology delivery experience.
Excellent stakeholder, team and delivery management skills.
Strong Agile/SAFe experience with Jira, Jira Align and Confluence.
Good understanding of APIs, REST API lifecycle and integration delivery.
Experience with capacity, resource and financial management.
Excellent communication and executive-level reporting skills.




Desirable: MuleSoft/CloudHub, IBM ACE/MQ, CI/CD, cloud, Splunk/observability, and Insurance or Financial Services experience.
