Overview

The IT Operational Readiness Manager is responsible for leading and governing operational readiness across technology change portfolios, ensuring that new services, systems, infrastructure, and significant changes are transitioned into production in a controlled, supportable, secure, and sustainable manner.
The role serves as the central authority for Operational Readiness and Service Transition, partnering with project delivery, engineering, architecture, security, risk, service management, and business stakeholders across MUFG MPMS to ensure operational requirements are embedded throughout the lifecycle, from design through implementation.
This position is accountable for influencing delivery outcomes, identifying and challenging readiness gaps, driving remediation activities, and providing independent readiness assessments to support go-live decision-making. The role ensures that operational risks are identified, assessed, managed, and resolved prior to production deployment, while continuously enhancing operational readiness frameworks, governance practices, processes, metrics, and controls across the organisation.
Key Accountabilities and main responsibilities
Strategic Focus

Contribute to the ongoing maintenance and continuous improvement of the MUFG MPMS enterprise Operational Readiness Framework, governance model, standards, processes, and methodologies. 
Influence project, programme, and technology leadership teams to embed operational considerations early in the solution design and delivery lifecycle. 
Provide thought leadership and subject matter expertise in Service Transition, Operational Acceptance Testing (OAT), Service Introduction, and Production Readiness practices.

Operational Management

Lead end-to-end Operational Readiness assessments across assigned technology projects, infrastructure changes, platform implementations, and strategic initiatives. 
Facilitate cross-functional readiness workshops to identify, document, and track operational requirements, risks, dependencies, and ownership. 
Establish and govern operational readiness checkpoints throughout the project delivery lifecycle. 
Ensure that operational support models, processes, monitoring, alerting, knowledge management, resilience, disaster recovery, and service support capabilities are implemented prior to go-live. Ensure any exceptions are formally documented and approved by the appropriate IT Service Owners. 
Drive accountability across delivery and support teams to ensure all operational readiness actions are completed within agreed timelines. 
Provide independent operational readiness assessments and recommendations to support production deployment approval and go-live decision-making.

People Leadership

Act as the primary Operational Readiness advisor for project sponsors, programme managers, service owners, and technology leaders across assigned projects. 
Build and maintain strong relationships across Technology, Operations, Risk, Security, Architecture, Vendor Management, and Business teams to ensure successful service transitions. 
Influence stakeholders at all organisational levels to proactively address operational risks and readiness gaps. 
Facilitate the resolution of conflicting priorities and negotiate outcomes that support service stability and customer experience. 
Lead forums, workshops, and governance meetings to drive decision-making and organisational alignment. 
Guide and coach project and support teams on Operational Readiness expectations, Service Transition requirements, and industry best practices. 
Lead and influence cross-functional teams within a matrix environment to achieve operational readiness objectives. 
Foster collaboration between project, engineering, operations, security, and business stakeholders. 
Coach and mentor project teams on IT Operational Readiness principles, methodologies, and best practices. 
Act as a change champion, driving organisational adoption of IT Operational Readiness practices, standards, and continuous improvement initiatives.

Governance & Risk

Identify, assess, and escalate operational, service, security, supportability, and resilience risks associated with technology change initiatives. 
Ensure Operational Readiness requirements align with MUFG's regulatory, audit, security, compliance, and risk management obligations. 
Drive mitigation and remediation activities where readiness requirements are not met. 
Ensure service acceptance criteria are achieved prior to recommending production deployment. 
Support internal and external audits by providing evidence of readiness governance, controls, compliance, and assurance activities. 
Promote proactive risk management practices across technology delivery and support functions. 
Track and report Operational Readiness KPIs, KRIs, and Service Transition metrics. 
Establish and monitor metrics that measure production stability, support readiness, deployment success, and post-implementation outcomes. 
Analyse incidents, service disruptions, and transition outcomes to identify improvement opportunities and strengthen operational maturity. 
Lead initiatives to enhance operational readiness capabilities, improve deployment quality, and reduce transition-related risks. 
Provide executive-level reporting and insights on readiness health, operational risks, service transition performance, and organisational capability maturity.

The above list of key accountabilities is not an exhaustive list and may change from time-to-time based on business needs.

10 years’ experience of total exp and 5+ years in Operational Readiness, Service Transition, IT Service Management, Technology Delivery, Infrastructure Operations or related disciplines.

Proven ability to influence senior stakeholders and drive outcomes in matrix organisational structures.
Strong experience facilitating governance forums and managing competing stakeholder priorities.
Experience implementing and operationalising cloud, infrastructure, application and digital technology solutions.
Strong understanding of Service Introduction, Service Transition, Release Management, Change Management and IT Operations.
Experience developing governance frameworks, standards and operational processes.
Experience identifying and managing operational risk within complex technology environments.
Demonstrated ability to lead continuous improvement initiatives and measure business outcomes.
Experience within highly regulated industries such as Financial Services, Banking, Insurance, Utilities or Government is highly desirable.

Proactive and collaborative approach to driving organisational change and service maturity.
