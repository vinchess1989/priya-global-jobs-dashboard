About The Role

We are seeking an experienced Release & Deployment Manager to lead the planning, governance and delivery of releases across the EOHR Program.

Reporting to the Program Manager, this role is responsible for coordinating complex system upgrades, configuration releases, data migrations and implementation activities across a statewide environment. The position works closely with the program team, ICT, Infrastructure, Security, Statewide Systems, vendors and business stakeholders to ensure releases are delivered safely, efficiently and with minimal disruption.

This is a hands-on role suited to someone with strong technical release management experience. In addition to leading release planning and governance, the successful candidate will be comfortable rolling up their sleeves and actively participating in release activities, deployment readiness assessments, testing coordination and implementation support when required.

The role operates in a fast-paced environment with multiple concurrent releases and requires strong stakeholder management, communication and presentation skills.

Key Responsibilities


Lead the planning, coordination and delivery of UMM system upgrades, configuration releases, data migrations and implementation activities.
Manage release governance processes, including change approvals, deployment readiness assessments, risk management and release reporting.
Coordinate technical and business teams to ensure successful release preparation, deployment, validation and post-release support.
Develop and maintain release, implementation and cutover plans to ensure releases are delivered in line with program objectives, timelines and quality standards.


Essential Experience


Demonstrated experience managing and delivering multiple enterprise system implementations and releases.
Strong experience with release and deployment management, including system upgrades, configuration releases and data migration activities.
Experience participating in and preparing submissions for Change Advisory Boards (CABs) or equivalent change approval forums.
Strong understanding of release lifecycles, deployment planning, implementation methodologies and production support processes.
