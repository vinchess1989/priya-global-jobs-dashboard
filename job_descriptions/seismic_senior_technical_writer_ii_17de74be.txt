About Us:

Seismic is the Go-To-Market Performance company, driving how organizations turn their strategy into revenue. Seismic brings together signals from customer conversations, buyer engagement, governed content, coaching and seller behavior to turn intelligence into winning action. Through its AI revenue execution platform, the company equips customer-facing teams with the insights, governance, and contextual guidance they need to execute with confidence and deliver measurable business outcomes. Trusted by 2,500 organizations and over 3.5 million users around the globe, Seismic is headquartered in San Diego with offices across North America, Europe and Asia-Pacific. Learn more at seismic.com.

Overview:

Technical writers play an instrumental role at Seismic, delivering product documentation that drives product adoption, reduces Support demand, and serves as the source of truth for the AI-powered answers our customers rely on.

Our writers use DITA for authoring AI-ready product documentation, and we deliver it through an AI-powered, enterprise-class, multi-channel content delivery platform. Our documentation team sits within Product & Engineering with shared governance, tools, and processes, and our writers are embedded with product development teams in India, China, Europe, and the US.

We have had technical writers in India for three years, and we're now growing a team in our Hyderabad office. We're looking for experienced technical writers who will help us keep pace with growing demand and shape how we work.

We're implementing agentic documentation workflows to help us keep up with accelerating product innovation and focus our energy where it delivers the most value. Our writers will continue to own the quality of everything that reaches our customers, and increasingly will help shape the templates, prompts, skills, and quality criteria our AI workflows run on. If you're curious, empathetic, detail-oriented, and thrive in fast-paced environments, you sound like one of us. If you want to help redefine what a documentation professional looks like in an agentic world, this is your chance.

As a Senior Technical Writer II, you'll own documentation for one or more product areas and take an active hand in the content strategy, information architecture, and AI tooling the whole team depends on.

Who you are:


BA/BS degree in English, Technical Communication/Writing, Computer Science or a related technical discipline, or relevant professional experience (required)
8+ years relevant experience in Scrum/Agile development environments (required)
Experience with DITA and structured, topic-based authoring (required)
Experience with Oxygen, Ixiasoft, Fluid Topics, Jira, or similar tools a plus
Hands-on experience using generative AI tools (Claude or comparable) to research, draft, and edit documentation (required)
Experience developing Claude skills (or comparable) and publishing and maintaining them in GitHub (preferred)
Experience contributing to AI-assisted or agentic content workflows, such as prompts, templates, quality rubrics, or review checkpoints, a plus
Experience with API documentation, information architecture, content strategy, or web technologies (HTML/CSS) a plus
Impeccable oral and written English skills
Flexibility to accommodate synchronous work across time zones as needed


What you’ll be doing:


Deliver documentation for new product features and updates using Oxygen XML and Ixiasoft, our DITA authoring and cCMS tools
Analyze existing documentation and update, revise, replace, and retire as needed
Serve as documentation lead for one or more product areas: scope and plan work, track feature development in Jira, coordinate with other writers, and partner with product managers, designers, and engineers to deliver all required documentation on schedule
Build deep knowledge of our products, technology, and customers, and relationships with the experts who inform your work
Help establish best practices, drive quality and consistency, and build a positive team culture
Help drive continuous improvement of our documentation using analytics and feedback from customers and internal users
Use AI-assisted authoring tools and Claude skills in day-to-day work, and develop and iterate on skills in our shared GitHub repository
Contribute to content strategy, information architecture, and reuse decisions, and help implement new solutions that improve how technical information is delivered to our customers
As our agentic workflows mature, you will also:
Review and ensure the integrity of agent-generated drafts and act as a human quality gate for your product areas
Contribute golden examples, test cases, and feedback that improve agent output, and help identify where human judgment is essential
Help test agentic documentation workflows: contribute to prompt libraries and the assessment criteria used to judge agent output
