Key Responsibilities


Gather, analyse, and document business and technical requirements in collaboration with business stakeholders.
Translate business requirements into technical specifications, user stories, use cases, and process flows.
Collaborate with Product, Engineering, QA, and Delivery teams to ensure solutions are aligned with requirements and quality standards.
Support solution design activities by recommending scalable, performant, and maintainable technology solutions.
Create and maintain project documentation, including BRDs, FRDs, technical specifications, and process documentation.
Support system testing, defect analysis, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), and issue resolution.
Facilitate communication between business and technical stakeholders, ensuring alignment throughout the project lifecycle.
Identify and recommend process improvement opportunities to enhance business efficiency through technology.
Assist project delivery by ensuring requirements traceability and successful implementation of technology initiatives.


Mandatory Skills


Proven experience as a Technical Business Analyst or similar role within an IT/software development environment.
Strong understanding of API-driven architectures and system integrations.
Knowledge of system and application architecture concepts.
Hands-on experience with SQL and database querying.
Experience working within DevOps environments and delivery processes.
Strong requirement gathering, analysis, and documentation skills.
Ability to create technical specifications, functional specifications, user stories, and process diagrams.
Understanding of Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and Agile methodologies.
Experience using requirement management and collaboration tools such as JIRA and Confluence.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to provide actionable insights.
Excellent verbal and written communication skills.
Ability to work effectively with cross-functional business and technical teams.
Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Business, or a related field.


Desirable Skills


Experience supporting cloud-based applications and integration platforms.
Exposure to microservices architecture and enterprise application integration.
Experience in stakeholder management across multiple business functions.
Knowledge of testing methodologies, UAT coordination, and defect management processes.
Experience in process improvement and business process optimisation initiatives.
Relevant certifications in Business Analysis, Agile, Scrum, or related domains.
