Mō te tūranga | About the role

We are looking for an experienced Software Specialist (Test) to join our Payments Tribe, working on domestic payment systems that must be secure, resilient and available when customers need them.

You will be part of a cross-functional, agile squad delivering and supporting domestic payment capabilities for business and corporate customers. The work spans modern and established systems, complex integrations and high-throughput payment flows, where quality, performance and reliability matter.

You will play a key role in shaping how testing is delivered within the squad, with a strong primary focus on automation, performance testing and quality engineering rather than manual-only testing.

What You’ll Do


Review requirements and technical designs with a quality-first mindset.
Define and execute pragmatic, risk-based test strategies, plans, estimates, coverage and exit criteria.
Own, maintain and evolve the squad’s test automation suite in C# and .NET.
Build, maintain and evolve automated test frameworks using ReqNRoll or SpecFlow.
Design and execute performance testing using LoadRunner, JMeter or equivalent tools.
Test across functional, system, integration, API and end-to-end layers.
Analyse and clearly communicate test results, defects and release risks.
Investigate issues within payment flows and advise on likely root cause, impact and next steps.
Work closely with engineers, product owners and stakeholders to support end-to-end testing and release sign-off.
Act as the voice of test engineering within the squad, lifting quality standards and practices.
Mentor less experienced test automation engineers in the squad to support their growth.


Ngā mea kia whai angitū ahau? | What will help me succeed?


Strong test engineering experience and a quality-driven mindset.
At least three five years of testing experience within payments, or comparable experience with high-throughput regulated platforms.
Hands-on programming experience with C# and .NET.
Hands-on automation testing experience using ReqNRoll or SpecFlow.
Proven capability across functional, system, integration, API, end-to-end and performance testing.
Exposure to Azure cloud technologies such as Functions, Service Bus and CosmosDB
Experience working with Azure DevOps and CI/CD pipelines.
Experience defining test strategies, test plans, estimates and exit criteria.
Confidence working in agile, cross-functional teams, mentoring others and influencing testing best practice.


Nice to have


Banking or broader financial services experience.
ISTQB Foundation certification.
Exposure to cloud technologies.
