Release & Environment Manager – Applications
Melbourne | Hybrid | Contract | Major Transformation Program

We are seeking an experienced Release & Environment Manager to join a large-scale enterprise technology transformation program.

This role will focus on managing application releases, environment governance and deployment coordination across multiple enterprise platforms, integrations and vendors.

You will play a key role in ensuring application releases are delivered in a controlled, secure and predictable manner, working closely with technical delivery teams, business stakeholders and external vendors.

Key Responsibilities

Own and coordinate end-to-end application release management across multiple enterprise systems and platforms.
Develop and maintain release schedules, deployment plans and environment calendars.
Manage application environments across Development, Test, SIT, UAT and Production.
Coordinate application deployments, upgrades, integrations and production releases across multiple vendors.
Facilitate release readiness assessments, Go/No-Go decisions and post-implementation reviews.
Manage release risks, dependencies, deployment runbooks and rollback strategies.
Ensure releases comply with change management processes, including CAB approvals.
Drive consistency in DevOps, CI/CD and application deployment practices.
Work closely with development, testing, infrastructure, security and operational support teams.
Ensure successful production validation and transition of applications into BAU support.
About You:

5+ years' experience in Release Management, Environment Management or a similar position.
Strong experience managing enterprise application releases within complex technology environments.
Demonstrated experience coordinating releases across multiple applications, integrations and third-party vendors.
Strong understanding of SDLC, CI/CD pipelines and DevOps delivery practices.
Experience with Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions or similar release management and deployment tools.
Experience managing non-production environments, including SIT, UAT and production readiness.
Strong understanding of ITIL change management, CAB processes and release governance.
Excellent stakeholder engagement, communication and coordination skills.
Experience delivering within large-scale transformation or application modernisation programs.
Experience working across ERP platforms, enterprise applications, healthcare systems or complex integration environments will be highly regarded.

What's on Offer?

Join a significant enterprise technology transformation program.
Work across a complex landscape of enterprise applications and integrations.
Collaborate with experienced technical teams and external delivery partners.
Contract opportunity aligned to a major program of work.
