We’re looking for a versatile DevOps Engineer to help shape and scale cutting-edge tools that bridge advanced hardware and software. In this role, you’ll design and maintain CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure that support diverse languages and platforms for devices that have real-life application and change lives.




You’ll work across both on-prem and cloud environments (AWS), building systems that handle everything from traditional deployments to containerised applications.




What you’ll do:

Support multi-platform development (Windows, macOS, Linux)
Build and optimise CI/CD pipelines for a variety of codebases
Manage hybrid infrastructure across cloud and on-premises environments
Automate provisioning, deployment, and monitoring at scale
Integrate best-practice security into every step
Collaborate with developers to streamline builds and workflows




What we’re looking for:

Strong AWS knowledge
Programming experience with C++, Python or similar
CI/CD build and configuration experience
Docker/Kubernetes containerisation
Hands-on experience with infrastructure-as-code (e.g. Terraform, Ansible, CloudFormation)




Bonus points if you have:

Background in hardware development or HPC workflows
DevSecOps and compliance experience with various information security frameworks (e.g. SOC2, ISO27k).
