Summary

We’re looking for a Technical Product Owner to own the bridge between our business, our clients, and our engineering team. You’ll translate client and business requirements into a clear, prioritised product backlog, make sure the development team always knows what to build and why, and ensure what we ship meets requirements, deadlines, and quality standards. Sitting at the centre of delivery, you’ll bring order and clarity to how work flows from idea to release — combining strong technical understanding with sharp prioritisation and excellent communication. This is a hands-on, high-impact role in a fast-growing iGaming platform business, working closely with developers, account management, and leadership.




Responsibilities

Own, maintain, and prioritise the product backlog, turning client and business needs into clear, well-defined user stories with proper acceptance criteria.
Act as the primary link between stakeholders (clients, account management, leadership) and the development team, ensuring requirements are understood, realistic, and correctly scoped before they reach development.
Lead sprint planning, backlog refinement, and reviews together with the Team Lead, and keep sprints focused, achievable, and aligned to priorities.
Make day-to-day prioritisation decisions and trade-offs, and clearly communicate what is in, what is out, and why.
Track delivery against timelines, proactively flag risks and dependencies, and provide clear status updates to stakeholders and clients.
Ensure deliverables meet product requirements and quality standards, and that definition-of-done and acceptance criteria are consistently applied.
Coordinate with third parties, providers, and integration partners where features depend on external inputs.
Help improve delivery processes, documentation standards, and the overall product development workflow.
Maintain visibility of the roadmap and keep it aligned with the company’s strategic direction.




Qualifications

Proven experience as a Product Owner, Technical Product Owner, or similar role in a software/technology environment (iGaming, sportsbook, or platform experience is a strong plus).
Strong technical understanding — comfortable discussing APIs, integrations, architecture trade-offs, and technical constraints with engineers, without necessarily coding day to day.
Hands-on experience with Agile/Scrum and backlog management tools (e.g. Azure DevOps, Jira).
Excellent requirements-gathering, user-story writing, and prioritisation skills.
Strong, clear communicator who can translate between technical and non-technical audiences and manage multiple stakeholders.
Highly organised, proactive, and comfortable owning decisions and trade-offs in a fast-paced environment.
A problem-solving, delivery-focused mindset with a strong eye for quality.
Based in or able to work from Malta (adjust to your requirement).
