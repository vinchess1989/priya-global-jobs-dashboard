Ready to take your career global?

Make your mark at one of the biggest names in payments. We’re looking for a Cloud Infrastructure Engineer to join our ever-evolving Infrastructure Operations Team and help shape the future of global commerce.

What You’ll Own


Implement and operate GCP infrastructure using Terraform (IaC) and Kubernetes (GKE / container orchestration), standardising environment builds and enforcing consistency across dev/test/prod.
Deploy and maintain Kubernetes clusters (GKE), configuring namespaces, RBAC, ingress controllers and service mesh components as required, and building and deploying containerised workloads while managing scaling, upgrades and patching.
Own infrastructure configuration management (Ansible or equivalent), writing and maintaining playbooks and roles for VM and cluster configuration, and automating patching, security hardening and system updates.
Ensure security, scalability and high availability across our cloud and VM platforms, and support cloud cost optimisation and capacity planning.
Participate in the on-call rotation, providing tier 3/4 support for critical infrastructure services, taking part in incident response and root-cause analysis, troubleshooting pod, node and networking issues in production clusters, and driving automation of monitoring, alerting and remediation workflows.
Partner with cross-functional teams to align delivery models (Kanban for BAU, Agile for projects), oversee Jira governance for infrastructure projects, and produce technical assessments and documentation that uphold standards and provide recommendations to management.


What You’ll Bring


A Bachelor's degree and 3+ years' experience as an Infrastructure Engineer, SRE or Cloud Engineer in enterprise environments.
Proven expertise in Google Cloud Platform (GCP), with strong skills in VM workloads, Terraform (IaC) and Kubernetes (GKE or equivalent).
Hands-on experience with Ansible (or equivalent configuration management).
Broad knowledge of networking, databases, Linux systems and hybrid infrastructure.
Experience with monitoring, observability and incident response tooling.


It’s a bonus if you have


GCP Professional Cloud Architect certification or equivalent hands-on experience, DevOps / SRE hands-on experience, and experience in regulated environments (PCI-DSS, ISO, SOC 2, etc.).
Strong communicator, able to influence both technical engineers and senior stakeholders, with an organised and pragmatic approach, strong ownership and accountability, and a collaborative style that balances governance with delivery speed.
Demonstrated success implementing Kanban and Agile delivery within Infrastructure/Cloud Operations, the ability to document and enforce technical standards, and a proven track record of improving operational performance and cross-team delivery.
