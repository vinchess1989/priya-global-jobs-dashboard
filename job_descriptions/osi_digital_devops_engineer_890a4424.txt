Job Title : Devops Engineer

Location : Hyderabad

Experience : 3+ years

OSI Digital is looking for a hands-on DevOps Engineer with 3+ years of experience in cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, automation, and observability, who is eager to leverage AI-powered tools and intelligent automation to improve engineering productivity, operational reliability, and incident resolution.

Responsibilities


Configure, manage, and monitor container orchestration platforms (Docker, Kubernetes).
Manage source code repositories (Git/GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket) and branching strategies.
Implement, manage, and monitor CI/CD pipelines for application and infrastructure deployments.
Set up and maintain monitoring, alerting, and logging solutions (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, CloudWatch, etc.).
Maintain a good understanding of networking concepts.
Automate repetitive tasks using scripting languages such as Bash, Python, or PowerShell.
Provision and maintain cloud resources primarily on Azure and manage infrastructure as code (Terraform/ARM).
Collaborate with development, QA, and support teams to streamline build, deployment, and release processes.
Troubleshoot and resolve infrastructure, build, and deployment issues in a timely manner.
Document configurations, procedures, and environments for future reference.


AI-Enabled DevOps and Intelligent Automation


Leverage AI-powered tools and coding assistants to accelerate infrastructure automation, scripting, troubleshooting, and CI/CD pipeline development.
Use AI-assisted analysis of application logs, infrastructure metrics, alerts, and deployment failures to identify potential root causes and recommend corrective actions.
Explore and implement AI-driven automation for incident triage, root cause analysis (RCA), operational reporting, and repetitive operational tasks.
Integrate AI capabilities with existing DevOps tools and workflows through APIs, webhooks, and automation frameworks.
Validate AI-generated scripts, configurations, and remediation recommendations for correctness, security, reliability, and operational impact before implementation.
Follow security best practices when using AI tools, ensuring sensitive information, credentials, and production data are handled appropriately.


Required Skills


Hands-on experience with CI/CD tools (Jenkins, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps, etc.).
Experience in at least one scripting language (Bash, Python, or PowerShell).
Experience with cloud platforms (preferably Azure).
Good understanding of infrastructure as code (Terraform/CloudFormation/Ansible).
Good background in Linux/Unix administration.
Working knowledge of Kubernetes concepts.
Familiarity with source code management and version control systems.
Knowledge of monitoring and logging tools.
Good problem-solving skills and the ability to troubleshoot complex issues.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills.


AI And Intelligent Automation Skills


Hands-on experience using AI-powered development assistants or coding tools to improve scripting, infrastructure automation, and troubleshooting productivity.
Familiarity with AI-assisted log analysis, incident investigation, root cause analysis, and operational problem-solving.
Understanding of Python scripting, REST APIs, and automation workflows for integrating DevOps tools with AI-powered services.
Awareness of the limitations of AI-generated outputs, including the need for validation, security checks, and human approval for production-impacting changes.
Willingness to learn and experiment with AI agents, AI-powered observability, and intelligent automation in DevOps environments.


Good to Have


Familiarity with AI agents and integrations with DevOps tools such as Jenkins, GitHub, Azure DevOps, Jira, or observability platforms through APIs or integration protocols such as MCP.
Awareness of AI security practices, including least-privilege access, audit logging, protection of secrets, and approval controls for production-impacting actions.


We Are Not Looking For Someone Who


Has only theoretical knowledge without hands-on practical experience.
Lacks willingness to learn new tools, automation, AI-assisted workflows, or adapt to changing technologies.
