Overview

Shape the future of Quality at Waters Corporation.

Are you passionate about quality systems, continuous improvement, and using technology to drive smarter ways of working? Join Waters Corporation, a global leader in analytical technologies and medical devices, and help transform how quality is delivered across our organisation.

As our Senior Quality Specialist, you'll take ownership of Document Control and our Document Management System, leading the evolution of key quality processes while championing digital and AI-enabled solutions. This is an opportunity to make a real impact by improving compliance, efficiency, and user experience across a highly regulated environment.

Working closely with local and global stakeholders, you'll play a key role in strengthening our Quality Management System, supporting business growth, and embedding a culture of operational excellence.

Responsibilities

What You'll Be Doing

In this role, you'll become the go-to expert for Quality System Guidance, Document Control and the Document Management System, ensuring compliance with ISO 9001, ISO 13485, FDA, and other regulatory requirements.

You'll lead the development and continuous improvement of document control processes, driving greater efficiency and effectiveness across the business. A key focus will be identifying opportunities to modernise the function through automation, digital tools, and AI-enabled solutions that enhance document lifecycle management and data integrity.

You'll also collaborate with global teams on Quality Management System initiatives, support audits and regulatory inspections, investigate quality system issues, analyse performance metrics, and coach colleagues to build quality capability and foster a proactive quality culture.

Qualifications

What We're Looking For

You may have a degree or HND in Engineering, Science, or a related discipline, or equivalent experience gained within a regulated environment.

To be successful, you'll bring:


Strong knowledge of ISO 9001, ISO 13485, 21 CFR Part 820 and Part 11
Experience managing and improving electronic document management systems
A track record of supporting audits, regulatory inspections, and compliance programmes
Experience leading quality improvement, business transformation, or process optimisation initiatives
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills
Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities
A passion for continuous improvement and driving positive change



Experience implementing automation, digital technologies, or AI-driven process improvements would be particularly valuable.

Why Join Waters?

At Waters, you'll be part of an innovative organisation where quality, technology, and continuous improvement go hand in hand. You'll have the opportunity to influence global processes, work alongside talented colleagues, and contribute to projects that make a real difference.

Alongside a competitive salary, we offer:


Flexible working arrangements
25 days' holiday
Generous pension contribution
Private medical insurance
Life assurance and income protection
Excellent family-friendly benefits
Ongoing development and career growth opportunities



If you're ready to combine quality expertise with innovation and digital transformation, we'd love to hear from you.
