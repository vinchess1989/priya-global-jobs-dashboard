About Your Team

The Change Management Lead Analyst/Specialist is currently responsible for reviewing, supervising, discussing, and coordinating change requests and initiatives across all IT departments. This role ensures that all changes are implemented effectively within approved non-conflicting windows, and have proper verification tasks in place, with established stakeholder communications. IT Change Management utilizes Service Now, Jira, Confluence, Nautobot, and IB Trading Platform knowledge for daily approvals and reviews. There are continuous efforts to enhance/onboard non-IT departments into centralized Change Management, while ensuring scalability and ITIL/DORA compliance. Additionally, this role will also be requested to manages allocation of available IT hardware resources, initiating new IT projects based on trends/observations, build out curates and maintains overall IT Service Catalog of the IB Trading Platform, and assists Solution Architecture and Production Engineering departments for with ad-hoc/critical efforts project tasks.

Key Responsibilities

Change Request Review & Supervision


Review and assess change requests queues, for change suitability, completeness and validity
Supervise change initiatives from initiation through implementation and closure
Coordinate with stakeholders to ensure alignment with organizational objectives


Change Implementation & Verification


Certify that change requests are implemented within approved maintenance windows, including proper verification procedures
Validating that changes meet defined success criteria with documented rollback plans
Revisit and troubleshoot failed changes or conflicting maintenance for understanding, optimization and product improvement


Impact Assessment & Communication


Evaluate and document the potential impact of change requests on systems, processes, and users, building toward long term IT Service Catalog
Communicate impact analysis sessions with technical and business teams
Escalate trends/observations to IT Project Management for proposed remediation or enhancement


Process Support & Compliance


Manage overall allocation and assignment of available IT hardware resources
Assist in the overall change management process and continuous improvement initiatives
Ensure requirement understanding across all stakeholders
Build and maintain compliance with change management policies, procedures, and industry standards across third-party, hardware and software groups
Support the development and refinement of change management documentation and workflows to include all system change requests
Support Solution Architecture and Production Engineering departments by owning ad-hoc efforts through supporting efforts


Required Qualifications


Bachelor’s degree: Information Technology, Business Administration, or related field
2-5 years of experience in change management, IT operations, IT service management, or related IT fields.
Experience with Service Now, Jira, Nautobot, ITIL framework and change management best practices


Skills & Competencies


Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
Ability to assess risk and impact of technical changes
Detail-oriented with strong organizational and prioritization skills
Ability to work under pressure and manage multiple priorities
Understanding of IT infrastructure, applications, and systems


Preferred Qualifications


ITIL Foundation or higher certification
Experience with change management tools (e.g., ServiceNow, Jira, Nautobot)
Knowledge of regulatory compliance requirements
Project management experience
