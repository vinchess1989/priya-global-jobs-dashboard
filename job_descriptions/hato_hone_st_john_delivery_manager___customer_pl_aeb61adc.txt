Ko wai mātou - About us:



Hato Hone St John has been serving New Zealanders since 1885. Today, we have nearly 12,000 kaimahi working across Aotearoa to enhance the health and wellbeing of our communities. From responding to 111 calls to supporting people to stay healthy through health education, access and monitoring, we make a life-changing difference every day.





Job Description



He aha te tūranga - About the role:



Join Hato Hone St John in a key leadership role responsible for the delivery and ongoing success of our customer platform, supporting services including Telecare, Training, Fundraising, Products & Services, and Ambulance Membership.



Leading a high-performing cross-functional team, you'll provide clear direction, manage competing priorities, and ensure delivery outcomes align with organisational goals. Combining strategic oversight with hands-on leadership, you'll foster collaboration, drive accountability, and enable your team to deliver high-quality solutions that create value across the organisation.



Lead, coach, and develop a cross-functional digital delivery team, fostering accountability and high performance.
Own the delivery pipeline, ensuring work is prioritised, planned, and delivered across multiple business streams.
Partner with stakeholders to align delivery priorities with organisational goals and manage expectations.
Oversee releases, governance, and change management to ensure reliable, secure, and high-quality outcomes.
Manage team capacity, risks, dependencies, and competing priorities while providing clear delivery visibility.


Qualifications



Ko wai koe - About you:



You'll be a confident and adaptable leader with strong people management skills and experience leading multi-disciplinary teams. You thrive in fast-paced environments, build strong relationships, make sound decisions, and take ownership of delivery outcomes from start to finish.



To be successful in this role, you will have:



Proven experience leading and managing cross-functional or technical teams.
Experience leading enterprise platform or digital delivery initiatives.
Proven people leadership experience across technical or multi-disciplinary teams.
Strong understanding of Salesforce, requirements gathering, and delivery lifecycles.
Experience managing competing priorities, multiple delivery streams, and stakeholder expectations.
Excellent communication skills, with the ability to engage executives, stakeholders, and external partners.
Relevant tertiary qualification in IT, Business, or a related field, with Salesforce and project management certifications highly regarded.
