Overview

Reporting to the Ops Manager Digital Factory Lead, the Digital Factory Business Lead represents the business within the global Digital Factory programme, ensuring that manufacturing and quality needs are translated into practical, clearly defined digital solutions and consistently implemented across participating sites.

The role combines a detailed understanding of plant operations with the technical understanding needed to connect business stakeholders, IT, automation specialists and implementation partners ensuring that effective collaboration results in solutions that are usable, supportable and aligned with business priorities.

The role leads business requirements definition, operational design alignment, enhancement demand governance, user acceptance testing, change management and training. Its scope includes filling and batching processes, with business input to structured work instructions, reporting requirements and sustained site adoption.

Responsibilities

Manage and develop a team to deliver on the responsibilities described below:


Act as liaison and manage relationship between DF Team and plant teams
Translate business needs into clear, testable requirements, process maps and acceptance criteria. Maintain visibility of assumptions, dependencies, decisions and unresolved questions throughout delivery.
Combine knowledge of plant operations and Digital Factory functionality to challenge requirements constructively, improve processes and optimise use of the available capability.
Engage plant leadership and cross-functional stakeholders to agree the common design blueprint, resolve conflicting requirements and support consistent implementation across sites.
Represent production and quality interests in programme decisions, ensuring that impacts on normal operations are understood and communicated to business leadership.
Manage site demand for system changes and enhancements. Help requestors define requirements and benefits, work with technical teams to assess effort and complexity, and facilitate transparent, value-based prioritisation.
Maintain clear documentation and visibility of enhancement requests, business benefits, priorities, approvals and delivery status through agreed governance processes.
Lead business test planning and coordinate user acceptance testing (UAT) activities with plant teams and superusers. Ensure that representative operating scenarios and exceptions are tested and that outstanding issues are visible before deployment.
Coordinate business readiness for deployments and major changes, including stakeholder engagement, training, communications and transition to support.
Develop and maintain change-management plans and training materials. Coordinate training across sites and establish measures of training effectiveness and adoption.
Support site superuser capability and reusable knowledge content, ensuring that operational learning and system guidance can be maintained and shared across sites.
Lead the development of the detailed manufacturing and quality requirements for DF batch and other DF projects through the assessment of current business requirements, process steps, procedures and processes including equipment and manual processes.
Coordinate business input to batching and structured work-instruction requirements, including equipment variants, access and non-standard processing. Work with the relevant owners to resolve process and master-data dependencies.
Coordinate operational and quality reporting requirements early in solution design. Work with reporting and technical specialists to align information needs with available execution and historian data.


Qualifications


5-7 years of relevant manufacturing operations experience, with a thorough understanding of batching, filling, and quality requirements of concentrate-manufacturing is particularly relevant.
Experience in a leadership role in significant manufacturing-system implementation, including change management, business testing, and operational readiness.
Ability to lead cross-functional work across sites and geographies, influence stakeholders without relying on direct authority and resolve competing requirements.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain operational and technical issues clearly to plant teams and senior leadership.
Data-analysis capability, with the ability to interpret information, identify business implications and support prioritisation and decisions.
Experience applying continuous-improvement principles to process design and implementation.
Degree-level qualification in a relevant discipline or equivalent relevant professional experience.


Additional Skills

Differentiating functional / technical competencies


Formal training and/or change-management qualification.
Experience of developing and implementing training programs.
Knowledge of MES, automation and digital manufacturing systems, including the relationship between business processes, equipment, enterprise systems and operational data.
Lean Six Sigma experience and a practical continuous-improvement mindset.
Familiarity with collaboration, workflow and reporting tools used to support transparent requirements and delivery management.
Ability to work independently, structure ambiguous problems and maintain clear, traceable documentation.
Practical judgement in balancing global standardisation, local operating needs, usability and technical feasibility.


For the purposes of external advertising we have called this role Project Manager – MES Business Lead. However please note that the internal job title the successful candidate will be Project Management Assoc Manager.
