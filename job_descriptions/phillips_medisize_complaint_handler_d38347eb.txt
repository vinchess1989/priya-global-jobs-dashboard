Your Job

Are you passionate about improving patient outcomes and ensuring product quality in a regulated healthcare environment? Phillips-Medisize is seeking a Customer Complaints Handler to support our global complaint handling operations. In this role, you will play a critical part in managing customer complaints, supporting investigations, identifying product and process improvement opportunities, and ensuring compliance with global quality and regulatory requirements for medical devices.

Our Team

You will be part of the Global Quality & Regulatory organization within the Customer Complaints team. Working closely with Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Manufacturing, Product Sustenance, and Post-Market Surveillance teams across India, Europe, and China, you will help establish and operate a robust complaint handling capability that supports global medical device customers.

What You Will Do


Drive resolution of customer complaints while ensuring compliance with quality and regulatory requirements.
Partner with cross-functional stakeholders to support investigations, risk assessments, and corrective actions.
Analyze complaint data and trends to identify emerging risks and opportunities for continuous improvement.
Support product quality, patient safety, and customer satisfaction through effective complaint management.
Contribute to the development and enhancement of complaint handling processes, procedures, and training materials.
Collaborate with global teams to support post-market quality activities, audits, inspections, and compliance initiatives.
Facilitate effective communication between customers and internal stakeholders to ensure timely issue resolution.



Who You Are (Basic Qualifications)


Bachelor's degree in Life Sciences, Pharmacy, Engineering, or a related discipline.
Experience working in a regulated environment such as medical devices, pharmaceuticals, drug-delivery devices, or life sciences.
Experience in complaint handling, quality assurance, customer quality, post-market quality, or related quality/regulatory functions.
Knowledge of complaint investigations, CAPA processes, escalation management, and documentation practices.
Experience collaborating with cross-functional and global teams.
Strong analytical, organizational, and communication skills.
Experience with Microsoft Office and electronic quality management/business systems such as SAP or equivalent.



What Will Put You Ahead


Experience handling medical device complaints.
Knowledge of ISO 13485, FDA 21 CFR Part 820, EU MDR, or other regulated quality frameworks.
Experience with complaint investigations, vigilance activities, post-market surveillance (PMS), or CAPA processes.
Experience supporting global customers in a regulated healthcare environment.
Knowledge of IMDRF coding, vigilance reporting, and risk management processes.
