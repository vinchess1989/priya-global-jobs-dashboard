Job Title: DevOps Engineer
Location: Pune
Job Type: Full-time
Job Description:

Senior DevOps Engineer to manage both DevOps operations and toolchain administration. This role is critical in ensuring seamless CI/CD workflows, maintaining infrastructure reliability, and optimizing DevOps tools like Nexus, SonarQube, GitHub, AWS EKS, ArgoCD, and Jenkins. The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience with at least one of these tools and a deep understanding of DevOps best practices.

Responsibilities :

DevOps Operations:
Design, implement, and maintain CI/CD pipelines using Jenkins and GitHub Actions.
Deploy, manage, and troubleshoot applications running on AWS EKS using ArgoCD.
Ensure security, compliance, and performance across the entire DevOps lifecycle.
Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting solutions for proactive issue resolution.
Support developers in optimizing deployments and improving reliability.
DevOps Tools Management:
Administer and maintain Nexus (artifact repository) and SonarQube (code quality analysis).
Manage upgrades, configurations, and integrations of Jenkins, GitHub, Github Action Runners, ArgoCD, and Kubernetes.
Automate infrastructure provisioning, backups, and recovery strategies.
Troubleshoot tool-related issues and provide operational support to development teams.
Ensure high availability and performance of DevOps tools.
Requirements:
Hands-on experience with of the following tools: Nexus, SonarQube, GitHub, AWS EKS, ArgoCD, or Jenkins.
Strong Knowledge on SDLC
Strong experience in CI/CD pipeline design, automation, and maintenance.
Strong experience in atleast on programming languages [Java, JS or .Net]
Proficiency in Kubernetes, containerization, and cloud infrastructure (AWS preferred).
Experience with scripting (Bash, Python) and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Helm).
Understanding of security best practices in DevOps environments.
Strong troubleshooting skills and ability to work in a fast-paced environment.
Ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams and drive operational efficiency.
Employee Benefits:
Group Medical Insurance
Cab facility
Meals/snacks
Continuous Learning Program
Company Profile:
Stratacent is a Global IT Consulting and Services firm, headquartered in Jersey City, NJ, with global delivery centres in Pune and Gurugram plus offices in USA, London, Canada and South Africa.
We are a leading IT services provider focusing in Financial Services, Insurance, Healthcare and Life Sciences. We help our customers in their transformation journey and provides services around Information Security, Cloud Services, Data and AI, Automation, Application Development and IT Operations.
