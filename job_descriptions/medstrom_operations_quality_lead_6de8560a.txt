Operations Quality Lead
Castle Donington, Derbyshire

Are you passionate about quality, operational processes, and continuous improvement? Would you like to play a key role in ensuring that medical devices and healthcare services meet the highest quality assurance standards? If so, we'd love to hear from you.

At Medstrom, we are proud to provide proven, well-evidenced products and services that support our busy customers and help make a real difference to patient outcomes.

Established in 2010, Medstrom has grown substantially while remaining patient-focused and committed to maintaining an open, supportive culture. In 2022, Medstrom became an Employee Ownership Trust (EOT), giving every employee a vested interest in the company and the opportunity to contribute to and share in our success.

We are looking for an enthusiastic and experienced Operations Quality Lead to join our support team at our manufacturing facility in Castle Donington.

The Benefits

- Starting salary of £34,000 - £40,000, depending on experience
- Contributory pension scheme
- 33 days’ annual leave entitlement (incl. Bank Holidays)
- Discounts and cashback on shopping, supermarkets, and holidays as part of our rewards scheme
- Salary sacrifice schemes, including Cycle to Work
- Structured training, ongoing professional development, and mentoring opportunities
- Death in Service benefit provided
- Birthday voucher
- Employee Ownership Trust (EOT) membership, with the associated benefits of having a stake in the company

The Role

Reporting to the Director of Quality Assurance and Regulatory Affairs, you will lead and oversee quality assurance and quality control activities across Medstrom's operational functions, including Manufacturing, Service and Logistics.

The Operations Quality Lead will take ownership of day-to-day operational quality activities, ensuring that appropriate inspection and quality control processes are established.

Responsibilities include:

- Take ownership of incoming goods inspection processes
- Ensure appropriate inspection plans, sampling requirements and acceptance criteria are established and maintained
- Identify, document and manage defective, damaged or non-conforming incoming materials
- Oversee quality inspection activities across Manufacturing, Service and Logistics
- Monitor the quality of products handled, stored, prepared and dispatched by Logistics
- Take ownership of operational non-conformances, ensuring issues are accurately recorded, investigated and appropriately managed
- Identify opportunities to improve operational quality, reduce defects, minimise waste and improve right-first-time performance
- Work with Manufacturing, Service and Logistics management teams to implement improvements to operational processes and quality controls

Working hours: Monday to Friday, 8am - 4pm.

About You

To succeed in this role, you'll have:

Essential

- Previous experience in a quality-focused role within an operational, manufacturing, service or logistics environment
- Good understanding of quality control principles, inspection processes and non-conformance management
- Experience of investigating quality issues and identifying root causes
- Strong attention to detail and a methodical approach to work
- Good IT skills, including the ability to maintain quality records, analyse data and prepare reports
- Strong communication and interpersonal skills

Desirable

- Experience working within a regulated manufacturing or medical device environment
- Knowledge and understanding of ISO 13485 and QMS requirements
- Experience of supplier quality management and incoming goods inspection
- Understanding of CAPA, root cause analysis and continuous improvement methodologies
- Knowledge of quality data analysis and KPI reporting
- Relevant quality qualification or training (e.g. ISO 13485 Internal Auditor, Quality Management or equivalent)

In addition to the salary and related benefits, you will join a company that has grown extensively within the last few years and, through our unique ownership model, is continuing to grow, whilst offering great career opportunities for those who are keen to progress.
