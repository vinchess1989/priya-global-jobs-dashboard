THE ROLE




Churchill is seeking an experienced Document Controller/Manager to support the Data Center Operations team for a Fortune 100 technology firm. In this role, you will be managing document architecture and process standardisation. This role requires someone who has experience with information consolidation, document management and the creation and implementation of operational playbooks, guidelines and websites/intranets. The Document Manager will play a key role in reviewing, creating and publishing regional guides and templates to be embedded across the full EMEA portfolio.




RESPONSIBILITIES

Design and Implement standardised document templates.
Enforce semantic structures optimised for AI skimming and LLM parsing.
Creation and publish a unified regional style guide, detailing typography, document structure and publication standards.
Build and maintain a Master Document Catalog
Extract and consolidate technical knowledge from legacy slide decks into structured document repositories and regional website/intranet sites.
Set up and ground a dedicated NotebookLM repository
Establish automated workflows to trigger review cycles by document owners
Change Management to drive adoption of new document standards across EMEA
Deliver Change Management roadshows and user guides to transition teams to the new document ecosystem.
Facilitate training workshops to transition long term maintenance of deliverables.
Coordinate cross-functional meetings with internal stakeholders and external partners where required.
Prepare and distribute meeting agendas and meeting minutes, capturing decisions, action items, and follow-ups.
Hybrid Working - Minimum 2 days on site (Waltham Cross nearest Train Station) / 1 Office (London Bridge) / 2 working from home
