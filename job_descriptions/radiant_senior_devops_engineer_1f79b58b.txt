Job Title: Senior DevOps Engineer

Reports to: Engineering Manager

Location: UK / Europe

Company Overview:

Radiant is redefining how AI infrastructure is built.

We design and operate AI-native cloud platforms engineered for sovereignty, performance, and scale. Our infrastructure powers GPU-native workloads, multi-tenant control planes, and high-performance AI systems designed for the most demanding environments.

We are not building a generic cloud. We are building purpose-built AI infrastructure — from powered land, to compute, to software.

As we scale, we’re building this organisation from the ground up. We’re looking for people who’d rather build the playbook than inherit one — who are comfortable doing the work themselves as well as directing others, and who can adapt as fast as our strategy does. This is a startup model: attitude and adaptability matter more to us than years of experience or the size of the team you’ve previously managed.

About The Role

As a Senior DevOps Engineer at Radiant, you’ll be a hands-on engineer at the heart of our engineering organisation, owning the design, delivery and reliability of critical infrastructure. You’ll automate and continuously improve our deployment pipelines, cloud and bare-metal environments, and Kubernetes-based orchestration layer, so our systems scale reliably to support high-performance AI and GPU workloads.

You’ll partner with Infrastructure, SRE and Product Engineering teams to embed a strong DevOps culture across the organisation. You’ll champion infrastructure as code, observability and automation at every layer of the stack, and help improve how we build and operate systems at scale.

Our engineering teams cover Infrastructure, Cluster, Network, Storage, Cloud, Observability, Security, and AI (Training & Inference).

Our Engineering teams are grouped as follows:


Infrastructure: Compute, Infra, Network & Storage
Platform: Cloud, Observability & Security
AI: Training & Inference


Key Responsibilities


Design, build, and continuously optimise CI/CD pipelines in GitHub Actions to support fast, reliable, and repeatable software delivery
Own and evolve Infrastructure as Code practices using Terraform and Ansible, ensuring infrastructure is version-controlled, peer-reviewed, and consistently provisioned across dev, staging, and production
Scale CI to handle agent-driven change volume, including merge queues and fast, parallel test runs
Identify and eliminate human and agent bottlenecks across the software development lifecycle
Instrument DORA metrics and agent adoption: agent versus hand-written merges, defect and rework rates
Manage and maintain hosted application environments, ensuring high availability, scalability, and smooth deployment of services into production
Partner closely with Engineers to deeply understand their development workflows and release processes, identifying and removing friction points that slow delivery
Measure and improve the developer experience, improving productivity
Champion a self-service DevOps culture, enabling development teams to own their deployments and operational concerns through well-designed tooling, documentation, and guardrails
Help make runbooks and decision records a natural part of our definition of done


Your Profile


Proven experience in DevOps engineering and best practices
Hands-on experience running and automating infrastructure across public cloud and bare-metal environments
Strong experience with CI/CD workflows, observability, security and automation best practices
Strong experience with Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform and Ansible
Deep understanding of Kubernetes, Helm and other containerisation related tooling
Demonstrable scripting ability in Go, Python and Shell
Strong knowledge of branching, deployment strategies including the use of Git based version control


What You Bring


Automation instinct: If you do it twice, you automate it.
Engineering quality: Write clean, tested, maintainable code, and review others’ work with care.
Ownership: Take features from design through production, and stay accountable for how they run.
Pragmatism: Ship what matters, simply, and improve it from real-world feedback.
Collaboration: Work openly with product, infrastructure and SRE, and share what you learn.


Qualities we look for:


Set the standard: Every single day, you spot opportunities to constructively shake things up
Inspire the change: There's no blueprint for the future. You'll embrace challenges and change
You're real and you're true to yourself: We cherish and celebrate diversity so you'll feel right at home whoever you are and whoever you're talking to, you treat everyone the same.


Why should you join us?

What sets us apart is our blend of modern technology, competitive benefits, and an open, welcoming work culture that enables our people to thrive.

Here are just some of the great things you can expect from us:


25 days of annual leave
A culture that emphasises results over hierarchy, process & ego: we place great emphasis on the quality, ingenuity and creativity of work.
Open communication, regular feedback: we value smooth collaboration, direct and actionable feedback, and believe that leading with empathy and a growth mindset makes us better together.
Learning Time: we all have dedicated learning time to focus on new skills, projects or interests that lay outside of your day-to-day job.
