We are looking for an experienced IT Specialist with strong Kubernetes expertise to manage, support, and optimize containerized applications and infrastructure. The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience deploying, scaling, maintaining, and troubleshooting Kubernetes clusters across both on-premises and public cloud environments, along with a strong understanding of cloud and bare-metal infrastructure.

This role requires strong troubleshooting skills, an automation-focused mindset, and the ability to collaborate effectively with development and infrastructure teams in a fast-paced technical environment.

Responsibilities


Deploy, configure, manage, and maintain Kubernetes clusters across on-premises and cloud environments
Monitor, troubleshoot, and optimize the performance and availability of Kubernetes clusters and containerized applications
Implement and maintain CI/CD pipelines to support automated application deployment and infrastructure changes
Diagnose and resolve issues related to Kubernetes, Docker, networking, storage, and container orchestration
Collaborate with development and infrastructure teams to design scalable, reliable, and highly available solutions
Implement and maintain security best practices within Kubernetes environments
Automate infrastructure provisioning, configuration, and deployment using tools such as Terraform and Ansible
Perform regular system audits, upgrades, patching, capacity planning, and performance tuning
Develop and maintain technical documentation for Kubernetes environments, configurations, procedures, and operational processes
Evaluate new Kubernetes capabilities, technologies, and industry best practices to continuously improve the environment
Support both cloud-based and bare-metal infrastructure supporting containerized workloads


Required Qualifications


Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience
Proven hands-on experience with Kubernetes and container orchestration
Strong knowledge of Docker and containerization technologies
Minimum three years of experience working with Microsoft Azure, including Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS)
Experience deploying and managing Kubernetes within both cloud and on-premises environments
Strong understanding of infrastructure, networking, storage, and Linux-based systems
Proficiency with scripting and automation using PowerShell, Python, or similar languages
Experience with CI/CD tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, or similar technologies
Experience with infrastructure-as-code and configuration management tools such as Terraform and Ansible
Strong troubleshooting, analytical, and problem-solving skills
Excellent technical communication and collaboration skills
Strong attention to detail and ability to work effectively in a fast-paced technical environment


Nice-to-Have Certifications


CNCF Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA)
Microsoft Certified: DevOps Engineer Expert
