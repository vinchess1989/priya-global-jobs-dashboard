Have you got previous experience writing data cards and standard operating procedures (SOPs) with a high precision manufacturing environment?

Are you familiar with any of the following processes such as Machining, EDM Machining, Multi axis laser, welding or forming metals with hydraulics presses?




If so, then please read on.




The basics:

Job Title: Technical Author - Aerospace/Power & Energy
Environment: Manufacturing
Location: near Redditch
Hours: Monday to Thursday (7.30am – 4.30pm) Friday (7.30am – 12.30pm) - 39 hours per week
Holidays/Benefits: Bonus scheme, salary sacrifice pension, Life assurance, sick pay, 25 days holiday + bank holidays,

Overview:

With manufacturing facilities located across the UK, we now require a Technical Author to join the team. You will be part of the Process and Manufacturing Engineering teams with a key focus around creating new methods of manufacture, writing standard operating procedures (SOPs) for new product introduction and data cards.




The ideal candidate / Technical Authormay have some of the following experiences, however, please note these are only desirable and not essential as all applications will be looked at individually:

Previous experience working with any of the following processes - EDM Machining, Multi axis laser, welding or forming metals with hydraulics presses.
Previously worked as a manufacturing engineer, process engineer, methods engineer, Technical author with a competency to write and define the manufacturing steps and create the technical process data cards

Commutable from:

Redditch, Alcester, Solihull, Bromsgrove, Coventry, Worcester, Birmingham and surrounding areas.

Interested:

If you are interested in the Technical Author role, please apply immediately due to the urgency of this role.
