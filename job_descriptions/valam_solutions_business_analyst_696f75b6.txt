Key Responsibilities


Run backlog refinement with the PO and SMEs; write user stories with Given/When/Then acceptance criteria.
Produce agent-ready specifications: context, business rules, edge cases, examples and sample data.
Define role/permission matrices, agency and user lifecycle rules (onboarding, delegation, deactivation) and configuration rules.
Use AI to draft and refine stories, rules tables and test scenarios, and validate them against policy and SME input.
Keep traceability from requirement to story, test and defect; support UAT and acceptance.


Identity and access; agency and user setup/onboarding; role-based access control (RBAC); system configuration.

Technical Skills


Requirements engineering, BPMN/process modelling and decision tables
Azure DevOps (backlog, traceability)
Reading data models and APIs at a functional level
