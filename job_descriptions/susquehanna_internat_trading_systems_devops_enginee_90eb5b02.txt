Overview

Join Susquehanna’s trading technology team to support, deploy and automate the highly distributed, on-premises Service Fabric/Kubernetes microservices platform powering our trading systems across Asia Pacific. Working within the trading systems development team, you’ll collaborate daily with developers and traders, improving application deployment, monitoring and operational reliability through Octopus Deploy, GitLab pipelines, Ansible, Python and PowerShell.

What You’ll Do


Become a subject matter expert in our in-house trading systems and the highly distributed microservices platform and architecture they run on
Collaborate closely with trading system developers on design, releases, and operational readiness, so new features run reliably in production
Scope and build out new sites end-to-end, covering hardware, network, cluster deployment, and monitoring
Support, deploy, and troubleshoot microservices across our on-premises Service Fabric/Kubernetes environment
Troubleshoot service-to-service communication (gRPC) and multicast data feeds across the platform
Automate deployments using Octopus Deploy, GitLab pipelines, and Ansible
Automate configuration and routine operational tasks using Python and PowerShell scripting
Support, build, and improve our monitoring and observability (metrics, logs, dashboards, and alerting) using tools such as Grafana, Prometheus, and ELK, so we get early warning of issues in our trading environment
Act as the link between traders, in-house technology teams, and external parties (exchanges, vendors), keeping information on system issues and changes flowing
Find ways to improve the stability, resilience, and reliability of our platform and trading environment through better use of technology
Develop automated system tests to validate releases before they go live
Keep technical and user documentation up to date for the systems you support


What We're Looking For


A degree in Computer Science or a related technical field
At least 3 years of commercial experience in financial services or a similarly demanding production environment, ideally in trading or trading technology
Experience supporting highly distributed, microservices-based systems in production
Strong communication skills with technical and non-technical colleagues and external parties
The ability to handle tight deadlines and shifting priorities in a trading-room environment


Technical Skills


Strong Windows administration skills, including supporting and deploying applications on Windows servers
Hands-on experience with Service Fabric and/or Kubernetes, running microservices platforms in on-premises or hybrid environments
Experience automating deployments with Octopus Deploy, GitLab pipelines, and Ansible (CI/CD)
Solid understanding of highly distributed and microservices architectures, ideally including Microsoft Orleans, enough to trace and troubleshoot issues across services
Working knowledge of gRPC and Protobuf for service-to-service communication
Understanding of multicast data feeds, and the ability to debug them and related protocols such as TCP/UDP
Scripting and automation experience in Python and PowerShell. You don't need to be an expert in both, but you do need prior scripting or programming experience and the ability to learn
Experience supporting monitoring and observability tooling, including Prometheus, the ELK stack (Elasticsearch, Logstash, Kibana), and building and using dashboards in Grafana
Experience with InfluxDB for storing and querying time-series monitoring data
Experience with Azure Service Fabric for hosting and managing microservices
Experience with Kafka for streaming and messaging
Familiarity with MongoDB, enough to investigate system issues (advanced knowledge isn't required).


Nice to have


An understanding of financial markets and instruments, across equities and derivatives (not essential)
C# development skills (not essential, but highly regarded)
Experience with containerisation using Docker
Experience with analytical data stores such as ClickHouse
Experience supporting a trading environment and working directly with developers and end-users.


What’s In It For You

Private healthcare and gym allowances, plus wellness initiatives
Additional leave entitlements including marriage leave
Fully stocked kitchen, with daily breakfast and lunch
Regular social, sporting and community events including annual poker tournament
Matched donations and corporate discounts.


About Susquehanna

Susquehanna is a global quantitative trading firm powered by scientific rigor, curiosity, and innovation. Our culture is intellectually driven and highly collaborative, bringing together researchers, engineers, and traders to design and deploy impactful strategies in our systematic trading environment. To meet the unique challenges of global markets, Susquehanna applies machine learning and advanced quantitative research to vast datasets in order to uncover actionable insights and build effective strategies. By uniting deep market expertise with cutting-edge technology, we excel in solving complex problems and pushing boundaries together.

What We Do

Susquehanna’s deep integration of trading, technology and quant research makes us experts in trading essentially all listed financial products and asset classes, with a focus on derivatives. We handle millions of trading transactions around the world every day as both a market maker and market taker. Our efforts provide liquidity and ensure competitive prices for buyers and sellers. While our presence in the market is broad, our trading desks are highly specialized to allow for a deep understanding of the unique drivers of each asset class.
