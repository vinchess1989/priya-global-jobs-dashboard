Räätälöi ansioluetteloni
The DevSecOps Application Engineering Manager is responsible for providing strategic leadership, governance, and operational oversight of the Bureau’s software engineering, DevSecOps, and release management capabilities. The role leads the development and implementation of enterprise frameworks, standards, tooling, and practices that enable secure, scalable, high-quality, and sustainable software delivery across the organisation.

This position drives the adoption of modern DevSecOps methodologies, software engineering best practices, and continuous improvement initiatives to support the Bureau’s digital transformation objectives and service delivery outcomes. It provides expert advice to senior stakeholders, leads workforce capability development, and fosters collaboration across technical teams, business areas, and external partners to ensure solutions are secure, supportable, and aligned with organisational priorities and government requirements.

The role is accountable for building a high-performing team culture, strengthening software development capability across the Bureau, monitoring enterprise software quality and security outcomes, and ensuring operational readiness for new and enhanced services. As a senior leader within IT Services, the position contributes to strategic planning, innovation, organisational change, and the achievement of broader Bureau objectives while maintaining accountability for service performance, governance, risk management, and after-hours escalation management responsibilities.
