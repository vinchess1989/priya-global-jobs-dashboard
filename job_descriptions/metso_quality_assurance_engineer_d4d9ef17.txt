Metso’s Axo33 platform combines real-time digital modelling, decision support and automation for mine-to-port operations. Deployed globally across stockyards, rail and shipping environments, it helps improve throughput, storage efficiency and operational safety.

We are seeking a Quality Assurance Engineer to help ensure Axo33 software is well tested, clearly documented and ready for deployment in live industrial environments.

The role may suit someone from testing, QA, technical writing, software support, engineering delivery or industrial systems. You need not be a developer or have worked in mining, but should be comfortable investigating software with technical teams.

About the Role

You will test software, investigate defects and support release readiness while creating clear documentation for engineers, delivery teams and end users. Experienced engineers will help you learn Axo33 and industrial delivery.

Core Responsibilities

Develop and run test plans, cases and regression tests across Axo33.

Verify web applications, backend services, APIs, data flows and industrial integrations.

Investigate, record and track defects through to resolution.

Clarify expected behaviour, acceptance criteria and edge cases with the team.

Support release readiness, acceptance testing, deployment validation and handover.

Maintain user guides, release notes, configuration guides and test documentation.

Improve QA coverage, processes and documentation using deployment and customer feedback.

What You Bring

You bring practical testing and writing skills, sound judgement and a collaborative approach:

Experience in QA, testing, technical writing or a related technical role.

Ability to turn software behaviour into clear tests, defect reports and documentation for varied readers.

Attention to detail and a practical approach to complex systems and ambiguity.

Clear communication and collaboration with product, engineering and delivery teams.

Interest in software used in live industrial environments.

Highly Regarded

Testing web applications, APIs, databases or real-time data systems across functional, integration, regression or acceptance scenarios.

Industrial automation or OT in mining, ports, rail, logistics, manufacturing or heavy industry.

Software integrated with PLC/SCADA, telemetry or third-party platforms, or deployed on-premise.

Test automation, API testing, database validation or scripting.

Jira, Confluence, Git-based workflows or software releases.

Commissioning, FAT/SAT, deployment validation or customer acceptance testing.

Software user guides, release notes, support material or documentation standards.

Stockyards, conveyors, train or ship loaders, reclaimers, digital twins, optimisation or industrial decision support.

If you meet the core requirements but not every highly regarded skill, we encourage you to apply.

What’s in it for you

Purpose-led work: Contribute to enabling sustainable modern life and transforming the industry.

Performance-based bonus: Participate in the Metso Bonus program, tied to business and individual performance.

Health and wellbeing: Access discounted private health insurance, fitness programs and a confidential Employee Assistance Program for you and your family.

Growth: Develop through Growth Dialogues, the Metso Training Academy, mentoring and internal mobility across a global business.

Flexibility: Hybrid working options to support work-life balance.

Why Metso?

At Metso, your work will support software used in live mining and bulk materials operations, where reliability, performance and safety matter. You will join a collaborative Axo33 engineering team and help improve products deployed across stockyard, rail and shipping environments worldwide.
