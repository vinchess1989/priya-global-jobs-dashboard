About SAVA

All the health information we need is within us. Just below the skin. SAVA is redefining the way people interact with their health by developing the most advanced biosensing technology science has to offer, capable of accessing bodily information in a painless, real-time and affordable way.




The Role

At SAVA, we are rapidly scaling our next-generation biosensor designed to shift healthcare from reactive to preventative. We are looking for a passionate Senior QA Specialist to join our team! As the Senior QA Specialist, you will work closely with our Quality Manager across all quality activities - with a primary focus on implementing and maintaining an effective Quality Management System (QMS). Your role will help ensure compliance with industry regulations / standards, and drive continuous improvement across all aspects of our organisation's quality processes.

The business is at a critical growth stage, and the successful candidate will work closely with our QA / RA Lead and QA Manager to push the business closer to launching our device. You’ll collaborate cross-functionally every day to build and improve processes to allow the business to operate in an efficient and compliant way.

We’re looking for someone who is self-sufficient, can easily determine what is required vs what is ‘nice to have’ and who can embed a quality mindset throughout the organisation.




What You’ll Do
Review SAVA’s processes to support implementation of a practical Quality Management System (QMS) aligned with ISO 13485
Collaborate cross-functionally to improve SAVA’s processes while maintaining compliance
Own the design and implementation of assigned procedures from scratch
Drive QMS implementation / maintenance pre- and post- ISO 13485 certification
Own assigned product and process change management activities from a QA perspective
Own assigned nonconformances (NCs) and Corrective Actions / Preventative Actions (CAPAs), and provide cross-functional support to other functions when raising them
Review design verification / validation plans, protocols and reports for alignment with design requirements
Promote the value of a solid QMS across the business
Embed quality principles early in SAVA’s operations
Coach junior staff members







What We’re Looking For
>4 years of quality assurance experience
Experience in medical device industry, specifically new product development
Extensive knowledge of quality management principles, regulatory requirements (ISO 13485, FDA QMSR, EU MDR) and industry best practices
Practical experience working within an ISO 13485 QMS and independently running / improving quality processes
Experience owning product / QMS change requests, as well as NCs / CAPAs
Experience in regulatory audits
Strong understanding of risk management principles, design verification and design validation
Analytical and problem-solving skills with high attention to detail
Self-sufficient and proactive
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills to collaborate across departments and levels







Bonus Points For
A BSc or MSc in an engineering or science discipline
Experience implementing an ISO 13485 QMS
Experience in ISO 13485 stage 1 and stage 2 audits
Experience owning and creating processes independently from scratch







Why Sava

Few people get to work on technology that could change how millions of people understand their own bodies. Here, you will. You’ll own real problems from your first week, work alongside people who are exceptional at what they do, and see your work go straight into the product.

The problems are hard, the pace is fast and the work is often messy. If that’s what gets you out of bed in the morning, we’d love to hear from you.
