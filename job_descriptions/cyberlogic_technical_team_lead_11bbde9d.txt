Job Title

Technical Lead / Engineering Lead (.NET / Integration / DevOps Transformation)

Location

Stellenbosch (Hybrid/Remote)

Who we are

Scriptex is a software engineering firm delivering modular, cloud‑native systems that help scale‑ups and enterprises accelerate innovation - without compromising on reliability or quality. We build composable frameworks and platforms that offer operational maturity from day one, enabling faster delivery and scalable software solutions.

Who we are looking for

We are looking for a strong technical leader to join a critical engineering team operating within a large enterprise environment undergoing a significant modernisation journey.

This role is ideal for someone who understands how to lead effectively in a complex, legacy-heavy enterprise landscape while helping drive a gradual transition toward modern engineering practices, Agile delivery and DevOps maturity, and modern architecture patterns.

You will lead a team of engineers across software development and SRE/production support functions, providing technical direction, delivery leadership, mentorship, and hands-on engineering oversight.

About the Environment

This is a predominantly on-prem enterprise environment with a large portfolio of internal systems currently built on:

.NET Framework 4.7
WCF Services
SQL Server
Windows Services
IIS-hosted applications
TFS source control
ActiveBatch v10/v12 scheduling and automation

The organisation is actively modernising and transitioning toward:

.NET 8 / .NET 9
RESTful APIs
Azure DevOps (on-prem)
Git-based workflows
CI/CD pipelines
Agile delivery practices
Improved engineering standards and DevOps maturity
API management via Gravitee APIM

This role sits directly within that transformation journey.

The Opportunity

You will play a key leadership role in helping evolve the engineering culture, practices, and technical standards of the team while still operating effectively within an enterprise environment that currently follows a largely waterfall-based SDLC.

The successful candidate must be comfortable balancing:

Legacy system support and enhancement
Enterprise governance and project structures
Technical leadership and mentorship
Engineering modernisation initiatives
Agile coaching and delivery improvements
Hands-on architecture and development oversight

This is not a “greenfield startup” environment. It requires someone pragmatic, patient, technically strong, delivery-focused, and capable of driving change incrementally and collaboratively.

Responsibilities

Technical Leadership

Lead and mentor a team of engineers across development and SRE functions
Conduct regular 1-on-1s, coaching, and performance feedback
Provide technical direction across projects and production initiatives
Review code and enforce engineering standards and best practices
Drive improved software design, maintainability, and operational stability
Assist in defining and implementing branching strategies and Git workflows

Delivery & Agile Enablement

Lead Agile ceremonies including:
Daily standups
Sprint planning
Backlog refinement
Sprint reviews
Retrospectives
Work closely with Business Analysts, Project Managers, Architects, and stakeholders
Help bridge the gap between waterfall governance and Agile delivery execution
Encourage earlier technical engagement during requirements and solution definition phases
Assist the team in estimating, planning, and delivering work effectively

DevOps & Engineering Modernisation

Drive migration efforts from TFS to Azure DevOps
Help implement and mature CI/CD pipelines
Improve release and deployment processes
Assist with engineering operational maturity and DevOps practices
Work closely with SRE/production support teams on incident-driven backlog improvements
Support migration from WCF services toward modern REST API architecture
Contribute toward API strategy using Gravitee APIM

Architecture & Solution Design

Collaborate with Solution Architects on enterprise solution design
Provide engineering input into architectural decisions
Promote scalable, maintainable, and supportable solutions
Identify opportunities for modernisation and technical debt reduction

Required Experience and Skills

Technical

Strong experience with C# and the Microsoft ecosystem
Solid experience with:
.NET Framework
.NET Core / .NET 8+
REST APIs
WCF Services
SQL Server
Stored procedures and database-driven systems
IIS and Windows Server environments
Experience with Azure DevOps and Git-based workflows
Experience implementing CI/CD pipelines
Strong understanding of software engineering best practices and SDLC processes

Leadership

Experience leading or mentoring engineering teams
Strong stakeholder communication skills
Comfortable working with Business Analysts, Project Managers, Architects, and Operations teams
Experience driving engineering standards and technical direction
Ability to guide junior and intermediate developers effectively

Nice to Have

Experience with enterprise modernisation/transformation initiatives
Exposure to SRE or production support environments
Experience with API Management platforms
Experience migrating legacy enterprise systems
Exposure to Agile transformation initiatives
Understanding of DevOps operating models and release governance

Attributes

Understands enterprise environments and legacy system realities
Can balance delivery with engineering environment
Is patient and pragmatic when driving change
Can influence teams and stakeholders positively
Wants to help shape a more modern engineering culture
Is comfortable being hands-on while also leading people
Will embrace the transformation journey

Team Structure

You will work closely with:
Developers
SRE / DevOps Engineers
Business Analysts
Project Managers
Solution Architects
IT Leadership and Enterprise Stakeholders

The engineering function is evolving, and this role will have significant influence in shaping future ways of work, engineering standards, and delivery practices.

This is an opportunity to:

Lead meaningful engineering transformation
Influence the future engineering direction of a large enterprise environment
Build modern engineering practices from within
Mentor and grow engineering talent
Help modernise a substantial legacy technology estate
Drive real operational and delivery improvements

The environment offers both complexity and opportunity for the right technical leader who enjoys solving enterprise-scale challenges while helping teams evolve toward a more mature engineering culture.

Why us?
Real Systems, Real Impact: We’re not hype-driven. We build systems people rely on.
Modern Architecture, Day One Maturity: Help define what good looks like and how best to extend what we already have.
End-to-End Ownership: From architecture and DevOps to deployment and evolution, you’ll see your impact through every stage.
Hybrid Setup in Stellenbosch: Collaborate in person and remotely with a nimble, value-driven team.
