Content Compliance Specialist, EMEA (DePuy Synthes)

About The Role

The Content Compliance Specialist plays a critical role in supporting the review and approval of advertising, promotional materials, and claims across the DePuy Synthes EMEA region. Acting as a moderator within the copy approval process, this role coordinates submissions, facilitates review activities, ensures compliance with established procedures, and supports timely delivery of compliant content to the business.

The successful candidate will work closely with Marketing, Regulatory Affairs, Quality, and other cross-functional stakeholders to drive operational excellence, maintain audit-ready documentation, and continuously improve the efficiency and quality of content approval processes.

Key Responsibilities


Coordinate and moderate copy approval workflows for advertising, promotional materials, and claims.
Conduct submission quality and completeness reviews to ensure efficient processing.
Assign reviewers, facilitate review meetings, document outcomes, and track required follow-up actions.
Monitor submissions throughout approval, renewal, reapproval, and closure activities.
Provide process guidance and workflow support to submitters, reviewers, and stakeholders.
Maintain complete, accurate, and audit-ready records in accordance with applicable procedures and quality requirements.
Support SOP implementation, audit activities, CAPAs, and other quality and compliance initiatives.
Contribute to operational reporting and identify opportunities to improve submission quality, cycle times, and process efficiency.
Partner effectively with colleagues and stakeholders across multiple EMEA markets.


Qualifications


Bachelor's degree or equivalent professional experience.
Minimum 2 years of experience in Compliance, Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Content Review, Marketing Operations, or another regulated environment.
Experience working with approval workflows, documentation controls, and compliance processes.
Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
Fluent English required; additional European languages are an advantage.


What You'll Bring


Strong coordination and workflow management capabilities.
High attention to detail and commitment to quality.
Effective communication and stakeholder engagement skills.
Sound judgment and problem-solving abilities.
A continuous improvement mindset focused on delivering efficient and compliant business processes.


Why Join Us

Join a dynamic regional team that enables compliant commercialization across DePuy Synthes EMEA. You'll have the opportunity to collaborate with diverse stakeholders, contribute to strategic business objectives, and help shape best-in-class content compliance operations in a highly regulated environment.
