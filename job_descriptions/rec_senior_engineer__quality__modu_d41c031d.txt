Responsibilities

ROLE / JOB SUMMARY:

The incumbent shall execute the quality assurance role with good technical rationale and understanding, acquired from developing an appreciation of the industry practices, and applicable technology developments.

Scope of work includes quality assurance planning, supplier management, in-process quality control design and outgoing quality controls. Entry level candidates are also encouraged to apply, and for those who wish to make a career in Quality in a World Class High Tech Manufacturing Facility in Singapore.

Essential Duties And Responsibilities


Quality Assurance Planning
Develop and implement quality control plan for adequacy and suitability for the production and during new product introduction
Drive improvement of material, process and product quality by monitoring quality indicators (SPC, defect rates)
Non-conforming materials and products management
Benchmark, develop/ innovate and implement inspection methods and procedures to meet quality objectives, product quality, quality assurance, technical goals and productivity savings. 
Support customer audits and 3rd party inspection routines (by way of preparing quality data presentation, technical write ups).
Develop understanding of product reliability and its corresponding components' contribution (based on product performance characterization and defect understanding)
Support product qualification process (Provide quality requirements in FMEA, SPC, DOE, Control plan and other processes and procedures as required/suitable)
Supplier Management
Manage the qualification of new materials/ suppliers & incoming quality of the materials
Drive supplier quality through audits to ensure conformance to specification and continuous improvement in system, procedures and processes.
Supervision of Quality Inspectors that support Production Operations
Manage team of quality inspectors on daily routines and communications
Plan shift activities with inspector team leads
Quality Management System
Participate in internal quality audits to drive conformance and continuous improvement in product quality
Execute in accordance to QMS requirements (ensure timely updates to documents, WI, SOPs)


Essential Job Competencies

Technical


Understanding of the technological developments in the industry on both products and components
Experience in reliability engineering would be advantageous


Analytical & Problem Solving


Engineering knowledge in using quality tools such as 5 Whys, DMAIC for problem solving


Soft Skills & Professional Attributes


Aptitude in higher level use of Excel (E.g.: Formulas and Macros) is an added advantage
Knowledge in SPC controls or lean and six sigma methods.
Expect to develop as an expert in Quality Management and related standards and requirements (ISO 9001, IEC62941), if not already adopt.


Leadership


Experience in leading or driving a team in group activities.


Project Management


Ability to handle experimental planning and perform product testing and report writing.


Qualifications

Minimum bachelor's degree (engineering discipline).

Other Information

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS AND EXPERIENCE:


Proficient in MS Excel and Word.
Experience in SAP, Knowledge of Toyota Production System (TPS) and Lean Manufacturing principles will be added advantage.
Strong sense of ownership and self-motivation, good initiative, work independently.
Proactive working attitude - hands on approach
Good time management with ability to handle pressure and multitask under tight timelines
Team player. Ability to work well with others.
Excellent interpersonal skills with the ability to communicate with cross functional team.
Excellent presentation, interpersonal and communication skills with demonstrated ability to communicate effectively with all levels of management, both internally and externally.
Experience in leading or driving a team in group activities
Experience in project handling, follow up timeline, meeting targeted goals.
Ability for a 2nd / 3rd spoken language is an advantage.
Willing to travel
No color blindness.
Physically fit.
