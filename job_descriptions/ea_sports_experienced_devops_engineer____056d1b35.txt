Description & Requirements

Electronic Arts creates next-level entertainment experiences that inspire players and fans around the world. Here, everyone is part of the story. Part of a community that connects across the globe. A place where creativity thrives, new perspectives are invited, and ideas matter. A team where everyone makes play happen.

The EA SPORTS™ Technical Team is seeking an experienced DevOps Engineer with a passion for technology and a strong background in designing, deploying, and operating solutions in public cloud environments integrated with corporate infrastructure and services.

You will collaborate with peers and technical leadership to deliver technical initiatives, support large scale systems, and improve platform reliability and operational efficiency. The role requires hands on expertise in cloud infrastructure, Linux, networking, and automation, along with the ability to troubleshoot complex issues and balance competing priorities. On-call support is also required.

Your Responsibilities


Cloud Engineering: Design, implement, and maintain secure, scalable, and supportable cloud infrastructure.
Troubleshooting: Diagnose and resolve deployment, networking, Linux, performance, and availability issues in production environments.
Automation: Develop tools and scripts to automate routine tasks and improve operational efficiency.
Production Support: Support always on gaming infrastructure and participate in on-call support for live events, feature launches, and emergency escalations.
Continuous Improvement: Evaluate and implement technologies that improve reliability, scalability, and team productivity.
Collaboration: Work with peers, technical leadership, Development, QA, IT, and external partners to deliver technical initiatives.
Documentation: Maintain system documentation, operational procedures, and support runbooks.


Your Qualifications


Experience: 3+ years designing, implementing, and operating public cloud infrastructure and production systems.
Cloud Platforms: Experience with Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
Infrastructure & Containers: Experience with Terraform, Kubernetes, Helm, Docker, Chef, or Ansible.
Automation: Proficiency in at least one scripting or programming language, such as Python, Ruby, or Go.
Monitoring & CI/CD: Experience with Prometheus, Grafana, Jenkins, GitLab, or GitHub.
Version Control: Working knowledge of Git.
Linux, Security & Networking: Strong understanding of Linux administration, system security, and cloud networking, including DNS, routing, subnetting, NAT, firewalls, and load balancing.
Databases: Working knowledge of relational and NoSQL databases, performance tuning, and database security.
Cloud Migration: Experience migrating or modernizing applications for public cloud environments is an asset.
Continuous Learning & Innovation: Passion for learning new technologies, exploring emerging DevOps practices, and applying new skills to improve automation, infrastructure reliability, and platform capabilities.
