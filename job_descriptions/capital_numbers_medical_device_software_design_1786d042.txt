Job Description:




Position Overview:




We are seeking an experienced Medical Device Software Design Control & Regulatory Specialist to support the development of a regulated, software-driven surgical navigation system. The role will work closely with the software engineering team and the client's Regulatory Affairs and Quality Assurance (RA/QA) stakeholders to establish, implement and maintain software design controls, verification and validation (V&V) activities, risk-management documentation and regulatory compliance deliverables.




The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience with medical-device software development and quality processes, particularly IEC 62304, FDA 510(k) submissions, ISO 14971 and ISO 13485. The candidate should be capable of translating regulatory requirements into practical software engineering processes and documentation.




Project Context

The project involves an image-guided surgical navigation application used during Total Ankle Replacement procedures.




The system includes:

An offline, Linux-based software application.
Integration with third-party camera SDKs.
Registration and tracking algorithms.
An off-the-shelf AI-based segmentation model.
Encrypted patient case-file exchange through USB.
The software is being developed within a regulated medical-device environment and
requires robust design controls, software lifecycle documentation, risk management
and verification and validation processes.




Key Responsibilities:




1. Software Design Controls & Lifecycle Management

Establish and maintain software design-control processes in accordance with FDA 21 CFR 820, QMSR and ISO 13485 requirements.
Develop, maintain and manage IEC 62304 software lifecycle documentation.
Prepare and review Software Development Plans, software requirements specifications, software architecture and detailed design documents.
Ensure software development activities are aligned with the applicable software safety classification.




2. Software Verification & Validation

Define and implement software verification and validation strategies.
Prepare and review test protocols, test reports and verification evidence.
Establish and maintain end-to-end traceability across software requirements, design, implementation and testing.
Ensure test results and verification evidence are traceable to specific software versions.
Support unit, integration and system-level verification activities.




3. Software Risk Management

Work with the client's risk-management team to identify software-related hazards and define appropriate risk controls.
Support ISO 14971 risk-management activities, including hazard analysis and software risk assessments.
Ensure risk-control measures are implemented and supported by verification evidence.
Maintain software-related contributions to the overall risk-management documentation.




4. SOUP & Software Bill of Materials (SBOM) Management

Identify and maintain Software of Unknown Provenance (SOUP) components, including
operating systems, third-party SDKs, open-source libraries and commercial off-the-shelf software.
Maintain SBOM documentation, component versions and supporting justifications.
Assess known anomalies, vulnerabilities and potential risks associated with third-party software components.




5. Medical Device Cybersecurity

Prepare and maintain software cybersecurity documentation in accordance with applicable
FDA premarket cybersecurity expectations.
Develop and review threat models and cybersecurity risk assessments.
Support security risk-management activities, vulnerability assessments and related documentation.
Ensure cybersecurity considerations are incorporated into the software development lifecycle.




6. Regulatory Documentation & FDA 510(k) Support

Prepare and maintain software documentation required for FDA 510(k) submissions.
Support software documentation requirements in accordance with FDA&#39;s 2023 guidance,
Content of Premarket Submissions for Device Software Functions.
Understand the applicability of Basic and Enhanced Documentation Levels.
Prepare DHF-ready software documentation and ensure engineering deliverables support the client's regulatory submission requirements.




7. Human Factors, Design Reviews & Quality Processes

Support usability engineering and human-factors documentation in accordance with IEC 62366-1 and relevant FDA guidance.
Participate in software design reviews and technical documentation reviews.
Support configuration management, change control and software problem-resolution activities.
Maintain software anomaly records and ensure appropriate investigation and resolution in accordance with IEC 62304.




8. Engineering & Stakeholder Collaboration

Work closely with software developers, engineering leads and the client&#39;s RA/QA team.
Review software architecture and technical documentation and communicate regulatory and quality requirements to engineering teams.
Act as the interface between the engineering team and the client's regulatory and quality stakeholders.




Mandatory Qualifications & Skills

Minimum 5 years of experience in medical-device software development, software quality, design assurance or regulatory affairs; 8+ years preferred for senior-level candidates.
Experience working on at least one Class II medical device, preferably Class III, through design controls and an FDA 510(k) or PMA pathway.
Hands-on experience authoring IEC 62304 software lifecycle documentation, preferably for Class C medical-device software.
Strong working knowledge of ISO 14971 and its application to software risk management.
Practical experience with FDA 21 CFR 820 design controls, QMSR and ISO 13485.
Direct experience preparing software documentation for FDA 510(k) submissions.
Strong knowledge of software verification and validation, including test protocols, test reports and requirements traceability.
Experience with medical-device cybersecurity documentation, including threat modelling, SBOM and SOUP management.
Familiarity with IEC 62366-1 and FDA human-factors guidance.
Technical understanding sufficient to review Python/C++ codebases, Linux-based software architecture and software traceability.
Experience preparing and maintaining Design History File (DHF) documentation.
Excellent technical documentation, communication and stakeholder-management skills.




Preferred Qualifications

Experience in surgical navigation, computer-assisted surgery, image-guided or stereotaxic medical-device systems.
Exposure to orthopedic devices, total-joint replacement or surgical navigation platforms.
Experience with AI/ML-enabled medical devices or Software as a Medical Device (SaMD).
Familiarity with FDA guidance for AI-enabled medical-device software and Good Machine Learning Practice (GMLP).
Experience with AAMI TIR45 and TIR57.
Exposure to IEC 60601-1.
RAC-Devices certification or equivalent regulatory certification.
Previous involvement in FDA-cleared surgical navigation or medical robotics 510(k) submissions.




Applicable Standards & Regulations

Candidates should have practical working knowledge of the following:
IEC 62304 – Medical Device Software Lifecycle Processes.
ISO 14971 – Medical Device Risk Management.
ISO 13485 – Medical Device Quality Management Systems.
FDA QMSR / 21 CFR 820 – Quality System Regulation and Design Controls.
FDA 21 CFR 882.4560 – Orthopedic Stereotaxic Instrument.
FDA 21 CFR 892.2020 – Digital Image Communications.
FDA Premarket Software Documentation Guidance (2023).
FDA Premarket Cybersecurity Guidance and FD&C Act Section 524B.
IEC 62366-1 – Medical Device Usability Engineering.
AAMI TIR45 / TIR57.
IEC 60601-1 – Medical Electrical Equipment: General Requirements for Basic Safety and Essential Performance.




Key Deliverables

The specialist will be responsible for developing or supporting the following project deliverables:
Software Development Plan.
Software Requirements Specification.
Software Architecture and Detailed Design Documentation.
Software Verification and Validation Protocols and Reports.
Software Requirements Traceability Matrix.
SOUP Register and Software Bill of Materials (SBOM).
Software Cybersecurity Documentation and Threat Model.
Software Risk-Management Documentation.
Human Factors and Usability Engineering Inputs.
Configuration-Management Plan and Problem-Resolution Records.
DHF-ready software documentation package, including software-related inputs to the FDA 510(k) submission.




Reporting & Collaboration

The specialist will work as a dedicated resource embedded within the engineering project team, collaborating closely with the client's RA/QA stakeholders.

The specialist will ensure that the software design-control, verification, validation and regulatory documentation produced by the engineering team meets the applicable requirements and can be integrated into the client&#39;s DHF and regulatory submission.

This is a hands-on, full-time project role requiring ownership of software design-control and V&V deliverables, rather than a part-time regulatory review or advisory position.
