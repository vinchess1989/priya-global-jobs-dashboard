ABOUT THE ROLE

We are looking for a proactive, hands-on QA Automation & CI / Lab Engineer in Hong Kong to own automated testing pipelines, conduct offline evaluations, and manage our on-site test hardware lab. You will maintain automated test suites for 3D reconstruction and spatial computing projects, execute and automate offline evaluation workflows, keep our lab hardware fleet healthy and up to date, and build scripts and tooling that improve the daily engineering workflow of the team. Strong proficiency in using AI/LLM coding tools to rapidly build and maintain automation is highly valued.




KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Automated Testing & Maintenance: Take ownership of automated test suites for projects in maintenance and active development across macOS, iOS, and visionOS. Monitor scheduled build and test jobs, analyze test failures, and file

detailed bug reports.

Offline Evaluation: Carry out and automate offline evaluation runs for computer vision and media projects—executing batch evaluation pipelines across datasets, aggregating quality metrics, analyzing regressions, and generating evaluation reports for engineers.

CI/CD & Scripting: Develop and maintain scripts and tools (Bash, Python, Jenkins/CI pipelines) to automate periodic builds, OS/firmware updates, and recurring regression/evaluation jobs.

Test Hardware & Lab Management: Manage and maintain the office test device fleet (Macs, iPhones, iPads, and head-mounted displays). Keep test hardware updated, connected, and reliable for automated and remote execution.

Lab & Network Room Upkeep: Organize, cable-manage, and maintain the physical lab environment and network room to ensure high availability and a clean, well-documented hardware setup.

Developer Quality-of-Life: Proactively identify bottlenecks in the team's testing, evaluation, or hardware setup and build practical tools or automations to solve them.

MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

Solid experience in QA automation, CI/CD administration (e.g., Jenkins), and scripting (Bash/Shell, Python).

Hands-on experience managing macOS/iOS device fleets, hardware provisioning, or physical test lab environments.

Basic hardware troubleshooting and physical lab organization skills.

Clear written and verbal communication skills in English, with a self-starter mindset.




PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS (BONUS)

AI / LLM Tool Proficiency (Strong Plus): Strong, hands-on ability to leverage AI coding assistants and LLM-powered tools

(e.g., CLI agents, AI IDEs) to rapidly write, debug, refactor, and maintain automation scripts, CI pipelines, and internal developer tooling.

Knowledge and hands-on experience in networking (routers, switches, VLANs, subnetting, firewall/lab network setup), databases (SQL/NoSQL for storing test/evaluation results and telemetry), and backend development (building internal APIs, dashboards, or automation services).

Familiarity with Xcode command-line tools (xcodebuild, xcrun, device management) and Apple platform testing frameworks (XCTest).● Proficiency in Cantonese and/or Mandarin is a plus
