Key Responsibilities


Design, implement, and maintain robust and automated CI/CD pipelines leveraging AWS DevOps services and other tools like Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, or GitHub Actions.
Automate build, test, and deployment processes for applications, ensuring rapid and reliable releases to environments.
Integrate security scanning (SAST/DAST), vulnerability management, and compliance checks into the CI/CD pipeline (DevSecOps).


Infrastructure As Code (IaC)


Develop and maintain Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform for provisioning and managing resources.
Automate repetitive operational tasks and infrastructure configurations through scripting.


Containerization & Orchestration


Hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes for containerizing, deploying, and managing applications.
Knowledge of Helm charts for managing Kubernetes applications.


Monitoring, Logging & Alerting


Implement and manage comprehensive monitoring and logging solutions using AWS monitoring tools, Prometheus, Grafana, or other relevant tools to ensure application and infrastructure health.
Configure alerts and dashboards to proactively identify and resolve performance bottlenecks and system issues.


Scripting & Automation


Proficiency in scripting languages such as Python, Bash, or PowerShell for automating tasks, developing custom tools, and interacting with APIs.


Collaboration & Mentorship


Collaborate effectively with development, QA, and operations teams to streamline workflows and promote DevOps principles across the organization.
Provide technical guidance and mentorship to junior engineers on DevOps practices and cloud technologies.
Champion a culture of continuous improvement, knowledge sharing, and automation.


Troubleshooting & Problem Solving


Strong analytical and problem-solving skills to diagnose and resolve complex technical issues in production and non-production environments.


Required Skills & Qualifications


Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
6-8 years of proven hands-on experience as a DevOps Engineer, with significant experience on AWS, Azure platform.
Demonstrable experience with DevOps services (Code Repositories, Build Pipelines, Deployment Pipelines, Artifact Registry, Container Registry).
Strong experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform.
Proficiency in containerization technologies (Docker) and container orchestration (Kubernetes).
Hands-on experience with CI/CD tools (e.g., Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, GitHub Actions).
Experience with monitoring and logging tools relevant to AWS / Azure/GCP, Prometheus, Grafana, ELK).
Strong scripting skills (Python, Bash).
Experience with version control systems (Git).
Understanding of networking concepts within a cloud environment (VCNs, Load Balancers, VPNs).
Excellent communication, collaboration, and interpersonal skills.
Ability to work independently and as part of a cross-functional team.
AWS certifications are highly preferred.
