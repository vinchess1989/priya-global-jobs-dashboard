Job Description

DevOps – Technical Specialist

About Citco

Since the 1940s Citco has provided specialist financial services to alternative investment funds, investors, multinationals and private clients worldwide. With over 13,000 employees in 45 countries we pioneer innovative solutions that meet our clients’ evolving needs, and deliver exceptional service.

Our continuous investment in learning means our people are among the best in the industry. And our corporate social responsibility programs provide meaningful and fulfilling work in the community.

A career at Citco isn’t just a job – it’s an opportunity to excel in an environment that genuinely supports your personal and professional development. For more information about Citco, please visit www.citco.com .

About The Role

Citco seeks a DevOps Technical Specialist to drive our automation and DevOps initiatives within the IT Operations group. This role will focus on bridging the gap between traditional IT operations and modern DevOps practices, ensuring the reliability, scalability, and efficiency of our IT infrastructure while supporting continuous integration and deployment processes. The successful candidate will manage a portfolio of automation initiatives and w ill be responsible for leading the design, implementation, and optimization of our infrastructure and configuration automation strategies .

Working as part of the Platform Services management group, this role interacts with all functions across the department. The ideal candidate will have a proven track record in both the IaC and CaC spaces, will be a creative thinker, problem solver, teacher, and posses superior interpersonal skills. He/she will serve as the organization's primary expert in Infrastructure as Code (IaC), Configuration as Code (CaC), and automation, guiding teams and setting standards for best practices. This is a hands-on technical leadership role with extensive experience of DevOps technologies and essential concepts.

Job Duties In Brief


Active member of the IT Operations Management Team
Implement and maintain automation for system provisioning, configuration, and deployment
Lead the development, implementation, and maintenance of Infrastructure as Code (IaC) solutions
Design and oversee the implementation of continuous integration / continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines
Develop automation strategies and roadmaps aligned with IT Operations goals
Evaluate and recommend new technologies to enhance our automation capabilities
Establish best practices and standards for automation across the IT Operations department
Lead cross-functional teams to improve infrastructure reliability and efficiency
Implement and maintain security best practices
Conduct regular audits of automation processes and coordinate implementation of improvements
Mentor and guide team members on DevOps and automation best practices
Collaborate with IT Operations Leadership Team to align automation initiatives with strategic objectives
Develop and maintain infrastructure as code (IaC) using tools like Terraform or CloudFormation
Implement and manage configuration management tools (e.g., Ansible, Rundeck, Sempahore)
Provide governance and guidance to all IT Operations team members
Serve as the technical advocate for complex automation and infrastructure issues


Experience And Technical Skills


6+ years of experience in DevOps or similar role, with at least 3 years in a lead or senior position
Strong knowledge of Linux/Unix systems administration
Proficiency in scripting languages (e.g., Python, Bash, PowerShell)
Deep understanding of CI/CD principles and experience with enterprise-level implementations (e.g., Jenkins, GitLab CI, CircleCI, Semaphore)
Proven experience in cloud architecture, preferably across multiple platforms (AWS & Azure)
Knowledge of containerization and orchestration technologies (Docker, Kubernetes)
Extensive experience with task orchestration tools like Rundeck
Advanced knowledge of configuration management tools, especially Ansible
Expert-level proficiency in IaC tools (Terraform, CloudFormation)
Understanding of networking concepts and protocols, security best practices, and compliance requirements
Strong background in implementing and managing monitoring and observability solutions (e.g., Prometheus, ELK stack, Zabbix, SolarWinds)
Strong problem-solving and analytical skills
Experience with microservices architecture
Excellent communication and teamwork abilities
Excellent leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills.
Proactive, enthusiastic and ambitious with an ability to work effectively in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.


About You

The successful candidate is an experienced and energetic contributor, with the ability to build customer relationships, while driving alignment and strategy across the IT Operations department. You can impact both day-to-day service delivery and long-term structural group alignment and strategy definition. Mature communicator, with the ability to effectively convey information of a technical nature to non-technical audiences

Education


Bachelor's degree or technical qualification prevalent to the position
Certifications such as AWS Certified DevOps Engineer / SysOps Administrator.
