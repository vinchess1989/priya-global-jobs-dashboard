Engineering Change Management Lead – Electronics




Location: Hertfordshire

Salary: Up to £60,000




An established engineering and manufacturing business is looking for an Engineering Change Management Lead to manage product changes, technical documentation and engineering updates across its electro-mechanical products.




The Role

Coordinate engineering changes from review through to implementation.
Manage ECOs/ECNs, drawing revisions, BOMs and technical documentation.
Review 2D CAD drawings and support straightforward engineering modifications.
Work with electronics, mechanical engineering and manufacturing teams.
Support PCB updates, product improvements and production releases.
Use ERP systems to manage changes and maintain accurate records.
Ensure changes meet functional, manufacturing and compliance requirements.




About You

Experience in electronics engineering, engineering change management or a similar technical role.
Good understanding of electronic products and manufacturing.
Experience with engineering changes, revision control and technical documentation.
Able to interpret 2D drawings and electronic schematics.
Organised, proactive and confident coordinating changes across teams.




Experience with Epicor, Altium or regulated products would be beneficial.




This is an opportunity to take ownership of an important engineering function, improve processes and help deliver product updates from concept through to production.




APPLY NOW - If you have an electronics background and enjoy problem-solving and driving engineering changes, we'd be keen to hear from you.
