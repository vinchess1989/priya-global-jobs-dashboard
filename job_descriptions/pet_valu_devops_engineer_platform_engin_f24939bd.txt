Job Description

Position: DevOps Engineer/Platform Engineer - Azure

Type: Full-Time | Permanent | Hybrid

Location: Markham, ON

Reports to: Director, Security and Infrastructure, IT

About Us

Pet Valu is Canada’s leading pet specialty retailer, dedicated to strengthening the bond between dedicated pet lovers and their pets. With 50 years of animal care expertise, we operate over 800 stores across Canada under several banners, including Pet Valu, Bosley’s, Total Pet, Paulmac's Pet, Tisol & Chico. Guided by our mission to deliver products, care, expertise, and memorable moments, Pet Valu is a place where people build meaningful careers while making a positive impact on pets and communities.

Job Summary

Looking for DevOps Engineer/ Platform Engineer to help design and operate the infrastructure and CI/CD platform supporting our cloud-based applications. This role focuses on building scalable deployment pipelines, managing infrastructure as code, and architecting secure Azure environments across multiple deployment stages. The ideal candidate has strong hands-on experience with GitHub Actions, Terraform/Terragrunt, and Azure cloud architecture, and is comfortable designing solutions that support multiple engineering teams.

Essential Duties And Responsibilities


Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions, including reusable and shared workflow components
Build and manage infrastructure as code using Terraform and Terragrunt
Architect and maintain Azure cloud environments across Dev, QA, and Production
Design and manage Azure networking, including VNets, subnets, routing, and security controls
Establish and maintain deployment standards and automation patterns across repositories
Manage GitHub repositories, branching strategies, and pipeline governance
Collaborate with engineering teams to design scalable and secure cloud solutions
Troubleshoot CI/CD, infrastructure, and cloud platform issues


Skills, Experience, Education, Certifications


University/College Diploma in Computer Science or a related field.
5 years of experience as a platform engineer, cloud engineer, or in a related field.
Strong experience with GitHub Actions and CI/CD pipeline design
Experience building reusable workflow pipelines
Strong experience with Terraform and Terragrunt
Strong knowledge of Microsoft Azure cloud services
Experience designing multi-environment architectures (Dev / QA / Prod)
Experience with Azure networking and security controls
Strong knowledge of Git and GitHub repository management


Preferred Skills


Experience with platform engineering or internal developer platforms
Experience working in enterprise cloud environments
Experience with container platforms or Kubernetes
Experience building reusable infrastructure modules
Any Certification is a bonus.


What You'll Actually Work On

You will help design and maintain the engineering platform that supports our cloud applications and development teams. The environment is heavily automated and infrastructure-driven.

Examples of the work you will be involved in include:


Building and maintaining GitHub Actions CI/CD pipelines, including reusable workflows shared across multiple repositories
Managing infrastructure as code using Terraform and Terragrunt, with modular infrastructure patterns
Designing and maintaining Azure cloud environments with clear separation between development, QA, and production
Implementing and supporting Azure networking architecture, including virtual networks, private connectivity, routing, and security controls
Improving deployment automation and platform reliability across multiple services and teams
Partnering with engineering teams to design scalable cloud solutions and deployment strategies


This role is well suited for engineers who enjoy working at the intersection of cloud architecture, automation, and developer platforms, and who want to build systems that enable teams to deploy and operate software efficiently.

Compensation

The targeted salary range for this position is $110,000 - $130,000 annually. The final offer will be based on factors such as market location, relevant skills, experience and internal equity.
