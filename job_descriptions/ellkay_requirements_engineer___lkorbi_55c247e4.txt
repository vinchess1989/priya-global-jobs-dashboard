Job Description

As requirements engineer, you will be responsible for ensuring product requirements are expertly evaluated and implemented to meet their intended outcome. You will be part of the product management team, working closely with many parts of the organization.

Expectations for Your Role

BASE | Prepare development-ready requirements and handle support ticket resolution

GROWTH | Become a key technical liaison across engineering, QA, and product teams

FUTURE | Champion product data quality and usability to ensure scalable product growth

Essential Duties & Responsibilities


Support Product Management with technical assessments and write-ups to guide development
Analyze and troubleshoot product issues, coordinating with upstream teams as needed
Assist in product design, UI/UX input, and ensuring alignment with functional goals
Break down requirements into user stories with clear acceptance criteria
Collaborate closely with development teams to ensure shared understanding of goals
Run queries and conduct data structure analysis to support product and feature readiness
Evaluate feasibility and business impact of change requests
Participate in QA-approved functionality checks for Product acceptance
Interface with other product teams to support workflow and data interoperability
Act as a cross-functional collaborator with project management, QA, data engineering, and development


Qualifications


Experience: 3+ years in Healthcare Industry, with emphasis on Healthcare Data Standards (HL7, CCD, FHIR), Health Information Networks (HIE, QHIN, Large Data Networks), & Healthcare Regulatory Standards
Experience: 3+ years as a Product or Business Analyst or Requirements Engineer enabling teams to meet business needs through detailed understanding of user requirements
Exposure: Agile methodology, including writing and managing requirements in JIRA
Experience: Collaborating with cross-functional teams on software development projects
Exposure: Working in healthcare IT companies delivering innovative software products
Expertise: Writing clear, actionable technical and business requirements
Experience: Troubleshooting data issues using SQL or similar toolsthe healthcare industry


Education


Bachelor's degree in Healthcare Informatics, Computer Science or IT-related field strongly preferred.


How to Stand Out


Bilingual: Strong command of both Hindi and English language in speaking and writing
Exposure: Experience with API integration and documentation
Experience: Working knowledge of provider, payer, and interoperability workflows
Tools: Proficient in JIRA, Confluence, OneNote, Visio, and Microsoft Office suite
Exposure: Familiarity with healthcare operations, EHRs, and data sharing practices
Experience: Involvement in Agile software development lifecycle (SDLC)
