Mō te tūranga | About the role

We're looking for an experienced Software Specialist - Technical Analyst to join our Digital and Engineering practice within the Customer Tribe.

As a Software Specialist - Technical Analyst, you'll lead analysis within a cross-functional squad, working closely with the Product Owner, engineers and operations teams to turn complex problems into clear, deliverable outcomes.

In This Role, You Will


Partner with stakeholders and subject matter experts to understand business problems, define scope, and translate needs into clear requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria.
Map current-state and future-state processes, identifying pain points, control gaps, and opportunities to simplify, automate and improve the customer experience.
Work alongside technical teams to ensure requirements are understood, feasible and traceable, including data needs, business rules, integrations and workflow behaviour.
Identify and help manage the squad’s risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies (RAID), ensuring they are visible, owned and factored into planning and decision-making.
Support backlog shaping and prioritisation, helping the squad focus on the highest-value work and manage trade-offs transparently.
Facilitate workshops, walkthroughs and decision forums, keeping the right people involved, driving clarity on outcomes and ensuring a consistent understanding.
Contribute to test planning and execution by validating that delivered solutions meet requirements, are operationally workable, and support regulatory and risk obligations.
Help prepare the business for change, including operating-model impacts, procedures, training materials and handover to business as usual.


Ngā mea kia whai angitū ahau? | What will help me succeed?

You're an experienced Technical Analyst who is comfortable working between business and technology, and you know how to turn ambiguity into outcomes that teams can build and deliver.

Skills And Experience


At least seven years of experience in technical analysis within complex environments. Experience in financial services or regulatory change would be an advantage.
A strong business analysis toolkit, including process mapping such as BPMN, requirements elicitation, business rules definition, user stories, use cases and clear acceptance criteria.
Proven process-improvement experience, including identifying bottlenecks, simplifying workflows and designing future-state processes that are practical for operations and technology.
Confidence managing RAID items by identifying risks and dependencies early, articulating their impact, and working with the CXO and stakeholders to agree mitigations and owners.
Enough technical fluency to engage confidently with engineers and architects, including discussing data flows, integrations and system behaviours without needing to write the code.
Strong stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to navigate competing priorities, challenge constructively, and build trust with operations, risk, compliance, technology and vendor partners.
Experience educating and coaching business stakeholders on new technology, digital tools and automation opportunities, translating how technology works into what it means for them.
Experience working in Agile delivery environments, including squads, stand-ups, refinement and incremental delivery, and using tools such as Azure DevOps or Jira.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to turn complex or incomplete information into clear summaries, visuals and recommendations for different audiences.
A curious mindset, strong attention to detail, and a focus on delivering outcomes that are right for our customers, our people and our regulators.
