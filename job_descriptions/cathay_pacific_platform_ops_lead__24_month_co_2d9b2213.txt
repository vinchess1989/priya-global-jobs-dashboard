Role Introduction

Reports to: Platform Ops Senior Lead

Lead the platforms that power how Cathay builds, tests and releases digital products. As Platform Ops Lead, you are responsible for delivering, running and continually improving DevOps Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) platforms that allow web and mobile application teams to move quickly, securely and at scale.

Working closely with application teams, you translate real business needs into reliable platform services, combining CI/CD toolchains, container platforms and hybrid cloud infrastructure. The role blends hands-on technical leadership with service ownership — from automation and self-healing capabilities, to performance testing, proactive monitoring and secure access controls — all built in line with Cathay’s infrastructure strategy and roadmap.

This is a role for someone who enjoys shaping platforms that others rely on every day. By guiding engineers, partnering with vendors and applying FinOps practices to optimise cost, you help create a stable, modern DevOps environment that supports innovation, resilience and long-term value for the business.

Key Responsibilities


Build, operate and continuously enhance automation, CI/CD and self-healing hybrid cloud container platforms to deliver high availability and scalable infrastructure services, supporting web and mobile application development
Maintain Observability platform's stability and self-service automations for application team. ie Elastic Stack (ElasticSearch, Logstash, Kibana)
Ensure reliable day-to-day operations of DevOps platforms, covering continuous integration, test automation, environment configuration and version control
Partner with application teams to translate requirements into a clear Platform Ops service catalogue
Govern the quality and compliance of base images, libraries, CI/CD pipelines and container platforms against corporate security and quality standards
Evaluate, retire and replace obsolete or redundant CI/CD toolsets to maintain market-aligned solutions
Enable secure segregation of duties through tailored access controls across DevOps toolsets
Apply FinOps practices to optimise cloud and DevOps platform cost efficiency at scale
Lead, mentor and support platform engineers in daily operations, performance improvement and career development
Communicate infrastructure designs, risks, dependencies and delivery progress clearly with project managers, stakeholders, vendors and delivery partners, including availability for 24/7 support when required


Requirements


Bachelor’s degree or above in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field
Minimum 8 years of IT experience, including at least 5 years in IT infrastructure roles
Proven experience working in complex enterprise IT infrastructure environments.
Strong ability to design solutions and make technical decisions with minimal supervision
Demonstrated experience influencing stakeholders and building relationships at senior levels
Solid understanding of DevOps, Continuous Integration / Continuous Delivery (CI/CD), and Agile or Lean methodologies
Hands-on knowledge of cloud operations, DevOps, DevSecOps, and FinOps practices
Broad technical exposure to cloud platforms (Azure and/or AWS), container platforms, microservices, and Infrastructure as Code
Strong analytical, problem‑solving and root cause analysis skills, with clear written and verbal communication
Ability to manage multiple initiatives in a cross‑functional environment, align technology with business needs, and support 24/7 operations when required
