Sanmina Corporation (Nasdaq: SANM) is a global powerhouse in integrated manufacturing solutions serving the fastest growing segments of the global Electronics Manufacturing Services (EMS) market. We are a global engine providing the end-to-end manufacturing backbone for the world's most essential and innovative brands. Whether it's autonomous vehicles, life-saving medical tech, next-gen aerospace, or AI/cloud infrastructure - our integrated solutions don’t just meet standards—they redefine them.

Summary

We are seeking a detail-oriented and technically accomplished Engineer II (QA) to lead Incoming Quality Assurance (IQA) and support Supplier Quality Engineering (SQE) functions across our plant. In this role, you will maintain plant incoming procedures under our Quality Management System (QMS), establish inspection control plans and First Article Inspection (FAI) workflows for New Product Introductions (NPI), enforce counterfeit parts avoidance protocols, manage calibration and validation programs, and drive rigorous multi-tier audit compliance covering ISO 13485, FDA, and international medical regulatory frameworks.

What You'll Do


Maintain and update plant procedures, work instructions, and forms for Incoming Quality to ensure full compliance with the overarching QMS framework.
Oversee the creation, review, and execution of incoming inspection control plans and First Article Inspection (FAI) documentations to support timely NPI qualifications.
Provide expert guidance to Incoming Quality personnel on component specifications, inspection control plans, and non-conformance dispositions.
Support Customer Focus Teams (CFT) on product quality issues, monitor customer returns, and implement corrective and preventive actions (CAPA).
Direct equipment maintenance and calibration tracking schedules for all inspection, measurement, and test equipment within IQE and IQA sections.
Enforce counterfeit supplies avoidance controls and measures in accordance with Corporate policies and plant QMS requirements.
Maintain qualification and validation statuses for environments, machinery, tools, and equipment across IQE and IQA areas.
Actively manage risk management processes and documentation associated with QMS, EMS, DCC, and CAPA systems.
Conduct comprehensive training for peers and subordinates on incoming quality procedures, work instructions, and quality forms.
Support, coordinate, and participate in 1st, 2nd, and 3rd-party audits, including internal manufacturing/ESD audits, customer and supplier audits, and regulatory body inspections.
Drive supplier quality performance by executing supplier system/compliance audits for qualification, evaluation, and continual quality improvements.
Review and approve NCMRs with "Use-As-Is" dispositions, ensuring materials in the Material Review Board (MRB) are rigorously qualified and classified.
Prepare and submit timely Corporate and Plant performance reports covering IQA operations and SQE deliverables.
Drive continuous improvement and Lean Six Sigma (LSS) initiatives—utilizing Kaizen and Mini-company approaches—to eliminate operational waste and enhance line productivity.
Execute other quality engineering roles and duties as defined in Plant procedures and assigned by management.



What You'll Bring


Degree or Diploma in Electronics, Mechanical, or Manufacturing Engineering or equivalent qualification.
Experience as a QA Engineer in an electronics manufacturing environment, particularly within Integrated Manufacturing Services (IMS) or EMS.
Prior experience in incoming quality assurance (IQA) operational deliverables is an added advantage.
In-depth understanding of international industrial and medical quality standards (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 13485, US FDA 21 CFR Part 820, US FDA 21 CFR Part 11, Japan PMDA MHLW Ordinance 169, MDSAP).
Working knowledge of PCBA manufacturing workflows, ESD control protocols, and IPC standards.
Proficiency with quality systems and enterprise software (Agile PQM, Agile PLM, Receiving Maestro, Inspection Maestro, SQR, Oracle, MRB).
Applied knowledge of Quality Tools, structured problem-solving methodologies, FMEA, and technical documentation writing.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) and Google Looker Studio.
Exposure to Lean Manufacturing, Six Sigma principles, and the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and automation in quality operations.
Strong leadership, interpersonal communication, and analytical thinking abilities.



How To Stand Out

The ideal candidate pairs extensive incoming quality and supplier audit leadership in a high-reliability PCBA/EMS setting with hands-on regulatory compliance across ISO 13485, MDSAP, and US FDA requirements, proficiency in tools like Inspection Maestro and Agile PLM, and a forward-thinking drive to implement AI and automation into quality assurance workflows.
