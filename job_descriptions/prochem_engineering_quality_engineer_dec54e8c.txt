Role Summary:

Our client, based in County Kilkenny, is looking for a Quality Engineer to join the team at their medical device manufacturing site.
This role supports the Supplier Quality function across a diverse supplier base.
The successful candidate will manage supplier non-conformances, change notifications, audit activities and validation support, while driving supplier performance improvements.




Core Duties and Responsibilities:

Manage Supplier Corrective Action Requests (SCARs) from initiation through verified closure
Process Supplier Change Notifications (SCNs) and assess quality, regulatory and validation impacts
Support supplier process validations, First Article Inspections (FAIs) and PPAP-style submissions
Conduct desktop and on-site supplier audits and manage follow-up actions
Monitor supplier quality performance and participate in scorecard reviews
Maintain supplier quality records and quality agreements within the eQMS
Support material review activities




Minimum Qualifications and Experience:




Essential Experience:

Degree in Engineering, Science or a related discipline
Minimum 3 years' experience in Supplier Quality or Quality Engineering within a regulated industry
Working knowledge of ISO 13485 and FDA QMSR
Experience with SCAR/CAPA systems
Experience supporting process validation activities (IQ/OQ/PQ)
Knowledge of risk management tools such as PFMEA
Good statistical analysis skills (Minitab, SPC, capability studies)
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills




Preferred Experience:

ISO 13485 Lead Auditor qualification and/or ASQ CQE/CSQE certification
Veeva QMS experience
Experience with mechanical or moulding suppliers
