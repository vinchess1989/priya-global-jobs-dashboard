Selexa Life Sciences is supporting a leading Medical Device client in the search for a Quality Engineer to support quality assurance, product compliance and continuous improvement within a specialist medical device manufacturing environment.




Key Areas of Focus:

Supplier Quality Management
Distributor Qualification and Oversight
NPD Quality Assurance
Design Transfer QA
Process validation, equipment qualification & validation oversight




Key responsibilities:

Maintain and improve Quality Management Systems (QMS)
Knowledge of supplier qualification and SQA processes
Understanding of design controls, risk management and product development processes
Lead & support investigations relating to non-conformances, deviations and CAPAs
Support change control activities and quality assurance assessments
Support ISO 13485 compliance and regulatory requirements
Manage non-conformances, CAPAs and root cause investigations
Support internal audits, risk management and process improvements
Work collaboratively with engineering, manufacturing and regulatory teams







The ideal candidate: An experienced Quality Engineer with a background in medical devices, strong QMS knowledge and a proactive approach to quality and continuous improvement.
