Senior Manager (Veeva Vault RIMS Expert) | Vadodara/Mumbai | Hybrid- 3D WFO/2D WFH

We are seeking an experienced Senior Manager with specialist expertise in Veeva Vault RIM (Regulatory Information Management Systems) to lead regulatory technology strategy, implementation and operational excellence. This role will oversee Veeva Vault RIM solutions, responsible for managing, configuring, and maintaining the Veeva Vault platform to support the regulatory publishing and RIMS maintenance activities of ATNAHS’ markets within Pharmanovia, manage key stakeholder relationships and ensure regulatory information management capabilities align with business and compliance objectives.

Senior Manager - Key responsibilities (Veeva Vault RIM strategy & implementation)


Platform managed services
Veeva Release Management – Planning, Impact Assessment, Implementation, Validation & Testing, Documentation, coordinating with Veeva
Training session & demo to the Pharmanovia team once Veeva releases have been released in Veeva and impact the end user
Incident Management
Day to day management & resolution of incidents
Co-ordinating with Veeva for any platform or product level issues
Propose lean processes to reduce and minimise the ticket issues raised by the team by up to 20% through training and trend analysis subject to user adoptability and implementation by Pharmanovia.
Change Management & internal release management
Configure changes that are permissible to be implemented, such as workflows, lifecycle states, security, CVs
Reporting & Dashboards
Creation of new reports, updates to existing reports as deemed necessary and/or on request from Pharmanovia.
Creation of dashboards, updates to existing dashboards as necessary and/or on request from Pharmanovia.
To work methodically with a keen eye for detail ensuring any data anomalies are highlighted.
Access Management
Control of access management of end user aligned with governance model for data management.
User Adoption Services to Support continuous user adoption of Vault RIMS Functionality
Support on implemented functional processes within Pharmanovia Vault, either through Checklists or work instructions.
Training
Conduct regular monthly training with Veeva users on gaps/deficiency areas identified.
Providing ad hoc and on demand training.
Data management services
Manage creation and update to foundational data [Product, Product Family, Product Variant, Packaging] as per the defined data standards.
Monitor & Manage Data Quality within Vault proactively, based on pre- defined KPIs, identify corrective & preventive actions, agree with Pharmanovia stakeholders and support the implementation of data corrections.
Manage Volume Data Extraction requests from Vault and Content & Data upload requests into Vault.
Management of all electronic publishing systems and peripheral components which support those systems. This individual assists with the development and maintenance of procedural and operational documentation related to Atnahs’ regulatory technology platform(s).
Cleansing large data sets from source destination in support of a system migration and to maintain a logging system / data quality plan.
To assign appropriate taxonomy and metadata to source data where required.
Maintain naming conventions and structure protocols and validate accuracy.
Support continuous improvement opportunities
Quarterly updates with measurable improvements/opportunities that end users could utilise to increase efficiencies.


Qualifications and professional attributes


Bachelor’s degree is required. Scientific/health field/ Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
Veeva Vault, regulatory systems, project management or business analysis certification is advantageous.
Strategic, delivery-focused and pragmatic, with a strong commitment to regulatory compliance and operational excellence.
Confident in making evidence-based decisions and communicating complex technical concepts to both technical and non-technical audiences.
Highly organised with strong attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple global initiatives simultaneously.
Resilient and adaptable, with a continuous improvement mindset and commitment to developing colleagues and technology capabilities.


What we offer (benefits & career development)


Competitive salary and performance-related bonus.
Professional development, Veeva training and opportunities to build expertise in regulatory technology.
Opportunity to lead high-impact global transformation programmes within a collaborative life sciences environment.
Inclusive workplace culture with emphasis on well-being and work-life balance.


How to apply (application process & equal opportunities)

Please submit your CV outlining your relevant Veeva Vault RIM, regulatory affairs systems and regulated-industry experience. We are an equal opportunities employer and welcome applications from candidates of all backgrounds. Only shortlisted applicants will be contacted.

Pharmanovia is a dynamic, fast-growing international pharmaceutical company with a portfolio of over 20 brands across more than 160 markets. Our mission is to improve patient health globally by revitalising niche, tried-and-trusted medicines. Since our inception in 2013, we have grown consistently year on year, focusing on delivering high-quality branded prescription medicines to patients, prescribers and healthcare providers around the world. We are an agile, committed and innovative global speciality pharmaceutical business and a preferred partner for innovator pharmaceutical companies. We are passionate about unlocking the potential of established medicines, engaging with clinical communities and creating sustainable value through effective lifecycle management and innovation. Our therapeutic areas include Cardiovascular, Oncology, Endocrinology and Neurology.
