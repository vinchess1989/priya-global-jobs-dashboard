What You Will Be Working On

You will be involved in planning, reviewing and implementing innovative solutions to address operational challenges:


Reviewing business processes and identifying improvement opportunities
Work with business owners, operational, legal, policy and technical teams to understand business needs and identify gaps, pain points and opportunities for process and service enhancement
Work with the various stakeholders to define the problem statement, product vision, objectives and measurable outcomes, as well as formulate potential solutions
Lead the gathering and alignment of requirements across stakeholders, and translate requirements into clear process flows, user stories and acceptance criteria
Create clear and compelling presentations to secure stakeholder buy-in across various levels of the organisation
Develop documentation, templates and guides to ensure compliance with new SOPs and processes
Supporting product development and change management
Coordinate across business owners, users and technical teams throughout the product lifecycle, providing clarity on requirements and intended outcomes to guide product development
Collaborate with technical partners or in-house development team to plan, coordinate and execute testing, refinement and operationalising of the product.
Support change management and stakeholder engagement to ensure user readiness and smooth adoption of new product or processes, including gathering user feedback for evaluation and continuous improvement
Analysing Data to guide design and development of processes
Work with data teams to identify and structure datasets that enable process improvement, data analytics, and predictive insights.
Analyse operational data and trends to validate problem statements, inform requirements and support prioritisation and decision-making.
Design feedback loops to continuously monitor process performance, capture user feedback, and iterate on solutions based on real-time data and evolving business needs.
Evaluating new tools and solutions
Stay abreast of emerging tools and platforms offered by WOG or technical partners that could support operational transformation.
Identify potential applications or use cases based on business needs, user value and operational outcomes.
Work with technical team to assess feasibility and suitability of new tools for the identified potential use cases.


What We Are Looking For


Professional qualifications in Computer Science, Business Analytics, Infocomm Technology, Engineering or possess at least 3 years of experience in related fields.
Demonstrate strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to understand and communicate complex information effectively, craft compelling narratives and engage diverse group of stakeholders.
Good understanding of digital product or system development, with a portfolio demonstrating user-centric design solutions. Familiar with process mapping, service design, and design thinking methodologies.
Experience with collaborative, process-mapping and prototyping tools such as Miro and Figma would be advantageous.
Working knowledge of digital, automation and data analytics platforms such as Dataiku, Databricks, Microsoft Power Platform, UiPath, and Python, or similar technologies, would be advantageous
