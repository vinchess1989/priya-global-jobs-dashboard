Job Description:




Tech Delivery Lead – Engineering Platforms

Skills

1. Hands-on engineering: CI/CD with GitHub Actions, GitHub Enterprise, Terraform and Kubernetes.

2. Delivery planning: Roadmaps, backlogs, OKRs, dependencies and risk management.

3. Outcome measurement and experimentation: DORA, adoption and developer-experience metrics; planning time-boxed tool trials with clear success measures.

4. AI-enabled delivery and leadership: Effective use of AI coding assistants and agents, coaching engineers in engineering practices and safe agentic ways of working, and stakeholder management across tribes and domains.

5. Delivery and platform knowledge: Shared platforms, regulated-environment change/risk/controls, platform-as-a-product, and pipeline/supply-chain security.

6. Useful extras: Agentic workflow design and evaluation, Backstage or similar developer portals, cloud platforms, and experience leading a large engineering transformation.
