We are seeking an experienced Senior DevOps Engineer / Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) to provide technical leadership across modern Kubernetes-based platforms operating in both cloud and on-premise environments.
This role is ideal for a seasoned infrastructure professional who is passionate about platform engineering, automation, reliability, and operational excellence. You will play a key role in improving system stability, scalability, deployment processes, and engineering standards while mentoring other team members and collaborating across multiple technical disciplines.
Duties & Responsibilities
Key Responsibilities
Platform Engineering & Operations
Design, manage, and continuously improve Kubernetes platforms across cloud and on-premise environments.
Ensure the reliability, availability, performance, and scalability of critical production systems.
Support and maintain live production platforms while driving ongoing operational improvements.
Participate in incident investigation, root-cause analysis, and reliability enhancement initiatives.
Develop and implement best practices for platform operations and system resilience.
Infrastructure as Code & Automation
Build and maintain infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code (IaC) tools.
Develop reusable automation frameworks and standardized deployment patterns.
Improve provisioning, upgrade, and lifecycle management processes.
Drive automation initiatives that reduce manual operational effort and improve efficiency.
Enhance deployment consistency and operational repeatability across environments.
Kubernetes & Platform Management
Provide technical leadership on Kubernetes architecture, administration, and optimization.
Manage GitOps-based deployment workflows and release management processes.
Support application deployment and configuration management within containerized environments.
Improve deployment safety, rollback strategies, and environment consistency.
Manage stateful workloads and storage solutions within Kubernetes ecosystems.
Production Support & Reliability Engineering
Support platforms with demanding uptime and availability requirements.
Improve backup, disaster recovery, and system recovery processes.
Implement monitoring, alerting, and observability best practices.
Ensure platform stability through proactive performance tuning and reliability initiatives.
Contribute to service availability and operational maturity.
Technical Leadership & Mentorship
Mentor junior and intermediate engineers within the DevOps function.
Promote engineering excellence through documentation, knowledge sharing, and coaching.
Support onboarding and technical skills development.
Establish and maintain operational standards and best practices across teams.
Collaboration & Architecture
Partner with software engineering, data, and integration teams to deliver robust platform solutions.
Contribute to platform strategy, technical roadmaps, and architectural decisions.
Communicate technical risks, recommendations, and design considerations to stakeholders.
Drive continuous improvement and innovation across infrastructure and platform services.
Required Skills & Experience
Cloud, Infrastructure & Kubernetes
Strong hands-on experience operating Kubernetes in production environments.
Proven experience managing cloud-native platforms.
Extensive experience with Terraform and Infrastructure as Code methodologies.
Experience implementing and managing GitOps practices.
Strong Linux systems administration skills.
Experience supporting stateful applications and workloads.
Experience using automation tools such as Ansible.
Knowledge of containerisation technologies and deployment pipelines.
Operational & Reliability Engineering
Experience supporting mission-critical production environments.
Strong understanding of infrastructure reliability and operational excellence.
Experience implementing monitoring, alerting, and observability solutions.
Solid knowledge of:
DNS
TLS/SSL
Load Balancing
Ingress Controllers
Networking Fundamentals
Platform Engineering
Experience working across both cloud and on-premise infrastructure environments.
Strong understanding of scaling, performance optimisation, and high-availability architectures.
Experience developing and improving deployment, recovery, and operational procedures.
Preferred Skills
Experience with one or more of the following will be highly advantageous:

AWS
Azure
ArgoCD
Helm
Kustomize

Cloudflare
Kubernetes Storage Solutions
CSI Drivers
Longhorn

Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practices and Service Level Objectives (SLOs)
Software Development experience
Customer-hosted platform support
Certifications
The following certifications would be beneficial:

Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA)
Certified Kubernetes Application Developer (CKAD)
Other cloud or infrastructure-related certifications
Key Competencies
Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
Ability to work independently and take ownership of platform outcomes.
Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills.
Strong mentoring and leadership capabilities.
Meticulous attention to detail and commitment to operational excellence.
Passion for automation, continuous improvement, and modern engineering practices.
Ability to thrive in complex and fast-paced technical environments.
