Role Overview

TEAM


At easyJet, we are transforming Airline Operations through modern product-led technology, operational excellence, automation, data and AI-enabled capabilities.
The Airline Operations Platform is the single point of contact for the applications and source data availability assigned to the Platform, enabling four operational missions across four business critical applications. It owns the crew, flight planning, network control and engineering systems that keep the network flying, AIMS, TOPS, LIDO and AMOS.
As Delivery Manager, you will operate the delivery system between the Platform and the missions. You will coordinate the platform squads against mission sprints, own delivery cadence, and manage vendor commitments as dependencies rather than assumptions — so what Platform commits to a mission is deliverable, visible and honoured.
This is a delivery role in a live operational environment. Much of the work involves interdependent third-party systems, so your value lies in sequencing, dependency management and honest, early communication when a commitment is at risk.
Working closely with Mission Product Managers, Platform Leads, Technical Specialists, Architects, Service Support and Supplier Management, you will make delivery predictable, keep the backlog credible, and ensure change reaches the operation safely and with the right cover in place.
This role reports to the Platform Lead – Demand & Capability and is one of two Delivery Managers, with mission and portfolio coverage allocated between them. The role has no direct reports and leads through influence, coordinating squad members who sit within the Platform capability chapters.



Job Purpose


Coordinate the platform squads against mission sprints, owning delivery cadence across the allocated portfolio.
Own the commitment position with the missions: what Platform has committed to each sprint, what has changed, and what is at risk.
Track and manage vendor commitments as external dependencies, surfacing the vendor critical path before it becomes a delay.
Own dependency, risk and issue management across products, missions, suppliers and enterprise platforms.
Ensure work entering a sprint is sized, understood and free of unresolved dependencies.
Support the demand rhythm through triage, sizing and the reuse-first challenge, keeping the single backlog credible and current.
Ensure change reaches the operation safely, with readiness, supportability, documentation and cover agreed before release.
Define and report delivery metrics that give visibility of flow, predictability and delivery health.
Champion Product and Platform ways of working, treating the missions as customers of the Platform.
Provide clear, early communication on progress, risk and change to mission and Platform leadership.
Drive continuous improvement in how the Platform delivers, removing repeated impediments rather than working around them.



Job Accountabilities

Delivery Cadence & Squad Coordination


Coordinate platform squads across the capability chapters — Data Availability, API & Integration; Proactive App Management; Automation — against mission sprint commitments.
Own the delivery cadence: backlog refinement, sprint planning, stand-ups, reviews and release planning, run consistently rather than when there is time.
Maintain one backlog and one plan for the allocated portfolio, so squad members working across several missions have a single view of priority.
Sequence work so that run and support, mission demand and improvement work coexist rather than compete, protecting the agreed capacity allocation.
Keep the plan honest: reflect actual progress and capacity, not the plan as originally committed.



Mission Interface & Commitment Management


Run the per-sprint interface sync with the Mission Product Managers for the allocated missions: what Platform commits to that sprint, and what has changed since the last one.
Own the commitment record, so a mission always knows what it is getting, when, and what it depends on.
Surface changes to commitment early, with the reason and the option set, rather than reporting a miss after the fact.
Ensure missions are not building platform capability that is already coming, by making the Platform roadmap and backlog visible to them.
Escalate to the Platform Lead – Demand & Capability where commitments and capacity diverge, with the trade-off articulated.



Demand Support, Sizing & Dependencies


Support daily triage and the weekly demand review, contributing sizing, dependency mapping and the reuse-first challenge.
Ensure nothing enters a sprint unsized, without dependencies mapped, or without the vendor critical path named in the commitment.
Own dependency management across products, missions, suppliers, enterprise data, architecture and I&O, coordinating resolution rather than reporting the blockage.
Maintain and work the risk and issue log for the allocated portfolio, with mitigations owned, monitored and escalated where required.
Identify where the same dependency or impediment recurs, and work with the Platform Leads and Architects to remove the cause.



Vendor & Supplier Delivery


Track vendor commitments — releases, fixes, roadmap items, API and integration changes — as external dependencies with dates, owners and evidence.
Hold suppliers to their delivery commitments day to day, escalating to the Supplier Management Specialist where contractual leverage is needed.
Ensure vendor-driven change is sequenced through the same route as internal work, and impact assessed before it is accepted.
Feed delivery evidence — missed dates, repeated defects, performance under load — into supplier performance management.
Coordinate vendor activity around the operational calendar, so third-party change does not land at the wrong moment for the operation.



Release, Readiness & Transition into Support


Coordinate release planning and deployment across products, suppliers and operational stakeholders.
Ensure every release meets agreed readiness criteria before it goes live: testing and quality gates with the QE function, monitoring, documentation, training, supportability and named cover.
Work with the Technical Specialists and Service Support Lead so support and callout arrangements are in place before change reaches the operation, not after.
Coordinate the delivery and readiness elements of applications transitioning between the Platform and the missions.
Run lessons learned after significant releases and major incidents with a delivery cause, and feed the outcome into how the next one is planned.



Delivery Performance & Continuous Improvement


Define and maintain delivery metrics and flow measures for the allocated portfolio, including predictability, throughput, unplanned work and time to triage.
Provide reporting that gives mission and Platform leadership a true view of delivery health rather than a reassuring one.
Identify and remove systemic impediments affecting delivery performance, escalating those that need leadership intervention.
Improve delivery practice across the squads: estimation, definition of ready and done, release discipline and handover quality.
Promote a culture of continuous improvement, experimentation and learning within the delivery cadence.



Product & Platform Ways of Working


Operate the Platform as a product with the missions as its customers: a defined offer, a single backlog, published interfaces and a clear route in.
Work within the product operating model rather than around it, so demand is met through sprint commitments and roadmaps rather than side-door escalation.
Coach squad members and stakeholders in modern delivery practice, lean governance and outcome-focused execution.
Partner with Mission Product Managers so platform capability is treated as a dependency in their roadmaps and reused across missions rather than rebuilt in each one.
Collaborate with PMO, Portfolio and Transformation teams to align governance, funding and reporting approaches, and support the wider Product and Platform transformation.



Stakeholder Management & Communication


Act as the primary day-to-day delivery contact for the allocated missions and for stakeholders across Technology and Operations.
Build strong working relationships with Mission Product Managers, Platform Leads, Technical Specialists, Architects, Data, Service Support and suppliers.
Communicate progress, risk, dependency and change clearly and early, adapting the message to the audience without softening the substance.
Support quarterly planning, portfolio and capacity forums and Platform governance activities with accurate delivery input.
Represent Platform delivery credibly during operational disruption, where the priority is clarity rather than detail.



Key Skills Required


Proven delivery management experience in a complex technology environment, coordinating multiple teams or squads against competing priorities.
Strong understanding of Product and Platform ways of working, including platform-as-a-product thinking, treating internal teams as customers, and reuse over rebuild.
Strong understanding of Agile and Lean delivery practice, including backlog management, sizing, sprint-based commitment models and release planning.
Strong dependency, risk and issue management experience across products, suppliers and enterprise functions.
Experience managing third-party and vendor delivery commitments, including holding suppliers to dates and quality.
Experience of release management and operational readiness, including transition into support in a live environment.
Experience delivering change into a 24x7 operational environment, with an understanding of the constraints that imposes.
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to give an unwelcome message early and clearly.
Experience leading through influence, without line management authority over the people doing the work.
Strong understanding of delivery metrics, flow measurement and performance reporting.
Ability to balance planning discipline with the pragmatism a live operation demands.
Proficiency with Jira, Azure DevOps, Confluence, ITSM tooling and related delivery platforms.
Strong analytical, facilitation and organisational skills, with genuine attention to detail.



Desirable Skills


Experience within aviation, airline operations, airport operations or transport environments.
Familiarity with crew, ops control, flight planning or maintenance systems and their operational rhythms.
Experience delivering against third-party product roadmaps where the critical path sits with a vendor.
Experience of integration, API or data delivery between vendor systems and internal products.
Service management grounding (ITIL or equivalent), particularly change, release and service transition.
Experience supporting a team or function through a transition to product-led working.
Understanding of AI, automation and data-driven operational improvement opportunities.

This full-time role will be based in Luton with hybrid working arrangements.

Some flexibility is required around release windows, major change events and periods of operational disruption, with occasional travel to operational locations and supplier sites.
