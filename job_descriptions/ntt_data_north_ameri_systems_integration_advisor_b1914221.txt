We are currently seeking a Systems Integration Advisor to join our team in Bangalore, Karnātaka (IN-KA), India (IN).

External Tittle :Release Manager

Key Responsibilities:


Plan and manage end-to-end release management activities.
Coordinate with development, testing, infrastructure, and operations teams.
Ensure releases follow ITIL processes and organizational policies.
Manage release schedules, deployment plans, and implementation activities.
Ensure proper testing, approvals, and rollback plans are in place.
Monitor release progress and resolve deployment-related issues.
Communicate release status and risks to stakeholders.
Conduct post-release reviews and drive continuous improvement.
