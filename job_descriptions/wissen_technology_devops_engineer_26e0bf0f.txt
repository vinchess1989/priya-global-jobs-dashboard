Senior DevOps Engineer – Data Protection Services (Cyber)

Client: Tier-1 Investment Bank, Cyber Division, Data Protection Services

Positions: 2

Location: Glasgow, UK (client office, hybrid at manager discretion)

Experience: 6+ years in infrastructure or DevOps, with at least 3 years in security or data protection tooling

Role summary

Hands-on DevOps engineer in the bank's Cyber Data Protection team. You will engineer, automate and scale the platforms that discover, classify and protect sensitive data across the enterprise, and onboard new data protection tools into the bank's global ecosystem.

Key responsibilities

Engineer and operate data scanning platforms covering file shares, SharePoint/O365, databases and cloud storage.
Rapidly prototype and evaluate new data protection tools, and take the chosen ones through to production.
Integrate tools with the enterprise ecosystem, including AD/LDAP, SIEM, ticketing (ServiceNow) and the CMDB.
Automate deployment, configuration, upgrades and patching of scanning infrastructure.
Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines for tooling and policy deployments.
Monitor scan coverage, performance and failures, and tune the platforms for scale.
Support DLP and classification policy rollout with the Cyber and business teams.
Take part in design and code reviews.
Keep all work aligned with security policy, regulatory requirements and the risk control framework.
Share knowledge across global teams and contribute to monthly status reporting.

Must-have skills

Strong Windows Server and Linux administration in a large enterprise.
Automation using Python and PowerShell.
Configuration management and infrastructure-as-code with Ansible and/or Terraform.
CI/CD and Git, using Jenkins, GitLab or Azure DevOps.
Integrating systems through REST APIs, with working knowledge of AD/LDAP and service accounts.
Understanding of data protection concepts: DLP, data discovery and classification, data at rest versus data in motion.
Operating in a regulated environment, including change management and audit evidence.

Good-to-have skills

Hands-on with one or more data protection platforms: Microsoft Purview, Symantec/Broadcom DLP, BigID, Varonis, Netskope, or a classification tool such as Titus or Boldon James.
SIEM and logging with Splunk or ELK.
Docker and Kubernetes/OpenShift.
Azure or AWS, particularly scanning storage and SaaS.
Prior experience in the cyber function of a bank or other BFSI organisation.
Certifications such as CISSP, Security+, or a vendor certification for any of the platforms above.

Schedule: Monday to Friday

Work Mode: Hybrid

Job Type: Full-time
