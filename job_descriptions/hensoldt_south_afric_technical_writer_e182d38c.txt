About The Role

The Technical Writer develops, manages and maintains technical documentation, manuals and support information for complex electronic products and systems. The role works closely with engineering, production, training and Integrated Logistics Support (ILS) teams to ensure documentation is accurate, compliant and user-focused, supporting product operation, maintenance, training and lifecycle support.

Qualifications and experience:


National Higher Diploma or BTech in Electronic Engineering (Light Current) or Mechanical Engineering.
Minimum 3 years’ experience in technical writing and developing technical documentation for complex electronic systems.
Experience with technical publishing tools and documentation management systems.
Experience with configuration management, document control and quality assurance processes.
Experience in the operation, maintenance or support of Radar, Datalinks or related electronic systems.


Key technical skills:


Knowledge of the operation, maintenance and repair of electronic systems and subsystems.
Knowledge of Radar, Datalinks and related technology domains will be advantageous.
Ability to read and interpret engineering drawings, wiring diagrams, functional diagrams, PCB layouts and schematics.
Ability to develop assembly, installation and preventive and corrective maintenance procedures and checklists.
Technical authoring and maintenance of operator manuals, maintenance manuals, fault isolation guides, Illustrated Parts Catalogues (IPCs), datasheets and other logistics support documentation throughout the product lifecycle.
Understanding of product lifecycle support and Integrated Logistics Support (ILS) principles.
Technical illustration and graphics development using tools such as Creo and IsoDraw.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications.
Ability to conduct technical reviews and validation trials with systems engineers to verify documentation accuracy and usability.
Knowledge of structured authoring, XML and S1000D will be advantageous.
Familiarity with MIL-STD-38784 and MIL-STD-40051 will be advantageous.
Basic scripting skills, including macros and Python, to automate repetitive tasks will be advantageous.


Key responsibilities:

Technical Documentation Development

Develop and maintain operator, maintenance and Integrated Logistics Support (ILS) documentation in accordance with product requirements, defence-industry standards and contractual specifications to provide accurate, comprehensive and compliant information that supports product operation, maintenance and lifecycle support.

Information Gathering and Cross-functional Collaboration

Collaborate with Engineering, Industrialisation, Production, Training and ILS teams to gather, interpret and validate technical information against current product designs and configurations to ensure documentation accuracy, technical consistency and alignment with product requirements.

Information Design and Illustration

Develop task flows, technical diagrams, illustrations and Illustrated Parts Catalogues (IPCs) to translate complex technical information into clear visual guidance that enables users to understand product configurations and perform operational and maintenance procedures safely and efficiently.

Compliance, Standards and Quality Assurance

Review and verify technical documentation against applicable military standards, S1000D specifications, quality requirements and contractual obligations to ensure accuracy, consistency, document integrity and compliance with industry and organisational standards.

Product Lifecycle Support and Continuous Improvement

Support product development, testing, acceptance and sustainment activities through technical documentation inputs, participation in design reviews and contributions to ILS deliverables, while improving documentation processes, templates and tools to enhance efficiency, usability and product lifecycle support.
