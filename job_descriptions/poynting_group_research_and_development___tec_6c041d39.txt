Introduction

The Technical Writer supports the antenna development lifecycle by producing clear, accurate, and production-ready technical documentation. This role focuses on assembly instructions, work instructions, and industrialisation documentation to enable efficient transition from prototype to small-batch and MVP production. The Technical Writer will work as part of a rapid MVP team and the main development team, collaborating closely with engineering, production, and quality stakeholders.

Duties & Responsibilities

Technical Documentation

Develop, review, and maintain assembly instructions, work instructions, and manufacturing documentation for antenna products.
Translate engineering designs, drawings, and specifications into clear, structured, and user-friendly documentation suitable for production environments.
Create and update documentation supporting MVP builds, pilot production, and low-volume manufacturing.
Ensure documentation aligns with design intent, manufacturing processes, and quality requirement

Industrialization Support

Support the industrialisation phase of antenna development by ensuring documentation readiness prior to production handover.
Work closely with R&D, Production, and Quality to capture process changes, lessons learned, and continuous improvement updates.
Assist in standardising documentation formats, templates, and naming conventions across R&D and production.

Cross-Functional Collaboration

Work closely with engineers, technicians, and production staff to validate assembly steps and technical content.
Participate in team meetings, design reviews, and build feedback sessions as required.
Support production and quality teams by clarifying documentation and incorporating feedback.

Health, Safety, Environmental & Quality (HSEQ)

Ensure all relevant documentation complies with internal Quality Management System requirements (e.g. ISO 9001).
Adhere to document control, version control, and traceability requirements.
Support internal and external audits by providing accurate and up-to-date documentation.

Continuous Improvement

Identify opportunities to improve clarity, usability, and effectiveness of technical documentation.
Contribute to reducing assembly errors, rework, and production inefficiencies through improved documentation.

Values and Conduct

Promotes strong support for the Company’s values
Consistent behaviour is maintained and clear personal values demonstrated, which are aligned with those of the Company
Adhere to company policies and procedures
Ongoing focus on departmental cost saving
Seeks innovative solutions
Demonstrate willingness to adapt to change in the work environment as well as commitment to ongoing learning
Actively sought feedback to identify strengths and learning opportunities
Maintain healthy relationships with all stakeholders, both internal and external
Professional image always portrayed, including in all correspondence.
Desired Experience & Qualification
Technical Diploma or Degree, Engineering, Industrial Design, or a related field (advantageous)
2–5 years’ experience in a technical writing role within manufacturing, engineering, or industrial environments.
Experience with assembly instructions, work instructions, or production documentation preferred
Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English
