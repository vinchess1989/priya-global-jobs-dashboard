About The Role

As a Senior DevOps Engineer, you will help build, operate, and enhance highly available cloud-based platforms that support critical fintech solutions. You will collaborate closely with engineering, product, and operational teams to streamline software delivery, improve platform reliability, and ensure production stability. This role combines infrastructure engineering, automation, site reliability, and operational support to drive secure, scalable, and resilient technology environments. Success is measured through system reliability, deployment efficiency, operational excellence, and outstanding stakeholder support.

What You Will Be Doing


Design, implement, and maintain scalable cloud infrastructure supporting business-critical applications and services
Develop and enhance CI/CD pipelines to accelerate software delivery and improve deployment reliability
Monitor production environments and proactively address performance, availability, and capacity issues
Partner with engineering and product teams to improve platform architecture, operational readiness, and release processes
Troubleshoot complex application, infrastructure, and cloud-related incidents across production environments
Automate infrastructure provisioning, configuration management, and operational workflows using Infrastructure as Code practices
Define, measure, and support service level objectives and operational performance metrics
Implement security controls, access management, encryption, and compliance-focused infrastructure practices
Support disaster recovery, business continuity, and resilience initiatives across cloud environments
Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to resolve technical issues and continuously improve service delivery


Required Qualifications


Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience
Proven experience in DevOps or Software Engineering
Strong experience with public cloud platforms, particularly AWS
Hands-on expertise with Kubernetes, Docker, container orchestration, and cloud-native technologies. Proficiency in at least one high-level programming language (e.g. Clojure, TypeScript, Python)
Experience developing and maintaining CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation frameworks
Proficiency in at least one programming or scripting language such as Python, TypeScript, Java, or similar
Strong understanding of infrastructure automation, configuration management, and Infrastructure as Code tools
Experience monitoring, troubleshooting, and optimizing distributed systems in production environments
Strong communication, stakeholder management, and technical documentation skills
Ability to manage multiple priorities while driving operational excellence and continuous improvement


Preferred Qualifications


Experience with Terraform or similar Infrastructure as Code platforms
Knowledge of observability and monitoring tools such as Datadog, Grafana, Prometheus, or similar solutions
Experience designing and supporting highly available, client-facing cloud architectures
Strong understanding of networking fundamentals, security best practices, and enterprise infrastructure standards
Experience supporting business continuity planning and disaster recovery programs
Relevant cloud, Kubernetes, DevOps, or security certifications
