We're Hiring for Configuration Management Consultant

For one of the Leading Global Manufacturing Industry

Location: Chennai

Experience: 10 -12 years overall, with 5+ years in Configuration Management, CMDB governance or ITSM




About the role




We are hiring a Configuration Management Consultant to strengthen CMDB governance, data quality and service modelling across a global technology organization. This is a functional, advisory and process-led role. It is not a ServiceNow development position.




Key responsibilities




Drive CMDB governance, CI ownership and accountability across Application, Service and CI Owners
Identify and remediate stale, duplicate, orphaned or unowned CIs using CMDB Health
Facilitate CSDM workshops to define business applications and application and technical services
Embed accurate CI usage across Incident, Problem, Change, Asset and Service Portfolio processes
Prepare audit evidence and governance reports for operational and leadership audiences




Requirements




10+ years in IT, including 5+ years in Configuration Management, CMDB governance or ITSM
Hands-on experience with CMDB remediation, CSDM adoption or service modelling
Functional knowledge of ServiceNow CMDB, CMDB Health and CSDM
Experience facilitating workshops and working with global teams
Strong communication and the ability to influence without direct authority




Good to have




ITIL 4 certification

ServiceNow CMDB, ITSM or CSDM training

Manufacturing, consumer products or life sciences background
