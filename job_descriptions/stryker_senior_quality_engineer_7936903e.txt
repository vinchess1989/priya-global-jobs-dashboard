Position Summary:

The Senior Engineer, Quality Assurance will establish, improve and maintain the quality system by providing quality engineering support within operations and other business functions.

Advocate and lead the execution of initiatives & projects to enhance quality performance within the business. Ensure to deliver high quality products that meet customer needs. Ensure compliance with regulations and standards.

Essential Duties & Responsibilities:


Work closely with operations and the business functions to ensure quality performance of product and processes
Collaborate cross-functionally with internal and external teams to resolve quality issues
Manages internal and supplier-driven Non-Conformances (NCs), ensuring timely investigation and closure
Owns Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPAs) through root cause analysis to closure
Disposition of non-conforming product through MRB, issuing NCs as appropriate
Initiates and leads in the development and improvement of the manufacturing processes for existing and new products.
Review and approval of change management activities, challenges change effectiveness and drives strong review.
Interpret KPI trends, act as necessary, driving a continuous improvement process.
Support the execution and analysis of manufacturing complaints and product field actions
Provide QA support during internal and external audits
Advocate of Human Factor practices, proficient in the science of Human Factor identification and mitigation.
Proficient in risk management practices and concepts and applies knowledge to manufacturing operations and identify opportunities and weaknesses
Lead and participate in the Lean philosophy – standardization and elimination of waste
Understanding of Human Error Prevention and the application of those principles to the manufacturing environment.
Support process/equipment validation, qualification, and Measurement System Analysis (MSA)
Support with product holds and executing containment Plans
Manage supplier relationships, including but not limited to, coordinating communications, monitoring performance, managing in house and external nonconformities and ensuring timely delivery of quality materials and services.


Education & Special Trainings:


Honours Bachelor’s Engineering/Science Degree
2+ years’ experience in a Quality or Regulatory Affairs role; prior industry experiences desirable
Proficient in understanding of Med Device manufacturing processes, ISO 13485, GMP.
Lean Six Sigma training is a distinct advantage.
Excellent communication skills and attention to detail.
Proficient understanding of engineering and quality principles, including Risk Management, CAPA, Audits, and Statistics
High level knowledge/experience of Risk Based and statistical techniques, in particular process risk, statistical sampling plans, Process Capability, Gauge R&Rs.
High level of PC skills. Must be proficient with Microsoft Word, Microsoft Excel and PowerPoint packages.
Experience in interacting with regulatory agencies (FDA, MoH, TUV, etc.) desired.
Strong knowledge of Quality Concepts (e.g. Risk Management, CAPA, Audits, Statistics).
Exhibits strong leadership and mentoring behaviors.
Strong problem-solving, analytical, and communication skills with high attention to detail
Builds strong relationships through open communication, respect, and trust
Team player with a customer/stakeholder-focused approach
Diplomatic, tactful, and effective at leveraging interpersonal skills
Embraces change with a continuous improvement mindset.
Fluent in written and oral English - B2 Level (writing documents, meeting minutes, conference calls, technical writing, presenting etc.)
Confident representing Quality in cross-functional settings
Self-starter able to manage multiple tasks in a fast-paced environment
Willing to work as part of a multi-site team; some travel required
