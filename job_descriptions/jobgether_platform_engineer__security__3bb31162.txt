This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Platform Engineer (Security) based in South Africa.

We are looking for a skilled Platform Engineer specializing in cloud security and secure infrastructure to strengthen the security, reliability, and performance of production environments. In this role, you will focus on implementing secure cloud-native platform architectures, improving application security, and protecting critical infrastructure against emerging threats. You will work across engineering and operations teams to identify vulnerabilities, mitigate security risks, and establish robust security practices. Your responsibilities will include infrastructure hardening, access management, compliance monitoring, and automation of security processes. You will also contribute to maintaining industry-recognized security certifications and ensuring that systems meet established regulatory and operational standards. This is an excellent opportunity to contribute to the development of secure, scalable, and reliable technology solutions within a collaborative, remote-first engineering environment.

Accountabilities


Security Vulnerability Management: Identify, assess, and mitigate security vulnerabilities, including Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVEs), penetration testing findings, and other infrastructure or application security issues.
Infrastructure & Application Hardening: Strengthen the security of production environments by implementing secure configurations across containers, Kubernetes clusters, cloud infrastructure, and application services.
Cloud Security & Platform Engineering: Design, implement, and maintain secure cloud-native infrastructure, ensuring that platform architectures follow established security principles and operational best practices.
Identity & Access Management: Manage secrets, credentials, and automated rotation processes while implementing least-privilege access controls and zero-trust security principles.
Security Monitoring & Compliance: Monitor infrastructure and application environments for security incidents, configuration weaknesses, and compliance deviations. Identify risks and implement appropriate remediation measures.
Security Automation: Develop and improve automated security processes, leveraging modern technologies and AI-assisted solutions where appropriate to support vulnerability remediation, configuration drift detection, compliance monitoring, and infrastructure hardening.
Access Reviews & Incident Preparedness: Conduct regular user access reviews and participate in tabletop exercises to evaluate security readiness, incident response procedures, and operational resilience.
Technical Documentation: Maintain and improve security and system documentation, including infrastructure diagrams, architecture specifications, security procedures, and operational guidelines.
Security Certifications & Standards: Support the implementation, maintenance, and continuous improvement of security certifications and compliance frameworks, including SOC 2, ISO 27001, and NIST.
Cross-Functional Collaboration: Work closely with engineering, technical operations, and support teams to integrate security practices into development and infrastructure workflows while ensuring secure and reliable service delivery.


Requirements


Between 3 and 6 years of professional experience in Platform Engineering, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), DevOps, Cloud Engineering, or Security Engineering.
Hands-on experience securing and maintaining production cloud infrastructure across platforms such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
Strong programming skills and experience developing or maintaining applications and automation solutions using languages such as C#.
Good understanding of cloud security principles, infrastructure security practices, and secure platform architecture.
Practical experience implementing security policies, procedures, and compliance requirements aligned with frameworks such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, and NIST.
Experience working with container technologies and orchestration platforms, including container security and Kubernetes environments.
Solid networking knowledge, including TLS, firewalls, VPNs, network segmentation, and secure communication protocols.
Experience identifying and remediating vulnerabilities, managing security configurations, and improving the overall security posture of production systems.
Understanding of secrets management, credential rotation, identity and access management, least-privilege principles, and zero-trust architectures.
Familiarity with infrastructure monitoring, security incident detection, configuration management, and compliance automation.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to investigate complex technical issues and implement effective security improvements.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work effectively across technical, engineering, and support teams.
A proactive and detail-oriented mindset, with a strong commitment to security, reliability, continuous improvement, and operational excellence.
A Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related engineering discipline, or equivalent professional experience.
Previous experience in the traffic management or mobility technology sector is considered an advantage.


Benefits


Competitive Compensation: Annual salary of approximately 155,000, as advertised. The currency and detailed compensation structure should be confirmed during the recruitment process.
Fully Remote Work: Enjoy the flexibility of working remotely, with the opportunity to contribute from your preferred location.
Full-Time Employment: Secure a full-time opportunity within a technology-focused engineering environment.
Professional Development: Expand your expertise in cloud security, platform engineering, DevOps, and modern infrastructure technologies.
Security & Compliance Exposure: Gain valuable experience working with recognized industry frameworks, including SOC 2, ISO 27001, and NIST.
Technical Innovation: Work with modern cloud-native technologies, container orchestration platforms, and security automation solutions.
Cross-Functional Collaboration: Collaborate with engineering, platform, and support professionals on meaningful infrastructure and security initiatives.
Career Growth: Develop your technical expertise while contributing to the security, scalability, and reliability of critical technology platforms.
