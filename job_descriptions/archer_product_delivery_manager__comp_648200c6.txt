Job Description

Are you ready to make an impact at the core of infrastructure modernization? Join our dynamic team where your expertise will drive the delivery of robust, scalable, and secure infrastructure solutions that power the enterprise. We value diversity, collaboration, and a growth mindset, and are committed to creating an inclusive environment where everyone can thrive. If you are passionate about enabling technology at scale and delivering exceptional customer experiences, we encourage you to explore this exciting opportunity.

As a Product Delivery Manager at JPMorganChase within the Compute Infrastructure team, you will serve as a key leader responsible for orchestrating end-to-end delivery of foundational compute platforms and services. You will act as the voice of the customer, ensuring infrastructure solutions deliver real value, reliability, and performance. Drawing on your deep understanding of infrastructure product delivery, you will guide successful launches, manage dependencies, and drive continuous improvement — collaborating closely with infrastructure engineers, architects, security, and application teams to deliver high-quality compute solutions that exceed customer expectations.

Job Responsibilities


Develop and execute a product delivery strategy that leverages modern compute technologies — including virtualization, cloud, containers, and bare metal — to maximize customer value and operational efficiency
Collaborate across the infrastructure and application ecosystem to define direction, set priorities, and execute initiatives aligned with business goals and technology advancements
Manage requests, inform and review priorities, make critical decisions, communicate updates, and escalate issues as needed to keep delivery on track
Build awareness of dependencies and cross-functional impacts to enable efficient, informed decision-making across teams
Lead discovery efforts and research to identify customer needs and integrate emerging compute capabilities — such as hybrid cloud, edge computing, and hardware acceleration — into the delivery roadmap
Own, maintain, and evolve a delivery backlog that supports the strategic roadmap and value proposition, with a focus on infrastructure features and enhancements
Build frameworks and track key delivery metrics including cost, capacity, utilization, risk posture, reliability, and performance indicators
Guide successful product launches, gather critical feedback, and ensure top-tier client experiences, particularly for mission-critical infrastructure services
Coordinate preparation, logistics, and material development for quarterly planning sessions with key stakeholders across the product and technology ecosystem
Monitor delivery progress, identify areas for optimization, and implement improvements to maximize efficiency, reliability, and overall impact


Required Qualifications, Capabilities, And Skills


Formal training or certification on product delivery concepts and advanced applied experience
Demonstrated experience in product delivery, product management, or program management, with exposure to compute infrastructure such as data center operations, cloud platforms, virtualization, or container orchestration
Advanced knowledge of the product development life cycle and delivery methodologies including Agile, Scrum, and Kanban, as well as infrastructure concepts such as compute resource management, virtualization, cloud computing, and containerization
Proven ability to lead product delivery activities including discovery, ideation, strategic development, requirements definition, and value management with a focus on infrastructure products and services
Demonstrated success managing and implementing operational effectiveness and portfolio management initiatives for large-scale infrastructure environments
Proven ability to operate within agile methodologies and deliver scalable, resilient, and stable compute infrastructure solutions


Preferred Qualifications, Capabilities, And Skills


Experience working in a highly matrixed, complex organization with multiple stakeholder groups
Experience collaborating with infrastructure engineering, cloud, security, and application teams
Familiarity with infrastructure metrics such as uptime, latency, capacity, and utilization, along with best practices for operational excellence
Knowledge of emerging infrastructure technologies and trends including hybrid/multi-cloud, edge computing, and infrastructure as code
Understanding of regulatory, compliance, and security requirements in enterprise infrastructure environments
