Who are we?



Lyka is an Australian founded pet wellness company that's shaking up an outdated industry and paving the way for happier, healthier pets. Despite medical advancements, animal lifespans are on the decline. Today, the average pet only reaches 37% of their full potential lifespan and statistics have shown us that many suffer from dental disease, cancer, and obesity. Simply put, the status quo is not enough.



As humans, we've experienced firsthand the positive impact fresh wholefoods can have on our health and wellbeing, and we believe our furry friends deserve the same. We're on a mission to do just that, starting with nutritionally balanced, wholefood meals delivered direct to doors around Australia. As the world's most sustainable pet wellness company, we're putting pets and our planet first - one bowl at a time.



Today, we are proud to share we've served over 100 million meals to puppers and their parents across Australia, and this is just the beginning. Our vision is to create multiple nutritional products, adding disease prevention meals and supplements, and expand into new markets.



Currently, we have a team of 250+ pet-obsessed people, and we're continuing to grow (yes, looking at you!). These carefully selected people are dedicated to building the next generation of technology and products to help empower pet parents to take control over their pet's wellbeing.



Our Mission



The time we share with our pets is being cut short. Despite medical advancements, they're getting sicker earlier. They're dying prematurely. The industry has it backwards - prioritising treating symptoms instead of addressing the root cause. That's where we come in.



At Lyka, we take a preventative, holistic and personalised approach to pet care. We look at the full picture, factoring in the diet, lifestyle and environment of each individual animal.



We don't stop at symptoms, instead we dig for the root cause. We create nutrition plans unique to each pet, because it's not just about living longer, but living well too.



Our mission is to increase the lifespan of dogs and cats.



The Role

As a Senior DevOps Engineer, you will be a technical expert responsible for building and maintaining the foundational infrastructure and tooling that enables our Engineering teams to deliver exceptional experiences to customers efficiently and securely.

You will combine expertise in cloud infrastructure, automation, and software engineering to design and implement robust, scalable solutions that enhance developer productivity while maintaining the highest standards of reliability, security, and cost efficiency. Working closely with our Engineering, Security, and Product teams, you will be instrumental in shaping our DevOps culture and practices.

This is a critical technical role where you will drive automation initiatives, oversee our AWS infrastructure, and build the tools and processes that empower our teams to move fast while maintaining quality. You will balance hands-on technical work with strategic thinking, ensuring our infrastructure scales with our business growth.



What you'll do:

Infrastructure Management: Design, implement, and maintain scalable AWS infrastructure with a focus on performance, reliability, security and cost efficiency.
CI/CD: Continuously improve our GitHub build and release pipelines, managing build configuration and instrumentation, ensuring seamless deployment processes and providing metrics the team can use to address bottlenecks and failures.
Developer Productivity: Build and maintain self-service tooling and automated workflows that reduce manual overhead, including access management systems, data synchronisation/migration, release automation, specialised LLM agents for safe targeted actions, and operational tools/dashboards.
Monitoring and Observability: Lead the evolution of our monitoring strategy using CloudWatch, Honeycomb.io , and Rootly to ensure the Engineering team has comprehensive visibility and and can proactively respond to emerging issues across all systems.
DevOps Advocacy: Champion DevOps best practices and culture across the Engineering team, providing expert guidance on solution designs and ensuring infrastructure considerations are embedded in our development processes.
Security Collaboration: Partner closely with our Security team to design, build, and deploy centralized security infrastructure, automated vulnerability tracking, and compliance tooling.
Engineering Heatlh: Collaborate with the Engineering leadership team to define, track, and alert on core Engineering health metrics, owning the complete data pipeline for engineering measurement and reporting.
Technical Mentorship: Provide guidance to Engineering teams on infrastructure best practices, troubleshooting complex issues, and promoting a culture of operational excellence.


Requirements


Strong fluency in at least one modern programming language.
Extensive hands-on experience with AWS services including Lambda, DynamoDB, Athena, EC2, RDS, API Gateway, WAF, CloudWatch, CDK, Amazon Glue, and IAM.
Proven experience designing and implementing CI/CD pipelines and build automation systems.
Experience managing and optimising cloud infrastructure costs while maintaining performance and reliability.
Strong background in monitoring, logging, and observability practices for distributed systems.
Experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) principles and tools, particularly CloudFormation and AWS CDK.
Demonstrated ability to build developer tooling and automation that improves team productivity.
Solid understanding of security best practices in cloud environments and DevSecOps principles.
Experience with incident response, troubleshooting, and maintaining high-availability systems.
Excellent communication skills and ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.
A high degree of autonomy and self-direction in driving technical initiatives.
Good judgement in when, where and how to leverage LLM-powered tools in your workflow to increase quality while maintaining pace and achieving token efficiency.


Preferred Qualifications

Proficiency in the NodeJS/TypeScript ecosystem.
Experience with GitHub administration and advanced CI/CD configuration.
Familiarity with Honeycomb.io , Rootly, or similar observability and incident management tools.
Experience with e-commerce solutions.
Mixed background in Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) and Software Engineering (SWE) practices and methodologies.
Experience with data pipeline tools like Amazon Glue and Athena for analytics workloads.
Knowledge of container orchestration and serverless architecture patterns.
Experience with workflow automation tools and platform (e.g. Slack applications, Google AppScript, LangChain, etc.)
Experience with building, maintaining and optimising harnesses for safe agentic workflows leveraging LLMs.
