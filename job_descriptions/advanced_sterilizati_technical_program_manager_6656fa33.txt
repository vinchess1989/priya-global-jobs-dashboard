Position Summary

ASP is seeking a Senior Project Manager based on-site at the Bangalore India Development Center (IDC) to lead software releases and medical device product development from planning through commercial release and lifecycle management. This role coordinates global, cross-functional teams and ensures products and releases meet business, customer, quality, cybersecurity, and regulatory requirements.

Location: On-site, Bangalore IDC, India

What Success Looks Like


Software and medical device releases are planned and delivered predictably, with clear scope, ownership, readiness criteria, and decision points.
Products are developed and released in compliance with applicable design controls, quality system procedures, risk management processes, and regulatory requirements.
Risks, dependencies, defects, and resource constraints are identified early and actively managed.
Local and global teams remain aligned through clear communication, effective governance, and shared accountability.


Duties And Responsibilities


Lead Product and Software Releases: Own integrated release plans and calendars from scope definition through readiness review, deployment or commercial release, and post-release follow-up.
Lead Regulated Medical Device Programs: Guide software-enabled medical device products and product changes from concept through development, verification and validation, regulatory submission or clearance, launch, and lifecycle management.
Ensure Release Readiness and Compliance: Confirm that required design control, quality, risk management, cybersecurity, traceability, verification and validation, regulatory, manufacturing, service, and launch deliverables are complete before release. Facilitate documented go/no-go decisions with accountable stakeholders.
Drive Cross-Functional Execution: Align Engineering, Systems, Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Cybersecurity, Product Management, Operations, Marketing, Service, Support, and external partners on scope, priorities, resources, timing, and deliverables.
Strengthen Planning and Predictability: Maintain integrated plans and actively manage milestones, critical path, dependencies, resources, assumptions, and trade-offs across concurrent initiatives.
Manage Risks, Issues, Defects, and Decisions: Identify concerns early, drive mitigation and resolution, and escalate decisions based on customer, compliance, quality, schedule, and business impact.
Lead Agile Delivery and Continuous Improvement: Apply Agile and Lean practices appropriate to regulated product development, remove impediments, and use retrospectives and metrics to improve delivery.
Provide Clear Governance and Communication: Deliver concise, decision-ready updates on program and release health, readiness, risks, dependencies, and required actions to working teams and senior leaders.
Use Data and FBS Tools: Use Jira, Confluence, dashboards, KPIs, Visual Project Management, risk management, and Kaizen or comparable tools to improve transparency and performance.
Build a Collaborative Team Environment: Promote trust, shared ownership, accountability, and constructive problem-solving across local and global teams.

Strong project and program management skills across scope, schedule, resources, dependencies, critical path, risk, and stakeholder alignment.
Knowledge of medical device software lifecycle practices, design controls, risk management, cybersecurity, and configuration and change management.
Strong Agile leadership and the judgment to adapt delivery practices to regulated product development.
Clear written and verbal communication, with the ability to influence global teams and present decision-ready information to senior leaders.
Structured problem-solving and sound decision-making using data, root-cause analysis, and business and customer impact.
Proficiency with Jira, Confluence, Microsoft Project, Miro, Microsoft Office, dashboards, and related collaboration tools.
PMP, PgMP, PMI-ACP, CSM, SAFe, or equivalent certification preferred.

About Advanced Sterilization Products

ASP is a leading global provider of innovative sterilization and disinfection solutions, and a pioneer of low-temperature hydrogen peroxide sterilization technology.ASP’s mission is to protect patients during their most critical moments. We support healthcare facilities in the fight to protect patients against hospital acquired infections, which are a leading cause of morbidity and mortality. ASP’s products, which are sold globally, include the STERRAD system for sterilizing instruments and the EVOTECH and ENDOCLENS systems for endoscope reprocessing and cleaning.
