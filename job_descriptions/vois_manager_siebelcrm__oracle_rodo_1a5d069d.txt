About This Role

We are seeking an IT Operations Readiness Solution Architect to ensure new services, applications, infrastructure changes and technology transformations are ready for production deployment, acting as a key connection point between Project, Engineering, Development, Vendor and Operations teams.

What You’ll Do


Lead Operational Readiness Reviews and define Operational Acceptance Criteria
Coordinate go-live readiness, Go/No-Go meetings, hypercare planning and exit criteria
Define support models, RACI, escalation paths, SLAs, KPIs and governance reviews
Ensure monitoring, alerting, logging, dashboards and service health visibility are in place
Validate incident, problem, change, recovery, runbook and knowledge management readiness
Review capacity, performance, resilience, DR, BCP and scalability requirements.



Who You Are


Experienced in IT Operations, Service Management, SRE or Application Support
Skilled in Incident, Problem, Change, Release and Service Transition processes
Confident managing production go-lives and large-scale transformations
Knowledgeable in ITIL, operational governance, risk management and stakeholder communication
Familiar with tools such as ServiceNow, Jira, Splunk, Dynatrace, Grafana or AppDynamics
Experience in telecom OSS/BSS, Managed Services, Oracle, CRM, Middleware, Cloud or Digital platforms would be an advantage.

What's In It For You


Opportunity to contribute to reliable production deployments and smooth service transitions
Exposure to cross-functional delivery across technology, operations, engineering and vendor teams
Ability to influence operational governance, service reliability and launch readiness practices



What Skills You Will Learn


Advanced operational readiness and service transition governance
Hypercare management, production go-live planning and risk mitigation
Practical experience with observability, resilience, support models and ITIL-aligned operations.
