About The Role

We are looking for an AI Project Manager to join a growing AI team within a leading global retail organization.

You will manage the end-to-end delivery of AI, GenAI, and digital product initiatives, working closely with business stakeholders, product teams, developers, Data Scientists, and QA teams.

This role goes beyond traditional project tracking. We are looking for someone who understands what the product is trying to achieve, who the users are, and how the solution creates business value, while ensuring projects are delivered successfully from initial planning through production launch.

Key Responsibilities


Lead AI and technology projects from initiation through delivery and launch
Manage project scope, timelines, resources, risks, dependencies, and stakeholders
Establish project governance and maintain delivery plans, RAID logs, status reports, and change controls
Work closely with developers and technical teams to understand dependencies and delivery constraints
Coordinate testing, UAT, defect resolution, and production readiness
Work with Product and Business teams to ensure solutions deliver the intended business value
Identify delivery issues early and proactively drive solutions
Explore practical ways to use AI and automation to improve project delivery and team productivity


What We’re Looking For


Strong Technology Project Management / Delivery Management experience
Proven experience independently leading projects end-to-end
Good understanding of the product lifecycle and software development lifecycle
Practical knowledge of project governance and PM methodologies
Strong understanding of testing, UAT, and release management
Excellent communication and stakeholder-management skills
Commercial mindset with an understanding of customer and business value
Proactive, resourceful, and able to work independently
Genuine interest in AI, with examples of using AI tools or automation to solve real problems or improve workflows
