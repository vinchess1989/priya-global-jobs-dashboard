Zensar is looking for Delivery Lead/ Scrum Master in Cape Town




Job Summary:




A Delivery Lead oversees end-to-end project or Epic or initiative execution, ensuring deliverables meet scope, budget, timeline, and quality standards while aligning teams and stakeholders.

Role Overview

The role bridges business goals, technical execution, and client expectations. This Delivery Lead role combines project management, Agile/Scrum facilitation, stakeholder communication, and account or operations oversight.

Key Responsibilities

Delivery & Scope Management: Plan roadmaps, track milestones, manage scope changes, and drive projects from kickoff to release.
Risk & Impediment Resolution: Identify bottlenecks, cross-team dependencies, and risks early, and escalate issues when needed.
Budget & Resource Planning: Monitor budgets, forecasts, SLA compliance, and team capacity or staffing.
Stakeholder & Client Alignment: Act as a key point of contact, share clear progress updates, and manage expectations with leadership and clients.
Team Support & Process Improvement: Facilitate team collaboration, remove blockers, and refine delivery workflows or Agile/Lean practices.
Monitor Value and Outcomes, Dependencies and Risks: Measure quality improvements, incident reduction for production bugs fixed, technical debt and manage dependencies with external teams and programs

Qualifications & Skills

Methodologies & Tools: Familiarity with Agile, Scrum, Kanban, Waterfall, along with tracking tools like Jira or Confluence.
Certifications (Often Preferred): PMP, PRINCE2, Certified ScrumMaster (CSM), SAFe, or ITIL.
Core Strengths: Clear communication, conflict resolution, structured problem-solving, and leadership without relying solely on formal authority.
