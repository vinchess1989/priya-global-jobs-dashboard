About Us

Kumaran Systems: Engineering Future-Ready Transformations Since 1992, Kumaran Systems has been helping enterprises bridge the past and the future, reimagining legacy systems and embracing AI-led possibilities. With over 34 years of delivery excellence, we specialise in re-engineering core applications, driving cloud transformation, and building automation-first, GenAI-enabled ecosystems across the Banking and Financial Services, Insurance, Telecom, and Automotive sectors. We help organisations not just upgrade their systems but reimagine what’s possible.

Why Kumaran?

We blend engineering discipline with AI innovation, helping clients modernise with confidence, automate with clarity, and scale with purpose. Our global delivery model ensures agility, responsiveness, and seamless collaboration, with clients always at the heart of every engagement. At Kumaran, we don’t just solve problems, we engineer future-ready transformations.

Qualifications


Experience supporting enterprise application releases, upgrades, and project delivery
Proven ability to manage application roadmaps and planning cycles
Experience with vendor engagement and third-party coordination
Strong background in change request management
Hands-on experience in release management, including patches, emergency releases, and mandatory upgrades
Ability to gather requirements and document specifications
Experience participating in and driving design discussions
Strong project planning and progress tracking capability
Ability to manage cross-functional dependencies, including infrastructure and integration impacts
Strong stakeholder communication and risk management
Experience in financial services/banking technology preferred
Ability to work in a Solution Consultant-type role bridging business needs and technical delivery
(Nice to Have) Solid working knowledge of CGI - Trade360 and/or WAL (Solifi’s (formerly Datascan) Wholesale Automotive Lending)
