STEM Recruitment Partners is seeking an experienced R&D Project Manager to lead complex technical projects within a global medical device engineering environment in the Dublin region.
This role will involve overseeing new product development, design changes and sustaining engineering initiatives, working closely with multidisciplinary teams to deliver projects on time, within budget and in compliance with regulatory and quality requirements.
This is a 12-month contract position that offers lucrative hourly rates (Up to &euro;80/hour DOE) and requires 3 days on-site per week.

Key Responsibilities
Lead the planning and execution of complex medical device engineering projects, including new product development, design changes and sustaining engineering initiatives.
Manage end-to-end project delivery, including scope, budgets, resources, schedules, stage-gate reviews and design reviews.
Drive cross-functional collaboration across engineering, quality, regulatory, clinical, manufacturing and commercial teams to achieve project objectives.
Identify and manage project risks, develop mitigation strategies and proactively resolve technical and operational challenges.
Monitor project performance against scope, schedule, quality and cost targets, providing regular updates and escalating issues where required.
Coordinate external suppliers and testing laboratories, ensuring project milestones and deliverables are achieved.
Key Requirements
Degree or Master's qualification in Engineering or a related technical discipline, with 10+ years' experience in medical device engineering, new product development and/or project management.
Proven experience leading complex medical device development and sustaining engineering projects, ideally involving FDA-regulated 510(k) or PMA products.
Strong knowledge of medical device regulatory requirements, design controls, FDA regulations, ISO 13485 and ISO 14971.
Experience managing multidisciplinary engineering teams and complex projects involving mechanical, electrical, software and systems engineering.
Strong understanding of verification and validation processes, risk management and medical device quality systems.
Excellent stakeholder management, communication and organisational skills, with experience using project management tools such as Jira, Smartsheet and MS Office. PMP certification would be advantageous.
