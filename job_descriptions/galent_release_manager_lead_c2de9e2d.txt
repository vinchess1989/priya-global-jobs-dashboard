Role: Release Lead

Location: Toronto, ON (Hybrid- 4 days onsite)




Key Responsibilities

Domain: BFS, Commercial Banking, OLBB

Tech Skill: Playwright/Python or Python ROBO framework, ability manager automation team

Experience: 8+ years




Roles and responsibilities:

Own and drive the end-to-end coordination of release activities to ensure successful and timely deployments into production.
Monitor and track production defects, ensuring appropriate prioritization, visibility, and resolution through collaboration with development and support teams.
Coordinate defect fixes and deployment schedules, ensuring commitments are met and releases are executed as planned.
Serve as the central communication point for QA ensuring alignment on release activities, risks, and dependencies.
Review deployment packages and code migration requests, assess potential code conflicts, and provide deployment approvals in accordance with established release governance processes.
Organize, facilitate, and lead defect triage and release readiness calls with all relevant stakeholders to drive issue resolution and decision-making.
Prepare and distribute regular release status reports, providing stakeholders with visibility into deployment progress, open risks, critical issues, and upcoming milestones.
Proactively identify, track, and escalate release risks, blockers, and dependencies to minimize impact on production delivery timelines.
Well versed with Testing Concepts,
Hands on testing experience
Should have managed the end-end to testing projects and coordination with stakeholder
Excellent verbal and written communication
