Role: Product Owner - Healthcare

Location: Remote-first with quarterly travel to Ireland

Annaul Salary: €65,000 - €70,000 (dependent on experience)

Industry: HealthTech / Clinical Software




A growing healthcare technology company is looking for a delivery-focused Product Owner to join their team.




The company builds software used by HSE and NHS trusts and private healthcare providers to manage diagnostic referrals, test ordering, and clinical decision support across areas such as radiology, pathology, and cardiology.




In this role, you will take full ownership of the product backlog and work closely with the engineering team to deliver practical, reliable software.




Key Priorities

Agile & Scrum Delivery (Essential): Run sprint ceremonies, manage and prioritise the product backlog, and write clear user stories with practical acceptance criteria. Act as a self-starter who can help guide sprint pace.
Technical Communication (Highly Desirable): Work closely with software engineers, understand system design, and communicate comfortably about APIs, cloud infrastructure, and data flows.
Healthcare Interoperability (Distinct Advantage): Experience or familiarity with healthcare messaging standards (HL7, FHIR) and integrating software with hospital Electronic Patient Record (EPR) systems.
AI & Workflow Automation (Distinct Advantage): Interest or practical experience with AI tools, workflow automation, or agent-based software solutions.




Candidate Requirements

2-5 years of experience as a Product Owner, Business Analyst, or in a similar software delivery role.
Strong working knowledge of Agile and Scrum frameworks.
Clear communication skills with the ability to turn complex clinical or user requirements into manageable development tasks.
A background in software engineering, computer science, or working on cloud-based SaaS products is a strong plus.
Prior experience in healthcare IT or HSE/NHS-facing software is helpful, but not essential.
