Job Summary

This role exists to ensure the quality, resilience, stability and successful deployment of technology solutions through effective test strategy, automation engineering and service delivery oversight. The role provides leadership across large-scale technology programmes, establishing automated testing capabilities that accelerate delivery while maintaining high standards of quality, control and operational resilience.

The role is accountable for defining and implementing automation frameworks, integrating vendor and internally developed solutions, and ensuring comprehensive testing throughout the software development lifecycle. Working closely with technology teams, business stakeholders and external vendors, the role drives continuous improvement in testing practices, deployment readiness and service reliability.

The role contributes to business outcomes by reducing delivery risk, improving release quality, enabling faster deployment cycles and ensuring technology solutions support business objectives, regulatory expectations and customer requirements within a complex financial services environment.

Key Responsibilities

Test Strategy & Quality Assurance Leadership


Define and maintain enterprise-wide automation testing strategies aligned to programme objectives and technology roadmaps.
Establish testing standards, methodologies and governance frameworks that promote quality, consistency and delivery excellence.
Ensure comprehensive test coverage across functional, regression, integration, performance and security testing activities.
Drive continuous improvement of testing processes, tooling and quality metrics.


Automation Engineering & Framework Development


Design, implement and optimise automation testing frameworks for complex technology solutions.
Develop scalable automation capabilities using approved tools and frameworks including Selenium and comparable testing technologies.
Enable automated validation throughout the development lifecycle to improve deployment quality and reduce manual effort.
Promote test automation best practices, reusable assets and engineering standards across delivery teams.


Vendor Solution Integration & Delivery Oversight


Partner with technology vendors to understand product architectures, release schedules and delivery models.
Develop and execute testing approaches that ensure seamless integration between vendor platforms and internal applications.
Validate vendor deliveries against business requirements, operational standards and technical specifications.
Support risk identification and resolution throughout implementation and deployment activities.


Service Reliability & Release Readiness


Ensure all technology releases meet defined quality, performance and operational readiness criteria.
Coordinate end-to-end testing activities across multiple applications, environments and stakeholder groups.
Identify and mitigate deployment risks that could impact service availability, performance or customer outcomes.
Support incident reviews and contribute to root-cause analysis and continuous service improvement initiatives.


Stakeholder Engagement & Delivery Collaboration


Collaborate with engineers, architects, product owners, programme managers and business teams to align testing activities with business priorities.
Provide testing insights, delivery status and risk assessments to senior stakeholders.
Facilitate effective communication between technology teams and external partners.
Influence testing practices and quality outcomes across cross-functional delivery programmes.


People Leadership & Capability Development


Lead, mentor and develop testing and quality engineering professionals.
Establish clear performance expectations and promote a culture of accountability, learning and collaboration.
Build team capability in automation engineering, testing methodologies and emerging technologies.
Support succession planning, knowledge sharing and continuous professional development.


Risk, Governance & Compliance


Ensure testing practices comply with technology governance standards, regulatory obligations and organisational policies.
Identify, escalate and mitigate technology, operational and delivery risks.
Promote secure development and testing practices throughout the delivery lifecycle.
Support audit, compliance and control activities through effective documentation and evidence management.
