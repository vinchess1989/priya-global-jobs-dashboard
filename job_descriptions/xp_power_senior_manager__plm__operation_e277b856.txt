This critical role leads the PLM Office for Operations as the single owner and “front door” for every product lifecycle event – prototypes and samples, NPI, engineering changes (ECR/ECO/PCN), parts transfers and End-of-Life – across all XP Power factories and the supply chain. Its mission is to ensure every product enters, changes and leaves production on time, at the right cost, with zero uncontrolled customer impact. The role is based in Singapore.




Prime Responsibilities and Duties

• Prototypes and samples: Intake and prioritise build requests, kit long-lead parts, book factory slots, track builds through to shipment and feed build issues back to Engineering.

• New Product Introduction (NPI): Own Operations readiness from concept gate to volume, running NPI concurrently to APQP standards; provide DFM/DFT and supply-risk input; ensure BOM, AVL and routing are ready in ERP; lead PPAP/FAI, pilot runs and ramp plans through to signed manufacturing release.

• Engineering change control (ECR/ECO/PCN): Assess the Operations impact of every ECR on stock, WIP, POs, cost and capacity within 5 working days; plan effectivity and cut-in; disposition obsolete stock; and trigger customer PCNs.

• EOL and obsolescence: Monitor supplier PDN/EOL notices, score risk, plan last-time buys (LTBs) and funding, request alternate-part qualification and plan product discontinuation with Sales.

• Parts and portfolio transfers: Validate and release all internal and external part transfers within XP Power’s global operations footprint.

• Process and data ownership: Act as process owner for Operations PLM workflows and business owner of PLM/ERP Operations data (item master, BOM, change workflow), maintaining one integrated lifecycle pipeline and dashboard.

• Financial and compliance control: Manage the E&O, scrap and LTB financial impact of lifecycle events, and ensure ISO 9001 / ISO 13485 and customer change-control compliance, including copy-exact and formal PCN notice periods.

• Decision rights and governance: Decide effectivity dates, cut-in, stock disposition and prototype build priorities; recommend LTB quantities and funding and NPI manufacturing go/no-go; escalate cost above threshold and customer-impacting conflicts to the VP Operations. Chair or co-chair the weekly Ops Change Control Board, NPI gate reviews, and the monthly EOL & LTB board, lifecycle pipeline review (S&OP input) and parts transfer review.

• Stakeholder partnership: Provide one voice on lifecycle topics to Product Development and Product Management (with Product Development retaining design authority and no ECO released without Operations sign-off), Sales and customers, manufacturing sites, Supply Chain and Quality & Compliance, escalating to the COO and CTO where required.

• Team leadership and capability: Build four capabilities – NPI Program Management, Change & Configuration, EOL/Obsolescence & Components, and Parts/Portfolio Transfers – initially by redeploying existing roles and sizing the team after the 90-day baseline; lead a network of site PLM champions (dotted line); and close gaps in competencies, methods and data.




To be successful in this role, you will be/have:

• An Engineering degree; MBA or PMP is advantageous.

• 5+ years in operations, NPI or PLM in electronics manufacturing.

• 5+ years leading teams across several sites and countries; power electronics or regulated markets (semi cap, medical) preferred.

• Strong knowledge of PLM/ERP change workflows, BOM and item master, and configuration management (CMII a plus).

• Working knowledge of obsolescence tools, LTB/E&O costing, PPAP/FAI, IPC, ISO 9001 and ISO 13485.

• Proven ability to build data-system architecture (Claude level 6 is highly desirable), modern cross-functional workflow management and visual PLM boards.

• Able to influence Product Development and Sales without direct authority, and to remain calm and credible with customers during escalations.
