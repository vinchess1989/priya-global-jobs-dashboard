About Us:

A global SaaS leader for seamless digital document workflows, Nitro offers a suite of solutions for PDF, eSigning, identity verification and analytics supported by a best-in-class customer success and change management team. With more than 3 million licensed users and 13,000+ business customers across 157 countries, we serve 67% of the Fortune 500.

How We Work:

We aim to create an environment where talented individuals are empowered to excel. How we collaborate, innovate, and engage with one another is important to us. Our work is driven by 5 key principles:


One team, One mission Our collective dedication to Nitro's mission defines us. Together, we are building an environment where everyone feels like a valued part of something bigger than themselves.
Own it We take full ownership of our actions and decisions. We empower one another to lead with confidence, creativity, and a solutions-focused mindset.
Accountable to our customers We are dedicated to our customers and take our commitments seriously. We do what we say we are going to do.
Excellence in execution Driven by passion and precision, we exemplify excellence in our delivery with innovative, top-quality results.
Be bold, fail fast, learn faster We learn as we grow, dare to try, and bravely question. We are not chasing perfection but forever iterating towards it.


These guiding values shape our approach to work, fostering a culture where everyone is inspired to contribute their best.

The Role:

As Nitro grows, our biggest initiatives span Product, Engineering, Sales, Marketing and Finance. This Program Manager will turn strategy into delivery, you’ll own outcomes, not just timelines.

What You'll Be Doing:


Turn strategy into plans. Surface requirements, break company goals (OKRs) into workstreams, milestones and owners
Run delivery across teams. Lead the operating rhythm, surface risks and dependencies early, and ensure decisions get made quickly.
Communicate clearly. Write concise updates for executives and stakeholders, and escalate appropriately when things are off track.
Measure what matters. Define success metrics up front, track them in dashboards, and run retrospectives that change how the next program is run.
Improve how we work. Use AI and automation to cut status-chasing and admin so teams can spend their time on the work itself.
Manage change, Drive adoption, not just delivery. Prepare teams, customers and partners for what’s launching, with communications, training and handovers.
Track adoption and outcome metrics after go-live, and close the program only when the change sticks.


What We're Looking For:


5+ years delivering complex programs that run cross functionally ideally in SaaS / B2B software.
A track record of programs that delivered business outcomes, not just ones that shipped.
Strong judgement in ambiguous situations: you can create structure without slowing teams down.
Excellent written communication; you can delivery and land a one-page status update with executives and stakeholders.
Fluency in modern tools such as Jira &Confluence and AI assistants & tools.
The ability to influence team members across our org.
