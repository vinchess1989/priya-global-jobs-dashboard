About The Role

As a DevOps Analyst Lead (IST/ePay), you will make an impact by driving automated, secure, and well-governed software releases across payment and financial services platforms. You will be a valued member of the IST/ePay team and work collaboratively with engineering, release management, security, risk, and compliance stakeholders. In this role, you will:


Lead the design, build, and maintenance of CI/CD pipelines and automation using Harness, Jenkins Pipelines, Ansible, and Python
Manage controlled releases in regulated and audited environments, including formal change gate processes
Coordinate security scans and track remediation to closure, partnering with risk and security teams on enterprise risk acceptance and mitigation
Oversee software asset and End-of-Life (EOL) lifecycle management using Flexera, keeping the environment supported and compliant
Guide and mentor team members, and serve as a point of escalation for release, automation, and compliance matters


What You Need To Have To Be Considered


Proven hands-on expertise building and managing automated pipelines with Harness, Jenkins Pipelines, and Ansible
Strong Python skills for scripting and automation
Demonstrated experience managing controlled releases in regulated and audited environments, including change gate processes
Practical experience with enterprise risk acceptance and mitigation processes, along with security scan coordination and remediation tracking
Hands-on experience with Flexera and End-of-Life (EOL) software lifecycle management


These will help you stand out


Background supporting payment or financial services platforms
Familiarity with ServiceNow for change and incident management
Experience working within PCI-regulated environments
