Our Commodities and Global Markets Central Portfolio Performance and Delivery team supports the effective prioritisation, governance and delivery of change. We strengthen product and agile practices across business and technology platforms to support clear accountability, adaptive planning and disciplined delivery.

We are a global financial services group operating in 30 markets with 57 years of unbroken profitability. At Macquarie, you’re empowered to shape a career that is fulfilling and creates value. You will bring your insights and expertise to the task at hand and feel supported as you make your own kind of impact for a better future.

What role will you play?

In this role you will lead the adoption and continued improvement of agile and product management practices across Commodities and Global Markets, helping embed our platform operating model across business and technology teams. You will partner with platform, product, business, technology, data and delivery leaders to translate customer needs into clear outcomes, prioritised roadmaps, well-managed backlogs and practical delivery plans. You will also take hands-on responsibility for administering and improving Jira, establishing consistent planning and delivery practices, strengthening governance and reporting, managing dependencies, and coaching teams to use agile ways of working to deliver measurable customer and business value.

What You Offer


Deep, hands-on Jira administration experience in a complex enterprise environment, including configuration, maintenance and support.
Proven ability to resolve Jira configuration, data and user issues and implement controlled, scalable solutions.
Experience working within a platform-based operating model and connecting customer outcomes and delivery.
Strong knowledge of product discovery, roadmaps, backlog management, agile practices and outcome measurement.
Collaborative and practical communication, coaching and facilitation skills, with the ability to influence senior stakeholders.
