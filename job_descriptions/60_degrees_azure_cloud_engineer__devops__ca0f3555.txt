THE OPPORTUNITY THAT AWAITS YOU




Don't just support the platform. Build the platform.

Some engineers maintain environments. Others design, automate and scale the infrastructure that powers critical software platforms.

We're partnering with a growing global technology business looking for an experienced Azure Cloud Engineer to help drive the reliability, automation, security and scalability of its cloud platform.

This is a hands-on role for someone who has personally designed, deployed and operated production-grade cloud infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines. You'll be trusted to improve engineering efficiency, create robust deployment processes and help shape modern cloud architecture.

We're looking for engineers who can demonstrate what they've automated, the scale they've supported, the technical challenges they've solved, and the impact they've made on delivery, reliability and performance.




WHAT YOU WILL BE BUILDING

Cloud-native Azure environments
Scalable CI/CD pipelines and release processes
Infrastructure-as-Code frameworks and reusable platform components
Monitoring, logging and observability solutions
Secure, highly available production environments
Automation tooling that improves developer productivity




YOUR RESPONSIBILITIES

Design, build and maintain Azure cloud infrastructure
Own CI/CD pipeline architecture and deployment automation
Implement Infrastructure-as-Code using modern tooling and best practices
Improve platform reliability, performance and security
Support engineering teams with deployment, monitoring and troubleshooting
Drive cloud governance and operational excellence initiatives
Build monitoring, alerting and observability capabilities
Manage containerised workloads and orchestration platforms
Investigate and resolve complex production issues
Champion automation and continuous improvement across the engineering environment




OUR REQUIRED EXPERTISE

5+ years' experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering or Cloud Infrastructure
Strong Azure experience across compute, networking and security services
Hands-on Infrastructure-as-Code experience (Terraform preferred)
Strong CI/CD experience using Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions or similar tooling
Experience supporting cloud-native applications and microservices
Containerisation experience with Docker and Kubernetes
Strong scripting and automation capabilities
Experience with monitoring and observability tools
Solid understanding of cloud security and governance best practices
Experience operating production environments at scale




IDEALLY YOU WILL ALSO HAVE

Azure certifications
Experience with Kubernetes administration
Experience supporting SaaS products
Exposure to distributed systems architectures
Experience with FinOps, cloud optimisation and cost management
Security and compliance experience within cloud environments




A KEY PART OF THE ROLE

Ownership matters. This role isn't limited to maintaining environments.

You'll be expected to:

Drive improvement initiatives proactively
Build solutions rather than manually support processes
Take ownership of production reliability
Partner closely with software engineering teams
Create automation wherever repetitive work exists

We're looking for someone who sees a problem and builds a scalable solution.




MORE IMPORTANTLY, SHOW US WHAT YOU'VE OWNED

We are looking for evidence, not just technology keywords.

You should be able to demonstrate situations where you personally:

Built and implemented CI/CD pipelines from the ground up
Automated infrastructure provisioning and deployment processes
Reduced deployment risk and improved release velocity
Improved platform availability, security or performance
Solved complex production incidents and reliability challenges
Introduced tooling that improved engineering effectiveness
Measured and communicated the impact of your work




WHAT'S IN IT FOR YOU?

Genuine ownership and influence
Modern Azure cloud technologies
Complex engineering and infrastructure challenges
Opportunity to shape platform strategy and best practice
High-impact work with visible outcomes
Strong engineering culture focused on quality and continuous improvement
Opportunity to work alongside experienced software and cloud engineers




YOUR REWARD

Opportunity to influence cloud and infrastructure strategy
Exposure to modern Azure and DevOps practices
Work on business-critical, scalable platforms
International collaboration with distributed engineering teams
Hybrid working environment with 2 days in office
Cape Town – Tygervalley
Competitive package aligned to experience and impact

If you've automated it, scaled it, secured it and can demonstrate the value you've created, we'd love to hear from you.
