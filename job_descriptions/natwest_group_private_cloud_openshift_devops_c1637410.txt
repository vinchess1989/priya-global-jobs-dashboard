Join us as a Private Cloud OpenShift DevOps Engineer


Take on an exciting Cloud engineering challenge in our Private Cloud OpenShift Container as a Service (CaaS) Engineering team to help deliver secure and scalable cloud capabilities
You’ll use modern engineering practices and tooling to develop cloud products that enable real-time capabilities, faster responses, and more seamless customer experiences
With continued investments in our engineering capability and strong executive support, you’ll have opportunities to grow your skillset, share best practice, and pursue relevant professional certifications to achieve your potential


What you’ll do

As a Private Cloud OpenShift DevOps Engineer, you’ll work at the forefront of our cloud transformation, using modern engineering practices to develop resilient cloud capabilities. You'll explore emerging technologies, challenge existing approaches and help turn innovative ideas into products and experiences that deliver meaningful value for our customers.

With customers at the heart of your approach, you'll combine engineering, automation and operational expertise to solve complex challenges quickly and iteratively. You'll collaborate across technology teams and partners to continuously improve our Openshift platforms, strengthen reliability and enable successful adoption of cloud-native capabilities. We engineer, we protect, we operate in NatWest Digital X.

You'll Also Be


Using infrastructure as code (IaC) to develop, deploy and scale cloud applications and products with greater speed, consistency and control
Promoting automation across testing and deployment to create efficient and reliable routes to production for new products and capabilities
Supporting engineered products throughout their lifecycle, responding to customer feedback, production issues and support requests to maintain effective service
Contributing to platform development and cross-team knowledge sharing, helping strengthen engineering capability and best practice across the organisation


The skills you'll need

To succeed in this role, you’ll understand how engineering decisions translate into customer outcomes, from the core development of technology to making risk-based operational decisions. You’ll bring a strong understanding of infrastructure layers and technology stack, with knowledge spanning servers, operating systems, virtualisation, Kubernetes, middleware and networking. You’ll also have experience contributing to roadmaps and strategies, working with third parties to assure the quality of their delivery.

In Addition, You’ll Have


Experience engineering, automating and supporting enterprise Kubernetes and Red Hat OpenShift platforms to deliver scalable, secure, and reliable solutions for business-critical workloads
Hands-on experience deploying and supporting platforms running containerised applications using cloud-native and DevOps practices, including Terraform, Ansible, Python, GitLab, and CI/CD pipeline automation
Knowledge of the OpenShift ecosystem technologies, which may include ACM, ACS, ODF, networking, service mesh, ingress, routes, load balancing and application exposure patterns
The ability to work within Agile, cross-functional teams to deliver strategic platform outcomes, champion DevOps practices and support customer adoption of cloud-native technologies
Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts clearly and build effective relationships across teams
