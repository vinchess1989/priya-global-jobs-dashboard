Support the delivery of customer-facing digital capabilities on the Digital Customers Platform. The role translates business intent, customer needs and regulatory requirements into clear requirements and user stories, and works closely with business stakeholders, technology and vendor teams through to implementation.




Key Responsibilities:

Product Vision & Roadmap

Support the Product Lead in shaping the product vision and roadmap for the Digital Customers Platform (mobile app and web portal), aligned with business objectives and platform targets.
Translate strategic priorities into a sequenced release plan and communicate it clearly to stakeholders and delivery teams.




Backlog & Delivery Management

Support backlog management by preparing and maintaining well-defined backlog items that reflect customer needs, business priorities and regulatory obligations.
Provide input on scope, priority and sequencing to support the Product Lead in maximising customer and business value.
Engage business and operations stakeholders to gather and analyse requirements, translating them into epics and user stories with clear acceptance criteria.
Track delivery milestones to ensure deliverables meet agreed specifications and timelines.




Customer Journey & Experience

Develop a deep understanding of end-to-end insurance customer journeys, business processes and service models.
Identify customer pain points and capability gaps and partner with UX design and customer research teams to validate solutions.




Testing, Release & Post-Production

Define test scenarios, coordinate User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and validate that deliverables meet acceptance criteria before sign-off.
Drive release readiness, production verification and operational handover, including communication to Operations and Customer Service teams.
Support post-production health checks, defect triage and resolution of routine issues.




Data-Driven Insights

Monitor product performance metrics (e.g. adoption, usage, conversion and customer feedback) to identify trends and recommend improvements for future releases.




Governance & Compliance

Ensure security, privacy and regulatory requirements are embedded by design across all product features.
Support audit, risk, third-party and compliance reviews through clear and complete documentation, in line with internal project management standards.




We are looking for people who

Have at least 3 years of experience in product ownership, business analysis or digital delivery, preferably in insurance or financial services.
Have hands-on experience working across different delivery methodologies; Product Owner or equivalent certification is an added advantage.
Can translate business and customer needs into clear requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria, bridging business requirements and technology solutions.
Are passionate about digital innovation and creating simple, seamless customer journeys across digital platforms.
Can work effectively across business, operations, technology and vendor teams, bringing clarity and alignment to complex initiatives.
Have a platform mindset, with the ability to consider end-to-end journeys, system dependencies, integrations, and downstream impacts when shaping solutions.
Are structured, data-driven problem solvers who can balance customer experience, business value, delivery constraints and risk.
Are comfortable working across diverse digital domains and can connect requirements across different customer journeys and platforms.




How you succeed

Champion and embody our Core Values in everyday tasks and interactions.
Demonstrate a high level of integrity and accountability.
Take initiative to drive improvements and embrace change.
Take accountability of business and regulatory compliance risks, implementing measures to mitigate them effectively.
Keep abreast with industry trends, regulatory compliance, and emerging threats and technologies to understand and highlight potential concerns/ risks to safeguard our company proactively.




Who we are

Founded in 1908, Great Eastern is a well-established market leader and trusted brand in Singapore and Malaysia. With over S$100 billion in assets and more than 16 million policyholders, including 12.5 million from government schemes, it provides insurance solutions to customers through three successful distribution channels – a tied agency force, bancassurance, and financial advisory firm Great Eastern Financial Advisers. The Group also operates in Indonesia and Brunei.

The Great Eastern Life Assurance Company Limited and Great Eastern General Insurance Limited have been assigned the financial strength and counterparty credit ratings of “AA-” by S&P Global Ratings since 2010, one of the highest among Asian life insurance companies. Great Eastern’s asset management subsidiary, Lion Global Investors Limited, is one of the leading asset management companies in Southeast Asia.

Great Eastern is a subsidiary of OCBC, the longest established Singapore bank, formed in 1932. It is the second largest financial services group in Southeast Asia by assets and one of the world’s most highly-rated banks, with an Aa1 rating from Moody’s and AA- by both Fitch and S&P. Recognised for its financial strength and stability, OCBC is consistently ranked among the World’s Top 50 Safest Banks by Global Finance and has been named Best Managed Bank in Singapore by The Asian Banker.
