As a Staff Technical Writer at GitLab, you’ll shape how users, and the AI agents working on their behalf, understand, adopt, and succeed with GitLab. You’ll partner with engineers, product managers, designers, and UX researchers to turn features and design decisions into clear, scalable documentation and intuitive UI text across multiple product stages and sections.

As AI increasingly drafts and reviews our content, you’ll bring the judgement that upholds quality and the system thinking that scales that judgement across our documentation. When you see the same problem repeating, you’ll fix the cause rather than just the page, whether that’s through a style guide update, a lint rule, or an AI skill. You’ll model effective use of AI, lead improvements to our docs site and processes, and contribute to one of the world’s largest open-source projects while engaging with our wider community.

What You’ll Do


Own and maintain documentation across product areas (which GitLab organizes by engineering stages and sections), driving improvements and balancing priorities.
Champion AI best practices by modeling approved tool use, establishing governance and quality checks, and integrating AI workflows that improve efficiency and quality.
Lead efforts to improve the product user experience through UI text, creating model examples and improving the style guide.
Design scalable information architecture and content types optimized for both human readability and AI retrieval.
Model review practices that improve the quality of contributions and feed back into AI review flows.
Design and lead process improvements. Identify systemic problems and the most efficient solution, building alignment with stakeholders and driving adoption.
Partner with engineers to translate documentation, content-quality, and user needs into improvements to documentation systems and docs-site functionality.
Use data to prioritize work, measure impact, and establish team KPIs.
Lead large cross-stage content initiatives, driving collaboration across functions to achieve shared customer and company outcomes.
Address feedback from internal teams, customers, support, and community contributors, identifying patterns for wider improvements.
Act as a thought partner to management on technical and project decisions, and act as primary lead for a specialist domain such as AI, UX writing, metrics, or tooling.
Mentor technical writers and team members on best practices and prioritization.


What You’ll Bring


End-to-end expertise in planning, researching, writing, and editing software documentation.
UX and content design skills, with a track record of refining interface text, labels, and naming conventions to improve the product user experience.
Experience designing and using AI workflows, including evaluating and refining AI-generated output and guiding quality checks for others.
Proficiency working in a docs-as-code environment, using the same workflows and tooling as engineering, operations, and product teams.
Fluency using the Linux shell and command-line Git, coupled with general familiarity with one or more programming languages.
Excellent grammar, minimalist documentation design skills, and effective information architecture.
Strong organizational skills, with the ability to triage and prioritize numerous issues and projects across stages and sections.
A track record of leading cross-team projects and process improvements, building alignment with stakeholders, and driving adoption.
Data-driven decision making, using analytics and feedback to guide content strategy and demonstrate impact.
The ability to succeed in a remote, globally distributed environment, including collaborating across countries and time zones, and to model respectful, professional communication.
Adaptability and readiness to work in a rapidly changing environment.
Familiarity with DevSecOps tools and open-source software is helpful, not required.


About The Team

Our technical writing team works at the intersection of product, engineering, design, and marketing. We define how GitLab’s features are communicated across documentation and UI text, and we influence product direction by bringing a deep understanding of how words shape user success in a rapidly evolving DevSecOps platform. We also manage the systems that ensure quality at scale, from the governance and orchestration of AI workflows to deterministic testing and linting. Guided by GitLab’s operating principles, we work in a globally distributed, remote-first environment where clear communication and reliable follow-through matter.
