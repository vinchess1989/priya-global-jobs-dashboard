Role Introduction

Reports to: Head of IT Solutions – Operations and Services

Join Cathay as a Portfolio Solution Manager, you will be at the heart of shaping and evolving our Engineering business application portfolio. Partnering closely with Engineering business teams and IT teams, you will identify technology needs, strengthen application capabilities and drive improvements across performance, technical health, productivity and cost effectiveness. You will take end-to-end ownership of the operational health, safety and security of applications while ensuring alignment with IT policies and regulatory requirements. Your expertise will help define application roadmaps, influence future investment priorities and turn business needs into effective IT solutions, making a meaningful impact on how technology supports and enables our business.

Key Responsibilities


Develop and drive the IT investment plan and application roadmap, aligning priorities with business needs and strategic objectives
Own the overall health and performance of the application portfolio, covering business capability, technical health, cost effectiveness and operational stability
Collaborate with Enterprise Architecture and cross-functional IT teams to maximize existing technology capabilities, promote reuse and optimize solutions
Stay ahead of airline industry and technology trends, leveraging emerging technologies and innovation to create competitive advantage
Promote modern technology practices, including DevSecOps, CI/CD and Agile/Scrum, to improve team efficiency, productivity and delivery
Maintain strong application and data governance, ensuring clear ownership and compliance with IT, security, industry and regulatory requirements
Take end-to-end accountability for solution formulation, delivery and ongoing support, ensuring projects are delivered on time, within budget and to required standards
Build, lead and develop a high-performing application team, including coaching, capability development and succession planning
Manage strategic vendors and external service providers, ensuring contractual performance, cost-effective delivery and access to relevant technical expertise and innovation
Own financial planning and budget management for application delivery, including annual budgeting, cost control, tracking and management reporting


Requirements


A minimum of 12 years of IT experience with 2 years of management experience
Tertiary qualifications
Proven experience in managing a team of technology professionals (that include in-house, external, off-shore resources) to run strategic IT projects and support IT systems
Ability to listen and articulate ideas verbally and in written formats to a broad range of audiences; ability to ask probing questions and deliver presentations
Strong interpersonal skills; promoting healthy relationships and team dynamics
Well-developed analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills, leveraging both logic and creativity; strong troubleshooting skills; ability to identify patterns and generate ideas
Focus on the end user’s or customer’s needs; ability to set expectations and understand end user behaviour
Solid experience in business analysis, software development and project management
Good understanding of technology concepts, trends and capabilities
