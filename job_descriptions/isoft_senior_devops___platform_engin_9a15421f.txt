About the Role

We are seeking an experienced Senior DevOps / Platform Engineer to support the engineering, integration and operationalisation of enterprise data and intelligence platforms.

The role requires strong hands-on expertise in Microsoft Azure, Snowflake, CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code and DevSecOps. The successful candidate will work closely with centralised platform and data engineering teams, leveraging established engineering patterns, pipelines, security controls and operational processes.




Key Responsibilities

Design, develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines for application, data and platform workloads.
Automate build, testing, security scanning and deployment processes across development, test and production environments.
Implement Infrastructure as Code using Terraform and Git-based workflows.
Support Azure cloud engineering, Snowflake platform configuration, workload deployment, RBAC and secure connectivity.
Implement secure integration between Snowflake, enterprise data sources, cloud services and downstream applications.
Embed DevSecOps practices, security controls, approvals and audit requirements into deployment pipelines.
Support deployment and operationalisation of data, analytics, Snowflake Cortex, LLM and AI capabilities.
Implement monitoring, logging, observability, resilience and production support processes.
Collaborate with centralised platform, data engineering, development and security teams to adopt reusable engineering patterns and minimise configuration drift.




Mandatory Skills & Experience

10+ years of experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering or Cloud Engineering.
Strong hands-on expertise in Microsoft Azure and Snowflake platform engineering.
Experience with CI/CD tools such as GitHub Actions, Jenkins or Azure DevOps.
Strong experience in Infrastructure as Code, particularly Terraform.
Proficiency in Git, Python, Bash and/or PowerShell automation.
Experience with cloud security, identity and access management, secrets management and private connectivity.
Practical experience implementing DevSecOps in large, complex or regulated environments.
Experience with monitoring, logging, troubleshooting and production support.
Ability to work within established enterprise engineering standards and centrally managed platform capabilities.




Good to Have

Experience with AWS and/or GCP.
Exposure to data engineering, analytics, dbt or Snowflake data pipelines.
Experience with Snowflake Cortex, AI/ML, LLM or agentic AI platforms.
Banking or financial services experience.
Familiarity with Docker and Kubernetes.




Ideal Candidate

A hands-on DevOps / Platform Engineer with strong Azure, Snowflake and Terraform expertise, capable of delivering secure, automated and scalable platform solutions while working within established enterprise engineering and governance frameworks.
