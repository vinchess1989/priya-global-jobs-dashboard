Your Role:

AI Automation Development

Design, develop and maintain AI agents and automation workflows that support use cases.
Write and maintain production-quality Python code for automation scripts, integrations, and data pipelines connecting AI agents with enterprise systems.
Apply AI knowledge (LLMs, prompt engineering, agent frameworks, and applicable Microsoft/Anthropic/Google AI platforms) to evaluate and deliver practical automation and AI use cases.




Platform Engineering & Deployment

Experience on Microsoft 365 services, including Exchange Online, Teams, SharePoint Online, OneDrive, Entra ID, and related M365 workloads.
Develop and maintain Ansible playbooks/roles using Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform for repeatable infrastructure operations (e.g., patching, health-information collection, disaster-recovery automation).
Own end-to-end software deployment activities, including build/release pipelines, environment promotion, configuration management, and post-deployment validation.
Integrate automation and AI agents with key systems, security tools and management tools.




Operations & Governance

Prepare and execute change-management activities, including risk/impact assessments, testing plans and post-implementation validation.
Maintain technical documentation, runbooks and operational procedures to support service continuity, knowledge transfer and audit requirements.
Collaborate closely with cross-functional technical teams (Infrastructure, Linux/Kubernetes, M365, Security Operations) to ensure smooth delivery of automation initiatives.
Communicate progress, risks and technical concepts clearly to both technical teams and non-technical stakeholders.




To Succeed in this role:

Degree holder in Computer Science, Information Technology, Software Engineering or a related discipline.
At least 2 years of hands-on experience in software development, automation engineering, platform engineering, or Microsoft 365 administration within an enterprise IT environment.
Strong hands-on programming skills in Python, including scripting, API integration and building automation tools.
Practical knowledge of AI/GenAI technologies — such as LLMs, Copilot Studio, prompt engineering, or agent-based automation frameworks — and the ability to apply them to real operational use cases.
Experience with Red Hat OpenShift (or equivalent Kubernetes/container platforms), including deployment, configuration and lifecycle management; or Ansible (Red Hat Ansible Automation Platform) would be an advantage.
Solid experience with software deployment / release management, including CI/CD pipelines, environment configuration and change execution.
Excellent communication skills, both written and verbal, with the ability to explain technical concepts clearly to diverse audiences.
High proficiency in English; Chinese (Cantonese/Mandarin) is an advantage for local stakeholder engagement.
