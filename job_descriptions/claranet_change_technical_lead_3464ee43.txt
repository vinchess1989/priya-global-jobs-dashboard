Position Summary




The Technical Solutions Change Team is responsible for the provision of in-life change to customers across the breadth of Claranet’s portfolio. This includes the management of technical configuration across customer solutions, as well as the development and implementation of change and governance processes relevant to Claranet customers.

In particular, the Technical Solutions Change Team Leader will be accountable for resourcing and prioritising workloads within the Technical Solutions Change Team, and for the enablement and enhancement of strong outcomes and KPI fulfilment across the team. c.6 Technical Solutions Change Engineers will report into this role.




Objectives & Key Results




Key Responsibilities

Understand and protect Claranet’s autonomous culture within the Technical Solutions Change Team, ensuring all Technical Solutions Change Engineers are empowered in their roles and encouraged to develop their skills
Ensure that Technical Solutions Change Engineers can effectively prioritise their time and develop and implement a diverse range of change and governance processes
Lead, mentor and manage the Technical Solutions team to ensure their skills and knowledge are up-to-date and aligned with the organisation's goals
Ensure in-life KPI’s are delivered in line with customer contractual obligations




Essential Duties

Manage, coordinate, lead and develop a team of Technical Solutions Change Engineers, being accountable for the high quality and timeliness of their outputs
A player-coach role that leads by example with excellent technical expertise and soft skills
Drive forward and progress Technical Solutions Change initiatives and technical configuration, using best practice change management methodologies and principles where relevant
Develop and implement change management policies and procedures to ensure the effective resolution to all customer change requests
Ensure that we are delivering change in the most effective manner that delivers our contractual outcomes
Ensure the customer change framework allows for assessment of impact and success criteria and that requests are planned, approved, tested, and implemented in a controlled and standardised manner
Design and implement automation where possible to reduce complexity and improve customer experience and effectiveness
Provide resourcing for in-life managed services projects, including end-of-life and decommissioning activities
Troubleshoot and rectify issues associated with the implementation of customer change and work with colleagues within the Technical Services department for escalation and consultation




People Leadership

Manage the day-to-day performance and activity of the Technical Solutions Change team, ensuing all staff are sufficiently experienced, trained, and qualified, and can access relevant opportunities to upskill
Lead, direct, coach, and develop direct reports, providing inspirational and forward-looking leadership
Build trusted relationships with senior stakeholders across the business




Critical Competencies – Technical Fit

Strong organisational skills, including the delegation, prioritisation and management of multiple tasks simultaneously and effectively
Strong knowledge and experience relevant to technical change management – in particular the delivery of in-life change to key customers – in order to perform complex tasks competently, confidently, and consistently
Proven leadership capacity, with the ability to empower and motivate direct reports
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, in order to influence and engage stakeholders at all levels, and to synthesise technical change processes into their clear impacts and benefits
Able to investigate, gather technical insight and data to resolve complex customer problems
