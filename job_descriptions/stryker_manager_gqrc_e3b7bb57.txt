What you will do:


Lead and develop teams supporting Corporate Regulatory Affairs, Clinical Affairs, and Quality Management Systems (QMS), fostering a high-performing and compliant organization.
Partner with Global Quality, Regulatory, and Clinical (GQRC) leaders to translate strategic priorities into clear operational plans, deliverables, and measurable outcomes.
Drive functional excellence by establishing governance, accountability, and performance metrics that ensure consistent, high-quality execution across regulatory, clinical, and quality functions.
Build scalable capabilities, processes, and operating models that enhance efficiency, standardization, and organizational effectiveness across supported teams.
Champion a culture of quality, compliance, and continuous improvement while ensuring alignment with global medical device regulations and quality standards, including ISO 13485.
Lead cross-functional collaboration with Regulatory, Clinical, Quality, R&D, and other business partners to support product development, lifecycle management, and market readiness activities.
Oversee resource planning, prioritization, and capability development to meet evolving business and regulatory requirements.
Develop, coach, and mentor talent by setting clear performance expectations, fostering career growth, and building strong functional communities of expertise.
Monitor emerging regulatory and quality requirements, assess business impact, and support implementation of changes across products, processes, and systems.
Leverage data, dashboards, and digital quality systems to drive informed decision-making, operational transparency, and continuous process improvement.


What you will need:


Bachelor's or master’s degree in engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Biotechnology, Life Sciences, Pharmacy, Regulatory Affairs, or a related discipline.
12+ years of relevant experience in medical devices or another regulated industry.
Demonstrated experience leading teams, complex programs, or cross-functional initiatives.
Experience supporting global functions is preferred.
