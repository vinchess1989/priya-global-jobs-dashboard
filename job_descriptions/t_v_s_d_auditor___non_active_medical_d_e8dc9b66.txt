Position Summary

To perform conformity assessment, quality management system audits, supplier audits, and technical reviews for manufacturers of non-active medical devices in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements, standards, and certification schemes. The auditor will evaluate compliance with quality management systems, regulatory requirements, and applicable medical device regulations.

Your Tasks


Conduct audits of medical device manufacturers, suppliers, and subcontractors for non-active medical devices.
Assess compliance with:
ISO 13485 Quality Management Systems
Applicable Medical Device Regulations and standards
Risk Management processes (ISO 14971)
Design, manufacturing, sterilization, packaging, and post-market surveillance processes where applicable.
Prepare audit plans, conduct on-site and remote audits, and complete audit reports within defined timelines.
Review quality system documentation, procedures, technical files, and manufacturing records.
Identify nonconformities and evaluate corrective action plans.
Participate in witness audits, qualification activities, and technical reviews as required.
Maintain auditor qualifications and continual professional development.
Support certification and regulatory assessment activities.
Communicate effectively with clients and internal stakeholders regarding audit findings and compliance requirements.
Client management as customer specialist

Your Qualifications

Education

Bachelor's Degree In One Of The Following Disciplines


Engineering (Mechanical, Biomedical, Industrial, Manufacturing, Electronics, Materials, Chemical or related disciplines)
Life Sciences
Biological Sciences
Medical Sciences
Physics, Chemistry, or other relevant scientific disciplines


Experience

Candidates must meet either of the following criteria:


Minimum 2 - 4years of experience in non-active medical device manufacturing, quality assurance, quality control, regulatory affairs, engineering, production, design, validation, process development, or related functions within a medical device organization.
Minimum 2 years of experience performing audits, technical reviews, product assessments, or certification activities for non-active medical devices within a Notified Body, Certification Body, Regulatory Authority, or similar conformity assessment organization.


Preferred Experience

Experience with one or more of the following technologies:


Orthopedic implants
Surgical instruments
Wound care products
Dental products
Catheters
Disposable medical devices
Sterile medical devices
Ophthalmic products


Required Knowledge


ISO 13485.
ISO 14971
Medical Device Regulations (EU MDR, MDSAP, national regulations)
CAPA systems
Design Controls
Process Validation
Sterilization Processes
Cleanroom Manufacturing
Packaging Validation
Supplier Quality Management
Post-Market Surveillance and Vigilance
Usability Engineering
Risk Management


Auditor Competencies


Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Excellent report writing and documentation skills.
Effective verbal and written communication skills in English.
Ability to work independently and manage multiple audit assignments.
Professional conduct and integrity.
Willingness to travel nationally and internationally for audits.


Additional Qualification Preferences

The following certifications are advantageous but not mandatory:


Qualified Lead Auditor in ISO 13485 or equivalent auditing scheme.
Experience auditing or manufacturing non-active medical devices such as:
Knowledge of EU MDR 2017/745 and MDSAP program requirements.


Additional certifications in quality management, regulatory affairs, or auditing.
