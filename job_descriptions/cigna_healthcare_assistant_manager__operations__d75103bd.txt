Key Responsibilities

Operations Implementation & Solution Delivery


Lead end-to-end delivery of operational and system implementation initiatives.
Drive requirement gathering, business analysis, process design, solution assessment, and user acceptance testing (UAT).
Ensure business requirements are aligned with operational principles, regulatory requirements, and customer experience objectives.
Identify process improvement opportunities and recommend practical solutions to optimize service delivery.


Business Analysis & Requirement Management


Facilitate stakeholder discussions to capture business requirements.
Review and challenge solution designs to ensure completeness, operational feasibility, and scalability.
Prepare and maintain business requirement documents, process flows, gap analyses, and operational procedures.
Support business sign-off activities and ensure proper governance throughout project lifecycles.


Operational Readiness & Change Management


Develop operational readiness plans for new products, system implementations, and business initiatives.
Coordinate training, communication, procedure updates, and post-implementation support.
Ensure impacts to frontline operations are understood and adequately managed.
Drive smooth transition from project implementation to BAU operations.
