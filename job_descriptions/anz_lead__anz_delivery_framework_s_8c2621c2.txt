About Us

At ANZ, we're shaping a world where people and communities thrive, driven by a common goal: to improve the financial wellbeing and sustainability of our millions of customers.

About The Role

Supports the creation, maintenance and continuous improvement of ADF framework assets, including lifecycle content, templates, toolkits, process maps, project setup guidance and practical adoption support.

Role Type: Permanent

Role Location:Melbourne

Work Hours: Full Time (40Hrs)

What will your day look like?

As a Lead, ANZ Delivery Framework Standards, Design & Improvement, you will:


Maintain ADF portal content, lifecycle assets, templates and toolkits
Support process maps, taxonomy and role guidance updates
Manage feedback logs, version control and release notes
Support new project setup and PIR materials
Assist ADF Lead with AI embedment and continuous improvement
Support Delivery Assurance recommendations where projects need to align to ADF



What will you bring?

To grow and be successful in this role, you will ideally bring the following:


Content management
Delivery lifecycle knowledge
Framework documentation
SharePoint / Confluence
Process thinking & mapping
AI enablement support



You’re not expected to have 100% of these skills. At ANZ a growth mindset is at the heart of our culture, so if you have most of these things in your toolbox, we’d love to hear from you.
