JOB TITLE: Regulatory & Compliance Administrator


LOCATION: Folkestone, Kent (hybrid working)


SALARY: £15ph to £17ph


TERM: 12 months +


HOURS: Monday to Friday, 37hrs a week




We have an exciting opportunity to join a global employer in Folkestone as part of their Regulatory team. Although this is initially a temporary 12 month role, there are always internal vacancies and career opportunities. You will be based in Folkestone, with flexible hybrid working on offer and an early finish on a Friday!

The key duties would be:

Support commercial teams, distributors and internal stakeholders with regulatory enquiries and product registration activities.
Prepare, coordinate and submit documentation required for new product registrations and maintenance of existing licences.
Research, interpret and apply local regulatory requirements across EMEA markets.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including Quality, R&D, Marketing, Supply Chain and Legal, to compile and maintain technical documentation and dossiers.
Manage registration projects, ensuring submissions and approvals are delivered within agreed timelines.
Monitor product changes and coordinate regulatory notifications and submissions to distributors and authorities as required.
Prepare, arrange and track the signing, legalisation and authentication of regulatory documents.
Maintain accurate records of registrations, licences and submissions within internal regulatory databases and systems.
Organise and maintain electronic regulatory files and documentation in accordance with company procedures.
Liaise with distributors, regulatory authorities, trade associations and other external stakeholders to support market access activities.
Generate reports and provide updates on registration status, compliance activities and project milestones.
Support continuous improvement initiatives to enhance regulatory processes and operational efficiency.
Undertake general administrative activities to support the Regulatory Affairs team.

We'd love to speak to candidates who have:

Understanding of EU Medical Device Regulation (EU MDR 2017/745) and associated regulatory requirements.
Strong organisational and project management skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously.
Excellent attention to detail and commitment to maintaining high levels of accuracy.
Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to build effective working relationships.
Proactive and self-motivated, with the ability to work independently and use initiative.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, Teams and SharePoint.
