Looking for a Senior Systems Engineer with strong medical device experience to support the development of injector and drug delivery products.




The role will involve:

Leading Systems Engineering activities across development projects
Defining system and subsystem requirements
Translating user needs into design inputs
Acting as a system-level SME for technical decisions
Supporting risk management, design controls and regulatory activities
Collaborating across Verification, Validation, Quality, Clinical and Regulatory teams




Requirements:

5+ years’ Systems Engineering experience in regulated products

3+ years’ medical device development experience

Experience with auto-injectors, pen injectors or similar drug delivery devices

Knowledge of ISO 13485 and ISO 14971
