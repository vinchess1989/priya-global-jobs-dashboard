Role Description

Senior DevOps Engineer – AWS & Terraform

Job Summary

We are looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer with strong expertise in AWS, Terraform, Docker, and Python Scripting. You will design, build, automate, and operate reliable cloud infrastructure and containerized workloads, helping make platform delivery scalable, predictable, and resilient.

Key Responsibilities


Design, build, and operate Docker-based containerized workloads on AWS.
Provision, manage, and maintain AWS cloud infrastructure using Terraform.
Build reusable and maintainable Terraform configurations to support reliable infrastructure provisioning.
Develop and maintain Python Scripting solutions for infrastructure automation, operational tooling, and deployment support.
Troubleshoot infrastructure and deployment issues and implement sustainable fixes.
Apply appropriate security, reliability, and cost-management practices across AWS environments.
Collaborate with development, QA, and product teams to improve deployment reliability and operational efficiency.
Take ownership of cloud infrastructure availability, stability, and continuous improvement.


Required Skills

Proficient


AWS
Terraform
Docker
Python Scripting


Experience


5+ years of experience in DevOps, cloud engineering, or site reliability engineering.


Why Join UST

At UST, you’ll have the opportunity to work on technology-led solutions and digital transformation initiatives while collaborating with teams across a global organization. You’ll also have opportunities to strengthen your technical expertise through continuous learning and career growth.
