About Capgemini

Capgemini is an AI-powered global business and technology transformation partner, delivering tangible business value. We imagine the future of organizations and make it real with AI, technology and people. With our strong heritage of nearly 60 years, we are a responsible and diverse group of over 420,000 team members in more than 50 countries. We deliver end-to-end services and solutions with our deep industry expertise and strong partner ecosystem, leveraging our capabilities across strategy, technology, design, engineering and business operations. The Group reported 2025 global revenues of €22.5 billion.

Responsibilities


Collaborate with development, operations, and quality teams to understand project requirements and implement DevOps best practices.
Design and implement CI/CD pipelines to automate software delivery processes across multiple applications.
Configure and maintain infrastructure as code (IaC) using tools like Terraform
Manage containerized environments using Docker and orchestration tools like Kubernetes.
Implement monitoring dashboards and logging solutions to ensure system stability and performance optimization while building meaningful alerts for the team.
Continuously optimize and improve DevOps workflows to enhance productivity and reduce time-to-market.
Provide technical guidance and support to development teams on DevOps tools and practices.
Collaborate with security teams to implement and maintain best practices for secure application deployment and infrastructure management.
Participate in on-call rotations to provide timely support for production incidents.


Requirements


Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field; or equivalent work experience.
Proven experience as a DevOps Engineer or similar role, with a strong understanding of software development lifecycle and DevOps principles.
Hands-on experience with CI/CD tools such as GitLab CI/CD.
Proficiency in scripting languages such as Bash, Python, or Javascript.
Knowledge of cloud computing platforms, preferably Amazon Web Services(AWS).
Familiarity with containerization technologies and container orchestration.
Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills with a focus on automation.
Excellent communication and collaboration abilities, with a proactive approach to sharing knowledge and best practices.


Good To Have Qualifications


Experience with infrastructure as code (IaC) tools like Terraform.
Certification in DevOps-related technologies (e.g., AWS Certified DevOps Engineer, Kubernetes Administrator).
Familiarity with security best practices for cloud-based environments.
