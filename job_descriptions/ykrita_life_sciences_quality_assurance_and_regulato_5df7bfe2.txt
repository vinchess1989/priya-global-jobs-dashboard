Company Description

Ykrita Life Sciences Pvt Ltd is an innovation-driven healthcare company focused on developing advanced medical technologies such as liver-assist devices to improve patient outcomes.
The organisation works at the intersection of clinical medicine, biomedical engineering, and translational research to bring novel therapies from concept to clinical practice.
The company emphasises robust quality systems, regulatory compliance, and continuous improvement across its operations.
Ykrita Life Sciences offers opportunities to work in a regulated environment with clear standards, processes, and cross-functional collaboration. 

Role Description

This is a full-time, on-site role based in Bengaluru for a Quality Assurance and Regulatory Affairs Executive.
The role involves preparing and reviewing regulatory documentation, ensuring compliance with national and international regulatory requirements, and supporting dossier preparation for product submissions.
Day-to-day activities include coordinating with cross-functional teams to gather data, maintaining regulatory records, assisting in audits and inspections, and monitoring regulatory updates relevant to the company’s products.
The executive will also support quality assurance activities such as document control, deviation and CAPA tracking, and adherence to internal SOPs and quality standards.
The position requires close collaboration with manufacturing, quality control, and management to ensure that all regulatory and quality obligations are met in a timely and accurate manner.

Qualifications

Candidates should possess a degree in Biomedical Engineering/ Pharmacy/ Life Sciences/ Biotechnology, or a related field, along with relevant experience in Medical devices, Pharma or life sciences regulatory roles. 
Candidates should possess strong skills in English Language, Regulatory Documentation and Dossier Preparation, including compiling, reviewing, and maintaining submission packages.
Candidates should possess knowledge of Regulatory Requirements and Regulatory Compliance, with the ability to interpret and apply relevant guidelines and standards.
Candidates should possess expertise in Regulatory Affairs, including interaction with regulatory agencies and support for product registrations and approvals.
Candidates should possess familiarity with quality assurance principles, SOPs, and GDP practices, and the ability to support audits and inspections.
Candidates should possess strong attention to detail, analytical skills, effective written and verbal communication, and the ability to work collaboratively on-site in Bengaluru.

Salary range and benefits: as prevailing in the organisation based on experience and skills
