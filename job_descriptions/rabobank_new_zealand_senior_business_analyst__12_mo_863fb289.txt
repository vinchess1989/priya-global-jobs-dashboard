About the role | Mō te tūranga

Make an impact where regulation, resilience and transformation come together!

We’re looking for an experienced Senior Business Analyst to join a significant, multi-year regulatory transformation programme supporting Rabobank’s implementation of the New Zealand Deposit Takers Act (NZ DTA).

With a key focus on Operational Resilience, this is an opportunity to work on a programme that will have a lasting impact on how we protect our critical operations, manage operational risk and strengthen resilience across our business, technology and third-party environments.

Based in Wellington, you’ll work alongside Product Owners and senior stakeholders across Risk, Compliance, Operations and Technology, helping turn complex regulatory obligations into practical, sustainable business outcomes.

This is a 12-month fixed-term position.

What You’ll Be Doing

This is a broad Senior BA role where you’ll be involved across the full delivery lifecycle, from discovery and design through to testing, implementation and adoption.


Lead discovery and analysis across complex regulatory and operational resilience requirements
Help identify and document critical operations and the processes, systems, people, data and third parties that support them
Lead end-to-end process mapping, capturing current-state operations and designing future-state processes and operating models
Translate regulatory obligations into clear business requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, processes and controls
Assess business, operational, technology and organisational impacts
Facilitate workshops and working sessions with stakeholders across the organisation
Partner with business and technology teams to shape system, process and control enhancements
Support the development of policies, procedures and business readiness materials
Maintain requirements, decisions and end-to-end traceability
Support testing and assurance activities, including UAT
Identify and manage risks, issues, dependencies and assumptions
Support implementation and change activities to ensure new requirements are successfully embedded


About you | Ō pūkenga

You’ll bring strong Senior Business Analyst capability, along with the confidence to navigate ambiguity, unpack complex requirements and influence stakeholders across both business and technology.

Ideally, you’ll have:


7+ years’ experience as a Business Analyst, including experience operating at Senior BA level
Experience delivering complex regulatory, risk, compliance or governance initiatives
Strong requirements elicitation, documentation, process mapping and traceability skills
Experience translating regulatory obligations into practical business requirements, processes and controls
Strong workshop facilitation and stakeholder engagement capability
Experience working across large-scale programmes or multiple interconnected projects
Experience supporting change through design, testing and implementation
Exposure to New Zealand banking regulation, including the NZ Deposit Takers Act and Operational Resilience requirements, and/or experience with APRA CPS 230
A relevant tertiary qualification or equivalent practical experience


Experience within banking or financial services, operational resilience, business continuity or operational risk would be highly regarded. A Business Analysis certification such as CBAP, CCBA or BCS would also be an advantage.
