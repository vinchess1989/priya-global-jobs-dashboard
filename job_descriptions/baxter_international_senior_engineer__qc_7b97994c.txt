We are looking for a Senior Engineer, Quality Control (QC) to join our team in Marsa, Malta. In this role, you will be responsible for supporting and coordinating Quality Control activities across incoming, in-process, and finished product testing, while ensuring compliance with regulatory, customer, and Baxter quality requirements.

Quality In-Process activities:

•Handling of quality issues arising during manufacturing or during in-process and finished testing of product.

•Responsible for the Hold process for non-conforming raw materials/sub-assemblies/finished products.

•Support the quality control operations by maintaining adequate sampling plans and acceptance criteria in order to satisfy both internal and customer quality requirements.

 

Labelling implementation activities

•Maintain and follow the labelling implementation process for the issuance of new/revised labelling for Baxter Malta ensuring compliance with corporate quality procedures, regulatory and customers requirements.

•Support Global Labelling functions on matters related to specific manufacturing requirements.

 

People management activities:

•Coordinate the QC laboratory team members

•Manage daily activities related to all QC sections (i.e. involving incoming, in-process, finished product)

 

Other activities

•NCR/CAPA owner: Own investigations, as well as form part of investigation teams, to analyze and mitigate product issues.

•Support during internal and external audits and perform internal audits and in-process assessments.

•Support manufacturing and R&D projects involving product improvement and new product development.

•To assist in adopting/applying LEAN initiatives within the section and take part in continuous improvement activities.

 

General activities:

•Perform duties in accordance with procedures aligned with EN ISO 13485.

•Ensure that local procedures related to the role are in line with Regulatory and Corporate requirements and that any corrective actions triggered by TUV/Corporate/Internal/Other audits are always implemented within the established time frames.

•Consider the aspects of Environment Health and Safety procedures to ensure that all activities within her role will support EHS targets and will not jeopardize the health and safety of employees and environment.

•Follow good laboratory and documentation practices.

Qualifications:


Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Quality, Life Sciences, or a related technical field.
Minimum 3 years of experience in a similar role or another Engineering-related position.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
A team-oriented professional who enjoys working cross-functionally, building strong relationships, and contributing to collective success.
Ability to coordinate and lead team activities while fostering a collaborative and supportive working environment.
