Quality Engineer (Medical Devices) | Open to Freah Graduates!

Job Summary

Responsible for ensuring product and process compliance with medical device regulations and quality standards while supporting continuous improvement, risk management, validation, and regulatory compliance activities.

Key Responsibilities


Maintain and improve QMS in compliance with ISO 13485, ISO 9001, FDA QSR, and GMP requirements.
Lead CAPA investigations and effectiveness verification.
Conduct internal, supplier, and regulatory audits.
Manage NCRs, product deviations, and quality investigations.
Support risk management activities including FMEA and hazard analysis in accordance with ISO 14971.
Review and maintain quality documentation including:
DHF
DMR
DHR
Support IQ/OQ/PQ, process validation, and software validation activities.
Collaborate with R&D, Manufacturing, and Regulatory teams for new product development.
Customer Quality roles and responsibility - Point of contact on overall quality on product and process compliance.
Drive Critical Quality KPI’s.
Manage supplier quality performance and qualification activities.
Drive continuous improvement initiatives using Lean and Six Sigma methodologies.
Assist NPI on new programs / projects.


Qualifications


Bachelor's Degree in Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Life Sciences, or related field.
3–8 years of experience in Medical Devices, Healthcare Products, or Pharmaceutical industries.
Strong knowledge of:
ISO 13485
FDA 21 CFR Part 820
ISO 14971
GMP
CAPA
Validation

Preferred


CQE or Six Sigma certification.
Experience with Class II/III Medical Devices.
Knowledge of CE Marking and 510(k) submissions.
Experience with electronic QMS systems.
PCBA/SMT manufacturing experience.


Key Performance Indicators


CAPA Closure Rate
Audit Compliance Score
NCR Reduction
Process Improvement Metrics
Supplier Quality Performance


and experience.
