Responsibilities

Being a part of the Hollywoodbets Purple Team is an opportunity to become a part of one of South Africa’s biggest success stories. From humble beginnings in Durban, Hollywoodbets has grown into a powerhouse brand operating across Africa and the United Kingdom. And that’s just the beginning. Our talented Team is on a mission to increase our global footprint, ready to take on any challenge because at Hollywoodbets, not even the sky is the limit. Join our Team and let’s reach for the stars.

We have an amazing opportunity for a Product Specialist (x2) based in Umhlanga, KZN. Do you think you have what it takes to be our newest Purple Star?

The Product Specialist is responsible for managing, optimizing, and growing the company’s online product portfolio — including Slots, Casino, Virtuals, Instant Games, Sports Betting and other betting or gaming platforms. The role encompasses end-to-end product lifecycle management, market analysis, platform enhancement, partner coordination, and ongoing performance optimization. A key focus of the position is improving customer experience, ensuring platform stability, driving innovation, and contributing to revenue growth. The Product Specialist serves as the link between technical teams, business stakeholders, and external providers while ensuring compliance with Internal & Regulatory requirements.

With Hollywoodbets You Will

Innovate and create as part of a like-minded, authentic Team eager to achieve goals.

Embrace challenges and the thrill of working in a vibrant and fast-paced industry.

Grow with our development plans and culture that allows you to further your career.

Product & Platform Management


Manage the full product lifecycle — ideation, specification, testing, launch, maintenance, and performance optimisation.Maintain and enhance all betting platforms (website, Apps, USSD, Syx, TUV, Refer-a-Friend, gaming platform, Etc.)
Identify platform shortfalls in usability, UX/UI, customer engagement, and functionality.
Recommend and implement improvements, new features, and technical upgrades.
Test all new games, features, odds, and system changes before go-live.
Conduct ongoing testing of current products to ensure optimal performance across devices and platforms.
Compile Business Requirement Documents (BRDs) for enhancements and integrations.
Evaluate customer behavior and insights to improve product usability and brand perception.
Ability to be flexible and work after hours if required.


Innovation & Market Insight


Conduct comprehensive competitor benchmarking (local and international) to identify content trends, new innovations, and improvement opportunities.
Recommend innovative product concepts, features, and enhancements aligned with market and player behaviour.
Stay updated on global iGaming developments, technologies, and regulatory changes.
Assess promo tools from providers and guide internal teams on effective usage. Provide strategic input into game prioritisation, provider engagements, and content positioning across all platforms.


Project & Stakeholder Management


Collaborate with cross-functional teams – Tech, Marketing, CRM, Compliance, Risk, BI, Support, Etc
Act as the voice of the customer in Agile ceremonies.
Ensure alignment between product initiatives and operational teams such as Support and Fraud.
Communicate updates, feature releases, and product performance to internal stakeholders.


Testing, Quality Assurance & Compliance


Oversee all user acceptance testing (UAT) and ensure product readiness before release.
Verify product functionality, performance, and odds accuracy prior to going live.
Identify and mitigate product-related risks, including fraud and data inconsistencies
Ensure products comply with provincial and national gaming regulations.
Collaborate with Compliance teams on GB approvals and maintain up-to-date documentation.


Data Analysis & Reporting


Analyze daily, weekly, and monthly product KPIs (e.g., GGR, engagement, conversion, player activity, margins).
Report on product performance following business or competitor-driven changes.
Identify trends, opportunities, risks, and areas requiring intervention.
Make data-driven recommendations to improve product performance, revenue, and customer engagement.
Monitor customer patterns, big wins, and betting behaviour to guide strategic decisions


Customer Experience & Issue Resolution


Review and respond to betting-related queries, ensuring swift resolution and improved service quality.
Analyse customer feedback to identify recurring issues and propose improvements.
Recommend UX/UI updates based on player feedback and analytical findings.


Marketing & Customer Engagement


Support marketing teams with product-specific campaigns, promotions, new game launches, and engagement strategies.
Provide insights on promotional tools and campaign effectiveness.
Assist with producing advertising, educational, and brand content relating to product verticals. Monitor and identify high- impact wins or events that should be marketed.
