Job Description:

Intermediate Software Implementation Specialist Location: Cape Town, Western Cape Employment: Full-time, permanent Salary: R28,000–R35,000 gross per month (depending on experience)

Purpose of the role Take ownership of client software implementations after the sale. Gather and document requirements for customised software modules, coordinate delivery and rollout, train users, provide first-line support and relay actionable feedback to the development team.

Key responsibilities

Receive sales handovers and meet clients to understand workflows, objectives and required functionality.
Document functional requirements, process steps, agreed scope and clear development briefs.
Coordinate implementation schedules, configuration, testing and user acceptance testing (UAT).
Attend client sites for rollout, go-live support and user training.
Provide first-line functional support, log issues and follow through on resolution.
Capture client feedback, distinguish bugs from enhancement requests and coordinate priorities with developers.
Communicate progress, risks and next steps to clients and internal stakeholders.
Obtain client acceptance/sign-off and hand over to ongoing support. Candidate requirements
Approximately 2–4 years of experience in software implementation, application support, business analysis or IT project coordination.
Strong client communication, documentation, organisation and problem-solving skills.
Ability to translate operational requirements into clear instructions for developers.
Practical experience with software testing, user training and issue tracking.
Comfortable handling multiple client projects.
Valid Driver's licence and willingness to visit client sites.

Useful additional experience CRM/ERP and workflow software; modular business applications; Jira, Trello, Monday.com or Azure DevOps; basic API/integration knowledge; Agile delivery.
