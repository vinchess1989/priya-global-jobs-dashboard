About The Company

EROAD is a global fleet management technology company, listed on both the NZX and ASX, helping fleet operators run safer and more compliant operations through its connected hardware and software platform. As the business continues to grow, it's looking for a Delivery Project Manager to lead complex technology and transformation initiatives from its Albany site.

What You'll Do


You'll take ownership of delivery across a programme that spans multiple teams and moving parts.
Lead delivery of complex technology, systems or business transformation initiatives from planning through to implementation
Manage multiple workstreams, dependencies, vendors and cross-functional delivery teams in parallel
Own delivery planning, milestone tracking, RAID logs and governance reporting through to executive level
Guide teams and stakeholders through ambiguity, supporting change management as initiatives evolve
Coordinate requirements, solution delivery, testing, UAT, release and implementation activities across the programme
Engage senior stakeholders directly, facilitating decisions and negotiating outcomes across competing priorities
Partner closely with the Programme Manager, Workstream Leads, Business Analysts, technical teams, architects and business SMEs to keep delivery on track


What You Need


We're after someone who can bring order and pace to complex delivery. 5+ years delivering complex technology, systems or business transformation initiatives
Track record managing multiple workstreams, dependencies, vendors and cross-functional teams at once
Comfortable operating in ambiguity and confident leading people through change
Skilled at engaging and influencing senior stakeholders
Disciplined approach to risk management, deliverable tracking and governance process
Solid working knowledge of Agile, SAFe and traditional project delivery approaches
Strong facilitation and negotiation skills, with experience running requirements, testing, UAT, release and implementation coordination Familiar with Jira and Confluence
A relevant tertiary qualification or equivalent practical experience, with formal PM certification such as PMP, PRINCE2, SAFe or Agile an advantage


WHY JOIN


This is a chance to lead meaningful delivery work inside a true Kiwi tech success story operating at global scale.
Be part of a high-growth, innovative technology company making a real difference across New Zealand, Australia, the Philippines and the USA
Work alongside a talented and collaborative delivery team
Benefit from continuous learning support, EAP offerings and AI tooling that help you grow
Join a multicultural organisation that genuinely values diversity Competitive salary and benefits package
