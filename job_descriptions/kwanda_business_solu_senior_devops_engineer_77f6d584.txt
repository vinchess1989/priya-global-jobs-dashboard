Introduction

A leading telecommunications company is seeking an experienced Senior DevOps Engineer to join its team on a contractual basis.

The successful candidate must have at least 5+ years of hands-on DevOps engineering experience, with strong technical expertise in cloud environments, CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure automation, scripting and containerisation.

We are looking for a technically strong individual with excellent problem-solving abilities, who can work independently and contribute to complex enterprise technology environments. 

Duties & Responsibilities

Senior DevOps Engineer – Key Duties & Responsibilities

DevOps Strategy & Implementation
Design, implement and maintain DevOps processes and practices.
Improve software development, deployment and release processes.
Drive automation and continuous improvement across IT environments.
CI/CD Pipeline Management
Design, develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps, Jenkins and GitLab.
Automate application builds, testing and deployments.
Manage release processes and troubleshoot pipeline failures.
Cloud Infrastructure Management
Design, deploy and maintain cloud infrastructure across Azure and/or AWS.
Manage cloud environments, configurations and resources.
Ensure infrastructure scalability, availability, security and performance.
Infrastructure as Code (IaC)
Automate infrastructure provisioning using Terraform.
Implement configuration management using Ansible.
Maintain reusable infrastructure templates and deployment scripts.
Containerisation & Orchestration
Build, deploy and manage Docker containers.
Manage Kubernetes environments and container orchestration.
Support microservices deployments and container-based applications.
Scripting & Automation
Develop automation scripts using Python, Bash, PowerShell and Node.js.
Automate repetitive operational tasks and infrastructure processes.
Integrate systems and services through APIs.
Monitoring & Performance Management
Implement monitoring and observability solutions using Grafana, Prometheus and Elastic Stack.
Monitor system performance, application availability and infrastructure health.
Identify and proactively resolve performance bottlenecks.
Security & Compliance
Implement DevSecOps practices within development and deployment pipelines.
Manage access controls, credentials and security configurations.
Identify vulnerabilities and ensure secure deployment practices.
Troubleshooting & Incident Management
Investigate and resolve complex infrastructure and deployment issues.
Perform root cause analysis.
Support production environments and minimise service disruptions.
Technical Leadership & Stakeholder Management
Collaborate with software developers, architects and infrastructure teams.
Provide technical guidance and mentor junior engineers.
Prepare technical documentation and operational reports.
Communicate technical challenges and solutions to management and stakeholders.

11. Management Presentations & Stakeholder Engagement

Prepare and deliver technical presentations to senior management and executive stakeholders.
Translate complex technical concepts into clear, business-friendly language.
Present DevOps strategies, project progress, implementation plans and technical recommendations.
Provide regular reporting on infrastructure performance, system availability, deployment progress and operational risks.
Communicate technical challenges, risks, dependencies and proposed solutions.
Present incident reports, root cause analysis and recommendations for improvement.
Engage confidently with senior stakeholders and participate in technical and strategic discussions.
