Join us as a Release Train Manager


Joining a fast paced and Agile environment, you'll be facilitating the planning, timing and execution of our delivery roadmaps and pipelines, within and across domain platforms
With no two days the same, you'll have the chance to be a true servant leader and help ensure consistent Agile delivery
In this exciting role, you'll enjoy regular engagements with key stakeholders as well as the opportunity to build your network across the bank
We're offering this role at vice president level


What you'll do

As Release Train Manager, you’ll be developing and maintaining the release train. You’ll be managing and optimising the flow of business value delivery of programmes using Agile tools and techniques.

It’s a highly collaborative role that will see you coordinating with product owners, domain and platform leads and other stakeholders to ensure strategy and delivery alignment. You’ll also encourage collaboration across teams, value streams, domains and centres of excellence, making sure they have a clear understanding of how their work helps the customer.

Your Other Responsibilities Will Include


Driving continuous improvement across delivery via various channels, improving or removing manual steps and enhancing the deployment and management phases of the development lifecycle
Building and communicating release calendars and iterative planning cycles, synchronising with other release train managers across domains
Assisting with the execution and delivery of business outcomes, features and capabilities by reporting, escalating and tracking impediments


The skills you'll need

To join us in this role, you'll be responsible for enabling effective delivery across the BAU, KSOR and Toolkit workstreams, ensuring a predictable release cadence and smooth execution of the Agile Release Train (ART). You'll also, work closely with Scrum Masters, Product and Technology teams to coordinate delivery, manage cross-team dependencies, remove impediments and maintain effective governance across the ART.

We'll Also Look For You To Demonstrate


Own and manage the release cadence across BAU, KSOR and Toolkit workstreams, ensuring delivery readiness and alignment across teams
Coordinate with Scrum Masters and delivery teams to manage cross-team dependencies, blockers and sequencing required for successful ART execution
Plan and facilitate PI Planning and key PI/ART ceremonies, including readiness activities, dependency management, confidence checks and follow-through on PI commitments
Maintain and manage ART-level RAID, ensuring risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies are visible, owned and escalated appropriately
Support delivery escalations, bringing the right stakeholders together to drive timely resolution and minimise impact to committed outcomes
Track PI delivery and provide clear visibility of progress, risks, dependencies and release health to Platform and Technology leadership
Alongside the RTM responsibilities, provide hands-on Scrum Master support for a Scrum team, facilitating sprint planning, daily stand-ups, reviews and retrospectives.
Promote strong Agile/SAFe ways of working, continuous improvement, collaboration and accountability across the teams
