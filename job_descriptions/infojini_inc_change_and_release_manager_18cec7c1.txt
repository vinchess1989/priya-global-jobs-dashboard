Position: Change & Release Coordinator/Manager

Focus: Release Engineering, Automation & Environment Management (Prod & Non-Prod)

Experience: 5–8 Years

Position Summary

We are seeking an experienced Change & Release Coordinator to manage, oversee, and

optimize end-to-end change and release deployment processes across both production

and non-production environments. You will bridge the gap between Development, DevOps,

QA, and IT Operations to drive reliable, repeatable releases using automated deployment

pipelines and strict governance framework protocols.




Key Responsibilities




Release Orchestration & Coordination

• Own the Release Management Lifecycle for scheduled software releases,

continuous integration builds, and hotfixes across multi-environment landscapes

(Dev, Test, Staging, Prod).

• Lead Release Readiness reviews, Go/No-Go checkpoints, Deployment Planning

meetings, and post-implementation reviews (PIRs).

• Coordinate deployment cutovers and dry runs across engineering, infrastructure,

database, and security teams.

Change Governance & Compliance

• Evaluate Change Requests (CRs) for risk, impact, environment dependencies, and

technical readiness prior to CAB (Change Advisory Board) reviews.

• Enforce compliance with organizational change policies, audit requirements, and

ITIL standards across all changes.

• Track change success/failure metrics, maintaining accurate release schedules and

release documentation.

Automation & Environment Management

• Collaborate with DevOps and Release Engineers to standardize automated

deployment scripts, CI/CD pipelines, and environment configuration controls.

• Drive transition strategies from manual deployment steps to fully automated

pipeline deployments.

• Manage environment access, code branch cutoffs, configuration drift monitoring,

and synchronization between non-production and production environments.

Incident & Risk Mitigation

• Establish standardized rollback protocols and contingency plans for high-risk

changes.

• Serve as the escalation point for deployment failures, environment contention

issues, or pipeline blockages during release windows.

Qualifications & Key Skills

• Experience: 5+ years of dedicated experience in IT Change, Release, or

Environment Management within cloud or hybrid enterprise landscapes.

• Release Engineering & Automation: Knowledge and experience in working with

team DevOps and automation work

• ITSM & Governance: Deep working knowledge of ITSM frameworks and tools

(ServiceNow, Jira, Confluence, BMC Helix).

• Environment Knowledge: Familiarity with managing code deployments across

microservices, containers (Docker, Kubernetes), databases, and legacy platforms.

• Methodologies: Strong understanding of Agile, DevSecOps, Continuous Delivery,

and ITIL methodologies (ITIL Foundation certification preferred).

• Communication: Excellent coordination, stakeholder management, and crisis

control skills during critical deployment cutover windows
