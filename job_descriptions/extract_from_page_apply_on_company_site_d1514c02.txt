Job Description of ﻿Release manager in ﻿Brisbane or Melbourne or Sydney:

﻿Key Responsibilities

Develop, maintain and manage overall release management criteria and plan.

Package up change as part of regular releases to ensure production stability and discipline in migrations.

Assess and approve the quality of migration readiness in conjunction with the project lifecycle process, data quality and testing signoff's.

Manage and co-ordinate vendors involved in the release where appropriate.

Ensure that operations staff handovers have been successfully completed prior to release.

Communicate across the team and with the business release management schedule.

Work with change manager to communicate proposed change to business process, training schedules, overall benefits of project deployment and recognition of success.

Promote the segregation in duty of migration activities.

Establish, configure and maintain non-production environments

Liaise with vendor for issue resolution and upgrade planning.

Manage source code, including version control.

Support releases and manage migrations between environments.

Develop release batch schedules.

Develop and manage medium to large implementation plans

Build and maintain broad Release Management calendar and integrate release management processes across portfolio.

Identify, prepare 'crunch', analyse and present data utilising available technologies

Build constructive working relationships and engagement with Team Members, Stakeholders, Customers, Partners, Vendors and all levels of Management
