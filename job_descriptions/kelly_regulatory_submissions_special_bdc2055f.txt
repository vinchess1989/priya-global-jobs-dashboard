Role overview




We’re seeking a Regulatory Submissions Specialist to support regulatory submissions and product registrations for medical devices, with a focus on the European Union Medical Device Regulation (EU MDR 2017/745). You’ll work with cross-functional teams to prepare, maintain and track regulatory documentation that supports product approvals and continued market access.




Key responsibilities




Prepare, compile and submit regulatory documentation for medical devices in accordance with EU MDR and applicable regional requirements.
Support submissions and interactions related to CE marking, including technical documentation and conformity assessment activities.
Coordinate the collection and review of submission materials with teams such as Quality, R&D, Clinical, Manufacturing and Product Management.
Maintain accurate regulatory records, submission trackers and product registration information.
Monitor regulatory guidance and changes that may affect product submissions or market access.
Support responses to questions or requests from Notified Bodies, Competent Authorities and other regulatory stakeholders.
Assist with change assessments to determine whether product, manufacturing or documentation changes may require regulatory updates or notifications.
Contribute to continuous improvement of regulatory processes, templates and submission practices.




Required qualifications and experience




Bachelor’s degree in a relevant discipline, such as life sciences, engineering or a related field, or equivalent experience.
3–5 years of regulatory affairs experience in the medical device industry.
Working knowledge of EU MDR 2017/745 and its application to medical device regulatory submissions.
Experience compiling or maintaining regulatory submissions and supporting documentation.
Strong attention to detail, organization and written communication skills.
Ability to manage multiple priorities and work effectively with cross-functional teams.




Preferred qualifications




Experience supporting CE marking and Notified Body interactions.
Familiarity with technical documentation, clinical evaluation, post-market surveillance and vigilance documentation.
Knowledge of other medical device regulations or markets.
Experience using electronic document management or regulatory information management systems.
