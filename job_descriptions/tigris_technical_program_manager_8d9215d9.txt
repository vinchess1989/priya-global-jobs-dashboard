Technical Program Manager

Location 

London (Chancery Lane), 4 days a week in the office 

Type 

Full-time, permanent 

 




About Tigris 

Logistics still runs on emails, Excel and phone calls. Every company in the logistics chain keeps its own version of the truth, and billions of dollars move on data nobody can fully trust. 

That's about to change! 

Tigris connects all those scattered systems into one live picture of vessels, cargo and schedules, so every company in the chain can work from data it can trust. On top of that, AI agents read the documents, watch the ships, predict what's coming and make the calls people still make by hand today. It's a mix of AI, data and real-world operations that almost nobody is building yet. 

We're starting in maritime, the hardest place to get it right, and building it for any company to run on. If you want to build something that doesn't exist yet, this is the place. 




Why we're hiring now 

After three years of building, Tigris is live with its first customer and has a growing pipeline of logistics businesses behind it. We're a small, hands-on team in the City of London, and we're now hiring the people who will turn a working platform into a scaling business. 

We build system software and algorithms at the same time, and more customers means more moving parts. We need someone to make sure the right work is clearly defined, properly sequenced and shipped. 




The role 

Leadership sets the direction of the system. Your job is to turn that direction into clear, buildable work and drive it through to delivery. 

As our Technical Program Manager, you'll be the organising layer between vision and engineering. You'll close ambiguity, manage dependencies and keep delivery moving across our system software and algorithm workstreams. It's a hands-on execution role: you'll spend most of your time with engineers, writing specifications, untangling dependencies and getting work over the line. 

You'll report to our CTO and work daily with our Head of Engineering, engineers, Product Manager and UI/UX designer, and give senior leadership a clear view of where delivery stands. The Product Manager owns what we build and why, and engineering owns how it's built; you own sequencing, triage, UAT and release. 




What you'll do 

Lead delivery across our system software and algorithm workstreams 
Write clear technical requirements and specifications that engineers can build from, agreed with our Head of Engineering 
Turn the Product Manager's priorities into a delivery plan that fits the team's capacity, and make sure work is well defined and ready before it's picked up 
Own sequencing, triage, UAT and release 
Identify and manage dependencies, risks and blockers across workstreams, and escalate early when something can't be resolved 
Work day to day with engineers to clarify scope, unblock issues and keep delivery on track 
Recommend release go / no-go decisions to our Head of Engineering 
Give our CTO and Head of Engineering clear visibility of progress, risks, capacity and timelines 
Create regular, metric-focused reports for senior leadership 
Work with our Product Manager and UI/UX designer so system capabilities reach customers in a form they can use 
Capture what customers need to build but can't yet, with our Product Manager, and feed it into planning 
Design lightweight processes that keep a small team aligned without slowing it down 




What we're looking for 

Experience as a Technical Program Manager, Release Manager or in a similar technical coordination role 
A track record of running complex, cross-functional technical programmes through to delivery 
Strong technical writing: you create clarity, not ambiguity 
Technical fluency: you don't need to write code, but you're comfortable discussing APIs, data pipelines and system architecture with engineers 
The ability to drive delivery without direct authority, with strong communication and negotiation skills across engineers, leadership and customers 
Organised and detail-oriented, with good instincts for what needs to be written down and what needs talking through 




Nice to have 

Experience at a system or infrastructure company (B2B, developer tools or similar) 
Background in logistics, supply chain or operations technology 
Exposure to data systems or algorithm-driven systems 
A technical background (engineering, data or similar) 




Why join us 

Real ownership. Every role here makes an impact. You'll shape how a team building system software and algorithms at once plans and ships its work. 
A hard, unsolved problem. We're not building a feature. We're building infrastructure that an entire industry can run on. 
Real momentum. We have live production systems, a live customer and carrier integrations covering more than 90% of major trade lanes. 
Built for the long term. Our backing comes from within the shipping industry, not external venture capital, so we're not on someone else's timeline. It also gives us direct access to industry expertise, introductions and data, and a natural path to customers. 
Close to leadership. A small, hands-on team with direct access to leadership: your work is visible, and so is your impact. 
AI-native from day one. We use the best AI tools every day, so a small team can move fast and ship in weeks, not quarters.
