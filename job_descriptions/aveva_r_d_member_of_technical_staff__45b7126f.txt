Job Title: R&D Member of Technical Staff - II, Technical Writing

Location: Bengaluru, India – Hybrid

Employment Type: Full-time regular (flexible working options available)

The job

As part of our global technical writing group, you’ll collaborate with one or more teams of skilled software engineers from our global research & development group in writing and publishing technical documentation and associated content for our products and solutions. You’ll help us research and transform complex technical concepts, specifications, and procedures into user-oriented information for non-technical and technical audiences for developing high-quality software documentation that meets user needs.

Key Responsibilities


Learn complex work from self-learning, training, and shadowing seniors
Work under minimal supervision on advanced, unstructured, and complex work
Improve the quality of content, adhere to the established standards and guidelines, and deliver on time
Design, develop, review, and publish high-quality technical documentation


Essential Requirements


Experience in working with products and solutions in industrial software
Experience in working with global agile and DevOps teams
Experience in working with Author-it Cloud and Microsoft Azure


Desired Skills And Competencies


Master’s or bachelor's degree in English, Computer Science, or Engineering
3-5 years of experience in technical writing and documentation development
Knowledge of software product development and cloud computing services
Competent at concepts, processes, and tools of technical writing and information development
Competent in using productivity tools from Microsoft and content management tools from Adobe
Competent at DITA, structured authoring, and component content management systems
Competent in using tools for screen capturing and recording and audio and video editing


R&D at AVEVA

Our global team of 2000+ developers work on an incredibly diverse portfolio of over 75 industrial automation and engineering products, which cover everything from data management to 3D design. AI and cloud are at the centre of our strategy, and we have over 150 patents to our name.
