What you'll do

We are expanding our Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practice within the enterprise security organization. Our security ecosystem encompasses diverse systems and environments—ranging from third-party enterprise SaaS solutions to internally developed full-stack platforms deployed across commercial and sovereign government clouds. As a Site Reliability Engineer – Security, you will focus on hands-on deployment, automation, monitoring, and operational maintenance of the infrastructure and tooling supporting our security systems. In this role, you will partner closely with senior engineers, security domain owners, and Cloud/NetOps teams to build resilient environments, automate routine operations, and uphold high availability standards across our security platforms.

This is an individual contributor role reporting to engineering leadership within Identity Security and Security Solutions Engineering.

Responsibility


Write, test, and maintain modular Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform or OpenTofu) and Configuration-as-Code to provision and maintain repeatable environments for security platforms across cloud environments - Infrastructure Automation
Maintain and enhance continuous integration and deployment pipelines (leveraging GitHub Actions and Azure DevOps), incorporating automated testing, linting, and security scanning gates - CI/CD & Release Engineering
Assist in deploying and integrating third-party security tools and SaaS platforms, ensuring secure network connectivity, identity integration (SSO/federation), and credential management - Systems Integration & Tooling Support
Implement and tune telemetry, dashboards, and alerting rules (e.g., Prometheus/Grafana, Datadog, Azure Monitor) to track service health, SLIs/SLOs, and performance bottlenecks - Observability & Health Monitoring
Participate in on-call rotations and operational troubleshooting for security infrastructure, executing runbooks, resolving incidents, and contributing to blameless postmortems - Incident Response & Operational Support
Support the execution of backup routines, redundancy configurations, and disaster recovery validation exercises to ensure platforms meet required RTO and RPO objectives - Disaster Recovery & Resilience
Author and maintain clear operational runbooks, environment diagrams, and onboarding documentation to promote operational rigor across the team - Runbooks & Documentation

What you bring

Basic


3–7 years of hands-on professional experience in Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), DevOps, Cloud Infrastructure, or Systems Engineering with a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field (or 2–5 years with a Master’s degree, or equivalent practical experience)
Practical experience administering and deploying infrastructure in public cloud environments (Azure preferred; AWS or GCP experience also welcome)
Solid working experience writing and modifying modular Infrastructure-as-Code using Terraform or OpenTofu, alongside configuration tools (such as Ansible, Helm, or cloud-init)
Hands-on experience creating or maintaining automated build/release pipelines using GitHub Actions or Azure DevOps
Direct experience configuring monitors, dashboards, and alert policies using tools such as Azure Monitor, Datadog, Prometheus, or Grafana
Proficiency with at least one scripting or programming language for automation and tooling (e.g., Python, Bash, PowerShell, or Go)


Preferred


Exposure to operating or deploying systems within compliance boundaries such as FedRAMP, DoD IL4/IL5, or SOC 2 Type II
Practical experience deploying and managing containerized applications on Kubernetes (AKS or EKS) and working with Docker
Familiarity with modern identity standards (SAML, OIDC, OAuth2, Microsoft Entra ID) and secrets management solutions (Azure Key Vault or HashiCorp Vault)
Understanding of core SRE principles such as error budgets, SLIs/SLOs, toil reduction, and automated recovery patterns
Foundational or associate-level certifications such as HashiCorp Certified: Terraform Associate, Microsoft Certified: Azure Administrator Associate, Azure DevOps Engineer Expert, or Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA)
