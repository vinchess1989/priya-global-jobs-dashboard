Purpose of Job

To oversee QA/QC activities in operational area.

To control legal documentation as required.

Key Accountabilities


Plan, develop, and implement QA / QC programmes for field staff.
Manage the accredited Ex. certification mark scheme for Sasol Group mining central workshops in accordance with health and safety regulations.
Implement, maintain, and lead continuous improvement of quality management system with focus finished goods.
Create quality inspection reports in the field.
Administer and maintain automation scripts and review product changes across different BUs.
Coordinate with product owners to provide efficient feedback.
Align equipment provided by manufacturers / suppliers and subcontractors with the principle of explosion protection.
Qualify new suppliers through an audit of their facilities and / or evaluation of their capabilities and business practices.
Set requirements for raw material or intermediate products for suppliers and monitor their compliance.
Perform internal and external audits of QC systems and repair procedures according to mark scheme requirements.
Evaluate and detect quality trends to identify significant problems, and obtain / recommend appropriate action to be taken.
Compile and amend legal flameproof certification reports in adherence to SANS IEC specifications.
Approve design specifications and technical drawings within area of responsibility. Oversee compliance with legal requirements about MHSA reg. 21.
Review and assess technical reports on data accuracy, relevancy, and completeness.
Monitor the effectiveness of the project quality management system. Implement improvements when required.
Identify and mitigate potential risks. Implement corrective action plan to avoid downtime.
Provide expertise and leadership to quality team.
Escalate quality issues to business leaders and senior management in a timely manner.
Apply evidence-based SHE practices in alignment with set standards for safe operations.
Implement Sasol Group's risk philosophy and enterprise risk management framework.
Build and maintain positive relationships with internal and external stakeholders to enhance the quality of products.
Continuously improve own skills and knowledge.
Complete, implement, and track personal development plan to attain personal performance goals.
Manage team performance and development formally and informally based on standard practices in use across Sasol Group.
Identify and activate learning as required based on individual and collective needs.
