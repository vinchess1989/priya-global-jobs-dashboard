Kia ora!




Air New Zealand is on an accelerated journey to become the world’s leading digital airline. We’re looking for a Senior Systems Analyst to help support and improve the systems behind operational risk and safety at Air New Zealand.




You’ll provide technical analysis and support across business applications, becoming a trusted subject matter expert in the systems and processes they support. You’ll also lead enhancements that improve how our services work for the business.




As a senior member of the team, you’ll share your knowledge, mentor other Systems Analysts and build strong relationships with stakeholders across the business.




How you’ll make an impact




Gather requirements for system and process enhancements, and explain technical concepts clearly to non-technical stakeholders
Advise on application configuration, access, upgrades, maintenance, compliance and change impacts, identifying improvements and guiding others
Triage incidents, service requests, errors, access issues and user queries, working to agreed service measures and applying service management practices
Build and improve support articles, reusable documentation, user guidance and training materials
Manage vendor relationships, identify service level agreement (SLA) gaps, review contract terms and contribute to RFI and RFP activities
Find practical ways to improve and automate services, using available and emerging technologies, including AI where appropriate
Share Systems Analysis practices, mentor colleagues and contribute to onboarding, Chapter initiatives and knowledge sharing
Connect technology strategy with the needs of our systems, services and wider business, while working with other specialists to support broader team outcomes




What you’ll bring




At least four years’ experience in systems analysis, business analysis, application support, product operations or a related technology role
Experience gathering requirements and documenting business and technical concepts for different audiences
Knowledge of application configuration and lifecycle activities, including access, upgrades, maintenance, compliance and change impact analysis
Understanding of service management practices, including incident, change, problem and service configuration management
Strong systems analysis, process mapping, problem-solving and requirements elicitation skills
The ability to connect business needs, technical constraints and operational service outcomes
Experience working with vendors and partners, creating support documentation, and providing training or guidance to others
An improvement mindset, with the ability to turn opportunities into practical changes or automation pathways




Why join Air New Zealand?




At Air New Zealand, you'll join an iconic Kiwi organisation where you can make a real difference.




We want our people to thrive at work and enjoy life beyond it, with benefits including:




Generous, discounted travel opportunities for you, your friends and whānau
FlexiLeave, giving you the option to purchase up to four additional weeks of leave each year
Learning and development opportunities to grow your leadership capability and career
Access to discounted medical insurance and a range of health and wellbeing support services
Discounts across hotels, activities, retail and other everyday essentials.




Sounds like you? Apply today!




If you bring the core experience and skills for this role, we’d love to hear from you.

You’ll join a highly engaged team that enjoys tackling complex challenges and learning from one another in an inclusive, supportive environment.
