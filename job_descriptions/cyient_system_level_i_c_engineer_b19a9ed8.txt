Position:- System Level I&C Engineer (DOORS 9-Classic / DOORS Next)

Experience:- 3-6 Years

Education: B.E./B.Tech in Instrumentation, Electrical, Electronics or Control Engineering

Job Requirement:-

· Capture functional and non-functional I&C system requirements from customer specifications, design basis documents, licensing documents and standards.

· Break high-level system requirements down into subsystem, hardware and software requirements, with clear, atomic and verifiable statements.

· Import, structure and maintain requirement modules in IBM DOORS 9 / DOORS Next, including attributes, views, baselines and change history.

· Allocate requirements to the right I&C systems, subsystems and components (safety and non-safety).

· Create and maintain trace links (parent–child, satisfies, verifies) across requirement, design and test modules in DOORS.

· Develop and maintain the Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) and run gap, orphan and impact analyses.

· Support Verification & Validation by defining verification methods (test, analysis, inspection, review) and linking requirements to V&V test cases and results.

· Prepare and maintain the Requirements Management (RM) and Configuration Management (CM) Plans, including baselining, change control and version management.

· Review requirements for clarity, consistency, completeness and testability, and take part in requirement reviews with the customer and cross-functional teams.

· Apply working knowledge of nuclear I&C standards and the regulatory framework, such as IEC 61513, IEC 60880, and IEC 62138.

· Understand I&C system architecture, safety classification, defense-in-depth, diversity and independence principles.

· Use DXL scripting or DOORS Next reporting and automation to improve efficiency (preferred).

· Work with system architects, hardware, software and V&V engineers, and the onshore customer team in a global delivery model.

· Follow strict QA, documentation and nuclear safety culture practices in all deliverables.

· Communicate well in writing and speaking, with strong analytical and technical writing skills.
