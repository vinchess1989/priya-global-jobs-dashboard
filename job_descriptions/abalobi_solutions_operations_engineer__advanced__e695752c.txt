Introduction

An exciting opportunity is available for an experienced Operations Engineer (Advanced) to join a dynamic global IT environment, supporting the development, maintenance and operational excellence of enterprise applications.

The successful candidate will form part of a DevOps team responsible for maintaining and enhancing central ordering applications, ensuring system stability, operational efficiency and quality service delivery.

This role is suited to a technically strong professional with solid IT infrastructure knowledge, advanced Agile toolchain capabilities and experience in IT service management and DevOps operations.

Duties & Responsibilities

Develop, implement and maintain reliable IT solutions independently.

Provide ongoing DevOps support and maintenance for existing applications.

Participate in Agile ceremonies and contribute to sprint planning and delivery.

Engage with business stakeholders and end users to understand requirements.

Contribute to user story refinement, estimation and sizing.

Identify, manage and resolve technical risks and impediments.

Review and present proposed technical solutions to Product Owners.

Ensure compliance with coding standards, unit testing and quality requirements.

Prepare and maintain technical and system documentation in line with ISO standards.

Provide knowledge sharing and technical guidance to team members.

Participate in standby support, including after-hours, weekends and public holidays, according to a rotation schedule.

Skills and Experience

Skills and Experience

Candidates must demonstrate practical experience in the following areas:

Agile Toolchain: Advanced hands-on experience with JIRA, Confluence, X-Ray and Bitbucket, including the ability to coach and guide others.

IT Infrastructure: Solid understanding of PostgreSQL, Payara and GlassFish.

Cloud Technologies: General understanding of cloud solutions, particularly AWS, serverless computing and cloud architecture implementation.

IT Security: Understanding of IT security principles and compliance requirements.

IT Service Management: In-depth knowledge of Problem, Incident and Change Management processes.

AI in Operations: Experience or knowledge of AI tools used to support operational excellence.

Advantageous Skills

Experience working with scaled Agile frameworks such as SAFe, LeSS or Nexus.

General Test Management knowledge.

Ability to interpret technical architecture designs, including conceptual, functional and execution views.

QlikView Operations KPI knowledge.

Understanding of business and technical processes.

Advanced project administration and business management skills.

Strong understanding of Agile principles throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

Configuration Management experience, including technical and business configuration management.

ITIL certification or IT Service Management certification.

Minimum Qualifications and Requirements

IT Degree, Diploma or equivalent relevant experience.

3–5 years' experience in a Developer or Engineer role.

Practical experience implementing and monitoring IT solutions.

Strong problem-solving, communication and stakeholder engagement skills.

Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively within a DevOps team.

Willingness and availability to participate in standby support rotations.
