About Zinkworks

Zinkworks partners with leading Telecommunications and Financial Services organizations to modernize legacy systems, migrate mission-critical platforms to the cloud, and engineer AI-driven automation. From OSS transformation to rApp development and network intelligence, our teams simplify complexity and turn it into competitive advantage. Based in Ireland and operating across the EU, UK, and US, Zinkworks combines deep domain expertise with delivery excellence to help clients modernize faster and operate smarter.

The Role

We are looking for a DevOps Engineer to work closely with development teams to build, operate, and continuously improve CI/CD pipelines and deployment workflows. This is a hands-on role focused on enabling fast, reliable delivery of applications running on OpenShift/Kubernetes, with strong ownership of CI/CD and release processes.

Key Responsibilities


Own and manage CI/CD pipelines end-to-end (design, implementation, maintenance)
Work directly with development teams to integrate build, test, and deployment processes
Package and deploy applications using Helm on OpenShift / Kubernetes
Build and maintain containerised applications using Docker
Support and optimise Spring Cloud-based microservices deployments
Troubleshoot build, deployment, and runtime issues
Improve deployment speed, reliability, and rollback strategies
Implement and maintain logging, monitoring, and alerting
Drive best practices around CI/CD, versioning, and release management
Maintain clear technical documentation


Required Skills & Experience


Strong experience building and owning CI/CD pipelines
Strong hands-on experience with OpenShift / Kubernetes
Strong experience with Helm (chart development, templating, lifecycle management)
Solid experience with Docker and containerisation
Good understanding of Spring Cloud ecosystem
Strong Linux administration and troubleshooting skills
Experience debugging issues across pipelines, containers, and cluster environments
Scripting skills (Bash, Python, or similar)
Experience with monitoring tools (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK)


Nice to Have


Familiarity with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Ansible)
Familiarity with Java and Spring Cloud (useful for working closely with development teams)
