Major Responsibilities


Creates governing procedures for gathering, capturing and writing requirements
Creates governing procedures for decomposing system level requirements to subsystem/component level requirements
Drives verification and validation activities for a product/system line, including component, subsystem and full system testing
Creat0se governing procedures for verification and validation activities
Coordinates across engineering functions in defining system strategy and architecture
Provides subject matter expertise during technical reviews
Provides subject matter expertise and inputs into the formal design reviews across a system architecture
Approves design reviews and designates tasks to other systems engineers as required
Creates governing procedures for creation and maintenance of block and boundary diagrams and Interface Control Documents
Creates governing procedures for system level documentation
Identifies and analyzes risks and opportunities from a system architecture perspective, using this perspective to update governing procedures to address these possible risks
Informs other systems engineers of process and procedure updates
Acts as a resource to mentor and teach less experienced engineers
Supports product development across the system architecture in delivery of on-schedule, on-quality, compliant solutions.
