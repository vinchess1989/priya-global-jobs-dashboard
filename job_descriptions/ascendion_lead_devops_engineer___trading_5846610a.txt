Role:

We are seeking a Lead DevOps Engineer to support infrastructure automation, cloud platform engineering and operational resilience for mission-critical Front Office Trading systems.
The role focuses on enabling reliable software delivery, improving infrastructure scalability and strengthening the availability and performance of financial trading platforms.




Responsibilities:

Design and enhance cloud infrastructure and DevOps automation.
Build, maintain and optimise CI/CD pipelines.
Develop infrastructure-as-code solutions using Terraform.
Manage container-based environments using Docker and Kubernetes.
Support cloud infrastructure across AWS and/or MS Azure.
Improve system reliability, high availability, monitoring and disaster recovery.
Implement observability solutions across logging, metrics, tracing and alerting.
Support incident management, root-cause analysis and production engineering.
Strengthen infrastructure security, IAM and secrets management practices.
Contribute to cloud cost optimisation and FinOps initiatives.
Collaborate with global engineering, architecture and SRE teams.




Required Skills:

10+ years of relevant DevOps, cloud or platform engineering experience.
Strong hands-on experience with AWS and/or MS Azure.
Expertise in Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform and infrastructure-as-code.
Strong experience designing and maintaining CI/CD pipelines.
Knowledge of cloud networking, IAM, security and secrets management.
Experience with observability solutions such as Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, Datadog, Splunk or equivalents.
Strong understanding of high availability, disaster recovery, incident management and root-cause analysis.
Experience with production reliability and cloud cost optimisation.
Experience in managing, deploying or supporting infrastructure and DevOps environments for front office trading platforms.
Strong collaboration skills across engineering, architecture and SRE teams.
