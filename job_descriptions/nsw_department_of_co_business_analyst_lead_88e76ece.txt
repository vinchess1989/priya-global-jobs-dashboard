Your role

Lead complex business analysis for the Maintenance Application, related Salesforce applications and supporting integrations across the product and delivery lifecycle. Working with the Product Manager and Business Product Owner, the role shapes pipeline initiatives and business cases, translates strategic, customer, policy and operational needs into evidence-based outcomes, options, processes, requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, and collaborates with business, delivery and vendor technical teams to achieve fit-for-purpose solutions. The role provides authoritative business analysis advice and assurance, maintains requirements quality and traceability, and strengthens business analysis practices across the product team.

What You’ll Do


Lead end-to-end business analysis across complex digital, data and service improvement initiatives.
Set fit-for-purpose analysis approaches, stakeholder engagement, governance and quality controls.
Investigate current-state processes and business problems using operational data and customer evidence.
Elicit, prioritise and manage functional and non-functional requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria.
Design and assure future-state processes and service models with business and technology specialists.
Facilitate difficult decisions, resolve competing requirements and provide credible options and recommendations.
Assure requirements traceability, solution alignment and business readiness throughout delivery.
Lead business analysis methods, standards, peer review, coaching and practice improvement.


What We’re Looking For


Expertise in business situation analysis, process improvement, requirements lifecycle management and traceability.
Ability to resolve ambiguous problems and conflicting stakeholder priorities through evidence-based advice.
Experience in collaborating with architects, Salesforce or integration vendors, testers and operational teams.
Demonstrated ability to uplift analysis quality and guide analysts and delivery partners.


Essential Requirements


Tertiary qualifications in business analysis, information technology, business or a related discipline, or equivalent relevant knowledge, skills and experience, with a demonstrated commitment to ongoing professional development.


Desirable Requirements


Experience with Salesforce, enterprise platforms and systems integration.
Experience working across agile, waterfall and hybrid delivery environments.
Business analysis certification or professional association membership.
