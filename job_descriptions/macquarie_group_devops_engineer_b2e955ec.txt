Join our Group Technology and Security team responsible for building and operating secure, scalable, and reliable platforms across the organisation. As part of our team, you will be involved in automating infrastructure and delivery workflows, improving system reliability, and enabling engineering teams to ship and run services efficiently in production.

We are a global financial services group operating in 30 markets with 57 years of unbroken profitability. At Macquarie, you’re empowered to shape a career that is fulfilling and creates value. You will bring your insights and expertise to the task at hand and feel supported as you make your own kind of impact for a better future.

What role will you play?

In this role, you will design, provision, and manage cloud and on-premise infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code, while building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines and automation to enable reliable, secure, and efficient software delivery. You will develop and support containerised applications and orchestration platforms, leveraging monitoring, observability, and troubleshooting practices to optimise system performance, scalability, and availability. Additional you will collaborate across engineering, security, and infrastructure teams to drive operational excellence and continuous platform improvement through maintenance, upgrades, governance, and post-incident follow-up.

What You Offer


Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience), with 3-5 years’ experience in DevOps, platform engineering, site reliability engineering, or similar production support roles.
Strong hands-on experience with AWS, Azure, or GCP, including core cloud services, networking, storage, and identity/access management.
Proven experience with Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD, and automation technologies such as Terraform, CloudFormation, Ansible, Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Python, and Bash.
Practical expertise in containerisation and orchestration using Docker and Kubernetes, supported by solid Linux and/or Windows administration skills.
Strong understanding of monitoring, observability, security, and release management practices, with excellent problem-solving, communication, and stakeholder engagement skills.
