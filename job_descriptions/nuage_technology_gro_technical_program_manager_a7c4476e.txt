Senior Technical Program Manager | Sydney | Full time | On Site




We're recruiting a customer centric Senior Technical Program Manager to lead the delivery of complex, customer facing technical programs for our client. This hire will be the bridge between engineering, operations and customer facing teams (Sales, Customer Success, Solutions Architecture), making sure initiatives land with minimal customer disruption and maximum business value.




What you’ll do

Own customer communications, including executive updates and status meetings
Build phased rollout plans with clear scope, timelines and milestones
Run weekly syncs, internally and with customers, on readiness, milestones and impact
Orchestrate delivery across engineering, operations and customer facing teams
Hold enough technical depth to answer customer questions and validate impact assessments
Make technical decisions that balance internal speed with customer impact




What you’ll bring

10+ years in technical program or project management, including 4+ years leading large scale technical programs
Strong delivery of software platform solutions, ideally cloud or platform services
Direct external customer exposure
Technical fluency across software platforms, cloud architecture, networks and supporting infrastructure
Practical exposure to infrastructure delivery and how readiness milestones affect service delivery
Experience blending agile software delivery with waterfall infrastructure planning
Hands on Jira experience (backlogs, sprint planning, dependencies, release coordination)
Strong stakeholder management across Sales, Customer Success, Product, Operations and Engineering
Comfort in high ambiguity environments where structure needs to be created
Excellent written communication, including clear and empathetic customer advisories
