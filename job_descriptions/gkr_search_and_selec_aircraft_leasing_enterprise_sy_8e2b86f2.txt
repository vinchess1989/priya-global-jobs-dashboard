We are supporting an established aircraft leasing company in the search for an Enterprise Systems Analyst to join its Dublin office on a permanent basis. This is a fully on-site position, based in Dublin.




This opportunity will suit an IT professional with experience in business applications, systems analysis or enterprise software support who enjoys solving problems, working with people and improving how technology supports business operations.




The role offers broad exposure to enterprise applications, systems enhancements, integrations and technology projects. Working closely with an international IT team, software vendors and colleagues across the business, the successful candidate will act as a key link between business users and technical teams, helping to investigate issues, identify practical solutions and improve existing systems and processes.




With a number of technology initiatives underway, this is an excellent opportunity for someone looking to develop their experience in enterprise systems, project coordination and business process improvement within the aircraft leasing sector.




Responsibilities

Work closely with business users to understand operational requirements, investigate system issues and identify practical solutions.
Analyse issues across enterprise applications, including system functionality, workflows, configuration, data and integrations.
Act as a key point of contact between business users, internal IT teams, software vendors and external service providers.
Take ownership of systems-related issues, coordinating their investigation and resolution through to completion.
Coordinate system testing, user acceptance testing (UAT), software releases and implementation activities.
Identify opportunities to improve existing workflows, make better use of system functionality and reduce manual processes.
Liaise with software vendors and external providers to manage support requests, enhancements and ongoing systems issues.
Develop and maintain systems documentation, user guidance and operational procedures.




Requirements

Minimum 3 years' experience in systems analysis, business application support, enterprise software support or a related IT role.
Experience with requirements gathering, process mapping, system testing or UAT would be advantageous.
Exposure to enterprise applications such as Workday, Microsoft 365 or Microsoft Power Platform would be beneficial.
Familiarity with systems integrations, data flows, reporting or application configuration would be an advantage.
