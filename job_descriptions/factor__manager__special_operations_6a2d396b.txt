S'more About The Team

We are seeking a highly skilled and versatile Manager (Ops Tech), Special Operations, to join Factor (factormeals.ca), a healthy, ready-to-heat meal delivery service under HelloFresh. This role owns the operations technology roadmap for Factor Canada, bridging the gap between operations, data engineering, and global platform teams. You will lead the design, integration, and deployment of systems that power our production, warehousing, procurement, and quality functions across multiple sites. Reporting to the General Manager, you will operate as the connective tissue between floor-level operations and the technical teams that build and maintain our systems.

Lettuce Share What This Role Will Be Responsible For

Platform Migrations & Go-Live Management


Lead end-to-end platform migrations (e.g. legacy systems to modern platforms), including dual-maintenance strategies, deprecation timelines, cutover risk assessments, and go-live communications.
Manage phased rollouts across functional areas of production, coordinating with global development teams for requirements reviews and sprint prioritization.


Systems Architecture & Data Source Design


Own the data architecture strategy for operational tools - mapping data flows across SKU master data management, recipe management, Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), and production tracking systems. Evaluate integration approaches and design future-state architectures that don't disrupt downstream tools during transitions.
Compile and maintain data source requirements documentation, including source-to-target mapping and ingestion cadences.


Operational Tool Development & Ownership


Own the lifecycle of production floor tools - from requirements gathering and process flow mapping through development coordination, training material creation, and deployment.


Cross-Functional Stakeholder & Vendor Coordination


Serve as the primary liaison between local operations and global platform teams - translating operational needs into technical requirements and managing prioritization across competing roadmaps.
Lead data inconsistency investigations - root-causing discrepancies between reporting systems, reviewing automation logic, and coordinating fixes with data engineering.


Multi-Site Operations & New Site Launches


Ensure systems parity across production facilities - replicating all systems, tools, and documentation, and validating readiness before go-live.


Roadmap Management


Define and maintain the ops tech roadmap aligned to business Objectives and Key Results (OKRs), sequencing projects based on dependencies and critical path. Run regular steering calls to communicate status and manage risk.
Other duties assigned


Sound a-peeling? Here's what we're looking for:


Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, supply chain, computer science, or a similar field
3–5 years of experience in operations technology, systems integration, tech consulting, or manufacturing/supply chain operations
Demonstrated experience writing functional/technical specifications and leading them through development teams
Track record of leading platform migrations or system go-lives in production environments with low tolerance for downtime
Strong data literacy - comfortable mapping data flows across systems, reviewing data source structures, and identifying integration patterns (you don't need to write code, but you need to understand schemas and pipelines)
Excellent stakeholder management - able to translate between floor supervisors and software engineers, and escalate effectively when blocked
Entrepreneurial with an ownership mindset: you thrive in small teams where ambiguity is the norm
Strong written communication - your project updates, go-live comms, and stakeholder messages are clear, structured, and drive action
Quantitative and analytical: expertise with spreadsheet tools, data visualization, and comfort reviewing data pipelines and dashboards


Nice to have


Experience with Warehouse Management Systems including rollout and floor training
Familiarity with cloud data warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery) and orchestration tools (Airflow)
Experience with food manufacturing traceability systems (CCP, FIFO, lot tracking)
Background in food manufacturing, meal kit, or CPG operations
Experience with agile sprint planning and working with distributed development teams
