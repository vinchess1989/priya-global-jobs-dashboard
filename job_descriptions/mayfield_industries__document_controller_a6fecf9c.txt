About the Role

Mayfield is seeking an organized and detail-oriented Document Controller to provide hands-on document control and project administration support across our engineering and manufacturing projects. Working closely with Project Managers, Engineers and site teams, you will manage project documentation, drawings, certifications and records, ensuring accuracy, version control and timely distribution throughout the project lifecycle.

Duties

Manage, register, track and distribute project documentation, drawings and transmittals using document control systems.
Maintain accurate document registers, electronic filing systems and records, ensuring version control and traceability.
Process redlines markups and update As-Built drawings and documentation.
Monitor and follow up document reviews, approvals and outstanding submissions.
Liaise with clients, suppliers, subcontractors and internal stakeholders regarding document requirements and submissions.
Assist in compiling Manufacturing Data Records (MDRs), Inspection and Test Plans (ITPs), manuals, production packs and project close-out documentation.
Review, scan and maintain quality records, certifications and inspection and testing documentation.
Monitor supplier documentation schedules and follow up overdue deliverables.
Ensure documentation meets internal quality standards and client specifications.
Provide project administration support, including reporting, data entry, mobilisation records and general coordination.
Contribute to continuous improvement of document control processes and systems.

About You

Proficiency in Microsoft Office, including Outlook, Word and Excel, along with PDF editing and creation tools.
Experience using document management systems such as QDMS, Aconex, InEight, Team Binder, SharePoint or BIM 360.
Strong organizational skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities and meet deadlines.
Excellent attention to detail and a commitment to maintaining accurate and traceable documentation.
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
A Certificate or Diploma in Business Administration, Project/Contracts Management or relevant Document Control training (highly regarded).
