Technical Writers are currently required for a six-month contract, with an option to extend for a Services Organisation working with a client in the Transportation space. This role is pivotal in producing clear, concise, and high-quality technical content that meets strict regulatory requirements, industry standards, and internal best practices.

You will be responsible for distilling complex technical information into easily understandable documentation for various stakeholders and partners. Working closely with cross-functional teams-including subject matter experts, developers, and project managers-you will ensure the accuracy, consistency, and completeness of all technical collateral. A key focus for this role is the practical application of AI tools to enhance documentation efficiency, editing, and updates, ensuring all work remains compliant with State and Federal privacy regulations. Key Responsibilities
Content Creation: Research, write, edit, and proofread technical documentation, including user manuals, guides, specifications, and release notes.
AI Integration: Leverage AI as a tool to support documentation updates, editing, and process improvements within regulatory frameworks.
Documentation Planning & Maintenance: Develop comprehensive plans and schedules to meet project milestones. Proactively update existing documentation to reflect changes in software, processes, or user requirements.
Quality Assurance: Conduct thorough audits of documentation to ensure adherence to style guides, compliance, and industry standards.
Distribution: Coordinate the accessibility of documentation via online portals and knowledge bases.
To be successful in this position you must be a highly experienced Technical Writer with proven experience crafting user-friendly content and translating complex information into structured, engaging documentation for IT/Engineering projects.
You must also have solid experience in the following:
AI Proficiency: Demonstrated working knowledge of AI tools for documentation support, including a clear understanding of data privacy and security legislation.
Problem-Solving: A proactive mindset, capable of taking new perspectives on existing solutions and exercising judgment based on analysis.
Standards Awareness: Familiarity with ISO Management Systems (Quality, OH&S, Environmental & Information Security) is highly desirable.
Adaptability: Ability to manage changing priorities effectively within a fast-paced project environment.
