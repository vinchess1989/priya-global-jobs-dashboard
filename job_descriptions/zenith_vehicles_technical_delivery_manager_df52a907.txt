At Zenith, we've been leading the transition to sustainable vehicle solutions for over 30 years. As the UK's largest independent vehicle solutions provider, we're proud of our people, our award-winning service, and our bold ambitions to make mobility smarter, greener, and better for everyone. We're powered by people, driven by innovation, and committed to creating an inclusive environment where everyone can thrive.




We're looking for a Technical Delivery Manager to join our Core Platforms team. In this role, you'll be responsible for the effective delivery of Zenith's Group products and services, managing the change process from demand management and technical requirements gathering through to successful implementation to time, cost, and quality. You'll build and manage detailed plans that define how items in the product backlog will be defined, built, tested, and deployed, while maintaining progress reports and managing risks, issues, and resources.




At Zenith, ourcustomers benefit from the latest technology investment, including next-generation digitised fleet management services delivered via modernised portals and intelligent dashboards. You'll collaborate closely with senior leadership, cross-functional teams, project managers, and the Software Engineering Manager to ensure smooth delivery of programmes, small changes, and software patching.




What's the Role

Manage the relationship between business deliverables, cross-functional internal delivery, and technical delivery plans
Facilitate Sprint Planning, Daily Stand-up, Sprint Review, and Retrospective sessions
Monitor progress and performance, helping teams to make improvements
Ensure the Software Engineering team are motivated, collaborating, and working well
Identify obstacles and help the team overcome them
Actively address internal and external risks, issues, and dependencies
Work collaboratively with contracted suppliers to ensure smooth and timely delivery of services




What Can You Offer

A strategic thinker with a proven history of delivering impactful tech projects, ideally in agile or consultancy settings
Strong knowledge of using ADO Boards for managing delivery
Knowledge of DevOps tools and methodologies
Ability to explain complex issues clearly to technical and non-technical audiences
Experience applying Agile principles in practice and selecting the best delivery methods
Excellent verbal and written communication skills with strong stakeholder management capability
Demonstrable leadership skills with the ability to motivate and manage delivery teams




What's in it for You

Salary between £55,000 to £65,000 per annum + car allowance and annual bonus
24 days annual leave plus your birthday off, with the option to buy more
Private Medical Insurance, Life Insurance, Critical Illness Cover, Dental and Health Cash Plans
Tech Scheme, Car Sacrifice Scheme, Cycle to Work Scheme, Season Ticket Loans
Development opportunities through our MyGPS personal development framework
Perks at Work - discounts on everyday essentials
Onsite GP, Employee Assistance Programme, Mental Health First Aiders
Sports and social clubs, company activities, and team events
Inclusive culture with colleague-led equality networks
Free onsite parking
Agile working - you might have the opportunity to work remotely when it works for the Customer, Zenith and yourself, and you'll work with your manager to agree how this works best




Why Zenith?

Award-winning - Leasing Company of the Year (Fleet News 2023), Large Business of the Year (Yorkshire Post Excellence in Business Awards)
Sustainability champions - Over 41% of our fleet are Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs), and we're a carbon-neutral organisation
Innovators - Investing £25 million in transformation, with a thriving fleet of 169,910 vehicles
Inclusive - Six colleague-led diversity groups and a workplace where everyone can thrive
A place to grow - Clear pathways through our MyGPS personal development framework
