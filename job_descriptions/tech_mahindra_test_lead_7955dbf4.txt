Role Overview

Tech Mahindra is seeking a skilled Test Lead to manage end-to-end quality assurance activities across complex telecommunications and digital transformation programs. The successful candidate will define testing strategies, govern quality processes, lead automation initiatives, and provide objective release readiness assessments within Agile and DevOps environments.

The role requires strong telecom domain knowledge and experience working with OSS/BSS applications, APIs, enterprise integrations, and customer-facing platforms.




Key Responsibilities

Test Strategy & Governance

Define and manage project test strategies and test plans.
Establish test governance frameworks, quality gates, and release criteria.
Ensure alignment with Tech Mahindra quality standards and customer requirements.
Drive risk-based testing approaches across projects.

Testing Leadership

Lead end-to-end testing activities including:
Functional Testing
Integration Testing
API Testing
Regression Testing
User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
Performance Testing Coordination
Security Testing Coordination
Manage testing teams, schedules, environments, and execution plans.

Automation & DevOps

Define and implement automation strategies.
Integrate automated testing within CI/CD pipelines.
Promote Shift-Left Testing practices.
Support Agile, TDD, and BDD delivery methodologies.
Collaborate with development and DevOps teams to improve quality throughout the software lifecycle.

Quality Management

Govern defect management processes.
Ensure complete requirements-to-test traceability.
Monitor test execution metrics and quality KPIs.
Provide management with quality status reports and release recommendations.

Telecommunications Testing

Validate OSS/BSS workflows and integrations.
Test order-to-activate journeys.
Verify service provisioning and service assurance processes.
Support testing of billing, CRM, activation, and enterprise service platforms.




Required Experience

Minimum 5 years of experience in Software Testing and Quality Engineering.
Minimum 2 years leading testing activities or teams.
Experience within telecommunications or large enterprise environments.
Proven experience managing end-to-end testing across multiple systems and integrations.
