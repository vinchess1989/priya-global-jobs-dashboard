We are seeking a curious and collaborative Quality Engineer who is motivated by emerging technologies and creative problem-solving. You will contribute your ideas, perspective, and technical expertise to new features and digital solutions while helping a supportive team take shared ownership of quality outcomes.

Position Responsibilities


Develop and implement quality engineering code that improves the quality, reliability, and maintainability of software products.
Establish processes and tools to maintain automation scripts and generate timely bug-tracking and quality reports.
Analyze automated test results, investigate failures, and collaborate with partners to resolve root causes.
Design test patterns to assess performance, scalability, reliability, and overall solution quality.
Conduct code and deliverable reviews, identify quality risks, and recommend practical mitigation strategies.
Promote quality-focused practices throughout the software development lifecycle.
Document and implement leading practices that support continuous quality assurance improvement.
Prepare and present formal quality audit reports.
Define and track product-quality KPIs, including defect density, defect frequency, and first-time pass rate.
Partner with developers, business analysts, product managers, and other cross-functional team members to identify and resolve quality issues.
Maintain and communicate new or revised quality standards and technical changes.
Stay current with front-end and back-end technologies and apply continuous learning to diverse application challenges.
Participate in peer reviews and knowledge sharing to support continuous learning and improvement.


Required Qualifications


3–5 years of experience as a Quality Engineer.
Experience in the investment industry or Fundserv or applicable regulatory requirements and regulatory bodies.
Working knowledge of black-box and white-box testing practices and open-source testing frameworks.
Experience building test automation and validating end-to-end business use cases.
Experience developing and executing both manual and automated tests.
Ability to apply appropriate quality tools and testing techniques to data and machine learning applications.
Experience implementing or improving quality management system processes using software development lifecycle leading practices.
Proficiency in developing testing tools and automation scripts.
Knowledge of cloud fundamentals and experience supporting or testing cloud-based applications.


Preferred Qualifications


Experience with test automation frameworks and automated testing practices.
Experience with data quality, ETL, machine learning model, and API testing.
Knowledge of continuous integration and continuous delivery methods.
Familiarity with DevSecOps practices.
Experience working in Agile delivery environments.
