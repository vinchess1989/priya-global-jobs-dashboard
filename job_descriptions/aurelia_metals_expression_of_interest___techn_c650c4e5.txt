12-month fixed-term opportunity | Brisbane + site travel

This isn’t a role that comes up often at Aurelia.

We’re looking for an experienced Technical Writer to join us for 12 months and take on a significant documentation improvement program across our operations.

With 400+ operational and statutory documents identified for review, you’ll have the opportunity to get right into the business, work alongside our mining, maintenance, processing and technical teams, and turn complex operational knowledge into documentation that our people can actually use.

This isn’t about sitting behind a desk formatting documents. You’ll spend time with the people who know the work, visit our operations when needed, understand how things are done and help us improve the quality, consistency and practicality of our documentation.

What You’ll Get Stuck Into


Developing and revising statutory documentation, including Principal Hazard Management Plans and Principal Control Plans
Supporting documentation aligned with NSW mining legislation and regulatory requirements
Reviewing and improving operational standards, procedures and work instructions
Sitting down with SMEs and extracting the technical knowledge that needs to be captured
Identifying gaps, duplication and opportunities to simplify how we document work
Using Microsoft 365 and Copilot to improve quality, consistency and efficiency
Travelling to our operations when needed to see the work firsthand and engage directly with our teams


We’d love to hear from you if you have


Technical writing experience within mining, heavy industry or another highly regulated environment
Experience developing operational procedures, standards and work instructions
A knack for taking complex technical information and making it clear, practical and easy to follow
Confidence working directly with operational and technical SMEs
Strong attention to detail and the ability to manage a substantial body of work independently
Knowledge of NSW mining legislation and regulatory requirements would be highly regarded


If you’re a Technical Writer who likes getting close to operations and wants to spend 12 months delivering a body of work you can genuinely point to, we’d love to hear from you!
