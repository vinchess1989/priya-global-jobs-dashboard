Overview

We are seeking a highly motivated NPI Specialist to support the successful introduction of new In Vitro Diagnostic (IVD) products from product development into manufacturing. The successful candidate will work closely with Research & Development, Operations, Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Supply Chain, and Product Management teams to ensure products are transferred efficiently, compliantly, and on schedule.

This is an exciting opportunity to play a key role in bringing innovative healthcare products to market while helping to improve manufacturing processes, product quality, scalability, and cost effectiveness.

Responsibilities

New Product Introduction & Design Transfer


Support and coordinate design transfer activities for new and updated IVD products in accordance with ISO 13485, 21 CFR Part 820, and company quality system requirements.
Work with cross-functional product development teams to ensure Design for Manufacturing (DfM) principles are incorporated from early development through commercialization.
Develop and maintain design transfer documentation, plans, and deliverables.
Support manufacturing readiness activities, including process mapping, validation planning, and risk assessments.



Manufacturing Introduction


Coordinate the transfer of new products, prototypes, and product improvements into manufacturing.
Partner with Operations and Supply Chain teams to identify risks and opportunities associated with product launch and scale-up.
Support pilot builds, process validation activities, and production implementation.
Ensure projects are delivered on time, within scope, and aligned to business objectives.



Product Lifecycle Management


Support change management activities for products already on the market.
Participate in customer complaint investigations, non-conformance investigations, and continuous improvement initiatives.
Work with manufacturing and quality teams to implement product and process improvements.



Qualifications

Education: Degree in science related field.

Experience:

Proven experience of working in an operations environment

Ability to operate under a Quality System and knowledge of Good Laboratory Practice (GLP).

Experience working in an R&D environment (pharmaceutical or medical device) would be advantageous.

Microsoft excel training or substantial experience utilizing excel is required.

Understanding of the requirements of quality management systems & applicable regulations- ISO13485 & 21CFR820

Knowledge of Non-Conformance, Change Control and CAPA systems

Competencies:

SAP training or experience

Travel (If Applicable):
