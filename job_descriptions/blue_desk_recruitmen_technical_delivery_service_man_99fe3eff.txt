Introduction

We assist companies in South Africa and the UK, primarily within the research and investment process. We provide a powerful toolkit that allows our clients to build customized solutions and models based on their unique data requirements. We rely on a dynamic team of skilled Data Engineers to assist clients from both a support and project perspective.

 

Candidate profile:

Essential: Experience in technical service delivery, IT operations, software delivery or data engineering environments. (DATA AND ANALYTICS)

Essential: Direct people-management experience, ideally leading developers, data engineers or other technical specialists.

Essential: Enterprise client management, SLAs, major incident escalation and release/change management.

Preferred: Financial services experience, especially investment management, banking, trading systems or financial market data.

 

Applicants must have demonstrable experience in technology or IT service delivery and technical team leadership.

 

Duties & Responsibilities

This is a critical leadership role responsible for the operational success and entrenchment of two strategic enterprise accounts. The Technical Account Manager will lead a dedicated team of Data Engineers, ensuring the successful delivery of complex market data warehousing and transaction processing pipelines. This role bridges the gap between high-stakes client management, strict SLA adherence, and technical team leadership. Key Responsibilities Client & Service Management • Own the end-to-end operational relationship with enterprise clients, serving as the primary point of contact for technical delivery, SLA adherence, and account entrenchment. • Conduct monthly service reviews, presenting performance metrics, incident reports, and capacity planning to senior client stakeholders. • Manage the end-to-end incident lifecycle, acting as the ultimate escalation point for severity issues and ensuring timely communication of workarounds and root-cause analyses. • Liaise directly with third-party data vendors to resolve blockers and maintain SLA compliance for market data availability. • Identify opportunities for account growth and software entrenchment, working with client Business Analysts and Architects to propose new use cases for our data solutions. Team Leadership & Operations • Directly manage a team of Data Engineers, fostering a supportive, growth-oriented culture while ensuring optimal resource allocation. • Oversee release management processes, coordinating with your team and the client to schedule and execute upgrades, bug fixes, and environment testing (Dev, QA, Pre-Prod, Prod). • Protect the engineering team from scope creep and manage stakeholder expectations regarding turnaround times for change requests and custom development. • Ensure institutional knowledge is documented, preserved, and distributed across the team to prevent single points of failure. Technical Translation • Maintain a solid understanding of the client’s data processing pipelines, market data warehousing, and reporting requirements to facilitate high-level technical discussions. • Know when to bring in Business Analyst/Architect colleagues into the discussions. companies in South Africa and the UK, primarily within the research and investment process. We provide a powerful toolkit that allows our clients to build customized solutions and models based on their unique data requirements. We rely on a dynamic team of skilled Data Engineers to assist clients from both a support and project perspective Exceptional stakeholder management skills, with a track record of handling high-pressure situations, navigating difficult client conversations, and managing tight SLA deadlines. • Strong technical fluency in data pipelines, database architecture, and market data workflows. (Note: You will not be writing code, but you must be able to understand the high level implications in architectural discussions with developers and enterprise architects). • Experience managing enterprise change control processes and release management schedules. Working Conditions • Given the nature of the financial data SLA, this role requires occasional flexibility to oversee critical weekend deployments and act as the escalation point for severe after-hours incidents. Reporting • The candidate will report to the Managing Director.

Desired Experience & Qualification

Relevant tertiary and at least 5 years in a technical account manager role
