Cloud / DevOps Engineer

Brisbane, QLD
Permanent
$120k-$140k/year + super
We're working with a growing technology and managed services business in Brisbane that's looking to bring on a Cloud / DevOps Engineer to join its team.
  
This is a hands-on role sitting across cloud infrastructure, DevOps and automation, with a strong focus on AWS, Terraform and CI/CD.
  
You'll be working across multiple customer environments, helping to manage and improve cloud infrastructure, automate deployments and support the ongoing delivery of managed services.
  
We're looking for someone with a solid infrastructure background who has developed their skills across cloud and DevOps, and understands what it takes to support customer environments within an SLA-driven environment.

What you'll be doing

Managing and supporting AWS cloud infrastructure across multiple customer environments.
Building, maintaining and improving infrastructure using Terraform and Infrastructure as Code.
Developing and maintaining CI/CD pipelines to automate infrastructure deployments.
Identifying opportunities to automate processes and improve operational efficiency.
Troubleshooting cloud infrastructure issues and providing technical support and escalations.
Working within agreed SLAs to ensure customer environments remain reliable and well maintained.
Contributing to cloud infrastructure projects, improvements and ongoing optimisation.
Working closely with internal teams and customers to deliver effective technical solutions.
What we're looking for
Previous experience working within an MSP or managed services environment.
Strong hands-on experience with AWS.
Solid experience with Terraform and Infrastructure as Code.
Experience building or maintaining CI/CD pipelines.
A background in Cloud Engineering, Infrastructure Engineering or DevOps.
Experience supporting production environments and working to customer SLAs.
Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
Comfortable working in a customer-facing environment and dealing with technical escalations.
Desirable experience
Scripting experience with Python, PowerShell or Bash.
Experience with automation and configuration management tools such as Ansible.
Exposure to containerisation and Kubernetes.
Relevant AWS or Terraform certifications.
