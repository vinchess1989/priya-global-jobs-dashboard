Role: DevOps Manager
Location: Remote
Employment Type: Full-time
Compensation: Competitive salary




Role Overview:

We are actively seeking a innovative DevOps Manager to join our growing remote team. In this pivotal role, you will be instrumental in shaping our operations, driving strategic initiatives, and delivering exceptional results within your functional area. The ideal candidate will combine big-picture strategic thinking with hands-on, tactical execution.




Key Responsibilities:

Act as a trusted subject matter expert and advisor for cross-functional initiatives.
Take complete ownership of complex project execution, strategy, and final delivery.
Develop and execute comprehensive automated and manual testing protocols.
Participate actively in code reviews, sprint planning, and architectural discussions.
Collaborate cross-functionally with product managers and engineers on new features.




Required Skills & Qualifications:

Exceptional written, verbal, and asynchronous communication capabilities.
Highly organized methodology with a strong ability to prioritize effectively.
Proven ability to translate complex business requirements into technical solutions.
Proven comfort and productivity operating in a fully remote, distributed environment.
5-8 years of demonstrated, progressive success in a relevant functional role.




What You’ll Bring:

The proven ability to thrive and drive results in an ambiguous, fast-paced setting.
An unwavering commitment to operational quality, accuracy, and overall excellence.
High emotional intelligence, empathy, and a deeply collaborative team spirit.




Benefits:

Fully remote, flexible work hours
Performance-based bonus structure
Annual learning & development stipend
Health and wellness benefits (varies by location)
Opportunity to work on high-impact projects
