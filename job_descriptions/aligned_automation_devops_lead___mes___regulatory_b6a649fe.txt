Aligned Automation is seeking a talented DevOps Lead to spearhead our engineering practices and delivery infrastructure for the Manufacturing Execution System (MES) programme in Limerick, Ireland. The role entails designing, implementing, and maintaining CI/CD pipelines to ensure reliable deployments while managing the DevOps toolchain, such as source control and monitoring. Ideal candidates will possess direct experience with MES platforms like Syncade and thrive in regulated environments.

The successful applicant will collaborate closely with various stakeholders and demonstrate strong problem-solving skills to navigate complex infrastructure challenges. Join us to drive innovation in regulated manufacturing!
