Job Description

Our client is seeking an experienced Regulatory Affairs Specialist to provide maternity leave cover within its Regulatory Affairs team. The successful candidate will support regulatory compliance, technical documentation and ongoing product lifecycle activities.

Responsibilities

Preparation, maintenance and lifecycle management of regulatory technical files, primarily supporting CE marking requirements
Support additional technical documentation for international product registrations
Support regulatory activities arising from post-market surveillance
Review product labelling and Instructions for Use (IFUs) for regulatory compliance
Support regulatory audits and inspections, including notified body reviews
Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Quality Assurance, Medical Affairs, Supply Chain, Demand Planning and Commercial
Manage multiple competing priorities in a busy regulatory environment.


Requirements

Bachelor's degree or higher in a relevant discipline, such as Life Sciences, Biomedical Science, Chemistry or Biotechnology
5+ years' relevant Regulatory Affairs experience preferred; minimum 3 years
Strong preference for IVD experience and knowledge of EU IVDR
Candidates with medical device regulatory experience (EU MDR) will also be considered
Experience preparing and maintaining technical files and supporting CE marking
Experience with post-market surveillance, labelling and regulatory inspections desirable.


Benefits

Competitive and attractive employee compensation package
Pension contributions
Health insurance
Paid public holidays.
