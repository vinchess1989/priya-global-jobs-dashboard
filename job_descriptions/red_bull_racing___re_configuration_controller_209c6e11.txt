Join Red Bull Powertrains, where we're revolutionising power unit technology to drive the next era of Formula 1 success.




We are continuing an exciting new era as we continue our first season in Formula 1 with our very own in house designed, manufactured, built and tested powertrain. Powered by the best internal combustion engine (ICE) on the grid, we are pushing the boundaries of performance, innovation and teamwork at the highest level of motorsport. This is a unique opportunity to join a fast-paced organisation where ambition, cutting-edge technology and a relentless drive to win are at the heart of everything we do. As we continue to build for the future, we're looking for talented individuals who are ready to make a real impact and help shape our journey on and off the track.

We are seeking a driven, motivated, and proactive Configuration Controller with a keen eye for detail to ensure any engineering change, or related power unit configuration data is accurate across multiple systems.




Your responsibilities:

Create, maintain, and update Bills of Materials (BoMs) within TeamCenter.
Ensure BoM structures accurately reflect approved product definitions and engineering changes.
Perform BoM audits and validation activities to verify completeness, accuracy and compliance.
Investigate and resolve BoM discrepancies in collaboration with engineering and operational teams.
Monitor configuration status and maintain records of approved changes.
Administer the creation, review, release and revision of technical specifications and controlled documents.
Ensure specifications are maintained under appropriate document control and version management processes.
Verify that approved changes are reflected consistently across specifications, drawings, BoMs and related documentation.
Maintain specification registers and configuration records.
Support periodic reviews of controlled documentation to ensure currency and compliance.




Your experience:

Experience working within configuration control, document control, engineering data management or a related discipline.
Understanding of Bill of Materials (BoM) structures and change control processes.
Experience administering controlled technical documentation.
Strong attention to detail with the ability to manage large volumes of data accurately.
Good organisational and planning skills.
Proficient in Microsoft Office applications, particularly Excel.
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
Ability to work independently and as part of a cross-functional team.
Working knowledge of Product Lifecycle Management Software (such as Siemens TeamCentre) would be preferred, but not essential.
