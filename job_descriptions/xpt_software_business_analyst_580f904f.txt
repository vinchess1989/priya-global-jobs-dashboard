The Role:

5+ years exp as Business Analyst

• Multiple positions for permanent role
• Full Time position
• Sydney/Melbourne/Brisbane

Remuneration:
85,000 – 165,000 AUD for permanent positions for various experience levels

Job Description:
• 5+ years exp as Business Analyst preferrable in Banking/Insurance domain
• Create formal user stories based on functional and non-functional requirements
• Proficient in requirement decomposition to epics, features, user stories, Product backlog creation & grooming
• High level of flexibility with the ability to adapt to change
• Work closely with project owner in backlog management and continuous delivery of features.
• Initiate and support Agile development of scrum multi-function team
• Sprint planning, Solution design, Estimation, Impact Assessment
• Forecasting Data Migration and Build/Development effort by constructing dashboards
• Use JIRA for end-to-end project and team management process and guide the team to use JIRA for various activities
• Validating and redrafting test cases for the Testing teams, making sure all scenarios are covered
• Creating user and training documentation, and conducting formal training
• Work closely with User Experience specialists, developers and testers to provide clarification when required
• Write acceptance criteria in collaboration with the testing team
• Defect tracking, reporting, triaging and retesting.

• We have served 100+ clients globally fulfilling their onsite-offshore needs through local hiring or sponsored work visas
