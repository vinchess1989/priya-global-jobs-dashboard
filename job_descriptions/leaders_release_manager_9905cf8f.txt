An exciting opportunity exists for an experienced Release Manager to join a large-scale technology portfolio delivering critical business and customer-facing system enhancements across a complex enterprise environment.
This role will see you responsible for the end-to-end release management lifecycle, ensuring successful delivery of multiple concurrent releases across a diverse project landscape.

Key Responsibilities

Manage release planning, scheduling, governance and deployment activities
Coordinate SIT, UAT and Production releases across multiple projects
Develop deployment strategies, cutover plans, run sheets and rollback plans
Facilitate Change Advisory Board (CAB) activities and release approvals
Manage release calendars and environment schedules
Identify, manage and mitigate release risks, issues and dependencies
Coordinate technical teams, project teams, business stakeholders and vendors
Support project and delivery managers with release and deployment activities
Drive stakeholder engagement and release communications
Raise and manage change requests and release documentation
Support Agile delivery activities including sprint planning, release planning and backlog management
Assist with reporting, dashboards and governance across delivery streams
About You
6+ years' experience managing releases and deployments within large, complex ICT environments
Strong experience coordinating concurrent releases across multiple projects and teams
Demonstrated expertise in cutover planning, deployment execution and release governance
Experience facilitating CAB processes and change management activities
Advanced Microsoft Project skills
Experience with ServiceNow, Jira and Confluence
Strong stakeholder engagement and vendor management capabilities
Experience working within Agile and Scrum delivery environments
Ability to work effectively under pressure and manage competing priorities
Desirable Experience
Utilities, energy, billing or customer platform environments
Microsoft Dynamics 365
Large-scale transformation or regulatory change programs
Environment management and enterprise application delivery
