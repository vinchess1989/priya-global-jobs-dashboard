BBI exists to help our customers create advances that improve health and save lives. We are seeking a Global QMS & Projects Lead to join our team. This is a permanent, full-time position based at our site in Crumlin. Join our team: advancing health, together.

The Role

The Global Quality Management Systems & Projects Lead is a key role on the Global Quality & Regulatory leadership team. The role is responsible for the design and implementation of the Global QMS, for driving the definition of global quality procedures, work instructions and processes and providing a consistent message about BBI’s Quality vision and plans. In addition, the role will identify, manage & drive efficiencies for continuous improvement of the electronic Quality Management System. The role is also responsible for the oversight and delivery of key Quality improvement projects whilst also contributing to BBI’s corporate audit program.

In This Role Your Responsibilities Will Include


Responsible for designing and maintaining a Global eQMS in accordance with ISO 13485 Medical Device and Pharmaceutical cGMP guidelines
Accountable for ensuring full quality and regulatory compliance needs of customers are incorporated into the Global BBI eQMS
Responsible for preparing and executing a global education program for the use and application of the QMS
Prepares and executes any specific Quality Management System audits.
Works with sites to remediate QMS deficiencies
Maintains positive agency relationships, liaisons with regulatory agencies and participates in external technical forums
Participates in and drives the execution of BBI’s Quality Improvement projects
Owns global compliance assessment and remediation plans while influencing site’s full execution.
Influences Global policy and processes, development programs, communication packages and training requirements
