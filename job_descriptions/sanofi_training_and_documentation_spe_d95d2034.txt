Job title: Training and Documentation Specialist


Location: Singapore


About The Job

Sanofi's Manufacturing & Supply organization is building the future of vaccine and Specialty Care Biologics production through the Modulus Facility programme, a new generation of modular, agile, multi-product facilities. The Tuas (Singapore) greenfield site mirrors the one in Neuville (France) and will be highly digitalized, using technologies such as AGVs, cobots and cloud systems. In this role, you will develop and deliver GMP training, administer the iLearn learning management system (learning objects, assignments, catalogue, reports and learner support), and manage the GxP documentation lifecycle. You will keep the documentation lean, accurate and compliant, support audits and inspections, and contribute to QMS processes such as Deviation, CAPA and Change Control. The ideal candidate holds a Bachelor's degree in Pharmacy, Science, Engineering or a related field, has at least 4 years of pharma or biotech experience, and brings strong analytical, communication and collaboration skills, along with familiarity with Digital 4.0 concepts in Quality.

About Sanofi

We’re an R&D-driven, AI-powered biopharma company committed to improving people’s lives and delivering compelling growth. Our deep understanding of the immune system – and innovative pipeline – enables us to invent medicines and vaccines that treat and protect millions of people around the world. Together, we chase the miracles of science to improve people’s lives.

Main Accountabilities

For Training


Contributing to develop and implement annual training programs to ensure compliance with Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP) and company standards
Contributing to identify training needs and developing customized training plans
Supporting the managers in the establishment of the curricula for the different areas
Developing training materials with the support of the relevant subject matter expert to address the training needs and plans
Conducting training sessions for new hires and existing employees on specific topics
Evaluating training effectiveness through assessments, feedback, and performance metrics
Support relevant subject matter expert in updating training materials to reflect changes in regulations, procedures, and best practices
Leading continuous improvement initiatives to enhance training material and processes
Providing support during regulatory inspections and audits by presenting training records, training process and material
Contribute to QMS processes related training activities (e.g., Deviation, CAPA, Change Control) for the site
Perform iLearn administration activities (e.g., management of Learning Objects, Groups, Assignment, execute dedicated reports), to support the Quality and HSE training needs for his/her perimeter according to his/her permissions
Identify best approach to operate the learning offer in iLearn by applying approved Ways of Working
Support manager and training specialist to elaborate training plan in compliance with iLearn best practices Stay abreast of the tool functionalities evolution by following the corresponding training within defined timelines
Ensure alignment with other decentralized administrators by participating in regular coordination meetings
Ensure the updating and management of the training catalog available in iLearn
Contribute to site projects requiring LMS expertise
In the framework of the permissions granted, execute the administrative activities in iLearn in compliance with all applicable work instructions such as:
Creation, modification, and re-versioning and inactivation of specific learning objects such as Online Courses, On the Job Training, creation of event/session for face-to-face or virtual training, according to the approved processes (i.e., incl. Test in P2)
Request the creation of non-Workday user profiles in accordance with iLearn User profile requirements, with global interface for requests.
Management of groups
Learning assignments, registration and completion management.
Run reports
Liaise with global iLearn Operations team to perform activities not included in the decentralized administrator permissions.
Ensure full compliance of all activities with iLearn Operations Global Quality documents applicable to the role and respond diligently to iLearn Quality Control requests for data updates and corrections
May contribute to deviation investigation or analysis (linked to the tool or the interface with other tools)
Support audits and inspections as needed e.g. to demonstrate the tool operation, providing inspection reports.
Ensure level 2 support to learners
Contribute to iLearn Learner support improvement (knowledge base enrichment
Contribute in iLearn administration evolution projects (process improvement, testing…).
May contribute to the Decentralized activities for other sites or entities as back up or support. Three levels of permissions related to the scope / perimeter can be granted to administrators based on their level of proficiency and according to M&S needs:
Local Administrator: Access limited to a specific entity, site or country.
Regional Administrator: Access across multiple sites within a defined region.
Global Administrator: Access to data across all perimeters (M&S).
Commitment to Compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) As part of our commitment to safeguarding personal data and maintaining trust with our employees, this role includes key responsibilities and knowledge of General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) compliance and data protection principles.

For Documentation


Managing the GxP documentation lifecycle (creation, revision, and obsolescence)
Collaborating with other departments to ensure document content addresses the business needs and processes
Ensuring, with the support of relevant experts that the documentation content is compliant with regulatory requirements and company standards
Ensuring the overall documentation package is kept lean (i.e., avoiding redundancies and duplication)
Ensuring accuracy, completeness, and compliance of the documentation with writing rules (incl. clarity and grammar)
Collecting needs for documentation changes, prioritizing and managing document update
Ensuring the documentation is regularly updated to remain current and aligned with the needs and processes
Ensuring document distribution and access complies with the information classification level
Supporting internal and external audits and inspection by ensuring all required documents are up-to-date and retrievable
Representing the site in the documentation CoP led by global and, if requested, participating in the CoE and ensuring local communication

Excellent analytical, consultative, and diagnostic skills with ability to make sound, data-based quality decisions
Engaging, dynamic Adaptable, relationship focused Self-organized, able to work independently with little supervision.
Proactive, Result-oriented Driven team player with the ability to collaborate and work with cross-functional teams (including global partners) to meet project timelines.
Good communication skills to present strategies to the site management and other functions.
Familiar with Digital 4.0 concepts and potential applications in Quality.
