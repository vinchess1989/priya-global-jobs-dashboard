Responsibilities / Tasks

Job Description:

The Technical Author will manage, plan and co-ordinate all aspects of the technical manual writing activities in order to produce installation,

Operating and Maintenance manuals and pertaining technical documents, in the most efficient manner to achieve the delivery, cost and quality

requirements and targets of the business.

The Technical Author will be responsible for the training of Technical Administrators in the correct compilation of Technical Manuals.

A good understanding of translation requirements is essential with the ability to interpret and translate documents utilizing all tools at his / her

disposal.

The Technical Author is to have exceptional word processing skills, and a high skill level is required in both Microsoft Word and Adobe PDF.

Job Responsibilities


Provide technical writing support to engineering teams in a cost-effective, functional and reliable manner aligned with industry standards.
Produce IOM manuals from technical documentation and research for company equipment.
Plan and prioritize manual workload, estimate hours for specific tasks, fulfil contractual requirements and report progress.
Create, store and maintain reusable standard documents for modular compilation of manuals across standard equipment.
Maintain communication with design teams to incorporate changes and improvements into the standards library.
Control amendment instructions archived manual updates, project-specific filing, electronic archiving and file management.
Manage translation quotations and related material with approved translator suppliers.

documentation methodologies.


Proficient in documentation and illustration tools including Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Acrobat Pro, Microsoft Word, and


other related graphics/document management applications.


Basic knowledge of ERP-systems (SAP, IFS etc.)

Awareness of technical safety standards and applicable norms.
Good ability to read and analyse an entire productive process.
Team player
