Do you want a job with a purpose?

And do you want to make healthcare safer, better and more reliable?

Join our Team!




Join us as a Cloud Engineer at Dedalus, one of the World’s leading healthcare technology companies, on our CTO Team in Brisbane, Australia to do the best work of your career and make a profound impact in providing better care for a healthier planet.




What you’ll achieve

As a Cloud Engineer you will be you will be required to provide technical expertise, manage deliverables and at times be responsible for coordinating activities such as root cause analysis activities, as well as working closely with the solution architecture group and application teams to design workload packages for deployment to the Dedalus Cloud Platform.

The Dedalus cloud platform provides a secure, automated, and monitored platform for the delivery of our application portfolio as a service to our regional customer base.




Working with an extended highly skilled team, you will be making a profound impact throughout the healthcare sector.




You will:

Be responsible for designing, scripting and deploying the cloud-based solutions for supporting those applications.
Ensure all components align to deliver a safe, robust and reliable products to our customers.
Develop automated solutions to support application onboarding processes.
Participate in the platform development lifecycle with global stakeholders to expand the capabilities for current and future workloads.
Provide expertise and guidance around deployment options for high availability and high-performance infrastructure.
Assist with solution testing to develop protocols/patterns or plan for testing revised applications and reviewing of test results.
Collaborate closely with project architects and product teams to design and recommend solutions that meet business, security, and service-level requirements while enhancing automated cloud-based application deployment capabilities.
Engage with client business stakeholders to understand the short and long-term business implications (people, process, systems, change) of proposed or potential changes.
Participate in planning, implementation, and growth of the Dedalus Cloud Platform.
Deliver solutions utilizing virtual teams comprised of offshore and onshore resources.




Take the next step towards your dream career

At Dedalus Life flows through our software. Every day we do something special by helping caregivers and health professionals deliver better care to their served communities. Take the next step in your career that will make a profound impact.

Here’s what you’ll need to succeed:




Essential Requirements

Minimum of 5 years of experience as a technology professional.
Minimum of 2 years of demonstratable experience working with Azure and / or AWS including networking.
Microsoft / AWS Certification in cloud related competencies.
Experience with programming and infrastructure automation.
At least one of: Microsoft DevOps Pipeline, Terraform, AWS Elastic Beanstalk / CodeDeploy etc.
High Availability Architectures.
Automation/configuration management
Analytical and problem-solving skills and ability to troubleshoot complex technical problems
Experience with task management tools (e.g., JIRA, etc.).




Desirable Requirements

Demonstrated skills and experience in

Terraform / Ansible
SQL and SQL PaaS
GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket or equivalent RedHat Openshift
Docker/Kubernetes, Elastic Stack
Understanding of ITIL processes.
Experience working with a healthcare vendor or healthcare employer.

Dedalus is committed to providing an engaging, rewarding work experience that reflects the passion our employees bring to our mission of helping clinicians and nurses deliver better care to their served communities.

Our company fosters a culture where employees are encouraged to learn and innovate, and to enable and enhance clinical co-operation and processes while making a meaningful difference for millions of people around the world. Each person is the end point and the starting point of the Group’s activities and the ultimate beneficiary. For this reason, we are so proud of doing our very special jobs each day.

Our company is enriched by a diverse population of 7,600 people in more than 40 countries that work together to innovate and drive better healthcare options for millions of patients around the world. We are the people of Dedalus.
