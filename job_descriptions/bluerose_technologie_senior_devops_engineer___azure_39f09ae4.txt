We are seeking an experienced DevOps Engineer to enable reliable, automated, and secure delivery of enterprise applications and platforms.

Key Responsibilities

Build and manage CI/CD pipelines
Support blue‑green, active‑active, and resilient deployment patterns
Automate environment provisioning and application releases
Monitor system health, performance, and availability
Collaborate with development and platform engineering teams

Skills and Experience

Expert Develops Engineer with hands-on expertise in developing application-side CI/CD pipelines in Azure DevOps using YAML with automation experience.
Possess a minimum of two years' experience authoring Application CI/CD pipelines in YAML and 5+ years overall.
Experience with DevSecOps tool-chain would be an advantage
Hand-on Azure DevOps, JFrog, Sonar tooling. Additional experience with DotNet (Containerised apps, SPA, Azure apps) and Java (Maven) applications will be considered advantageous.
Expertise in CI/CD tools and monitoring platforms
Experience supporting mission‑critical systems




Personal Attributes

Strong analytical, diagnostic, and problem-solving capability.
Clear communication and effective stakeholder management.
Ability to work independently and collaboratively across cross-functional teams.
Commitment to operational excellence, automation, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement.










Minimum Years of Experience: 8-10 yrs




Key Responsibilities:




Review high level integration solution designs and provide technical leadership to project teams, ensuring approved integration patterns and architectures are consistently applied throughout detailed design and implementation.
Proactively identify, assess, and escalate technical and delivery risks, and define alternative technical or functional approaches where required, following established governance and approval processes.
Define, govern, and approve API standards, frameworks, and processes, shaping the API strategy to align with AEMO’s broader corporate and technology strategy.
Represent the team in gas and electricity industry forums, gathering feedback and ensuring external participants can effectively connect to and interoperate with Business‑to‑Business systems in accordance with interface specifications and architectural standards.
Design and maintain dashboards and reports to track key operational metrics and business SLAs using enterprise application monitoring and observability tools.
Identify repetitive operational and administrative tasks and develop a roadmap to automate processes, improving platform reliability, scalability, and operational efficiency.
Partner with enterprise and solution architecture teams to define and evolve integration standards, patterns, and best practices that meet current and future business needs.
Support project teams in scaling and onboarding new integration environments, providing hands‑on assistance with setup, troubleshooting, and resolution of configuration issues to meet project timelines.
