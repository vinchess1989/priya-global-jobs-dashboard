Brigade Electronics Group Plc is a trusted, multi-national, multi-award-winning global market leader developing innovative vehicle safety systems for commercial vehicles and mobile machinery.

Our cutting-edge technologies help prevent collisions, protect vulnerable road users and save lives worldwide. As part of our continued growth, we are looking for an experienced Senior Product Compliance Engineer to support product regulatory compliance, ensure our technologies meet applicable international standards and help bring innovative safety solutions to global markets.




The Position

Play a key role in ensuring Brigade's products meet applicable regulatory, legislative and technical requirements across the UK, Europe and international markets.
Assess product compliance against relevant legislation and standards, including electromagnetic compatibility (EMC), radio equipment, electrical safety and product security requirements.
Support product certification, conformity assessments and the preparation and maintenance of technical compliance documentation.
Work closely with Engineering, Product Development, Quality and other departments to identify regulatory requirements and integrate compliance considerations throughout the product lifecycle.
Monitor changes in international product legislation and standards, assessing their impact on existing and future products.
Provide technical guidance on regulatory requirements relating to environmental compliance, waste electrical and electronic equipment (WEEE), batteries and responsible materials sourcing.
Support product compliance investigations, identify potential risks and work collaboratively with internal teams and external stakeholders to resolve compliance challenges.
Liaise with suppliers, testing laboratories and certification bodies to support product testing, approvals and regulatory compliance.
Contribute to the development and continuous improvement of product compliance processes, documentation and best practices across the business.




Skills & Experience

Proven experience in product compliance, regulatory affairs, certification or a related engineering discipline, ideally within electronics, automotive, manufacturing or a similar technical environment.
Strong understanding of UK and EU product compliance legislation, particularly EMC, electrical safety and radio equipment requirements.
Knowledge of relevant regulatory frameworks, including CE and UKCA conformity requirements.
Familiarity with environmental and product sustainability legislation, including WEEE, battery regulations and responsible minerals sourcing.
Experience interpreting technical standards, assessing regulatory applicability and translating complex requirements into practical engineering solutions.
Understanding of product testing, technical documentation, conformity assessments and certification processes.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with excellent attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple technical priorities.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the confidence to work collaboratively across engineering, quality, suppliers and external regulatory bodies.
A proactive, organised and results-driven approach, with a commitment to maintaining high standards of product safety and compliance.
