Job Description

Location: Healix Group, Ground Floor, Brookside Office Park, 11 Imam Haron Rd

Lead the delivery of technology change that makes a real impact.

At Healix, we're looking for an experienced IT Delivery Manager to drive the successful delivery of technology projects, business change initiatives, and digital transformation programmes across our global organisation.

This is an exciting opportunity for a delivery-focused professional who thrives in complex environments, enjoys bringing people together to achieve shared goals, and has a proven track record of delivering technology solutions that create measurable business value.

Working across business, technical, operational and third-party teams, you'll lead the planning, coordination, governance and delivery of a diverse portfolio of projects, ensuring solutions are delivered on time, within scope and to the highest quality standards.

This is a 3 day per week Fixed Term Contract for one year. You will be required to spend 1-2 days per week in the office. We are looking to recruit for this position either in Esher, United Kingdom, or in Cape Town, South Africa. The salary for the role represents a 3-day working week.

As we are expecting a high number of applicants, we may close the job posting before the date shown.

What we're looking for

You'll be an organised, proactive and collaborative delivery professional with strong stakeholder management skills and the ability to balance multiple priorities.

We're Particularly Interested In Hearing From Candidates Who Have


Experience delivering technology projects and business change initiatives within complex organisations.
A proven track record of managing software, infrastructure, cloud and enterprise technology projects.
Experience coordinating cross-functional teams within a matrix environment.
Strong knowledge of Agile, Scrum, Kanban and traditional project delivery methodologies.
Experience using delivery management tools such as Azure DevOps, Jira, Microsoft Project or similar.
Excellent communication, facilitation and relationship-building skills.
The ability to influence stakeholders and build consensus across technical and non-technical teams.
Strong planning, problem-solving and risk management capabilities.
A delivery-focused mindset with a strong sense of ownership and accountability.


Desirable Qualifications


Agile PM Practitioner
PRINCE2 Practitioner
Professional Scrum Master (PSM)
Certified Scrum Master (CSM)
Project Management Professional (PMP)
ITIL Foundation (or above)


Why Healix?

At Healix, Our Values Guide Everything We Do

Put people first - We prioritise the wellbeing of our colleagues and clients in every decision.

Think independently - We use expertise, judgement and integrity to make sound decisions.

Earn trust - We act transparently, deliver reliably and build strong relationships with colleagues, clients and partners.

You'll join a business where collaboration, accountability and continuous improvement are valued, and where you'll have the opportunity to shape how technology delivers meaningful outcomes for our people, clients and customers.

Success in this role will be measured by


Successful delivery of projects within agreed timescales.
Achievement of planned scope and quality outcomes.
Effective management of risks, issues and dependencies.
Positive stakeholder feedback and confidence in delivery performance.
Realisation of expected business benefits and adoption of solutions.
Compliance with governance, security and audit requirements.


If you're passionate about delivering technology change, building strong relationships and helping organisations achieve their strategic goals, we'd love to hear from you.

About The Role

What you'll be doing


Leading the end-to-end delivery of technology projects, product enhancements and strategic change initiatives.
Managing project plans, timelines, resources, risks and dependencies across multiple workstreams.
Building strong relationships with stakeholders across the business, IT teams and external suppliers.
Providing clear and transparent reporting on project progress, milestones, risks and issues.
Facilitating governance forums and steering groups to support informed decision-making.
Supporting Agile ways of working and promoting continuous improvement across delivery teams.
Removing delivery obstacles and helping teams stay focused on achieving successful outcomes.
Ensuring projects comply with governance, security, audit and regulatory requirements.
Driving the successful adoption of delivered solutions and the realisation of business benefits.


Required Criteria


Proven IT Delivery Experience
Strong Stakeholder & Delivery Management Skills
Agile & Project Delivery Expertise


Desired Criteria


Professional Project Management Certifications
Enterprise Technology & Cloud Exposure
Governance & Compliance Experience
