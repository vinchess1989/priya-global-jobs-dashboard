Sonova is seeking a Requirements Analyst to support the development of innovative hearing instruments and charging solutions within our Research & Development organization. This role will collaborate with cross-functional teams to gather, analyze, document, and manage product requirements, ensuring alignment with stakeholder expectations, regulatory standards, and business objectives.

Responsibilities:


Gather, analyze, document, and maintain product requirements for hearing instruments, charging solutions, and related technologies.
Collaborate with Engineering, Business, Quality, Verification, and other stakeholders to establish clear requirements and product specifications.
Support the planning and development of new product features and functionalities while ensuring alignment across teams.
Develop and maintain detailed requirements documentation in accordance with medical device regulations and established operating procedures.
Ensure traceability between user needs, product requirements, specifications, test cases, and identified risks.
Partner with Requirements Analysts, Engineers, and Managers to maintain consistency and compliance across products and development projects.
Contribute to continuous improvement of requirements engineering practices, processes, and tools while supporting interdisciplinary Agile development teams.



More about you:


Engineering degree, technical diploma, or equivalent apprenticeship qualification, with further education in Requirements Engineering or Systems Engineering.
2+ years of engineering experience in a product development environment, preferably within medical devices or a similar regulated industry.
Experience gathering, documenting, analyzing, and managing product requirements across hardware and software development projects.
Strong understanding of product development processes, stakeholder communication, and requirements traceability.
Excellent analytical, conceptual modeling, problem-solving, and organizational skills, with the ability to manage complexity and changing priorities.
Strong communication, collaboration, and conflict-resolution skills, with the ability to work across global teams and influence without direct authority.
Experience with requirements management tools such as Polarion, Jira, Confluence, or Microsoft Teams is an asset; IREB or INCOSE certification is preferred.



Travel: Rare travel may be required depending on business needs.
