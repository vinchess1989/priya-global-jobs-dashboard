Are you ready to make your mark in the world of luxury automotive? Adecco working in partnership with Bentley motors aftersales team are looking for a Technical Author – Owner Handbooks to be a vital part of delivering world-class technical documentation for their prestigious brand!







Location: Crewe, UK

Contract Type: Temporary (until 26/06/2029)

Pay Rate: Competitive outside IR35

Work Arrangement: Hybrid (3 days on-site)

Parking: Available nearby







About the Role:

In this exciting position, you will craft clear, engaging, and precise technical documentation that supports both customers and technicians around the globe. Your expertise will play a crucial role in the lifecycle of our client's remarkable vehicles, from developing Owner Handbooks.







Key Responsibilities:

Owner Handbooks

Develop and maintain Owner Handbooks aligned with new product launches and model year updates.
Interpret Product Description Books (PDBs), Technical Product Descriptions (TPDs), and Engineering Change documentation.
Ensure content is user-friendly, brand-aligned, and technically accurate.







Quality & Communication

Ensure documentation meets internal standards and regulatory requirements.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Engineering, Product Planning, Legal, and Marketing.
Support internal and external communications to promote Aftersales initiatives.







Skills & Experience

Proven ability to write clear, concise technical documentation for diverse audiences.
Proficient in digital platforms and data management systems.
Experience in external publication and catalogue systems preferred.







Knowledge Requirements

Understanding of Bentley product and retailer processes.
Familiarity with automotive engineering and Aftersales practises.
Knowledge of VW Group systems and legislative requirements.
Proficient in handbook and illustration systems.







Qualifications

Strong content writing skills
Qualification in English
Good knowledge of systems with minimal training provided
Ability to arrange moving images/text for documentation
Essential understanding of automotive parts







Why Join Our Client?

At our client's organisation, you will be part of a legacy of excellence and innovation. Collaborate with passionate professionals in a vibrant environment, contributing to the future of luxury automotive experiences!
