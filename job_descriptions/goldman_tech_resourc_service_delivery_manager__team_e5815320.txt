Service Delivery Manager (Team Lead)
Goldman Tech Resourcing (Pty) Ltd
I'm interested

Our client is seeking an experienced Service Delivery Manager (Team Lead) to oversee the delivery of software services, strengthen client relationships and lead a service delivery team. This opportunity is suited to a service delivery professional with a strong IT background, proven people management capabilities and a passion for operational excellence and continuous improvement.

 

Key Responsibilities:

Take ownership of end-to-end service delivery for assigned clients and platforms, including support and project services.
Ensure delivery aligns with agreed Service Level Agreements (SLAs), Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and contractual obligations.
Act as the primary escalation point for major incidents, service risks and client concerns.
Manage incident, problem and change management processes in accordance with ITIL practices.
Monitor service performance, analyse trends and implement corrective actions.
Build and maintain strong relationships with senior internal and external stakeholders.
Lead service reviews and present performance reports and improvement plans.
Manage client expectations, service transitions, contractual requirements and scope changes.
Lead, coach and mentor the customer team, supporting performance management, goal-setting and professional development.
Ensure appropriate resource allocation, workload balance and team wellbeing.
Identify opportunities for service optimisation, automation and process improvement.
Support budget management, cost control, forecasting and resource utilisation.
Manage risks relating to service continuity, capacity and client satisfaction.

 

Minimum Requirements:

At least 5 years of experience in IT software and service delivery.
A minimum of 3 years' experience in people management or a team lead role.
Strong experience in software service delivery within an IT, SaaS or managed services environment.
Solid understanding of ITIL frameworks, including incident, problem, change and service level management.
Experience managing SLAs, KPIs and client-facing service dashboards.
Proven leadership, stakeholder engagement and conflict-resolution skills.
Excellent communication, presentation and reporting abilities.
Strong analytical, decision-making and problem-solving capabilities.
Ability to balance operational demands with strategic priorities.
Experience in fast-paced or high-availability IT environments.

 

Qualifications:

A tertiary qualification in IT, Information Systems, Business or a related field is preferred.
ITIL Foundation certification is the minimum specified certification level; Intermediate or Expert certification will be advantageous.
