Position

DevOps Engineer – Temenos T24 Wealth Management Platform




Domain

Preferable - Banking / Wealth Management




Job Summary

We are seeking a highly skilled DevOps Engineer to support a leading Wealth Management client. The ideal candidate will be responsible for building, automating, deploying, monitoring, and maintaining T24 applications and associated infrastructure while ensuring high availability, security, and operational excellence across development, testing, and production environments.

The role requires strong expertise in CI/CD automation, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), containerization, cloud-native technologies, monitoring, and DevOps best practices.




Key Responsibilities

Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines for T24 applications and related services.
Automate deployment, release, and infrastructure provisioning processes.
Manage T24 environments and support application deployment activities.
Implement Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform and Ansible.
Support containerized application deployments using Docker and Kubernetes.
Configure and maintain automated build and deployment pipelines.
Monitor platform health, performance, and availability using enterprise monitoring tools.
Troubleshoot production issues and perform root cause analysis.
Collaborate with development, infrastructure, security, and operations teams.
Establish DevOps standards, governance, and best practices.
Support release management and change management processes.
Implement logging, monitoring, and observability solutions.
