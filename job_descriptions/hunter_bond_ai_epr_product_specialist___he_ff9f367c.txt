Job title: AI EPR Product Specialist

Client: HealthTech

Salary: £80,000-£100,000 + Bonus

Location: Remote

Skills: AI, EPR, Product Management, Integration, Enablement, Ambient Voice Technology (AVT)




The role:




My client are seeking a Product Specialist who has a focus on AI and EPR technologies to join their team.




You will have a broad understanding of EPR systems, and a strong understanding of various related NHS systems. You will be part of a team which has responsibility for various product aspects, such as quality, documentation, pre-sales, training and testing.




The successful candidate will have experience of working with AI in order to increase efficiency within EPR and Healthcare systems, and is keen to utilise AI to build new functionality within the system.




Any experience around Ambient Voice Technology (AVT) would be very useful.




Responsibilities:




Utilising AI tooling to improve functionality develop new features within EPR systems
Working on Ambient Voice Technology (AVT) products
Working broadly across the EPR (Order Comms, ePMA, Mon + Obs, PAS etc.)
System documentation
Working closely with both Development and QA teams
Providing technical sales support for the Sales team
Working on first of type solutions




Please apply ASAP for more information.
