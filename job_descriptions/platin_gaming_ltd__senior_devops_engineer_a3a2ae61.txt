Build and maintain cloud infrastructure for high-traffic gaming platform

About The Role

Design and manage our cloud infrastructure on AWS/GCP to support millions of gaming transactions. You'll implement CI/CD, infrastructure as code, and ensure platform reliability and scalability.

Responsibilities


Design and maintain cloud infrastructure (AWS/GCP)
Implement and improve CI/CD pipelines
Manage Kubernetes clusters and orchestration
Automate infrastructure with Terraform/Ansible
Monitor system performance and reliability
Implement security best practices
Handle incident response and troubleshooting
Mentor junior DevOps engineers


Requirements


6+ years of DevOps/SRE experience
Strong AWS or GCP experience (certifications a plus)
Expert in Kubernetes and Docker
Experience with Terraform and infrastructure as code
Strong scripting skills (Python, Bash, Go)
Knowledge of CI/CD tools (GitLab, Jenkins, ArgoCD)
Understanding of monitoring tools (Prometheus, Grafana)
Gaming or high-traffic platform experience preferred


Benefits


Salary €48,000 - €62,000
Hybrid work arrangement
Health insurance
Work with modern cloud stack
Cloud certifications support
Career growth opportunities
