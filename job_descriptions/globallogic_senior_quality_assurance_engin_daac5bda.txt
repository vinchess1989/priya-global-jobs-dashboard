Description

GlobalLogic is hiring an experienced Senior Quality Assurance Engineer with strong Agile expertise and the ability to design, develop, and execute both manual and automated test cases. The successful candidate will play a critical role in our product development lifecycle, monitoring all stages of software development to identify and resolve system malfunctions, ensuring software runs smoothly, meets client needs, and adheres to high-quality standards. Testing scope covers Functional and Non-Functional, Load, Performance, and Accessibility testing.

Requirements



Proven Experience: 4+ years of hands-on experience in software quality assurance and software development.
QA Methodologies: Strong understanding of software QA methodologies, tools, processes, and Agile/Scrum development frameworks.
Test Planning & Documentation: Practical test knowledge of test documentation (Test Strategy & Plans), scripting, defect management, and test reporting.
Automation & Tools: Hands-on experience with automated testing tools and open-source automation frameworks.
Technical Skills: Solid knowledge of SQL and scripting languages.
Desirable Experience:
Experience working in large-scale central Government or enterprise environments.
NFQ Level 6 qualification (or higher) in Computer Science or a relevant discipline.



Job responsibilities



Design, develop, and execute automated test scripts using open-source tools.
Create detailed, comprehensive, and well-structured test plans and test cases.
Estimate, prioritize, plan, and coordinate testing activities.
Identify, record, document thoroughly, and track bugs and defect metrics (e.g., defect densities and open defect counts).
Perform thorough regression testing when bugs are resolved.
Liaise with internal teams (e.g., software developers and product managers) to clarify system requirements and specifications.
Review requirements, specifications, and technical design documents, providing timely and meaningful feedback.
Investigate the causes of non-conforming software and assist/train users to implement solutions.
