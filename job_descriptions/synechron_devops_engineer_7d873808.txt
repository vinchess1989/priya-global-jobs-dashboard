We began life in 2001 as a small, self-funded team of technology specialists. Since then, we’ve grown our organization to 17,000 people, across 58 offices, in 22 countries, in key global markets.




Innovative tech solutions for business

We're now a leading global digital consulting firm, providing innovative technology solutions for business. As a trusted partner, we're always at the forefront of change as we lead digital optimization and modernization journeys for our clients.




Customized end-to-end solutions

Our expertise in AI, Consulting, Data, Digital, Cloud & DevOps and Software Engineering, delivers customized, end-to-end solutions that drive business value and growth.




Our Challenge

Responsible for managing and coordinating production releases, ensuring deployments are planned, executed, monitored, and completed successfully. This role provides the team’s only dedicated production release coverage and works closely with Development, QA, SRE, Infrastructure, Network, and Operations teams.




Responsibilities:

Coordinate production releases, deployment schedules, approvals, and communications.
Manage CI/CD pipelines and support production deployments.
Monitor releases, troubleshoot issues, and coordinate rollback procedures.
Support Kubernetes and Docker-based environments.
Automate operational and release processes using Python and shell scripting.
Maintain release documentation, runbooks, and post-release reviews.
Collaborate with SRE and production engineering teams to improve reliability and deployment efficiency.




Skills:

3-5 years DevOps and release management experience
Kubernetes (K8s) and Docker
CI/CD and GitHub
Python and shell scripting
Linux administration
Networking fundamentals
SRE and production engineering practices
