We are looking for a structured, reliable and curious Business Analyst to support digital products in clinical study planning and start-up through practical analysis and close user collaboration.

The position

You will support Country Allocation, Site Selection and Study Start-Up for one year. The Product Manager will prioritise user support and analytical assignments across the products, provide domain mentoring, and retain responsibility for product direction and final decisions. You will own agreed day-to-day activities, work independently after onboarding and seek guidance on complex issues. You will work closely with the Product Manager, users and development teams across three StudyHub products to connect user needs and data insights with product improvements.

Main Responsibilities:


Manage user feedback and enquiries:
Act as the first point of contact, investigate observations and assess their relevance, impact and urgency.
Translate validated needs into clear bug reports or user stories in Azure DevOps, consolidate duplicates and keep users informed of actions and outcomes.
Apply automation where useful while preserving personal communication and a focused, actionable backlog.
Support baseline and product value analysis:
Help identify and investigate relevant baseline measures using available data.
Assess data quality and limitations, analyse historical cycle times and decision patterns, and present findings and recommendations for discussion.
The Product Manager, with relevant business stakeholders, will make the final decisions on baselines and success measures; your role is to support those decisions with evidence.
Work with data specialists where extraction is complex.
Complex programming, data pipeline development and advanced statistical modelling are outside the scope of this role.
Turn findings into actionable input:
Bring together evidence from data and user feedback, communicate insights through presentations and documents, and translate findings into clear backlog items where appropriate.
Resolve enquiries independently where possible and escalate with sufficient context and a proposed next step.


Key Requirements:


Master's degree.
At least 3 years of experience on a similar/relevant position.
Practical business and data analysis experience.
Familiarity with digital product development.
Strong communication skills.
Ability to investigate business questions using analytical tools.
Explain findings and limitations.
Proactive in suggesting areas to investigate, with a willingness to learn and suggest areas for investigation.
