Technical Writer




Location: Limerick

Duration: 12 months

Hours: 39




To provide support for the technical documentation and support activities within Plant Readiness Team to ensure that all activities associated, and general Operational support tasks are completed in a fully compliant manner in accordance with the requirements of Johnson & Johnson Validation/Quality Policies and Procedures. The role requires collaboration with Quality Engineering to ensure Johnson & Johnson Quality Policies and Procedures are maintained.




Roles and Responsibilities

Perform technical writing activities and assist the Plant Readiness Team in the generation of all documentations including SOP’s/HECP’s/Standard Work/PM ITR’s and any deviations and discrepancies.
Update and edit Operations Procedures and Work Instructions, project manuals, project presentations, doc. retention control and reports.
Assist with purchasing and Spare parts ordering
Provide training and support on Procedural changes to Quality systems.
Manages and monitors the change control metrics.
Ensure accuracy and consistency of the technical documentation.
Ensure all Quality/Operations documents are authorized and appropriate compliance approval is gained right through from approval loop to release on the internal Quality Systems
Expertise in the use of Adobe, Microsoft Word, Excel and Power Point and electronic document management systems.




How to Succeed

Bachelors Degree in Technical Writing
Qualification in an appropriate discipline (e.g. Engineering, Information Systems, or Life Science with additional IT qualification) with a broad technical/educational skills base
Knowledge of 21 CFR 820, 21 CFR 11 and European regulations associated with the medical device industry
Understanding of GMP, Compliance, Validation Practices including the system development life cycle and Regulatory expectations.
Experience in Windchill/ThingWorx document change control Platform




Benefits

This role offers a very competitive hourly rate. This contract will run for 12 Months.
