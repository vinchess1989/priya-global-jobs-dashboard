Program Manager – New Product Development (NPD) Domain: Medical Devices (Peritoneal Dialysis / Renal Care / Connected Medical Devices)

Experience: 15+ Years Overall | 8+ Years in NPD Program Management

Location: Pune




Job Summary

We are seeking an experienced Program Manager to lead end-to-end execution of a complex Medical Device New Product Development (NPD) Program involving Software, Firmware, Electrical Hardware, Mechanical Design, Cybersecurity, Verification & Validation, Usability Engineering, and Regulatory Compliance.

The ideal candidate should possess strong expertise in managing large-scale regulated medical device programs from concept through commercialization, while coordinating global cross-functional teams and ensuring compliance with FDA, MDR, ISO 13485, IEC 62304, ISO 14971, IEC 62366, and cybersecurity requirements.

This role will act as the primary interface between client stakeholders, engineering teams, regulatory groups, quality teams, manufacturing partners, and executive leadership.




Key Responsibilities

Program Leadership & Delivery

Lead end-to-end NPD program execution from concept through product launch.
Define and manage program scope, timelines, budget, risks, dependencies, and deliverables.
Establish program governance, communication framework, and escalation mechanisms.
Drive phase gate reviews and ensure successful completion of all program milestones.
Track program progress against schedules, KPIs, and quality objectives.
Ensure alignment across multiple engineering workstreams.




Design Controls & Compliance

Ensure compliance with:
ISO 13485, IEC 62304, ISO 14971, IEC 60601 Series, IEC 62366, IEC 81001
FDA Design Controls, MDR Requirements
Monitor Design Input, Design Output, Verification, Validation, and Design Transfer activities.
Ensure proper DHF, DMR, and technical documentation management.







Technical Expectations

Candidate should possess solid understanding of:




Software Development

Medical device software development lifecycle
Agile & Hybrid V-Model development

Firmware & Embedded Systems

RTOS-based systems
Device connectivity

Hardware Development

Electronics product development lifecycle
PCB development process

Verification & Validation

Risk-based verification strategy

Cybersecurity

Secure product development
Threat modeling
