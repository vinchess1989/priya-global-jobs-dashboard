We are looking for a Platform / Devops Engineer with strong experience in Ansible and Apigee .

Key Responsibilities

Knowledge and experience with Ansible via CLI and Ansible Automation Platform.
Onboarding work for API Gateway Services, including:
Attending design workshops and project meetings.
Requesting platform requirements and providing costings based on pre-created cost templates.
Updating design documentation, Excel spreadsheets, and wiki documents.
Updating JIRA with status and required details.
Tracking onboarding status and checklists.
Implementing process and documentation improvements.
Implementing onboarding in Apigee software.
Managing API keys, developer apps, developer creation, and rotations.
Creating and updating communication matrices.
Managing PTW (Permit to Work), creating changes and MOPs, and coordinating approvals.
Performing occasional change window activities between 2300 AEST and 0700 AEST.
Developing and improving Ansible scripts.
Implementing static routes on Red Hat VMs.
Performing other ad hoc onboarding and platform maintenance activities.
