Job title: Quality Engineer

Location: Galway Business Park, Dangan Galway

Benefits: Salary, bonus, pension and healthcare




Company:

My client is a specialist medical device designer and manufacturer of catheter-based medical devices employing more than 50 people in Galway, with its team expanding as it develops its products and commercial operations.

One of Galway's notable homegrown medtech companies, who’s clinical and regulatory progress makes it an interesting company




Job Overview:

As a Quality Engineer, you will provide support in quality systems, and manufacturing activities with a focus on continuous improvement of products and processes. You will support the execution of initiatives and projects to enhance quality performance within the business, ensuring compliance to regulations and standards.




Responsibilities:

Support the maintenance of a quality management system that complies with ISO 13485, the Medical Device Regulation (MDR) EU 2017/745, the FDA Quality Management System Regulation 21 CFR 820 and the requirements of the Medical Device Single Audit Program (MDSAP).
Support quality activities related to outsourced manufacturers and suppliers to ensure compliance is maintained for products.
Support risk management activities including hazard/failure mode effects analysis.
Drive risk-based problem solving to assure product quality and patient safety through both design activities and manufacturing processes.
Work closely with cross functional engineering colleagues to lead product transfer quality deliverables.
Support process verification and validation activities in accordance with quality system policies and practices.
Coordinate internal non-conformances (NCs) and CAPAs, ensuring timely containment actions, corrections, root cause investigation, implementation of corrective actions, and closure.




Requirements:

Degree in Engineering or Science discipline.
Relevant quality or manufacturing experience in the medical device industry
Thorough knowledge of Good Manufacturing Practice as described in US and European regulations for medical device manufacture.
