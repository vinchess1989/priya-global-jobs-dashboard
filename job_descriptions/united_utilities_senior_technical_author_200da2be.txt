The role

Location: Warrington (Lingley Mere) 
Hybrid Working: Flexible hybrid working arrangements available 
Salary: Competitive + Excellent Benefits 

 

Help Shape the Knowledge Behind Some of the UK's Most Critical Infrastructure Projects 

 

At United Utilities, we're delivering more than water. We're helping to create a stronger, greener and healthier North West. Behind every major infrastructure investment, treatment works upgrade, and operational improvement sits clear, accurate and accessible technical information that enables our assets to operate safely, efficiently and compliantly. 

 

We're looking for an experienced Senior Technical Author to lead the development, governance and continuous improvement of technical documentation across our Capital Delivery Programme. This is a unique opportunity to combine technical expertise, leadership and innovation while working at the heart of one of the UK's largest infrastructure investment programmes. 

 

If you're passionate about transforming complex engineering information into meaningful, practical documentation that supports operational excellence, we'd love to hear from you. 

What you'll be doing

You'll have responsibility for:

 

Leading the creation, review and quality assurance of technical documentation across major capital projects.

 

Producing and overseeing a wide range of documentation, including:  

   

Operation and Maintenance Manuals 
Site-Specific Technical Documentation 
Training Materials 
User Guides 
Asset Handover Documentation 

 

Translating complex engineering and operational information into clear, user-friendly content for operational and technical audiences. 
Acting as the technical lead for documentation governance, ensuring consistency, quality and compliance across all deliverables. 
Working collaboratively with engineers, commissioning managers, designers and operational teams to gather, validate and maintain technical information. 
Driving the development of innovative documentation strategies, standards and best practices. 
Designing and maintaining document templates, publication structures and governance frameworks. 
Leading governance sessions with stakeholders to ensure documentation is accurate, approved and effectively implemented within the business. 
Managing and developing the team's SharePoint environment, improving collaboration, document control and approval workflows. 
Maintaining robust version control processes and ensuring documentation remains current and compliant. 
Developing supporting training materials to facilitate the introduction of new assets, technologies and processes. 
Supporting communications across Capital Engineering and Construction and the wider organisation. 
Coaching and mentoring technical authors and new team members to enhance capability across the function. 
Identifying and implementing continuous improvement opportunities to enhance documentation systems, processes and user experience.

 

Why this Role Matters

 

Every treatment works upgrade, pumping station improvement, network enhancement and operational asset relies on accurate technical documentation to ensure safe operation and long-term performance. 

 

As Senior Technical Author, you'll be a key link between engineering delivery and operational success, ensuring the knowledge behind our infrastructure investments is captured, transferred and preserved effectively. 

 

The work you produce will support engineers, operational colleagues, regulatory compliance and ultimately help deliver exceptional service to millions of customers across the North West.

 

What We're Looking For

 

We're seeking an experienced technical documentation professional who can combine technical understanding with exceptional communication and stakeholder management skills. 

 

 

You'll bring: 

 

Significant experience producing technical documentation within engineering, infrastructure, utilities or other highly regulated environments. 
Exceptional technical writing skills with the ability to communicate complex concepts clearly, accurately and concisely. 
Strong understanding of structured document management, governance and quality assurance processes. 
Excellent attention to detail and a commitment to producing high-quality outputs. 
Proven experience gathering and synthesising technical information from multiple subject matter experts. 
Strong stakeholder management skills and the confidence to challenge, influence and drive standards. 
Experience managing multiple deliverables across a diverse project portfolio. 
Strong organisational and prioritisation skills with the ability to meet demanding programme deadlines. 
Experience working with SharePoint, document control systems and collaborative platforms. 
A passion for continuous improvement and improving the way technical information is communicated and managed. 

 

About You 

 

You'll likely be educated to Degree or HND level in a relevant discipline such as: 

 

Technical Writing 
Engineering 
Environmental Science 
Asset Management 
Information Management 
Equivalent industry experience will also be highly valued. 

 

You'll also bring: 

 

Knowledge of water and wastewater treatment processes and operational environments. 
Understanding of industry regulations, compliance requirements and infrastructure documentation standards. 
Experience supporting large-scale engineering, construction or capital delivery programmes. 
A collaborative mindset with the ability to build strong relationships across technical and operational teams.

Join Us 

 

This is an exciting opportunity to become the driving force behind technical documentation excellence within a business investing billions in the future of water and wastewater services. 

If you're passionate about technical communication, knowledge management and supporting engineering excellence, we'd love to hear from you. 

Apply today and help ensure the knowledge behind our assets is as strong as the infrastructure itself.
