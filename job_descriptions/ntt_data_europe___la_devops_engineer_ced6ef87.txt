The team you'll be working with:

We are seeking an experienced Salesforce DevOps Lead to design, implement, and continuously improve DevOps, CI/CD, release management, and deployment practices for enterprise Salesforce implementations.

The role will be responsible for establishing scalable, secure, and reliable delivery processes across the Salesforce development lifecycle, including source control, branching strategies, automated build and deployment pipelines, release governance, environment management, quality controls, and the removal of barriers to production.

Working closely with Salesforce developers, architects, product owners, business stakeholders, security teams, and operational support functions, you will translate delivery requirements into robust DevOps solutions that improve deployment frequency, quality, traceability, and operational stability.

You will provide technical leadership to engineering teams, contribute hands-on to the implementation and management of Salesforce CI/CD solutions, and act as an escalation point for complex deployment, release, and production incidents. You will also identify opportunities to automate manual processes, address technical debt, strengthen monitoring and observability, and continually improve the client service.

The successful candidate will have strong experience in Salesforce development and release management, combined with practical expertise in DevOps tools, Git-based source control, CI/CD pipeline design, security, automation, and agile delivery. Experience with AWS or other cloud infrastructure is valuable where it supports the wider Salesforce delivery ecosystem, integrations, tooling, or platform services.




What you'll be doing:




DevOps Leadership

Define and implement DevOps strategy, standards, and best practices across projects.
Lead, mentor, and develop a team of DevOps engineers.
Drive automation initiatives to improve software delivery quality, speed, and reliability.
Collaborate with architecture, development, testing, and operations teams.

Salesforce DevOps

Lead Salesforce deployment and release activities across environments.
Implement Salesforce DevOps best practices including:
Source-driven development
Change management
Release governance
Environment strategy
Work with Salesforce DX (SFDX), Copado, Gearset, or similar DevOps tools.
Support deployments involving Apex, Lightning Components, Flows, and integrations.

AWS Cloud Management

Design, build, and maintain AWS-based infrastructure.
Manage and optimize services such as:
EC2
ECS/EKS
Lambda
S3
RDS
Route 53
CloudFront
IAM
CloudWatch
Implement Infrastructure as Code (Terraform or CloudFormation).
Ensure cloud environments are secure, scalable, and cost-effective.

CI/CD & Source Control

Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines using tools such as:
GitHub Actions
GitLab CI/CD
Jenkins
Azure DevOps
Define Git branching, version control, release, and deployment strategies.
Support automated testing, build validation, and deployment processes.

Security & Compliance

Implement DevSecOps practices throughout the software lifecycle.
Ensure compliance with organisational security standards and regulatory requirements.
Perform vulnerability management and security reviews.

Monitoring & Support

Establish monitoring, logging, and alerting solutions.
Manage incident response and root cause analysis activities.
Continuously improve platform availability, performance, and resilience.




What experience you'll bring:

Significant experience in DevOps, Cloud Engineering, or Platform Engineering.
Experience in a leadership or technical lead role.
Strong hands-on experience with AWS Cloud Platform.
Expert knowledge of Git and modern source control practices.
Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines.
Experience with:
Terraform
CloudFormation
Docker
Jenkins/GitHub Actions/GitLab CI
Experience implementing monitoring and observability solutions.

Salesforce Experience

Proven experience supporting Salesforce development and release processes.
Experience with:
Salesforce DX (SFDX)
Salesforce CLI
Copado, Gearset, or AutoRABIT
Change Sets and deployment automation
Understanding of Salesforce architecture and integration patterns.

Leadership Skills

Strong stakeholder management and communication skills.
Experience managing technical teams and third-party suppliers.
Ability to drive continuous improvement and foster DevOps culture.
Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities.

Desirable Qualifications

AWS Certified Solutions Architect or DevOps Engineer.
Salesforce Administrator, Platform Developer, or Architect certification.
Agile/Scrum or SAFe certification.
