Quality Manager




Location: North Yorkshire, YO62
Salary: £50,000 + 10% Bonus
Hours: 37 hours per week | Monday–Friday | Flexi-time Available
Working Arrangement: Office/Site-Based




We’re recruiting a Quality Manager for an established engineering and manufacturing business operating within a highly technical, project-driven environment.




This is a hands-on leadership role, responsible for overseeing quality assurance and quality control across manufacturing, assembly, testing and final product delivery.




You’ll manage a quality team, maintain and improve the Quality Management System and work closely with Operations, Engineering, customers and suppliers to drive quality performance and continuous improvement.




Key Responsibilities:

Manage and continually improve the ISO 9001 Quality Management System
Lead internal and external audits, ensuring continued compliance
Oversee quality inspection, testing and final product acceptance
Manage non-conformances, root cause investigations and corrective actions
Drive supplier quality performance and support supplier audits
Lead, develop and support the Quality team
Work closely with customers on quality requirements, audits and technical issues
Implement continuous improvement initiatives across manufacturing operations




About You:

Proven Quality Management experience within an engineering or manufacturing environment
Strong knowledge of ISO 9001 and lead auditor training
Experience within machining, fabrication or multi-disciplinary manufacturing
Strong problem-solving, root cause analysis and continuous improvement skills
Previous experience managing quality teams and engaging with customers and suppliers
An engineering or technical qualification, or equivalent relevant experience




Experience within complex engineered products, project-based manufacturing or offshore-related industries would be advantageous.




The Package:

£50,000 basic salary
10% annual management bonus (company performance related)
25 days' holiday + 8 bank holidays
Private healthcare
5% salary sacrifice pension
Life assurance (2x basic salary)
37-hour working week with flexi-time
Early finish on Fridays
