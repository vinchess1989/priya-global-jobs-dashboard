An established payments technology organisation is seeking a Senior DevOps Engineer to support its platform engineering operations. This fully remote role is open to candidates based anywhere in the UK.

The Role


Take operational ownership of critical, customer-facing platform environments
Design, implement, and maintain robust infrastructure as code solutions
Build, enhance, and manage automated CI/CD pipelines to support efficient software delivery
Collaborate across platform engineering teams to maintain high availability, scalability, and performance


What You'll Need


Proven experience as a DevOps Engineer, Platform Engineer, or in a similar infrastructure-focused role
Strong hands-on expertise with AWS cloud services
Practical experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) principles and tooling
Solid background building and managing automated CI/CD pipelines


What's On Offer


Fully remote working arrangement from anywhere within the UK
Opportunity to work on critical, customer-facing platforms at a global scale
Collaborative and modern platform engineering environment
