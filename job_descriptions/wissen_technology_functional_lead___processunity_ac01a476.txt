About the job




The Wissen team continues to expand its footprint in the USA, Canada, UK, Australia, and India. More openings to come as we continue to grow the team!




Please read below for a brilliant career opportunity.




Role: Functional Lead – ProcessUnity / TPRM

Title: AVP/VP

Location: Montreal, QC

Experience: 8+ Years

Work type - Fulltime

Mode of Work: Onsite 3 days per week




We are seeking an experienced Functional Lead with strong expertise in Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM), non-financial risk platforms, and business process implementation. The ideal candidate will lead functional requirements gathering, solution design, stakeholder engagement, and end-to-end functional delivery, with proven hands-on proficiency in the ProcessUnity platform.

Key Responsibilities

Lead business requirements gathering, functional analysis, and solution design in collaboration with TPRM stakeholders.
Engage with business users, risk teams, compliance teams, and technology stakeholders to understand business needs and translate them into functional requirements.
Define and document functional specifications, business requirements, workflows, and acceptance criteria.
Own backlog refinement activities, including requirements prioritization, story elaboration, and alignment with business objectives.
Maintain end-to-end requirements traceability throughout the project lifecycle.
Drive functional solution design and ensure alignment with business processes, regulatory requirements, and organizational standards.
Lead the preparation and review of functional documentation, process flows, user guides, and supporting materials.
Develop user communications, training content, and knowledge-transfer materials to support business readiness and adoption.
Coordinate with technical teams, developers, testers, and business stakeholders to ensure successful functional implementation.
Act as the primary functional escalation point during testing, go-live, and post-implementation hypercare.
Support UAT planning, execution, defect resolution, and business sign-off.
Ensure smooth transition to business-as-usual operations following implementation.

Required Skills & Qualifications

8+ years of experience working as a Business Lead, Functional Lead, or Functional Consultant on third-party risk or non-financial risk management platforms.
Proven hands-on proficiency or certification in the ProcessUnity platform.
Strong understanding of Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM) processes and workflows.
Experience in requirements gathering, functional solution design, business process mapping, and acceptance criteria definition.
Strong experience in backlog refinement, requirements prioritization, and traceability management.
Ability to lead functional discussions and collaborate with business, compliance, risk, and technology stakeholders.
Experience preparing functional specifications, process documentation, user guides, and training materials.
Strong stakeholder management, communication, and presentation skills.
Experience supporting UAT, go-live, and hypercare activities.
