Help drive our high-performance culture and own a robust Quality Management function within our Maryborough Service Delivery Centre


At Downer we plan, create and sustain. Downer is the leading provider of integrated services in Australia and New Zealand. Through trusted relationships and world leading insight we work closely with our customers to design, build and sustain assets, infrastructure, and facilities.


The Opportunity

We have an opportunity for an experienced Quality Manager to join our Rail & Transit Systems team at the Maryborough Service Delivery Centre on the Fraser Coast.
This is a highly visible leadership role where you'll shape quality across a complex operational environment, influencing how we manage risk, improve performance and deliver safe, reliable outcomes for our customers.
Leading the site's quality function, you'll work closely with Production, Engineering, Projects, Procurement and Supply Chain, bringing quality insight into operational decisions and turning performance trends into meaningful improvements. You'll also develop the capability of your Quality team and build strong relationships with customers, suppliers and external stakeholders.
For a quality professional looking to broaden their leadership impact, the role offers exposure across operations, project delivery, continuous improvement and senior stakeholder engagement.

Key Responsibilities


Shape the quality approach across the facility, connecting assurance and control activities with safe, reliable and efficient delivery.
Influence operational and project decisions by identifying quality risks early and turning performance insights into practical action.
Partner across Engineering, Production, Projects, Procurement and Supply Chain to improve quality outcomes and reduce defects and rework.
Strengthen audit, assurance and management systems to support ISO 9001 compliance and continuous improvement.
Lead and develop the Quality team, creating accountability, capability and shared ownership of quality performance.


What You Bring


Demonstrated quality leadership experience within manufacturing, rail, automotive, heavy industry or another complex operational environment.
Strong knowledge of ISO 9001, Quality Management Systems and effective assurance and control practices.
Experience leading audits, quality investigations and improvement initiatives.
Strong analytical capability with the ability to turn quality data into clear priorities and recommendations.
A collaborative leadership style and the credibility to influence operational leaders, technical teams, customers and suppliers.
