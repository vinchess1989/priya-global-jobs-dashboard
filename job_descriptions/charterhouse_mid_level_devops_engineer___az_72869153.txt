Build, secure and optimise enterprise CI/CD platforms in a complex, business-critical environment.

We are looking for an experienced Senior DevOps Engineer to support the design, implementation and ongoing optimisation of enterprise CI/CD infrastructure within a highly regulated environment.

This is a hands-on engineering role, with a strong focus on Azure DevOps Server, Jenkins, JFrog Artifactory and Xray, platform automation, security and operational resilience.

Experience supporting banking, payments or other business-critical platforms will be highly regarded.

Key Responsibilities


Design, configure and maintain secure CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps Server, Jenkins and JFrog.
Develop YAML pipelines, reusable templates and automated deployment processes.
Manage Artifactory repositories, access controls, artifact promotion and migration.
Automate platform provisioning, monitoring, configuration and remediation.
Troubleshoot CI/CD, authentication, infrastructure and deployment issues.
Support platform upgrades, disaster recovery, high availability and production releases.
Implement DevSecOps practices, vulnerability scanning and security controls.
Produce technical documentation, implementation plans and operational runbooks.
Skills & Experience

8+ years in IT, including at least 5 years in DevOps or CI/CD engineering.
Strong hands-on expertise in Azure DevOps Server, Jenkins and YAML pipelines.
Practical experience with JFrog Artifactory and Xray, including repository administration and artifact management.
Strong scripting skills using PowerShell, Python or shell.
Experience across Windows/Linux environments, Git, databases and networking.
Solid understanding of DevSecOps, access management and infrastructure security.
Experience with high availability, disaster recovery and production support.
Exposure to banking, payments or regulated enterprise environments.
Nice to have: Docker, Maven, NuGet, npm, REST APIs and observability tools such as Splunk, Grafana or Prometheus.

Interested?
If you have strong enterprise CI/CD engineering experience and enjoy working on complex, business-critical platforms, I'd love to hear from you.
