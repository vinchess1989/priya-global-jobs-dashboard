DevOps Engineer x 2 - Azure - Up to £55k - New

2 days a week in Cardiff

Keywords: Azure, CI/CD automation, Git, PowerShell, Terraform, Bicep, Docker, DataDog, App Insights.




A leading organisation in central Cardiff requires a Platform / DevOps Engineer to join its growing delivery team. Suitable candidates will have the following:




A strong knowledge of DevOps practices and CI/CD automation across hybrid environments.
Experience of technologies such as Git, PowerShell/Bash, and infrastructure automation (e. g. Terraform, Bicep).
Experience with containerisation (e. g. Docker) and microservices-based delivery.
Familiarity with both cloud and on-premise infrastructure models.
Working knowledge of secure SDLC practices and non-functional testing requirements (e. g. resilience, availability, performance, security).
Experience with monitoring, logging, and observability tooling (e. g. Datadog, App Insights).
Knowledge of Agile principles and Dev Ops practices.




The successfu candidate will be comfortable performing the following:




Designing and delivering DevOps and environment automation solutions across development and test lifecycles.
Building and evolving CI/CD pipelines using Azure, including deployment automation and infrastructure-as-code.
Supporting both cloud and on-premise delivery, reducing friction for teams through reusable platform components.
Enabling non-functional testing (e. g. security, performance) through platform capabilities and automation.
Promoting DevOps best practices and mentoring teams in platform capabilities, security, and delivery metrics.




Please send CV for an informal chat and full job description. Excellent opportunity to join a market leader!




Please note, there is a requirement to be on siite two days a week in Cardiff.
