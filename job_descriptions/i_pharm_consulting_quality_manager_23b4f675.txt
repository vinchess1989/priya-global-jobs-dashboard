About the Company


An established Australian medical device company is seeking a hands-on Quality Manager to lead its Quality function and support its operations. Profitable and self-funded, the business combines the agility of a small, engineering-led team with an established quality management system and a strong audit history. Key decisions and QMS ownership sit in Sydney, with manufacturing outsourced offshore and products supplied to international markets.

About the Role


Reporting directly to the business leader, you will take ownership of the Quality Management System and work closely with engineering, regulatory and manufacturing partners to maintain compliance and support business priorities.

This is a practical, hands-on position suited to someone who enjoys broad responsibility, direct access to decision-makers and working across functions.

The business is recruiting a permanent Quality Manager or Immediate Quality Manager Contractor.

This role is a hybrid working model with option of mostly remote work.

Responsibilities

Own, maintain and continually improve the Quality Management System in line with ISO 13485 and applicable international medical device requirements.
Lead preparation for external audits and inspections, coordinate responses and ensure timely completion of follow-up actions.
Manage CAPA, nonconformances and related investigations, ensuring effective corrective actions.
Oversee quality activities associated with outsourced manufacturing and work collaboratively with offshore manufacturing partners.
Partner with engineering and regulatory colleagues to ensure quality requirements are embedded in day-to-day activities.
Maintain accurate, current and effective quality documentation.
Provide practical guidance on quality risks, compliance priorities and improvement opportunities.
Coordinate contributions from colleagues supporting Quality and promote accountability across the business.
Required Skills

Strong experience in medical device quality assurance and practical ownership of a QMS.
A sound working knowledge of ISO 13485, with experience supporting FDA requirements, MDSAP and EU MDR compliance.
Hands-on experience managing audits, CAPA, nonconformances and quality investigations.
The ability to work effectively with outsourced manufacturers and cross-functional stakeholders.
A pragmatic approach to problem-solving, balancing compliance requirements with operational needs.
Strong communication skills and the confidence to work directly with business leadership.
The flexibility to manage varied responsibilities within a small, collaborative team.
Applications are welcomed from experienced Quality Managers and strong Senior Quality Assurance professionals or Quality Leads ready to step into broader ownership.
Pay range and compensation package

Salary: $160,000–$180,000 plus superannuation, depending on experience
Contract rate: Negotiable
Ownership of an established Quality function within a financially secure Australian medical device business.
Market leading remuneration package
Direct access to leadership and the opportunity to influence decisions.
Broad exposure to international markets and medical device quality requirements.
Flexible working hours and working-from-home arrangements, with Sydney office attendance agreed according to business needs.
An immediate-start six-month contract option, with rate negotiated according to experience and scope.
