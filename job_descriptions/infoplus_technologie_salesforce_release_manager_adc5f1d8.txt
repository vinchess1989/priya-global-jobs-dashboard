Key Responsibilities:

Manage end-to-end release cycles (planning, scheduling, and deployment) using Copado.
Coordinate with development, QA, and admin teams to resolve deployment conflicts.
Configure and maintain Copado pipelines, CI/CD jobs, and environment strategies.
Oversee code merges, backups, and sandbox refreshes.
Troubleshoot deployment errors and manage rollback procedures.

Requirements:

3+ years of Salesforce release management experience.
Hands-on expertise with Copado (certification is a plus).
Strong knowledge of Salesforce DevOps, Git, and CI/CD pipelines.
Experience managing complex deployments across multiple sandboxes and production environments.
