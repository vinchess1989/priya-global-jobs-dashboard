Senior Platform Engineer (Software Consultant)

Build the platforms and automation that power Payments

Great platform engineering is often invisible to customers, but it's essential to everything they do. Every reliable payment, every seamless deployment and every resilient technology service depends on strong engineering foundations.

We're looking for a Senior Platform Engineer to help build those foundations. This is a hands-on role focused on automation, cloud infrastructure, platform tooling and operational excellence within ASB's Payments technology environment.

You'll join a team on a journey towards greater automation and modernisation. There is significant opportunity to implement new capabilities, improve how infrastructure is managed and help move complex operational processes towards a more scalable, automated future.

About The Team

You'll join Shield Squad, a platform engineering team within ASB's Payments technology function. The team provides the infrastructure, tooling and operational services that support payment platforms across the organisation.

The squad is responsible for areas including cloud infrastructure, container platforms, deployment pipelines, monitoring, observability and operational reliability. As Payments continues to evolve, the team is investing heavily in automation, platform modernisation and cloud capabilities across Azure and AWS environments.

About The Role

This is a hands-on engineering role focused on designing, building and improving platform capabilities. You'll work alongside experienced engineers to deliver automation solutions, improve deployment pipelines and help create a more resilient and efficient technology ecosystem.

A key focus will be enabling infrastructure automation and reducing manual operational effort through modern engineering practices and tooling. You'll contribute directly to platform initiatives that improve reliability, scalability and operational efficiency across Payments.

Key Responsibilities


Build and maintain Infrastructure as Code solutions using Terraform
Deliver automation initiatives that improve platform operations and reliability
Develop and enhance CI/CD pipelines and deployment tooling
Support cloud infrastructure across Azure and AWS environments
Work with container platforms and supporting platform technologies
Improve infrastructure lifecycle management through automation
Contribute to resilience, monitoring and observability initiatives
Support incident response and operational improvement activities
Partner with engineers across the Payments technology function to deliver platform capabilities
Share knowledge and mentor less experienced engineers where appropriate


About You

You're a hands-on engineer who enjoys building solutions and seeing the impact of your work. You have strong platform engineering foundations, enjoy solving technical problems and are motivated by opportunities to automate, improve and modernise technology environments.

You'll be excited by the opportunity to work across cloud infrastructure, automation, containers and platform tooling while contributing to meaningful engineering outcomes that support critical banking services.

Key Skills & Experience


Proven experience as a Platform Engineer, DevOps Engineer, Site Reliability Engineer or similar
Strong hands-on experience with Terraform and Infrastructure as Code
Experience building automation solutions within cloud or infrastructure environments
Solid understanding of CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
Experience working with Azure cloud services
Strong scripting and troubleshooting capability
Experience supporting container-based platforms and modern infrastructure environments
Knowledge of operational excellence, monitoring and observability practices
Strong collaboration and problem-solving skills
Exposure to AWS cloud environments
Experience with Ansible or infrastructure automation tools
Experience supporting highly available production environments
Financial services or regulated industry experience beneficial


Working for ASB


Hybrid working with flexibility built into how we work
A strong focus on learning, growth and career development
Access to banking, insurance and lifestyle benefits
Market-leading parental leave and employee support programmes
Health, wellbeing and mental health initiatives
An inclusive and supportive culture where diverse perspectives are valued
Opportunities to work with modern cloud, automation and platform technologies


Your Opportunity

If you enjoy building modern platform capabilities, automating complex processes and solving meaningful engineering challenges, this is an opportunity to make a genuine impact while growing your platform engineering career within one of New Zealand's leading technology environments.
