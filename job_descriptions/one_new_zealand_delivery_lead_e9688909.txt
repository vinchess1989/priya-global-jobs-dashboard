Ko tō tūranga – your role

As the Delivery Lead your role is to lead the end-to-end delivery of products, services and initiatives, aligning cross-functional teams to achieve agreed outcomes with efficiency, quality and a strong customer focus. You’ll coordinate delivery cycles, priorities, risks and dependencies across business and technology teams, working as a Tribe, Squad or Initiative Delivery Lead depending on the needs of the work.

Ko Tō Mahi – What You’ll Do


Lead delivery of initiatives and team commitments within agreed scope, timelines, budgets and quality standards.
Develop and maintain delivery plans, milestones, dependencies, resources, testing and deployment readiness.
Bring business, technology, vendors and operations together to align priorities and remove blockers.
Facilitate Agile ceremonies including stand-ups, sprint planning, reviews and retrospectives where required.
Balance operational and project priorities with team capacity and stakeholder expectations.
Monitor progress, identify risks early, challenge assumptions and drive timely decisions.
Build strong stakeholder relationships, provide clear delivery reporting and resolve escalations.
Use delivery metrics to improve processes, tools and team performance.
Manage delivery budgets, forecasts, resource costs and financial risks within agreed delegations.
Coordinate vendor purchase orders and track expenditure, commitments and timesheets.
Support successful implementation and transition of delivered outcomes into business as usual.


Na Tōu Rourou - What You’ll Bring


At least four years’ product development or project experience, including two years leading teams.
A strong track record of delivery in an enterprise environment and focus on customer outcomes.
Experience choosing and applying Lean, Kanban, Scrum or phased delivery approaches.
Strength in team coaching, facilitation, quarterly planning and project management.
Proven ability to build relationships with stakeholders, vendors and leaders.
Confidence navigating complex issues, resolving tension and managing competing priorities.
An organised, detail-focused approach that enables teams without micromanaging.
Situational leadership skills and a commitment to continuous improvement and learning.
