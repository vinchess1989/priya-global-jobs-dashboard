About TMGM

TMGM is a global financial services provider at the forefront of online trading and investment solutions. We are proud to be the Official Regional Online Forex and Trading Partner of Chelsea FC in the Asia-Pacific region.

With a strong global presence and offices across four major Australian cities—Sydney, Melbourne, Adelaide, and Canberra—TMGM continues to expand its operations and deliver high-quality services to clients worldwide.

About the role

We are seeking an experienced DevOps Engineer to join our Trading Solutions Team in Sydney. The successful candidate will be responsible for designing, developing and maintaining cloud-based infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines and automated deployment solutions supporting our FX and CFD trading operations.

Working closely with software engineering and trading technology teams, this role will focus on automating software delivery, managing cloud environments, monitoring system performance, and ensuring the security, availability, stability and reliability of our production trading environments.

Responsibility

Collaborate with software engineering, quantitative development and trading technology teams to design, build, integrate and deploy cloud-based applications supporting the company's FX and CFD trading operations, coordinating workflows between teams and automating hand-offs to improve delivery efficiency and system availability, stability and reliability.
Design, build and continuously improve the company's continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) pipelines, automating the build, testing, deployment and upgrade of trading software and platforms and ensuring compatibility with existing infrastructure and operational requirements.
Configure, document and deploy cloud operating environments by developing and maintaining infrastructure-as-code, automated provisioning and configuration management tooling, with an emphasis on efficiency, consistency, scalability and security.
Develop and maintain monitoring, logging, alerting and observability solutions for critical trading infrastructure and applications, collecting and analysing operational metrics such as system performance, availability, network connectivity and latency to identify and resolve technical issues affecting production trading environments.
Implement and automate security controls across the delivery pipeline and cloud environments, such as access management, secrets management, vulnerability scanning and automated security patching, and identify and remediate vulnerabilities throughout the software lifecycle in accordance with the company's governance processes, security policies and industry best practice.
Provide technical support to software engineering teams in designing, testing and debugging software solutions, including diagnosing and correcting build, integration, configuration and environment-related defects within established testing and release standards.
Safeguard the stability, performance and security of the trading environment and critical trading infrastructure by identifying and mitigating risks across the software delivery lifecycle, investigating production incidents and anomalies, conducting root cause analysis and implementing corrective and preventive measures.
Maintain technical documentation, environment configuration records, deployment runbooks and operational procedures, and recommend improvements to DevOps tooling, cloud services and platform upgrades to support system deployments, maintenance and infrastructure improvements.

Requirements

Bachelor's degree or higher in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering (with a major in computing) or a related discipline.
At least 2 years of relevant experience in DevOps engineering or a similar role, including CI/CD, cloud automation and system deployment.
Strong knowledge of cloud platforms, operating systems, network infrastructure and system security.
Experience in infrastructure automation, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), configuration management, CI/CD pipelines, system monitoring and troubleshooting.
Knowledge of cloud security practices, access management, vulnerability management and automated security controls.
Strong analytical, problem-solving and communication skills.
Experience in financial services or trading technology environments will be highly regarded.

Benefits

Competitive salary and performance-based bonuses.
Opportunities for career growth and development within a global company.
Collaborative and inclusive work environment with exposure to cross-region operations.
Health and wellness benefits.
Flexible working arrangements.
