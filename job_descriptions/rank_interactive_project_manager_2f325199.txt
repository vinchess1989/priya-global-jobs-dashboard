Rank Interactive, the digital arm of The Rank Group, stands as a leading global digital gaming company fuelled by top-tier talent and technology. Recognised through iconic brands, our products and our people, we bring entertainment to millions of people worldwide on various devices daily.

Operating with a multi-branded strategy, we offer online entertainment, bingo and slot gaming, along with a social gaming mobile experience, using a mix of proprietary and licensed software.

With offices in the UK, Gibraltar, Mauritius and South Africa while being part of a Rank Group with approximately 8,000 employees globally; we unite people and entertainment across diverse digital channels.

Joining our high-performing and inclusive culture, you'll instantly become part of a team supporting our customer-facing initiatives.

We are seeking an experienced and results-driven Project Manager to join our dynamic team in the gambling sector. The Project Manager is responsible for the day-to-day management and coordination of projects, ensuring delivery is well planned, well controlled and communicated effectively across stakeholders. The role supports predictable delivery by managing scope, schedules, risks, dependencies, resources, budget tracking and business readiness activities in line with team standards and governance

Key Responsibilities


Deliver assigned projects end-to-end on a day-to-day basis, ensuring scope, schedule, resources, risks and dependencies are effectively managed.
Manage and control project scope, ensuring requirements are clearly understood, documented and changes are appropriately assessed and governed.
Develop, maintain and track project plans and milestones, proactively identifying risks to timelines and driving follow-up actions to keep delivery on track.
Ensure delivery is supported by appropriate quality controls, documentation, testing coordination and readiness activities.
Provide clear, concise and timely communication to stakeholders, building strong relationships across delivery teams and business functions.
Coordinate project resources and inputs across cross-functional teams, highlighting resourcing constraints and supporting prioritisation where needed.
Use agreed project management methods, tools and governance processes consistently, including RAID management, reporting, actions and decision logs.
Proactively identify and manage project risks, issues and dependencies, ensuring mitigation plans and escalation paths are clear.
Monitor project costs and forecasts, supporting financial control and raising any budget concerns promptly.
Support business readiness and change activity to help ensure successful adoption of project outcomes into operational use.
Capture lessons learned and contribute to continuous improvement of team standards, templates and delivery practices.


Requirements


At least 2 years’ experience managing multidisciplinary software development projects in fast-paced, Agile environments.
Demonstrated ability to lead and coordinate multiple engineering teams, including software, hardware, and operations disciplines.
Knowledge of project management methodologies, tools (e.g., Jira, Confluence, MS Project), and best practices.
Experience within the gambling or gaming industry is desirable.
Excellent communication, interpersonal, and leadership skills.
Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities, with a focus on achieving results.


Preferred Qualifications


Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Product Management, or a related field desirable
Familiarity with industry-specific regulations, compliance standards, and responsible gambling principles desirable
PMP, PRINCE2, or Agile certifications are a plus
