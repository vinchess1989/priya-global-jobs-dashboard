We are searching for the best talent for a Transcend: Innovative Medicine (IM) Deployment Manager!

This hybrid position will be located in Cork, Ireland. Alternate hybrid locations may be considered in other Innovative Medicine Supply Chain (IMSC) hubs such as Latina, Italy, and/or Leiden, Netherlands.

Transcend is a global business transformation program that will modernize our foundational transactional processes and harmonize them into one “clean” ERP as a standardized platform for growth and efficiency gains.

This program will simplify the Pharmaceutical ERP landscape from 7 to 1, standardizing processes to have a cost-effective, fit-for-purpose digital backbone that will enable us to support the Pharmaceutical business with agility.

Additionally, the program will remediate the end-of-life system exposure that we risk towards the end of this decade.

The IM Transcend Deployment Manager is responsible for leading the planning, coordination, and execution of global ERP deployment releases across multiple functional workstreams. They partner with business, technology, testing, cutover, hypercare, change management, and program leadership teams to ensure successful deployments that meet program standards, timelines, quality requirements, and governance expectations. The Deployment Manager drives deployment readiness, manages risks and dependencies, coordinates release activities, and supports execution across the full deployment lifecycle.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Deployment Planning & Execution


Lead deployment execution across global ERP releases and functional workstreams, developing and managing integrated deployment plans, KPIs, deliverables, and readiness activities.
Monitor deployment progress, identify risks and issues, and drive resolution to ensure successful delivery against program objectives and timelines.


Governance & Readiness


Support deployment governance, stage-gate reviews, and release readiness assessments while ensuring adherence to Transcend methodologies, standards, and operating procedures.
Coordinate status reporting, executive communications, and leadership updates, maintaining transparency of risks, issues, assumptions, and dependencies.


Cross-Functional Leadership


Partner with business, technology, testing, compliance, change management, training, cutover, and hypercare teams to ensure coordinated deployment execution.
Influence stakeholders and facilitate decision-making across highly matrixed global teams to drive alignment and achieve deployment objectives.


Risk & Dependency Management


Identify, assess, and mitigate deployment risks and cross-functional dependencies, raising critical issues and driving timely resolution.
Maintain executive-ready reporting and deployment health metrics to support informed decision-making and effective governance.


Continuous Improvement


Promote standardization, simplification, and adoption of deployment best practices across releases and workstreams.
Drive continuous improvement initiatives that enhance deployment efficiency, scalability, operational excellence, and future release success.


Other


Develop and document Contracting, Pricing, and Billing user requirements with acceptance criteria.
Demonstrate ability to work independently, prioritize, and effectively meet deadlines with little supervision.
Map Contracting, Pricing, and Billing business processes with a focus on testing, reporting, and documenting findings.
Provide Contracting, Pricing, and Billing support on best practices implementation and rollout to all Regions.
Visually design communication content based on best practices to efficiently showcase Contracting and Pricing Customer Service team successes and ensure consistent messaging in the communication campaigns.
