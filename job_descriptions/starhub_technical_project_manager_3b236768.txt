We are seeking a Technical Project Manager with strong experience in agile delivery and backend engineering environments to lead the development and operational rollout of StarHub’s core digital platforms.

You will work cross-functionally with Domain Owners (DOs), Engineering Managers (EMs), DevOps teams, QA, Solution Designers (SDs), and Release Program Managers (RPMs) to drive predictable, high-quality delivery of back-end services that support consumer and enterprise customer journeys.

Your ability to manage modern cloud-native development cycles — across CI/CD pipelines, containerized microservices, and API ecosystems — will be critical to enabling secure, scalable, and resilient platforms that power StarHub’s digital evolution.

Key Responsibilities Delivery & Agile Management


Drive agile ceremonies (sprint planning, stand-ups, retrospectives) and maintain team rhythm and cadence.
Manage backlog hygiene, ensuring stories are well-defined, estimated, and prioritized.
Plan and execute phased delivery strategies, ensuring release readiness through Blue/Green deployments, canary releases, and feature flagging.
Track delivery health through velocity, throughput, and scope stability metrics.
Identify and resolve blockers, risks, and cross-domain dependencies early.
Maintain clear delivery dashboards and status reporting to senior stakeholders.



Technical Coordination & Planning


Partner with Domain Owners and Tech Leads to align on architecture, delivery scope, and capacity planning.
Coordinate with DevOps and QA/QE teams to ensure readiness for integration, testing, and release.
Support implementation of continuous delivery and observability practices across teams.
Define, Monitor and report on key delivery KPIs, including deployment frequency, Mean Time To Recovery, defect Leakage rate, change failure rate ensuring all releases meet defined Service Level Objectives and performance baselines.
Participate in sprint demos and ensure acceptance criteria are met for each increment.



Stakeholder & Cross-Domain Collaboration


Work with Journey Solution Designers (SDs) and Release Program Managers (RPMs) to align sprint deliverables to journey-level milestones.
Collaborate with Journey Owners (JOs) and business stakeholders to clarify priorities and expected outcomes.
Communicate risks, decisions, and delivery trade-offs clearly across key stakeholders.



Continuous Improvement & Governance


Drive adoption of agile and DevOps best practices across DXP teams.
Analyze delivery metrics to identify improvement opportunities in velocity, predictability, and quality.
Support incident RCA reviews and ensure learnings are converted into actionable backlog improvements.
Contribute to standardized delivery governance, templates, and documentation across domains.



Requirements & Qualifications Education & Certifications


Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Information Systems, or related field.
Agile, PMP, or DevOps certifications (e.g., CSM, SAFe, PMI-ACP) preferred.



Experience


8+ years of experience in software delivery or digital product management, with at least 3 years in a technical project management, delivery leador scrum master capacity.
Demonstrated experience with CI/CD pipelines, cloud-native architecture (e.g., Kubernetes, EKS), and backend service delivery
Strong understanding of API management, event-driven architecture, observability tooling and modern deployment strategies (Blue/Green, Canary, Feature Flags).
Strong familiarity with tools such as Jira, Confluence, GitLab, Jenkins, Monday.com, Splunk.
Experience in telecommunications, digital platform delivery, or large-scale integration programs is an advantage.



Skills


Deep understanding of agile, DevOps, and continuous delivery practices.
Ability to translate technical complexity into clear, actionable plans.
Technical fluency with backend engineering patterns and delivery pipelines
Exceptional communication, facilitation, and stakeholder management skills.
Analytical and detail-oriented mindset with a bias for execution.
Highly organized, proactive, and resilient in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.
Passion for continuous improvement and operational excellence.
