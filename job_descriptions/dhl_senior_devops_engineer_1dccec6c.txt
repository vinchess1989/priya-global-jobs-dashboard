Your IT Future, Delivered

DevOps Engineer - GCP

With a global team of 6000+ IT professionals, DHL IT Services connects people and keeps the global economy running by continuously innovating and creating sustainable digital solutions. We work beyond global borders and push boundaries across all dimensions of logistics. You can leave your mark shaping the technology backbone of the biggest logistics company of the world. Our offices in Cyberjaya, Prague, and Chennai have earned #GreatPlaceToWork certification, reflecting our commitment to exceptional employee experiences.

Digitalization. Simply delivered.

About the Project - Diana: Step into Diana, DHL’s central hub for shipment data. Named after the goddess of crossroads, Diana is where the many roads of shipment data become one. It collects shipment data from multiple business units in different formats, transforms it into one common model, and serves it back through REST APIs in real time whenever a 2D Data Matrix code is scanned.

#DHL #DHLITServices #GreatPlace #DigitalPlatforms #API #Diana

Ready to embark on the journey? Here’s what we are looking for:

As a DevOps Engineer, you will build, run and improve the cloud infrastructure behind Diana, our API integration platform, on Google Cloud. You will play a key role in automating our deployments with Terraform and CI/CD pipelines, running workloads on Kubernetes, and keeping our services reliable through proactive monitoring. We are looking for a hands-on engineer who is passionate about learning new technologies, building new projects, and going the extra mile to ensure project success.

You are a communication & technology aficionado; therefore, you have a good understanding of related technologies and communication styles. You can work independently, prioritize and organize your tasks under time and workload pressure. Working in a multinational environment, you can expect cross-region collaboration with teams around the globe, thus being advanced in spoken and written English will be certainly useful.

Technical competencies



3+ years of experience with Google Cloud Platform
2+ years of experience with Kubernetes (GKE preferred)
Experience with Infrastructure as Code (Terraform)
Proficiency in CI/CD and version control systems (Jenkins, GitHub Actions, Argo CD, Git)
Experience with monitoring tools (Prometheus, Grafana, Zabbix)
Experience with NoSQL databases such as MongoDB and Firestore



Soft Skills



Excellent verbal and written English
Experience working in an Agile framework (Scrum preferred)
Interest in working in a multicultural, dynamic environment
Passion for learning new things and building new projects



Nice to have



Experience with Apigee or other API management tools
Experience with Helm
Knowledge of MySQL
Scripting skills (Bash, Python)



An array of benefits for you:



Annual Leave – 42 days off apart from Public / National Holidays
Medical Insurance - Self + Spouse + 2 children. An option to opt for Voluntary Parental Insurance (Parents / Parent -in-laws) at a nominal premium covering preexisting diseases
In House training programs - professional and technical training certifications
Flexible Work options



Great Team

Hello! I'm Ana. I joined DHL in 2021, starting in the Service Desk team, where I quickly advanced to a Team Leader position and remained for almost a year and a half. In 2024, I embraced a new challenge by joining the API department in Digital Solutions as a Scrum Master, and since 2025, I had the exciting opportunity to take on a managerial role, leading one of our teams.

Leadership has always been at the heart of what I do. I love bringing people together, fostering collaboration, and creating an environment where teams can thrive. At DHL, I have the privilege of working with an incredibly talented group of API developers and DevOps engineers, all dedicated to shaping the future of digital connectivity.

Our digital landscape is constantly evolving, and our team plays a key role in shaping how developers interact with our APIs. My goal is to nurture an environment where innovation thrives, challenges are tackled efficiently, and we continuously improve the way we work.

If you're into APIs, digital transformation, or just love solving complex problems, let’s connect—there’s plenty of exciting work ahead!
