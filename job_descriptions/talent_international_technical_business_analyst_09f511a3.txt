We are seeking an experienced Technical Business Analyst to join a major enterprise transformation initiative focused on establishing a new digital orchestration platform.

This 12-month contract will see you working across business and technology teams to define the requirements for an enterprise-wide orchestration capability, enabling improved system integration, workflow automation and streamlined digital service delivery.

You will play a key role in translating complex business and technical needs into clear requirements, collaborating with architects, delivery teams and stakeholders to support the design and implementation of scalable technology solutions.

Key Responsibilities

Engage with business, technical and operational stakeholders to gather, analyse and document business, functional and technical requirements.
Facilitate workshops to map current and future-state processes, workflows and system interactions.
Analyse APIs, interfaces, data flows and system integrations to define orchestration and automation requirements.
Identify opportunities to streamline business processes and improve interoperability across enterprise platforms.
Work closely with Enterprise and Solution Architects to support solution design, technical assessments and integration approaches.
Develop detailed process maps, user stories, acceptance criteria and technical documentation.
Identify system dependencies, constraints and non-functional requirements, including security, performance and resilience.
Support Agile delivery teams throughout implementation, ensuring requirements are clearly understood and traceable.

Skills & Experience
To be successful in this role, you will bring:

Minimum 5+ years’ experience as a Technical Business Analyst, Senior Business Analyst or Solution Analyst within complex technology environments.
Strong experience delivering integration, orchestration, workflow automation or enterprise digital transformation initiatives.
Demonstrated expertise in analysing APIs, system interfaces, integrations and data flows.
Proven ability to translate complex technical concepts into clear business and technical requirements.
Strong experience facilitating stakeholder workshops and documenting current and future-state processes.
Experience developing functional specifications, user stories, acceptance criteria and integration documentation.
Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills.
Exposure to Azure Integration Services, Logic Apps or similar integration technologies highly regarded

Interested?
If you’re an experienced Technical Business Analyst with strong integration and API experience, we’d love to hear from you.
