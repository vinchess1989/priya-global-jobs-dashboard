At IAG, we’re ready for you. We're the largest general insurance group in Australia and New Zealand. Our family of brands turns the IAG purpose of making your world a safer place into action by helping everyday Aussies and New Zealanders, supporting their ambitions and making insurance accessible.

Lead the next chapter of engineering delivery and help shape how AI-enabled technology teams create value. As RIA Digital Experience Acceleration’s Technology Delivery Manager, you'll establish and lead a newly created function focused on uplifting technical leadership, engineering quality, resilience and governance. You'll work across modern and legacy technology environments, partnering with engineers, architects and delivery teams to build stronger engineering practices and enable smarter ways of working. This is a rare opportunity to influence how technology is delivered at scale, while helping teams confidently navigate the shift towards a more AI-enabled and agentic future. This is a permanent full-time role based in Gadigal Country (Darling Park 2, Sydney), Naarm (Melbourne) or Meanjin (Brisbane).

What You'll Do


Lead technical direction across engineering teams, translating strategic priorities into clear roadmaps, delivery plans and sustainable engineering practices.
Drive the adoption of AI-enabled and agentic engineering capabilities across the software delivery lifecycle, embedding AI agents and automation into analysis, design, development, testing, deployment and operational workflows to improve speed, quality and engineering productivity.
Establish reusable agentic patterns, guardrails and ways of working that enable engineering teams to safely scale AI-assisted and increasingly autonomous delivery, with appropriate human oversight and governance.
Build capability by coaching engineers, strengthening technical leadership and creating an environment where teams can do their best work.
Establish engineering standards, governance frameworks and reusable patterns that improve quality, consistency and long-term maintainability.
Partner with architecture, cyber, platform and delivery teams to shape solutions and support successful outcomes from concept to production.
Oversee technical risk, production readiness and operational resilience, ensuring solutions are secure, reliable and fit for purpose.
Drive continuous improvement by using insights, metrics and delivery outcomes to enhance engineering performance and AI adoption.


What You'll Bring


Experience leading engineering teams through technology transformation and organisational change.
Strong technical leadership with the ability to influence across multiple teams.
Sound judgement balancing customer outcomes, delivery pace, quality and risk.
Exposure to AI-enabled engineering, agentic delivery or modern software development practices.
Confidence working across both established and evolving technology environments.
Ability to simplify complex technical concepts for a broad range of stakeholders.
A collaborative leadership style that supports growth, learning and high performance.


What We Offer


Boosted superannuation with 13% as standard.
20 days annual leave + 5 days MyLeave (extra leave for what’s important to you).
Up to 50% off personal insurance, including home and motor insurance.
Partner discounts on private health insurance, tech & appliances, and many more.
Access to LinkedIn Learning, the award-winning IAG Academy, study assistance and secondment opportunities.
