At AROA, our people come to work every day with passion and ideas that fuel our business and go home knowing they have made a real difference in the lives of others.

AROA, which is Maori for ‘understanding’, is proudly New Zealand born and based. We develop, manufacture, and export innovative wound care products, improving the lives of millions around the world by unlocking extraordinary outcomes for our patients. Our mission is to be the global leader in regenerative healing, making our products and services accessible to everybody.




About the role:

We are seeking a motivated QA Technical Engineer to oversee quality assurance across manufacturing and project activities. In this role, you will provide essential technical quality oversight for validation, equipment qualification, and risk management. Other key responsibilities include:

Provide quality oversight and approval of validation activities and documentation.
Actively engage with production manufacturing processes, equipment, and personnel to ensure quality standards are upheld throughout design transfer and manufacturing.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams and effectively support multiple projects, balancing priorities to meet deadlines.
Serve as a point of contact for QA technical issues, drive root cause analysis, and communicate quality concerns to cross-functional teams.
Contribute to AROA’s continuous improvement efforts by identifying areas for improvement in product quality and safety




What We’re Looking For:

Bachelor’s degree in science or engineering
Previous experience in a similar Quality role in a regulated industry
Experience with or interest in computer system/software validation is a plus
Good understanding of Quality Management Systems and cGMP is essential
Some previous working knowledge of ISO 9001 or ISO 13485 and 21 CFR Part 820
Experience in process validation or equipment qualification is recommended
Proficiency in technical writing and reviewing documentation for compliance and clarity.
