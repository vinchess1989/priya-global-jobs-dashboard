He whakamārama mō te tūranga mahi | About the role

We’re looking for an experienced Senior Technical Business Analyst to join our Digital team and play a key role in delivering complex technology change across Watercare.

As our Senior Technical Business Analyst, you’ll work across the business, architecture, engineering, platforms, testing and delivery teams to turn business needs into clear, practical and delivery-ready technical requirements.

You’ll be involved in complex initiatives spanning multiple applications, platforms, integrations and vendors. You’ll bring clarity to technical complexity, identify dependencies and risks early, and make sure requirements are complete, traceable and testable.

You’ll also help lift our technical analysis capability, providing guidance, peer review and coaching to other analysts while helping us continually improve how we deliver technical analysis across Watercare.

In This Role, You’ll


Lead technical analysis across complex and high-impact digital initiatives
Translate business requirements into detailed technical requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria
Define integration, interface, API, data and non-functional requirements
Map system interactions, information flows, dependencies and technical processes
Identify and communicate technical risks, assumptions, constraints and dependencies
Work closely with architects, engineers, platform teams, testers and delivery teams
Facilitate workshops with technical and non-technical stakeholders
Maintain clear requirements, documentation and traceability using Jira and Confluence
Lead peer reviews and help maintain high standards across technical analysis
Coach and mentor other analysts and contribute to the development of our wider BA capability


Ngā pūkenga e rapu nei mātou | About you

You’ll be an experienced Senior Technical Business Analyst who is comfortable operating in complex technology environments. You can get into the technical detail when needed but, just as importantly, you know how to make that complexity clear for both business stakeholders and delivery teams.

We’re Looking For Someone With


Significant experience across business, systems or technical analysis
Strong experience translating business requirements and solution direction into technical requirements
Strong understanding of systems, integrations, APIs, interfaces and data flows
Excellent requirements analysis, modelling, documentation and traceability skills
Experience working closely with architecture, engineering and technical delivery teams
Confidence working across multiple applications, platforms and technology domains
Strong visual modelling skills across systems, processes, information flows and dependencies
Excellent stakeholder management and workshop facilitation skills
The ability to navigate ambiguity and turn complex problems into clear requirements and outcomes
Experience leading complex analysis activities and mentoring or supporting other analysts
Strong communication skills and the ability to translate between technical and non-technical audiences


Experience working in complex enterprise technology environments, across Agile and structured delivery approaches, and with third-party technology vendors would be advantageous.

If you’re a strong technical BA who enjoys getting into the detail, connecting the dots across complex systems and helping teams turn requirements into successful technology change, we’d love to hear from you.
