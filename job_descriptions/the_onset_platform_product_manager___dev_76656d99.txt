Platform Product Manager – Developer Experience

Shape a new developer platform for a healthcare organisation. Own the roadmap, solve engineering friction and drive adoption.


First product hire in a new Developer Experience platform team.
Own platform strategy, discovery and adoption across engineering teams.
Help engineers deliver faster, with security and compliance built in.


Build a platform engineers choose to use.

Our client, an established healthcare organisation undertaking a major technology modernisation, is building a Developer Experience platform to do exactly that. The ambition is to give engineering teams clear, pre-approved, self-service paths from code to production, with security, compliance and reliability built into the process. As the first product appointment in the new platform team, you’ll shape what gets built, which problems get solved first and how the platform proves its value.

The opportunity

Reporting to the Head of Digital Enginering, you’ll own the platform vision, roadmap and backlog. Your customers are internal engineering teams, and understanding how they actually work will be central to your success. You’ll spend time with developers, uncover problems that haven’t made it into a feature request and translate those insights into useful platform capabilities.

Your impact will be measured through adoption and improvements in software delivery.

What You’ll Own


Platform direction: Define the service offering, prioritise investment and make clear decisions about scope.
Developer discovery: Understand the journeys behind launching services, releasing changes and diagnosing production issues.
Usable releases: Partner with engineering, infrastructure, architecture and security to deliver capability in practical increments.
Adoption: Make onboarding, documentation, templates and support work well enough for teams to self-serve.
Security and governance: Help turn enterprise requirements into automated checks within the delivery process.
Outcomes: Establish baselines, measure improvement and use evidence to shape what comes next.


What You’ll Bring

You’re a technically credible Product Manager who understands engineering problems and brings strong discovery, prioritisation and stakeholder skills.

You’ll Have


At least five years’ product management experience, including ownership of a technical or platform product.
A background in software engineering, platform engineering, infrastructure or cloud, or equivalent technical depth.
Strong understanding of source control, CI/CD, automated testing, artefact management and release practices.
Experience with AWS or Azure and hybrid cloud/on-premise environments.
Evidence of using engineering performance measures to guide product decisions.
The confidence to negotiate competing priorities and explain platform investment to non-technical stakeholders.


Experience with internal developer platforms, developer tooling or DevOps transformation would be particularly relevant. Healthcare or regulated industry experience, application security knowledge and familiarity with AI-assisted software delivery would also be valuable.

Why this role?

You’ll join while the platform’s direction is still taking shape, with meaningful ownership over its purpose, priorities and adoption. Your work will strengthen the engineering capability behind digital services that support healthcare delivery.

Apply now if you want to build a platform that makes engineers’ working lives better, and can demonstrate the difference.
