The Position

Customer Notification Partner

Department & Team Overview
The Technical Service team plays a critical role in supporting our external customers across the UK and Ireland by delivering clear, reliable, and technically accurate communications. Working closely with cross-functional stakeholders—including Technical Services/RCSC, Regulatory Affairs, Product Managers, Logistics, global notification authors, the Senior Leadership Team (SLT), as well as NHS/Healthcare stakeholders and Laboratory Diagnostics partners—this function maintains quality oversight, regulatory compliance, and seamless operational support across the organisation.

Role Introduction

The Customer Notification Partner is an individual contributor role based in Burgess Hill reporting directly to the Data Quality Manager. In this role, you are responsible for the end-to-end lifecycle of affiliate notifications issued to external customers in the UK and Ireland. Serving as the subject matter expert for documentation architecture, you lead through technical excellence and influence, ensuring all communications are accurate, clear, and easy for diverse audiences to understand, while supporting teammates and educating commercial teams to self-serve customer notification data.

The Opportunity


Notification Lifecycle Management: Take full responsibility for all aspects of the affiliate notification lifecycle, including tracking, writing, and publishing updates in a timely manner
Documentation Architecture: Maintain and optimize the information architecture for all external documentation, managing revisions to ensure content remains current, structured, and effective
Cross-Functional Collaboration: Liaise directly with Technical Services, Regulatory Affairs, and Product Management to escalate technical issues and effectively communicate resolution plans
Reporting & Strategy: Monitor notification volumes and types, pulling quarterly and yearly reports for Senior Leadership and Regulatory teams to ensure ongoing compliance and quality oversight
Commercial Support: Provide input into tender questions and support commercial teams with customer notice figures to meet submission deadlines
Stakeholder Engagement: Act as a central bridge across internal functions (Technical Services/RCSC, Regulatory, Product Managers, Logistics, SLT) and external stakeholders (Customers, NHS, Laboratory Diagnostics partners)
Leadership & Enablement: Lead through technical excellence, fostering an exchange of ideas, supporting teammates, and educating commercial teams to self-serve notification data



Who You Are

Education: Possess a degree or postgraduate degree in a Science subject (Biomedical Science preferred)
Experience & Technical Writing: Proven track record in writing customer-facing technical communications and managing complex documentation lifecycles. - Expert in transforming complex diagnostic/technical escalations into clear, accessible communications; experienced in governing documentation architecture, version control, and compliance sign-offs.
Technical Systems & Industry Knowledge: Experienced with CRM systems (e.g., Salesforce/Rexis) and Google/Microsoft suites, with operational knowledge of the NHS, healthcare context, and the laboratory diagnostics market being a plus
Decisive & Customer-Centric Mindset: Decisive and logical problem-solver who gathers necessary information to make timely decisions, dedicated to engaging with customers to understand their "pain" and "gain" points to continuously improve communication
Collaborative & Influential: Able to work effectively and collaboratively across highly complex, cross-functional environments without direct authority
