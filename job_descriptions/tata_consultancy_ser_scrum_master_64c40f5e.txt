Join Tata Consultancy Services, Asia Pacific and be part of an organization committed to sustainable development for our future. TCS follows the Tata group philosophy of building sustainable businesses that are rooted in the community and demonstrate care for the environment. Our unique values position us to combine a purpose-driven worldview with digital innovation, collaborating with customers, communities and governments to lead and shape innovative solutions for a sustainable future. TCS has been carbon neutral in its operations across 11 countries, 12 delivery centres and 18 offices in Asia Pacific since 2022. This is only the initial stage in TCS’ journey as we strive to achieve long-term net zero emissions by 2030.




Corporate sustainability is embedded in our triple-bottom-line, focusing on people, the planet, and our purpose. Our offices are designed with eco-friendly features that significantly reduce our carbon footprint and enhance energy efficiency. We actively champion green initiatives, such as promoting paperless operations, implementing energy-efficient practices, and fostering employee engagement in sustainability efforts. When you become part of the TCS family, you will play an essential role dedicated to innovation, excellence, and crafting a brighter, greener future together. Join us and be a part of our mission to drive sustainability through technology and talent at Tata Consultancy Services, APAC today




Experience Range

6-8 years of IT experience with Insurance background and Managing Agile deliveries
Experience in Insurance Industry (Life and Health)
Proven experience leading cross-functional teams, vendors, and improvement programs
Experience delivering solutions using Agile and Scrum methodologies.




Responsibilities

Key Responsibilities

Lead end-to-end system integration projects across enterprise and external systems..
Work with business stakeholders to identify workflow automation and process improvement opportunities.
Oversee workflow, document management, imaging, and content repository solutions.
Review deliverables from internal teams and vendors to ensure quality and compliance with standards.
Lead SIT, UAT, performance, regression, and security testing activities.
Manage application support, including Incident, Problem, Change, and Release Management processes.
Coordinate operational activities such as SSO integration, non-production support, cloud services, and scheduled maintenance.
Maintain technical documentation, support procedures, and knowledge repositories.
Manage vendors and stakeholders to ensure successful project delivery and system support.
Mentor team members and promote best practices in solution design and delivery.
Drive continuous improvement, platform modernization, and operational efficiency initiatives.







Responsibilities

Agile & Scrum Responsibilities

Participate in Agile and Scrum delivery teams as Scrum Master, Technical Lead, or senior team member.
Facilitate Scrum ceremonies including Sprint Planning, Daily Stand-ups, Sprint Reviews, and Retrospectives.
Work closely with Product Owners and stakeholders to prioritize backlogs and deliver business value.
Support sprint planning, effort estimation, dependency management, and release planning.
Track delivery progress, risks, and issues, ensuring timely resolution of impediments.
Promote Agile, Scrum, and DevOps best practices across the team.
