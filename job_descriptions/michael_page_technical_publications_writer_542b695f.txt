As a Technical Publications Writer in Abingdon, you'll be responsible for creating and maintaining clear, concise documentation to support the organisation. Your work will play a key role in ensuring accurate and accessible information is available for business operations.

Client Details

My client, who are based in Abingdon, operate within the medical industry. They pride themselves on their close-knit company culture.

Description

Key responsibilities of the Technical Publications Writer include:

Draft and edit technical documents, including manuals, guides, and reports.
Ensure all publications meet industry standards and regulatory requirements.
Collaborate with subject matter experts to gather and verify information.
Organise and maintain technical documentation libraries.
Adapt complex technical information into user-friendly content.
Review and update existing documents to ensure accuracy and relevance.
Ensure consistency in style, format, and terminology across all publications.
Work closely with cross-functional teams to meet project deadlines.

Profile

The successful Technical Publications Writer will have:

Strong written English and the ability to communicate information clearly and accurately.
Experience in technical writing, technical publications or procedure writing.
Strong editing skills, organisational skills, and attention to detail.
Competent in Microsoft Word and Excel.
Experience with Adobe InDesign, Illustrator, Photoshop or similar software (desirable).
Ability to work independently and take ownership of assigned work.
Confident communicating with colleagues.
