HK Express is Hong Kong’s first and only low-cost carrier, always offering great value, affordable fares on one of the youngest and most modern aircraft fleets in the world.

Like you, and just like many people across Asia, we love to travel and discover new places across the world’s most diverse and dynamic region; and at HK Express, we’re opening up new travel experiences that inspire and invigorate millions of people across the region we’re proud to call home. Everything we do is focused on encouraging the spirit of adventure. Our routes provide the inspiration and spontaneity to try somewhere new, disrupting monotonous modern life. As we continue to open up new routes, often in secondary and emerging destinations, our passengers will gain access to an incredible range of places, exploring hidden gems and experiencing local cultures.

We have a diverse and vibrant team that embodies the adventurous spirit of our customers, and loves to travel too. Join us in this journey to make your career even more fulfilling and rewarding.

Role Purpose


Manage and support HK Express passenger service and airline distribution platforms, with primary focus on Navitaire New Skies and related applications, to ensure platform stability, service continuity and operational readiness.
Assist in defining and executing the Passenger Services System (PSS) roadmap, with consideration of airline operations, commercial objectives, customer needs, platform capabilities, technical feasibility and regulatory requirements.
Provide first-level functional and technical support for platform incidents, user access, role and configuration management, booking and payment issues, and coordinate investigation and resolution with internal technology teams, vendors and business partners.
Coordinate integrations and enhancements across direct and indirect channels, including direct channel, Global Distribution System (GDS), Online Travel Agent (OTA), interline and codeshare partners, ensuring alignment of end-to-end booking, servicing and fulfilment flows.
Support the delivery of platform initiatives through the full lifecycle, including discovery, requirement definition, solution review, configuration, test planning, UAT, deployment, cutover, go-live support and post-launch optimization.


Qualifications


Degree holder in Business, Information Systems, Computer Science, Aviation Management, or a related discipline.
Minimum 5 years of working experience, including at least 3 years in airline reservation systems, passenger service systems (PSS), airline distribution, ERP/SAP platforms, or other business-critical technology systems within the airline, travel, transportation, or related industries.
Strong analytical, communication, stakeholder management, and negotiation skills.
Proven project management experience working with multiple internal and external stakeholders.
Commercially minded with strong problem-solving capabilities, attention to detail, and a passion for continuous improvement.
A proactive and collaborative team player with the ability to work effectively across business, operational, and technology teams.


Desired Qualifications


Strong understanding of business processes and system operations, with exposure to airline reservation and distribution concepts such as booking, servicing, ancillary products, payments, inventory
