ELLKAY started out providing connectivity solutions to laboratories and within a few years, grew to also provide data management solutions to ambulatory organizations. ELLKAY is now a trusted data management partner in five healthcare segments. ELLKAY’s solutions continue to serve laboratories and ambulatory practices and have expanded to empower hospitals and health systems, healthcare IT vendors, ambulatory practices, health plans, and other healthcare organizations with cutting-edge technologies and solutions that drive their growth and interoperability strategies.

Today, ELLKAY remains true to our core values, building strong partner relationships and offering unparalleled service and support while providing innovative, scalable solutions to the challenges our customers face in today’s data-rich world.

ELLKAY's experience, customer-focused approach, and reputation for innovation, speed, and accuracy differentiate ELLKAY as a premier partner for your interoperability needs and data management strategy.

Job Description

ELLKAY is seeking a high-velocity Senior Release & Delivery Manager to standardize, optimize, and scale our release and delivery ecosystems across all engineering teams. You are a hands-on leader who isn't just managing a checklist, but orchestrating complex cross-functional releases while fundamentally changing how project management is executed using Next-Gen AI capabilities.

You will own the enterprise release management lifecycle, architect our Jira object model, and act as the primary operational bridge between senior engineering leaders, product management, and compliance.

Essential Duties & Responsibilities

Enterprise Release Orchestration & Governance


Design, implement, and govern a unified enterprise release management strategy across multiple complex, parallel product streams with rigorous, multi-tenant delivery cadences.
Manage dependencies, risks, and release schedules across all engineering squads, ensuring zero downtime, continuous integration, and strict adherence to healthcare compliance standards.
Establish clear metrics for release quality, deployment velocity, and operational readiness.


Jira Architecture & Agile Coaching


Act as the ultimate domain expert for Jira Scrum and Kanban frameworks.
Architect and optimize our Jira Development Process and Object Model (Epics, Features, Initiatives, Custom Fields, and Workflows) to establish cross-team visibility, clean data lineages, and seamless portfolio-level release planning.
Coach, mentor, and guide engineering teams on Jira best practices to ensure high-fidelity data input, minimizing developer friction while maximizing tracking accuracy.


AI-Powered Productivity & Operations


Personal & Team Productivity: Savvy at the use of AI assistant tools (e.g., Claude Cowork, ChatGPT Work) to automate personal administrative overhead, synthesize notes, and expedite workflows.
Enterprise AI Tooling: Deeply integrate and leverage tools like Jira Rovo to accelerate search, automate repetitive issue tracking, map complex knowledge gaps, and drive contextual insights across Jira spaces.
Predictive Reporting: Leverage AI-driven analytics to move from reactive project tracking to predictive forecasting (e.g., automated sprint velocity degradation alerts, AI-synthesized release notes, and risk identification).


Executive Reporting & High-Fidelity Dashboards


Construct and maintain high-fidelity, real-time executive dashboards that provide unambiguous clarity on project health, release readiness, and team capacity.
Translate deep technical metrics into highly consumable, actionable status updates for the executive leadership team.


Qualifications


Experience: 8–10+ years of dedicated experience in Release Management, Technical Project Management, or Engineering Operations within a high-growth SaaS, enterprise software, or healthcare tech environment.
Jira Master: Proven track record of configuring complex Jira schemas, advanced roadmaps, automation rules, and portfolio management frameworks from scratch.
AI Fluent: Practical, hands-on experience using LLM-based productivity tools and agentic workflows to augment project management capabilities. Strong familiarity with Jira’s AI features (Jira Rovo) is required.
Agile Expertise: Deep understanding of Scaled Agile frameworks (SAFe), Scrum, and continuous delivery (CI/CD) pipelines
