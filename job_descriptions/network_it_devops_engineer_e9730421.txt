Reference: ITA006195-Caity-2

Are you a passionate DevOps Engineer looking to work with cutting-edge cloud technologies, automation frameworks, and IoT solutions? This opportunity offers the chance to play a key role in building scalable infrastructure, improving system reliability, and supporting innovative products that deliver real-world impact across multiple industries
Duties & Responsibilities
A leading technology business is seeking a DevOps Engineer to join its high-performing engineering team. This role is ideal for a technically driven professional who enjoys cloud architecture, infrastructure automation, and collaborating with software engineering teams to build reliable, secure, and scalable solutions. You will take ownership of cloud infrastructure and platform services, ensuring optimal performance across environments while supporting the ongoing development of advanced IoT and data-driven solutions. The role combines infrastructure engineering, automation, monitoring, and security, providing exposure to modern technologies and challenging projects. This is an excellent opportunity for someone who thrives in a collaborative environment and enjoys solving complex technical problems while contributing to innovative products and services.

Key Responsibilities
Design, manage, and optimise cloud and container-based environments.
Develop and enhance CI/CD pipelines to support efficient software delivery.
Implement infrastructure security measures and vulnerability management processes.
Build and maintain monitoring, observability, and alerting solutions.
Troubleshoot production issues and implement preventative measures.
Support products and services throughout their lifecycle, including deployments and updates.
Collaborate with development, quality assurance, and operational teams to improve delivery processes.
Drive automation initiatives that enhance reliability, performance, and scalability.
Job Experience and Skills Required
Bachelor's Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field.
Equivalent relevant qualifications and experience will be considered.
2 to 5 years of DevOps experience.
Experience managing cloud infrastructure and containerised environments.
Exposure to infrastructure automation and deployment pipelines.
Experience supporting production environments and resolving operational issues.
Database administration and design experience.
Strong Linux administration knowledge.
Proficiency in scripting using Bash, Python, or Node.js.
Experience with containerisation technologies.
Hands-on experience with Terraform, Ansible, Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, or similar automation tools.
Knowledge of cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, GCP, Akamai, or Hetzner.
Experience with PostgreSQL and Timescale databases.
Monitoring and observability experience using tools such as Prometheus and Grafana.
Understanding of infrastructure security, compliance, and vulnerability management.
AWS DevOps Engineer or similar cloud certifications.
UI and UX exposure.
Experience working within quality management environments.
