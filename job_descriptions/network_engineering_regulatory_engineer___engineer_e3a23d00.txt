Regulatory Engineer – Engineering & Compliance
Network Engineering
I'm interested
Reference: NES028955-Phind-1

Our client, an organisation operating within the advanced engineering and industrial technology sector, for an experienced and technically competent Regulatory Engineer.
The successful candidate will play a key role in managing regulatory requirements, coordinating technical interfaces between multiple entities and ensuring regulatory compliance throughout the engineering design and development phases of projects.
Duties & Responsibilities
This position requires an individual with a strong understanding of engineering design processes, regulatory frameworks, technical documentation and stakeholder coordination. The ideal candidate will be comfortable working across multidisciplinary teams, engaging with external regulatory bodies, engineering contractors, consultants, suppliers and other relevant stakeholders.

Key Responsibilities
Manage and coordinate regulatory interfaces between various entities during the engineering design phase.
Ensure that engineering designs comply with applicable legislation, regulations, codes and industry standards.
Liaise with regulatory authorities, engineering consultants, contractors, suppliers and other project stakeholders.
Identify regulatory requirements and compliance obligations applicable to engineering projects.
Review engineering design documentation to ensure regulatory compliance.
Coordinate regulatory submissions, approvals, permits and technical documentation.
Facilitate communication between internal engineering teams and external regulatory stakeholders.
Identify potential regulatory risks and provide recommendations to address compliance gaps.
Participate in design reviews, technical meetings and project coordination sessions.
Ensure regulatory requirements are incorporated into engineering specifications and design deliverables.
Monitor changes in applicable legislation, regulatory standards and industry requirements.
Maintain regulatory registers, compliance records and project documentation.
Provide guidance to engineering teams on regulatory matters throughout the project lifecycle.
Support project teams in resolving regulatory and technical interface challenges.
Minimum Requirements
Relevant engineering degree or BEng/BTech qualification.
Experience in engineering project environments.
Knowledge of regulatory compliance and engineering standards.
Experience coordinating interfaces between multiple organisations or entities during the design phase.
Ability to interpret technical specifications, engineering documentation and regulatory requirements.
Experience engaging with regulatory authorities, consultants, contractors and technical stakeholders.
Strong understanding of engineering design and project development processes.
Excellent technical documentation and report-writing skills.
Strong communication, coordination and stakeholder management abilities.
Excellent analytical, organisational and problem-solving skills.
Advantageous Experience
Experience in the following areas will be highly advantageous:

Industrial process engineering and chemical processing environments.
Regulatory compliance within highly regulated industries.
Engineering design reviews and technical assurance.
Project engineering and multidisciplinary design coordination.
Licensing, permitting and regulatory approval processes.
Quality management systems and technical governance.
Risk assessments and compliance management.
Experience working with international engineering standards and regulatory frameworks.

Interested candidates who meet the above requirements are invited to submit their updated CV, together with relevant qualifications and supporting documentation.
