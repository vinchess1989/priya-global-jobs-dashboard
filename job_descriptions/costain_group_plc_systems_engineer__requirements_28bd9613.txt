Job Description

HS2 is Great Britain’s new high-speed railway. Once operational, HS2’s trains will provide journeys between Birmingham and London, with services continuing on to Manchester, the Northwest and Scotland using the conventional railway. The new route comprises 140-miles of track, four stations, two depots, 32 miles of tunnel, and 130 bridges. The first HS2 services will run between Birmingham Curzon Street and Old Oak Common in London between 2029 and 2033.

HS2 are in the process of procuring c12 railway systems contracts, in excess of £4b, for the design, supply, construction, test & commissioning and maintenance for the route between London and Birmingham

Costain has successfully awarded 2 of these packages worth multiple hundreds of £millions:


Package 2: Tunnel and Lineside Mechanical & Electrical Fitout
Package 3: High Voltage Traction and Non-Traction Power Distribution


Responsibilities

RESPONSIBILITIES


Reporting to the Systems Integration and Assurance Manager, the Systems Engineer (Requirements & Configuration) works with the project engineering and design teams and other specialists to:
Elicit, understand, analyse, and document the requirements for Package 3 of the HS2 Rail Systems Contracts.
leading the change and configuration management, to manage the technical change process across the Package 3 of the HS2 Rail Systems Contracts and maintain configuration management
Developing the Requirements Management processes, Configuration / Change Management processes and procedures in line with both the Costain and Client (HS2) requirements.
Ensuring the application of the Requirements Management Plan and processes to elicit, analyse, record and verify / validate the requirements of the Package 3 Rail Systems contract for HS2.
Ensuring the application of the Configuration Management Plan and processes on the Package 3 Rail Systems contract for HS2.
Working with design teams subcontractors and subject matter experts to allocate requirements to Asset level requirements specification and to determine the relevant appropriate verification and validation activities.
Production and submissions of Verification Validation Matrices (VVM’s) and Configuration Status Reports (CSR’s) to support the technical design assurance process.
Reporting on the performance of the Requirements Management, Verification & Validation process as well as Configuration Management.
Maintain the Change Management process, including maintaining change and configuration registers.
Driving innovation in the requirements management and configuration management approach.
Prepare and deliver training and briefings to promote awareness of the requirements, configuration / change management process and activities within the project team.
Fostering collaborative working within the team and the project team through data base management, team integration and workflows.
Collaborative working within the HS2 Subject Matter Experts as required, and credible in building relationships.


Essentials

Knowledge, Skills, and Experience


Experienced in the use of DOORS to manage project requirements
Experience of the application of configuration / change management processes in major projects
High standard of verbal and written communication skills and interpersonal skills, able to effectively communicate concepts to variety of audiences
Good information management and analytical skills, able to quickly and efficiently seek out the relevant and pertinent points from large volumes of project information
Capable of working collaboratively and effectively with project delivery teams, management and the project leadership and credible in building relationships with all levels of stakeholders


Desirable


Previous experience of working on large infrastructure projects, multidisciplinary teams, in particular experience in civil engineering or rail would be an advantage


Qualifications

Essential


Appropriate professional qualification or equivalent experience in System Engineering
