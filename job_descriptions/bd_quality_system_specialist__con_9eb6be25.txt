Job Description

The Quality System Specialist for APAC Quality Regional Integration is accountable for supporting the regional leaders in the transition planning for Quality Regional teams at Asia-Pacific region and countries-readiness program.

This person will cover transition and sites readiness activities at multiple locations at ASIA and Pacific region.


Support the identification and implementation of quality requirements for the establishment and expansion of global quality presence at APAC region.
Ensure the implementation of Quality Management System (QMS) processes in compliance with applicable regulatory requirements, including QMS certification (ISO 13485, ISO 9001) as appropriate.
Identify when Quality Agreements with Third-Party Logistics providers (3PLs), distributors, and other external partners are required.
Identify when Quality roles are mandatory requirement per country-specific regulations.
Identify requirements and timing for Post-Market Quality at each country.
Coordinate dependencies and communication with Regulatory Affairs, Supply Chain, Commercial, and Operations teams.
Ensure Quality is implemented in compliance with applicable regulations and requirements.
Perform other duties and responsibilities as assigned.
