Mō Mātou | About us

At ASB, we believe progress is more than just financial - it's about helping New Zealanders, communities, and our environment thrive together. We're a purpose-led organisation with a proud history and a clear vision: to support Kiwis in getting one step ahead. Our work is guided by strong values - care, courage and curiosity - and brought to life by a diverse team of people who genuinely care about making a difference.

As one of Aotearoa's most trusted banking partners, we combine the stability and scale of a large organisation with a culture that ensures you're recognised as an individual. Your ideas, your growth, and your contribution matter here - and you'll be supported to do meaningful work that has a real impact, every day.

Mō te tūranga | About the role

As a Senior Software Engineer - Platform Engineer, you'll strengthen the reliability, security and operational resilience of some of ASB's most important customer-facing platforms. You'll combine deep infrastructure expertise with modern engineering practices to help build a more resilient, automated and scalable technology environment.

You'll join the SIS Squad within Customer Tribe, a platform engineering and Site Reliability Engineering team responsible for infrastructure, server platforms and operational excellence across the Tribe. This role works across multiple squads and services rather than a single application, helping uplift platform reliability, automation and operational capability across critical customer systems.

Working across a predominantly on-premises environment with growing cloud adoption, you'll support and improve Windows and Linux platforms while driving greater automation, observability and operational excellence. The role combines hands-on engineering with strategic thinking, giving you the opportunity to improve reliability today and help shape a stronger operating model for the future.

In This Role, You Will


Maintain and optimise critical Windows and Linux server environments.
Drive vulnerability remediation, platform security improvements and more effective patching through automation.
Support reliable, secure and highly available services across Customer Tribe.
Design and implement operational tooling, monitoring, alerting and observability solutions.
Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines supporting platform and infrastructure delivery.
Identify operational risks and implement preventative controls.
Participate in incident response, problem management and post-incident reviews.
Develop automation that improves platform management, security and operational efficiency.
Partner with engineering teams to promote Site Reliability Engineering principles and practices.
Create and maintain technical documentation, runbooks and operational procedures.
Influence platform strategy and recommend improvements that enhance reliability and reduce operational effort.
Collaborate across squads to solve complex platform and infrastructure challenges.
Participate in on-call support when required.


Ngā mea kia whai angitū ahau? | What will help me succeed?

You're an experienced platform, infrastructure or Site Reliability Engineer who enjoys solving complex technical problems and improving how technology operates. You'll bring strong technical depth, a pragmatic approach to problem-solving and the ability to balance operational excellence with longer-term strategic thinking.

Essential Experience


At least five years of experience in infrastructure engineering, platform engineering, Site Reliability Engineering or a related discipline.
Strong Windows and Linux server-administration experience in enterprise-scale infrastructure environments.
Expertise in PowerShell and Python scripting, with proven experience developing automation across infrastructure and operational processes.
Strong vulnerability-management and patching experience.
Experience supporting critical production systems within complex enterprise environments.
Azure cloud experience and exposure to hybrid infrastructure environments.
Strong experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines, using Git-based source control and modern DevOps practices.
Strong monitoring, observability and operational-support experience.
Experience driving reliability, operational improvement or SRE-focused initiatives.
Strong problem-solving skills, with the ability to influence technical direction and platform improvement.
Excellent communication skills, with the ability to engage technical and non-technical stakeholders.


Desirable Experience


Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS).
Experience working in Site Reliability Engineering or platform engineering teams.
Ansible or similar automation and orchestration tools.
Observability platforms and monitoring tooling.
API gateway technologies such as Kong.
Enterprise application-hosting platforms.
Improving patching and vulnerability-management processes through automation.
Certificate management and enterprise networking.
Large-scale or highly regulated environments.
Azure Administrator Associate certification (AZ-104) or equivalent.
