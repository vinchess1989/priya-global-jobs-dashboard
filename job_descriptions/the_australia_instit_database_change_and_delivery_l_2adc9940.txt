The Australia Institute is one of Australia’s leading independent think tanks, conducting research and advocacy on economic, social, environmental and political issues. Our work is grounded in economic fairness, democratic accountability, environmental sustainability and evidence-based public policy.

We are seeking a Database Change and Delivery Lead to manage the end-to-end delivery of a migration program. The role coordinates technical work with internal teams and external partners, and makes sure staff are ready, confident and supported to work in the new system from day one.

This is a delivery and engagement role first. You will not be expected to write the migration code yourself, but you must understand database migration well enough to plan it realistically, challenge assumptions, manage the process and spot risk early.




Key responsibilities

Program and change delivery

Plan, schedule and lead delivery of the database migration program, including scope, milestones, dependencies, resourcing and budget tracking.
Apply a structured change and project methodology.
Coordinate the migration lifecycle: current-state discovery, data mapping, cleansing and de-duplication, test loads, user acceptance testing, cutover and post-go-live stabilisation.
Maintain the program's risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies log and escalate early with clear options.
Manage external vendors, implementation partners and contractors against agreed deliverables, timeframes and cost.
Define and run go/no-go criteria, cutover runbooks, rollback plans and hypercare arrangements.




Stakeholder engagement and change management

Identify and map stakeholders, and build a tailored engagement and communications plan for each group.
Act as the main point of contact between leadership, end users, data and IT staff, and external partners.
Run workshops to capture business requirements, current processes and future-state design decisions.
Lead change impact assessments and plan training, guides and support so staff can adopt the new system.
Develop and deliver practical staff training, providing hands-on guidance, ongoing support and troubleshooting throughout the transition to Salesforce, ensuring staff are confident and capable of using the new system effectively.
Build and support a network of internal champions or super-users.
Provide regular, plain-English status reporting to the executive and any steering committee.




Data governance and quality

Work with data owners to agree data migration rules, retention decisions and acceptance criteria.
Make sure the migration complies with privacy obligations, including the Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles, and any consent and fundraising requirements.
Oversee reconciliation and validation so migrated records are complete, accurate and auditable.
Document decisions, data dictionaries and new processes so knowledge stays with the organisation after the program ends.




Selection criteria




Essential

Proven experience leading or delivering at least one end-to-end database or CRM migration program, from discovery through cutover and post-go-live support.
Demonstrated project and change delivery experience, including planning, scheduling, risks, assumptions, issues and dependencies log management, budget tracking and status reporting.
Strong stakeholder engagement skills, with a track record of building trust across executive, operational and technical audiences.
Practical understanding of data migration concepts: data mapping, transformation, cleansing, de-duplication, test loads, reconciliation and cutover planning.
Experience managing vendors or implementation partners against contracted deliverables.
Excellent written and verbal communication, including the ability to explain technical risk in plain English.
Ability to work independently in a part-time, hybrid arrangement and prioritise effectively across competing demands.

Desirable

Hands-on experience with Salesforce migrations or implementations, ideally Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud, NPSP or a similar CRM data model.
Familiarity with migration and integration tools such as Salesforce Data Loader, Data Import Wizard, dataloader.io, MuleSoft or SQL-based ETL.
Experience in the not-for-profit, research, policy or advocacy sectors, including supporter, donor or membership data.
Experience with reporting tools such as Power BI or Salesforce reports and dashboards.




Working arrangements and how to apply

3 days per week. Work is remote or from our Canberra office, with on-site days in Canberra required around workshops, UAT and cutover. Occasional flexibility outside core hours may be needed during cutover windows.




To apply as soon as possible:

A current CV (doc. or pdf.)
A cover letter of no more than two pages (doc. or pdf.)
A short summary of one database migration program you delivered (doc. or pdf.)
