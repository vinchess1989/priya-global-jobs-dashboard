Quality Manager – Medical Manufacturing




As we continue to expand, we are looking for an accomplished Quality Manager to provide strategic and operational quality leadership across our Longford, Ireland campus. Because at Technimark, quality isn't simply a function. It's fundamental to how we develop and manufacture products our customers and their end users depend on.




As Quality Manager, you'll have overall responsibility for the effectiveness, compliance and continuous improvement of the site's Quality Management System (QMS).

You'll work closely with Operations, Engineering, Supplier Quality, Corporate Quality and site leadership to manage risk, strengthen process capability, improve quality performance and maintain regulatory readiness.




Just as importantly, you'll build and develop a high-performing Quality organization capable of supporting the continued success of our Longford operations.




Key Duties and Functions of the role

Provide leadership for all Quality functions across the Longford campus, ensuring alignment with regulatory, customer and internal requirements.
Serve as the site's QMS Management Representative, maintaining oversight of QMS processes, compliance, effectiveness and continuous improvement.
Lead regulatory and audit readiness for ISO 13485, EU MDR, FDA QMSR, Notified Body and customer requirements.
Own critical quality decisions involving product release, nonconforming product and significant customer or regulatory quality risk.
Develop and monitor site Quality KPIs, using trend analysis to identify emerging risks and drive effective countermeasures.
Lead Management Reviews, CAPA Review Boards and Quality Trend Reviews.
Serve as a senior Quality representative for strategic customers, leading significant customer complaints, investigations, escalations, corrective actions, audits and improvement initiatives.
Partner cross-functionally to strengthen process stability, capability, validation, risk management and manufacturing controls.
Ensure robust root cause analysis and sustainable corrective and preventive actions.
Establish and develop the Quality organization, including training, competencies, performance expectations and employee development.
Ensure recurring GMP, regulatory and quality-system training requirements are successfully executed.
Drive systemic continuous improvement using data, statistical analysis and risk-based decision making.




Desired Skills and Experience

5+ years of demonstrated Quality Management leadership within medical device manufacturing, including cross-functional or multi-site responsibility.
Third-level qualification in Engineering, Science or a related discipline.
Strong working knowledge of ISO 13485, EU MDR and FDA QMSR requirements and demonstrated experience maintaining an effective QMS.
Knowledge of injection molding and value-added manufacturing processes.
Experience with risk management, validation planning, protocol development and execution, process controls and statistical analysis.
Demonstrated expertise in CAPA, root cause analysis, customer requirements and continuous improvement.
Understanding of cleanroom operations, ISO 14644, environmental monitoring and Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP).
Ability to develop statistically valid sampling plans and interpret process and quality data.
Proficiency with statistical analysis tools such as Minitab.
Lean Six Sigma Green Belt or higher certification.
Strong technical writing, presentation and customer communication skills.
Demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional teams and influence senior-level decisions using facts, data and risk-based thinking.




Why Technimark?

Technimark is a global manufacturing partner specializing in precision injection molding, complex assembly and contract manufacturing, combining capabilities across product development, mold building, precision molding, automation, contract manufacturing and global manufacturing.




Our approach connects innovation with execution, helping customers move products from concept through commercialization while emphasizing quality throughout the product and manufacturing lifecycle.




Learn more about Technimark at www.technimark.com
