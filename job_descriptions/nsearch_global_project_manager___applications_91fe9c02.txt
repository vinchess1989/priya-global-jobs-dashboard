Our client, one of Asia-Pacific’s leading organizations is looking for:




Project Manager - Applications / RPA




Responsibilities:

Manage day-to-day operations of business systems, ensuring service stability and optimal performance across the operational environment
Provide oversight of vendor-led incident and problem management, ensuring timely resolution and compliance with service expectations, including validating root cause analyses and resolution proposals from vendors
Coordinate releases and deployments with technical teams and vendors, managing delivery dependencies, timelines, and environment management in collaboration with application and infrastructure vendors
Work with vendors and stakeholders to review, validate, and confirm business and technical requirements, managing delivery dependencies and ensuring alignment with business needs, technical standards, and operational constraints
Manage user expectations, maintain good rapport with stakeholders, and understand their requirements
Manage project budgets, including planning, tracking, and reporting on expenditure to ensure projects are delivered within approved financial parameters
Support contract management activities and monitor vendor performance against agreed service levels
Deliver enhancements and small projects within a live operational environment, maintaining clear documentation for delivery decisions, risks, and changes







Requirements:

Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, Business Information Systems, or a relevant with minimum 2 to 5 years of relevant experience in technology project delivery, RPA, automation or application operations
Ability to understand and review automation workflows, investigate failures, troubleshoot issues and assess proposed technical solutions.
Ability to independently review and challenge vendor automation designs, root cause analyses and proposed fixes
Experience managing technology projects or automation initiatives involving business users, technical teams and external vendors
Good understanding of RPA and automation development and operational best practices, including process assessment, solution design, exception handling, logging, monitoring, credential management, testing, deployment and production support
