Job Description




Responsibilities:

Responsible for developing system architectures and detail them to design specifications to meet the customer’s requirements.
Analyse customer’s requirements as well as conduct project conceptual and feasibility studies prior to design.
Create System design specification
Owns and co-develop the project BOM with rest of the team.
Manage and document the system configuration.
To ensure that design verifications and validations are carried out to confirm design with the assistance from the project EE/ME/SW engineers.
Ensure adequate design reviews are conducted for design improvement.
Organize and ensure technical milestones are met
Understand projects’ goals and considerations, and necessary compromises to be made between timeline, quality and budget.
Create and ensure technical documentation to ensure efficient planning and execution in project
Develop the comprehensive/FAT/OSAT test plans with the team.
Technical risk assessment of project
Create and recognize ownership of the system solution paper
Conduct FMEA and include findings in system flow diagram
Coordinate system development tasks to include design, integration and formal testing.

Requirements:

Degree in Mechanical/Electrical/Software Engineering or Equivalent
2 to 3 years of system engineering experience.
Experience in leading a project team of between 3-5 engineers.
Significant business process experience and knowledge of engineering and manufacturing processes of the Business Unit he/she is working for .
Requires knowledge of PLM software, ERP Systems and Product Change Control processes
Demonstrate Leadership capabilities and possess excellent Interpersonal and Communication Skills (both written and verbal), as the successful candidate will facilitate the link between Project Engineering Team, Project Management team and the customer.
Able to read drawings, understand engineering documents, and work closely with members of the engineering, operations and purchasing team.
Technical knowledge to work with electrical, mechanical and Software architecture.
Results oriented, metrics driven and capable of working in a fast paced, changing environment.
Ability to work under pressure and time constraints.
"Can do" attitude combined with hands-on approach
Good facilitation and presentation skills
