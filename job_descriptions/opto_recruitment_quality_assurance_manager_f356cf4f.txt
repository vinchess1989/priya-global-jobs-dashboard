Job Summary

The Quality Manager provides strategic and operational quality leadership across the campus, with responsibility for the effectiveness, compliance, and continuous improvement of the Quality Management System (QMS). This position is responsible for all Quality functions at the and ensuring alignment with regulatory, customer, and internal requirements. Serving as the Management Representative and senior quality leader forthe site, this role engages with Operations, Engineering, Corporate Quality, customers, and site leadership to manage quality performance and risk, drive systemic improvement, and ensure consistent execution of quality requirements.




Knowledge | Experience | Skills

5+ years demonstrated leadership of Quality management in a medical device manufacturing environment, including multi-site or cross-functional responsibility.
Knowledge of injection molding and subsequent value-added manufacturing processes.
Clear understanding of ISO 13485, EU MDR, and FDA QMSR requirements, including other applicable regulations along with demonstrated experience maintaining and ensuring effectiveness of the Quality Management Systems.
Strong experience interpreting customer specifications and requirements, performing root cause analysis, and utilizing process improvement techniques.
In-depth knowledge of and demonstrated experience in risk management; validation planning, test protocol development, execution, and reporting; process development and controls; and statistical analysis with interpretation of results.
Understanding of cleanroom operation and maintenance, ISO 14644 standards, environmental monitoring, and Good Manufacturing Practices.
Proven ability in leading cross functional teams, managing complex quality issues, methodically solving problems, and influencing leadership decisions using facts and data.
Ability to identify need and drive systemic improvement.
Effective written and oral communication through well-organized technical writing, presentations, and internal and/or customer communications.
Competent in creating statistically valid sampling plans.
Proficiency in statistical analyses using analytical software such as Minitab.
Lean Six Sigma Green Belt or higher certification.




Education

At a minimum, a Third-level qualification in Engineering or Science discipline is required




Responsible for control of nonconforming products.

Responsible for product release decisions, significant quality-risk escalation, and decisions affecting customer or regulatory compliance.
Serve as the Quality Management System Management Representative, maintain oversight of all QMS processes, and ensure the QMS remains effective, providing improvement when necessary.
Develop and monitor campus quality KPIs, identify trends, risks, mitigations, and countermeasures.
Communicate quality performance and improvement priorities to leadership through periodic trend analysis and review meetings; CAPA review board meetings; and QMS Management Review meetings.
Serve as the Chairperson for Trend Analysis/Trend Review meetings, for CAPA Review Board meetings, and for Management Review meetings.
Ensure regulatory readiness, including customer audits, certifying body audits, and regulatory inspections (FDA, Notified Body, etc.), and ensure appropriate corrective actions and sustained compliance.
Lead all Quality functions at the campus and ensure alignment with regulatory, customer, and internal requirements.
Serve as a primary Quality representative for strategic customers, leading significant escalations, complaints, investigations, corrective actions, audits, and quality improvement initiatives.
Partner with Operations, Engineering, Supplier Quality, and Corporate Quality to ensure process stability and capability are maintained, manage quality risk, standardize processes, and drive cross functional and campus improvement.
Establish the campus Quality organization, ensure Quality personnel possess the necessary job skills, and create performance and development plans for Quality personnel.
Ensure Quality department training requirements and needs are fulfilled to ensure necessary skills and competencies are maintained.
Ensure recurring Quality and Regulatory training requirements are fulfilled for the campus. i.e., GMP training, QMSR training, etc.
