EDES Deployment SME - Electronic Documentation



Location: Cork
Working arrangement: Hybrid, with regular site presence required
Contract: Daily-rate contract
Day rate 600-650 per day

We are seeking an experienced EDES (Electronic Document Execution Solution) Subject Matter Expert to support the deployment and integration of an electronic documentation solution within a major pharmaceutical manufacturing site in Cork.

This is a hands-on site deployment role, focused on driving the adoption of EDES across the project lifecycle and ensuring that electronic validation and GMP documentation processes are effectively embedded into site operations.

Working closely with site project, engineering, commissioning, CQV, Quality and digital teams, as well as external commissioning service providers and the central EDES team, you will coordinate the creation and approval of electronic documentation, update local procedures, support training and ensure effective integration of EDES into project execution.



Key Responsibilities



Lead and coordinate the site-level rollout and adoption of EDES across relevant project and commissioning activities.

Drive the creation, review and approval of electronic documents, templates and protocols within the EDES environment.

Work with engineering, commissioning, CQV and project teams to translate existing paper-based documentation and site processes into electronic workflows.

Coordinate the collection of existing templates, protocols, examples and discipline-specific documentation for EDES configuration and deployment.

Support template configuration activities, dry runs and user acceptance testing in collaboration with the EDES Digital Core team and commissioning service providers.

Review existing site procedures and coordinate updates to SOPs, work instructions and associated training materials to align with EDES requirements.

Develop and coordinate user training, support adoption and act as a key point of contact for site users.

Integrate EDES field execution activities into live site projects, ensuring documentation is available and approved in line with project requirements.

Support site change control, electronic records and electronic signatures assessments, business continuity planning and site acceptance activities.

Monitor implementation progress, resolve deployment issues and coordinate actions across internal and external stakeholders.

Provide ongoing guidance and first-line support to users following implementation.



Required Experience



Demonstrable experience implementing, deploying or supporting Kneat / EDES within a pharmaceutical or biotechnology environment.

Strong understanding of electronic commissioning, qualification and validation documentation and associated GMP requirements.

Experience creating, reviewing and managing electronic protocols, templates and controlled documentation.

Practical understanding of commissioning / CQV workflows and how documentation is generated, reviewed, approved and executed in the field.

Experience coordinating SOP updates, procedural changes, training and user adoption within a regulated environment.

Familiarity with GMP, data integrity, electronic records and electronic signatures requirements.

Strong stakeholder management skills, with the ability to work across Engineering, CQV, Quality, project teams and external service providers.



Desirable



Previous EDES deployment experience within Pfizer or another large pharmaceutical organisation.

Direct experience with Kneat Gx and electronic document execution workflows.

Experience supporting site change control, ERES assessments, UAT, go-live and post-implementation support.

Experience working with commissioning service providers and integrating digital documentation into capital project delivery.

This is an excellent opportunity for a specialist who combines hands-on EDES / Kneat knowledge with pharmaceutical commissioning documentation experience and the ability to drive a site-wide digital deployment
