Job Description

What if you could shape a career as unique as you?

At WSP, you can always find opportunities to grow and do what matters to you. Make the most of our global reach to discover new challenges and chances to work with diverse, talented individuals who will help you expand your horizons.

A Little More About The Role…

We are a collaborative and environmentally focused Information Management team, supporting WSP’s growing portfolio of projects. As a centre of excellence within our Earth & Environment discipline, we provide project and information management expertise across the full project lifecycle.

You’ll join a supportive team environment, with opportunities to develop your skills while working on a diverse range of high-profile infrastructure and environmental projects across the UK.

In this role, you will oversee information management processes aligned to ISO 19650 standards, ensuring effective project information flow and governance. You will work closely with project teams and digital leads to embed consistent standards, support delivery, drive quality, and develop people to build confidence, capability and high-quality information management practice.

Our projects span major infrastructure and development programmes, including nationally significant schemes across sectors such as energy, transport, defence, and the built environment.

Key responsibilities


Manage information processes in line with ISO 19650 standards
Maintain and optimise the Common Data Environment (CDE)
Develop and manage BEP, MIDP and TIDP documentation
Coordinate information exchange requirements across stakeholders
Support and embed project information standards and procedures
Apply a security-minded approach to data access and governance
Provide guidance and training on information management systems
Monitor quality and ensure compliance with agreed standards
Manage the review, validation and distribution of project asset information, including COBie, associated non-graphical data, and graphical model outputs, ensuring that information model reviews (including both graphical and non-graphical/data checks) are undertaken in accordance with ISO 19650 information management requirements.


What We Will Be Looking For You To Demonstrate…


Extensive experience operating within BIM environments and leading or supporting Project Information Management
Confident and effective communicator, able to engage stakeholders at all levels across both in-person and digital channels
Demonstrates effective collaboration, with the ability to influence, drive outcomes, and solve complex problems
Proven experience working within complex project or PMO environments, often across multidisciplinary teams
Good working knowledge of Common Data Environments (CDEs), including implementation and governance
Good understanding and practical application of ISO 19650 standards and wider information management frameworks
Experience leading, supporting and developing people, helping others build confidence, capability and high-quality information management practice

WHAT’S IN IT FOR YOU?

Work-life balance

At WSP, we understand that work is just one aspect of your life. It’s important to make time for you, your family, friends, interests and your community.

Our hybrid working policy offers the flexibility to work from home two days a week, while also providing opportunities to collaborate in our modern offices across the UK.

Inclusivity & Belonging

We welcome applicants with varied backgrounds and experiences. We enable rewarding careers by encouraging people to bring their whole and authentic selves to work so that our work represents the fullest spectrum of society. We also provide part-time and flexible working arrangements, the option to purchase additional leave, and the ability to use your bank holiday entitlement to suit you.
