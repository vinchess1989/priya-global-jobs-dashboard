Change and Release Manager

NSW, VIC or TAS | Permanent Opportunity

 

The opportunity to join our team

This is an exciting opportunity to join Wrap Technology at a pivotal time and play a key role in shaping how technology change is governed, managed and delivered. Working across more than 20 technology squads, you'll have the opportunity to influence delivery outcomes, strengthen governance practices and help create a more scalable and resilient technology environment.

 

As Change & Release Manager, you'll work closely with Delivery, Engineering, Production Support and Risk teams to drive continual improvement across change and release processes. This is a role where you'll have the autonomy to challenge the status quo, identify opportunities for improvement and help embed best practice across a complex and evolving technology landscape.

 

Key responsibilities include:

Owning and evolving the Change and Release Management framework across Wrap Technology
Leading Change Advisory Board (CAB) activities and release governance forums
Managing the release calendar and coordinating technology releases across multiple teams
Assessing risk, readiness and deployment plans to support successful releases
Driving process improvements, reporting and operational maturity initiatives
Partnering with technology, risk and business stakeholders to improve delivery outcomes
Supporting automation and modern release management practices across the technology landscape

 

What you will bring

We're looking for someone with experience in Change Management, Release Management or Environment Management who enjoys building process maturity and influencing outcomes across complex technology environments.

Experience managing technology change and software releases
Knowledge of ITIL Change and Release Management practices
Experience facilitating CAB forums and making risk-based decisions
ServiceNow, Jira and Agile delivery experience
Strong stakeholder engagement and influencing skills
A continuous improvement mindset with the ability to balance governance and delivery outcomes
