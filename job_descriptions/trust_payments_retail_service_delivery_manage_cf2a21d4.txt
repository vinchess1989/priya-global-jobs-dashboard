Find out more at www.trustpayments.com.




Trust Payments has an exciting opportunity for a Retail Service Delivery Manager to join their team.




Location: Hybrid - Bournemouth

Salary: Competitive + Benefits




How will you make an impact in this role?

The Retail Service Delivery Manager will work with a number of key functions within the IT department that enable the delivery of a high-quality service to end users, and will follow Service Delivery processes in place to meet business needs. This is a stakeholder-facing role, both internal and external, and requires establishing and managing expectations within the business, then guiding the IT team to meet those expectations to a high standard.




What We Expect of You, Day To Day:

You will be responsible, in part, for the following:




Managing completion of test cycles and the deployment of high-quality products to customers (including change requests, maintenance releases and new customer implementations)
Working with Development and Test managers to determine delivery dates
Monitoring deliverables and reporting on dates, issues and risks
Reporting to the Head of Delivery and Operations to mitigate risks and manage issues to ensure deadlines are met
Running stand-ups
Facilitating the clearing of blockers
Agreeing priority of tickets
Feeding into Development and Test resource allocation
Release readiness management
Handover to Operations
Managing deployment to Production
Ensuring production testing is complete before rolling out to the customer




Qualifications

Minimum of 2 years' experience in a similar role
Able to work under pressure and meet deadlines
Excellent organisational skills
Able to manage confidential information with discretion
Motivated and accountable
Able to manage, prioritise and allocate tasks and time efficiently
Able to demonstrate initiative and a proactive approach to daily tasks
Excellent team management and people skills
Excellent written and verbal communication skills
