Adactin Group is a leading Australian IT consulting company providing a comprehensive suite of services and solutions. By aligning our tech expertise with our client's objectives, we strive to be their trusted IT partners, whom they can rely on to navigate in an ever-changing digital era.

Our approach provides customers with unparalleled digital transformation benefits and outcomes, driving their growth and success. Adactin is always committed to delivering with conviction and belief in our people. Seize the opportunity and advance your career in a dynamic environment to augment your growth and success.




Key Responsibilities

Manage the provisioning, configuration, refresh, maintenance, and decommissioning of IT environments across Dev, SIT, UAT, Pre-Production, and Production-aligned environments.
Ensure environments are stable, available, secure, current, and aligned to agreed production baselines, delivery needs, and operational standards.
Coordinate environment planning activities across projects, releases, testing phases, and BAU operations to ensure the right environments, data, access, and dependencies are available when required.
Monitor environment health, availability, performance, and dependencies, escalating risks, defects, and blockers in line with agreed support processes and service expectations.
Support database refreshes, data reload activities, environment resets, and controlled data management processes required for testing and release readiness.
Act as the primary coordination point for environment-related issues, working with technical teams to triage, investigate, resolve, and communicate impacts to stakeholders.
Maintain accurate environment records, schedules, status reporting, known issues, configuration information, and release readiness updates.
Ensure change, release, governance, audit, risk, and compliance requirements are followed for all environment management activities.
Identify opportunities to improve environment management practices, reduce recurring issues, increase automation, and improve deployment and operational efficiency.

Key Deliverables

Environment plans, schedules, status updates, and health reports.
Fit-for-purpose environments available for development, testing, release, and operational activities.
Accurate environment registers, dependency tracking, configuration records, and issue logs.
Coordinated environment refreshes, provisioning activities, access management support, and release readiness inputs.
Timely escalation, communication, and resolution coordination for environment-related incidents, defects, and delivery blockers.




Skills and Experience

Demonstrated experience in IT Environment Management.
Strong understanding of IT Environment Management.
Strong understanding of multi-environment landscapes, including Dev, SIT, UAT, Pre-Production, and Production or production-like environments.
Experience coordinating environment setup, refresh, database refresh, data reload, configuration, access, and dependency management activities.
Sound knowledge of change, release, incident, problem, and service management practices, including use of ITSM tools such as ServiceNow.
Ability to work across technical and non-technical teams to prioritise requests, resolve issues, manage risks, and support delivery timelines.
Familiarity with monitoring and alerting tools, CI/CD pipelines, deployment processes, cloud and on-premise systems, and enterprise technology environments.

Key Capabilities

Strong coordination, planning, and stakeholder management skills.
High attention to detail and commitment to process, governance, and documentation quality.
Ability to prioritise competing requests and manage environment constraints across multiple initiatives.
Proactive approach to identifying risks, resolving blockers, and escalating issues appropriately.
Continuous improvement mindset with a focus on automation, repeatability, reliability, and operational efficiency.
