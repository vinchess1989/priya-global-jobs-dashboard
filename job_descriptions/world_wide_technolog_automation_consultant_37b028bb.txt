About the role

The resource will be the technical lead for the automation track of a multi-year patch management program at a large Singapore financial institution. Ansible Automation Platform is the orchestration layer: it decides what gets patched, in what order, under which change record, and proves it happened.

You will design and drive the orchestration architecture, lead the engineers building it, and hand a working framework to the client’s own team.

What you’ll do

Architecture and design

Own the Ansible framework architecture: one workflow (discover, prioritize, approve, sequence, execute, verify) and one evidence trail across every platform
Author framework sections of the high-level and low-level designs; support the Principal Automation Consultant at Architecture Review Board
Shape the intended-state approach for agentless targets (NetBox or policy-as-code)

Build and technical leadership

Lead the Senior Automation Engineer, Automation Engineer and ServiceNow Engineer
Deliver workflow templates for the ServiceNow change gate, owner approvals, five-ring promotion logic and evidence to Splunk
Set standards for execution environments, collections and content-as-code in GitHub Enterprise and JFrog, aligned to client coding standards
Drive the first agentless canaries: ESXi host evacuation and patch, and a network platform firmware update
Build Robot Framework test coverage; own component, integration and security testing for automation content

Client partnership and handover

Coordinate with the Tanium security team on integration points and shared approval paths
Work with platform owners on cluster-aware sequencing, rollback and validation criteria
Mentor client automation engineers until they author playbooks in the framework unaided; contribute to SOPs, recorded training and supervised knowledge transfer
Build reusable patterns for later tiers: databases, middleware, storage, firmware, AIX and Solaris

Required experience

10+ years in infrastructure automation, with 8+ years hands-on Ansible in production
Deep Ansible Automation Platform expertise: workflow templates, execution environments, automation mesh across segmented networks, RBAC, credential plugins
Prior technical lead or architect role on an enterprise automation program
Python, Git, REST APIs and CI pipelines
Ansible Automation Platform integration with ServiceNow Change and CMDB
Patch or change orchestration at scale: rolling and cluster-aware sequencing, maintenance windows, rollback
Delivery in a regulated industry (financial services preferred): change control, separation of duties, audit-ready evidence
Clear written design documentation suitable for architecture and change review boards

Preferred

Red Hat certifications (RHCE, EX374, EX467)
Agentless patching for VMware ESXi and vCenter, Nutanix and network operating systems (Cisco, F5, Palo Alto, Juniper)
CyberArk, PingID, Splunk and Datadog
NetBox or Nautobot as a source of truth
