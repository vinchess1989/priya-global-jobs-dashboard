We are looking to recruit an experienced Quality Engineer to support the day-to-day activities of our UK Quality Department.

The successful candidate will have previous experience working within a Quality environment and will be responsible for the administration and coordination of key Quality Management System (QMS) activities, with particular emphasis on customer complaints, non-conformances, Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA), quality documentation and associated quality records.

This position will be joining our UK medical device manufacturing company and will work closely with Quality, Production, Engineering, Customer Services, Supply Chain and other departments to ensure quality activities are effectively managed, progressed and completed in accordance with company procedures and regulatory requirements.

The role will also involve supporting quality investigations, maintaining accurate quality records, monitoring outstanding actions and helping ensure complaints, non-conformances and corrective actions are progressed through to effective closure.

Key Responsibilities


Coordinate and administer the customer complaint process, ensuring complaints are logged, investigated, documented and progressed within required timescales.
Maintain complaint records, supporting information, investigation evidence and associated correspondence.
Assist with complaint investigations by gathering production records, traceability information, inspection results and other relevant information.
Coordinate and administer internal non-conformances, ensuring records are correctly raised, investigated, reviewed and progressed to closure.
Support the control and administration of non-conforming materials, components and finished products.
Maintain records relating to quarantine, rework, scrap, concessions and other agreed dispositions.
Support the administration and coordination of Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA).
Monitor outstanding complaints, non-conformances and Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA) actions and follow up with responsible personnel to ensure completion within agreed timescales.
Assist with quality investigations using appropriate root cause and problem-solving techniques.
Support the maintenance and control of Quality Management System (QMS) documentation, including procedures, work instructions, forms and quality records.
Assist with document change control and ensure documentation is appropriately reviewed, approved and maintained.
Support the administration of calibration and equipment verification records where required.
Assist with validation documentation and associated quality records.
Prepare and maintain quality information and data relating to complaints, non-conformances, Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA) and Quality KPIs.
Assist in identifying recurring quality issues and adverse trends.
Support internal and external audits by preparing documentation, retrieving records and following up resulting actions.
Provide general Quality support to Production and other departments.
Support continuous improvement and Quality Management System (QMS) improvement activities.


Essential Experience/Qualifications


Previous experience working within a Quality Department, preferably within a manufacturing environment.
Experience dealing with customer complaints and/or non-conformances.
Experience working with a Quality Management System (QMS).
Experience maintaining controlled documentation and quality records.
Understanding of root cause investigation and corrective action.
Good working knowledge of Microsoft Office, including Word, Excel and Outlook.
Good standard of written and spoken English.


Desirable Experience/Qualifications


Experience within a medical device or other regulated manufacturing environment
Knowledge or experience of ISO 13485 and/or ISO 9001
Experience of Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA) processes
Experience of electronic Quality Management System (QMS), Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) or Manufacturing Execution System (MES)
Understanding of calibration, validation and change control processes
Experience of basic inspection, measurement or testing activities
Experience using problem-solving techniques such as 5 Why, Ishikawa or similar root cause analysis tools


Key Skills


Excellent verbal and written communication skills
Excellent attention to detail
Methodical, organised and structured approach to work
Able to multitask and prioritise workload
Able to manage and follow up multiple open quality actions
Able to work well under pressure and meet deadlines
Able to work independently as well as within a team
Able to build good working relationships across different departments
Strong administrative and record-keeping skills
Good analytical and problem-solving skills
Able to gather, evaluate and communicate information clearly
Self-motivated with a proactive approach to work
Keen and willing to learn
Able to work effectively within a highly regulated environment with a strong focus on compliance
Able to maintain confidentiality when dealing with customer and quality information
Good IT skills, including Microsoft Office
A positive, professional and can-do attitude
