Are you a seasoned DevOps/Cloud Engineer with a passion for Azure infrastructure, automation, and modern deployment practices? Join our dynamic team in Denmark and help shape scalable, secure, and efficient cloud solutions using cutting-edge technologies like Kubernetes, Helm, Docker, and Azure DevOps.

Key Responsibilities


Design and manage Azure cloud infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines.
Orchestrate containers using Kubernetes, Helm, and Docker.
Automate processes and script in PowerShell and Linux.
Build and deploy Angular/React applications.
Configure and maintain application servers (NGINX, IIS).
Monitor systems, troubleshoot issues, and optimize performance.
Collaborate with stakeholders and mentor team members.


Essential Requirements


Proven experience with Microsoft Azure and its managed services.
Strong knowledge of DevOps principles and CI/CD practices.
Apply AIOps practices across CI/CD and Kubernetes environments.
Expertise in Kubernetes, Helm, Docker, and microservices architecture.
Proficiency in PowerShell, Linux scripting, and YAML.
Experience with infrastructure-as-code tools (ARM Templates/Bicep).
Build AI-enabled DevOps pipelines and cloud automation.
Familiarity with application deployment and server configuration.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
Fluent in English (spoken and written).
Leadership capabilities including mentoring and team development
