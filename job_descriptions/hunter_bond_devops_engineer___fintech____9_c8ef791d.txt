Job title: DevOps Engineer

Client: FinTech

Salary: £90,000-£110,000 + Bonus

Location: London

Skills: Linux, CI/CD, Kubernetes, Kafka, Docker, Maven, Prometheus, Grafana, Python




The role :




My client, an expanding and established FinTech, are seeking a DevOps Engineer to join their team in London.




This role requires 4 days in the office per week, with 1 day working from home.




Responsibilities:




Automation first mindset, designing automated solutions for CI/CD pipelines
Working across various teams to work collaboratively during projects
Ops work including route cause analysis and technical Linux deep dives
Designing and implementing deployments tools using Helm, Ansible or similar
Working on large containerised environments – Kubernetes and Docker
Monitoring and Observability utilising tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, Mimir, Loki, Tempo etc.
Scripting in a variety of languages including Python, Java, Groovy, React, Swift etc.




Experience required




3-5 years + experience working in a varied DevOps position
Strong Linux systems experienced
Competent scripting in Python and Bash
Good knowledge of container platforms – Kubernetes, Docker, Podman
Strong knowledge of CI/CD tooling such as Bamboo, GitLab, Travis etc.
Good understanding of software delivery practices such as Git branching and Configuration Management
Experience working with Kafka is highly desirable
Knowledge of software delivery practices
Good overall understanding of the SDLC and Agile
Strong knowledge of build tools such as Maven / Gradle
Any experience with Xcode / Swift / associated iOS technologies is highly desirable




This is an excellent opportunity to work for an established but expanding firm who have a deep footprint in some of the world’s largest financial institutions.




Please apply ASAP for more information.
