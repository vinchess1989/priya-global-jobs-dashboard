Exciting opportunity to join S64 Ventures Limited (“S64”), a rapidly growing fintech business which provides financial solutions to enable private clients access to private markets. S64 is working with top tier private markets GPs, asset managers and private banks across a wide range of alternative asset classes.




Our team have worked together for over a decade and previously pioneered the market's largest alternative investment platforms with a depth of private wealth, alternative asset, fund management and investment banking experience amongst them. We are defining a new era in private markets access through our proprietary technology, our unrivalled delivery capabilities and our deep industry expertise. Today, S64 is the trusted partner to the world’s leading private banks and asset managers.




Opportunity

We are looking for a forward-thinking DevOps Engineer, based in Singapore, to join our dynamic technology team. In this pivotal role, you will architect, secure, and optimise critical cloud environments that power both our customer-facing solutions and internal platforms. You’ll collaborate closely with developers, designers, and stakeholders to drive innovation and operational excellence across our infrastructure.




Key Responsibilities

Architect and Evolve Cloud Infrastructure: Design, build, and maintain scalable, secure, and resilient cloud environments that support our full technology stack.
Drive Automation and CI/CD Excellence: Enhance our build pipelines and infrastructure through automation, ensuring rapid, reliable, and repeatable deployments.
Empower Development Teams: Provide tools, guidance, and architectural insight to enable developers to build and deploy efficiently and securely.
Support Pre-Sales and Client Engagements: Partner with sales and client teams to provision environments, respond to technical queries, and showcase our platform capabilities.
Champion Innovation: Stay ahead of industry trends, emerging technologies, and best practices—sharing insights and driving continuous improvement across the engineering team.




Qualifications & Skills

Proven experience managing and scaling cloud infrastructure—Azure expertise preferred.
Proficiency with Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform or Pulumi.
Strong automation skills, particularly in CI/CD pipelines (Azure DevOps is a plus).
Solid understanding of networking principles and cloud security best practices.
Experience with SQL and NoSQL databases in production environments.
Familiarity with Git and modern version control workflows.
Agile mindset with experience in Agile/Scrum methodologies.
A pragmatic, solution-oriented approach with a passion for using the right tool for the job.
Self-starter with a strong sense of ownership, accountability, and a drive for excellence.
Excellent problem-solving skills and a passion for delivering exceptional user and customer experiences.
Experience in financial services and private market is highly desirable




People who choose to build their careers at S64 will help shape the future of private markets in an era of rapid regulatory, market and technology change.




Our Culture

Our vision is to dominate private markets access for private wealth through proprietary technology, unparalleled solutions and deep industry expertise.




We have three core operating principles:

Client Focus: We put the client at the centre of everything we do and strive to deliver a world class client experience. This is grounded in being responsive, collaborative and bringing a partnership mindset.
Flawless Execution: We pride ourselves on execution excellence, holding ourselves to the highest standards in everything we do. This requires careful planning, strong attention to detail and being accountable.
Risk Management: We believe it our collective responsibility to ensure our ecosystem remains safe and secure and place great emphasis on risk management, compliance and control.




Delivering the S64 vision would not be possible without creating an environment where team members feel welcomed, valued and supported. We go to extraordinary lengths to hire the very best people for every role and then provide them with rapid and rewarding career growth opportunities.
