Job Description

DePuy Synthes is recruiting for a(n) Technology Analyst, SC Technology, Digital Mfg Systems

Locations: Loughbeg, Ringaskiddy, Ireland and Leeds, Beeston, England.

Job Overview

The Digital Mfg Systems Analyst, SC Technology plays a key role in advancing digital capabilities across DePuy Synthes Supply Chain by connecting business needs with scalable technology solutions. This role partners with Supply Chain stakeholders, product teams, engineering teams, and technology partners to translate business priorities into product requirements and deliver low-code and automation capabilities that improve productivity, user experience, and operational effectiveness.

The successful candidate will support the vision and priorities for Supply Chain technology products, help translate product roadmap features into well-defined requirements, and collaborate across functions to deliver solutions using low-code and related digital technologies. Consistent with the Technical Product Management job family, the role will contribute to product planning, requirements definition, Agile delivery, experimentation, and continuous improvement.

Key Responsibilities


Partner with Supply Chain stakeholders to understand business challenges, process needs, and opportunities that can be addressed through low-code, automation, and digital technology solutions.
Translate product roadmap capabilities and Supply Chain business needs into well-defined product requirements, features, user stories, and acceptance criteria, consistent with the catalog role.
Lead discovery sessions and process assessments with Supply Chain users to identify opportunities to simplify workflows, automate manual activities, and improve the employee experience.
Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders, including analytics, design/user experience, engineering, and user enablement, to support product development and successful delivery.
Evaluate low-code and automation capabilities, including Microsoft Power Platform and related technologies, to determine fit for Supply Chain use cases.
Work with architecture, engineering, data, security, and compliance partners to ensure solutions align with applicable technology and governance requirements.
Support Agile product delivery by reviewing business needs, refining priorities, defining milestones and deliverables, and identifying opportunities and risks.
Partner with product and technology teams through testing, deployment, adoption, and ongoing enhancement of Supply Chain digital solutions.
Use feedback and solution performance insights to identify opportunities to enhance existing services and product features.
Develop clear product documentation, process flows, presentations, and communications appropriate for both business and technical audiences


Qualifications

Education


Bachelor’s degree required in Information Systems, Computer Science, Engineering, Supply Chain, Business, Data Analytics, or a related field.
Master’s degree, MBA, or equivalent advanced degree preferred.


Experience and Skills

Required


6+ years of relevant experience in technical product management, business analysis, technology consulting, digital product delivery, Supply Chain Technology, or related technology roles.
Experience gathering business needs and translating them into product requirements, features, user stories, acceptance criteria, and delivery priorities.
Experience with low-code/no-code technologies, such as Microsoft Power Platform, Appian, Mendix, ServiceNow, or comparable platforms.
Experience collaborating across business, product, engineering, architecture, analytics, and user-focused teams.
Working knowledge of Agile product delivery, including prioritization, milestones, deliverables, opportunities, and risks.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to simplify complex Supply Chain processes and translate them into actionable technology solutions.
Strong stakeholder management, facilitation, and communication skills with the ability to operate across technical and non-technical audiences.
Ability to manage multiple priorities and contribute effectively to cross-functional technology initiatives.


Preferred


Experience supporting Supply Chain Technology, including business processes across areas such as planning, manufacturing, quality, logistics, inventory, procurement, or distribution.
Hands-on experience delivering solutions using Microsoft Power Platform, including Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI, or related Microsoft technologies.
Experience with AI-enabled solutions, intelligent automation, workflow automation, or process digitization.
Experience working within healthcare, medical device, life sciences, manufacturing, or another regulated environment.
Experience with process mapping, value-stream analysis, or continuous improvement methodologies.
Knowledge of data analytics, reporting, dashboards, APIs, and enterprise system integrations.
Experience supporting technology adoption, organizational change, training, or end-user enablement.
Experience coaching colleagues or supporting product or business-analysis workstreams.


Other


English proficiency required; additional languages are a plus.
Up to 10-15% domestic and international travel may be required.
Certifications preferred: Microsoft Power Platform certifications, Agile or Scrum certifications, CBAP, PMI-PBA, or relevant AI/automation certifications.
