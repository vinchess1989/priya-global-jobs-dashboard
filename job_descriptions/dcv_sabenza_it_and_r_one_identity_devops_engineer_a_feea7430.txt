ABOUT THE POSITION

Join a focused delivery team working on Identity and Access Management solutions built around the One Identity platform. This is a hands-on DevOps engineering role with a dual functional and technical focus. You will contribute to the design, build, automation, and ongoing operation of IAM environments, with a clear emphasis on practical outcomes, security, and reliable delivery.


The position sits within an established team (no direct reports). The assignment is planned for five years, with a firm commitment to active engagement for a minimum of three years. No travel is required.


What You’ll Do




Own and deliver DevOps workstreams for One Identity / IAM solutions, covering both functional requirements and technical implementation.

Design, build, and maintain automation and pipelines using Azure DevOps, with supporting work in C#, VB .NET, PowerShell, and related technologies.

Apply strong information-security principles to every stage of the solution lifecycle.

Work pragmatically and proactively to move solutions from requirement to stable production outcome.

Collaborate as part of the wider team on implementation, enhancement, and operational improvement of IAM platforms.



Requirements
Solid experience with One Identity (or equivalent deep IAM platforms) and a clear understanding of Identity and Access Management concepts and practices.

Proven Azure DevOps capability (pipelines, automation, release management).

Hands-on development or scripting experience with technologies such as C#, VB .NET, and PowerShell.

A good information-security background or formal training.

A solution-oriented, pragmatic, and proactive working style.

8–10 years total IT industry experience, including at least 5 years of hands-on DevOps engineering.
