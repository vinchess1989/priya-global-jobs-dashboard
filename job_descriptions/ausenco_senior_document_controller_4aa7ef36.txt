Ausenco is a fast-growing company with big ideas. We redefine what’s possible in some of the world’s most complex projects and toughest environments. Delivering innovative, value-add consulting, project delivery, asset operations and maintenance solutions is what we do. From 21 offices in 9 countries, with projects in over 80 locations worldwide, we create sustainable outcomes for our people, clients and communities.

We’re looking for an experienced Senior Document Controller to join our team in Perth. In this role, you’ll be a key contributor to our Project Delivery Team, supporting projects across Australia West and Africa region.

You’ll take ownership of ensuring project documentation is managed efficiently and accurately, working closely with project teams to maintain compliance and streamline processes.

This is an exciting opportunity for a proactive, detail-oriented professional with experience in document control for engineering projects. If you’re a motivated team player who thrives in a collaborative environment and want to make an impact across a diverse and dynamic portfolio, we’d love to hear from you!

About The Role


Collaborate with Project Managers to develop clear, project-specific work instructions and processes.
Be the trusted advisor for document management best practices, supporting project teams and running training sessions on the nominated Document Management System (DMS) to build user capability.
Keep projects on track by maintaining accurate document registers and managing version and revision control.
Stay informed and proactive by participating in project meetings and reporting on the status of key deliverables.
Work closely with clients and external stakeholders to ensure compliance with document control procedures and confidentiality standards.
Manage and process Vendor Data throughout all phases of the project lifecycle and represent document control in vendor progress and kick-off meetings.


About You


Proven document control experience within engineering projects.
Proficiency in Aconex, SharePoint or similar document management systems, along with Microsoft Office.
Experience developing and implementing document control procedures and best practices.
Strong organisational, communication and attention-to-detail skills.
Experience mentoring junior team members and supporting team development.
Knowledge of ISO 9001 quality management systems (desirable).
