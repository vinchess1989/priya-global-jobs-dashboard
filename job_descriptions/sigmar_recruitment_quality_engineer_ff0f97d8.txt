Quality Engineer




About Your New Employer

This role is with an established and innovative manufacturing organisation specialising in the design and manufacture of highly engineered components and automated systems for the global automotive industry. As part of its continued growth, the organisation is seeking an experienced quality engineer professional to join its team and contribute to its ongoing success.




About Your New Job

Investigate customer complaints, conduct root cause analysis and implement corrective actions using the 8D methodology.
Drive continuous improvement projects to enhance product quality and manufacturing processes.
Prepare PPAP documentation and manage customer submissions and approval processes.
Work closely with production and engineering teams to improve internal quality performance, while developing PFMEAs, control plans and work instructions.
Support IATF 16949 quality system audits and ensure compliance with company EHS standards and safety requirements.




What Skills You Need

Relevant Engineering or Quality qualification, with a minimum of 3 years' experience in a manufacturing environment.
Strong technical aptitude, with statistical and process-oriented skills and excellent computer literacy.
Knowledge of quality tools and problem-solving techniques, including 8D, PPAP, PFMEA, SPC, MSA and Quality Auditing.
Customer-focused team player with strong communication, organisational and problem-solving skills, excellent attention to detail and the ability to work under pressure.
