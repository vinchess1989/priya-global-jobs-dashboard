Senior DevOps Engineer
Duration: 6 months
Location: Sandton, Gauteng

Description
The Senior DevOps Engineer designs, builds and runs the automation, CI/CD pipelines and cloud infrastructure that let the company's product teams ship software quickly, safely and reliably. As the senior hands-on engineer in the DevOps & Cloud team, the role sets technical standards, solves the hardest platform problems, and mentors other engineers, so that delivery stays fast while quality, security, availability and cloud cost are kept under control.

Requirements


Bachelor's degree in computer science, Information Technology, Engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

Experience:


Minimum of 6 years in DevOps, cloud, site reliability or infrastructure engineering, with at least 2 years at senior level.
Proven delivery of CI/CD pipelines and cloud infrastructure in complex, enterprise-scale or high-transaction environments.
Experience in fintech, payments or telecoms is an advantage.
Experience mentoring other engineers.

Skills:


Expert knowledge of CI/CD tools and practices, such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps or Jenkins.
Strong hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes, including Helm and managed services (EKS, AKS or GKE).
Infrastructure as code with Terraform or equivalent.
Solid experience with at least one major cloud platform: AWS, Azure or Google Cloud.
Configuration management with Ansible, Puppet or Chef.
Scripting in Python, Bash or PowerShell; Go is an advantage.
Monitoring and observability tools, such as Prometheus, Grafana, ELK or Datadog.
DevSecOps practices: secrets management, vulnerability scanning and policy as code.
Strong Linux administration, networking (DNS, load balancing, VPNs) and Git workflows.
Clear written and verbal communication, and calm problem-solving under pressure
