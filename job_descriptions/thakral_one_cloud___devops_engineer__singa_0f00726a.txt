As a Cloud & DevOps Engineer, you will help build, automate, and operate secure, reliable, and scalable cloud environments across public cloud platforms. You will contribute to cloud engineering standards, DevOps automation, Infrastructure as Code initiatives, and project delivery activities while working closely with technical and business stakeholders.




What You'll Do and How You'll Succeed




Cloud Infrastructure and Platform Engineering

Design, deploy, and support cloud services across Microsoft Azure, AWS, and Google Cloud environments.
Implement and support cloud infrastructure and platform services that meet security, reliability, operational, and business requirements.
Support cloud networking, identity and access management, security, monitoring, backup, high availability, and disaster recovery capabilities.
Contribute to cloud architecture patterns, standards, reusable templates, and configuration management practices.




DevOps and Automation

Support the build, maintenance, and execution of CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, or similar platforms.
Develop and maintain reusable Terraform modules and templates for standardised cloud deployments.
Manage infrastructure using Git, pull requests, code reviews, and automated deployment workflows.
Minimise manual configuration activities and reduce configuration drift through automation.




Project Delivery and Operations

Participate in project delivery activities including requirements analysis, implementation, testing, documentation, and handover.
Work closely with architecture, security, infrastructure, networking, application, and operations teams throughout the delivery lifecycle.
Troubleshoot Terraform, CI/CD, cloud infrastructure, networking, and deployment issues.
Contribute to engineering artefacts, technical documentation, and operational standards.




We'd Love to Hear From You If...




Experience

Experience working with cloud platforms such as Microsoft Azure, AWS, or Google Cloud.
Experience supporting DevOps, cloud infrastructure, or platform engineering initiatives.
Exposure to project delivery environments and cloud engineering practices.




Technical Expertise

Experience with CI/CD concepts and tools such as Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions.
Foundational knowledge or hands on experience with Infrastructure as Code, preferably Terraform.
Working knowledge of cloud services including compute, storage, networking, identity, and databases.
Familiarity with Microsoft Entra ID, role based access control, multi factor authentication, and least privilege access concepts.
Understanding of high availability, backup, business continuity, and disaster recovery principles.
Knowledge of Git, branching strategies, pull requests, and code review practices.
Experience with Python, PowerShell, or Bash scripting is an advantage.
Exposure to containers and Kubernetes platforms such as AKS, EKS, or GKE is a plus.
Understanding of cloud security, monitoring, governance, and configuration management practices.
Cloud, Terraform, DevOps, Kubernetes, or security certifications are advantageous.




Ways of Working

Strong teamwork and collaboration skills.
Ability to communicate technical information clearly to both technical and non technical audiences.
Comfortable working with stakeholders across engineering and project delivery teams.
Automation first mindset with a commitment to continuous learning and improvement.
Values documentation, standardisation, testing, peer reviews, and controlled production changes.

Who We Are




Thakral One is a consulting and technology services company headquartered in Singapore, with a pan-Asian presence. We focus primarily around technology-driven consulting, adoption of value-added bespoke solutions, enabling enhanced decision support through data analytics, and embracing possibilities in the cloud. We are heavily inclined towards building capabilities collaboratively with clients and believe strongly in improving grounded and practical outcomes.




This approach is possible through our partnership with leading global technology providers and internal R&D teams. Our clients come from Financial Services, Banking, Telco, Government, Healthcare, and Consumer-oriented organisations.
