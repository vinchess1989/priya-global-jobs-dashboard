Position Summary

This is a Fixed Term Specific Purpose Contract of 9 - 12 months to cover Maternity Leave

The purpose of this position is to support calibration, equipment qualification and test method validation projects in a regulated environment.

Authorised To Perform Specific Activities Including


develop, modify, verify and validate methods under guidance
analysis of results, statements of conformity and opinions / interpretations under guidance
report, review and authorise results


Duties


Support Qualification and Validation planning activities and attending daily / weekly meetings where required 10%
Create and execute validation documents – 15%
Create and execute URS, IQ, OQ, PQ, TMV, Risk assessments– 15%
Review data and generate reports – 15%
Review calibrations certificates – 20%
Cross train to calibration methods if required – 10%
Document technical review and approval – 15%
Support equipment and test method improvements – 10%


Duties - cont'd

Education Degree


Bachelor's Degree in Science or Engineering General


Required Experience


Working in a regulated environment (Med Dev, Pharma etc.)
Customer facing activities.
Hands on test development and execution
Science/Engineering
Excellent communication & technical writing abilities
