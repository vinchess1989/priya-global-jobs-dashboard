Summary:

UST is hiring for IT Business Analysis




Experience: 6+ years

Location: Bangalore




A Business Analyst with 6+years of experience, strong Jira + Confluence + ServiceNow expertise, Agile project experience, stakeholder management skills, and preferably exposure to Automotive/Connected Vehicle/Telematics environments would be the best match for this requirement




Mandatory Requirements

6+ years of Business Analysis experience in IT/software projects.

Strong expertise in:

Requirement gathering & elicitation
Functional & non-functional requirement analysis
Gap analysis & impact analysis
User story creation
Process flows, use cases, acceptance criteria
Agile/Scrum experience.
Stakeholder management and end-user interaction.
UAT coordination and testing support.
Incident, change, and problem management exposure.
Strong documentation and communication skills.

Must-Have Tools

Jira
Confluence
ServiceNow
MS Office (Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook)
SharePoint

Key Responsibilities

Gather and analyze business requirements.
Work with Product Owners, business users, and technical teams.
Manage backlog and requirements traceability.
Support sprint planning, backlog refinement, and Agile ceremonies.
Coordinate UAT and defect management.
Support runtime/production issues and continuous improvement initiatives.

Preferred / Good-to-Have

Automotive, Manufacturing, or Commercial Vehicle domain experience.
Connected Vehicle / Telematics / Digital Services exposure.
Azure or AWS knowledge.
DevSecOps exposure.
Power BI or Qlik Sense experience.
Experience working with global/distributed teams.




If your experience and skills align with the job requirements, please send your updated resume to sujina.surendran@ust.com. We look forward to hearing from you.
