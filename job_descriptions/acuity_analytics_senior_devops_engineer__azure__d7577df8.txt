About us




Ascent has recently been acquired by Acuity Analytics. This is both a significant milestone for us and a tremendous opportunity for you. Acuity Analytics is a business with a strong global reputation, an impressive client base and ambitious growth plans. We deliver deep insights and domain-led digital transformation to high-growth and heavily regulated organizations. To our customers, we bring a partnership that provides the talent, technology and capability to enhance performance and operational efficiency.




About the role

We are looking for a senior, hands-on DevOps Engineer (Azure) to support a high-priority initiative for our client. This is a critical position: you will work closely with a solution architect on orchestration-related topics and help get solutions reliably from development into production.

The core of the role is CI/CD: designing, building and maintaining pipelines, including artifact promotion across environments.




What you will do

Design, build and maintain CI/CD pipelines, including artifact promotion across environments
Build integrations with Azure services
Automate and script tasks in Python
Set up and maintain secure authentication for pipelines
Run and support services in production
Support orchestration-related topics in close collaboration with the solution architect
Contribute to the adoption of AI tooling such as GitHub Copilot in an enterprise setting




Must-haves

Hands-on CI/CD experience: designing, building and maintaining pipelines, including artifact promotion across environments
Integrations with Azure services
Python scripting
Knowledge of authentication mechanisms for pipelines
Experience running services in production
Experience with GitHub Copilot and AI adoption in an enterprise environment
Solution architecture background
Hands-on mindset
Broad view of data topics, including Databricks as a standalone platform




Nice-to-haves

Azure Databricks (Workflows, Asset Bundles) and Spark
LLM orchestration and monitoring, ideally with Azure OpenAI
Azure Document Intelligence and AI-ready document processing
Experience aligning several platform teams and managing dependencies
