What will your typical day look like?

This role is responsible for driving the successful planning, coordination, and delivery of complex technology initiatives across multiple teams. The individual will provide Release Train Engineer support, facilitate SAFe and Agile ceremonies, manage project scope, schedules, budgets, risks, and dependencies, and ensure teams remain aligned throughout the delivery lifecycle. This role will also partner closely with business, technology, and leadership stakeholders to remove impediments, promote Agile best practices, and support successful program outcomes.

Primary Duties:


Provide release train engineer support services including, but not limited to, coordinating the teams from a product standpoint to help ensure that multiple teams working on the same release product and features plan and execute as a single release train, identifying and managing risks, facilitating release planning meetings, supporting the scrum of scrums, and resolving and escalating impediments
Facilitates ART level ceremonies such as PI Planning, Scrum of Scrums, Sprint Demos, Inspect and Adapt
Coach team members and other stakeholders in SAFe and Agile practices, as needed
Work with management to ensure smooth rollout of SAFe to the organization, including providing input and direction to help establish department standards and procedures
Ensure that all projects are delivered on-time, within scope and within budget
Assist in the definition of project scope and objectives, involving all relevant internal stakeholders and ensuring technical feasibility
Develop a comprehensive project plan to monitor and track progress while also keeping clients and staff aware of key milestones
Manage changes to the project scope, project schedule, and project costs using appropriate verification techniques
Measure performance using appropriate project management tools and techniques
Report and escalate to management as needed
Manage the relationship with the client and relevant stakeholders
Perform risk management to minimize potential risks
Establish and maintain relationships with third parties/vendors
Create and maintain comprehensive project documentation
Meet with clients to take detailed ordering briefs and clarify specific requirements of each project
Delegate project tasks based on junior staff members' individual strengths, skill sets, and experience levels
Track project performance, specifically to analyze the successful completion of short and long-term goals
Meet budgetary objectives and make adjustments to project constraints based on financial analysis
Use and continually develop leadership skills
Develop spreadsheets, diagrams and process maps to document needs
Assists Engagement Manager with status reporting as required by the S4 Technology Team leadership
Responds proactively to both business and program issues and escalates appropriately


Limited travel may be required.
