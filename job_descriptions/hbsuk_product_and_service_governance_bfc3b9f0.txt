ABOUT THE ROLE

The Product & Service Governance Lead is a critical role responsible for establishing, maintaining, and evolving our product and service governance framework. This individual will ensure continuous compliance with MHRA Class II medical device standards and other relevant regulations, minimising risk and enabling the continuous enhancement of our offerings. This role is pivotal in safeguarding product integrity and promoting a user-centric approach across all product lifecycles, collaborating with various internal and external stakeholders.




MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES

Product and Service Governance Oversight: Own the continuous implementation and adherence to all product governance processes. This includes maintaining and evolving our comprehensive Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) to align with evolving regulatory requirements and best practices, including all cybersecurity and usability studies.
Regulatory Compliance & Risk Management: Champion MHRA and relevant regulatory compliance, integrating updates into our product and service lifecycle. Lead product risk review meetings (with representation from operations, clinical, and product), ensuring all risk registers are current at each release point and maintaining the product and class 2 medical device technical file. Audit the effectiveness of controls and mitigations, ensuring they are safe, effective, timely, and meet regulation.
Release Management & Documentation: Oversee the release sign-off process, ensuring all necessary documentation for Class II medical device registration is meticulously prepared and maintained for every product iteration. This includes verifying contributions from all team members to ensure compliance.
Feedback & Continuous Improvement: Drive product and service enhancement by owning the accumulation of feedback across the business and running monthly review meetings to action insights, ensuring these insights feed back into governance processes.
Governance Representation: Serve as the primary product representative at key governance forums, including Clinical Governance, quarterly reviews, and complaints meetings, risk meetings, ensuring product integrity and a user-centric approach are maintained.
Cross-functional Governance Collaboration: Collaborate closely with the Director of Product & Services, Product Manager, and other relevant stakeholders to ensure seamless integration of governance processes across product strategy.
Data Privacy: Liaise with the Data Protection Officer to ensure data privacy is always considered in design.




FLEXIBILITY

This job profile is not intended to be exhaustive, and it is likely that duties may be altered from time to time in the light of changing circumstances, in discussion with the post holder. This role profile is intended to provide a broad outline of the main responsibilities only.




EDUCATION QUALIFICATIONS & TRAINING

Educated to degree level or equivalent in a relevant field (e.g., Quality Management, Regulatory Affairs, Product Management).
Experience with quality management systems (e.g., ISO 13485).




EXPERIENCE

Proven experience in product governance, regulatory affairs, or quality assurance within the healthcare technology or medical device industry (Class II medical device experience highly preferred).
Strong understanding of MHRA, GDPR, and other relevant healthcare regulations.
Experience in managing risk registers and leading risk review meetings.
Demonstrated ability to manage documentation for regulatory submissions and product releases.
Experience in driving continuous improvement processes.
Knowledge of Privacy by Design principles.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.




KNOWLEDGE AND SKILLS

In-depth knowledge of product lifecycle management within a regulated environment.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Ability to interpret and apply complex regulatory guidelines.
Exceptional organizational skills and attention to detail.
Proficiency in documentation management systems.
Ability to collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams.
