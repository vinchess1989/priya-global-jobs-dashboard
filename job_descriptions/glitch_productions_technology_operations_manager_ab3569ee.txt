About Us

Glitch Productions is an independent animation studio based in Australia making original, long-form animated shows distributed on YouTube. Our channel has over 23 million subscribers, tens of millions of views per episode, and is still growing strong. Our studio is filled with incredibly talented and passionate artists that are determined to change the way animation is produced and treated in the industry. Our biggest IP; Digital Circus, has amassed over 1.5 billion views across the entire series, and we have a lot more still to come.

About The Role

Glitch Productions is looking for an experienced Technology Operations Manager to oversee the day-to-day operations of our technology and systems functions. Working closely with our department heads, you will establish effective processes, manage technical project delivery, and drive operational improvements, with a primary focus on software development and agile management.

This is a senior, hands-on role requiring strong independent judgment, critical thinking, and the ability to bring structure to complex technical environments. We're looking for someone who can proactively solve problems, challenge existing practices, and build scalable operational systems alongside the department heads rather than simply coordinate tasks.

What You’ll Do


Oversee daily technology operations, including priorities, resources, schedules, and project delivery.
Design and implement operational processes and workflows to improve efficiency and accountability.
Own and optimise Jira workflows, project structures, agile practices, reporting, and governance across technology teams.
Manage technical roadmaps, project milestones, dependencies, risks, and deliverables.
Establish operating cadences, including sprint planning, backlog management, and project reviews.
Identify operational bottlenecks, resolve challenges, and make independent decisions to keep teams moving.
Manage resource allocation and prioritisation across IT and software development teams.
Lead change management for new technologies and processes, including rollout planning, communications, and training.
Drive company-wide adoption of new systems, addressing resistance and identifying areas for improvement.
Establish and maintain technology governance, operational standards, and documentation.
Partner with technology leadership to evaluate priorities, challenge existing practices, and improve team operations.


What We're Looking For


Extensive experience in technology operations, software delivery, technical program management, or a similar senior role.
Advanced Jira expertise, including implementation, workflow design, automation, reporting, and governance.
Strong understanding of software development lifecycles, agile methodologies, backlog management, and release planning.
Proven experience establishing operational processes, standards, and delivery frameworks for technical teams.
Excellent critical thinking, independent judgment, and a practical, solutions-focused approach to problem-solving.
Confidence partnering with technical leadership, challenging decisions constructively, and driving accountability.
Working knowledge of IT operations, service management, infrastructure projects, and technical support workflows.
Strong project management, resource planning, prioritisation, and risk management skills.
Ability to balance thoroughness with speed, keeping solutions practical and proportionate to the problem.
Demonstrated experience leading organisational change, including system rollouts, stakeholder engagement, and user adoption.
Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with confidence working across departments and levels of seniority.
Technically adaptable, quick to learn new systems, and comfortable working across a range of software tools.
Proactive and self-directed, with the confidence to take ownership of ambiguous or evolving work.
An engaging personality, collaborative mindset, and genuine enthusiasm for improving how teams operate.


Nice to have


Experience with the Atlassian ecosystem beyond Jira, including Confluence, Jira Service Management, and integrations or automation between tools.
Experience working with Airtable or similar low-code database tools.
Experience working in a fast-growing company where systems and processes are still being developed and refined.
Experience working in animation, gaming, entertainment, or another creative industry.
An understanding of or interest in the arts, animation, gaming, and/or ‘internet nerd’ culture.


What We Offer


Competitive salary and package commensurate with experience
A flexible environment with opportunities for hybrid work.
A fun office environment where individual input is valued.
Regular work functions and free entry to conventions and events.
Close to public transport in the heart of Parramatta and Melbourne CBD.
Regular company breakfasts/lunches.
A diverse team that values independent input and career development.


Our values

Glitch Productions Has An Art-first Mindset. Regardless Of Which Team We’re In, Every Single Team Member’s Core Mission Is To Create The Best Art Possible, And Delight Our Audience That Has Given Us So Much. We Achieve This Mission Through Our Values

Passion: First and foremost, every team member has a passion for the work we do and why we do it. It’s what allows us to contribute our creative energy, pitch new concepts, and give everything we make a personal touch that resonates with our fans.

Innovation: From cobbling together sets in a living room, to inventing new pipelines using breakthrough technology, we have always had a focus on innovation and new ideas. We always try to push the boundaries of our industry and keep things fresh and exciting for both the team and our audience.

Teamwork: Animation takes a big team of incredibly talented people and everyone is always ready to pitch in and lend a hand. We believe that our diversity and range of skills are what allows us to create works none of us could do on our own.

Integrity: Our integrity is the heart of our core mission. It’s what allows us to do right for our projects and for each other.

At Glitch Productions, we’re passionate about fostering a diverse and inclusive workplace that reflects our values as a creative studio.

We hire based on skills, passion and potential, and celebrate the diverse perspectives that make our team stronger. We welcome people of all races, genders, sexual orientations, gender identities, ages, educational backgrounds, abilities, and experiences.

Simply put, if you’re a good person and share our passion for animation, we want to hear from you!
