About Role

We are hiring a Senior DevOps Engineer with 4+ years of experience with hands-on expertise in Azure cloud infrastructure, CI/CD automation, containerization, Kubernetes, and Infrastructure as Code. Experienced in building scalable, secure, and reliable cloud environments and automating end-to-end deployment workflows.

Working Hours: (6:00 PM IST to 2:00 AM or 3:00 AM IST)

What You'll Do

As part of the job

Multi-Cloud Management


Design, implement, and manage multi-cloud infrastructure environments (Azure Primary, AWS, Google Cloud) to support the development and deployment of our applications.
Troubleshoot and resolve issues related to infrastructure, deployment, and performance across multiple cloud platforms.
Work closely with security teams to implement best practices for securing multi-cloud environments and ensuring compliance with industry standards across multiple cloud platforms.
Implement highly available and scalable systems, ensuring proper redundancy and fault tolerance across multiple cloud platforms.
Conduct regular security assessments & vulnerability scanning across multiple cloud platforms.
Implement and maintain security controls and access management policies across multiple cloud platforms.


Automation And Continuous Integration/Deployment (CI/CD)


Develop and maintain automated deployment pipelines using tools like Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, or CircleCI.
Implement continuous integration and continuous deployment practices to enable faster and more reliable software releases.
Streamline the software build, test, and release processes, ensuring efficient delivery of software artifacts.


Infrastructure As Code (IaC)


Design, build, and maintain the company's infrastructure using cloud platforms (such as AWS, Azure, or GCP) and infrastructure-as-code tools (such as Terraform or CloudFormation).
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define infrastructure requirements and make architectural decisions.


Monitoring And Troubleshooting


Implement and maintain monitoring, alerting, and logging solutions to ensure high availability and performance of cloud-based applications
Conduct performance analysis and optimization of infrastructure components, including servers, databases, and network configurations.
Troubleshoot infrastructure and deployment issues, working closely with development and operations teams.
Perform root cause analysis of incidents and implement preventive measures.
Overseeing routine maintenance procedures and performing diagnostic tests across multi cloud platforms.


Collaboration And Documentation


Collaborate with cross-functional teams to optimize application performance, scalability, and reliability.
Stay up to date with industry trends, emerging technologies, and best practices in DevOps and multi-cloud environments.
Mentoring junior software developers in gaining experience and assuming DevOps responsibilities.
Create and maintain technical documentation, including infrastructure diagrams, configuration guides, and standard operating procedures.
Share knowledge and best practices with team members and contribute to the overall growth of the DevOps practice.


Requirements

Must have technical skills


Bachelor's degree in computer science, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent work experience).
Proven 4+ experience as a DevOps Engineer or in a similar role, with a focus on infrastructure automation, deployment, and operations.
Hands-on expertise in architecting and managing cloud-native infrastructure on Azure.
Proficient in building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps, containerizing workloads with Docker, and orchestrating deployments with Kubernetes (AKS).
Skilled in infrastructure as code (ARM/Bicep/Terraform), implementing Prometheus-based monitoring stacks, and enforcing cloud security controls across environments.
Strong independent contributor with experience in automating end-to-end delivery workflows, optimizing system reliability, and managing secure, scalable infrastructure at scale.
