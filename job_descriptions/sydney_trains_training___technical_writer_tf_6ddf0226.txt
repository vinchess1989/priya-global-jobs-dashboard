You’re someone who wants to create outcomes that have a real impact on the people of NSW.

You know that to make a meaningful contribution there needs to be connection and collaboration across the teams. Here’s your opportunity to help our people make a big impact.

In this role, you'll:

Be working as part of a team delivering training and documentation for critical Control Systems projects, ensuring critical rail systems are supported by clear, accurate and compliant documentation.

You’ll develop technical documentation, user manuals and training materials that support safe, effective operations.

You’ll build clear, compliant documentation and training solutions in a complex, safety critical environment, driving consistency and continuous improvement.

This is a full-time fixed-term opportunity for up to 12 months, based in Flemington, Sydney NSW.

For more information on this position and business unit, view the role description and information pack.

About You

Your technical writing, communication and stakeholder engagement skills, developed in rail, engineering, technology or similar environments, will see you deliver clear, high-quality documentation and training materials. You’re skilled at turning complex information into practical, user focused content.

You’re experienced in developing documentation for critical systems, engaging multiple stakeholders, interpreting technical requirements and maintaining accuracy in structured environments.

You’ve got knowledge of railway signalling, train operations or complex engineering systems, strong problem-solving skills, and experience producing professional technical documentation.

Who We Are

Transport for NSW provides a safe, integrated, and efficient transport system. We connect people, communities and industry every day.

At Sydney Trains, our rail services keep Sydney moving by putting our customers at the centre of everything we do. We work with our local communities to deliver safe, timely and efficient rail services 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

Our trains and network are evolving to meet the needs of our customers now and well into the future. We’re continuing to integrate technological innovations that help us deliver a smarter and more sustainable network.
