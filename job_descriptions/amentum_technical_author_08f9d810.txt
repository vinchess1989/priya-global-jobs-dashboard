About The Opportunity

Amentum is looking for an experienced Technical Author who has an excellent understanding of the AMS review process.

Qualifications, Skills & Experience


Full understanding of the AMS review process
Expertise on the correct formatting and arrangement of EDF Technical Documentation. Enable AMS creation and review process
Interpret engineering drawings and specifications to produce clear concise content in the correct format and context required by the document control centre and the end user
Full, tracking, project compliance and control of project documents through to the successful completion of the project. Ranging from: Project contract enquiry technical specification (CETS), factory acceptance test (FAT), project life-time record pack (PTRP, operation and maintenance manuals (O&M's)
