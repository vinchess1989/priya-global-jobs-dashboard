Test Automation Engineer - Strong intermediate level looking for the next opportunity




Exceptional human with great communication skills and a team first mindset then come Join our QAEnhance Team
Strong' intermediate' level looking for the next opportunity to grow and contribute to the future of automation testing.
Work with clients and contribute to the test automation & orchestration design at an enterprise scale
Initial assignment with a highly respected NZ and Global brand
Hybrid Working - home + our office or on-site with client




What does our next exceptional team member possess?

Great communication skills and a team first mindset - if not stop reading and go back to scrolling
Modern current automation frameworks - Playwright, Microsoft cloud -aligned tooling
Java, Selenium is a bonus not a necessity
AI Automation testing embedded in CI/CD pipelines [GitHub Actions - Azure Dev/Ops]
Passion, curiosity and awareness
IQ - EQ - MQ - SQ - Yes, we think about things differently and it's what sets us apart!




What You Will Be Doing

As a strong intermediate QAEngineer

You will be working with a Team of QAEnhancers and closely aligned to one of our exceptional team members who already has the client product knowledge so you won't be flying solo.
You will hit the ground running working on a modernised automation framework Playwright - your happy place.
To build your product knowledge you will start by running regression for current releases and doing all the normal things in terms of operational practices during a regression. You will be using AI to update existing test cases.
In between these and as your product knowledge grows you will be working closely with the product team member automation lead on AI initiatives and improving the regression and deployment lifecycle.
Engaging with clients, building credibility quickly.
Contribute to our growing AI-enabled testing offering and wider capability.




What We're Looking For - When we meet you, we will instantly know

First and foremost - great communication skills and team first mindset
Strong intermediate level - wanting to grow and wanting to invest in us to do so
You speak with confidence - when we ask a technical question you describe with ease because you have done it so many times
Genuine curiosity about AI and how it can transform quality engineering
Can handle complexity and a fast paced environment
No AI responses - just exceptional humans!




Who are We

QAEnhance is a boutique specialist quality assurance business, and we pride ourselves on not being a body shop.




Our model is to partner with our clients to provide real business outcomes where the value is real and measured.




We only hire exceptional people that are good humans first, who love working in a team with like-minded, passionate QA engineers




If you think this is you, apply now by following the button below or connect with us on via our website www.qaenhance.co.nz
