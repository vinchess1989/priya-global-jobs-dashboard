High-Impact Career Opportunity




Are you ready to step into a high-visibility role where your technical expertise directly protects product quality and enhances global human health?




Partnering with an industry-leading global healthcare brand, this is a career-defining opportunity to drive operational excellence across a multi-year strategic initiative. The organisation operates at the forefront of healthcare innovation, delivering best-in-class nutritional, diagnostic, and pharmaceutical products relied upon by millions worldwide.




As a Validation Engineer, you will own delivery of mission-critical validation protocols within an enterprise-scale, highly regulated manufacturing environment. Operating within a high-performing, cross-functional engineering team, you will steer equipment qualification projects, drive compliance standards, and champion continuous manufacturing improvements that keep life-changing healthcare solutions flowing to global markets.




Why You’ll Love This Role

Create Lasting Impact: Your work directly guarantees the safety, quality, and supply of products that save and improve lives globally.
Enterprise-Scale Visibility: Gain hands-on exposure across the full project lifecycle within a market-leading enterprise.
Autonomy & Flexibility: Enjoy a modern hybrid work model, blending remote work with collaborative execution at an industry-leading tech hub.
Uncapped Career Growth: Accelerate your development within a global culture designed to recognize, foster, and advance top-tier engineering talent.




What Success Looks Like

Deliver measurable outcomes by successfully qualifying modern equipment on schedule within a fast-moving, high-standard facility.
Achieve operational excellence through flawless compliance execution, minimizing risk and ensuring zero-defect quality transitions.
Establish best-in-class practices in validation documentation and change control that elevate overall plant performance.




What You’ll Bring

3+ years of hands-on validation engineering experience within regulated manufacturing environments.
Proven track record in pharma, medical devices, or food manufacturing handling FAT/SAT, commissioning, and change control.
Strong technical leadership and stakeholder engagement skills to drive cross-functional outcomes.
