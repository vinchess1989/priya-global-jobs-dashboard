The Council for Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) is a leading scientific and technology research organisation that researches, develops, localises and diffuses technologies to accelerate socio-economic prosperity in South Africa. The organisation's work contributes to industrial development and supports a capable state.

Senior Business Analyst (Two positions)

(Twenty-four months contract)

About The Job

The CSIR has two fixed term contract vacancies for Senior Business Analysts in the e-Government Impact Area within the Next Generation Enterprises and Institutions Cluster. The incumbents will be responsible for identifying, researching, analysing and documenting business processes and user, functional and non-functional requirements. This position is based in Pretoria and will report to the Impact Area Manager.

For more information about the division/cluster/portfolio, please visit https://www.csir.co.za/nextgen-enterprises-and-institutions.-systems

Key Responsibilities


Map current and future service journeys with participating departments, including the steps, rules, decisions, documents, data and handoffs involved and the points where citizens get stuck.
Analyse business processes to identify problems, root causes and options. Recommend improvements based on feasibility, benefits and impact.
Elicit, challenge and validate business and user needs, resolve conflicts between them, and express them as epics, features, user stories, acceptance criteria and non-functional requirements.
Facilitate the prioritisation of requirements with use-case leads and the product owner. Keep requirements traceable from their source to delivered and tested functionality.
Identify the information each service needs, where it comes from and who is authoritative for it. Work with the Solution Architect, Data Architect and engineers to document system and data requirements and gaps.
Identify where a requirement raises privacy, consent, security, legal or standards questions, including what information is shared, retained, corrected or revoked, and route it to the appropriate owner rather than resolving it.
Use available evidence on user experience, including for people without smartphones or reliable internet access, to identify pain points and inform design with the UX Designer.
Support user acceptance testing by defining scenarios, test data and coverage against acceptance criteria. Document and triage defects and confirm that business and user needs are met.
Assess how changes affect departmental roles, processes, policies, data and systems. Help define measurable outcomes and support departmental readiness before services go live.


Qualifications And Skills


A Bachelor's degree in informatics, information technology, computer science, engineering, public administration, economics or related fields with at least five years' practical experience working in software development life cycle management, stakeholder engagement and client relationship management;
Experience with the following would be required:
Working in multidisciplinary technology development teams as a Business Analyst;
Gathering, documenting, analysing, validating, and managing business and functional requirements;
Business process modelling, process mapping, and workflow analysis;
Agile software design, development, and implementation;
Agile and Waterfall project delivery methodologies;
Agile frameworks such as Scrum and SAFe;
Stakeholder engagement, workshop facilitation, and requirements elicitation;
User story development, use case modelling, and acceptance criteria definition;
Requirements traceability, change management, and impact analysis;
Supporting solution design, testing, and user acceptance testing (UAT);
Business process improvement and digital transformation initiatives.
Experience in the following would be advantageous:
Power BI;
SQL;
Tableau;
JIRA, Confluence, Azure DevOps, or similar collaboration and requirements management tools;
Business intelligence, reporting, and data analytics
Professional Business Analysis certifications such as CBAP, CCBA, PMI-PBA, or Agile Business Analyst certifications.
Proven track record of contributing to successful project implementations and business transformation initiatives; and
Leading workshops, facilitating requirements sessions and supporting change management activities.
