Your new company

Permanent opportunity | Sydney CBD

$180,000–$185,000 + superannuation




Join a major multi-year technology transformation and combine hands-on business analysis with the leadership of a small BA team. This role will suit an experienced Lead Business Analyst or BA Manager with strong ServiceNow and IT service management knowledge, followed by experience across Identity and Access Management.




Your new role

Around 80% of your role will remain hands-on, leading complex technology initiatives from discovery through to implementation and operational readiness. The remaining focus will be on coaching Business Analysts, maintaining quality standards and coordinating BA capability across the technology portfolio.




You will:

Lead end-to-end business analysis across complex technology projects
Shape requirements across ServiceNow ITSM, ITOM, CMDB, discovery and service mapping
Support service catalogue, employee self-service and monitoring integration initiatives
Apply ITIL principles to service ownership, service dependencies and operational processes
Define current and future-state processes, requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria
Maintain traceability through testing, implementation and operational readiness
Translate risk and control obligations into practical workflows, approvals and reporting
Define requirements across IAM, Identity Governance and Administration and Privileged Access Management
Coach and support a small team of Business Analysts
Work closely with Technology, Cyber, Risk, business teams and external partners




What you'll need to succeed

Extensive senior or lead-level Business Analysis experience across complex technology programs
Hands-on experience with ServiceNow ITSM
Exposure to ITOM, CMDB, discovery, service mapping or monitoring integrations
A practical understanding of ITIL, service ownership and service dependencies
Knowledge of ServiceNow’s Common Service Data Model would be highly regarded
Experience across IAM, IGA or PAM initiatives
Understanding of identity lifecycles, access governance, roles, entitlements and privileged access
Experience coaching, mentoring or managing Business Analysts while remaining hands-on
Strong workshop facilitation, process mapping and requirements management skills
The ability to work effectively across technical, business, risk, cyber and vendor stakeholders
Experience within banking, financial services or another regulated environment will be advantageous.




What you'll get in return

Permanent salary of $180,000–$185,000 + superannuation
A senior role combining hands-on delivery and BA leadership
Exposure to a funded, multi-year technology transformation
The opportunity to influence ServiceNow, IT service management and identity initiatives
A collaborative environment with strong visibility across the technology portfolio
