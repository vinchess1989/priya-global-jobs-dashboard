Somnath Dey
Somnath Dey

Experience: 12+ years in software delivery, 4+ years managing engineering teams




Role summary: Accountable for delivery, people and engineering health across both squads (Mobile Apps and Digital), and the main day-to-day engagement point for the client's technology and product leaders.

Key responsibilities

Own sprint and release delivery for both squads: scope, schedule, quality, and budget
Run Agile ceremonies and governance; report status, risks, and dependencies to client stakeholders
Lead, coach, and grow onsite and offshore team members; manage capacity and onboarding
Drive engineering practices: definition of done, code quality, test automation, CI/CD and DevOps maturity
Track delivery metrics (velocity, cycle time, DORA metrics, defect leakage) and act on them
Lead adoption of AI-assisted development across the squads with sensible guardrails
Handle escalations, change requests and commercial conversations with the account team




Core skills

Proven management of distributed onsite/offshore teams
Agile/Scrum/Kanban delivery and tools (Jira, Confluence, Azure DevOps)
Solid technical background in web and/or mobile (ideally .NET/Sitecore or React Native)
Working knowledge of cloud (Azure/AWS), CI/CD pipelines, DevOps, and AI developer tools
Stakeholder management, risk management, and reporting




Nice to have: Scrum Master/SAFe or PMP certification, insurance or financial-services domain, experience on managed-services or squad-based engagements

Soft skills: calm under pressure, clear communicator, builds trust with clients and teams
