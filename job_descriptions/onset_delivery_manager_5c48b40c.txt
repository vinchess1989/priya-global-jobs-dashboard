Delivery Manager – Technology Sydney | Fintech | Hands-on Delivery

We’re partnering with an Australian fintech looking for a Delivery Manager to work closely with the CTO and bring greater structure, visibility and momentum across its Technology portfolio.

This isn’t a traditional PMO role. You’ll operate across Engineering and the broader business, taking sometimes loosely defined problems and turning them into clear, executable plans.

The role You’ll be responsible for:

Driving multiple technology initiatives from requirements through to production
Coordinating Engineering and stakeholders across Product, Operations, Risk, Compliance, Sales and Marketing
Creating clear plans, milestones, dependencies and delivery visibility
Identifying risks and resolving blockers before they impact delivery
Supporting quarterly Technology planning and prioritisation
Improving delivery practices and ways of working without adding unnecessary process
Using Jira and Confluence to maintain visibility and accountability
What we’re looking for You’ll have:
5+ years’ experience in Technology Delivery, Project or Program Delivery
Strong end-to-end technology delivery experience
Enough technical understanding to work confidently with Engineering
Experience managing multiple initiatives and competing priorities
Strong stakeholder management across technical and business teams
The ability to work through ambiguity and create structure where it doesn’t already exist
A pragmatic approach to Agile, Waterfall or hybrid delivery
Experience across fintech, lending, banking, payments or financial services is a must. Salesforce exposure would also be valuable.
Why join? This is an opportunity to work directly with the CTO, take real ownership and influence how Technology delivery operates.
There’s no large PMO or rigid playbook to inherit. You’ll have the autonomy to introduce the right level of structure, stay close to the technology and help build a stronger delivery capability as the business evolves.

If you enjoy bringing clarity to complexity and getting technology initiatives moving and comfortable to be in the office 5 days a week, we’d love to hear from you.
