At Alstom, we understand transport networks and what moves people. From high-speed trains, metros, monorails, and trams, to turnkey systems, services, infrastructure, signalling and digital mobility, we offer our diverse customers the broadest portfolio in the industry. Every day, more than 80 000 colleagues lead the way to greener and smarter mobility worldwide, connecting cities as we reduce carbon and replace cars.

Could you be the full-time Project Configuration & Change Controller in Toronto, ON, we’re looking for?

Your future role

Take on a new challenge and apply your technical expertise in a cutting-edge field. You will work alongside collaborative and innovative teammates.

You will play a vital role in keeping the project configuration under control throughout its lifecycle and managing the change process.

Day-to-day, you will work closely with teams across the business (Project Managers, Technical Leads, and cross-functional departments), organize and manage project evolutions through change activities, and ensure rigorous traceability and reporting—and much more.

We’ll look to you for:


Ensuring configuration and change management processes, tools, policies, and procedures are adhered to throughout the project lifecycle
Updating and maintaining the Configuration Management (CM) Plan and associated documentation
Planning and organizing Configuration Control Board (CCB) meetings
Providing configuration and change management reporting and indicators
Identifying and defining configuration items
Supporting team members in the use of configuration and change management tools
Managing and publishing configuration baselines
Collecting and verifying Change Requests and ensuring changes are well-documented
Coordinating and engaging all relevant stakeholders regarding configuration and changes
Suggesting improvements to the configuration and change management process based on KPI reporting


All About You

We value passion and attitude over experience. That’s why we don’t expect you to have every single skill.

Instead, we’ve listed some that we think will help you succeed and grow in this role:


Bachelor’s Degree in Engineering or a related technical discipline
5 years of experience in a similar role
Knowledge of Configuration Management processes and tools, including working knowledge of IBM's EWM (RTC), ETM (RQM), and DOORs Next Gen.,
Experience in organizing and managing Change Control Board (CCB) activities
Strong collaboration, communication, and teamwork skills
Excellent oral and written communication and interpersonal skills
Strong communication skills – both oral and written.
Analytical and multi-tasking skills.
Ability to ensure rigorous traceability, reporting, and documentation practices.
Knowledge of Railway Signaling products and systems (preferred but not required)
