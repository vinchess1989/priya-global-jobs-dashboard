About the Role

As our Atlassian Platform Administrator, you will be the technical owner of the enterprise Atlassian stack. You will design scalable solutions aligned with business and ITSM requirements, lead cloud migration and Jira consolidation initiatives, and partner with Agile teams and stakeholders to drive tool adoption and process improvement.

Key Responsibilities

Own Jira, JSM, and Confluence administration across the enterprise
Configure and maintain workflows, schemes, screens, custom fields, permission models, and automations
Design scalable Atlassian solutions aligned with business and ITSM requirements
Manage JSM implementations, including SLAs, queues, portals, and service request workflows
Lead cloud migration and Jira instance consolidation initiatives
Implement and maintain integrations with enterprise systems and DevOps tools
Provide L2/L3 support, troubleshooting, and platform governance
Manage user provisioning, access controls, SSO, and security compliance
Develop dashboards, reports, and executive metrics using JQL and reporting tools
Partner with Agile teams, Product Owners, and stakeholders to optimize processes and tool adoption
Evaluate, install, and manage Atlassian Marketplace applications
Drive platform standardization, best practices, and continuous improvement

Required Qualifications

7+ years of hands-on experience administering Jira, JSM, and Confluence in an enterprise environment
Strong command of workflow design, permission schemes, custom fields, and Jira automation
Proven experience implementing JSM, including SLAs, queues, request types, and customer portals
Experience with Atlassian Cloud migrations and Jira instance consolidation
Proficiency in JQL, dashboards, and reporting
Working knowledge of SSO, user provisioning, and access governance
Experience integrating Atlassian tools with enterprise and DevOps systems
Solid understanding of ITSM practices and Agile delivery methods
Strong troubleshooting skills with L2/L3 support experience
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills

Preferred Qualifications

Atlassian certifications (ACP-100, ACP-120, ACP-620 or equivalent)
Experience with Marketplace app evaluation, licensing, and lifecycle management
Scripting or API experience (REST APIs, Groovy, or Python)
Familiarity with ITIL frameworks and security compliance requirements
