Key Responsibilities

Design, deploy, and manage scalable AWS cloud infrastructure, including compute, storage, databases, and Kubernetes environments, using Infrastructure as Code (IaC).
Build, maintain, and enhance CI/CD/CT pipelines, incorporating automated testing, security controls, and deployment best practices.
Lead DevSecOps initiatives, establishing automation, security, and operational standards across cloud platforms and development teams.
Partner with Software Development, Product, and QA teams to improve deployment efficiency, application performance, and software delivery processes.
Implement Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practices, including SLIs/SLOs, incident management, error budgets, and continuous reliability improvements.
Develop and maintain monitoring and observability solutions, including logging, metrics, tracing, alerting, and performance dashboards.
Ensure cloud environments meet requirements for availability, scalability, security, disaster recovery, and regulatory compliance.
Provide technical guidance on infrastructure architecture, production readiness, and operational best practices.
Drive cloud cost optimization, operational improvements, and DevOps maturity through performance metrics and continuous improvement.




Required Qualifications & Technical Skills

Strong hands-on experience with AWS cloud infrastructure, including building, managing, and troubleshooting large-scale production environments and working with AWS APIs.
Proven experience designing and maintaining CI/CD/CT pipelines, deployment automation, and security integration.
Hands-on experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and configuration management tools such as Terraform and/or Ansible.
Experience with Kubernetes and/or Docker for containerized infrastructure and applications.
Strong scripting and automation experience.
Knowledge of DevSecOps practices, cloud security, and secure software delivery.
Familiarity with the Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC) and tools such as Git and Jira.
Experience working within regulatory or compliance frameworks such as SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, or HITRUST.
Experience supporting and troubleshooting complex infrastructure environments, including cloud, virtualization, and distributed systems.
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
