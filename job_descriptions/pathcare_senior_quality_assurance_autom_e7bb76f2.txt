PathCare is a leading medical diagnostic services provider. Digital systems drive our specimen journeys from request, specimen collection, processing in high-tech laboratories to data analysis and value-adding reporting to assist clinical decision making.




Join us for a major systems-renewal project! We are looking for a passionate, detail-oriented Software Quality Assurance Automation Engineer with strong expertise in test automation to become part of our dynamic team. You will play a crucial role in designing, developing and implementing automated testing solutions to ensure the quality, reliability and performance of enterprise and client-facing web and mobile applications.

 

The position will be based at our headquarters at Pathcare Park, N1 City in Cape Town.

 

Responsibilities:

Work both individually and as part of a team to design, develop and maintain automated test scripts to validate functionality, performance and reliability.
Develop and implement automation strategies to improve test coverage and software quality
Build and maintain reusable, scalable automation frameworks
Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines to support continuous testing and faster releases
Generate and maintain test execution reports, providing visibility into test results, coverage and quality metrics
Apply coding standards, version control and best practices to ensure automation scripts are maintainable and reliable
Support the configuration and maintenance of test environments and test data
Mentoring team members on QA best practices and automation techniques




Key Competency Requirements:

Solid experience in test automation using tools like selenium, playwright, cypress
Solid experience in performance testing
Experience working with test management tools like Jira and Xray

 

 Advantageous skills:

SQL experience
Experience working in an agile team highly beneficial
Experience working with CI/CD and version control tools like Git, Jenkins, docker and GIT

 

Candidate Profile:

Minimum of 5 years post-qualification relevant experience required with a bachelor’s degree in computer science, Information Technology, or a related field.
Excellent interpersonal and analytical skills.
An innovative and creative thinker with a passion for staying ahead of current industry trends.
Proven delivery record and the ability to work effectively without close supervision.




What We Offer (Our Employee Value Proposition)

Competitive salary and performance-based bonuses.                                               
A collaborative, innovative, and inclusive work culture where your input matters.

 

Application Process

Shortlisted candidates will be invited to participate in a multi-stage evaluation process. If you are a strong match for our requirements and are passionate about making a difference through what you do, please apply online with your current CV.
