Introduction

Vertec Scientific SA (Pty) Ltd is a leading South African supplier of specialised medical imaging and women's health solutions, with over 20 years of experience serving the healthcare sector. As the sole distributor of leading international brands and technologies, Vertec supports healthcare providers across South Africa and sub-Saharan Africa through equipment supply, installation, training and technical support

We are seeking a highly organised and detail-oriented Quality Manager / Regulatory Officer to lead our Quality Management System (QMS) and regulatory compliance activities. This is an excellent opportunity for a quality professional with experience in the medical device or other regulated industries to play a key role in maintaining compliance, supporting patient safety and driving continuous improvement across the organisation.

Duties & Responsibilities
Maintain, implement and continuously improve the company Quality Management System (QMS).
Lead ISO certification, surveillance and audit readiness activities.
Coordinate internal audits, management reviews and corrective action processes.
Manage SAHPRA-related regulatory compliance requirements, product registrations, amendments and renewals.
Oversee document control processes and management of controlled documentation.
Monitor supplier qualification, compliance and performance activities.
Drive CAPA, risk management, non-conformance investigations and change control processes.
Manage complaint handling, vigilance reporting, recalls, safety notices and product traceability.
Chair periodic compliance and quality meetings.
Develop and deliver internal compliance and quality training programmes.
Maintain training and competency records.
Prepare quality metrics and management reports and escalate quality, regulatory and patient-safety risks where required.
Work closely with service, sales, applications, logistics, warehouse and administration teams to ensure compliance across the business.
Desired Experience & Qualification

Minimum Qualification:

Bachelor's Degree in Quality Management, Life Sciences, Biomedical Science, Pharmacy, Engineering, Health Sciences or a related field.

Advantageous:

Postgraduate qualification in Quality Management or Regulatory Affairs, together with ISO 13485 Lead Auditor and/or Quality Management certifications.

This qualification requirement will align well with candidates who have experience managing quality systems, ISO compliance and SAHPRA regulatory requirements within the medical device environment.

Package & Remuneration
Basic: Market Related
Once off Bonus on completion and successful award of ISO certificate
Yearly discretionary bonus to a max of 10% of yearly salary for maintaining ISO verification / standards.
