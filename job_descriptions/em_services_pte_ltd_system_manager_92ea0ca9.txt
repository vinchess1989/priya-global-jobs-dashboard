Role Summary

To manage, govern and continuously improve TownSphere, Microsoft Project, SharePoint and Power BI as integrated digital platforms supporting project, contract, budget, document and management control. The role is responsible for ensuring system reliability, data integrity, effective user adoption, management reporting and the successful implementation of TownSphere Phase 2 and future digital enhancements.




Responsibilities

System Administration & Governance

Manage system configuration, users, access rights, approval workflows, delegation and audit trails.
Establish system governance, data standards, access controls and administration procedures across TownSphere, SharePoint and Power BI.




Phase 2 Implementation & Digital Roadmap

Coordinate requirements, workflow design, configuration, data migration, UAT, training, rollout and post-implementation review.
Maintain and prioritise the TownSphere Phase 2 and wider digital enhancement roadmap




Business Process & Workflow Management

Work with PMs, QSs, AAs and stakeholders to translate operational requirements into practical digital workflows.
Identify opportunities for automation, standardisation, integration and reduction of manual processes.




Data Quality, Analytics and Management Reporting

Monitor completeness, accuracy and timely updating of project, budget, EOT, RVO, claims, programme, DLP and contract closure information
Develop and maintain Power BI dashboards, KPIs, alerts and exception reports for management.
Escalate significant data gaps and overdue actions to responsible parties.




SharePoint & Document Management

Maintain appropriate SharePoint structures, permissions, document libraries and project records.
Support document governance, version control, retention and integration with TownSphere and other records-management requirements.




User Adoption, Support and Training

Coordinate user onboarding, training, SOPs and ongoing system support
Monitor adoption and reduce unnecessary parallel spreadsheets, manual registers and offline processes.




System Performance & Business Continuity

Monitor system availability, incidents, defects, backup, recovery, storage and performance.
Coordinate resolution, contingency and recovery arrangements with IT and system vendors.




Vendor, Cost & SLA Management

Monitor vendor performance, support response, maintenance and defect resolution against agreed SLAs.
Monitor development, hosting, storage, licence, maintenance and enhancement costs against approved budgets.




Change, Security & Audit Management

Maintain enhancement, change and release controls, including UAT, approvals and implementation records.
Ensure user access, approvals, system changes and key transactions are auditable.
Support cybersecurity, records-retention and audit requirements in consultation with IT.




Continuous Improvement & Benefits Realisation

Monitor platform effectiveness and recommend improvements based on usage, user feedback and operational requirements.
Measure improvements in automation, data visibility, project controls and reduction of manual work.




Requirements

Degree in Information System/Project Management/Business/Engineering or equivalent
At least 5 years experience in business systems administration, project controls, data management, UAT, digital transformation and vendor coordination.




EM Services Pte Ltd is a leading township and estate management company in Singapore with more than 35 years of experience in managing residential, commercial and public-use properties. Established in 1988, the company works closely with public agencies and community stakeholders to deliver reliable, sustainable and people-centred estate management services.
