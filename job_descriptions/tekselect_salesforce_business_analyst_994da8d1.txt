Salesforce Business Analyst – Dublin

Location: Dublin (Hybrid: 3 days in office)
Salary: Up to €60,000

We are looking for an experienced Salesforce Business Analyst to join a growing team in Dublin, supporting the ongoing development and optimisation of a Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud environment.

Key Responsibilities:

* Gather, analyse and document business requirements from key stakeholders.
* Translate business needs into Salesforce solutions and functional specifications.
* Work closely with Salesforce Administrators, Developers and business teams.
* Identify opportunities to improve processes, automation and user experience.
* Support UAT, system enhancements and ongoing Salesforce projects.

Requirements:

* Previous experience as a Salesforce Business Analyst.
* Strong knowledge of Salesforce Sales Cloud and/or Service Cloud.
* Experience gathering requirements, mapping processes and writing user stories.
* Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
* Understanding of Salesforce configuration, workflows and automation.

Salary: Up to €60k + benefits
Working Model: Hybrid – 3 days per week in Dublin office.
