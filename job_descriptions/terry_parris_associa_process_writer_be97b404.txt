Process Writer
Location: Pulborough
Salary: £35 – £40K DOE
Full Time Permanent position


Are you passionate about improving manufacturing processes and making things work better?
We are looking for a proactive and technically minded Process Writer to join an established manufacturing environment.
This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys working hands-on with production teams, understands manufacturing processes and takes pride in creating clear, practical documentation that makes a real difference on the shop floor.

You will play an important role in improving manufacturing efficiency, product quality and consistency by developing and maintaining accurate work instructions and assembly processes. Working closely with production operators, Manufacturing Engineering, NPI and Quality teams, you will help ensure products are manufactured using the most effective, reliable and repeatable methods.

If you enjoy problem-solving, collaborating with different teams and driving continuous improvement, this could be the ideal next step in your career.

The Role
As the Manufacturing Process & Work Instructions Specialist, you will be responsible for developing, reviewing and improving manufacturing documentation across new and existing products.
You will spend time on the shop floor understanding how products are assembled, engaging with operators to capture practical knowledge and translating this into clear, user-friendly work instructions.
Your work will directly contribute towards reducing manufacturing errors, improving product quality and creating a more efficient production environment.

Key Responsibilities

Develop, create and maintain clear and accurate assembly work instructions and electronic manufacturing routes.
Work closely with production operators to understand assembly methods, tooling, processes and potential areas for improvement.
Collaborate with Manufacturing Engineering, Design, NPI and Quality teams to establish the most effective manufacturing processes.
Observe production activities and translate practical knowledge into easy-to-follow documentation.
Ensure work instructions include relevant quality checks, tooling, materials and process requirements.
Work alongside operators to trial, validate and refine documentation, ensuring it is accurate and practical.
Manage documentation updates and revisions, ensuring changes are communicated effectively to production teams.
Support the introduction of new products into manufacturing, providing input into process development and design for manufacture.
Assist with investigating manufacturing issues and non-conformances, identifying opportunities to improve processes and prevent repeat problems.
Identify opportunities to improve production efficiency, consistency and quality through better processes, tooling and equipment.
Support continuous improvement initiatives across the manufacturing operation.
Work collaboratively with Quality, Engineering, Purchasing and other departments on manufacturing improvement projects.
Identify opportunities for additional operator training and improvements to the overall production process.
Monitor and report on the progress of documentation development, validation and improvement activities.
Ensure manufacturing documentation aligns with relevant quality, environmental and health and safety standards.
About You
We are keen to hear from candidates with a technical background who enjoy working closely with manufacturing teams and improving how products are made.
Ideally, you will have:

A technical background in Electronics, Electro-mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering or a related discipline, supported by relevant qualifications or practical experience.
Previous experience creating work instructions, assembly documentation or manufacturing process documentation.
Experience using ProPlanner or similar manufacturing planning and work instruction software would be highly desirable.
Knowledge of Autodesk Inventor would be beneficial.
Familiarity with ERP systems, particularly Glovia, would be an advantage.
The ability to read and interpret engineering drawings, schematics and bills of materials.
Excellent communication skills and the ability to build strong working relationships with production operators and engineering teams.
A practical, hands-on approach with a willingness to spend time on the shop floor.
Strong organisational skills and excellent attention to detail.
The confidence to question existing processes and suggest improvements constructively.
The ability to work independently, manage priorities and deliver against deadlines.
A genuine interest in continuous improvement, manufacturing excellence and product quality.
Knowledge of Lean Manufacturing principles or previous experience supporting process improvement projects would also be advantageous.

What's on Offer?
This is an opportunity to join a technical manufacturing environment where your ideas, experience and improvements will have a direct impact on production performance and product quality.
You will have the opportunity to:

Make a genuine difference to manufacturing processes and efficiency.
Work closely with experienced production and engineering professionals.
Influence how products are manufactured and documented.
Contribute to new product introductions and continuous improvement projects.
Develop your technical knowledge and broaden your manufacturing experience.
Take ownership of projects and see the results of your work.
Interested?
If you have experience in manufacturing documentation, process improvement or engineering and are looking for a role where you can make a tangible difference, we would love to hear from you.
Apply today to find out more about this opportunity.
