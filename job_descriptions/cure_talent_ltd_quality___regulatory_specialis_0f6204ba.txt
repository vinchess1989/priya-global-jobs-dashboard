Company Overview

We are delighted to be partnered with a growing healthcare technology company focused on improving ear and hearing health. Its integrated platform supports ear and hearing assessment, diagnosis and treatment within a single appointment, complemented by a training programme that helps practitioners deliver confident, effective care. As the business continues to expand, it is seeking a Quality and Regulatory Specialist to join its team on a permanent basis, based in London with hybrid working once a week on-site.

Position Overview

The Quality and Regulatory Specialist will support quality and regulatory compliance across the company's medical device software. The role will have a predominant focus on quality assurance, ensuring software development, changes and releases meet applicable medical device standards and regulatory requirements across the UK, EU and US.

Working closely with software engineering teams, the successful candidate will manage software lifecycle compliance, maintain technical documentation and support regulatory submissions. They will also manage quality activities including nonconformances, CAPA, change control, internal audits and software validation, whilst identifying opportunities to improve existing processes. This is an opportunity to make a visible contribution within a collaborative, fast-paced healthcare technology environment.

About the Ideal Candidate

The ideal candidate will be a pragmatic and detail-oriented quality and regulatory professional with experience in Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) or medical device software. They will be confident working alongside software development teams, maintaining technical documentation and managing quality system activities. Strong organisation, communication skills and the ability to work independently will be important.

Role Responsibilities

Ensure compliance with IEC 62304 throughout the software development lifecycle, working with engineering teams to embed quality and regulatory requirements.
Prepare and maintain software technical documentation, design history files, risk management documentation and verification and validation records.
Support regulatory submissions across the UK, EU and US, assessing the regulatory impact of software changes.
Maintain and continuously improve the quality management system, managing nonconformances, CAPA, change control, SOPs and quality metrics.
Conduct internal audits, support external audits and inspections, and undertake software tool validation.
Support software-related post-market surveillance, complaint handling and vigilance activities.

Key Requirements / What We Look For

Experience in quality assurance and/or regulatory affairs within SaMD or medical device software.
Working knowledge of IEC 62304, ISO 13485, ISO 14971, EU MDR and FDA 21 CFR Part 820 (QMSR).
Experience preparing and maintaining software technical documentation and supporting regulatory submissions.
Experience with QMS processes, document control, SOPs, nonconformances, CAPA, change control and internal audits.
Understanding of software development practices, including agile development, version control and testing.
Strong organisation, attention to detail, communication skills and a pragmatic approach to quality and regulatory compliance.
