David Sebastian Straub
Investor | Director @ SEBDATA | Owner of SELMA SIS

SELMA International Limited | Newmarket, Auckland | 12 month fixed term, with a view to extension




SELMA is a New Zealand CRM and student information system for higher education and vocational providers, operating across New Zealand, Australia and Singapore. We have three large scale implementation projects running through 2027 and we are hiring a Project Manager to lead that delivery.




The work

You will own delivery across concurrent implementations, from kick-off and discovery through configuration, data migration, testing, training and cutover. That means running the plan, the RAID log and the governance cadence with each client, holding the stage gates, and making the go or no-go call stick when the evidence says wait.




The data we migrate drives government funding returns, regulatory reporting and learner access to student loans and allowances. A reconciliation that does not balance has direct financial consequences for the client, and a missed compliance deadline has regulatory ones. We need someone who already works to that standard.




What we are looking for

Proven delivery of system implementations, migrations or integrations in an environment where errors carry financial, regulatory or audit consequences. Finance, banking, insurance, health or utilities experience is a real advantage.
Strong business analysis capability alongside project management. You are comfortable running the workshop, writing the requirement and then holding the schedule.
Genuine ownership of cutover, reconciliation and rollback planning, not oversight of someone else doing it.
Confidence working directly with senior stakeholders and holding a line on scope and sign-off.
Based in Auckland, with willingness to travel to client sites in New Zealand as required.



Tertiary sector experience is not required. We have deep compliance capability in the team already and will teach you the regulatory context. What we cannot teach is the discipline that comes from delivering where mistakes cost money.




What you get

A dynamic, international team that works hard and has delivered outcomes we are genuinely proud of. Real ownership of significant projects, direct access to the people making the decisions, and an office in Newmarket with a public transport subsidy.

NZD $100,000 to $130,000 depending on experience. Applications close 23 November.




When you apply, please answer these four questions

Describe an implementation or migration you delivered where an error would have had financial or regulatory consequences. Name the system, the year, the scale, and what your actual role was.
Walk us through a cutover plan you personally owned, including how you handled reconciliation and rollback.
Tell us about a time you stopped or delayed a go-live. What did you see, and what happened next?
How do you keep traceability between what was agreed, what was built and what was signed off?
