Job Description:




Design, implement, and manage cloud infrastructure and services across Microsoft Azure / Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
Experience in cloud deployment, configuration, monitoring, and administration of cloud-based environments.
Manage cloud networking, storage, compute resources, security, and access management.
Implement automation, CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code (IaC), and cloud cost optimization practices.
Troubleshoot cloud-related issues, ensure high availability, and maintain security, performance, and compliance standards.




About Happiest Minds Technologies:




Happiest Minds Technologies is an AI-first, customer-centric digital engineering and IT services company focused on helping enterprises accelerate their digital transformation journey. Headquartered in Bengaluru, the company delivers innovative solutions across digital engineering, cloud, data analytics, cybersecurity, automation, and Generative AI. Guided by its philosophy of "Happiest People. Happiest Customers.", Happiest Minds serves global clients across diverse industries through a culture of innovation, agility, and excellence.
