This is a great opportunity to join the quality team of an established manufacturing business in Ebbw Vale. Hunter Selection are working with an expanding business looking for a Quality Manager. This role will see you involved in auditing, certifications, management of a QMS as well as driving quality standards and continuous improvement activities. This role will enable you to develop your own team and drive improvements across a growing business.

Role And Responsibilities


Lead and continuously improve the Quality Management System, ensuring compliance with ISO 9001, IATF 16949, customer requirements, and business objectives.
Drive quality performance through analysis of audits, customer feedback, supplier performance, and risk assessments, identifying opportunities for corrective action and continual improvement.
Manage and oversee all audit activities, including risk:based internal and layered process audits, ensuring process effectiveness, compliance, and support for certification requirements.
Act as the primary quality representative for customers and product safety, overseeing APQP, PPAP, corrective actions, product conformity, and adherence to customer and regulatory requirements.
Lead supplier quality and product governance activities, including supplier development, performance monitoring, specification approval, outsourced process control, and maintenance of robust quality controls and traceability systems.


Knowledge, Skills And Experience


Experience maintaining and improving a certified Quality Management System.
Experience planning and conducting internal audits.
Experience supporting certification:body and customer audits.
Practical experience of structured problem solving, root:cause analysis and 8D.
Working knowledge of automotive Core Tools, including APQP, PPAP and FMEA.
Experience reviewing PPAP submissions,
