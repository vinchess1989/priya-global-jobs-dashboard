Delivery Manager | London (Hybrid 2–3 days) | £70,000–£80,000




A leading B2B SaaS company building the next generation of its platform for regulated financial institutions (wealth managers, asset managers and investment platforms) is looking for a Delivery Manager to join their senior engineering team.




This is not a traditional project management role focused on ceremonies and Gantt charts. The foundations of a lean delivery process are already in place (release candidate stage, standard deploy requests, templated QA/release notes and a fortnightly release train). Your job is to run what exists from day one and take it to best practice, so delivery is predictable, measurable and no longer dependent on a few key people.




What you’ll own

The path from “ready for release” to “live and confirmed” across both the current and next-generation platforms
Release calendar, train, deploy request review, production sign-off and post-release smoke tests
Readiness gate: incomplete requests, missing QA evidence or no rollback plan go back
QA strategy and process (AI-assisted, automated and manual), with potential to build/manage a nearshore QA team as the company grows
Honest Jira board so “ready to ship” is a filter, not an investigation
Client change control (registers, client-specific regression, contractual notice)
Monthly SLA reports, service credits and contractual commitments
Delivery side of incidents (comms, timelines, reports, corrective actions and close-out)
Client delivery calls, implementation plans, decision/action logs and a weekly delivery risk summary for the CTO




What success looks like in year one

Predictable cadence, feature flags, smaller releases, automated notes and pipeline-based evidence
Written test strategy, automated regression for key journeys, clear environment criteria and independent testing
Proper Definition of Ready/Done, flow metrics, client change process, API versioning policy
Automated SLA monitoring, single contractual obligations register and easy delivery evidence for due diligence
Formal incident process with severity levels, roles, templates and blameless retrospectives
Monthly DORA metrics and documented deputies for every release role




What we're looking for

End-to-end ownership of software delivery for a B2B SaaS product (regulated financial services experience strongly preferred)
Enough technical fluency to read a PR, runbook and Jira history and spot what’s missing — without needing to be a developer
Working knowledge of SLAs, service credits, change notice clauses and statements of work
Experience setting QA strategy (ideally including leading a remote/nearshore test team)
Clear, calm writing — release notes, incident reports and client plans will go out under your name
Habit of writing things down; exacting without being obstructive; able to say no politely with a reason
Comfort working alongside AI-assisted development and QA, with an interest in governing it well
Jira, Confluence, GitHub and AWS familiarity useful; ITIL/SOC 2/ISO 27001 a bonus




Practicalities

Reports to the CTO with authority to stop a release and escalate directly
Small, high-trust senior team; the right amount of process, not process for its own sake
Direct access to product, research, sales and client teams
London hybrid — 2–3 days per week in the office
Salary: £70,000–£80,000
