We are seeking an experienced Technical Writer to support the development, review, and implementation of Safe Work Standards, Procedures, Processes, and Work Aids. The successful candidate will be responsible for reviewing existing documentation, identifying future-state requirements, and developing clear, simplified, and user-friendly documents that align with the organization's operating system framework.




The role requires strong technical writing and stakeholder engagement skills, with the ability to facilitate discussions, coordinate input from subject matter experts (SMEs), and incorporate feedback into finalized documentation. The Technical Writer will also assess the impact of documentation changes, develop implementation plans, and support the transition of new and revised documents into operational use and sustainment.




Key Responsibilities

Review existing Safe Work Standards, procedures, processes, and work aids to identify gaps, inconsistencies, and opportunities for improvement.
Develop and draft clear, concise, and simplified technical documents aligned with the organization's operating system framework.
Translate complex technical and operational information into practical, easy-to-understand documentation.
Consult with internal stakeholders and subject matter experts to gather, validate, and incorporate technical information.
Facilitate document review sessions, coordinate stakeholder feedback, and manage revisions through to final approval.
Ensure consistency, accuracy, clarity, and alignment across all documentation.
Conduct change impact assessments to identify the implications of introducing new or revised documentation.
Develop implementation plans to support the effective rollout and adoption of updated documents.
Support the transition of new documentation into operational use and ongoing sustainment.
Track documentation deliverables, communicate project progress and potential risks, and ensure completion within established timelines.




Required Qualifications & Skills

Proven experience as a Technical Writer, preferably within industrial, operational, engineering, or safety-related environments.
Strong experience developing and maintaining technical documentation, including standards, procedures, processes, work instructions, and work aids.
Excellent technical writing, editing, and document simplification skills.
Ability to interpret complex technical information and translate it into clear, structured, and user-friendly documentation.
Experience engaging with subject matter experts and cross-functional stakeholders to gather and validate technical content.
Experience with document review, revision, change impact assessment, and implementation planning.
Strong communication, facilitation, and organizational skills.
Ability to manage multiple documentation deliverables and meet targeted project deadlines.




Preferred Experience

Experience working within an established operating system or management system framework.
Familiarity with Safe Work Standards, operational procedures, and workplace safety documentation.
Experience supporting organizational change and the implementation of revised operational documentation.
