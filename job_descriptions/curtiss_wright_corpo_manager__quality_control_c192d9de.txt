Quality Control Manager

We are looking for a Quality Control Manager to join our Galway, Ireland team!

This is an opportunity to join a metal surface treatment technology facility with an established industrial company that is the world leader in this field.

Location: Galway, Ireland

Salary: £60,000 - £68,000

Your Challenge


Implementing, reviewing and maintaining QMS.
Managing and administrating of the certification ISO13485, ISO9001, AS9100 and other related standards.
Reviewing, maintaining and enforcing QMS documentation.
Responsible for internal, external (regulatory and customer) audits.
Education and training all employees as per the quality compliance standards.
Authorizing and issuing Certificates of Compliances.
Maintaining the calibration and systems compliance.
Implementing and controlling customers’ requirements on production documentation.
Managing of QC staff (1-2 employees).
Representing the site during customer audits and other agencies.
Responsible for root cause, preventative action, corrective actions.
May require some travel to customer facilities or other CW sites.


What You Bring


Bachelor's degree
3 to 5 years of experience doing similar work in an industrial production environment.
Experience in implementation and maintenance of QMS.
Experience as QMS audits.
Parylene coating and printed circuit board experience is preferred.


Who We Are

Our Values

Environmental, Social and Governance

Curtiss-Wright Surface Technologies ("CWST") is the group name for the collective technical services provided by E/M Coating Services, Everlube Products, FW Gartner Thermal Spraying, Para Tech Coating, Component Coating & Repair Services, Bolt's Metallizing, Keronite, and IMR Test Labs.

CWST is a Division of Curtiss-Wright Corporation, a global innovative company that delivers highly engineered, critical function products and services to the commercial, industrial, defense and energy markets.
