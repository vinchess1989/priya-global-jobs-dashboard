Role Overview

We are seeking a proactive, self-driven Technology Delivery Lead to manage the end-to-end delivery of Digital and Process Automation solutions within the Banking and Financial Services domain. Operating with minimal direction, you will bridge technical execution and Agile delivery—managing project timelines, coordinating deployments, overseeing production stability, and guiding engineering team. The role will require extensive engagement with Business areas to align technology delivery with business objectives.

Key Responsibilities


Delivery & Implementation Management:
Develop, own, and manage comprehensive end-to-end implementation plans and project timelines.
Control and manage project scope, identifying risks and dependencies early to ensure on-time delivery.
Liaise across cross-functional engineering, infrastructure, and business teams to coordinate seamless deployments.
Agile & Scrum Leadership:
Facilitate core Agile ceremonies, backlog refinement, and team effort estimation sessions.
Champion Agile and Kanban practices (with familiarity in Scrum/Sprints) using Jira to maintain workflow transparency and delivery velocity.
Release & Production Operations:
Manage end-to-end release lifecycles and change management processes through Remedy.
Act as a key escalation point to respond to, triage, and manage production incidents, ensuring rapid resolution and minimal business disruption.
Technical Collaboration:
Partner closely with other development teams to resolve dependencies

Required Qualifications & Skills


Technical Domain: Proven hands-on delivery experience with Process Automation or Digitalisation platforms such as Kofax, Tungsten, Enterprise Content Management (ECM), Document Management Systems (DMS), Java and Microservices architectures, BPM and Robotic Process Automation suits such Blueprism.
Business Domain: Strong background delivering technology initiatives within the Banking / Financial Services sector.
Ways of Working: Practical expertise in Agile and Kanban methodologies, with strong proficiency in Jira.
Service & Release Management: Hands-on experience managing releases and change tickets using Remedy, alongside production incident management.
Soft Skills & Work Style: Highly proactive, autonomous leader capable of managing stakeholders, driving implementation independently with minimal direction
