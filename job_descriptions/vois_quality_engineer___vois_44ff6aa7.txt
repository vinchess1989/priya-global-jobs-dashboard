About This Role

We are seeking a Software Test Automation Engineer with hands-on experience across manual and automated software testing. You will analyse business requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria; design and execute functional and integration tests; build and maintain reusable automated tests; perform API testing; support CI/CD execution; and manage defects through to closure.

The role requires a sound understanding of SDLC, STLC, test-design techniques and the defect lifecycle, together with practical experience working in Agile teams. You will collaborate with developers, product owners, business analysts and other stakeholders to embed quality throughout the delivery lifecycle and proactively manage quality risks.

What You’ll Do


Analyse business requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria to identify test scenarios, dependencies and quality risks
Design, document and maintain test plans, test scenarios, test cases and test data for functional and integration testing
Perform manual testing, including functional, integration, system, regression, smoke, sanity and exploratory testing
Design, develop, execute and maintain automated test scripts for suitable functional and regression scenarios
Identify appropriate automation candidates and improve automation coverage while avoiding unnecessary or low-value automation
Perform API and integration testing, validating requests, responses, error handling and end-to-end workflows
Execute automated test suites within CI/CD pipelines and investigate test failures
Manage defect identification, logging, prioritisation, retesting and closure, providing clear evidence and reproducible steps
Conduct root-cause analysis of test failures, distinguishing product defects from test-data, environment and automation issues
Collaborate with developers, product owners, business analysts and other stakeholders to establish clear acceptance criteria and embed quality throughout delivery
Participate in Agile ceremonies, including backlog refinement, sprint planning, daily stand-ups, reviews and retrospectives
Maintain requirements-to-test traceability and appropriate coverage of critical business journeys
Track and communicate test execution status, defect trends, regression results and automation coverage
Contribute to the continuous improvement of test processes, automation frameworks, reusable components and quality-engineering standards
Support test-environment and test-data readiness, highlighting dependencies that could affect delivery
Proactively identify quality risks and improvement opportunities, helping teams prevent defects as well as detect them



Who You Are


You hold a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering or a related discipline, or have equivalent practical experience
You have 5yrs- 6yrs hands-on experience in manual and automation testing across the software development lifecycle
You understand functional, integration, regression, system and exploratory testing
You have practical experience developing and maintaining reusable automated test scripts
You have experience with API testing and validating system integrations
You understand SDLC, STLC, the defect lifecycle and test-design techniques
You have experience using defect-tracking and test-management tools
You understand Agile/Scrum methodologies and CI/CD practices
You can apply analytical, troubleshooting and problem-solving skills to investigate test failures and quality issues
You communicate effectively and collaborate across development, product, business and technology teams
You have experience with defect identification, reporting, retesting and root-cause analysis

What's In It For You


Work across manual testing, test automation, API testing and CI/CD-integrated quality engineering
Contribute to quality throughout the software delivery lifecycle rather than focusing solely on test execution
Collaborate across development, product, business analysis and other stakeholder groups
Influence test automation coverage, reusable testing components and continuous improvement
Build experience in risk-based quality practices, including defect prevention, root-cause analysis and critical business-journey validation



What Skills You Will Learn


Test automation design and maintenance, including identification of suitable automation candidates
API and integration testing, including request, response and error-handling validation
CI/CD test execution and failure investigation
Advanced root-cause analysis and troubleshooting across product, data, environment and automation issues
Requirements-to-test traceability and critical business-journey coverage
Quality reporting using indicators such as defect trends, regression results, test status and automation coverage
Cross-functional collaboration within Agile software delivery teams
