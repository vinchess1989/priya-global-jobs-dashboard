Your Role And Responsibilities

We are looking for a Sr Business System Analyst to join us. In this role, you will work as a member of a highly dynamic and collaborative technical team. The team is tasked with design, development, and functional support for the Salesforce Sales Cloud and Salesforce Marketing Cloud. You will implement and execute strategy to help the team deliver high-quality products and build a quality-first culture within the development team. You will collaborate closely with the Product Owner, Salesforce Engineers, Enterprise architects, and testing and support teams to execute and deliver solutions and tools in alignment with the org vision and strategy.

What You Will Do


Work closely with global and regional stakeholders, development teams, and architects to solve business problems
Writing user stories, acceptance criteria as per business needs
Driving team refinement sessions and implement solutions using salesforce configuration
Analyze business requirements perform gap analysis and root cause analysis
Work with cross-functional teams to identify dependencies
Ensure compliance with SOX, business controls, and IT general controls
Identify system gaps and propose, design, and build solutions


What You Will Bring


Graduate university degree in computer science, computer engineering, or a similar field
5+ years of SFDC experience
Demonstrated experience working as part of an agile development team
Experience with CI and CD tools like Git, VS Code, SonarQube, and the release process
Solid knowledge of customer relationship management (CRM) software
Understanding of the overall SFDC ecosystem
Solid problem-solving, critical thinking, and analytical skills to set up and interpret logic, data tables, and rule relationships
Desire to learn and apply new technologies
Ability to learn quickly and master new skills as necessary
Exemplary interpersonal skills
