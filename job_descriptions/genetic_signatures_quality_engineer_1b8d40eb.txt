At Genetic Signatures, every patient matters. We develop innovative molecular diagnostic solutions that empower healthcare professionals to make informed decisions, improve patient outcomes and build trust in every treatment journey. We are an Australian molecular diagnostics company with a growing global presence, committed to delivering high-quality solutions that make a meaningful difference to healthcare worldwide.

We are seeking an experienced and detail-oriented Quality Engineer to join our Regulatory Affairs & Quality Assurance team on an initial 6-month fixed-term contract. This role is critical in ensuring quality is embedded throughout product development, validation activities and regulatory compliance processes.

The Quality Engineer will provide independent quality oversight across development projects, validation programs and outsourced service providers, helping ensure products progress efficiently from development through to manufacture and market release. This role offers the opportunity to work closely with cross-functional teams while contributing to the continued growth of our innovative molecular diagnostics portfolio.

Directly reporting to the Global Regulatory Affairs, as the successful candidate you will:

Act as the independent quality reviewer for design and development activities across the product lifecycle.
Participate in design phase reviews and ensure development documentation is complete and compliant.
Review and support risk management activities, including risk assessments, hazard analysis and FMEA reviews.
Provide quality oversight for software validation and computer system validation activities.
Review and approve validation documentation for laboratory equipment, methods and software systems.
Support the qualification and oversight of contract research organisations (CROs), contract laboratories and critical suppliers.
Contribute to CAPA investigations, non-conformance management and quality system improvement initiatives.
Participate in internal audit activities and support compliance with ISO 13485 and applicable regulatory requirements.
Collaborate with Technology, Production, Regulatory Affairs and Quality teams to support successful project delivery.
Support the responsible use of AI-assisted tools within quality and compliance processes.

Essentially to succeed in this role you will possess:

A bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Biomedical Science, Life Sciences, Computer Science or a related discipline.
Minimum 5 years' experience in Quality Assurance or Design Assurance within a medical device or IVD manufacturing environment operating under ISO 13485.
Experience reviewing design control documentation and supporting product development projects through to design transfer.
Solid knowledge of ISO 13485 and ISO 14971 requirements.
Experience developing, reviewing or approving validation protocols and reports.
Excellent attention to detail and analytical problem-solving skills.
Strong written and verbal communication skills.
The ability to work independently while building effective relationships across multiple teams.

It is advantageous, but not essential, if your personal talent toolkit includes:

Experience within molecular diagnostics, PCR or in vitro diagnostic (IVD) products.
Knowledge of IEC 62304 software lifecycle requirements.
Experience with computer system validation approaches such as GAMP 5.
Familiarity with FDA Quality System Regulations, IVDR and MDSAP requirements.
Experience with eQMS implementation or migration projects.
ISO 13485 Internal Auditor or Lead Auditor training.
Experience conducting supplier, CRO or laboratory audits.
Experience with process, equipment or software validation activities (IQ, OQ, PQ).
Knowledge of eQMS platforms such as MasterControl and tools such as Jira or Git.
Experience using AI tools to support technical documentation review and quality processes.
