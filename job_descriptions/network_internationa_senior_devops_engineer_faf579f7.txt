JOB DESCRIPTION

Network International is the largest Financial Technology company in Middle East and Africa. Payments is our core business where we provide services in more than 50 countries – UAE, Jordan, South Africa, Egypt are some of our key markets. Apart from payments, we provide services on Data and Insights, Lending, Insurance, Risk Solutions, etc. Our core customers are businesses at every scale and segment, though recently we are growing in direct to consumer card segment as well.

Job Description

We're looking for a Senior AWS DevOps Engineer to design, build, and operate the cloud infrastructure powering our high availability payment processing platform. You'll own reliability, automation, and security across a complex, regulated production environment handling live financial transactions. This is a hands-on senior role combining deep AWS expertise with a strong automation-first, infrastructure-as-code mindset.

Responsibilities

Develop and maintain procedures to comply with security policies and IT best practices


Develop and maintain documentation of systems configurations and procedures
Monitor systems via applications, log files, Elk/OpenSearch and sanity checks
Actively work on building and migrating PayFast to Kubernetes.
Maintain our configuration and build management systems
Database maintenance and implementations
Serve as an escalation point to resolve problems in Servers and users’ environment
Work closely with technical team members (cultural fit)
Must be able to troubleshoot complex issues quickly and effectively
Research and evaluation of new technologies for our environment
CI, Testing and release management
Firewall Administration
Security and incident response
Patch management
Agile working methods, agile planning, time management and planning/prioritisation skills


Requirements


Certification (Bachelors / RHCE / ISO 27001/PCI) is a plus but not required
High level of proficiency in Linux based OS
Cloud Infrastructure (AWS) Associated Certified is a must have
Containers (Docker + Kubernetes)
Pipelines (Jenkins/Gitlab Ci)
Proficiency in a scripting language and application language: Bash, Python, PHP, Perl, Java , Go
Proven IaC experience in one of: SaltStack, Chef, Ansible, Puppet, Teraform
Web Servers (Apache, Nginx, fpm, node)
Load Balancers Experience
Knowledge of Database technologies: AWS RDS (especially Maria and MySQL)
NoSql DBs,pub/sub and Key/Value stores: Redis/mongo
Replication technologies
Experience in Networking: OSI, (AWS TGW and basic on prem OSI model)
Queues – Rabbit, MQ
