Client:




Our client is seeking an experienced Infrastructure Automation Engineer to join their Platform Engineering team. This is a senior hands-on role focused on building scalable automation platforms, Infrastructure-as-Code solutions and developer tooling across hybrid cloud environments.




Role:




Staff Infrastructure Automation Engineer




Responsibilities:




Design and develop infrastructure automation platforms using Python, Terraform and Ansible.
Build reusable Infrastructure-as-Code modules and self-service infrastructure solutions.
Develop API-driven automation and orchestration across cloud and virtualisation platforms.
Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and implement GitOps and automated compliance workflows.
Automate provisioning and lifecycle management across Nutanix, VMware and KVM environments.
Support hybrid cloud automation across AWS and Azure.
Establish engineering best practices and provide technical leadership and mentoring to other engineers.




Requirements:




5–8 years' experience in Software Engineering, Platform Engineering, Infrastructure Automation or DevOps.
Strong Python development skills.
Expert-level Terraform and strong Ansible experience.
Experience with Infrastructure-as-Code, APIs, Git, CI/CD and GitOps.
Experience automating at least two of the following: Nutanix AHV/Prism/AOS, VMware vSphere/ESXi or KVM/QEMU.
Experience with AWS and/or Azure.
Strong understanding of infrastructure architecture, networking and hybrid cloud environments.
Previous technical leadership or mentoring experience.
Experience with Kubernetes, internal developer platforms, Python web frameworks, observability or policy-as-code would be advantageous.




The Rewards:




As a reward for your commitment to this role you will receive an excellent salary and a competitive benefits package.
