Release Manager

About the team

Worldline Service Australia (WSA) delivers end-to-end payment services and technology support to Worldline clients within the Australian market. Our team focuses on ensuring reliable payment processing, proactive service management, and a superior customer experience through robust operations, incident response, and continuous improvement.

Our Team

The Service Delivery function provides operational governance, customer engagement and IT Service Management capability for Worldline Service Australia. The team is responsible for ensuring payment services remain stable, resilient and continuously improving through effective operational processes, customer governance and service assurance.

Your mission

Lead the planning, coordination and governance of technology releases across WSA, ensuring releases are delivered in accordance with change management standards, release schedules and operational readiness requirements.

The opportunity

We are looking for an energetic release manager who can help us uplift our release management practices across all environments who has exposure to banking, finance or payment organisations.

Day-to-day responsibilities

Key Accountabilities

Release Planning & Governance
• Maintain the release calendar.

Coordinate release planning across projects and BAU.
Define release scope and schedules.

• Facilitate governance and approvals.

Release Coordination
• Coordinate production releases.
• Manage dependencies.
• Validate implementation and rollback plans.
• Chair release readiness meetings.

Operational Readiness
• Confirm testing, documentation and approvals.
• Ensure support readiness.
• Validate operational acceptance.

Risk & Change Management
• Assess release risks.
• Work with the Change Manager and CAB.
• Escalate issues.

Deployment Oversight
• Oversee production deployments.
• Coordinate hypercare.
• Lead PIRs.

Reporting
• Maintain release metrics and documentation.
