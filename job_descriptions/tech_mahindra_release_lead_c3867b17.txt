Release Lead

Location: MELBOURNE (Hybrid/Onsite)

Employment Type: Full-Time




About Us:

At Tech Mahindra (Tech Mahindra | Connected World, Connected Experiences), we live the philosophy of a connected world and connected experiences. We thrive on change powered by the intelligent symphony of technology and humans designing meaningful and sustainable experiences. Consumer experiences are driving and disrupting industries like never before. Businesses must build seamless yet simple enterprises that collaborate, synergize, and drive change—connecting the digital, physical, and convergent worlds to deliver truly connected experiences.







Lead the planning and coordination of releases for a banking migration program, ensuring a controlled transition to production with minimal disruption to customers and banking operations




Key responsibilities

Own the release calendar, deployment plans and cross team dependencies.
Coordinate application, infrastructure, testing, data migration and business teams.
Confirm release readiness, including testing completion, data reconciliation, operational readiness and required approvals.
Coordinate cutover rehearsals, deployment activities and rollback planning.
Facilitate go/no go reviews and ensure compliance with the bankâ€¿s change management processes.
Manage release risks, issues, stakeholder communications and escalations.
Coordinate post release validation, hypercare and handover to operational support




Required experience and skills

Experience in release management within banking or financial services.
Experience supporting complex migration or technology transformation programs.
Strong understanding of release, change and cutover management.
Strong planning, stakeholder coordination and risk management skills.
Ability to manage multiple teams and dependencies under tight delivery timelines.
