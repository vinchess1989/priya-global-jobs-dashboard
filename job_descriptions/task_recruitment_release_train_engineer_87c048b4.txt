Lead Agile delivery for next-gen mining tech combining robotic equipment, AI and advanced geospatial features for a leading global OEM.




Lead a 60-person Agile Release Train delivering next-gen mining technology
Work across robotic mining equipment, AI & state-of-the-art geospatial tech
12-month contract | 5 days onsite | Flexible start & finish times




Are you an experienced Release Train Engineer (RTE) who enjoys bringing multiple teams together, removing roadblocks and helping complex technology programs deliver?




Join a leading global OEM of mining equipment and technology and take a key delivery leadership role within a large-scale global software and technology program.




This team is helping deliver the next generation of mining technology, bringing together robotic mining equipment, AI capabilities and state-of-the-art geospatial technology which will taking running and controlling of mining operations to another.




You’ll be working in a highly sophisticated environment where software, data and physical mining equipment come together to provide real-time guidance, tracking, monitoring and supervisory control across mining operations.




This is a 12-month contract opportunity requiring you to work onsite in the office 5 days per week. While the position is office-based, there is flexibility around start and finish times, giving you some flexibility in how you structure your working day.




You’ll lead an Agile Release Train (ART) of approximately six teams and 60 people, forming part of a broader global technology program involving more than 300 professionals across approximately 25 teams.




The Opportunity




As the Release Train Engineer, you’ll play a key role in creating alignment, transparency and delivery confidence across your Agile Release Train.




You’ll work closely with Product Management, Engineering Managers, Scrum Masters, Product Owners, technical leaders and delivery teams to keep priorities aligned, dependencies visible and teams focused on delivering meaningful outcomes.




With multiple teams contributing to the next generation of the product, you’ll help coordinate delivery across a complex technology landscape incorporating robotics, AI, geospatial capabilities, software, data and mining equipment.




This isn't simply a coordination role. You’ll be a trusted delivery partner who can facilitate at scale, coach teams and leaders, constructively challenge existing ways of working and use data to identify where delivery can be improved.




What You’ll Be Doing




You’ll:

Lead and facilitate PI Planning, ART Syncs, Scrum of Scrums, System Demos, Inspect & Adapt workshops and other SAFe events
Coach Scrum Masters, Product Owners, teams and stakeholders in SAFe, Lean-Agile practices and continuous improvement
Coordinate approximately six Agile teams within your ART
Help drive delivery of next-generation mining technology incorporating robotics, AI and advanced geospatial capabilities
Partner with Product Management, Engineering Managers and senior stakeholders to align delivery with strategic and customer priorities
Manage cross-team dependencies, risks, blockers and ROAM activities
Improve visibility and coordination across shared objectives and complex dependencies
Support continuous value delivery across a large-scale mining technology solution
Partner with engineering and technical leaders to promote quality, reliability, predictability and sustainable delivery
Provide clear, data-driven reporting covering delivery progress, ART health, flow, capacity, quality, risks and dependencies
Use delivery metrics, flow data, feedback and retrospectives to identify systemic bottlenecks and drive measurable improvements
Act as a trusted delivery partner, providing clear communication and practical options to support decision-making




About You




We’re looking for someone with significant experience delivering Agile programs at scale who is comfortable working across multiple teams, disciplines and stakeholder groups.




You’ll ideally bring:

Extensive experience leading Agile delivery at scale
Strong knowledge of the Scaled Agile Framework (SAFe) and Lean-Agile principles
Experience leading or coordinating multiple Agile teams with significant cross-team dependencies
Excellent facilitation and workshop leadership skills
Strong coaching capability across different Agile roles and levels of seniority
Excellent stakeholder management, communication and conflict resolution skills
Strong analytical skills and confidence using delivery metrics and flow data
The ability to influence outcomes without relying on formal authority
A genuine continuous improvement mindset focused on removing process waste and improving delivery




Highly Regarded




It would be advantageous if you have:

SAFe Release Train Engineer (RTE) certification
Experience working in large-scale technology environments involving 250+ people
Experience operating across multiple Agile Release Trains or portfolios
Experience with Azure DevOps, JIRA or equivalent
Exposure to mining, autonomous or robotic systems, AI, geospatial technology, operational technology, heavy equipment or complex engineering/software environments




Why Join?




This is an opportunity to work with a leading global OEM of mining equipment and technology on the next generation of a major mining technology product.




Rather than working on a conventional enterprise software program, you’ll be part of an environment where advanced software interacts with the physical world – including robotic mining equipment, AI-driven capabilities and state-of-the-art geospatial technology.




You’ll have genuine scale and complexity, leading an ART of around 60 people while collaborating across a global technology organisation of more than 300 professionals.




You’ll also benefit from flexibility around your daily start and finish times, while working onsite alongside the teams and stakeholders you’ll be supporting.




If you enjoy Agile leadership, solving complex delivery challenges, coaching teams and helping deliver genuinely advanced technology, we’d love to hear from you.




12-month contract | 5 days per week onsite | Flexible start & finish times
