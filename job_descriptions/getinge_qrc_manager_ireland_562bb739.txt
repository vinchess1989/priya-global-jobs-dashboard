Job Title: QRC Manager Ireland

Home based in Republic of Ireland

Job Overview

QRC Manager IE responsible for maintaining and improving a compliant Quality Management System (ISO9001, ISO 13485), including audits, CAPAs, regulatory monitoring, product registrations, and ensuring adherence to quality and compliance targets, providing support to the QRC Regional Director. Acting as the Local Quality Manager (LQM) for Getinge Ireland according to the role within the QMS.

Job Responsibilities And Essential Duties

Job responsibilities and essential duties split for Getinge Ireland as follows:

QMS


Implementing and maintaining a compliant Quality Management System according to the corporate (Getinge) directives, ISO 9001 and ISO 13485.
Ensuring the implementation of Quality Planning activities
Act as the point of contact for all related questions to documentation management system, supporting the Irish sales and service organization with the implementation of the QMS as integral part of the operations
Responsible for monitoring and maintaining quality and compliance targets, KPIs and objectives related to the QMS.
Ensuring the availability of continuous training and supporting on the Quality Management Systems
Responsible for and supporting the CAPA process within the UKI region
Participating in internal and external audits and follow up on the audit findings, in cooperation with the QRC Director UKI
Evaluate non-conformities and ensure implementation appropriate corrective/preventive actions
Ensure Field actions and Quality holds are implemented
Ensure complaints are reported in a timely manner and investigations performed for late reporting
Responsible for maintaining supplier quality
Ensure an effective Audit Program within the organization
Cross collaboration with other QRC Regions and Areas to maintain and improve the Global Sales QMS.


Regulatory Affairs


Ensuring that required importer and distributor checks are conducted according to EU MDR requirements
Interact with the Authorities as a point of contact
Organizing and contributing to inspections


Training & Communication


Ensure training and awareness to the SSU’s in QMS / processes as required


KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)


Monitor KPIs as defined by GET / GS objectives
Implement QMS actions to achieve the defined targets


Centre of Excellence


Act as COE participant in the CAPA Center of Excellence within Global Sales and Service Organization
Moderate and coordinate CAPA Review Boards (CRB)
Support QRC Director with Global CAPA metrics and KPIs


General


Providing back-up for QRC Director
Supporting QRC Director with defining the vision for the region
Communicating, escalating appropriate information
Facilitate meetings with SSU


Minimum Requirements


Bachelor degree in the field of medical technologies, business administration or related field experience.
Minimum 5+ years hands-on experience in Quality & Regulatory
Very good knowledge in Medical device regulation including, MDR, UKMDR, MDD, EUMDR and other local regulations
Very good knowledge of ISO 13485 / ISO 9001
Very good knowledge and experience of working with NCs and CAPAs
Excellent language skills in English – verbal and written
Experience performing both internal and supplier audits


Required Knowledge, Skills And Abilities


Project management & risk analysis
SharePoint, E-learning, TrackWise experience
Customer oriented
Results oriented
Facilitation of meetings/training
Development of indicators / dashboard
