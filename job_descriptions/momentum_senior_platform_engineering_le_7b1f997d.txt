Role Purpose

The Senior Platform Engineering Lead is accountable for shaping, governing and evolving the platform capabilities

that product and engineering teams consume to build, deploy, secure, observe and operate technology solutions at scale.

The role translates enterprise technology strategy into platform capabilities, platform guardrails, technicalstandards and platform roadmaps. It provides technical leadership across cloud, developer experience, observability, security enablement and runtime platforms.

The role owns the architecture of platform products and the platform-facing compliance envelope. It does not own business solution architecture, application architecture, domain design or detailed software engineering design. The primary objective is to enable engineering teams to deliver safely and rapidly through self-service platforms, automation, reusable capabilities and measurable guardrails.

Technology Environment

The Role Operates Across


AWS cloud platforms and landing zones
GitLab CI/CD and DevSecOps tooling
Backstage Developer Portal
Kubernetes and container platforms
Infrastructure as Code platforms
Observability platforms (Datadog, OpenTelemetry, Grafana)
Identity and Access Management platforms
API and Integration platforms
Shared engineering services and platform products
Enterprise architecture standards and technology roadmaps


Requirements

Minimum Requirements


Bachelor's degree or equivalent in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering or related discipline.
10+ years experience across software engineering, cloud engineering, platform engineering or architecture.
3+ years experience in a senior platform architecture or platform engineering leadership role.
Experience building and operating enterprise-scale cloud platforms.
Experience defining platform standards and engineering guardrails.
Experience leading cross-functional technical initiatives.


Preferred


Financial Services or Insurance industry experience.
AWS certifications.


Duties and Responsibilities

Platform Strategy and Roadmap


Define and maintain the platform capability model.
Develop platform target architectures and roadmaps.
Identify technology opportunities and platform improvements.
Guide long-term platform investment decisions.
Support enterprise modernisation initiatives.


Outputs


Platform strategy
Platform roadmaps
Platform capability maps
Technology lifecycle plans
Platform investment recommendations


Developer Experience


Improve developer productivity through platform capabilities.
Promote self-service engineering practices.
Lead Backstage adoption and evolution.
Support reusable engineering templates and golden paths.


Outputs


Platform onboarding journeys
Backstage catalog structures
Self-service capabilities
Engineering accelerators
Golden path guidance


Platform Guardrails

Define Measurable Platform Guardrails Covering


Security
Operational supportability
Deployability
Observability
Resilience
Compliance
Cost management


Focus on automated enforcement wherever possible.

Outputs


Platform guardrails
Policy-as-Code controls
Compliance dashboards
Conformance scorecards
Platform standards


Technical Leadership


Provide architectural guidance to Platform Engineers.
Facilitate technical decision-making.
Mentor technical leads and engineers.
Lead platform architecture communities and forums.
Resolve cross-platform architectural concerns.


Outputs


Platform Architecture reviews
Technical coaching
Platform engineering guidance
Community of practice leadership


Operational Excellence


Partner with Production Engineering and SRE teams.
Improve platform resilience and operability.
Ensure observability standards are implemented.
Reduce operational toil through automation.


Outputs


Reliability improvements
Operational readiness standards
Platform telemetry standards
Recovery and resilience designs

The role is accountable for:


Platform architecture strategy
Platform product ownership models
Platform guardrails and standards
Platform reference architectures
Cloud platform architectures
