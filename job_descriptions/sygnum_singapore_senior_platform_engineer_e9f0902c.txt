About Sygnum

Sygnum is a global digital asset banking group, founded on Swiss and Singaporean heritage. We empower professional and institutional investors, banks, corporates and distributed ledger technology (DLT) companies to invest in digital assets with complete trust. Our team enables this through our institutional-grade security, expert personal service and portfolio of regulated digital asset banking, asset management, tokenisation and B2B services.




In Switzerland, Sygnum holds a banking licence and has CMS and Major Payment Institution Licences in Singapore. The group is also regulated in the established global financial hubs of Abu Dhabi and Luxembourg.




We believe that future has heritage. Our crypto-native team of banking, investment and digital asset technology professionals are building a trusted gateway between the traditional and digital asset economies that we call Future Finance. To learn more about how Sygnum’s mission and values are shaping this digital asset ecosystem, please visit sygnum.com and follow us on LinkedIn and X.

About the role

Sygnum’s Platform team builds and operates the cloud foundation that every product engineering team ship on: the AWS landing zone, our Kubernetes platform, and the shared services (API gateway, event streaming, secrets, observability, delivery) that turn raw cloud into a product-ready runway. We’re a small team with a large surface area, and we run a regulated fintech environment where every change is auditable.




We’re hiring a Senior Platform Engineer to help evolve that platform — pushing the developer experience forward while keeping reliability, security, and cost under control. You’ll split your time roughly between building (new platform capabilities, module work, self-service tooling) and running (upgrades, on-call, incident response, hardening).




What you’ll do

Design and ship improvements to our AWS multi-account landing zone and shared networking / IAM baselines, all through Terraform and a PR-driven plan/apply workflow — no click-ops in production.
Operate and evolve our managed Kubernetes platform: cluster lifecycle, workload identity, autoscaling, node image hardening, and GitOps-based delivery.
Own or co-own shared platform services: API gateway, event streaming, service mesh, certificates, secrets management, and the metrics/traces/SLO stack.
Improve the paved road for product teams — templates, modules, guardrails, and self-service — so the safe path is also the easy path.
Participate in the platform on-call rotation, lead incident response when needed, and drive blameless postmortems that actually change how the system behaves.
Contribute to our reliability posture: SLO tiers, backup and DR readiness, and security baselines (encryption, TLS, least-privilege IAM).
Keep changes auditable and release-disciplined — we operate in a regulated environment, and infrastructure changes must remain traceable.




Our ideal candidate

Must-have:

5+ years in Platform / SRE / DevOps roles with production ownership of cloud infrastructure at meaningful scale.
Deep AWS hands-on experience: IAM, VPC and networking, and managed Kubernetes (EKS or equivalent).
Terraform in production, with a PR/plan-review workflow (Atlantis, Terragrunt, or similar).
Solid Kubernetes operational chops: troubleshooting real workloads, autoscaling, GitOps delivery.
Comfortable on an on-call rotation and leading incident response under pressure.
Clear written communication — runbooks, postmortems, and design notes that other teams can actually use.

Nice-to-have:

Regulated-industry experience (banking, fintech, healthcare) with audit and change-control constraints.
Familiarity with any of: API gateways (Kong or similar), event streaming (Kafka/MSK), service mesh (Istio/Envoy).
Exposure to a secondary cloud (Azure/GCP) or multi-cloud operations.
Contributions to internal developer platforms, shared Terraform modules, or open-source infrastructure tooling.
