The Position

As the Strategy Enabling Lead – mQMS Portfolio Manager, you will play a pivotal role in shaping and executing the strategic landscape for the Roche DIA global quality management system, the mQMS. You will lead the strategic centralization, prioritization, and release management of our global pipeline and continuous improvement portfolio ensuring cohesive and end to end value chain implementation.

In this high-visibility role, you will bridge strategic vision and operational execution. You will orchestrate enterprise-level portfolio governance, align senior cross-functional leaders, and optimize capacity allocation—ensuring that every system initiative is sequenced to deliver maximum business value while minimizing organizational and system disruption, and maintaining compliance.

Operating at the heart of our matrix organization, you will apply rigorous portfolio management disciplines within our global environment. You will partner closely with subject matter experts in Quality, Regulatory, Compliance, Training, and Operations to translate complex compliance and system requirements into a structured, executable roadmap. Together with key leadership teams, you will balance resource constraints and turn our portfolio into a streamlined, business-enabling asset.

If you excel at bringing structure to multi-faceted portfolios, optimizing resource capacity, and leading strategic alignment in a matrixed environment, this role offers an exceptional platform to drive lasting enterprise impact.

The Opportunity

Enterprise Portfolio Governance & Strategic Alignment


Establish, maintain, and continuously refine a unified global change and continuous improvement portfolio across the global quality management system
Facilitate strategic portfolio prioritization sessions with executive stakeholders and functional leads; evaluate proposed change initiatives based on strategic impact, delivery risk, resource availability, and value generation
Define, deploy, and enforce standard intake criteria—including Definition of Ready (DoR) and Definition of Done (DoD)—to govern initiative submission, bundling, and deployment readiness


Centralized Scheduling & Capacity Optimization


Design and maintain the master portfolio release pipeline to prevent overlapping deployments, leverage synergies, mitigate resource bottlenecks, and reduce operational friction across the full product/service value chain
Partner with functional operations, documentation, and training teams to map end-to-end implementation workflows, evaluate capacity constraints, and establish a predictable release cadence
Implement portfolio guardrails to group minor initiatives into structured release cycles, significantly reducing re-change frequency and training impact on all functions


Matrix Leadership & Stakeholder Alignment


Manage complex stakeholder networks across all relevant DIA Partners ( Customer Areas, R&D, Global Operations, Affiliates etc). You will represent the global continuous improvement portfolio at relevant senior/executive leadership forums.
Serve as a strategic portfolio partner to Quality Process Owners, Quality Planners, and Senior Leadership, fostering shared accountability across an Agile matrix structure
Partner with Quality and Regulatory leaders who provide deep domain expertise, translating their compliance objectives into structured portfolio roadmaps and actionable release plans
Establish working structures across the full value chain to manage competing interests, negotiate resource trade-offs, and drive collaborative trade-off decisions
Deliver transparent portfolio health metrics, capacity analytics, dependency maps, and risk assessments to leadership teams to enable data-driven decision-making


Continuous Improvement & Portfolio Infrastructure


Establish and scale portfolio management tools, dashboards, and reporting frameworks to enhance real-time visibility, dependency tracking, and decision-making efficiency
Drive an organizational culture centered on portfolio simplification, process optimization, and disciplined execution


Enable New Ways of Working


Act as a role model for Agile Matrix Leadership behaviors (Vision, Architect, Coach, Catalyst) and support our global organizational culture and mindset
Guide teams through portfolio transformation, fostering adaptable, collaborative, and value-focused planning practices
Foster a work culture committed to customer focus, psychological safety, and operational excellence. Boldly innovate, break down functional silos, and constructively challenge the status quo
Ensure all portfolio activities directly align with and support our ten-year strategic ambitions to expand access to high-value solutions, grow our insight capabilities, and improve patient lives worldwide



Who You Are

Education: Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Operations Management, Engineering, Project Management, or a related field (Advanced degree/MBA preferred)
You have 7+ years of dedicated experience in Portfolio, Program, or Enterprise-level Resource & Release Management within a complex, highly regulated environment (Medical Devices, Diagnostics, Pharmaceuticals, Aerospace, or Financial Services preferred)
Certifications: Recognized Portfolio or Project Management certification (e.g., PMI-PfMP, PMI-PMP, SAFe LPM/Agile, PRINCE2, or equivalent process improvement certification) is highly desirable
Proven track record of establishing formal portfolio governance frameworks, intake models, and capacity planning systems across matrixed enterprise organizations
Demonstrated strength in leading prioritization and dependency mapping across high business impact or highly interconnected operational pipelines without direct line authority
Experience partnering with cross-functional technical/compliance SMEs (e.g., Quality, Regulatory, Legal, IT) to translate complex requirements into sequenced project roadmaps



Knowledge, Skills & Abilities

Portfolio & Strategic Capacity Planning: Deep expertise in portfolio management methodologies, pipeline prioritization, resource capacity modeling, and release train coordination
Matrix Leadership & Executive Influencing: Exceptional stakeholder management and communication skills; proven ability to align cross-functional leaders around competing priorities
Strategic Vision: A resilient systems thinker who navigates high-ambiguity environments to shape long-term strategy and decisions guided by corporate goals
Analytical & Data-Driven Reporting: Strong aptitude for translating complex portfolio data into clear executive dashboards, dependency maps, and actionable risk analyses
Cross-Functional Partnership: Demonstrated ability to build strong collaborative alliances with domain experts (e.g., Quality & Regulatory leads) to ensure portfolio execution meets external and internal compliance parameters without requiring direct domain expertise yourself
Process & Value Stream Optimization: Strong grasp of process mapping, release grouping, and workflow efficiency methodologies
Agile Governance & Adaptability: Proficiency in applying flexible, iterative governance structures to enterprise-scale systems and evolving business priorities
