Build the infrastructure backbone of a global quant trading powerhouse, up to SGD 250K + bonus. 🌏




📍 Singapore (Hybrid / Remote)

💰 Up to SGD 250K + bonus

🏦 Leading quant trading firm




One of the world’s leading quantitative trading firms is hiring an SRE / Platform Engineer to own the platforms behind mission-critical trading systems that run at global scale, around the clock. You’ll have real technical ownership and work alongside exceptional engineers.




What you’ll do

🔹 Design, build, and run scalable, resilient platform infrastructure

🔹 Own and optimise CI/CD pipelines to speed up software delivery

🔹 Strengthen observability: monitoring, logging, tracing, alerting

🔹 Build automation and self-service tooling engineers genuinely rely on

🔹 Support containerised workloads and orchestration at large scale

🔹 Drive reliability, performance, and scalability across distributed systems and middleware




What you’ll bring

✅ Background as a Platform Engineer, SRE, or DevOps Engineer

✅ Strong Linux and automation skills

✅ Proven track record building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines

✅ Hands-on Kubernetes and Docker

✅ Solid grasp of monitoring, logging, and observability platforms

✅ Infrastructure as Code experience

✅ Scripting or programming in Python, Go, Bash, or similar

✅ Real passion for reliable, highly available platforms




Missing one or two? Apply anyway.




Why this role

🚀 Real ownership of large-scale, mission-critical infrastructure

🤝 Work with exceptional engineers in a high-performance environment

📈 Complex engineering challenges using modern DevOps/SRE practices

💰 Strong compensation, bonus, and comprehensive benefits




📩 Apply now, or email hrubin@hunterbond.com for a confidential discussion.
