We're hiring at Dawon Drone India 🚀




Business Analyst – New Technology

📍 New Delhi / NCR




DDI designs and builds unmanned aircraft and advanced payloads for India's defence and security. We're scaling fast, and we need a sharp analyst at the centre of the new technology development.




We're looking for an analyst who can sit between our scientists and the business. You'll turn lab tests, tech development and trials into the evidence that drives decisions: requirements, test results, costs and the market case.




You'll:

• Capture user requirements and keep them traced through design and test

• Plan test campaigns with our scientific team and analyse the data

• Build cost models, including cost per drone engaged against guns and missiles

• Track competing systems and opportunities

• Support proposals and gate reviews




You bring:

• 3+ years as a business or systems analyst in defence or deep-tech product development

• An engineering degree (MBA a plus)

• Strong data analysis and technical writing skills

• Demonstrates strong interpersonal and communication skills




Exposure to counter-drone systems, or test and evaluation at a lab or OEM, is a strong plus.




Interested, or know someone who'd be great? DM me or apply here: [aradhana.kaushik@dawondroneindia.com]
