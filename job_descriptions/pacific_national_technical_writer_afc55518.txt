About Your New Role

Pacific National is seeking an experienced Technical Writer to develop, review and maintain technical documentation that supports safe, compliant and effective locomotive maintenance. In this role, you'll work with subject matter experts across engineering, maintenance and operations to create clear, accurate and user-friendly documentation that meets industry standards and compliance requirements. You will thrive in a fast-paced environment, managing multiple documentation projects, stakeholder reviews and competing priorities while maintaining high standards of accuracy and compliance.

Why you'll love this role


Competitive remuneration
Opportunity to influence critical safety and maintenance documentation
Work alongside highly experienced rail, engineering and maintenance professionals
Attractive employee benefits including paid parental leave, novated leasing options, Fitness Passport and wellbeing programs
Supportive and collaborative team environment
12-month fixed term opportunity with Australia's largest private rail freight operator


What You Will Do


Research, develop and author technical documentation in collaboration with engineering and operational SMEs
Translate complex engineering and maintenance information into clear, structured and accessible content
Edit, format and standardise documents in line with approved templates and style guides
Coordinate document reviews, stakeholder feedback and approval processes
Proofread and validate technical documentation to ensure accuracy and compliance
Manage document publication, version control and release through SharePoint and Assetnet
Support audits, compliance reviews and continuous improvement initiatives
Respond to technical documentation and standards queries across the business
Drive continuous improvement of documentation frameworks and quality processes


What We're Looking For


Significant experience in a Technical Writer position, ideally within rail, engineering, manufacturing or other highly regulated industries
Strong ability to interpret complex engineering concepts and translate them into practical documentation
Experience with document control, configuration management and publication processes
Proficiency in Microsoft Office, particularly Word, and SharePoint
Knowledge of quality management systems and compliance requirements
Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to engage a wide range of stakeholders
Experience within the rail industry and knowledge of locomotive maintenance documentation will be highly regarded


Why Pacific National?

Every day we transport millions of tonnes of critical freight around the country for our customers. With operations spanning mainland Australia, our people play a vital role in keeping Australia's supply chains moving safely and efficiently. We are committed to creating an inclusive workplace where everyone feels valued, respected and empowered to succeed.
