Want to build platforms rather than just keep them running?




This is a global trading and technology firm who have grown on the strength of its own technology, and the culture reflects that: non-hierarchical, collaborative and relaxed in style, with a real focus on continuous development whatever your level.




The Dublin technology team has been growing quickly, and engineers are trusted to take ownership.




The environment is on-premises, Linux-based and built for the people who trade on it every day.

You’ll sit at the centre of this, building and architecting the platform, writing Python and software that automates and extends it, and working directly with developers, quants and traders to make sure it does what they need. Working on site five days a week, you’ll be close to the infrastructure and close to the people who rely on it.




If you love Python, live in Linux and get a kick out of designing platforms from the ground up, we want to hear from you. You might come from DevOps, platform, infrastructure or systems engineering, or you might be a software engineer who keeps getting pulled towards the infrastructure side. What matters is that you enjoy building things properly and want to see them used.




If you want to work closer to the core of a technology-led business, this is that move.




Get in touch and apply here or reach out to benj@saragossa.io




No up-to-date CV required.
