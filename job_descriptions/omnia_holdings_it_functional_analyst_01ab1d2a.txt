ROLE IMPACT

This role will be responsible for bridging the gap between business requirements and technology solutions by ensuring that business needs are accurately translated into functional and technical specifications that can be effectively implemented across ERP, enterprise applications, and system integration solutions.

Key Responsibilities

Requirements Elicitation & Analysis

Stakeholder Engagement & Elicitation


Run workshops, interviews and process walkthroughs with business stakeholders, subject matter experts and operations teams.
Challenge stated requirements to establish the underlying business need, the decision being supported and the value being sought.



Requirements Analysis


Identify gaps, contradictions, duplication and unstated assumptions in source requirements, and drive them to resolution rather than escalating them unresolved.
Maintain the requirements catalogue in line with BABOK v3 and ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 practice.



Business Process Analysis


Document current-state (as-is) processes and define target-state (to-be) processes in BPMN 2.0, including the operational and control changes required.
Compliance & Risk



Assess regulatory, audit and data-protection (POPIA) implications of each requirement and flag where compliance or IT GRC input is needed.

Functional Specification

Functional Specification Documents (FSD)


Author FSDs that define system behaviour in business terms: process flows, use cases or user stories, business rules, decision tables, validation and calculation logic, roles and permissions, notifications and reporting outputs.
Define exception paths, error handling and edge cases explicitly, not only the happy path.



Acceptance Criteria & Sign-off


Write clear, testable acceptance criteria for every requirement.
Secure formal business sign-off on the FSD before build commences.



Technical Specification

Architecture & Data Design


Translate the approved FSD into a Technical Specification Document (TSD) that engineering can build against, covering the system and component architecture in scope and the changes required to each.
Specify data model changes: entities, attributes, data types, cardinality, referential integrity and retention.



Integration & Data Migration


Specify interfaces and integrations: API contracts, request/response payloads, protocols, authentication, error and retry behaviour, throughput and volumetrics.
Define data migration and transformation rules, including source-to-target field mappings and cleansing logic.



Design Decisions & Non-Functional Requirements


Document configuration versus customisation decisions, with rationale, in line with Omnia’s architecture standards.
Define non-functional requirements: performance, availability, scalability, security, auditability, logging and monitoring.



Feasibility & Validation


Work with solution architects and developers to validate technical feasibility, sizing and effort, and reconcile the specification with platform constraints.



Document assumptions, dependencies, constraints and out-of-scope items.

Delivery & Quality Support

Traceability & Backlog Management


Maintain a Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM) linking business requirement, functional specification, technical specification, test case and delivered feature.
Groom and maintain the backlog; ensure work items entering a sprint meet the team’s definition of ready.



Build Support


Support developers through build, acting as the authority on intended behaviour.



Testing & Validation


Author or review test scenarios; support System Integration Testing (SIT) and UAT.
Triage defects and distinguish genuine defects from change requests.



Handover & Documentation


Contribute to release notes, training material, user guides and operational handover documentation.



Confidentiality and Compliance


Handle highly confidential employee, and executive information with discretion.
Ensure adherence to company policies, governance standards, and relevant legislation.
Support audit requirements by maintaining accurate documentation and records.



Qualifications

MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS


Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems, Computer Science, Business Informatics, Industrial Engineering or a related field.
Postgraduate qualification in business or systems analysis – advantageous.



Experience


5+ years’ experience in Business Analysis and/or Systems Analysis within an IT delivery environment (mid to senior level).
Proven track record of authoring functional and technical specifications that build teams implement without rework.
Hands-on experience on ERP (preferably Microsoft Dynamics AX / D365), enterprise application and integration projects.
Experience defining data models, API and interface contracts, and data migration mappings.
Experience in Agile delivery (backlog grooming, definition of ready) and in supporting SIT and UAT.
Exposure to regulated environments with audit, internal control and data-protection (POPIA) requirements.



Key Competencies


Analytical Thinking – Ability to break ambiguous, incomplete requests into precise, structured requirements.
Critical Questioning – Challenges stated requirements to uncover the real business need and value.
Attention to Detail – Specifies rules, exception paths and edge cases completely, not only the happy path.
Collaboration & Communication – Translates clearly between business and technical audiences, in writing and verbally.
Ownership & Accountability – Owns requirements from intake to accepted delivery; resolves issues rather than escalating them unresolved.
Problem-Solving – Reconciles business needs with platform constraints to reach workable solutions.
Integrity & Governance – Applies traceability, version control and data-protection discipline consistently.
