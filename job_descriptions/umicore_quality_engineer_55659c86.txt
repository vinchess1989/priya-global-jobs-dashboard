Job Purpose

The Quality Engineer is responsible for supporting and improving product and process quality across manufacturing. The role works closely with Production, Technical, Quality, Suppliers and Customers to ensure products and processes meet customer, regulatory and internal requirements.

The Quality Engineer will use techniques, data analysis and continuous improvement methodologies to identify and resolve quality issues, lead continuous improvement projects and improve process performance across production.

Your Key Accountabilities

Product and Process Quality


Assist with ensuring products are manufactured in accordance with customer specifications, drawings, procedures and internal quality standards
Support incoming, in-process and final inspection activities
Carry out incoming inspections of raw materials, components and consumables. Record and collect data to better evaluate supplier performance
Collaborate with production teams to identify opportunities to improve process capability, consistency and product quality
Ensure inspection and scanning areas are organised and appropriately equipped to efficiently manage product flow
Support the creation and implementation of new process flows covering goods-in through to goods-out.
Support post-processing activities, including batch scanning and baking



Quality Inspection and Data Analysis


Collect, analyse and interpret quality and production data to identify trends, recurring issues and opportunities for improvement
Maintain accurate quality records and ensure inspection results are appropriately documented
Utilise statistical and analytical techniques to support data-driven decision making
Support the investigation of non-conforming product and ensure appropriate containment actions are implemented



Root Cause Analysis and Corrective Actions


Participate in root cause investigations and corrective action activities
Participate in 8D investigations at the request of the Quality Manager
Develop and implement corrective and preventative actions to eliminate the recurrence of quality issues
Monitor the effectiveness of corrective actions and verify that improvements have been sustained



Continuous Improvement and Operational Excellence


Identify, support and lead continuous improvement initiatives across production
Lead and participate in Operational Excellence projects focused on improving quality, productivity and process reliability
Support improvement projects
Use lean and continuous improvement methodologies to reduce defects, waste and variation
Identify opportunities to improve process controls and reduce the risk of human error



Customers and Suppliers


Work with suppliers to ensure materials, components and consumables meet UCS requirements
Support the investigation and resolution of supplier quality issues
Maintain the incoming substrate inspection process ensuring all records are accurate and any non-conforming material is reported and contained
Support the investigation and resolution of customer quality concerns



Who we are looking for


3+ years experience within a regulated manufacturing, engineering or quality environment
Excellent IT skills with proficiency in data analysis and statistical methods e.g Minitab, SigmaXL
Strong attention to detail and commitment to excellence
Understanding of Continuous Improvement and Operational Excellence and how it relates to precision manufacturing
Excellent communication, interpersonal and collaboration skills
Understanding of Six Sigma Methodologies
Ability to work with multi-national sites and personnel
Ability to work independently and as part of a team
Excellent record keeping
