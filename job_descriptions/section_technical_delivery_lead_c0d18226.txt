We’re hiring a Technical Delivery Lead to join Section in Singapore.




Section combines human-centred thinking, creativity, and technology to solve real business and customer problems. We care about useful outcomes, strong foundations, and leaving the organisations we work with better than we found them.




In this role, you will lead engineering delivery for assigned projects—from turning an agreed outcome into a realistic technical plan, through execution, release, and operational handover.




You will work with colleagues across Singapore and Vietnam, partnering with project managers, product owners, designers, QA, engineers, and technical specialists. You will report to the Technical Delivery Director, with technology strategy and architecture assurance from the CTO.




This is not a role for someone who only reviews from the sidelines. You will remain hands-on where it matters: resolving difficult problems, reviewing critical implementation, guiding technical decisions, and unblocking the team. At the same time, you will coach engineers, delegate meaningful ownership, and build delivery practices that do not depend on one person.




You will be responsible for:

Turning approved scope and architecture into executable engineering plans, estimates, dependencies, and realistic commitments
Tracking engineering effort and forecast-to-complete, identifying delivery or budget variance early
Managing technical risks, scope changes, dependencies, and recovery plans
Leading quality through code review, automated testing, secure development, performance, and accessibility validation
Establishing practical CI/CD, release, rollback, monitoring, runbook, and operational-handover practices
Communicating delivery health clearly to project managers, clients, and technical leadership
Strengthening team capability through coaching, delegation, automation, AI tools, and reusable components
Supporting qualified presales and RFP work with credible estimates, assumptions, and risks




We are looking for someone with at least seven years of relevant software-engineering experience, including hands-on technical leadership and delivery of production systems. You should be comfortable making trade-offs across customer impact, effort, risk, quality, and cost—and confident enough to challenge unrealistic commitments constructively.




Experience with cloud production environments, CI/CD, automated testing, observability, incident response, APIs, integrations, and modern frontend or backend stacks will be important. Experience leading distributed teams across Singapore and Vietnam is highly valued.




If you are practical, curious, commercially aware, and committed to delivering dependable technology without compromising quality, we would like to hear from you.
