Job Description

The Digital department at INSEAD supports the school's global operations by delivering cutting-edge technological solutions that enhance teaching, research, and administrative functions. It ensures seamless integration of digital tools and infrastructure across all campuses, fostering innovation and operational efficiency.

INSEAD is seeking a Senior Lead Delivery to join our Digital department. This is a full-time, permanent contract position based at our Asia Campus in Singapore. A hybrid working model is in place – 3 days on campus, 2 days from home.

About The Job

The Senior Lead Selivery owns agile delivery execution for one or more platforms or projects within a Value Stream, ensuring the team ships the prioritized backlog reliably. The role facilitates the team's delivery cadence, removes blockers, and coordinates cross-team dependencies, partnering closely with the Product Manager and Technical Lead.

Your role within the team


Facilitate the weekly Delivery Sync; manage sprint planning, execution, and reporting
Remove blockers and manage cross-team dependencies within the stream
Coordinate with the Technical Lead on delivery feasibility, sequencing, and technical risk
Track and report delivery velocity, risk, and roadmap progress to the Product Manager and Business Partner
Coach the team on agile / scrum practices and drive continuous improvement
For multi-team streams, coordinate Program Increment-level planning (SAFe Agile Release Train cadence) where applicable
Work with ODC on resourcing and delivery
Manage project delivery timelines, budgets, risks, and dependencies to ensure committed outcomes are delivered on time and within agreed scope.
Engage stakeholders proactively to understand priorities, align expectations, and ensure delivery outcomes meet business needs.
Communicate clearly with technical and business stakeholders, translating delivery progress, risks, and dependencies into concise updates and actionable decisions.
Build strong working relationships across cross-functional teams to drive alignment, unblock delivery, and support shared outcomes.


The ideal candidate profile


3–5+ years of delivery management / Scrum Master experience in an agile enterprise environment
Experience managing distributed or blended teams (internal plus ODC/vendor) is an advantage
Strong facilitation and risk-management skills
Track record of predictable delivery against committed sprint and release plans
Experience delivering complex digital or technology projects within the education sector.
