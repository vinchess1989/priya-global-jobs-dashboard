Your role

Lead the establishment, operation and continuous improvement of deployment and environment management practices across complex digital and technology initiatives. The role plans and governs the controlled movement of changes through development, test, staging and production environments; coordinates integrated deployment outcomes across delivery teams and suppliers; and provides authoritative advice on readiness, risk, implementation, rollback and post-deployment assurance to protect service continuity and customer outcomes.

What You’ll Do


Establish deployment and environment management standards, governance and controls.
Lead integrated release calendars, deployment plans, cutover sequencing and rollback arrangements.
Coordinate environment capacity, configuration, access, data refresh and readiness across teams.
Apply deployment and service acceptance gates using testing, security and operational evidence.
Direct high-risk deployments, command and control, incident escalation and recovery.
Provide authoritative go/no-go advice on readiness, dependencies, residual risk and service impacts.
Improve deployment automation, CI/CD, DevSecOps, monitoring and release reliability.
Manage delivery partners and uplift deployment practice through coaching and lessons learned.


What We’re Looking For


Expert knowledge of release management, CI/CD, DevSecOps, configuration management and rollback.
Experience in managing shared environments, service acceptance and deployment risks across multiple suppliers.
Ability to coordinate high-pressure implementations and provide clear executive readiness advice.
Demonstrated improvement of deployment reliability, automation and operational handover.


Essential Requirements


Tertiary qualifications in information technology, computer science, engineering, business or a related discipline, or equivalent relevant knowledge, skills and experience.
The role is required to support planned deployments, cutovers, recovery activities and major incidents outside normal business hours and may participate in an on-call arrangement


Desirable Requirements


Experience with Salesforce, cloud services and integrated enterprise applications.
ITIL 4, DevOps, cloud, release or change management certifications.
Experience in public sector or similarly regulated service environments.
