ABOUT THE POSITION

Location: Sandton, Johannesburg
Contract Duration: 6 Months
Working Hours: Monday to Friday, 08:00–17:00
Work Arrangement: Primarily office-based, with the possibility of working from home a few days per week.
Start Date: As soon as possible
Job Purpose
The Senior DevOps Engineer is responsible for designing, building and managing automation, CI/CD pipelines and cloud infrastructure to enable product teams to deliver software quickly, safely and reliably. The role provides senior technical leadership within the DevOps and Cloud team, establishes technical standards, resolves complex platform challenges and mentors other engineers, while ensuring quality, security, availability and cost efficiency.

Key Responsibilities


Design, build and maintain CI/CD pipelines for application and infrastructure code.
Automate build, testing, security scanning and deployment stages.
Introduce safe release strategies, including blue-green deployments, canary releases and automated rollbacks.
Provision and manage cloud infrastructure across AWS, Azure or Google Cloud using Terraform or equivalent tools.
Maintain reusable, version-controlled modules and environment standards.
Manage configuration using Ansible, Puppet or Chef.
Build and maintain Docker images and Kubernetes clusters, including networking, scaling and upgrades.
Manage Helm charts or equivalent deployment manifests.
Optimise workloads for performance, resilience and cost.
Implement logging, metrics, tracing and alerting across platforms and services.
Define and track service level objectives with product and operations teams.
Participate in the on-call rotation and lead the technical resolution of major incidents.
Conduct blameless post-incident reviews and drive fixes to root causes.
Maintain backup, disaster recovery and capacity plans.
Build security into pipelines through secrets management, dependency and image scanning, and policy as code.
Apply least-privilege access and hardening standards to cloud and container environments.
Support audit, risk and regulatory requirements, including PCI DSS and POPIA where applicable.
Monitor cloud expenditure, identify waste and anomalies, and optimise resource usage.
Provide input to the Senior Manager on the DevOps and Cloud budget.
Act as technical lead on complex DevOps and cloud projects.
Review code, pipelines and infrastructure changes to ensure quality and compliance with standards.
Mentor and coach junior and intermediate engineers.
Conduct knowledge-sharing sessions and assist with onboarding new team members.
Contribute to technical interviews when hiring.
Collaborate with development, QA, operations and security teams to remove delivery bottlenecks.
Translate business and internal client needs into platform solutions.
Maintain runbooks, architecture documents and standard operating procedures.
Evaluate new tools and recommend improvements to the DevOps and Cloud strategy.

Minimum Requirements

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
Minimum of 6 years' experience in DevOps, cloud, site reliability or infrastructure engineering, with at least 2 years at senior level.
Proven experience delivering CI/CD pipelines and cloud infrastructure in complex, enterprise-scale or high-transaction environments.
Experience mentoring other engineers.
Experience in fintech, payments or telecommunications will be advantageous.

Technical Skills

Expert knowledge of CI/CD tools and practices, such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps or Jenkins.
Strong hands-on experience with Docker and Kubernetes, including Helm and managed services such as EKS, AKS or GKE.
Infrastructure as Code experience using Terraform or equivalent tools.
Solid experience with at least one major cloud platform: AWS, Azure or Google Cloud.
Configuration management experience with Ansible, Puppet or Chef.
Scripting experience in Python, Bash or PowerShell. Go will be advantageous.
Knowledge of monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, ELK or Datadog.
Strong understanding of DevSecOps practices, including secrets management, vulnerability scanning and policy as code.
Strong Linux administration, networking and Git workflow knowledge.
Clear written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to solve problems calmly under pressure.

Professional Certifications
At least one relevant certification, such as:


AWS Certified DevOps Engineer – Professional.
Microsoft Certified: Azure DevOps Engineer Expert.
Google Professional Cloud DevOps Engineer.
Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA).

HashiCorp Terraform Associate will be advantageous.

Key Competencies

Analytical thinking, confidence and assertiveness.
Flexibility, resilience and a positive attitude.
Technical leadership and accountability.
Ability to mentor and develop others.
Collaboration and relationship-building.
Clear communication of technical information.
Strong problem-solving and decision-making abilities.
Innovation and initiative.
Planning, organisation and time management.
Adaptability and the ability to work under pressure.
Customer focus and business awareness.
Systems and solution design.
Cloud architecture, automation and scripting.
Incident and problem management.
Technical documentation and project planning.

Only candidates who meet the essential requirements will be considered.

CI/CD tools
Docker and Kubernetes
