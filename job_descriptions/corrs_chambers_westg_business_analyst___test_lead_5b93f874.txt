Join Corrs Support Services, Australia's leading independent law firm.

We are looking for an experienced Business Analyst & Test Lead to play a key role in strengthening Business Analysis and Testing capability across our technology portfolio.

This is a senior, hands-on position combining complex project delivery with functional leadership. You will personally lead business analysis on strategic and high-risk initiatives, while setting consistent standards, improving quality and building capability across the BA team and broader Testing function.

Working closely with the Head of Portfolio Delivery, Project Managers and business and Technology stakeholders, you will provide day-to-day leadership to Business Analysts, helping coordinate priorities, coach the team and ensure high-quality analysis and testing practices.

This is not a traditional people-management role. Instead, it is an opportunity to lead through expertise, influence and functional authority, while remaining actively involved in delivery.

Key responsibilities

In this role, you will:

Lead end-to-end business analysis for complex, strategic and high-risk technology and transformation initiatives.
Facilitate discovery, requirements gathering and process analysis with senior business and Technology stakeholders.
Translate business needs into clear functional, non-functional and technical requirements, maintaining traceability through design, testing and implementation.
Step into challenging projects where requirements, scope, stakeholders or delivery require senior BA support.
Establish and continuously improve practical Business Analysis frameworks, standards, templates and quality controls.
Provide day-to-day functional leadership to Business Analysts, including work allocation, prioritisation, coaching and quality assurance.
Coordinate BA capacity and demand in partnership with the Head of Portfolio Delivery and Project Managers.
Build BA capability through coaching, feedback, knowledge sharing and a collaborative community of practice.
Establish and mature a proportionate Testing framework, including standards for test planning, test cases, defect management, evidence, sign-off and traceability.
Guide project teams and business stakeholders through UAT, defect triage and go-live readiness.
Develop more repeatable regression testing practices and help shape the future use of test automation and AI-assisted testing.
Introduce appropriate quality gates for significant or high-risk initiatives, identifying risks early and recommending corrective action.
Use lessons learned and recurring delivery challenges to continuously improve standards, tools and ways of working.
Champion contemporary BA and Testing practices, including effective use of AI, automation and Microsoft 365 tools.

Essential criteria

You will be an accomplished Senior or Lead Business Analyst who enjoys being close to delivery while also lifting the capability of the wider team.

Ideally, you will bring:

Significant Senior or Lead BA experience across complex technology and business transformation programs.
Strong hands-on capability across discovery, requirements elicitation, process analysis, stakeholder facilitation, requirements management and traceability.
Experience establishing or improving BA frameworks, standards and communities of practice.
Demonstrated experience developing test strategies, test plans, test cases, UAT approaches and defect-management practices.
Experience coaching Business Analysts and reviewing the quality of BA deliverables.
The ability to influence and provide direction within a matrix environment without relying on formal people-management authority.
Excellent communication, facilitation, influencing and negotiation skills, including with senior business, legal and Technology stakeholders.
Strong organisational and prioritisation skills, with the ability to work across multiple concurrent initiatives.
Working knowledge of recognised BA frameworks such as IIBA BABOK.
Strong understanding of Agile, Waterfall and Hybrid delivery approaches, and when each should be applied.
Sound knowledge of manual testing, UAT, regression testing, defect triage, test evidence and quality assurance.
Familiarity with requirements and test-management tooling, traceability and reporting.
Experience with Smartsheet, Microsoft 365 or similar portfolio and project delivery tools.
Experience with test automation or AI-enabled analysis and testing will be highly regarded.
Experience within legal, professional services, financial services or another complex enterprise environment will also be advantageous.

This is an opportunity to make a tangible impact beyond individual project delivery. You will have the scope to shape how Business Analysis and Testing operate, lift capability across the team and introduce practical, contemporary ways of working, while still remaining hands-on with complex and meaningful transformation initiatives.
