At NUHS Population Health & Community Care (PHCC), we believe that better health starts in the community.

As the Regional Health Manager for western Singapore, we bring together NUHS family of hospitals and polyclinics, alongside general practitioners, social care providers, and community partners to improve the health and wellbeing of residents through an integrated ecosystem of care. Together, we are reimagining how health and care are delivered by strengthening prevention, enabling seamless care experiences, and empowering individuals to take charge of their health.

Join us on this meaningful journey. Whether advancing innovative programmes, strengthening community partnerships, or shaping the future of population health and community care, your work will help create lasting impact for residents and communities across western Singapore.

Overview

Join our System team as a Senior Executive/Assistant Manager, System, supporting and independently managing assigned healthcare information system workstreams. The role focuses on requirements gathering, user access and access governance, system support, reporting and stakeholder coordination across NGEMR, disease registries and population health management tools. You will also support system rollouts, enhancements, testing and user adoption activities

Key Responsibilities


Facilitate stakeholder sessions to gather functional requirements and maintain comprehensive documentation for system enhancements and new implementations
Administer user access provisioning and deprovisioning for NGEMR, ADID, and M365 systems whilst ensuring full compliance with security frameworks and AIAM protocols
Support user access governance, periodic access reviews, audits and compliance activities across healthcare systems to ensure that access remains appropriate, secure and compliant
Monitor system performance metrics and provide first-line technical support to healthcare clusters, coordinating escalations when required
Generate reports using Epic tools (Reporting Workbench, SlicerDicer) and assist in data validation activities for disease registries and population health metrics
Support system rollout projects and coordinate user training sessions to ensure smooth adoption of new features and enhancements
Coordinate system maintenance activities and communicate updates to users whilst supporting user acceptance testing for system improvements
Maintain communication logs, follow up on outstanding requests, and provide analytic and administrative support for population health analytics projects



Key Requirements


A degree from a recognized university, with minimum 2-3 years of relevant working experiences
Experience in healthcare information systems, business analysis, system implementation, user access governance or digital health would be advantageous
Understanding of healthcare workflows and electronic medical record systems
Good communication and interpersonal skills
Good analytical and problem-solving skills
