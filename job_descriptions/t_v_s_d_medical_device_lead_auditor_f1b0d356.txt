TÜV SÜD Group – A global leader in the field of testing, inspection, management system certification and product certification renowned for quality, integrity and technical excellence.

Established more than 150 years ago in Germany, TÜV SÜD Group is a leading global provider of technical services with more than 30,000 highly skilled employees across 1,000 offices worldwide. TÜV SÜD Group offers a wide range of testing & product certification, inspection, auditing & system certification, training and knowledge services. We are a premium quality, safety, and sustainability solutions provider.

The Customer Specialist/Lead Auditor manages and coordinates assigned Singapore Accreditation Council (SAC)-accredited ISO 13485 certification projects across the full certification lifecycle, from application review to certification activities. The role conducts audits within authorized competence, evaluates client quality management systems against ISO 13485, and acts as the primary point of contact for customers.

Key Responsibilities

Project Management and Conformity Assessment


Manage assigned SAC-accredited ISO 13485 certification projects throughout the certification lifecycle, including application review, audit planning, audit execution, nonconformity management, and certification activities.
Conduct Stage 1, Stage 2, surveillance, recertification, special, and change audits, onsite or remotely, within authorized competence areas.
Evaluate the effectiveness and conformity of client quality management systems against ISO 13485 requirements and applicable certification criteria.
Identify, document, and communicate audit findings, including nonconformities, opportunities for improvement, and audit conclusions.
Prepare and submit audit reports and associated certification records for technical review and certification decision.
Review nonconformity corrections, corrective actions, and supporting evidence submitted by clients within authorized competence.


Technical Support


Escalate technical, regulatory, competence, impartiality, compliance, or certification-related concerns to the Technical Certifier or Certification Body Manager.
Support accreditation assessments, witness audits, internal audits, auditor qualification activities, and continual improvement initiatives.
Maintain impartiality, confidentiality, and professional conduct in accordance with certification body policies and accreditation requirements.


Key Requirements

Education


Bachelor's degree in Biomedical Engineering, Engineering, Life Sciences, Medical Technology, Pharmacy, or a related discipline.


Experience


Minimum 3 years of experience in medical device manufacturing, R&D, quality assurance, regulatory affairs, or related conformity assessment activities.
Experience with ISO 13485 quality management systems is preferred.
Experience conducting audits or participating in quality management system assessments is an advantage.


Skills


Strong communication, project management, and stakeholder management skills.
Ability to conduct audits independently and manage multiple certification projects simultaneously.
Proficient in written and spoken English.


Others


Willingness to travel


Mandatory Competency Development

The Successful Candidate Is Required To Obtain And Maintain


ISO 13485 Auditor Authorization
Continuing professional development and ongoing maintenance of auditor competence in accordance with internal procedures and SAC requirements
