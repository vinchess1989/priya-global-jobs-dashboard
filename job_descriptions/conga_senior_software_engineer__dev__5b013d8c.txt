Sr. Software Engineer, DevOps

Location: Canada

Reports to: Senior Director, Cloud Operations

A quick snapshot…

As a Senior Software Engineer, DevOps at Conga, you will play a critical role in designing, building, and optimizing the cloud infrastructure and delivery capabilities that power our industry-leading revenue lifecycle management solutions. You will collaborate closely with Engineering, Product, and Operations teams to establish and advance modern Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) practices that enable teams to innovate rapidly while maintaining scalability, reliability, and security.

This role is ideal for an engineer who thrives in dynamic environments, enjoys solving complex infrastructure challenges, and is passionate about automation, cloud technologies, and operational excellence. You will help build the foundation that enables Conga’s engineering teams to deliver world-class software experiences for customers around the globe.

Why it’s a big deal…

At Conga, DevOps is a strategic enabler of product innovation and platform reliability. As we continue to scale our cloud-native platforms and global customer footprint, the need for secure, automated, and resilient infrastructure has never been greater.

As a Senior Software Engineer DevOps, you will help drive the evolution of our development and deployment ecosystems, empowering engineering teams to deliver high-quality software faster and more efficiently. Your expertise will directly influence system availability, deployment velocity, operational excellence, and the overall developer experience across the organization.

You'll work alongside a highly talented team of engineers dedicated to solving complex technical challenges while supporting products that help customers streamline and transform their revenue operations.

What You’ll Lead…

Continuous Integration & Delivery Excellence


Design, implement, and maintain scalable CI/CD pipelines that automate software build, testing, deployment, and release processes.
Drive continuous improvement initiatives that increase deployment efficiency, reliability, and developer productivity.
Partner with engineering teams to establish DevOps best practices across the software development lifecycle.
Champion automation-first approaches that reduce manual effort and improve operational consistency.


Cloud Infrastructure & Platform Engineering


Design and support highly available, secure, and scalable cloud infrastructure across AWS and Microsoft Azure environments.
Develop and maintain Infrastructure-as-Code (IaC) solutions using tools such as Terraform and CloudFormation.
Optimize platform performance, cost efficiency, and operational resilience across production and non-production environments.
Partner with engineering teams to build and maintain cloud-native architectures that support growth and scale.


Containerization & Kubernetes Strategy


Design, deploy, and manage containerized workloads utilizing Docker and Kubernetes.
Establish deployment standards and operational best practices for Kubernetes-based environments.
Support container orchestration, monitoring, scaling, and lifecycle management initiatives.
Drive platform modernization efforts through the adoption of cloud-native technologies.


Security, Reliability & Operational Excellence


Implement and maintain infrastructure and application security best practices, including encryption, access controls, network security, and vulnerability management.
Design solutions that support fault tolerance, disaster recovery, and business continuity objectives.
Collaborate with teams to improve observability, monitoring, alerting, and incident response capabilities.
Proactively identify risks and implement solutions that improve system reliability and uptime.


Cross-Functional Engineering Partnership


Collaborate with software engineers, architects, and product teams to support scalable application development and deployment strategies.
Provide technical guidance on infrastructure architecture, cloud adoption, and operational best practices.
Participate in architectural reviews and contribute to long-term platform strategy discussions.
Foster a culture of continuous learning, innovation, and engineering excellence.


Are you the person we’re looking for?


Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
5+ years of hands-on experience architecting, building, and supporting highly scalable software and infrastructure solutions.
Proven experience deploying and managing production workloads within AWS and/or Microsoft Azure cloud environments.
Experience implementing and supporting Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery practices within modern development organizations.
Demonstrated success designing solutions that deliver high availability, scalability, and reliability.


Technical Expertise


Strong understanding of microservices architectures and modern cloud-based application design principles, including SaaS, PaaS, IaaS, multi-tenancy, and distributed systems.
Deep knowledge of Infrastructure-as-Code frameworks such as Terraform and CloudFormation.
Experience with container technologies, including Docker and Kubernetes.
Working knowledge of networking concepts, including TCP/IP, DNS, VPNs, firewalls, routers, and switches.
Familiarity with cloud and application security best practices.
Knowledge of modern software development technologies including .NET Core/.NET Framework, Java, and JavaScript.
Experience analyzing and solving complex technical challenges within large-scale distributed systems.


Key Competencies

DevOps & Automation Mindset

You are passionate about automation, process optimization, and building repeatable, scalable solutions that improve engineering efficiency.

Infrastructure & Cloud Expertise

You bring a strong understanding of cloud-native technologies and modern infrastructure practices that support enterprise-scale applications.

Problem Solver

You can quickly analyze complex systems, identify root causes, and implement effective solutions in fast-paced environments.

Collaboration & Communication

You partner effectively across engineering teams and communicate technical concepts clearly to diverse stakeholders.

Operational Excellence

You are committed to reliability, security, scalability, and maintaining high standards for platform performance and availability.

Here’s What Will Give You An Edge…


Cloud certifications in AWS, Microsoft Azure, and/or Kubernetes.
Experience architecting or deploying enterprise solutions on Kubernetes platforms.
Experience designing highly available cloud architectures with fault tolerance and disaster recovery capabilities.
Familiarity with security standards and frameworks including OAuth, WS-Security, encryption in transit, and encryption at rest.
Experience working within Agile development environments.
Hands-on experience with Atlassian Jira and modern software delivery toolchains.
Background supporting large-scale SaaS products and cloud-native platforms.
