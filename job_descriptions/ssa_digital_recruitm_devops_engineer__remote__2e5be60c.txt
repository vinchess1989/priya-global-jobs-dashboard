Azure DevOps Engineer | Terraform | GCP | £425pd | Remote

£425 per day | UK Remote | 1 Day per Month in London or Leeds

We're looking for an experienced Azure DevOps Engineer with strong Terraform, Azure DevOps and GCP experience to support the automation and delivery of a large-scale cloud data platform.

This is a hands-on engineering role focused on Infrastructure as Code (IaC), CI/CD pipelines, deployment automation and cloud environment provisioning.

What we're looking for:

Strong Azure DevOps experience, including Azure Pipelines, CI/CD and release management.

Hands-on Terraform experience, developing and maintaining Infrastructure as Code.

Good understanding of Google Cloud Platform (GCP) architecture and cloud infrastructure.

Experience with automated cloud provisioning, infrastructure deployment and environment management.

Strong knowledge of Git, YAML, scripting and deployment automation.

Experience building and maintaining reusable CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure configurations.

Understanding of cloud engineering best practices, automation and infrastructure standards.

Ability to collaborate with engineering teams to ensure reliable, consistent deployments.

You'll be supporting deployment automation, infrastructure provisioning and CI/CD improvements across a modern cloud data platform.

Day Rate: £425 per day
Location: UK Remote – 1 day per month onsite in London or Leeds

If you're a hands-on Azure DevOps Engineer with strong Terraform and GCP experience, we'd love to hear from you.

Apply now with your latest CV to find out more.
