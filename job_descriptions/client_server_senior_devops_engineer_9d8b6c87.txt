Senior DevOps Engineer (AWS VMWare GitHub) Bracknell / WFH to £95k




Are you a Senior DevOps Engineer who enjoys building scalable, resilient platforms and improving the way software is delivered?




You could be progressing your career in a senior, hands-on DevOps Engineer role a global financial services company as they rebuild the international payments platform, working within the Corporate Payments division.




As a Senior DevOps Engineer you will build, deploy and operate production applications across AWS, VMware and Kubernetes environments. You'll develop and improve CI/CD pipelines, implement Infrastructure as Code (Terraform) and configuration management and create self-service environments that empower developers to work more independently.




You'll also enhance monitoring and observability to identify and resolve production issues, drive zero downtime deployments and design highly available systems. With plenty of scope to improve existing platforms and services, you'll help establish a strong DevOps culture, sharing your expertise and guiding multiple development teams.




Location / WFH:

You can work from home most of the time, joining colleagues in the Bracknell office twice a week.




About You:

You have strong experience building and maintaining software delivery pipelines and CI/CD processes
You have hands-on experience with Infrastructure as Code using Terraform and configuration management tools such as Ansible or Puppet
You have experience with Kubernetes, Docker, GitHub, Artifactory and AWS services such as ECS or EKS
You have experience managing production hosting environments, AWS and VMware
You have a solid understanding of Linux, Windows Server and networking
You're familiar with monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, Icinga2 or APM platforms
You're collaborative with great communication skills




What's in it for you:

Salary to £95k + bonus
23 days holiday, rising to 28 + your birthday off
Pension, Life Assurance, Income Protection, Critical Illness Cover
Healthcare
