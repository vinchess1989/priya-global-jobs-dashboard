Life changing therapies. Global impact. Bridge to thousands of biopharma companies and their patients.

We are PCI.

Our investment is in People who make an impact, drive progress and create a better tomorrow. Our strategy includes building teams across our global network to pioneer and shape the future of PCI.

Reporting to the Training & Compliance Manager, this role will provide

support to the Production & Warehouse functions ensuring efficient management of

system documentation and product quality investigations from creation through to validated

closure.


Provides support, information and assistance with the initiation, investigation, submission, and


Closure Of The Following Documents


Change Controls (CC’s)
Management of Change (MOC’s)
TER’s
Operational Deviations & Events
Customer Complaints
Corrective / Prevention action plans
Facilitates reviews of and revises department related documents i.e., SOP's and Logbooks


etc


Develop, implement and report appropriate KPI’s relating to the role.
Represents the Operations function in NPI projects to ensure the necessary documentation is


completed and reviewed as required.


Through identification of root causes and corrective actions identifies opportunities for


continuous improvement across similar processes and procedures.


To undertake such tasks and to manage specific or ad hoc projects as and when required to


meet department and business needs and within the scope of the job-holder’s capabilities

Essential

Knowledge / Skills & Experience


Minimum 2 years of working with quality and regulatory requirements in the pharmaceutical


industry, including pharmaceutical GMPs, or related experience.


Technical qualification at 3rd Level or equivalent in Engineering or Quality Assurance.
Excellent analytical and critical thinking skills with a record of successfully implementing


change management.


Maintains knowledge of current Good Manufacturing Practices.
Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written.


Join us and be part of building the bridge between life changing therapies and patients. Let’s talk future
