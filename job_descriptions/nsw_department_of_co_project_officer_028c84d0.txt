Project Officer – Technical Training and Business Improvement

Location – Parramatta (Hybrid – 50%)

Employment type – Ongoing – Fulltime (35 hours per week)

Salary – Clerk Grade 7/8 ($116,981 - $129,492) plus superannuation and annual leave loading

Your role

Do you have a passion for developing content and would like to help make a positive difference in NSW social housing maintenance space?

Homes NSW is an Agency of the Department of Communities and Justice (DCJ). Our vision is for NSW to be a place where everyone has access to safe and secure housing, and where experiences of homelessness are rare, brief, and non-recurring.

We lead work to deliver more quality social housing to our 262,000 tenants across NSW, empowering our tenants to live fulfilling lives and achieve their potential in inclusive communities with a focus on breaking, rather than managing disadvantage.

We strive for excellence in service delivery and most importantly, Homes NSW puts people at the heart – including our staff. Working for us means you are joining a team where your expertise and skills will be valued.

The Technical Training and Business Improvement team is focused on developing and delivering Housing Portfolio-wide training and continuous improvement initiatives informed by the Quality Management System. This includes the development, creation, and continued update of processes and procedures, user guides, quick reference guides, and internal eLearning modules, as well as the delivery of monthly onboarding training and the provision of training on new systems and processes.

What You’ll Do

This role will have a focus on working with stakeholders to document processes and procedures.

The Project Officer Role Will Be Responsible For


facilitating stakeholder discussions to identify relevant process sponsors and subject matter experts (SMEs)
creating and updating process maps in Visio and procedure documents in Word for stakeholder feedback and endorsement
identifying and coordinating supporting resources
maintaining project documentation in Content Manager (TRIM)
implementing and monitoring project plans to ensure projects are delivered on time, within budget, and to quality standards
preparing Asset Service Notices (ASNs) to communicate new or updated process maps or procedure documents


The Project Officer Will Be Required To


work collaboratively with all levels of the organisation
manage stakeholder expectations
track project progress against milestones and deliverables
identify emerging issues to anticipate timely resolution


What We’re Looking For

Tertiary qualifications in a related discipline and/or equivalent knowledge, skills and experience with demonstrated commitment to ongoing professional development.

A knowledge of maintenance or experience in the industry is not required but desirable.

The Project Officer Should Possess


a willingness to build relationships with internal and external stakeholders of all levels
an outcome driven approach
the ability to juggle multiple projects
an openness to receiving feedback
a keen interest in applying consistency in look, feel, and language throughout content produced
a clear and professional written and oral communication style
an awareness of applying the standard for Australian Government writing and editing
