Hiring: Automation Consultant – Ansible Automation Platform




📍 Location: Singapore (Hybrid – client onsite sessions required)

⏳ Duration: 12-month contract




We are looking for a highly experienced Automation Consultant / Ansible Architect to lead the automation track of a large-scale enterprise infrastructure patch management program for a leading financial institution in Singapore.




This is a senior technical leadership role involving automation architecture, framework development, engineering leadership, and enterprise platform integration.




Key Responsibilities:

Own the architecture and design of enterprise Ansible automation frameworks.
Lead the development of automated infrastructure patch management workflows.
Design Ansible Automation Platform (AAP) workflow templates, execution environments, automation mesh and RBAC.
Integrate Ansible with ServiceNow Change Management and CMDB.
Implement approval gates, patch sequencing, rollback and audit evidence workflows.
Lead automation engineers and establish coding, testing and deployment standards.
Drive automation for VMware ESXi, vCenter and network infrastructure patching.
Develop automated testing frameworks using Python and Robot Framework.
Prepare HLD/LLD documentation and support architecture reviews.
Mentor client teams and lead knowledge transfer.




Required Skills & Experience:




10+ years of experience in infrastructure automation.
8+ years of hands-on Ansible experience in production environments.
Strong expertise in Ansible Automation Platform (AAP).
Experience designing enterprise automation frameworks and leading technical teams.
Hands-on experience integrating Ansible with ServiceNow Change Management and CMDB.
Strong Python, REST API, Git and CI/CD experience.
Experience with enterprise patch management, rolling upgrades, cluster-aware sequencing and rollback.
Financial services or other regulated industry experience is highly preferred.




Good to Have:




Red Hat certifications (RHCE, EX374, EX467).
VMware ESXi, vCenter, Nutanix or network infrastructure automation.
Cisco, F5, Palo Alto or Juniper firmware automation.
CyberArk, Tanium, NetBox or Nautobot experience.
