About GF

GlobalFoundries (GF) is a leading manufacturer of essential semiconductors, enabling AI at scale from the cloud to the physical world. Through deep partnerships with customers, GF delivers differentiated, power‑efficient and high‑performance solutions for high‑growth markets. With global manufacturing operations across the U.S., Europe and Asia, GF is a trusted and holistic technology partner for customers around the world. GF’s talented, global team remains focused every day on security, longevity and sustainability. For more information, visit www.gf.com.

What We Offer

We offer full-time career opportunities that provide meaningful learning experiences, cross-functional collaboration, and professional development. Employees benefit from mentorship, networking, and leadership opportunities that support skill development, career progression, and long-term success.

Summary Of Role

We are looking for a Manufacturing Quality Engineer to join our team as part of the MQA operation. The successful candidate will be responsible to oversees the operation of Quality systems relating to manufacturing and engineering processes, maintains compliance and procedures to achieve high-quality products. This position is based at our GlobalFoundries office at Singapore

Candidates without prior work experience are welcome to apply. Comprehensive training and development will be provided.

Essential Responsibilities Include


Ensure compliance and consistent execution according to documented quality systems (PCRB / MRB / ICAR / ISHARE / FMEA / Calibration)
Customer specific requirement (CuSR) execution and compliance enforcement
Timely tracking of operational activities to ensure no delay according to schedule. (Validation, facilitation, system closure/ lot disposition, action item tracking)
Timely tracking and publishing operational KPIs and customer reports.
Perform investigation into quality related incidents.
Participation in continuous improvement activities to improve system/workflow robustness, overall quality, speed, and cost of operations.
Analyze data to identify trends and opportunities for improvement and work with business functions to implement corrective actions.
Ready to get certified as Auditor and support the organization as Internal Auditor


Other Responsibilities


Perform all activities in a safe and responsible manner and support all Environmental, Health, Safety & Security requirements and programs.


Required Qualifications


Master/bachelor’s degree in engineering or science
Fast learner, strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to analyze data and identify trends and opportunities for improvement.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
Self-starter with the ability to work independently and as part of a team


Preferred Qualifications


Prior related internship or co-op experience
Prior related work experience in quality assurance, process improvement, SPC or related field, high-tech and/or semiconductor experience
Demonstrated prior leadership experience in the workplace, school projects, competitions, etc.
Project management skills, i.e., the ability to innovate and execute on solutions that matter; the ability to navigate ambiguity.
Strong interest in leveraging AI tools and emerging technologies to enhance productivity, streamline workflows, and support effective decision-making.
Hands on experience on problem solving/troubleshooting skills
Working knowledge of ISO9001:2015/IATF16949
Auditor experience or certified ISO/IATF/VDA Auditor
