Team Lead at Ventula Consulting

AI SaaS scale-up now requires a talented person to own delivery of their product roadmap, from scoping through to release and post-launch analysis.




This role will work directly with engineers, product, product marketing and Customer Success to ensure releases are executed and measured post launch.




The role:

Own Roadmap plans, timelines, dependencies and progress across all active initiatives. Collaborate with engineers to clear blockers/challenge estimates and report progress/risks to product leadership.
Run weekly stand ups and retros in a fast-paced SaaS environment to ensure efficient releases.
Turn objectives and customer requirements into clear briefs with scope, acceptance criteria and measurable success criteria (Via the use of AI tools).
Own our Jira workflow, automation, reporting and delivery gates, and fix the process problems that cause delays and manual effort.
Manage releases, maintain schedule, assign release tiers and confirm testing, documentation, comms and enablement are ready before launch.




Key Requirements:

Strong background in product delivery at a B2B SaaS company.
Ecommerce, SaaS/Marketing experience is preferable.
Enough technical fluency (APIs, front-end, architecture) to talk with engineers and challenge them constructively.
Hands-on daily use of AI tools such as Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor to draft, prototype or automate.
Experience with Mixpanel, Amplitude or similar, and confidence turning data into a clear recommendation
Strong Jira skills, including workflows and automation
A proactive, persistent approach to ensure delivery.




This is a permanent opportunity which provides a base salary of up to £75,000 + package.




In terms of working structure, this is role is remote (UK).
