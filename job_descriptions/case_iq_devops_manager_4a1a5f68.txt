Case IQ is a leading B2B SaaS company in the governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) space, helping organizations build safer, more accountable workplaces. We offer a comprehensive suite of solutions that enable organizations to move from reactive approaches to proactive risk management. Our software helps teams across compliance, HR, legal, and investigations report, manage, investigate, and prevent workplace risk - including ethics violations, fraud, harassment, discrimination, security incidents, and third-party issues - so they can better protect their people, culture, and business.




Backed by over 25 years of domain expertise and trusted by Fortune 500 companies, Case IQ has supported the management of millions of cases and helped mitigate billions in financial and reputational risk.




Role Summary

We are seeking a Manager of DevOps Engineering to lead our DevOps function as a combination of Platform Team and Enabling Team, aligned to Team Topologies principles, across four product lines.




This role is responsible for defining and executing a modern DevOps strategy (≈60%), while remaining hands-on (≈40%) to actively guide engineers and development teams in adopting modern practices. The DevOps organization exists to enable fast flow, reduce cognitive load for product teams, and provide a reliable, self-service platform for delivery and operations.




The Manager of DevOps Engineering will lead 3+ full-time DevOps Engineers and coordinate external DevOps service providers, partnering closely with Architecture, Development Managers, QA/Test Automation, and Product Management to ensure DevOps capabilities are scalable, discoverable, and adopted across teams.




AI-Forward Ways of Working

At Case IQ, AI is part of how we build. We look for engineers who can use AI-assisted development and agentic workflows with strong technical judgment: defining clear intent and constraints, validating outputs, protecting customer data, and keeping humans accountable for production decisions. You will help establish practical patterns for responsible AI use across discovery, design, implementation, testing, observability, and operations.




DevOps Mission (Team Topologies Lens)

As a Platform Team: Provide internal, self-service DevOps capabilities that product teams can use independently to build, deploy, and operate software safely and efficiently.
As an Enabling Team: Temporarily embed with product teams to help them adopt modern DevOps practices, remove bottlenecks, and upskill teams—then step back once capabilities are internalized.




This role ensures the organization does not rely on DevOps as a centralized delivery gate but instead uses DevOps to accelerate all teams.




What You'll Do

Platform Leadership & Strategy (≈60%)

Define and evolve a DevOps Platform strategy that serves four product lines with shared, reusable capabilities
Establish platform standards and golden paths for:
CI/CD pipelines and release orchestration
Infrastructure as Code (IaC)
Cloud architecture, scalability, and cost management
Observability, logging, metrics, and alerting
Secure-by-default environments and pipelines
Ensure the platform minimizes cognitive load for development teams while enabling autonomy
Partner with Architecture to align DevOps platform capabilities with long-term technical direction
Prioritize platform investments based on flow efficiency, reliability, and developer experience
Maintain a high standard of security awareness, ensuring data protection, compliance with company policies, and adherence to industry standards in all aspects of work.

Enabling Team Leadership (≈40% Hands-On)

Lead by example through direct technical involvement and coaching
Temporarily embed DevOps engineers with product teams to:
Improve deployment pipelines and release safety
Enable teams to own their services and environments
Reduce manual steps and DevOps dependencies
Teach and model modern DevOps practices rather than owning execution indefinitely
Create documentation, templates, and automation that allow teams to self-serve

Team & Vendor Management

Manage and develop 3+ full-time DevOps Engineers, emphasizing platform thinking and enablement behaviors
Direct and coordinate external DevOps service providers, ensuring work aligns with platform and enablement goals
Set clear expectations that DevOps success is measured by team independence, not ticket throughput
Foster a culture of continuous improvement, experimentation, and learning




Metrics You'll Own

Deployment Frequency (partnering with QA and Test Automation)
DORA Metrics
Deployment Frequency
Lead Time for Changes
Change Failure Rate
Mean Time to Recovery (MTTR)
Change Failure Rate
Runtime of Updates (deployment and infrastructure update duration)
Cycle Time to Perform a Release
Reduction in DevOps-related handoffs and dependencies for product teams




What Success Looks Like

Product teams deploy independently using shared DevOps platform capabilities
DevOps engineers spend less time firefighting and more time enabling flow
DORA and release metrics show sustained improvement across all product lines
Reduced operational risk and faster recovery from incidents
DevOps is viewed as a force multiplier, not a bottleneck




Who You Are

6+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or Platform Engineering
3+ years of experience managing or leading engineers
Strong hands-on experience with modern cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
Expertise in:
CI/CD tooling (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, etc.)
Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, CloudFormation, Pulumi, etc.)
Containers and orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes, ECS, etc.)
Experience supporting multiple product teams or product lines
Demonstrated success improving delivery speed and reliability through enablement, not centralization

Nice to Haves (Assets)

Practical experience applying Team Topologies concepts
Experience building or evolving internal developer platforms
Background in SaaS or B2B software environments
Experience managing external vendors or managed service providers
Strong communication and influence skills across technical and product leadership
