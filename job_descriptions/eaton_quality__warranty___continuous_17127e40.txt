Eaton is an intelligent power management company dedicated to protecting the environment and improving the quality of life for people everywhere. We make products for the data center, utility, industrial, commercial, machine building, residential, aerospace and mobility markets.

What You’ll Do


Manage the Eaton Quality Assurance System. Ensure QA documentation is current and up to date. Run internal and external audit processes ensuring compliance.
Create a master schedule to expedite production activities for the facility to meet established sales forecasts and customer demand. Scope includes Clutch, Trimline, Workshop, Reman and Kitting accessories.
Manager Eaton Quality System –
Assist with the development & revision of Standard Operating Procedures
Run internal & external audit processes
Ensure all QA documentation is up to date & accurate
Manage customer quality queries
Manage (including refunds) for incorrect/faulty products received from our suppliers
Support the production planning and coordination the workflow for OEM and Aftermarket (AM) establishing priorities to meet sales forecasts and specific customer orders.
Liaise with manufacturing team, Supply Chain Management and sales to ensure production schedules are adhered to.
Track new customer orders and back orders to determine where schedules need to be revised. Advise changes to manufacturing team.
Monitor and report progress of weekly and monthly schedule to Engineering & Operations Manager. Recommend process improvements to meet department targets.
Schedule VORs and Warranty orders as high priority ensuring timely communication with sales, SCM and manufacturing team.
Assist with jigs & fixture development
Recommend and implement continuous improvement initiatives to promote efficiency, productivity and customer service in conjunction with Engineering & Operations Manager.
Escalate concerns/discrepancies to Engineering & Operations Manager.
Other duties as required by Engineering & Operations Manager


Qualifications


3-5 years experience in production scheduling
Customer Service focus -commitment to provide a timely, quality and accountable service
Overall understanding of SCM and SiOP processes.
Strategic minded and open to change
Able to multi task, work under pressure and within short time frames
Attention to detail
Basic technical knowledge of Transmission & Clutch part numbers is desirable.
Proficiency with MS Excel and Oracle ERP software
