About Revolut

People deserve more from their money. More visibility, more control, and more freedom. Since 2015, Revolut has been on a mission to deliver just that. Our powerhouse of products — including spending, saving, investing, exchanging, travelling, and more — help our 80+ million customers get more from their money every day.

As we continue our lightning-fast growth,‌ 2 things are essential to our success: our people and our culture. In recognition of our outstanding employee experience, we've been certified as a Great Place to Work™. So far, we have 15,000+ people working around the world, from our offices and remotely, to help us achieve our mission. And we're looking for more brilliant people. People who love building great products, redefining success, and turning the complexity of a chaotic world into the simplicity of a beautiful solution.

About The Role

Our Technology team builds the systems and experiences that keep Revolut moving forward. From the infrastructure behind our innovative app to the features used by millions of people around the world, they bring sharp thinking, speed, and a focus on meaningful impact to everything they do.

We're looking for a Technology Manager to focus on technology operations, risk management and resilience, and regulatory readiness.

You'll work closely with Engineering, Product, Operations, and Compliance teams, as well as local regulators, to ensure our platforms operate in line with regulatory requirements while remaining aligned with group standards.

Up to shape what's next in finance? Let's get in touch.

What You'll Be Doing


Assessing initiatives that require relevant IT changes
Identifying dependencies, potential impact, and risks, ensuring that they have been satisfactorily analysed, tested, and managed
Collaborating on larger change and transformation programmes
Representing the local Technology team to take ownership of appropriate tasks and provide input as required
Building and optimising scalable processes to continue Revolut's hyper-growth
Leading and executing projects through global stakeholder management
Overseeing frameworks to ensure compliance with local regulations (e.g., MAS requirements for TRM)
Ensuring adherence to PDPA Act 2012 for customer data


What You'll Need


7+ years experience in financial services or fintech (or serving it from a leading technology or IT services provider)
Expertise in project management
Solid risk-awareness and an understanding of risk management frameworks, such as the three lines of defence in financial services
A good understanding of technology within financial services
Familiarity with applicable financial services regulations in Singapore, such as PDPA, MAS TRM, and Digital and Operational Resilience guidelines
Experience partnering with an executive team, as well as the ability to work closely with global team members to deliver results
Experience in retail banking, and familiarity with the features offered by Revolut
Fluency in English with exceptional communication and presentation skills
Great problem-solving and decision-making skills, with the ability to analyse complex information to identify the key issue/action and drive resolutions


Nice to have


Technology and change delivery experience at a leading/global consultancy firm
Experience building and/or scaling up the technology function of a financial services institution or fintech
Relevant industry-recognised certifications or qualifications, such as Certified Change Management Professional (CCMP), ITIL, ITSM, or PMP
Proficiency in SQL
