About The Role

McKesson is seeking a Sr. SRE Engineer to lead the strategy, roadmap, and delivery of enterprise platform capabilities that enable reliable, scalable, and resilient healthcare technology solutions. This role combines product management leadership with deep understanding of cloud infrastructure, site reliability engineering, production operations, and platform modernization.

You will partner with engineering, architecture, cloud, operations, and business stakeholders to deliver platforms that improve reliability, accelerate delivery, enhance observability, and support McKesson's long-term technology strategy. This role requires balancing customer value, business outcomes, financial investment, operational excellence, and technical innovation.

What You'll Do


Define and execute platform product strategy, vision, roadmaps, and investment priorities aligned to business and technology objectives.
Lead Product Centric Delivery across Platform Engineering, Reliability Engineering, Cloud Operations, and Infrastructure teams.
Establish product outcomes and KPIs using SLIs, SLOs, SLAs, reliability metrics, adoption metrics, and business value measurements.
Drive integrated planning, roadmap development, prioritization, and stakeholder alignment across multiple platform initiatives.
Partner with engineering teams to improve platform scalability, resiliency, availability, and operational maturity.
Lead initiatives related to Kubernetes platforms, cloud infrastructure, observability, automation, and operational excellence.
Support incident management, operational readiness reviews, disaster recovery planning, and continuous improvement programs.
Manage platform investments, business cases, financial performance, and value realization to ensure solutions meet enterprise and customer needs.



Basic Requirements


7+ years of experience in Product Management, Platform Product Management, Site Reliability Engineering, Cloud Platform Engineering, DevOps, or related technology leadership roles.
Experience developing product strategies, roadmaps, business cases, and investment plans for technology platforms.
Experience partnering with software engineering, architecture, cloud, infrastructure, and operations teams.
Dynatrace set up dashboards
Experience delivering products using Agile and iterative delivery methodologies.
Experience with cloud platforms including Azure
Experience defining and measuring KPIs, service reliability metrics, and business outcomes.
Bachelor's degree or equivalent combination of education and experience.



Preferred Skills/Experience


Knowledge of Reliability Engineering, Production Operations, and Operational Excellence principles.
Experience with SLIs, SLOs, SLAs, incident management, root cause analysis, and operational readiness practices.
Experience with Kubernetes, Docker, and containerized platform architectures.
Experience with Infrastructure as Code including Terraform, Bicep, or CloudFormation.
Experience implementing and supporting CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, or Jenkins.
Experience with observability platforms including Dynatrace, Datadog, Splunk, AppDynamics, Prometheus, and Grafana.
Experience developing automation solutions using Python, PowerShell, Shell scripting, or Go.
Experience with financial management, value management, capacity planning, multi-region architectures, disaster recovery, and business continuity planning.



Travel / Work Arrangement / Physical Requirements


Travel up to 10% may be required.
Position may be remote, hybrid, or office-based based on business needs.
Ability to work extended hours during critical incidents or major platform releases when required.
Ability to use standard office technology and participate in virtual collaboration environments.
