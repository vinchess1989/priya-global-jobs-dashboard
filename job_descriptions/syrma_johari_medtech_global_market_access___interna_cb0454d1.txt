Title - Global Market Access & International Registration Specialist

Experience - 4 to 6yrs

Location - Pune (5 days WFO Mandate)




Job Description




Own first-time registrations, renewals and lifecycle maintenance for multiple medical software product lines across prioritized international markets.




Roles & Responsibility

Medical device or SaMD market-access experience across multiple jurisdictions.
Country-specific classification, submission pathway, local representative, labeling/IFU and renewal planning capability.
Ability to convert regulations into product-country checklists and actionable evidence requests.
Understanding of software, AI, privacy, cybersecurity and interoperability dependencies.
Build product-country registration plans based on commercial priority and product readiness.
Assess classification, applicable regulations, submission route and technical-document localization.
Coordinate local agents/authorized representatives and language requirements.
Prepare submissions and manage authority questions, renewals and post-market obligations.
Track timelines, known fees, validity and maintenance commitments.
Escalate AI-specific, software-specific, privacy or cybersecurity prerequisites.




Specific Skill

Bachelor’s in technology life sciences or Computer science with 4+ years of experience
Technical writing, English communication and teamwork.
Detail oriented, ability to read various standards and guidelines & define
EU MDR
National medical device registration requirements
FDA pathway awareness where relevant
Privacy/cybersecurity/interoperability requirements by market




General Skill

Strong attention to detail and documentation accuracy.
Good understanding of engineering documentation practices.
Ability to work with cross-functional engineering teams.
Effective communication and documentation skills.
Critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
Must be a Team Player.
