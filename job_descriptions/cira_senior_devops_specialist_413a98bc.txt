Join the team that is building a trusted internet for Canadians! CIRA may be best known for managing the .CA domain but our impact reaches far beyond that. We’re at the forefront of advancing cybersecurity technologies and leading projects that improve the digital experience for users across Canada and the world. Our broad scope of activities is driven by one central goal: to strengthen and secure Canada’s digital landscape.  

By working with the CIRA registry team, you’ll play a part in advancing the CIRA Registry Platform, which supports a wide range of domains globally. Help us drive innovation and maintain the high standards of stability and security that our platform is known for. Join us in advancing digital identity and technology in Canada and beyond. 

Who You Are

As a Senior DevOps Specialist, you will collaborate with development, security and product teams to build and maintain critical internet infrastructure. You are passionate about designing secure, reliable and scalable systems through automation, cloud-native technologies and operational best practices.

You are a hands-on engineer who takes ownership of complex technical challenges, drives initiatives independently and shares knowledge to strengthen the team's engineering capabilities.

What You'll Do


Design, implement and maintain secure, scalable Amazon Web Services (AWS) infrastructure across multiple environments using Terraform and infrastructure as code best practices.
Manage the lifecycle of Amazon Elastic Kubernetes Service (EKS) clusters and platform components, including upgrades, networking, ingress, storage, security, autoscaling and infrastructure releases.
Collaborate with development teams to support and troubleshoot Kubernetes deployments and improve deployment workflows using Helm and GitOps tools such as Argo CD.
Design and improve observability solutions, including metrics, logging and alerting, to optimize platform reliability, performance, resource utilization and cost efficiency.
Build reusable platform tooling, standardize deployment workflows and automate infrastructure operations to improve developer experience, reliability and efficiency.
Independently own and deliver complex infrastructure and platform engineering initiatives, from technical design through implementation and production deployment.
Collaborate with development and security teams to implement automated security controls, identity and access management (IAM), secrets management and infrastructure security best practices.
Support on-premises infrastructure and hybrid cloud environments, including disaster recovery and business continuity.
Share knowledge through code reviews, mentoring and technical documentation.
Participate in a 24/7 on-call rotation, including production incident response, troubleshooting and root-cause analysis.


What You'll Bring


5+ years of experience in DevOps, cloud engineering, site reliability engineering or platform engineering.
Strong hands-on experience with AWS services, particularly EKS, Amazon Elastic Compute Cloud (EC2), IAM, Amazon Virtual Private Cloud (VPC), Amazon Simple Storage Service (S3), load balancing and cloud networking.
Extensive experience operating and troubleshooting production Kubernetes environments.
Strong proficiency with Terraform, infrastructure as code practices and Helm.
Demonstrated expertise in at least one of the following:
GitOps and deployment automation using tools such as Argo CD or Flux.
Observability and monitoring using technologies such as Prometheus, Grafana, Loki or equivalent solutions.
Strong Linux administration, networking fundamentals (Transmission Control Protocol/Internet Protocol (TCP/IP), Domain Name System (DNS), Transport Layer Security (TLS) and troubleshooting skills.
Proficiency in scripting and automation using Bash and/or Python.
Experience with continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) practices, cloud security and infrastructure automation.
Experience operating, troubleshooting and improving the reliability of business-critical production infrastructure.
Ability to independently own technical initiatives, evaluate architectural trade-offs and collaborate effectively across teams.
