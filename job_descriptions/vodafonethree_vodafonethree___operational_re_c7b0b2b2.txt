What You'll Do

At VodafoneThree, we're on a mission to deliver the UK's best customer experience, and you'll play a pivotal role in making that happen. As an Operational Release Lead, you'll sit at the centre of Channel Operations, ensuring new customer and colleague-facing capabilities are launched smoothly, safely, and successfully across our Contact Centre, Retail, and Indirect channels. You'll bring together teams from across the business, turning complex change into seamless operational delivery that keeps colleagues informed, customers supported, and releases on track.


You'll lead the operational coordination of customer platform releases across Consumer channels, ensuring business readiness every step of the way
You'll assess the operational impact of proposed changes and identify risks, dependencies, and customer experience considerations before release
You'll represent Channel Operations within release governance forums, providing operational input and supporting Go/No-Go decisions
You'll work closely with Product, Technology, Commercial, Change, and Operations teams to ensure alignment throughout the release lifecycle
You'll oversee readiness activities across Contact Centres, Retail, and Indirect channels, making sure teams are prepared to support new capabilities from day one
You'll coordinate testing, readiness checkpoints, and sign-off activities to support stable and successful implementations
You'll own operational communications relating to release plans, timelines, outages, and key milestones, ensuring stakeholders receive timely and actionable updates
You'll proactively identify and mitigate operational risks while driving continuous improvement in release governance, controls, and operational readiness processes
You'll act as the key bridge between delivery teams and operational functions, helping to turn technical change into positive customer and colleague experiences



Who You Are


Experience in operational readiness, release management, business implementation, change delivery, or a similar operational role
A strong understanding of Contact Centre, Retail, and/or Indirect channel operations and the impact change can have on customer and colleague experiences
The ability to assess complex changes, identify risks and dependencies, and drive effective mitigation plans
Strong stakeholder management and influencing skills, with the confidence to collaborate across Operations, Technology, Product, Commercial, and Change teams
Experience working within governance frameworks and contributing to release sign-off or Go/No-Go decision-making
Excellent communication skills, with the ability to translate technical change into clear operational actions and business updates
Strong planning, organisation, and problem-solving abilities, with experience managing multiple priorities and deadlines
An analytical mindset combined with sound judgement and a focus on delivering practical outcomes
Experience supporting testing, readiness activities, and deployment processes to ensure successful release delivery
A collaborative, inclusive approach and a passion for driving operational excellence and outstanding customer experiences
