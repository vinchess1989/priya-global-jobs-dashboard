Site Reliability Engineer




Location: Remote, South Africa




Hours: US working hours (Central Time) - 4pm-12am SAST




Working pattern: Week 1: Monday-Friday, Week 2: Saturday-Wednesday (Working Saturday and Sunday every other week)




Company: Just After Midnight (JAM) – Part of Trustmarque Ultima Group




Just After Midnight (JAM) is a global next-gen managed service provider with teams across the UK, Europe, the US, Australia, and APAC. We provide 24/7 application and cloud support, SRE, managed AWS and multi-cloud services, DevOps, and observability to mission-critical applications for scale-ups, SaaS product companies, and enterprise brands. We're an AWS Advanced Consulting Partner and hold security accreditations including ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials Plus, and PCI DSS. Our mantra: Always On, So You Can Switch Off.




The Site Reliability Engineer is part of a global team delivering 24/7 best-in-class reliability and support services to our clients. Working as part of a geographically dispersed team, your time will be focused on ensuring the availability, performance and resilience of our clients’ platforms. The role requires you to develop and maintain knowledge of technologies and languages commonly used by the client platforms, and to apply engineering practices that reduce manual operational effort. The platforms we support are primarily web based and utilise cloud technologies provided by public cloud providers such as AWS, Azure and GCP, using software languages such as .NET, PHP, NodeJS etc.




The ideal candidate will be analytical and have excellent written and verbal communication skills, along with a calm and professional approach under pressure and a willingness and ability to develop themselves within the IT industry.




Key Responsibilities:

● Investigate, diagnose and resolve incidents and automated alerts affecting our clients’ platforms, leading incident response and root cause analysis.

● Create and maintain technical runbooks, automation scripts and the online knowledge base used as part of the support process.

● Play a key role in defining and improving client monitoring, alerting and service level objectives (SLOs).

● Participate in a rotating on-call schedule with other engineers to ensure support coverage during key hours dependent on your geographical location. As a 24/7 business, there is a requirement to provide support coverage at weekends and public holidays.

● Maintain a commitment to developing and maintaining your knowledge around relevant technologies through online learning and certifications. Such learning will be agreed as part of onboarding and ongoing performance reviews.

● Provide technical guidance and mentoring to less experienced colleagues, including Service Desk Engineers, during their onboarding and beyond.

● Understand and implement the company’s information security policies as applicable to day-to-day activities.




Essential experience:

● Experience working in a site reliability, DevOps or cloud operations role

● Excellent written and spoken communication skills. The role requires a high level of communication through both written and verbal channels.

● The ability to develop and maintain an expert level of knowledge across cloud technologies.

● Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities, including root cause analysis.

● Experience with incident and change management processes and tools such as ServiceNow.

● Excellent organisation and interpersonal skills with the ability to play an active role within a team setting.

● Working knowledge of Git and CI/CD pipelines.

● Solid experience with Windows / Unix / Linux environment administration.

● Experience working with either AWS or Azure, including Infrastructure as Code (e.g. Terraform, Bicep, CloudFormation).




Nice to have:

Experience with Python, Shell scripting or PowerShell
Experience with object-oriented languages such as Java, C# or TypeScript
Working knowledge of Bitbucket, GitLab, GitHub or similar
Experience with Docker and Kubernetes
Understanding of network security, infrastructure management and authentication methods
Experience with Cloudflare, Azure Front Door, CloudFront or Akamai
Experience with Web Application Firewall technologies such as Cloudflare WAF, AWS WAF or Azure WAF
Experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Datadog, Site24x7, Application Insights, CloudWatch or Grafana




Successful candidates will be measured on KPIs including:

Service availability against agreed SLAs and SLOs for client platforms
Mean time to detect and resolve incidents (MTTD / MTTR)
Reduction in repeat incidents and manual operational effort through automation
Feedback from internal stakeholders and clients
Periodic assessment of technical knowledge
Attainment of agreed certifications
