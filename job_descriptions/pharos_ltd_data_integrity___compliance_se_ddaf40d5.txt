PharOS is seeking for a Data Integrity & Compliance Senior Specialist within the Quality department as follows:




Role and Responsibilities

Data Governance Leadership

Establishes, maintains, and continuously improves the Site Data Governance Program.
Develops and maintains the Data Governance Master Plan and associated roadmap.
Establishes, chairs and coordinates periodic Site DI review meetings.
Defines and oversees Data Owner and Data Steward responsibilities across all business functions.
Drives site-wide governance initiatives to ensure sustainable compliance and data ownership.
Prepares and presents data governance metrics and program effectiveness reviews to senior management.
Data Integrity Program Management
Owns and maintains the Data Integrity Management System.
Develops and implements Data Integrity policies, procedures, standards, and governance frameworks.
Leads site-wide Data Integrity initiatives and remediation programs.
Monitors and verifies effectiveness of Data Integrity controls across all GxP areas.
Acts as the primary escalation point for Data Integrity concerns and investigations.

Data Integrity Risk Management

Develops and maintains a formal Data Integrity Risk Assessment (DIRA) program.
Facilitates cross-functional risk assessments covering manual, hybrid, and electronic systems.
Establishes risk-ranking methodologies and prioritization criteria.
Ensures timely implementation and effectiveness verification of remediation actions.
Periodically reviews and updates Data Integrity risk profiles.
Computerized Systems & Electronic Records Governance
Provides oversight of Data Integrity controls within computerized systems including but not limited to:
SAP/ TrackWise/ Empower/ Environmental Monitoring Systems/ Electronic Document Management Systems/ Electronic Archiving Systems
Ensures compliance with: FDA 21 CFR Part 11/ EU GMP Annex 11/ PIC/S Data Integrity Guidance/ MHRA Data Integrity Guidance/ GAMP 5 principles

Computer System Validation (CSV) Oversight

Supports validation lifecycle activities from system selection through retirement.
Reviews validation documentation to ensure appropriate Data Integrity controls are incorporated.
Participates in risk assessments related to computerized systems.
Supports periodic system reviews and compliance assessments.
Collaborates with IT and Validation teams to ensure ongoing regulatory compliance.

Audit Trail Governance

Establishes and maintains site-wide audit trail review procedures.
Ensures appropriate audit trail reviews are performed and documented.
Monitors effectiveness of audit trail processes.
Trends audit trail findings and identify recurring risks.
Escalates significant compliance concerns to management.

Data Lifecycle Management

Ensures data governance principles are applied throughout the entire data lifecycle.
Defines controls for: Data creation/ Data processing/ Review and approval/ Archiving/ Backup and recovery/ Record retention/ Data destruction
Ensures integrity of both paper-based and electronic records.

Quality Systems Integration

Ensures Data Integrity requirements are effectively integrated within: Deviations/ Investigations/ CAPA systems/ Change Management/ Internal Audit Programs/ Qualification and Validation Systems/ Supplier Quality Management Processes/ Management Review Activities
Participates in quality risk management activities and quality system reviews.

Training, Awareness & Culture

Develops and maintains the site's Data Integrity training strategy.
Delivers Data Integrity awareness programs across all departments.
Evaluates training effectiveness and compliance trends.
Fosters a culture of accountability, transparency, and data ownership.
Supports leadership teams in embedding Data Integrity principles into daily operations.

Inspection Readiness & Regulatory Support

Serves as the site's Data Integrity SME during regulatory inspections and customer audits.
Coordinates responses to Data Integrity observations and commitments.
Supports preparation of inspection readiness programs.
Assists in developing and implementing remediation strategies arising from audits and inspections.

Performance Monitoring & Continuous Improvement

Develops KPI and KQI metrics for Data Integrity and Data Governance.
Tracks program effectiveness through trend analysis and management reporting.
Identifies opportunities for process improvement and compliance enhancement.
Benchmarks practices against industry standards and regulatory expectations.




Experience & Qualification

Bachelor’s degree in chemistry, Pharmacy, Engineering, Computer Science, Biotechnology, Life Sciences, or a related scientific discipline. Master's Degree in a scientific or technical field is considered an asset.
Minimum two (2) years of experience in Data Integrity, Data Governance, Quality Assurance, Computerized Systems Validation, Compliance, or related disciplines within a regulated pharmaceutical or life sciences environment.
Demonstrated experience supporting regulatory inspections and quality system investigations.
Experience working with cross-functional teams in Manufacturing, Quality Control, Validation, and Information Technology environments.
Strong knowledge of: cGMP and cGxP regulations/ FDA 21 CFR Part 11/ EU GMP Annex 11/ EU GMP Chapter 4/ MHRA Data Integrity Guidance/ PIC/S Data Integrity Guidance/ GAMP 5/ ICH Q9 and ICH Q10 principles
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
Ability to interpret and apply complex regulatory requirements.
Strong organizational and project management capabilities.
Ability to communicate technical concepts effectively to diverse audiences.
High level of integrity, professionalism, and quality mindset.
Commitment to continuous improvement and operational excellence.
