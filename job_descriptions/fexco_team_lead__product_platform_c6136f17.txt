We are seeking an experienced Team Lead to lead the product platform team behind Fexco's payUnite payment platforms. This is a hands-on technical and delivery leadership role: you will make engineering decisions independently, set the team's priorities, and keep the workload and on-call arrangements sustainable.




You will keep a PCI DSS scoped, Azure-hosted payments platform reliable and secure, and help build the team that runs it.




About the Role

As Team Lead, you will lead the delivery and operation of the payUnite product platform on Azure: private Kubernetes clusters across several regions and environments, Git-driven deployment, a layered infrastructure-as-code estate, CI/CD pipelines and the monitoring and alerting that keep cloud infrastructure healthy. You will build on the shared tooling provided by the core platform team, and the product platform team owns everything that sits between that tooling and the payment applications. Networking and Security are owned by specialist teams, so you will work with them rather than run them. The platform processes payment card data, so security, change control and compliance are part of everyday engineering.




You review designs and unblock engineers without escalating. You turn a stream of requests from product owners and project managers into a sequenced, realistic plan. You assign work and plan capacity, and you stay close enough to the platform to step in on a complex incident. You will be hands-on in the early life of the team, with the balance moving towards leadership as the team grows.




You will collaborate with application teams, the core platform team, security, networking, architecture and DBA colleagues, and report on delivery, reliability and risk to senior stakeholders. You line manage the engineers in the team, alongside providing technical and delivery leadership. Your hierarchical manager supports your people leadership and career development, and your functional manager sets the day-to-day priorities with you.




This role is based in Ireland and offers a hybrid working arrangement, with occasional travel to The Fexco Innovation Centre in Kerry as required.




What You'll Be Doing




Technical Leadership and Decision-Making

Make and own platform decisions across Kubernetes, deployment, infrastructure as code, observability and data services, weighing reliability, security, cost and delivery time
Agree requirements with stakeholders outside the team, such as Service Delivery managers, application teams, network and security, and hold the line on what the platform needs from them
Review infrastructure-as-code, deployment and pipeline changes and set engineering standards for the team
Lead technical design discussions and architecture reviews for new capabilities, migrations and infrastructure changes
Act as the senior technical escalation point for complex incidents and lead investigation, containment and remediation
Decide when to invest in simplification, resilience and removal of technical debt alongside new project work




Kubernetes, GitOps and Infrastructure as Code

Own the delivery and operation of private, multi-zone Kubernetes clusters across regions and environments, including upgrades, capacity and workload security settings
Run a GitOps delivery model: platform and application configuration declared in Git, synced automatically, with image and chart changes promoted through merge requests
Maintain a layered infrastructure-as-code estate (thin deployable projects, composed modules, per-environment configuration) with pipeline-driven plan and apply
Keep workload identity, secrets and in-cluster policy (pod security, network policy, admission control) defined as code
Maintain the metrics, alerting and log routing for the platform infrastructure, within the data-handling rules for payment systems
Onboard new tenants and applications onto the shared platform in a repeatable way




Prioritisation and Delivery

Take in work from product owners, project managers and other teams, clarify scope, and turn it into a sequenced plan with clear owners
Weigh competing requests against reliability, compliance and delivery commitments, and say no or not yet with a reason
Keep delivery visible: maintain the team's plan and board, track progress, flag risk early, and report to stakeholders
Make sure each piece of work has a clear definition of done, including documentation and runbooks




Workload Allocation and Capacity Planning

Assign work according to skills, development goals and availability, and avoid single points of knowledge
Plan team capacity across delivery, business-as-usual support, on-call load, leave and training
Forecast demand against capacity and make the case for headcount, contractors or reduced scope when the two diverge
Track effort and time spent so planning improves over time




On-Call and Operational Ownership

Take part in on-call for technical issues and act as an escalation point, with the balance changing as the team grows
Organise the on-call rota with the team: keep it fair, cover swaps and leave, and keep escalation paths clear
Own alert quality: page only for what is worth waking someone up for, and give every alert a tier, a route and a runbook
Run post-incident reviews, publish postmortems and drive the follow-up actions to completion
Lead business-as-usual support and ongoing maintenance for the platform and adjacent PCI applications, and keep the boundary between planned work and support clear




Security, Compliance and Change Control

Make sure platform changes meet PCI DSS requirements and the change approval process for production
Work with the security team on vulnerability remediation, scanning, penetration test findings and policy enforcement in CI/CD
Record any manual production change as infrastructure-as-code debt when it happens and drive it to closure




People and Mentorship

Line manage the engineers in the team, including one-to-ones, performance reviews and development planning
Coach and mentor engineers and grow their technical depth and independence
Provide input into hiring, onboarding and the development of the team
Build a team culture of ownership, clear writing and blameless review




Documentation and Continuous Improvement

Own runbooks, operating procedures and architecture documentation, and keep them current
Set and track delivery and reliability measures for the team (for example SLOs, incident volume, alert noise) and report on them
Drive automation and simplification to reduce toil




What We're Looking For




Essential

Significant experience at senior level in cloud platform, DevOps or site reliability engineering, including experience leading engineers with line management responsibility
Strong hands-on production experience with Kubernetes: operating clusters and upgrades, configuring workloads and ingress, and applying workload security controls (pod security, network policy, admission policy)
Experience running GitOps delivery with tools such as ArgoCD or Flux, plus Helm or Kustomize, including how to structure configuration across multiple environments
Strong Infrastructure as Code skills, ideally Terraform, including module design, state management and pipeline-driven change
Solid working knowledge of at least one major public cloud (Azure, AWS or GCP), enough to design infrastructure on it and to work effectively with the DBA, application, network and security teams. You do not need Azure experience, but you need the willingness to learn it and to coach others as you do
Experience designing and operating CI/CD pipelines
Experience with observability and alerting, and with running a production on-call rotation, including alert tiering and incident management
Proven ability to prioritise competing demands from multiple stakeholders and to plan team capacity and allocate work
Experience working in a regulated or security-sensitive environment (PCI DSS or comparable), including formal change control
Sound independent judgement on architecture and trade-offs, with the confidence to make and explain decisions
Scripting and automation capability (for example Python, Bash or PowerShell)
Excellent communication skills with experience engaging technical and non-technical stakeholders, including senior management




Desirable

Experience with Microsoft Azure
Experience with workload identity and secrets management for Kubernetes workloads
Experience running managed database services alongside a platform
Relevant cloud or Kubernetes certifications
Experience in payments, financial services or other regulated industries
Experience with PCI DSS scoped environments and audit evidence
Experience with incident tooling such as Jira Service Management and structured postmortem practice




Why Join Us?

Lead the product platform team for a live, PCI DSS scoped payments platform on Azure
Shape how a newly formed team plans, supports and improves its platforms
Work on a modern stack: private Kubernetes, GitOps and infrastructure as code
Grow a team and mentor engineers, with the autonomy to decide how the work gets done




If you take ownership of outcomes, make sound technical decisions and help the people around you grow, we'd love to hear from you.
