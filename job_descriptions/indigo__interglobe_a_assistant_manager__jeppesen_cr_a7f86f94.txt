Brief About The Role / Team / Candidate Requirement

The Assistant Manager - Crew Systems Implementation will be responsible for supporting the end-to-end implementation, testing, deployment, and stabilization of Jeppesen Crew Tracking and Training systems. The role acts as a key liaison between Crew Resources, Digital & IT teams, Project Management, external vendors, and business stakeholders to ensure successful delivery of system capabilities aligned with operational requirements.

The incumbent will drive requirement validation, process mapping, test planning and execution, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), documentation, training, and stakeholder coordination throughout the project lifecycle. This role requires a strong blend of business understanding, system implementation expertise, testing experience, and project coordination skills.

Preference will be given to candidates with exposure to airline crew management, workforce planning, rostering, training, operational systems, or Jeppesen crew management solutions.

Qualification & Experience


Bachelor's Degree in any discipline.
MBA, PMP, PRINCE2, Agile, or relevant Project Management certification will be an advantage.
Minimum 5 years of experience in enterprise application or system implementation projects.
Hands-on experience in:
Business requirement gathering and validation
Test scenario and test case development
User Acceptance Testing (UAT)
System Integration Testing (SIT)
Business process documentation
User manual and training material preparation
Stakeholder management and project coordination
Experience working in Agile, Waterfall, or Hybrid delivery models.
Exposure to workforce management, crew management, rostering, training management, or operational systems preferred.
Aviation industry experience will be a strong advantage.
Experience with Jeppesen Crew Tracking, Crew Training, Crew Management, or similar airline operational platforms is highly desirable.

Job Responsibility

Project Implementation & Business Support


Support implementation and deployment of Jeppesen Crew Tracking and Training modules.
Collaborate with business stakeholders to understand operational requirements and translate them into system requirements.
Coordinate with Digital, IT, Project Management teams, vendors, and business users throughout the implementation lifecycle.
Ensure business requirements are accurately reflected in system configuration and functionality.


Testing & Validation


Develop comprehensive test strategies, test plans, test scenarios, and test scripts.
Design and execute SIT, BVT, and UAT activities.
Create operational test scenarios covering crew planning, training, qualifications, compliance, and tracking processes.
Track defects, coordinate resolution, and validate fixes with stakeholders.
Maintain traceability between business requirements and test outcomes.


User Acceptance Testing (UAT) Management


Prepare UAT plans, schedules, trackers, and execution reports.
Coordinate testing efforts across multiple business functions.
Monitor testing progress and ensure timely closure of observations and defects.
Ensure successful UAT sign-off within agreed timelines and quality standards.


Documentation & Training


Develop user manuals, SOPs, quick reference guides, and operating procedures.
Prepare training materials and facilitate end-user training sessions.
Document process changes, enhancements, and implementation learnings.
Maintain project documentation and knowledge repositories.


Stakeholder Management & Governance


Collaborate with Crew Tracking, Crew Training, Roster Planning, Crew Logistics, and Crew Pre-Ops teams.
Partner with Digital and IT teams for solution delivery and issue resolution.
Coordinate with Operations Excellence and Project teams to manage risks, dependencies, and milestones.
Facilitate cross-functional discussions and drive timely closure of action items.


Reporting & Continuous Improvement


Prepare project status reports, dashboards, and governance updates.
Track implementation risks, defects, and mitigation plans.
Monitor project milestones and implementation readiness.
Support post-implementation stabilization and continuous improvement initiatives.
