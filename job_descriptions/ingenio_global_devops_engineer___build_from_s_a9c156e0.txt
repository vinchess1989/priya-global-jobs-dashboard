Ever wished you could build the thing instead of just keeping it alive?




Good news, this DevOps role is exactly that!

We’re working with a really interesting Irish tech company that’s bringing together AI, scientific computing and drug discovery. They’re hiring a DevOps Engineer to build the infrastructure behind their compute-intensive R&D, and you’ll get to shape it from the very beginning.




A taste of what you’ll get stuck into

Building CI/CD pipelines from scratch
Setting up containerised environments with Docker and Podman
Creating monitoring, logging and alerting systems
Automating infrastructure and engineering workflows
Troubleshooting and optimising Linux-based infrastructure
Chatting architecture and scalability with the CTO and R&D team




What would make you a great fit

3+ years’ experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering or SRE
Strong hands-on experience with Docker, Docker Compose or Podman
Proven experience building CI/CD pipelines with GitHub Actions, GitLab CI/CD or Jenkins
Strong Linux fundamentals, including shell scripting, networking, systemd and storage
Experience with monitoring and observability, such as Prometheus and Grafana
Infrastructure-as-Code experience, ideally Ansible
Python scripting and automation skills
A self-directed mindset, comfortable building, troubleshooting and owning things end-to-end




So, why this one?




Because it’s not about ticket queues or looking after somebody else’s setup. You’ll build systems from the ground up and see your work help drive genuine progress in drug research and discovery.




If you’re a DevOps Engineer who loves getting under the hood and building the good stuff, we’d love to hear from you.
