Make your technical judgement count

When you review a release, you want to understand what is being deployed, how it was tested and what it could affect.

Our healthcare customer is looking for that depth of thinking in a Technical Release and Test Manager who can bring technical assurance, testing and people leadership together.

This permanent management role offers the opportunity to shape release and testing practices across an interconnected applications environment, working closely with the leader of IT Application Services.

An organisation with a clear purpose

Our client supports Australians through residential the provision of exceptional healthcare outcomes.

Behind those services is an award-winning technology environment supporting employees, business operations and the information people rely on. Your work will help ensure that changes are delivered reliably and with a clear understanding of their impact.

A role with ownership and variety

You will take an enterprise view of releases across internal applications, integrations and third-party platforms.

That means establishing a clear release calendar, coordinating dependencies and ensuring readiness decisions are supported by the right technical and test evidence.

You will remain close to the detail. You will inspect Azure DevOps pipelines, challenge branching and version decisions, and work with developers and suppliers to strengthen release packaging and environment integrity.

You will also lead a small testing team, work with an established testing partner and improve the approach to regression automation. There is scope to introduce better ways of working and explore appropriate AI-assisted testing.

What will make you successful

You bring a technical foundation and experience owning releases across multiple applications or suppliers.

You understand pipelines, source control, build artefacts and deployment versions. You can investigate an issue yourself, ask informed questions and make a clear recommendation.

Your testing experience includes risk-based coverage, release readiness and practical automation improvement. You also enjoy developing people and working constructively with business and technical stakeholders.

You may currently be a Technical Release Manager, Release and Test Manager, technically strong QA leader or engineering leader with substantial release and testing responsibility.

Why explore this role

This is an opportunity to combine technical involvement with broader ownership. You will help shape the function, develop its capability and see the effect of your work across the organisation.

If you enjoy taking responsibility, improving how things work and helping a small team deliver well, we would welcome a conversation.
