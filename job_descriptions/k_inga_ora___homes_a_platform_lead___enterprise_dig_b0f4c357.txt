Te whiwhinga mahi - Our opportunity

Kainga Ora are looking for an experienced Platform Lead to take ownership of the performance, delivery, and continuous improvement of our Enterprise Digital Tooling Platforms. This is a leadership role responsible for ensuring platform services are secure, reliable, scalable, and aligned with business and technology objectives.

As the Platform Lead, you will lead a team of Platform DevOps Engineers and Senior DevOps Engineers, driving a culture of operational excellence, continuous improvement, and high-quality delivery. You will balance delivery priorities, operational stability, and long-term platform sustainability, ensuring the platform continues to meet current and future organisational needs.

You will act as the primary point of accountability for platform outcomes, fostering strong collaboration and enabling consistent, effective service delivery across the technology landscape.

Ngā Pūmanawatanga Ōu - What You Will Bring

You’ll be a proven platform leader with a strong track record of building high-performing teams and delivering secure, reliable, and scalable technology platforms that support business outcomes.

You will have proven experience across platform operations and delivery, including incident and problem management, production support, service reliability, operational excellence, and continuous improvement.

Here’s What You Will Bring To The Role


Strong experience working with relevant platform technologies (ServiceNow and Atlassian products including Jira and Confluence), including experience in a Platform, Infrastructure, or DevOps leadership role
Deep technical expertise and hands-on experience supporting, operating, and delivering business-critical applications and digital services
Proven success leading Platform Engineering and DevOps teams, delivering reliable, scalable, secure, and business-aligned platform services
Demonstrated ability to lead, coach, and develop high-performing teams, fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration, continuous improvement, and servant leadership
Experience translating business strategy and organisational priorities into platform roadmaps, delivery plans, and measurable outcomes
Proven experience driving operational excellence through effective IT Service Management (ITSM), governance, compliance, service performance, and continuous improvement practices.


We are looking for a leader with strong technical credibility who can confidently engage in technical discussions, challenge assumptions, make informed technical decisions, and is prepared to be hands-on when needed.

Mō mātou - Our Organisation

At Kāinga Ora, ours is the important responsibility of transforming New Zealand’s housing choices, outcomes and the entire housing sector by creating homes and communities that allow New Zealanders to thrive.

What is exciting about working for Kāinga Ora is getting the chance to make a real and positive impact on New Zealanders’ lives. With skills, planning and purposeful action, our people are creating the homes and neighbourhoods that will build the future of Aotearoa.

We are passionate about transforming New Zealand for the better. That includes being a trusted partner for Māori and iwi, protecting and enabling their rights, interests and aspirations under the guidance of Te Tiriti o Waitangi.
