Job Description

Pragma is a South African asset management and reliability engineering company (established in 1990), developer of the On Key EAMS platform. We foster a team environment in which each individual is recognised, valued and developed. We encourage people with disabilities and from diverse backgrounds to apply.

You'll be one of the founding members of Pragma's new Innovation Hub, a small, newly created division. The initial team is intentionally lean: a Department Head, this DevOps Engineer role, and a Business Analyst, with a Developer to join later. That means real ownership and visibility from day one, but also fewer layers to lean on — you'll be expected to make calls independently and help shape how the team works as it grows.

This is a hybrid DevOps Engineer / Developer role within Pragma's newly formed Innovation Hub. For at least the first year, the successful candidate will spend a significant portion of their time hands-on with development, building and supporting internal tools alongside the Business Analyst and Department Head, while also owning the DevOps function: CI/CD, cloud infrastructure, and production support. Because the Innovation Hub is a small, lean team with limited in-house mentoring capacity, we need someone who can work independently from day one, exercising sound judgement with minimal oversight.

Minimum Requirements


A tertiary qualification in an IT-related degree or diploma is preferred
A minimum of 5 years experience in a DevOps role
Hands-on development experience, with the ability to write production-quality code, not just scripts
Basic server hardware knowledge
Comfortable in a Linux administration environment
Experience with deployment automation and CI/CD tooling
Experience setting up and configuring cloud environments
Strong problem-solving ability and a self-starter, continuous-learning mindset
Great communication skills, able to work closely with non-technical team members (Business Analyst, Department Head)


Preferred Technical Skills


Python
C#
PostgreSQL
Microsoft Azure (environment setup, configuration, and administration)


Duties & Responsibilities


Build and maintain internal tools, automations and applications alongside the Business Analyst and Department Head
Write and maintain backend code in Python and/or C#, working against Postgres databases
Translate business requirements into working software, with limited in-house technical mentoring
Design and implement CI/CD pipelines for the Hub's applications
Set up, configure, and maintain Azure cloud environments
Determine and apply hosting environment best practices
Determine and apply product performance monitoring and observability strategy
Own build and deployment automation end to end
Contribute to release planning and execution
Deliver production support, including troubleshooting and incident response
Ensure deployment environments are secure against cybersecurity threats
