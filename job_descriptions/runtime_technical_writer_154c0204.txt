Technical Writer




Salary: £80,000 - £90,000

Location: UK&I (Fully Remote)

Benefits: 35 days holiday, Private Healthcare, Private Dental & Vision, Remote Working, Flexi-Time, Extended Paternity / Maternity Leave, Home-Office Set-Up Allowance




Runtime is partnering with a VC-backed DevSecOps company to hire a Technical Writer into their documentation team.




Their platform sits at the heart of how enterprise engineering teams build and secure software, and their documentation is one of the main reasons customers choose them. This hire is about keeping that bar high as they scale.




What you’ll be doing




Owning documentation for specific product areas, working directly with engineers to understand features before they ship
Writing guides and best-practice content for a developer audience
Reviewing and publishing contributions from others, and managing the product changelog
Partnering with product, customer success and sales to spot where docs fall short and fix them
Prioritising your own content backlog: what’s now, next and later




What they’re looking for




5+ years of technical writing, ideally for software developers
Comfortable with docs-as-code, Markdown, HTML and git-based workflows
Working familiarity with package managers, registries or DevOps tooling (npm, Docker, Maven, Terraform or similar)
Clear, precise communicator who keeps asking engineers “why” until it clicks




Why it’s worth a look




Remote-friendly across the UK & Ireland
£80,000 - £90,000 + Equity
Well-funded, fast-growing business on a strong growth trajectory
Small, high-calibre team where docs are treated as part of the product, not an afterthought
