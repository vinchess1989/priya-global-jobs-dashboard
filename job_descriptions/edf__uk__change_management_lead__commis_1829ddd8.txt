About The Role

Ready to help shape the safe delivery of one of the UK's most significant energy projects? Join a team where your expertise will influence critical commissioning decisions and support the transition to a lower-carbon future. At EDF, Success is Personal.

The Opportunity

As a Change Management Lead within the Commissioning/Pre-Operations organisation, you'll play a key role in managing configuration control across the Hinkley Point C commissioning programme. You'll help ensure design changes are implemented safely and effectively as commissioning activities continue to grow across the project.

You’ll join us on a salary starting from £67,000 per annum, with the potential for an annual bonus. This role is based full-time on site at #Bridgwater, supporting a major nuclear commissioning programme at Hinkley Point C.

You'll work with senior leaders, technical specialists and regulators while helping shape configuration management and change management processes. You'll also have opportunities to broaden your leadership experience as EDF continues its journey towards An Electric Britain.

Who You Are

We’re looking for a Change Management Lead to support the Commissioning/ Pre-Operations organisation who combines strong engineering expertise with experience of managing technical change in complex regulated environments. You'll be comfortable working across multiple stakeholders and driving high-quality outcomes. Are you experienced in…?


Managing technical design changes within large engineering, infrastructure, energy or nuclear projects
Coordinating engineering change governance, review and approval activities
Assessing engineering impacts, technical risks and implementation requirements arising from design changes
Working within highly regulated engineering environments with a strong focus on safety, compliance and quality
Using engineering knowledge to support configuration management and commissioning activities throughout project delivery


What You’ll Be Doing


Coordinating the day-to-day delivery of commissioning and Pre-Operations design change activities and configuration management processes
Representing Commissioning/Pre-Operations at key design change forums and supporting effective project-wide communication
Supporting the development and continuous improvement of configuration management strategies, processes and guidance
Engaging senior stakeholders and providing reporting that supports accountability and delivery of project objectives
Supporting the Commissioning Manager and acting as a deputy when required across leadership, governance and stakeholder activities


Pay, Benefits And Culture

Alongside a starting salary from £67,000 per annum, potential for an annual bonus, and a market-leading pension scheme, your package will include customisable benefits such as electric vehicle leasing, discounted gym membership, life assurance, tech vouchers, experience days, and more.
