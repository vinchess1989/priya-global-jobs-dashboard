Why this job mission

The luxury retail environment is rapidly changing, especially in North Asia, where customers expect a seamless blend of traditional elegance and cutting-edge digital engagement. We face the challenge of integrating digital solutions that not only meet but exceed the sophisticated expectations of our discerning clients. Adapting to the progressive retail landscape without compromising the storied heritage of our brand is paramount.

The Digital Solutions team plays a critical role at Chanel driving the digital transformation for our client and retail experience in the North Asia markets. This team addresses the need to modernize and enhance the luxury client experience, aligning with the digital age's demands and maintaining Chanel's legacy. This position will be at the heart of Chanel’s digital transformation and will play a critical role for Chanel North Asia and its 3 Divisions (Fashion, Watches & Fine Jewelry, Fragrance & Beauty Products) to achieve the Digital Roadmap.

The Boutique Experience Solutions team owns the portfolio of digital products that enable Boutique advisors and Client Care teams to deliver exceptional client service. The team provides end-to-end product ownership across Online Appointment, Queue Management, After Sales, Client Care and related capabilities, covering strategy and roadmap, delivery, integration, operational excellence and continuous improvement.

You will take end-to-end ownership of technology solutions, from initial conception through delivery and ongoing operation. This role requires technical expertise combined with strong business acumen to translate business requirements into effective technical solutions while managing their ongoing evolution. Working closely with various stakeholders, you will drive CHANEL's digital boutique landscape through new solution implementations, strategic enhancements, and operational improvements that deliver concrete business value.

He/She contributes to providing efficient Digital Solutions in a lean manner, seamlessly integrating with back-end systems and digital touchpoints. He/she makes recommendations to management on opportunities for process improvements by utilizing and leveraging applications in this space.

The Delivery of these solutions should be managed in a manner that maximizes efficiency, both in cost and time. The team will also manage the support and stability of digital solutions ensuring tools deliver optimal value for users and clients.
