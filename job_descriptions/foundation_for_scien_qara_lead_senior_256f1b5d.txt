🚀 We're Hiring | QARA Lead – Medical Devices

📍 Location: Bengaluru, India (IISc Campus)

💼 Experience: 5–7 Years

We are looking for an experienced Quality Assurance & Regulatory Affairs (QARA) Lead to drive quality management systems, regulatory compliance, and product certification for innovative medical devices.

🔹 Key Responsibilities:

Establish and implement an end-to-end QMS aligned with ISO 13485:2016.
Lead design controls, risk management, and technical documentation.
Drive regulatory submissions and approvals for CDSCO, US FDA (510(k)), and EU MDR.
Manage internal/external audits, CAPA, and quality performance metrics.
Oversee supplier quality, manufacturing validation, and process controls.
Support packaging, sterilisation validation, and post-market surveillance.
Implement effective document control and training systems.
Collaborate with cross-functional engineering and product development teams.

🔹 Qualifications & Skills:

Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Biomedical Sciences, Life Sciences, or a related field.
5–7 years of experience in Quality Assurance and Regulatory Affairs for medical devices.
Hands-on experience implementing ISO 13485 and preparing regulatory submissions.
Strong understanding of ISO 14971, IEC 62304, IEC 60601, IEC 62366-1, ISO 10993, ISO 11607, and Indian Medical Devices Rules, 2017.
Familiarity with Class II or higher-risk medical devices and international regulatory pathways.
ISO 13485 Internal Auditor certification preferred.

🌟 What We're Looking For

A proactive, detail-oriented professional who can take ownership of quality and regulatory activities, work closely with multidisciplinary teams, and help bring safe, compliant medical devices to market.
