About Rivian

Rivian is on a mission to keep the world adventurous forever. This goes for the emissions-free Electric Adventure Vehicles we build, and the curious, courageous souls we seek to attract.

As a company, we constantly challenge what’s possible, never simply accepting what has always been done. We reframe old problems, seek new solutions and operate comfortably in areas that are unknown. Our backgrounds are diverse, but our team shares a love of the outdoors and a desire to protect it for future generations.

Role Summary

Rivian’s Commercial Technology organization builds and scales the digital products and platforms that power our end-to-end customer journey – spanning direct-to-consumer digital commerce, vehicle ordering and delivery, billing and financing, digital charging experiences, and global fleet management platforms.

We are looking for a high-impact Engineering Manager for Developer Experience (DevEx) to spearhead engineering productivity across Commercial Technology. In this role, your primary mission is to eradicate friction, accelerate delivery cycles, and empower our engineers to ship resilient, high-quality software safely and quickly.

This is a role for a former software engineer turned leader. You understand codebases, distributed architectures, and delivery bottlenecks firsthand. You know the frustration of broken pipelines, slow test runners, and fragmented documentation, and you will use that technical empathy to treat developer tooling as a first-class internal product for Commercial Technology.

Responsibilities


Spearhead Engineering Productivity: Define and drive the engineering productivity vision and roadmap across the Commercial Technology organization, removing systemic blockers in day-to-day software development.
People & Team Leadership: Manage, coach, and grow a dedicated team of DevEx and platform engineers; conduct 1:1s, support technical career ladders, and maintain a high-ownership engineering culture.
Treat Tooling as a Product: Act as the product owner for internal engineering systems. Partner with commercial engineering teams (commerce, billing, fleet, order management), gather direct feedback, identify friction points, and deliver measurable productivity wins.
Modernize CI/CD & Delivery Pipelines: Optimize our GitLab & GitLab CI infrastructure and deployment pipelines. Drive down build and test cycle times, tackle test flakiness, and streamline artifacts and caching.
Champion GitOps & Kubernetes Deployments: Deepen GitOps adoption using ArgoCD targeting Kubernetes clusters, enabling repeatable, self-healing, zero-downtime application deployments.
Expand the Internal Developer Portal (Backstage): Drive the strategy and rollout of our internal portal powered by Backstage, establishing "Golden Paths," reusable service templates, centralized API documentation, and automated service catalogs.
Measure What Matters: Track and systematically improve developer velocity metrics (DORA metrics, pipeline durations, time-to-first-commit for new hires, and Developer Net Promoter Scores / DevNPS).
Cross-Functional Alignment: Partner with Cloud Platform, InfoSec, Quality Engineering, and commercial product squads to embed compliance, observability, and security standards seamlessly into our developer workflows.


Qualifications


Software Engineering Foundation: 5+ years of prior hands-on experience as a Software Engineer writing production code, building services, and participating in code reviews, distributed systems design, and debugging.
Engineering Management Experience: 2+ years of direct people management experience leading software, platform, or infrastructure teams, with a track record of hiring, mentoring, and shipping cross-cutting initiatives.
CI/CD Mastery: Proven experience with architecting, managing, and optimizing enterprise pipelines in GitLab CI (or equivalent CI engines).
GitOps & Cloud-Native Depth: Hands-on operational experience with ArgoCD, Kubernetes, containerization (Docker), and cloud environments (AWS/GCP).
Developer Portals: Experience implementing, customizing, or driving organizational adoption of Backstage (or similar internal developer portals) for service catalogs, scaffolding, and documentation.
Product-Driven DevEx Mindset: Experience measuring developer friction quantitatively (DORA, SPACE frameworks) and qualitatively, turning developer pain points into structured roadmaps.
Strong Communication & Influence: Ability to build consensus and advocate for engineering best practices across multiple commercial squads and technical stakeholders.


Nice to Have


Experience supporting modern e-commerce, payment, order fulfillment, or large-scale digital web/mobile architectures.
Experience with monorepo tooling, build caches (e.g., Turborepo, Nx, Bazel), and ephemeral/preview testing environments.
Active contributions to open-source developer tooling or the CNCF ecosystem (e.g., Backstage plugins, Argo projects).
