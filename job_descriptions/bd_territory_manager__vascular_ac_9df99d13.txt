Job Description




Are you an experienced Quality or Regulatory leader with deep expertise in meeting the requirements of EU MDR and/or IVDR as a Legal Manufacturer? Looking to play a meaningful role in ensuring regulatory compliance throughout Europe, Switzerland, and the UK?




We are seeking a Senior Quality Manager to come and join us at our offices in Limerick or Wexford. In this highly visible role, you will serve as the senior Quality leader for our European Authorized Representative (AR), Swiss Authorized Representative (CH-REP), and UK Responsible Person (UKRP) operations. You will lead all aspects of regulatory compliance activities for manufacturers outside the European Union.




In the role of Person Responsible for Regulatory Compliance (PRRC), you will ensure compliance with EU IVDR, EU MDR, Swiss IvDO, and UK MDR requirements while acting as a key interface between represented manufacturers, Proficient Authorities, Notified Bodies, and internal collaborators.




This role is vital for maintaining market access in Europe, Switzerland, and the UK. It supports post-market surveillance and vigilance. It also helps fulfill economic operators' responsibilities and strengthens our regulatory and quality infrastructure.




Main Responsibilities Will Include:




Act as the designated PRRC supporting EU AR, Swiss CH-REP, and UKRP activities, ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements.
Verify that technical documentation, declarations of conformity, certifications, registrations, and regulatory records are maintained and up to date.
Be responsible for economic operator obligations, including UDI, EUDAMED, and other applicable market registration requirements.
Provide quality and regulatory oversight for post-market monitoring, safety reporting, complaint handling, and field safety corrective actions.
Serve as the primary liaison in interactions involving Authorities with proficiency such as MHRA, Swissmedic, and Advised Bodies throughout assessments, verifications, and compliance investigations.
Support the maintenance, continuous improvement, and enhancement of the Quality Management System (QMS) to improve efficiency, including audits, CAPAs, and management reviews.
Partner with cross-functional teams to ensure regulatory compliance is maintained throughout the product lifecycle and across all represented manufacturers.




Skills and Experience Required:




Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Science, Law, a field related to Regulatory Affairs, or equivalent experience
Demonstrable experience working directly for an organization acknowledged as a Legal Manufacturer operating under EU MDR and/or EU IVDR.
Expert-level knowledge of ISO 13485 and the European medical device regulations is required.
Experience in Medical Device Regulation and/or the In Vitro Diagnostic Regulation. Acquired within a regulatory manufacturing environment.
Strong understanding of Economic Operator requirements, EUDAMED, UK MDR, Swiss medical device ordinance (IvDO), post-market surveillance, vigilance, and PRRC obligations is essential.
Proven experience in Quality Systems, Quality Engineering, in the field of Regulatory Affairs or related functions within the Medical Device, IVD, or Pharmaceutical industries.
Experience of regulatory or QMS experience related to IVD products.
Experience supporting regulatory inspections, audits, and interactions with Proficient Authorities, MHRA, Swissmedic, and/or Notified Bodies preferred.
Experience leading QMS standardization, site consolidations, or operating model transformations is desirable.
Regulatory inspection and audit readiness expertise.
Strong project management, problem-solving, and decision-making skills.
Strategic approach with the ability to translate regulatory requirements into practical business solutions.
