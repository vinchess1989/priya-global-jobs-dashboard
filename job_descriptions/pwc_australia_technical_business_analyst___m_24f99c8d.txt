At PwC Australia, we recognise the power of transformation and the potential of human-centred innovation. As a technical Business Analyst within Managed Services Technology, you'll be at the forefront of a strategic initiative focused on shaping and scaling the technology capabilities that underpin PwC Australia’s managed services. You’ll work across business and technology teams to translate complex workflow and operational needs into clear, actionable requirements that improve service quality, productivity and client outcomes.

Why PwC?

At PwC Australia, your skills meet purpose. We tackle big challenges across industries like finance, technology, energy, and health, giving you the chance to make a real impact. Here, your growth is our priority. You’ll work with leading teams, explore new technologies, and unlock your full potential.

Join a global community of more than 370,000 people who value bold ideas, collaboration, and lasting change. Together, we’re building trust and shaping the future.

What you’ll do:


Own requirements end to end, from the initial business problem and value case through discovery, design, build, testing, release and post-go-live outcomes, holding yourself accountable for the solution delivering its intended value.
Translate business needs into clear requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, process maps, business rules and decision logic.
Work closely with Technical Leads, Engineers and other delivery teams to ensure requirements are technically feasible, appropriately scoped and aligned to reusable technology patterns.
Maintain traceability from the business problem and value case through design, build, testing and release, partnering with QA and business stakeholders on acceptance criteria, UAT and defect triage so solutions deliver the intended business outcomes.
Facilitate workshops and working sessions across business, technology, operations, risk and governance stakeholders.
Identify reusable requirements, workflow patterns, business rules, prompts, agent skills and operational controls that can improve future delivery. Use operational feedback and lessons learned to keep improving discovery, design and backlog prioritisation.
Support governed AI and agentic delivery by ensuring behaviours, data use, controls and requirements are clearly documented.



What we’re looking for:


Strong experience as a Technical Business Analyst and/or Product Analyst within a technology delivery environment.
Hands-on experience applying AI tools in your own work and in delivered solutions, with a practical understanding of how to use it responsibly to improve productivity, quality and outcomes.
Demonstrated experience in requirements elicitation, process mapping, workflow analysis, user stories and acceptance criteria.
Strong ability to translate complex business problems into clear, technically actionable and testable requirements.
Experience working with data mapping, business rules, exception analysis and control documentation.
Communicates clearly and concisely, both written and verbal, and can explain complex technical detail simply to senior and non-technical audiences.
Experience supporting testing, UAT, defect triage, release readiness and operational handover.
Experience delivering in Agile sprint teams, working closely with engineers through backlog refinement, sprint planning and release.
Ability to pick up new domains quickly and apply consistent analysis patterns across different service lines.
A curious, experimental mindset. You actively try new tools and ways of working, and you have the confidence to challenge unclear scope and untested assumptions.
Relevant business analysis, Agile, product, process improvement, data or service management qualifications are preferred but not essential.
Successful applicant should be able to describe something you’ve built, automated or improved using AI in your own work, and how you checked the output.



What you’ll gain:


The opportunity to work with leading organisations and cutting-edge technology that challenge and expand your expertise.
Flexibility means trusting you to choose when, where, and how you work—balancing what’s best for you, your team, and your clients to grow and thrive together.
Clear paths for career progression supported by continuous learning and leadership development.
Generous leave entitlements to support your work-life balance - including floating public holidays, birthday leave and ability to purchase additional leave on top of your four weeks.
