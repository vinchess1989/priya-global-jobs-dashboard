What You’ll Be Doing...

As an Analyst in a Quality Engineering team, you will be working with external stakeholders communicating with business partners, Dev, UAT, E2E teams and the PMO teams.


Working with a team of quality engineers, partners like Developers, SME and Architect on test activities for deliverables.
Defining test scenarios, test cases, executing of tests, publish test reports for given set of requirements.
Working on automation using standard test automation tools for the respective area of work.
Utilizing the testing lifecycles tools, DevOps/Agile tools for day-to-day operations, status reporting and to collaborate with other partners.
Improving the overall delivery excellence by working through project dynamics.


What We’re Looking For...

You enjoy working on testing principles and applying different testing techniques to deliver a High Quality product. You are a great teammate and have a go-getter kind of attitude with good interpersonal skills. You stay focused and enjoy the challenge that comes with this role and are able to manage work effectively in a dynamic, complex and deadline-oriented environment.

You'll Need To Have


Bachelor’s degree or one or more years of relevant experience required, demonstrated through work experience and/or military experience.
Experience with automation tools, developing software test plans, managing defects lifecycle using standard tools.


Even better if you have one or more of the following:


Strong communication and critical thinking skills.
Experience in working in Agile/SAFe teams.
Strong experience with testing principles applying different testing techniques to deliver a High Quality product.
Ability to adopt an automation first approach and execute the automation test strategies.
Knowledge in reusable component development.
Collaboration skills to manage the peers, partners and other stakeholders.
