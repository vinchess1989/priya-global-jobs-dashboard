Medtronic

Our Purpose

At Medtronic Patient Care Systems (PCS), we power the digital heartbeat behind life-saving cardiac therapies. We build the connected ecosystem that enables clinicians to monitor, manage, and protect millions of patients living with implantable cardiac devices around the world.

From secure device connectivity and real-time data platforms to intelligent clinical tools and patient-facing experiences, PCS transforms complex cardiac data into clarity, helping physicians make faster decisions, improving outcomes, and giving patients greater confidence in their care.

Every line of code we write supports someone’s parent, spouse, child, or friend. Our work ensures that life-saving therapies are not only delivered safely and reliably, but continuously improved through innovation, insight, and global scale.

If you want to build technology that truly matters, where uptime means peace of mind and innovation means more birthdays, join us. Together, we are shaping the future of connected cardiac care and changing lives every single day

Come for a job, stay for a career!

As a Prin Cloud Engineer within PCS organization, you will be involved in developing the software ecosystem supporting Medtronic implantable cardiac devices and will operate in all phases and contribute to all activities of the software development process.

Medtronic PCS teams develop the next generation medical technologies that save lives, improve quality of living for millions of patients across the world. The team works with various software technologies in application software, network software, mobile software, BT/BLE communication software, and interfacing with embedded software for management and control of implantable medical devices.

The team is seeking a proactive, self-motivated, and highly skilled software development engineer to lead the charge in building the next generation of CRM solutions. This role demands innovative thinking, a strong background in cloud-based software development, and a passion for creating robust, scalable systems that drive exceptional user experiences.

You will play a pivotal role in shaping the architecture and development of cutting-edge PCS software, collaborating with cross-functional global teams to deliver impactful solutions.

A Day in the Life


Provide technical leadership and architectural direction for enterprise-scale CI/CD capabilities supporting development teams across Patient Care Systems.
Design, build, and evolve reusable Golden Path and Golden Gate pipeline templates that standardize software build, analysis, packaging, security scanning, SBOM generation, and delivery while allowing appropriate extension for product-specific needs. These templates are the organizational baseline for CI/CD.
Lead the ongoing transition of CI/CD workloads from Azure DevOps toward GitHub and GitHub Actions, establishing scalable patterns that development teams can adopt with minimal friction.
Architect shared pipeline capabilities using technologies including Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, JFrog Artifactory/Xray, SonarQube, Docker, Kubernetes, Helm, and Terraform.
Establish reusable patterns for multiple build technologies and application architectures rather than creating one-off implementations for individual teams. The team's Golden Path model already uses technology-specific templates to abstract build, analysis, and publishing activities.
Embed security and compliance directly into the developer workflow through automated quality gates, vulnerability scanning, artifact management, SBOM generation, code analysis, and policy enforcement.
Drive the architecture and reliability of build-agent and GitHub Actions runner infrastructure, including scalability, monitoring, capacity, and troubleshooting of complex pipeline workloads.
Improve developer productivity by eliminating repetitive pipeline engineering, simplifying onboarding, creating self-service workflows, and reducing the time required to move a new application or service from repository creation to a production-ready delivery path.
Partner with application engineering, Product Security, Quality, infrastructure, Kubernetes, cloud, and platform teams to establish common engineering standards and solve complex cross-platform delivery problems.
Work directly with development teams when needed to understand complex build and release requirements, identify reusable patterns, and turn those requirements into capabilities that benefit the broader organization.
Lead technically difficult migrations and modernization efforts across large numbers of repositories and pipelines. The Plumbers team has already migrated 547 pipelines across 37 Scrum teams while developing nine reusable Golden Path templates.
Influence architecture and technology decisions beyond the immediate Scrum team through design reviews, technical standards, architecture decisions, prototypes, and reference implementations.
Establish observability and measurable health indicators for the CI/CD platform, including pipeline health, runner reliability, adoption, performance, and developer experience.
Mentor engineers and serve as a technical escalation point for complex CI/CD, software supply-chain, artifact-management, and developer-platform problems.
Help evolve DX capabilities toward greater automation, including AI-assisted PR review, workflow automation, CI/CD troubleshooting, and agentic support experiences.
Maintain a hands-on role through code, pull requests, prototypes, troubleshooting, and technical implementation while simultaneously helping define the longer-term architecture and roadmap.



Key Skills & Experience

Core Competencies: Minimum Requirements


Bachelor's degree in computer science, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Information Systems, or related technical discipline with 7+ years of relevant experience, or an advanced degree with 5+ years.
Strong technical depth in software, cloud, infrastructure, platform, or data systems.
Experience designing, building, operating, and improving reusable platform services, automation, CI/CD pipelines, Infrastructure as Code, or shared engineering capabilities.
Ability to deliver scalable, reliable, secure, maintainable, and cost-effective cloud-native or distributed solutions.
Experience troubleshooting production systems, performing root cause analysis, and implementing lasting improvements.
Ability to independently solve difficult technical problems and own projects, processes, or technical outcomes within the job area.
Experience improving engineering practices, influencing technical direction, and mentoring less experienced engineers.
Strong communication skills with the ability to collaborate across engineering, architecture, leadership, customer, and vendor groups.
Mastery of a specialty area and full knowledge of industry practices, typically obtained through advanced education combined with experience



Platform Domain Experience

CICD Platform


Strong hands-on experience with GitHub Actions and/or Azure DevOps Pipelines, including reusable pipelines, templates, workflows, runners/agents, security controls, and deployment automation.
Advanced experience with source control and Git-based software-development workflows.
Strong software engineering and automation experience using languages such as Python, Go, JavaScript/TypeScript, Bash, or PowerShell.
Experience with artifact/package management and software supply-chain tooling such as JFrog Artifactory and Xray.
Experience integrating automated testing, static analysis, code coverage, vulnerability scanning, SBOM generation, secrets management, and other DevSecOps controls into CI/CD pipelines.
Experience with container technologies including Docker and Kubernetes, and familiarity with Helm and cloud-native deployment patterns.
