Who We Are



SWTCH is pioneering EV charging solutions for multifamily and commercial properties across North America. Since 2016, our team of entrepreneurs, energy professionals, and technologists has been focused on unlocking the social, economic, and environmental benefits of widespread EV adoption. Today, our solutions are deployed in tens of thousands of locations —many of which are low-to-moderate income housing—helping to make clean mobility accessible for all.

With an expanding footprint across Canada and the U.S., we're scaling quickly and looking for mission-driven talent to help accelerate our growth.


The Opportunity



SWTCH is looking for a highly motivated DevOps Engineer to join our growing tech team. Reporting to the Director of Engineering, you will design, build, and optimize our AWS container platform, CI/CD pipelines, and infrastructure-as-code tooling. This is a collaborative role: you'll work closely with development and operations teams to streamline deployment, testing, and software delivery, and help lead our path to push-to-deploy continuous delivery.

Responsibilities


Develop and maintain our AWS ECS platform (Fargate and ECS on EC2), along with our existing EC2 and Docker Compose hosts
Manage CI/CD pipelines on GitHub Actions, improving reliability and build speeds and moving us toward push-to-deploy with feature flags, canary or blue-green deploys, and automated rollback
Collaborate with Development and Architecture teams to build highly available cloud environments, including our Traefik-based OCPP edge and self-hosted Redpanda streaming platform
Build and extend our toolchain, including AWS (ECS, Fargate, Secrets Manager, Parameter Store), OpenTofu, and shell scripting, with Checkov, tflint, and plan checks on every PR
Maintain and enhance automation tooling and deployment infrastructure in line with SOC 2 best practices
Identify parts of the architecture that lack redundancy and improve reliability, including supporting our MySQL to Postgres migration
Build monitoring, alerting, and tooling (Vector, Grafana, VictoriaMetrics, OpenTelemetry) that surfaces front-end and back-end issues as soon as they happen, including per-service SLOs and burn-rate alerts
Write and maintain clear documentation for infrastructure and processes



Requirements


5+ years of experience in DevOps, release engineering, or build automation, including 3+ years with large-scale distributed infrastructure and client-server architecture
Hands-on experience with AWS ECS (Fargate and/or EC2), Docker, and container technologies in a microservices environment
Experience deploying and managing infrastructure as code with OpenTofu or Terraform
Strong experience with AWS and cloud infrastructure automation
Experience with CI/CD (ideally GitHub Actions), continuous testing, and monitoring and alerting tools such as Grafana and Prometheus-compatible metrics (e.g., VictoriaMetrics)


Nice to Have

Experience with Kafka-compatible streaming systems such as Redpanda or Kafka
An AWS certification. If you don't have one yet, we're happy to sponsor your exam
Experience working in a SOC 2 environment
