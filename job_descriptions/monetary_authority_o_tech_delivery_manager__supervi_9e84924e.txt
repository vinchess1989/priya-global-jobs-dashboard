What The Role Is

You will be part of a dynamic team within Supervision Platforms Division under Information Technology Department.

What You Will Be Working On

In this role, the officer is expected to undertake the following areas of work:


Manage stakeholder relationships
Product and project delivery
Responsible for the quality and timely delivery of project/sprint
Establish project plan, resource requirement, project schedule and task allocation plan together with business users, developers and stakeholders
Identify, track and communicate progress of project milestones, deliverables, dependencies and risks during the project
Coordinate project activities across multiple teams and applications across the organization
Secure acceptance and approval of the key deliverables based on the project management framework
Solution engineering, testing and delivery
Coordinate and work with other technical teams (e.g. Infrastructure, Enterprise, Applications) to set up infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, application integrations and resolve deployment and integration issues;
Lead and liaise with software engineers and other technical teams to resolve impediments, incidents and issues.
Design, manage and execution test and quality assurance for solutions
In concert with subject matter experts, design solutions which include system architecture, application architecture, including security and data design, resiliency, performance, reusability, product competency and programming skills
Solution business analysis and design
In conjunction with business and product teams, to design and deliver a solution experience that meets the needs of user through human, digital, and physical interactions, over time, across a journey (in conjunction with policy, process, digital enablers).
Mapping of the service/business/functional requirements to solution/application system/technical requirements and ensure overall design, development and implementation remains consistent with the overall architecture of the project, and its objectives and needs
Product technical management and operations
Work closely with the business product owner and software engineers on regular deployment of product features;
Support system operations after project go-live to ensure system availability and SLA
Conduct maintenance and technology lifecycle management on the applications and systems
Budgeting and planning
Budget and plan resources for the team, products, and projects
Vendor contracting and management
Conduct procurement
Manage vendors towards delivery of outcomes
Governance, Risk and Compliance
Ensure compliance to ICT Governance policies and standards
Ensure risk framework is adhered to, and carry out risk management for projects and products

What We Are Looking For


Bachelor Degree or Master Degree with minimally 5 years of relevant working experience in project management, business analysis and system operation
Preferably more than 3 years of relevant experience in agile project management
Prior experience in managing or leading SDD (Software Design and Development) delivery would be an advantage, with good understanding of the end-to-end software delivery lifecycle from requirements and solution design through development, testing, deployment and transition to operations
Strong experience in managing business, IT and senior stakeholders
Sound understanding of the technical aspects of systems integration in an environment with diverse technologies
Strong communication skills and ability to influence to gain consensus from multiple stakeholders when required
Working experience in using project management software like JIRA to track, monitor, and reporting of project/issue progress and producing reports
Familiarity with Government Instruction Manual 8 will be an added advantage
Familiar with containers, microservices, DevOps and AW Cloud technologies
Resourceful and possess excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills
Good analytical skills, and able to mulit-task and deliver results in a timely manner
Ability to resolve complex problems creatively
Self-driven, creative and team-oriented person with good interpersonal and organizational skills


This is a 2-years contract role. As part of the shortlisting process for this role, you may be required to complete a medical declaration and/or undergo further assessment.

All applicants will be notified on whether they are shortlisted or not within 4 weeks of the closing date of this job posting.
