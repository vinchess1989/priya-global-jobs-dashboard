Who We Are

Solera is a global leader in data and software services that strives to transform every touchpoint of the vehicle lifecycle into a connected digital experience. In addition, we provide products and services to protect life’s other most important assets: our homes and digital identities. Today, Solera processes over 300 million digital transactions annually for approximately 235,000 partners and customers in more than 90 countries. Our 6,500 team members foster an uncommon, innovative culture and are dedicated to successfully bringing the future to bear today through cognitive answers, insights, algorithms, and automation. For more information, please visit solera.com.

Job Summary

We are looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer who will have a holistic approach about systems when driving the definition, building, deployment and operations of Solera applications in close collaboration with Dev, QA and Ops peers.

We are seeking an individual who will show ownership and can help build the appropriate bridges and processes across the various teams, leveraging experience with minimal direction, while balancing trade-off and strict processes and methodologies in a fast-paced environment. This individual will lead the creation and evolution of our CI/CD pipelines for deployment and operations of solutions across Solera’s various environments which may include on-prem or cloud systems.

Our products intersect with all the current and exciting trends in the automotive and transportation industries, and our business is growing every year. Our people and our platforms are the foundation and enabler of that growth.

We are expanding our team looking for technologists with a passion for developing highly available and distributed applications in the cloud, and a drive to deliver software products that make a meaningful difference in the lives of others.

Our Stack

We expect the ideal candidate to be proficient in the following technologies and tools (list not exhaustive), in particular the CI/CD and Infrastructure stacks:


CI/CD: GitHub Actions, Helm Deploy, Spinnaker, Octopus Deploy, Jenkins, NuGet, SonarQube
Source Control and Processes: Jira, Confluence, Git/GitHub
Languages: Python, PowerShell, batch, bash
Infrastructure: AWS (EKS, MSK, EC2, S3, ECR, Secrets Manager, Cognito, etc...), Terraform, Linux administration, Windows Server administration, Docker
Backend: MS SQL Server, Cassandra, MongoDB, Redis, Elasticsearch, Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog
Traditional internet and security protocols (HTTP, TLS, PKI)


Our Team

The IT team runs the entire infrastructure supporting Solera services and customers, powered by on-prem systems in our datacenter and a large public cloud footprint. The team, whose ownership and drive has no match, is composed of experts in DevOps, DataOps, Production Operations, IT, and Security. Our team is a global operation with support around the clock to ensure we fulfill the Solera Promise to our customers.

Your Role


Lead the transition of legacy/manual deployment processes to a completely automated, DevOps ready posture
Ownership in a very agile and fast paced environment of the CI/CD pipeline for deployment and operations of Solera AWS footprint
Create and maintain the continuously evolving Solera DevOps CI/CD pipelines across Dev, QA and Production
Create and maintain all cloud resources with Infrastructure as Code (IaC)
Maintain stability across all environments with IaC
Develop with cross-functional teams’ strategies towards higher organizational throughput via automation and flexible processes
Design and create reliable continuous deployment solutions that will yield higher availability and uptime for our internal and external customers
Manage operational cost with optimal resource utilization across on-prem and cloud services, including evolving capacity planning
Help the team balance and plan longer term projects against the demands of a fast-changing environment
Enforce security policies by ensuring vulnerabilities are tracked and timely addressed
Diagnose, troubleshoot and resolve issues related to Solera environments
Support Dev and QA teams along the steps of the Software Development Life Cycle by maintaining environments and tools
Maintain and develop documentation to facilitate understanding of products, features, and tools, including training material
Participate in rotations as necessary in a 24x7x365 organization


Minimum Qualifications


B.S. or M.S. Degree in Computer Science/Computer Information Science or equivalent (M.S. highly desirable)
8+ years of experience driving CI/CD for systems being deployed to cloud providers (proficiency with AWS services and associated setup/operations)
8+ years of experience in a 24x7x365 global SaaS/PaaS operation
8+ years of experience with virtualization and Docker containers
Proficiency with scripting languages (PowerShell, batch, bash, Python, or equivalent)


Preferred Qualifications


You demonstrate proficiency the technologies in our stack (see above) with 8+ years of experience in a large portion of the tools and technologies we leverage, in particular the CI/CD and Infra stacks.
You demonstrate a high sense of urgency and ownership
You take great pride in helping teams and individuals
You have competence in building and designing complex systems leveraging multiple technologies (legacy and new)
You can demonstrate and ensure consistency in line with industry best practices for CI/CD, code quality, operational readiness, and security
Strong organizational skills in a fast paced and fast changing environment
You are driven, self-motivated to learn and ramp up fast
You are candid and can hold your own as the DevOps representative in cross-functional teams
Be able to perform training and knowledge transfers
