🚀 Hiring Alert | Delivery Manager – Infotainment & Telematics | Chennai

Mahindra Research Valley (MRV), Chennai is looking for an experienced Delivery Manager with strong expertise in Infotainment & Telematics Systems.




Key Responsibilities:

✅ Lead program discussions with Vehicle Project Teams, Manufacturing, Quality, and Cross-Functional Stakeholders

✅ Drive end-to-end delivery activities for Cockpit / Infotainment domain

✅ Manage JIRA and ensure timely tracking, resolution, and closure of project issues

✅ Coordinate with internal teams and suppliers for delivery milestones, design releases, TKO, and SOR activities

✅ Drive change management, stakeholder alignment, and process adherence across programs

✅ Resolve validation concerns (IR & Vehicle) and support manufacturing issue closure

✅ Support launch readiness and ensure line issues are addressed within project timelines




Preferred Experience:

✔ Experience in Automotive Infotainment, Telematics, Connectivity, Cluster, or Cockpit Systems

✔ Strong experience in stakeholder management, program delivery, and supplier coordination

✔ Knowledge of JIRA, Vehicle Integration, Validation, and Release Management

✔ Exposure to OEM/Tier-1 automotive programs
