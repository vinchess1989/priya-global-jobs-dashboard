Job Overview

We are seeking a highly motivated, self-driven Product Regulatory Compliance Engineer to support global product compliance across Penguin Solutions’ AI/HPC systems, AI Factory infrastructure, and integrated rack-level solutions. This role operates at the intersection of engineering, quality, manufacturing, sourcing, and external certification partners to help ensure products are compliant, and ready for global market access.

The role supports the product lifecycle from early design and requirements definition through certification, launch, sustaining engineering, and product change management. The successful candidate will bring strong technical curiosity, disciplined project execution, and the ability to collaborate across multifunctional and multilocational teams.

This position works across engineering, operations, manufacturing, quality, supply chain, and external certification organizations. It is suited to an engineer who takes ownership, learns quickly, and can translate regulatory requirements into practical actions that support product execution and global deployment.

Responsibilities


Support regulatory compliance for AI/HPC servers, storage, networking, compute systems, and AI Factory solutions throughout the product lifecycle.
Coordinate product certifications for global markets, including FCC, CE, UKCA, KCC, VCCI, RCM, and other applicable regional requirements.
Support product safety compliance with IEC 62368-1 and related international standards, working with engineering and certification partners to address requirements and findings.
Support environmental compliance programs, including RoHS, REACH, WEEE, conflict minerals, and other applicable or emerging global requirements.
Partner with contract manufacturers and accredited third-party laboratories to plan testing, manage certification projects, resolve findings, and obtain regulatory approvals.
Maintain and organize compliance documentation, including technical construction files, declarations of conformity, CB reports, test reports, risk assessments, and compliance matrices.
Collaborate with electrical, mechanical, quality, sourcing, manufacturing, and program teams to identify regulatory requirements early and incorporate compliance into product development plans.
Review product changes, component substitutions, manufacturing changes, and sustaining engineering activities for potential regulatory impact.
Track regulatory and standards developments and communicate potential impacts, actions, and timing considerations to engineering and program management.
Support audits, customer inquiries, and requests for regulatory documentation while maintaining accurate and controlled compliance records.
Contribute to repeatable compliance processes, project tracking, and cross-functional practices that improve launch readiness and execution efficiency.


Qualifications


Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering or a related engineering discipline; 3+ years of relevant experience.
Working understanding of EMC/EMI, electrical safety, product certification, and regulatory compliance principles.
Knowledge of FCC, CE, IEC 62368-1, RoHS, REACH, or similar standards and regulations is desirable.
Strong organizational and project coordination skills, with the ability to manage multiple certification activities and deadlines.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to communicate technical and compliance topics clearly to cross-functional stakeholders.
Ability to work effectively across global, cross-functional teams and with contract manufacturers, certification agencies, and third-party laboratories.
Strong analytical, troubleshooting, documentation, and follow-through skills.
Comfort working in a fast-paced, multi-project engineering environment where priorities evolve with product and customer needs.


Preferred Qualifications


Experience supporting compliance for servers, storage, networking equipment, rack-level systems, or data center infrastructure.
Exposure to global certification programs, accredited test laboratories, CB Scheme processes, or technical construction documentation.
Familiarity with product lifecycle management, engineering change processes, and sustaining engineering.
Interest in AI/HPC infrastructure, AI factories, accelerated computing, or high-density data center systems.
