Around the world, SYSTRA’s specialists plan, design, integrate, test, commission, project manage and deliver mass transit and mobility solutions that are relied on by more than 50 million people every day.

For more than 60 years, the Group has been committed to helping cities and regions contribute to their development by creating, improving and modernising their transport infrastructure with sustainability, accessibility and innovation at the heart of our designs. With over 10,300 colleagues globally and around 1000 in the UK & Ireland we are growing significantly and seeking out the very best talent to join the SYSTRA signature team and be part of leading the way in infrastructure design.

Context

SYSTRA are now hiring a Change Controller based in Dublin to play a key role in supporting high-profile rail and transport infrastructure projects in Ireland. If you have strong organizational skills, a keen eye for detail, and experience managing project change, we’d love to hear from you.

Missions/Main Duties

The Change Controller will assume responsibility for ensuring there are robust, effective and agile change management procedures in place at both a CBCIW Core level and CBCIW Site Level throughout the delivery lifecycle of CBCIW to ensure that whenever change arises, it is handled efficiently and effectively.

The Change Controller will oversee the development of a robust and agile approach to change management for CBCIW through the design of procedures, processes and templates for managing, driving and approving change aligned to the agreed baseline for the CBCIW. In addition, the role holder will work with all relevant functions of the organisation to bring a holistic approach to change management.

Profile/Skills

Required to have change control experience with a proven track record of carrying out change control activities across the project lifecycle through to completion in high value road and / or infrastructure projects or projects of a similar scale and complexity to CBCIW.


Further demonstrated experience and knowledge in the following is desirable:
Requirements for successful governance for major projects;
Capital projects and programmes with an emphasis on programme / project controls and how this supports and integrates with a wider high functioning environment;
Developing and implementing programme controls frameworks and processes, including improvement practices across large programmes and multi-disciplinary teams;
Design and delivery requirements of technically complex projects in the architectural, engineering or construction sectors;
Leading the Change Control Process;
Advising on complex requirements and communicating analysis and corrective measures effectively;
Managing projects controls delivery of medium / high complexity projects/programmes;
Playing a key role in multi-disciplinary teams and take lead role in creating plans or analysing trends using numerical and statistical techniques;
Focusing on attention to detail and checking project control inputs, recording and reports produced.
