Job Title: Electronic Batch Record Specialist

Career Level: C

About Singapore ADC Site

Be part of history in the making. AstraZeneca is building its first biologics manufacturing campus in Singapore — a groundbreaking USD 1.5 billion investment that will serve as a global hub for innovation and excellence.

This innovative facility will deliver end-to-end Antibody-Drug Conjugate (ADC) capabilities with different manufacturing units/buildings in one campus:


Small molecule- chemical API production
Large molecule- monoclonal antibody manufacturing
Conjugation
Fill-and-finish operations, including sterile filling and lyophilization
Final secondary packaging


The campus will be equipped with innovative digital and automation infrastructure, enabling autonomous manufacturing powered by artificial intelligence. In addition, it will be designed for carbon neutrality, embedding sustainability into every aspect of operations and setting a new benchmark for environmentally responsible biologic's manufacturing.

In Manufacturing Team, we are looking for passionate individuals with relevant experience who thrive in dynamic environments and bring a “make-it-happen” attitude. Joining us means contributing to a project that will not only transform AstraZeneca’s global supply chain but also advance life-changing medicines for patients everywhere.

If you’re ready to help define the future of biologics and be part of a once-in-a-generation project, we want you on our team.

Introduction To Role

Are you ready to harness digital manufacturing to accelerate the delivery of life-changing medicines? In the role Electronic Batch Record Specialist, you will connect innovative Manufacturing Execution System with the shopfloor. You will turn complex processes into simple, reliable, and compliant digital execution. Your work will directly reduce batch cycle times, enhance right-first-time performance, and improve patient supply continuity.

In this role, you will own the design, deployment, and continuous improvement of Electronic Batch Records within our Manufacturing Execution System. You will partner with colleagues across manufacturing, quality, engineering, IT, and supply teams to deliver robust, audit-ready solutions that scale with our pipeline. How would you shape a digital batch record that prevents deviations before they happen?

Accountabilities


MES Deployment and Improvement: Drive deployment, implementation, and continuous improvement of MES within manufacturing, delivering safer, faster, and more reliable execution.
User Requirement Specifications: Define and maintain clear, testable User Requirement Specifications aligned to process needs and regulatory expectations.
Cross-Functional Coordination: Coordinate stakeholders to create, configure, and test MES objects (batch records, equipment, logbooks, system interfaces) on time and to standard.
Commissioning and Validation: Lead commissioning, testing, qualification, and validation of MES objects to ensure compliant, audit-ready systems.
Documentation and Training: Create and maintain MES trainings, Standard Operating Procedures, and technical documentation that enable consistent, high-quality operations.
User Enablement: Provide effective training for MES users in partnership with the training team, ensuring adoption and sustained performance.
Object Lifecycle Management: Build and maintain MES content to support new products, expansions, evolving process requirements, and continuous improvement initiatives.
Quality Management: Drive deviation and change control activities in compliance with procedures and applicable regulations, safeguarding product quality.
Shopfloor Support: Deliver real-time troubleshooting and guidance during operations to minimize downtime and protect patient supply.
Audit Readiness: Ensure audit readiness and support regulatory inspections and certifications with accurate records and robust processes.
Compliance and Safety: Apply comprehensive knowledge of GMP, SHE standards, and SOPs to maintain safe, compliant operations.
Communication and Reporting: Translate complex technical concepts for both technical and non-technical audiences; contribute to improvement strategies and internal reporting.
Digital Adoption: Embrace new digital tools and technologies; collaborate to increase digital adoption across the area.
Coaching and Development: Invest in personal development and support the growth of less experienced team members to build capability across the team.


Essential Skills/Experience


Possess or pursuing a degree in a relevant technical or scientific discipline OR High School/Technical/Associate/Vocational diploma with equivalent experience
Demonstrated expertise in manufacturing or engineering processes within GXP environments
Possess relevant MES experience as recipe author/ batch coordinator for PAS-X/ Syncade/ Opcenter/ Pomsnet
Practical knowledge of SHE, and SOP protocols
Hands-on experience with continuous improvement strategies and tools
Foundational understanding of project execution and ability to contribute to minor projects
Proficiency in engaging diverse audiences with clear and effective communication


Desirable Skills/Experience


Hold a professional qualification
Committed to formal technical development
Awareness of product supply chain concepts
Understanding of machinery and process performance interactions
