About the role 

You'll be hands-on across our cloud environment, keeping it secure, reliable and efficient. You'll work closely with application teams and vendors to make that happen. You'll also help us explore how AI-enabled automation can improve the way we operate. 

Please note that the role includes taking part in our on-call support rotation. 

What you'll do

Provide first- and second-level support for cloud issues, troubleshooting across our infrastructure and recommending new services and solutions 

Build and maintain cloud infrastructure as code with Terraform 

Help deliver cloud projects from design and cost analysis through to build 

Introduce and improve the automation tools, principles and processes that keep our platforms reliable 

Strengthen our core cloud capability, and build a knowledge base and community of practice around our chosen technologies 

What you'll bring 

Demonstrated experience using Terraform to implement and maintain secure, reusable, version-controlled infrastructure 

Experience building, deploying and supporting workloads in AWS and/or Azure 

Competency in at least one programming or scripting language 

Experience with CI/CD tools, modern software development practices and IT change control processes 

Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills, and clear written communication, including technical documentation 

A degree in IT, Computer Science, Engineering or a related field, or relevant certifications (AWS and Azure certifications are highly regarded) 

Great to have 

Observability, monitoring and incident management, including SRE practices and automated remediation 

Containers (Kubernetes, Docker, ECS) 

Building AI-enabled and agentic solutions that automate operational workflows 

Cloud security, governance and cost management 

Active Directory, EntraID, IAM and/or DNS experience   

ServiceNow, Jira and Agile approaches 

Experience in energy or financial trading environments with 24/7 mission-critical systems
