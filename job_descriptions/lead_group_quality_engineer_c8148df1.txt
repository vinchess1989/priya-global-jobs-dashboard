Lead Group is recruiting a Quality Engineer for a Sydney-based medical device company developing wearable technology to help clinicians make more informed decisions during pregnancy and childbirth.




Reporting to the Director of Quality and Regulatory, you’ll help build and improve the Quality Management System and work across the medical device lifecycle, from design and development through manufacturing and post-market support. You’ll partner with Engineering, Clinical, Manufacturing and Product teams and take ownership of selected quality workstreams as the business grows.




What you’ll do

Represent Quality on new product development and design changes, reviewing quality plans and design documentation.
Support manufacturing quality, including process and software validation.
Lead selected site certification activities, conduct internal and supplier audits, and support external audits through to closure.
Investigate nonconformances and complaints, lead root cause analysis, and drive corrective and preventive actions (CAPA).
Improve QMS procedures, supplier quality processes, training and document control.
Depending on your experience and interests, contribute to regulatory submissions and responses to regulator queries.




What you’ll bring

At least four years of relevant experience in medical device quality, with exposure across design, manufacturing and post-market activities.
Practical experience applying ISO 13485, 21 CFR Part 820 and ISO 14971, with exposure to MDSAP and a working understanding of FDA and TGA requirements.
Experience supporting devices that combine software, electronics and mechanical components.
Audit training and experience, plus the ability to write clear, audit-ready QMS and design documentation.
A degree in engineering, science or a related discipline.




Experience implementing a QMS, working with wearable devices or maternal health products, or familiarity with machine learning is welcome
