Job Description

Biocomposites is an international medical devices company that engineers, manufactures, and markets world leading products for use in infection management in bone and soft tissue. Based in Keele, UK, it has global operations across Europe, USA, Argentina, Canada, China, and India. Biocomposites is a world leader in the development of innovative calcium compounds and bone cements for surgical use. Its products regenerate bone and target infection risks across a variety of specialties, including musculoskeletal infection, orthopaedics, trauma, spine, foot and ankle, podiatry, and sports injuries. Biocomposites products are now used in over one million procedures per annum and sold in more than 100 countries around the world.

Job Summary

This role will undertake and support engineering activities relating to new product development, sustaining engineering, product improvements and manufacturing changes across the medical device lifecycle. The role combines practical engineering and technical project delivery with the application of risk-based design controls to ensure products meet defined performance, safety, manufacturing, regulatory and quality requirements. This includes maintaining the Design Dossier (Design History File) and associated Technical Documentation in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements and the Quality Management System (QMS).

Location & Working Pattern

Primary working location: Keele office (IC4/5)

Standard working hours: Monday-Friday, 09:00-17:00 / 08:00-16:00

Duties & Responsibilities


Undertake and support product development, sustaining engineering and technical projects, contributing to product performance, reliability, manufacturability and continuous improvement
Evaluate and implement product, component, material and manufacturing-process changes through established change-control processes.
Develop, review and maintain product specifications, component specifications, engineering drawings and associated technical documentation.
Lead the application of risk-based design controls for new product developments and changes to existing products, ensuring design activities address user and customer needs, product requirements, safety, performance and applicable regulatory requirements.
Compile and maintain design documentation, including design inputs, outputs, traceability and the Design History File, throughout the product lifecycle in accordance with regulatory, quality and business requirements.
Conduct and support risk management activities, including hazard analysis and risk assessments, ensuring appropriate risk controls are identified and evaluated.
Participate in design reviews and provide engineering input into design verification and validation planning, execution and assessment of results.
Collaborate with subject-matter experts across R&D, Manufacturing, Quality, Regulatory Affairs and other functions to deliver technical activities in line with requirements and project timelines.
Support design transfer, new product introduction and supplier development activities to ensure products meet design and manufacturing requirements.
Assess applicable regulatory standards and support regulatory submissions, audits, inspections and assessments by providing relevant technical evidence and documentation.
Support activities within Quality, Technical, Research and Regulatory Affairs relating to product development, engineering changes and technical documentation


PERSON SPECIFICATION

Qualification & Training

Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Life Sciences or a related discipline, or equivalent relevant experience.

Experience

Ideally 3-5 years' relevant experience in product development, sustaining engineering, manufacturing engineering or related technical project work.

Experience applying design controls, risk management and technical documentation practices in a regulated environment. It would be a bonus to have experience with design transfer, supplier development, new product introduction or manufacturing process improvement.

Skill & Knowledge

Practical engineering problem-solving and the ability to translate technical requirements into specifications and acceptance criteria. Knowledge of electronic document management systems. Ideally you will have working knowledge of ISO 13485 and risk-based design control principles. You will have good understanding of design inputs and outputs, design reviews, verification and validation, and design change control.

Aptitude & Attributes


Enthusiastic and motivated to develop a career in medical-device development engineering and design assurance.
Analytical, organised and proactive, with sound engineering judgement and attention to detail.
Proficient in Microsoft Office and able to prepare clear, accurate technical documentation.
