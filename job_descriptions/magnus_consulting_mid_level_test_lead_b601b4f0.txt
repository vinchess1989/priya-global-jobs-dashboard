Introduction

Lead and govern testing and quality engineering across complex telecommunications delivery environments. The role owns test strategy, planning, traceability, automation direction, defect governance, quality reporting and evidence-based release-readiness recommendations.

Duties & Responsibilities

Key Responsibilities

· Define and govern test strategies, plans, quality gates and release-readiness criteria.

· Establish a proportionate test-automation approach and integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines.

· Embed quality practices within Agile and DevOps delivery, including TDD, BDD, continuous testing and shift-left testing.

· Coordinate functional, API, integration, regression and non-functional testing across teams and environments.

· Maintain end-to-end requirements-to-test traceability, including test data and environment considerations.

· Govern defect identification, triage, prioritisation, closure and reporting.

· Analyse test evidence and quality metrics, communicate quality risks and recommend mitigation actions.

· Provide objective, evidence-based release-readiness recommendations to delivery and governance stakeholders.

· Coordinate with business, engineering, architecture, security, infrastructure and delivery teams.

Required Knowledge

· Modern testing and quality-engineering principles and the complete testing lifecycle.

· Test automation, continuous testing and shift-left practices.

· Test-driven development (TDD) and behavior-driven development (BDD).

· Agile, DevOps and CI/CD quality practices.

· Risk-based testing, API and integration testing, regression testing and non-functional testing.

· Requirements traceability, test data, test environments and defect management.

· Quality gates, test metrics and release-readiness criteria.

Required Skills

· Create and govern practical test strategies and detailed test plans.

· Lead testing across complex, integrated delivery environments.

· Use measurable quality gates and interpret evidence objectively.

· Communicate defects, quality risks and release recommendations clearly to technical and non-technical stakeholders.

· Hands-on exposure to automation frameworks, service/API testing and quality-engineering practices is highly desirable.

Expected Deliverables / Artefacts

· Test strategy, master test plan and work-package test plans.

· Requirements traceability matrix and test-coverage reporting.

· Automation approach, automated regression packs and CI/CD quality gates.

· API, integration, functional, regression and non-functional test evidence.

· Test data and environment readiness records.

· Defect dashboards, triage records and quality metrics.

· Test-completion reports, risk assessments and release-readiness recommendations.

· Governance evidence, decision logs and knowledge-transfer / handover packs.

Telecommunications and Business Requirements

· Demonstrated experience in telecommunications IT environments, including OSS/BSS, wholesale or enterprise telco programmes.

· Working knowledge of TM Forum Frameworx, eTOM, SID and TMF Open APIs.

· Understanding of order-to-activate, service provisioning, service assurance and billing processes.

· Understanding of B2B and wholesale telecom customers, partners, commercial models, connectivity, data, cloud and managed services.

· Ability to translate B2B product and business requirements into practical role-specific outcomes.

Professional Competencies

· Strong analytical thinking, structured problem solving, written and verbal communication, facilitation and stakeholder management.

· Effective planning, prioritisation, risk escalation, collaboration and adaptability.

· Ability to use approved delivery, documentation, modelling, test-management, automation and collaboration tools.

· Maintain current documentation, conduct walkthroughs, transfer knowledge, prepare handover packs and support delivery continuity.

· Provide a detailed project portfolio and at least two contactable client references.

Qualifications and Certifications

· ISTQB Foundation or an equivalent testing certification is desirable.

· Experience integrating automated tests into delivery pipelines and applying measurable quality gates is highly desirable.

· At least five years of software testing and quality-engineering experience, including leading or coordinating testing in complex delivery environments.

Performance Expectations

· Deliver at least 95% of committed artefacts by agreed milestone dates.

· Achieve at least 90% first-time acceptance without major rework.

· Maintain full compliance with agreed templates, security, integration, data and governance standards.

· Submit complete weekly status updates and approved timesheets, including progress, risks, blockers, decisions and next commitments.

· Escalate material risks promptly with mitigation and recovery actions; complete handover before resource exit or replacement.

Desired Experience & Qualification

Role level: Mid-Level

Experience: Minimum 5 years relevant experience

Domain: B2B / wholesale telecommunications IT

Delivery mode: Hybrid / remote; onsite attendance when agreed

Package & Remuneration

Remuneration 280p/hr
