Job Overview

The Manager, Specification Excellence leads the development and ongoing management of the company's specification excellence capability, ensuring ingredient, formula, packaging, process, finished product and customer specifications are complete, accurate, current and appropriately governed. The role leads the Spec Excellence team and establishes the standards, processes and controls required to create a single, trusted specification backbone across the organization. Working cross functionally with Regulatory Affairs, Product Development, Food Safety & Quality, Commercialization, Operations, Procurement, Master Data and ITS, the Manager ensures specification information is execution ready, appropriately controlled and effectively integrated across SAP/IPD and related systems.

Key Responsibilities
Lead the Spec Excellence operating model across ingredient, formula, packaging, process, finished product and customer specifications.
Establish and maintain specification standards, templates, controlled terminology, units of measure, workflow requirements and completeness rules.
Define clear ownership, decision rights, approval requirements and escalation paths across the specification lifecycle.
Establish effective version control and change management practices so teams consistently work from current, approved information.
Drive consistent application of specification standards across functions, product categories and business units.
Identify systemic specification gaps and establish priorities for remediation and continuous improvement.
Oversee specification intake, creation, maintenance, revision, approval and lifecycle management.
Ensure specification information is complete, accurate, structured and appropriately controlled before progressing through downstream workflows.
Support the transition and ongoing integrity of specification information within SAP/IPD and related enterprise systems.
Partner with Master Data, ITS and functional teams to align specification structures, data requirements, workflows and system roles.
Lead legacy specification cleanup and enrichment activities and establish sustainable standards for maintaining information after migration.
Monitor specification completeness, accuracy, timeliness and outstanding exceptions.Act as the central coordination point for specification workflows across Regulatory Affairs, Food Safety & Quality, Product Development, Packaging, Procurement, Operations, Supply Chain, Master Data and Commercialization.
Route specifications to the appropriate subject matter experts for technical review and approval while maintaining ownership of workflow completeness and status.
Ensure specification requirements and dependencies are understood early enough to support development, trials, commercialization and production.
Identify incomplete, inconsistent or conflicting information and coordinate resolution before specifications move forward.
Ensure finished product, customer, packaging and processing specifications are ready to support commercialization and plant execution.
Support launch and change readiness by ensuring approved specification information is available to the appropriate downstream teams.
Establish controls that support regulatory, customer, food safety, quality, traceability and audit requirements.
Partner with Regulatory Affairs and Food Safety & Quality to ensure required technical and compliance information is captured within approved specifications.
Identify risks caused by incomplete, inaccurate or outdated specifications and lead corrective actions.
Improve specification workflows to reduce rework, duplication, ambiguity and late stage execution issues.
Maintain readiness to support audit, recall, customer and internal documentation requests.
Use recurring specification issues and workflow failures to identify opportunities for process, system or governance improvement.
Lead, coach and develop the Spec Excellence team.
Establish clear role accountabilities, priorities, workload allocation and performance expectations.
Build team capability across specification governance, systems, data quality and cross functional execution.
Establish training and ways of working for functions that create, review, approve or consume specification information.
Build a culture of accountability, accuracy, collaboration and continuous improvement.
Support successful adoption of SAP/IPD specification processes and future specification management capabilities.

7 to 10 years of progressive experience in food manufacturing, CPG, specification management, product data, Regulatory Affairs, Quality, R&D, commercialization or a related function.

Demonstrated experience improving processes and establishing disciplined ways of working.
