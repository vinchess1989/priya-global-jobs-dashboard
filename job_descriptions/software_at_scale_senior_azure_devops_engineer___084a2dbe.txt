Our black belt specialists are leaders in their domains, serving as digital champions, delivery focused experts, top tier security professionals, AI thought leaders, and engineering best practice advocates.

As 1 of Australias fastest growing software businesses, Software at Scale delivers cutting edge technology solutions that power mission critical platforms. We solve complex engineering challenges at scale, driving quality, performance, and resilience through the strength of our people.

Empower Your DevOps Career


We are currently building a dedicated, elite Azure DevOps squad to solve some very interesting and highly complex enterprise engineering problems. This is your opportunity to be a foundational part of that team, take extreme technical ownership, and truly empower your DevOps career as a trusted technical partner rather than a transactional ticket taker.

The Role and The Challenge


We are seeking an exceptional, highly proactive Senior Azure DevOps Engineer who acts as an elite technical partner. You will inject momentum into complex enterprise environments, eliminate operational inertia from day 1, and lead the technical delivery of resilient Azure cloud infrastructure.

Make no mistake: this is a very hands-on role. We are not looking for a passive ticket-taker or a pure slide-deck architect. We want an execution-focused practitioner who can walk into a tangled, legacy Azure setup, untangle the chaos, and write the code required to fix it. Your ultimate goal is to transition the platform to a state of 1-click automated environments and frictionless, zero-downtime deployments.

Access to AI Tooling: We believe in removing friction so you can focus on solving hard problems. You will have full access to enterprise AI tooling, including Claude Code, to assist you in accelerating infrastructure automation, pipeline refactoring, and code generation.

Key Responsibilities

Thought Leadership and Squad Uplift: Act as an energetic domain leader, energising engineering squads, providing constructive architectural guidance with empathy, and raising cloud engineering standards from day 1.
Hands-on Azure Remediation and Architecture: Take extreme hands-on ownership of existing, complex Azure environments, planning and directly executing remediation strategies to untangle legacy configurations and build highly resilient infrastructure using Azure Kubernetes Service and App Services.
Optimal CI/CD and Deployments: Architect and build elite CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps Pipelines or GitHub Actions to drastically streamline releases, implementing 1-click builds and automated blue-green or canary deployment strategies.
AI-Driven Pipelines and Shift Left: Build dedicated AI components into our CI/CD workflows to aggressively shift left, integrating agentic tooling and platforms to automate infrastructure policy checks, vulnerability scanning, and security validations.
Infrastructure at Scale: Design and manage advanced Azure networking and compute environments, configuring Hub-Spoke network topologies, Azure Firewall, Application Gateways, and ensuring applications scale dynamically based on real-time load.
Infrastructure as Code & Security: Drive platform stability by maintaining automated IaC using Terraform or Azure Bicep while securing the perimeter via Virtual Networks, subnets, User Defined Routes, and strict Key Vault access policies.
What You Bring (Attitude and Mindset)

Consultant Identity: You present as a trusted technical partner who takes complete ownership of business outcomes, eliminates team friction, and drives solutions rather than waiting for top down direction.
High Energy Thought Leader: You bring infectious enthusiasm, strategic vision, and hands on technical leadership to the team, acting as a true champion of cloud best practices.
Proactive Problem Solver: You do not wait to be handed answers. When faced with broad or open ended problems, you pause, clarify boundaries and constraints, and collaborate to map out execution paths.
Master of Constraints: You respect legacy design decisions and regulatory limitations while pragmatically navigating constraints to keep project momentum high.
What You Bring (Technically)

Hands-on Execution Mastery: You are a practitioner who loves to get into the portal and codebase, with a proven history of hunting down answers and directly implementing solutions to fix messy infrastructure.
Azure Mastery: Deep commercial experience architecting and rebuilding enterprise Azure environments, with advanced expertise in Azure Landing Zones, AKS, App Services, auto-scaling, and modern Azure services.
Advanced CI/CD and AI Tooling: Mandatory, extensive experience building frictionless CI/CD pipelines to achieve 1-click environment provisioning, combined with a strong understanding of how to use AI tools like Claude Code to automate operations.
IaC Fluency: Strong commercial track record writing clean, maintainable Infrastructure as Code using Terraform or Azure Bicep.
Production Operations & Testing: Deep commitment to infrastructure observability, structured logging, correlation IDs, and robust testing disciplines across deployment lifecycles.
What We Offer

The unique opportunity to be a foundational member of a brand-new, elite Azure DevOps squad solving complex scaling challenges.
Deeply hands-on infrastructure work across enterprise-grade platforms using cutting-edge tech.
Clear growth pathways from Senior to Staff and beyond.
A low-ceremony, delivery-focused environment that values engineers who own outcomes.
Competitive remuneration and benefits.
