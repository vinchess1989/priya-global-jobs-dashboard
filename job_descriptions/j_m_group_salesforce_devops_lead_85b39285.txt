Seeking an experienced Salesforce DevOps Lead to manage CI/CD pipelines, deployment automation, environment management, and release governance for enterprise Salesforce programs.
Strong expertise in Salesforce DX, Salesforce CLI, Git, Metadata API, Apex, LWC, Salesforce security/configuration, and branching, source-control, and release strategies.
Hands-on experience with Copado, Gearset, AutoRABIT, Flosum, or similar tools, along with Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or GitLab CI/CD; automate builds, testing, validation, quality gates, and deployments.
Lead production releases, rollback planning, metadata dependency management, troubleshooting, and post-deployment validation while mentoring teams and improving DevOps maturity across large-scale, multi-team Salesforce environments.
