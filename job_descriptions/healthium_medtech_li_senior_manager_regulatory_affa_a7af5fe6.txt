To lead and execute regulatory affairs activities for assigned Healthium Medtech businesses, products and facilities, ensuring regulatory compliance throughout the product lifecycle and supporting timely market access in India and international markets. The role will provide regulatory direction to cross-functional teams, manage regulatory submissions and approvals, maintain licences and certifications, and strengthen regulatory systems, governance and inspection readiness across the organisation. The role will also provide dedicated regulatory leadership and execution support to the Orthopaedic Division, with responsibility for regulatory planning and lifecycle management of its product portfolio, including arthroscopic and sports-medicine devices, instruments, implants, accessories and associated systems, as applicable.




Key Responsibilities




Regulatory Strategy and Market Access

Develop and implement regulatory strategies, plans and submission pathways for new products, product changes, manufacturing sites and market expansion projects.
Assess applicable regulatory requirements and advise business and project teams on product classification, registration routes, technical documentation, evidence requirements and approval timelines.
Lead and coordinate product registrations, amendments, renewals, notifications and establishment / manufacturing site registrations in India and assigned international markets.
Support regulatory due diligence and integration activities for acquisitions, business transfers, new facilities and changes in legal-entity or manufacturing arrangements.
Monitor regulatory intelligence, emerging guidance and changes in applicable laws and standards; evaluate their impact and drive timely implementation.




Regulatory Compliance and Licence Management

Oversee regulatory compliance across assigned HML facilities and legal entities, ensuring that applicable licences, registrations, product approvals and certificates remain valid and conditions are fulfilled.
Coordinate with State and Central Licensing Authorities, CDSCO and other relevant regulatory authorities for applications, clarifications, inspections, permissions and regulatory correspondence.
Maintain a robust system for tracking regulatory commitments, licence conditions, renewals, variations, regulatory fees and submission deadlines.
Review regulatory impact of changes to products, intended purpose, design, materials, manufacturing processes, suppliers, labelling, packaging and sites before implementation.
Provide regulatory guidance for domestic and export documentation, including certificates, declarations, free sale / marketability documentation and customer or tender requirements, as applicable.




International Regulatory Affairs

Lead or coordinate regulatory submissions and lifecycle maintenance for assigned markets, including applicable requirements of the US FDA, EU MDR / IVDR where relevant, and other national regulatory authorities.
Coordinate with Notified Bodies, certification bodies, local representatives, authorised representatives, importers and in-country consultants to progress registrations and maintain market access.
Ensure regulatory dossiers and supporting evidence are complete, consistent, technically justified and submitted within agreed timelines.
Support regulatory responses, deficiency / query resolution, technical meetings and follow-up actions with authorities and external conformity assessment organisations.
Maintain oversight of country-wise regulatory status, product approvals, registration commitments and renewal requirements.




Orthopaedic Division – Regulatory Affairs Support

Act as the Regulatory Affairs point of coordination for the Orthopaedic Division, working closely with business, R&D, product development, clinical / medical, quality and manufacturing teams to define and execute regulatory plans for the division’s existing and new product portfolios.
Lead and coordinate regulatory strategy, classification, registrations, submissions, amendments, renewals and lifecycle maintenance for Orthopaedic products in India and assigned international markets.
Provide regulatory oversight for arthroscopic and sports-medicine product portfolios, including (where applicable) arthroscopy instruments, fixation and repair devices, implants, anchors, sutures, shavers, fluid-management products, powered equipment, accessories and combination / procedure kits.
Demonstrate the ability to assess product-specific regulatory requirements and evidence expectations for arthroscopic devices, including intended purpose, risk classification, technical documentation, design and performance evidence, biological safety, sterilisation, shelf life, clinical evaluation and post-market obligations, as applicable to each product.
Partner with R&D and product development teams on design changes, new product introduction, product upgrades, line extensions and transfer of products between manufacturing sites, ensuring timely regulatory impact assessments and approvals before implementation.
Coordinate regulatory responses and deficiency management for Orthopaedic products, including preparation and review of technical documentation, clinical / performance evidence and responses to regulatory authorities and Notified Bodies.
Support regulatory readiness for new technology platforms, complex product launches and strategic Orthopaedic projects, ensuring regulatory deliverables, dependencies, timelines and risks are clearly identified and tracked.
Maintain oversight of Orthopaedic product registrations, country-wise approval status, licence conditions, renewals, regulatory commitments and market-specific restrictions.
Provide regulatory inputs for product labelling, Instructions for Use, surgical technique guides and customer-facing product information, ensuring consistency with approved intended purpose and market authorisations.




Quality Management Systems, Audits and Regulatory Inspections

Work closely with Quality Assurance to ensure that regulatory requirements are appropriately embedded in the Quality Management System and implemented across applicable facilities.
Support compliance with relevant requirements and standards, including ISO 13485, applicable ISO 9000-series requirements, ISO 14971, applicable Good Manufacturing Practices and market-specific regulatory requirements.
Coordinate regulatory preparation for external audits, certification audits, surveillance / recertification audits and regulatory inspections; support audit responses and timely closure of observations.
Provide regulatory inputs to procedures, work instructions, technical documentation, change control, CAPA, post-market processes, labelling and product release activities.
Where formally appointed and legally eligible, discharge applicable responsibilities of the Person Responsible for Regulatory Compliance (PRRC) in accordance with EU MDR Article 15 and the defined appointment.




Product Lifecycle and Cross-Functional Support

Provide regulatory inputs from concept and product development through design transfer, launch, post-market surveillance and product discontinuation.
Partner with R&D, Product Development, Clinical / Medical, QA, QC, Manufacturing and Marketing to ensure regulatory deliverables are built into project plans and stage-gate reviews.
Review and guide regulatory aspects of product claims, intended purpose, classification, labelling, Instructions for Use, e-IFU and promotional / customer-facing regulatory statements.
Support complaint, vigilance, field safety corrective action, recall and post-market surveillance activities by providing regulatory assessment and reporting guidance.
Provide regulatory documentation and technical support to institutional, domestic, export and OBL / private-label customers, within approved business and regulatory commitments.




Team Leadership, Governance and Capability Building

Lead, coach and develop the assigned Regulatory Affairs team; allocate responsibilities, review performance and build technical capability and succession depth.
Establish clear priorities, work plans, submission schedules and escalation mechanisms aligned with business and regulatory objectives.
Conduct induction and role-specific regulatory training and promote awareness of regulatory obligations across relevant functions.
Drive process standardisation, documentation discipline, knowledge management and continuous improvement across the regulatory function.
Prepare periodic management reports on registration status, compliance risks, audit readiness, project progress, resource needs, budgets and key performance indicators.




Decision-Making and Accountability

Provide regulatory recommendations on product approvals, market access, regulatory pathways and compliance risks within the delegated authority.
Escalate significant compliance gaps, potential regulatory breaches, product safety concerns and risks to market authorisation to the General Manager – Regulatory Affairs and relevant leadership.
Ensure regulatory submissions and external commitments are reviewed for accuracy, completeness and consistency before approval and submission, in accordance with the document-control and delegation-of-authority process.
Coordinate cross-functional actions but do not independently approve quality, design, manufacturing or commercial decisions outside the assigned authority.




Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

On-time completion and submission of regulatory applications, renewals, variations and regulatory commitments against approved plans.
Achievement of planned product registrations, establishment registrations and market-access milestones.
Validity and timely renewal of applicable licences, registrations and certificates across assigned facilities / markets.
Regulatory audit and inspection readiness; timely and effective closure of regulatory observations and actions assigned to Regulatory Affairs.
Quality, accuracy and right-first-time acceptance of regulatory dossiers and responses, including effective management of authority queries.
Timely regulatory impact assessment and communication of changes to products, processes, sites and applicable requirements.
Effective management, development and retention of the assigned team; completion of training and capability-building plans.
Adherence to approved regulatory budgets, external service-provider timelines and agreed project deliverables.




Qualifications

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Pharmacy, Life Sciences, Biomedical Engineering, Biotechnology, Chemistry or a related scientific / technical discipline.
A postgraduate qualification in Regulatory Affairs, Quality Management, Medical Technology or Business Administration will be an added advantage.




Experience

Typically 10–15 years of relevant experience in Regulatory Affairs within the medical device, pharmaceutical or other regulated healthcare industry, with substantial experience in medical devices preferred.
Demonstrated experience in managing medical device product registrations, regulatory submissions, licence maintenance and regulatory authority interactions in India and international markets.
Practical working knowledge of US FDA medical device requirements, EU MDR, ISO 13485 and applicable national regulatory frameworks; experience with additional international markets is desirable.
Experience in leading cross-functional regulatory projects, supporting audits / inspections and managing a team or significant workstream independently.
Experience with multiple manufacturing sites, diverse product portfolios, acquisitions / business transitions or global market expansion will be an advantage.




Functional and Technical Competencies

Strong understanding of medical device regulatory frameworks, product classification, conformity assessment, technical documentation and lifecycle requirements.
Working knowledge of relevant standards and guidance, including ISO 13485, ISO 14971, applicable sterilisation / biocompatibility / performance standards, and market-specific requirements.
Understanding of design and development controls, risk management, clinical evaluation / performance evidence, post-market surveillance, vigilance and change management.
Ability to interpret regulations and guidance and translate requirements into practical business and operational actions.
Strong regulatory writing, dossier review, technical documentation, data analysis and record-management skills.
Proficiency in regulatory project planning, submission tracking, audit preparation, stakeholder management and presentation.




Leadership and Behavioral Competencies

Strategic thinking and sound regulatory judgement.
People leadership, coaching, delegation and performance management.
Planning, prioritisation and execution discipline.
Clear written and verbal communication, negotiation and stakeholder management.
Problem-solving, risk-based decision-making and attention to detail.
Customer focus, business orientation, cost consciousness, integrity and accountability.
Ability to work effectively across functions, facilities, cultures and geographies.




Working Relationships

The position will work closely with internal stakeholders across Regulatory Affairs, Quality, R&D / Product Development, Manufacturing, Marketing, Supply Chain, Clinical / Medical, Finance and Legal, and externally with regulatory authorities, Notified Bodies, certification bodies, auditors, consultants, distributors and customers.
