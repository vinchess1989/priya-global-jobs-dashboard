For more than two decades, Beamtree has helped solve healthcare challenges globally by implementing technology in more than 1,000 locations around the world. We believe it starts with better data and ends with better care. Through automation, analytics and AI-driven solutions, we empower healthcare organisations to turn data into insights and action.

Our focus is on high-quality healthcare data that supports clinical and operational decision-making, guides best practice and improves performance and efficiency. We provide solutions specialising in coding automation and workflows, auditing, revenue assurance, clinical decision support, advisory services and benchmarking.

About the Role

We are seeking a Business Analyst to join our Coding Products team. Reporting to the Delivery Manager of Coding and working within a cross-functional Agile squad, you will collaborate closely with Product Owners, Feature Leads, Delivery Managers, Developers and Quality Assurance specialists to support the delivery of Beamtree's coding solutions, including the Integrated Coding Platform (ICP), PICQ® and RISQ.

This is an excellent opportunity for a Business Analyst who enjoys working with product requirements, integrations, data flows and Agile delivery practices while contributing to solutions that improve healthcare outcomes.

What You'll Do


Elicit, analyse and document business, functional and technical requirements
Facilitate workshops and discussions with clients, users and internal stakeholders to understand business needs and translate them into clear requirements
Create and maintain user stories, acceptance criteria, process maps and supporting documentation in Jira and Confluence
Support Product Owners and Feature Leads in refining backlog items and preparing work for development teams
Assist with sprint planning, backlog refinement, stand-ups and other Agile ceremonies
Work with technical teams to analyse integrations, APIs, data exchanges and system interfaces
Support solution design discussions and help ensure requirements are clearly understood by development and testing teams
Coordinate and support User Acceptance Testing (UAT), defect triage and release readiness activities
Assist in validating delivered functionality against agreed business requirements and acceptance criteria
Support client demonstrations, training activities and release communications where required
Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives across product delivery processes
Collaborate with Product and Design teams to support UI/UX activities and ensure solutions meet user needs


About You

The successful candidate will bring:


Approximately 3+ years' experience as a Business Analyst or in a related product delivery, systems analysis or software delivery role
Experience gathering, analysing and documenting business and system requirements
Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to engage technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Experience working in Agile environments and familiarity with Scrum or Kanban methodologies
Proficiency with Jira, Confluence or similar delivery management tools
Experience supporting testing activities, including UAT planning and execution
An understanding of integrations, APIs, data flows or system interfaces
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with attention to detail
A collaborative mindset and willingness to work within cross-functional product squads
Relevant tertiary qualifications or equivalent practical experience


Successful candidates may require satisfactory completion of a general background check, reference check and/or criminal history check.

If you're interested in building innovative products, working with modern Agile delivery practices and helping improve healthcare data and outcomes, we'd love to hear from you. Apply now.
