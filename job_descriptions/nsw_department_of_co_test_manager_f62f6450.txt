Your role

Lead test management and quality engineering for complex digital, technology and business change initiatives across the delivery lifecycle. The role establishes proportionate, risk-based test strategies, governance, plans and controls; directs function, integration, non-functional, regression and user acceptance testing; provides independent, evidence-based advice on solution quality and implementation readiness; and develops testing capability, automation and continuous improvement to support reliable services and customer outcomes.

What You’ll Do


Develop risk-based test strategies, governance, plans and controls aligned to the organisational test framework.
Lead system, integration, end-to-end, regression, non-functional and user acceptance testing as appropriate.
Manage test scope, schedules, resources, environments, data, tools and entry and exit criteria.
Direct defect triage, coverage analysis, quality gates, retesting and residual risk assessment.
Provide independent quality reporting and clear recommendations on release readiness and go-live risk.
Manage the development of test automation suite.
Adopt reusable testing assets across delivery teams.
Manage test suppliers, multidisciplinary teams and specialist assurance partners (e.g. for performance testing and penetration testing).
Coach practitioners and uplift testing standards, capability, tooling and continuous improvement.


What We’re Looking For


Expert knowledge of risk-based test management, integration, regression, user acceptance and non-functional testing.
Experience managing environments, test data, defects, automation and quality metrics.
Confidence providing independent assurance and challenging release readiness under pressure.
Demonstrated success coordinating business testers, technical teams, external maintenance contractors, vendors and senior stakeholders.


Essential Requirements


Tertiary qualifications in information technology, computer science, engineering, business or a related discipline, or equivalent relevant knowledge, skills and experience. ISTQB Certified Tester Advanced Level Test Management, or an equivalent recognised advanced testing qualification, is desirable.
The role may be required to support planned test cycles, implementation-readiness activities or releases outside normal business hours.


Desirable Requirements


Experience with Salesforce, MuleSoft or comparable enterprise integration platforms.
Experience with CI/CD, automated testing and quality engineering practices.
ISTQB Advanced Level Test Management or equivalent recognised certification.
