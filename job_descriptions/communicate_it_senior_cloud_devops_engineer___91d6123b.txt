If you're the type of engineer who gets excited about automation, scalable cloud architecture, eliminating manual processes, and building platforms that can support real-world impact at scale, this opportunity is worth exploring.
 
My client is looking for a Senior Cloud DevOps Engineer to join a growing technology business where you'll have significant influence over cloud infrastructure, deployment strategy, security, reliability, and performance. You'll work on modern cloud technologies, collaborate directly with developers and data teams, and play a key role in shaping the future of a rapidly evolving SaaS platform.
 
This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys ownership, values engineering best practices, and wants to see the direct impact of their work.
Duties & Responsibilities

Job Description:
 
As the Senior Cloud DevOps Engineer, you will be responsible for designing, building, maintaining, and optimising cloud infrastructure while ensuring security, scalability, uptime, and operational excellence.
 
Responsibilities include:

Designing and managing scalable AWS infrastructure
Building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines
Managing containerised environments using Docker and Kubernetes
Deploying and supporting cloud-hosted applications
Implementing Infrastructure as Code solutions
Automating deployments and operational processes
Establishing monitoring, logging, and alerting capabilities
Driving cloud security best practices and compliance initiatives
Managing backup, recovery, and disaster recovery processes
Troubleshooting production issues and improving system reliability
Optimising cloud performance, scalability, and cost efficiency
Collaborating closely with development teams to streamline releases and improve platform stability
Maintaining infrastructure documentation and operational standards

Skills & Experience:

Strong commercial AWS experience
Experience working with services such as EC2, ECS, RDS, S3, CloudFront, and Lambda
Advanced Docker and Kubernetes experience
CI/CD experience using GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar tools
Experience with Infrastructure as Code tools such as Terraform, CloudFormation, or Ansible
Experience with monitoring and observability tools including Prometheus, Grafana, and CloudWatch
Knowledge of PostgreSQL and exposure to NoSQL technologies
Strong scripting skills using Python, Bash, Ruby, or similar languages
Solid understanding of networking, security, and cloud architecture
Experience supporting highly available and scalable production environments
Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities
Excellent communication skills and ability to work across technical teams

Qualification:

Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Mathematics, or a related field preferred
Relevant AWS certifications highly advantageous
