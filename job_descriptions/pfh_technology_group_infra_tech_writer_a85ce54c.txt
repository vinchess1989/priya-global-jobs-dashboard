We are seeking an Technical writer who understands infrastructure with a strong understanding of enterprise IT infrastructure and operations. The successful candidate will work directly with technical SMEs, architects and operational teams to develop high-quality technical documentation, including runbooks, design documents, policies, standards, procedures and Disaster Recovery documentation.

The role requires sufficient technical knowledge to understand complex infrastructure environments, challenge incomplete information and independently translate SME knowledge into clear, structured and executable documentation. Strong knowledge of Infrastructure, Cloud, IT Operations and Disaster Recovery concepts is highly desirable.

Documentation will include:

Technical runbooks and operational procedures
High-Level Designs (HLDs)
Low-Level Designs (LLDs)
Build and configuration standards
IT policies and standards
Disaster Recovery and recovery procedures
Service and system documentation
Operational checklists
Technical control and governance documentation
