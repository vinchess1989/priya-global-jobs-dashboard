DEVOPS ENGINEER - AWS PLATFORM & INFRASTRUCTURE
Tumaini Consulting
I'm interested

Our client is seeking a hands-on DevOps Engineer with strong AWS infrastructure and platform engineering experience. This role will focus on supporting and evolving AWS-based infrastructure, modernising Infrastructure as Code, and enabling reliable CI/CD practices across a broader Loyalty and Rewards environment.

 

Requirements

Proven hands-on experience working with AWS infrastructure.
Strong Infrastructure as Code experience using CloudFormation and/or Terraform.
Solid understanding of CI/CD principles and practical pipeline experience.
Experience with infrastructure automation and platform support.
Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting capabilities.
A proactive learning mindset with the ability to adapt to changing technical requirements.
Azure DevOps experience would be advantageous.
Exposure to DataOps or data platforms would be advantageous.

 

If you would like to apply for this position, please send transcripts with applications.

Should you meet the requirements for this position, please email your updated CV attached to it.jobs@tumaini.co.za,  alternatively contact 031 350 4018 or visit our website, www.tumaini.co Correspondence will only be conducted with short listed candidates. Should you not hear from us within 3 days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
