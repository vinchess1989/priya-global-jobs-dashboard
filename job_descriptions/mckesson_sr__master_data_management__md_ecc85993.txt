About The Role

McKesson is seeking a Senior MDM Governance Policy & Knowledge Enablement Specialist to strengthen the policies, documentation, knowledge-management, training, and adoption capabilities that support Enterprise Master Data and MDM operations. You will help ensure governance policies, standards, SOPs, work instructions, decision records, and learning content are centrally managed, current, accessible, and aligned with enterprise data and compliance requirements.

This role partners with Data Owners, Data Stewards, MDM Operations, business stakeholders, subject matter experts, Compliance, Technology, and project teams. You will serve as a trusted advisor on governance policy, content strategy, knowledge management, training, and user adoption while supporting ERP modernization, analytics, automation, AI, and data-transformation initiatives.

What You'll Do


Manage the lifecycle of MDM policies, standards, SOPs, work instructions, and governance artifacts, including ownership, version control, review, approval, publication, retention, and retirement.
Conduct documentation gap assessments and coordinate updates that address missing, outdated, duplicative, or inconsistent content.
Lead development and governance of the Enterprise MDM Knowledge Hub and related collaboration environments, using consistent taxonomy, templates, metadata, and ownership models.
Design role-based training, onboarding materials, job aids, guides, communications, and self-service learning resources that strengthen governance adoption.
Measure training effectiveness and adoption, gather stakeholder feedback, and continuously improve content and learning experiences.
Partner across Governance, MDM Operations, Compliance, Technology, and business teams to improve documentation, controls, messaging, and process understanding.
Support governance councils and strategic initiatives, including ERP modernization, analytics, automation, AI, and data transformation.
Identify opportunities to improve policy controls, content workflows, document management, and AI-enabled knowledge-management capabilities.



Basic Requirements


7+ years of relevant experience in Data Governance, Master Data Management, policy management, knowledge management, documentation, training development, instructional design, organizational enablement, or related disciplines.
Bachelor's degree in Business, Information Management, Knowledge Management, Communications, Education, Instructional Design, Data Analytics, Information Systems, or a related discipline.
Experience developing and maintaining governance policies, standards, SOPs, work instructions, process documentation, training materials, or related governance artifacts.
Experience establishing document lifecycle practices, content standards, taxonomies, templates, ownership models, or knowledge repositories.
Experience partnering with business stakeholders, subject matter experts, Compliance, project teams, and technology teams.
Strong written communication, facilitation, stakeholder engagement, and content-development skills.



Preferred Skills/Experience


Strong understanding of Master Data Management, data quality, data governance, and regulatory compliance.
Experience with Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Teams, policy lifecycle tools, document-management systems, or knowledge platforms.
Experience supporting SAP, SAP S/4HANA, ERP modernization, enterprise transformation, analytics, automation, or AI-enabled knowledge-management initiatives.
Knowledge of change management, instructional design, information architecture, and adoption-measurement practices.
CDMP, change management, instructional design, knowledge management, or a related certification.



Work Environment, Travel & Physical Requirements


Hybrid / traditional office environment.
Approximately 10% travel.
A large percentage of time performing computer-based work is required.
