Labcorp is seeking a remote Tool Governance & Document Control Lead to join our Central Labs team in Singapore.

Work Schedule

Monday - Friday; standard hours

Job Responsibilities

Tools Governance


Manage, own and maintain library of all new and existing tools
Evaluate how tools are being used by end users (incl. clients)
Usage and performance monitoring incl. corrective actions
Feedback collection and analysis, decommission of redundant or obsolete tools
Maintenance and integration support, user support and non-technical troubleshooting
Collaborate with Strategy and Transformation group to stay current with tools and technologies, act as change agent, and serve as the point of contact. Coordinate the testing of new tools to drive innovation and improve efficiency.
Establish governance standards for GPM tools.
Approve introduction of new tools and retirement of existing tools.
Maintain tool ownership matrix and accountable stakeholders.
Lead governance reviews and compliance assessments.
Define tool usage standards and best practices.
Develop and monitor governance KPIs.


Document Controller


Manage the lifecycle of controlled documents from creation through review, approval, implementation, revision, and retirement.
Ensure all SOPs, policies, methods, forms, templates, specifications, and procedures are current.
Coordinate periodic document reviews with document owners.
Ensure documenta on complies with internal quality standards and regulatory requirements.
Monitor document review and approval timelines.
Verify document completeness, formatting, and compliance prior to issuance.
Support internal and external audits and inspections (as needed)
Maintain inspection readiness at all times.
Manage controlled records and retention schedules.
Coordinate document archiving ac vi es according to retention requirements.
Create and maintain document performance KPI


Minimum Qualifications


Bachelor's degree in Biology, Business, or Computer Science
3 or more years of experience in governance, quality systems, document control, project management operations, or business process management
2 or more years of experience managing enterprise systems or controlled documentation.


Preferred Qualifications


PMP, Lean Six Sigma, Quality Certification, or Change Management Certification
2 or more years of Labcorp Global Project Management experience with knowledge of capabilities and expertise of the Labcorp organization or equivalent companies
2 or more years of experience with document management systems
2 or more years of experience with governance frameworks and risk management.


Additional Job Standards


Flexibility in schedule to coincide with global operations and meetings
Ability to manage multiple priorities.
Strong relationship management and collaboration skills
Understanding of risk assessment and mitigation practices
Ability to support audit preparation and maintain audit readiness
Strong data analysis and problem-solving skills
Ability to map, analyze, and optimize business processes
