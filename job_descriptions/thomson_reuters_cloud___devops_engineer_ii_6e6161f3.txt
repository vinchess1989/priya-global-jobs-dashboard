Job Description

Job Description

Are you excited about applying machine learning to real-world software problems in Legal, Tax, Compliance, and News? Thomson Reuters Labs is seeking a Cloud & DevOps Engineer II who enjoys solving problems in collaboration with a global team.

We experiment, we build, we deliver. TR Labs supports the organization and our customers through applied research and development of new product features and internal developer tools. We innovate across Legal, Tax & Accounting, Government, and Reuters News, and we invest in long-term research that can shape the future of the industry.

As a Cloud & DevOps Engineer II, you will join the Cloud and DevOps team, a central platform function serving engineering and research teams across Labs. Our mission is to enable everyone to deliver better software, faster.

Key Responsibilities

As a Cloud & DevOps Engineer II, You Will


Build and operate: Design, deploy, and maintain AWS infrastructure using infrastructure as code and CI/CD automation, with reproducible deployments and well-managed software delivery in support of Labs as a Research and Development function.
Deliver with ownership: Take ownership of your work from design through ongoing support. In a fast-paced, dynamic environment, turn complex platform problems into right-sized solutions with clear scope, and deliver them on time.
Cloud operations and support: Handle day-to-day cloud operations and support for engineering and research teams, including monitoring, debugging, root-cause analysis, and issue resolution, and document what you learn.
Partner with research: Work closely with research scientists and research engineers to turn their experiments into reliable, secure, scalable cloud solutions, including AI/ML workloads.
Enable developers: Build internal developer tools, automation, and documentation that make teams more productive.
Secure and optimize: Apply AWS security best practices, including least-privilege IAM and compliance requirements. Run security scanning with tools such as Snyk, and identify opportunities to reduce cloud cost through FinOps practices.
Innovate and grow: Try new approaches, learn new technologies, and iterate based on feedback. Communicate your work clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.


Required Qualifications


Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience; a Master's degree is a plus
3–5 years of hands-on experience using AWS to solve platform engineering and software delivery problems
Practical experience with AWS services including ECR, ECS, Lambda, EC2, S3, RDS, VPC, IAM, SageMaker, and Bedrock
Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines with GitHub Actions and AWS CodeBuild
Proficiency with infrastructure as code using AWS CDK and CloudFormation
Strong Python skills for automation, scripting, and building AWS Lambda functions, plus Bash scripting
Understanding of AWS security fundamentals, including least-privilege IAM and network design
Ability to break down ambiguous problems into manageable pieces and deliver iteratively
Strong critical thinking and debugging skills: you can troubleshoot across code, infrastructure, networking, and permissions to find root causes and fix them
Comfort working in a fast-paced environment with complex problems and time-managed delivery
A strong sense of ownership: you take responsibility for outcomes, follow through without close direction, and raise risks early
Strong communication and collaboration skills, including working with research scientists and engineers


Preferred Qualifications


AWS certifications such as Solutions Architect or DevOps Engineer
Experience with monitoring and logging tools such as Datadog and AWS CloudWatch
Knowledge of cloud security and code security practices, including security scanning tools such as Snyk
Demonstrated experience optimizing cloud resource utilization and cost
Understanding of machine learning concepts and workflows
Familiarity with container orchestration tools such as Kubernetes
Experience using AI-assisted development tools such as Claude Code or Codex
