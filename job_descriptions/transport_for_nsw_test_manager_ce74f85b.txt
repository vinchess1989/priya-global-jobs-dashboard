If you’re after impact, there’s no other organisation in Australia that delivers work on this scale.

Your innovative mindset is key to creating solutions to complex problems. Our systems drive our organisational success and underpin every journey our customers take. This is your opportunity to be part of an expert team who build and refine seamless technology experiences.

In this role, you'll:

Working as part of a team delivering quality assurance and testing activities for project data and analytics solutions, ensuring solutions meet required specifications for quality, function, reliability and ongoing improvement across the solution lifecycle.

This role will see you oversee and manage quality assurance processes; ensure critical quality control checkpoints occur at the right lifecycle stages; confirm testing strategies align with business requirements; validate that new and improved solutions, configurations and interfaces perform as specified; manage the development of test scripts across all testing phases; promote automation and best‑practice QA tools; review detailed test reports; and provide quality assurance, validation and feedback on stakeholder‑reported project information, with development opportunities in strategic QA leadership, automation uplift, risk management, technical communication and cross‑functional delivery.

You will lead new ways of improving testing effectiveness, reducing risk and strengthening quality assurance compliance in a complex environment balancing competing demands, intangible QA benefits, tight timeframes, and the need to ensure effective, defensible testing outcomes across diverse stakeholders.

For more information on this position and business unit, view the role description and information pack.

About You

Your strong analytical ability, integration and problem‑solving skills, time‑management strength, and capacity to analyse and explain complex technical information to non‑technical audiences formed across quality assurance, testing, data, technical and multi‑project delivery environments will see you excel in overcoming challenges such as balancing stakeholder requirements with compliance, maintaining support for QA activities and delivering results under time, cost and quality pressures, and bring structured QA governance, clear communication and robust assurance processes to lead to higher‑quality, more reliable project data and analytics solutions.

You’re recognized as an adept communicator and collaborative QA leader, skilled at explaining complex concepts, guiding testing activities, reviewing detailed test outcomes and promoting enhanced analysis tools, experienced in multi‑phase testing, QA oversight, stakeholder engagement, and managing risks, issues and solution readiness across the project lifecycle.
