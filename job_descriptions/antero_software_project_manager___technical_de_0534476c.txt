Position Description

The Project Manager / Technical Delivery Manager is a key role at Antero, where we build SaaS products and deliver bespoke software for our clients. In this role you will own the delivery of client projects from kick-off through to release, working between our engineering team and our clients to make sure work is scoped well, planned realistically and delivered to a high standard.

You will be the person our clients and our team rely on to know where a project stands, what is at risk and what happens next. You will need to be comfortable keeping clients on task, holding the line on agreed scope and having direct conversations when requests fall outside it. Clear communication, sound judgement and a calm approach under pressure are critical to success in this role.

You will be expected to apply your range of talents wherever necessary across the following areas:


planning and running Agile delivery across multiple concurrent client projects and retainers, including sprint planning, stand-ups, reviews and retrospectives
working closely with our financial services clients on Incept, our loan origination and customer onboarding platform, to understand their lending workflows and translate them into clear requirements for the team
working with clients to scope projects, define requirements and agree priorities, then breaking that work down into estimated, deliverable tickets for the team
keeping clients focused on agreed priorities and actively managing scope creep, including pushing back constructively and running a clear change request process when new work comes up
managing timelines, budgets and resourcing, and raising risks and issues early
configuring and maintaining JIRA boards, and keeping delivery reporting accurate and up to date
running client meetings and status updates, and being the main point of contact for delivery questions
coordinating developers, designers, QA and DevOps so that work flows smoothly through dev, UAT and production environments
owning release planning and go-live activities, including UAT sign-off and handover to support
supporting our security and compliance practices (we hold ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type 2 certification), including change management and delivery documentation
being on hand to assist any other areas of the business as might be required


Ideally You Will Also Have


experience in financial services, particularly lending (strongly preferred). You do not need to be a credit expert, but time spent in a lender, broker or finance business, and an understanding of how loan applications move from enquiry through assessment, approval and settlement, will set you apart
experience delivering software in other regulated industries such as health or aged care
experience with cloud service providers (eg. AWS, Azure, GCP)


What else can I expect from this role?

As a Project Manager / Technical Delivery Manager at Antero, a large part of your focus will be Incept, our loan origination and customer onboarding platform for financial services. Understanding how our clients assess, approve and manage loans is key to delivering well for them, so a background in finance or lending will help you hit the ground running. You will also be across other products and client engagements, so you will need to be comfortable prioritising competing demands and switching context. You do not need to be a developer, but you will need enough technical understanding to have credible conversations with engineers and to translate technical detail into plain language for clients (familiarity with web applications, APIs and cloud hosting is a plus).

You will most likely have progressed from a Product Owner, Project Coordinator, BA, QA or Developer role, or already be working as a Project or Delivery Manager in a software or digital agency environment. You will use this experience to keep projects on track, keep clients informed and confident, and help our team do their best work.

How do I know if I qualify for this role?

Capability in this role will be measured via the below activities:


a demonstration of your experience leading software projects from scoping through to release
you will be asked to review a project brief and produce a high level delivery plan, including milestones, risks and assumptions
you will review a simple JIRA project board and complete a planning exercise by grouping items into epics for a release schedule
you will work through a scenario where a project is behind schedule and explain how you would communicate and manage it with the client


We are constantly looking to find new and better ways of working. Having ideas and experiences of both good and bad delivery methods are great talking points during our discussions. As you can imagine, we want to hear solutions, not just problems.

A Little About Us


we are an established business with SaaS products and professional services
we have a mixed client base, from niche tech product-focused clients to educational providers. Essentially, we work with anyone that has an interesting project
we have a development team alongside a highly talented creative team, keeping the work visually impressive and of incredible quality
you’ll be surrounded by a team of highly capable technologists with ample opportunity for on the job training


If this sounds like your kind of place the next steps for you to follow are:


email a copy of your CV to careers@antero.com.au
if we see a possible alignment, within a few days we will contact you to organise a quick 15 minute call to assess culture fit
following the culture fit, you will receive a short practical exercise which helps us to better understand your experience and approach to delivery
for those who successfully complete the exercise you will receive an interview where we can discuss your results and where you might fit within our team
