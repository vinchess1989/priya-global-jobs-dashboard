Mō te tūranga | About the Role

As a Senior Platform Engineer, you will design, build, and operate secure, scalable, and resilient platform solutions that enable product teams to deliver high-quality applications efficiently and reliably. Leveraging your expertise in cloud technologies, automation, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), and modern engineering practices, you will create and evolve platform capabilities that enhance developer productivity, improve operational excellence, and support the delivery of customer-focused solutions.

In this role, you will be responsible for developing and maintaining platform services that are secure, highly available, performant, and cost-effective. You will design, build, and continuously improve infrastructure and platform components using Infrastructure as Code and CI/CD practices, with a strong focus on reliability, scalability, and operational resilience.

Working closely with engineering and development teams, you will drive automation across provisioning, deployment, monitoring, and operational processes, while promoting Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) principles to improve platform stability and service performance. You will also play a key role in troubleshooting complex technical issues, optimising platform performance, and enhancing the overall developer experience.

As a senior member of the team, you will contribute to engineering standards, mentor other engineers, evaluate emerging technologies, and identify opportunities to improve platform capabilities, operational efficiency, and organisational resilience through continuous improvement and innovation.

Nā tāu rourou | What will you bring?


Experience in high-scale environments and SRE practices.
Experience building automated CI/CD Pipelines using Jenkins.
Strong Linux administration skills.
The ability to implement Infrastructure as Code (IaC) including knowledge of appropriate technologies for configuration management (eg. Templating, Terraform, Chef, Puppet, Ansible) is essential.
Hands on experience leveraging monitoring and observability tools such as Elastic, Splunk, Prometheus, Dynatrace (or similar).
Certifications in cloud technologies is an advantage.
Knowledge of security engineering practices and tools is necessary, implementing compliance and security in an automated way.
Knowledge in Agentic AI is an advantage.
Ability to diagnose and solve technical issues.
Excellent problem-solving and communication skills.
Ability to work in cross-functional teams and lead technical initiatives.
Ability to review and recommend new deployment techniques and vendor products and make recommendations on the acquisition of advance technology toolsets.
Solid understanding of networking, security, and distributed systems.
