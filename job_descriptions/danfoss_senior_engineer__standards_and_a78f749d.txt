The Impact You'll Make

As our Senior Engineer - Product Compliance & Standards, you will play a key role in ensuring that our products, engineering processes and technical documentation meet applicable regulatory, certification and Danfoss requirements throughout the product lifecycle.

Your impact? Compliance requirements will be identified early and implemented consistently, while the development of relevant standards will be monitored and influenced through the DCS Standards & Compliance organization. Certifications and assisting documentation will remain current, reducing compliance risk, delays and rework for Engineering and the wider business.

You will collaborate with Engineering, New Product Development, Product Compliance, Quality, Procurement and global experts, while taking ownership of standards coordination, compliance projects, certification support, competence development and regulatory implementation.

What You’ll Be Doing

Maintain the DCS Standards Matrix in collaboration with subject-matter experts, recommend Segment Monitor ownership where needed, and collaborate assessments of how relevant directives and standards affect PL01 and PL40.
Act as the PL01 and PL40 contact for compliance projects and facilitate product compliance improvements across CTM, ensuring clear ownership, follow-up and timely implementation.
Represent Danfoss India in the BIS MED 03 Sectional Committee, actively participate in committee and panel meetings, and review, comment and vote on amendments to IS, ISO and IEC standards.
Monitor standards developments and, through the DCS Standards & Compliance organization, contribute to influencing relevant standards. Align positions with global experts and convey updates, impacts and recommended actions to Engineering and other stakeholders.
Support certification and surveillance audits with Quality and project teams, convey certificate updates, and ensure effective compliance documentation, competence mapping and training recommendations against internal standards.


What We're Looking For

Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Materials Science or another relevant technical field, combined with experience in product compliance, standards, certification or regulatory engineering in an industrial environment.
Experience interpreting technical standards and translating regulatory requirements into clear engineering actions, processes and documentation.
Knowledge of product development and engineering change processes; experience with areas such as CE, PED, RoHS, REACH, ATEX, IECEx or UL is an advantage.
Strong communication and stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to facilitate cross-functional and cross-cultural activities in written and spoken English.
A structured, collaborative and improvement-oriented mindset, with strong ownership, sound judgement and the ability to turn complex requirements into practical solutions.
