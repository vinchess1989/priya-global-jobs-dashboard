Do you have the expertise to oversee a global QMS, navigate complex regulatory environments, and champion a culture of quality across the organization?

Proclinical is seeking a Quality Manager to lead and oversee the day-to-day operations of the Quality Assurance function. This role involves managing the global quality management system (QMS) across multiple regions, ensuring compliance with regulatory standards, and driving continual improvement. You will play a key leadership role in developing the quality team, maintaining inspection readiness, and ensuring operational excellence.

Responsibilities

Lead, coach, and develop the quality team, ensuring clear accountabilities and fostering professional growth.
Manage workload prioritization across regulatory compliance, QMS maintenance, audits, supplier qualification, and operational demands.
Operate, maintain, and improve the global QMS to meet regulatory, corporate, GDP, GWP, and GCP requirements.
Oversee deviations, non-conformances, investigations, CAPAs, change controls, complaints, and recall processes.
Ensure proper document and records control, including archival, review, and adherence to good documentation practices.
Maintain GDP and GWP compliance across storage, depot, warehouse, procurement, and distribution activities.
Manage supplier qualification, approval, audits, quality agreements, and follow-up activities.
Host sponsor, CRO, and client quality audits, ensuring accurate responses and commitments.
Provide quality leadership for GxP-relevant systems, including validation, change control, and data integrity.
Represent Quality in cross-functional projects, promoting scalable and compliant processes.
Key Skills And Requirements

Strong background in quality assurance within regulated pharmaceutical, biotechnology, clinical trial supply, or medical products environments.
Experience managing QMS processes such as deviations, CAPAs, change controls, complaints, and recalls.
Knowledge of GDP, GWP, and regulatory frameworks for therapeutic goods in Australia.
Proven ability to lead and develop teams, manage workloads, and conduct performance reviews.
Skilled in hosting audits and inspections, with experience in supplier qualification and quality agreements.
Clear written and verbal communication skills suitable for diverse audiences, including sponsors, regulators, and executives.
Practical, risk-based approach to compliance and process improvement.
Desirable: GMP experience, clinical trial supplies expertise, familiarity with regulatory frameworks in New Zealand, Singapore, or Japan, and lead auditor training.
