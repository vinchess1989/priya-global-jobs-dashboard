Responsibilities

CMDB Implementation & Ownership
Lead the design, build and population of the new CMDB, defining the configuration item model, taxonomy, attribute schema and relationship mapping in line with ITIL best practice.
Act as the primary product owner for the CMDB platform, managing configuration, integrations and ongoing development with the tooling vendor and internal teams.
Define and document the Configuration Management process, policy and procedures, ensuring these are approved, communicated and adhered to across IT.
Establish discovery and import mechanisms to automate configuration item population where possible, reducing reliance on manual data entry.
Data Quality & Governance
Own the ongoing accuracy, completeness and currency of CMDB data, establishing audit and reconciliation routines to identify and remediate data quality issues.
Define and report on data quality KPIs and metrics, escalating persistent issues to IT leadership.
Implement configuration item lifecycle management, ensuring items are created, updated and retired in a controlled and timely manner.
Process Integration
Work closely with the Change Manager to ensure all changes affecting configuration items trigger appropriate CMDB updates.
Collaborate with IT Asset Management to align the CMDB with asset lifecycle and financial data.
Support Incident and Problem Management teams in using CMDB data for root cause analysis and impact assessment.
Integrate the CMDB with other ITSM processes and tooling, including the service catalogue and monitoring platforms.
Stakeholder Engagement & Training
Act as the subject matter expert for Configuration Management across IT, providing guidance and support to teams on CMDB use and data ownership.
Deliver training and awareness activity to ensure teams understand their responsibilities for maintaining CMDB accuracy.
Build and manage a network of configuration item data owners and custodians across infrastructure, application and network teams.
Produce regular CMDB health reports and present findings to IT leadership and relevant governance forums.

Essential

Demonstrable experience in a Configuration Management, ITSM or IT Asset Management role.
Proven experience owning or significantly contributing to a CMDB implementation or maturity programme.
Strong understanding of ITIL Configuration Management processes and their integration with Change, Incident and Problem Management.
Experience working with ITSM platforms and CMDB modules, for example ServiceNow, BMC Helix or Ivanti.
Ability to define configuration item models, attribute schemas and relationship maps for complex technology estates.
Strong data analysis skills, comfortable interrogating data and presenting findings clearly.
Excellent stakeholder management skills with the ability to influence without direct authority.

Desirable

ITIL v4 Foundation certification or higher.
Experience with discovery tooling such as ServiceNow Discovery or Lansweeper.
Knowledge of IT Asset Management frameworks and their relationship with the CMDB.
Familiarity with cloud environments and the challenges of configuration item management in hybrid estates.
Experience in a regulated industry environment, particularly where audit and compliance requirements drive data integrity standards.
