Who We're Looking For

We are seeking a Solution Delivery Manager reporting to the Senior Manager, Residential Solution Delivery. In this role as Solution Delivery Manager, you will provide end-to-end leadership for the planning, solutioning, sequencing, and delivery of initiatives within an assigned workstream, squad, or program. You will establish trusted relationships with Product, Business, Architecture, Delivery, Security, Quality Assurance, and domain teams, translating product roadmaps and project requirements into aligned, executable delivery plans.

You will balance business outcomes, technical feasibility, risk, capacity, and release constraints. The role is accountable for driving clarity across scope, solution, estimates, dependencies, approvals, quality, and timelines while supporting teams through execution and issue resolution.

What You'll Do

Stakeholder Partnership and Roadmap Alignment


Build and sustain trusted relationships with Product and Business stakeholders, creating transparency on priorities, trade-offs, risks, and delivery outcomes.
Develop a detailed understanding of the product roadmap for the assigned workstream, squad, or program and translate it into actionable delivery priorities.
Partner closely with the Product Owner to shape the backlog, negotiate priorities, clarify acceptance outcomes, and maintain alignment to business value.
Represent Solution Delivery in cross-functional discussions and ensure decisions, dependencies, and commitments are documented and communicated.


Requirements, Scope, and Solution Leadership


Read and understand project requirements; challenge assumptions, identify gaps and dependencies, provide feedback, and sign off on the scope committed for delivery.
Provide direction and guidance to the Business Analyst throughout project execution, including requirements clarification, dependency management, decision tracking, and issue resolution.
Work closely with the Solution Architect to finalize the end-to-end solution, ensuring alignment across business requirements, architecture, integration, data, operations, security, and support considerations.
Coordinate solution reviews with domain teams and Business stakeholders and obtain the required sign-offs before delivery proceeds.
Work closely with the Cyber Security prime and the Business Systems Analyst to close security requirements, risks, assessments, and required approvals.


Integrated Planning and Delivery Management


Work with domain leads and the Project Manager to sequence deliveries and formulate the Plan of Intent and Plan of Release in alignment with business goals, dependencies, team capacity, and release windows.
Negotiate delivery timelines with domain teams, release dates, and other delivery commitments, while clearly identifying prerequisites and trade-offs.
Obtain and consolidate estimates from all contributing teams; validate assumptions, dependencies, and resource needs to support an achievable integrated plan.
Proactively identify delivery risks, scope gaps, resource constraints, and cross-team dependencies, and drive timely mitigation or escalation.
Maintain clear delivery governance, decision records, status reporting, and accountability across the end-to-end lifecycle.


Facilitation, Decisions, and Governance


Schedule and lead Joint Application Design (JAD) sessions with domain teams and relevant stakeholders.
Ensure every JAD session and key delivery meeting has a clear agenda, required pre-work, appropriate decision-makers, and a specific outcome to be achieved.
Drive timely decisions and sign-offs by surfacing options, impacts, risks, and recommendations in a concise and fact-based manner.
Promote collaborative ways of working across Business and Technology teams, resolving conflict constructively and keeping teams focused on shared outcomes.


Quality Assurance and Delivery Support


Ensure the QA Test Plan and Test Cases fully cover the approved project scope and end-to-end solution and align with expected business and technical results.
Provide technical or process support during QA to help resolve defects, clarify intended behaviour, remove major blockers, and coordinate the appropriate domain expertise.
Validate readiness for release by confirming scope completion, solution and security sign-offs, test evidence, operational readiness, dependencies, and outstanding risks.
Support post-release reviews and continuous improvement by capturing lessons learned and strengthening delivery practices.


What You Bring


Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, Business, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
7+ years of progressive experience in technology delivery, solution delivery, program delivery, systems implementation, or a related discipline, including demonstrated leadership across complex initiatives.
Proven ability to lead cross-functional delivery involving Product, Business, Architecture, Engineering, Security, Quality Assurance, Operations, vendors, and multiple technology domains.
Strong understanding of the software delivery lifecycle, requirements practices, solution design, estimation, release planning, testing, defect management, and operational readiness.
Demonstrated ability to understand technical and business requirements, evaluate end-to-end impacts, and make sound scope and delivery decisions.
Experience facilitating JAD or solution-design sessions and driving groups toward clear, documented decisions and accountabilities.
Strong negotiation and influencing skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities, timelines, capacity constraints, and stakeholder expectations.
Excellent written, verbal, presentation, and executive communication skills; able to communicate complex topics clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.
Strong analytical, organizational, and problem-solving skills with high attention to detail and a bias for action.
Working knowledge of cybersecurity, privacy, risk, and governance requirements within enterprise technology delivery.
Experience working in Agile, hybrid, and project-based delivery models; familiarity with backlog management and iterative planning practices.
A collaborative leadership style that builds trust, protects sustainable team capacity, and creates accountability without compromising quality.


What Success Looks Like


Stakeholders have a shared understanding of scope, solution, priorities, dependencies, timelines, and decisions.
Delivery plans are integrated, realistic, and aligned to roadmap outcomes, team capacity, security obligations, and release constraints.
JAD and governance forums produce clear decisions, owners, and next steps.
Quality plans provide full coverage of the committed scope and defects are resolved through coordinated, timely support.
Risks and blockers are identified early, managed transparently, and escalated with options and recommended actions.
Teams deliver sustainable outcomes through clear roles, practical processes, and trusted cross-functional relationships.
