The Wow

We are looking for an experienced DevOps Engineer to join our growing engineering team. As the DevOps Engineer Team Leader, you will be responsible for building, deploying, and continuously monitoring software projects using CI tools. You will also create and maintain infrastructure as code using Ansible and Terraform. In this role, you will have the opportunity to keep up to date with the latest technologies in the field and drive their adoption within the organization. Additionally, you will play a key role in helping to transform our organization towards an engineering culture.

Key Responsibilities


Lead and manage a team of DevOps engineers
Build, deploy, and continuously monitor software projects using CI tools
Create and maintain infrastructure as code using Ansible and Terraform
Keep up to date with the latest technologies in the field and drive their adoption within the organization
Help to transform the organization towards an engineering culture
Provide technical guidance and mentorship to team members
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure the successful delivery of software projects


Requirements


5+ years of experience as a DevOps engineer
3+ years of experience leading and managing a team
Strong experience with CI/CD tools such as Jenkins, GitLab, or CircleCI
Proficiency with Ansible/Terraform for infrastructure as code
Experience with cloud infrastructure (e.g. AWS, GCP, Azure)


Nice to Have


Experience within the online gaming or iGaming industry.
Knowledge of security best practices and DevSecOps principles.
Experience managing high-availability, distributed systems.
Familiarity with GitOps workflows and modern cloud-native architectures.
