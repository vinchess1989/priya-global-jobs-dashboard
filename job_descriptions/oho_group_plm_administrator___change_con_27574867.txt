I'm working exclusively with a growing technology business looking for an experienced PLM Administrator & Change Controller to join its expanding product development team.

This is an opportunity to take ownership of a critical PLM function, driving product configuration, BoM control and engineering change management across the business.




Key Responsibilities:

Own and administer the Oracle Agile PLM system.
Manage product configuration, BoM structures and product variants.
Oversee engineering change orders, part creation and revision control.
Ensure accurate product data packs and timely releases.
Drive PLM system enhancements and continuous process improvements.
Work closely with Development Engineering, Operations and external manufacturing partners.




What We're Looking For:

Experience administering an engineering PLM/PDM system.
Strong knowledge of BoMs, document control and part numbering.
Experience with engineering change management and revision control.
Background in a manufacturing environment.
Excellent attention to detail and stakeholder management skills.




A great opportunity to take real ownership, influence engineering processes and play a key role in the evolution of the company's PLM infrastructure.
