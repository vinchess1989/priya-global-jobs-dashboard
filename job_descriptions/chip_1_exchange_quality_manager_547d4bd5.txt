Calling all quality-driven professionals with a passion for excellence!




Chip 1 Exchange is looking for a Quality Management superhero to join our team in Singapore!

Are you passionate about quality, continuous improvement, and making a real impact? Do you have an eye for detail and a mindset that never settles for anything less than excellence? Then we want YOU on our team!




Your Role

As our Quality Manager, you'll be the driving force behind maintaining and improving our quality standards.

Your mission? To ensure quality excellence, optimize processes, support audits, and build a strong quality culture across our international organization.




What You'll Bring

A technical qualification or degree in Electrical Engineering, Quality Management, or a related field.
Several years of experience in Quality Management, Quality Assurance, or Supplier Management.
Strong knowledge of ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and quality tools such as 8D, CIP, and PDCA.
Bonus points for knowledge of ISO/IEC 17025, AS6081/AS6171, EcoVadis, or an auditor/QMR qualification!
A proactive, analytical mindset with excellent problem-solving and communication skills.
Strong English communication skills, willingness to travel, and the confidence to work across international teams.




What's in It for You?

Room to Grow: Develop your career in a dynamic, international environment.
Make an Impact: Your expertise and ideas will help shape our quality standards.
Global Team Spirit: Collaborate with passionate professionals around the world.
Work Hard – Play Hard: Be part of the Chip 1 Exchange team culture, where dedication and teamwork go hand in hand!




Who Is Chip 1 Exchange?

Chip 1 Exchange is a leading global hybrid distributor of electronic components, combining franchise distribution with a strong presence in the open market.

We connect businesses worldwide, support global supply chains, and deliver reliable electronic component solutions to our customers.




What's Next?

Ready to become our next Quality Management superhero?

We'd love to hear from you!

Apply now and join the Chip 1 Exchange family in Singapore!
