Job Title: Content Developer




Terralogic Software Solutions is seeking a skilled Content Developer with 3- 5 years of experience to create, manage, and maintain clear, accurate, and user-focused technical documentation in a technology-driven environment.




Key Responsibilities

1. Content Creation

Develop clear, concise, and accurate technical documentation, including:
Knowledge base articles, FAQs, how-to guides, and troubleshooting steps.
Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
Training materials.
Use AI tools and AI-assisted content development techniques to support research, drafting, editing, summarization, and the creation of accurate, user-focused technical content, with appropriate human review and validation.




2. Knowledge Management

Organize and maintain technical information within knowledge management systems such as Confluence to ensure easy discoverability and accessibility.




3. Content Updates and Maintenance

Regularly review, update, and retire documentation to keep content accurate, relevant, and aligned with current processes.
Track content ownership, review cycles, and required updates.
Identify content gaps, duplication, and opportunities for simplification.




4. Stakeholder Collaboration

Partner with subject matter experts (SMEs) across BizTech and other cross-functional teams to gather information.
Validate technical accuracy, process details, and audience relevance before publication.




5. User Experience and Continuous Improvement

Understand the needs, roles, and use cases of target audiences.
Design documentation that is intuitive, task-oriented, and effective for users with varying levels of technical knowledge.
Gather and incorporate feedback from content users and stakeholders to continuously improve knowledge resources.




Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in Technical Communication, English, Computer Science, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
3 - 5 years of experience in technical writing, content development, or knowledge management roles in a technology-driven environment.
Demonstrated ability to write clear, concise, and grammatically correct technical content for various audiences.
Proficiency with common documentation tools and knowledge management platforms, such as Confluence and SharePoint.
Basic understanding of software development lifecycles and/or IT Service Management (ITSM) principles.
Strong organizational skills and attention to detail.
Experience using AI tools, analytics, or search behavior to improve knowledge content.
Ability to manage multiple tasks and prioritize effectively in a fast-paced environment.
Excellent interpersonal and communication skills to collaborate with cross-functional teams.




Preferred Skills

Familiarity with industry-specific standards and regulations.
Knowledge of marketing principles and the ability to create engaging content.
Excellent organizational and time management skills.
Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a team.
