Quality Engineer - Kerry




This role reports to the Operations Manager. The role as a Quality Engineer will be to provide support to our department’s functions, working in accordance with cGMP standards.




Essential Knowledge & Experience




3rd level qualification in an Engineering, Science or Technical discipline.
Practical Hands-on knowledge of QA Systems.
Experience in the use of Microsoft systems
Six Sigma Green or Black Belt an asset.
Ability to Problem Solve.
Preferred Knowledge & Experience
Previous GMP Experience an Asset.







Duties will include




Provide Technical and Quality related support services to the unit.
Support the training and learning aspects of the reporting area
Ensuring regulatory compliance in respect of dept training
Ensure an effective & efficient line & processes.
Develop and maintain operating procedures
Drive continuous performance improvement.
Maintain a visible line & process Quality Management System.
Support the unit by developing robust process & systems to ensure delivery of effective quality.
Support Continuous Improvement through Kaizen and Lean / Six Sigma methodologies.
Manage, plan & coordinate relevant performance improvement projects. Generate and approve associated change controls and relevant protocols.
