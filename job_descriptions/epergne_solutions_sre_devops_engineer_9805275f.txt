Job Role:- SRE DevOps Engineer




Job Location: Singapore




Experience:- 5-12 Years




Role Summary:-

We are looking for a skilled DevOps / SRE Engineer to support cloud infrastructure, automation, deployment, reliability, and containerized environments. The role will work closely with development and operations teams to improve system scalability, availability, and delivery efficiency.

Key Responsibilities:-
Design, implement, and maintain scalable cloud infrastructure.
Manage and optimize AWS/GCP cloud environments; Alibaba Cloud experience is an advantage.
Develop and maintain infrastructure using Terraform.
Build and maintain Kubernetes environments and containerized workloads.
Develop Python scripts for automation and operational efficiency.
Implement CI/CD pipelines, deployment automation, and infrastructure monitoring.
Troubleshoot production issues and improve system reliability and performance.
Apply DevOps/SRE best practices for availability, scalability, security, and incident management.
Collaborate with development, infrastructure, and security teams on technical initiatives.
Skills & Requirements:-
5–12 years of experience in DevOps, SRE, Cloud Engineering, or related roles.
Strong hands-on experience with AWS and/or GCP.
Alibaba Cloud experience is an advantage.
Strong working knowledge of Kubernetes and containerization.
Proficiency in Python scripting and automation.
Strong experience with Terraform / Infrastructure as Code (IaC).
Experience with CI/CD, monitoring, troubleshooting, and production support.
Strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
Good communication and stakeholder management skills.
