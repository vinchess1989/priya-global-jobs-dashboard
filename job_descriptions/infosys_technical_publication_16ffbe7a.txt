Technical Authoring / Technical Publication experience. Knowledge of Aero Engine systems and aerospace engineering concepts. Experience in creating maintenance and technical manuals. Understanding of engineering drawings, GD&T, and BOM structures. Proficiency with XML, SGML, Arbortext, FrameMaker, or similar authoring tools. Strong technical writing and documentation skills. Experience with configuration management and document control processes

Develop and maintain technical publications for Aero Engine products and systems. Create maintenance manuals, overhaul manuals, component maintenance manuals (CMM), and service documents. Interpret engineering drawings, 3D models, BOMs, and design data for technical documentation. Collaborate with design, engineering, manufacturing, and quality teams to gather technical information. Ensure documentation complies with aerospace standards and customer requirements. Perform document revisions, updates, and configuration management activities. Create illustrations, exploded views, and technical graphics for publications. Support change management activities and incorporate engineering changes into manuals. Review and validate technical content for accuracy, completeness, and consistency.

Knowledge of S1000D, ATA iSpec 2200, or aerospace documentation standards. Experience with CAD tools such as CATIA, Creo, or Siemens NX. Exposure to PLM tools like Teamcenter or Windchill. Understanding of aerospace maintenance and repair processes.
