Introduction

 

A leading financial institution is seeking an experienced IT Change & Training Lead to drive organisational readiness, user adoption, knowledge transfer, and operational handover as part of a major technology modernisation programme.

The successful candidate will be responsible for ensuring that business users, technical teams, super-users, and internal IT support teams are fully prepared to adopt, operate, maintain, and govern a modernised Oracle reporting and analytics ecosystem.

The role will bridge the gap between technology delivery and business readiness, ensuring effective training, clear communications, comprehensive technical documentation, and a controlled transition into business-as-usual operations.

Duties & Responsibilities

 

 

Develop and implement a comprehensive Change Management and Training Strategy aligned with the overall technology transformation programme.
Assess organisational readiness, stakeholder impact, training needs, and adoption risks across business and IT teams.
Develop structured training curricula tailored to different user groups, including business users, super-users, IT support teams, and technical administrators.
Create user-friendly training materials, quick-reference guides, process documentation, FAQs, and knowledge articles.
Design and facilitate hands-on training workshops covering the modernised Oracle Analytics Server (OAS) environment, reporting capabilities, dashboards, and self-service analytics functionality.
Develop training programmes that enable users to confidently navigate the modernised reporting environment and adopt new ways of working.
Identify adoption barriers and implement targeted interventions to support successful user transition.
Develop and maintain comprehensive As-Built technical documentation and operational runbooks.
Author technical runbooks covering areas such as:
WebLogic administration
Application monitoring
Log management and rotation
Backup and recovery procedures
ETL failure recovery
Operational troubleshooting
Escalation procedures
Routine maintenance activities
Ensure technical documentation accurately reflects the final implemented solution and is suitable for ongoing operational use.
Coordinate and manage structured business and IT communications throughout the programme lifecycle.
Develop communication plans covering UAT scheduling, key milestones, production cutovers, planned maintenance, system outages, and Hypercare activities.
Ensure stakeholders receive timely, accurate, and appropriately targeted communications.
Coordinate business readiness activities leading up to production cutover and go-live.
Develop and manage Hypercare support processes, ensuring issues are appropriately logged, escalated, communicated, and resolved.
Coordinate the formal transition of Tier-1 and Tier-2 application support responsibilities to the internal IT service desk.
Facilitate knowledge-transfer sessions between project teams, technical specialists, application support, and service desk teams.
Ensure support teams have the required documentation, knowledge articles, troubleshooting guides, escalation paths, and access to successfully assume operational responsibility.
Define and track change adoption and training effectiveness metrics.
Monitor user feedback and identify opportunities for further training, communication, or process improvement.
Support stakeholder engagement and ensure business and IT teams remain aligned throughout the transformation.
Maintain appropriate change, training, communication, and operational readiness documentation for governance and audit purposes.
Desired Experience & Qualification

 

5+ years of experience in IT Change Management, Organisational Change, User Adoption, and/or Corporate IT Training.
Proven experience managing change and adoption initiatives within complex enterprise technology environments.
Demonstrated experience developing and delivering technical training programmes and curricula.
Excellent technical writing and documentation capabilities.
Strong experience developing:
Training materials
User guides
Quick-reference guides
Knowledge articles
Technical runbooks
Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
Experience supporting user adoption for complex BI, analytics, ERP, or enterprise technology implementations.
Experience working with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Strong understanding of IT service management and operational handover principles.
Experience coordinating UAT, go-live, cutover, and Hypercare activities.
Proven experience facilitating knowledge transfer between project teams and operational IT support teams.
Strong stakeholder engagement and communication skills.
Experience within financial services or other highly regulated environments is advantageous.
