Company Description

Eurofins Scientific is an international life sciences company, which provides a unique range of analytical testing services to clients across multiple industries. The Group believes it is the world leader in food, environment, pharmaceutical and cosmetics products testing and in agroscience CRO services. It is also one of the global independent market leaders in certain testing and laboratory services for genomics, discovery pharmacology, forensics, CDMO, advanced material sciences and for supporting clinical studies. In addition, Eurofins is one of the leading global emerging players in specialty clinical diagnostic testing.

In 2020, Eurofins generated 5.4 billion Euro proforma turnover in 800 laboratories across 50 countries, employing about 50.000 staff.

Job Description

Position Summary: We are seeking a Document Writer (Scientific Documentation & Technical Communication) to support scientific writing outputs through coordination, technical oversight, and document lifecycle management. This role sits at the intersection of science, communication, and project management, ensuring that scientific documentation is complete, consistent, timely, and publication‑ or audit‑ready.

While this role is not primarily responsible for authoring scientific content, it serves as a key coordinator of scientific documentation by managing reviews, integrating subject matter expert input, and ensuring document readiness within a regulated environment.

Key Responsibilities

Scientific Document Coordination


Coordinate scientific input from multiple authors, reviewers, and subject matter experts
Track document components across drafting, review, and approval stages
Ensure consistency in scientific terminology, formatting, and messaging across documents


Technical Communication & Quality Oversight


Review documents for clarity, completeness, and alignment with regulatory or client expectations
Identify gaps, inconsistencies, or unresolved comments and drive resolution
Ensure document outputs meet internal quality standards and external regulatory requirements


Project & Timeline Management


Develop and manage document timelines to support project milestones
Monitor document readiness and proactively escalate risks to delivery
Interface with project teams to align documentation activities with testing, study, or submission schedules


Cross‑Functional Collaboration


Act as a central liaison between scientific, quality, regulatory, and client‑facing teams
Support structured handoffs between authoring, review, and document coordination functions
Maintain clear communication regarding document status, timelines, and expectations.


Role Context in Large or Regulated Organizations

In alignment with best practices in CROs and regulated laboratories, this role functions within a segmented documentation model, where:


Authors generate technical and scientific content
Reviewers verify accuracy, compliance, and scientific integrity
Document Coordination ensures integration, consistency, governance, and readiness


This position plays a key role in the document coordination layer, while contributing directly to high‑quality scientific writing outputs.
