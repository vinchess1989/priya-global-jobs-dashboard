PFB Job Description:

BOM Release Engineer




The role ensures that engineering releases are completed accurately, on time, and in compliance with company standards, quality requirements, and customer expectations. The Release Engineer co-ordinates a team of engineers, designers, or release coordinators and works closely with cross-functional departments including Manufacturing, Quality, Supply Chain, Program Management, and Product Engineering.




Key Responsibilities:

Supervise and manage the engineering release process from concept through production.
Review and approve engineering drawings, Bills of Materials BOMs, Engineering Change Requests ECRs, and Engineering Change Orders & Change Actions.
Ensure released engineering data complies with company standards, customer requirements, and regulatory guidelines.
Coordinate product releases with design, manufacturing, quality, procurement, and program teams.
Monitor release schedules and ensure timely completion of deliverables.
Maintain data integrity within PLMPDM systems e.g., Teamcenter, Windchill, Enovia, Agile PLM.
Own the release meetings with programme support and schedule release meetings to support BoM deadlines.
Release processes - corrections, completions and cancellations, and ownership of cross-carline concerns.
PST / LMM / LMLR Release metrics preparation can present at PST / LMM.
Release Tracker maintenance and Night letter issue, ensuring accurate reporting.
Support new product introduction NPI, design changes, and all product lifecycle management activities.
Develop and maintain release procedures, work instructions, and best practices.
Track and report release metrics, KPIs, and project status to management.
Ensure proper configuration management and document control practices are followed.
