Preeti Bagdi
Preeti Bagdi
Assistant Manager Human Resources @ ACG Worldwide | MBA | Welingkar institute | Mumbai

Company: SciTech Centre - R&D Centre of ACG Group

Location: Jogeshwari, Mumbai

Education: BE in Mechanical/Production/Electronics engineering/Diploma in Technical Writing

Experience: 8-10 years

Industry: Pharma Manufacturing/Heavy Machinery




Job Objective-

Preparation and review of Master Validation Documents (FS, HSD, SDS, HDTR, SDTR, I/O, ITR, FAT, IQ and OQ) to fulfil Master Documentation development plans




Primary responsibilities-




Strategic and Planning:

Planning for executing on-time delivery of Master Validation Documents in-line with departmental goals
Participate/Contribute in continuous improvement of Master Validation Documents




Functional

Understand the PLM (Product Lifecycle Management), PDLC (Product Development Lifecycle) and DDLC (Document Development lifecycle)
Understand the technical writing standards of the company and adhere to that during daily basis documentation tasks.
Understand Editorial Guide/SOP’s, Base as well as Global modules and Company standards for Validation Documents and adhere to released documentation processes
Understand the product and processes to be documented in Master Validation Documents
Understand user requirements, design specifications, engineering drawings, BOM, ECN, URS, scope of supply, various features and options to create operating instructions and qualification documents.
Planning for on-time delivery of documents in-line with departmental goals
Collaborate with cross functional teams to obtain information and to review Master Validation Documents
Develop and review operating instructions
Develop and review the Design Qualification (DQ), Installation Qualification (IQ), Operational Qualifications (OQ) and Factory Acceptance Test (FAT)
Support QA during the FAT process for documentation sign-off
Prepare and maintain reports of CFT feedback and improvement actions suggested by various functions
Assist to keep track of change requests and follow correct change management process
Drive and maintain customer centric culture within documentation team to fulfill desired customer needs
Develop machine specific Master modules to prepare chapters of the Master Validation Documents
Self-review of the Master modules, Master Validation Documents to ensure accuracy of documents
Drive CFT review to receive inputs from various functions for improvement of Validation Documents
Ontime & ‘First Time Right” completion of Master Validation Documents with reference to Editorial Guide/SOP’s, Technical Terms and Company standards.
Collaborate with Engineering, Controls, Process, Service & QA team to Validate Documents
Complete the project with “Project Acceptance Form” involving all stakeholders
