The Innovation Lead (Product Development) will bridge product strategy, innovation, and technical delivery to ensure business needs are translated into scalable, high-quality solutions. Start-up role combines product thinking, stakeholder alignment, innovation leadership, and hands-on technical oversight to deliver business value and guide engineering execution. The role also requires applying thought leadership to shape product direction, technical decisions, and innovation initiatives, including the adoption of AI-enabled capabilities.

Lead the end-to-end delivery of digital, software, data and innovation engagements, from discovery and planning through execution, release and client handover.
Translate business and client requirements into clear solution requirements, delivery plans, prioritised backlogs, milestones and measurable outcomes.
Provide credible technical leadership across software engineering, data engineering, cloud, analytics, AI and related digital solutions, while drawing on MOYO South Africa for specialist architecture and deep technical expertise.
Engage in early project and pre-execution phases to assess feasibility, delivery approach, resource requirements, dependencies, risks and solution options.
Own project delivery disciplines including scope, timelines, budget awareness, resource coordination, risk management, quality, governance and status reporting




5 years in product, delivery, or engineering lead role, interfacing with internal & external stakeholders

Experience building scalable solutions

Consulting experience (advantageous)

Relevant IT/Engineering degree

Ability to demonstrate exposure to AI and data concepts and their practical application
