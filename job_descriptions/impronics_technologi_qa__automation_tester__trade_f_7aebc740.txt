Job Summary

We are seeking a proactive and detail-oriented Automation Tester – Quality Assurance to join our team working on Eximbills from China System within the Trade Finance and Payments domain. The ideal candidate will have strong automation testing experience, particularly with Selenium, Cucumber, and Jenkins, along with in-depth knowledge of SWIFT MT Messages and ISO20022-based messaging flows. Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to work effectively within a global team are essential.

Key Responsibilities


Analyze business and functional requirements to determine appropriate automation test strategies.
Design, develop, maintain, and execute Selenium-based automation test scripts.
Maintain and update regression test suites regularly to ensure product quality.
Review and analyze automation test results and create Test Summary Reports.
Prepare test scenarios, test cases, and document test execution results clearly.
Conduct smoke testing to validate new features and ensure they meet business expectations.
Analyze and improve existing payment processing solutions and workflows.
Work with stakeholders to transition current processes to ISO20022-compliant messaging.
Collaborate closely with internal teams to ensure clear understanding of requirements and deliverables.
Take ownership of systems, acting as Product Owner/Backlog Keeper when required.
Document software products, systems, and workflows with high clarity and detail.
Understand and work with APIs and microservices architecture.
Use Postman or SoapUI for API testing and perform database validations using SQL queries.
Ensure alignment with global team standards and participate in cross-border project initiatives.


Required Skills & Experience


3+ years of experience in automation testing.
Strong hands-on experience with Selenium, Cucumber, and test automation frameworks.
Solid understanding of Jenkins CI/CD pipelines.
Experience with SWIFT MT message formats and ISO20022 payment messaging is essential.
Proven work experience within Trade Finance and Payments (processing, clearing, SWIFT) domain.
Strong SQL skills and familiarity with data validation techniques.
Understanding of APIs, microservices, and their interrelationships.
Experience working on Eximbills from China System is a strong advantage.
Familiarity with tools such as Postman or SoapUI for API validation.
Ability to communicate effectively with global stakeholders and internal teams.
Strong documentation skills with the ability to produce clear and concise material.
Excellent written and verbal communication in English.
Ability to work collaboratively in global teams.


Educational Qualifications


Bachelor’s Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Business Management, or a related field.
