Job Summary

Support the Workstream Leads with all activities relating to the end-to-end management of data risks in the Bank’s Non-Financial Risk Taxonomy. The main objectives are to:


Enable / drive CIB to achieve and maintain regulatory compliance with the Group & Country requirements for the data risks effectively and efficiently.
Support timely completion of any regulatory reviews / examinations / audits in relation to data risk management with the objective of not having any regulatory findings in such exercises
Build and continuously strengthen the relationship with CIB clients and counterparties through effective, transparent and fair usage of data including the upholding of principles and commitments that we have made
Ensure full visibility on the data risk profile and book of work / priorities to relevant stakeholders across 1st, 2nd and 3rd line of defence
Implement and maintain data by design across CIB so that data and data risks are considered and managed as part of the design of any process, systems and/or organisational setup.


The immediate focus for this role at point of hiring is to


drive the review of existing data risk controls and metrics in current state,
(re)design what changes and/or new controls / metrics are to be implemented in line with the Control Simplification Programme (CSP) and relevant Group / Country requirements,
Develop delivery / implementation plan for the revised / enhanced data risk controls and metrics and agree with all relevant stakeholders across 1 and 2 LOD
Deliver the above-mentioned implementation plan within the committed timelines


Key Responsibilities

Strategy


This role is positioned to support the Workstream Lead and broader CIB Data Governance & Management Centre of Excellence (CoE) in enabling CIB business to achieve compliance with the Bank’s Data Management Standards at the Group and Country level, and underlying regulations.
This includes but is not limited to supporting the workstream leads in designing, developing and executing the data risk management strategy.


Business


Develop a deep understanding of the current state of CIB’s operational, organisational and architecture landscape. This includes but is not limited to familiarising with different CIB processes, systems and teams across geographies. This also entails knowing the data lineage and flows across / between processes, systems and teams from point of creation to storage and archival / disposal.
Build effective and meaningful relationship with stakeholders across 1st 2nd and 3rd line of defence particularly but not limited to Product Owners, Process Owners / Process Owner Support teams / Business Risk Managers, and Data Risk Framework Owners / CFCRs in the CIB context as well as centralised teams such as Group CDO.
Embed data by design across CIB businesses
Act as a trusted advisor and partner to the CIB businesses in relation to managing data and associated data risks effectively and efficiently
Support and/or execute change management activities including but not limited to development of training materials, development of operational guidance etc.


Processes


Scope would be specific to Corporate & Investment Banking (CIB) covering various products such as Markets, Lending, Transaction Banking and more as well as activities related to managing of CIB business book.


Requirement Analysis and Discovery


Analyse the Group and/or Country Data Risk Standards / Local Addenda rolled out by CFCR as well as the underlying regulation to determine applicability to CIB business, process(es), system(s) and team(s)
Identify and crystallise the above-mentioned requirements (where applicable) into specific actions to be taken to achieve regulatory compliance
Provide feedback to the Group / Country Standard Owners where there is further clarity and/or enhancement required to the requirements
Facilitate the simplification and/or operationalisation of the Group standards and accompanying Country Addenda / Schedules (where applicable) for CIB
Perform discovery of the current state organisational, operational and system / architecture landscape against the requirements & actions mentioned above to identify if there are any gaps


Gap analysis, impact and risk assessment


Impact assess the gaps using the Bank’s methodology, develop remediation / implementation plan where required and agree on the outcomes with all relevant stakeholders across 1st, 2nd and 3rd Line of Defence.
Work with the relevant stakeholders to perform regular / ad hoc risk assessments including but not limited to the Risk and Control Self Assessments (RCSAs) and Residual Risk Assessments (RRAs)


Remediation solution design & delivery / End to End control management


Deliver the activities required to remediate / address the gaps and/or implement the Standards within the agreed timelines. This may also take the form of executing specific Risk Treatment Plans
Design, build, implement and maintain controls required to reduce risk to low and/or sustain the low risk environment specifically for data risks. These controls should be in line with the Group and Country Standards as well as applicable relevant guidelines, relating to control design, implementation, maintenance and decommissioning.
Coordinate with centralised teams / functions and/or programmes to roll out enablers that support the digitisation and/or transformation of the data risk control landscape and facilitate the adoption of these enablers by the CIB businesses in a timely, efficient and effective manner.


Governance


Setup new and/or support existing governance mechanisms including but not limited to governance forums, performance metrics / indicators and management reporting
Support the design and implementation of the Target Operating Model, Business Impact Assessment, and data management capabilities in the CIB business context
Support the workstream leads and senior members in the design, implementation, and continuous improvement of Data Governance, Data Risk, and Data Management processes across the business.
Support the design and hosting of Data Governance and Data Risk forums, committees, and working groups to provide effective oversight of data-related risks, issues, controls, and remediation activities.
Facilitate and support responses to data-related audits, regulatory reviews, compliance assessments, and governance reviews, ensuring timely and effective closure of findings and commitments.
Develop and maintain governance processes for managing business demand, prioritisation, advisory requests, regulatory commitments, transformation initiatives, and data-related change activities.
Represent the business in enterprise-wide Data Governance, Data Risk, technology, regulatory, and transformation programmes, ensuring business requirements, risks, and control considerations are appropriately addressed.
Establish management information, dashboards, metrics, and reporting processes to provide transparency over Data Governance maturity, Data Quality performance, Data Risk exposure, remediation progress, and control effectiveness.
Supports the setup and/or maintenance of the relevant governance mechanisms including metrics, forums, and management reporting.


People & Talent


Foster a culture of accountability, collaboration, continuous improvement, and innovation across the team and wider stakeholder community.
Promote and embed the Bank’s values, behaviours, and conduct standards, ensuring they are reflected in day-to-day decision-making and leadership practices.
Effectively engages with peers within CIB CDO and relevant stakeholders across CIB Business, CIB COO and CIB CFCR as well as the broader Group CDO function
Coach, mentor, and develop other team members especially those who are less experienced / senior to help them build and enhance their professional capabilities in Data Governance and management as well as deepen their CIB business (process / system) expertise


Risk Management


Work closely with the workstream leads as well as relevant stakeholders across 1st, 2nd and 3rd line to execute / deliver the agreed book of work for the particular data risk type in the context of CIB. Where appropriate, perform the upwards escalations of key risks, issues and/or challenges encountered.


Regulatory & Business Conduct


Display exemplary conduct and live by the Group's Values and Code of Conduct.
Take personal responsibility for embedding the highest standards of ethics, including regulatory and business conduct, across Standard Chartered Bank. This includes understanding and ensuring compliance With, in letter and spirit, all applicable laws, regulations, guidelines and the Group Code of Conduct.
Effectively and collaboratively identify, escalate, mitigate and resolve risk, conduct and compliance matters
