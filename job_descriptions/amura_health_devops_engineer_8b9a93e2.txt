Role Overview

We are looking for a DevOps / Platform Engineer with 3–4 years of experience to help manage AWS cloud infrastructure, automation, and AI-ready platform operations. You will work closely with engineering and AI teams to ensure scalable, secure, and reliable deployments.

Key Responsibilities


AWS & Infrastructure: Manage core AWS services (EC2, EKS, Lambda, RDS, S3, VPC) using Infrastructure-as-Code (Terraform, CloudFormation, or CDK).
AI/ML Workflows: Support infra for AI applications, model serving, vector databases, and GPU-based workloads.
CI/CD & Automation: Build and maintain deployment pipelines using GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins.
Containers & Kubernetes: Deploy and maintain containerized applications using Docker and Kubernetes (EKS) or ECS.
Observability & Incident Support: Maintain dashboards and alerts with CloudWatch, Prometheus, or Grafana, and participate in on-call response.
Security & FinOps: Apply AWS security best practices (IAM, encryption, secrets) and assist with cloud cost optimization.


Must-Have Skills & Qualifications


3–4 years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or Cloud Infrastructure Engineering.
AWS & Containers: Good working knowledge of core AWS services, Docker, and Kubernetes (EKS).
Infrastructure-as-Code: Practical experience with Terraform, CloudFormation, or AWS CDK.
Linux & Networking: Solid fundamentals in Linux systems, DNS, routing, and load balancing.
Scripting: Hands-on scripting proficiency in Python, Bash, or Go.
CI/CD & Observability: Experience building CI/CD pipelines (GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, or Jenkins) and using monitoring tools (CloudWatch, Prometheus, Grafana, or Datadog).


Nice-to-Have Skills


Basic exposure to AI/ML infrastructure, model serving, or GPU-based compute.
Experience with event-driven messaging systems (Kafka, Redis, or SQS).
Familiarity with GitOps tools and Helm (ArgoCD, Flux, or OpenTelemetry).
AWS Certifications (Solutions Architect Associate, SysOps Administrator, or DevOps Engineer).


Skills:- Amazon Web Services (AWS), Kubernetes, Docker, CI/CD, Linux administration and Scripting
