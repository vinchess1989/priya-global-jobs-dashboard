About The Role

The Quality Assurance Representative – Production provides quality assurance oversight across supplier qualification, manufacturing, inspection and product acceptance activities. The role ensures that production processes and products comply with approved specifications, quality management systems, customer requirements, contractual obligations and applicable regulatory standards to support product conformity and successful customer acceptance.

Qualifications and experience:


National Diploma in Electrical or Mechanical Engineering, or equivalent (NQF Level 6).
Advanced Diploma in Quality Management or equivalent (NQF Level 7) will be advantageous, e.g. ASQ or City & Guilds.
ISO 9001:2015 Implementation Certificate.
Six Sigma Green Belt, Total Quality Management (TQM) or equivalent.
3–5 years’ experience in quality assurance within a complex manufacturing or defence systems environment.
Experience in quality assurance across design, manufacturing, supplier quality and production processes.


Key technical skills:


Quality Management Systems (ISO 9001, EN9100 and IPC Standards).
Supplier Quality Assurance and supplier process capability assessment.
Production Quality Assurance.
First Article Inspection (FAI).
Production Part Approval Process (PPAP).
Root Cause Analysis and Failure Reporting, Analysis and Corrective Action Systems (FRACAS).
Configuration Management.
Product acceptance and Certificate of Conformance (CoC) management.
Process, internal and supplier auditing.
Continuous improvement methodologies, including Lean, Six Sigma and TQM.
Interpretation of engineering drawings and technical documentation.
ERP systems.
Proficiency in Microsoft Excel and Word.
Contractual and regulatory compliance.


Key responsibilities:

Supplier Quality Assurance and Qualification

Evaluate supplier capability, process performance and product quality through supplier qualification, inspections, audits and performance monitoring to ensure suppliers consistently meet organisational, contractual and regulatory requirements, resulting in reliable supplier performance and compliant products.

Production Quality Assurance and Product Acceptance

Verify manufacturing processes, production activities, configuration compliance and product acceptance requirements against approved specifications, quality standards and customer expectations to ensure product conformity and the delivery of compliant, production-ready products.

Inspection, Documentation and Configuration Control

Review production documentation, build history, configuration records, inspection results and acceptance documentation to ensure complete traceability, accurate certification and configuration integrity, supporting compliant product release and successful customer acceptance.

Quality Improvement and Compliance

Drive quality assurance activities, process audits, root cause analysis and continuous improvement initiatives to strengthen manufacturing processes, improve product quality, minimise non-conformances and maintain compliance with established quality management systems.

Stakeholder Support and Quality Governance

Collaborate with Engineering, Production, Configuration Management, Procurement and suppliers to resolve quality issues, provide technical quality guidance and support governance activities to enable effective decision-making, maintain compliance and achieve sustained operational excellence.
