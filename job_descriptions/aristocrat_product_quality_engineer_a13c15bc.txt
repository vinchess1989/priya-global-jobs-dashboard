At Aristocrat, we are deeply committed to delivering world-class gaming experiences. As a Product Quality Engineer, you will ensure our products meet the highest standards of excellence. You will help develop the world's greatest gaming experience every day. You will join a dynamic team, working with different collaborators to improve the delivery of quality products. You will also support our Product Compliance Submissions team. This is your chance to join an ambitious and innovative company pushing the limits of technology and creativity!

What You'll Do
Deliver submission packages within a strict turnaround time.
Perform software analysis to develop efficient submission plans.
Compile and sign source code to build signed software binary images.
Verify software meets regulatory RTP percentages and volatility through code and math calculations review.
Ensure game submission helpscreens meet regulatory requirements.
Run and document signature calculations for algorithms like Gat 3.5 and Sha1.
Build CSV files and concatenated bin images.
Review product documentation for accuracy and communicate any discrepancies to collaborators.
Ensure all associated software is accounted for, packaged, and submitted accurately.
Analyze ECOs and Master Item Parts for inconsistencies.
Build emulation drives to support reel strip and combination testing.
Generate game builds on production media for debug and production environments.
Boot game software on compatible cabinets to verify accurate compilation and signing.
Drive down "withdraw activities" through test case analysis against regulatory and quality standards.
Communicate issues and bugs found in game software or documentation to relevant teams for resolution.
Create and Maintain software in the Copy Protection System.
Review and prepare tradeshow games and medias.


What We're Looking For
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field, or equivalent experience in Software Quality Assurance.
Minimum 1+ years of experience in Quality Assurance testing in a client/server environment.
Demonstrable experience in accomplishing test cases and scripts from Functional Specifications or Business Rules.
Experienced with slot machines, accounting systems, peripherals, progressive controls, and related hardware/software.
Proficient in test automation techniques and tools such as proprietary slot OS tools/routines and emulation tools.
Solid skills in using Linux, programming various media types, and understanding C++.
Familiarity with data query and analysis techniques.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
Outstanding attention to detail and problem-solving skills
Ability to work independently with minimal supervision and under tight deadlines.
Ability to readily adapt to changing business needs and acquire new skills and knowledge as required.
Capable of multitasking and prioritizing tasks to meet deadlines.
