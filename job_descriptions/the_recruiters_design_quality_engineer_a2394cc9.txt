Key Responsibilities & Skills:

Design History File (DHF) and Risk Management File (RMF) remediation.
Medical Device Design Controls and Design Documentation.
Traceability of User Needs, Design Inputs/Outputs, and V&V Evidence.
Design Verification & Validation (DV/V).
Risk Management and Quality/Regulatory Compliance.
ASME GD&T and Medical Device Manufacturing Processes.
Cognition Cockpit (Electronic DHF System).
Knowledge of FDA and Global Medical Device Regulations.
Minitab, Statistics and Design for Six Sigma (DFSS) are desirable.

Education & Experience:

Bachelor's degree in Mechanical, Biomedical, Aerospace or a related Engineering discipline.
3–5 years of relevant engineering experience.
Prior experience in medical device design controls and DHF/RMF remediation preferred
