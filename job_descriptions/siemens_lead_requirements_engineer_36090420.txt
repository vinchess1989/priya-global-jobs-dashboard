Job Summary
The Senior Software Requirements Engineer is responsible for deriving, specifying, and maintaining software requirements from approved system requirements for safety‑critical systems, including SIL‑2 and SIL‑4 applications. The role ensures that software requirements are correct, complete, traceable, and verifiable, in compliance with defined lifecycle processes and applicable safety and quality standards.
Key Responsibilities
Derive and document software requirements based on system requirements
Create and maintain Software Requirement Specifications (SRS) for safety‑critical systems
Ensure requirements are unambiguous, testable, and audit‑ready
Maintain bidirectional traceability between system requirements, software requirements, and verification artifacts
Participate in formal requirement reviews, safety reviews, and audits
Perform impact analysis for requirement changes under controlled configuration management
Collaborate with system engineering, development, testing, quality, and safety teams
Provide requirement evidence and traceability support during audits and assessments
Required Skills & Experience
9+ years of experience in software requirements engineering
Proven experience in SIL‑2 and/or SIL‑4 safety‑critical projects
Strong experience in System → Software requirement derivation
Good understanding of V‑Model–based development lifecycle
Experience with requirements / ALM tools (e.g., Polarion, DOORS, Jira)
Strong documentation, analytical, and communication skills
