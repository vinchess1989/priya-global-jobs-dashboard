Job Overview

This is a great opportunity to join Arm’s Quality Group and play a key role in crafting quality across our global supply chain. As a Supply Chain Quality Assurance Engineer, you’ll connect customer expectations with supply chain execution.

This is an individual-contributor engineering role working across the product lifecycle with Arm engineering and business teams, suppliers and partners. The role covers requirements flow-down, leading multi-functional quality activities, coordinating interested parties and driving actions through technical expertise, collaboration and influence.

Responsibilities


Lead and coordinate collaborative quality initiatives across Engineering, Quality, Supply Chain, Procurement, Operations and other subject-matter guides, driving actions and work to completion.
Interpret customer, product, quality and regulatory requirements and ensure applicable requirements are effectively flowed down and traceable throughout the supply chain.
Act as a key contact point for Arm customers on compliance matters — customer audits, assessments, questionnaires, scorecards and other quality and compliance activities.
Guide suppliers and internal teams on applicable APQP/PPAP and quality core-tool requirements, including FMEA, MSA and SPC.
Investigate non-conformances, drive root cause analysis, and verify preventive actions.
Track compliance performance metrics and deliver assurance reports that strengthen leadership decisions and customer confidence.
Build trusted customer and partner relationships with OEMs and Tier-1s.


Required Skills And Experience


Degree in Engineering or equivalent experience.
Semiconductor experience and understanding of the semiconductor product lifecycle, manufacturing processes and supply chain.
Demonstrated ability to lead multi-functional teams and activities, coordinate subject-matter experts and drive actions to successful completion without direct management authority.
Experience in auditing against IATF 16949, ISO 9001, or VDA 6.3.
Practical knowledge of APQP and other quality core tools (FMEA, MSA, SPC, PPAP).
Clear, confident communication skills across both technical and executive levels.


"Nice To Have" Skills and Experience:


Experience with data analytics, automation or AI-enabled tools applied to quality, supplier performance or risk management.
