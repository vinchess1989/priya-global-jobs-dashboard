Job Summary

We are looking for a detail-oriented and organised Quality Assurance & Regulatory Affairs professional to support the maintenance of the Quality Management System (QMS), documentation, production records, quality processes, and regulatory compliance activities. The candidate will be responsible for maintaining accurate quality records and supporting internal quality investigations, audits, and regulatory requirements.

Key Responsibilities
Prepare, review, update, and maintain assigned Quality Management System (QMS) documents, procedures, formats, and records.
Issue controlled documents and maintain document control registers and records.
Review Batch Manufacturing Records (BMRs) and supporting production documentation for accuracy and completeness.
Maintain and update quality-related logs and trackers, including:
Customer Complaints
Corrective and Preventive Actions (CAPA)
Non-Conformities (NC)
Deviations
Other quality-related records.
Maintain reference samples, stability records, and associated documentation as per established procedures.
Support customer complaint investigations and internal quality investigations.
Prepare, review, and update assigned regulatory and technical documentation.
Assist in regulatory submissions and ensure timely availability of required documentation.
Support internal and external audits, regulatory inspections, and compliance activities.
Ensure proper filing, traceability, retrieval, and maintenance of quality records.
Coordinate with relevant departments to obtain documentation and ensure compliance with established quality procedures.
Required Skills & Competencies
Knowledge of Quality Management Systems (QMS) and documentation practices.
Familiarity with ISO 13485 and applicable regulatory requirements will be an advantage.
Basic understanding of CAPA, deviations, non-conformities, and complaint handling.
Good documentation, record-keeping, and coordination skills.
Proficiency in MS Office, particularly Excel and Word.
Strong attention to detail and organisational skills.
Ability to maintain confidentiality and meet documentation deadlines.
Educational Qualification

B.Pharm / M.Pharm / B.Sc. / M.Sc. / B.Tech or equivalent qualification in a relevant discipline.

Preferred Experience

1–3 years of experience in Quality Assurance, QMS documentation, or Regulatory Affairs, preferably in the medical devices, pharmaceutical, or healthcare manufacturing industry.
