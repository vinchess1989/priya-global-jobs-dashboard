Description & Requirements

The Bloomberg CTO Office is the future-looking technical arm of Bloomberg L.P. We envision, design, and guide the creation of our next generation infrastructure, hardware and applications that interface in all aspects of the company. We are passionate about what we do.

The Bloomberg Terminal is built on top of an internal technology platform that supports thousands of unique, mission critical financial applications with tens of millions of executions daily. The software development lifecycle (SDLC) tooling that underpins this platform enables hundreds of engineering teams to build, test, release, and operate software reliably, securely, and at scale.

This role will be responsible for reimagining how Bloomberg engineers build and deliver Terminal applications by shaping the next generation of SDLC tooling, workflows, and integrations needed to support AI-enabled development, increased automation, and future-ready engineering practices.

What’s in it for you

We are looking for a Technical Product Manager to shape and execute the vision and roadmap for the SDLC tooling ecosystem that supports the Bloomberg Terminal. As a product manager, you will focus on the end-to-end developer experience across the software lifecycle, share ownership of the product roadmap for our internal development tools, and build new capabilities and integrations that empower engineering teams to move faster with confidence. You’ll be making decisions on how to evolve the develop, build, test, CI/CD, release, and product support workflows for the age of AI.

You will work in a group of other highly motivated product managers that work on a range of adjacent projects around application development and platform enablement. You will interact very closely with internal stakeholders across product, infrastructure, and engineering. This is a high-leverage role in a very cross-functional environment, so you’ll need to be comfortable wearing many hats and balancing technical depth with business and operational priorities.

We’ll trust you to


Define the next-generation Terminal application SDLC tooling strategy, setting principles for automation, consistency, and AI-assisted development
Shape standards, workflows, and tooling that improve developer velocity, software quality, and operational reliability across the Terminal ecosystem
Build strong relationships with key stakeholders, providing transparency into roadmaps, dependencies, and prioritization decisions
Advocate for and project manage wide-spread adoption of application SDLC best practices and tooling across engineering teams; measure adoption and impact
Own processes for intake, evaluation, and prioritization of new tooling and workflow requests from engineering teams
Balance competing needs – developer experience improvements, platform reliability, security requirements, and modernization efforts – to prioritize the backlog
Review and prioritize development of and/or updates to relevant technical documentation, onboarding materials, and training resources


You’ll need to have


7+ years of experience in technical product management or a senior engineering role
Experience building or owning developer tools, internal platforms, or SDLC systems with engineers as your primary users
A proven track record of collaboratively creating product visions and roadmaps, evaluating technical trade-offs, prioritizing at macro and micro levels, and managing releases
The ability to work across teams to break down complex engineering and operational problems into achievable milestones
Deep understanding of software development lifecycle best practices
Organizational and communication skills to effectively coordinate and work with engineers, SREs, security partners, other product managers, and senior management


We’d love to see


A degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, similar field of study or equivalent work experience
Familiarity with build systems, CI/CD pipelines, testing frameworks, and release automation tooling
Familiarity with AI-assisted development tools, agentic workflows, and emerging standards for AI integration in SDLC processes
Experience with developer experience design, tooling observability, and measuring developer productivity and quality outcomes
Open source involvement or community presence


If indicated, please note that years of experience are a guide; we will consider applications from all candidates who can demonstrate the skills necessary for the role.
