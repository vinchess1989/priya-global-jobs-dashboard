Barangaroo, Sydney | Technology Portfolio Delivery




This is an opportunity to combine hands-on senior business analysis with the leadership of a small BA team. You'll play an important role in Teachers Mutual Bank Limited's funded, multi-year technology transformation, shaping practical solutions across service management, operational resilience, identity and privileged access.




Around 80% of your role will focus on delivering complex technology initiatives, with the remaining time dedicated to coaching, quality assurance and coordinating BA capability. You'll work across legacy systems and emerging platforms, influencing the future state while seeing the direct impact of your work within a purpose-led mutual bank.




Why this role matters




Technology is central to the bank's growth and the experiences it provides to members and employees. Your work will help create clearer system information, stronger technology controls and more resilient services that can be maintained beyond individual projects.

In your first year, you'll contribute to initiatives spanning ServiceNow, IT service management, identity governance and privileged access management.




What you'll do




Lead end-to-end business analysis across complex technology projects, resolving ambiguity and aligning business, technology, Risk, Cyber and external partners.
Define current and future states, process flows, requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria and traceability through testing, implementation and operational readiness.
Shape ServiceNow ITSM, ITOM, CMDB, discovery, service mapping, monitoring integrations, service catalogue and employee self-service requirements.
Translate risk, control and regulatory obligations into testable workflows, approvals, evidence and reporting.
Define requirements across IAM, IGA and PAM, including identity lifecycles, access governance, application onboarding and privileged access.
Coach and support a small team of Business Analysts, maintaining quality standards and coordinating BA demand across technology projects.




What you'll bring




Essential

Senior business analysis experience delivering complex technology initiatives from discovery through to implementation and operational readiness.
Hands-on experience with ServiceNow ITSM, with exposure to ITOM, CMDB, discovery, service mapping, employee self-service or monitoring integrations.
An understanding of ITIL, service ownership, service dependencies and ServiceNow's Common Service Data Model.
Significant experience across Identity and Access Management, Identity Governance and Administration or Privileged Access Management initiatives.
Experience defining identity lifecycle, access approval, role and entitlement, segregation of duties, certification or privileged-access requirements.
Experience coaching or managing Business Analysts while remaining actively involved in project delivery.
Strong facilitation and process-design capability, with the judgement to align technical, business, risk and vendor stakeholders.




Nice to have

Experience working in banking, financial services or another regulated environment.
Experience translating regulatory or risk obligations into operational controls and evidence.
Tertiary qualifications in information technology, business analysis or a related discipline.
A recognised business analysis certification.




If you bring much of this experience but not every element, we'd still encourage you to apply.




About TMBL




Teachers Mutual Bank Limited is one of Australia's largest mutual banks.

For 60 years, we've been helping Australians achieve their financial goals while staying true to our purpose: banking for good.




As a 100% member-owned bank, our profits are reinvested to benefit our members, communities and the causes that matter most. Our commitment to social and environmental impact is embedded in everything we do - from the products and services we offer to the way we support our people and communities.




We're proud to be a Certified B Corp, reflecting our commitment to high standards of social and environmental performance, transparency and accountability.




Across our five retail brands, we support more than 280,000 members, including many working in education, emergency services and healthcare - people who make a difference in communities every day.




When you join our bank, you'll become part of a values-led organisation where passion, excellence, growth and sustainability guide the way we work. Together, we're creating an inclusive culture, delivering great member experiences, and proving that banking can be a force for good.
