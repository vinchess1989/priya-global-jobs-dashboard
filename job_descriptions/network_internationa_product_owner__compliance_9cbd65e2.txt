About Us

JOB DESCRIPTION

Network International is the largest Financial Technology company in Middle East and Africa. Payments is our core business where we provide services in more than 50 countries – UAE, Jordan, South Africa, Egypt are some of our key markets. Apart from payments, we provide services on Data and Insights, Lending, Insurance, Risk Solutions, etc. Our core customers are businesses at every scale and segment, though recently we are growing in direct to consumer card segment as well.

Our EVP's

At Network International, we always stay ahead. . In the fast-paced world of financial services, we thrive on innovation, agility, and purposeful collaboration. We invest first in our people, empowering you to make bold decisions, learn fast, and grow your expertise alongside industry leaders. Here, solving complex problems means more than using cutting-edge technology; it’s about creating meaningful value for our customers, together. We foster a culture where trust, accountability, and achievement go hand in hand—because success isn’t just a goal; it’s how we work, every day, as one team.

Job Description

Own and prioritise the compliance backlog, balancing mandate deadlines, risk, and business value.


Track Visa, Mastercard, and other scheme releases and mandates, and assess their impact on our products.
Manage requirements from bank sponsors and partners, and agree timelines and scope with them.
Write clear user stories and acceptance criteria for engineering and QA.
Work with Technology, Risk, Operations, Legal, and Programme teams to plan and deliver releases.
Report progress, risks, and dependencies, and escalate deadline risks early.
Keep delivery evidence so compliance readiness can be demonstrated when asked.


What You Bring


Experience as a Product Owner or Business Analyst in payments, card processing, or banking.
Working knowledge of card scheme rules, mandates, and certification.
Strong agile delivery, backlog management, and requirements writing skills.
Confidence with senior stakeholders and external partners.
Clear communication and attention to detail under tight deadlines.


Responsibilities

Backlog and prioritisation
