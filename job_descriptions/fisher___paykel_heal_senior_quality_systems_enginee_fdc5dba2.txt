Job description:

At Fisher & Paykel Healthcare, we're developing the next generation of medical devices that have the potential to improve patient outcomes worldwide.

We're looking for a Senior Quality Systems Engineer to join our Surgery & Emerging Technology team. Working within a small, agile team, you'll provide technical quality input across new product development programmes while helping shape and improve the quality systems that support emerging technologies.

About The Role

As a senior member of the team, you'll work closely with engineers, scientists and project teams to ensure quality is effectively integrated throughout product development.

Key responsibilities include:


Acting as a strategic quality partner for cross-functional teams to solve complex technical and quality challenges
Influencing quality strategy and decision-making across development programmes
Continuously improving practices and develop innovative quality solutions
Supporting design control and risk management activities


About You

You are an experienced quality professional who enjoys working in an environment where innovation, collaboration and problem-solving are valued.

You will bring:


A minimum of 4 years related work experience in either Quality Engineering, or in a similar regulated industry
Strong knowledge of quality systems and quality engineering principles
A pragmatic, solutions-focused approach to quality
Confidence working in situations with ambiguity and limited precedent
Excellent communication skills, with the ability to explain complex concepts, positively influence stakeholders, negotiate outcomes, and challenge constructively with respect and diplomacy
A collaborative mindset and openness to new ideas and ways of working


Experience In Medical Devices Is Advantageous But Not Essential.

Highly Desirable


A working knowledge of sterilisation regulation and processing
Experience supporting product development activities
Understanding of design controls and risk management
