Associate Director – Project Controls & Data Centre (Ireland and Europe)

Senior Document Control Lead | US Projects (Dublin-Based / Hybrid)

Location: Dublin (Hybrid)

Hours: US Time Zone Aligned

Are you an experienced Document Controller looking for a high-impact, global role with a premier, award-winning construction & engineering consultancy?

Our client—an industry-leading, B Corp certified consultancy—is expanding its international footprint and looking for a Document Control Lead to own and standardise information management across high-profile US developments (Data Centres, Energy, Infrastructure, and Life Sciences).

💡 Why You’ll Love This Role

World-Class Reputation: Award-winning (ICE & Irish Construction Industry Awards) and fully accredited across ISO standards (including ISO 19650).
Work-Life Balance & Flexibility: Modern hybrid setup with full US-time alignment from Dublin.
Top-Tier Package: Competitive salary, annual reviews, matched pension, 25 days leave + bank holidays, paid parental leave, Wellchoice wellness benefit, TaxSaver, and Bike to Work.
Growth & Innovation: Clear CPD pathways, chartership support, and a forward-thinking culture that actively encourages using AI and automation to innovate traditional workflows.

🎯 Key Responsibilities

End-to-End Ownership: Set up and lead document control standards, folder structures, metadata, naming conventions, and revision control on major US projects from mobilization to close-out.
CDE Administration: Manage Common Data Environments (SharePoint, Autodesk Forma/ACC, Procore) and oversee user access, permissions, and review workflows.
Standards & Compliance: Draft and maintain Information Management Plans in line with ISO 19650 and specific US client requirements; keep project records audit-ready.
Cross-Functional Partnering: Provide clean, traceable, and dated records for planners, project analytics teams, and delay analysts.
Point of Contact: Serve as the primary document control lead during US working hours for client and contractor leads.

🔍 What We’re Looking For

Strong, hands-on document control leadership experience within construction, engineering, or major infrastructure projects.
Expertise administering CDE environments (SharePoint, Autodesk ACC/Forma, Procore).
Solid working knowledge of ISO 19650 standards and setting up document control systems from scratch.
Advanced Excel skills for register management, lookups, and status reporting.
Comfortable working US-aligned hours ongoing from a Dublin hybrid base.
A proactive, tech-minded approach—experience leveraging AI or automation tools to streamline transmittals and registers is a big bonus!




What’s on Offer?

This role offers significant scope for professional progression and long-term career development. Alongside a highly competitive salary, the client provides a site-based structure with flexible, hybrid working arrangements subject to agreement.
