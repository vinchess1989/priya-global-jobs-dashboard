We invest in our employees, providing them with the support and resources they need to succeed. As a member of our global team, you can expect exciting, varied responsibilities as well as a wide range of development prospects. Because we believe that your career path should be as unique as you are.

Group Summary

The Magna Exteriors portfolio of products includes access systems such as liftgates, exterior trim, modular systems, front-end modules including fascia, active aerodynamic systems and other lightweight structural components for automotive, commercial truck and other industrial markets.

Recognized globally as an innovator in all aspects of vehicle exteriors, Magna provides everything needed, from materials development and design through manufacturing and assembly, to help automakers create sleek, state-of-the-art vehicles across the world.

Job Responsibilities

SKILLS/EXPERIENCE REQUIRED:

Educational Requirements: University degree in Engineering, Quality, Manufacturing or a related technical discipline; an equivalent combination of relevant post-secondary education and extensive automotive quality experience may be considered.

Minimum Experience: Five to eight years of progressive automotive manufacturing quality experience, including program launch, direct customer contact and leadership of complex quality issues.

Knowledge Required: Advanced knowledge of IATF 16949, AIAG Core Tools, PPAP, APQP, Process Flow Diagrams, PFMEA, Control Plans, MSA, SPC, capability analysis, GD&T, blueprint interpretation, metrology, customer-specific requirements and structured problem-solving methods including 8D, 7D, 5 Why, PRR and QPR.

Computer Skills: Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Visio; ability to analyze quality data and prepare clear technical reports and management presentations.

Additional Certificates/Diplomas: ASQ Certified Quality Engineer, IATF 16949 Internal Auditor, VDA 6.3, Six Sigma Green Belt or Black Belt, and AIAG Core Tools training are preferred.

Licenses/Permits Required: N/R

Physical Requirements: N/R

Communication Skills: Excellent written, verbal, presentation, negotiation and interpersonal skills, with the ability to communicate technical issues effectively to customers, suppliers, shop-floor personnel and senior management.

Additional Education/Experience: Demonstrated experience leading cross-functional teams, customer escalations, audits, corrective actions, supplier quality improvement and the mentoring of quality personnel.

Key Competencies For This Position


Advanced and independent application of AIAG Core Tools, customer-specific requirements and quality-system standards.
Strong analytical judgment and disciplined problem solving using statistical and risk-based methods.
Technical leadership, accountability and sound decision-making under customer and production pressure.
Ability to influence and lead Customers, Suppliers, Operators, Engineers and cross-functional teams without direct authority.
Effective coaching and mentoring of quality and manufacturing personnel.
Continuous improvement focus with demonstrated ability to prevent defects, reduce variation and improve customer satisfaction.
