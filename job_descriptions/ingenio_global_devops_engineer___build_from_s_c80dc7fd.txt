Ever wished you could build the thing instead of just keeping it alive?

Good news, this DevOps role is exactly that!




We’re working with a really interesting Irish tech company, a UCD spin-out backed by national and European funding, that’s using cutting-edge technology for Crystal Structure Prediction to speed up drug research and development. They already work with leading global pharma companies and research institutes, and they’re now hiring a DevOps Engineer to build and deploy the ETL pipeline platform behind it all.




A taste of what you’ll get stuck into

Building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines for self-hosted runners
Designing, deploying and running a self-hosted observability stack (metrics, logs and alerting) for services and containers
Managing containerised workloads across distributed infrastructure
Writing infrastructure-as-code to provision and manage services
Chatting architecture and scalability with the CTO and R&D team




What would make you a great fit

3+ years’ experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering or SRE
Strong Linux fundamentals, including filesystem hierarchy and permissions, process and system management, command-line proficiency, basic shell scripting and network basics
Proficiency with containerisation (Docker, Podman etc.) and orchestration (Docker Compose or Swarm)
Strong Python fundamentals, including core data structures and algorithms, data modelling, package and environment management, and testing practices
Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders
A self-directed, problem-solving mindset, comfortable taking initiative and driving projects through to completion




What’s in it for you

Competitive salary based on experience
10% annual bonus
Participation in the company ESOP
Company pension scheme




So, why this one?

Because it’s not about ticket queues or looking after somebody else’s setup. You’ll build the platform from the ground up, work directly with the CTO, and see your work help drive genuine progress in drug discovery.

If you’re a DevOps Engineer who loves getting under the hood and building the good stuff, we’d love to hear from you.
