Role : Data Compliance Specialist




Overview : The Data Compliance Specialist provides day-to-day quality and data integrity oversight for maintenance and project records in the site maintenance management system (CMMS) and related controlled documentation. The role reviews and approves completed work orders, supports technicians with work order and technical queries, and checks project handover packs before they are presented to the client for acceptance. Acting as the client’s quality and data integrity representative, the Specialist helps ensure records are complete, accurate, traceable and compliant with site procedures and applicable GxP requirements.




Key responsibilities

Work order review and approval: Review completed work orders for technical accuracy, clear evidence of work performed, appropriate materials and readings, required checks, supporting documents and complete approvals. Approve or return records in accordance with delegated authority and site procedures. Record deficiencies and follow them through to resolution before approval.
Technician support: Respond to technician queries on work order completion, record requirements, data entry and technical documentation.
Project handover packs: Review project handover documentation for completeness, consistency and traceability before submission for client acceptance.
Client quality and data integrity representation: Represent the client’s requirements in quality compliance and data integrity matters.
CMMS and controlled data: Support consistent asset master data, work order data and maintenance history records within GMARS and associated systems.
Issues, trends and improvement: Raise recurring record-quality issues, deviations or potential data integrity concerns through the site process. Support deviation investigations, CAPA development and effectiveness reviews related to documentation and data integrity issues.
Communication and reporting: Maintain clear communication and monitor/report KPIs relating to documentation quality, review cycle times, recurring deficiencies, data integrity trends and right-first-time completion rates.




Quality and compliance expectations.

Apply site procedures and ALCOA+ principles and work in a manner consistent with GxP requirements.

Authority and working relationships

The Specialist may approve completed work orders and quality records only within formally delegated authority, following completion of approved training and qualification requirements, and in accordance with applicable site procedures. The role works closely with Maintenance Technicians, Shift Leads/Chargehands, Planners, Engineers, Project Managers, Quality Assurance and Client Representatives.




Skills and experience.

Experience reviewing maintenance work orders or controlled records in a regulated manufacturing, pharmaceutical or similar environment.
Working knowledge of GMP/GxP expectations and data integrity principles, including ALCOA+.
Ability to understand maintenance and project documentation and identify missing, inconsistent or technically unclear records.
Experience with a CMMS such as SPA/Maximo.
Strong attention to detail, sound judgement and confidence to raise concerns constructively.
Clear written and verbal communication skills.

Desirable background

Relevant qualification in Engineering, Science, Quality Assurance, Validation, Maintenance Management or a related discipline, or equivalent industry experience.
A technical trade qualification or engineering background is desirable.
Experience in a maintenance planning function is advantageous.
Working knowledge of GMP documentation standards, change control processes, deviation management and data integrity expectations within regulated manufacturing environments.
