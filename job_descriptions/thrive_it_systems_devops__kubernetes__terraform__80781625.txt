Senior Recruitment Consultant @ Thrive IT Systems Ltd | Bachelor of Science - BS



Seeking a Specialist with Hands on years of experience in DevOpsCICD to design automate and manage containerized infrastructure using Ansible Docker Terraform Shell Scripting and Kubernetes.







Responsibilities







Roles and Responsibilities Lead the design and implementation of Kubernetes based infrastructure solutions aligned with organizational goals.
Automate routine operational and deployment tasks using Ansible playbooks Shell scripts Terraform modules and Docker workflows.
Ensure adherence to security best practices throughout container orchestration and infrastructure automation.
Collaborate with cross functional teams to gather requirements and deliver robust scalable and secure infrastructure solutions.
Participate in capacity planning disaster recovery and backup strategy formulation for Kubernetes environments.
Mentor and provide technical guidance to junior team members to foster knowledge sharing and skill development.
Document infrastructure configurations automation workflows and troubleshooting procedures to maintain a comprehensive knowledge base.
Drive continuous improvement initiatives within the DevOpsCICD skill cluster.







Required Skills
