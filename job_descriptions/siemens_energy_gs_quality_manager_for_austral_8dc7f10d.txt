A Snapshot of Your Day

We are currently on the lookout for a Senior Quality Engineer/Manager to join our team considering the expanding market opportunities in the dynamic energy sector.

Quality has always been a cornerstone of our organization and business. Therefore, we are seeking someone with a solid understanding of Quality fundamentals and extensive experience in developing, implementing, and managing all aspects of Quality Assurance and Quality Control within both project and manufacturing environments.

As a Senior Quality Engineer/Manager, you will play a pivotal role in interfacing with various stakeholders. This makes strong communication and leadership skills essential. Additionally, collaboration attributes are highly valued. Your ability to work effectively with diverse teams, foster a cooperative environment, and build strong relationships will be critical in helping us achieve our goals more efficiently.

How You’ll Make an Impact

Act as the Quality Champion for the Australian business, driving quality initiatives and ensuring alignment with regional, business, and global quality requirements, management systems, standards, and applicable codes.
Lead and maintain the country Quality Management System (QMS), conducting gap assessments and driving continuous improvement of quality processes, procedures, and standards.
Plan, perform, and coordinate internal, project, and external audits, working closely with regional teams, business stakeholders, customers, regulatory bodies, and accreditation organizations to ensure timely closure of findings.
Drive effective Non-Conformance (NC) management, guiding cross-functional teams through Root Cause Analysis (RCA), corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), and follow-up to ensure sustainable resolution and prevent recurrence.
Partner with internal and external stakeholders throughout the project and operational lifecycle, providing quality guidance, training, reporting, and support to ensure requirements are effectively understood and implemented.
Champion continuous improvement through Lean Management, Six Sigma, simplification, digitalization, and other improvement initiatives to strengthen quality performance and contribute to key business KPIs.

What You Bring 

Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Quality, or a related technical field, with 8–12 years of hands-on Quality Management experience covering both Quality Assurance and Quality Control.
Relevant experience within the energy, power generation, power plant, or oil & gas industry, with a strong understanding of quality requirements in technical, project, or operational environments.
Proven hands-on experience in Non-Conformance (NC) management, Root Cause Analysis (RCA), and corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), with the ability to drive issues through to effective closure.
Strong knowledge of Quality Management Systems (QMS), applicable quality standards and codes, together with an ISO 9001 Lead Auditor or Internal Auditor certification.
Strong analytical, leadership, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to guide and collaborate with cross-functional teams and drive continuous improvement.
Excellent written and verbal English communication skills in a global environment. Experience with Lean/Six Sigma, integrated management systems, EHS auditing, or Power BI would be an advantage.

About the Team

Our Gas Services division offers Low-emission power generation through service and decarbonization. Zero or low emission power generation and all gas turbines under one roof, steam turbines and generators. Decarbonization opportunities through service offerings, modernization, and digitalization of the fleet.

Who is Siemens Energy?

At Siemens Energy, we are more than just an energy technology company. With ~100,000 dedicated employees in more than 90 countries, we develop energy systems of the future, ensuring that the growing energy demand of the global community is met reliably and sustainably. The technologies created in our research departments and factories drive the energy transition and provide the base for one sixth of the world's electricity generation.

Our global team is committed to making sustainable, reliable, and affordable energy a reality by pushing the boundaries of what is possible. We uphold a 150-year legacy of innovation that encourages our search for people who will support our focus on decarbonization, new technologies, and energy transformation.
