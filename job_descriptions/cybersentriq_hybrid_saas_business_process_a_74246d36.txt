CyberSentriq in Galway, Ireland, seeks a Business Systems Analyst on a 12-month maternity cover. You will bridge the gap between business needs and the systems that support them, including Salesforce, Maxio, NetSuite and Freshdesk, delivering well-designed, tested and usable solutions.

Day to day, you will map processes, gather requirements, run workshops, write test cases and support UAT, while configuring logic and tracking changes in Jira. A hybrid pattern with a Galway office is available.
