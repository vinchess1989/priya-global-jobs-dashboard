📌 About Us




Posha is building robot kitchens for the future.




Our first product, the Posha Countertop was recognized as one of TIME’s Best Inventions of 2025 and received rave reviews from The Verge and WIRED for bringing robotics and AI into everyday kitchens.




We’re a fast-growing Series A–funded startup backed by Accel Partners.




See it in action: https://youtu.be/Ubd6NkZOEkM?si=SFex-isehKHzvZs_




🤔 What can I expect from this role?




Posha is one of the rare robotics startups building production-grade consumer robots, with hundreds of units deployed in real homes.




This role involves working on defining system level requirements across different functions of mechanical, electrical, firmware and software.




🔧 What will my work responsibilities look like if I join Posha?




Convert business, customer & product needs into clear, measurable engineering requirements and specifications.
Work with design teams to translate system requirements into subsystem-level specifications & resolve gaps or trade-offs.
Work with the validation team to define acceptance criteria and confirm that the final design meets all requirements.




😍 What makes you a match for us?




3+ years hands-on experience turning product needs into measurable requirements for electromechanical products and allocating them across subsystems.
Proven ability to resolve cross-subsystem trade-offs and verify that the finished product meets its requirements.

Why Posha?




Few organizations offer challenges similar to ours. Building completely autonomous cooking robots requires thinking from first principles and building from ground up.
We believe that Posha is a generational company with the opportunity to alter the trajectory of how food is cooked inside homes today. This opportunity is parallel to how autonomous cars have made an impact.
This role will be at the forefront of this opportunity.
