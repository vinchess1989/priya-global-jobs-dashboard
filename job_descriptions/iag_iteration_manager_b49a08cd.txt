At IAG, we’re ready for you. We're the largest general insurance group in Australia and New Zealand. Our family of brands turns the IAG purpose of making your world a safer place into action by helping everyday Aussies and New Zealanders, supporting their ambitions and making insurance accessible.

Drive delivery that makes a real impact

Join IAG's Data & AI team and help turn ideas into outcomes. As the Iteration Manager, you'll play a vital role in creating an environment where teams can do their best work. You'll support delivery squads to plan effectively, maintain momentum and achieve meaningful results. Working across business and technology teams, you'll help bring priorities to life through strong agile practices, collaboration and continuous improvement. If you enjoy bringing people together, removing barriers and helping teams succeed, we'd love to hear from you.

This is a permanent full-time role with the flexibility to be based at one of our locations across Aotearoa New Zealand.

Ngā Mahi Hei Whakatutuki Māu | What You'll Do


Facilitate iteration planning and agile ceremonies to support consistent and predictable delivery.
Coordinate work across teams to ensure priorities are understood and delivered effectively.
Monitor delivery progress and proactively address risks, blockers and dependencies.
Support squads to maintain strong workflow, collaboration and continuous improvement practices.
Maintain visibility of delivery through tools such as Jira and provide meaningful progress reporting.
Collaborate with leaders and stakeholders to ensure delivery outcomes align with business priorities.


Ōu Pūkenga me ōu Wheako | What You'll Bring


Experience supporting agile delivery in complex technology or transformation environments
Strong understanding of Scrum, Kanban or other agile delivery frameworks.
Ability to coordinate competing priorities and manage multiple dependencies
Experience facilitating workshops, planning sessions and team ceremonies.
Confidence working with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
A focus on teamwork, continuous improvement and helping others achieve shared outcomes.
