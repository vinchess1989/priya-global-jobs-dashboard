Company Overview

PRESCRIRE Solutions is building AICA, an AI-powered clinical assistant platform that automates end-to-end healthcare workflows from patient intake to billing submission. Backed by NRC IRAP funding and positioned for global expansion, we're revolutionizing healthcare administration while establishing Montreal as a global leader in healthcare AI innovation.

Our Mission

To eliminate the administrative burden crisis affecting millions of healthcare professionals worldwide, enabling physicians to reclaim 10-15 hours per week and focus on what matters most: patient care.

About The Role

Build and maintain the cloud infrastructure supporting AICA's global deployment. You'll ensure 99.9% uptime while managing multi-region deployments, automated scaling, and disaster recovery for mission-critical healthcare systems built to serve thousands of physicians.

Key Responsibilities


Infrastructure Management: Maintain and scale cloud platform deployment with data residency and multi-region capabilities.
CI/CD Pipeline Development: Build sophisticated automated deployment pipelines enabling rapid iteration and zero-downtime deployments.
Monitoring & Alerting: Implement comprehensive observability, logging, and alerting systems for 24/7 operations.
Scaling Architecture: Design and implement auto-scaling solutions for rapidly growing user base and enterprise customers.
Disaster Recovery: Implement robust backup, recovery, and business continuity procedures for mission-critical data protection.


Required Qualifications


3+ years DevOps or cloud infrastructure experience with enterprise-scale deployments.
Strong experience with Google Cloud Platform or AWS including compute, storage, and networking services.
Proficiency with containerization technologies including Docker and Kubernetes orchestration.
Experience with Infrastructure as Code tools including Terraform, Ansible, or similar automation platforms.
Knowledge of monitoring, logging, and alerting tools for production system management.


Preferred Qualifications


Healthcare IT infrastructure experience with compliance-focused cloud deployments.
Experience with multi-region deployments and Canadian data residency requirements.
Knowledge of high-availability system design and disaster recovery planning.
Background in security-focused infrastructure and compliance automation.


Why Join PRESCRIRE Solutions?

We're not just building software, we're revolutionizing how healthcare works globally, creating massive value for providers, patients, and shareholders.

4. Final Interview: Strategic discussion with CEO Patrick Mousseau.
