Risk & Quality Manager | Drive Governance, Quality & Continuous Improvement
Full time Permanent | Hawthorn, Melbourne | Hybrid working structure (2 days from Home)


We're partnering with a respected organisation delivering complex projects and services that support government and community outcomes. As the Risk & Quality Manager, you'll play a critical role in ensuring robust governance, compliance, quality assurance and continuous improvement across the organisation.

This is an excellent opportunity for professionals who have built their careers in the public sector, local government, healthcare, community services, infrastructure, education, or other highly regulated environments and are looking to apply their expertise in a strategic leadership role.


About the Organisation
Our client is a well-established organisation operating within a highly regulated environment, delivering projects and services that require strong governance, accountability and compliance.

With a commitment to best practice, continuous improvement and operational excellence, they are seeking an experienced Risk & Quality Manager to strengthen and evolve their risk and quality frameworks.


About the Role
Based in Hawthorn, Victoria, this permanent full-time position will lead the development and implementation of risk management and quality assurance initiatives across multiple programs and projects.
You will work collaboratively with project teams, operational leaders and executive stakeholders to identify risks, improve quality performance, ensure compliance with regulatory requirements, and drive a culture of continuous improvement throughout the organisation.

This role is ideal for someone who enjoys balancing strategic oversight with hands-on leadership and influencing positive organisational outcomes.

 
Key Responsibilities

Develop and implement risk management frameworks, strategies and policies across projects and operational functions
Identify, assess and mitigate organisational and project-related risks
Conduct quality audits, assessments and compliance reviews to ensure adherence to internal standards and regulatory requirements
Partner with project teams to identify risk factors and implement practical action plans
Prepare and present risk, compliance and quality reports to senior leadership and key stakeholders
Facilitate risk management and quality improvement workshops across the organisation
Promote a culture of quality, accountability and continuous improvement
Lead investigations into quality-related issues and implement appropriate corrective actions
Monitor emerging industry trends, legislation and best practice frameworks to ensure ongoing compliance and effectiveness
Support governance activities and contribute to organisational planning and decision-making
  
About You

Bachelor's degree in Risk Management, Quality Assurance, Public Administration, Business, Health, Governance or a related discipline
Minimum 4 years' experience in Risk Management, Quality, Compliance or Governance
Experience working within the public sector, healthcare, community services, local government, infrastructure, education or similar regulated environments
Strong analytical skills with the ability to assess complex challenges and develop practical solutions
Experience leading audits, investigations and continuous improvement initiatives
Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills
Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and projects
Experience using risk management systems, quality frameworks and reporting tools
Relevant certifications such as CRM, Quality Management Systems (QMS), Governance or similar qualifications will be highly regarded
