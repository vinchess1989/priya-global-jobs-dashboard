Site Systems Data Integrity Lead (South Leinster)




Role purpose

This position provides leadership for the governance of data integrity and the validation of GxP-relevant computerised systems. The role ensures that systems are appropriately validated, controlled and maintained in line with cGMP and applicable regulatory expectations throughout their lifecycle. It also provides validation direction for new implementations, upgrades and digital transformation activity, helping systems move into operation in a compliant and fully validated state.




What success looks like

Data integrity risks are visible, assessed and actively managed through suitable controls, metrics and follow-up actions.
GxP computerised systems remain in a validated state through disciplined change control, periodic review and lifecycle management.
Validation requirements are built into projects from the outset rather than addressed after implementation.
Documentation and operational practices support ongoing audit and regulatory inspection readiness.




Core accountabilities




1. Data integrity governance

Lead the management of data integrity across the operation and serve as the principal subject matter expert for this area.
Coordinate with data integrity subject matter experts to identify gaps and ensure that required actions are addressed.
Own and maintain the data integrity policy.
Maintain data integrity risk assessment and establish relevant KPIs to support ongoing risk management.
Confirm that effective data integrity controls are embedded in systems and business processes.
Deliver data integrity training to relevant groups when required.




2. Computerised system validation and lifecycle control

Act as subject matter expert for computer system validation and the associated regulatory compliance requirements.
Ensure GxP systems are validated and continue to meet applicable regulatory expectations.
Maintain validated status through change control, periodic review and system lifecycle activities.
Apply risk-based validation principles so the level of assurance is appropriate to system risk and intended use.
Coordinate change controls, quality records and other compliance activities within the function.




3. Validation documentation

Create and maintain validation master plans, protocols, reports and support traceability records.
Develop and maintain SOPs and WISOPs that define validation strategy and lifecycle processes.
Prepare and execute qualification and test documentation, including IQ, OQ and PQ protocols.
Confirm that system qualification packages and related deliverables meet required compliance standards.




4. Project and digital change support

Provide validation oversight for new system implementations, upgrades and digital transformation initiatives.
Introduce validation requirements at project initiation using a validation-by-design approach.
Work with IT, Quality and Business stakeholders to support successful project delivery.
Support the controlled transfer of implemented systems into an operational, validated state.
Partner with the Quality Validation team to complete required validation deliverables.




5. Inspection and audit readiness

Lead and support preparation activities for audits and regulatory inspections.
Perform or facilitate risk assessments to determine the appropriate validation approach and level of effort.
Interpret regulatory requirements, including US FDA expectations, and translate them into practical, compliant controls and ways of working.




Professional capabilities

Strong analytical thinking and structured problem solving.
Excellent technical writing and documentation skills.
Ability to convert regulatory expectations into workable, compliant practices.
Effective stakeholder engagement and cross-functional collaboration.
High attention to detail, supported by strong organisation and prioritisation.
