Our client has reached out to us to assist in the search for a talented DevOps Engineer to join the Software Delivery Team. This role is integral to ensuring the reliability, scalability, and efficiency of the software delivery platforms and infrastructure. You’ll work closely with a passionate team of technologists, leveraging modern tools and platforms to support, optimise, and continuously improve how we build, deploy, and operate our systems.




Your will focus on automating infrastructure, optimising CI/CD pipelines, and ensuring the reliability and availability of our systems. Working closely with Development and QA teams, you will enable and support automation initiatives while ensuring seamless integration across development, testing, and production environments. Your technical expertise, problem‑solving ability, and proactive approach will be essential to success in this role.




The position also involves participation in a 24×7 on‑call roster, supporting business‑critical systems and ensuring service continuity outside standard working hours.




Key responsibilities

Design, implement, and maintain CI/CD pipelines to support continuous integration and delivery processes
Manage and optimize containerized environments using Docker and Kubernetes (on premises)
Administer and maintain database systems including MariaDB, MySQL, Oracle, and PostgreSQL
Configure and manage load balancers, SSL certificates, and secure system communications
Monitor system performance, availability, and reliability using tools such as Grafana and Prometheus
Develop automation scripts and tooling using Python and/or Bash
Collaborate closely with Software Developers & QA Engineers to support test automation within CI/CD pipelines
Troubleshoot infrastructure, deployment, and performance issues across environments
Continuously improve system scalability, resilience, and operational efficiency




Requirements

Educational Background: Bachelor’s degree in computer science, Software Engineering, or a related field
Professional Experience: Minimum of 3 years’ experience in a DevOps or similar role
Technical Skills: Strong experience with containerization technologies (Docker, Kubernetes)
Hands-on experience with CI/CD tools, preferably GitLab CI/CD
Solid understanding of database technologies (MariaDB/MySQL, Oracle, PostgreSQL)
Experience with load balancing and SSL configuration
Familiarity with monitoring and observability tools such as Grafana and Prometheus
Proficiency in scripting languages such as Python and Bash
Problem-Solving Abilities: Strong analytical and troubleshooting skills, with the ability to resolve complex infrastructure and deployment issues
Project Management: Strong organizational, time management, and prioritization skills
Communication: Ability to clearly communicate technical concepts and collaborate effectively with cross-functional teams, including QA and Development
