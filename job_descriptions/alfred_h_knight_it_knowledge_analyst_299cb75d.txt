WHAT IS ON OFFER




Alfred H Knight has an exciting opportunity for a detail-oriented, articulate IT Knowledge Analyst to join our growing IT function at Pegasus House (Prescot, Liverpool) on a 12-month fixed term contract. As an independent, family-owned business spanning five generations and operating across 51+ countries, we are continuously reinvesting in our digital capabilities—and this impactful role sits at the heart of how we empower our global workforce through clear, accessible IT self-service and AI-driven support tools.




Acting as a vital bridge between IT support teams, technical subject matter experts, and end-users, you will capture, structure, and optimise our technical knowledge base—reducing ticket resolution times, boosting first-contact resolution, and ensuring our self-service portals and virtual agents deliver accurate answers first time.




In this role, you will:

Knowledge Lifecycle Management: Draft, edit, format, and publish clear, standardised knowledge articles, troubleshooting guides, SOPs, and FAQs, managing the full lifecycle from peer review through to periodic validation and archiving.
KCS Best Practice & Coaching: Embed Knowledge-Centered Service (KCS) principles across the IT Service Desk and wider IT teams, facilitating knowledge-sharing sessions and coaching Tier 1 and Tier 2 analysts to capture reusable solutions in their daily workflow.
IT Training & Enablement: Design and deliver engaging training materials—including quick-reference guides, interactive walkthroughs, video tutorials, and onboarding curricula—for new enterprise software rollouts, system upgrades, and IT security awareness.
Content Auditing & AI Readiness: Audit the knowledge base to eliminate content gaps, duplicates, and broken links, while structuring metadata, taxonomies, and keywords to optimise searchability across ITSM portals and conversational AI virtual agents.
Analytics & Continuous Improvement: Monitor article usage, zero-result searches, ticket deflection rates, and recurring incident trends to prioritise high-impact documentation and evaluate training effectiveness.




ABOUT US




Alfred H Knight is a totally independent, family owned business spanning five generations. A global network of strategically placed offices and laboratories enable global trade by providing independent inspection, analysis and consultancy services to the metals and minerals, solid fuels and agriculture industries.




We have honed and carefully crafted our reputation. Delivering knowledge and professionalism in all aspects of weighing, sampling and analysis. We thrive by continuing to re-invest in our facilities, technology and people. Click here to find out more about AHK.




DO YOU HAVE WHAT IT TAKES?




To be successful at Alfred H Knight you will need to display the following:




REQUIRED KNOWLEDGE AND WORK EXPERIENCE




Essential

Enterprise IT Fluency: Broad foundational understanding of enterprise IT environments (such as Microsoft 365, Windows/macOS, Google Workspace, identity/access management, and SaaS applications) sufficient to translate complex technical steps into plain English.
ITSM & Knowledge Tools: Hands-on experience with enterprise ITSM and knowledge management platforms (such as ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, Confluence, Zendesk, or Freshservice).
ITIL & KCS Principles: Working knowledge of ITIL practices (specifically Knowledge, Incident, and Problem Management) and Knowledge-Centered Service (KCS) methodologies.
Exceptional Writing & Information Architecture: Strong written communication, editing, and structuring skills, with the ability to tailor tone and technical depth for both end-users and Tier 2/3 engineers.

Required Competencies

Analytical & Data-Driven Mindset: Ability to interpret search analytics, zero-result queries, ticket volume trends, and user feedback to prioritise high-value documentation.
Engaging Facilitator & Coach: Confidence delivering live training sessions, interactive walkthroughs, and workshops to upskill service desk colleagues and drive KCS adoption.
High Attention to Detail: Methodical approach to content governance, metadata tagging, version control, and maintaining accurate, up-to-date self-service resources.
Collaborative Bridge-Builder: Proactive interpersonal style with the ability to partner effectively with technical SMEs, support analysts, and non-technical stakeholders.

Required Work Experience

Proven background working within an IT Service Desk, technical writing, IT Service Management (ITSM), or knowledge management function.
Demonstrable experience creating end-user self-service guides, SOPs, and digital training materials for enterprise software or system rollouts.
Experience curating or structuring content for enterprise AI chatbots, virtual agents, or enterprise search engines (desirable).
Familiarity with HTML/Markdown formatting and basic UI/UX principles for self-service portals (desirable).

Required Qualifications

ITIL 4 Foundation certification or KCS v6 Fundamentals certification (desirable).
Relevant degree or professional qualification in Information Technology, Technical Communication, or a related discipline—or equivalent practical industry experience.
