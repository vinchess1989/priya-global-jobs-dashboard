If you’re looking to make a difference in your own backyard, this career is for you.

If finding complex technological solutions is your game, we've got the team for you. You're going to help us create seamless operations that result in efficiency for millions of customers.

In this role, you'll:

Coordinate and manage application releases across test and production environments, ensuring all development, testing and approval requirements are met prior to deployment. You will maintain the Forward Schedule of Releases, lead release readiness reviews and facilitate Go/No-Go decision forums to support successful implementations. Working closely with project, development, testing and support teams, you will manage release risks, stakeholder communications and governance requirements. You will prepare release documentation and release notes, oversee deployment activities, and ensure alignment with change and infrastructure schedules. During implementation, you will monitor progress, resolve issues and escalate risks as required. Following deployment, you will support Early Life Support activities and ensure all release records and actions are formally completed.

For more information on this position and business unit, view the role description and information pack.

About You

You are an experienced Release Management professional with a strong understanding of software development lifecycles, release and deployment processes, and ITIL-based service management practices. You have successfully coordinated complex technology releases within large enterprise environments and can confidently manage competing priorities and stakeholder expectations. You possess excellent communication and relationship management skills and can influence decisions across both technical and business audiences. Your ability to identify risks early, solve problems and maintain service stability enables you to deliver high-quality outcomes. You are highly organised, detail-oriented and committed to continuous improvement. Relevant tertiary qualifications or equivalent professional experience will be highly regarded.
