🚀 We're Hiring: Change, Release & Transition Manager




📍 Location: Leeds / Newcastle

📌 Employment type - Permanent




Are you passionate about driving seamless change, successful releases, and smooth service transitions in complex IT environments?




Join Mastek as a Change, Release & Transition Manager and play a pivotal role in ensuring that business-critical changes are delivered safely, efficiently, and with minimal disruption across a multi-supplier environment.




What You'll Be Doing

✅ Lead end-to-end Change, Release, and Service Transition governance

✅ Drive ITIL v4 Change Enablement and Release Management best practices

✅ Coordinate multiple suppliers within a SIAM environment

✅ Oversee service transitions, operational readiness, testing, deployment, and early-life support

✅ Manage release schedules, dependencies, risk assessments, and CAB processes

✅ Ensure changes are thoroughly validated, approved, communicated, and successfully implemented

✅ Lead post-implementation reviews, continuous improvement initiatives, and supplier performance management

✅ Act as the SME for Change & Release Management, influencing service quality and operational excellence




What We're Looking For

🔹 5+ years of Change and Release Management experience

🔹 Strong experience working within SIAM and multi-supplier environments

🔹 Proven expertise in Service Transition Management

🔹 Solid understanding of ITIL Change Enablement, Release Management, and Service Management governance

🔹 Excellent stakeholder management and supplier coordination skills

🔹 Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication abilities

🔹 ITIL v4 Certification preferred (ITIL v3 desirable)




Why Join Mastek?

At Mastek, you'll be part of a collaborative and innovative team delivering high-impact services for our clients. We offer a challenging environment where your expertise will directly influence service stability, customer confidence, and operational success.




If you're ready to lead change with confidence and make a real impact, we'd love to hear from you.

📩 Apply now or reach out directly for more details.
