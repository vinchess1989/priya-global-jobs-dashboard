Your Role at Baxter

As a Quality Engineer, you will serve as a key technical expert responsible for ensuring manufacturing systems, equipment, and processes meet regulatory, quality, and data integrity requirements. You will lead validation initiatives that enable reliable product quality, support continuous improvement, and help maintain inspection readiness in a highly regulated healthcare manufacturing environment.

 

In this role, you will collaborate across multiple functions to develop and execute validation strategies, provide technical guidance, support change management activities, and help ensure the site remains compliant with Health Canada, GMP, ISO, and corporate quality standards. Your expertise will help safeguard patient safety while enabling innovation and operational excellence.

What You'll Be Doing

Lead the development, execution, and completion of validation programs for manufacturing equipment, processes, cleaning and computerized systems to ensure a validated state of control.
Author validation protocols and reports, analyze validation data, and apply statistical techniques to demonstrate compliance with regulatory and quality requirements.
Partner with Engineering, Production, Quality Control, and external vendors to plan, coordinate, and execute qualification activities including IQ, OQ, and PQ protocols.
Serve as a validation subject matter expert by providing technical guidance on process validation, cleaning validation, equipment qualification, computerized systems validation, and data integrity practices.
Support change control and quality system activities by assessing validation impacts, contributing to investigations, and leading resolution of validation-related non-conformances and corrective actions.
Participate in internal audits, regulatory inspections, and compliance activities while helping ensure ongoing readiness and adherence to applicable standards.
Drive continuous improvement initiatives by identifying opportunities to strengthen validation processes, improve efficiency, and support Baxter's operational excellence objectives.


What You'll Bring

Required Qualifications

Bachelor's degree in engineering, Chemistry, Microbiology, Pharmacy, Life Sciences, or a related scientific discipline.
5+ years of validation experience within a GMP-regulated pharmaceutical, biotechnology, medical device, or healthcare manufacturing environment.
Experience executing equipment qualification, process validation, cleaning validation and computerized systems validation, or test method validation activities.
Working knowledge of GMP regulations, PICs, ISO standards, data integrity requirements, and regulatory compliance expectations.
Strong technical writing skills with experience developing protocols, reports, investigations, technical assessments, and validation documentation.
Demonstrated ability to manage multiple projects while collaborating effectively with cross-functional teams.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and critical-thinking skills, including experience interpreting validation data and applying statistical tools.
Effective communication and relationship-building skills with the ability to influence stakeholders across all levels of the organization.


Preferred Qualifications

Experience supporting pharmaceutical manufacturing operations or liquid processing systems.
Knowledge of Health Canada regulations and experience supporting regulatory inspections.
Experience with TrackWise Change Management and CAPA systems.
Working knowledge of Minitab, Statistical Process Control (SPC), and data analysis methodologies.
Lean Manufacturing, Continuous Improvement, or Six Sigma Green Belt certification.
Experience supporting commissioning activities, technical investigations, and root cause analysis initiatives.
Familiarity with Baxter or similar corporate quality systems and validation requirements.
Experience working with ERP systems
