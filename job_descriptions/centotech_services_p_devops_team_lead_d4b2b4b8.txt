We are seeking a highly skilled DevOps Team Lead to join our dynamic team in Mumbai, Maharashtra. The ideal candidate will have extensive experience in leading DevOps initiatives and managing teams to ensure the successful delivery of software projects. As a DevOps Team Lead, you will be responsible for overseeing the development and deployment of applications using modern DevOps practices and tools. Key Responsibilities: - Lead and mentor a team of DevOps engineers, fostering a culture of collaboration and continuous improvement. - Design, implement, and manage CI/CD pipelines using tools such as Docker and Kubernetes to automate deployment processes. - Collaborate with development and operations teams to streamline workflows and enhance system reliability. - Manage cloud infrastructure on Azure DevOps, ensuring optimal performance and security. - Troubleshoot and resolve issues in development, test, and production environments. - Stay updated with the latest industry trends and technologies to drive innovation within the team. Required Skills: - Proficiency in Docker and Kubernetes for container orchestration and management. - Strong experience with Azure DevOps for cloud-based application deployment. - Excellent leadership and team management skills. - Ability to work in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple projects simultaneously. - Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail. If you are a passionate DevOps professional with a proven track record of leading teams and driving successful project outcomes, we encourage you to apply for this exciting opportunity.
