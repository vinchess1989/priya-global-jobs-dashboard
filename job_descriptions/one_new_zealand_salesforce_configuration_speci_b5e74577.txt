Ko tō tūranga – your role

We’re looking for a Salesforce Configuration Specialist to help improve, automate and support our Enterprise Salesforce environment.

You’ll turn business requirements into practical Salesforce solutions, delivering low-to-medium complexity configuration and automation changes across areas including Flow, objects, fields, validation rules, layouts and permissions.

This is a hands-on role that sits between traditional Salesforce administration and technical development. You’ll work closely with our Salesforce functional and technical specialists, supporting change from initial requirement refinement through configuration, testing, release and ongoing support.

You’ll also help simplify business processes, reduce manual work and contribute to future Salesforce transformation activity.

Ko Tō Mahi – What You’ll Do


Deliver Salesforce configuration and automation changes using Flow, objects, fields, validation rules, page layouts, permissions and other declarative tools.
Translate business requirements into practical Salesforce solutions that meet agreed platform and security standards.
Support Salesforce enhancements from requirement refinement through configuration, testing, release and post-release validation.
Support Salesforce CPQ, account management, order initiation and related business workflows.
Investigate and resolve configuration, automation, data and user issues, escalating complex development or integration problems when required.
Maintain clear and accurate configuration, testing and release documentation.
Identify opportunities to simplify processes, automate manual activity and improve the Salesforce user experience.
Contribute to future Salesforce transformation and migration work through configuration analysis, testing and knowledge transfer.


Na Tōu Rourou - What You’ll Bring


Salesforce experience across administration, configuration, functional support or platform enhancement.
Strong hands-on experience with declarative Salesforce configuration, including Flow, objects, fields, layouts, validation rules, permissions and automation.
Experience supporting Salesforce change through configuration, testing, release and ongoing operational support.
Strong troubleshooting and analytical skills across configuration, workflow, data and user issues.
An understanding of Salesforce environments, release processes, data quality, security and access management.
Enough understanding of APIs, integrations, Apex and Lightning to diagnose issues and work effectively with technical specialists.
Clear communication skills and the confidence to work with stakeholders to understand requirements and recommend practical solutions.
Experience with Salesforce CPQ, Sales Cloud or B2B sales processes would be useful. Salesforce Administrator, Advanced Administrator or Platform App Builder certification would also be an advantage.
