Purpose of the job

To assist the Quality Operations Manager in the implementation of the MGS Quality Management System whilst supporting the overall quality team in MGS. The Quality Engineer (QE) shall be responsible for monitoring the production and release of finished parts and ensuring quality policies and procedures are adhered to on a daily basis, in accordance with the requirements of the Quality Management System.

Key responsibilities

Area Of Responsibility 1

Be interfacing with the customer to realise customer satisfaction and be responsible for production GMP standards. Develop strong links with customer organisations and other project stakeholders. Supporting customer and surveillance accreditation audits.

Area Of Responsibility 2

Initiating and completing CAPA actions, engineering change requests and actions, non-conformance reports co-ordinating product/production related NCR’s in a timely manager. Adherence to incoming control requirements and support SCAR process.

Area Of Responsibility 3

Investigation of root cause and following through on effective corrective actions to prevent re-occurrence for customer and internal quality issues and prepare/update quality system documentation

Area Of Responsibility 4

Establishing and monitoring inspection standards, plans, frequencies and test methods. Establishing and maintaining FMEA’s.

Area Of Responsibility 5

Ensuring that on hold material is dispositioned (MRB) and actioned from QAHOLD in a timely manner. Ensuring that all retained samples are stored correctly, and that sample storage area is maintained. Ensuring that all quality records are completed as per MGS procedure comply with GDP

Area Of Responsibility 6

Performing an active role in further development and continuous improvement of the quality management system and new product introduction. Support Operations area projects and initiatives Pro-actively ensuring GMP, EHS and housekeeping standards are maintained throughout the MGS site
