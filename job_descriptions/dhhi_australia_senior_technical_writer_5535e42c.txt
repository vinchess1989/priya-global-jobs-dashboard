About DHHI Australia


DHHI Australia is a leading supplier of stackers, reclaimers and shiploaders, delivering engineering, manufacturing and lifecycle support solutions to the mining, ports and bulk materials handling industries. Based in Osborne Park, our team works on complex projects across Australia, supporting clients through design, delivery, commissioning and ongoing equipment support.

About the Role


We are seeking an experienced Senior Technical Writer to develop and maintain high-quality technical documentation across engineering, project delivery and operational activities.

Working closely with engineers, project managers and subject matter experts, you will prepare manuals, procedures, work instructions and supporting documentation to ensure equipment, systems and processes are clearly documented and aligned with client and project requirements.

This role offers the opportunity to work on large-scale mining and materials handling projects while helping establish documentation standards and best practices across the business.

Key Responsibilities


Develop operations and maintenance manuals

Prepare technical procedures and work instructions

Create and maintain project documentation packs

Work with engineers and SMEs to gather technical information

Ensure documentation is accurate, consistent and user-friendly

Manage document revisions and version control

Support project handovers and client documentation requirements

Maintain document standards and templates

About You


Previous experience as a Technical Writer / Senior Technical Writer in engineering, mining, manufacturing or heavy industry

Ideally from an engineering background

Strong technical writing and editing capability

Excellent attention to detail and document control skills

Ability to understand and interpret engineering documentation

Advanced Microsoft Office skills

Strong communication and stakeholder engagement skills

Ability to manage multiple priorities and deadlines

Why Join DHHI?


Work on major mining and bulk materials handling projects

Collaborative and experienced engineering team

Diverse and interesting technical work

Osborne Park office location

Competitive remuneration package
