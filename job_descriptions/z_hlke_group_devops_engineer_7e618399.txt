The Role.

As a DevOps Engineer at Zühlke, you will join a cross-functional delivery team working

on a greenfield initiative for a Hong Kong–based client, building a next-generation cloud

platform powered by automation, GitOps, and cloud-native engineering practices.

You will play a key role in designing and implementing a GitOps-driven operating model

and an “Everything as Code” approach to enable scalable Environment-on-Demand

capabilities across AWS. Working at the intersection of platform engineering, cloud

infrastructure, and software delivery, you will help development teams accelerate

delivery through self-service tooling, automated provisioning, and advanced CI/CD/CT

pipelines.

This role is highly hands-on and engineering-focused. You will design and implement

reusable platform capabilities, improve developer experience, and contribute to

scalable, secure, and observable cloud infrastructure. You will collaborate closely with

DevOps, Security, QA, Data, and Software Engineering teams to build resilient systems

and continuously improve engineering practices across the delivery lifecycle.

You will also contribute to platform reliability, observability, quality engineering, and

operational excellence by integrating monitoring, testing, security, and compliance

automation into modern cloud-native delivery workflows.




What’s Important To Us.

• Strong experience with GitOps practices and tooling such as Argo CD, Argo

Workflows, and Argo Events.

• Hands-on expertise with Infrastructure as Code using Terraform Cloud, reusable

modules, and automation frameworks.

• Proven experience with Kubernetes and container orchestration in production

environments, ideally using EKS, Docker, Helm, and Kustomize.

• Strong knowledge of CI/CD engineering using GitHub Actions and pipeline-as-

code approaches.

• Experience developing automation and integration tooling using TypeScript,

Python, Bash, or Groovy.

• Solid AWS platform knowledge including services such as EKS, RDS Aurora

PostgreSQL, MSK (Kafka), S3, Lambda, API Gateway, and VPC networking.

• Experience with observability and monitoring platforms such as Datadog,

CloudWatch, distributed tracing, and custom dashboards.

• Familiarity with automated testing and quality engineering practices, including

integration testing, Testcontainers, SonarQube, security scanning, and quality

gates.• Understanding of cloud security, compliance automation, secrets management,

and secure delivery practices.

• Strong collaboration and communication skills with experience working in agile,

cross-functional teams.

Nice to Have.

• AWS certifications such as DevOps Engineer Professional, Solutions Architect

Associate, or Security Specialty.

• Kubernetes certifications such as CKA or CKAD.

• HashiCorp Terraform Associate or GitOps-related certifications.

• Experience within financial services, regulated industries, or high-availability

environments.

• Exposure to chaos engineering, service mesh technologies, and resilience

testing frameworks such as Gremlin or Chaos Toolkit.

• Experience building developer platforms, self-service infrastructure, or

Environment-on-Demand capabilities.




What we offer.

• Work life blend: we offer a safe & healthy workplace, with flexible working hours

and the possibility to work from home

• Profit share scheme: In addition to your annual salary, you may receive a profit

share defined by the company’s success in the previous year

• Global and Diverse Zühlke community: witness how colleagues from all our 17

offices across the globe come together to create a unique, positive and inclusive

work culture, learning from one another at annual team camps, and celebrating

year-end parties and other local festivities.

• Committed to development: we are committed to the growth of our people and

are investing in your development. We’re empowering you to build the skills you

need to make a positive impact, both personally and for our clients, today and in

the future.
