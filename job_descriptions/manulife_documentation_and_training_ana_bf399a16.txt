The Documentation and Training Analyst will bring together approved future-state designs, business rules, exception scenarios, controls, system steps and readiness requirements into an integrated learning and documentation package. The role will partner closely with Product Owners, Business SME Leads, the SME pool, Change Management and Delivery teams while preserving clear accountability: SMEs establish the operational facts, Product Owners approve business outcomes and acceptance, and this role converts approved content into usable procedures and learning assets.

This opportunity is well suited to someone who combines strong instructional design and technical-writing skills with business analysis, facilitation and disciplined document control. The successful candidate will be comfortable working in an agile transformation environment where designs evolve through validation, build, testing and release readiness.

Position Responsibilities

Develop operational documentation


Create and maintain end-to-end procedures, standard operating procedures, job aids, quick-reference guides, checklists and knowledge articles for transformed business processes.
Translate approved future-state designs, requirements, business rules, controls, systems steps and exception paths into clear role-based instructions.
Validate procedure accuracy and completeness with the Business SME Lead, assigned specialists, Product Owner, Risk and Delivery partners.
Maintain version control, review history, ownership, effective dates and traceability from documentation to approved process and release artifacts.


 

Design and deliver learning solutions


Conduct learning-needs analysis and define role-based learning objectives, curricula and delivery approaches for impacted employees and leaders.
Develop facilitator guides, participant materials, e-learning content, simulations, practice exercises, knowledge checks and performance-support tools.
Use realistic scenarios, business rules and exception cases to prepare users for both routine processing and human-in-the-loop decisions.
Deliver or coordinate instructor-led and virtual training, train-the-trainer sessions, knowledge transfer and post-launch reinforcement.


 

Enable readiness and adoption


Partner with Change Management to align training with stakeholder impacts, communications, readiness activities, release timing and adoption measures.
Confirm that required procedures, learning assets, environments, access and facilitators are ready before deployment.
Support UAT and production-readiness activities by converting issues, defects and process clarifications into final documentation and learning updates.
Monitor completion, learner feedback, knowledge-check results, early performance indicators and support themes; recommend targeted reinforcement where needed.


 

Support integrated transformation delivery


Work within sprint and release cadences, manage content dependencies and surface decisions or blockers that could affect readiness, controls, benefits or AWD retirement outcomes.
Coordinate inputs across business units while distinguishing common enterprise content from business-unit, product, customer, system or control variations.
Apply practical AI and automation tools to accelerate drafting, content analysis and maintenance only where approved, with human validation of accuracy, controls and sensitive information handling.
Build reusable templates and standards that improve documentation and learning quality across later Project waves.


 

Required Qualifications


Experience in operational documentation, technical writing, instructional design, learning development, business transformation or a related field.
Demonstrated experience creating procedures, job aids, facilitator guides, participant materials and digital or blended learning content.
Strong ability to synthesize process maps, requirements, business rules, controls and system information into clear role-based guidance.
Experience facilitating working sessions, content reviews, train-the-trainer sessions or employee learning.
Strong written communication, editing, stakeholder-management and organizational skills, with close attention to accuracy and document control.
Ability to manage multiple deliverables in an agile environment where requirements, designs and release plans continue to evolve.
Experience supporting financial-services operations, process or technology transformation, controls, UAT, Salesforce, workflow or AI-enabled change is preferred.
Knowledge of adult-learning, instructional-design, change-management, Lean or continuous-improvement approaches is an asset.
