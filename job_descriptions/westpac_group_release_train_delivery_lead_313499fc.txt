Job Description

What’s the role?

You’ll be joining our Awareness & Consideration Release Train, helping shape how customers discover, choose and get more value from their relationship with Westpac.

As the Release Train Delivery Lead, you’ll lead delivery across multiple squads, bringing together product, engineering, data, design and delivery teams to turn ideas and priorities into meaningful customer and business outcomes.

You’ll create an environment where teams can work with clarity, autonomy and pace, while keeping quality, risk and the customer at the centre of how we deliver. You’ll help teams navigate dependencies, remove roadblocks and find simpler and more effective ways of working.

This is a highly collaborative leadership role where you’ll have the opportunity to influence across the Release Train, challenge the way we do things and help our people and teams perform at their best.

What do I need?


Proven experience leading delivery across multiple product and technology teams within a complex environment.
Strong understanding of Agile and Lean ways of working, with experience helping teams deliver effectively and continuously improve.
Experience leading complex planning, managing dependencies and helping teams make practical decisions in fast-moving environments.
Strong stakeholder management skills, with the ability to build relationships and influence across product, engineering, data, design, risk and business teams.
People leadership experience, with a genuine passion for coaching, developing and empowering others.
Strong communication and problem-solving skills, with the ability to simplify complexity and bring people together around clear priorities.
A good understanding of technology delivery, operational resilience and managing risk within a complex environment.
