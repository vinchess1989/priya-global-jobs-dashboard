Most DevOps roles ask you to maintain what’s already there. This one gives you the opportunity to build it.




Ingenio is partnering with an innovative Irish technology company operating at the intersection of AI, scientific computing and drug discovery to hire a DevOps Engineer.




This is a hands-on builder role. You’ll help create the infrastructure that supports compute-intensive R&D — from CI/CD and containerisation to automation and observability.




What you’ll do

Build CI/CD pipelines from the ground up
Create and manage containerised environments with Docker/Podman
Build monitoring, logging and alerting systems
Automate infrastructure and engineering workflows
Troubleshoot and optimise Linux-based infrastructure
Partner with the CTO and R&D team on architecture and scalability




What we’re looking for

3+ years’ experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering or SRE
Strong hands-on experience with Docker, Docker Compose or Podman
Proven experience building CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions, GitLab CI/CD or Jenkins
Strong Linux fundamentals – shell scripting, networking, systemd and storage
Experience with monitoring and observability, such as Prometheus and Grafana
Infrastructure-as-Code experience, ideally Ansible
Python scripting and automation skills
A self-directed mindset – comfortable building, troubleshooting and owning infrastructure end-to-end




Why this role?

This isn’t about maintaining somebody else’s setup or working through a queue of infrastructure tickets.

You’ll have the opportunity to build systems from the ground up and see your work directly support technology being used to advance drug research and discovery.




If you’re a DevOps Engineer who enjoys getting under the hood, solving complex problems and building rather than just maintaining, we’d like to hear from you.
