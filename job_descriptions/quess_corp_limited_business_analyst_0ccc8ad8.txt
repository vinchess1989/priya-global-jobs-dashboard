Business Analyst [ 6+ ]

Description

Highly self-driven professional with strong ownership, accountability, and bias for action, consistently driving outcomes from requirements through delivery with minimal supervision.
Strong stakeholder management, relationship-building, and expectation management skills.
JIRA and Aha, SQL,Databrcicks
Ability to navigate challenging conversations, influence outcomes, and resolve conflicts through effective negotiation.
Excellent written, verbal, and presentation communication skills.
Strong requirements elicitation, analysis, documentation, validation, and acceptance criteria development expertise, with exceptional attention to detail to ensure completeness, accuracy, and minimal rework.
Skilled in facilitating meetings, workshops, backlog refinement sessions, and Agile ceremonies.
Strong analytical, critical thinking, and problem-solving capabilities.
Ability to manage competing priorities, ambiguity, risks, dependencies, and impacts effectively.
Experience collaborating with Product Owners, Developers, QA, UX teams, and business stakeholders.
Demonstrated ability to work independently, proactively identify gaps and opportunities, drive decisions, and move initiatives forward without requiring significant direction.
Experience conducting UAT and validating solutions against business requirements.
Demonstrated ability to recommend process improvements and provide strategic business value.
Experience with SAFe Agile, sprint planning, backlog management, and Agile delivery practices.
Proactive communication of project status, risks, dependencies, and next steps.
