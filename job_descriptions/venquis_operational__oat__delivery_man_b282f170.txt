Operational (OAT) Delivery Manager - Contract




Location: London -hybrid

Start: ASAP

Duration: Through to platform go-live (currently expected April or July 2027)




The opportunity

We are looking for a hands-on Operational (OAT) Delivery Manager to support a major technology platform programme through Operational Acceptance Testing (OAT), live service readiness and go-live.




This is a delivery-focused role for someone who can turn a high-level programme plan into a detailed, actionable schedule and make sure tasks are completed by the people responsible. You will work closely with engineering, QA, infrastructure, security, service and external delivery teams, ensuring operational readiness is properly tested, evidenced and signed off before the platform goes live.




Key responsibilities

Build and own the detailed delivery plan, including tasks, owners, dates, dependencies and critical path.
Operationalise OAT, mapping readiness requirements to test cases, owners, evidence and sign-off.
Coordinate test windows, environments, change requests and CAB approvals.
Track OAT results, manage defects through to closure and prepare the OAT exit pack.
Drive actions across engineering, infrastructure, DevOps, security, QA, Product and external suppliers.
Track service transition, operational knowledge transfer, runbooks, cutover rehearsals and hyper care readiness.
Produce clear weekly status reports, maintain action logs and feed risks, issues and decisions into programme governance.
Escalate delivery risks early, with clear facts, impacts and proposed options.




What we're looking for

5+ years' experience in technology change or programme/project delivery.
Strong experience building and managing detailed delivery plans, dependencies and milestones.
Hands-on experience with OAT, operational readiness or service transition into live operations.
Working knowledge of ITIL processes, particularly change, release and incident management.
Experience delivering alongside a systems integrator or managed service provider.
Confident coordinating technical and business stakeholders and holding teams to agreed deadlines.
Strong written communication and senior stakeholder reporting skills.




Desirable: Platform migration or re-platforming experience, Azure exposure, financial services or London Market insurance experience, and tools such as ServiceNow, Jira, Confluence, MS Project or Smartsheet.




Why apply?

You will play a key role in getting a major technology platform ready for live operations. This is an opportunity to take real ownership of delivery, work across a broad range of technical and operational teams, and make a visible impact on a programme approaching a critical go-live milestone.
