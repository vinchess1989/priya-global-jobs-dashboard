Your new company

Hays Engineering is delighted to be working with a leading manufacturing organisation based in Antrim as they recruit for a Technical Author to join their established engineering team. This is an excellent opportunity to join a successful manufacturer with a strong reputation within its sector.

Your new role

As Technical Author, you will be responsible for producing and maintaining clear, accurate technical documentation to support the company's product range.

Key responsibilities will include:

Creating and updating technical manuals, operating instructions and product documentation
Producing clear technical information using engineering drawings, specifications and product data
Working closely with Design, Engineering, Production and Quality teams
Gathering technical information from engineers and converting it into easy-to-understand documentation
Maintaining existing documentation and ensuring information remains accurate and up to date
Supporting documentation requirements for new and existing products
Ensuring documentation is produced to a consistently high standard

What you'll need to succeed

You will ideally come from an engineering, manufacturing or technical documentation background with the ability to interpret engineering drawings and technical information.Experience in technical authoring, CAD, engineering documentation or mechanical design would be particularly beneficial. You should have excellent written communication skills, strong attention to detail and the ability to work effectively with engineering teams.

What you'll get in return

You will have the opportunity to join a well-established Northern Ireland manufacturer in a varied technical role where you can work closely with engineering and product teams.
You will receive a competitive salary and benefits package, alongside long-term career opportunities within an established engineering environment.
