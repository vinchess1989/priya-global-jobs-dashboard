Job Description:

Job Title: AI-First DevOps Engineer

Department: Engineering

Location: Canada Remote

Employment Type: Full-Time

Base Salary Range: $155,000-$165,000 CAD

Position Summary

Computrition is seeking an AI-First DevOps Engineer to lead our cloud infrastructure, deployment automation, and DevOps practices. This role will be the primary owner for building and deploying our AWS environment using Terraform, while establishing reliable, secure, and scalable delivery processes.

This individual will bring strong experience in AWS, infrastructure as code, CI/CD, and operational excellence, along with the communication skills and leadership needed to work effectively across a remote team. This role will also lead the practical adoption of AI tooling, AI agents, and MCP servers — integrating them into CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure automation, and day-to-day engineering workflows to measurably improve delivery speed and operational quality.

Key Responsibilities


Lead AWS infrastructure design, deployment, and automation.
Build and maintain reusable, secure, and scalable infrastructure using Terraform.
Design, implement, and improve CI/CD pipelines to support efficient, high-quality software delivery.
Support a multi-tenant SaaS platform with strong focus on reliability, security, scalability, and maintainability.
Partner with the engineering and architecture teams to align infrastructure and deployment practices with platform goals.
Establish best practices for monitoring, logging, alerting, access control, and operational readiness.
Identify and implement AI solutions that improve DevOps workflows, reduce manual effort, and increase team velocity.
Design and implement AI agent workflows and MCP server integrations to automate DevOps tasks, reduce toil, and accelerate engineering delivery.
Communicate clearly with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Lead with accountability, responsiveness, and a strong commitment to quality and on-time delivery.


Required Qualifications


Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or related field, or equivalent experience.
5+ years of experience in DevOps, Cloud Engineering, Platform Engineering, SRE, or similar role.
Strong hands-on experience with AWS.
Deep experience with Terraform and infrastructure as code best practices.
Strong experience building and managing CI/CD pipelines.
Solid understanding of cloud security, secrets management, access control, monitoring, and deployment automation.
Experience supporting production systems with a focus on uptime, scalability, and operational quality.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
Proven ability to work independently and collaboratively in a remote environment.
Strong organizational skills, follow-through, and commitment to delivering quality work on time.


Preferred Qualifications


Experience with multi-tenant SaaS platforms.
Familiarity with modern application environments using technologies such as C#/.NET, React, PostgreSQL, and AWS.
Experience with observability and monitoring tools.
Experience using AI to improve engineering workflows and delivery processes.
Hands-on experience building or operating production AI agent pipelines, or integrating MCP servers into engineering toolchains.


Core Competencies


Strong technical judgment
Clear and professional communication
Ownership and accountability
Quality and delivery focus
Leadership and collaboration
Continuous improvement mindset
Passion for technology and innovation


Why Join Computrition

Computrition, Inc. is a leading provider of enterprise foodservice, nutrition management, and retail technology, trusted by hundreds of organizations across North America, including healthcare systems, military installations, correctional facilities, and higher education institutions. For more than 44 years, we’ve built technology in partnership with our customers, helping them improve operational efficiency, strengthen financial performance, and deliver better nutritional care.

We're investing in the future of our platform by adopting modern technologies and working closely with customers to solve complex operational challenges. Our solutions have real-world impact by driving performance enhancement and show up directly in how hospitals, military installations, and other institutions operate every day.

Computrition offers the stability of an established industry leader and the momentum of a company accelerating growth through innovation. We invest in our people and foster a culture of collaboration, accountability, continuous learning, and professional growth where employees can make a meaningful impact. You'll join experienced professionals who are passionate about solving meaningful problems and delivering measurable outcomes for customers.

With team members across the U.S. and Canada, and as part of a growing portfolio of technology companies owned by Constellation Software Inc. (TSX: CSU.TO), we are well-positioned to continue advancing the future of foodservice and nutrition technology.

If you're looking to build technology that makes a meaningful impact while growing your career with a stable, innovative company, we'd like to hear from you.
