Scope Of Job

Accountable for the day-to-day ownership, administration, and continuous improvement of Burberry's customer platforms - Salesforce Service Cloud, Braze, Hightouch, and Quest - ensuring they are stable, secure, well-governed, and configured to meet the needs of the business. Works predominantly in the Customer platform space alongside the Senior Product & Delivery Manager for Customer, while also supporting Retail and Marketing stakeholders. Combines hands-on platform administration and configuration with structured analysis of demand, value, and performance, helping to shape priorities and the platform roadmap.

PURPOSE

The Platform Manager is a permanent role responsible for the health, configuration, and evolution of the platforms that underpin Burberry's customer service, clienteling, and customer engagement capabilities. The role has two complementary halves: owning the day-to-day administration of Salesforce Service Cloud as a Salesforce Administrator would, with administration support and governance reporting across Braze, Hightouch, and Quest; and providing the analysis, evidence, and structure that help the business decide what to change next, including demand management, value sizing, and input to prioritisation and roadmaps.

The Platform Manager works within the Customer platforms team, reporting to the Senior Product & Delivery Manager for Customer, and supports Retail and Marketing teams who rely on these platforms. They work alongside the Senior Business Analysts who lead day-to-day change, committed roadmaps, and forecast planning for the platforms, and in close collaboration with Solution Architects, Digital Operations, data and engineering colleagues, and third-party implementation and support partners, acting as the knowledgeable point of contact for how the platforms are configured, used, and performing.

Responsibilities


Own the day-to-day administration of Salesforce Service Cloud, including user management, profiles, roles, permission sets, queues, and access controls, ensuring appropriate security and data protection standards are applied.
Configure and maintain Service Cloud using declarative tools (objects, fields, page layouts, record types, validation rules, flows, assignment and escalation rules, routing, knowledge, and reports and dashboards) to deliver business requirements and make best use of standard functionality.
Administer and configure Braze, including workspace access, user permissions, segments, data and attribute setup, and consent and subscription settings, supporting Marketing and CRM teams to use the platform effectively and compliantly.
Provide governance and reporting for Hightouch by reviewing how audiences, models, and syncs have been created and are being used, including sync volumes against the contracted allowance, whether usage is within budget, and who is using the platform, and raising findings and recommendations to the Senior Product & Delivery Manager.
Support the administration and ongoing improvement of Quest, Burberry's in-house clienteling application built on Salesforce, helping with configuration, issue triage, and requirements, while release readiness remains with the Senior Business Analyst and Delivery Manager for that team.
Provide second-line support for the platforms: triage and resolve user incidents and requests, carry out root cause analysis, and work with Digital Operations and support partners to meet agreed service levels and reduce recurring issues.
Maintain clear platform documentation, configuration records, and user guides, and support user training and adoption so that teams get full value from the platforms.
Work alongside the Senior Business Analysts on day-to-day platform change: capture and refine requirements from Customer Service, Retail, and Marketing, write clear user stories and acceptance criteria, and advise on solution options and effort.
Support demand management by logging, categorising, and triaging requests, maintaining a transparent demand pipeline, and communicating status and outcomes to requesters.
Support value sizing by estimating benefits, effort, cost, and risk for requests and initiatives, building simple business cases that help the Senior Business Analysts and the Senior Product & Delivery Manager make investment decisions.
Support the Senior Business Analysts with committed roadmaps and forecast planning for the customer platforms, providing data, dependency, and capacity inputs to prioritisation, and awareness of vendor roadmaps, new platform features, and licence usage.
Monitor platform performance, usage, and adoption through dashboards and reporting (for example case volumes and service levels, sync and campaign performance, and Quest usage), turning findings into clear recommendations.
Support and challenge third-party delivery and support partners, scrutinising approach, estimates, and quality, and holding them to Burberry standards.


Experience


Hands-on experience administering Salesforce, ideally Service Cloud, including declarative configuration, flows, the security and sharing model, and reporting. Salesforce Administrator certification (or working towards it) is expected; Advanced Administrator or Service Cloud Consultant is beneficial.
Experience of, or strong appetite to learn, marketing and customer data activation platforms such as Braze and Hightouch, or comparable tools.
Proven ability to capture and refine business requirements, write clear user stories and acceptance criteria, and coordinate testing and UAT.
Strong analytical skills, comfortable working with data and building dashboards and reports, with the ability to turn findings into clear recommendations. Experience building simple business cases or sizing benefits is beneficial, as is working knowledge of SQL.
Comfortable working in an agile, scrum-based delivery model, with practical understanding of backlog management, release management, and incident and change processes.
Good understanding of system integrations, data flows, and data protection and customer consent considerations (including GDPR).
Sufficient confidence and credibility to scrutinise and challenge third-party partner output where appropriate.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, able to translate between business and technical colleagues and work across Customer Service, Retail, and Marketing.
Demonstrated curiosity, commercial awareness, and appetite to grow towards broader platform and product ownership responsibilities.
