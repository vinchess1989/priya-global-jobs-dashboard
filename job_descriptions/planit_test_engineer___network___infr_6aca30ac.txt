About the Role

Planit is seeking an experienced Senior Test Analyst to join a high-performing team delivering critical infrastructure and operational technology initiatives. This role will suit a strong functional tester who has experience working closely with infrastructure, server, networking, and operations teams to validate complex enterprise environments.

You will play a key role in ensuring the quality, stability, and reliability of infrastructure-related projects, working across testing strategy, planning, execution, defect management, and stakeholder engagement.

What You'll Do



Develop and execute comprehensive test plans, test cases, and test scripts.
Perform functional, integration, system, and end-to-end testing activities.
Validate infrastructure-related changes across servers, networking, command and control platforms, and operational environments.
Work closely with infrastructure, operations, engineering, and project teams to understand requirements and testing impacts.
Manage defects through to resolution and retesting.
Participate in test planning, estimation, risk assessments, and reporting.
Support release activities and production verification testing.
Contribute to continuous improvement of testing processes and quality practices.

What We're Looking For



Proven experience as a Senior Test Analyst within large enterprise environments.
Strong functional testing background across complex technology ecosystems.
Experience testing solutions involving:
Infrastructure platforms
Server environments
Networking technologies
Command and Control systems
Operational technology (Ops) environments
Strong understanding of the Software Testing Life Cycle (STLC).
Experience with defect management and test management tools.
Excellent stakeholder engagement and communication skills.
Ability to work autonomously while collaborating with technical and business teams.

Desirable Experience



Experience supporting infrastructure transformation or migration programs.
Exposure to monitoring, operational support, or service management environments.
Experience working within government, utilities, defence, telecommunications, or large enterprise organisations.
ISTQB Certification or equivalent testing qualifications.
