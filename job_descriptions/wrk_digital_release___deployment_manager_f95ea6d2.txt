Release & Deployment Manager




Location: Skipton (Hybrid) 3 Days a Week

Salary: Competitive + Excellent Benefits




Are you passionate about helping engineering teams deliver software faster, safer, and with greater confidence?




This is a fantastic opportunity to shape and evolve release practices within a large-scale technology environment, enabling teams to improve delivery flow, reduce risk, and accelerate the adoption of modern engineering practices.




The Role

As a Release & Deployment Manager, you'll sit at the heart of software delivery, working closely with Platform Owners, DevSecOps, Quality Engineering, and Service teams to coordinate releases across multiple platforms and products.




You'll play a key role in evolving release capabilities through automation, improved governance, enhanced observability, and modern deployment practices, ensuring teams can move quickly without compromising stability or compliance.




What You'll Do

Release Strategy & Delivery

Define and evolve release and deployment strategies across multiple technology platforms
Drive safe, efficient, and repeatable release processes
Coordinate releases across multiple engineering teams and business stakeholders
Ensure dependencies, risks, and environment readiness are identified and managed effectively




Modern Engineering & Continuous Improvement

Support the adoption of modern release practices, automation, and progressive delivery models
Champion approaches such as canary releases and blue/green deployments
Identify bottlenecks and opportunities for improvement across the software delivery lifecycle
Foster a culture of continuous improvement and shared ownership of release outcomes




Metrics, Performance & Insight

Use engineering metrics including Lead Time, Deployment Frequency, Change Failure Rate and MTTR to improve delivery performance
Drive data-led decision making across release and deployment activities
Improve release predictability, reliability, and engineering effectiveness




Leadership & Stakeholder Management

Lead and develop a small team focused on release excellence
Work closely with engineering, platform, operations, and business teams
Influence stakeholders across technology functions to improve delivery outcomes
Build strong relationships across the organisation to ensure successful delivery of change




What We're Looking For

Proven experience leading Release, Deployment, or Service Transition activities within complex technology environments
Strong understanding of modern software delivery practices including CI/CD, DevOps, and release automation
Experience coordinating releases across multiple teams and managing dependencies at scale
Ability to leverage metrics and data to improve delivery performance and reduce risk
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills
Experience working within regulated or enterprise-scale environments would be advantageous




What's In It For You?

Impact: Lead the evolution of release and deployment capability across a major technology organisation
Flexibility: Hybrid and flexible working arrangements
Modern Engineering Culture: Work alongside Platform, DevSecOps, Engineering and Quality practices
Career Growth: Ongoing investment in training and professional development




Benefits Package

Annual discretionary bonus
25 days holiday + bank holidays, increasing with service to 30 days
Holiday trading scheme
Up to 10% matched pension contribution
Private medical insurance
Electric and hybrid car salary sacrifice scheme
Colleague mortgage benefits
3 paid volunteering days per year
Gym discounts and wellbeing support
Inclusive employee networks and communities
