WHO ARE WE?

Our client is an established technology business working across complex digital environments, delivering scalable and reliable technology solutions. They are looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer who combines strong technical troubleshooting capabilities with a software engineering mindset.

They offer an environment where you can take technical ownership, influence engineering decisions and contribute to the ongoing improvement of sophisticated technology platforms.




WHAT WILL YOU BE DOING?

You will work across cloud infrastructure, software and automation, ensuring the performance and resilience of large-scale digital systems. You will tackle complex engineering challenges, improve operational processes and develop solutions that reduce technical debt and increase platform stability.

Working alongside experienced technical teams, you will contribute to software improvements, optimise delivery pipelines and enhance visibility across production environments. This opportunity would suit someone who enjoys getting under the hood of complex systems rather than focusing solely on infrastructure provisioning.




WHAT YOU NEED TO HAVE...

Strong commercial experience within DevOps, Platform Engineering or Software Engineering
Experience with C# and .NET / Java
Hands-on experience working with Microsoft Azure
A strong understanding of software development principles
Experience troubleshooting complex distributed systems
Experience building and optimising CI/CD pipelines
Strong scripting and automation capabilities
Experience with modern monitoring and observability tools
Ability to diagnose and resolve complex technical issues independently
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills
Comfortable providing occasional out-of-hours support




IT'S NICE TO HAVE...

Exposure to modern frontend technologies (React, Node, Vue, JavaScript)
Experience working with complex web-based applications
Experience within customer-facing technical environments




TO APPLY...

Please either apply online or email me directly at eoin.meehan@searchability.com.au. For further information, please call me on 0480 509 259. I am available from 7am until 10pm, and if I don't answer, please leave a message and I'll get back to you as soon as possible.
