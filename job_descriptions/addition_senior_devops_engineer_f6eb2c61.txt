Senior DevOps Engineer




Join a forward-thinking technology consultancy delivering innovative solutions across a diverse range of industries. You'll play a key role in shaping infrastructure, driving automation and helping engineering teams deliver secure, scalable and reliable software for both internal and client-facing projects.




Role Overview:

Location: London – Hybrid (1 day per week in the office)
Package: Competitive salary + annual performance bonus and excellent benefits
Industry: Technology Consultancy




What You’ll Be Doing:

Designing and maintaining scalable infrastructure across Azure, AWS, GCP and on-premise environments.
Building and optimising CI/CD pipelines, embedding automated testing, security and quality controls throughout the deployment lifecycle.
Managing containerised applications and orchestration platforms, ensuring efficient and reliable deployments.
Driving operational efficiency through infrastructure-as-code, scripting and automation.
Enhancing system reliability through effective monitoring, logging and alerting solutions.
Supporting incident management, disaster recovery and infrastructure security initiatives.
Working closely with software engineers to improve development environments and release processes.
Collaborating directly with clients to design and implement DevOps solutions tailored to their technical and commercial requirements.
Leading technical discussions, contributing to architecture reviews and supporting successful project handovers.
Championing DevOps best practices and continuous improvement across engineering teams.




Main Skills Needed:

Strong hands-on experience with Linux and production-level Docker environments.
Commercial experience with Azure or AWS, with wider multi-cloud exposure being advantageous.
Proven experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines using tools such as GitHub Actions, Azure DevOps, GitLab CI or Jenkins.
Experience with infrastructure-as-code tools such as Terraform, Bicep or CloudFormation.
Strong scripting and automation skills using Python or Bash.
Solid understanding of networking, cloud security and access management.
Familiarity with container orchestration technologies such as Kubernetes, ECS or AKS.
Experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana or Datadog.
Confident communication skills, with the ability to translate technical concepts for clients and stakeholders.
A proactive, collaborative approach with the confidence to take ownership of technical solutions.




What’s in It for You:

Competitive salary and annual performance bonus.
26 days of annual leave, plus bank holidays and your birthday off.
Private healthcare and enhanced family leave.
£1,000 annual professional development budget.
Flexible hybrid working with just one day per week in the London office.
The freedom to work remotely from anywhere in the world for up to four weeks each year.
Your choice of high-spec Windows or Mac equipment.
Exposure to varied projects, emerging technologies and multiple industries.
A supportive, entrepreneurial culture where autonomy and fresh ideas are encouraged.
Regular team socials and complimentary office refreshments.
