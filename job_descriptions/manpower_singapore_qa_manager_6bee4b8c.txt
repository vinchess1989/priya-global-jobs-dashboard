Role Details:

Define and maintain enterprise-wide QA policies, standards, processes, and templates.
Establish common testing methodologies and quality gates across all projects.
Develop QA metrics, KPIs, and report mechanisms to measure quality performance.
Promote best practices and drive a quality-first culture across the organization.
Conduct periodic QA audits to assess compliance with organizational standards.
Review project deliverables, test artifacts, defect management, and release readiness.
Identify quality risks, process gaps, and improvement opportunities.
Mentor QA teams and promote knowledge sharing across projects.
Drive process improvements, test automation adoption, and QA transformation initiatives.
Collaborate with PMO, Development, Business, and Architecture teams to improve delivery quality.


Qualification Details:

Bachelor's degree in computer science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field.
12+ years of experience in Software Quality Assurance or Testing.
Minimum 3+ years of experience in QA leadership, governance, or quality management roles.
Proven experience establishing QA standards, processes, and audit frameworks.
Understanding of SDLC, STLC, Agile methodologies, and quality management principles.
Experience with test automation, integration testing, and enterprise applications.
Excellent leadership, stakeholder management, communication, and analytical skills.
