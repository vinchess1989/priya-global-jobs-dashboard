Great projects don’t happen by accident. They happen when people have clarity, understand what matters, ask the right questions, and work together towards a shared outcome.




As our Project Manager, you’ll bring those pieces together. You’ll plan, lead, and coordinate projects from initial idea through to delivery and a controlled transition into Business as Usual (BAU).




You’ll work across teams to turn plans into action, connecting the right people, identifying challenges early, and keeping everyone focused on what we’re trying to achieve and why.




Purpose of the role

You’ll lead and coordinate projects that support our business strategy, client commitments, operational improvements, transitions, transformation initiatives, and technology change.
As the central point of coordination, you’ll bring impacted teams and stakeholders together around clear outcomes. You’ll make sure projects stay focused on their agreed scope, schedule, budget, and quality expectations, while creating the governance and visibility people need to make informed decisions.
You’ll also keep a close eye on risks and dependencies, asking the right questions when something doesn’t look right and helping teams find practical solutions.
And because delivery isn’t finished at go-live, you’ll make sure each project moves smoothly into BAU, with clear ownership and the right support in place.




Key performance areas

Project planning and delivery

What you’ll be doing

Define clear project scope, objectives, deliverables, milestones, and success criteria.
Develop and manage integrated project plans that give everyone clarity on what needs to happen and when.
Bring cross-functional teams together and coordinate delivery from initiation through to closure.
Keep a close eye on progress, asking questions, removing obstacles, and taking corrective action where needed.







What success looks like

Milestones achieved in line with agreed plans.
Delivery within agreed scope and schedule.
Controlled project closure and successful transition into BAU.

Governance and reporting

What you’ll be doing

Put clear, practical project governance and meeting rhythms in place.
Prepare project status reports, dashboards, and executive updates that make complex information easy to understand.
Help teams make timely decisions through effective steering and delivery forums.
Keep project records clear, accurate, and up to date.

What success looks like

Timely and accurate reporting.
Effective governance and escalation.
Clear decisions, ownership, and accountability.

Risk, issue, assumption, and dependency management

What you’ll be doing

Maintain clear risk, assumption, issue, and dependency (RAID) and decision logs.
Look ahead, not just at what’s happening today, to identify potential challenges and dependencies early.
Understand potential impacts and make sure mitigation actions are clear and tracked.
Escalate material risks and issues at the right time, with the information people need to make informed decisions.

What success looks like

Risks and potential threats identified early.
Fewer unresolved critical items.
Timely mitigation and resolution.

Stakeholder and client management

What you’ll be doing

Build trusted relationships with sponsors, clients, and workstream leads.
Take the time to understand different perspectives and keep expectations clear through straightforward, honest communication.
Facilitate workshops, project reviews, and alignment sessions that move conversations forward.
Work through conflicting priorities constructively and keep people engaged in the shared outcome.

What success looks like

Strong stakeholder engagement.
Concerns understood and resolved in a timely way.
Positive sponsor and client feedback.

Financial and resource management

What you’ll be doing

Keep track of project budgets, forecasts, actuals, and financial risks.
Understand resource needs across workstreams and coordinate them effectively.
Support benefits tracking and identify constraints early so we can understand what needs to change.

What success looks like

Delivery within the approved budget.
Effective use of resources.
Clear and transparent financial reporting.

Quality and change control

What you’ll be doing

Define clear acceptance criteria and make sure deliverables meet agreed quality expectations.
Understand the impact of proposed changes to scope, schedule, budget, or solutions before they’re agreed.
Manage project changes through the appropriate governance processes.
Encourage teams to learn from delivery and keep looking for smarter, simpler ways of working.

What success looks like

Deliverables accepted against agreed criteria.
Project changes managed effectively.
Compliance with agreed governance requirements.

Transition and operational readiness

What you’ll be doing

Coordinate operational readiness across impacted areas of our business.
Make sure the right questions have been answered before go-live, including readiness criteria, ownership, ongoing support, and handover requirements.
Coordinate hypercare, lessons learned, and post-implementation reviews where appropriate.
Make sure teams have the clarity and support they need to take ownership once the project moves into BAU.

What success looks like

Readiness confirmed before go-live.
Minimal operational disruption.
A successful and controlled transition into BAU.

Responsible use of AI in project delivery

We’re curious about how technology can help us work smarter, but we don’t use it simply because we can. We make sure there’s a clear purpose, the right controls are in place, and people remain accountable for the decisions we make.

What you’ll be doing

Use only approved artificial intelligence (AI) tools and authorised data for project activities.
Look for suitable opportunities to use AI to help with planning, analysis, reporting, meeting follow-up, and administrative automation.
Make sure there’s a clear business purpose, ownership, risk assessment, and appropriate approval before introducing or materially changing an AI solution.
Protect confidential, personal, client, and regulated information when working with prompts, source data, and AI-generated outputs.
Apply meaningful human review and remain accountable for AI-supported decisions and deliverables.
Question and validate AI-generated outputs for accuracy, completeness, bias, fairness, source quality, and intellectual property considerations.
Be transparent when AI has materially contributed to an analysis, recommendation, decision, or deliverable.

What success looks like

AI use follows approved processes and client commitments.
Sensitive information is protected from unauthorised disclosure.
Appropriate reviews and approvals are recorded.
AI-related risks and limitations are actively understood and managed.
AI delivers productivity benefits without compromising quality or human accountability.

What you’ll bring




Professional Know-how:

Education

Grade 12 / Matric or equivalent.
A relevant tertiary qualification in Project Management, Business, Commerce, Operations, Information Technology (IT), or a related discipline.
A recognised project management certification such as Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK), PRINCE2, Project Management Professional (PMP), Association for Project Management (APM), Agile, or Scrum would be advantageous.

You’ll bring:

Demonstrable experience coordinating or managing cross-functional projects.
Experience across project planning, governance, reporting, stakeholder management, and RAID control.
Experience in business change, operational transition, technology implementation, client onboarding, or transformation would be an advantage.




Technical capability

You’ll be comfortable using the tools and information around you to keep projects moving and make informed decisions.

You’ll bring:

Proficiency in Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
Working knowledge of project planning, scheduling, financial tracking, governance reporting, and collaboration tools.
A practical understanding of approved AI tools for project planning, reporting, analysis, risk management, and workflow automation.
Awareness of data protection, security, privacy, transparency, fairness, human oversight, and accountability.
The ability to write clear prompts, assess sources, and critically validate AI-generated outputs rather than simply accepting them at face value.
A commitment to completing required AI training and staying up to date with approved guidance.







What will help you thrive

Leadership and ownership

You create clarity, bring people together, and take ownership of getting things moving. You’re comfortable leading through influence rather than relying on direct authority.

Planning and organisation

You can take something complex and make it manageable. You prioritise effectively, connect the moving parts, and stay focused when deadlines compete.

Stakeholder management

You build trusted relationships, listen to different perspectives, influence decisions, and manage expectations openly.

Communication and facilitation

You communicate complex information simply and directly. You’re confident presenting to senior audiences and know how to facilitate conversations that lead to clear outcomes.

Problem-solving and judgement

You’re curious about what sits behind a challenge. You use evidence, analysis, and sound judgement to understand the problem before recommending a way forward.

Commercial and financial awareness

You understand that great project delivery needs to make commercial sense. You’re comfortable working with budgets, forecasts, benefits, and commercial commitments.

Risk and control mindset

You look ahead and ask,“What could get in the way?” You identify threats early, apply proportionate controls, and know when something needs to be escalated.

Adaptability and resilience

Projects change. Priorities move. New information comes to light. You stay focused, rethink the plan when needed, and maintain momentum through ambiguity and change.

Collaboration and customer focus

You work inclusively, put shared outcomes ahead of silos, and keep client, customer, and operational needs at the heart of project delivery.

AI and digital fluency

You’re curious about what AI can make better, while understanding that smarter technology still needs human judgement. You use approved AI tools responsibly, protect data, question outputs, and keep human accountability at the centre of project decisions.




How we’ll measure success

Success in this role will include:

Delivery against agreed project milestones and plans.
Budget and forecast accuracy.
Quality and acceptance of project deliverables.
Timely and accurate governance reporting.
Effective management of critical risks, issues, assumptions, and dependencies.
Stakeholder, sponsor, and client satisfaction.
Operational readiness and successful transition into BAU.
Achievement and tracking of agreed project benefits.
Responsible and measurable use of AI, including appropriate approvals, data controls, human review, and output validation.
