Well established federal government organization looking for a Environment & Release Manager

Hybrid working model | 12 months + possible extensions | AU Citizens Eligible | Melbourne




Your new company




This federal government agency supports a better life for hundreds of thousands of Australians with a significant and permanent disability and their families and carers. The priority is to ensure people with disability continue to get the support they need. The changes that are required to existing disability support systems are significant.




Your new role and experience

Support the development, implementation, and ongoing improvement of Release Management practices in collaboration with key stakeholders and external vendors.
Manage releases covering project and program deliverables, application enhancements, infrastructure upgrades, security updates, and operating system maintenance activities.
Coordinate regular release planning and review sessions, ensuring alignment with prioritised business initiatives, non-functional requirements, service requests, third-party solutions, and platform updates.
Maintain and update release calendars, balancing business priorities, compliance obligations, technical dependencies, and delivery timelines.
Participate in release governance meetings, utilising project and work management tools to provide visibility of release activities.
Partner with release engineering teams to assess the impact of code integrations, branch management, and deployment activities.
Ensure compliance with Change Management requirements, including cyber security reviews, penetration testing activities, and governance checkpoints.
