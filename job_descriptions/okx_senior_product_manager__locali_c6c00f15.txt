About The Opportunity

We've built an AI-native localization stack: agentic translation workflows that already publish content with minimal human touch. The translation agent itself works. What decides whether it scales is everything around it — the terminology assets that keep it consistent, the automated testing that catches what it breaks in the live product, and the evaluation infrastructure that tells us when it can be trusted to publish without a human.

That surrounding platform is what this role owns.

This is a genuine bridge role, and we mean both halves. You bring localization domain depth — you know what makes a translation wrong, what breaks in-product, how terminology and locale rules actually work — and the AI product instinct to turn that judgment into agents, metrics and automated tooling. You will define what "good" means, build the systems that measure it automatically, and make our human experts dramatically more leveraged.

What You’ll Be Doing


Own the terminology and glossary system that ensures consistent localization across markets and product surfaces, including term proposal, approval, versioning, integration into agentic workflows, and compliance monitoring.
Build agent-driven tools that scan live mobile and web experiences to identify localization issues that translation review alone may miss, such as text truncation, layout defects, hardcoded strings, unlocalized imagery, and incorrect number, date, or currency formats.
Deliver the MVP for automated localization auditing and evolve it into localization testing embedded within internal product-testing infrastructure, enabling issues to be caught automatically before release.
Define and operate the translation quality gate for human-free publishing, supported by golden datasets, standardized error-classification annotation tools, automated evaluation, and quality metrics.
Balance automation coverage against content risk across announcements, Help Center content, and product UI; investigate systemic gate failures, identify root causes, and drive improvements back into the localization agents.
Integrate annotation workflows with internal AI infrastructure, enabling linguists to provide standardized, real-time feedback that continuously improves agent performance.



What We Look For In You


5+ years in product management, including ownership of backend or infrastructure systems that business operations run on — not only user-facing surfaces.
Localization or translation domain depth. You can look at a translation and say precisely why it's wrong, and you understand terminology management, locale rules and what breaks when content meets a real UI.
Shipped AI/ML or evaluation-driven products, including internal tooling. You partner with AI teams as a peer rather than consuming their output.
Strong data fluency. You define metric frameworks for fuzzy, subjective quality problems and turn them into measurable, automatable systems — and you reason clearly about precision/recall trade-offs, such as false-flag rate vs. miss rate.
Excellent cross-functional execution across engineering, AI, design and external partners, in a multi-market, multi-language, multicultural environment.



Nice to Haves


LLM evaluation and eval-driven development: eval harnesses, golden datasets, prompt and QE design, tuning against gold sets, and reading failure modes well enough to tell a prompt problem from a data problem from a model problem.
Hands-on experience with TMS platforms such as Phrase or Smartling, and i18n standards (ICU/CLDR).
Bilingual in English and Chinese.
Transcreation, marketing localization or brand voice adaptation across markets.
Content systems experience — CMSes such as Contentful or Webflow — or familiarity with design tooling such as Figma.
Crypto, fintech or another high-compliance regulated domain.
