Job Description




We are looking for an experienced Senior Design Quality Engineer to play a key role in ensuring the quality, safety and regulatory compliance of medical devices throughout the product lifecycle.

Collaborating with the Senior Manager, Quality Engineering, you will deliver quality engineering mentorship across product build, development and manufacturing.

Working closely with R&D, Manufacturing, and Regulatory Affairs, you will help ensure quality and risk management principles are embedded into product and development decisions from the beginning.




This role offers an advanced technical position responsible for build assurance, build control, and risk management. It also includes providing technical mentorship to other Quality Engineers.




What you'll be doing

Lead and participate in develop control activities, including design reviews, verification and validation, and design transfer to manufacturing.
Lead risk management activities in accordance with ISO 14971, including hazard analysis, risk assessment and risk control.
Ensure product and build activities adhere to relevant medical-device regulations and standards, such as the Medical Device Regulation of the EU, ISO 13485, and FDA QMSR.
Partner with R&D, Production, and Regulatory Affairs to provide quality engineering expertise throughout product development and sustaining activities.
Develop and review quality plans, test protocols and reports, ensuring design assurance activities are detailed, accurate and appropriately documented.
Investigate and resolve quality issues associated with product design, including non-conformances, CAPAs and customer complaints.
Support internal and external audits as a subject-matter expert specializing in Build Quality Control.
Mentor and provide technical guidance to junior Quality Engineers.
Drive continuous improvement across the Quality Management System.




Skills and Experience Required:

A bachelor's degree or equivalent experience in Engineering, such as Biomedical, Electrical, Mechanical or Industrial Engineering, or a relevant scientific discipline.
Quality Engineering experience within the medical-device, Automotive or Aviation industry, with strong experience in Design Assurance.
In-depth knowledge of EU MDR, ISO 13485 and ISO 14971, together with a strong understanding of the international medical-device regulatory environment would be an advantage
Proven practical experience of building controls alongside hazard evaluation.
Strong analytical, problem-solving and decision-making capabilities.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including presenting sophisticated technical topics clearly to different audiences.
The ability to work independently while optimally operating within fast-paced, cross-functional teams.




Why this role?

This is an opportunity to influence product quality at a senior technical level and work at the intersection of Engineering, Quality, Regulatory and Manufacturing.

You'll combine hands-on Design Assurance expertise with the opportunity to guide others, solve complex quality challenges and help ensure medical devices meet the standards expected throughout their lifecycle.




Please note: This role is based at our Limerick site, where here you will be required to be on site 4 days per week.
