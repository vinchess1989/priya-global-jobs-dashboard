Role: EAM Technical Writer
Location: Brisbane CBD, Queensland
Engagement: 12 months plus likely extension

Seeking an EAM Technical Writer to support the Asset Systems and Delivery function by producing high-quality documentation for S/4HANA EAM configuration design and data standards. 

Key deliverables / responsibilities

Document S/4HANA EAM configuration design, including key design decisions, processes, and controls.
Develop and maintain EAM data standards documentation (definitions, standards, usage guidelines).
Produce documentation in a format suitable for both technical and business stakeholders.
Work with stakeholders to elicit, validate and refine requirements and documentation content.
Ensure documentation is consistent, version-controlled, and aligned to organisational standards
Required skills & experience
Significant SAP EAM experience 
Experience documenting configuration design and data standards in structured environments.
ArcGIS experience
Strong stakeholder engagement skills (workshops, interviews, validation).
Ability to translate complex technical detail into clear, usable documentation.
