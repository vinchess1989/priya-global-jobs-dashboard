Quality Manager
Power Generation & Engineering | £600–£700 per day | Outside IR35
We are currently supporting a leading engineering business within the energy and power generation sector, who are looking to engage an experienced Quality Manager on a contract basis to support project delivery.
This is an excellent opportunity for a quality professional with experience in engineering, manufacturing or power generation projects to take ownership of project quality activities, ensuring all documentation, inspections and compliance requirements are delivered to the required standards and within agreed timescales.
As Quality Manager, you will act as the central point of contact for all project-related quality matters, working closely with the client, internal engineering teams and supply chain to ensure quality requirements are met throughout the project lifecycle.
Key Responsibilities:

Act as the main point of contact for project quality matters, liaising with client representatives, internal teams and suppliers.
Ensure project quality milestones, targets and audits are completed within agreed timescales.
Prepare and review quality documentation to ensure compliance with client and project requirements.
Review supplier and internal Inspection and Test Plans (ITPs).
Manage material certification, manufacturing data, O&M manuals and compliance documentation.
Coordinate inspections and witness points with clients, suppliers and project teams.
Monitor progress, resolve quality issues and ensure activities are completed and formally closed out.
Work with technical authors to develop O&M manuals and supporting documentation that meet client expectations and required standards.
We are keen to speak with experienced Quality Managers who have:

A background in quality management within engineering, manufacturing, energy or power generation.
Experience managing project quality requirements from documentation through to inspection and final close-out.
Knowledge of ITPs, quality audits, inspection and witness points.
Experience reviewing material certification, manufacturing records and technical compliance documentation.
The ability to interpret client specifications and ensure all deliverables meet the required standards.
Strong stakeholder management skills and experience working with clients, suppliers and multidisciplinary teams.
