Title:

Lead Document and Information Management Specialist

Are you looking to expand your horizons and deliver high profile, exciting projects and leave a legacy? Join us, on the journey.

 

Rex Minerals is an Australian mining company dedicated to the development of its flagship A$1.0B Copper-Gold Hillside Project located on the Yorke Peninsula in South Australia. The project will be a significant contributor to the South Australian economy and is providing employment opportunities for locals and for those looking to move to the area.

 

Hillside is in a highly attractive regional location approximately 150km from Adelaide and is one of the largest copper/gold projects under development in Australia. The project is fully approved and early-stage site access work, major Highway diversions, site utilities and awarding of contracts and building the team to execute the project has commenced.

 

KBR is a global provider of full life cycle professional services supporting clients in delivery of major projects like Hillside and is providing expertise as part of an Integrated Owners Team delivering the Project on behalf of Rex Minerals.

 

The Opportunity

 

We are looking for a dynamic person to manage the document management function on the Hillside project You’ll be empowered  to work in a way that suits your style to deliver great outcomes and build success on the project.

 

As the Lead Document Management Specialist, you will be working on the Rex Minerals Hillside Copper project. The role is full time based in the project office in Adelaide CBD (flexibility negotiable). The focus of this role is to undertake all document management accountabilities including but not limited to:



Manage the Document Management system and distribution matrix
Manage additional project personnel needs for resource surge
Management of periodic transfer and storage of information from project management systems to the Document Management system
Checking/validation/verification of all documentation, including correct numbering, revisions, status, format, title and legibility
Maintenance of electronic records and document registers
Processing and recording incoming and outgoing vendor data and engineering design drawings
Maintaining accurate and up-to-date internal Document Management system for proactive reporting of key performance metrics
Responsible for the recording of document flow to and from all necessary parties
Issue and track the status of all documents and drawings
Train the project teams on Document Management systems and/or project specific systems and procedures
Managing and setting up the document review via workflow in InEight

 

Who are you?




As a Lead Document Management Specialist, you will have the following qualifications and experience:



Significant experience (typically 7+ years) in any related technical field or document control/ document management position
Significant experience in document control/ document quality or information management positions
Any experience in Document Management software including InEight,  Aconex, and Documentum would be an advantage, especially setting up and managing document review via workflow.
Proven history managing documentation of multiple contractors and consultants while contributing to a team effort in project environments
Ability to prioritize, multi-task and manage own workload
Strong work ethic and ownership of accountabilities
