Global Hedge Fund seeking Site Reliability Engineer's capable of building and maintaining internal platforms supporting Equity Derivatives portfolio managers, quants, and technology teams, across compute, messaging, data, and application infrastructure.




In this role you will design, build, and operate containerized workloads across on-premises and hybrid Kubernetes estate, with responsibility for deployment, reliability, capacity, infrastructure integration, networking and storage. Partner with development teams to containerize applications, move them onto Kubernetes, and set deployment standards. Develop robust, automated tooling for deployments, configuration management, and failover procedures using infrastructure-as-code, GitOps, and CI/CD.




Qualifications

An advanced degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, Physics, Financial Engineering, or a related field.




Required Skills

A strong platform or infrastructure engineering background, building and supporting production systems in Unix/Linux environments, with solid systems troubleshooting skills.
Proven hands-on experience designing, building, and operating production on-premises Kubernetes clusters. This includes upgrades, networking, storage, security, and high availability.
Experience with Kubernetes and container platforms such as Podman/Docker, CI/CD, infrastructure-as-code and observability tooling.
Hands-on programming experience with Python, Groovy, or C++, and scripting in Bash or Go.
Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to stay composed under pressure and work effectively with diverse stakeholders.
A proactive, detail-oriented mindset with strong ownership, critical thinking, and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
