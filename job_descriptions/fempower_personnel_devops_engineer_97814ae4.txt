ABOUT THE POSITION

Pretoria | Permanent



A leading investment management organisation is seeking an experienced DevOps Engineer to join its technology team.



The successful candidate will be responsible for improving software delivery processes, automating deployment pipelines, enhancing system reliability, and driving DevOps best practices across development and operations teams.



Minimum Requirements



Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Information Systems or related field


Agile/Scrum certification


4-5 years' experience in application development, support, and DevOps environments


Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines


Strong understanding of DevOps principles and Agile delivery methodologies


Experience with Azure DevOps, GitHub, GitLab, Jenkins, Docker, Kubernetes, or similar tools


Proficiency in one or more programming languages such as C#, .NET, Java, JavaScript, Python, HTML5, XML, or CSS


Microsoft Azure certifications advantageous




Key Responsibilities


Develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines


Automate build, testing, and deployment processes


Monitor application performance and service quality


Troubleshoot and resolve production issues


Collaborate with development, operations, security, and QA teams


Drive continuous improvement of software delivery processes


Research and implement DevOps tools and best practices




Competencies


Strong analytical and problem-solving skills


Excellent communication and stakeholder engagement skills


Ability to work in a fast-paced Agile environment


Strong organisational and time management skills




If you have a passion for automation, continuous improvement, and modern software delivery practices, we'd like to hear from you.
