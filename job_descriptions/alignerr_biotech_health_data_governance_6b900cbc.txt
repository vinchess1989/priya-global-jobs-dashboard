About The Role

What if your expertise in data governance could directly shape how the world's most advanced AI systems understand biotech and clinical research? We're looking for a Biotech Health Data Governance Lead to ensure that the data powering scientific discovery, regulatory submissions, and life sciences AI is accurate, traceable, secure, and compliant.

This is a fully remote, flexible contract role built for experienced professionals who understand the high stakes of governed data in regulated research environments.


Organization: Alignerr
Type: Hourly Contract
Location: Remote
Commitment: 10–40 hours/week


What You'll Do


Govern biotech research and clinical trial datasets to ensure accuracy, lineage, and full auditability for scientific analysis and regulatory submissions
Define, document, and enforce data policies covering classification, access controls, security standards, and metadata management across research, clinical, regulatory, and partner teams
Enable secure, governed access to sensitive datasets for analytics, innovation, and external collaborations — while protecting confidential and patient-related information
Identify and resolve data quality issues that could compromise research integrity or compliance posture
Collaborate with scientific, IT, compliance, and business stakeholders to align data standards, workflows, and governance frameworks
Support data annotation, evaluation, and quality processes that feed into AI training pipelines


Who You Are


Experienced in leading or implementing data governance programs within biotech, life sciences, clinical research, or other regulated data environments
Deeply knowledgeable about data privacy, security, compliance, and regulatory expectations for research and clinical trial data (e.g., HIPAA, GxP, 21 CFR Part 11)
A skilled cross-functional collaborator — equally comfortable working with scientists, IT architects, compliance officers, and business leads
Detail-oriented and systematic — you understand that in regulated research, the integrity of the data is the integrity of the science
Self-directed and capable of owning governance initiatives from design through implementation


Nice to Have


Prior experience with data annotation, data quality frameworks, or AI training data evaluation
Familiarity with clinical data standards such as CDISC, HL7, or FHIR
Background working with biobanks, genomics data, or electronic data capture (EDC) systems
Experience supporting regulatory submissions (FDA, EMA) with data governance artifacts


Why Join Us


Work at the intersection of life sciences and cutting-edge AI — your governance work directly enables better science and smarter AI
Fully remote and flexible — structure your hours around your life, not the other way around
Freelance autonomy with meaningful, high-impact project work
Collaborate with world-leading AI research teams and life sciences organizations
Potential for contract extension as projects grow and evolve
