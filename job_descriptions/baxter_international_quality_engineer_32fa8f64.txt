Quality Engineer

 

Baxter Thetford | Quality Manufacturing Team

 

Are you a problem-solver who thrives in a fast-paced manufacturing environment? Do you enjoy tackling new challenges every day and taking ownership of improvements that make a real difference to products and patients? If so, we'd love to hear from you.

 

At Baxter Thetford, we're looking for a Quality Engineer to join our Quality Manufacturing team. This is an opportunity to play a key role in ensuring our life-saving healthcare products meet the highest standards of quality, compliance, and safety.

 

 

We offer some fantastic benefits which are listed below:

25 days annual leave + bank holidays
Blue light card
Fantastic internal progression opportunities
Subsided canteen
Westfield healthcare
Up to 8% pension contribution
Employee assistance programme


 

 

What you'll be doing

Performing QA tests, e.g. leak analysis.
Reviewing all quality processes and procedures & advising on compliance with Corporate and regulatory requirements.
Reviewing atypical events and complaints and initiating local holds.
Completing Quality Systems/QA commitments
Working in close collaboration with Batch release teams & operations.
Performing Continual improvement and compliance monitoring Gembas
Assessing change controls.
Investigating and approving any Quality NCR. Owning and approving and Quality CAPA and change control.


 

What you'll bring

Experience in the use of quality improvement and problem-solving tools and the interpretation of data. Experience of critical equipment and process change control and validation
Qualified in a Scientific or Engineering degree subject area (crucial).
Experience and/or knowledge of equipment calibration.
Manufacturing & Quality Assurance exposure
Excellent stakeholder engagement
Experienced in owning the investigation process
Experienced in Trackwise (or similar) desirable


 

 

What happens next?

This role gives you the scope of a global healthcare organisation while keeping your contribution close to the work, customers and products it supports. If the Quality Engineer role matches your experience and the kind of responsibility you are looking for, we’d like to hear from you.

 

Thank you for your interest in Baxter, and we look forward to seeing your application!
