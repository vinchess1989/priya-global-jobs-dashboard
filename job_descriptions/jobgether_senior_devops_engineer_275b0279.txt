This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, which manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer based in South Africa.

We are seeking an experienced Senior DevOps Engineer to help scale and strengthen a globally distributed cloud infrastructure supporting innovative digital and financial technology services. In this hands-on role, you will design, automate, and maintain secure, resilient infrastructure across Google Cloud Platform and Kubernetes environments. You will drive the adoption of Infrastructure as Code, improve deployment workflows, and strengthen security and observability as services prepare for continued growth. Working within a small, senior, fully remote engineering team, you will take ownership of services end to end and make pragmatic technical decisions. You will also collaborate with other teams to improve infrastructure practices and help them become more self-sufficient. This is an opportunity to shape a highly automated, scalable platform in a fast-moving technology environment.

Accountabilities


Design, build, and maintain Google Cloud infrastructure using Terraform, ensuring production infrastructure is defined as code and changes are reviewed through pull requests.
Operate and continuously improve Google Kubernetes Engine (GKE) clusters, including upgrades, capacity planning, autoscaling, networking, and workload isolation.
Migrate manually deployed services to Kubernetes, establishing documented, consistent, and repeatable deployment processes.
Consolidate configuration, access, and user management across Google Cloud projects into a centralized source of truth.
Maintain clearly separated and secure environments for internal services, partner workloads, and future community-facing services.
Collaborate with internal engineering teams to migrate their infrastructure to Infrastructure as Code and Kubernetes-based environments.
Maintain and improve GitHub Actions workflows, applying security best practices such as pinned dependencies, least-privilege tokens, efficient runner usage, and cost controls.
Develop and maintain observability capabilities across metrics, logging, and alerting to ensure services are monitored and actionable issues are identified promptly.
Apply least-privilege access controls across Google Cloud, Kubernetes, and GitHub, regularly reviewing temporary administrative permissions.
Contribute to quarterly and annual technical roadmaps, provide realistic project estimates, and recommend discovery phases for complex initiatives.
Take ownership of infrastructure services from design through operation, ensuring reliability, security, scalability, and maintainability.


Requirements


Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.
At least 5 years of experience in DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), platform engineering, or infrastructure engineering, including at least 2 years operating production workloads at a senior level.
Strong hands-on experience managing Kubernetes in production, including deployments, networking, upgrades, autoscaling, and troubleshooting.
Advanced experience with Terraform, including module design, state management, and infrastructure management across multiple environments.
Solid practical knowledge of Google Cloud Platform, particularly GKE, IAM, networking, Pub/Sub, and similar managed services.
Experience implementing and operating observability solutions for metrics, logs, and alerting using Grafana or comparable tools.
Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines, ideally with GitHub Actions, as well as developing and operating containerized services using Docker.
Strong understanding of infrastructure security practices, including secrets management, identity and access management (IAM), and least-privilege principles.
Solid Linux and networking fundamentals, with scripting skills in Bash.
Proven experience migrating manually managed services to Infrastructure as Code.
Ability to work independently in a fully remote and asynchronous environment, manage priorities effectively, and communicate clearly with stakeholders.
Excellent written and spoken English.


Nice To Have


Google Cloud certifications, such as Professional Cloud DevOps Engineer or Professional Cloud Architect, or Kubernetes certifications such as CKA or CKS.
Experience operating infrastructure in environments with strict security requirements.
Experience managing large-scale push notification infrastructure, including Firebase Cloud Messaging or Apple Push Notification service (APNs).
Familiarity with peer-to-peer technologies or distributed systems.
Knowledge of Node.js services and their production deployment and operation.


Benefits


Fully remote working arrangement, with the opportunity to work from anywhere worldwide.
Opportunity to join a globally distributed team of experienced technology professionals.
Hands-on ownership of critical infrastructure and the opportunity to influence technical architecture and engineering standards.
Opportunity to work with modern cloud technologies, including Google Cloud, Kubernetes, Terraform, and automation tooling.
Collaborative environment that encourages independent decision-making, technical innovation, and continuous improvement.
Exposure to large-scale digital infrastructure and services in the evolving fintech and technology sectors.
