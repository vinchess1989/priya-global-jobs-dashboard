Who are we?

Sanlam Life and Savings (SLS) is focused on serving our retail and corporate clients in South Africa and further developing our strategic advantages in the South African market. Sanlam Life and Savings consists of the following business units Retail Mass, Corporate, Risk and Savings, Glacier and various business enabling functions. The Sanlam Life and Savings Office provides strategic direction, coordination and support to the four clusters, as well as performing governance oversight that includes assurance provided by second line of defense functions in SLS, to enable us to meet our business objectives.

Business Transformation and Shared Services Technology, or BTSS Tech, owns and enables important technology platforms that allow existing clients to engage with Sanlam through servicing, claims, complaints, collections and payments. We also provide the technology that enables Sanlam’s Customer Service teams to support clients effectively.

Purpose of the Role

The Intermediate Delivery Lead enables cross-functional product and technology teams to deliver customer and business value in a predictable, sustainable and transparent way.

Working closely with Product Owners, Tech Leads, Engineering Managers and delivery teams, the role facilitates effective Agile ways of working, improves delivery flow, identifies and removes impediments, manages dependencies and helps teams continuously improve how they plan and deliver.

The role supports BTSS Tech’s move towards product-based delivery and increasingly autonomous teams. It goes beyond facilitating Agile ceremonies or coordinating status. The Delivery Lead helps the team understand how work is flowing, where delivery is constrained and what needs to change to improve outcomes.

At intermediate level, the role is expected to independently support two to three squads, depending on delivery complexity, dependency load and team maturity, while escalating systemic issues and drawing on senior delivery or Agile practitioners where broader organisational intervention is required.

The Opportunity

BTSS Technology runs the platforms that Sanlam customers and Customer Service teams rely on every day. At the same time, we are modernising our technology landscape and changing how technology is delivered.

As an Intermediate Delivery Lead, you will help teams establish effective delivery rhythms, organise around customer and business outcomes, manage dependencies and continuously improve how value flows from idea to production.

This is not a project administration or status coordination role, and success is not measured by running perfect ceremonies. Success means helping teams become more autonomous, transparent, predictable and able to deliver valuable outcomes continuously.

Overview Of Role Responsibilities


Enable effective team delivery
Facilitate effective planning, refinement, daily collaboration, reviews, retrospectives and other appropriate delivery cadences.
Help teams translate priorities into clear, achievable increments of work and meaningful delivery goals.
Encourage shared accountability, ownership and self-management rather than creating dependency on the Delivery Lead.
Create an environment where Product, Engineering, Quality Assurance, Business Analysis and other specialists collaborate as one product team.
Help teams organise work around customer and business outcomes rather than activities and outputs.
Facilitate Agile ways of working
Facilitate Scrum, Kanban or other appropriate practices based on the team’s delivery context.
Ensure ceremonies and cadences have a clear purpose and result in decisions, alignment or action.
Help teams apply Agile and Lean principles pragmatically rather than following practices mechanically.
Support backlog refinement and readiness in partnership with the Product Owner.
Help teams break larger outcomes into smaller, deliverable increments.
Improve delivery flow and predictability
Use delivery information to help teams understand how work moves through the system and where improvement is needed.
Make Work in Progress, blocked work and ageing visible and challenge excessive parallel work.
Use measures such as cycle time, lead time, throughput, sprint goal achievement, defect trends, release frequency and dependency health as learning signals, not as measures of individual performance.
Identify bottlenecks and work with the team to improve flow, quality and delivery predictability.
Manage dependencies and impediments
Identify delivery dependencies, risks and potential blockers early.
Make dependencies, owners, target dates and required outcomes visible.
Coordinate interlocks with other squads, platforms, suppliers and business or technology stakeholders.
Facilitate resolution of impediments within the team’s control and escalate systemic constraints appropriately.
Follow issues through to resolution rather than simply recording them.
Support delivery planning and confidence
Help translate product priorities into realistic, capacity-informed delivery plans.
Facilitate decisions where demand exceeds available capacity.
Help teams distinguish between targets, forecasts and firm commitments.
Identify emerging delivery risks early and support replanning when priorities, capacity or dependencies change.
Provide evidence-based delivery insights rather than relying solely on subjective status reporting.
Enable continuous improvement
Facilitate meaningful retrospectives and improvement conversations.
Help teams identify root causes rather than repeatedly treating symptoms.
Translate improvement opportunities into practical actions and assess whether the changes improved outcomes.
Encourage experimentation, feedback and learning.
Help reduce unnecessary hand-offs, waiting time and rework.
Share useful practices and learning with other Delivery Leads.
Support product-oriented delivery
Work closely with Product Owners to connect product priorities to team execution.
Help maintain clear, prioritised and actionable backlogs.
Encourage smaller increments of value and faster feedback.
Support the transition from project-oriented behaviours towards persistent, outcome-focused product teams.
Respect role boundaries: Product Managers and Product Owners retain accountability for product direction and priority.
Promote healthy team dynamics
Create inclusive and constructive team conversations.
Encourage team members to surface delivery concerns early.
Help teams address conflict constructively and build shared accountability.
Model servant leadership, openness, empathy and respectful challenge.
Build team confidence without creating command-and-control behaviours.


What Success Looks Like

You are succeeding when:


Teams increasingly manage routine delivery without depending on you.
Delivery risks and impediments are identified earlier rather than emerging late.
Dependencies are visible, owned and actively managed.
Delivery forecasts become more realistic and transparent.
Work flows with less waiting, rework and unnecessary Work in Progress.
Product and Technology share an understanding of priorities and delivery confidence.
Agile practices support delivery outcomes rather than becoming an end in themselves.
Teams use evidence and feedback to improve.
Retrospectives result in visible improvements.
Customer and business outcomes increasingly shape how teams plan and deliver.


Qualifications


Relevant bachelor’s degree, diploma or equivalent tertiary qualification in Information Technology, Business, Project Management or a related discipline would be advantageous.
Recognised Agile or Scrum certification such as PSM I, CSM, SAFe Scrum Master or equivalent.
Additional exposure to Kanban, Lean, Disciplined Agile, SAFe or DevOps would be advantageous.


Experience


Typically 3 to 5 years’ experience in Agile technology delivery environments.
Typically 2 to 4 years’ experience as a Scrum Master, Delivery Lead, Agile Delivery Lead or similar role.
Practical experience supporting cross-functional technology teams.
Experience facilitating Scrum and/or Kanban ways of working.
Experience identifying and coordinating delivery dependencies and impediments.
Experience using delivery information and metrics to support team improvement.
Exposure to scaled or multi-team technology delivery environments.
Experience in financial services or another complex, regulated environment would be advantageous.

Delivery planning and forecasting
Flow and delivery metrics

Backlog and refinement practices

Personal Qualities

We Are Looking For Someone Who Brings


Delivery ownership: Helps the team maintain momentum and transparency without taking ownership away from the team.
Coaching presence: Builds team capability and ownership rather than dependency.
Facilitation: Structures productive conversations that result in clarity, decisions and action.
Courage: Surfaces delivery risks and challenges constructively.
Influence: Builds alignment across Product, Engineering, Business and Technology without relying on positional authority.
Empathy: Understands different perspectives within multidisciplinary teams.
Systems thinking: Looks beyond individual tasks to understand the overall flow of value.
Outcome orientation: Focuses on what changed for the customer or business, not simply what was completed.
Adaptability: Applies practices according to context rather than enforcing a framework mechanically.
Resilience: Brings steady energy and constructive challenge in a complex, changing environment.


Why Join SLS BTSS Tech?

BTSS Tech sits at the intersection of technology transformation and customer experience. The solutions we build influence how clients engage with Sanlam and how effectively our service teams can support them.

You Will Have The Opportunity To


Help modernise a complex and important technology landscape.
Work across established enterprise platforms and emerging technologies.
Build practical experience across product, software, cloud, data and integration delivery.
Learn from experienced colleagues while bringing new thinking and energy into the team.
Help teams improve delivery confidence, flow and customer outcomes.
Contribute to Sanlam’s Customer North Star and Vision 2030 ambitions.
