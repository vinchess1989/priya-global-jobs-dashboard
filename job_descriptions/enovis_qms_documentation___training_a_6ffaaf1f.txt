Job Title: QMS Documentation & Training Analyst

Reports To: Sr. Director, QA/RA Shared Services

Location: Hyderabad, India

Business Unit Description: Regulatory Affairs and Quality Assurance Shared Services

Position Summary: The QMS Documentation & Training Analyst supports the development, harmonization, implementation, and maintenance of Quality Management System documentation and associated training for a global medical-device organization.

This role combines technical writing, Quality Systems expertise, and instructional design. The analyst will partner with Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Engineering, Operations, IT, and subject-matter experts to convert regulatory requirements and business processes into clear, controlled QMS documentation and engaging training content.

A key responsibility will be converting complex procedures and processes into role-based, interactive learning experiences that help employees understand not only what the procedure requires, but how to perform the process correctly.

Key Responsibilities

QMS Documentation


Draft, revise, and harmonize global:
Policies
SOPs
Work instructions
Forms and templates
Process maps
Job aids and quick-reference guides
Interview SMEs and translate complex processes into concise, executable procedures.
Ensure consistency between policies, procedures, work instructions, forms and related records.
Support document remediation and harmonization across legacy Quality Management Systems.
Perform document gap assessments and cross-reference affected QMS documentation.
Support controlled-document routing, review, approval, revision and implementation activities.
Apply Good Documentation Practices and document-control requirements.

This aligns well with your existing QMS change process. For example, Change Control and MDG Documents describes responsibilities including creating/updating document content, evaluating QMS impact across governing and impacted documents, maintaining references and formatting, and completing the training assignment before document release.

Training / Instructional Design


Conduct training-needs analyses associated with new and revised QMS processes.
Translate SOPs and work instructions into role-based learning objectives.
Develop:
Interactive e-learning modules
Scenario-based learning
Knowledge checks
Assessments
Short instructional videos
Process simulations
Microlearning
Instructor-led training decks
Job aids
Build training that emphasizes practical decision-making rather than simply repeating procedure text.
Develop assessments demonstrating employee understanding and training effectiveness.
Maintain training source content as controlled procedures change.
Collaborate with process owners to determine appropriate training depth based on role and risk.

FDA 21 CFR Part 820 / QMSR
ISO 14971
EU MDR 2017/745
MDSAP principles
CAPA
Nonconformance
Complaint handling
Design controls
Change control
Document control
Supplier quality

Training and competency requirements


They do not need to be a Regulatory Affairs expert, but they need enough medical-device knowledge to recognize when procedure language has regulatory significance.

Technology Skills Required


Microsoft Word, including advanced formatting and templates
PowerPoint
Excel
SharePoint

Document-control or eQMS platforms


Strongly Preferred


Microsoft Forms
Power Automate
ComplianceWire or similar LMS
MasterControl, Veeva Vault, TrackWise, Agile PLM or comparable validated systems

Qualifications Preferred


Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Life Sciences, Quality, Regulatory Affairs, Instructional Design, Technical Communication or related discipline.
4 to 8 years professional experience.
Minimum 2 to 3 years supporting medical devices, pharmaceuticals, diagnostics or another regulated industry.
Demonstrated experience writing controlled QMS documentation.
Demonstrated portfolio of e-learning or interactive instructional content.
Excellent written English.
Experience working remotely with US-based teams.
