Quality Manager role based in Burnley. Working for a well established, growing, automotive, aerospace and industrial manufacturing company

Client Details

Our client is a well established, growing industrial automotive manufacturing and engineering company based in Burnley.

Description

* The role of Quality Manager is to be responsible for Managing a Quality Engineering team ensuring that products,
services, or processes meet specified quality standards and requirements for the automotive division

* Be accountable for the company automotive IATF Quality Management System

* Managing the Quality Engineers / Technicians & Inspectors in each business unit, setting goals and targets (monitoring via appraisals)

* Mentor and upskill current Quality Department.

* Ensure compliance to the Integrated Management system (IMS)

* Promote a proactive Quality culture


* Developing and implementing quality control procedures and guidelines to ensure that products or services meet the

desired quality standards

* Oversee the various stages of production or service delivery to ensure adherence to quality standards. This may

involve conducting inspections, audits, or tests to identify any defects, deviations, or areas for improvement

* To be the main Quality contact to the internal manufacturing team & external customers, effectively managing their

expectations, to increase trust, and build strong lasting relationships

* Analysing data to identify trends, failure modes, patterns, themes & recurring issues, and use this information to

improve processes or address quality concerns

* Implementing corrective actions: When quality issues arise, investigate the root causes, and implements robust

corrective actions to prevent repeat concerns

* Manage 8D's for all internal & customer issues

* Ensuring all corrective and preventative measures are validated with data, and lessons learned are carried across to other applicable products and processes

* Provide and maintain KPI's related to non-conformance, Cost of Poor Quality, and concern closure timing adherence

* Leading all Quality review meetings

* Manage product, process audits & LPA's

* Ensure control plans, PFMEAs, and associated documentation are implemented effectively and maintained

* Ensure products are being manufactured in line with customer requirements

* Manage all quality engineering activities for new products in line with APQP/PPAP

* Review feasibility from a Quality perspective on our ability to measure and test to all customer requirements in line with design records provided (Ensure error protection and poke yoke is considered and agreed)

* Supporting PFMEA reviews to understand process risk and agree on risk mitigation measures

* Leading process capability studies and process validation prior to production

* Manage all measurement equipment - Calibration

* Ensuring MSA studies are conducted in line with the MSA procedure, and meet internal and customer requirements

* Assess the competency levels of all trainees and review with Group Director of Quality where appropriate

* Assist with training of quality awareness / quality culture

* Ensure complicity with Health & Safety regulations, the Company Handbook, Quality and Environmental standards, and all other Company policies and procedures

* Maintain a visible presence on the shop floor, supporting production teams and quality personnel in resolving day-to-day quality concerns.

* Lead investigations (8D's) containment activities and root cause analysis

* Work directly with production managers, operators, technicians, inspectors and engineers to identify and implement practical process improvements

* Demonstrate a proactive, hands-on leadership style and willingness to roll up their sleeves when necessary

* Maintain a visible presence on the shop floor, supporting production teams and quality personnel in resolving day-to-day quality concerns.

Profile

* Proven track record as a Quality Manager in an Automotive IATF accredited manufacturing environment.

* Senior Quality Engineers in the automotive sectors with this background please also apply if you are looking for that next step up in your career.

* Hands-on leader with a practical, solutions-focused approach and willingness to work directly on the shop floor

alongside operational teams.

- Excellent interpersonal skills, with the confidence to manage a team

* Articulate, confident communicator at all levels

* Excellent ability to work as part of a teamwork, understanding the importance of team structure and contributing to

problem solving activities

* Proven experience of successfully working within an engineering focused environment which incorporated elements

of:

- Quality Assurance standards

- Process improvement techniques

- Production and manufacturing processes and techniques

- Qualifications: Relevant manufacturing/engineering degree, HND, or HNC

- industrial manufacturing procecces - mechanical production, automtoive, aerospace sectors or alike
