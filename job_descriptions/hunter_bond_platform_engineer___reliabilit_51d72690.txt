Job title: Platform Engineer – Reliability

Client: Elite FinTech

The role:




My client are seeking a knowledgeable Platform Engineer to work on their low latency Linux estate.




This role sits somewhere in between Platform / DevOps based work, and SRE. There is a focus on uptime, reliability and availability, whilst also conducting the necessary engineering, project work and infrastructure building that Platform work demands.




You will work in both a project and BAU capacity, automating wherever possible.

Python

AWS or GCP
DR

HPC
