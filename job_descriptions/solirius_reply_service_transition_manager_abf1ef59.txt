The Role

The IT Service Transition Manager ensures that new or updated IT services are moved seamlessly into live operational environments, without disrupting business activities.

Acting as the bridge between Project Delivery, Software Development and Service Operations, the role governs release readiness, operational handovers and service validation across the organisation.

Responsibilities


Transition Governance & Planning: Define, maintain and enforce the Service Transition framework, gate criteria and readiness checklists across all project lifecycles.
Operational Readiness & Handover: Partner with project managers, development teams and 3rd-party vendors to ensure complete handover packages (SOPs, KB articles, runbooks, monitoring rules and escalation paths).
Release & Deployment Management: Coordinate deployment schedules, post-implementation reviews (PIR) and early-life support (ELS) periods to minimise risk during production go-lives.
Knowledge & Asset Management: Ensure service management databases (CMDB), configuration items and support documentation are accurate before sign-off.
Stakeholder & Vendor Management: Facilitate operational sign-offs across Service Desk, Infrastructure, Cybersecurity and Business Units.


Requirements


5+ years in IT Service Management, with at least 3 years specifically in Service Transition, Release Management or Project Handover.
Strong cross-functional facilitation, risk management, conflict resolution and communication skills.
Experience managing service transition from development into live support environments.
Knowledge of acceptance into service processes and service readiness criteria.
ITIL 4 Foundation required, ITIL Managing Professional (ITIL v3 Service Transition) highly preferred.
Experience communicating with stakeholders, working to client-defined governance, and coordinating cross-functional teams across delivery, support, and operations.
Proficiency with enterprise ITSM tooling (ServiceNow, Jira Service Management, Halo) and knowledge of CI/CD pipeline deployments.
Experience working with Service Owners.
Experience with ServiceNow.
Experience with JIRA.
