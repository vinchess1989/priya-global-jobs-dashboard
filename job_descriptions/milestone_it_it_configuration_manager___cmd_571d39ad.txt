Milestone IT is partnering with a leading healthcare organisation to recruit an experienced IT Configuration Manager / CMDB Specialist for an initial 12-month contract.

This is an excellent opportunity to join a large hospital environment and play a key role in strengthening its IT configuration management practices, ensuring accurate, reliable and well-maintained configuration data across its technology landscape.

Working closely with IT infrastructure, applications and service management teams, you will take ownership of the organisation's Configuration Management Database (CMDB), ensuring all configuration items, relationships and associated data are accurately maintained within ServiceNow.

Key Responsibilities

Manage, maintain and continuously improve the organisation's CMDB within ServiceNow.
Ensure configuration items (CIs), including hardware, software, applications and infrastructure assets, are accurately recorded and maintained.
Establish, maintain and validate relationships and dependencies between configuration items.
Conduct regular CMDB audits, data quality checks and reconciliation activities.
Identify and resolve data inconsistencies, gaps and duplication to maintain the integrity of the CMDB.
Develop and implement configuration management processes, standards and procedures.
Work closely with infrastructure, application support and IT service management teams to ensure accurate configuration data.
Support ITIL-aligned configuration management practices and ongoing service improvement initiatives.
Provide reporting and insights into CMDB health, data accuracy and compliance.
Skills & Experience

To be successful in this role, you will ideally have:


Demonstrated experience in a CMDB, Configuration Management or IT Configuration Management role.
Strong hands-on experience with ServiceNow CMDB, including managing configuration items, relationships and data quality.
A recognised Configuration Management certification or relevant formal qualification.
Strong understanding of ITIL and configuration management principles and processes.
Experience conducting CMDB audits, data reconciliation and data quality improvements.
Understanding of IT infrastructure, applications, hardware, software and network environments.
Excellent stakeholder engagement skills, with the ability to work across technical and non-technical teams.
Strong analytical skills and attention to detail.
What's on Offer?
Initial 12-month contract opportunity.
Work within a well-established healthcare organisation.
Opportunity to make a meaningful contribution to IT service management and configuration management practices.
Collaborative environment working alongside experienced IT teams.
If you have strong ServiceNow CMDB experience and a passion for maintaining accurate, reliable configuration data, we'd love to hear from you!
