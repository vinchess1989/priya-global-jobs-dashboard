We deliver on our purpose by providing simple, meaningful ways to help Kiwis improve their financial wellbeing, today and tomorrow. We achieve this by doing what we do best, championing innovation and fresh thinking, so we can open new opportunities for more people to stay one step ahead.

We have an opportunity for a sharp minded technologist to join our Engineering Practice in a Systems Engineer position. The role is embedded in the core team, which is a key part of the Financial Crime Tribe. Squads in the tribe primarily focus on, prevention and management of the impact of financial crime happening.

About the role: 

As a Systems Engineer, you will take the ownership and proactively manage the availability and performance of all supported technologies along with other SRE engineers in the platform. This role will support ongoing project and BAU tasks, aligning with DevOps practices.

What You'll Do


Linux and Windows operating systems server support
Services in Tribe - Applications/Systems support
Develop your technical competence using fault situations as an opportunity to learn.
Assist in the resolution of technical incidents and problems as Tiers 1 & 2 support.
Vulnerability management
Create, maintain and support GitHub pipelines
Involvement in process management and improvement.
Comfortable working in a highly regulatory environment


About You

Willingness to learn, proven initiative, knowledge sharing, documentation skills and problem solving abilities are what we look for in every engineer to lead from their position.

With regards to background, you will need the following capabilities:


3+ years of experience as a systems engineer or software engineer.
Intermediate level Windows and Linux systems administration skills
Automation mindset to automate highly repetitive tasks
Scripting (PowerShell and Bash)
Deployment/release automation - Octopus, Ansible etc
Exposure to working with GitHub
Azure Kubernetes experience
Exposure to SQL Database or Oracle Database administration
Observability knowledge and experience with Elastic


These Capabilities Are Not Mandatory But Would Be Favourable


Experience working in Agile environment
Exposure to Cloud Technologies
RHCSA/ RHCE/ RHCA certifications

ASB's sense of who we are and what we stand for is grounded in our values. They guide our business in our day-to-day interactions with our team, customers and community. They are what makes ASB - ASB.   

Along with our interactions, values guide our actions and conduct, making ASB a safe and inclusive environment for both our people and our customers. They show that we care, are passionate and courageous, and that we act with integrity. Most importantly they unite us - because they are ASB.   

We offer a flexible and supportive work environment as well as an open and inclusive culture; a place where we welcome and value your individuality, ideas and goals.   

If we've nailed our pitch and this role sounds like it might be the one for you then please click "apply"… we'd love to hear from you!

At ASB, we are dedicated to our purpose of accelerating financial progress for all New Zealanders. It's the 'why' behind everything we do. We are committed to supporting our customers, people and community as we continue to innovate, so more Kiwis can build the future they want.
