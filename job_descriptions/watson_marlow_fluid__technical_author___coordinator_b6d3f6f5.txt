WATSON MARLOW FLUID TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS – INDIA ENGINEERING HUB

Engineering | Pune | Ground-Up India Engineering Hub

Be part of the founding team building a prestigious new India Engineering Hub for WMFTS




TECHNICAL AUTHOR – COORDINATOR




CORE REQUIREMENTS

Education: Graduate Engineer – Mechanical / Electronics / Mechatronics or related engineering discipline.
Experience: 8+ years of relevant professional experience.
Global Exposure: Experience working in a multinational/global engineering environment, with exposure to global stakeholder management.
Authoring Expertise: Strong experience in technical authoring / customer-facing technical documentation.
Manual Creation: H&s-on experience in creating technical documentation like manuals related to engineered products.
Cross-Functional Synthesis: Proven ability to interpret technical information from engineering, product management & test activities.
Execution Discipline: Ability to deliver coordinated documentation outputs to agreed priorities & timelines.
Systematic Approach: Systematic approach to authoring, task coordination, change control & consistency.




GOOD TO HAVE

Experience in creating technical documentation standards & templates.
Experience in using MadCap Flare / Adobe is preferred.
Experience in using PTC Creo Illustrator / Photoshop / Adobe Illustrator to create & edit images.
Experience in language translation will be an added advantage.




WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING

Create, update & maintain customer-facing technical documentation using WMFTS standards & structured authoring practices.
Apply controlled terminology & Simplified Technical English principles.
Source & interpret technical information from engineering, product management & test activities.
Coordinate Technical Communications work in the India Hub- workload planning, progress tracking & reporting.
Coordinate h&-offs & feedback between India Hub resources & UK-based Technical Authors / Senior Technical Author.
Prepare documentation for senior technical review & maintain traceability, version control & approval workflow.
Support continuous improvement in coordination efficiency, task definition & delivery consistency.




WHO YOU’LL BE WORKING WITH

Technical Communications Manager & Senior Technical Author.
UK-based Technical Authors & India Hub Technical Authors/publishing resources.
Engineering, Product Management, Quality, Compliance & third-party translation/localisation partners.




INCLUSION - EVERYONE IS INCLUDED
