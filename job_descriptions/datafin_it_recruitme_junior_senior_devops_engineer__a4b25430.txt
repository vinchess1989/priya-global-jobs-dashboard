Duties & Responsibilities

ENVIRONMENT:

A strong technical Junior-Senior DevOps Engineer is wanted to join the team of an innovative Software Solutions company in Cape Town.Your role will include the administration and support of Linux, Tomcat, Postgres, Oracle, Unix Scripting in Bash and Python and network management, including firewall and Linux iptables configuration. The ideal candidate should preferably possess a suitable Degree, but highly relevant experience will also be considered, and your tech tools must include Linux and Postgres. You will also need Tomcat, Oracle, Bash, Python and Ansible skills. This is a hybrid position with the team meeting at least 4 times a month.

DUTIES:

Administration and support of Linux, Tomcat, Postgres, Oracle.
Unix Scripting in Bash and Python.
Network management, including firewall and Linux iptables configuration.
Automation system such as Ansible.
The design and implementation of monitoring systems.
Administration of a cloud-based infrastructure as a service platform.
Technical and user-guide documentation.

REQUIREMENTS:

A relevant Degree is preferred, but highly relevant experience will also be considered.
Technical Skills: A combination of Linux administration skills and Postgres experience is preferred.
Desired Experience & Qualification
Junior, Senior, DevOps, Engineer, Linux, Postgres, CPT, Hybrid,
