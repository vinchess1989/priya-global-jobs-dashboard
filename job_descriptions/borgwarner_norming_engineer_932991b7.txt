Key Responsibilities

Management and coordination of standardization projects across TTT BorgWarner sites.

The Norming Engineer (Technical Writer) should be an expert in standards writing. He coordinates an internal BorgWarner standardization project in all working stages.

The tasks are


Assist the requester with the BorgWarner Standard Request
Develop the standardization concept (e.g., one standard, multiple standards, a series of standards with multiple parts, other types of documents such as forms, templates, reports, process/work/design instructions)
Check plausibility. Review of applicable or relevant internal and external standard documents
Assign BorgWarner Standard number according to our internal rule book.
Log progress (e.g., update BWS Request Form, update tracking list, informing participants)
Editorially revise a manuscript provided by the requester (e.g., templates, format, check of technical standards)
Create a preliminary draft of the standard based on the requester’s manuscript
Obtaining missing information from the requester
Manage the project with the aim of achieving consensus (e.g., project schedule, organization and chairing of meetings, following up on work items)
Support and moderate the BorgWarner Standard Forum. The BorgWarner Standard Forum serves to foster technical discussion. In general, a discussion forum is opened for each standardization project after consensus has been reached among primary stakeholders
Administer BorgWarner Standard Approval Workflow
Release the approved BorgWarner Standard


Furthermore, the Norming Engineer (Technical Writer) ensures accessibility, and communication of technical standards across the organization. This role supports internal departments by maintaining the standards database, managing documentation updates, and providing expert guidance on standard-related topics.

Technical Documentation Management


Edit and verify updates to technical documentation related to engineering and corporate standards.
Ensure documentation complies with internal quality requirements and external regulations.


Standards Database Administration


Maintain and update the internal standards database.
Ensure data accuracy, version control, and user accessibility.


Change Communication


Inform relevant departments about updates or changes to standards.


Internal Advisory Services


Act as a subject matter expert for standards-related inquiries.
Support internal customers with interpretation, application, and compliance with standards.


Norming Engineer reports to Team Leader Product Compliance, Standards, & Sustainability (ESPS)

Education & Experience

Experience - 2- 4 Years


Technical apprenticeship or degree, preferably in mechanical engineering
Business fluent in spoken and written English
Expertise in standardization
Work experience (in standardization, design, quality management, manufacturing beneficial)
Familiarity with ISO, DIN, or other relevant standards systems.


Skills & Competencies


Outstanding MS Office skills
Ability to understand complex interrelations, logical thinking and ability to work independently are crucial prerequisites
Detail-Oriented Ability to spot inconsistencies and ensure high-quality documentation.
Communication Clear and professional communication with technical and non-technical audiences.
Analytical Thinking Ability to assess the impact of standards changes and advise accordingly.
Collaboration Works effectively across departments and with diverse teams.
Initiative Takes ownership of tasks and proactively identifies areas for improvement.


Strong proficiency in document control systems and database tools
