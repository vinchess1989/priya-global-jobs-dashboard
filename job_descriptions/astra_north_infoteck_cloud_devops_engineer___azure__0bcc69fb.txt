Cloud DevOps Engineer - Azure, Terraform, Kubernetes, CI/CD & Data Platforms


Key Responsibilities & Requirements


Cloud Infrastructure & Platform Management


The successful candidate will demonstrate strong working experience with major cloud platforms such as Azure with the ability to design, deploy, and maintain scalable cloud infrastructure.


You will be proficient in network troubleshooting for cloud environments and capable of diagnosing and resolving complex infrastructure issues.


Infrastructure as Code & Automation


You will have solid working experience with Terraform and the ability to create reliable, scalable infrastructure through code.


Beyond implementation, you must be able to troubleshoot and resolve Terraform-related issues, optimizing infrastructure definitions for maintainability and efficiency.


Containerization & Orchestration


Hands-on experience with Kubernetes and containerization technologies is essential.


You will work with container platforms to ensure efficient deployment and management of applications across cloud environments.


CI/CD Pipeline Development


You will have demonstrated experience creating and maintaining CI/CD pipelines, ensuring automated and reliable deployment processes.


Data Platform Expertise


Familiarity with modern cloud data engineering platforms such as Databricks and Snowflake is required, including query federations and fundamental data engineering concepts.


You will understand how to work within these environments to support data workflows.


Programming & Development Skills


Hands-on experience with Python is essential.
