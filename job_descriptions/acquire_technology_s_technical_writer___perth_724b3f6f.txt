Shape the future of technical documentation with a global mining technology leader.

Are you an experienced Technical Writer who enjoys making complex software easy to understand? Are you curious about how AI is changing the way technical content is created, maintained and delivered?

This is an opportunity to join Acquire, a global software company supporting some of the world's leading mining and exploration organisations, and play an important role in helping customers get more value from their technology.

Based in our Perth office, you'll join our Learning team and report to the Learning Lead. Working closely with Product Owners, Software Engineering, Product Specialists, Support and Product Marketing, you'll take ownership of developing and maintaining high-quality technical documentation across Acquire's software products.

From Online Help and in-product guidance through to implementation, administration and integration guides, you'll translate complex technical information into clear, accurate and accessible content that helps customers and internal teams understand and use our solutions effectively.

This is more than a traditional Technical Writer role. As an AI-enabled position, you'll have the opportunity to explore and develop AI-assisted and agentic documentation workflows, using emerging technologies to improve how content is researched, created, maintained and delivered.

You'll be the skilled human in the loop, bringing together technical knowledge, editorial judgement and AI capabilities to ensure every published piece of documentation is accurate, relevant and genuinely useful.

This role is ideal for someone who combines strong technical writing expertise with curiosity about technology, a passion for continuous improvement and an appetite to help shape the future of technical communication.

You'll have the opportunity to deepen your understanding of mining technology, work alongside talented global teams and develop your expertise in an evolving area of software documentation.

What you'll do

As a Technical Writer, you will:

Develop, maintain and improve technical documentation across Acquire's software products, including Online Help, technical guides, release notes and in-product user assistance.
Work closely with Product Owners, Software Engineering and Product Specialists to understand product functionality, identify documentation requirements and keep content aligned with each release.
Plan, prioritise and deliver documentation projects in collaboration with stakeholders across the business.
Build and maintain a consistent documentation framework, including templates, style guides, glossaries and reusable content structures.
Design, develop and maintain AI-assisted and agentic documentation workflows that improve the efficiency, consistency and quality of content production.
Curate and prepare source material, design effective prompts and workflows, and ensure AI tools have the right context to generate accurate and relevant documentation.
Apply strong editorial judgement to review and validate AI-generated content, test procedures within the product and take accountability for the accuracy of all published information.
Optimise Online Help and technical content for search and AI-assisted help, ensuring customers and AI tools can easily find reliable answers.
Conduct regular content audits and peer reviews to maintain documentation quality, consistency and technical accuracy.
Use analytics, customer feedback and Support team insights to identify content gaps and opportunities for improvement.
Explore and champion new tools, technologies and approaches that enhance technical documentation and content development practices.
Collaborate with the Learning team to support the development of complementary learning resources, including videos, webinars and other digital content.
Share knowledge, provide peer review and support the development of documentation capabilities across the business.
Represent the Learning team professionally and contribute to a collaborative, continuous improvement culture.

What you'll bring

We're looking for an experienced Technical Writer who combines excellent writing skills with a genuine interest in software, emerging technologies and finding better ways to communicate complex information.

You'll bring:

At least 5 years' experience as a Technical Writer, preferably documenting software products.
Demonstrated experience with topic-based authoring and single-sourcing, ideally using MadCap Flare.
Hands-on experience using generative AI tools in technical writing or documentation, including the ability to craft effective prompts and critically evaluate AI-generated content.
Excellent technical writing and editing skills, with the ability to explain complex concepts clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.
Strong research skills and the confidence to engage with technical specialists, ask the right questions and verify information through product testing and source documentation.
Experience working with Agile development methodologies and software release cycles.
An understanding of information architecture and how to structure content for effective search, knowledge retrieval and AI-assisted support.
A qualification in Technical Communication, Professional Writing, Information Technology or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.

It would also be advantageous to have:

Experience developing or maintaining AI-assisted documentation pipelines, automated content workflows or agentic AI solutions.
Working knowledge of HTML and CSS, with familiarity with docs-as-code practices such as Markdown and Git.
Experience creating digital learning content, including instructional videos, webinars and e-learning resources.
Experience with tools such as Articulate 360, Snagit, Camtasia and SharePoint.
Exposure to geoscience, natural resources, mining software or scientific data management.

Above all, you'll be curious, methodical and quality-focused. You'll be comfortable working with complex and sometimes changing information, confident collaborating with technical specialists and willing to challenge AI-generated content when accuracy or clarity is at stake.

Why join Acquire?

At Acquire, you'll be joining a global team where your work directly contributes to the customer experience and the continued development of technology used across the resources sector.

You'll have the opportunity to work with modern documentation technologies, experiment with AI-enabled workflows and help shape how technical knowledge is created and shared across a global software business.

We offer:

Flexible hybrid working – 3 days in the office and 2 days remotely.
Employee Assistance Program (EAP) to support your wellbeing.
Dedicated training levy and training leave to support your ongoing professional development.
Specialised leave for community engagement and volunteer work.
Commuter benefits designed to encourage sustainable and green transport options.
Social Club events and functions throughout the year.
Opportunities to work with emerging AI technologies and develop new skills in AI-enabled content development.
Global career opportunities as we continue to grow across APAC, EMEA and NAM.

About Acquire

Acquire provides strategic software solutions that help organisations make better business decisions by ensuring the right information is available to the right people.

We work with the world's leading organisations to tackle increasingly complex data challenges impacting Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG), as they relate to the earth's resources, the natural environment and their communities.

Founded in 1996, Acquire originated in Perth, Western Australia, and since 2018 has been part of Constellation Software, Inc., through its operating group, Vela Software.

Today, Acquire has six offices around the globe, with customer support centres operating across each major time zone.
