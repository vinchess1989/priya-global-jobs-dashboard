Super Retail Group is the proud owner of four iconic brands: Supercheap Auto, rebel, BCF and Macpac, and is one of Australia and New Zealand's largest retailers. The talent of our teams and their passion for providing an experience which inspires and enhances our customers’ leisure time drives our culture of innovation and opportunity.

About The Role

We are seeking a Squad Delivery Lead & Business Analyst to play a key role in delivering customer outcomes across our Customer X Assist and Fulfil squads. These journey-aligned capability squads are responsible for creating reusable customer journeys and enabling capabilities that support our brands and channels.

Combining delivery leadership with business analysis expertise, you'll drive predictable, incremental delivery by managing delivery flow, commitments, dependencies, risks and release coordination across both squads. You'll also partner closely with stakeholders to translate validated customer and business problems into clear requirements, user stories and prioritised backlog outcomes, ensuring solutions deliver meaningful value for customers and the business.

Working in a collaborative, agile environment, you'll help squads stay focused, aligned and positioned for success.

This role offers the flexibility to be based in Brisbane (Strathpine) or Sydney (Rhodes), with a hybrid work model requiring at least three days per week onsite.

About You

To be successful in this role, you'll bring experience across both business analysis and squad delivery, supporting complex customer, service, digital or platform initiatives. You have a proven ability to lead discovery, define clear outcomes and measures of success, and build strong relationships with stakeholders to align priorities, navigate competing demands and drive successful delivery outcomes.

In Addition, You Bring


Experience analysing customer journeys and service processes to define clear business requirements and customer outcomes.
Experience translating customer and business needs into process models, requirements, user stories and acceptance criteria.
Experience managing requirements through prioritisation, validation, business acceptance and operational transition.
Strong workshop facilitation, stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to resolve ambiguity, negotiate trade-offs and build alignment across business, product, technology and vendor teams.
Experience supporting testing, release readiness, cutover, operational transition and hypercare activities.
