Meet the Moment with Alteryx

We're living through a once-in-a-generation shift in how work gets done. Data, automation, and AI are quickly becoming the center of every business decision - and Alteryx is leading the transformation.

You'll be working on the challenges that sit at the heart of modern business. No matter your role, the work you do will help organizations move faster, see more clearly, and tackle questions that used to feel impossible.

If you're ready to meet the moment with innovation, curiosity, and excellence, there's a place for you here.

Our Global Content team is looking for a talented technical writer who wants to create valuable content experiences for our audience. Our team writes content for Alteryx Documentation. Our site is available in 8 languages and has over 100,000 monthly users.

You'll fit right in if you are passionate about online technical communication and helping customers to find the answers they need. You will thrive if you believe that all customers should have a helpful, positive, and accessible experience, regardless of the language they choose and the technologies they use to navigate and consume our content.

About The Role

We're hiring a Senior Technical Writer (in India to own documentation end to end for one or more Alteryx product areas. Our team writes the content for help.alteryx.com and affiliated sites, and that same content powers our GenAI-based in-product help.

You'll fit right in if you care about helping customers find answers and believe every customer deserves a clear, accessible experience, whatever language or assistive technology they use. We look for friendly people who work well with others, treat feedback as a gift, and enjoy coaching.

What You Will Own And Improve

At the senior level you own the outcome for your product area, not just a list of articles. You set your own approach, handle ambiguity without being told how, and escalate trade-offs. You're judged on whether customers can do their jobs with our content.


Documentation for your product area. Plan the content for a feature or product area from start to finish: audience, structure, and the customer journey, aligned with the product roadmap. This covers release notes, help articles, in-product help copy, tutorials, and examples.
Accuracy. Own the correctness of your content by validating it against product behavior and working with subject matter experts. Write content that is inclusive, accessible, and ready for localization.
Authoring and publishing. Create and manage structured content in Paligo, and own generating, testing, and publishing the outputs.
Reusable content and automation. Build reusable components and templates, automate repetitive checks, and design AI-assisted workflows. Nothing is published without human and subject matter expert review.
Success measures. Define how you'll know your content worked, such as task success, search success, deflection, and comprehension. Use those measures to decide what to fix next.
Team standards. Review your teammates' work, set an example in reviews, and help shape our styles and standards.
Our live site (optional). If you have the skills and want the challenge, you can help improve help.alteryx.com with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. This isn't required.


What You Already Know


You have 4 to 6 years of experience in technical writing for software products. Extra points for cloud and on-prem experience. This can include graduate programs.
You write outstanding English. All of our content is in American English.
You have a bachelor's degree, or equivalent experience, in a field relevant to the role.
You've created and managed structured content, such as DITA. Experience with Paligo isn't required.
You can turn complex technical details into clear, precise, engaging documentation, and you can simplify difficult concepts.
You've led documentation for large or ambiguous projects, and you can sequence work, name trade-offs, and support a launch.
You work well with engineering, product, design, localization, support, and content strategy. You tailor your message to the audience and use customer, support, and product evidence to get buy-in.
You're a team player who thrives in multicultural, diverse environments.


What You Will Learn, And Your Team

If you don't know these already, you'll pick them up here:


Writing in plain language, with a voice and style that work for translation and accessibility.
Creating structured content in Paligo, generating HTML5 outputs, and publishing them in AWS.
Our software development lifecycle and Agile/Scrum practices, for both on-prem releases and continuous cloud delivery.
Jira and Confluence, plus GitLab for in-product copy.
How websites are built and optimized for localization and accessibility. Intermediate skills in AWS (S3, CloudFront), HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Google Analytics, Search Console, and SEO are a bonus.
Our products.


You'll join our Technical Writing team, which reports to the senior manager of the team. We're all remote and work flexible schedules, and most work happens asynchronously in Slack, Confluence, and email. You may occasionally need to join an early morning or evening call to overlap with colleagues in the US and Europe.
