About The Role

We are looking for a hands-on IT Business Analyst (100%) to join our Group IT Life and Health Underwriting and Claims application team. Based in Dublin or Toronto, this is primarily a delivery and operational role responsible for ensuring that PartnerRe's Medical Underwriting Manual and Automated Underwriting Applications remain stable, well maintained, and effectively support internal users and external clients. The successful candidate will work independently across changing priorities, exercise sound judgement, and collaborate with business stakeholders, medical underwriting specialists, clients, suppliers, and multidisciplinary IT teams in a global and multicultural environment.

Your Responsibilities Will Include


Take end-to-end responsibility for the day-to-day delivery, operation, support, and continuous improvement of our client facing medical underwriting solutions, with a strong focus on service stability, quality, and timely resolution of user and client issues.
Manage application lifecycle, production support, incidents, service requests, defects, user access management and change requests, working hands-on to progress issues through to resolution.
Assess competing operational and delivery priorities, identify risks and dependencies, and make sound recommendations and decisions with appropriate escalation when required.
Work with the business product owner, global medical underwriting subject-matter experts, and suppliers to manage the Medical Underwriting Manual content review cycle, version control, testing, and release of accurate and up-to-date medical underwriting guidance.
Gather and analyse business and operational requirements, challenge unclear assumptions, translate needs into actionable user stories or specifications, and support solution design, validation, implementation, and quality assurance.
Coordinate effectively across business, architecture, development, infrastructure, security, and supplier teams, while remaining accountable for progress, follow-through, and clear stakeholder communication.
Contribute flexibly to both strategic improvements and routine operational activities, completing recurring and administrative tasks consistently and to a high standard.


About You


Relevant qualifications and experience: Bachelor's or Master's degree in IT, Business Administration, Insurance, or a related field, or equivalent professional experience, plus at least 5 years as an IT Business Analyst, Application Analyst, or in a similar delivery and operational role. Life and Health insurance, reinsurance, or medical underwriting application experience is preferred.
Hands-on application support and ownership: Proven experience supporting business-critical applications across incidents, service requests, defects, releases, access management, data or content updates, and user support, with accountability for both complex assignments and routine tasks.
Independent judgement and problem-solving: Strong critical-thinking and prioritisation skills, with the ability to work independently, resolve problems, deliver consistent quality, and escalate appropriately.
Collaboration and communication: A strong team player who builds trust across disciplines, locations, cultures, seniority levels, and external suppliers, with excellent written and spoken English. Additional languages are welcome but not required.
Business analysis and quality assurance: Proven skills in requirements elicitation and analysis, process modelling, user stories, acceptance criteria, solution validation, and testing. Responsible use of AI-assisted requirements analysis and experience in AI-based software development project is an advantage.
Delivery methods and technical knowledge: Good understanding of application lifecycle management, IT service management, software development frameworks, Agile or Scrum, and tools such as Azure DevOps or Asana. Working knowledge of HTML, CSS, version control, workflow tools, or rules-based platforms is an advantage.
