Content Platform & Services Manager

About The Role

ZS seeks a Content Platform & Services Manager to join our team in Bengaluru, India. This role will own end-to-end content operations for marketing and promotional materials and Medical-Legal-Regulatory (MLR) review workflows. The position sits at the intersection of content production and compliance process management, ensuring that content moves efficiently and compliantly from creation through approval to publication and archival.

The ideal candidate has hands-on experience managing content lifecycle platforms, such as Veeva Vault PromoMats or an equivalent platform, in a regulated industry and is comfortable partnering with Marketing, Medical, Legal, and Regulatory stakeholders to keep review cycles on track. The role will also lead the evaluation, build, and rollout of AI-based accelerators, such as automated tagging, claims and reference checking, and pre-MLR quality screening, that reduce manual effort and accelerate review cycles.

We are looking for someone with 10+ years of experience in content operations, marketing operations, or promotional review management, ideally within pharmaceutical, life sciences, or healthcare industries. A bachelor’s degree in life sciences, pharmacy, marketing, communications, or a related field is required; an MBA is preferred.

What You'll Do


Own content operations. Manage intake, tagging and metadata, routing, review coordination, approval, publishing, and archival of promotional and non-promotional materials.
Manage MLR reviews. Schedule review cycles, track reviewer comments and claims, ensure timely turnaround, and maintain complete audit trails.
Administer the platform. Optimize Veeva Vault PromoMats or similar DAM/CLM systems, including user access, workflows, and metadata taxonomy.
Enable review-ready content. Partner with Brand and Marketing teams to plan content pipelines and ensure correct references, claims substantiation, and formatting.
Coordinate cross-functionally. Work with Medical, Legal, Regulatory, and Compliance reviewers to resolve bottlenecks and maintain SLA adherence.
Maintain compliance. Ensure content meets internal standards, brand guidelines, and applicable pharmaceutical marketing and regulatory requirements prior to release.
Govern the content lifecycle. Maintain accurate records of approved content, expiry and renewal dates, and withdrawal of outdated materials.
Improve operations. Identify inefficiencies and drive continuous improvement across workflows, templates, and SOPs.
Report performance. Create leadership dashboards covering review cycle time, throughput, bottlenecks, and compliance metrics.
Build capability. Train internal stakeholders and agency partners on platform usage and MLR submission best practices.
Shape AI use cases. Identify opportunities in metadata tagging, claims and reference validation, pre-review compliance checks, content reuse, and modular content, and build business cases.
Deliver AI accelerators. Lead requirements, pilots, testing, output validation against MLR standards, and impact measurement with Technology, Data, and vendor partners.
Establish AI governance. Set validation, human-in-the-loop, auditability, and compliance guardrails for AI-assisted content workflows.
Integrate and scale AI. Evaluate Veeva AI features, GenAI tools, and workflow automation, and lead stakeholder and agency adoption.


What You'll Bring


Content operations expertise. 10+ years of experience in content operations, marketing operations, or promotional review management in pharmaceutical, life sciences, healthcare, or another regulated industry.
Platform experience. Hands-on experience with content lifecycle or DAM platforms such as Veeva Vault PromoMats, Aprimo, or similar systems.
MLR knowledge. Working knowledge of MLR review processes and regulatory and compliance requirements for promotional materials.
AI and automation delivery. Demonstrated experience building, piloting, or testing AI/GenAI accelerators or automation solutions, including defining success metrics and validating outputs.
Technical partnership. Ability to convert operational pain points into AI and automation requirements and work effectively with technical teams through delivery.
Modular content. Experience with content reuse and modular content strategies enabled by AI.
Stakeholder leadership. Ability to coordinate effectively across Marketing, Medical, Legal, Regulatory, Compliance, Technology, Data, and agency partners.
Operational discipline. Excellent organization and attention to detail, with the ability to manage concurrent review cycles and deadlines.
Communication. Strong written and verbal communication skills.
Education. Bachelor’s degree in life sciences, pharmacy, marketing, communications, or a related field; MBA preferred.


Preferred Experience


Industry background. Prior experience at a pharmaceutical company or healthcare-focused marketing or content services organization.
Content governance. Familiarity with taxonomy, metadata standards, and digital asset management best practices.
Process improvement. Experience driving automation or continuous improvement within content operations.
Reporting tools. Exposure to Power BI or Tableau for operational reporting and dashboards.
GenAI tools. Hands-on exposure to GenAI/LLM tools, prompt design, or low-code automation applied to content or compliance workflows.
Responsible AI. Understanding of validation, auditability, data privacy, and responsible AI in regulated industries.


What Success Looks Like


Faster, more predictable review cycles with improved throughput and sustained SLA adherence.
Accurate, compliant, and audit-ready content across the complete lifecycle.
Measurable reduction in manual effort through validated AI accelerators and workflow automation.
Strong adoption of content platforms, MLR best practices, and AI-enabled ways of working across internal and agency teams.
Clear operational visibility through reliable metrics on cycle time, bottlenecks, throughput, quality, and compliance.
