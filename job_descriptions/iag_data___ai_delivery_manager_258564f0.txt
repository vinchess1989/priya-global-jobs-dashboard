At IAG, we’re ready for you. We're the largest general insurance group in Australia and New Zealand. Our family of brands turns the IAG purpose of making your world a safer place into action by helping everyday Aussies and New Zealanders, supporting their ambitions and making insurance accessible.

Build the future of Data & AI delivery at IAG

Join us at a pivotal moment as we reshape how Data & AI products are delivered across the business. As Data & AI Delivery Manager, you'll help establish and lead a new delivery capability across data, AI, insights and reporting, turning ambitious priorities into practical business outcomes.

This is an opportunity to influence how work gets done, build confidence in delivery and shape how squads deliver. Working with product, business and technology leaders, you’ll remove barriers, manage dependencies and lead data and AI delivery across the full lifecycle - from experimentation and release to ongoing monitoring, evaluation and improvement in operation. You’ll ensure teams consistently apply and embed our new delivery practices, combining agile ways of working with data science principles - helping teams turn promising ideas into reliable solutions that continue to create value long after launch.

This is a permanent full-time role based in Tāmaki Makaurau (Auckland), Ōtautahi (Christchurch) or Te Whanganui-a-Tara (Wellington).

Ngā Mahi Hei Whakatutuki Māu | What You'll Do


Lead how Data & AI products are built, delivered, supported and continuously improved across their entire lifecycle.
Own end to end delivery outcomes for business aligned Data & AI products, ensuring they create lasting value for customers and stakeholders.
Guide delivery squads and cross functional teams to turn priorities into practical, scalable and well governed solutions.
Deliver trusted business insights, reporting and analytics capabilities that support better decision making across IAG.
Drive migration and decommissioning initiatives, helping modernise our data landscape while reducing complexity and technical debt.
Identify opportunities to streamline processes, improve delivery performance and strengthen the ongoing operation of Data & AI products.
Partner closely with Product Managers, engineers and business leaders to balance strategic priorities with operational excellence.


Ōu Pūkenga me ōu Wheako | What You'll Bring


Experience leading the end to end delivery and operation of data, analytics, AI or technology products within a complex environment.
A strong understanding of product lifecycle management, from initial delivery through to support, optimisation and retirement.
Experience leading cross functional teams and navigating competing priorities across business and technology stakeholders.
The ability to translate business needs into practical delivery outcomes that create measurable value.
Experience delivering business insights, reporting, analytics or data driven solutions.
A continuous improvement mindset, with a track record of simplifying processes and improving ways of working.
Curiosity about emerging Data & AI capabilities and how they can be applied to solve real business problems.
