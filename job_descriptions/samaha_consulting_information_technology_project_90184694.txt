Role Summary

The Project Manager is responsible for leading the end-to-end delivery of complex

software engineering initiatives, ensuring alignment with business objectives, technical

strategy, delivery standards, and commercial commitments. The role provides leadership

across cross-functional engineering teams, managing scope, schedules, budgets, resources,

dependencies, risks, quality, and stakeholder expectations. Working closely with product,

architecture, engineering, quality assurance, security, operations, vendors, and business

stakeholders, the Project Manager enables predictable delivery, effective

governance, and continuous improvement throughout the software development lifecycle.




Key Responsibilities

• Lead the end-to-end planning and delivery of complex software engineering

projects, from initiation and discovery through development, testing, release,

transition, and closure.

• Define project scope, objectives, delivery approach, milestones, success measures,

governance structures, and integrated delivery plans in partnership with business

and technical stakeholders.

• Manage project schedules, budgets, resource plans, procurement requirements,

vendor commitments, and financial performance, including forecasting, invoicing,

and delivery acceptance.

• Coordinate cross-functional teams across product management, software

engineering, architecture, quality assurance, security, infrastructure, DevOps, data,

operations, and business functions.

• Apply Agile, Scrum, Kanban, hybrid, or predictive delivery practices as appropriate,

ensuring disciplined backlog management, sprint planning, release planning,

estimation, and delivery tracking.

• Identify, assess, and actively manage project risks, issues, assumptions, constraints,

dependencies, and critical decisions, escalating promptly where executive

intervention is required.

• Establish and maintain project governance, including steering forums, status

reporting, decision logs, action tracking, change control, stage gates, and audit

ready documentation.

• Partner with engineering leads to manage technical dependencies, environments,

integration points, non-functional requirements, release readiness, and operational

handover.

• Track delivery performance using meaningful metrics such as milestone

achievement, sprint predictability, throughput, quality, defect trends, budget

variance, resource utilisation, and business outcomes.

• Maintain strong engagement with senior stakeholders by providing concise,

accurate, and transparent reporting on progress, risks, financials, dependencies, and

decisions required.




Job Specification

• Manage scope and change requests through structured impact assessment,

prioritisation, approval, and communication processes.

• Ensure delivery complies with organisational standards for software engineering,

cybersecurity, privacy, architecture, quality, risk, procurement, and regulatory

governance.

• Lead vendor and partner delivery, including statement-of-work alignment,

performance management, resource onboarding and roll-off, acceptance criteria,

and contractual obligations.

• Facilitate resolution of delivery impediments and cross-team conflicts while fostering

accountability, collaboration, and a strong focus on outcomes.

• Mentor project managers and delivery team members, promote project management

best practices, and drive continuous improvement across the delivery function.




Qualifications

• Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering,

Business, Project Management, or a related field.

• Recognised project or programme management certification such as PMP, PRINCE2,

or an equivalent qualification is preferred.

• Agile delivery certification such as Scrum Master, SAFe, or an equivalent

qualification is advantageous.




Experience

• 5–7 years of relevant project delivery experience, including at least 3 years leading

complex software engineering or digital transformation initiatives.

• Demonstrated experience delivering software products or platforms across the full

software development lifecycle.

• Strong working knowledge of Agile and hybrid delivery methods, with experience

coordinating multiple squads or distributed teams.

• Proven capability in project planning, estimation, budgeting, resource management,

dependency management, risk control, and executive reporting.

• Experience with engineering delivery tools such as Azure DevOps, Jira, Confluence,

or equivalent platforms.

• Experience managing third-party suppliers, statements of work, commercial

commitments, and acceptance criteria.

• Sound understanding of modern software engineering practices, including MADAPI,

CI/CD, cloud platforms, APIs, microservices, test automation, cybersecurity, and

release management.

• Excellent stakeholder management, negotiation, facilitation, written communication,

and leadership skills, with the ability to operate effectively at executive level.

• Demonstrated ability to lead through ambiguity, resolve complex delivery challenges,

and maintain accountability in fast-paced environments.
