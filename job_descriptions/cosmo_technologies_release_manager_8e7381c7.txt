The role

You'll own how Cosmo ships. Every release has to land across firmware, software, and cloud services, and you'll be the person who makes that happen on time and correctly. You'll run the release calendar, readiness reviews and go/no-go calls, manage staged firmware rollouts to devices in the field, and keep our release tooling working. You'll partner closely with our Senior TPM, who owns program health and the overall operating rhythm.

What you'll do


Own the release calendar and versioning for firmware OTA updates, mobile app releases and backend deployments
Run release readiness reviews and go/no-go calls, and coordinate release-night execution with engineering, QA, support and our offshore teams
Manage staged FOTA rollouts: define cohorts and stages, monitor device health, and pause or roll back when needed
Own code freezes, including holiday freezes, and the exception process
Own pre-release builds and distribution for UAT, internal testing, influencers and media
Own the release configuration in Jira: fix versions, release workflows and automated release notes
Plan release and incident coverage for holidays and high-risk launches, with clear escalation paths
Write and maintain release runbooks so anyone on the team can follow the process
Track release quality after every release and share what you learn with the Sr TPM and engineering leads


What success looks like


In 30 days, you've run a release alongside the interim owner and documented every manual step
By 90 days, you own the release calendar and FOTA rollouts, and release runbooks are written for firmware, app and backend
By six months, releases ship on a predictable cadence, release notes are fully automated and rollbacks are rare and fast


Requirements

What you bring


4+ years in release management, release coordination or a closely related role
Hands-on experience releasing software for connected devices, consumer electronics, wearables or IoT, including firmware updates in the field
Working knowledge of branching and release strategies, CI/CD pipelines and QA cycles. You partner with engineers on pipelines rather than building them
Experience with app store review cycles and staged rollouts
Strong Jira skills, including versions, release workflows and automation rules
Calm, clear communication when a release is at risk, and the confidence to call a no-go


Nice to have


Experience with wearables and vertical integration
Experience coordinating releases with offshore teams across time zones
