About The Role

As a Senior MES Recipe Author – Syncade, you will make an impact by implementing recipe changes and supporting the systems that enable reliable pharmaceutical manufacturing operations.

You will be a valued member of a collaborative Manufacturing Execution System (MES) team supporting a large multinational biotechnology manufacturing facility in Cork. You will work across Electronic Batch Records, Weigh and Dispense, and Equipment Management modules within the Emerson Syncade MES solution.

In This Role, You Will


Manage the ServiceNow incident queue, investigating and resolving MES issues in a timely manner.
Initiate and implement change-control activities in accordance with site quality standards, procedures and Good Manufacturing Practice requirements.
Configure, test and deploy MES recipes and system changes, producing high-quality documentation that is clear and maintainable.
Create computer system validation documentation and perform system and integration testing in line with site procedures and project schedules.
Collaborate with business users and cross-functional teams to understand requirements, recommend improvements and deliver effective MES solutions.


Work model

We believe hybrid work is the way forward as we strive to provide flexibility wherever possible. Based on this role’s business requirements, this is a hybrid position requiring three days a week at our client’s site in Cork, Ireland. Regardless of your working arrangement, we are here to support a healthy work-life balance through our various wellbeing programmes.

The working arrangements for this role are accurate as of the date of posting. This may change based on the project you’re engaged in, as well as business and client requirements. Rest assured, we will always be clear about role expectations.

What You Need To Have To Be Considered


Three to five years of experience supporting IT applications, including troubleshooting, incident management, change control, design documentation, test-case development and system or integration testing.
Experience in MES recipe authoring, ideally using Emerson Syncade.
Experience implementing or supporting application changes within a Good Manufacturing Practice-regulated environment.
Knowledge of programming, Visual Basic, configuration management and the deployment of developed code.
Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with business users and cross-functional teams.


These will help you stand out


Experience working with Electronic Batch Records, Weigh and Dispense, or Equipment Management modules.
Knowledge of Microsoft SQL Server.
Experience producing computer system validation documentation within a pharmaceutical or biotechnology manufacturing environment.
A strong understanding of quality-control requirements within pharmaceutical production.
The flexibility to participate in out-of-hours support as the role evolves.
