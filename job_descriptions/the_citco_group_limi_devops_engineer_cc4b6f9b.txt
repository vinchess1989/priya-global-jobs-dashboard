Job Description

DevOps Engineer

About Citco

Since the 1940s Citco has provided specialist financial services to alternative investment funds, investors, multinationals and private clients worldwide. With close to 12,000 employees in 45 countries we pioneer innovative solutions that meet our clients’ evolving needs, and deliver exceptional service.

Our continuous investment in learning means our people are among the best in the industry. And our corporate social responsibility programs provide meaningful and fulfilling work in the community.

A career at Citco isn’t just a job – it’s an opportunity to excel in an environment that genuinely supports your personal and professional development.

About The Role

The DevOps Engineer will design, deploy, and maintain the Jenkins CI/CD platform and supporting infrastructure tools that enable Citco's multi-cloud deployments across AWS and on-premise environments. This role combines hands-on technical execution with mentoring junior engineers, driving Infrastructure as Code practices (Terraform, CloudFormation), and collaborating closely with Jenkins users and platform stakeholders to ensure reliable, scalable, and secure deployments.

The DevOps Engineer will work with the Platform Services teams, Development Teams, and IT Operations to translate business requirements into robust platform capabilities.

Job Duties In Brief

Design, deploy, and maintain the Jenkins platform; manage Jenkins controllers, agents, and scaling across AWS and on-premises environments


Support Jenkins users; troubleshoot pipeline failures, agent connectivity issues, and plugin/configuration problems
Develop and maintain Infrastructure as Code using Terraform and CloudFormation; drive adoption of IaC best practices across teams
Manage and optimize AWS CodePipeline integrations; ensure seamless CI/CD workflows across AWS services
Manage platform tools and services (SonarQube, JFrog, Octopus Deploy, Kong, Kiro), including configuration, upgrades, troubleshooting, and documentation
Oversee Code Assistant (Kiro) management (Licensing, usage metrics, user support, and optimization)
Design and implement centralized logging, monitoring, and tracing solutions (Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, CloudWatch, Jaeger, OpenTelemetry) across all platforms
Migrate and manage platform tools to Kubernetes environments; design Helm charts and manage Kubernetes deployments
Automate operational procedures and infrastructure improvements using scripting and IaC; identify and implement automation opportunities
Participate in on-call rotations; respond to and resolve critical platform incidents with minimal customer impact
Collaborate with development teams to capture requirements and translate them into platform improvements
Document architecture, runbooks, and operational procedures; maintain up-to-date knowledge base
Participate in capacity planning, cost optimization, and vendor management discussions


About You

You are a hands-on DevOps engineer with strong AWS expertise and solid experience building and maintaining CI/CD platforms. You are comfortable diving deep into Jenkins troubleshooting, writing Infrastructure as Code, and optimizing cloud deployments. You have growing Kubernetes experience and understand how to migrate workloads to containerized environments. You are also beginning to think beyond just "making things work" and you are interested in how systems scale, how teams operate, and how to mentor others. You communicate clearly with both technical and non-technical stakeholders and take ownership of the platforms you support.

Experience And Technical Skills


4–6 years of professional hands-on experience with CI/CD platforms, cloud infrastructure, or DevOps engineering
Strong hands-on experience with deploying, configuring, troubleshooting, and scaling Jenkins controllers and agents
Strong AWS background, particularly EC2, VPC, IAM, auto-scaling, CloudWatch; ability to architect and optimize cloud infrastructure
Proven hands-on experience with Terraform and CloudFormation; ability to write, review, and refactor Infrastructure as Code
Solid experience with AWS CodePipeline and CI/CD pipeline design; understanding of pipeline optimization and best practices
Hands-on experience with deploying applications, managing Helm charts, understanding networking and storage to Kubernetes; experience migrating workloads to Kubernetes
Hands-on experience with SonarQube, JFrog, and/or Octopus Deploy; ability to configure, troubleshoot, and support these tools
Familiarity with Kong infrastructure management and API gateway concepts
Experience managing or supporting Code Assistant tools (Kiro or similar); understanding of licensing and usage metrics
Strong experience with monitoring and observability stacks, such as Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, CloudWatch; ability to design dashboards and alerts
Solid understanding of Linux/Unix administration and container concepts
Scripting experience (Bash, Python, or similar) to automate operational procedures and infrastructure improvements
Experience participating in on-call rotations supporting production systems
Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting skills; ability to work through ambiguous issues methodically
Good communication skills; ability to document decisions and explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders
Willingness to mentor junior engineers and contribute to team knowledge-sharing


Education


Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or equivalent hands-on experience
Relevant certifications (AWS Solutions Architect Associate, AWS DevOps Engineer, CKA, Terraform Associate) are a plus but not required
