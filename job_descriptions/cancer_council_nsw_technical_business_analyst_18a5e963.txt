Cancer Council NSW (CCNSW) is a community funded and focused not for profit. We are the largest cancer charity in NSW and our point of difference is that we work across every area of every cancer, from research right through to support. 

At CCNSW we understand that nothing feels better than doing work you really care about. As Australia’s leading organisation supporting all people impacted by all cancers, we understand how good it feels to make a difference. We know that almost 1 in 2 Australians are affected by cancer during their lifetime, by joining the passionate team at CCNSW you’ll know that each day the work you’re doing will positively impact people in your life and Australia.  

About The Team/About The Role

The Technical Business Analyst partners with business stakeholders to elicit, analyse and document business requirements, improve business processes, and translate business needs into well-defined, prioritised and delivery-ready outcomes.

The role combines business analysis, stakeholder engagement and solution analysis capabilities with hands-on configuration expertise across Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement (D365 CE), Microsoft Power Platform and Copilot Studio.

Working closely with stakeholders, delivery teams, vendors and technology partners, the role facilitates workshops, evaluates solution options and supports successful delivery of strategic initiatives and business-as-usual enhancements. The role also contributes to AI innovation discovery and continuous improvement and ensures solutions align with organisational governance, security and platform standards.

Key responsibilities of the role: 


Facilitate business discovery workshops, stakeholder interviews, process mapping and solution review sessions to gather and validate requirements.
Elicit, analyse and document functional and non-functional requirements, translating business needs into user stories, acceptance criteria and delivery-ready artefacts.
Map and improve business processes, ensuring traceability from requirements through design, development, testing and implementation.
Analyse business challenges, risks and dependencies, recommending fit-for-purpose solutions and technology improvements.
Collaborate with technical teams, vendors and solution architects to support D365 CE, Power Platform, Copilot Studio and automation initiatives.
Act as the key liaison between business and technical stakeholders, managing priorities, backlog items, scope, risks and release planning activities.
Support testing, training, user adoption and knowledge transfer to ensure solutions meet business requirements and deliver value.
Maintain functional and technical documentation while promoting best practices, governance, security standards and continuous improvement.


About You,

Essential: 


Tertiary qualification in Information Technology, Business Analysis or a related discipline, or equivalent professional experience.
Experience working with Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement (D365 CE) and Microsoft Power Platform solutions.
Proven ability to facilitate workshops, stakeholder interviews, requirements gathering and process-mapping activities.
Strong skills in documenting business requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, process models and functional specifications.
Ability to analyse business needs and translate them into clear, practical and delivery-ready solutions.
Experience driving business process improvement, automation, change initiatives and technology implementations.
Strong understanding of CRM governance, security, data management and application lifecycle management practices.
Excellent analytical, communication and stakeholder engagement skills, with experience working in Agile delivery environments.


Desirable: 


Experience configuring and supporting Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement (D365 CE), including workflows, dashboards, forms and business rules.
Strong knowledge of the Microsoft Power Platform, including Power Apps, Power Automate, Power BI and AI-driven solutions such as Copilot Studio and Microsoft Copilot.
Experience working with vendors and implementation partners, with relevant Microsoft certifications (PL-200, PL-400, Dynamics 365 or similar) highly regarded.
