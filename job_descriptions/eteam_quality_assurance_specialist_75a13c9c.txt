About the Role




A fantastic opportunity has arisen for a QA Validation Records Specialist within the Quality Assurance team. This role will support a strategic project focused on the reconciliation, digitisation, and management of archived validation documentation, including implementation of a new archival system and electronic True Copy process.




Responsibilities




Bring energy, knowledge, innovation to carry out the following
Review, reconcile, organise, and digitise archived validation documentation.
Support implementation of a new document archival system for retrospective and prospective records.
Facilitate conversion of paper records to compliant Electronic True Copy records.
Assess validation document completeness, criticality, and retention requirements.
Support archive reorganisation activities in partnership with CQV, Validation, and Document Control.
Ensure compliance with GMP, GDP, data integrity, and site document management procedures.
Support compliance reporting and quality system objectives related to documentation management.




Qualifications




BSc in Science, Engineering, Quality, or related discipline (or equivalent relevant experience).




Required Skills




Experience within a pharmaceutical or other regulated quality environment.
Knowledge of GMP, GDP, and data integrity requirements.
Understanding of validation lifecycle documentation and CQV processes.
Strong document management, organisational, and communication skills.
Able to multi-task in fast-paced, dynamic working environment.




Preferred Skills




Experience with document archival, digitisation, or electronic document management systems is desirable.
