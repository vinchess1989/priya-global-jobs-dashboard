Service Management Configuration Specialist (Post Level 8)

(Stellenbosch Campus)

Ref. IT/333/0826

The IT Shared Services, Governance and Risk Management is part of the IT Division at Stellenbosch University's (SU) Shared Services. The unit provides standardised, reliable, and scalable support functions that underpin core activities at SU. These services are designed to promote consistency, operational efficiency, and service quality across SU through centralised oversight and optimisation. IT governance is established by policies, standards, and decision-making structures that guide the responsible use of technology. This includes oversight of software licensing to ensure compliance, cost control, and value realisation across the software lifecycle. Licensing activities are managed to balance legal obligations, vendor agreements, and user requirements, while enabling strategic planning for future capability needs. Risk management is embedded across all functions of the IT Division, with a focus on identifying, assessing, and mitigating technology-related risks that could impact service continuity, information security, regulatory compliance, or institutional reputation. Through structured risk processes and alignment with recognised governance and risk frameworks, this portfolio informs decision-making at both operational and strategic levels. Software licensing, awareness and training build organisational capability through targeted training and awareness initiatives. These initiatives ensure that SU stakeholders are equipped to use institutional systems effectively, comply with governance requirements, and adapt to evolving digital and regulatory environments. Training is positioned not only as a support function, but as a strategic enabler of sustainable digital transformation.

The IT Shared Services, Governance and Risk Management, as part of the IT Division, is seeking a skilled Front-End Atlassian Administrator to manage and optimise our Jira Software, Jira Service Management, Confluence, Bamboo, and Bitbucket environments. This role focuses on configuring user-friendly interfaces, streamlining workflows, and enhancing the overall user experience across the Atlassian platform as an anchor for the Service Management Practice. The role will also contribute to IT Service Management (ITSM) research and development, technical risk management, and security initiatives to ensure our platforms remain robust, secure, and aligned with industry best practices.


Duties/Pligte
Configuring and maintaining Jira software projects, including boards, workflows, fields, screens, and issue types.
Setting up and managing Jira Service Management projects, including customer portals, request types, SLAs, queues, and automation rules.
Administering and optimising Jira Asset Management (Insight) for asset tracking, CMDB configuration, and integration with Jira Service Management workflows.
Designing and managing advanced reporting and visualisation using Dashboard Hub Pro for Jira and Dashboard Hub for Confluence to deliver executive-level dashboards and cross-project analytics.
Designing and building custom dashboards and filters using JQL to support team-specific reporting needs.
Administering and optimising Confluence spaces, templates, macros, and permissions for knowledge management.
Supporting user onboarding, group and permission management, and front-end interface customisation.
Troubleshooting user issues, providing day-to-day support, and communicating system changes or updates.
Collaborating with stakeholders to understand requirements and translating them into efficient Jira/Confluence configurations.
Maintaining and enhancing the front-end user experience for internal users and customers across all Atlassian platforms.
Job Requirements/Pos Vereistes
NQF Level 7 in an IT or IT related field with three years' experience in IT service management, OR NQF Level 6 in an IT or IT related field with five years' experience in IT service management.
ITIL certification.
Proven experience with administering Jira software, Jira Service Management, and Confluence (Cloud or Server/DC).
Hands-on experience with Jira Asset Management (Insight) for asset and configuration management.
Experience with Dashboard Hub Pro for Jira and Dashboard Hub for Confluence for creating advanced dashboards and reports.
Strong understanding of JQL (Jira Query Language) for building filters, dashboards, and reports.
Experience with configuring Jira workflows, custom fields, screens, permissions, and automation rules.
Familiarity with Jira Service Management SLAs, request types, queues, and portal customisation.
Experience in managing Confluence spaces, page templates, and user permissions.
Working knowledge of Atlassian Marketplace apps (e.g., ScriptRunner, Refined, Insight, eazyBI).
Handling user and group management for projects and spaces.
Recommendation/Aanbeveling
Basic knowledge of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for light UI enhancements or scripting (via ScriptRunner, etc.).
Strong analytical, communication, and troubleshooting skills.
Experience with integrating Confluence as a knowledge base for JSM.
Familiarity with Agile/Scrum or ITIL methodologies.
The ability to write end-user documentation and conduct training sessions.
Atlassian certifications.
Power BI experience.
