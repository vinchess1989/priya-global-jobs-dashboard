WATSON MARLOW FLUID TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS – INDIA ENGINEERING HUB

Be part of the founding team building a prestigious new India Engineering Hub for WMFTS

CONFIGURATION MANAGEMENT LEAD ROLE

Engineering | Pune | Ground-Up India Engineering Hub




CORE REQUIREMENTS

Graduate Engineer: Mechanical / Electronics / Mechatronics or related engineering discipline.
Experience: 8+ years of relevant professional experience.
Global Exposure: Experience working in a multinational/global engineering environment, with exposure to global stakeholder management.
Change Governance: Strong working experience in configuration management & engineering change control of products & components.
ECR / ECO Management: Proven experience in ECR / ECO management & change governance within manufacturing or engineering.
PLM & Version Control: Experience in working with PLM / product data & version control tools like Windchill / Teamcenter / Enovia / SAP PLM.
Change Process Expertise: In-depth knowledge of engineering change processes & best practices.
Technical Record Interpretation: Working experience in the ability to interpret engineering drawings, specifications, bill of materials, product structures, technical records & change documentation.

GOOD TO HAVE

CAD/drawing & specification control.
Cross-functional engineering/quality/manufacturing coordination.
Risk, compliance & auditability.
Process & tool continuous improvement.

WHAT YOU’LL BE DOING

Manage & progress engineering change requests across WMFTS sites.
Maintain product configuration models & specifications to ensure traceability of product versions & documentation.
Maintain & facilitate change-control reviews, reports & communications.
Coordinate with design engineers to ensure specifications, CAD models & drawings are correctly version controlled.
Coordinate engineering changes with engineering, quality, manufacturing & other business functions.
Track change activities, risks, issues & improvement opportunities.
Ensure compliance with internal policies, industry standards & regulatory requirements & contribute to continuous improvement.




WHO YOU’LL BE WORKING WITH

Multi-functional project teams comprising Engineers, Product Managers, Marketers, Supply Chain, Quality & Factory representatives.
Stakeholders across multiple WMFTS sites & disciplines.
Design, engineering, quality, manufacturing & product-related teams involved in change control.
