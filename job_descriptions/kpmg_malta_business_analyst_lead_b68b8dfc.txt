The Role

KPMG is currently seeking a Business Analyst Lead - Digital Transformation to join our Internal Digital Transformation (IDT) team. In this role, you will lead business analysis across digital transformation initiatives within KPMG Malta and the KPMG Islands Group (KIG), acting as the bridge between business stakeholders, technical teams and management. You will ensure that business needs are clearly understood, translated into practical technology-enabled solutions and governed effectively from discovery through deployment.




You will provide leadership across requirements management, solution assessment, project coordination, data and reporting, and business analysis standards. You will also mentor team members, strengthen stakeholder collaboration and help ensure that transformation initiatives deliver measurable value in line with the firm's strategic priorities.




Job Responsibilities

As a Business Analyst Lead, you will:




Lead business analysis for digital transformation initiatives, engaging sponsors, business owners and subject matter experts to understand business challenges, opportunities and strategic objectives.
Facilitate detailed workshops and meetings to gather, analyse, validate and prioritise requirements, while resolving conflicting needs across stakeholder groups.
Analyse existing business processes, identify improvement and automation opportunities, and produce clear process maps and supporting documentation.
Translate business needs into clear and traceable requirements, user stories, functional requirements and acceptance criteria, and manage changes throughout the project lifecycle.
Develop business cases outlining the business problem, proposed solution, expected benefits, costs, risks and outcomes.
Produce and maintain solution documentation, including detailed requirements, solution charters, risk assessment forms and supporting governance materials.
Lead design reviews and solution validation activities to confirm that proposed solutions meet agreed requirements before build and deployment.
Assess transformation opportunities and solution options using feasibility, value, organisational impact, fit-gap and build-versus-reuse analysis, while considering technical, security and licensing constraints.
Support the evaluation of Microsoft Power Platform, Robotic Process Automation (RPA), Artificial Intelligence (AI), Business Intelligence (BI) and other technology-enabled solutions, recommending the most suitable approach to stakeholders.
Coordinate initiatives from discovery through deployment by agreeing scope and timelines, managing dependencies and risks, and keeping stakeholders and technical teams aligned.
Facilitate the assessment of new and in-flight initiatives, support prioritisation and approval decisions, and track progress, risks and resource constraints across the portfolio.
Help shape the digital transformation roadmap by aligning initiatives with strategic priorities and communicating governance decisions, outcomes and expected benefits to management.
Support prototyping, proof-of-concept activities, User Acceptance Testing (UAT), defect triage, change management, user adoption and solution validation.
Maintain up-to-date software architecture documentation, particularly for RPA and BI solutions, covering system components, integrations, data flows, dependencies and relevant changes.
Work with stakeholders to define reporting requirements and key performance indicators, and design Power BI reports and dashboards that support management decision-making.
Analyse business data to identify trends and improvement opportunities, helping to ensure that reporting solutions are accurate, reliable and fit for purpose.
Act as a key liaison between business functions, technical teams and management, communicating technical concepts and solution options in clear business language.
Guide and mentor team members, coordinate team activities and knowledge sharing, and support the development of business analysis capability.
Establish consistent business analysis methodologies and standards, and review requirements, specifications and related deliverables for quality and completeness.




Required Qualifications and Skills




Bachelor's degree in Business, Information Systems, Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering or a related field.
At least five years of relevant experience in business analysis, digital transformation, technology projects or a related area.
Proven experience leading requirements gathering, process analysis and solution definition for complex or cross-functional initiatives.
Strong understanding of business analysis methodologies, requirements management and process improvement.
Experience coordinating projects involving multiple stakeholders and technical teams.
Knowledge of Agile, Waterfall or hybrid delivery methodologies.
Excellent analytical and problem-solving capabilities, with the ability to assess options and make practical recommendations.
Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to influence stakeholders and build effective working relationships.
Strong organisational and time management skills, with the ability to manage multiple initiatives and competing priorities simultaneously.
Experience mentoring or guiding junior analysts and promoting consistent quality standards.

Preferred Qualifications and Skills




A Business Analysis or project management certification, such as IIBA CCBA or CBAP, PRINCE2, PMP or a similar qualification.
Experience working within a professional services or other complex organisational environment.
Experience with Microsoft Power Platform or developing Power BI dashboards.
Understanding of RPA and AI-enabled business solutions.
Experience using process modelling techniques and tools such as BPMN, Microsoft Visio, Lucidchart or similar tools.
