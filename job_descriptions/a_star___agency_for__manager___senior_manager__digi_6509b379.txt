About The Role

A*STAR directs and undertakes mission-oriented research to transform Singapore into a knowledge-based and innovation-driven economy. The AIR Group advances this mission by developing research talent through the ASTAR Graduate Academy (AGA), fostering global collaborations via the International Partnerships Office (IPO), and ensuring strong research governance and grant oversight through the Research Office (RO). Together, AIR builds talent, partnerships and research excellence across A*STAR.

We are looking for a resourceful and solution-oriented professional to join AIR Group’s Digitalisation Team. The role will support the operation, stabilisation and continuous improvement of AIR’s enterprise systems, while driving digitalisation, automation and data initiatives to improve operational efficiency and user experience.

Key Responsibilities


ERP Business Analysis & System Management
Serve as Business Analyst for A*STAR ERP systems, including but not limited to SAP SuccessFactors, SAP S/4HANA, SmartRecruiters and Reimburse, working closely with IT teams, Finance, Human Resources, AIR business users and vendors.
Gather, analyse and translate business requirements into functional requirements, system enhancements and change requests.
Coordinate requirements clarification, solution design, UAT, deployment and post-implementation support.
Investigate system issues, assess business impact and coordinate with relevant stakeholders to drive timely resolution and system stabilisation.
Review end-to-end business processes and identify opportunities for system optimisation, process improvement and automation.
Monitor and support the ongoing performance and operational effectiveness of ERP systems and enhancements.
Gather and define reporting requirements, and work with IT teams and vendors to ensure ERP systems provide appropriate reporting capabilities to meet business needs.
Support system administration, GRC access management, documentation, governance and audit requirements.
Support periodic user access reviews for ERP systems to ensure access remains appropriate and compliant with governance and audit requirements.
Provide guidance and coaching to junior team members to build capabilities and ensure effective delivery of work.
Digital Transformation & Process Improvement
Partner with AIR business units to identify operational pain points, assess business processes and identify opportunities for digitalisation, automation and AI-enabled solutions.
Gather, analyse and document business requirements for non-ERP digitalisation initiatives.
Recommend appropriate digital solutions and lead process redesign to improve operational efficiency, user experience and service delivery.
Drive and support the implementation of AI, workflow automation, analytics and other emerging technology initiatives across AIR.
Evaluate the effectiveness of implemented solutions and identify opportunities for further optimisation and scaling.


Job Requirements


Degree in Information Systems, Computer Science or a related discipline.
Experience in business analysis, ERP systems and digital transformation, with exposure to platforms such as SAP SuccessFactors, SAP S/4HANA, SmartRecruiters or Reimburse.
Familiarity with AI, automation, analytics and digitalisation tools, such as Microsoft Power Platform, Power BI/Tableau, workflow automation and data analytics tools, would be advantageous.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to translate business needs into practical system and digital solutions.
Strong stakeholder engagement and communication skills, with the ability to work effectively across business users, IT teams and vendors.
Ability to manage multiple priorities and projects in a fast-paced digital transformation environment.
Meticulous, with strong attention to detail and follow-through.
