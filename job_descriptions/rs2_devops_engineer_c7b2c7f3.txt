About the Company




RS2 offers secure payment services, payment software and managed services to clients in over 35 countries. The company upholds the highest industry standards and RS2’s in-house designed payments solution is the software of choice by many of the world’s leading and most innovative banks and financial institutions.




About the Role




We are seeking a proactive DevOps Engineer to manage our application deployments, cloud infrastructure, and CI/CD automation across production environments. Leveraging Linux, AWS/Azure, Docker, Kubernetes, and Bash, you will optimize system performance, automate workflows, and ensure high availability.




Responsibilities




Deployment & Automation: Design continuous deployment solutions and manage code releases across environments, including production.
System Management: Install and configure 3rd-party middleware and proprietary applications.
Reliability & Monitoring: Monitor infrastructure and environments to ensure minimal downtime.
Continuous Improvement: Research and implement process improvements while maintaining documentation.
Optimization & Security: Assist in performance tuning and IT security initiatives.




Qualifications




Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related technical qualification.




Required Skills




Cloud & Containers: Hands-on experience with AWS and/or Azure, plus experience using Docker and Kubernetes.
Linux & Scripting: Strong background in Linux production environments and proficiency in Bash scripting.
Troubleshooting: Demonstrated strong problem-solving and analytical skills.
Communication: Fluency in written and spoken English.
