St John of God Health Care is one of Australia's largest and most respected private health care providers, employing over 15,000 caregivers across a diverse network of hospitals and community services. With 25 hospitals and services, more than 3,200 hospital beds, home nursing, disability support, and social outreach programs, we are committed to delivering exceptional care and supporting healthier communities.

An exciting opportunity is available for an ITSM Governance Specialist to join our Service Delivery team.

Based in either our Melbourne or Perth office, you will lead the maturity, governance and optimisation of St John of God Health Care's IT Service Management practices across a complex enterprise environment.

Working closely with Digital & Technology project and operational teams, you will drive improvements in ITSM processes, ServiceNow, CMDB/CSDM governance, service transition and service reporting to enhance service quality, operational effectiveness and compliance.

The Position:


Design, govern and continuously improve ITSM processes, standards and policies aligned to ITIL best practice.
Lead the development and maturity of the ServiceNow CMDB and Common Service Data Model (CSDM), including governance, service mapping, ownership and data quality.
Develop service management reporting, KPI frameworks and dashboards that support performance, compliance and continual improvement.
Govern and improve Transition to Support and Operational Readiness processes, ensuring new and changed services are effectively transitioned into operational support.
Enhance ServiceNow functionality, workflows, reporting and self-service capabilities.
Deliver stakeholder engagement, training and process improvement initiatives that strengthen organisational capability and adoption of service management practices.


Experience In ServiceNow Administration Is Highly Desirable.

Your skills and experience

You will have proven experience leading the development, governance and maturity of IT Service Management practices within a large and complex enterprise environment.

We are specifically seeking candidates who have:


Led ITSM process improvement and governance initiatives.
Developed CMDB and CSDM governance frameworks and configuration management practices.
Administered or significantly enhanced ServiceNow capabilities.
Established or governed Transition to Support or Operational Readiness processes.
Delivered reporting, KPI frameworks and continual service improvement initiatives.


Applicants must be able to demonstrate experience developing, governing and improving enterprise ITSM processes, rather than solely operating within established ITSM processes.

Experience limited to Service Desk, End User Computing, Application Support, Incident, Problem, Change or Request Management operations will not be sufficient for success in this role.

Above all, patient care will be at the core of everything you do committing to and supporting our Mission and Values.

Application Requirements

All applicants are requested to submit a covering letter of no more than two pages demonstrating how they meet the position requirements, including specific examples of:


ITSM governance or process maturity initiatives.
CMDB, CSDM or configuration management improvements.
ServiceNow administration or platform enhancement activities.
Transition to Support or Operational Readiness frameworks.


Applications that do not clearly demonstrate experience in these areas may not be progressed.
