Software Engineer / QA Specialist

About The Role

XNET IT PTY LTD is seeking a versatile Software Engineer with a strong focus on quality assurance and systems validation. You won’t just be writing code; you will be the guardian of our software integrity. This role is perfect for a technical professional who enjoys dissecting complex user stories, building robust automation frameworks, and ensuring that every production release is seamless and bug-free.

You will join a collaborative environment where your technical expertise directly influences our development lifecycle—from initial requirements analysis to final production verification.

Key Responsibilities


Analysis & Design: Interpret user stories and functional specifications to design comprehensive test cases and prepare sophisticated test data.
End-to-End Testing: Execute manual and automated testing suites, including smoke, functional, regression, and integration testing.
Defect Management: Log and manage bugs via JIRA and Zephyr, ensuring every issue is tracked from discovery to resolution.
Cross-Platform Validation: Conduct testing across various browsers, devices, emulators, and test labs to ensure a consistent user experience.
Automation Development: Contribute to the growth of our test automation capabilities to increase efficiency and coverage.
Stakeholder Collaboration: Work closely with business stakeholders and technical teams to clarify requirements and validate system functionality.
Low-Level Analysis: Perform business analysis to troubleshoot and resolve underlying system issues.
Release Management: Verify production releases and manage testing activities across multiple concurrent projects


Key Requirements

To be successful in this role, you should possess:


Technical Proficiency: Strong experience in software development and testing methodologies.
Tooling Expertise: Hands-on experience with JIRA, Zephyr, and automated testing frameworks.
Analytical Mindset: The ability to perform low-level business analysis and translate complex requirements into actionable test plans.
Attention to Detail: A disciplined approach to capturing test evidence and maintaining accurate documentation.
Adaptability: Proven ability to manage priorities across multiple projects in a fast-paced environment.
Communication: Excellent verbal and written skills for reporting test outcomes and collaborating with cross-functional teams


How To Apply
