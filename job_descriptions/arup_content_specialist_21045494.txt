Full job description

Dedicated to sustainable development, Arup is a collective of designers, consultants and experts working globally.

At Arup you will have the opportunity to collaborate on ambitious projects - delivering remarkable outcomes for clients and communities, and to do socially useful work that has meaning. Arup's purpose, shared values and collaborative approach has set it apart for over 75 years, and now is your opportunity to join.

AMS is a global workforce solutions partner committed to creating inclusive, dynamic, and future-ready workplaces. We help organisations adapt, grow, and thrive in an ever-evolving world by building, shaping, and optimising diverse talent strategies.

We partner with Arup to support their contingent recruitment processes. Acting as an extension of their recruitment teams, we connect them with skilled interim and temporary professionals, fostering workplaces where everyone can contribute and succeed.

On Behalf of Arup, we are looking for a Content Specialist for a 12-month contract based in London, hybrid working 3 days per week on site.

Purpose of the role:

As a Content Specialist, you will create and maintain clear, user-focused content for the Digital Manual, transforming complex policies, standards and ways of working into practical guidance. You will work closely with stakeholders and subject matter experts to ensure content is accurate, accessible and aligned to user needs, while helping to develop and embed AI-enabled content creation processes that improve the efficiency, consistency and ongoing maintenance of content through appropriate governance, quality assurance and human oversight.

What you'll do:

Create, edit and maintain clear, engaging content for the Digital Manual using plain language principles.
Transform complex policies, standards and processes into practical, user-friendly guidance.
Work with subject matter experts and stakeholders to gather information and ensure content accuracy and consistency.
Review and improve existing content, identifying gaps, duplication and opportunities for enhancement.
Organise and structure content to make information easy to find, understand and apply.
Support the development of AI-enabled content creation processes, using automation and AI tools to improve content efficiency and quality.
Ensure all content meets governance, quality assurance and accessibility standards.
Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives, helping shape content best practice across the Digital Manual.

The skills you'll need:

Proven experience in content creation, content design, technical writing or knowledge management within a corporate environment.
Excellent writing, editing and proofreading skills, with the ability to simplify complex information into clear, user-friendly content.
Strong understanding of plain language principles and best practices for digital content.
Experience working with subject matter experts and stakeholders to gather, validate and improve content.
Ability to identify content gaps, inconsistencies and opportunities for continuous improvement.
Experience using content management systems and digital collaboration tools.
Knowledge of AI and automation tools for content creation, analysis or maintenance would be advantageous.
Strong organisational skills, attention to detail and ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.
Experience supporting content governance, quality assurance and content lifecycle management.
Ability to work independently and collaboratively within cross-functional teams.
