Technical Writer – Google Cloud Learning




Location: South Africa – Remote

Company: SureSkills

Employment Type: Independent Contractor

Contract Duration: Until 31 March 2027, with potential for extension




About SureSkills

SureSkills is a global learning and technology services company working with some of the world's leading technology organisations, including Google, AWS, Microsoft, VMware, Meta and Coursera.

We support the design, development, maintenance and operation of large-scale technical learning programmes used by learners, customers and partners around the world.




The Role

SureSkills is looking for an experienced Technical Writer to join our team supporting the ongoing maintenance and improvement of Google Cloud's technical learning and certification content.

We're looking for someone who is technically minded, highly organised and comfortable working within structured, process-driven environments. The ideal candidate will have a background in technical writing, technical documentation, software documentation or technical content maintenance, along with an understanding of Instructional Design principles.




This is not a traditional Instructional Design role focused on developing new courses from scratch. Instead, the emphasis is on reviewing, maintaining, troubleshooting and improving existing technical learning content, ensuring materials remain accurate, consistent, up to date and aligned with established quality standards.




You'll support the maintenance of both Instructor-Led Training (ILT) and On-Demand (OD) learning materials, including technical documentation, presentations, hands-on lab instructions, assessments and supporting multimedia content.




The role involves reviewing learner feedback, investigating content issues, managing updates through structured workflows, maintaining documentation and version histories, and supporting quality assurance and publishing activities.




What You'll Be Doing

Technical Writing & Content Maintenance

Review, edit and maintain existing technical learning content, including presentations, technical readings, hands-on lab instructions, quizzes, assessments and supporting documentation.
Identify outdated information, technical inaccuracies, broken links, formatting issues and inconsistencies across course materials.
Apply content updates based on technical SME guidance, documented requirements and established maintenance procedures.
Ensure technical instructions and explanations are clear, accurate, consistent and easy for learners to follow.
Maintain consistency between instructor-led and on-demand versions of technical courses.
Translate technical feedback and documented issues into clear, actionable content updates.




Issue Tracking & Process Management

Review learner feedback, course data and technical issue reports to identify required maintenance activities.
Use structured data, reporting and AI tools to support feedback analysis and issue identification.
Investigate, categorise and prioritise content issues based on technical complexity and the type of update required.
Create, update and manage tickets through issue-tracking systems such as Jira, GitHub Issues or similar platforms.
Follow structured workflows for content review, approvals, testing and publication.
Track maintenance activity, outstanding issues, dependencies and completion status.
Coordinate with technical SMEs and other team members to clarify issues and support their resolution.




Documentation & Version Control

Maintain accurate changelogs, technical documentation, content repositories and version histories.
Document before-and-after changes, including relevant issue references and impacts on related course assets.
Maintain course metadata, repository links, file structures and supporting documentation.
Follow established naming conventions, documentation standards and content management procedures.
Support Git-based or repository-based workflows where required.
Ensure completed work is properly documented, archived and prepared for future maintenance cycles.




Quality Assurance & Publishing

Perform detailed quality assurance checks on updated technical learning materials.
Verify technical accuracy, content consistency, formatting, links and learner-facing instructions.
Prepare learner and instructor materials for publication.
Support content publishing across staging and live learning platforms.
Conduct post-publication checks to confirm updates have been implemented correctly.
Identify and document defects or issues requiring further investigation.
Ensure all updates follow established review, approval and release processes.




Learning Content Support

Apply Instructional Design principles when reviewing and updating technical learning content.
Ensure updates maintain the intended learning objectives, instructional flow and overall learner experience.
Identify when technical content changes affect related videos, voice-overs, captions or other multimedia assets.
Support the creation, updating and maintenance of multimedia learning materials.
Use Generative AI tools where appropriate to support content analysis, drafting, quality checks and workflow efficiency.




Essential Experience

3+ years of professional experience in technical writing, technical documentation, software documentation, technical content maintenance or a closely related field.
Strong experience reviewing, editing and maintaining existing technical content, rather than solely creating new materials.
Excellent technical writing and documentation skills, with the ability to present complex information clearly and accurately.
Experience working within structured, process-driven environments involving content reviews, issue tracking, quality assurance or release management.
Strong attention to detail and the ability to identify technical inconsistencies, documentation errors and content quality issues.
Experience using issue-tracking or workflow management tools such as Jira, GitHub Issues or similar platforms.
Strong working knowledge of Google Workspace, particularly Docs, Sheets, Slides and Drive, or equivalent tools.
Comfortable working with technical subject matter and collaborating with SMEs to understand and implement content changes.
A working understanding of Instructional Design principles, including learning objectives, content structure and the learner experience.
Strong organisational skills, with the ability to manage multiple updates, maintain accurate records and follow established procedures.
Excellent written and verbal English communication skills.
