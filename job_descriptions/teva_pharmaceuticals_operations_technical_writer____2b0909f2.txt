Our Team, Your Impact

We are seeking an Operations Technical Writer to support our Manufacturing team in a GMP-regulated pharmaceutical production environment. In this role, you will be responsible for creating, revising, and maintaining controlled manufacturing documentation while supporting investigations related to deviations, non-conformances, and quality events. This position is ideal for someone who enjoys technical writing, problem-solving, and collaborating across multiple departments to drive compliance and operational excellence.

How You’ll Spend Your Day

Technical Documentation


Author, revise, and maintain controlled GMP documentation, including:
Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
Work Instructions (WIs)
Job Aids
Equipment Manuals
Cleaning Procedures
Manufacturing Support Documents
Forms and Templates
Training Materials
Ensure documents meet site quality standards, formatting requirements, and regulatory expectations.
Translate complex technical processes into clear and user-friendly instructions for operational personnel.

Investigations & Quality Support


Lead and support investigations involving manufacturing deviations, non-conformances, procedural events, equipment issues, and quality concerns.
Gather and assess information from batch records, equipment logs, electronic systems, environmental data, and employee interviews.
Perform Root Cause Analysis (RCA) using tools such as:
5 Whys
Fishbone Diagrams
FMEA
Assess impact on product quality, compliance, patient safety, and operational performance.
Author investigation reports and support timely closure of quality events and CAPAs.

Training & Continuous Improvement


Develop and update training materials.
Partner with subject matter experts to document best practices and manufacturing standards.
Support document lifecycle management within electronic quality management systems.
Promote consistency and standardization across manufacturing documentation.


Required

Your Skills and Experience


College Diploma in Science or a related discipline.
Minimum 1 year of experience in a GMP-regulated pharmaceutical, biotechnology, medical device, or manufacturing environment.
Experience authoring and revising controlled documentation.
Strong technical writing and editing skills.
Knowledge of Good Manufacturing Practices (cGMP).
Proficiency with Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Visio).


Preferred


Bachelor's Degree in Science, Engineering, or a related field.
4+ years of pharmaceutical manufacturing experience, preferably in oral solid dosage manufacturing.
Experience using quality systems such as TrackWise, Veeva, MasterControl, or similar platforms.
Experience conducting investigations and developing CAPAs.


Knowledge & Skills


Technical Writing Excellence
Root Cause Analysis & Investigations
Critical Thinking & Problem Solving
Attention to Detail
Document Control & Quality Systems
Knowledge of Health Canada, FDA, ICH, and PIC/S GMP requirements
Understanding of oral solid dose manufacturing processes, including dispensing, granulation, compression, coating, encapsulation, packaging, and cleaning
