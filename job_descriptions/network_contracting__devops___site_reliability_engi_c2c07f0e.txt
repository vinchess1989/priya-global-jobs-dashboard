DevOps / Site Reliability Engineer (SRE)
Network Contracting Solutions
I'm interested
Reference: NCM003585-Glad-1

Can you improve software delivery and keep critical platforms reliable, observable and resilient?
Duties & Responsibilities
We are building our network of DevOps and Site Reliability Engineering professionals for current and future opportunities. We are looking for engineers who can automate software delivery, manage cloud infrastructure, improve observability and build reliable deployment practices. If you have hands-on experience with CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code and production operations, we would like to connect with you.
 
Key Responsibilities:

Build, maintain and optimise CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation.
Provision and manage cloud infrastructure using Infrastructure as Code.
Improve system reliability through monitoring, alerting, incident management and automation.
Work with development and operations teams to strengthen release quality and platform resilience.
 
Job Experience & Skills Required / Ideal Candidate Profile:
Education

Diploma or degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or a related field.
Relevant AWS, Azure, Kubernetes or DevOps certifications advantageous.
Linux, networking or systems administration certifications beneficial.
SRE, cloud operations or automation training advantageous.
 
Experience

5+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, cloud engineering or systems automation.
Strong experience with CI/CD tools such as Jenkins, GitLab CI or GitHub Actions.
Hands-on experience with Docker, Kubernetes and cloud platforms.
Experience with Terraform or similar IaC tools, monitoring and production support.
 
Skills 

Azure/AWS, Linux and scripting (Python, Bash or PowerShell).
CI/CD pipelines, Git and release automation.
Docker, Kubernetes and Infrastructure as Code.
Observability, incident response, reliability engineering and automation.
 
Location: Gauteng / South Africa
