Are you ready to join a world leader in the exciting and dynamic fields of the Pharmaceutical and Medical Device industries? PQE Group has been at the forefront of these industries since 1998, with 40 subsidiaries and more than 2000 employees in Europe, Asia and the Americas.




Due to our continuous growth, PQE Group is seeking a Recipe Author to join our team in Dublin




Context

As part of the launch of the eBR Acceleration Program, a multi-site initiative to accelerate electronic batch record (eBR) deployment across our network, we are looking for experienced eBR Recipe Authors for Phase 1 of the program (Q1 2027 to Q4 2028).







Responsibilities include, but are not limited to:







Author, configure, and maintain eBR recipes in line with site requirements and program standards.
Collaborate closely with production, quality, engineering, automation, and IT teams across sites.
Contribute to the standardization and harmonization of eBR recipes across the network.
Support testing, validation, and go-live activities for eBR recipes.
Ensure GMP compliance, data integrity, and documentation quality.
Support deviation resolution and troubleshooting related to eBR recipes.
Document activities and provide support to local teams during deployment.
Contribute to knowledge transfer and training of key users as required.







Required Qualifications:




Minimum 6 years of experience in a pharmaceutical/biotechnology environment, ideally in recipe authoring, MES/eBR, validation, or production.
Proven experience in creating and managing eBR recipes / electronic Batch Records.
Good knowledge of GMP requirements and data integrity principles.
Ability to work in a multi-site and multicultural environment.
Autonomous, rigorous, analytical, and strong coordination skills.










Next Steps

Upon receiving your application, if a match is found, the Recruiting department will contact you for an initial HR interview.

If there's a positive match, a technical interview with the Hiring Manager will be arranged.

In the case of a positive outcome coming from Hiring Manager interview, recruiter will contact you for further steps or to discuss our proposal.

Alternatively, if the outcome is negative, we will contact you to halt the recruitment process.
