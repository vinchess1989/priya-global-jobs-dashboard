Ko Wai Mātou – About Us

Kia ora. We’re Foodies. You know New World, PAK’nSAVE and Four Square right? That’s us!

With 300+ stores and over 27,000 Foodies across Te Ika-a-Maui, our North Island Co-op has the privilege of helping New Zealanders get more out of life. Locally owned, we reflect the communities we serve, and we celebrate our people - our taonga, every day for being uniquely them.

Our Support Centre is 1,500+ people strong, which includes 500+ IT employees, who all play a vital role in ensuring the cogs of our Co-op stay turning and our systems stay ‘byte’-sized and ‘bug’-free.”.

Te Tūranga – About The Role

We're looking for an experienced IT Service Assurance Owner to take ownership of Major Incident Management and IT Change Management across Foodstuffs North Island.

This is a key role where you'll lead the response to major incidents, ensuring the right people are brought together, decisions are made quickly and critical services are restored with minimal business impact. You'll also own our IT change-management process, ensuring technical changes are properly assessed, governed and implemented without compromising service stability.

Working across technology teams, outsourced service providers and business stakeholders, you'll drive strong service-management practices, provide clear visibility of risks and upcoming changes, and continuously improve how we protect our live services.

What You’ll Do


Lead Major Incident Management, coordinating cross-functional teams to drive timely resolution and restoration of critical services.
Take ownership of incident communications, escalations, stakeholder updates and post-incident reviews.
Govern outsourced service providers' major incident responses outside business hours, ensuring consistent processes and service standards.
Own and continuously improve the IT Change Management process, ensuring changes are assessed, prioritised and approved based on risk and business impact.
Facilitate Change Advisory Board (CAB) meetings, bringing the right stakeholders together to make informed change decisions.
Oversee complex and emergency changes, ensuring appropriate planning, approvals, risk management and implementation.
Manage the change calendar, maintenance windows and change-freeze periods, providing visibility of upcoming changes across the business.
Monitor change outcomes, identify trends and provide reporting and insights to strengthen service assurance and governance.
Embed security and risk management into incident and change practices, protecting the stability and resilience of our technology environment.

He Kōrero Mōu - About You


Proven experience in IT Service Management, particularly Major Incident Management and/or Change Management.
Strong understanding of ITIL frameworks and Incident, Problem and Change Management practices.
Experience leading high-impact incidents in complex technology environments.
Strong experience in change governance, risk assessment, approvals and implementation planning.
Experience managing outsourced service providers, including escalations and performance expectations.
Confidence coordinating technical teams and stakeholders to drive resolution under pressure.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to make complex situations clear and actionable.
Strong analytical skills, with the ability to identify trends, risks and opportunities for continuous improvement.
An ITIL qualification in Service Transition or equivalent, with 3–5 years' experience in Change Management.
A strong focus on security, operational resilience and protecting business-critical services.
