Job Description of ﻿Specialist DevOps Engineer in Perth:

The Engineer will develop and support digital solutions so that our people have the right technology and the right data analytics skill to perform their roles safely and efficiently. Strong DevOps pipeline creation experience

Strong experience with data query languages such as SQL.Experience in working with cloud infrastructure & development platforms (Azure preferred).Understanding of CI/CD concepts.Troubleshooting techniques and fixing the code bugs experience
