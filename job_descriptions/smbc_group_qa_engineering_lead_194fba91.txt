Role Description

SMBC is seeking an experienced QA Engineering Lead to support the implementation of SAP SuccessFactors. This is a hands-on QA leadership role spanning application testing, UI/UX, test automation and data validation, with a strong focus on Python, advanced SQL and Databricks.

The role will lead the overall QA strategy and testing approach while collaborating with global technology and business teams. Responsibilities will include testing governance, test strategy and planning, release readiness, quality risk and defect management and ensuring testing activities adhere to established SDLC processes and controls.

This role will report to the Director of Quality Assurance Testing.

Role Objectives: Delivery

QA Leadership & Test Strategy


Own the overall QA strategy and testing governance for the SuccessFactors implementation, ensuring appropriate standards, controls and quality measures are established throughout delivery.
Lead a team of QA Engineers and provide direction on testing approach, coverage and execution.
Develop comprehensive test plans and strategies across application, UI/UX, automation, database and ETL testing.
Review business and technical requirements to identify testable conditions, gaps, risks and acceptance criteria.
Provide senior stakeholders with clear assessments of quality risk, testing progress and release readiness, escalating significant issues where required.
Support release planning and ensure appropriate testing coverage ahead of production releases.
Drive continuous improvement across QA processes, testing standards and automation practices.
Ensure testing activities align with established SDLC processes and controls.


Application & Data Testing


Lead and oversee application, automated and ETL testing activities, remaining hands-on where required.
Validate application functionality and integrated backend systems.
Perform end-to-end data validation across extraction, transformation and loading processes.
Write and execute complex SQL queries to validate data across multiple stages and systems.
Develop Python scripts to validate large datasets and identify defects or data-quality issues.
Review Databricks jobs and pipeline runs to ensure ETL processes complete successfully and meet required data-quality standards.


Test & Defect Management


Define, document and track test scenarios and test cases in Jira, ensuring appropriate coverage across integrated platforms and backend systems.
Identify, document and manage defects through to resolution.
Collaborate with developers and wider technology teams to investigate issues and support timely remediation.
Provide oversight of significant quality issues and drive appropriate escalation and resolution.
Establish and continuously improve QA processes and testing practices.


Qualifications And Skills


Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering or a related discipline, or equivalent relevant experience.
7+ years of experience across application, database and automation testing, with demonstrated experience leading complex QA delivery.
Proven experience leading QA teams and providing direction across complex technology programmes.
Strong hands-on experience with database testing, advanced SQL, Python and Databricks.
Strong Python and SQL skills with experience using test automation frameworks such as Playwright or similar.
Strong understanding of ETL processes, data validation and testing across integrated systems.
Experience developing test strategies, establishing QA governance and assessing release readiness.
Experience using test and defect management tools such as Jira.
Proven ability to introduce and improve QA processes, testing standards and automation practices.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to operate across complex technology environments.
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills with experience working across global teams and engaging senior stakeholders.


Advantageous Experience


Experience testing SAP SuccessFactors or another enterprise HR platform.
Experience with Java, JavaScript or VBScript.
Experience with RFT, HP Quality Center or similar testing and test-management tools.
Experience using Confluence.
