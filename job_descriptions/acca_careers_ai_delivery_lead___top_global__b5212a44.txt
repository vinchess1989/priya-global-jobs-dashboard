Delivery Lead | Global Conglomerate | AI

Your new company

This prestigious Global Conglomerate is looking for an experienced Delivery Lead in Project Management to join its dynamic business here in Hong Kong and support the delivery of AI and data initiatives by coordinating day-to-day activities across business stakeholders and different teams.

Your new role


Coordinate day-to-day delivery activities across business stakeholders, technical teams, QA, support, and rollout teams.
Track actions, owners, timelines, dependencies, risks, issues, decisions, and blockers.
Support meeting preparation, meeting facilitation, meeting summaries, and follow-up tracking.
Maintain delivery documentation, including action logs, delivery plans, status updates, readiness checklists, and handover materials.
Support requirements clarification, backlog tracking, sprint planning, and delivery scope alignment.
Help prepare and coordinate UAT, release readiness, rollout readiness, and production handover activities.
Work with business users and technical teams to clarify user needs, delivery scope, priorities, and constraints.
Escalate risks, blockers, unclear ownership, missing inputs, or delivery gaps in a timely manner.
Use AI tools to improve documentation, reporting, risk tracking, action tracking, requirements structuring, and project knowledge management.
Support continuous improvement in delivery coordination practices, templates, and ways of working.


What you'll need to succeed


Experience coordinating delivery of technology, data, analytics, AI, or digital initiatives.
3+ years of experience in delivery lead, project manager, project coordinator, or related roles.
Strong ability to organise work across multiple stakeholders, teams, timelines, actions, dependencies, and risks.
Comfortable working with both business users and technical teams.
Strong follow-up discipline: able to track actions, clarify owners, chase blockers, and keep work moving.
Familiarity with agile delivery, sprint planning, backlog tracking, Jira, Azure DevOps, Confluence, Notion, Monday.com, or similar tools.
Understanding of product delivery concepts, even if not a formal product manager.
Experience preparing rollout, UAT, release readiness, or production handover materials.
Good documentation skills, including meeting notes, action logs, status updates, delivery plans, readiness checklists, and handover materials.
Able to understand requirements, user needs, delivery scope, and business context well enough to support structured execution.
Strong proficiency using AI tools in daily work, such as ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude, Gemini, or enterprise AI assistants.
Practical experience using AI to summarise meetings, draft documents, structure requirements, prepare reports, track risks, or improve productivity.
Detail-oriented, proactive, reliable, and comfortable operating in a fast-moving environment.
Strong communication skills in English, both written and verbal.


What you'll get in return


Market Leading Compensation Package
High exposure to cutting-edge AI projects
Stable Working Culture
