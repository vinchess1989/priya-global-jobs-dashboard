Join ICOMAT – Revolutionising the Future of Lightweight Manufacturing!

iCOMAT is an advanced manufacturing company that invented Rapid Tow Shearing (RTS) - the only proven technology for producing defect-free curved-fiber composite parts at industrial scale. We deliver the lightest structures possible for aerospace, automotive, defense, and space, at a rate and cost efficiency that far exceeds existing composite production processes.

We build and operate automated factories for composite materials, delivering components to Tier 1 suppliers and OEMs. The business has three main divisions: development of automated production machines, development of software design and production tools, and operation of the factory. We work with 25+ customers and completed the largest ever Series A in composites manufacturing to fuel a significant scale-up, with growth plans that will see the business multiply in size over the next two to three years and expand operations into the UK, Europe and the US.

The business has currently more than 130,000 sqm of production capacity with manufacturing facilities in the UK (Gloucester and Swindon) and the US (Dayton, Ohio); a Hardware R&D Centre in Bristol, UK; and a Software R&D Centre in Athens, Greece.

The Role

As Quality Engineer, you own the quality management system (QMS) that lets us ship flight- and road-ready lightweight structures with confidence. You'll write the quality plans and procedures, run audits, manage our approved supplier list, produce FAIR reports and turn production data into improvements. You'll shape quality from the ground up as the factory scales.

What will you be doing?


Build and run quality control: Develop, implement and maintain procedures that ensure every part meets design specifications and regulatory standards.
Solve problems at the root: Investigate product and process failures, run root cause analysis and drive corrective actions that stick.
Make production better: Recommend and lead improvements that reduce defects, raise quality and increase efficiency as we scale.
Own the paper trail: Compile and maintain inspection, audit, test and corrective action records (including FAIR and ISIR), and report findings clearly to management.
Audit and keep us compliant: Run internal audits against our quality standards and regulatory requirements.
Manage supplier quality: Work with suppliers so materials and components meet our standards, and resolve issues quickly when they don't.
Grow quality culture: Train production staff on quality standards, tools and techniques.
Collaborate across teams: Work with engineering, production and R&D to feed quality learnings back into product design and manufacturing processes.
Let the data lead: Analyse defect rates, returns and other quality data to spot trends and prioritise continuous improvement.


What do we need from you?

Essential


3+ years in a Quality Engineering or related role
Working knowledge of QMS frameworks (e.g. ISO 9001, AS9100), tools (e.g. FMEA, SPC) and techniques (e.g. root cause analysis, Six Sigma)
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Proficiency with quality testing equipment, measurement tools and software
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the confidence to work with both shop-floor teams and management
Sharp attention to detail and a commitment to high standards


Desirable


Quality management systems certification
Experience with statistical analysis software and data analysis tools
Familiarity with industry standards in aerospace, automotive or composites
