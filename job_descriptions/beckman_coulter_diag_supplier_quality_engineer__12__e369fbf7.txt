The Supplier Quality Engineer is responsible for completing Supplier Quality Assurance activities within Beckman Coulter Ireland ensuring Quality system regulations are complied with.

This position reports to the Senior Manager Supplier Quality Europe and is part of the Global Supplier Quality team and will be an on-site role.

This role is a 12 month FTC.

In This Role, You Will Have The Opportunity To


Support new supplier/material introductions to ensure that all requirements under the Beckman Coulter Supplier Qualification process are maintained.
Supplier liaison and evaluation – including participation in the Supplier Selection Process and review of supplier supplied information via self-assessments, SQA etc.
Facilitate root cause analysis and corrective actions of supplier quality issues through the NC/SCAR processes.
Manage Supplier Change Notifications. Participate in cross-functional teams in the development of new products or changes related to current products
Report writing and presenting progress at project meetings. Attend meetings and action/communicate instructions as applicable
Support DBS improvement initiatives, utilizing DBS tools including participation in Visual Daily Management, Problem Solving and Kaizen events to ensure quality, safety, productivity, on time delivery and supporting internal customers.


The Essential Requirements Of The Job Include


BSc in chemistry, biochemistry, engineering or other relevant technical field with at least 3 years’ experience in Quality/Supplier Assurance in a FDA regulated industry.
A good knowledge of ISO9001/ISO13485, FDA and GMP requirements.
Excellent time management and organisation skills as well as effective interpersonal and verbal/written communication and strong proof-reading skills with emphasis on attention to detail


It would be a plus if you also possess previous experience in:


Lean/Six Sigma experience. Demonstrated capability in problem solving
Proficiency in Power BI/Smartsheet/Miro/Excel
