About the job




Medical Device & IVD Consultancy

Location: UK (Remote / Hybrid)

Salary: £90k+ (Bonus, Car Allowance, Pension and other benefits negotiable)




The Role in 20 Seconds

You’ve spent your career in medical device regulatory affairs, and at some point you realised you enjoy the client side as much as the technical side. You know MDR, IVDR and UK MDR inside out, you’ve compiled and reviewed more technical files than you can count, and you can scope, price and lead a project as confidently as you can critique a clinical evaluation. If you've got a specialist intererst in Software and SaMD, then we really want to hear from you!




You're probably a Regulatory Consultant now, or you've held titles like Head of Regulatory, Principal Consultant or RA/QA Manager. You don’t need a rigid job description to know what needs doing. You’re the person who spots it and gets it done.




If that’s you, and the idea of leading the consulting function of a growing consultancy excites rather than unsettles you, keep reading. This is an opportunity to develop a team, develop a business and develop personally...




About us

LFH Regulatory is a well-established, growing UK regulatory and quality consultancy supporting medical device manufacturers across the UK, EU and US. Our clients range from early-stage innovators to established manufacturers, across devices, IVDs, digital health and SaMD. We are a small team who are settled and highly capable. The business has a clear growth plan, and this role sits at the heart of it.




What You’ll Actually Do

The core of it: Lead and mentor the senior consulting team, briefing them on clients and projects and holding the quality bar on everything that goes out
Own client relationships and be their main point of contact - not just the escalation route
Scope new projects, prepare quotes and build regulatory strategies that clients can actually act on
Review client deliverables, including technical documentation, QMS and regulatory submissions, across the UK, EU and US
Lead next-step and feedback calls, and own the feedback and complaints process
Create and improve the internal processes the business runs on
Represent the business at exhibitions, conferences and networking events, and spot the opportunities that come out of them




The less obvious bits that matter just as much: Setting the direction for how the consulting team works, with the autonomy to make decisions and the accountability that comes with them
Bringing structure and forward planning to a fast-moving consultancy - meetings, events and client deadlines handled in good time rather than in a last-minute rush
Knowing when a client needs the full regulatory answer and when they need a pragmatic, proportionate one - and having the credibility to make that call




Who We’re Looking For

You’ll be a natural fit if:You have 10+ years in medical device and/or IVD regulatory affairs, with a scientific, engineering or clinical background
You’ve personally compiled and reviewed technical documentation for CE and UKCA marking under MDR/IVDR and UK MDR - this is essential
You understand ISO 13485 and FDA QMSR (21 CFR Part 820) well enough to build a QMS from scratch or fix one that isn’t working
You’ve led people, ideally in a consultancy or multi-client environment where priorities shift weekly
You’re commercially minded - comfortable scoping, quoting and having the “what this will actually cost” conversation with a client
US regulatory experience is a real plus




Essential:

Demonstrable regulatory depth across medical devices and/or IVDs
Client-facing confidence and strong interpersonal and communication skills
Excellent attention to detail, and the judgement to know what matters most
A hands-on, get-stuck-in attitude - in a business this size, everyone pitches in
Willingness to travel within the UK and occasionally internationally for clients and events




What’s Actually In It For You

A senior leadership role on the leadership team, with real ownership of how the consulting function performs and grows
A varied client base, so no two months look the same
A settled, supportive team who are good at what they do
Bonus, a car or cash allowance, and flexible working, including early Friday finishes
The chance to shape how the consultancy runs, not just inherit it




The Honest Bit

We are a small consultancy, and that means the role is broad.

If you like being the person who connects the client, the team and the delivery, and you can switch between leading, reviewing and selling in the same afternoon, you’ll find this energising.




A note for independent consultants: if you’ve been working independently and love the consulting itself but miss being part of a team, this could be a great next step. You’d keep doing the work you enjoy, with the backing of a bigger team, colleagues to share the load and a wider spread of projects. You’d still have the atmosphere of a small company and real impact on where the business goes.
