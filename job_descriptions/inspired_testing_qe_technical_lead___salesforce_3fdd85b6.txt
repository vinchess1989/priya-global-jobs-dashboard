Job Title: QE Technical Lead – Salesforce & Copado

Level: Lead / Architect

Location: Gauteng




About the Role

Inspired Testing is looking for a hands-on QE Technical Lead with strong Salesforce, Copado and automation architecture experience to establish and technically lead the Quality Engineering capability for a major Salesforce transformation within financial services.

This is not a traditional functional Test Lead role. The successful candidate will take ownership of the Quality Engineering strategy, automation architecture and framework, and establish the engineering standards, reference patterns and continuous-testing controls that will underpin delivery across Salesforce, APIs, integrations, data and downstream enterprise systems.

The role requires someone who has previously established Quality Engineering and test automation from the ground up for a major Salesforce implementation or transformation. The QE Technical Lead will remain hands-on during mobilisation and initial framework implementation, then provide technical direction to the Senior and Intermediate Quality Engineers who will scale the capability across multiple delivery streams.




Key Responsibilities

Quality Engineering Strategy & Architecture

• Own and establish the Quality Engineering and technical test strategy for the Salesforce transformation.

• Translate business journeys, solution architecture and integration dependencies into a risk-based, multi-layered quality approach.

• Define how quality will be engineered across Salesforce, APIs, integrations, services, data and downstream enterprise systems.

• Establish technical quality gates, engineering standards, reusable patterns and governance controls for concurrent delivery streams.

• Identify architecture, integration, testability, environment and data risks early and drive their resolution with engineering and delivery teams.




Automation Strategy & Framework Ownership

• Own the automation strategy, architecture and framework from inception.

• Remain hands-on during mobilisation and initial implementation, creating the framework foundations, reference implementations and reusable components that the wider Quality Engineering team will extend.

• Determine the appropriate balance across UI, API, service, contract, integration, component, data and selective end-to-end automation.

• Establish coding standards, framework conventions, test-data patterns, execution approaches, reporting and evidence requirements.

• Ensure the framework is scalable, maintainable and suitable for multiple Salesforce delivery streams and significant integration complexity.

• Technically direct Senior and Intermediate Quality Engineers as automated coverage is implemented and scaled.




Salesforce Test Automation

• Establish the Quality Engineering and automation approach for a major Salesforce implementation or transformation rather than operating only within an inherited mature framework.

• Lead quality engineering across complex Salesforce business journeys, Lightning Experience, Lightning Web Components, configuration, customisation, APIs, integrations and data flows.

• Understand the testing implications of Apex, Visualforce, SOQL, platform configuration and custom development.

• Work with Salesforce Developers and Solution Architects to improve testability, coverage and engineering quality at the appropriate layer.

• Ensure end-to-end business journeys are validated across Salesforce and connected enterprise systems without over-relying on slow, brittle UI automation.




Copado DevOps & CI/CD

• Own the integration of automated quality engineering into the Salesforce and Copado delivery lifecycle.

• Use Copado to support Salesforce CI/CD, deployment validation, release automation and continuous testing across environments.

• Define how the automation framework integrates with Copado pipelines, source control, deployment stages and release quality gates.

• Establish automated checks across pull requests, builds, deployments and releases, including appropriate smoke, integration and regression execution.

• Apply strong Git-based practices, including branching, pull requests, code review and traceable promotion of test assets.

• Ensure pipeline failures are diagnosable and automated feedback remains stable, timely and valuable.




API & Integration Testing

• Define and lead API, service, contract, integration and data-validation approaches across Salesforce and connected enterprise systems.

• Validate REST and SOAP APIs, data exchange, business rules and end-to-end integration workflows.

• Use SQL and SOQL for backend validation and data-integrity checks.

• Determine where mocks, stubs, service virtualisation and synthetic data are required to provide reliable feedback.




Quality Engineering Leadership & Collaboration

• Act as the technical authority for Quality Engineering within the transformation.

• Lead the technical direction of Senior and Intermediate Quality Engineers and review framework, pipeline and automation changes.

• Coach engineers in automation architecture, Salesforce testing, API and integration quality, data validation and failure diagnosis.

• Collaborate with Developers, Salesforce Specialists, Solution Architects, DevOps Engineers, Product Owners, Business Analysts and programme stakeholders.

• Convert strategy into working frameworks, standards, reference implementations and measurable engineering capability.

• Communicate technical risks, trade-offs and recommendations clearly to technical and senior business stakeholders.




Essential Skills & Experience

To be successful in this role, you must have:

• Proven experience as a QE Technical Lead, Quality Engineering Architect, Automation Architect or hands-on Quality Engineering Technical Lead.

• Proven experience establishing a Quality Engineering and test automation capability from the ground up.

• Proven ownership of automation strategy, architecture, framework implementation and CI/CD integration.

• Significant experience establishing Quality Engineering and automation for a major Salesforce implementation or transformation.

• Strong Salesforce Quality Engineering experience across Lightning, configuration, custom development, APIs, integrations and data.

• Strong hands-on Copado experience within a Salesforce DevOps and CI/CD environment.

• Strong hands-on Playwright automation experience, preferably using C#/.NET.

• Experience with NUnit and Reqnroll/SpecFlow or equivalent BDD and test-runner tooling.

• Strong API, service, integration and data-layer automation experience.

• Strong SQL and SOQL capability for backend and data validation.

• Experience with Azure DevOps, Git, pull requests, code review, pipeline integration and automated quality gates.

• Experience designing frameworks for maintainability, scalability, isolation, synchronisation, parallel execution, test data and reporting.

• Experience technically leading Quality Engineers, Automation Engineers or SDETs and developing engineering capability through others.

• Excellent communication, stakeholder engagement, analytical and problem-solving skills.




Skills & Experience

The following would be beneficial:

• Experience in banking, corporate and investment banking, or a similarly regulated financial-services environment.

• Salesforce certifications such as Platform Administrator, Platform App Builder or Platform Developer.

• Copado DevOps certifications.

• Experience with Copado Robotic Testing or Provar.

• Knowledge of Salesforce DX, Apex and Lightning Web Components.

• Experience with service virtualisation, mocks, stubs and synthetic test data.

• Experience establishing Quality Engineering standards across multiple concurrent delivery streams.




Qualifications

A relevant qualification in Information Technology, Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related field is advantageous. Equivalent practical experience will also be considered. ISTQB Foundation certification or an equivalent recognised software testing certification is preferred.




What We're Looking For

We are looking for a technical leader who can establish the Quality Engineering foundations of a major Salesforce transformation, not simply manage test execution or maintain an existing automation suite.

You will thrive in this role if you:

• Can move from architecture and strategy into hands-on framework implementation and working reference solutions.

• Understand how Salesforce, Copado, APIs, integrations, data and CI/CD work together to provide continuous quality feedback.

• Can make clear decisions about what should be automated at UI, API, integration, component and data layers.

• Take ownership of complex technical challenges and make risks, constraints and trade-offs visible.

• Balance long-term engineering quality with practical delivery requirements.

• Build capability through standards, code review, pairing, coaching and deliberate delegation.

• Influence Developers, Architects, DevOps teams and senior stakeholders with credible technical judgement.

• Are comfortable working in a fast-paced transformation with concurrent workstreams and significant integration complexity.
