Job Description

Platform Lead (Europe ERP, D365/AX)

Role Summary

The Platform Lead will be part of the Sysco Europe Technology team, reporting directly to the Europe ERP Environment & Release Manager. This role is responsible for supporting the ERP platform by ensuring environment stability, coordinating technical activities, and driving operational excellence across deployments.

Key Responsibilities


Environment support: Manage ERP environments, ensuring readiness and stability for development, testing, and release activities.
Release coordination: Partner with the Environment & Release Manager to plan, track, and execute release activities across D365 environments.
Data refresh management: Coordinate and execute environment and database refreshes, including obfuscation where required, to ensure data integrity and readiness for testing and operational use.
Technical leadership: Provide guidance to development and integration teams, ensuring adherence to environment guidelines and best practices.
Collaboration: Work closely with functional, data, and integration teams to align environment usage with project requirements.
Roadmap execution: Support the creation and implementation of environment roadmaps, ensuring timely refreshes and environment availability.
Governance: Ensure compliance with environment governance standards and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives.


Skills & Experience


Strong knowledge of D365/AX ERP environments and release processes.
Hands‑on experience with environment provisioning, refreshes, and lifecycle management.
Proven ability to manage data refresh activities and coordinate across multiple teams.
Excellent communication and documentation skills.
Knowledge in Azure DevOps TFVC, GitHub repositories, and build & release pipelines.
