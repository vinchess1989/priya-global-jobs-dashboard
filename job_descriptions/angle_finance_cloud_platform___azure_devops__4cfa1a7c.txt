We’re hiring a Cloud Platform and Azure DevOps Engineer. This is a hands-on, mid-level role that supports the delivery, operation, and continuous improvement of Angle Finance’s applications, Azure cloud platform, infrastructure, and CI/CD services. 




Working closely with the engineering team, you will design, configure, test, deploy, and support secure, scalable solutions across Azure. You will work with .NET applications, infrastructure as code, automation, monitoring, networking, storage, backup, and disaster recovery services. 




You will contribute across the full technology lifecycle—maintaining and migrating applications, managing CI/CD pipelines, improving cloud infrastructure, resolving operational issues, and identifying opportunities to improve reliability, efficiency, and cost. The role may occasionally require work outside standard business hours to support planned changes, releases, or service restoration. 

Your role: 

Design, deploy, support, and monitor secure, scalable Azure platform systems and applications. 
Maintain Azure infrastructure, including networking, virtual machines, storage, backup, monitoring, and disaster recovery services. 
Implement and improve infrastructure as code using Terraform or Bicep. 
Develop and maintain reliable CI/CD pipelines for application and infrastructure releases. 
Troubleshoot platform and deployment issues and support development teams with cloud and DevOps tools and practices. 



What you bring: 

At least two years’ experience working with cloud technologies. 
Practical experience using Azure DevOps to build pipelines and deploy .NET applications to Azure. 
Experience configuring, operating, and troubleshooting .NET applications and Azure infrastructure. 
Working knowledge of infrastructure as code, cloud security, monitoring, and networking concepts. 
Clear communication skills, including the ability to explain technical and security concepts in business terms. 
Curiosity, collaboration, and a practical can-do attitude. 
Experience in Financial Services preferred.
