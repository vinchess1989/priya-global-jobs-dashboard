Job Summary

We are looking for a detail-oriented Product Documentation Engineer responsible for preparing, maintaining and managing technical documentation related to products, machinery and manufacturing equipment. The candidate will work closely with Engineering, Design, Production, Quality and Service teams to ensure accurate technical documents, drawings, manuals and product specifications are maintained throughout the product lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities


Prepare, develop and maintain technical documentation for products, machinery and engineering equipment.
Create and update product manuals, operation manuals, installation guides, maintenance manuals and troubleshooting documents.
Coordinate with Engineering and Design teams to understand product specifications, technical drawings, BOMs and assembly procedures.
Prepare technical datasheets, product specifications, assembly instructions and standard operating procedures (SOPs).
Review engineering drawings, CAD layouts and technical information to ensure accuracy and consistency in documentation.
Maintain product documentation records, revision history and document control systems.
Coordinate with Production and Quality teams to document manufacturing processes, inspection procedures and quality standards.
Ensure all documentation complies with company standards, technical requirements and applicable industry guidelines.
Support the preparation of technical documents for client presentations, product approvals, audits and project submissions.
Update documentation whenever there are product modifications, engineering changes or process improvements.
Maintain a structured digital repository of technical documents, drawings, manuals and product records.


Required Skills & Qualifications


Diploma / Bachelor's Degree in Mechanical Engineering, Production Engineering or a relevant technical discipline.
2–5 years of experience in technical documentation, product engineering or manufacturing documentation.
Proficiency in MS Office, particularly Word, Excel and PowerPoint.
Working knowledge of AutoCAD, SolidWorks or other CAD software.
Understanding of engineering drawings, BOMs, manufacturing processes and mechanical components.
Strong technical writing, documentation and coordination skills.
Excellent attention to detail and ability to maintain document accuracy.
Ability to understand technical concepts and convert them into clear, structured documentation.


Preferred Experience


Experience in manufacturing, industrial equipment, machinery or engineering companies.
Exposure to product development and engineering change management.
Knowledge of ISO documentation, quality standards and document control procedures.
Experience preparing operation, maintenance and installation manuals.


Key Competencies


Technical understanding and analytical skills.
Strong written and verbal communication.
Documentation accuracy and record management.
Cross-functional coordination.
Time management and attention to detail.
