Job Description - Medical Writer




Company: Sahajanand Laser Technology Ltd. ( SLTL Medical )

Department: Medical Division

Location: Gandhinagar, Gujarat




Job Summary




We are looking for a Medical Writer to prepare and maintain accurate medical, clinical, technical, and regulatory documentation for our medical-device products.




Key Responsibilities




Prepare and review medical, clinical, technical, and regulatory documents.
Conduct scientific literature searches and prepare literature reviews.
Support preparation of Clinical Evaluation Reports (CERs) and clinical documentation.
Prepare and review IFUs, product descriptions, clinical summaries, and technical documents.
Review and summarize scientific publications, clinical data, and research papers.
Coordinate with QA, RA, R&D, and Clinical teams for documentation requirements.
Ensure documents comply with applicable regulations and standards such as EU MDR, MDR 2017, and ISO 13485.
Maintain document control, references, revisions, and supporting records.
Assist in preparing documentation and responses for regulatory submissions and queries.
