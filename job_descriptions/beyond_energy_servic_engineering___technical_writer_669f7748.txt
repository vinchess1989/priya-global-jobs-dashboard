Job Description

Beyond is looking for a Technical Writer to join our company. As our Technical Writer, you'll create high-quality technical documentation for both internal and external audiences. You'll also assist in managing our document control processes, ensuring that all materials are accurate, up-to-date, and adhere to our high standards. Occasional travel is required to Nisku and Blackfalds.

Key Responsibilities


Develop and maintain user manuals, technical guides, and process documentation for our proprietary hardware and software solutions in both electronic and hardcopy forms
Collaborate with different teams to gather information and transform complex technical concepts into clear, concise content by meeting with and interviewing field staff, engineers, management and office and facility-based employees
Implement and maintain document control processes, including version control and content management using SharePoint
Create and update internal and external communication materials, including presentations and web content
Continuously improve existing documentation and processes to ensure content remains current, relevant, and useful
Ensure all documentation adheres to corporate style guides and best practices
Manage the organization and maintenance of our electronic document management system
Establish and maintain document files in accordance with records retention standards


Expert Proficiency With Technical Communication Tools, Including


Microsoft SharePoint
Microsoft Office Suite
Adobe Acrobat or equivalent


Required Qualifications


Bachelor's degree, college diploma with demonstrated writing and technical abilities, or equivalent education and related work experience.
5+ years of experience in technical writing, preferably in a technology or engineering environment
Proven ability to translate complex technical information into clear, user-friendly documentation
Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple projects and meet deadlines
Excellent interpersonal and communication skills
Self-motivated with a keen attention to detail
Experience with document control processes and systems


Preferred Qualifications


Experience in the Oil and Gas industry
Experience with Document Control and systems/software
Experience in using MadCap Flare software
Experience in using Canvas Envision


Who’s B.E.S.T?

Beyond Energy Services and Technology began in Canada in December 2015 and is a Managed Pressure Drilling (MPD) leader with a mission to provide sustainable, innovative, and best-in-class MPD solutions to our customers. Our diverse experience and focus on innovative and inventive technologies allow us to remain on top of our game and reduce our customer's non-productive time, save money, and ensure safe operations. Today, with headquarters in Calgary, Alberta, and in The Woodlands, Texas, our team comprises nearly 300 dedicated employees
