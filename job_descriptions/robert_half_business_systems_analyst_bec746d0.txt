The Company

Our client is a private equity investment firm with a long-term, focused approach and a strong presence across Asia's leading cities. It works closely with outstanding entrepreneurs to build and grow successful businesses, with a culture that rewards passion, initiative, curiosity and innovation, underpinned by high integrity and collaboration.

The Role

You will support the administration, enhancement and ongoing operation of business applications, working closely with business users, stakeholders and vendors to investigate issues and translate business needs into practical system solutions.

Provide day-to-day administration and support for enterprise business applications.
Investigate system and process issues and coordinate resolution with stakeholders and vendors.
Maintain configurations, reference data, workflows, approval structures and application settings.
Gather requirements and translate them into system configurations or improvement recommendations.
Prepare test scripts and coordinate system, regression and User Acceptance Testing.
Support go-live preparation, post-implementation validation and stabilisation.
Develop reports and dashboards, and perform data validation, reconciliation and migration activities.
Administer user accounts, roles and permissions, including joiner, mover and leaver changes and access reviews.
Prepare procedures, user guides and training materials, and support user onboarding and adoption.

Your Profile

Bachelor's degree in Information Systems, Business, Finance, Accounting or a related discipline.
Approximately 3-5 years of experience in business systems, enterprise applications, application support, business analysis or finance systems.
Experience with Yardi is preferred or another real estate/finance ERP, SAP Concur or similar procure-to-pay platforms, and SQL or Power BI would be advantageous.
Hands-on experience supporting an ERP or other enterprise business application in an operational environment.
Experience with system configuration, workflow-based applications, user access and permissions.
Experience preparing or executing system testing and User Acceptance Testing.
Strong Excel skills, with experience in reports, data extracts and data validation.
Strong written and verbal communication skills, including process and system documentation.
Analytical, detail-oriented and able to work independently across multiple priorities.
