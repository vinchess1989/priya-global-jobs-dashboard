WHO ARE WE?

The Australian Institute of Music (AIM) offers a range of externally accredited foundation, undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Music and related fields for more than 50 years. We seek to empower our students by encouraging them to embrace their skills and become the creative practitioners and professionals of the future.

WHAT'S THE ROLE?

The Business Applications & Data Manager leads AIM’s data quality, operational reporting and business application improvement. The role coordinates recurring reporting and compliance checks, reviews how platforms are used and prioritises improvements with business owners. The position works with departments across platforms such as Salesforce and RIO Education, Canvas, ActiveCampaign, Xero, Microsoft 365 and connected applications. It reviews data structures, workflows and integrations, agrees requirements and acceptance criteria, and coordinates delivery with the Systems Administrator and vendors. The role delivers the agreed Student Experience and Success (SES) data checks, reconciliations and reporting to meet internal and government reporting deadlines. It works with SES to confirm requirements, resolve data exceptions and allow time for review and sign-off.

WHAT ARE YOUR KEY DUTIES?


Allocate clear responsibilities for application reviews, requirements, implementation and acceptance, and coordinate delivery across the team. Manage allocated technology staff, setting priorities and supporting their development.
Plan the team’s work around reporting deadlines, data checks and operational IT commitments.
Investigate data and reporting issues and coordinate technical fixes with IT or vendors.
Review application suitability, configuration, use and data flows across Salesforce and RIO Education, Canvas, ActiveCampaign, Xero and connected platforms
Define required changes to data structures, workflows and validation rules, and coordinate their implementation with the Systems Administrator.
Document business application and data requirements with owners across admissions, enrolment, student services, learning, finance and other departments.
Lead business testing, verify outcomes and confirm readiness for deployment with the Systems Administrator.
Establish and maintain practical data quality controls with business owners, including validation, reconciliation and exception reporting.
Investigate incomplete, inconsistent, duplicate or incorrectly mapped records and coordinate their resolution.
Coordinate authorised data corrections with business owners and the Systems Administrator, and verify results.
Agree on recurring compliance checks and reporting requirements with SES and other business owners.
Maintain a calendar of internal and government reporting deadlines, with dates for checks, review and sign-off.
Run agreed checks and reconciliations early enough to investigate and correct errors.
Review integration performance and data flows, identify improvement needs, and define mapping, validation and reporting requirements for technical implementation.
Define exception monitoring and recovery requirements, review recurring failures and coordinate corrective action with the Systems Administrator.
Develop reporting queries, validation routines and analytical automation within approved tools.
Maintain business rules, test evidence and handover requirements for critical automations, and confirm that supporting technical operating instructions are documented.
Build and maintain recurring reports and dashboards for SES, Academic, Marketing, Finance, Operations and leadership, with agreed delivery dates.
Coordinate production data pipeline changes through the Systems Administrator or vendors.
Assess whether access, user permissions and privileged or connected-application access meet approved requirements, and coordinate remediation with the Systems Administrator.
Provide practical training, guidance and documentation for staff using core platforms and processes.
Work with departments to identify improvements that reduce manual effort, delays and recurring errors.


Knowledge, Skills & Experience


A tertiary qualification in Information Technology, Business Systems, Data or a related discipline, or an equivalent combination of relevant experience and training.
Demonstrated experience reviewing and improving business applications and data processes, with strong understanding of a student management system, CRM or comparable platform.
Experience managing a small technical team or leading technical service delivery, including prioritisation, coaching and escalation.
Ability to investigate data and reporting issues, assess application configuration and controls, and define practical improvements for technical implementation.
Experience extracting, validating and reconciling data using SQL or equivalent query tools, and developing reliable reports and dashboards in a BI tool.
Practical understanding of application workflows, integration mappings and automation, with experience defining requirements, testing outcomes and coordinating technical delivery.
Ability to translate business requirements into clear specifications, acceptance criteria, repeatable checks and reliable reporting.
Experience documenting systems and processes so they can be operated and supported by others.
Sound judgement concerning access, privacy, change management and escalation.
Ability to communicate clearly with technical staff, operational teams, department heads and senior leaders.
Working With Children Check.


KEY PERFORMANCE INIDICATORS


Operational reporting: agreed reports and dashboards are delivered to schedule, with delays flagged early.
Data quality: priority exceptions are resolved, recurring errors decrease and corrections remain traceable.
Compliance reporting: scheduled checks, reconciliations and submission data are completed accurately by agreed review dates. Evidence and approvals are retained and risks to reporting deadlines are escalated early.
Platform assurance: priority review findings are recorded, remediation is assigned and completion is verified against agreed requirements.
Improvement: delivered changes reduce manual effort, processing time or error rates against agreed baselines.
Team capability: responsibilities are clear, staff receive appropriate support and critical processes have documented backup coverage.
Change acceptance: business rules and acceptance criteria are documented, data and reporting outcomes are tested, and implemented changes are verified with the Systems Administrator and business owners.
