Role Overview

The Application Delivery Manager is responsible for managing the delivery of application projects from the development stage through to production go-live. This role focuses on hands-on delivery execution for individual projects, ensuring testing, release and launch activities are properly coordinated and completed to a high standard.




Key Responsibilities

Take ownership of assigned projects from the development phase through to go-live, ensuring timelines, scope and risks are actively managed.
Coordinate QA, testing and security assessment activities with relevant teams to ensure release readiness.
Oversee deployment and launch preparation, including environment readiness, monitoring and operational handover.
Identify delivery risks early and work with project stakeholders to resolve issues before they impact timelines.
Liaise with engineering, QA, business stakeholders and any external vendors involved in the project.
Provide regular, concise status updates to management and relevant stakeholders.




Requirements

At least 5 years of experience in technology project management or delivery management.
Demonstrated experience managing software or application projects through to successful release.
Solid understanding of QA, testing, security and deployment processes.
Strong stakeholder management, communication and organisational skills.
Experience working with vendor or outsourced delivery teams is advantageous.
Relevant certification such as PMP, Scrum or ITIL is a plus but not essential.
