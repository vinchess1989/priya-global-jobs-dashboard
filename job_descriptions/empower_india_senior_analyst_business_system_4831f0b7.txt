The Senior Business Systems Analyst serves as a critical link between business stakeholders and technical teams. This role is responsible for translating business needs into detailed, testable technical requirements for both user interface (UI) and API development work.

In addition to core business analysis responsibilities, this position performs hands-on quality assurance activities, including test case creation, execution, defect documentation, and validation of system functionality. The ideal candidate is highly detail-oriented, excels at clarifying ambiguity, and ensures that delivered solutions align precisely with documented requirements.

This role operates in a collaborative Agile environment and partners closely with product owners, developers, and QA to ensure successful delivery of high-quality software solutions.

What You Will Do


Partner with business stakeholders to gather, analyze, and document functional and non-functional requirements
Translate business needs into clear, structured user stories with detailed acceptance criteria
Create clear and succinct technical requirements for:
User Interface (UI) components, workflows, validations, and user interactions
API functionality, including request/response structures, data mappings, business rules, and integration requirements
Develop process flows, use cases, data mappings, and system documentation to support development efforts
Ensure requirements are complete, testable, and traceable through the development lifecycle
Facilitate story grooming with engineers and QAs
Review completed work to confirm alignment with documented requirements
Collaborate with QA to develop test strategies, test plans, and detailed test cases based on documented requirements
As needed, execute functional, integration, and regression testing
Validate UI behavior, data flows, and API responses against defined specifications
Perform data validation and basic query analysis using SQL to ensure accuracy and completeness
Support user acceptance testing (UAT) by preparing test scenarios and assisting business users

What You Will Bring


5+ years of experience as a Business Systems Analyst or in a hybrid BA/QA role
Demonstrated ability to translate business requirements into detailed technical specifications for both UI and API development
Hands-on experience writing and executing test cases
Strong understanding of REST APIs, data flows, and system integrations
Working knowledge of SQL and ability to validate and analyze data
Proficiency with Jira, Confluence, and requirements documentation tools
Strong analytical, documentation, and organizational skills, with excellent attention to detail
Excellent written and verbal communication skills


What Will Set You Apart


Bachelor’s degree in a related field preferred
Experience in financial services or other regulated environments
Comfort working closely with developers while maintaining an independent validation perspective
Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple concurrent workstreams
A proactive approach to identifying requirement gaps and edge cases
