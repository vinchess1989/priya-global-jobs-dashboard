We're looking for an experienced DevOps Engineer II to help drive secure, scalable, and reliable software delivery across our cloud and data platforms.

This role combines traditional DevOps engineering with deep expertise in Kubernetes, AWS, Infrastructure as Code, and DevSecOps practices. You'll play a key role in designing and operating production platforms, enabling secure cross-account access patterns, mentoring engineers, and supporting customer-facing products running at scale.

As a trusted technical specialist, you'll work closely with Engineering, Data, Analytics, and Machine Learning teams to deliver resilient platform capabilities that accelerate innovation while maintaining high standards of security, reliability, and operational excellence.
