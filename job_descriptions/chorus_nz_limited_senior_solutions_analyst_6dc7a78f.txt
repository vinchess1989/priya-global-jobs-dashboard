Job description:



Nau mai, haere mai. We’re Chorus. You may have already heard about us and know we’re the team empowering the digital future of Aotearoa with our ultra-fast fibre. We’re a talented bunch who are passionate about our purpose: Unleashing potential through connectivity. Enabling better futures for Aotearoa.  

 

Te Momo Tūranga | Contract:  Permanent, Full Time

Te Wāhi | Where:  Auckland

Te Pūtake | Why we’re hiring: As we move toward 80% fibre uptake by 2030, we’re entering a new phase - one defined by greater complexity, smarter systems, and a step change in how we deliver for our customers.

 

Te Whiwhinga Mahi | The Opportunity 

As a Senior Solutions Analyst, you’ll play a key role in turning business, customer and partner needs into practical, end-to-end solutions across Salesforce, digital channels and connected platforms. This is a hands-on role spanning discovery, functional design, prototyping and configuration, where you’ll work with business stakeholders, architects, developers and testers to explore options, make the most of existing platform capability and validate ideas early.

 

Working across everything from smaller enhancements to complex, cross-platform initiatives, you’ll bring together customer journeys, business processes, data, integrations and technology to shape solutions that are practical, valuable and fit for purpose. You’ll stay closely involved through design and delivery, helping resolve ambiguity, challenge assumptions and ensure the solution continues to address the original business need as it moves from idea through to implementation.

 

As a senior member of the squad, you’ll also have an opportunity to make a broader impact. You’ll share your expertise, help connect the dots across business and technology, and contribute to how the squad approaches solution design and problem solving. You’ll help grow capability across the team, including finding practical and responsible ways to use AI-assisted tools to work smarter and improve how solutions are shaped and delivered.

 

Ō Wheako, ō Pūkenga | Your Experience 

You’ll bring strong business analysis, functional consulting or product analysis experience, but importantly, you’ll be comfortable going beyond requirements gathering to shape solutions and maintain a clear line of sight from the original business problem through to delivery.

 

We’re looking for someone who brings:

 

·         Experience leading end-to-end solution definition across multiple platforms, working closely with business, architecture, platform and engineering teams.

·         Hands-on experience with Salesforce and digital platforms, with the ability to contribute to functional design, configuration and prototyping.

·         Strong discovery, facilitation and stakeholder influencing skills, with the confidence to challenge assumptions and bring different perspectives together.

·         Experience assessing solution options and making clear recommendations that balance customer and business value, platform fit, cost, complexity and risk.

·         A solid understanding of Agile delivery, including working across backlogs, testing, integrations, releases and operational handover.

·         The ability to understand and communicate processes, customer journeys, data flows, integrations and non-functional requirements.

·         An interest in using modern and AI-assisted tools to improve the quality and efficiency of your work, while applying sound judgement and human review

 

Mō mātou | About us 

We’re shaping a future where all New Zealanders are free to access opportunities that define their own lives, as we expand our network to help Aotearoa grow. 

 

With a new strategy in place and a powerful, inspiring purpose, Chorus is an exciting space right now. We’re on the path to moving from being the great network builder to being the great network operator. To do that, we embrace our mindsets: Be bold, ignite growth, Honour our past, reimagine our future, and Connect communities, empower people. We need people who not only embody these mindsets and the underpinning behaviours but who also inspire others to do the same. 

 

With our Chorus Care package benefits behind you, you’ll have the opportunity to learn and grow alongside a caring, open-minded team in an environment that embraces diversity, encourages innovation, redefines flexibility, and offers an enviable work life balance.
