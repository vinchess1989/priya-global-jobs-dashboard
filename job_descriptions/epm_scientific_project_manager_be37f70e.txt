Project Manager | Medical Device Manufacturing | 12-Month ContracT




Cork, Ireland

12-Month Contract

Predominantly Onsite (1 Day WFH per Week)




I'm currently supporting a leading medical device manufacturer that is looking for a Project Manager to join a manufacturing transformation programme.

This is a great opportunity to step into a highly visible role, managing equipment installation, validation and facility upgrade projects while working closely with Engineering, Operations and Quality teams.




What You'll Be Doing:

Managing multiple project workstreams simultaneously
Building and maintaining detailed project plans and schedules
Coordinating validation and qualification activities
Managing budgets, purchase orders and project spend
Leading stakeholder communication across Engineering, Operations, Quality and external suppliers
Organising project meetings and driving actions to completion
Presenting project updates to senior leadership teams
Coordinating external testing and laboratory activities
Ensuring contractors, materials and resources are available when required
Working with production teams to schedule downtime and minimise operational disruption




What We're Looking For:

Experience within medical devices, pharmaceuticals, diagnostics manufacturing environment
Strong project coordination and stakeholder management skills
Able to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
Experience working alongside engineering and manufacturing teams
Project management experience
