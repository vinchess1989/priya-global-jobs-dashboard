Why This Mission:

At CHANEL, we are dedicated to crafting an unparalleled luxury experience for our clients globally. Our Client Care team serves as a critical extension of this commitment, fostering relationships beyond the boutique and ensuring seamless, accessible service imbued with a personalized, human touch.

In an era of rapid digital and technological advancement, evolving client expectations, and the demand for effortless omni-channel interactions, Client Care has become an indispensable touchpoint. As the Client Care Program Manager, you will be the single point of business accountability for all Client Care digital applications and tools across North Asia and SEAA — today spanning case management, CCaaS/telephony, live chat, and AI-enabled tools, with scope designed to evolve as our technology landscape changes.

You own the Client Care business roadmap and requirements, and partner closely with Digital Solutions (Strategy & Governance, Digital and Data Partnership, Solution Factory, Data Enablement and Activation domains) to co-design, prioritize, and deliver technology solutions. You are not building the technology yourself — you are the voice that ensures what gets built actually serves Client Care's operational and strategic needs, and you drive the adoption that turns new capability into real impact.

Key to your success will be your ability to:

Platform Strategy & Roadmap



Own and evolve the end-to-end Client Care technology roadmap, ensuring platforms remain fit-for-purpose, scalable, and aligned to the regional Client Care strategy.
Act as the primary business owner and subject matter expert across all Client Care applications, representing Client Care's interests in all digital/tech projects and governance forums.
Translate business needs into clear requirements, user stories, and priorities that Digital Solutions can design and build against.
Work in close partnership and alignment with all Digital Solutions domains.



Product, Vendor & Budget Ownership



Manage vendor relationships, existing contracts, and user licensing across all Client Care platforms.
Own and prioritize the Client Care technology backlog across all 7 markets and all applications — establishing a structured intake, prioritization, and governance framework.
Own budget recommendations and tracking for platform investment, licensing, and project spend.



Operational Stability & Incident Oversight



Serve as the regional point of accountability for Client Care system performance and SLAs, coordinating with external support/vendor teams on incident resolution, root-cause analysis, and proactive communication to minimize disruption. (:30% of time)
Monitor platform health proactively, flagging risks before they become client-facing issues.



AI & Innovation Adoption



Identify, evaluate, and prioritize AI use cases relevant to Client Care based on business value and operational readiness.
Champion and drive adoption of new AI and platform capabilities across markets, ensuring advisors and managers are equipped, trained, and confident using new tools — adoption is the measure of success, not just go-live.



Enablement & Knowledge Transfer



Develop and maintain documentation (system flows, SOPs, access mappings) and ensure Client Care Advisors across markets and support teams have the knowledge and training to use all platforms effectively.
Support regional training and share best practices to drive consistent, high-quality platform utilization across markets.



The impact you can create at CHANEL



A resilient, future-proof Client Care technology ecosystem — built through disciplined roadmap ownership, structured backlog management, and close partnership with Digital Solutions.
Operational continuity clients never have to think about — by owning incident governance and SLA accountability across the region.
Measurable AI adoption — moving Client Care from near-zero AI usage today to a prioritized, business-led AI roadmap with real advisor adoption, not just pilots.
A trained, confident, platform-literate Client Care organization — across 7 markets and :120 advisors/managers, equipped to deliver the CHANEL standard of service through technology, not despite it.
A credible, trusted business voice in Digital Solutions governance — ensuring Client Care's needs shape the regional technology agenda rather than reacting to it.



You are energized by



Turning ambiguity into structure — organizing an unmanaged backlog into a disciplined, prioritized roadmap.
Influencing without direct authority — working across markets, Digital Solutions domains, and vendors to drive alignment and outcomes.
Being the translator between business needs and technical possibility.
Championing AI adoption in an organization just beginning its journey — you'd rather build the playbook than inherit one.
Developing deep fluency in Client Care operations across Fashion, WFJ, and Fragrance & Beauty.



What You Can Bring To The Team



Demonstrated experience owning or managing complex technology platforms — ideally CX/contact center, CRM, or case management systems (e.g., Salesforce Service Cloud, CCaaS platforms such as NICE CXone, Genesys, or similar).
Working knowledge of vendor and contract management, and comfort owning budget recommendations.
Strong analytical, requirements-gathering and stakeholder management skills — able to operate as both business analyst and business owner depending on the moment.
A practical understanding of AI capabilities in a CX context (copilots, agent-assist, conversational AI) and how to evaluate business value versus implementation complexity.
Comfort operating with :30% operational/incident accountability and :70% strategic/project focus — this is a build-and-run role, not purely strategic or purely operational.
Strategic thinking paired with hands-on execution — this role has no direct reports today and must personally drive outcomes.



What CHANEL Can Offer You



The opportunity to build a brand-new regional function from the ground up — defining how Client Care technology is governed, prioritized, and evolved across North Asia and SEAA.
Direct exposure to CHANEL's broader Digital Solutions organization and governance model.
A growth path: as the Client Care platform function matures, this role is positioned to expand in scope and potentially in team leadership.



At CHANEL, we are focused on creating an inclusive culture that nurtures personal growth, contributing to collective progress. We believe the uniqueness of each individual increases the diversity, complementarity and effectiveness of our teams. We strongly encourage your application, as we value the perspective, experience and potential you could bring to CHANEL.
