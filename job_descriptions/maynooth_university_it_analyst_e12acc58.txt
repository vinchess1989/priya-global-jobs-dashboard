Maynooth University is committed to a strategy in which the primary University goals of excellent research and scholarship and outstanding education are interlinked and equally valued.

Maynooth University wishes to appoint an IT Analyst on a full-time, permanent basis at Administrative Officer II grade. The post will report to the Business Systems Manager within the Enterprise Solutions area of IT Services.

The IT Analyst will work across a varied portfolio of functional and application support, system enhancement and development activities, working closely with colleagues across the University to understand requirements, resolve issues, improve existing services and support the effective delivery of new solutions.

The role will contribute to the ongoing development of the University’s student systems landscape, including business processes and system functionality, systems integration, data and reporting, and the adoption of new technologies where appropriate.
