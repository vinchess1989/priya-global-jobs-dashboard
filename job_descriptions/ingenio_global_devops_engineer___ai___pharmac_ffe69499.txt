Ingenio is delighted to be partnering with Biosimulytics, an innovative Irish technology company and UCD spin-out backed by national and European funding.




Biosimulytics is using cutting-edge AI and Crystal Structure Prediction technology to accelerate pharmaceutical research and drug development. Already working with leading global pharmaceutical companies and research institutes, they're now hiring a DevOps Engineer to build and deploy the ETL pipeline platform behind it all.




A taste of what you'll get stuck into:

Building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines for self-hosted runners.
Designing, deploying and running a self-hosted observability stack (metrics, logs and alerting).
Managing containerised workloads across distributed infrastructure.
Writing infrastructure-as-code to provision and manage services.
Collaborating directly with the CTO and R&D team on architecture and scalability.




What would make you a great fit:

3+ years' experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering or SRE.
Strong Linux fundamentals, including system management, shell scripting, permissions and networking.
Proficiency with containerisation (Docker, Podman) and orchestration (Docker Compose or Swarm).
Strong Python fundamentals, including data structures, algorithms, data modelling, package management and testing.
Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
A self-directed, problem-solving mindset, comfortable taking ownership and driving projects through to completion.




What's in it for you?

Salary up to €65,000, depending on experience.
10% annual bonus.
Participation in the company's Employee Share Option Plan (ESOP).
Company pension scheme.
Hybrid working – Dublin.
The opportunity to join an ambitious Irish technology company where your work will have a genuine impact on the future of pharmaceutical development.




If you're excited by the prospect of building technology from the ground up, taking real ownership and working at the intersection of AI, technology and pharmaceutical innovation, we'd love to hear from you.

Apply directly or reach out to Marie at Ingenio, for a confidential chat.
