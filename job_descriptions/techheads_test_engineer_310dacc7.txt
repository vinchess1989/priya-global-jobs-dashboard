Senior Test Automation Engineer – Medtech

Permanent ¦ Hybrid ¦ Limerick

€65,000 - €80,000 DOE + Excellent Benefits




We are recruiting an experienced Senior Software Test Engineer to join a leading global MedTech company based in Limerick, working on the development and testing of innovative medical device technologies.

This is an exciting opportunity to join a growing engineering team involved in new product development, embedded software testing, hardware/software integration and test automation.

We are particularly interested in engineers with strong C++ and Python experience, alongside a background in testing embedded systems, medical devices or complex hardware/software environments. You'll have the opportunity to take ownership of testing activities, develop automation frameworks and contribute to the verification and validation of new products.

You'll work closely with software and systems engineers, developing test systems, automation scripts and testing procedures to ensure products meet functional, quality and regulatory requirements




Key Responsibilities:

Develop, maintain and execute test plans, test cases and automated test scripts for medical device software.
Perform embedded software, firmware, hardware/software integration and system-level testing.
Design, develop and enhance test automation frameworks using C++ and Python.
Support testing of medical instrumentation, prototypes and connected devices.
Review software requirements, perform risk-based testing and ensure comprehensive test coverage.
Investigate software defects, troubleshoot complex system issues and collaborate with development teams to verify fixes.
Support software verification, validation and regulatory compliance activities.
Prepare and maintain technical documentation, including test plans, test reports, requirements traceability and validation evidence.
Contribute to continuous improvement of testing processes within an Agile engineering environment.




Requirements:

Level 8 degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Electronics or a related discipline.
Minimum 4 years' experience in software testing, ideally within MedTech, medical devices or another regulated engineering environment.
Strong hands-on C++ programming experience – essential.
Experience with Python scripting and test automation.
Experience in embedded software testing, system testing or hardware/software integration.
Experience developing, maintaining or improving automated testing frameworks and test scripts.
Familiarity with IEC 62304, IEC 80002-1 and/or IEC 80002-2 standards, including software lifecycle processes, risk management and validation within regulated medical device environments.
Strong understanding of software verification and validation, requirements traceability and technical test documentation.
Experience working in Agile/Scrum development environments.
Strong analytical, troubleshooting and problem-solving skills, with excellent attention to detail.




Why Consider This Opportunity?

New product development – work on innovative medical device technologies.
Technical ownership – contribute to automation frameworks, testing strategy and system validation.
Interesting engineering challenges – work across embedded software, instrumentation and connected devices.
Career development – join a growing engineering team within a global MedTech organisation.
Competitive salary and excellent benefits package.
