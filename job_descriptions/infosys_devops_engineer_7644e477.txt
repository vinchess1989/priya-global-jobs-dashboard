About the Role We are looking for an experienced DevOps Engineer to drive release management, deployment automation and continuous delivery for FLYR OOMS products. The role will automate build, test, deployment and infrastructure provisioning activities, manage CI/CD pipelines and collaborate with development and operations teams to deliver reliable releases with minimal downtime. Roles & Responsibilities


Automate deployment processes, build and manage CI/CD pipelines, and manage infrastructure as code.
Automate code builds, tests, deployments and infrastructure provisioning.
Plan, schedule and execute releases with development and operations teams.
Use Kubernetes and cloud-native technologies to support scalable delivery environments.
Manage CI/CD pipelines to accelerate software delivery and minimize downtime.
Provide troubleshooting and support for production issues.
Maintain technical documentation, estimates and knowledge sharing.
Work in a product-focused Scrum team against a product-owner-driven roadmap.
Stay current with microservices and cloud-native development practices. Required Qualifications
Bachelor degree in Computer Science, Engineering or related field.
5+ years of experience, including 3+ years as a DevOps Engineer.
Experience building CI/CD pipelines and working with Docker, Kubernetes and cloud-native technologies.
SQL/database proficiency plus understanding of REST APIs and microservices.
Experience with high-load/high-uptime production services, Agile/Scrum and QA processes.
Understanding of LLMs and agentic workflows within the SDLC. Preferred / Good to Have
