About Univers




Univers is the Platform for Physical AI, helping organizations orchestrate complex energy,

infrastructure and operational systems with intelligence that understands, predicts and acts in the

physical world.




Our AIP platform connects IT, OT and IoT data across enterprise systems, assets, sensors and edge

environments, creating a real-time operational foundation for monitoring, optimization,

automation and AI-driven action. Across energy, utilities, buildings, manufacturing, transportation

and logistics, Univers helps organizations improve performance, resilience, resource efficiency and

sustainability.




With more than 400 million connected sensors and devices, more than 1,005 GW of energy assets

under AI management and more than 800 customers globally, Univers operates one of the world’s

largest real-time intelligence platforms for physical infrastructure.

For more information, please visit https://univers.com/




Job Summary




We are seeking a highly capable and energized Product Release Manager (PMO) to help us build world class experiences for our customers. In this role you will have the opportunity to define and drive release processes that span across all aspects of the company. You will work closely with Development, Product and O&M teams to execute on the product release strategy. You will lead the release and launch activities related to our software product, and collaborate with leaders across the organization via exceptional written and verbal skills.




Key Responsibilities




· Drive quarterly feature release activities and monthly updates

· Define and drive best practices and consistency in the release process

· Ensure product releases are on schedule and meet quality standards

· Identify and manage dependencies across matrixed teams

· Communicate release progress status to the company and drive issues to closure

· Collaborate closely with O&M team to assure that our customer satisfaction is high




Basic Qualifications




· 3+ years of experience in software release management or equivalent

· Strong organizational and project management skills with exceptional attention to detail.

· Ability to identify risks, resolve dependencies, and navigate ambiguity in fast-paced environments.

· Experience with enterprise level software development management tools

· Excellent communication and presentation skills

· Excellent written and verbal communication skills

· Excellent cross-group/cross-discipline skills

· Industry experience delivering software services at scale

· Experience with agile methodologies and processes

· Experience in Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)

· Experience with and a passion for data-driven decision making




Preferred




· Familiarity with Jira and related project management tools.

· Experience with release management, deployment, or DevOps practices, including CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and feature flag management.

· Experience supporting client platform migrations, data migrations, or large-scale implementations in a SaaS environment.

· Familiarity with change management and incident management processes.
