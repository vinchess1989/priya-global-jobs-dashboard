About the role

FDM is a global business and technology consultancy seeking an Implementation & Migration Manager to work for our client within the financial services sector.

This is initially a 12-month fixed-term contract, based in Cheshire or Glasgow, with a hybrid working pattern requiring two days per week in the office.

Our client is undertaking a major migration and transformation programme and is looking for an experienced implementation leader to own the end-to-end implementation approach. You will bring together migration, technology, business operations and third-party teams, taking a lead role in implementation governance, command and control, go-live and transition into BAU

Responsibilities
Define and own the end-to-end implementation approach across migration, applications, infrastructure, business operations and service transition.
Design and lead command and control for dress rehearsals and live migration, including escalation routes, communications and go/no-go decision points.
Oversee integrated cutover planning and ensure readiness, acceptance and rollback criteria support safe implementation.
Manage implementation risks, issues and dependencies across multiple workstreams and third-party suppliers, providing clear updates to senior stakeholders.
Lead post-implementation activity including hypercare, service transition and handover into business as usual.
About You
Proven experience leading the implementation of large-scale technology, transformation or migration programmes.
Strong experience designing and running command and control for complex, high-risk go-live events.
Strong understanding of implementation governance, including change management, CAB, release management and service transition.
Confident operating with senior stakeholders and managing complex dependencies across business, technology and third-party suppliers.
Experience of data or platform migration within financial services would be highly desirable, particularly within life, pensions or protection.
