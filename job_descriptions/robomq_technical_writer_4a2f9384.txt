About RoboMQ

RoboMQ is a fast-growing SaaS company delivering disruptive Identity Governance and Administration (IGA) solutions to mid-market enterprise customers. Our flagship product, Hire2Retire, automates the employee identity lifecycle by integrating HR systems with Identity Management and other applications helping organizations achieve seamless onboarding, offboarding, compliance, and security with zero-trust and least-privilege security posture.




Hire2Retire is featured in Gartner Market Guides and has been a consistent high performer on G2 in user provisioning and IAM tools.




Role Overview

This role is focused on creating and maintaining clear, accurate documentation and technical content for RoboMQ’s products, with a primary focus on Hire2Retire. You will work closely with product managers and engineers to understand how features work in real customer environments and document them in a way that is practical and easy to use.

In addition to product documentation, you will contribute to whitepapers and solution-focused content like marketplace listings and product demo scripts. Much of our product docs content is maintained in GitHub, so comfort with version-controlled documentation is important.




What You’ll Be Responsible For

Product & Documentation Work

Write and maintain Hire2Retire documentation, including user guides, feature explanations, and configuration steps
Document end-to-end product workflows
Keep documentation aligned with product releases and changes by working closely with engineering and product teams
Review existing content and improve clarity, structure, and accuracy where needed

Whitepapers & Solution Content

Contribute to whitepapers, technical explainers, and solution briefs
Present technical concepts using a clear problem–solution approach that connects technology to business impact

GitHub & Collaboration

Create and update documentation stored in GitHub repositories using Markdown

Content Quality & Consistency

Maintain consistency in terminology, tone, and structure across all documentation
Ensure content is accurate, actionable, and easy to follow for both technical and semi-technical users
Identify gaps, outdated content, or unclear sections and propose improvements




What We’re Looking For

3+ years of experience as a Technical Writer in a SaaS, enterprise software, or B2B technology environment.

Proven experience writing:

Product documentation, demo video scripts and user guides
Technical or solution-focused long-form content (whitepapers, explainers)
Ability to understand technical systems such as APIs, integrations, and workflows, and document them clearly
Hands-on experience working with GitHub for documentation (Markdown, pull requests, version history)
Strong written communication skills and attention to detail
Comfortable working directly with product and engineering teams




Nice to Have

Familiarity with identity management, identity governance, or access management concepts
Experience documenting integrations involving HR systems, directories (Active Directory / Entra), or SaaS applications
Exposure to release documentation or change logs




Why Join RoboMQ

Work on a product that solves real operational and security challenges for enterprises.
High-impact role with ownership over core product documentation and technical content.
Direct collaboration with product and engineering teams.
Opportunity to influence how customers understand and use Hire2Retire.
