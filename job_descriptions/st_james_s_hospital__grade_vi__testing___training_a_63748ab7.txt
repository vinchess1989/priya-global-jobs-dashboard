Role Title: Grade VI: Testing & Training Analyst

Job Reference Number: 002817

Closing Date: Sunday, 22nd November 2026




Purpose of the role:

The Testing & Training Analyst will be a member of the Digital Health team responsible for developing and maintaining high-quality PAS (Patient Administration System) /EPR (Electronic Patient Record) e-learning and training content, delivering training and for coordinating structured testing across the PAS/EPR and its downstream systems.




Required:

Have not less than three years satisfactory experience in a in a relevant or transferable role.
Possess sufficient technical, analytical and administrative capability to discharge the functions of the grade.




And




Experience planning, coordinating, executing or documenting testing for an enterprise application, PAS, EPR or comparable information system.
Experience creating e-learning, user guidance, structured training content or instructional materials.




Desired:

Experience of Oracle Health PAS/EPR or another large-scale patient administration platform.
