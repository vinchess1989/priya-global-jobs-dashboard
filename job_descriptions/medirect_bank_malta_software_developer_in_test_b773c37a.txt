MeDirect is a Maltese digital bank, founded in 2004, with over 180,000 clients and operations in Malta and Belgium. We offer a wide range of financial services to both retail and corporate clients, including daily banking, savings, and corporate services.




We are always on the lookout for driven and creative individuals who are eager to grow their career while helping us deliver cutting-edge digital banking experiences. At MeDirect, we encourage collaboration, innovation, and continuous learning, ensuring our people thrive in a dynamic environment.




Role Overview:

We are seeking a talented Software Developer in Test to join our Development Team and play a key role in ensuring the reliability, performance, and quality of the bank’s digital solutions.




This position offers the opportunity to work with modern testing tools and methodologies in an agile environment, contributing to the delivery of scalable, high-quality, and innovative digital products shaping the future of banking services.




Duties and responsibilities

Maintain and continuously build upon and improve our automation testing frameworks
Define the automation roadmap and collaborate with the team to address areas of improvement or coverage
Develop internal tools to facilitate the
duties and work performed by the QA Team
Work with the developers to ensure component level tests are being implemented and reviewed correctly
Integrate automated tests with our CI/CD pipelines to execute both on demand and
nightly tests
Design test plans and develop automated tests to expand on the existing test repository
Identify automation gaps, plan, and delegate work to the QA Automation Engineers
Implement reporting and necessary integrations to translate test results into business readable form
Highlight, develop and execute performance tests as necessary
Monitor test executions, review test run results, and propose improvements in a timely manner
Contribute to the team’s roadmap tasks to upkeep the quality, reputability, and reliability of our products




Required knowledge, skills and experience:

University degree in Computer Science or relevant field.
At least 3 years of experience in a software development or automation role
At least 2 years of experience in QA or similar roles Industry knowledge of using API testing clients such as Postman, Jmeter, Bruno or SoapUI
Working experience with OOP development languages such as C# or Java
Using code repository and revisioning tools such as Git or SVN Scripting languages such as JS/Typescript/Groovyscript
Ability to navigate through service/API logs and perform troubleshooting and root cause analysis
Strong communication skills and approachable personality
Knowledge of trading, financial instruments, and/or banking experience will be considered a major asset
Experience with issue tracking tools such as Jira and test tracking tools such as Testrail or Xray
Working with databases such as MSSQL, PostGres or OraDB
Experience with REST/XML micro-service and event driven architectures
Experience
using CI/CD platforms such as GoCD, Jenkins, Github/Gitlab actions
