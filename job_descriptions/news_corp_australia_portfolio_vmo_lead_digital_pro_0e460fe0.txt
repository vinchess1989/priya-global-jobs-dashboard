What's the role?

As the Portfolio VMO Lead for Digital Products, you will drive operational excellence across our digital product strategy, bridging the gap between high-level business priorities and sequenced, executable roadmaps.

This is a newly created, highly hands-on role where you will step into a fast-paced, evolving operating model to create clarity out of complexity. Reporting to the General Manager Value Management Office (VMO) and partnering closely with the Director of Digital Product, your core purpose is to drive operational excellence and ensure the delivery of critical business outcomes. You will collaborate heavily with Product Heads, Engineering Squads, Managing Directors, and the Technology Leadership Team. From orchestrating cross-tier dependencies to facilitating publishing forums, you will be instrumental in breaking down bottlenecks and delivering on our most critical digital initiatives.

Responsibilities


Translate high-level business and digital priorities into structured, executable delivery roadmaps with clear ownership, capacity models, and milestones.
Lead operational ‘fast-forward’ pathways to rapidly mobilise urgent digital initiatives without compromising technical stability or release quality.
Actively hunt down bottlenecks and resolve roadblocks to improve continuous delivery flow and squad throughput, ensuring delivery readiness and appropriate resource mix.
Lead and facilitate high-level governance steering bodies and publishing forums with Managing Directors, ensuring clear inputs and transparent decision-making.
Orchestrate complex cross-tier dependencies across Application, Digital Capabilities, Enterprise Data & AI, and third-party vendors.
Author concise, data-backed executive publishing packs and dashboards to surface delivery risks, capacity conflicts, and necessary trade-off decisions early.


Who are you?


You have an extensive background driving technology operations, portfolio delivery, or value-stream execution, ideally within large, complex digital or media publishing organisations.
You possess a demonstrated ability to translate complex strategic goals into executable roadmaps, complete with realistic dependencies, capacity models, and delivery sequences.
You are a proven strategic partner and influencer, with a track record of successfully guiding and collaborating with General Managers, Product Heads, and Directors.
You thrive on delivery agility, bringing experience in improving processes to take products and features live quickly under compressed timelines. Experience working within a Scaled Agile environment is highly advantageous.
You bring strong commercial and technology acumen, including a deep understanding of software lifecycles, platform architecture limitations, and vendor partner dynamics.
You are highly proactive in risk management, able to identify downstream impacts and capacity conflicts and financial constraints early to provide structured impact analyses and recommended actions.
Applications close Tuesday, 17th November.
