Job Description

We are looking for a Senior Software Quality Engineer to lead quality engineering practices across multiple delivery teams. This role is responsible for driving an automation-first testing strategy, ensuring quality throughout the Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC), and providing technical leadership and mentorship to Quality Engineers.

You will work closely with Development, Architecture, and Business teams to define effective testing approaches, improve quality standards, and deliver high-quality software solutions.

Key responsibilities will include amongst others the following:

Quality Assurance Strategy & Leadership


Lead quality assurance initiatives across delivery teams, defining and implementing testing frameworks, standards, governance processes, and quality metrics to ensure consistent delivery of high-quality software solutions.


Quality Assurance Testing


Design, execute, and oversee functional, integration, system, and end-to-end testing activities. Validate complex business and technical solutions and provide recommendations to improve software quality and reliability.


Test Automation Engineering


Design, develop, and maintain scalable automation frameworks across front-end, back-end, API, and integration layers. Drive automation adoption and ensure automated testing is integrated into the delivery pipeline.


Testing Design & Quality Engineering


Collaborate with business, development, and architecture teams to ensure requirements are testable and aligned to quality objectives. Develop testing approaches, methodologies, and frameworks that improve test coverage and delivery outcomes.


Performance Engineering


Design and execute performance testing strategies using industry-standard tools. Analyse system performance, identify bottlenecks, and provide recommendations to improve scalability, resilience, and customer experience.


Security Testing


Drive security testing practices across applications by leveraging security testing tools and methodologies. Identify vulnerabilities, assess risks, and support remediation efforts to ensure secure software delivery.


CI/CD & DevOps Quality Enablement


Support and oversee quality practices within Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery pipelines. Work with engineering teams to embed automated quality checks and testing within DevOps processes and delivery workflows.


Application Quality Improvement


Monitor software quality trends, defect patterns, test effectiveness, and delivery health. Identify opportunities for improvement and implement initiatives that enhance product quality, efficiency, and engineering maturity.


Continuous Improvement


Review existing quality engineering practices and introduce innovative approaches, tools, and methodologies that drive efficiency, increase automation coverage, and improve overall software quality outcomes.


Stakeholder Engagement & Collaboration


Partner with business stakeholders, product owners, architects, developers, and technology leaders to ensure quality objectives are understood, aligned, and achieved throughout the SDLC.


Mentorship & Capability Development


Coach and mentor Junior and Intermediate Software Quality Engineers, providing technical guidance, skills development, and quality leadership to strengthen team capability and performance.


Documentation


Create and maintain high-quality technical documentation, test strategies, automation standards, quality metrics, testing frameworks, and operational procedures.


Qualifications and Experience required:


NQF Level 7 - Degree, Advance Diploma or Postgraduate Certificate or equivalent.
8+ years' experience in Software Testing and Quality Engineering.
Minimum 3 years' experience in a technical leadership, senior, or mentoring role.
Strong experience across functional, integration, API, automation, performance, and security testing disciplines.
Expertise in programming languages such as Java, JavaScript, or Python.
Extensive experience designing and implementing test automation frameworks across frontend and backend platforms.
Strong SQL skills and experience with test data management practices.
Experience with API testing and automation tools.
Mastery of performance testing tools such as Apache JMeter or k6 and the ability to analyse system performance and scalability.
Proficiency with security testing tools such as OWASP ZAP and Burp Suite.
Extensive experience with CI/CD technologies including Jenkins, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps, or similar platforms.
Strong understanding of DevOps practices including Infrastructure as Code, Continuous Testing, containerisation, and deployment automation.
Experience working with Docker and Kubernetes environments.
Experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform.
Proven ability to influence quality practices across multiple teams and stakeholders.
Experience working within Agile, Scrum, and DevOps delivery environments.
Financial Services industry experience will be advantageous.
