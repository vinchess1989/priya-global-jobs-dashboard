Job Title: Manager, IT – Platform Delivery

Location: Bangalore - India

Manager, IT – DevOps

The mission of the DevOps Platform is to make it easy for teams to Build and Run high quality Digital Products. The Platform aims to provide capabilities that make it faster, cheaper and easier to build products and is undergoing a significant change as our business becomes more Digital.

As part of the Kraft Heinz DevSecOps Platform team you will ensure that our community of builders has access to outstanding tools, processes, support and enablement activities in support of the DevOps cycle.

The Platform Team currently maintains & supports a suite of over 20 tools & technologies, however following our Capable, Lean & Heatly strategy we expect to be very deliberate in where we consolidate solutions and make strategic investments.

Primary Responsibilities


You govern the DevSecOps platforms that enable Kraft Heinz Development Community to operate and monitor Digital Products
You define the services provided by the Platform (catalogue, availability, availability, configuration, release, etc.) and their lifecycle.
You are responsible for the service operations and for the performance definition, measurement and reporting.
You work with service partners to enable, maintain and evolve the capabilities and the related services as required to enable both the customer (Product) and Platform’s ambitions and Strategy
You manage delivery & support of the Platform’s capabilities to ensure scalable, secure and integrated services that Product owners can trust to build their digital Products
You execute the Platform governance of existing routines, controls and monitoring activities, and you transform and improve these routines to continue to drive improved performance
You manage the delivery of new features, integrations and capabilities to enable and accelerate value creation in combination with the Platform strategy and ambitions.
You work closely with the Global IT teams to ensure that each new IT initiative with up/downstream impacts on your Platform is coordinated and aligned with the efforts of the Platform support service teams.


Qualifications


Hands on experience of an Enterprise DevSecOps tooling with a preference for experience of Jira, GitHub, Azure DevOps & Confluence amongst others
Proven experience of Platform Management including:
Defining platform strategies
Scaling platforms in an internationally distributed environment
Implementing Platform Governance
Assessing and selecting Platform Technologies
In addition to Platform Management experience, proven experience wide ranging Technology experience is expected including:
Project scoping, management & delivery
Requirements analysis, definition & mapping
Solution and process design
Systems administration
Solution architecture
Proven experience scaling technologies across an international business
Experience of working in diverse global teams and as part of large matrix teams
Knowledge or experience gained from within the CPG industry would be an advantage
