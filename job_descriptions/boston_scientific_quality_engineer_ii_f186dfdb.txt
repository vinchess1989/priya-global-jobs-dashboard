Boston Scientific is seeking a highly motivated Quality Engineer II on a permanent basis to join our Cork team.

Job Purpose

The Quality Engineer will play a key role in supporting, maintaining and continuously improving the Quality Management System (QMS) to ensure effective quality processes, accurate documentation, training compliance and adherence to applicable regulatory requirements and company standards.

In this role, you will collaborate with cross-functional teams to drive quality excellence through corrective and preventive action (CAPA) activities, document control, change management, quality records management and continuous improvement initiatives. You will also provide day-to-day quality support, identify opportunities for process improvement and help strengthen a culture of quality, compliance and operational excellence across the site.

Key Responsibilities


Lead the implementation of assurances, documentation control, training support and CAPA systems designed to meet or exceed internal and external requirements.
Maintain effective control of pharmaceutical quality documentation within MasterControl.
Create, issue, revise, archive, and retrieve controlled documents in accordance with established procedures.
Support the administration and maintenance of employee training requirements.
Create and manage training roles and assignments within the applicable training system.
Support the induction and training requirements for new employees.
Review training items, CAPAs, and other quality-related training requirements.
Ensure MasterControl job codes and training plans are appropriately aligned.
Support the development and maintenance of training courses, job codes, examinations, and other competency assessment materials.
Manage and support the Quality events and CAPA process, including tracking actions through to timely completion.
Support periodic quality reviews and identify opportunities for improvement.
Support the investigation, documentation, and closure of quality issues and CAPAs.
Support periodic quality reviews and identify opportunities for improvement.
Participate in continuous improvement activities across Quality Systems.
Users of this printed copy must verify that this document is current.
Review and implement relevant global Quality System updates and assess their impact on site processes.


Education & Experience


Level 8 Hons Bachelor Degree (or higher) in Engineering, Science, Quality, or a related discipline.
Experience working within a regulated manufacturing, pharmaceutical, medical device, biotechnology, or similar environment would be advantageous.
Experience working with Pharmaceutical Quality Management Systems and quality documentation.
Knowledge of CAPA, change control, document control, training systems, and quality records.
Experience with electronic Quality Management Systems; MasterControl experience would be highly desirable.
