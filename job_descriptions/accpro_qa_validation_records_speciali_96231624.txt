QA Validation Records Specialist

Dundalk, Co. Louth

Salary Range €25-35ph (DoE)




We are working on an excellent opportunity for a QA Validation Records Specialist to join a leading pharmaceutical organisation in Dundalk.




This role will support a strategic quality assurance project focused on the reconciliation, digitisation and management of archived validation documentation, including the implementation of a new document archival system and an electronic True Copy process.




Key Responsibilities

Review, reconcile, organise and digitise archived validation documentation.
Support the implementation of a new electronic document archival system.
Facilitate the conversion of paper-based validation records into compliant Electronic True Copy records.
Assess validation documentation for completeness, criticality and retention requirements.
Collaborate with CQV, Validation, Quality Assurance and Document Control teams.
Ensure compliance with GMP, GDP, data integrity and site documentation procedures.
Support quality reporting and continuous improvement initiatives.




Requirements

Degree in Science, Engineering, Quality, or a related discipline, or equivalent relevant experience.
Experience working in a pharmaceutical, biotechnology or regulated quality environment.
Good understanding of GMP, GDP and data integrity principles.
Familiarity with validation lifecycle documentation and CQV processes.
Strong document control, records management and organisational skills.
Experience with electronic document management systems, archival or digitisation projects is advantageous.
Excellent communication skills and attention to detail.




Why Apply?




This is an opportunity to contribute to a key quality and compliance project within a leading pharmaceutical manufacturing environment, supporting the transition towards digital validation records and improved document management processes.
