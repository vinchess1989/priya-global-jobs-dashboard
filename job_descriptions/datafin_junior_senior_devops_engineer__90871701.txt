ENVIRONMENT:

A strong technical Junior-Senior DevOps Engineer is wanted to join the team of an innovative Software Solutions company in Cape [URL Removed] role will include the administration and support of Linux, Tomcat, Postgres, Oracle, Unix Scripting in Bash and Python and network management, including firewall and Linux iptables configuration. The ideal candidate should preferably possess a suitable Degree, but highly relevant experience will also be considered, and your tech tools must include Linux and Postgres. Administration and support of Linux, Tomcat, Postgres, Oracle.
