At RACQ, our purpose is to make a positive difference in the lives of Queenslanders. As a Technical Business Analyst, you'll play a critical role in bridging business needs and technology solutions, helping us deliver exceptional experiences for our members and employees. Working within a collaborative product-led environment, you will analyse complex challenges, identify opportunities for improvement and ensure technology initiatives deliver meaningful outcomes that support RACQ's strategic goals and values.

At RACQ, we’re good people doing good things, making a positive impact every day, with opportunities to learn, grow, and be more.

About The Role

As a Technical Business Analyst, you'll work within cross-functional product teams to understand business challenges, gather and analyse requirements, and support the delivery of technology solutions that create value for our members and business stakeholders. You'll play a key role in ensuring solutions are well-defined, tested and aligned to business needs.


Partner with business and technology stakeholders to understand, analyse and document business requirements.
Facilitate workshops, interviews and discovery sessions to identify business needs and improvement opportunities.
Translate complex business requirements into clear user stories, functional specifications and acceptance criteria.
Analyse business processes, systems and data flows to identify opportunities for efficiency and innovation.
Support solution design, delivery and implementation by providing analysis and requirements expertise throughout the product lifecycle.
Develop and execute test scenarios, coordinate user acceptance testing and validate solutions against business outcomes.
Maintain traceability between business objectives, requirements and delivered solutions.
Contribute to continuous improvement initiatives that enhance customer experiences, business processes and operational performance.


About You


Demonstrated experience in business analysis, systems analysis and requirements management within complex environments.
Strong ability to elicit, document and manage business, functional and technical requirements.
Experience analysing business processes and identifying opportunities for process improvement and operational efficiency.
Well-developed analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to assess complex business and technology challenges.
Excellent stakeholder engagement, facilitation and communication skills, with the ability to build consensus across diverse groups.
Experience working in Agile, product-led or technology delivery environments, with a focus on delivering business value
