Black Duck Software, Inc. helps organizations build secure, high-quality software, minimizing risks while maximizing speed and productivity. Black Duck, a recognized pioneer in application security, provides SAST, SCA, and DAST solutions that enable teams to quickly find and fix vulnerabilities and defects in proprietary code, open source components, and application behavior. With a combination of industry-leading tools, services, and expertise, only Black Duck helps organizations maximize security and quality in DevSecOps and throughout the software development life cycle.

DevOps Engineer will be a key member of the Cloud Operations team responsible for developing SaaS and Hosted services across the company's product portfolio. They will work closely with Engineering, Product, IT, and other functional teams to build, operate, and improve customer-facing infrastructure on Google Cloud Platform (GCP).

The ideal candidate brings hands-on cloud and infrastructure experience, enjoys solving operational challenges, thrives in a collaborative environment, actively leverages AI tools to improve productivity, and is passionate about delivering secure, reliable, and scalable services for enterprise customers worldwide.

Responsibilities


Contribute to infrastructure automation using infrastructure-as-code, CI/CD, and GitOps practices
Build, maintain, and improve secure, reliable, and highly available infrastructure for SaaS and Hosted services on GCP
Leverage and develop AI tools to accelerate troubleshooting, automate routine tasks, generate scripts, and improve documentation quality
Collaborate with engineering teams to deploy and operate containerized applications using Kubernetes-based platforms
Build and improve operational tooling, monitoring, runbooks, and documentation to enhance service reliability and reduce operational effort
Participate in change management, release activities, operational readiness reviews, and on-call support processes
Work with Engineering, Product, Services, Security, and other departments to support business and customer requirements
Contribute to platform engineering initiatives that improve developer productivity, service reliability, security, and operational efficiency
Monitor platform health, investigate incidents, troubleshoot production issues, and participate in continuous service improvement efforts


Required Qualifications


3-5 years of experience supporting production SaaS applications, cloud infrastructure, or customer-facing services
Hands-on experience with Google Cloud Platform (GCP), including services such as GKE, Cloud Run, Cloud Storage, Networking, IAM, Monitoring, and Logging
Experience working with Kubernetes and containerized applications
Comfortable using AI and LLM tools (GitHub Copilot, ChatGPT, Claude, or similar) as part of everyday engineering workflows. Experience with Agentic development a plus.
Experience with Docker and container-based deployment practices
Experience with CI/CD tools such as GitHub Actions, Jenkins, ArgoCD, GitLab CI, or similar technologies
Experience with infrastructure-as-code tools such as Terraform or equivalent
Proficiency with scripting or automation using Python, Bash, Go, or similar languages
Understanding of networking fundamentals including DNS, TLS, routing, firewall concepts, and load balancing
Familiarity with observability and monitoring tools used to operate production systems
Experience troubleshooting applications and infrastructure in production environments
Familiarity with Java-based applications and services is beneficial
Strong communication and collaboration skills with the ability to work effectively across technical and non-technical teams


Education


Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or other technical discipline, or equivalent experience.
