Could you be the next TTC Global quality engineer?

At TTC Global we help organisations release software with confidence. 2027 is shaping up to be a big year for us. We have a strong pipeline of exciting new projects, and we will be growing our team to deliver them.

When we scope a new role, the first place we look is our Expression of Interest talent pool. Whether you are just starting out in testing or lead quality programmes for a living, registering your interest now puts you at the front of the queue.

Why TTC Global


Be part of AI-First Quality Engineering. Our approach rests on one principle: AI generates. Humans govern. You will work across our four governed workflows (functional, automation, performance and reporting), using AI to speed up the work while you own the quality decisions that matter to our clients.
Keep learning, always. Through TTC Elevate, our AI literacy programme, you progress through Foundations, Practitioner and Innovator badges. Our consultants are certified AI Practitioners on a continuous learning path, so your skills stay current. This sits alongside industry certifications such as ISTQB and Tosca.
Variety you will not find in-house. Our clients span banking and financial services, utilities, education, local government, agriculture and more. You will work with SaaS, ERP, CRM and custom-built applications, in code-based automation such as Playwright and on low-code platforms such as Tosca and UiPath.
A clear career pathway built around you. We have developed a clear career pathway at TTC, and we are always looking for new ways to support and develop our people. Whether you want to lead a team or become the go-to technical expert, there is a path for you. Our leadership and technical tracks both offer real progression, so you can grow your career in the direction that suits you best.
People who back you. Mentoring is built into every level at TTC. Senior consultants coach and support newer team members, and our teams share what they learn so everyone gets better together.
A global business with local teams. TTC Global operates across New Zealand, Australia, North America, the UK, India and the Middle East, giving you access to expertise, ideas and opportunities well beyond a single office.


Why professional services will elevate your career

In an in-house role you might test the same system for years. As a TTC consultant, you see how many different organisations build, release and support software. Each engagement brings a new domain, a new team and a new set of problems to solve, and you build breadth of experience much faster as a result.

You also grow skills that go beyond testing. You learn to work with business stakeholders, explain risk in plain language, influence decisions and earn a client's trust. These are the skills that move people into lead, principal and management roles.

And you never do it alone. Behind every consultant is the wider TTC team, our methodology and our own tools and accelerators, including aiDelta and the TTC Playwright Accelerator Framework. You bring your expertise to the client and TTC backs you with ours.

What We Look For In a Person

Skills can be taught. These qualities are what make someone thrive at TTC:


Curiosity and a love of learning. Software and AI move quickly, and we like to stay ahead, so there is always something new to learn.
An analytical mind. You enjoy pulling requirements apart, asking the awkward questions and spotting risk before it reaches production.
Clear communication. You can explain a technical issue to a developer and to a business owner and adjust your message for each.
A team-first attitude. You share what you know, support your colleagues and enjoy being part of something bigger.
Adaptability. You are comfortable moving between agile and traditional projects, and between clients, tools and priorities.
Ownership of quality. You use AI and automation with good judgement and take responsibility for the outcome.
Integrity and client focus. You do the right thing for the client and for TTC, and you build trust through the quality of your work.


How our talent pool works


Register your interest Share your CV, your experience level, your areas of interest and where you are based.
We review every submission and may reach out for an informal conversation about your experience and goals.
We look here first. When we scope a new role, our talent pool is the first place we search for the right person.
Stay in touch. You can update your details at any time as your experience grows.


Registering an Expression of Interest does not guarantee a role, and we will only contact you about opportunities that match your profile. Your details are stored securely and handled in line with privacy law.
