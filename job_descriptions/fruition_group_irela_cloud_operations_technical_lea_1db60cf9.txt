Cloud Operations Technical Lead




A top client and great hybrid work model and work life balance.




Job Description:

Our client, a leading Fintech organisation within the financial services sector, is seeking an experienced Cloud Operations Technical Lead to drive cloud strategy, operational excellence, and team leadership across their technology function. The client is one of the best employers to work for in Ireland.




Job Responsibilities

Lead and mentor a team of cloud engineers delivering scalable infrastructure solutions
Define and execute cloud strategies aligned with wider business and technology objectives
Ensure high availability, performance, and security across cloud environments
Collaborate with internal teams to integrate cloud services with existing systems and platforms
Act as a key point of contact between technical teams and business stakeholders, ensuring clear communication of priorities and delivery timelines
Support incident management processes, including participation in on-call activities
Drive optimisation initiatives to improve system performance and cost efficiency
Oversee build, release, and configuration management of production environments
Manage third-party vendors and cloud service providers
Promote continuous improvement across cloud operations, tooling, and delivery practices




Experience Required

Minimum of 7+ years’ experience in cloud engineering or infrastructure roles
Proven experience in a leadership or technical lead capacity
Strong hands-on experience with major cloud platforms such as AWS and Azure
Demonstrated experience implementing infrastructure-as-code solutions (e.g. Terraform)
Experience designing and maintaining CI/CD pipelines within modern DevOps environments
Strong understanding of cloud architecture, operational practices, and service management
Experience working in environments with defined SLAs/SLOs and service delivery expectations




Desirable Skills

Exposure to DevOps tooling and automation frameworks
Experience with monitoring, logging, and optimisation tools within cloud environments
Understanding of cloud cost management and optimisation strategies
Familiarity with cloud security principles and governance frameworks
Experience working within complex, enterprise-scale environments
Knowledge of Azure DevOps or similar platforms




Educational Requirements

Third-level degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline




Working Hours & Benefits

Competitive salary up to €100,000
Performance-related bonus
Pension contribution
Flexible and hybrid working options
Opportunities for career progression and professional development
