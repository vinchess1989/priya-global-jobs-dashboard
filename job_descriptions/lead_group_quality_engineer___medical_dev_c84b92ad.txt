Quality Engineer – Medical Devices

Sydney, NSW (Leichhardt) | Full-time | Flexible Working | Up to $150,000 + Super

An exciting opportunity for a Quality Engineer to join an innovative medical device company developing wearable technology designed to help improve outcomes for mothers and babies.

This is an opportunity to join a growing MedTech business where Quality plays a key role in product development, manufacturing and business growth.

Working closely with the Director of Quality and Regulatory, you'll help shape, implement and continuously improve the Quality Management System while collaborating with Engineering, Clinical, Manufacturing and Product teams.

The Opportunity You'll have the opportunity to contribute across the medical device lifecycle, from design and development through to manufacturing and post-market activities, while taking ownership of key quality initiatives and certification workstreams.

Key Responsibilities:


Represent Quality Assurance across new product development, design changes and design reviews, ensuring quality plans and documentation meet applicable requirements.
Support manufacturing quality activities, including process and software validation.
Lead and support site certification initiatives, internal and supplier audits, and external audit preparation and follow-up.
Manage nonconformances, product complaints and quality issues, including investigations, root cause analysis and CAPA implementation.
Develop, review and implement QMS procedures, work instructions and quality processes.
Manage supplier quality activities, including approved supplier lists, quality agreements and supplier performance assessments.
Support training, document control and quality record management.
Monitor, analyse and report quality metrics, identifying opportunities for continuous improvement.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify risks, improve processes and ensure product quality.
Depending on experience, contribute to regulatory submissions and responses to regulatory information requests.
About You To be successful in this role, you'll bring a solid foundation in medical device quality and the ability to work collaboratively across multidisciplinary teams.

A Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Science or a related discipline.
At least 4 years of experience in a similar Quality role within the medical device industry.
Demonstrated experience applying ISO 13485, 21 CFR Part 820 and MDSAP requirements.
Strong understanding of global regulatory requirements, including FDA and TGA.
Experience across Quality Management Systems, including design, manufacturing and post-market activities.
Exposure to medical devices incorporating software, electronics and mechanical components.
Strong knowledge and practical application of ISO 14971 risk management.
Quality audit training and practical auditing experience.
Experience preparing and maintaining audit-ready QMS documentation, design files, quality records and regulatory documentation.
Strong communication, problem-solving and organisational skills, with the ability to work independently and collaboratively.
Desirable Experience
Experience with wearable medical devices or maternal health products.
Prior experience implementing or improving a Quality Management System.
Familiarity with Machine Learning principles.
What's on Offer?
Salary up to $150,000 + Super.
Full-time opportunity with flexible working arrangements.
An opportunity to contribute to innovative medical technology with meaningful clinical impact.
A collaborative, cross-functional environment where you can take ownership and influence quality processes.
The opportunity to be part of a growing business and help shape its Quality systems and processes.
If you're passionate about medical device quality and want to be part of developing technology that makes a meaningful difference, we'd love to hear from you.
