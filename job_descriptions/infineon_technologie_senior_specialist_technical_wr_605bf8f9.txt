We're looking for a Senior Specialist Technical Writer with expertise in DITA XML, structured authoring, Python automation, and AI-enabled documentation workflows. If you enjoy creating high-quality documentation, driving content consistency, and leveraging technology to improve documentation processes, we'd love to connect with you.

Your role

Key responsibilities in your new role


Work closely with the development team to create user manuals, guides, and other documentation
Write clear, concise, and engaging content that meets the needs of the target audience
Edit and proofread documentation for accuracy, consistency, quality, and conformance to Infineon style conventions
Stay up to date with new features and updates to the tool and update documentation accordingly
Collaborate with product managers, engineers, and other stakeholders to understand technical information and product specifications.
Communicate effectively and collaborate well with stakeholders


Your profile

Qualifications And Skills To Help You Succeed


Educational qualifications
B.Tech/B.E/M.Tech in Electronics/Electrical/Computer Science with strong DITA XML background
Required qualifications
2-4 years of experience as a technical writer
Strong understanding of technical concepts and ability to communicate complex ideas in precise and simple language
Excellent writing, editing, and proof-reading skills
Ability to work independently and collaboratively as part of a team
Experience with documentation tools such as Oxygen XML
Strong attention to detail and ability to meet deadlines
