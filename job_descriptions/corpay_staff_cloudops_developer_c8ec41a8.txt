Your role

Corpay is looking to hire a Staff CloudOps Developer within our PayByPhone division. This position falls under our International Fleet line of business and is located in Vancouver, BC. As a Senior Cloud Operations Developer, you will collaborate with cross-functional teams to design, implement, and manage our infrastructure and deployment pipelines while providing technical guidance and mentorship to the team. Your expertise will be critical in ensuring our systems' reliability, scalability, and security. You are a self-starter with an innovative mindset that motivates you to keep up with the latest technologies and drives you to do great work, period. Your values include collaboration, curiosity, and a constant improvement mindset.

How We Work

As a Staff CloudOps Developer, You Will Be Expected To Work Out Of Our Office In Vancouver, BC. Corpay Will Set You Up For Success By Providing


Assigned workspace in the Vancouver, BC office
Company-issued equipment
Formal, hands-on training


What You'll Be Doing

The responsibilities of the role will include: 


Contributing to and driving large-scale technical and architecture discussions.
Collaborating with stakeholders such as development, security engineering, and compliance teams throughout the design, development, and operations lifecycle.
Providing technical support to developers who are using platform services.
Developing and maintaining infrastructure code, producing a high standard of well-factored and tested code
Building and managing CI/CD pipelines, constantly improve their reliability and speed, and reduce change lead time.
Improving our container (EKS/ECS)- based infrastructure to respond to company needs and demands for platform agility, security, and development teams' enablement.
Improving the team's development process, technical practices, and coding standards.
Developing tools to enhance and support Developer Productivity.
Championing automation of manual processes and reduce operational overhead
Making code review suggestions, responding constructively and learning from feedback
Participating in the on-call rotation to provide after-hours support to critical infrastructure.


Qualifications & Skills


7+ years' of industry experience where you have developed several significant feature sets and infrastructure automation projects from start to finish
Strong hands-on experience with a cloud provider, ideally AWS
Recent hands-on experience with EKS or Kubernetes for container orchestration, cluster management, and microservices deployment.
Recent experience with GitOps using FluxCD. ArgoCD.
Recent hands-on Experience with DevOps tools like VCS (GitHub, GitLab), Ansible, Artifactory, Synk, Infrastructure as Code (Terraform, Pulumi, Cloud Formation), etc.
Experience building CICD pipelines and with at least one programming language to deliver a production backend service
Strong fundamentals in Networking, infrastructure, Linux and Windows.
Life-long learner and self-starter, including the ability to quickly become productive in an unfamiliar technology or codebase.
Strong communication, interpersonal and collaboration skills, with experience working in cross-functional teams.
A keen interest and proven experience in improving team standards and process.
Mentoring experience in your area of expertise

Corpay is a global leader in business payments, laser focused on developing smarter ways for businesses to pay their expenses. Companies of all sizes, industries and geographies rely on our product portfolio to manage spending more quickly, efficiently and securely than ever before.
