DevOps Engineer
Network IT
I'm interested
Reference: ITA006509-Mel-1

Join a forward-thinking technology company at the forefront of IoT, cloud engineering, and intelligent automation.

This is an exciting opportunity for a DevOps Engineer to play a key role in building and supporting scalable cloud infrastructure that powers innovative solutions across industries such as renewable energy, telemetry, asset monitoring, and operational intelligence.

Working within a highly skilled engineering team, you will be responsible for maintaining secure, reliable, and high-performing cloud environments while driving automation and continuous improvement across the software development lifecycle.
Duties & Responsibilities
Requirements:
Bachelor's Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, or a related field
2-5 years' experience in a DevOps or Cloud Engineering role
Strong Linux administration skills
Experience with Bash, Python, or similar scripting languages
Hands-on experience with containerised environments
Exposure to CI/CD pipelines and automation tools including Terraform, Ansible, Jenkins, and GitLab CI/CD
Cloud platform experience across AWS, Azure, or GCP
Knowledge of infrastructure security and automation best practices
Experience with monitoring tools such as Prometheus and Grafana
Advantageous:
PostgreSQL and TimescaleDB experience
IoT, telemetry, or industrial technology exposure
Relevant cloud certifications
Quality management framework experience
Key Responsibilities:
Design, deploy, and maintain cloud infrastructure and services
Develop and improve CI/CD pipelines and deployment processes
Implement monitoring, alerting, and observability solutions
Ensure security, scalability, and high availability across environments
Troubleshoot and resolve production incidents
Collaborate with Developers, QA teams, and business stakeholders to improve delivery and operational performance
Why Apply?
Exposure to cutting-edge IoT and smart technology projects
Work on innovative cloud-based solutions with real-world impact
Be part of a collaborative engineering culture focused on continuous improvement
Opportunity to grow your skills across cloud, automation, security, and DevOps disciplines
Apply now to take the next step in your DevOps career.
