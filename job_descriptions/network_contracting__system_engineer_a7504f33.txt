SYSTEMS ENGINEER

Engineering | Make an impact through innovative systems engineering

Company and Job Description

An exciting opportunity is available for a Systems Engineer to contribute to the development, validation and delivery of complex engineering systems. Working within a multidisciplinary engineering environment, you will support systems engineering activities from requirements and design through testing, configuration and project delivery. This role offers the opportunity to work across hardware and software R&D and contribute to solutions where quality, reliability and safety are critical.

Key Responsibilities

Contribute to systems engineering activities, technical reviews and project milestones.
Analyse, manage and validate system requirements, architectures and designs; identify conflicts and assess proposed changes.
Conduct risk and cost/benefit assessments, support project planning and recommend mitigation measures.
Develop and maintain engineering documentation, test instructions, records and configuration traceability.
Support system testing, prototyping and modelling to validate requirements and designs.
Provide technical input to project teams, clients and contractors/suppliers, including reviewing deliverables.

Job Experience & Skills Required / Ideal Candidate Profile

Bachelor’s degree in Electronic or Computer Engineering (Master’s or doctoral qualification advantageous).
4–7 years’ experience in the electronic engineering field.
Hardware and software R&D experience is essential.
Knowledge of systems engineering processes, configuration management and formal qualification/certification procedures.
Experience with support ticketing systems and lifecycle management; familiarity with Atlassian Confluence and JIRA.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, written and verbal communication skills; able to work effectively in a multidisciplinary team.
Self-motivated, organised and flexible, with the ability to manage multiple priorities and willingness to travel.

Advantageous: Gas detection or collision warning/detection technologies, intrinsic safety, SCADA, instrumentation and control, mining/heavy engineering, SABS/MSHA/ATEx/ANZEx certification, Python, C, C++, ROS, PCB design or manufacturing.
