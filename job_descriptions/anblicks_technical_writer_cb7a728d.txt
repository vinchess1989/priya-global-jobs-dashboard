About the Role

Anblicks is looking for a proactive and enthusiastic Technical Writer who can transform complex technical concepts, product features, and workflows into clear, concise, accurate, and user-friendly documentation.

The ideal candidate will have strong experience in product documentation, a curiosity to learn new technologies, and the ability to independently explore products and collaborate with technical teams. Exposure to AI tools and emerging AI technologies is highly valued.




Key Responsibilities

Create and maintain high-quality product and technical documentation, including user manuals, product guides, SOPs, release notes, FAQs, and knowledge base articles.
Work closely with Product, Engineering, Data, AI, and other technical teams to understand products, features, and technical workflows.
Explore products and technologies independently and convert complex technical concepts into easy-to-understand documentation.
Research technical topics and ensure documentation is accurate, consistent, well-structured, and up to date.
Review and edit existing content for clarity, technical accuracy, grammar, and consistency.
Leverage AI tools responsibly for documentation research, summarization, content drafting, editing, quality improvement, and productivity.
Stay curious about emerging AI technologies and identify opportunities to apply them to modern documentation processes.

Required Skills & Experience

3+ years of experience in Technical Writing, Product Documentation, or a similar role.
Hands-on experience with documentation tools such as Confluence, SharePoint, Markdown, Microsoft Office Suite, or similar platforms.
Proven experience creating product documentation, user manuals, SOPs, release notes, and knowledge base articles.
Excellent written and verbal communication, editing, research, and information-structuring skills.
Ability to understand complex technical concepts and communicate them in a simple, clear, and user-friendly manner.
Strong attention to detail with a focus on documentation quality and accuracy.
Ability and enthusiasm to learn new technologies and explore products independently.
Experience using AI tools to improve documentation quality, content research, summarization, and productivity.

Good to Have

Basic understanding of Data Engineering, Data Warehousing, Cloud Platforms, APIs, or Analytics solutions.
Exposure to AI/ML, Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, or AI-powered products.
Familiarity with tools such as Jira, Git/GitHub, Swagger/OpenAPI, or similar platforms.
Experience working with Agile/Scrum and cross-functional technical teams.

Key Skills

Technical Writing, Product Documentation, User Manuals, SOPs, Release Notes, Knowledge Base, Confluence, SharePoint, Markdown, MS Office, AI Tools, Generative AI, Data Engineering, Data Warehousing, Cloud, APIs, Analytics
