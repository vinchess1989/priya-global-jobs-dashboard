A global, digitally enabled business that empowers a brighter future by connecting millions of people with their assets – safely, securely and responsibly. Through our two businesses MUFG Retirement Solutions and MUFG Corporate Markets, we partner with a diversified portfolio of global clients to provide robust, efficient and scalable services, purpose-built solutions and modern technology platforms that deliver world class outcomes and experiences. A member of MUFG, a global financial group, we help manage regulatory complexity, improve data management and connect people with their assets, through exceptional user experience that leverages the expertise of our people combined with scalable technology, digital connectivity and data insights.

Overview

The Manager, Solution Delivery Management is responsible for driving the end-to-end delivery of technology change for MUFG Retirement Solutions Hong Kong, including digital platform enhancements, regulatory initiatives, defect remediation, production fixes, releases and other approved technology changes.
The role provides clear delivery direction across participating business and technology teams and converts approved demand into controlled, technically deliverable and implementation-ready outcomes. Particular emphasis is placed on the Member Online Platform, Member mobile application and related interfaces and APIs, together with environment readiness, testing, deployment, release assurance and operational transition.
Operating within the MPMS Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) framework, the role applies practical digital and technical delivery knowledge to identify delivery risks, challenge assumptions, resolve cross-team constraints and ensure changes are safely implemented and transitioned into production support. Project management and business analysis disciplines are applied as enabling capabilities, while the primary accountability remains solution delivery, technical readiness and successful implementation outcomes.
Key Accountabilities and main responsibilities
Strategic Focus 

Provide clear delivery direction for assigned technology changes, ensuring priorities, intended outcomes and dependencies are understood across the organisation.
Maintain an integrated view of digital and technical change across the delivery pipeline and identify delivery risks, sequencing constraints and opportunities to improve platform outcomes.
Use release outcomes, production incidents, defects and stakeholder feedback to identify recurring themes and support continuous improvement of delivery quality, platform stability and user experience.
Ensure solution delivery remains aligned with business objectives, regulatory requirements, operational needs and enterprise technology standards.

Operational Management

Drive and govern the end-to-end solution delivery lifecycle for digital platform enhancements, regulatory changes, production fixes, releases and other approved technology changes, from approved demand through implementation and transition to production support.
Own the integrated delivery plan for assigned technology changes, covering scope, milestones, ownership, resource requirements, dependencies, risks, readiness criteria and implementation activities.
Act as the central delivery interface across business stakeholders, Business Application & Service Delivery, Application Development, Technology Assurance & Governance, Australia Technology teams, GCC resources, vendors and third-party providers.
Drive the delivery and ongoing enhancement of the Member Online Platform, Member mobile application and related interfaces and APIs, ensuring changes are technically deliverable, operationally supportable and aligned with intended customer and business outcomes.
Ensure business, technical and non-functional requirements are sufficiently clear, traceable and implementation-ready, working with relevant business analysis and technical resources to resolve gaps before delivery progresses.
Drive testing readiness across system, integration, regression and user acceptance testing, overseeing delivery-critical defects and ensuring evidence, entry and exit criteria, implementation plans and rollback arrangements are appropriately managed.
Lead delivery working groups and readiness reviews, apply technical delivery judgement to challenge assumptions, direct resolution or escalation of cross-team constraints, and ensure actions and decisions are progressed to closure.
Lead post-release verification and confirm implementation outcomes, including service handover, knowledge transfer, user guidance and post-implementation review activities.

Stakeholder Engagement 

Lead through influence across multidisciplinary and geographically dispersed teams, creating clarity on delivery outcomes, responsibilities, priorities and timelines.
Build a collaborative, accountable and delivery-focused culture across solution delivery participants.
Provide practical guidance and knowledge sharing to team members and delivery resources on solution delivery standards, readiness expectations and governance requirements.
Promote disciplined execution, proactive problem-solving and timely escalation while maintaining constructive stakeholder relationships.

Governance & Risk 

Ensure assigned changes comply with the MPMS SDLC framework, change management requirements, technology controls, audit expectations and applicable policies.
Identify, assess, document and escalate delivery, operational, technology, regulatory and third-party risks in a timely manner.
Validate that required governance approvals, testing evidence, quality controls, implementation prerequisites, rollback arrangements and support arrangements are complete before production deployment.
Support audit and assurance activity by maintaining complete, accurate and traceable delivery evidence.

The above list of key accountabilities is not an exhaustive list and may change from time-to-time based on business needs.
Experience & Personal Attributes

Minimum 5 years’ relevant experience in solution delivery, technology delivery, digital platform delivery or a related discipline, preferably within financial services or another regulated environment.
Demonstrated experience driving end-to-end technology change across business, application development, testing, infrastructure, service delivery and third-party teams.
Practical experience delivering or enhancing customer-facing web and mobile platforms, including digital journeys, interfaces, APIs, testing, release readiness and production transition.
Strong working knowledge of SDLC governance, change and release management, testing controls, operational readiness and service transition.
Ability to translate approved business requirements into clear delivery scope and end to end delivery plans..
Experience managing delivery risks, dependencies, priorities, stakeholder communications and governance artefacts across multiple concurrent initiatives.
Strong analytical, technical problem-solving, negotiation, influencing, written communication and presentation skills.
Demonstrated ability to lead through influence and create accountability across cross-functional teams without direct authority.
High attention to detail, sound judgement and resilience in a fast-paced delivery environment.
Working knowledge of JIRA, Confluence, Azure DevOps or comparable delivery management tools.
Relevant tertiary qualification in Information Technology, Business, Project Management or a related discipline. Relevant professional certification is desirable.
