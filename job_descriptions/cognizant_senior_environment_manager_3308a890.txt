Role Purpose

Owns planning, provisioning, governance, and stability of non-production and production environments supporting the integration landscape (APIs, messaging, ESB/iPaaS, B2B/EDI, event-driven platforms) — ensuring reliable, well-configured environments and test data for predictable, low-friction releases.

Key Responsibilities


Governance: Define environment strategy, topology, and lifecycle policies; maintain environment catalogue and enforce access/change controls.
Scheduling: Manage environment booking and demand, resolving conflicts across projects/releases; forecast capacity and flag risks.
Provisioning & Releases: Coordinate build/deployment of integration components; own go/no-go readiness checks (SIT, UAT, NFT, pre-prod); manage configuration drift and support cutover/hypercare.
Connectivity: Manage third-party/vendor connectivity, service virtualisation, certificates, keys, and network dependencies.
Test Data: Oversee test data provisioning, masking, and refresh in line with data protection requirements.
Monitoring & Incidents: Track environment health, triage outages, drive root cause analysis and permanent fixes; report on KPIs (availability, utilisation, deployment success).
Automation: Drive IaC/CI-CD-based provisioning automation and cost optimisation across cloud/on-prem.


Essential Skills


Enterprise integration/middleware environment management
API, messaging, event-driven, and B2B/EDI patterns
CI/CD and DevOps tooling (Azure DevOps, Jenkins, Terraform, Ansible)
Cloud platforms (Azure/AWS/GCP), networking, certs, secrets management
ITIL-aligned change/release/incident management
Strong stakeholder management and communication
