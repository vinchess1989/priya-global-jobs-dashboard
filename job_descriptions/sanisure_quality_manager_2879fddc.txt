About SaniSure

SaniSure is a high-growth multinational manufacturer of single-use products and systems used in the production of biological drugs, including cell therapies, gene therapies, and vaccines. With operations in the U.S. and Europe, we supply global pharmaceutical and biotech companies with the critical tools they need to develop and manufacture some of today’s most innovative and life-changing medicines.

As we continue to expand our product offerings and global footprint, SaniSure offers an exciting opportunity to join a nimble, customer-focused company at the forefront of the biotech industry.




Job Summary

As Quality Manager, you will lead the management and continuous improvement of our Plant Quality Systems. That includes customer and internal corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) and their effectiveness, customer specification management, internal audit, and testing and inspection processes. You will also provide training, leadership and professional development for your team.

You will report to the Chief Quality Officer.




Key Responsibilities

Ensure facilities, equipment, materials, organisation, processes, procedures and products align with ISO 9001, ISO 13485 and other applicable standards
Lead quality assurance processes across regions and product lines, ensuring products meet quality standards
Oversee the disposition of high-risk product nonconformances, ensuring thorough technical investigations and effective corrective actions to prevent recurrence
Develop, implement and maintain quality management systems and policies
Support internal and certification audits at SaniSure and its suppliers
Lead the Quality team to proactively identify quality signals and trends, and drive action to mitigate adverse trends
Train and mentor quality personnel on quality control processes and best practice, and ensure appropriate staff development
Ensure quality documentation follows industry standards, and stay up to date with changes in regulations and quality standards
Set and track department goals and objectives aligned with company goals
Identify opportunities for cost reduction and increased efficiency
Address employee concerns and resolve conflicts, working with Human Resources as needed
Collaborate cross-functionally across the SaniSure organisation so that Quality Management System processes are implemented, maintained and performed in line with established procedures and standards
Champion continuous improvement of the product QMS, identifying areas for improvement and ensuring remediation
Support the development and management of Quality Agreements, and supplier and third-party audits, together with Supply Chain
Ensure team members adhere to company policies and procedures, including PPE requirements and ISO standards
Perform other duties as reasonably required




Desired Experience & Competencies

Bachelor’s degree in Quality Management, Engineering or a related field
5+ years’ experience in quality management or a similar role
At least 3 years’ recent experience in the pharmaceutical, biopharma or medical device industry
Strong knowledge of quality assurance methodologies (e.g. Six Sigma) and of ISO 9001 and ISO 13485
Solid background in quality assurance, regulatory compliance and process improvement
Proven ability to lead and motivate a team, manage performance and resolve conflicts
Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret data, produce reports and make data-driven decisions
Excellent verbal and written communication skills
Proficiency in quality management software and tools
Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines
A proactive approach to identifying improvements and driving positive change

Preferred Qualifications

Master’s degree
Certification in Quality Management (e.g. CQE, CQM)
