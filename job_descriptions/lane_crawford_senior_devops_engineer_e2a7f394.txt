Lane Crawford is an internationally renowned luxury department store with over 175 years of success, delivering an exceptional and eclectic product curation with dynamic Asian spirit and values. As our Senior DevOps Engineer, you will drive cloud transformation, infrastructure reliability, and automation initiatives by designing scalable platforms, optimizing CI/CD processes, and ensuring secure, high-performing hybrid environments that support business growth.




The Role

Hybrid Infrastructure & Cloud Migration:
Manage, maintain, and optimize on-premises server infrastructure, data center/server room operations, and server virtualization systems.
Plan and execute seamless, risk-free migrations from on-premises environments to cloud platforms.
Oversee multi-cloud resource provisioning and architecture across Alibaba Cloud and Microsoft Azure.
CI/CD & Release Automation:
Architect, implement, and improve end-to-end continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD) pipelines to enhance software deployment delivery.
Automate infrastructure provisioning, configuration management, and backup management workflows.
Network Architecture & Management:
Maintain and optimize network configurations, routing, VPNs, and load balancing across hybrid cloud and on-premises environments.
Ensure high network uptime, security, and low-latency connectivity between data centers and cloud VPCs.
Monitoring, Observability & Incident Management:
Design, scale, and refine service monitoring, alerting, and logging systems to maintain high system performance and availability.
Lead technical troubleshooting and root cause analysis (RCA) for complex infrastructure issues and incidents.
Provide urgent support during off-hours as needed (with compensatory leave available).
Database Administration & Optimization:
Maintain, tune, and back up multi-engine enterprise databases, including Oracle DB, SQL Server, and MySQL.
Ensure high availability, performance tuning, storage allocation, and data recovery procedures.
Security & Compliance:
Implement security best practices across all infrastructure, networking, and cloud workloads.
Ensure data integrity through routine backup management, disaster recovery (DR) planning, and compliance audits.




The Ideal Candidate

Education & Experience:
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience).
3+ years of hands-on experience in DevOps, Infrastructure Engineering, or Systems Administration handling enterprise on-premise and cloud environments.
Technical Expertise:
Cloud Platforms: Hands-on experience with at least one (preferably multiple) major cloud providers: Alibaba Cloud, or Microsoft Azure
OS & Scripting: Expert proficiency in Linux operating systems (RedHat/CentOS/Ubuntu) and strong shell/Bash scripting abilities.
Containers & Networking: Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, HAProxy, NGINX, and load balancers.
Database Management: Proven hands-on maintenance experience with relational database management systems (Oracle DB, Microsoft SQL Server, MySQL).
CI/CD & Automation: Familiarity with CI/CD tools and Infrastructure-as-Code (e.g. Terraform, Ansible).
Server Room Operations: Hands-on experience with data center hardware, server room monitoring, virtualization platforms (vCenter/ESXi or Proxmox), and backup solutions.
Soft Skills & Mindset:
Excellent problem-solving capabilities with strong collaborative skills across cross-functional engineering teams.
Ability to take full ownership of projects while operating independently when needed.
Strong commitment to continuous learning, exploring new technologies, and promoting DevOps best practices.
