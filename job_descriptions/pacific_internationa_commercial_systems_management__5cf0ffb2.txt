Job Responsibilities

End-to-End Commercial Systems Portfolio Management


Act as principal business-side owner for assigned commercial systems and digital services.
Maintain an integrated view of users, business-process scope, ownership, vendors, interfaces, service performance, risks, enhancements and lifecycle status.
Prioritise interventions according to customer impact, business value, operational risk, compliance and strategic importance.
Exercise judgement within delegated authority and recommend material decisions to the Department Head or relevant governance body.


Commercial Systems RTB and Service Performance


Manage business-side RTB performance, incidents, recurring problems, service risks and cross-system dependencies.
Coordinate root-cause analysis and corrective action with ISD, vendors, Regional Offices, agencies and business owners.
Support controlled transition of changed solutions into sustainable RTB.


Business Requirements And Enhancement Management


Translate business needs into requirements, user stories, process flows, acceptance criteria and functional specifications.
Maintain and recommend enhancement priorities based on impact, value, risk, effort, architecture fit and dependencies.
Challenge unnecessary customisation and duplicate country solutions.


UAT, Release and Implementation Management


Coordinate test scenarios, UAT, defects, business sign-off, release readiness and post-release validation.
Coordinate data and integration readiness, communications, training, cutover, hypercare and stabilisation.
Ensure compliance with change, security, data-protection and governance requirements.


Rollout, Adoption And Benefits


Coordinate rollout and adoption across countries and agencies.
Develop or coordinate SOPs, training, communications and adoption plans.
Monitor usage, feedback, adoption barriers and benefits, securing corrective action where needed.


Vendor and Stakeholder Management


Coordinate vendors against scope, milestones, service levels, quality, risk and issue-resolution expectations.
Align agencies, Regional Offices, ANM departments, commercial functions and technology stakeholders.
Escalate unresolved vendor, service or delivery matters with evidence and recommendations.


Governance and Team Contribution


Maintain records of requirements, releases, risks, decisions, controls, benefits and lessons learned.
Share knowledge and support cross-portfolio work without diluting assigned portfolio ownership.


PIL Website And Customer-Facing Digital Experience


Where assigned, coordinate the PIL Website business-side roadmap, customer journeys, functional requirements, enhancement backlog, UAT, release readiness, analytics and continuous improvement.
Partner Corporate Communications on brand, editorial standards and corporate-content governance, and partner ISD or the appointed technical owner on architecture, hosting, cybersecurity and technical operations.
Use web and platform analytics, user feedback and service data to identify usability, content-discovery and self-service improvements.


Digital Solution Methods


Translate business and user needs into process maps, use cases, user stories and acceptance criteria.
Facilitate focused design workshops and coordinate proportionate prototypes or MVP validation where approved.
Assess low-code, automation and workflow options with ISD and Transformation Engine without creating unsupported local solutions.


Job Requirements


Tertiary education in Information Systems, Computer Science, Engineering, Business, Digital Transformation or equivalent.
Min 10 years of experience in commercial systems, digital products, business analysis, implementation, service management, vendor management and multi-country user adoption. Shipping, logistics or agency-network experience is strongly preferred
Experience in commercial systems and digital product portfolio management.
Strong understanding of business requirements gathering, process mapping, user stories and acceptance criteria.
Experience in Run-the-Business (RTB) service management, including incident/problem management and transition to operations.
Hands-on experience in UAT, system releases, implementation, user training and adoption/change management.
Experience managing vendors, service levels (SLAs), costs and key stakeholders.
Good understanding of APIs/system integrations, customer portals, CRM, case management and payment platforms.
Experience with customer journeys, website governance, web/product analytics and digital accessibility.
Able to facilitate structured workshops, solution assessments, prototyping/MVP validation and usability testing/feedback
Relevant certifications in Agile, ITIL, Product Management,
Project Management or Business Analysis would be advantageous.
