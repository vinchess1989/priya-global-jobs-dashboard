Milestone IT is seeking experienced Azure DevOps and Platform Engineers to help our client build its core Azure infrastructure as it transitions from AWS and Google Cloud.

These roles suit practical engineers who can personally design and build the cloud platform that application teams deploy onto. Strong Terraform skills and deep Azure networking experience are essential.

You will take responsibility for:
• Designing and implementing Azure foundations, including landing zones, subscriptions, governance and shared services.
• Building hybrid connectivity between on premises environments and Azure, including routing, firewalls, enterprise DNS, ExpressRoute, VPNs and private endpoints.
• Developing reusable Terraform modules to deliver consistent, repeatable infrastructure.
• Building and maintaining reliable CI/CD pipelines for infrastructure deployment.
• Designing resilient platforms with tested failover, disaster recovery and recovery procedures.
• Working with cloud, infrastructure and application teams to establish a secure, supportable Azure environment.

What you will bring
• Strong practical experience building enterprise Azure platforms.
• Advanced Terraform experience, including module development and infrastructure lifecycle management.
• Deep understanding of Azure networking and connectivity with on premises infrastructure.
• Experience making platform design decisions across security, governance, networking and resilience.
• Experience implementing automated infrastructure deployment pipelines.
• The ability to troubleshoot complex infrastructure issues and take ownership from design through to delivery.
• Clear communication skills and the ability to explain technical decisions to stakeholders.

Experience migrating workloads or services from AWS or Google Cloud to Azure will be highly regarded.

Help shape an Azure platform from its foundations, with substantial engineering responsibility and the opportunity to influence how infrastructure is built, deployed and operated.
