About the Role

We're looking for a DevOps and Infrastructure engineer to join our engineering team and help us build the reliability, observability and automation foundations behind our production platforms.

This is a hands-on role for someone who enjoys solving complex infrastructure problems, improving how systems operate in production, and automating the things that shouldn't need to be done manually.

What you'll do

Own observability and alerting across our production platforms — building and tuning monitoring, logging, metrics, traces and actionable alerts.
Deliver and own the Classic monitoring retrofit, including synthetic and heartbeat checks and log shipping, helping move the team from manual monitoring to proactive, alert-driven support.
Own infrastructure reliability, including cloud infrastructure, networking, environments, configuration, capacity, performance and resilience.
Support CI/CD and deployment, including safe release, rollback and incident-disable mechanisms.
Take a lead role in infrastructure incident response, diagnosing issues and restoring services during production incidents.
Participate in the primary on-call rotation and support P1 incidents alongside the Service Delivery Manager.
Build automation and tooling to reduce repetitive operational work and eliminate unnecessary toil.
Help implement and operate integrations across Jira Service Management, Confluence and Opsgenie.
Contribute to security and infrastructure hardening, including secrets management, patching and security escalation processes.
Create and maintain runbooks and help ensure monitoring, rollback and kill-switch capabilities are ready before systems go live.
What we're looking for


You'll ideally have 3+ years of experience in SRE, DevOps, Platform Engineering or Infrastructure Engineering, with a strong interest in reliability and automation.

You'll bring:

Strong experience with cloud infrastructure, CI/CD and Infrastructure as Code.
Hands-on experience with observability, including logging, metrics, tracing and alerting.
Strong scripting and automation skills — Python, Bash or SQL would be valuable.
Experience with production incident response and on-call environments.
A structured and methodical approach to troubleshooting complex infrastructure issues.
Experience introducing monitoring and observability into systems where it may not already exist.
A mindset of automate first, reduce toil and make systems easier to operate.
Nice to have

Experience with low-code platforms and integration/connector troubleshooting.
ITIL 4 Foundation or similar operational experience.
Security and infrastructure hardening experience.
