Lead L2/L3 application support and operations for business-critical software
Own incident management, production releases and service improvements
Drive technical team performance and collaborate with product engineering


We are seeking an experienced Product Operations Manager to lead L2/L3 application support, incident and problem management, and production software releases. Drive application stability, continuous improvement and operational excellence while leading a technical team and collaborating with Product and Engineering teams. Experience with Azure, OutSystems and ITIL is highly regarded.

Responsibilities


Leading and overseeing Level 2 and Level 3 application and product support across business-critical software environments.
Managing production incidents, escalations and complex application issues to maintain system availability and performance.
Driving Incident and Problem Management activities, including root cause analysis, permanent remediation and continuous improvement.
Coordinating and overseeing production software releases, ensuring deployment readiness, appropriate approvals and effective rollback or recovery procedures.
Monitoring application performance and identifying opportunities to improve system reliability, supportability and customer experience.
Collaborating with software development, product and platform engineering teams to investigate defects and implement sustainable solutions.
Establishing and maintaining effective operational processes, support arrangements and service management practices.
Leading, mentoring and developing technical support professionals to build a high-performing and collaborative team.
Working with stakeholders to ensure operational risks, service levels and compliance requirements are effectively managed.


Skills And Experience


7+ years’ experience in application support, product operations, technical operations or a related discipline, including at least 3 years in a leadership position.
Strong experience managing Level 2 and Level 3 support for customer-facing or business-critical applications.
Demonstrated expertise in Incident, Problem and Release Management, including major incidents and production issue resolution.
Experience coordinating software deployments, production changes and operational readiness activities.
Strong understanding of ITIL frameworks, service management principles and SLA-driven environments.
Experience supporting cloud-based and low-code application environments, ideally including Microsoft Azure and OutSystems.
Proven ability to work closely with software engineering and product delivery teams to improve application supportability.
Strong troubleshooting, stakeholder engagement and communication skills.
Demonstrated experience leading and developing technical teams.
