Role Overview:




Role: Senior DevOps Engineer (Azure)
Salary: £75,000-£85,000 (plus discretionary annual bonus of 7.5%)
Location: London Hybrid – 2 days per week in the office




The Opportunity:




Our client is an established, fast-growing MedTech business who specialise in advanced analytics and AI-led technology




As the business continues to grow and expand its technology platform, they're looking to appoint a Senior DevOps Engineer to take technical ownership of their cloud infrastructure, deployment pipelines and platform environments.




This is an opportunity to play a central role in improving the reliability, scalability, security and performance of a sophisticated, multi-tenant SaaS platform. You'll work closely with Development, IT, Business Solutions and Quality teams, driving infrastructure improvements, modernising deployment processes and helping shape the future of the platform.




They're looking for someone who can take ownership, challenge existing approaches and proactively identify opportunities to improve infrastructure, automation and engineering practices.




What We're Looking For:




Essential Experience:




Proven experience in a DevOps, Platform Engineering or Site Reliability Engineering role supporting production cloud workloads.
Strong hands-on experience with Microsoft Azure, including Azure Functions, App Service, networking, storage and Key Vault.
Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines, ideally using YAML pipelines within Azure DevOps.
Infrastructure as Code experience using ARM/Bicep and/or Terraform.
Experience working with containers and Kubernetes.
Scripting and automation experience using PowerShell, Bash or Python.
Strong understanding of Git workflows, branching strategies and release management.
Experience supporting production systems, including monitoring, troubleshooting and incident response.
Understanding of application architectures, including microservices, event-driven and queue-based systems.




What We're Looking For:




We're keen to speak with a technically strong DevOps Engineer who enjoys taking ownership of infrastructure and driving improvements rather than simply responding to issues as they arise.

You'll need to understand how cloud services fit together across a wider architecture, have a pragmatic approach to automation and be comfortable making decisions around cost, reliability, security and performance.




If you're looking for a role where you can take ownership, make improvements and have a real impact on the direction of a growing technology platform, we'd love to hear from you!
