Job Summary

NetApp is building a dedicated CI/CD organization to qualify and sustain the NetApp stack used in partnership with Google for their Google Distributed Cloud (GDC)project. GDC is a fully managed hardware and software solution that extends Google Cloud services to edge locations and on-premises data centers. GDC uses multiple NetApp technologies in this stack: ONTAP AFF, ONTAP Select, StorageGRID, and Trident.

The GDC CI/CD team owns how software is qualified, automated, released, patched, and kept current for Google before and after it leaves NetApp.

As a CI/CD Engineer , you will own major qualification and automation areas that span products, workloads, and teams. You will solve complex problems with substantial independence, define methods for new assignments, coordinate work across organizational boundaries, and mentor other engineers. Your work will directly shape the reliability and efficiency of GDC release qualification.

What You'll Do

CI/CD architecture and automation

Design and own major portions of the GDC qualification topology, including simulator-based continuous integration and dedicated hardware paths for head-of-line and patch releases.

Develop reusable automation frameworks, release gates, diagnostics, evidence packages, and recovery mechanisms.

Establish engineering standards for test reliability, maintainability, observability, and failure triage.

Identify systemic pipeline weaknesses and lead improvements that increase qualification speed without reducing coverage or confidence

Solution Acceptance Testing (SAT)

Translate Google-prescribed SAT requirements into maintainable automated coverage across file, block, object, and CSI control-plane workloads.

Lead interoperability and regression qualification across ONTAP, ONTAP Select, Trident, StorageGRID, firmware, and API changes.

Drive complex failures from initial signal through reproduction, isolation, defect ownership, fix validation, and closure.

Advise product and CI/CD peers on difficult qualification and testability issues.

GDC-representative environments

Lead integration of GDC-like and air-gapped qualification into CTL automation as long-lived, restricted testbeds.

Design reliable automation for disruptive hardware workflows such as cluster wipe, re-image, recreate, upgrade, and recovery.

Own qualification in one or more advanced areas such as bring-your-own-network, VXLAN/EVPN, or zero-day turn-up using pre-cluster REST, DHCP, and automated controller bring-up.

Release and operational leadership

Lead qualification for assigned head-of-line and patch streams and provide clear readiness assessments.

Coordinate defect triage and closure across CI/CD, product engineering, CTL, QEG, and support organizations.

Contribute technical leadership during incident collaboration, root-cause analysis, and urgent patch qualification.

Engage in incident management processes including the 24x7 on-call rotations (as per the sun model) to resolve production issues within agreed SLAs/SLOs.

Mentor engineers, improve team practices, and coordinate cross-functional execution without relying on management authority.

Qualifications

Required qualifications

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.

Typically, 8 or more years of relevant experience in software, systems, infrastructure, quality, or test-automation engineering.

Advanced programming or scripting ability in languages used for automation, such as Python, Go, PowerShell, or shell.

Deep hands-on experience with Linux, distributed systems, and automated build, test, or release pipelines.

Demonstrated ownership of complex qualification, CI/CD, site-reliability, or test-automation systems.

Strong troubleshooting skills across multiple components, with the ability to select and adapt methods when precedent is incomplete.

Experience coordinating cross-functional work, influencing adjacent teams, and mentoring engineers.

Engage in incident management processes including the 24x7 on-call rotations (as per the sun model) to resolve production issues within agreed SLAs/SLOs.

Preferred Qualifications

Experience with ONTAP, Trident, StorageGRID, ONTAP Select, or comparable enterprise storage and CSI stacks.

Kubernetes, container orchestration, and CSI qualification.

Jenkins, GitLab CI, GitHub Actions, or comparable CI/CD platforms at meaningful scale.

Terraform, Ansible, or equivalent infrastructure-automation tools.

Air-gapped, sovereign, or high-assurance environment qualification.

Network overlay experience, including VXLAN/EVPN and customer-provided networking models.

Experience leading acceptance-test programs, release qualification, or operational readiness for infrastructure software.

Experience working with hyperscaler, strategic partner, or customer engineering organizations.

Success in the first 12 months

You own a major GDC qualification area with reliable, maintainable coverage across simulator and hardware paths.

SAT, interoperability, and patch gates in your scope run predictably and provide trusted readiness evidence.

Complex cross-product failures are diagnosed faster because of the automation, observability, and working relationships you establish.

You independently resolve most blockers, anticipate major risks, and lead cross-functional mitigation.

Engineers around you deliver stronger designs and automation because of your technical guidance and mentoring.
