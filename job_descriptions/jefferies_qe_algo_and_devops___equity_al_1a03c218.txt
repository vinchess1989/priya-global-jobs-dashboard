Role Summary

JOB DESCRIPTION

We are seeking a highly motivated C++ DevOps, Tooling & Test Automation Engineer to

support our Equity Algorithmic Trading platform.

This role sits at the intersection of C++ engineering, DevOps, test automation, and platform reliability. You will build and enhance the engineering tooling, CI/CD pipelines, automated testing frameworks, simulation capabilities, observability, and deployment controls required to support a high-performance, mission-critical electronic trading environment.

You will partner closely with algorithmic trading developers, quantitative researchers, QA, production support, infrastructure teams, and trading stakeholders to improve software quality, release velocity, production stability, and operational control.

The ideal candidate combines strong C++ and Linux engineering skills with hands-on experience in CI/CD, automated testing, debugging, performance engineering, and production systems. Experience with electronic trading, equities, FIX, market data, order routing, or low-latency systems is highly desirable.

Key Responsibilities

C++ DevOps & Release Engineering


Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines for C++ Equity Algo applications. Automate build, test, packaging, deployment, validation, and rollback workflows.
Own and improve C++ build processes using tools such as CMake, Makeor equivalent.
Maintain compiler toolchains, dependency versions, build profiles, release artifacts, and reproducible builds.
Integrate static analysis, sanitizers, code coverage, automated testing, and performance benchmarks into CI/CD pipelines.
Develop automated deployment-readiness checks and post-deployment validation.
Improve release repeatability, traceability, and governance while reducing manual intervention.
Troubleshoot build failures, compiler and linking issues, dependency conflicts, and environment inconsistencies.


Test Automation & Simulation


Design and develop automated test frameworks for complex C++ trading applications.
Build comprehensive unit, integration, regression, end-to-end, performance, latency, soak, failover, and recovery testing.
Develop test automation using frameworks.
Build mocks, simulators, test harnesses, and replay capabilities for market data feeds, order gateways, FIX connectivity, risk engines, exchange interfaces, and downstream systems.
Develop deterministic replay capabilities using historical market data and order flow.
Automate validation of expected versus actual algorithm behavior across trading scenarios.
Improve test execution speed, coverage, reliability, reporting, and integration with CI/CD.


Equity Algo Platform Engineering


Support testing and deployment of equity execution algorithms, smart order routing, and associated trading services.
Validate algo behavior across order types, venues, market conditions, and client configurations.
Support simulation and certification for new algorithm functionality, exchange changes, and venue integrations.
Validate pre-trade risk controls, throttles, kill switches, circuit breakers, and configuration controls.
Partner with developers, quants, and trading stakeholders to translate trading behavior into automated tests and validation scenarios.
Help ensure the platform meets demanding standards for reliability, resiliency, performance, latency, and operational control.


Observability & Production Readiness


Build and enhance monitoring and observability for C++ trading services.
Develop tooling around structured logging, metrics, health checks, process heartbeats, and service availability.
Create actionable dashboards covering system health, latency, message rates, order flow, market data quality, connectivity, build quality, and release readiness.
Improve diagnostics for issues including order rejects, market-data gaps, connectivity failures, latency spikes, memory leaks, deadlocks, thread contention, and deployment failures.
Develop tooling that improves incident investigation, root-cause analysis, and production support effectiveness.
Enhance operational runbooks and automated health and readiness checks.


Configuration & Environment Automation


Improve configuration management across development, QA, simulation, UAT, certification, and production.
Automate configuration validation, comparison, promotion, and deployment verification.
Build schema validation and configuration-di􀆯 tooling to reduce configuration related production risk.
Support controlled rollout mechanisms, feature toggles, and safe deployment practices.
Improve environment consistency and reduce manual release and validation activities.


Engineering Collaboration


Work across development, quantitative research, QA, infrastructure, production support, and trading teams.
Identify engineering bottlenecks and build practical automation to improve developer productivity.
Communicate clearly around release risk, testing gaps, production readiness, and operational issues.
Promote engineering best practices around automation, testing, observability, resiliency, and controlled releases.
Drive continuous improvement with a strong focus on ownership, reliability, and engineering quality.


Required Qualifications

C++ & Systems Engineering


Strong hands-on C++ development experience, preferably C++17 or later.
Strong Linux/Unix engineering experience.
Experience building, debugging, profiling, and testing large-scale C++ applications.
Strong knowledge of modern C++, including STL, templates, memory management, concurrency, and multithreading.
Strong scripting skills using Python, Bash, or similar.
Experience with Git-based development workflows.
Strong troubleshooting and root-cause analysis capabilities.


Build, CI/CD & Automation

Hands-on Experience With Several Of The Following


CMake, Make
Conan or vcpkg
Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, GitHub Actions,
Artifactory
GCC or Clang


Experience automating build, test, release, deployment, rollback, and validation

workflows and implementing automated quality gates.

Testing & Code Quality

Experience With

Unit, integration, regression, and end-to-end automation
Test harnesses, simulators, mocks, and replay tooling
clang-tidy, cppcheck, SonarQube, or Coverity
Address Sanitizer, Thread Sanitizer, and Undefined Behavior Sanitizer

gdb / lldb

Strong understanding of Linux processes, networking, TCP/UDP, sockets, memory management, CPU utilization, concurrency, locking, thread contention, and system performance. Ability to diagnose latency, jitter, memory leaks, crashes, deadlocks, and runtime failures in performance-sensitive applications.

Trading Technology


Experience with financial markets, electronic trading, or similarly performance sensitive real-time systems.
Understanding of market data, order management, order routing, executions, rejects, and trade lifecycle processing.
Familiarity with FIX or comparable financial messaging protocols.
Understanding of pre-trade risk controls, throttling, kill switches, and trading system resiliency.
Experience supporting production or other mission-critical systems.


Preferred Qualifications


Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Mathematics, Physics, Finance, or a related discipline.
5+ years of software engineering, platform engineering, DevOps, or test automation experience.
3+ years of hands-on C++ development on Linux.
Previous experience supporting front-o􀆯ice electronic trading technology.
Experience with Equity Algorithmic Trading, Smart Order Routing, OMS/EMS platforms, FIX connectivity, market data, or exchange connectivity.
Experience with low-latency, high-throughput, or real-time architectures.
Experience with market-data replay, trading simulation, or back testing infrastructure.
Experience with technologies such as PostgreSQL, or MongoDB.
Experience operating within a regulated financial-services environment, including production change controls and audit requirements.


What Success Looks Like

Success in this role means creating an engineering environment in which changes to the Equity Algo platform can be delivered faster, tested more thoroughly, deployed more

safely, and diagnosed more effectively.

Key Outcomes Include


Greater automation of C++ build, test, and deployment workflows.
Increased automated coverage of critical algorithmic trading scenarios.
Faster and more reliable regression testing.
Earlier detection of functional, latency, and performance regressions.
Reduced manual e􀆯ort during release preparation and deployment.
Fewer production issues caused by configuration, deployment, or testing gaps.
Better observability and faster root-cause analysis.
Stronger release traceability and operational controls.
Improved developer productivity and overall platform reliability.
