Fulcrum Digitalis a global AI-first enterprise transformation company with over 25 years ofexperience. We partner with enterprises across financial services, insurance,healthcare, retail, manufacturing, higher education, and logistics to move fromAI experimentation to scalable business outcomes. Fulcrum Digital works withover 100 global clients, including Fortune 500 enterprises, combining deepindustry expertise with capabilities in enterprise AI, digital engineering,cloud modernisation, platform integration, and generative AI.

The Role

We're looking fora Salesforce Administrator to join one of our top client engagements — a globalenterprise undergoing Salesforce-led transformation, including acquisitionintegration and platform expansion across newly onboarded entities. This is aPermanent/Full-time, Hybrid position based in Dublin, Ireland.

You'll partnerwith business stakeholders to translate requirements into scalable Salesforcesolutions within an Agile/SAFe framework, acting as the bridge betweenbusiness, technical, and QA teams. You'll drive end-to-end delivery acrossmultiple initiatives, lead design discussions on the Salesforce Lightningplatform, and bring best practices for M&A system integration and datamigration to high-impact, cross-geography programs.

What You'll Do

Partner with business stakeholders to capturerequirements and translate them into scalable Salesforce solutions within anAgile/SAFe framework

Act as a bridge between business, technical, andQA teams to ensure alignment on requirements, dependencies, and deliveryoutcomes

Drive end-to-end delivery of initiatives,proactively managing timelines, dependencies, and cross-team coordination

Lead design discussions and contribute toarchitecture decisions and solution design on the Salesforce Lightning platform

Support Scrum teams in adhering to Agile bestpractices, including all ceremonies and delivery standards

Develop and maintain key documentation such asuser stories, functional specs, and design artifacts

Track and report progress to stakeholders,ensuring transparency and timely issue resolution

Build and maintain strong relationships withbusiness partners and internal teams

Provide ongoing recommendations and strategicinsights aligned to the product roadmap

Present demos, prototypes, and solutionwalkthroughs to business stakeholders

Influence stakeholders through data-driveninsights and thoughtful decision-making

Lead acquisition integration programs and otherhigh-impact initiatives involving significant system transformation

Support Salesforce platform expansion acrossnewly integrated entities

Bring best practices for M&A systemintegration and data migration

Manage multiple initiatives and stakeholderengagements simultaneously in a fast-paced environment

Drive consistent delivery of high-quality,scalable solutions

Collaborate with cross-functional teams acrossgeographies to define and implement best-fit designs

Ensure successful outcomes for business usecases while identifying opportunities for continuous improvement

Provide clear status updates to business andtechnology leadership

Requirements

Salesforce Administrator and Sales CloudConsultant certifications (required)

Proven ability to design and deliver solutionsleveraging Salesforce platform capabilities

Experience building or supporting AI-driven oragentic solutions

Strong understanding of system integrations, ETLprocesses, and BI tools

Hands-on experience with data management (dataloads, mapping templates, Excel models)

Exposure to Salesforce Mobile capabilities

Demonstrated success leading complex,multi-stream projects across global teams

Strong analytical, problem-solving, andcommunication skills

Ability to lead cross-functional teams throughinfluence (matrix leadership)

Experience presenting to stakeholders andtranslating technical solutions into business value
