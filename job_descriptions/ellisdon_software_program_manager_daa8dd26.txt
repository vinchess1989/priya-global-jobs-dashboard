You, As a Software Program Manager, Will


Agile Delivery: Coordinate and facilitate Scrum ceremonies, ensure adherence to agreed delivery practices, and drive continuous process improvement across Software Engineering teams.
Release Management: Track release plans, milestones, dependencies, risks, and commitments, ensuring timely visibility into delivery progress.
Planning & Governance: Support quarterly release planning, maintain delivery documentation, and coordinate reviews and approvals.
Delivery Tooling: Administer and continuously improve Jira and Confluence, including workflows, configurations, reporting, and team collaboration practices.
Financial Tracking: Support annual budgeting, CapEx/OpEx planning, and reconciliation of planned versus actual capitalizable hours, including timesheet reporting.
Software Procurement: Track software licensing, subscriptions, cloud consumption, renewals, and invoice validation to ensure accurate financial oversight.
Continuous Improvement: Identify delivery bottlenecks, improve team efficiency, and help evolve delivery practices as AI-assisted and agentic development workflows mature.
Cross-Team Coordination: Work with Engineering, Product, Finance, and other stakeholders to maintain alignment, accountability, and effective communication.


This Is The Right Role For You If You


Have 7+ years of experience in software delivery, Agile project management, Scrum Master, or similar roles.
Hold a university degree in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or equivalent education and experience.
Have strong knowledge of Agile/Scrum practices; Scrum Master, PMP, or equivalent certifications are preferred.
Have advanced Excel skills, with experience in financial tracking, reporting, and data reconciliation.
Have a solid understanding of software development processes; previous experience in Software Engineering or DevOps is a strong asset.
Are comfortable leveraging AI tools to improve productivity, analysis, and delivery processes.
Are highly organized, proactive, and an effective communicator who can confidently facilitate discussions, challenge inefficiencies, and keep teams focused on outcomes.
Have hands-on experience with Jira and Confluence, or a willingness to learn how to configure workflows, boards, dashboards, templates, and reporting.
The salary range for this role is $125,000 - $155,000.
