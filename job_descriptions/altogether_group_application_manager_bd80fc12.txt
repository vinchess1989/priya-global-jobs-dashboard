We’re hiring: Applications Manager




We’re looking for an experienced Applications Manager to take ownership of a diverse portfolio of 20+ business applications and help shape how technology enables our business.

This is a great opportunity for someone who likes being close to both technology and the business.




Who we are

At Altogether, we provide essential water and energy services to communities across Australia.

We’re growing and evolving, and technology and data have an increasingly important role to play in how we operate, make decisions and deliver for our customers.

We’re big enough to have interesting technology challenges, but small enough that you can genuinely influence how things are done. You’ll work with people right across the business and have the opportunity to see the impact of what you deliver.




The Opportunity

Our applications support everything from Finance and People & Culture through to Operations, Growth and Customer Operations. We’re looking for someone who can take a portfolio-wide view of that environment and make sure our systems are reliable, secure, integrated and delivering value.




You’ll own the application lifecycle and work across:

application strategy and lifecycle management
SaaS and on-premise applications
integrations, APIs and data flows
vendor, licensing and contract management
cybersecurity, privacy and application governance
system performance and continuous improvement
application rationalisation and automation
stakeholder engagement and user adoption




It’s a role with plenty of variety. One day you might be working through an integration issue or application upgrade; the next you could be reviewing a vendor renewal, simplifying a business process or helping decide whether an existing system should be improved, replaced or retired.




We’re looking for someone with around 5+ years’ experience in applications, systems administration or business systems management who is comfortable being both strategic and hands-on.




Experience across platforms such as Microsoft 365, SharePoint, HubSpot, Xero/Sage Intacct, Employment Hero/KeyPay, Jira or ServiceNow would be valuable, along with a good understanding of integrations, cybersecurity, data governance and IT service management.




What will really matter is your ability to understand the business problem behind the technology, build strong relationships with users and vendors, and take genuine ownership of outcomes.




If you enjoy making systems work better, simplifying complexity and helping a business get more from its technology investment, this could be a great next step.
