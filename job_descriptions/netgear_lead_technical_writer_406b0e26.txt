Reports to: Senior Manager of Industrial Design.

The NETGEAR Technical Publications team is a group of highly skilled writers and content professionals working together across continents and cultures in a friendly, fast-paced, and inspiring environment. We create the content that helps customers set up, use, and get the most from technology products under our brands: Orbi, Nighthawk, and NETGEAR.

As a Lead Technical Writer, you will lead the planning, writing, and delivery of user manuals and related content for consumer and networking products, including routers, WiFi range extenders, cable products, mobile hotspots, and USB adapters. These are high-volume products sold worldwide, and the successful candidate will own documentation from concept through product launch and beyond, turning complex networking technology into clear instructions that anyone can follow.

This is a hybrid position based in our Cork, Ireland office.

Key Responsibilities


Plan, write, edit, and publish user manuals, quick start guides, installation guides, and related content for consumer and networking products, including routers, WiFi range extenders, cable modems and cable modem routers, mobile hotspots, and USB adapters.
Own the documentation deliverables for multiple concurrent product launches, from content planning and scoping through final release, with minimal supervision.
Work closely with product management, hardware and software engineering, UX, quality assurance, customer support, regulatory compliance, and localization teams to gather information and verify technical accuracy.
Test products, firmware, web interfaces, and mobile apps hands-on to validate procedures and make sure that documented steps match the customer experience.
Translate complex networking concepts and technical specifications into clear, concise, task-based instructions for a nontechnical consumer audience.
Take a leadership role in defining documentation standards, templates, terminology, and content reuse strategies, and make sure that all content follows the NETGEAR Editorial Style Guide.
Mentor and guide other writers, review their work, and provide constructive editorial feedback.
Drive improvements to documentation tools, processes, and workflows, including structured authoring, content management, and the responsible use of AI-assisted authoring tools.
Prepare content for translation and coordinate with localization vendors to deliver documentation in multiple languages.
Make sure that documentation meets regulatory, safety, legal, and accessibility requirements for the regions where products are sold.
Use customer feedback, support data, and usability findings to continuously improve content quality and reduce support calls.
Manage schedules, priorities, and stakeholder expectations so that documentation is delivered on time for each product release.


AI-Driven Content Development

The use of AI tools is a core requirement of this role. You are expected to make AI part of your daily workflow to raise the quality of our manuals, shorten development time, and increase what the team can deliver.


Use AI tools to review and refine user manuals for clarity, accuracy, consistency, tone, and adherence to the NETGEAR Editorial Style Guide, and to find gaps, outdated steps, and conflicting information across the documentation library.
Speed up every stage of content development with AI, including drafting first versions from engineering specifications, product requirements, and release notes, summarizing source material, and updating existing manuals for new models and firmware releases.
Extend the capacity of the team by using AI to automate repetitive work, such as content reuse, reformatting, terminology checks, and translation preparation, so that the team can support more products and launches and focus on higher-value work.
Build and maintain reusable prompts, templates, and AI-assisted workflows, share them with the team, and coach other writers to use them effectively.
Verify all AI-generated content hands-on against the product and source material, and remain fully accountable for the accuracy and quality of everything that is published.
Use only approved AI tools and follow NETGEAR policies for confidentiality, data protection, and intellectual property.
Stay current with advances in AI, evaluate new tools and techniques, and recommend the ones that measurably improve documentation quality, speed, or scale.
Demonstrated hands-on experience using generative AI tools in a professional writing workflow is required. Be prepared to share examples.


Skills And Requirements


Bachelor's degree in Technical Communication, English, Journalism, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience.
Minimum 8-10 years of experience in technical writing, including experience leading documentation projects for hardware and software products.
Experience writing for consumer electronics or networking products is preferred.
Proven ability to write clear, accurate, user-focused content for a consumer audience, supported by a portfolio of published user manuals or similar documentation.
Working knowledge of home networking concepts, such as WiFi, routers, mesh systems, cable modems (DOCSIS), mobile broadband (4G LTE and 5G), and USB connectivity, is a plus.
In-depth knowledge of structured authoring and single-source publishing, including DITA or XML, and tools such as Heretto, Adobe FrameMaker, MadCap Flare, or Oxygen XML Editor.
Experience with content management systems, version control, and publishing to print, PDF, and web formats.
Experience preparing content for localization and working with translation vendors.
Strong editing skills and expertise in applying style guides and terminology standards.
Ability to read and interpret engineering specifications, product requirements, and UI designs, and to learn new technologies quickly.
Effective leadership, organization, and project management skills, with experience managing multiple projects against tight deadlines.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work across functions, time zones, and cultures.
Experience mentoring or guiding other writers.
Proven ability and willingness to take ownership of projects and get the job done with minimal supervision.
Self-starter who works well on a team.
