Keep the heartbeat of innovation running! Join our client in this quality engineering position and ensure cutting-edge operations never miss a beat!

Proclinical is seeking a Senior Quality Engineer to join a dynamic team focused on advancing medical device technology. In this role, you will ensure devices are designed, validated, manufactured, and released in compliance with ISO 13485 and FDA Quality Management System Regulation (QMSR). This position involves hands-on quality engineering tasks alongside quality assurance and management system responsibilities. You will play a key role in maintaining inspection readiness and supporting clinical use of products.

Responsibilities:

Oversee design controls, verification, and validation processes, ensuring regulatory compliance.
Develop and maintain Design History Files (DHF), Device Master Records (DMR), and Risk Management Files (RMF).
Plan and execute equipment and process validations, including IQ, OQ, and PQ protocols.
Manage cleanroom qualifications and environmental monitoring.
Qualify and audit suppliers, maintain approved supplier lists, and set incoming inspection plans.
Lead root cause investigations and manage nonconformance and CAPA processes.
Administer document control, change control, and eQMS systems.
Conduct internal audits and prepare for external inspections and audits.
Review device history records and release products for clinical use.
Provide quality evidence for regulatory submissions and clinical study packages.
Train staff on GMP, validation, and quality procedures, fostering a culture of quality.

Key Skills and Requirements:

Degree in engineering or science (e.g., biomedical, mechanical, electrical, chemical, or physical sciences).
Expertise in quality engineering within medical devices under ISO 13485.
Hands-on experience with process validation (IQ, OQ, PQ) and verification protocols.
Knowledge of design controls, DHF, and QMSR regulations.
Proficiency in supplier qualification, auditing, and risk management (ISO 14971).
Strong statistical analysis skills, including capability analysis and SPC.
Experience administering eQMS and controlled documents.
Desirable: Certification in ISO 13485 auditing, Lean Six Sigma Green Belt, and familiarity with FDA/TGA submissions.
