Sarah George

VAE Group is one of Australasia's largest commercial HVAC solutions companies with over 400 employees across 12 office locations in Australia, New Zealand and Papua New Guinea, with works spanning diverse and interesting client locations as well as some of the regions most exciting construction projects.




With support from the Head of HSEQ, the role will primarily focus on the control of HSEQ and other business documents across VAE, ensuring accessibility, quality and consistency as part of our Integrated Management System (IMS). Additionally, the role will administer, and actively participate in, various assurance activities to support business performance and compliance.




You’re organised and detail-focused, but also curious about how work happens and where processes can improve. You’re comfortable working with people across the business, turning requirements into practical tools and clear information, and following issues through to resolution. You’ll bring a continuous improvement mindset to document control, systems and assurance activities.




Your day to day looks like:

Organise controlled documents across the whole business and collaborate with business unit managers and document owners to ensure the currency and practicality of IMS content
Maintain HSEQ and related SharePoint sites, document libraries, forms and automated processes and workflows. Improve how they work and make information easier to find and use
Work with leaders to ensure that credential records are up to date and set up a reliable way to spot missing or expiring information and follow up with managers
Coordinate the execution of the internal audit plan and a simple, consistent way to record issues and track actions
Plan and carry out internal audits across branches and projects
Develop and implement an assurance and reporting schedule to provide management visibility of key risks and non-conformances
Play a central role in ensuring that VAE retain its ISO 9001, 14001 and 45001 certifications

About You

You thrive on order, consistency and continuous improvement and are skilled in identifying and implementing simple solutions to complex problems. You aren’t afraid to talk to managers and can easily switch from working independently to actively talking to managers about changes.




You have excellent SharePoint management experience beyond document uploads and like to focus on end-user experience.




Your ideal skill sets include:

Experience managing large, complex sets of company documents.
Can create and manage SharePoint pages, libraries, forms and workflows (you don’t need to be an advanced programmer!)
Have supported document audits, keeping documents current and easy to access.
Excellent at communicating, following up, and providing solutions.




Highly Advantageous

Experience with ISO certified safety, quality and environmental management systems.
Management system auditing qualifications and experience.
Previous employment in construction, engineering, mechanical services or another high-risk operational business.
