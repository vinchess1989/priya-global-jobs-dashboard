Quality Systems & Compliance Manager
Goldman Tech Resourcing (Pty) Ltd
I'm interested

One of the leading packaging manufacturing companies in Cape Town is currently looking for a skilled Quality Systems & Compliance Manager to join their team.

 

Minimum Requirements:

10 years’ experience in a QA, Quality Systems, Compliance role within the FMCG/Manufacturing industry.
5+ years’ experience in a managerial/supervisory role.
Strong working knowledge of GMP, HACCP/HARA, quality FMEA, and OH&S HIRA methodologies.
Experience managing an internal audit programme, including system, process, and contractor audits.
Experience owning CAPA, change management, and calibration / measurement control systems.
Degree in Quality Management, Food Science / Food Safety, Engineering, or a related field.
Lead Auditor certification (BRCGS, ISO 9001, or ISO 45001).
Matric

 

Should you meet the requirements for this position, please email your CV to trade.vacancy@stand-outstaffing.co.za or you can also contact the team on 021 555 0952.

Correspondence will only be conducted with short listed candidates. Should you not hear from us within 3 days, please consider your application unsuccessful.
