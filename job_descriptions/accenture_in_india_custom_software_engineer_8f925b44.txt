Project Role : Custom Software Engineer

Project Role Description : Develop custom software solutions to design, code, and enhance components across systems or applications. Use modern frameworks and agile practices to deliver scalable, high-performing solutions tailored to specific business needs.

Must have skills : Automotive ECU Software

Good to have skills : NA

Minimum 7.5 Year(s) Of Experience Is Required

Educational Qualification : 15 years full time education

Summary:

The Software Requirements Engineer defines, analyzes, documents, and manages software requirements for automotive embedded systems and tire-intelligence solutions. The role connects customer and system needs with embedded software architecture, development, integration, and verification teams, ensuring requirements are clear, feasible, testable, traceable, and controlled throughout the product lifecycle. The engineer will work closely with OEMs, systems engineers, algorithm developers, embedded software engineers, functional-safety specialists, cybersecurity teams, and validation teams to support production-ready software delivery.

Roles & Responsibilities:

Requirements Development: Elicit, analyze, decompose, and document software requirements from customer, stakeholder, system, safety, cybersecurity, and interface requirements.

Embedded Engineering Alignment: Translate requirements into implementable behavior for ECUs, sensors, gateways, algorithms, and embedded applications review feasibility with software architects and developers.

Requirements Quality: Ensure requirements are unambiguous, complete, consistent, atomic, verifiable, and compliant with internal templates and Automotive SPICE expectations.

Traceability and Change Control: Maintain end-to-end traceability from stakeholder and system requirements through software requirements, architecture, implementation, test cases, defects, and releases using Polarion and JIRA.

Technical Reviews: Lead requirement workshops and peer reviews with embedded software, hardware, algorithm, integration, functional-safety, cybersecurity, and validation teams.

Verification Support: Define acceptance criteria and support MIL, SIL, HIL, bench, ECU, and vehicle-level test-case development assess verification coverage and resolve requirement defects.

Release and Compliance: Support baselines, impact analysis, change requests, audits, release readiness, and evidence preparation for Automotive SPICE, ISO 26262, and ISO/SAE 21434 activities.

Collaboration: Coordinate with global OEM, Tier-1, supplier, and internal engineering teams to clarify requirements, interfaces, assumptions, dependencies, and technical risks.

Professional & Technical Skills:

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical, Electronics, Computer, Automotive, Systems Engineering, Computer Science, or a related discipline.

5+ years of automotive or embedded product-development experience, including 3+ years in software or systems requirements engineering.

Hands-on understanding of embedded software development in C/C++, ECU integration, RTOS concepts, memory and timing constraints, diagnostics, and software-hardware interfaces.

Proficiency with Polarion, IBM DOORS, or equivalent requirements-management tools practical experience with JIRA and configuration/change-control workflows.

Working knowledge of Automotive SPICE, ISO 26262 functional safety, ISO/SAE 21434 cybersecurity, and Agile/Scrum development practices.

Knowledge of automotive communication and diagnostic protocols such as CAN/CAN FD, LIN, Ethernet, UDS, OBD-II, J1939, or SOME/IP.

Ability to interpret software architecture, SysML/UML models, MATLAB/Simulink or Stateflow models, interface specifications, logs, test results, and source-code behavior.

Strong analytical, technical-writing, facilitation, and cross-functional communication skills in a global engineering environment.

Preferred Experience :

Experience with AUTOSAR Classic or Adaptive, model-based development, code generation, or embedded algorithm integration.

Experience with intelligent tire systems, TPMS, vehicle dynamics, connected gateways, telematics, sensors, or safety-related automotive software.

Certification or formal training in IREB, Automotive SPICE, functional safety, cybersecurity, or systems engineering is an advantage.

Additional Information:


The candidate should have minimum 5+ years of experience in Automotive ECU Software.
A 15 years full time education is required.
