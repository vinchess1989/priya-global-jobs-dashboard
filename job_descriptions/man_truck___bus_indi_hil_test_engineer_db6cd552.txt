Your Tasks –

The Release Manager ensures that software and calibration releases are planned, coordinated, and delivered at the required quality and time. The role manages release scope, schedules, dependencies, risks, and readiness while coordinating closely with project management, configuration management, development, testing, and other internal stakeholders.




To ensure that you will-

Develop and maintain the Release Plan in coordination with the Configuration Management Plan.
Own product release management and facilitate release meetings.
Plan and coordinate release windows and cycles for software and calibration deliveries.
Coordinate software, calibration, testing, and supporting-document deliveries across teams.
Compile product releases and ensure readiness before delivery to testing, production, or aftermarket.
Conduct release-readiness reviews and support Go/No-Go decisions.
Ensure agreed release plans and milestones are achieved.
Manage deviations and delivery issues with project managers and development teams.
Track release risks, dependencies, deviations, and open points affecting scope, schedule, and quality.
Deliver development and production software or calibration packages to relevant stakeholders.
Maintain calibration libraries and associated databases.
Communicate release status, schedules, dependencies, risks, and process compliance to internal stakeholders.




Your qualification/ minimum requirements -

Experience: 8 to 12 years
Qualification: B.Tech./M.Tech. in embedded systems, mechatronics, computer science, electronics, or a comparable discipline. Relevant professional experience may also be accepted.
Broad understanding of software development, release management, and embedded systems.
Understanding of hardware-software integration and software or calibration delivery processes.
Strong analytical, planning, coordination, and organizational skills.
Ability to coordinate complex, cross-functional activities, projects, and dependencies.
Confidence in facilitating release decisions and maintaining transparency across teams.
