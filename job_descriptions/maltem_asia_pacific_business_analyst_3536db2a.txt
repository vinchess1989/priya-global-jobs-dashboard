We are looking for a Business Analyst to support requirements gathering, business process analysis, functional documentation and testing activities for a SaaS.




Key Responsibilities

Gather, analyse and document business and functional requirements.
Conduct stakeholder interviews and requirements workshops.
Prepare business requirement documents, functional specifications and user stories.
Analyse business processes and identify improvement opportunities.
Work closely with developers to clarify requirements and acceptance criteria.
Support estimation of development and testing activities.
Perform functional and exploratory testing, including browser testing.
Support system integration testing with external applications.
Conduct impact analysis of defects and proposed changes.
Validate completed development stories against acceptance criteria.
Support User Acceptance Testing (UAT) and coordinate issue resolution.
Track defects and ensure timely closure.
Maintain functional documentation throughout the project lifecycle.

Requirements

Relevant experience in IT business analysis or application implementation.
Experience gathering and documenting business requirements.
Knowledge of software development lifecycle (SDLC).
Experience with functional testing, UAT and system integration testing.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Ability to work with business stakeholders and technical teams.
Experience with SaaS platforms or LMS solutions is an advantage.
