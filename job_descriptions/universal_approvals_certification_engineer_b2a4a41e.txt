Company Description Universal Approvals was founded by a team of experienced Product Compliance Engineers to support electrical and electronic manufacturers, importers, and exporters in accessing local and international markets. The company specializes in guiding products through complex certification and regulatory requirements, helping clients achieve compliant and market-ready solutions. By combining deep technical expertise with practical industry experience, Universal Approvals streamlines approval processes and reduces time to market. Team members collaborate closely with global stakeholders, providing end-to-end compliance services and clear technical guidance.
Role Description This full-time, on-site Certification Engineer role is based in the Greater Melbourne Area. The Certification Engineer will manage product compliance and certification activities for electrical and electronic products, including planning test programs, liaising with test laboratories, and reviewing test reports. Daily tasks include interpreting standards and regulations, preparing technical documentation, analyzing product designs for compliance risks, and supporting corrective actions where needed. The role involves close collaboration with product development and R&D teams, providing guidance on regulatory requirements and certification pathways. The Certification Engineer will also communicate with clients and authorities, maintain organized records of certifications, and contribute to continuous improvement of compliance processes.
Qualifications

Strong skills in Product Certification and regulatory compliance for electrical and electronic products.
Experience with Testing processes, test planning, and coordination with accredited laboratories.
Background in Research and Development (R&D), including support for product design and validation.
Technical knowledge in Electronics, circuits, and relevant safety and performance standards.
Familiarity with Aircraft or aerospace-related products and associated certification frameworks is beneficial.
Degree in Electrical/Electronic Engineering or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.
Ability to read and interpret international standards (e.g., IEC, ISO, EN), and prepare clear technical documentation.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and attention-to-detail skills, with the ability to manage multiple projects.
Effective written and verbal communication skills and ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams and clients.
