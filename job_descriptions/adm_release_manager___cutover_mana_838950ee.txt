Release Manager:

As a member of the Global Technology Organization and a Release Manager, you are responsible for overseeing the successful planning, coordination, and execution of releases. Your role is critical in ensuring that the organization's landscape is up-to-date, stable, and compliant with industry best practices. You will work closely with various teams, including release management, environment management, administrators, development, testing, functional consultants, and project managers, to ensure a smooth and efficient release process.




Job Responsibilities:

Define release schedules, milestones, and deliverables to meet project deadlines and business objectives for releases
Work in a fast-paced environment and manage multiple releases simultaneously
Perform Release Management and Cutover Management activities across various technologies, applications and platforms such as ERPs, CRM systems, Enterprise Application systems, Full-Stack applications and should learn any technology specific details relevant to Release Management and Cutover activities quickly
Own integrated release and cutover planning for complex releases, including sequencing, dependencies, cross-functional coordination, and escalation
Own end-to-end release readiness across application, testing, environment, security, business, and operational dependencies, release quality gates before approving movement to production
Drive release risk and dependency management, proactively identifying conflicts, cross-product impacts, freeze-period constraints, and business-critical risks
Partner with Environment Management to confirm environment availability, configuration readiness, refreshes, access, data readiness, and production/non-production alignment
Manage enterprise release calendars and collision management across product families, applications, platforms, plant operations, blackout periods, major business events and system freezes
Own release metrics to identify areas for improvement and implement corrective actions
Drive continuous automation of release processes, including CI/CD integration, automated quality gates, deployment orchestration, release dashboards, and reduction of manual handoffs
Exhibit ability to work both in a team environment and independently
Should be able to work with ambiguity and bring clarity to the plan
Stay up-to-date with technologies, industry trends, and best practices, applying relevant knowledge to enhance Release Management practices
Drive process improvements to optimize the release management process and increase efficiency
Maintain a positive work environment through teamwork & conflict resolution
Expected to work occasional weekends, holidays, or a variety of shifts, when needed, to support production releases




Required Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in a technical discipline or equivalent with a minimum of 5+ years of experience in IT Releases or Delivery
Experience in large-scale implementations, releases, scheduled release such as daily, weekly, monthly quarterly, minor – across multiple technologies
Experience in managing end-to-end enterprise releases, including release readiness, cutover, deployment, go/no-go, rollback/backout, and post-release stabilization
Experience with release governance, controls, and audit/compliance requirements, including change approvals and evidence
Strong experience managing release risks, dependencies, conflicts, and escalations across multiple applications, platforms, and teams.
Experience with coordinating with teams for environment readiness and deployment across non-production and production environments
Experience in defining and using release metrics
Experience with Change Request creation using tools such as ServiceNow
Ability to learn application or technology specific tools and processes relevant to Release Management quickly and own the Release Management process
Strong stakeholder management and executive communication skills, including communicating risks, readiness, and release status to IT and Business leaders
Ability to lead cross-functional releases, and command centers during major releases and production deployment
Knowledge of software development lifecycle including agile, and waterfall
Working knowledge of DevOps / CI-CD concepts




Desired Qualifications:

ITIL certifications are a plus
Understanding of Cloud, non-Cloud, Digital applications, components, integration points among applications and teams
Project management skills
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Working knowledge of using AI
