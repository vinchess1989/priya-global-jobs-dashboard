Looking to join a tier-1 buyside firm? Explore multiple SRE and DevOps Engineers across a range of seniority levels in Hong Kong and Singapore.







What you’ll work on

• Build, support, and improve cloud infrastructure, distributed platforms and production trading environments.

• Automate deployments, configuration, and operational workflows.

• Develop monitoring, logging, and alerting to improve availability and performance.

• Troubleshoot complex issues, lead root-cause analysis and strengthen system resilience.

• Partner with developers, traders and infrastructure teams on releases and production readiness




Relevant experience

• At least 5 years of relevant experience in SRE, DevOps, infrastructure engineering, or production support.

• Strong troubleshooting, communication, and ownership skills.

• Experience operating business-critical systems; financial services or trading technology exposure is preferable for some of the openings




Technologies across the openings

• Scripting: Python, PowerShell, Linux

• Infrastructure: Linux, Windows, GCP, Kubernetes, Azure Service Fabric, Docker.

• Automation and delivery: Terraform, Ansible, GitLab CI/CD, Octopus Deploy, Artifactory.

• Observability: Datadog, Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, InfluxDB.

• Distributed systems and data: gRPC, Protobuf, TCP/UDP, multicast, Kafka, MongoDB, ClickHouse




Requirements vary by opening—you do not need experience with every technology listed. Interested in learning more? Apply now or message me for a confidential chat.
