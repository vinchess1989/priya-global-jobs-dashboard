He whakamārama mō te tūranga mahi | About the role

We’re looking for an experienced Senior Digital Delivery Lead to join Watercare on a contract through to the end of May 2027.

This is a hands-on senior delivery role focused on bringing structure, pace and clarity to complex digital initiatives. You’ll work across multiple teams and stakeholders, managing dependencies, improving processes and ensuring delivery remains focused on the right outcomes.

Your initial focus will be leading an initiative reviewing identity-related processes end to end and establishing an effective support model for third-party contractors. While this will be your first area of focus, this is not an identity specialist role. We’re looking for a strong, adaptable Delivery Lead who can quickly understand complex processes, systems and integrations and bring the right people together to get things moving.

Key Responsibilities Will Include


Leading complex digital initiatives from planning through to implementation
Reviewing and improving end-to-end processes
Coordinating delivery across multiple teams, systems and stakeholders
Managing system and process integrations, dependencies and impacts
Establishing clear delivery plans, governance and ways of working
Identifying and removing risks, issues and roadblocks
Leading prioritisation and capacity planning across delivery teams
Working closely with Product Owners, architects, engineers and other technology specialists
Providing clear visibility of delivery progress, risks and outcomes
Driving decisions and constructively challenging stakeholders when required
Creating an environment of accountability, collaboration and continuous improvement


Ngā pūkenga e rapu nei mātou | About you

You’ll be an experienced Senior Delivery Lead with a strong track record of successfully delivering complex digital or technology initiatives.

You’ll Bring


Significant experience leading complex digital or technology delivery
Strong experience managing end-to-end processes and delivery across multiple teams
Experience working across system integrations and technical dependencies
Strong governance, planning and delivery management capability
Experience working with cross-functional Agile delivery teams
Strong stakeholder management skills with the confidence to influence at senior levels
Proven ability to identify and remove delivery roadblocks
Experience working with Product Owners, architects, engineers and technology teams
The ability to quickly understand complex processes and technology environments
Strong commercial awareness and experience managing delivery budgets where required
The ability to bring structure and clarity to ambiguous or evolving pieces of work
A pragmatic, outcomes-focused approach with a strong focus on pace and execution
