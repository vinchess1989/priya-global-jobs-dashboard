Responsibilities

Act as technical owner of one or more functions, ensuring they work according to requirements and adapting requirements when needed
Manage requirements: update and maintain technical and functional requirements
Manage change requests: create and track them
Manage testing: design and execute test specifications, supported by a testing team
Manage defects: find and track them until the fix is released in an official software version
Produce periodic reports on planned changes, project milestones, open topics, change ticket statuses, and defect statistics
Document meeting minutes, telephone conferences, and agreements as protocols

Qualifications

Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or a related STEM field
At least 2 years of professional experience in systems development, including Linux/Android engineering
At least 2 years of experience in the Software Development Life Cycle, including systems engineering, integration, and testing
Fluent English (mandatory, as it is the official project language)
Strong understanding of automotive infotainment systems, including hardware and software components
Experience with automotive standards and protocols (e.g., CAN, LIN, Ethernet, UDS)
Excellent troubleshooting and problem-solving skills
Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to collaborate across teams
Ability to prioritize tasks and manage multiple projects simultaneously
Familiarity with Agile methodologies and the software development lifecycle

Nice to Have

Previous experience in leadership or managerial roles
Experience with Python, C/C++, or Java
Experience with software development and version control systems (e.g., Git)
Knowledge of Atlassian tools, primarily Jira and Confluence
