Quality Engineer – QMS







Role Overview

We are seeking an experienced Quality Engineer with strong Quality Management System (QMS) experience to support its manufacturing operations.

The successful candidate will provide Quality Engineering support across the site, with particular focus on maintaining and improving the Quality Management System, ensuring compliance with internal procedures and applicable regulatory requirements.

This role would suit an experienced Quality Engineer or QA professional from a medical device, pharmaceutical or similarly regulated manufacturing environment with hands-on experience in CAPA, non-conformance, change control, auditing and quality system improvement.







Key Responsibilities

Provide day-to-day support for the site Quality Management System.
Support the management and continuous improvement of Quality Systems in accordance with ISO 13485, ISO 9001 and applicable regulatory requirements.
Manage and support CAPAs, ensuring effective root cause investigation, corrective actions and timely closure.
Support non-conformance investigations, incident reporting and associated quality records.
Support change control activities and assess changes from a Quality and compliance perspective.
Prepare, review and maintain SOPs, Quality System documentation, protocols, reports and other controlled documentation.
Participate in internal audits and support external/regulatory audits.
Work closely with Manufacturing, Engineering, Validation and other cross-functional teams to ensure ongoing compliance.
Support quality investigations and the implementation of effective root cause analysis.
Monitor Quality System performance and support continuous improvement initiatives.
Ensure adherence to site GMP requirements and quality procedures.
Provide Quality input into manufacturing and process improvement activities.
Support customer complaint and quality issue investigations where required.







Qualifications & Experience

Degree in Engineering, Science, Quality or a related technical discipline.
Previous Quality Engineering / Quality Assurance experience within a regulated manufacturing environment.
Strong hands-on QMS experience is essential.
Good working knowledge of ISO 13485 and medical-device Quality System requirements.
Experience with CAPA, non-conformance/deviation management, change control and root cause analysis.
Experience preparing and reviewing controlled Quality documentation.
Experience supporting internal and/or regulatory audits.
Knowledge of FDA Quality System requirements would be advantageous.
Medical device experience is highly desirable, although strong pharmaceutical Quality Systems experience would also be relevant.
Experience with process validation, statistical methods/SPC or Six Sigma would be beneficial.
Strong communication skills with the ability to work effectively across Quality, Operations and Engineering functions.
