About the Organisation

Our client builds secure software for highly regulated industries, specialising in data security and anti-fraud platforms. They design, build and operate secure digital platforms for organisations handling sensitive data and mission-critical workloads.




The Opportunity

We're after a hands-on DevOps Team Lead, or a strong Senior Engineer ready to step into a 2IC role, to lead a small, growing team of Linux DevOps engineers. This is a build and run role. You'll stay hands-on across infrastructure and platform engineering while driving architecture and mentoring the team.




Open to candidates on the east coast, ideally Sydney, Melbourne or Brisbane.




What You'll Be Doing

Lead, mentor and grow a small team of Linux DevOps engineers
Stay hands-on across infrastructure and platform engineering
Build, maintain and evolve OpenStack-based private cloud environments
Design and implement automation, CI/CD pipelines and Infrastructure as Code
Support secure, segmented environments with strict isolation and compliance requirements
Work closely with development teams to deliver reliable, scalable platforms
Contribute to platform architecture and continuous improvement




What You Bring

Strong Linux systems administration experience in enterprise or service provider environments
Proven OpenStack deployment, operations and troubleshooting experience, highly preferred
Solid understanding of private cloud, hybrid setups and virtualisation
DevOps capability across CI/CD, IaC (Terraform, Ansible) and scripting (Python, Bash)
Strong networking fundamentals, routing, firewalls, segmentation
Experience in high-security or highly regulated sectors
Exposure to Zero Trust architecture or compliance frameworks (ISO 27001, PCI DSS, CPS 234) highly regarded.




You can apply directly via the job ad or send your CV to max@jenkinbeattie.com
