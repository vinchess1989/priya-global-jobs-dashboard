Job Description

We are looking for a driven and experienced Product Manager to effectively manage product streams and use cases in parallel, while also supporting live production systems, including change requests, patches, and incident management.

What Will You Do

Product Strategy & Roadmap


Collaborate closely with clients to define product vision, strategy, and roadmap
Translate business needs into clear product requirements across multiple use cases
Manage and prioritize multiple product tracks simultaneously


Agile Delivery & Multi-Stream Management


Lead Agile product development with at least 4+ years of hands-on experience
Oversee multiple concurrent development streams with different use cases
Manage cross-team dependencies and ensure alignment across all tracks
Drive sprint planning, backlog grooming, and prioritization
Ensure high-quality and timely delivery across all streams


Production, Change Requests & Incident Management


Manage change requests (CRs) for live production systems in collaboration with clients
Liaise with clients to scope, prioritize, and deliver enhancements and fixes
Oversee software patch releases, ensuring minimal disruption to operations
Lead and coordinate incident management, including triaging, resolution, and post-incident follow-ups
Ensure proper tracking, documentation, and closure of production issues
Continuously improve processes to enhance system stability and reliability


Stakeholder & Client Management


Act as the primary point of contact for clients and stakeholders
Manage expectations across development and production workstreams
Drive discussions confidently, including with senior and C-level stakeholders
Ensure continuous alignment on requirements, priorities, and timelines


Reporting & Communication


Prepare and deliver weekly and monthly updates to senior stakeholders
Provide consolidated updates across development, CRs, and production support
Clearly communicate risks, trade-offs, and dependencies


Planning, Coordination & Execution


Develop integrated project plans covering development and production activities
Track issues, risks, and interdependencies across all workstreams
Ensure stakeholders are aligned on deadlines and deliverables
Demonstrate strong multitasking ability with high attention to detail


Operations & Governance


Manage project financials, budgeting, and cost tracking
Oversee resource allocation across development and support activities
Handle client escalations across both delivery and production issues
Manage client PO, invoicing processes, and contractual obligations
Ensure adherence to tender specifications
Proactively identify and mitigate risks across the project lifecycle


Qualifications

The ideal candidate should possess:


Minimum 4+ years of experience in product management cycle
Proven experience managing multiple concurrent streams or complex programs
Experience handling production systems, change requests, and incident management
Strong multitasking and prioritization skills
Experience managing cross-team dependencies and delivery timelines


Preferred Attributes


Self-motivated with positive “can-do” attitude and creativity
Independent, self-driven, and accountable
Ability to lead, develop, and maintain respectful and trusting relationships
Strong communication, negotiation, and interpersonal skills
Strong presentation skills and persuasive techniques
Effective leadership, coaching, and performance management skills


Professional and/or Technical Certifications


Scrum Master Certified
Product Manager Certified
