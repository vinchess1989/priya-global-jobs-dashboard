Company Description

At #ONE, we are one of the world's largest liner shipping companies. Operating more than 280 vessels, we offer an expeditious and reliable international network of over 165 services to 120 countries and beyond. We have a fleet size of approximately 2.2 million TEU.

Job Description


Partner with the Product Lead and stakeholders to align the product roadmap with overall business strategy, translating technical insights and business intent into actionable product opportunities.
Treat requirements as hypotheses to validate through research, data analysis, and experimentation (e.g., proofs of concept), ensuring solutions are feasible, cost-effective, and outcome-driven.
Maintain roadmap boundaries (in/out of scope), translate approved initiatives into structured epics, and present preliminary estimates and investment analyses to secure stakeholder approval.
Own conceptual solution design and user journeys, coordinating refinements and design reviews to keep decisions aligned with the core product vision.
Write clear user stories and robust acceptance criteria that incorporate business value, technical constraints, and non-functional requirements (including security).
Balance new feature delivery against technical debt and architectural needs, managing scope trade-offs while protecting essential non-functional requirements from delivery pressure.
Ensure user stories are delivery-ready, accept or reject completed work against criteria, co-lead UAT with stakeholders, and lead product go/no-go readiness.
Establish measurable metrics ahead of release and analyze post-launch data to surface insights and improvement opportunities for data-driven decision-making.
Work closely with Technical Architecture, GPM, and SMEs across the lifecycle, document feature behavior and business rules, and capture post-release feedback to sustain product value.


Qualifications


Bachelor's Degree in Business, Information Systems, Computer Science, Engineering, Logistics, Supply Chain Management, or a related discipline.
At least 3 years of experience in product ownership, product management, business analysis, or a related role. (Candidates with more extensive experience may be considered for a Manager-level position)
Proven track record of collaborating with business and technical teams to deliver digital products.
Core technical and analytical skills, including product backlog and roadmap management; user stories and acceptance criteria; process and system analysis; product analytics and KPI monitoring; understanding of Agile, SDLC, system dependencies, and non-functional requirements.
Expertise in shipping and logistics is preferred.
Strong communication, stakeholder management, problem-solving, collaboration, facilitation, ownership, and decision-making abilities.
Knowledge of ERP systems is a strong plus.
