Anne-Marie Fox
Anne-Marie Fox

An exciting opportunity has arisen for an experienced Senior DevOps Engineer to join a leading global technology organisation.

This role offers the chance to become part of a forward-thinking R&D team, working with modern cloud technologies to enhance automation, strengthen security and optimise software delivery. You'll play an important role in developing scalable, reliable solutions while collaborating with talented engineers on innovative projects with a global impact.

Key Responsibilities

Develop, maintain and optimise secure CI/CD pipelines, supporting migration to AWS.
Improve automation, security and efficiency across DevOps environments.
Collaborate with Software Development, IT Security and Cloud Support teams.
Support the implementation of secure software development practices (DevSecOps).
Assist with cloud infrastructure modernisation using microservices and container technologies.
Monitor security scans, identify vulnerabilities and support continuous improvement.
Evaluate emerging technologies and recommend improvements to existing systems.
Ensure compliance with relevant security standards and internal policies.

Requirements

Degree or Master's qualification in Data Science, Mathematics, Physics or a related discipline.
3–5 years' professional experience in DevOps or software engineering environments.
Strong experience with AWS, CI/CD pipelines and cloud monitoring tools.
Proficiency with DevOps tools including Jenkins, Nexus, Bitbucket and Jira.
Experience working across Linux, macOS and Windows operating systems.
Programming knowledge across languages such as Python, Java, JavaScript, C#, C or SQL.
Excellent troubleshooting, communication and problem-solving skills.
Ability to collaborate across technical teams and demonstrate strong leadership skills.

Desirable Experience

Containerisation technologies including Docker and Kubernetes.
Configuration management tools such as Ansible, Puppet or Chef.
Familiarity with security and compliance standards including ISO 27001, NIST and GDPR.

Why Consider This Opportunity?

Join a forward-thinking technology environment where innovation, collaboration and continuous improvement are at the heart of everything. You'll work with modern cloud technologies, contribute to meaningful global projects and have the opportunity to develop your expertise within a highly skilled R&D team.

Ready to take the next step in your DevOps career?

Contact AMFConsulting today to learn more or submit your CV for a confidential discussion.
