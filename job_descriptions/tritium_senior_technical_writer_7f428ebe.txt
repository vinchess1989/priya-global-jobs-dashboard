About Tritium

Tritium has spent more than 20 years building the power electronics behind DC fast charging, with over 3 GW deployed across 50+ countries. Our products are designed in Australia, and our Brisbane engineering and testing centre is where the next generation of power delivery for the electric era takes shape: liquid-cooled power modules for fast-charging networks, 800 VDC rectifiers for AI data centres, and systems that connect battery storage and microgrids to the grid. New platforms like GRID-FLEX and DC-FLEX come out of this team, and every one of them needs clear, accurate documentation before it goes out into the world.

About the role

We're looking for a Senior Technical Writer to own the documentation for our cutting-edge new products. Reporting to our Technical Content Manager, you'll work side by side with the engineers designing these platforms, getting into the detail early and turning complex technical information into manuals, procedures and guides that installers, service technicians and customers around the world can rely on.

What you'll do


Write and maintain end-user, installation, operation, service and maintenance documentation for new and existing products
Work with engineering, QA and product teams to gather information, validate technical content and get review and sign-off
Build field service procedures, installation checklists and test plans
Apply editorial standards, templates and controlled document processes, and manage versioning and publishing
Keep documentation current as products change, regulations shift and feedback comes in from users and field technicians
Support localisation and publishing for a global audience
Find ways to improve content quality, reuse and consistency


What you'll bring


At least 5 years of technical writing experience in engineering, manufacturing, software or another technical field
Clear, concise writing and the ability to translate complex technical concepts into content people can actually use
Experience owning the full content lifecycle, from information gathering and audience profiling through to publication and maintenance
A track record of customer-facing and internal documents such as manuals, field service procedures and installation checklists
Proficiency with eDrawings and Microsoft 365 (Word, Visio). Confluence, Jira and SharePoint are a plus
Confidence working with engineers and stakeholders, and juggling multiple deadlines on your own
Bonus points for ISO 9001 or regulated documentation experience, localisation workflows, DITA or XML authoring, or a background in energy, EV or manufacturing


Why Tritium?


Document industry-leading technology that's powering the future of EV charging and wider energy infrastructure
Get in early on new products and shape how they're explained to the world
Work alongside experienced engineers at our Brisbane engineering and testing centre, with hybrid options
Free on-site DC fast charging for your EV
Flexible access to your earned wages
