Introduction

An opportunity is available for an experienced Service Delivery Manager to lead and oversee IT Managed Services operations, ensuring reliable, high-quality and commercially responsible service delivery to business customers.

This is a mid-senior operational management role requiring a strong background in IT Service Delivery, Managed Services, Service Desk Management and IT Operations leadership.

The successful candidate will be responsible for managing service delivery performance, SLA compliance, customer relationships, technical team coordination, operational governance, resource capacity and continuous service improvement.

The role requires a commercially aware, customer-focused leader who can effectively bridge technical operations and business requirements, ensuring that service commitments are delivered consistently, operational risks are managed proactively and customers receive reliable support.

The ideal candidate will have experience managing complex IT service environments, coordinating multidisciplinary technical teams and overseeing service performance across multiple business customers. Experience within a Managed Service Provider (MSP) or comparable IT service-delivery environment will be advantageous.

Duties & Responsibilities

IT Service Delivery and Operational Management

Oversee the overall IT service-delivery operating model, ensuring reliable, efficient and commercially responsible managed services.

Take accountability for service delivery outcomes, operational performance, customer satisfaction and service quality.

Establish clear ownership, priorities, responsibilities and delivery expectations across Service Desk, Technical Operations, Projects and customer-facing teams.

Ensure operational activities are effectively coordinated and aligned with customer requirements, contractual obligations and business objectives.

Identify, manage and escalate operational, customer, resource and commercial risks.

Drive accountability across technical and operational teams, ensuring agreed actions are completed and supported by appropriate evidence.

Maintain operational oversight of infrastructure, networking, cloud, security, backup and other managed IT services without replacing the technical ownership of specialist teams.

Implement effective operational governance processes and continuously identify opportunities to improve service reliability and efficiency.

Service Desk Management and SLA Governance

Lead and manage the operational performance of the IT Service Desk and associated support resources.

Oversee ticket intake, triage, prioritisation, assignment, escalation and resolution processes.

Monitor Service Level Agreement (SLA) and Operational Level Agreement (OLA) performance, ensuring services are delivered against agreed commitments.

Manage aged tickets, unassigned incidents, recurring service issues and operational backlogs.

Ensure effective Incident Management, Major Incident Management, Problem Management and Change Management practices.

Coordinate major incidents and customer escalations, ensuring timely communication, appropriate technical involvement and effective resolution.

Oversee Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and corrective actions to reduce recurring incidents and improve service reliability.

Apply ITIL-aligned service-management principles to strengthen operational governance and service performance.

Analyse service-management data, ticket trends and performance indicators to identify risks, inefficiencies and improvement opportunities.

Client Relationship Management and Service Governance

Take ownership of operational service-delivery relationships with assigned business customers.

Build and maintain strong relationships with customers, senior stakeholders and key decision-makers.

Conduct regular customer service reviews, governance meetings and Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs), where applicable.

Prepare and present accurate service-performance reports, operational dashboards and service-improvement updates.

Manage customer escalations, service risks, outstanding commitments and recurring service concerns.

Monitor customer satisfaction and proactively address issues affecting service quality and customer confidence.

Coordinate corrective actions and service-improvement initiatives where delivery falls below agreed expectations.

Support customer retention through consistent service delivery, proactive communication and effective relationship management.

Sales-to-Delivery Governance and Service Transition

Oversee the transition of customer commitments from Sales into Delivery and ongoing managed-services support.

Review sales-to-delivery handovers to ensure scope, customer requirements, dependencies, technical input, resources, documentation and acceptance criteria are clearly defined.

Identify and challenge incomplete or unrealistic delivery commitments before implementation.

Coordinate Sales, Finance, Technical Operations and Delivery stakeholders to ensure customer requirements are understood and achievable.

Ensure new customer services are appropriately onboarded, documented and transitioned into business-as-usual (BAU) support.

Maintain visibility of delivery commitments, outstanding dependencies and operational readiness requirements.

Technical Operations, Infrastructure and Monitoring Governance

Coordinate with technical teams responsible for infrastructure, networking, Microsoft 365, cloud environments, backup, cybersecurity and other managed services.

Maintain operational visibility of infrastructure stability, service availability, technical risks and dependencies affecting customer commitments.

Ensure monitoring and alerting platforms support effective incident response and proactive service management.

Work with technical specialists to address duplicate, repetitive, suppressed or unnecessary monitoring alerts.

Encourage automation and proactive problem management to reduce recurring incidents and operational inefficiencies.

Coordinate appropriate responses to critical infrastructure issues, security vulnerabilities and service disruptions.

Ensure backup, disaster recovery and business continuity considerations are incorporated into operational service governance.

Track material technical actions and escalate unresolved issues that may affect customer service outcomes or SLA performance.

Project Coordination, Change Management and Service Delivery

Maintain oversight of active IT projects, service changes, customer onboarding activities and material remedial work.

Ensure projects and changes have clearly defined scope, ownership, dependencies, risks, timelines and acceptance requirements.

Coordinate technical and operational resources to support project implementation and service delivery.

Monitor delivery commitments and identify resource or technical dependencies that may affect project outcomes.

Challenge delivery timelines or commitments where capacity constraints or unresolved dependencies create operational risks.

Support change governance and ensure appropriate assessment of operational risks before implementation.

Ensure completed projects and changes are effectively transitioned into ongoing support and managed-services operations.

Maintain accountability for outstanding actions, project-related service risks and operational handovers.

People Leadership and Resource Management

Lead, coordinate and manage Service Desk, operational support and allocated delivery resources.

Establish clear performance expectations, responsibilities and accountability across operational teams.

Manage workforce planning, resource allocation, utilisation, scheduling, leave, standby arrangements and customer coverage.

Identify capacity constraints, capability gaps and operational resilience risks.

Coordinate resources across multiple technical and operational teams to meet customer commitments.

Support employee coaching, training, certification and professional development.

Identify recruitment, succession planning and additional resource requirements.

Address performance concerns and implement appropriate operational or development interventions.

Work with Technical Operations to ensure appropriate secondary and tertiary technical coverage for critical services.

Commercial Management and Operational Performance

Maintain awareness of service-delivery costs, profitability, contractual commitments and operational resource requirements.

Monitor operational budgets, resource utilisation, unbilled effort, billing leakage and material service-delivery cost concerns.

Ensure customer commitments remain aligned with agreed contractual scope, delivery capacity and commercial requirements.

Coordinate with Finance, Sales and operational stakeholders to resolve commercial issues affecting service delivery.

Identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency, resource utilisation and service profitability.

Support commercially responsible decision-making while maintaining customer service quality.

Reporting, Documentation and Continuous Service Improvement

Develop and maintain meaningful operational reporting, dashboards, service metrics and Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

Monitor SLA performance, service availability, incident trends, customer satisfaction and operational risks.

Use service-management information and performance trends to support evidence-based decision-making.

Conduct daily operational reviews covering major incidents, priority tickets, SLA risks, resource constraints and urgent customer commitments.

Facilitate weekly reviews of Service Desk performance, projects, changes, recurring issues, customer risks and outstanding actions.

Review monthly service trends, operational performance, customer reporting, commercial concerns and resource requirements.

Support quarterly service reviews and formal continuous-service-improvement initiatives.

Ensure customer and service documentation is accurate, accessible and appropriately maintained.

Identify documentation gaps and establish clear accountability for corrective actions.

Drive measurable improvements in service reliability, operational processes, customer satisfaction and overall service-delivery performance.

Desired Experience & Qualification

Qualifications

Relevant tertiary qualification in Information Technology, Business, Service Management or a related discipline.

ITIL Foundation certification or equivalent service-management knowledge.

Additional ITIL, Project Management, Microsoft, Cloud or Service Management certifications will be advantageous.

Experience

Proven experience in IT Service Delivery, IT Operations Management or Managed Services Operations.

Demonstrated experience managing IT Service Desk operations within an SLA-driven service environment.

Experience leading multidisciplinary technical and operational teams, with responsibility for service performance, resource coordination and operational accountability.

Experience overseeing managed IT services, infrastructure operations and ongoing technical support environments.

Strong experience in IT Service Management (ITSM), including Incident Management, Major Incident Management, Problem Management, Change Management and service improvement.

Experience managing SLAs, operational performance indicators, escalations and service-delivery risks.

Experience engaging directly with business customers, senior management and executive-level stakeholders.

Demonstrated ability to manage customer service reviews, operational reporting, service escalations and ongoing customer commitments.

Experience coordinating technical teams, operational resources, projects and service transitions.

Experience in workforce planning, capacity management, resource allocation and operational scheduling.

Commercial awareness, including experience with operational budgets, service costs, contractual commitments and resource utilisation.

Experience producing and interpreting service-performance reports, operational dashboards, KPIs and service trends.

Experience identifying recurring operational issues and implementing measurable service improvements.

Experience within a Managed Service Provider (MSP) or comparable IT service-delivery environment will be advantageous.

Technical and Systems Knowledge

A practical working understanding of:

IT Service Management (ITSM) and Professional Services Automation (PSA) platforms.

ITIL service-management frameworks and operational governance practices.

Microsoft 365 and cloud-based IT environments.

IT infrastructure, networking, server environments and endpoint support.

Infrastructure monitoring, service availability and performance management.

Remote Monitoring and Management (RMM) platforms.

Backup, disaster recovery and business continuity.

Cybersecurity services, vulnerability management and associated operational dependencies.

Service Desk ticketing systems, incident tracking and escalation processes.

Operational dashboards, SLA reporting and service-performance metrics.

IT project coordination, change governance and service transition processes.

 

A strong practical understanding of IT infrastructure and managed-services environments is essential to effectively oversee technical delivery, identify operational risks, challenge technical issues appropriately and coordinate specialist resources.

Core Competencies

Strong operational leadership with a high level of ownership and accountability.

Excellent customer relationship management and stakeholder engagement skills.

Ability to manage competing operational priorities, major incidents and customer commitments.

Strong people leadership, delegation, coaching and performance-management capabilities.

Commercial awareness and the ability to balance service quality with operational costs and contractual requirements.

Analytical and evidence-based decision-making, supported by service metrics and operational reporting.

Excellent communication, problem-solving and organisational skills.

Ability to coordinate multidisciplinary teams and ensure accountability for service outcomes.

Proactive approach to operational risk management, service reliability and continuous improvement.

Strong customer focus with a commitment to service excellence and long-term customer satisfaction.
