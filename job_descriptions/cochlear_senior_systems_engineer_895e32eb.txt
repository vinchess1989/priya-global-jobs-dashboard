We are seeking an experienced Senior Systems Engineer to support the development of complex medical-device technologies while championing cybersecurity practices across R&D.

Working closely with systems, software, risk and cybersecurity specialists, you will combine hands-on systems engineering with helping product teams embed cybersecurity requirements and controls into everyday product development.






Key responsibilities



Lead and contribute to systems engineering activities, including requirements development, traceability, system design, integration and verification.
Champion cybersecurity processes and secure-by-design practices across R&D.
Review and assess threat-modelling and cybersecurity risk-analysis outputs, helping translate identified risks into appropriate product requirements and controls.
Ensure cybersecurity requirements and controls are appropriately implemented, traced, verified and supported by evidence.
Participate in architecture, design and technical reviews.
Work across R&D as a key point of contact for product cybersecurity matters, engaging specialist teams where required.
Support product issues, CAPAs, technical investigations, regulatory submissions and audits.
Provide practical cybersecurity guidance and knowledge sharing across engineering teams.





About you



A degree in engineering, computer science or a related discipline.
5+ years' engineering experience in R&D or product development.
Strong systems engineering experience across requirements, traceability, system design and verification.
Experience applying cybersecurity principles within product or software development.
The ability to interpret threat models, cybersecurity risk assessments and security controls.
Experience working with connected, embedded, mobile, software or cloud-enabled products.
A good understanding of secure-by-design and secure software development practices.
Experience within medical devices or another regulated or safety-critical industry.
Strong stakeholder engagement and technical communication skills.


Experience with ISO 14971, IEC 62304, IEC 81001-5-1, FDA medical-device cybersecurity guidance, SaMD, SBOMs or certifications such as CSSLP or CISSP would be highly regarded.

Additional hands-on experience conducting cybersecurity risk analysis or threat modelling would be advantageous but is not essential.

If you're an experienced systems engineer with strong engineering fundamentals and practical cybersecurity knowledge, we'd like to hear from you.
