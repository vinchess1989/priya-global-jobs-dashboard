🤖🏙️ AI Implementation Manager | AI & SaaS | Regulated Technology 🏙️🤖




Role: AI Implementation Manager

Location: London - 3 days p/w

Salary: up to £80,000 + bonus




One of the world’s most prestigious independent financial advisory firms is making a serious, long-term investment in AI.




They have already hired senior leaders across AI, product and responsible AI. The next step is turning that strategy into technology that people across the business can actually use.




They’re looking for an AI Implementation Manager to introduce specialist AI platforms across areas such as company research, document analysis, meeting preparation, relationship intelligence and deal execution.




This is a hands-on AI implementation role. You’ll take new tools and features from initial assessment through to production, owning:




Implementation planning and technical documentation
Platform configuration and integrations
Testing, user access and rollout
Vendor and stakeholder management
Adoption and continuous improvement
AI governance, security and regulatory controls




You do not need to come directly from investment banking. They’re open to people from financial services, legal, insurance, healthcare or other regulated and highly controlled environments where sensitive data, user permissions, auditability and AI guardrails really matter.




You’ll ideally bring:

Experience delivering SaaS or enterprise technology implementations
Hands-on exposure to implementing AI or AI-enabled platforms
A practical understanding of integrations, data flows, testing and technical documentation
Experience working with vendors, IT, security, compliance and business users
Good judgement around data, governance and regulatory risk




This is a genuinely unusual opportunity to help shape how AI is introduced into a globally recognised investment-banking business, working closely with senior AI leadership while taking meaningful ownership of a growing implementation portfolio.




If you’ve helped introduce AI technology into a regulated environment and want to take that experience into investment banking, please apply or message me directly.
