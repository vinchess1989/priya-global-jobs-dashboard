Position Overview

We are seeking a talented DevOps Engineer with minimum 2+ year of practical experience to join our team. As a DevOps Engineer, you will collaborate closely with experienced DevOps professionals to support the automation, deployment, and maintenance of our software systems and infrastructure. This role offers an excellent opportunity for individuals looking to advance their career in DevOps and gain hands-on experience in a dynamic environment.

Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field (or equivalent experience).

2 year of practical experience in a DevOps or related role.

Experience with scripting languages such as Python, Bash, or PowerShell.

Familiarity with cloud computing platforms (e.g., AWS, Azure, Google Cloud).

Exposure to containerization technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes) is a plus.

Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work effectively in a team environment.

Eagerness to learn and adapt to new technologies and methodologies.

What We Provide

Opportunities for professional growth and advancement within the organization.

Mentorship from experienced DevOps professionals.

Collaborative and supportive work culture.

Exposure to cutting-edge technologies and industry best practices.

If you are passionate about DevOps and eager to advance your career in a dynamic and supportive environment, we encourage you to apply and join our team!
