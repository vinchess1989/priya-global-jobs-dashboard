DevOps Lead – MES Program | Limerick (Pharma/Life Sciences)




Are you passionate about DevOps in regulated environments? We’re seeking a hands-on DevOps Lead to shape and support the engineering and delivery practices behind our Manufacturing Execution System (MES) program.




In this role, you will:

Build and manage CI/CD pipelines for fast, reliable MES deployments
Oversee DevOps toolchains, automation, and cloud/on-prem infrastructure
Collaborate with vendors and cross-functional teams to drive release, configuration, and system health
Translate platform architecture into automated, maintainable solutions
Ensure robust monitoring, audit trails, and compliance with industry standards
Proactively improve deployment speed, frequency, and resilience




What you bring:

Proven DevOps leadership in complex, enterprise settings (regulated industry experience a plus)
Strong skills with CI/CD toolsets (Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, etc.)
Scripting and infrastructure-as-code expertise
MES platform (especially Syncade) or other enterprise application experience strongly preferred
Excellent problem-solving, communication, and team skills




Ready to drive digital transformation at the heart of pharmaceutical manufacturing? Apply now to join our Limerick team!
