ABOUT THE POSITION

A senior-level Platform Engineering opportunity for an experienced engineer with a strong track record of building scalable, production-grade platforms and self-service capabilities for engineering teams. The ideal candidate will bring deep expertise across AWS, Kubernetes, GitOps, Infrastructure as Code, automation and internal developer platforms, with a strong focus on building reliable, scalable and cloud native solutions.


Expert Knowledge 


AWS cloud architecture and services, with strong understanding of AWS best practices and the Well-Architected Framework.

Building platform APIs, automation, system-level tooling and/or internal developer platforms.

Infrastructure as Code tooling and practices, including Terraform, CDK, Pulumi and Crossplane.

Container orchestration principles and Kubernetes; CKA certification preferable.

Cloud-native systems, distributed systems and service architectures, including associated design trade-offs.

Building and operating scalable, secure and highly available platforms within AWS environments.



RequirementsRequirements 

5+ years of experience in a Platform Engineering capacity – building self-service platforms and abstractions used by other engineering teams.

2 - 3+ years of experience as a Software Engineer using languages such as Java, JavaScript, Golang or Python.

6+ years of experience working with Public Cloud, with strong hands-on AWS experience.

6+ years of experience with containerisation and container platforms.

Strong experience with Kubernetes GitOps practices using ArgoCD / Flux and Helm/Kustomize.

Strong experience implementing monitoring and observability solutions using tools such as CloudWatch, Prometheus, Grafana, Loki and X-Ray.

Strong understanding of DevOps practices and CI/CD tools such as GitHub Actions and GitLab CI/CD.

Proven experience building complex, production-grade platforms and making these available for self-service consumption by engineering teams.

Hands-on experience with Crossplane, ArgoCD and Backstage is non negotiable. 

Proven experience building Kubernetes Operators is non-negotiable.
