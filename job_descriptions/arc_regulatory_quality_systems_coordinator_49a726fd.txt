Description

ARC Regulatory combines specialist IVD/CDx clinical research expertise with laboratory services and software solutions to support Sponsors across the clinical trial lifecycle and accelerate global precision medicine studies.

Through its Dx CRO and Sponsor Delegation services, ARC supports the planning, delivery and oversight of global CDx clinical trials, acting as an extension of the Sponsor team and providing clear ownership and accountability across delegated activities.

ARC Laboratories, based in Belfast, Northern Ireland, provides in-house assay and clinical trial sample testing capabilities across IHC, qPCR, ELISA and NGS-based testing. The laboratory is UKAS ISO 15189 and CAP accredited, providing high-quality biomarker testing for precision medicine clinical trials.

ARC’s Health Institution status, under the IVDR Article 5(5), provides a practical route for early-phase clinical trial sample testing in the EU and UK, helping reduce regulatory burden and enable faster sample testing for eligible studies.

ARC also combines its regulatory expertise with technology through ARC360® powered by AURA AI. ARC360® is a regulatory intelligence and submission collaboration platform scoped specifically for CDx clinical trials, bringing national regulatory requirements, submission workflows, document generation and project reporting into one place. AURA AI capabilities combine the speed of AI with human expertise to support structured, traceable and auditable regulatory outputs.

By connecting clinical research, laboratory and technology expertise, ARC helps Sponsors reduce complexity, manage risk and maintain control across their global precision medicine clinical trials.

Job Purpose

As Quality Systems Coordinator, you will report to the Head of Quality and help maintain and improve ARC’s quality management systems. Your work will focus on document control, internal and external audits, corrective and preventive actions (CAPAs) and supplier quality.

You will work with teams across ARC Regulatory and ARC Laboratories, supporting the ISO 13485 and ISO 15189 quality management systems and the ISO 27001 information security management system. You will help teams keep their records audit-ready, follow up on quality issues and put improvements into practice.

Responsibilities


Support the day-to-day running of the electronic quality management system (eQMS), including document creation, change requests, review workflows, version control and archiving.
Keep quality records and training files accurate and audit-ready, including laboratory standard operating procedures (SOPs) and validation records.
Plan and conduct scheduled internal audits, with guidance as needed, and clearly document nonconformities and opportunities for improvement.
Prepare evidence and documentation for external audits and inspections, including UKAS, CAP and client audits, and support teams during those audits.
Log and track nonconformities and CAPAs, contribute to root cause investigations and follow up with action owners to support timely closure.
Maintain approved supplier records and support supplier onboarding, qualification, performance monitoring and periodic reviews.
Compile quality metrics and prepare information for Management Review meetings.
Assist with preparing, reviewing and maintaining regulatory documentation, including technical files, submissions and supporting records.
Work with colleagues across ARC Regulatory and ARC Laboratories to improve quality processes and ensure documentation reflects applicable requirements.


Essential Criteria


A degree in engineering or a scientific discipline, or equivalent relevant industry experience.
Experience working in an IVD or medical device environment operating to Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) or Good Laboratory Practice (GLP).
Experience implementing and maintaining a quality management system.
Experience developing, reviewing and maintaining SOPs and quality records.
Experience conducting internal audits and hosting regulatory audits or inspections.
Experience managing nonconformities and corrective and preventive actions.
Experience onboarding, monitoring and evaluating suppliers.
Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to present information clearly and influence others.
Strong analytical, problem-solving and decision-making skills.
Strong planning, organisational and time management skills, with the ability to take ownership of work and deliver independently.
Ability to work collaboratively and a willingness to develop across both regulatory and laboratory quality environments.
Proficiency with word processing, spreadsheet, database and presentation software.


Desirable Criteria


Knowledge of ISO 13485, ISO 14971 or ISO 15189 would be an advantage, as would experience maintaining a QMS to ISO 13485 or ISO 15189.
ISO Lead Auditor experience
Good Clinical Practice (GCP) certification
Experience with electronic quality, document or audit management systems would also be welcomed.


Why Join ARC Regulatory?

At ARC, you’ll work across medical device and laboratory quality systems and gain exposure to information security. You’ll help teams prepare for audits, resolve quality issues and improve the processes that support global precision medicine studies.

We offer the following benefits.


A competitive salary, depending on experience.
32 days of annual leave, increasing with service to a maximum of 35 days.
Employer pension contributions.
An employee benefits programme, including private medical cover after probation.
Opportunities to develop your experience across regulatory, laboratory and information security standards.
