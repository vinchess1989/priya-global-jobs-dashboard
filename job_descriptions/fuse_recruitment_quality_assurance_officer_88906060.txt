Quality Assurance Officer
Park Ridge | $80,000 – $90,000 + Super | Permanent Full-Time
About the Company
Our client is an established and highly specialised manufacturing organisation with a strong focus on quality, innovation and advanced production. With decades of industry experience and operations across multiple international markets, the business combines manufacturing, engineering and research & development capabilities to deliver high-quality products to its customers.

With a strong emphasis on quality, compliance and continuous improvement, this is an opportunity to join a technically driven organisation operating within a highly regulated manufacturing environment.

About the Role
We’re looking for a detail-driven and quality-focused Quality Assurance Officer to join the team at our client's facility in Park Ridge.

In this role, you’ll help ensure products consistently meet the required standards for quality, safety, compliance and performance. You’ll work closely with production and technical teams to identify issues, maintain quality systems and drive continuous improvement across the manufacturing environment.

Key Responsibilities

Maintain and support the organisation’s Quality Management System
Conduct quality inspections, checks and audits across manufacturing processes
Investigate non-conformances and assist with corrective and preventative actions
Monitor product quality and ensure compliance with relevant standards and specifications
Maintain quality documentation, records and reporting
Assist with internal audits and continuous improvement initiatives
Work closely with production and engineering teams to identify and resolve quality issues
Support the development and review of procedures, work instructions and quality documentation
Identify opportunities to improve processes, consistency and overall product quality
Ensure quality requirements are understood and followed across the production environment
About You
We’re looking for someone who is hands-on, detail-oriented and genuinely passionate about quality, ideally with experience in a regulated manufacturing environment.
You will ideally have:

Previous experience in a Quality Assurance / Quality Control role within manufacturing
Experience in medical device manufacturing or another highly regulated manufacturing environment
Strong understanding of Quality Management Systems
Experience working with ISO 13485 or similar quality standards
Knowledge of GMP and regulated manufacturing requirements
Experience managing non-conformances, CAPA and corrective actions
Experience with internal audits and quality investigations
Strong attention to detail and a methodical approach to problem solving
Excellent communication and documentation skills
The ability to work collaboratively with production, engineering and technical teams
Medical device manufacturing experience will be highly regarded, particularly candidates who have worked within an ISO 13485 environment and understand the importance of traceability, documentation, risk management and maintaining strict quality standards.

What’s on Offer?

$80,000 – $100,000 + Super
Salary flexibility for the right candidate with strong industry experience
Permanent full-time opportunity
Park Ridge location
Join an established and technically focused manufacturing organisation
Opportunity to work across quality, compliance and continuous improvement
Work alongside experienced manufacturing, engineering and technical teams
If you’re a Quality Assurance professional with experience in medical device or regulated manufacturing and are looking for your next opportunity, we’d love to hear from you.
