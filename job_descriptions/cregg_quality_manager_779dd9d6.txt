Quality Manager

Excellent opportunity for an excellent Quality Manager to join a thriving company in Co. Limerick

The Quality Manager will lead Receiving Inspection Engineering and Inspection Operations to ensure incoming components and devices meet approved specifications, regulatory requirements and company quality standards. The Quality Manager with have overall accountability for Engineers and inspector performance, compliance, capability, capacity and continuous improvement.

Key Responsibilities:

Define and review metrics for safety, quality, compliance, service, capacity, productivity, ageing and right-first-time performance.
Ensure nonconforming incoming material is identified, contained, documented, assessed and escalated in a timely, risk-based manner.
Provide oversight of inspection methods, measurement systems, equipment, validation and technical problem-solving.
Maintain audit readiness and ensure effective implementation of changes involving new products, suppliers, components or inspection methods.
Ensure inspection records, system transactions, material identification, segregation, storage and movement meet approved requirements.
Lead projects and continuous-improvement initiatives that strengthen compliance, improve material flow and reduce inspection cycle time.
Partner with Operations, Supply Chain, Supplier Quality, Engineering and Quality Systems to resolve issues and support business priorities.

Key Requirements:

Bachelor’s degree in Science, Engineering or a related technical field, with relevant quality engineering, manufacturing quality or receiving inspection experience.
Previous experience in Quality Management & People Management essential
Experience supporting internal audits and external regulatory inspections in a medical-device
Experience with inspection operations, measurement systems, technical drawings and specifications, including GD&T.
Demonstrated delivery of cross-functional projects and operational improvements.
Excellent communication, negotiation, problem-solving and stakeholder-management skills.
Ability to use performance metrics, Lean and Six Sigma methods to improve compliance, service, flow and productivity.
Proficiency with Microsoft Office and relevant quality or manufacturing systems, such as JDE or equivalent.
