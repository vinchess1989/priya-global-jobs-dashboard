We are looking for candidates with 5+ years of experience in Quality Assurance and Quality Systems within the medical device industry. Strong knowledge of ISO 13485, Singapore HSA regulations, and hands-on experience with GDP/GDPMDS certification, QMS implementation, and audit management are essential.

Contract Duration - 12 months

Must-Have Requirements

Bachelor's degree in Life Sciences or related field
5+ years' experience in Quality Assurance and/or Quality Systems within the medical device industry
Experience implementing or improving ISO 13485-based QMS
Strong knowledge of Singapore medical device regulatory requirements
Proven experience supporting GDP/MDS/GDP certification projects
Experience obtaining and maintaining GDP certification
Stakeholder management with certification/accreditation bodies and regulatory authorities
Microsoft Office proficiency
Strong experience with ISO 13485 Quality Management Systems
Hands-on experience with GDP/GDPMDS certification and compliance activities
Strong knowledge of Singapore HSA medical device regulations
Experience leading external audits, certification audits, and regulatory inspections
Proven experience obtaining and maintaining GDP/GDPMDS certifications
Experience managing CAPA, deviations, non-conformance, and complaint investigations
Internal and supplier audit management experience
Strong stakeholder management with certification bodies and regulatory authorities




Responsibilities

Lead implementation, maintenance, and continuous improvement of QMS
Ensure compliance with ISO 13485, GDPMDS, and Singapore regulatory requirements
Drive GDP/GDPMDS certification readiness and audit support
Conduct gap assessments and remediation activities
Manage quality documentation, SOPs, and work instructions
Lead internal audits and supplier quality programs
Support regulatory inspections and compliance initiatives
Deliver quality training and drive continuous improvement programs

Education

Bachelor's Degree in Life Sciences, Biomedical Sciences, Pharmacy, Engineering, Quality Management, or related field
