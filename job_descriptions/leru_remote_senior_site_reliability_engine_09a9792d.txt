Senior Site Reliability Engineer

Microsoft Azure | IoT | Observability | Global Technology




Leru Remote is partnering with an exceptional technology client to identify a Senior Site Reliability Engineer to join their growing global engineering and support team.

Our client operates within an exciting technology environment, developing and delivering innovative IoT-enabled solutions that help businesses connect, monitor and manage critical operations.




With a strong focus on technology, service excellence and continuous improvement, they are looking for an experienced engineer who can take ownership of application reliability, strengthen observability and help deliver an outstanding experience for their customers.

This is an opportunity to work closely with a major client, take ownership of a live technology environment and collaborate with talented colleagues across an international team.




The Opportunity




As Senior Site Reliability Engineer, you'll play a key role in maintaining and improving the performance, availability and reliability of critical applications and infrastructure.

You'll combine hands-on technical troubleshooting with proactive monitoring, incident management and continuous improvement.




Your responsibilities will include:




Taking ownership of the technical health and stability of client applications.
Working across Microsoft Azure and Azure App Services to troubleshoot and resolve technical issues.
Developing and improving observability using Grafana, including dashboards, alerts and monitoring.
Investigating incidents, leading Root Cause Analysis and implementing preventative solutions.
Working directly with client stakeholders to improve application and infrastructure monitoring.
Maintaining technical documentation, runbooks and knowledge resources.
Supporting and mentoring colleagues, sharing technical knowledge and best practices.
Participating in a global support rotation covering UK and US working hours and weekends.




What you'll bring




We're looking for someone who combines strong technical capability with a proactive, customer-focused approach.




Core technical experience:




Microsoft Azure, particularly Azure App Services.
Observability, monitoring and incident management.
Grafana dashboards and alert configuration.
Linux-based operating systems.
Technical application support, service desk or SRE experience.
Ticketing platforms such as ServiceNow or Zendesk.
Strong written and verbal communication skills.




Additional experience that would be advantageous:




Azure IoT.
Scripting with Kotlin, JavaScript or Shell.
Java, C# or TypeScript development.
Git, GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket.
Network security, infrastructure management and authentication.




Why join?




This is more than a traditional technical support role. You'll have the opportunity to influence the reliability of a live technology platform, work directly with client stakeholders and help shape the monitoring and support practices that underpin its success.

You'll be part of an international team where technical ownership, collaboration and continuous development are valued.

If you're passionate about technology, enjoy solving complex problems and want to make a tangible difference to the performance and reliability of critical systems, we'd love to hear from you.




Interested in finding out more?

Apply directly or contact Leru Remote for a confidential conversation.
