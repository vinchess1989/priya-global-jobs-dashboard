Job Description

In this role, you will be responsible for designing, implementing, and managing cloud infrastructure and DevOps solutions to enhance the scalability, security, and efficiency of digital platforms. You will work closely with engineering teams to automate deployment, monitoring, and maintenance processes, ensuring seamless and reliable operations.

Your Impact


Design and implement cloud infrastructure solutions using best practices in AWS, Azure, or GCP.
Develop and maintain CI/CD pipelines to automate application deployment and testing.
Collaborate with engineering teams to optimize platform performance, scalability, and security.
Implement infrastructure as code (IaC) using tools like Terraform, CloudFormation, or ARM templates.
Ensure high availability, disaster recovery, and compliance of cloud-based systems.
Monitor and troubleshoot platform issues, ensuring minimal downtime and quick resolution.
Drive automation of repetitive tasks to improve operational efficiency.
Implement security best practices, including access control, encryption, and vulnerability management.
Work with cross-functional teams to align DevOps practices with business objectives.
Provide technical leadership and mentorship to junior team members.


Skills & Experience


8+ years of experience in DevOps, cloud infrastructure, or platform engineering.
Strong expertise in cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or GCP.
Hands-on experience with CI/CD tools like Jenkins, GitLab, or Azure DevOps.
Proficiency in scripting (Python, Shell, or PowerShell) and automation frameworks.
Experience with containerization and orchestration tools (Docker, Kubernetes).
Knowledge of monitoring tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, or CloudWatch.
Strong understanding of networking, security, and compliance in cloud environments.
Ability to work in an Agile environment and collaborate with cross-functional teams.
Excellent problem-solving, communication, and leadership skills.


Set Yourself Apart With


Certifications in AWS (DevOps Engineer, Solutions Architect), Azure (DevOps Engineer, Solutions Architect), or GCP.
Experience with serverless computing, microservices architecture, and infrastructure automation.
Knowledge of DevSecOps practices and security automation.
Background in large-scale enterprise cloud migrations and transformations.


Additional Information


An inclusive workplace that promotes diversity and collaboration.
Access to ongoing learning and development opportunities.
Competitive compensation and benefits package.
Flexibility to support work-life balance.
Generous paid leave and holidays.
Wellness program and employee assistance.
