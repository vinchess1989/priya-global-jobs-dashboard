Job Title

Senior DevOps Engineer – Azure & Kubernetes

Experience

5+ Years

Job Summary

We are looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer to design, implement, and manage enterprise DevOps platforms, cloud infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and container orchestration environments. The ideal candidate will have deep expertise in Azure cloud services, Kubernetes, automation, infrastructure management, monitoring, and observability tools.

Key Responsibilities

Design, build, and maintain scalable DevOps platforms and CI/CD pipelines.
Automate infrastructure provisioning, deployment, monitoring, and operational processes.
Manage Azure cloud environments ensuring security, availability, and performance.
Implement containerization and orchestration solutions using Docker and Kubernetes.
Develop and maintain deployment automation using Helm and Ansible.
Build and support monitoring, logging, and alerting frameworks.
Troubleshoot complex production and infrastructure issues.
Collaborate with development teams to improve release processes and platform reliability.
Implement DevSecOps and infrastructure-as-code best practices.
Ensure platform compliance, security, and operational excellence.
