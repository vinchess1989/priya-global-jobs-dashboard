Job Description

Location: Bristol (Hybrid)

In this position, you’ll be based in the Bristol office for a minimum of three days a week, with the flexibility to work from home for some of your working week. Find out more about our flexible work culture at computershare.com/flex .

This position is a fixed term contract until 31st March 2028.

We give you a world of potential

Computershare's Finance & People Teams use Oracle Fusion (incorporating ERP, EPM & HCM) to manage the product and employee lifecycle. To help embed the effective use of the system and associated processes, we need to manage the knowledge carefully, ensuring changes and enhancements are effectively supported and communicated to employees.

The successful candidate will have the opportunity to support effective management of knowledge for both the People and Finance teams.

A role you will love

We are looking for a Knowledge Analyst to help shape and enhance the way employees and managers interact with our Oracle platform and related processes. As part of the Service Excellence team, you will drive system adoption, support change initiatives, and identify opportunities for continuous improvement that deliver a seamless user experience. You will play a vital role in building user capability through effective knowledge management, change impact assessments, and targeted support, ensuring People and Finance subject matter experts remain confident and competent in using our systems and processes.

Key Responsibilities


Manage and optimise Oracle AI Agents, knowledge content, and digital guidance tools.
Create and maintain knowledge articles, technical documentation, and self-service resources.
Design and configure Oracle Guided Learning (OGL) content, Guided Journeys, Smart Tips, and in-app guidance.
Assess the impact of Oracle releases and process changes, implementing effective user adoption solutions.
Lead digital change communications and support user adoption across People & Finance systems.
Collaborate with stakeholders to deliver user-focused knowledge and learning solutions.
Monitor analytics, user feedback, and system performance to drive continuous improvement.
Maintain governance of knowledge platforms, including SharePoint and Oracle Knowledge.
Act as the subject matter expert for Knowledge Management and Oracle Guided Learning.
Work with Learning and Training teams to ensure content remains accurate and up to date.
Identify and implement new Oracle capabilities that improve user experience and self-service.
Own and deliver the Knowledge Management roadmap, driving improvements in service and adoption.


What will you bring to the role?


Experience creating and maintaining clear technical documentation, process maps, and user guidance.
Strong understanding of business systems, workflows, and change impacts.
Experience translating complex technical information into user-friendly content.
Knowledge of Oracle Fusion HCM; Oracle ERP, AI Agents, and Oracle Guided Learning (OGL) experience is desirable.
Ability to interpret technical release notes and assess impacts on users and processes.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a focus on continuous improvement.
Excellent stakeholder management skills, including working with senior leaders and subject matter experts.
Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to influence and educate others.
Ability to manage multiple priorities, work independently, and deliver to tight deadlines.
Proactive self-learner with a commitment to maintaining system and industry knowledge.
Collaborative team player with strong questioning, listening, and relationship-building skills.
Experience of knowledge management platforms, SharePoint, digital adoption, or AI-driven support tools is desirable.
Experience working in a global organisation is desirable; Financial Services experience is advantageous.
Resilient, adaptable, and able to drive change in a fast-paced environment.
