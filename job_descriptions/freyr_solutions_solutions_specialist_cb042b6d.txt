Medical Device Regulatory & Solutioning

Experience: 5–10 Years

Location: India (Remote)

Employment Type: Full-time







Role Overview

We are looking for an experienced professional with strong hands-on expertise in Medical Device Regulatory Affairs, Quality Management Systems (QMS), Quality Assurance and Regulatory Medical Writing. The role involves leveraging domain expertise to support complex client requirements, develop solutions and contribute to proposals across the medical device regulatory and quality landscape.




Key Responsibilities

Provide domain expertise across Medical Device Regulatory Affairs, Quality Assurance, QMS and Regulatory Medical Writing.
Understand and assess client requirements across regulatory submissions, registrations, quality systems, compliance and post-market activities.
Develop and review regulatory and quality-related solutions, proposals, RFP/RFI responses and Statements of Work (SOWs).
Support solution development, effort estimation, pricing inputs and preparation of technical and commercial proposals.
Coordinate with clients, internal delivery teams, SMEs and cross-functional stakeholders to develop feasible solutions.
Participate in client discussions, requirement assessments and proposal presentations.
Review solution documents and proposals to ensure technical accuracy, quality and regulatory alignment.
Guide junior team members, manage deliverable quality and support timely execution of solutioning activities.




Mandatory Skills & Experience

5–10 years of relevant experience, preferably within the Medical Devices industry.
Strong hands-on experience in Medical Device Regulatory Affairs and related functions.
Good understanding of Regulatory Submissions, Regulatory Compliance, Quality Assurance and Quality Management Systems (QMS).
Experience in Regulatory Medical Writing and technical documentation.
Exposure to ISO 13485, EU MDR, FDA regulations, post-market surveillance, vigilance, complaint handling and regulatory compliance.
Understanding of solutioning, consulting, Presales, RFP/RFI responses and proposal development would be an added advantage.
Strong communication, stakeholder management, analytical and documentation skills.
Proficiency in MS Excel and PowerPoint.




Educational Qualification

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Pharmacy, Engineering, Life Sciences, Biomedical Sciences or a related discipline.
