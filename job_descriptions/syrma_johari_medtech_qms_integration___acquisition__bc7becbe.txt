Title - QMS Integration & Acquisition Quality Lead

Exp - 8 to 10yrs

Location - Pune (WFO Mandate)




Job Description

Harmonize acquired-entity quality processes and tools into a centralized medical-device QMS without losing product history, compliance evidence or operational control.




Roles & Responsibility

ISO 13485 QMS implementation and process-mapping expertise.
Hands-on CAPA, complaint handling, supplier/vendor qualification, design control and risk-management linkage.
Acquisition or multi-QMS integration experience.
eQMS workflow assessment and cross-tool linkage experience.
Map current and target QMS processes and identify gaps, duplication and transition risks.
Harmonize CAPA, complaint, supplier quality, design control and risk-management processes.
Define migration, ownership, training, effectiveness checks and cutover controls.
Review supplier onboarding from quality, security and regulatory perspectives.
Assess QMS/engineering tool overlaps and integration gaps as part of the broader tools rationalization exercise.
Recommend process and system changes without assuming existing or proposed tools are automatically fit for purpose.




Specific Skill

Bachelor’s in technology life sciences or Computer science with 4+ years of experience
Technical writing, English communication and teamwork.
Detail oriented, ability to read various standards and guidelines & define
EU MDR
M&A integration
Complaint centralization
Supplier security review




General Skill

Strong attention to detail and documentation accuracy.
Good understanding of engineering documentation practices.
Ability to work with cross-functional engineering teams.
Effective communication and documentation skills.
Critical thinking and problem-solving skills.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills.
Must be a Team Player.
