Technical Writer

Location: Daventry
Contract: 6-month contract

We are looking for an experienced Technical Writer to support Manufacturing Engineering activities within a fast-paced automotive environment.

The role will focus on creating, updating and controlling clear and accurate technical documentation used across manufacturing and production. This will include work instructions, SOPs, process documentation, assembly instructions and visual operator guidance.

Working closely with Manufacturing Engineers, Production, Quality and other technical teams, the Technical Writer will take engineering and manufacturing information and translate it into structured, easy-to-follow documentation that can be effectively used on the shop floor.

Key Responsibilities

Create, update and maintain technical manufacturing documentation, including work instructions, SOPs, process sheets, assembly instructions and supporting technical documents.
Translate engineering, manufacturing and process information into clear, concise and practical documentation for production teams.
Produce visual work instructions, operator guidance and step-by-step manufacturing and assembly documentation.
Work closely with Manufacturing Engineers and wider technical teams to capture manufacturing processes accurately.
Review existing documentation and improve its clarity, structure, consistency and technical accuracy.
Proofread and quality-check technical documents before release.
Maintain document revision histories and ensure current documentation is available while superseded information is appropriately controlled.
Support documentation requirements associated with new product introduction, manufacturing process changes and new technologies.
Capture and document changes to manufacturing processes, assemblies and working methods.
Support the preparation and maintenance of BOM information, manufacturing assemblies and engineering standards where required.
Organise and maintain technical documentation, engineering information and associated records within SharePoint.
Maintain relevant engineering databases, trackers and supporting technical records.

About You

To be successful as a Technical Writer, you will ideally have previous experience producing technical documentation within an automotive, aerospace, engineering or manufacturing environment.

You will also have:

Experience producing technical documentation within automotive, aerospace, engineering or manufacturing.
Experience creating work instructions, SOPs, assembly instructions and visual documentation.
A good understanding of manufacturing and production processes.
The ability to interpret engineering information and turn it into clear, easy-to-follow documentation.
Strong attention to detail and excellent written communication skills.
Experience with BOMs, engineering drawings and SharePoint would be beneficial.

The Opportunity

This is a great opportunity for a Technical Writer who enjoys working closely with engineering and production teams to create clear, practical manufacturing documentation.

If you have a strong technical writing background within automotive, manufacturing, aerospace or engineering, we’d be keen to hear from you.
