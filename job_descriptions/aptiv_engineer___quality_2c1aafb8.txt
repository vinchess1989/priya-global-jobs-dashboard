Aptiv offers the unique opportunity to transform the future of mobility and to be a global leader in an industry that’s changing the world. To us, nothing is impossible when you have the people with the passion to make anything possible.

Job Position: Engineer

Department: Quality

Primary Job Purpose:


Develop, implement and improve the department and product total quality system to ensure product specifications are met.
Facilitate quality improvement to the product and process by providing technical guidance in QE tools applications.
Perform system and or process audit to ensure quality system compliance.
Develop and implement preventive quality initiatives to achieve customer quality target.
Lead Material Review Board meeting on disposition of non-conforming material and drive for actions to resolve non-conforming case.
Monitor First Time Quality reviews and drive for improvement on top issues.
Respond on customer returns and drive for effective actions to address customer returns.
Drive for achieve customer satisfaction and meeting targets in customer scorecard.


Job Requirements:


Degree in Electronics/Mechanical/Compute Engineering/Physics/Material Science.
Experience in a semiconductor or automotive electronics manufacturing environment is preferred.
Experience in application of quality engineering tools, Six sigma, quality system implementation (TS 16949/QS 9000/ ISO 9000), reliability or failure analysis will be an added advantage.
Good interpersonal, leadership, analytical and communication skills.
Fresh graduates are welcome.


Reports To: Manager
