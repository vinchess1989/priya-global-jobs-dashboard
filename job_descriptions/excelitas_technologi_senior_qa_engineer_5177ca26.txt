Job Description


Manage product quality, qualification, design validation, and New Product Introduction (NPI) activities.
Develop and maintain quality documentation, including inspection plans, test procedures, SOPs, and work instructions.
Drive process improvement, production yield enhancement, and continuous improvement initiatives using statistical tools and Six Sigma methodologies.
Manage customer complaints, returns (RMA), corrective and preventive actions (CAPA), root cause analysis, and customer quality performance reporting.
Participate in Material Review Board (MRB) activities and manage nonconforming materials, product concessions, and deviations.
Maintain the site's Quality Management System (QMS) in accordance with applicable standards, including ISO 9001, AS9100, and ISO 13485.
Support customer, certification body, internal, and supplier audits.
Manage supplier quality issues, including supplier corrective actions, containment, documentation reviews, and compliance with customer-specific requirements.
Review and approve supplier quality documents, including First Article Inspection Reports (FAIR), Control Plans, GR&R, and Cpk reports.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams and suppliers to resolve quality issues and improve measurement system capabilities.
Provide guidance to technicians, engineers, and suppliers on quality requirements and continuous improvement initiatives.


Job Requirements


Degree in Electrical, Industrial, Mechanical, or Optical Engineering preferred. Diploma holders with over five years of relevant manufacturing experience will also be considered.
At least five years of manufacturing experience preferred, with exposure to life sciences, medical devices, semiconductors, optoelectronics, or avionics being an advantage.
Experience in customer and supplier quality management, NPI, product quality planning, and change control.
Knowledge of ISO 9001, AS9100, ISO 13485, and/or ISO 14971 is an advantage.
Familiarity with quality tools and methodologies, including SQC, root cause analysis, 8D, A3, GR&R, GD&T, Lean, Kaizen, and Six Sigma.
Understanding of MRB processes, design controls, and measurement system analysis.
ISO 9001 or AS9100 auditor certification is highly desirable. ASQ Certified Quality Engineer (CQE) or Six Sigma Black Belt certification is an advantage.
Proficient in Microsoft Office and Microsoft Project, with good written and verbal communication skills.
