This role supports the development, implementation, and management of a new incoming inspection process to ensure materials and components meet defined quality standards and technical specifications.




Key Responsibilities

With key team members, develop and implement incoming inspection plans, procedures, sampling methods, and acceptance criteria.
Establish and implement robust incoming inspection processes to ensure consistent quality control of materials and components.
Manage and coordinate projects related to incoming inspection implementation, ensuring delivery against agreed timelines and milestones.
Collaborate with metrology teams to define inspection methods, measurement techniques, and equipment requirements.
Support manufacturing line start-up, new product introduction, production ramp-up, and transition to commercialisation.
Ensure compliance with QMS requirements, including quality documentation, non-conformance management, corrective actions, and change control.
Engage with cross-functional stakeholders, including Quality, Engineering, Manufacturing, Procurement, Supplier Quality, and Supply Chain, to resolve quality issues and ensure process readiness.
Prepare and maintain technical documentation, including inspection plans, procedures, work instructions, and quality reports.
Identify quality risks, drive continuous improvement, and ensure incoming inspection processes remain effective and compliant.




Required Qualifications and Experience

Experience in incoming inspection and development of inspection plans is desirable.
Experience working with metrology and measurement systems is of benefit.
Knowledge of Quality Management Systems (QMS) and quality compliance requirements.
Project management experience and strong cross-functional stakeholder engagement skills.
Experience supporting production line start-up, ramp-up, and transition to commercial production.
Strong technical writing, analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
