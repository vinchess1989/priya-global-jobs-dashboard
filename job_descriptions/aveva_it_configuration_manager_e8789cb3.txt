Job Title: IT Configuration Manager

Location: Hyderabad, India or Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

Employment Type: Full Time

The Job:

The IT Configuration Manager is responsible for establishing, managing and continually improving Configuration Management processes and the ServiceNow CMDB. The role ensures that configuration data remains accurate, current and appropriately governed, providing IT teams with reliable information to support service management and informed decision-making.

The role has a global remit, covering all configuration item (CI) classes and relationships within the agreed scope. It works across technology teams and with service and data owners, with responsibility for governance and data quality.

Key Responsibilities


Own and maintain the Configuration Management policy, processes, standards and operating model.
Define CI scope, how CIs are managed through their lifecycle, and the baselines and relationships that must be recorded.
Keep the CMDB accurate, complete and up to date so it supports Change, Incident and Problem Management, Risk, Security and service reporting.
Set clear data ownership and responsibilities through a RACI, schedule certifications and audits, manage exceptions and run the forums where decisions are agreed.
Protect the integrity of CI data through consistent data quality controls across sources.
Make Configuration Management part of the Change Management process.
Plan and oversee configuration audits and verification checks, and follow up on the issues they identify.
Bring new services and CI domains into the CMDB, agreeing systems of record, data flows and minimum data requirements with their owners.
Report on data quality, certification completion and compliance with controls, using these measures to identify and follow through on improvements.
Lead and support the Configuration Management community, giving CMDB users, CI owners, data owners and service owners the guidance, training and updates they need.
Lead a small team of Configuration Management Analysts.
Reporting:
Data quality: progress on fixing issues, duplicate records and missing mandatory data.
Compliance with controls: CI updates made through Change Management, exceptions and certification completion.
Application coverage: percentage of applications mapped.
Service coverage: percentage of services mapped.

Essential Requirements


At least 5 years’ experience leading Configuration Management in a global organisation, including ownership of a ServiceNow CMDB.
Experience setting up and running Configuration Management governance, including standards, ownership, certification, audits and exception handling.
A track record of improving data quality and helping technology and service teams adopt and maintain Configuration Management practices.
Experience linking the CMDB and Configuration Management with Change, Incident, Problem and IT Asset Management processes.
The ability to lead discussions, influence decisions and balance competing priorities across teams and levels of seniority.
Strong written and verbal communication skills, the ability to work independently and see work through from start to finish, and experience managing relationships with a range of stakeholders.
