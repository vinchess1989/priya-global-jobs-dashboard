Electronics Engineering & Change Control Lead




Location: Hertfordshire

Salary: Up to £60,000 + 10% bonus




An established engineering and manufacturing business is looking for an experienced electronics engineering professional to lead its engineering change function, overseeing product changes, BOM management and technical documentation across a range of electro-mechanical products.

This is a hands-on leadership role, combining electronics engineering knowledge, engineering change coordination and team management.




The Role

Lead and develop the engineering change team, overseeing day-to-day activities and departmental delivery.
Coordinate engineering changes from initial review and approval through to implementation.
Manage ECOs/ECNs, product revisions, BOMs and associated technical documentation.
Review and support modifications to 2D CAD drawings, PCB layouts, schematics and engineering documentation.
Work closely with electronics, mechanical engineering, manufacturing, quality and procurement teams.
Support electronic component changes, PCB updates, product improvements and production releases.
Use ERP/PLM systems to manage engineering changes and maintain accurate product records.
Ensure changes meet functional, manufacturing and compliance requirements.




About You

Previous experience in electronics engineering, electronics manufacturing, product engineering or a similar technical environment.
Experience managing or leading a team, with the ability to support, develop and motivate engineers.
Strong understanding of electronic products, PCB assemblies, components and manufacturing processes.
Practical professional experience using 2D CAD and interpreting engineering drawings, schematics and design documentation.
Experience managing engineering changes, BOM revisions, ECOs/ECNs and revision control.
Confident coordinating technical changes across engineering and wider business functions.
Organised, proactive and comfortable taking ownership of processes and departmental responsibilities.




Experience with Epicor, Altium or regulated products would be beneficial.




This is an opportunity to take ownership of an important engineering function, lead a team and improve engineering change processes across a varied electro-mechanical product range.




APPLY NOW – If you have an electronics engineering background, experience leading people and enjoy taking ownership of engineering changes and product improvements, we'd be keen to hear from you.
