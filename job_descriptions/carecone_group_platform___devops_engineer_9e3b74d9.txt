Role : Platform - Devops Engineer

Type of role - Permanent Position

Location : Sydney,Australia







Job Description :

Responsibilities:

Design, implement, and manage CI/CD pipelines to automate software delivery processes.
Collaborate with development teams to ensure seamless integration of new features and updates.
Monitor system performance and troubleshoot issues to maintain high availability.
Implement infrastructure as code (IaC) using tools such as Terraform or CloudFormation.
Manage cloud infrastructure on platforms like AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
Ensure security best practices are followed in all deployment processes.
Document processes, configurations, and best practices for team knowledge sharing.
Participate in on call rotations to support production systems.
Golden Paths & Standardized Delivery
Creates opinionated, reusable building blocks such as service templates, CI/CD pipeline primitives, Kubernetes baselines, and observability packages with clear upgrade and escape hatch strategies.
4. Self Service & GitOps Automation
Enables teams through APIs, CLIs, and GitOps workflows (e.g., ArgoCD), reducing manual approvals and support load while maintaining guardrails.
5. Containers & Multi Tenant Kubernetes Platforms
Deep understanding of containerized workloads, Helm based deployments, and platform level Kubernetes controls for isolation, quotas, and scalability across many teams.

Mandatory Skills:

Strong knowledge and hands on experience with CI/CD tools (e.g., Jenkins, GitLab CI, CircleCI).
Proficiency in scripting languages such as Python, Bash, or Ruby.
Experience with containerization technologies (e.g., Docker, Kubernetes).
Solid understanding of cloud services (AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud).
Familiarity with monitoring and logging tools (e.g., Prometheus, Grafana, ELK stack).
