The Document Control & Turnover Coordinator, Fabrication - Industrial West manages project documents and turnover records for BFL fabrication work. The role ensures current information is available to the people performing the work and that required records are complete, traceable and ready for client submission.

What You Will Be Working On


Communicate and apply the document-control and turnover requirements established for each project.
Receive, log, issue and distribute drawings, specifications, procedures, submittals and project correspondence.
Maintain drawing, document and revision registers.
Ensure current documents are available to BFL and external fabrication shops.
Control and archive superseded documents.
Prepare and track transmittals, submittals and required responses.
Maintain subcontractor document and deliverable registers.
Maintain turnover indexes and track completion status.
Compile and review fabrication and turnover records for completeness, consistency, accuracy and traceability, including material records and MTRs, weld mapping and weld logs, NDE records, coating records, calibration records, drawings and other required project documentation.
Review required fabrication documentation at project hold points and readiness gates, including before pressure testing and turnover, and identify and track missing or incomplete records to resolution.
Prepare, submit and track final turnover packages and data books.
Maintain organized, accurate and audit-ready records.



What We Are Looking For


Experience in document control, turnover, quality records or project information in industrial fabrication, construction or a regulated environment is preferred.
Ability to read and navigate piping isometrics, line lists, shop drawings and structural steel drawings.
Knowledge of MTRs, heat-number traceability, weld maps, spool records, NDE and coating records, calibration records and as-built documentation.
Experience with transmittals, submittals, revision control and turnover packages.
Proficiency with Bluebeam and Microsoft 365. Experience with SharePoint, Procore, Aconex or similar systems is an asset.
Relevant practical experience or technical education is an asset.



Working Conditions


Based primarily in Nisku, Alberta, with regular work in fabrication-shop and office environments.
All work must comply with company safety requirements, site rules and project confidentiality requirements.
