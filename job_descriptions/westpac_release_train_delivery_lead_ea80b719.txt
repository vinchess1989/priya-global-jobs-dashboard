Job Description

Create your best future as a Release Train Delivery Lead.

What’s the role?

As a Release Train Delivery Lead, you’ll help teams turn plans into predictable delivery by creating a clear rhythm across multiple squads. You’ll bring people together around release planning, coordinate dependencies, manage capacity and help remove the blockers that can get in the way of progress. You’ll lead quarterly planning and key delivery ceremonies, working closely with Product Owners, engineering and other partners to keep priorities aligned and delivery moving. You’ll provide clear reporting and insights, make day to day delivery flow decisions and ensure risks, issues and trade-offs are visible early. You’ll also lead and develop delivery resources across the Release Train, championing agile ways of working and creating an environment where teams can continuously improve how they deliver.

What do I need?


Strong delivery leadership, facilitation and coordination skills, with the ability to bring multiple squads together around shared outcomes
A deep understanding of agile delivery, scaled agile approaches and flow principles, and the ability to apply them in complex delivery environments
Experience facilitating release planning and cross squad ceremonies and coordinating capacity, sequencing and dependencies
The ability to influence across business, technology and enabling teams, confidently navigating ambiguity and resolving delivery challenges
Strong communication skills, with the ability to make delivery progress, risks and trade offs clear and meaningful to senior stakeholders
