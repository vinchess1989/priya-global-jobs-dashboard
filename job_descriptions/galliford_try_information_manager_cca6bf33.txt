Job Description

Information Manager

Location: Grangemouth - with travel to sites occasionally.

Business Unit: Environment

About Us

Galliford Try is one of the UK's leading construction and infrastructure businesses, delivering innovative engineering and environmental solutions that improve lives and create lasting value.

Our Environment Division delivers critical infrastructure projects across the UK water sector, supporting clients through the design, construction, commissioning and handover of essential assets. As digital delivery and information management become increasingly important to project success, we're investing in the systems, processes and people that will help drive consistency, compliance and operational excellence across our business.

Purpose of the Role

We are looking for an experienced and highly organised Information Manager to join our Environment Division.

Reporting to the Head of Digital, you will play a key role in ensuring information is managed consistently, efficiently and in line with both Galliford Try standards and client requirements across your assigned region or framework.

You will be responsible for developing and implementing Information Management Plans, managing Common Data Environments (CDEs), supporting digital tools such as Viewpoint for Projects and Field View, and providing training and support to project teams throughout the project lifecycle.

This is a highly collaborative role that sits at the heart of project delivery, helping ensure information is accurate, accessible, compliant and ready for successful project handover.

What You'll Be Doing

As an Information Manager, you will:


Develop and implement Framework and Project Information Management Plans.
Ensure compliance with Galliford Try information management standards and client requirements.
Act as the regional lead for information management processes and digital systems.
Configure, maintain and support Common Data Environments (CDEs) and project workspaces.
Support the deployment and use of digital construction tools, including Viewpoint for Projects and Field View.
Monitor project information performance, reporting progress and identifying risks or non-compliance.
Support project teams in establishing robust information management processes from project inception through to handover.
Work closely with operational, commercial, engineering and quality teams to ensure information requirements are embedded within project delivery.
Provide training, coaching and ongoing support to project teams, supply chain partners and clients.
Drive consistency, standardisation and continuous improvement across regional information management practices.


Key ResponsibilitiesInformation Management & Digital Delivery


Develop and maintain Framework Information Management Plans aligned to contractual and client requirements.
Ensure every project operates with an approved Project Information Management Plan.
Establish and configure project workspaces, workflows and document control structures.
Manage project information environments to ensure compliance with business processes and governance requirements.
Support the implementation of document control and information management procedures across projects.
Monitor information quality, completeness and readiness for project handover.
Support the management of drawings, records, quality documentation and project data.


Systems Management


Configure and manage Viewpoint for Projects and Field View workspaces.
Support users with system access, setup, workflows and reporting requirements.
Manage project templates, forms, workflows and reporting dashboards.
Support tablet deployment and digital site solutions where required.
Liaise with central system administrators to implement updates, enhancements and improvements.
Identify opportunities to improve efficiency through digital innovation and automation.


Training, Support & Assurance


Deliver information management training to project teams, administrators, document controllers and site staff.
Provide support and guidance to clients, designers and supply chain partners where required.
Assist with information management and quality audits.
Support the resolution of system and user issues.
Promote best practice and ensure lessons learned are shared across the business.
Contribute to the recruitment and development of document control and site administration teams where required.


About You

We're looking for someone who is highly organised, digitally focused and passionate about driving excellence through good information management.

You will be comfortable working with multiple stakeholders, able to influence teams positively and have a strong understanding of document control, digital project delivery and common data environments.

Essential Skills & Experience


Strong knowledge of information management principles and best practice.
Experience managing Common Data Environments (CDEs).
Strong understanding of document control processes.
Experience configuring and supporting digital project platforms.
Excellent organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple projects and priorities.
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills.
Ability to deliver training and support to users at all levels of the business.
Experience operating within quality assurance and management systems.
Strong IT skills and enthusiasm for digital innovation.
Ability to drive compliance while maintaining positive working relationships.


Technical Knowledge & Qualifications


BIM Awareness Training.
Understanding of BIM principles and ISO 19650 requirements.
Knowledge of design management processes and project information requirements.
Extensive experience using Viewpoint for Projects at an operational level.
Experience using Field View or similar digital construction platforms.
Good understanding of Quality Plans, Inspection & Test Plans (ITPs) and project records management.
Knowledge of ISO 27001 principles would be advantageous.
Strong Microsoft Office skills including Excel, Word, Outlook and PowerPoint.


What You'll Bring


A structured and methodical approach to work.
Excellent attention to detail.
Strong customer service mindset and passion for supporting project teams.
The ability to communicate effectively with everyone from site teams to senior leadership.
A collaborative approach and commitment to continuous improvement.
The confidence to influence positive behaviours and drive compliance without creating unnecessary barriers.
