SERVICE DELIVERY MANAGER – APPLICATIONS & INTEGRATION

Help shape the reliable delivery of the applications and integration services that support Laing O'Rourke.

This is an opportunity to bring together business priorities, technical delivery and service operations. You will coordinate change, improve operational performance and help ensure that business-critical services remain reliable, supportable and aligned with organisational needs.

Laing O'Rourke's IT function has renewed its strategy to support the company's ambition to transform the construction industry, making it more sustainable, more productive, and fit for the future.

Technology has a critical role to play in achieving this ambition. Our focus is on applying digital capability in ways that genuinely matter, shaping how complex projects are delivered, how decisions are made, and how innovation improves outcomes for people, communities, and the environment.

Our mission is clear: to create a modern and resilient technology environment where trusted data informs every decision, AI enhances every process, and digital capability enables the organisation to operate with greater scale and productivity.

To deliver this, we are building a different kind of IT function, one that is trusted by the business, forward looking in its thinking, and deeply connected to operational outcomes. We are looking for individuals who are curious, thoughtful, and motivated by contributing to work that has real industry impact.

The Role

As Service Delivery Manager – Applications & Integration, you will coordinate the reliable operation, continuous improvement and effective delivery of change across our applications and integration services.

Working with application teams, integration engineers, service operations, business stakeholders and external suppliers, you will provide a clear point of coordination for demand, delivery priorities, service performance and operational issues.

You will translate business needs into a prioritised and actionable backlog, support teams in delivering change safely and predictably, and help ensure that live services remain stable and supportable.

Key Resonsibilities


Coordinate the day-to-day delivery and continuous improvement of application and integration services, with a focus on availability, reliability, supportability and user experience.
Maintain a clear view of service health, incidents, problems, risks, service requests, planned changes and operational commitments.
Coordinate incident response and escalation across engineering teams, service operations, business users and third-party suppliers.
Ensure service issues have clear ownership, are communicated effectively and lead to practical improvements that reduce repeat incidents.
Manage the intake and clarification of demand for application changes, integration enhancements, defect fixes and service improvements.
Work with business stakeholders and technical teams to turn requests into clear requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria and measurable outcomes.
Maintain a transparent and prioritised backlog that balances business value, operational risk, technical debt, regulatory obligations and delivery capacity.
Coordinate delivery from discovery and refinement through development, testing, business acceptance, release and transition into live service.
Manage release schedules and dependencies, ensuring deployment plans, rollback arrangements, support readiness and communications are in place.
Build trusted relationships with business stakeholders, application owners, technical teams, service operations and suppliers.
Coordinate supplier and managed-service performance, following actions, dependencies, escalations and service commitments through to completion.
Ensure delivery follows agreed security, architecture, testing, change and operational controls.
Provide concise reporting on service performance, delivery progress, incidents, risks, dependencies and improvement activity.
Use service and delivery measures to identify recurring issues, bottlenecks and opportunities to improve reliability, quality and delivery flow.


Essential Experience


Significant experience in IT service delivery, application support, software delivery coordination or a related role within a complex organisation.
Experience coordinating work across application teams, integration engineers, business stakeholders, service operations and third-party suppliers.
Practical experience of incident, problem, service request, change and release management.
The ability to manage competing priorities and set clear expectations with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
The ability to translate business needs into actionable requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria and prioritised backlogs.
A sound understanding of Agile delivery, software development and testing lifecycles, release management and transition into live service.
Experience using service and delivery measures to identify risk, report performance and support continuous improvement.
Clear communication and facilitation skills, including the ability to provide timely and audience-appropriate updates during incidents, service disruption and planned change.


Desirable


Experience supporting enterprise applications and integration services, including APIs, messaging and legacy system-to-system interfaces.
Familiarity with ServiceNow, Jira, GitHub or CI/CD workflows used to track work, incidents, changes and releases.
Exposure to enterprise technology platforms such as Microsoft Azure, SAP or Salesforce.
Knowledge of scaled Agile or Waterfall delivery frameworks.
Knowledge of IT service management practices, including problem management, service levels and service improvement.


What Makes This Role Compelling

You will work across a broad portfolio of business-critical applications and integrations, connecting technical teams with the people who rely on their services.

The role offers the opportunity to improve how demand is prioritised, how change is delivered and how operational issues are resolved.

Your work will have a visible impact on service reliability, user experience and the organisation's ability to deliver change safely and effectively.

About Laing O'Rourke

Laing O'Rourke is an international engineering and construction business known for pushing boundaries in digital engineering, modern methods of construction and sustainable delivery. With more than 150 years of heritage, we are committed to creating environments in which diverse teams can excel and thrive.
