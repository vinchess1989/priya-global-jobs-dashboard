About Analog Devices

Analog Devices, Inc. (NASDAQ: ADI) is a global semiconductor leader that bridges the physical and digital worlds to enable breakthroughs at the Intelligent Edge. ADI combines analog, digital, AI, and software technologies into solutions that combat climate change, reliably connect humans and the world, and help drive advancements in automation and robotics, mobility, healthcare, energy and data centers. With revenue of more than $11 billion in FY25, ADI ensures today's innovators stay Ahead of What's Possible. Learn more at www.analog.com and on LinkedIn and X.

Analog Devices Inc. is looking for a Software Test Solutions Engineer to lead test automation solutions and manage stakeholder relationships within their QSS Test Operations group. The role focuses on integrating and supporting test automation infrastructure and continuous integration systems.

Responsibilities:


Gather and define stakeholder Test Automation requirements
Define project plans for implementing and installing Test Solution application for new and legacy projects
Identify how to integrate into existing CICD systems or recommend implementation of the QSS standard CICD pipeline to support Test Automation
Coordinate with other infrastructure teams to design and configure virtual systems for build and test environments
Troubleshoot issues and solve problems with Test Automation infrastructure, build systems as needed
Assist with configuring and setting up common relational database environments
Support training teams on the use of QSS Test Solution frameworks
Assist with integration of Test Solution applications.
Administering Linux operating systems
Support integration of CI test pipelines
Install and support code coverage tools to capture quality of test automation coverage
May also include assisting with development of test automation infrastructure
Serve as a champion for advancing Test Automation best practices and new methodologies within the organization


Minimum Qualifications/skills:


3 to 5 years of relative experience within software tests with at least 1 of those years in a Project Management related role
A University master’s degree in computer engineering, Telecommunications Engineering, Electronic Engineering or a related field is required. Candidates with a bachelor’s degree in engineering and equivalent professional experience in the sector will also be considered
Operations Project Management background
Background in managing test automation environments
Experience in software development and testing of code
Strong expertise in DevOps Agile CI/CD implementation
Experience with building infrastructure and tools such as GCOV/GCC, Coverity, Git/Bitbucket, Artifactory, Jira, Visual Studios, etc.
Experience with relational databases, such as SQL or PostgreSQL
Experience with developing and deploying Github actions
Experience with maintaining various scripting languages to build software from scratch, test, and deploy it for a specific use case
Experience with container technologies like Docker
Experience with Linux and cross-platform development
Experience with Python and bash scripting
Experience with C, C++ and Shell Scripting
Excellent analytical skills


Preferred Qualifications:


7+ Years of DevOps experience in managing test infrastructure and with 2 of the years in Project Management
Project Management skills
Exposure to embedded software product development with test background
Experience with Agile methodology
Experience with C#, Python and Java
Experience with non-relational databases such as MongoDB
Experience with time series databases, such as InfluxDB
Experience with visualization tooling, such as Grafana
Maintenance of Docker/Container clusters
Knowledge of hybrid cloud environments
Knowledge of OpenShift and CloudSmith highly desirable
