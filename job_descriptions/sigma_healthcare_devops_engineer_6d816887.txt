About the Team

This role is part of the Cloud & Platform Services team, which falls under the Infrastructure & Cloud department within IT. The team’s mission is to build, manage, and optimise the organisation’s core infrastructure technologies. The department comprises more than 50 specialised engineers and technical experts. This position plays a critical role in both managing and shaping our organisations network technologies and platforms.







About the Role

Reporting to the Team Lead – Cloud & DevOps Engineering, you’ll play a key role in supporting our technology transformation by designing, implementing and optimising enterprise platforms across cloud environments, data centres, warehouses and stores.




As a DevOps Engineer, you’ll apply modern DevOps practices, drive automation and continuous improvement, and help deliver secure, scalable and reliable technology solutions. Working collaboratively with teams across the business, you’ll contribute to strategic technology initiatives while ensuring the ongoing performance and reliability of our critical systems.







Role Responsibilities

Design and deliver innovative technology solutions across cloud (Azure, AWS and GCP) and on-premises environments.
Build, enhance and optimise infrastructure, platforms and tools to support evolving business needs.
Proactively monitor and improve platform performance, ensuring systems remain secure, reliable and efficient.
Lead the investigation and resolution of complex technical issues, identifying root causes and implementing long-term solutions.
Drive the adoption of modern DevOps practices, automation and continuous improvement across the technology environment.
Partner with cross-functional teams to design and deliver scalable, resilient and high-performing solutions.
Share your technical expertise, providing guidance, mentoring and support to engineers across the business.
Identify opportunities to streamline processes, enhance system capabilities and improve the way we work.







Who are we looking for?

We're looking for a proactive and collaborative DevOps Engineer who enjoys solving complex technical challenges, exploring new technologies and finding smarter ways of working.




To be successful in this role, you'll bring:

Proven experience in DevOps engineering, with a strong understanding of modern practices and their application in complex enterprise environments.
Strong cloud engineering expertise, ideally within Microsoft Azure.
Hands-on experience with CI/CD pipelines, source control tools and automation.
A continuous improvement mindset, with a genuine interest in learning and adopting new technologies.
Strong communication and collaboration skills, with a willingness to mentor others and share technical knowledge.

It would also be great if you have:

Experience in platform engineering and developing scalable, reusable infrastructure solutions.
Knowledge of observability tools, proactive monitoring and performance optimisation.
An understanding of cloud security principles and best practices.
A solid foundation in software engineering principles and practices.
Knowledge of networking technologies, including DNS, firewalls and routing.
