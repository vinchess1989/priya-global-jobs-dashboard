Non-Production Environment Manager | Melbourne




CloudMarc is looking for a hands-on Non-Production Environment Manager to keep integrated test environments stable and support the migration of Windows Server applications from VMware to Microsoft Azure.




What you’ll do

Manage PTEST, STEST and VTEST throughout the project lifecycle, keeping environments stable, secure and ready for testing.
Plan, schedule and help execute software deployments and patching.
Coordinate migration activities and application dependencies across infrastructure, cloud, application and testing teams.
Triage issues across integrated workflows, coordinate technical support, and keep stakeholders informed about outages, impact and recovery.
Maintain documentation on application dependencies, workflows and support procedures.




What you’ll bring

Practical experience supporting Windows Server application migrations from VMware to Azure.
Experience managing enterprise non-production environments, including deployment planning, incident triage and technical troubleshooting.
Clear communication skills and the ability to coordinate issue resolution across multiple teams.




Desirable: Mobile device management (MDM) experience and the ability to set up or update monitoring in New Relic or Sumo Logic.




About CloudMarc

CloudMarc is a technology consultancy specialising in software development, independent quality engineering and cloud services. Our teams across Australia, New Zealand, the Philippines and India work alongside client delivery teams on everything from test strategy and environment readiness to hands-on testing and release support.




To apply: Send us your CV, availability and a brief example of a VMware-to-Azure migration you supported. Tell us what moved and what you were responsible for.
