Our client is currently seeking an experienced Quality Manager to join their team.The successful candidate will be responsible for developing, implementing and maintaining quality management strategies, ensuring that all products and services meet customer expectations, industry standards and regulatory requirements.




Location: Co. Dublin

Salary: €40k - €50k




Responsibilities

Develop, implement and maintain quality management systems, processes and procedures.
Work closely with all departments to ensure Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) are relevant, accurate and up to date.
Lead internal and external ISO audits.
Monitor and evaluate the effectiveness of quality control measures.
Establish annual KPIs and drive continuous improvement initiatives.
Lead root cause analysis and problem-solving activities.
Identify and implement quality improvements across the business.
Provide training and guidance to employees on quality standards and procedures.
Prepare quality reports for senior management and coordinate annual Management Reviews in line with QMS requirements.
Manage customer complaints and oversee corrective and preventative actions (CAPA).
Complete and manage regulatory and customer questionnaires.
Maintain the Approved Supplier List and oversee supplier quality requirements.
Develop and monitor annual Environmental Management System (EMS) targets and objectives.
Oversee incoming inspections of purchased third-party products.
Coordinate and lead bi-weekly internal quality meetings.




Requirements

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Quality Management or a related discipline.
Minimum of 5 years' experience in a Quality Manager or similar position.
Strong knowledge and practical experience of ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 standards.
Understanding of ISO 27001 and ISO 45001 requirements.
Experience with internal and external audits.
Knowledge of Six Sigma, Lean Manufacturing or continuous improvement methodologies.
Strong understanding of root cause analysis and corrective actions.
Excellent communication and organisational skills.
Ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams and lead quality initiatives.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office and quality management systems.
