We are seeking a Senior Regulatory Compliance Engineer responsible for ensuring global regulatory and standards compliance for medical X-ray generator systems, high-voltage power supplies, RF products, and related electronic assemblies. This position serves as a senior technical resource for product safety, electromagnetic compatibility (EMC), radiation safety, environmental compliance, and quality management systems while supporting customer and market requirements.

You Will Be Accountable For/To


Lead regulatory compliance activities for medical X-ray generators, high-voltage power supplies, RF products, and related electronic assemblies.
Ensure products comply with applicable FDA Center for Devices and Radiological Health (CDRH) radiation product requirements, IEC 60601, IEC 62368-1, ISO 13485, ISO 9001, and ISO 14971 requirements.
Plan and coordinate EMC, electrical safety, environmental, and radiation safety testing and certification activities.
Prepare and maintain technical files, declarations of conformity, test reports, certificates, and other compliance documentation.
Support customer regulatory requirements by providing documentation for customer submissions, certifications, and market approvals.
Monitor changes to global regulations and industry standards and assess their potential impact on CPI products.
Provide regulatory and compliance input during design reviews, risk management activities, verification and validation, and product change assessments.
Support internal, customer, certification, and regulatory audits.
Manage relationships with third-party testing laboratories, certification bodies, and regulatory consultants.
Ensure compliance with applicable RoHS, REACH, WEEE, PFAS, Conflict Minerals, and other environmental requirements.
Provide technical compliance guidance to Engineering, Quality, Manufacturing, Product Management, and other cross-functional teams.
Identify compliance risks and recommend appropriate corrective or preventive actions.
Perform other duties as required.


YOU ARE THE RIGHT PERSON FOR THIS JOB IF YOU HAVE:


A bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, or a related technical discipline.
Seven or more years of experience in regulatory compliance, product safety, EMC, certification, or a related technical function.
Experience with medical electrical equipment, power electronics, high-voltage systems, RF products, or related electronic assemblies.
Strong knowledge of IEC 60601, IEC 62368-1, ISO 13485, ISO 9001, ISO 14971, applicable EMC standards, and environmental compliance requirements.
Experience interpreting technical standards and applying regulatory requirements throughout the product lifecycle.
Experience coordinating third-party testing and certification activities.
Strong technical writing, communication, problem-solving, and project management skills.
The ability to work independently, provide technical leadership, and lead cross-functional compliance initiatives.
The ability to meet the requirements of the Canadian Controlled Goods Program (CGP).
The ability to travel occasionally to the United States and certification laboratories.
