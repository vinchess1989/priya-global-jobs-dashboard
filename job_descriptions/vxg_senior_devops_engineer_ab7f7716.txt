Full-time role based in our office in the midtown Toronto.




VXG is seeking an experienced DevOps Engineer to join our team and help build reliable, automated, and scalable infrastructure for our cutting-edge video software platform. You will play a key role in streamlining deployments, monitoring, and troubleshooting across AWS and Kubernetes environments, ensuring that our systems remain robust, secure, and efficient.




Key Responsibilities




Automate the installation and deployment of VXG software on customers infrastructure - AWS and Kubernetes.
Install and maintain Kubernetes deployments on customers infrastructure - private data centres, on-premises servers.
Build and maintain scripts to configure, test, and optimize our solutions.
Continuously improve deployment pipelines with a focus on reliability and scalability.
Implement and enforce best practices for security, monitoring, and infrastructure automation.
Collaborate with development teams to integrate CI/CD practices and support smooth releases and updates for all customers.
Maintenance of production deployments, upgrades, health monitoring of services, and working with software engineers on new metrics to increase observability




Requirements




Strong experience with configuration management systems (Ansible) and infrastructure as code tools (Terraform, Helm Charts, CloudFormation).
Expertise with Git administration and CI/CD pipelines (GitLab CI).
Very strong Linux system administration experience.
Hands-on expertise with AWS services: EC2, AutoScaling, CloudWatch, RDS, S3, CloudFormation.
Knowledge of Docker, Kubernetes, and Helm Charts.
Experience with Semaphore UI.
Familiarity with VMWare vSphere.
Strong scripting skills in Bash and Python.
Understanding of network technologies and protocols (IP, TCP, UDP, TLS).




Nice to Have




Knowledge of software build tools: GNU Make, Meson, Autotools, SCons, Gradle.
Experience with monitoring and observability tools (Prometheus, Zabbix).
Hands-on experience managing centralized logging systems (ELK stack).




What We Offer




• Competitive salary and equity package.

• Work in a collaborative and fast-moving startup environment.

• Modern office space in the heart of Midtown Toronto.

• Health and wellness benefits.
