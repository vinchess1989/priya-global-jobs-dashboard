The Impact You’ll Make in this Role

As a Product Quality Engineer, You Will Have The Opportunity To Tap Into Your Curiosity And Collaborate With Some Of The Most Innovative And Diverse People Around The World. Here, You Will Make An Impact By



Driving Quality tools in NPI to accelerate projects while mitigating risk by ensuring consumer needs are met, test methods are robust, specifications are well defined, and manufacturing processes are capable
Leading cross-functional teams in the effective use of NPI tools, including DFMEA, PFMEA, Design Qualification Matrix, and House of Quality, by preparing inputs, facilitating completion, and coaching teams on best practices to maximize value throughout the NPI process.
Reviewing and providing feedback on design documentation deliverables, including product specifications, product claim documentation and substantiation, qualification plans, and reports, to support quality, completeness, and alignment with project requirements.
Collaborating with external vendors to ensure products and design deliverables meet quality expectations, project requirements, and applicable standards.
Providing statistical support to product design teams through sampling plans, process capability analysis, test method crossover studies, Design of Experiments (DOE), Measurement System Analysis (MSA), data analysis, and documented final reports.



Your Skills And Expertise

To set you up for success in this role from day one, 3M requires (at a minimum) the following qualifications:



Bachelor’s degree or higher (completed and verified prior to start) from an accredited institution
Five (5) years of combined experience Quality Engineering, Supplier Management, Quality Management Systems, Six Sigma, Statistical Analysis and/or Design Control within a private, public, government, or military environment
Five (5) years of combined experience in leading key Quality NPI deliverables such as DFMEA, PFMEA, House of Quality, Design Qualification Matrix (DQM) and output documentation.
Two (2) years of combined experience in applying statistical knowledge to Quality tools such as Measurement System Analysis (MSA), Design of Experiments (DOE), Test Method Cross-Over Studies and Sampling Plans.



Additional qualifications that could help you succeed even further in this role include:



Experience working with ISO9001:2015
ASQ or similar Quality certification
Experience working with suppliers in a quality improvement capacity
Experience with statistical analysis
Manufacturing experience
Experience facilitating cross-functional teams through virtual meetings



Location: Bangalore (Hebbal), Hybrid Mode
