Are you a collaborative AWS Systems Engineer looking to grow your ownership of cloud infrastructure for a mission-driven global organization?

Do you enjoy guiding development teams, improving automation, and working with large-scale Kubernetes environments while helping deliver reliable, cost-effective platforms?

About The Role

As a Senior Systems Engineer I, you will support and increasingly own key areas of AWS cloud infrastructure, automation, and operational excellence within the Entellect (CMDP) platform. Working alongside development teams, you will help shape scalable solutions, improve deployment practices, and contribute to the reliability, security, and efficiency of a large Kubernetes-based environment. This role offers the opportunity to deepen your expertise while driving meaningful improvements across cloud operations and developer enablement.

Responsibilities


Independently deploy, maintain, and troubleshoot AWS cloud infrastructure with minimal guidance.
Advise and assist developers on best-practice use of infrastructure, deployment tooling, and platform capabilities.
Automate operational tasks and workflows while proactively identifying opportunities for improvement.
Contribute to and help shape scalable, resilient application architectures within Entellect.
Own system documentation, operational procedures, runbooks, and developer guidance materials.
Collaborate with and provide informal guidance to less experienced team members and development teams.
Lead smaller process improvement and automation initiatives from planning through delivery.
Troubleshoot complex infrastructure and deployment issues while maintaining a strong focus on cost efficiency.


Requirements


Hands-on experience with AWS Cloud services and infrastructure.
Solid experience with Kubernetes, specifically EKS, in a production environment.
Practical experience with Helm for packaging and managing Kubernetes applications.
Practical experience with Terraform for provisioning and managing infrastructure as code.
Experience with Docker and containerized application workflows.
Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions or Jenkins.
A collaborative approach to problem solving and continuous improvement.
Willingness to learn new technologies and contribute to a culture of operational excellence.


Work in a Way That Works for You

We promote a healthy work/life balance across the organisation. We offer an appealing working prospect for our people. With numerous wellbeing initiatives, shared parental leave, study assistance, and sabbaticals, we will help you meet your immediate responsibilities and your long-term goals.

Working Pattern

Working flexible hours - flexing the times when you work in the day to help you fit everything in and work when you are the most productive.

About The Business

A global leader in information and analytics, we help researchers and healthcare professionals advance science and improve health outcomes for the benefit of society. Building on our publishing heritage, we combine quality information and vast data sets with analytics to support visionary science and research, health education and interactive learning, as well as exceptional healthcare and clinical practice. At Elsevier, your work contributes to the world's grand challenges and a more sustainable future. We harness innovative technologies to support science and healthcare to partner for a better world.
