We are looking for an experienced Supplier Quality Engineer to manage supplier quality activities for commercial vehicle and truck programs. The role involves supplier quality assurance, APQP/PPAP execution, supplier audits, problem resolution, new product introduction, and continuous quality improvement.

Key Responsibilities

Lead APQP, PPAP, FMEA, Control Plans, and supplier quality approvals.
Conduct supplier process audits and ensure IATF 16949 compliance.
Drive 8D problem solving, root cause analysis (RCA), and corrective actions.
Monitor supplier quality KPIs, PPM, defects, and warranty performance.
Support new product launches, engineering changes, and supplier development.
Collaborate with Purchasing, Engineering, Manufacturing, and Quality teams.
Required Skills

Supplier Quality Engineering (SQE)
APQP, PPAP, FMEA, Control Plans
8D, RCA, SPC, MSA
IATF 16949, VDA 6.3
Supplier Performance Management
Automotive Manufacturing Processes
