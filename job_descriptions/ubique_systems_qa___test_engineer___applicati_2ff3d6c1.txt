Applications Specialist ( One year extendable contract in client payroll)

Experience range 8 to 10 years with Key skill set of Testing




Application Specialists will be assigned discrete segments of functionality from a project and will be responsible for:

API, integration and system testing of products during development.
Interpretation of high-level marketing requirements into detailed user-centric specifications suitable for development purposes.
Create bug reports with detailed information and relevant supporting diagnostic information to allow the effective triaging and resolution of software issues.
Creating and updating test and regression plans for new features or products or replacement products
Creating and updating manual functional and non-functional test cases.
Developing, executing, and maintaining automated test scripts to optimize the testing process and reduce manual testing efforts
Continuous improvement of testing processes.
Helping diagnose, troubleshoot and reproduce customer escalated cases
Any other duties directed by Management as and when required




Skills / Competencies:

Minimum or 5 years experience in a relevant role, QA, Software Test Engineer or Software Engineer.
ISTQB Certified Tester "Foundation Level"/"Advanced Level"/"Agile Tester" certification or equivalent certification is beneficial.
Surveying, communications, civil construction or tech industry experience is beneficial but not required.
An understanding of C++, Web or Android code is beneficial but not required.
Experience with Auto Test writing, tools and framework
