As our highly valued DevOps Engineer based in Brisbane, your role will support, optimise and improve the technology platforms underpinning our critical digital and payment systems, with a strong focus on automation, stability, security and operational excellence.

Supporting our purpose of helping every Australian own their own home.

You'll make an impact by:


Supporting and optimising critical application platforms and AWS cloud environments to deliver high availability and reliability
Improving CI/CD pipelines and automating build, deployment and operational processes to enable faster, safer technology change
Managing system patching, vulnerabilities, capacity and platform lifecycle to maintain secure and resilient environments
Configuring and maintaining monitoring and alerting across critical 24x7 systems
Supporting project delivery teams with non-production environments, application builds, configuration and deployments
Leading technical incident response and stakeholder communications during service disruptions
Providing technical leadership and guidance to engineers and partner teams while driving continuous improvement
Participating in after-hours releases and the rotational 24x7 DevOps on-call roster

Do things differently with us

This is an opportunity to work at the heart of the technology that keeps our digital banking and payment services running.

You'll join a technically skilled, forward-thinking team focused on automation, reliability and continuous improvement. You'll work across cloud and on-premise environments, helping engineer, support and improve platforms that need to be secure, scalable and available around the clock.

It's a genuinely hands-on senior engineering role. You'll tackle complex technical issues, improve CI/CD and automation, strengthen platform resilience and provide technical leadership when it matters most. You'll balance project delivery with the operational rigour required to support business-critical technology.

If you enjoy solving complex problems, automating away manual effort and building technology environments that are stable, secure and ready to scale, you'll thrive here.

To succeed in this role, you will have:


10+ years' experience across IT environments, with strong capability in application support, cloud engineering, systems administration and/or technical delivery
Deep experience with AWS and cloud-native technologies, combined with exposure to on-premise environments, containerisation and platforms such as VMware, OpenShift or Fargate
Strong DevOps and automation capability, including CI/CD pipelines, build and deployment automation, orchestration and scripting using tools or languages such as Bash, PowerShell or Groovy
Broad technical knowledge across Windows and Linux environments, infrastructure, networking, storage, databases, integration, security and application technologies
Experience supporting business-critical, customer-facing systems, ideally digital banking or payment platforms, including incident, change and release management in controlled environments
Demonstrated technical leadership, stakeholder and vendor management skills, along with an understanding of technology risk, operational resilience and regulatory requirements within financial services
Degree qualifications in Information Technology or equivalent experience, with AWS, RHEL, Microsoft or other relevant technology certifications highly regarded
