This is not a traditional application support management role. ....




This is an opportunity to take ownership of a large and varied enterprise application landscape, with a genuine mandate to rationalise the portfolio, establish stronger application governance and lifecycle management, and help define the future application strategy.




Operating within a fast-paced hospitality and events business, you’ll work across a portfolio of circa 150 applications. The environment is predominantly cloud-based and vendor-supported, alongside several customised on-premise applications with dependencies across the broader technology and infrastructure environment.




Leading a small applications team, you’ll combine technical leadership with governance, strategy, rationalisation and stakeholder engagement, while remaining hands-on enough to support the environment when required.




The role

A major focus will be the rationalisation of the existing application portfolio. You’ll work across the business to understand how applications are being used, the processes they support, and where opportunities exist to simplify the environment.

You’ll be responsible for:

Leading application rationalisation, consolidation and decommissioning activities
Establishing stronger application governance, ownership and lifecycle management
Working with stakeholders to understand application usage, business processes and opportunities for greater efficiency
Identifying duplicated capability and opportunities to make better use of existing platforms
Understanding application integrations, infrastructure dependencies and downstream impacts before changes are made
Improving visibility around application ownership, support arrangements, risk and business criticality
Managing and technically engaging external application vendors
Helping define the future-state application strategy and roadmap
Ensuring new and existing applications are supported by appropriate governance and technical oversight




Technical leadership

You won’t need to be a developer, but this isn’t a hands-off leadership role.

You’ll be the technical escalation point for the applications team, able to troubleshoot complex issues, understand application and infrastructure dependencies, engage vendors at a technical level, and jump into application support when ticket volumes or operational demand increase.

You’ll need a practical understanding of:

Enterprise and business applications
Cloud and vendor-supported platforms
Customised and on-premise applications
Application integrations and dependencies
How applications interact with infrastructure, identity and data
Application support and technical troubleshooting
Upgrades, changes and releases
Technical considerations around application consolidation and decommissioning




Stakeholder management & governance

This role requires someone who can operate comfortably between technology and the business.

You’ll engage stakeholders across multiple business functions to understand what applications they use, why they use them, the processes that depend on them and what could be done differently.




You’ll need to confidently challenge existing ways of working, build support for change and translate technical considerations into practical business decisions.




You’ll also play a key role in designing governance across application ownership, lifecycle management, vendor management, technical dependencies, risk, change and end-of-life planning.




What we’re looking for

You’ll ideally bring:

Experience owning or supporting a sizeable enterprise application portfolio
Strong technical knowledge of applications, integrations and infrastructure dependencies
Experience across cloud, vendor-supported and on-premise applications
Exposure to application rationalisation, consolidation and decommissioning
Experience building or improving application governance and lifecycle management
Strong vendor management and technical escalation capability
Experience leading a small applications team while remaining hands-on when required
Strong stakeholder management across both technical and non-technical teams
The ability to balance operational requirements with longer-term application strategy and improvement




Above all, we’re looking for someone who can connect the dots between applications, technology, vendors, business processes and people, and turn a complex application landscape into a more streamlined, well-governed environment.




Why consider this opportunity?

This is an opportunity to do more than maintain an existing application environment. There is a genuine mandate for change, with the opportunity to rationalise a sizeable portfolio, introduce stronger governance and influence how applications are selected, managed and supported into the future. You’ll be joining a recognised hospitality and events business, with a package of up to $180,000 including super plus an impressive range of employee benefits.
