About The Role

Grade Level (for internal use):

12

The Team

Our Technology Governance & Delivery Enablement team at S&P Dow Jones Indices drives operational excellence through Lean Portfolio Management and Agile ways of working across complex product portfolios, value streams and Agile Release Trains. We foster a collaborative culture focused on accelerating technology delivery while embedding innovative AI-enabled practices and Responsible AI governance. As a globally minded team, we value continuous learning, transparency, accountability, and high-performing cross-functional partnerships that deliver measurable business outcomes at scale.

Responsibilities & Impact

ART Leadership & Delivery Management


Lead the planning and execution of Program Increments (PI) to deliver against prioritized business outcomes
Facilitating program increment planning readiness by fostering Continuous Exploration process which drives the combination of Vision, Roadmap, and backlog and through Pre and Post Pl planning Meetings
Coordinate with the Product Management and Tech Team to define the release on-demand plan as per prioritized epics/features for a program increment
Enhance planning efficiency by leveraging AI-generated summaries and action recommendations.
Facilitate core ART events, including PI Planning, ART Sync, System Demos, and Inspect & Adapt sessions, etc.
Ensure alignment between business strategy, portfolio priorities, and execution across value streams
Proactively manages risks, dependencies, and impediments impacting delivery across teams, including escalation as needed
Ensure effective prioritization aligned to business value and organizational goals
Drive predictability, quality, and efficiency in delivery across the ART(s)
Lead Scrum of Scrums/ART syncs and coordinates with scrum masters and other relevant members of an ART to effectively manage dependencies and risks
Use AI powered tools and predictive analytics to forecast feature completion timelines and capacity constraints.
Enable data-driven decision-making by integrating AI insights into Program Increment (PI) planning and execution.


Voice of Change & Team Enablement


Lead, coach, and develop Scrum Masters and/or Flow Masters to drive to build high-performing, self-organizing teams
Foster a culture of accountability, transparency, and continuous improvement across teams
Enable teams to deliver efficiently by removing impediments and improving flow
Support Product Management in leveraging AI to identify gaps, redundancies, or hidden dependencies in epics and features.


Agile Transformation & Coaching


Champion adoption and scaling of Lean-Agile and SAFe practices across the ART and broader organization
Coach leaders, teams, and stakeholders on Agile principles, practices, and mindset shifts
Support enterprise-wide transformation by mentoring across functions and value streams


Stakeholder Collaboration & Communication


Partner closely with Product Management, Business Owners, and Technology leadership to drive alignment and decision-making
Assist with economic decision-making by facilitating feature and capability estimation by teams
Communicate effectively across stakeholders to ensure transparency of progress, risks, and outcomes
Build strong cross-functional partnerships across teams and functions to enable seamless execution


Continuous Improvement & Metrics


Lead Inspect & Adapt workshops and implement improvement actions across teams and ARTs
Track and produce key program-level metrics reports (e.g. cumulative flow diagram, Pl burn-up, and burn-down charts, etc.)
Leverage metrics (flow, predictability, quality, outcomes) and insights to identify improvement opportunities
Leverage AI-powered tools to analyze sprint velocity, predict delivery risks, and identify bottlenecks across the Agile Release Train (ART).
Drive systemic improvements across ART and value stream operations


Cross-ART & Portfolio Alignment


Collaborate across multiple ARTs and valuestreams to manage dependencies and optimize integrated value delivery
Align execution with portfolio governance, enterprise priorities, and strategic themes


Responsible AI & Governance


Accountable for embedding Responsible AI practices across value streams
Promote adherence to standards for human oversight, data privacy, ethical use, and technical safeguards in delivery


Organizational Agility & Culture


Act as a change agent to advance Lean-Agile maturity across the valuestreams and divisions
Enable collaborative partnerships that support training, coaching, and adoption of Agile principles and scaled agile ways of working.
Influence cultural change toward a more adaptive, resilient, and learning organization
Must be able to be a voice of reason and authority and build consensus
Facilitate a healthy team dynamic with respect to priorities and scope


Knowledge Management & Collaboration


Utilize AI tools to capture, summarize, and surface key decisions, action items, and program knowledge across the ART.
Enable faster onboarding and alignment via AI-curated documentation and knowledge repositories.


What We Are Looking For


Minimum 7+ yrs of RTE experience across product teams
3+ years of experience being a team level coach and/or program level coach
5+ years of people management experience
Significant experience leading Agile business value delivery in complex, matrixed organizations
Proven experience operating at Program and/or Portfolio levels
Strong knowledge and hands-on experience with SAFe framework and ART execution
Experience coaching teams, Scrum Masters, and leaders to improve Agile maturity
Strong facilitation skills in large, cross-functional environments
Proven ability to manage risks, dependencies, and delivery across multiple teams
Strong communication and stakeholder alignment skills across business and technology
Analytical mindset with experience leveraging metrics for decision-making
Exposure and experience related to AI capabilities in SAFe and Scrum Practices
Ability to understand domain specific and technical discussions so that he/she can facilitate the agile events and discussions in a meaningful way   
Ability to partner with other Scrum Masters, different agile roles and teams to increase the effectiveness agile practices within the ART(s) and broader organization
Effective skills and experience in facilitation, situational awareness, conflict resolution, continual improvement, predictability, and increasing transparency
Extensive experience in identifying the risks upfront and taking the accountability to get the risks resolved in a timely manner
Proven track record of enabling the teams to deliver prioritized business value efficiently with a focus on accelerated delivery, enhanced quality and maximum ROI
Expert in agile metrics and utilization of metrics for continuous improvement
Experience with tools like – Azure DevOps, Confluence, power BI, MS Teams, SharePoint, JIRA. VersionOne, etc.
Excellent organizational, statistical, analytical, problem solving, written and oral presentation skills
Proven track record of identifying and improving Agile execution on a team
Basic knowledge of lean portfolio management
Familiarity with Kanban practices and metrics
Thorough understanding of enterprise software development processes and lifecycle with familiarity of product development life cycle
Works effectively with leaders at all levels


Preferred Qualifications


Bachelor´s in Business, Technology, or a related field (Master’s preferred)
Financial industry experience preferred
SAFe Release Train Engineer (RTE) certification preferred
Additional certifications such as SAFe Program Consultant (SPC), SAFe Agilist (SA), or SAFe LPM are a plus
Deep understanding of Lean-Agile principles, DevSecOps practices, and value stream delivery
Prior roles as VSE or RTE and/or agile coach preferred
Knowledge and/or experience with widely successful Agile practices: BDD, ATDD, TDD, CI/CD, Pair programming, Automation and Kaizen.


Overall Characteristics


Demonstrates confidence and sound judgment to guide teams, uphold Agile and SAFe principles, and make informed decisions in complex situations
Strong communication skills with the ability to clearly articulate complex technical and business concepts to diverse stakeholders
Effectively influences alignment and decision-making across cross-functional teams and leadership
Self-starter with a strong sense of ownership and accountability
Highly organized, methodical, and detail-oriented, with the ability to drive strategic alignment and execution at scale
Skilled in facilitating discussions, resolving conflicts, and enabling teams to evaluate alternatives and make effective decisions
Leverages AI and automation to enhance team productivity, streamline processes, and enable faster, data-driven decision-making

About S&P Global Dow Jones Indices

At S&P Dow Jones Indices, we provide iconic and innovative index solutions backed by unparalleled expertise across the asset-class spectrum. By bringing transparency to the global capital markets, we empower investors everywhere to make decisions with conviction. We’re the largest global resource for index-based concepts, data and research, and home to iconic financial market indicators, such as the S&P 500® and the Dow Jones Industrial Average®. More assets are invested in products based upon our indices than any other index provider in the world. With over USD 7.4 trillion in passively managed assets linked to our indices and over USD 11.3 trillion benchmarked to our indices, our solutions are widely considered indispensable in tracking market performance, evaluating portfolios and developing investment strategies.

S&P Dow Jones Indices is a division of S&P Global (NYSE: SPGI). S&P Global is the world’s foremost provider of credit ratings, benchmarks, analytics and workflow solutions in the global capital, commodity and automotive markets. With every one of our offerings, we help many of the world’s leading organizations navigate the economic landscape so they can plan for tomorrow, today. For more information, visit www.spglobal.com/spdji.
