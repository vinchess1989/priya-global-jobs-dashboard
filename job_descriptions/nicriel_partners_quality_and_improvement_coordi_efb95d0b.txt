Our client is a global heavy machinery manufacturer who are known for innovation and exceptional customer service, currently they are searching for a Quality and Improvement Coordinator to join their team.

In this role you will be expected to take ownership of the Quality Management System requirements across engineering, contractual and customer quality and turn these into practical quality plans.

More specifically you will look after:


Quality Planning and Operation control across engineering and contractual requirements, coordination of assurance activities and ensuring controls and timelines are met.
Inspection coordination and conformity through setting inspection priorities by translating technical requirements into inspection activities and the reviewing of inspection data.
A hands-on position you will own the NCR process and drive corrective actions internally and by working with suppliers, projects and customer quality functions to improving quality outcomes.
The person we are looking for will have a strong understanding of QMS, Audit and Compliance gained through exposure to ISO systems and have a mechanical aptitude to be able to understand drawings, specifications and inspection criteria in complex manufacturing environment.
 
Tertiary qualified in a suitable discipline you will be a safety first professional and have a strong ability to coach and train people on the importance of quality through a pro-active approach and you will have strong communication skills both written and verbal.
 
In return, this opportunity will allow you to join a market-leading original equipment manufacturer and play a significant part in development of their quality management systems.
