An exciting opportunity exists for a highly experienced Technical Documentation Writer to support the Pre-Production Department of a leading printing and manufacturing organisation in Craigieburn.

This confidential role requires a professional with a proven background in producing high-quality technical documentation, preferably within regulated, secure or sensitive environments. The successful candidate will be responsible for developing and maintaining documentation aligned with security protocols, quality management systems, and compliance standards.

Key Responsibilities

Develop and maintain clear and accurate documentation including:
Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
Work Instructions
Security -related operational processes
Quality control and compliance documentation
Liaise with the Security team to ensure all documentation reflects current practices
Maintain version control, document consistency, and adherence to internal standards
Support general administration duties as required

Essential Skills and Experience

Demonstrated ability to write and manage SOPs and Work Instructions
Proven experience in a technical documentation or technical writing role
Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, PowerPoint)
Exceptional written and verbal communication skills
High level of discretion, professionalism, and attention to detail
Strong organisational skills and ability to work independently and collaboratively

Highly Regarded (Not Essential)

Experience in an ISO-compliant or regulated environment
Background in quality management systems (QMS)
Exposure to security operations or other sensitive/controlled work environments
Certificate or Diploma in Project Management
