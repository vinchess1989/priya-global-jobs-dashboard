Queensland Rail Engineers … we’d love to talk.




There's a lot happening across Queensland's rail industry right now, with major infrastructure, signalling and technology programs shaping the future of the state's network.




As we continue to grow our Queensland team, we're looking to connect with talented professionals across:

✅ Systems Engineering & Safety Assurance (SESA)

✅ Verification & Validation (V&V)

✅ RAMS Engineering

✅ Systems Integration

✅ Testing & Commissioning

✅ SESA Independent Certification




You'll have the opportunity to work on some of Queensland's most significant rail programs, including ETCS Level 2 deployments, major brownfield upgrades, greenfield infrastructure projects and complex systems integration initiatives.




Whether your experience is in systems engineering, safety assurance, safety cases, hazard analysis, requirements management, RAMS, V&V, testing and commissioning, interface management, operational readiness or rail systems integration, we'd love to hear from you.

We're particularly interested in people who understand the challenges of introducing new technology into an operational railway, navigating safety and assurance requirements, and supporting projects from design through to testing, commissioning and entry into service.




And here’s the thing… you don’t need to tick every box.

You might be an experienced technical lead, a senior engineer looking for your next challenge, or someone earlier in their career who's developed some great specialist skills and wants to keep growing.

At CERTIFER Solutions Australia, we're building a team of people who are technically capable, collaborative, curious and genuinely passionate about delivering safe and reliable rail systems.

You'll work alongside rail operators, delivery partners, infrastructure managers and fellow engineers who enjoy solving complex problems and sharing knowledge.




Why people join us?




✔ Work on projects that will help shape Queensland's rail network for decades to come.

✔ Tackle complex technical challenges across signalling, systems engineering, safety assurance and integration.

✔ Learn from experienced specialists while continuing to develop your own expertise.

✔ Be part of a collaborative team that values curiosity, knowledge sharing and practical problem-solving.

✔ Enjoy flexible working arrangements and opportunities for long-term career growth.




Location & Flexibility

We have a mix of opportunities available. Some roles require engineers to be based in Queensland, while others offer hybrid working arrangements.

We're also keen to connect with talented engineers from across Australia who are interested in supporting Queensland rail programs.
