We are excited to recruit for a Business Analyst who bridges business needs and technology delivery by eliciting, analysing and documenting requirements for new and modernised applications.

The successful incumbent will be reporting to the Associate Director IT Digital Transformation, the role facilitates stakeholder engagement, translates business needs into clear functional and technical requirements and acceptance criteria, supports solution design and testing, and helps ensure that delivered solutions are usable, secure, compliant and aligned with JCU's digital transformation objectives.




Key Responsibilities

Gather, analyse and document business, functional, technical, data and integration requirements.
Translate business needs into clear user stories, process flows, use cases and acceptance criteria.
Analyse current business processes and identify opportunities for process improvement and digital solutions.
Facilitate requirements workshops and engage effectively with business owners, end users, technical teams and external vendors.
Assess solution options, business impacts, dependencies and implementation considerations.
Maintain requirements traceability throughout the design, development, testing and implementation lifecycle.
Plan and coordinate System Integration Testing (SIT) and User Acceptance Testing (UAT), including test scenarios, test cases and defect resolution.
Support Agile delivery activities, including backlog refinement, sprint planning, demonstrations and release planning.
Manage requirement and scope changes, ensuring appropriate impact assessment, prioritisation, approval and documentation.
Identify and document data, security, privacy and access-control requirements, including compliance with Singapore PDPA.
Support business readiness, user communications, knowledge transfer, operational handover and post-implementation reviews.
Work closely with stakeholders to ensure solutions are practical, secure, compliant and aligned with organisational digital transformation objectives.




Job Requirements

Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or a related field.
Minimum 4 years of Business Analyst experience, preferably supporting enterprise application development, system modernisation or integration projects.
Proven experience in requirements gathering, analysis, documentation and validation.
Experience developing user stories, process maps, swimlane diagrams, data-flow diagrams, traceability matrices and acceptance criteria.
Strong experience facilitating workshops and working with business stakeholders, software engineers, solution architects and external vendors.
Experience working in an Agile environment, including backlog refinement, sprint planning and release planning.
Hands-on experience supporting SIT, UAT, defect management and solution validation.
Familiarity with tools such as Confluence, Azure Boards, Microsoft Visio and Microsoft 365, or equivalent platforms.
Good technical understanding of APIs, system integrations, data flows, relational databases, authentication, authorisation and cloud-based solutions.
Exposure to SQL, Postman or programming languages would be an advantage.
Understanding of data protection, information security and access-control requirements, including Singapore PDPA.
Experience with higher education, Student, HR or Finance systems would be advantageous.
Certification in Scrum, product ownership or process modelling would be an advantage.
Excellent communication, facilitation, analytical and critical-thinking skills, with the ability to translate effectively between business and technical stakeholders.
Collaborative, proactive and adaptable, with strong attention to detail and the ability to manage competing priorities.
