About Itineris:

Itineris, with HQ in Belgium, is a global technology company purely focused on software and services for the energy and water industry. Itineris is the developer of UMAX, a customer information system (CIS) and ERP solution fully integrated with and leveraging the power of Microsoft’s Dynamics 365 platform.

Founded in 2003 and Microsoft Dynamics Global ISV, Itineris has a sound financial base and has established an impressive track record of successful implementations. Our clients are international and regional players such as De Watergroep, Eneco, Engie, SSE, City of New York, City of Baltimore, Boston Water and Sewer Commission, City of Dallas




There’s a saying at Itineris Together goes a long way. And we live by that. Whether your job is translating our customers’ needs into the best software, selling, coding, or keeping our business running smoothly, we’re united by the same mission: growing our company and delivering innovative software that empowers energy and water utilities to engage better with their customers.




Function:

The Software Release Manager coordinates the timely deployment of our UMAX software and is responsible for implementing & managing release processes and calendar. The Release Manager works collaboratively with the business unit and customer stakeholders to guarantee a successful & timely implementation of Itineris’ UMAX solution as used by our customers.

UMAX is the brand name of our software solution for water and energy utilities. It automates their business processes and enables them to better engage with their customers. UMAX is a cloud-based solution, built on the powerful Microsoft Dynamics 365 platform.

The role will require regular travel in line with requirements of the client (3 days/week – ¾ week per month)




As our Release Manager, you:

Plan and organize the schedules and cycles for releasing products to each customer, making sure to coordinate with all customers in the business unit.
Take ownership of the release process and continuously improve it over time.
Manage risks and address any issues that may impact the scope, schedule, or quality of the release.
Conduct reviews to assess the readiness for release, including milestone reviews and business go/no-go reviews.
Recognize the need for changes and adjustments to release plans as necessary.
Collaborate with internal stakeholders to understand the impact of branching and code merges.
Serve as the single point of contact for the customer regarding all release activities, including deployment, testing, documentation, and more.
Maintain a repository of release-related information and manage important details such as build and release procedures, dependencies, and notification lists.
Keep and communicate documentation related to releases.
Collaborate with the development team to automate testing and deployment processes and identify and resolve any issues with building or deploying the software.
Ensure that proper quality measures and approvals are followed before software releases.
Coordinate activities that take place after application upgrades and/or releases.




What you bring:

Extensive experience in the complete lifecycle of software development, including Development, Testing, Production, Configuration, and Software Delivery.
Able to plan multiple releases with different priorities simultaneously.
A strong analytical mindset and a solid understanding of business principles.
Excels in building relationships with clients on-site and remote and possesses excellent communication and presentation skills.
Strong problem-solving abilities and can effectively resolve complex and time-sensitive issues while instilling confidence in clients.
Preferably knowledge about the Microsoft Azure principles and comfortable working with Azure platform or another comparable cloud platform.
Can-do attitude.
