General Description

BeOne is seeking a highly motivated professional to support the development and preparation of submissions supporting IVDs, CDx and Medical Devices across EU, UK and emerging markets.

The Senior Associate, Regulatory Affairs, Dx/CDx and Medical Devices, will serve as the regional regulatory resource for assigned programs, partnering closely with local drug regulatory teams, cross-functional stakeholders, and external diagnostic partners, when requested, to develop and execute regulatory strategies that support product development and registration objectives for EU IVDR and UK Clinical Performance Studies (CPS) and associated regulatory requirements for BeOne clinical studies.

The individual will provide regulatory guidance, support regulatory submission planning and health authority interactions, and ensure alignment of regional activities with global development and registration strategies. This role will be expected to work independently, proactively drive assigned programs, and effectively manage multiple priorities across a dynamic regional environment.

Essential Functions Of The Job


Support the development and implementation of regulatory activities for assigned Dx/CDx and Medical Device programs.
Manage the preparation, including writing as necessary, assembly, review, and timely submission of regulatory documentation, as required for investigation and registration of products in applicable regions.
Review Dx/CDx-related language in protocols, ICFs and other study documents.
Ensure regulatory submissions and activities comply with applicable regulatory requirements.
Provide support for regional health authority interactions, including meeting preparation, briefing packages and responses to regulatory inquiries upon request.
Provide general regulatory guidance to study teams reflecting best practices as aligned with management on Dx/CDx and Medical Device matters.
May coordinate with diagnostic partners regarding product development deliverables and registration activities, as applicable.
Support cross-regional initiatives and Dx/CDx programs, as needed.
Monitor regulatory requirements, guidance and industry trends, and communicate regulatory impacts to stakeholders.
Support regulatory processes, tools, best practices, policy initiatives and regulatory intelligence activities across the region.
Maintain accurate regulatory records and documentation in accordance with internal procedures.
Support additional regulatory and cross-functional initiatives in line with evolving business objectives and organizational priorities.


Core Competencies, Knowledge And Skill Requirements


Excellent oral and written communications skills are a must as are superior planning, project management and interpersonal skills.
Working knowledge/experience in preparation of CPS and supportive amendments and supplements.
Working knowledge EU IVDR (Regulation (EU) 2017/746), particularly Articles 5 and 58.
Knowledge/experience with regulatory requirements for other regions also desirable.
Working knowledge of GMP, GLP, and GCP regulations, as well an understanding of the pharmaceutical product life cycle.
Knowledge of regulatory requirements and registration pathways for IVDs, CDx, and Medical Devices across EU and UK regions, and emerging markets as applicable.
Demonstrated knowledge of EU Clinical Performance Study requirements.
Experience determining appropriate notification versus CPS authorization pathways.
Understanding of CTA and IVDR interactions within EU clinical trials.
Knowledge of Competent Authority and Ethics Committee processes across Europe.
Proven ability to work effectively in a cross-functional and matrixed environment.
Demonstrated ability to independently and proactively drive projects to completion.
Ability to manage competing priorities and multiple projects simultaneously.
Detail-oriented self-starter with the ability to manage competing priorities in a fast-paced, cross-functional environment.


Experience


Undergraduate degree in science (e.g. Chemistry, Pharmacy, Biochemistry, Biotechnology, Biology) or equivalent.
Experience in regulated industry, IVDs preferred. Direct experience in IVD/CDx development and regulatory environment preferred.
Language: Fluent English required (oral and written).
Computer skills: proficiency with Microsoft Office (Word, Excel, Power point, OneNote etc.) and with Adobe Acrobat software for publishing.
