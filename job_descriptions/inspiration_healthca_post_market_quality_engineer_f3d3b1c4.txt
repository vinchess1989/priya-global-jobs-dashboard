Job Purpose (Role Definition):

Responsible for supporting the Regulatory Affairs & PMS Manager on the promotion of the Post Market Surveillance strategy and meeting Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

Main Duties and Responsibilities:


Provide responses to the Competent Authorities, Notified Bodies and Regulatory Agencies in relation to adverse events and FDA Medical Device Reporting
Development and delivery of post market surveillance plan(s) and report(s) for all products within the business
Development and delivery of Periodic Safety Update Report(s) (PSUR) for all products within the business
Ensure vigilance, post market surveillance and market surveillance data are entered into EUDAMED in a timely manner
Assist and support any field safety notices or corrective actions generated by the Company or a 3rd party manufacturer
Ensure compliance to company policies
Review documents and product changes for Quality Management System impact
Assist and support 3rd party inspections and audits
Support the development, monitoring and reporting on metrics and KPIs needed to gauge the performance of the Quality Management System and to Quality Objectives.
Receive, record and acknowledge customer complaints received via telephone, email and written correspondence.
Manage and coordinate complaint investigations, gathering relevant evidence and ensuring investigations are thorough, documented and completed within defined timescales.
Liaise with relevant departments and stakeholders to facilitate investigations, agree resolutions and ensure timely closure of complaints.
Maintain regular communication with customers, providing appropriate updates on investigation progress and outcomes.
Identify complaint trends, recurring issues and potential product or process risks, escalating these where appropriate.
Initiate corrective and preventive actions (CAPAs) where required, ensuring actions are appropriately implemented and documented.
Participate in risk reviews where triggered via complaint process.
Maintain accurate and complete complaint records in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements and company procedures.
Ensure compliance with relevant company policies, procedures and Quality Management System requirements.
Assist with and support external audits and regulatory inspections.
Support the development, monitoring and reporting of Quality Management System metrics and KPIs to assess performance and identify opportunities for improvement.
Contribute to initiatives aimed at improving complaint handling processes, product quality and the overall customer experience.


Management Responsibilities:

None

Experience and Qualifications Required:

Mandatory:


Previous experience in Post Market Surveillance and complaint management activities in a Medical Device organisation.
Familiar with Quality System concepts, practices, and procedures (for example, ISO13485, MDSAP).
Knowledge of Medical Device Directive and Medical Device Regulation.


Desirable:


Process improvement / Six Sigma experience.
Strong user of the Microsoft Office Suite.
Experience of electronic QMS systems.


Key Skills and Competencies:


Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to build relationships, influence internal and external stakeholders and achieve objectives.
Strong customer service skills, maintaining a high level of professionalism, empathy and understanding when dealing with customers, including those who may be upset or frustrated.
Strong problem-solving and critical thinking skills, with the ability to assess situations, identify appropriate solutions and drive issues towards resolution.
Ability to work effectively as part of a team and across multidisciplinary departments, contributing to projects and representing the department when required.
Ability to work independently, taking ownership of activities and managing responsibilities with minimal supervision.
Effective time management and organisational skills, with the ability to manage multiple projects, competing priorities and deadlines, including working under pressure.
High levels of attention to detail and accuracy, particularly in maintaining records and documenting activities.
Enthusiastic and committed to learning new skills, developing knowledge and contributing to continuous improvement.
