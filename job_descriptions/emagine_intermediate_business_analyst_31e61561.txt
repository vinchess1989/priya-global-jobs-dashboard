We are looking for an experienced IT Business Analyst / Application Support Specialist to support the ongoing administration, enhancement, and optimization of grant management systems, with a particular focus on SmartSimple and legacy applications. The successful candidate will play a key role in business analysis, application support, data migration, testing, reporting, and user adoption activities, working closely with business stakeholders, technology teams, vendors, and third-party providers.

Scope:


Involvement in SmartSimple configuration, testing, administration and support; overseeing grant schemes including EMFAF and Core funded schemes; L1/L2 support for SmartSimple and legacy systems (Fluent/GEMS); managing user onboarding, access management, and documentation.
Conducting business analysis and process design through requirements elicitation, user stories and acceptance criteria, process maps, workshops, and traceability.
Managing data migration from legacy EMFAF data from GEMS and Fluent into SmartSimple, including mapping, validation, and reconciliation.
Executing Testing and QA: creating test scripts, cases and data; providing System, Integration and UAT support.
Providing training and fostering user adoption.
Reporting: developing Power BI dashboards analyzing SmartSimple data.


Experience:


4-6 years of relevant experience in ICT project delivery or support roles within structured project environments.
Proven experience in L1/L2 application support and incident management.
IT Business Analyst experience within the public sector, grant administration, or other regulated environments.
Experience in supporting the implementation, enhancement, and administration of business applications, particularly grant management systems like SmartSimple.
Collaboration with business stakeholders, technology teams, vendors, and third-party providers.
Gathering, documenting, and validating business, functional, and reporting requirements.
Creation of process maps and implementation of process improvements.
Managing system transition and migration from legacy applications.
Experience with data migration tasks including mapping, cleansing, validation, reconciliation, and post-migration assurance.
Strong experience in planning and executing system, integration, and user acceptance testing (UAT), including defect management.
Utilizing reporting and dashboards using Power BI or similar tools.
Proven capabilities in change management and user adoption support.


Requirements:


Experience in project documentation including plans, requirements, process flows, and test scripts.
Working knowledge of MS Planner Pro, Visio, and the Microsoft Office suite.
Familiarity with data migration processes, reporting tools (e.g., Power BI), and structured testing methodologies.
Understanding of grants administration systems, workflow configuration, and business process automation.
