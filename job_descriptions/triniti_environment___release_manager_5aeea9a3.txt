IT Environment Manager | 12-Month Contract | Perth, WA | Daily Rate 
 
Our client, a large enterprise organisation in Perth, needs an experienced IT Environment Manager for a 12-month contract. You’ll make sure their non-production and production-like environments are stable, available and fit for purpose for development, testing and release.

You’ll be the main point of contact for anything environment-related, working with infrastructure, application, testing, release and product teams to plan ahead, clear blockers and keep delivery on track.

Key responsibilities


Manage provisioning, configuration, refreshes, maintenance and decommissioning across Dev, SIT, UAT and Pre-Prod
Keep environments aligned to production baselines, security standards and delivery needs
Plan environments, data, access and dependencies across projects, releases and BAU
Coordinate database refreshes, data reloads and environment resets
Triage environment issues, drive resolution and communicate impacts to stakeholders
Maintain environment registers, schedules, configuration records and status reporting
Make sure change, release and governance processes are followed
Reduce recurring issues through process improvement and automation
About you

Proven experience in IT Environment Management
Strong understanding of multi-environment landscapes (Dev, SIT, UAT, Pre-Prod, Prod)
Solid ITIL knowledge (change, release, incident and problem) and experience with ServiceNow or similar
Familiarity with CI/CD pipelines, deployment processes, monitoring tools, and cloud and on-premise environments
Strong planning, coordination and stakeholder management skills
High attention to detail and a continuous-improvement mindset
