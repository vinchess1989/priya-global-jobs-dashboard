Elaine Mifsud Western
Human Resources Professional

Join Crane Authentication as our Associate Manager, Digital Services and play a key role in supporting and developing the digital technologies that power our manufacturing operations. This is an exciting opportunity to lead a team of specialists while driving innovation, system performance and continuous improvement across advanced inspection, personalisation and digital manufacturing systems.




What you will be doing




Leading and developing the Digital Services team to ensure high levels of performance and technical expertise.
Managing and supporting digital manufacturing systems, including inspection and personalisation technologies.
Driving continuous improvement initiatives to enhance system reliability, efficiency and performance.
Supporting technical projects, new product introductions and system integrations.
Maintaining data traceability, reporting and digital system configurations.
Collaborating with Engineering, IT, Quality and Operations teams to resolve technical challenges.
Supporting audits, customer requirements and compliance activities.
Creating and maintaining technical documentation, SOPs and work instructions.
Coaching and developing team members while promoting knowledge sharing and best practices.




What we are looking for




MQF Level 6 or higher in ICT, Electrical Engineering or a related field.
Experience in digital systems, system integration, product inspection or manufacturing technologies.
Strong IT troubleshooting and technical problem-solving skills.
Experience leading projects and continuous improvement initiatives in a manufacturing environment.
Knowledge of vision inspection systems, data management and digital technologies.
Advanced analytical skills with the ability to translate complex data into actionable insights.
Programming and database experience, ideally including C# and SQL.
Strong communication skills and the ability to work effectively across multiple functions.







What’s in it for you?




Build your career with us through internal career progression opportunities and annual development reviews.
Be rewarded for your contribution with annual salary reviews and recognition for your achievements.
Invest in your future with dedicated learning and development support to help you grow professionally and personally.
Enjoy flexible work arrangements that help you balance work and life commitments.
Support your wellbeing through a range of employee wellbeing initiatives and benefits.
Be part of an inclusive culture where everyone is valued, respected and encouraged to succeed.
Connect, engage and have fun through an active Sports & Social Committee that organises events and activities throughout the year.
Be part of a global organisation while making a meaningful impact at a local level.
