RLDatix is on a mission to transform care delivery worldwide, ensuring every patient receives the safest, highest-quality care. Through our innovative Healthcare Operations Platform, we're connecting data to unlock trusted insights that enable improved decision-making and help deliver safer healthcare for all.

Job Description

RLDatix (RLD) is on a mission to help raise the standard of care…everywhere. Trusted by over 10,000 healthcare organisations around the world, our solutions help improve health and care. Our applications ensure that patients receive the best and safest care while supporting the providers who deliver it.Joining TeamRLD means being part of a global effort of over 2,000 team members in making a difference in healthcare…every day.

We're searching for a London-based Release Lead to join our Quality Compliance Systems (QCS) team, so that we can reliably deliver high-quality software from development through to production. The Release Lead will lead and optimise end-to-end software release and technical delivery processes, coordinating cross-functional teams to ensure delivery remains on schedule and aligned with business priorities.

How You'll Spend Your Time


Coordinate complex technical programmes and end-to-end releases across multiple teams to ensure delivery aligns with strategic objectives, agreed milestones and production timelines.
Own and manage release plans, dependencies, risks and transitions from development completion through to production deployment.
Coordinate activities across R&D, QA, Documentation, Localisation, Cloud Engineering, Product Management and Customer Success to ensure effective end-to-end delivery.
Establish and maintain release readiness criteria, quality gates and required documentation to ensure testing and business requirements are completed prior to deployment.
Establish programme governance, reporting and KPI management, using performance and operational data to drive accountability and continuous improvement.


What Kind of Things We're Most Interested in You Having


5+ years of relevant experience in software release management, technical programme delivery, or a closely related role, including 2+ years leading complex, cross-functional software releases across multiple teams.
Proven success establishing and managing release readiness criteria and quality gates, ensuring testing, business and operational requirements are completed prior to production deployment.
In-depth knowledge of how to develop and manage release and delivery plans, mapping milestones and dependencies while communicating changes, risks and decisions to stakeholders.
Ability to commute to London once a week.
Sincere interest in SaaS delivery within Agile and Scrum environments, with Kanban and hybrid delivery model experience highly desirable.
A knack for working collaboratively within cross-functional technical and business teams.
