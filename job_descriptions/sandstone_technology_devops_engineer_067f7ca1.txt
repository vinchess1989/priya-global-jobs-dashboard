Sandstone is seeking a DevOps engineer who is a highly technical individual to assist in transforming and architecting our development and business processes to improve our competitive advantage in time-to-market, cost and time of product development and implementation.




Sandstone Technology is a leading provider of loan origination, internet banking and mobile banking solutions to financial institutions globally. Headquartered in Sydney, New South Wales and with offices in Melbourne, Manila in the Philippines and Leeds in the UK, Sandstone is delivering world's best practice strategic banking solutions to financial institutions globally.




Key responsibilities :

Provide regular internal stakeholder updates and maintain up to date documentation
Ensure operational efficiency, reliability and security for the Cloud Platform
Become a DevOps advocate to our clients and help realise efficiency benefits for Sandstone
Conduct analysis and review of identified technology processes and provide recommendations for improvement
Provide operations support for hosted products as part of the Cloud services offering
Drive operational efficiencies through automation of repetitive tasks
Available for 24/7 on-call roster on rotation




About You

5 years’ experience in software support and deployment
Experience in production critical environments
Strong understanding of change management practices, ideally with ITSM experience
Proven experience in a number of these technologies: Terraform, Kubernetes, Docker, Packer, Git
Experience with scripting languages such as Python and BASH
Strong exposure and working experience with AWS. Strong experience with other cloud technologies can be considered (E.g. Azure or GCP)
Good understanding of TCP/IP networking concepts and web services
Good understanding of security best practices
Strong understanding of CI/CD processes




Desirable:

Rancher
Dynatrace
Sumo Logic




A bit about us...




Before “fintech” was a thing, our founders were dreaming up new ways to transform banking, simplifying the customer journey and the employee experience.




30 years later Sandstone Technology is still leading the charge, innovating and evolving as the industry evolves. Our high client retention rate is our proudest achievement with 30 financial institutions across Australia, New Zealand, Asia and the United Kingdom placing their trust in our solutions.
