Job Description

About Digital Human Health (DHH)

Digital Human Health (DHH) connects technology, data, and people to enable and accelerate Human Health business across global markets. DHH supports commercial and medical teams by delivering customer-centric digital platforms, data capabilities, and technology-enabled operating models that enable flexible go-to-market approaches, actionable insights, and personalized customer experiences in a regulated environment.

Within DHH, the Content Creation & Storage pillar of Digital Content & Customer Experience (DCCE) builds the platforms and capabilities that help Human Health create compliant, on-brand content faster. As part of the Content Accelerator, the pillar is moving content creation from manual production to AI-generated content that is built to brand and compliance standards from the start, combining agentic content creation platform (Content Studio), Adobe capabilities, and the design system.

Role Purpose

Own business analysis for Content Studio, AI-enabled content creation capability. Turn business needs into clear, build-ready, and traceable requirements, and work hands-on alongside engineering, UX, and implementation partners from discovery through release.

The role is the Product Owner’s day-to-day partner. As the person grows, they will be expected to take on product ownership for defined Content Studio features or components, including backlog decisions, stakeholder alignment, and release outcomes.

Scope of Ownership & Adjacent Responsibilities

Accountable for (owns the deliverable and its quality)


Content Studio requirements functional specifications, user stories, and acceptance criteria for assigned features and components.
Backlog readiness stories meet the agreed Definition of Ready before sprint planning.
The product source of truth (Confluence/JIRA) workflows, business rules, input/output requirements, integrations, known limitations, and the decision log.
Requirements traceability from business need to story, test, and released functionality.
UAT coordination with business users, including tracking feedback and gaps through to closure.
The user feedback loop capturing feedback from content creators, brand teams, and reviewers, and turning it into prioritized backlog items.


Partners on (contributes; another role makes the final decision)


Backlog prioritization and release scope, with the Product Owner.
Solution design and technical approach, with engineering and implementation partners.
Integration requirements with adjacent platforms (e.g., Adobe capabilities, design system, guardrails/rules engine, Veeva Vault PromoMats), with the respective product teams.


Adjacent


Flex across other Content Creation & Storage workstreams (e.g., Adobe Brand Intelligence experiments, design system) as priorities shift.


Key Responsibilities

Requirements & Backlog Ownership


Run discovery with business users to understand workflows, pain points, and desired outcomes before writing requirements.
Write user stories with clear acceptance criteria, edge cases, and dependencies, and resolve open questions before development starts.
Prepare refinement and sprint planning with the Product Owner. Bring a ready, sized backlog and a recommended priority order.
Turn workflows into clear product behavior, including user flows, error handling, and human-in-the-loop review points.
Manage requirement changes and log scope decisions so everyone knows what changed and why.


Working with Tech Teams & Validation


Work closely with engineering during sprints to clarify requirements and resolve open questions quickly.
Get hands-on with Content Studio’s AI features (e.g., prompts, agent inputs/outputs, brand rules) to understand how they behave and confirm they meet requirements.
Build a working understanding of the platform’s architecture, integrations, and APIs to have informed discussions with engineering and partners.
Coordinate UAT with business users, capturing feedback, defects, and gaps and following them through to resolution.


Documentation, Governance & Communication


Keep the Content Studio source of truth current and easy to understand for new team members, business users, engineers, and partners.
Maintain the decision log, open questions, risks, and dependencies across internal and partner teams.
Build compliance into requirements, including human-in-the-loop controls, AI labeling, auditability, and alignment with AI and content policies.
Share concise updates on progress, decisions, risks, and releases with the Product Owner and stakeholders.


Stakeholder & Vendor Collaboration


Facilitate requirement workshops and working sessions that end in clear decisions and owners.
Review vendor and partner solutions against documented requirements, and raise gaps early.
Act as the bridge between business and technical teams so both share the same understanding of what is being built and why.


What Success Looks Like


Stories reach sprint planning ready to build, with little rework or mid-sprint clarification.
Content Studio has one current, trusted source of truth that engineers, partners, and new joiners actually use.
Every released feature can be traced back to a business need and an agreed acceptance test.
Delivered features match what the business asked for, with gaps caught early through UAT.
User feedback is turned into prioritized improvements quickly and visibly.
Over time, the person independently owns defined features or components end to end, showing readiness for a Product Owner role.


Qualifications & Experience

Education

Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field required; Master’s degree preferred.

Experience


4–5+ years of experience as a Business Analyst, Product Analyst, or Product Owner/Manager in digital products or technology delivery.
Hands-on experience with GenAI tools or solutions, e.g., building, configuring, or testing prompts, AI workflows, agents, or LLM-based applications, beyond awareness level.
Understanding of agentic AI concepts (agents, orchestration, tool use, guardrails, human-in-the-loop) and practical experience in pilots, proofs of concept, or early rollouts.
Strong requirements, user story, acceptance criteria, and process mapping skills; experience in Agile/Scrum teams using JIRA and Confluence.
Working technical literacy APIs, integrations, and data inputs/outputs, with the ability to engage credibly with engineers and vendors.
Experience supporting UAT and validating solutions against requirements.
Experience working directly with business users and turning feedback into rapid iterations.
Excellent written and verbal communication; ability to work across a geographically distributed team (India, Europe, US).


Preferred / Nice To Have


Experience with content management, marketing technology, Digital Asset Management, or content supply chain platforms.
Familiarity with the Adobe content ecosystem (e.g., Firefly, AEM Assets, Workfront) or Veeva Vault PromoMats.
Familiarity with design systems or Figma-based creative workflows.
Basic scripting or query skills (e.g., Python, SQL) or experience with low-code/AI agent builders.
Product Owner certification (e.g., CSPO, PSPO) or experience owning backlog decisions for a product or feature area.
Experience in pharma or other regulated environments; awareness of MLR/content review and AI governance considerations.


Key Competencies


Ownership and accountability for deliverables
Business-first, hands-on problem solving
Attention to detail and strong analytical thinking
Learning agility in a fast-evolving AI environment
Clear, concise communication and documentation
Strong cross-functional collaboration
