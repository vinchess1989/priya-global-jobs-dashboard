DevOps Engineer

Location: London

Employment Type: Permanent







We are looking for a talented Mid-Level DevOps Engineer to join a growing technology team within a fast-paced financial services environment.




This is an excellent opportunity for a hands-on engineer with experience across Microsoft Azure, Terraform and Kubernetes to play a key role in developing, maintaining and improving cloud infrastructure and deployment environments.




You will work closely with software engineers and technical stakeholders to enhance infrastructure automation, optimise deployment processes and ensure the reliability, scalability and security of technology platforms.




Key Responsibilities




Design, implement and maintain cloud infrastructure using Microsoft Azure.
Automate infrastructure provisioning and management using Terraform.
Deploy, manage and troubleshoot containerised applications using Kubernetes.
Build, maintain and improve CI/CD pipelines.
Work with Docker and containerisation technologies.
Monitor infrastructure performance, availability and security.
Identify opportunities to improve automation and operational efficiency.
Troubleshoot infrastructure, deployment and application environment issues.
Collaborate with development and infrastructure teams.
Support cloud security, governance and infrastructure best practices.
Contribute to improving system resilience, scalability and reliability.




Essential Skills & Experience




3–5 years' experience in a DevOps, Cloud Engineering or Platform Engineering role.
Strong hands-on experience with Microsoft Azure.
Practical experience using Terraform and Infrastructure as Code.
Experience working with Kubernetes, ideally Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS).
Good knowledge of Docker and containerisation.
Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines.
Understanding of cloud networking, security and infrastructure management.
Scripting experience with Python, Bash or PowerShell.
Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving abilities.




Desirable Skills




Experience within financial services, fintech or trading environments.
Experience with Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions.
Familiarity with monitoring tools such as Prometheus and Grafana.
Experience with Helm and Kubernetes deployment strategies.
Knowledge of Linux administration.
Understanding of highly available and resilient infrastructure environments.







The Ideal Candidate




The ideal candidate will be a proactive, technically capable DevOps Engineer who enjoys solving infrastructure challenges and improving engineering processes.




You will have a strong interest in cloud technologies, automation and modern infrastructure practices, alongside practical experience working with Azure, Terraform and Kubernetes.




You should be comfortable taking ownership of tasks, working collaboratively with developers and contributing to a high-performing engineering environment.




This is an exciting opportunity to join a technology-driven business where you can develop your technical expertise and make a meaningful contribution to its infrastructure and engineering capabilities.
