Sales, Service and Project Execution Regional Leader - Critical Power - Northern Europe (NE)

Job Summary

Prepares, distributes, and stores accurate and timely documents for projects as per the company's/project's procedures, and ensures effective version control and status tracking.

Your Role And Responsibilities

In this role, you’ll help run what runs the world, by taking on meaningful work that drives real impact.


Work model: hybrid/remote
Create, maintain and update technical documentation, including Method of Procedures (MOPs), Field Procedures (FPs) and other documentation relating to Critical Power Products.
Coordinate documentation across service, projects and QHSE functions, supporting the standardisation and harmonisation of documentation formats globally.
Manage document control processes, ensuring accurate document registration, numbering, version control, distribution, filing and archiving.
Ensure all documentation complies with company, project and ISO procedures, including information classification and confidentiality requirements.
Communicate product changes and updates that may impact the existing installed base, ensuring relevant Field Service Teams (FSTs) are informed.
Set up and maintain document management systems and processes in collaboration with project teams.
Prepare, maintain and distribute project document lists, tracking documentation progress and providing regular status reports.
Coordinate documentation schedules, communicate deadlines to stakeholders and ensure deliverables meet agreed timelines and quality standards.
Provide guidance and support to colleagues on document control processes, sharing best practices and lessons learned.
Identify opportunities to improve document management processes, systems and tools, recommending enhancements to improve efficiency and consistency.



Qualifications For The Role


Bachelor’s degree, with at least 1 year of experience in a similar role within a service or manufacturing environment.
Solid written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to communicate effectively with stakeholders at all levels of the organisation.
Proficient in Microsoft Office applications, with experience using MS Project desirable.
Solid technical writing skills, with the ability to prepare detailed test plans, technical reports and document test results accurately.
Effective organisational and time management skills, with the ability to manage administrative responsibilities and meet deadlines.
Highly effective at building collaborative relationships and working well with teams across the organization.
Demonstrates initiative, is accountable for responsibilities, and works with speed to deliver results.

More About Us
ABB Smart Power provides energy distribution solutions for data centers, industrial and manufacturing plants, critical infrastructure and commercial buildings. The Division’s technical teams work closely with industry partners, delivering advanced solutions that support rapid growth, energy transition, and sustainability objectives. The Division’s portfolio includes industrial circuit breakers, low-voltage systems, motor starting applications, and safety devices like switches and relays. Its Power Protection unit supports the world’s largest data center companies with advanced energy-efficient UPS solutions. The Division’s ABB Ability™ Energy Manager provides a scalable, easy-to-use platform that helps organizations save energy and reduce CO2 emissions.
