Summary

The Senior Labeling Project Leader is responsible for independently managing larger, complex, and high-impact labeling projects from initiation through closure. This role provides strategic direction and ensures that labeling changes are developed and implemented across applicable projects in alignment with business, regulatory, and operational requirements.

The Senior Labeling Project Leader drives, facilitates decision-making with global stakeholders, and proactively manages scope, schedules, risks, issues, dependencies, and constraints to achieve commitments. The role requires advanced project management expertise, sound judgment, and the ability to resolve complex challenges, influence stakeholders, and operate with a high degree of autonomy. In addition, the Senior Labeling Project Leader serves as a subject-matter resource, mentors and trains Project Leaders, promotes consistent use of project management practices and tools, and identifies opportunities to improve labeling project processes and execution.


Own, manage, and monitor multiple complex, high-impact labeling projects from initiation through closure; define project scope, requirements, business rationale, deliverables, and implementation needs with stakeholders; and ensure project objectives, quality standards, service-level agreements, and business commitments are achieved.


Essential Duties And Responsibilities


Develop and execute project and change-management strategies for complex labeling initiatives, including activity plans, schedule baselines, milestones, and quality-system records; apply sound judgment to adjust plans and maintain delivery commitments.
Drive alignment among global stakeholders, business project teams, and manufacturing sites to implement complex labeling changes for marketed products, new product introductions, geographic expansions, third-party agreements, acquisitions, and other high-impact business needs.
Exercise independent judgment to monitor and control projects throughout the lifecycle by evaluating scope changes; anticipating and managing significant risks, issues, schedule delays, dependencies, and resource constraints; recommending solutions; and securing stakeholder agreement on revised plans.
Facilitate complex global project and plant-tracker meetings to establish direction, drive timely decisions, communicate status and implementation readiness, resolve cross-functional conflicts, strengthen team collaboration, and escalate critical matters with recommended actions.
Serve as a subject-matter resource by mentoring, coaching, and training Project Leaders; provide advanced guidance on project management practices, tools, processes, and challenging project situations; and educate key stakeholders on the Global Labeling process.
Advise management by integrating project status, risks, trends, and relevant project information and recommending actions to support prioritization, timely issue resolution, and effective escalation.
Close projects by completing tracking and metrics records and documenting lessons learned; identify recurring issues and improvement opportunities; develop, communicate, and implement standard work; guide process-improvement teams; and advance departmental initiatives through Labeling Governance and the Labeling Management team.


Qualifications


Advanced knowledge and demonstrated application of project management principles, tools, and methodologies in independently managing multiple complex, high-impact projects with limited direction.
Demonstrated success defining project scope and execution strategies; developing and managing schedules and change plans; and delivering complex projects in accordance with quality standards, service-level agreements, and business commitments.
Advanced ability to anticipate, assess, and manage significant project risks, issues, scope changes, schedule delays, dependencies, resource constraints, and escalations using independent judgment and effective decision-making.
Excellent written, verbal, presentation, meeting-facilitation, conflict-resolution, and stakeholder-management skills, including the ability to communicate complex information and recommended actions to management.
Strong business acumen and demonstrated ability to influence, negotiate, build alignment, and drive timely decisions across global, cross-functional environments.
Substantial experience in the medical device, pharmaceutical, or other regulated industries, with strong knowledge of quality management, change management, and document management systems.
Strong understanding of cross-functional processes, procedures, guidance documents, and manufacturing-site implementation requirements, with the ability to resolve complex implementation challenges.
Working knowledge of product labeling, translations, new product introduction processes, manufacturing processes, and basic graphic design principles.


Experience and Education


Bachelor’s degree or equivalent experience.
PMP certification preferred.
Minimum of five years of progressively responsible project management experience, including experience managing complex projects in a regulated industry.
Advanced proficiency with Microsoft Project, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, and other project-tracking or collaboration tools.
Demonstrated ability to serve as a subject-matter resource; mentor, coach, and train Project Leaders; and promote consistent application and improvement of project management practices, tools, and processes.
