Associated Health Systems Inc. (AHS Inc.) is a Canadian specialty medical/surgical distributor with custom manufacturing capabilities, ISO 13485 and MDSAP certification. Founded in 1990, AHS operates across Canada, serving acute, long term care, and education facilities. Our resource network is Canadian - and Global.




Our Team is made up of individuals with unique backgrounds, experiences, skills, and perspectives. We value diversity and teamwork, and we're motivated and inspired by doing work that matters.




The Role of Quality Associate is a full-time, remote-hybrid position based in Edmonton, AB. This role supports the implementation and maintenance of the company’s Quality Management System, including monitoring compliance with ISO 13485 and internal processes and procedures.




Day-to-day activities include performing quality checks on products and processes, assisting with internal and supplier audits, documenting non-conformities, and supporting corrective and preventive actions. Reporting to the Quality & Regulatory Manager, the Quality Associate collaborates with operations, manufacturing, and distribution teams to ensure consistent quality standards, accurate recordkeeping, and continuous improvement. The role also involves preparing quality reports and presentations, maintaining documentation, and contributing to training efforts related to quality policies and procedures.




Qualifications:

Strong analytical skills to review data, identify trends, and support problem-solving.
Knowledge of quality auditing practices, with the ability to participate in internal audits and support external or regulatory audits.
Background in quality assurance, including developing or following procedures, managing non-conformities, and supporting corrective and preventive actions.
Understanding of quality management principles and systems, ideally including ISO 13485: 2016, MDSAP, or related medical device/healthcare standards.
Familiarity with SOPs, inspections, documentation and data analysis.
Strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and ability to maintain accurate documentation in a regulated environment.
Effective communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work cross-functionally across departments.
Proficiency in MS Office Suite (e.g., Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Teams)
Experience in medical device, healthcare distribution, or a regulated manufacturing environment is an asset.
Post-secondary education in science, engineering, quality management, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience is preferred.

AHS Inc. offers a competitive salary, flexible work arrangements, and a robust benefits package - because your well-being matters to us.
