Wipro is looking for a Salesforce DevOps Lead to join our Digital Transformation team in Reading. As the Salesforce DevOps Lead, you will take ownership of DevOps strategy and delivery across the Salesforce platform, helping ensure a secure, scalable and reliable environment.

You will work closely with Salesforce technical leadership, architecture, development, product, security, change and release management teams to improve deployment automation, strengthen CI/CD practices and enable efficient delivery across the Salesforce estate.




About Wipro

Wipro is a global technology services and consulting organisation focused on building innovative solutions that address clients' most complex digital transformation needs.

You will join a collaborative environment where technology, continuous improvement and innovation are central to how we support our clients and their transformation programmes.




Your Benefits

You will be joining a growing, fast-paced and dynamic organisation. As a Salesforce DevOps Lead, you will receive a competitive salary and a benefits package that includes opportunities for training, professional development and career progression.




Your Responsibilities

As a Salesforce DevOps Lead, you will:

Lead the Salesforce DevOps strategy, establishing best practices for CI/CD, automated testing, release management and deployment automation.
Design and optimise CI/CD pipelines, version-control approaches and branching strategies to enable reliable, conflict-free deployments with minimal business disruption.
Manage Salesforce environments and release processes across development, testing and production, improving consistency, stability and scalability.
Champion security, monitoring and operational excellence by implementing appropriate controls, identifying risks, resolving incidents and driving root-cause prevention.
Drive continuous improvement by identifying automation opportunities, improving development workflows and collaborating with platform and product teams to resolve performance and delivery challenges.




Mandatory Skills

You will have strong knowledge and practical experience in:

Salesforce DevOps, including Salesforce DX, Salesforce CLI, deployment automation and release management.
CI/CD and source-control technologies such as Git, Jenkins, Azure DevOps, Bitbucket or Copado, including branching and version-control strategies.
Salesforce development technologies including Apex, Lightning Web Components (LWC), Visualforce and JavaScript, together with Salesforce APIs such as REST, SOAP and Platform Events.
Automation, infrastructure-as-code and scripting using technologies such as Python or Shell, alongside experience of Agile and DevOps delivery methods.
Salesforce security, integrations, microservices, monitoring and incident management, with the ability to communicate technical concepts clearly to technical and senior business stakeholders.

Relevant Salesforce certifications, such as Salesforce Administrator, Platform Developer or DevOps-focused credentials, are beneficial.




Desirable Skills

Ideally, you will also be familiar with:

Salesforce products including Service Cloud, Field Service, CPQ and Commerce Cloud.
Salesforce test automation and data protection or backup solutions such as VRA and OwnBackup.
Cloud infrastructure and services using AWS or Microsoft Azure.
Large-scale digital transformation programmes and complex enterprise technology environments.
Working within the utilities sector or similarly complex and regulated environments.




Equal Opportunities

Wipro is an advocate for positive change and conscious inclusion. As a global employer, we strive to create a diverse Wipro family by remaining committed to the development of our culture, diversity, equality and inclusion in the workplace. All applicants are welcome.
