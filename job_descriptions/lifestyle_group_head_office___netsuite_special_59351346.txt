About Db Group

db Group operates a diverse portfolio of hotels, restaurants, catering operations and other commercial businesses. NetSuite is a core business platform used across multiple subsidiaries, properties, outlets and operational locations.

Our environment supports finance, purchasing, inventory management, requisitions, purchase orders, stock counts and intercompany inventory transfers.

We are seeking an experienced NetSuite Specialist to strengthen the internal IT team and take day-to-day ownership of the platform, working closely with Finance, Purchasing, Cost Control, Operations and external NetSuite partners.

Role Overview

The NetSuite Specialist will be the internal owner and primary point of contact for NetSuite support, administration and continuous improvement. This is a hands-on functional role requiring a strong understanding of how NetSuite supports finance, procurement, inventory and multi-entity operations. The successful candidate will manage user access, resolve operational issues, improve workflows and reporting, coordinate system changes, and support integrations with other core platforms.

The role will also reduce the Group’s dependency on external consultants by ensuring that requirements are properly assessed, documented, tested and controlled internally before work is assigned to implementation partners.

Key Responsibilities


Act as the internal escalation point for NetSuite incidents, service requests and operational queries.
Administer users, roles, permissions, subsidiaries, locations and access restrictions.
Manage onboarding, role changes, temporary access and user offboarding.
Maintain an accurate register of users, roles, licences, subsidiary access and location access.
Support subsidiary-level reporting, reconciliations, saved searches, dashboards and management reports.
Support the control and maintenance of vendor records, bank details and finance-related approval processes.
Maintain and improve approval workflows and purchasing controls.
Support NetSuite assembly items, bills of materials and recipe-based inventory consumption.
Develop and maintain operational saved searches and reports for Purchasing, Cost Control, Finance and management.
Administer and support the SuiteLite portal used by operational teams.
Coordinate portal enhancements, testing, user documentation and operational training.
Support and monitor integrations between NetSuite and other Group systems.
Work with technical consultants on REST, SOAP, SuiteTalk, middleware and filebased integrations.
Maintain interface documentation, data mappings, test cases and support procedures.
Build and maintain saved searches, dashboards, KPIs and operational reports.
Translate business requirements into practical NetSuite solutions.
Maintain documentation covering configuration, workflows, roles, integrations and support procedures.
Coordinate NetSuite enhancements with external functional and technical consultants.


Required Experience And Skills


At least 3 years of hands-on NetSuite experience in an administrator, functional consultant, application-support or ERP-specialist role.
Strong working knowledge of NetSuite financials, purchasing and inventory processes.
Experience administering users, roles, permissions, subsidiaries and locations.
Practical experience with saved searches, dashboards, reports, CSV imports and workflows.
Strong documentation and stakeholder-management skills.
Ability to work directly with Finance, Purchasing, Cost Control, IT and operational users.
Ability to manage external consultants and challenge proposed solutions, effort and scope constructively.
Strong analytical skills and a methodical approach to root-cause analysis.


Desirable Experience


Experience with Oracle MICROS Simphony or another enterprise POS platform.
Understanding of menu items, recipes, bills of materials, assembly builds, theoretical food cost and inventory depletion.
Experience supporting SuiteLite, PortalsPro or comparable NetSuite portals.
Familiarity with NetSuite integrations using REST, SOAP, SuiteTalk or middleware.
Knowledge of Opera PMS or hotel finance integrations.


Personal Attributes


Takes ownership and follows issues through to resolution.
Can communicate clearly with both technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Is comfortable working in a fast-moving, multi-company environment.


What We Offer


Full-time employment within an established hospitality group.
A dynamic, supportive and professional working environment.
Opportunities for professional development and career progression.
The opportunity to work within an internationally recognised hospitality brand.
