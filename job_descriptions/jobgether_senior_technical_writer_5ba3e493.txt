This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, who manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior Technical Writer based in United Kingdom.

The Senior Technical Writer will take ownership of technical documentation and business analysis activities for a project in the electronics repair and refurbishment sector. Working directly with senior client stakeholders, including the IT Director and subject matter experts, you will translate complex business requirements and software functionality into clear, accurate, and practical documentation. You will maintain documentation throughout the software development lifecycle, review APIs and application features, and help ensure technical information remains aligned with system changes. The role also involves analyzing workflows, validating requirements, and contributing to process improvements through diagrams and structured documentation. This opportunity is ideal for an independent professional who combines strong writing skills with analytical thinking and confidence in stakeholder communication.

Accountabilities


Create and maintain technical and functional documentation in GitHub Markdown, covering manufacturing, repair, and related software processes
Analyze business requirements and translate them into clear, structured documentation for developers, business stakeholders, and other users
Review and update existing documentation following software development changes to ensure accuracy and consistency
Explore current application features and document system functionality, workflows, and business processes
Conduct interviews with subject matter experts (SMEs) to gather, clarify, and validate technical and business knowledge
Review UI/UX designs in Figma and provide relevant feedback to support usability and documentation quality
Create process, system, and architecture diagrams using tools such as Draw.io, Visio, or PlantUML
Review API documentation and validate requests using Postman or cURL
Check the accuracy and functionality of API code examples and report documentation or implementation issues
Identify and log bugs discovered during documentation reviews, API testing, or application feature analysis
Develop shared documentation templates and prepare Change Request documentation
Record training materials and product demonstration videos using Snagit and Camtasia when required
Collaborate directly with the client's IT Director, developers, and other stakeholders to ensure documentation meets business and technical needs


Requirements


At least 3 years of experience as a Technical Writer, Business Analyst, or in a comparable role
Hands-on experience with Git, GitHub, and docs-as-code practices using Markdown
Experience testing and reviewing APIs with tools such as Postman, Swagger/OpenAPI, or cURL
Working knowledge of Jira and Confluence for project coordination and documentation
Experience creating diagrams with Draw.io, Visio, and/or PlantUML
Ability to read, understand, and review UI/UX designs in Figma
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, combined with excellent attention to detail
Upper-intermediate English proficiency or higher, both written and spoken, with the ability to communicate directly with senior client stakeholders and subject matter experts
Ability to work independently, organize priorities, and manage documentation activities effectively
Experience with Snagit and Camtasia is an advantage
Relevant industry or technical background is a plus
Experience preparing Change Requests or formal business and technical requirements specifications is beneficial


Tools And Technologies


Git and GitHub
Jira and Confluence
Postman and Swagger/OpenAPI
Draw.io, Visio, and PlantUML
Figma and Visual Studio Code
Microsoft Teams and Outlook
Snagit and Camtasia
