I'm recruiting an experienced DevOps Engineer supporting a major banking client on a business-critical DevOps platform initiative.

Sydney or Melbourne

This is a hands-on engineering role focused on Azure DevOps, JFrog Artifactory, Xray and CI/CD automation, with a strong emphasis on implementation, configuration and technical delivery.

You'll work within an established engineering team, alongside a Technical Lead who will provide the technical specifications and direction.

Key Responsibilities

Build, configure and maintain CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps, including YAML pipelines and deployment automation.
Configure and support JFrog Artifactory repositories, permissions, service accounts and artifact management.
Implement and support JFrog Xray vulnerability scanning, policies and security controls.
Implement established DevSecOps patterns within CI/CD pipelines.
Automate platform configuration, provisioning and operational activities using scripting.
Support artifact migration, repository configuration and integration across environments.
Troubleshoot CI/CD pipelines, authentication, permissions and deployment issues.
Work closely with the Technical Lead, internal engineering teams and JFrog product specialists.
Support platform upgrades, production releases, testing and operational stability.
Skills & Experience
Around 5+ years of relevant hands-on DevOps or CI/CD engineering experience. Practical capability matters more than years of experience.
Strong hands-on Azure DevOps experience, particularly CI/CD pipeline development, configuration and automation.
Practical JFrog Artifactory experience, including repository management, configuration or integration.
Experience with JFrog Xray and vulnerability scanning within CI/CD environments.
Experience implementing DevSecOps practices and security controls.
Good scripting skills using PowerShell, Python or Bash.
Understanding of authentication, RBAC, permissions, service accounts and secure deployment practices.
Experience with Jenkins, observability tools or regulated enterprise environments would be advantageous.

Interested? If you're a hands-on DevOps Engineer with Azure DevOps and JFrog experience, I'd love to hear from you.

I've been directly briefed by the hiring manager and know exactly what the team is looking for, so I'm happy to share further details about the opportunity.
