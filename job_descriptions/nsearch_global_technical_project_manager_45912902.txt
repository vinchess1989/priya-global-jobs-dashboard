Our client, one of Asia-Pacific’s leading organizations is looking for:




Project Manager




Responsibilities:

Oversee the operational functionality and reliability of the system, ensuring optimal system uptime and performance
Act as the primary operations lead for issues, enhancements, and release coordination
Monitor key performance indicators (KPIs) and track service levels, implementing corrective actions where necessary
Manage the integration process for external systems, such as agency and backend systems
Ensure smooth onboarding, system upgrades, and data flow management between agencies and third-party systems
Support and manage incident response and resolution for system-related issues, ensuring clear communication and timely resolution
Coordinate change management and release processes, including testing, deployment, and stakeholder communications
Serve as the main liaison for third-party vendors and system integrators, managing escalations and service delivery
Maintain comprehensive system documentation, integration protocols, and operational playbooks




Requirements:

Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related discipline with 5+ years of experience in IT operations, system management, or technical project management
Strong problem-solving and solutioning skills to address diverse challenges across different teams
Proven experience managing backend systems with external integrations; Strong understanding of APIs, cloud systems, and data flow management
Familiarity with Whole-of-Government (WOG) systems and processes will be an advantage
Experience working with Government agencies, public-sector systems, or related environments
Excellent communication skills and ability to manage cross-functional teams across technical, vendor, and agency stakeholders
