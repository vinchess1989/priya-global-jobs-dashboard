Partnering Technologies Greatest Innovators -Senior Consultant

PLM Administrator / Change Controller (Immediate or 1 month notice only)

Location: Cambridge

The Opportunity

An established business is looking for a PLM Administrator / Change Controller to support its engineering and product data management activities.

This role will focus on maintaining accurate product information, managing Bills of Materials (BoMs), controlling engineering changes and supporting the ongoing development of internal systems and processes.

Working with a variety of stakeholders, you will help ensure product information is accurately maintained, changes are properly documented and data is available when required.

Key Responsibilities

Systems Administration

Administer and maintain the company's product data management systems.
Investigate system issues and support the implementation of improvements.
Maintain and improve workflows, processes and procedures.
Support ongoing system development and enhancements.

Product Data & Configuration

Create, maintain and validate product documentation and data records.
Manage Bills of Materials and associated product structures.
Maintain product variants and ensure information is accurately recorded.
Ensure data is available in line with project requirements.
Work with internal teams to maintain data quality and consistency.

Engineering Change Control

Coordinate and administer engineering change requests and orders.
Maintain part numbers, revisions and associated documentation.
Ensure changes are recorded, tracked and communicated effectively.
Identify opportunities to improve existing processes.

About You

The successful candidate will have:

Experience working with an engineering PLM or PDM system.
A good understanding of Bills of Materials and product data management.
Experience with engineering change control and revision management.
Knowledge of product configuration and variant management.
Experience within an engineering, technical or manufacturing environment.
Strong organisational skills and excellent attention to detail.
Confident communication skills and the ability to work with different stakeholders.
Good Microsoft Office skills, particularly Excel.

Desirable Skills

Experience with Oracle Agile PLM or a similar platform.
Knowledge of master data management and system workflows.
Experience supporting system improvements or implementations.
Familiarity with Jira or similar tracking tools.
