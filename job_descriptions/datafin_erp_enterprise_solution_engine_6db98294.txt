ABOUT THE POSITION

ENVIRONMENT:

A global & cutting-edge Biotech company seeks the technical expertise of an ERP Enterprise Solution Engineer who own its ERP architecture, technical design, live/test environments, SQL Server and application health, integrations, security, releases, monitoring, backup and recovery readiness, and technical delivery of new modules, fixes and rollouts. This role is responsible for understanding business needs, aligning technology solutions, and ensuring the successful stabilization, optimization, and utilization of ERP, MES and other related systems to support the achievement of strategic goals, including planning and executing new phased rollouts of modules, integrations, sites and entities. You will serve as a key advocate for leveraging technology to enhance operational efficiency and drive business outcomes. The successful incumbent should possess a Degree or Diploma in IT/Systems, or equivalent qualification from a recognized tertiary institution in Information Technology with 7+ years proven experience in a similar role, preferably with a focus on ERP systems including hands-on experience implementing, administering and supporting Epicor Kinetic is required or strongly preferred. You will also need SQL Server administration, including backups, recovery and maintenance, REST and OData integration and API troubleshooting & BAQ, dashboard and SSRS Report Development experience.

DUTIES:

ERP Architecture and Technical Ownership -



Own the global Epicor Kinetic architecture, technical roadmap and solution standards, ensuring new modules, fixes and enhancements align with approved business rules, processes, security and CSV controls.
Translate approved requirements into system configuration, technical specifications, integrations and controlled development using appropriate Epicor capabilities.
Provide technical leadership to the ERP Application Engineer and act as the final internal escalation point for complex ERP issues.

Platform, Server and Environment Management -


Administer various environments, SQL Server, application services, scheduled processes, monitoring, patches, upgrades and deployment registers.
Maintain system availability and recoverability through backup/restore verification, recovery planning, performance monitoring and coordinated infrastructure remediation.
Liaise with Solution Architects to align enterprise system architecture with broader enterprise architecture principles.

Integration, Security and Controlled Change -


Design, operate and troubleshoot ERP integrations, APIs and interfaces with finance, banking, tax, shipping, CRM, data and manufacturing systems.
Administer privileged ERP security, service accounts, credentials and technical access controls, and maintain controlled release, change and rollback procedures.

ERP Platform and Environment Lifecycle Management -


Administer the Epicor Kinetic environment estate, including LIVE, TEST and DEV environment management and refreshes, backup and restore verification, and version and patch management aligned to tested baselines.
Maintain deployment registers for customizations, BPM directives, Epicor Functions and configuration changes, and monitor scheduled processes and system agent health.
Operate monitoring and alerting for applications, databases, print and integration services.

Data, MDM, Reporting and BI Collaboration -


Support the Master Data Management team with ERP data models, DMT-related technical guidance, migration dependencies, troubleshooting and validation; MDM retains responsibility for preparing and executing master data uploads.
Support Data Architects and BI report writers with ERP source definitions, data lineage, extracts, BAQs/SSRS, integration dependencies and ERP-side troubleshooting.
Own technical configuration and controlled deployment of ERP reports, labels, CofA outputs and print-routing components where these reside within the ERP platform.

Incident, Rollout and Compliance Management -


Lead L2/L3 technical incident and problem resolution, root-cause analysis, vendor escalation and permanent corrective actions.
Lead technical release, cutover, recovery and hypercare activities for new modules, integrations, sites and entities.
Ensure technical changes protect traceability, label/CofA integrity, financial posting integrity and regulated-system compliance.

Project Management and Plan of Record -


Develop comprehensive project plans, timelines, and milestones for system initiatives, and maintain a single integrated plan of record with a RAID log, decision log and steering reporting cadence.
Ensure every task, decision and risk has a named owner and keep all planning documents coherent with the plan of record.

Regulated Systems Compliance -


Maintain CSV validation evidence and change control for configurations, customizations and lot mask changes.
Safeguard traceability, label and CofA integrity, and financial posting integrity in system design and operation.

REQUIREMENTS:

Qualifications –


A Degree or Diploma in Information Technology/Systems, or equivalent qualification from a recognized tertiary institution in Information Technology.

Experience/Skills –


7+ Years proven experience in a similar role, preferably with a focus on ERP systems.
Hands on experience implementing, administering and supporting Epicor Kinetic is required or strongly preferred.
Strong familiarity with various business processes across different departments, such as Finance, Supply Chain, Manufacturing, and HR, is crucial. This helps in translating business requirements into effective technology solutions.
Proficiency in defining data mapping, transformation, and conversion processes to ensure seamless data flow between systems.
Experience with data manipulation tools and techniques is essential.
Demonstrated experience with designing, implementing, and managing integrations between ERP systems and other applications using middleware solutions
Proven track record in managing projects, especially those related to ERP implementation, upgrades, or integration initiatives.
Skills in defining project scopes, timelines, resource allocation, and risk management are important.
Experience in Change Management strategies, including user training and adoption planning, to ensure smooth transitions during ERP implementations and updates.
Experience in working collaboratively with external partners, third party vendors, and consultants to facilitate successful system integrations.
Experience in regulated manufacturing environments (ISO13485, GMP or CSV governed), including validation evidence, change control, traceability, label and CofA integrity, and audit support.
Experience supporting multi-site, multi-company ERP operations with intercompany transactions across time zones.
Working knowledge of posting rules, month end close support, bank and tax integrations, and reconciliation controls.
SQL Server administration (backups, recovery models, maintenance) and REST or OData integration experience.
