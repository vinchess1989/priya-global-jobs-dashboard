Role: Configuration Lead (Architect) – ProcessUnity / Meridian

Title: AVP/ VP

Location: Montreal, QC

Experience: 8+ Years

Work type - Fulltime

Mode of Work: Onsite 3-4 days per week




We are seeking a highly experienced Configuration Lead (Architect) to lead the architecture, configuration, governance, and release management of the Meridian (ProcessUnity) platform. This role will be responsible for ensuring the platform's scalability, stability, and alignment with enterprise risk management requirements while driving configuration best practices, environment management, and production support.

The ideal candidate will possess deep expertise in enterprise workflow or risk management platforms, strong solution design capabilities, and hands-on experience managing complex platform configurations and releases.

Key Responsibilities

Own and drive the Meridian (ProcessUnity) solution architecture, configuration standards, governance frameworks, and platform roadmap.
Lead platform configuration activities, ensuring scalable, maintainable, and compliant solutions.
Manage multiple environments including development, testing, UAT, and production.
Oversee deployment planning, release management, and production rollouts to ensure minimal business disruption.
Configure and optimize ProcessUnity AI capabilities, including tools such as Evidence Evaluator and other intelligent automation features.
Establish and enforce platform configuration standards, design guidelines, and best practices.
Partner with business stakeholders, risk teams, and technical teams to translate requirements into effective platform solutions.
Drive technical debt remediation initiatives and continuous platform improvement efforts.
Provide Level 3 (L3) support and act as the escalation point for complex production issues.
Conduct architectural reviews and recommend enhancements to improve performance, scalability, and operational efficiency.
Support platform integrations and coordinate with development teams on dependency management.
Maintain platform documentation, release artifacts, architecture diagrams, and operational procedures.

Required Qualifications

8+ years of hands-on experience configuring and administering enterprise risk management, governance, compliance, or workflow platforms.
Strong expertise in ProcessUnity/Meridian platform configuration and architecture.
ProcessUnity Certification or demonstrable equivalent experience in enterprise implementations.
Proven experience with platform architecture, configuration governance, and release management.
Strong SQL skills with the ability to analyze data, troubleshoot issues, and support reporting requirements.
Experience managing multiple environments and overseeing application deployments.
Strong solution design, system analysis, and stakeholder management skills.
Experience supporting production environments and resolving critical platform issues.
Excellent communication and leadership skills with the ability to influence technical and business stakeholders.
