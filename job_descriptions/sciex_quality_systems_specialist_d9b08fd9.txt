The Quality Systems Specialist supports the maintenance and continuous improvement of the SCIEX Quality Management System in accordance with ISO 9001, ISO 13485, FDA Quality Management System Regulation, and other applicable requirements. This role supports audits, CAPA, document control, training, eQMS administration, quality metrics, and continuous improvement. The position works cross-functionally to strengthen compliance, process effectiveness, and inspection readiness.

This position reports to the Senior Manager, QMS, and is part of the Quality Assurance & Regulatory Affairs Team located in Concord, Ontario and will be an on-site role.

In This Role, You Will Have The Opportunity To


Support the maintenance and improvement of QMS processes in accordance with applicable regulatory and internal requirements, including supporting internal and external audit planning, readiness, execution, responses, and action-item closure.
Administer eQMS activities, including document changes, controlled records, training assignments, reporting, revising QMS procedures, work instructions, forms, and training materials.
Guide audit NC owners and CAPA owners through investigations, action planning, implementation, documentation, and effectiveness verification.
Monitor quality metrics, analyze trends, maintain dashboards, identify improvement opportunities and escalate significant compliance risks to QMS leadership.
Support non-product software validation and cross-functional QMS improvement projects.


The Essential Requirements Of The Job Include


Bachelor’s degree in science, engineering, quality assurance, or a related field, or equivalent education and experience.
Minimum three years of quality assurance or QMS experience in a regulated development or manufacturing environment such as IVDs, clinical or pharmaceuticals.
Working knowledge of ISO 9001, ISO 13485, and FDA quality-system requirements.
Experience supporting QMS audits, CAPA, document control, and quality records.
Experience with eQMS applications, procedure writing, and quality-data reporting.


It would be a plus if you also possess previous experience in:


Non-product software validation and AI applications.
Power BI, and data-analysis or statistical tools.
Internal auditor or lead auditor training.
