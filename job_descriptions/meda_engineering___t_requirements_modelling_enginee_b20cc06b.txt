We are recruiting an experienced Requirements Modeling Engineer (Feature Modeling Engineer) to join our team to support our client in the automotive industry.




RESPONSIBILITIES:

Develop operational, system, subsystem, and component models using MBSE methodologies.
Create and maintain UML/SysML models, use cases, state machines, and system architecture.
Establish traceability between engineering requirements, models, and test cases.
Support verification, validation, testing, and root cause analysis of identified issues.
Drive functional complexity reduction, specification quality improvements, and reduced integration issues.
Support MBSE implementation, process improvements, and standardization across engineering teams.
Monitor project deliverables, timelines, backlogs, and KPIs to ensure alignment with MBSE roadmaps.
Collaborate with system architects, designers, software engineers, and cross-functional teams.
Manage IBM Rhapsody projects, versions, repositories, and toolchain improvements.
Identify modeling training, coaching, and staffing needs.
Participate in Agile Scrum, planning, reviews, retrospectives, and technical meetings.
Ensure compliance with MBSE standards, processes, and engineering guidelines




REQUIRED SKILLS & QUALIFICATIONS:

Minimum 3+ years of hands-on experience with UML, SysML, and MBSE.
Strong knowledge of requirements engineering, system architecture, and systems integration.
Experience developing use cases, business models, state machines, and SysML artifacts.
Experience with IBM Rational Rhapsody or similar tools (MagicDraw).
Familiarity with EWM, DOORS, GitHub, and testing tools.
Understanding of communication protocols and electrical/electronic system requirements.
Object-oriented programming knowledge (C++/Java) and experience with automation and scripting.
Understanding of system verification, validation, and testing across the engineering lifecycle.
Strong communication, collaboration, problem-solving, and organizational skills.
Ability to work independently in an Agile environment.




PREFERRED SKILLS:

Java development, including API and plugin development for IBM Rhapsody.
INCOSE best practices and systems engineering lifecycle experience.
Agile certification or experience.
Automotive industry experience; MATLAB and CATIA Magic.
VEOS training and WPF GUI development.
AUTOSAR-compliant code generation and integration of auto-generated code.
Knowledge of or interest in AI and LLMs.
DFSS certification and familiarity with Orthogonal Arrays for System Behavior Testing.




COMPENSATION & BENEFITS

$41.52 - $45.52/hour ( depending on experience)
Paid Benefits after 90 days (individual and family) – extended medical, out-of-country coverage, dental etc.
Pension eligibility, with employer match, after 1 full year of work
