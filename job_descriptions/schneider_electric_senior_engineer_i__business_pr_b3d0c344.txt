Mission (About The Role)

The Business Process Referent (BPR) is the primary functional expert and key point of contact for assigned business processes and supporting systems across the East Asia (EA) region. The BPR translates operational needs into clear requirements, supports process standardization, coordinates issue resolution and enhancements, and promotes effective user adoption.

The role connects business users, process owners, Digital/IT teams, shared services, external partners, and project teams. The BPR influences process outcomes through expertise and facilitation.

What will you do?

Process Expertise and User Support


Serve as the subject-matter expert for assigned end-to-end business processes, controls, and ERP-supported activities across EA.
Advise users on correct process execution, system usage, exception handling, and business impacts.
Lead or coordinate investigation of complex and recurring incidents across process, master data, integration, system, and training areas.
Escalate risks and unresolved issues with clear impact assessments, options, and recommendations.
Promote consistent practices across EA countries, sites, and business units.


Requirements And Solution Delivery


Gather, analyze, challenge, and document business requirements for enhancements, interfaces, reports, workflows, and automation.
Translate business needs into process flows, user stories, functional requirements, acceptance criteria, and test scenarios.
Collaborate with Digital/IT and Requestor during solution design and delivery to ensure it meets the business requirements.
Assess upstream and downstream impacts, including master data, logistics, controls, EDI, reporting, and external partners.
Coordinate User Acceptance Testing, defect resolution, business sign-off, deployment readiness, and post-go-live stabilization.


Continuous Improvement and Standardization


Identify inefficiencies, control gaps, repetitive incidents, manual activities, and automation opportunities.
Facilitate current-state and future-state process reviews using data, root-cause analysis, and stakeholder input.
Support EA process harmonization while documenting justified local legal, regulatory, or operational variations.
Prepare improvement proposals with expected benefits, risks, dependencies, effort, and implementation approach.
Monitor whether implemented changes deliver intended and sustainable outcomes.


Stakeholder and Change Management


Build effective relationships with business users, process owners, Digital/IT teams, shared services, vendors, and management across EA.
Facilitate workshops, requirement reviews, issue-resolution meetings, and cross-functional decision discussions.
Communicate process and system changes clearly, including impacts, responsibilities, and effective dates.
Support organizational change, user readiness, adoption, and regional or global transformation initiatives.


Governance, Performance, and Knowledge


Maintain process documentation, work instructions, decision records, controls, and ownership matrices.
Support prioritization of incidents, enhancements, and improvement opportunities based on business value and risk.
Provide fact-based status reporting, insights, and recommendations to management and process owners.
Develop user guides, training materials, FAQs, and reusable knowledge content.


What qualifications will make you successful?


Bachelor Degree in Business Administration, Supply Chain, Information Systems, or a related discipline.
Candidates with Diploma will be considered depending on the years of experience
4 to 5 years of experience in business operations, process analysis, ERP functional support, continuous improvement, or transformation.
Practical understanding of SAP ECC/S/4HANA or Oracle ERP within an enterprise ERP environment.
Experience with requirements gathering, process mapping, incident analysis, user acceptance testing, deployment, and stakeholder coordination.
Experience in a multinational, shared-service, supply-chain, manufacturing, or cross-country environment is advantageous.
Experience supporting multiple countries is advantageous.
