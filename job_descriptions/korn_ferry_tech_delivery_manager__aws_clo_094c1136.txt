Role: Tech Delivery Manager (AWS Cloud & DevOps)

Rate: £640 per day

Duration: 12 months

Location: Remote (possible travel to site once or twice per month, London)

IR35 determination: Inside

Responsibilities:

Cloud & AWS Delivery

Overseeing delivery of platform capabilities and services built on AWS, ensuring secure, scalable, and well‑architected solutions.
Coordinating technical planning and roadmap delivery within the product team.
Ensuring cloud infrastructure, deployments, and environments are appropriately managed and maintained.

DevOps & Engineering Enablement

Championing DevOps practices including CI/CD, observability, automation, infrastructure‑as‑code and shift‑left testing.
Supporting the team to mature the build‑run‑improve cycle, including release automation, incident response, monitoring and alerting.
Improving and maintaining IaC standards (Terraform/CloudFormation), operational runbooks, and deployment pipelines.

Delivery & Governance

Maintaining delivery plans, sprint goals and release schedules for a long‑lived product team.
Managing risks, issues, blockers and dependencies related to the platform.
Ensuring platform changes meet agreed quality, security and operational controls.

Stakeholder & Cross‑Team Engagement

Acting as the main delivery contact point for team stakeholders including product leads, architects, business owners and other platform consumers.
Translating technical information into clear delivery decisions and communicating progress effectively.
Collaborating with adjacent teams (e.g., security, networks, shared services) where cross‑cutting decisions or approvals are required.

Requirements:

Hands‑on delivery experience in a cloud environment, ideally AWS, including working with teams building or running cloud‑native services.
Strong understanding of AWS fundamentals (IAM, VPC, EC2, Lambda, S3, CloudWatch, CloudFormation/Terraform).
Demonstrated experience with DevOps practices, CI/CD pipelines and automation tools (GitHub Actions, Jenkins, GitLab CI, Azure DevOps etc.).
Experience working within a single multi‑disciplinary agile team delivering continuous increments of value.
Ability to manage a product backlog, delivery plans, risks and dependencies.
Strong communication skills, able to translate between technical and non‑technical stakeholders

Desirable:

AWS certifications (Solutions Architect, DevOps Engineer etc.).
Experience with Kubernetes/EKS, containerisation (Docker), or serverless patterns.
Understanding of SRE concepts (SLIs/SLOs, error budgets) and operational excellence.
Participation in platform engineering or DevOps communities of practice.

Qualifications:

Essential: certification in a delivery methodology (Scrum, Kanban) and proven technical delivery experience.
Desirable: AWS and DevOps‑related certifications
