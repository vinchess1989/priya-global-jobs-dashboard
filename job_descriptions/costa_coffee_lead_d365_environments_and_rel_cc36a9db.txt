Full job description

At Costa Coffee, we are what we craft. We’re reimagining coffee experiences in over 50 countries and counting, as a key part of the Coca-Cola System. Whether you get your coffee in a store, from a machine, at home, or on the go – we’ve got you covered.

Shape the environments and release controls that keep our Microsoft Dynamics 365 landscape secure, resilient and ready for change. You’ll coordinate complex releases across internal teams and technology partners, balancing business priorities with governance, capacity and operational risk.

Our teams make a difference. Whether that’s working on new tech for the perfect pour, helping our teams grow, creating award-winning campaigns, crunching the numbers, or developing the latest exciting menu item; together, we stir up success.

We may be a global brand, but we haven’t forgotten our roots. That’s where the Costa Foundation and our fantastic community agenda come in. Whatever your role, you can help us change lives in coffee growing communities and help your local community too.

We also want to help you grow in your career through amazing experiences, our apprenticeship scheme, and development programmes. At Costa, you can go beyond the day-to-day.

And as an Environment and Release Manager, there’s never been a better time to join.

So, why Costa?

We didn’t become a global coffee brand by sitting back. When you work here, you join a community that values passion, progression and integrity, with some pretty brilliantperks to sweeten the deal:

Own a piece of Costa’s success by becoming a share owner in Coca-Cola with our Share Investment Plan (SIP)

An annual discretionary bonus scheme, based on business results and individual contribution

A smart pension that saves you money on tax and national insurance, and matches your contributions up to 10%

The Costa Financial Support Fund, supporting team members who find themselves in unexpected financial pressure

50% discount in all Costa-owned stores, and 25% off in other participating stores

Private medical cover thanks to our Private Healthcare scheme

And that’s not all. Explore even more of our perks here: https://costaperks

We’re passionate about being a great place to work, where you can bring your unique self into our mix. We firmly support diversity, equity and inclusion, and continue to work with our teams to shape the future of our culture and values: Disciplined to Deliver, Passion for Progress, Win with Warmth, Courage to Challenge and Trusted Team Players.

What you’ll do

Being an Environment and Release Manager is about creating the control, visibility and collaboration needed to deliver safe, reliable change across our Dynamics 365 landscape. It’s your chance to stir up real success, which means you’ll be:

Owning the build, configuration, management and maintenance of environments, with effective release and deployment controls in place at all times.

Coordinating the scheduling, provisioning and release of Dynamics 365 environments across technology teams, business stakeholders and our managed service provider.

Ensuring changes follow Costa’s internal change process and meet Information Security, risk, SOX and audit requirements.

Managing environment sizing and capacity, anticipating future demand and resolving risks before they affect delivery or service performance.

Using your knowledge of Microsoft Azure services, integrations, infrastructure and the wider technology landscape to improve release quality, resilience and efficiency.

Who you are

It’s your unique ingredients we’re interested in:

Strong experience of environment and release management techniques, tools and methodologies within a complex, multi-vendor environment.

A clear understanding of the full project lifecycle and how delivery stages affect environment planning, availability and control.

Solid knowledge of the Microsoft Dynamics 365 landscape, supported by an understanding of Microsoft Azure services, infrastructure, networking and system integrations.

An effective communicator who can build trusted relationships across business and technology teams, external partners and stakeholders at every level.

Outstanding problem-solving and investigation skills, with the organisation and attention to detail needed to manage competing releases and operational priorities.
