What You’ll Do

Key Responsibilities


Windchill Technical Leadership & Solution Governance
Act as the internal Windchill Technical Lead for PSDO / ONEPLM operations, enhancements, integrations, deployments, and production support governance.
Provide technical leadership and guidance across solution design, functional/technical requirements, configuration, customization review, testing, deployment readiness, and hypercare.
Review technical designs, customization proposals, integration approaches, and partner-delivered solutions to ensure scalability, maintainability, upgrade compatibility, and alignment to HPE process standards.
Lead technical impact assessments for Windchill changes, enhancements, upgrades, CPS updates, rehosting activities, and integration changes.
Partner with PTC, TCS, HPE IT, business stakeholders, and PSDO SMEs to drive timely issue resolution and permanent corrective actions.
PLM Operations & Governance
Own end-to-end Windchill PLM operational governance across PDMLink, ProjectLink, PartsLink, SUMA/AML, CAD/eCAD integrations, BOM, document, and change management processes.
Manage global support model including incident triage, escalation handling, vendor coordination, SLA/KPI adherence, RCA governance, and operational communication.
Govern user access, license usage, role-based authorization controls, configuration control, release readiness, and audit preparedness.
Establish and maintain operational dashboards, support cadence, lessons learned repository, SOPs, RACI models, and continuous improvement playbooks.
Windchill-SAP S/4 Integration & Data Quality
Lead operational resolution of Windchill-SAP S/4 / MDM integration issues including payload failures, BOM errors, UOM discrepancies, MPN/AML issues, material master synchronization, and data quality fallout.
Define and validate cross-system data mapping, reconciliation rules, exception handling, integration monitoring, and post-load validation controls.
Partner with business and IT teams to improve data integrity across Windchill, SAP S/4, MDM, downstream operations, and reporting ecosystems.
Support data migration dry runs, validation cycles, cutover readiness, UAT sign-off, and post-go-live stabilization for Compute, Aruba, Storage, HPC & AI, and future BU onboarding programs.
Development, Configuration & Customization Oversight
Guide Windchill configuration and customization across types, attributes, lifecycle templates, workflows, access policies, teams/roles, classification, reports, validators, listeners, and integration touchpoints.
Provide hands-on or review-level expertise in Java, JSP, JavaScript, XML/JSON, SQL, REST APIs, Info*Engine / Windchill APIs, build/deployment packaging, and troubleshooting.
Ensure all customizations follow governance standards, documentation discipline, security requirements, regression testing, and upgrade-safe design principles.
Coordinate build, deployment, validation, and rollback planning with partner developers, HPE IT, SaaS vendor, and business testers.
AI Enablement, Automation & Digital Operations
Drive AI-enabled capabilities for Windchill operations including intelligent incident triage, historical ticket recommendation, automated RCA support, knowledge retrieval, and support copilot use cases.
Implement AI-powered data validation and anomaly detection for BOM, UOM, MPN/AML, material master, integration payload, and lifecycle state mismatches.
Use automation and analytics to reduce manual investigation, improve ticket deflection, accelerate TAT, and enhance self-service support for business users.
Create reusable knowledge assets, SOPs, prompts, automation requirements, dashboards, quality checks, and AI adoption metrics for PSDO operations.
Stakeholder, Vendor & Program Leadership
Act as the technical interface between PSDO, HPE IT, PTC, TCS, business users, engineering teams, and external partners.
Provide executive-ready updates, risk escalations, technical options, trade-off analysis, and mitigation plans for critical ONEPLM initiatives.
Mentor team members and partner resources on Windchill concepts, troubleshooting methods, integration thinking, documentation standards, and operational best practices.
Support CoE development by building internal capability, knowledge transfer models, training materials, office hours, and certification-driven upskilling plans.


What You Need To Bring

Education and Experience Required:


7+ years of experience in PLM / Windchill technical, functional, operations, or enterprise engineering systems roles; 7–10 years may be considered for strong internal candidates with proven Windchill leadership experience.
Deep hands-on experience with PTC Windchill architecture, data model, business objects, access control, lifecycle management, workflows, BOM/change/document management, and configuration management.
Experience with Windchill 12.x / 13.x or equivalent recent Windchill versions, including cloud/SaaS and on-prem awareness, upgrade readiness, CPS updates, rehost, testing, and production stabilization.
Experience leading or governing Windchill integrations with SAP S/4, MDM, CAD/eCAD, partner systems, downstream reporting, or enterprise middleware/integration platforms.
Experience in production support, incident management, RCA, technical troubleshooting, deployment governance, release validation, data migration validation, and UAT/hypercare support.
Experience working with global stakeholders, external partners, SaaS vendors, business SMEs, engineering teams, and IT teams in a matrixed operating model.
Experience building technical documentation such as solution design, functional/technical design, configuration guides, support playbooks, test cases, release notes, and knowledge base articles.
