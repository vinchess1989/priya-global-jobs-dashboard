Job Purpose

The Product Owner – I&O Platform Engineering is accountable for defining, managing and continuously evolving the I&O Marketplace and Internal Developer Portal (IDP) as the common digital front door through which developers and technology teams discover, request and consume approved infrastructure, platform and engineering capabilities. The role owns the product vision, roadmap, backlog and value outcomes, working across I&O product teams to deliver standardised service offerings, reusable platform capabilities, automated fulfilment and secure developer self-service.

The role drives the shift from traditional, manually fulfilled service-request models toward a product-led and developer-centric experience. It improves developer experience, discoverability, adoption and delivery speed while ensuring that marketplace offerings remain governed, secure, supportable, cost-transparent and aligned with enterprise architecture, risk and operational requirements.




Job Responsibilities

The Product Owner is accountable for the end-to-end product lifecycle of the I&O Marketplace including strategy, customer discovery, roadmap prioritisation, service catalogue integration, developer experience, onboarding of product offerings, automation, governance, adoption and value realisation. The role acts as the primary interface between developers, I&O product teams, platform engineering, service management, architecture, cyber risk and delivery teams.




Product Vision, Strategy and Roadmap

Define and communicate the product vision, strategy, target experience and multi-year roadmap for the I&O Marketplace and IDP.
Align the marketplace roadmap to Platform Engineering, Infrastructure & Operations, cloud, DevSecOps and enterprise technology priorities.
Translate developer and consumer needs into clear product outcomes, epics, features and measurable acceptance criteria.
Maintain a prioritised product backlog that balances customer value, operational risk, technical debt, regulatory obligations and delivery capacity.
Use product analytics, customer feedback and operational performance data to inform investment and prioritisation decisions.

Internal Portal and Developer Experience

Own the Portal/Marketplace as the common front door for discovering and consuming infrastructure, platform and engineering products and services.
Create a coherent developer experience across service discovery, documentation, software templates, self-service actions, status visibility, support and feedback.
Promote reusable golden paths, paved roads and standard engineering patterns that reduce cognitive load and accelerate delivery.
Ensure the IDP provides accurate product ownership, service descriptions, eligibility, dependencies, support models, lead times, costs and lifecycle information.
Drive accessibility, usability and consistent information architecture across the marketplace and portal experience.

Marketplace Offering and Catalogue Management

Define the marketplace product model, onboarding standards, minimum information requirements and quality criteria for I&O offerings.
Partner with I&O Product Owners to onboard, publish, maintain, version and retire marketplace products and service offerings.
Ensure catalogue entries are standardised, searchable, understandable and linked to approved architectures, policies and support arrangements.
Govern the lifecycle of offerings, including demand intake, readiness assessment, publication, change, review, rationalisation and retirement.
Prevent duplication and fragmentation by promoting reusable enterprise capabilities and clear product boundaries.

Self-Service, Automation and Integration

Drive automated fulfilment and orchestration across infrastructure, cloud, network, database, middleware, observability, security and other I&O capabilities.
Work with engineering teams to integrate the IDP with ITSM workflows, CI/CD platforms, source control, cloud management, configuration management and automation tooling.
Promote API-first, event-driven and policy-as-code approaches where appropriate, with human approval retained only where risk or regulation requires it.
Reduce manual hand-offs, fulfilment lead times, rework and operational dependency through standard workflows and reusable automation.
Ensure fulfilment status, approvals, failures and support routes are transparent to consumers.

Customer Discovery, Adoption and Change Enablement

Conduct regular discovery with developers, engineers and technology teams to understand pain points, journeys and unmet needs.
Define customer personas, priority use cases and end-to-end journeys for marketplace and IDP capabilities.
Drive adoption through targeted communications, demonstrations, onboarding, documentation, training and product-community engagement.
Establish structured feedback channels and convert insights into prioritised improvements.
Champion a product-led culture focused on measurable customer and business outcomes rather than feature delivery alone.

Governance, Risk and Operational Readiness

Ensure marketplace and IDP capabilities comply with enterprise architecture, cybersecurity, data privacy, audit, access-control and technology-risk requirements.
Embed appropriate approval, entitlement, segregation-of-duties, logging and traceability controls into self-service journeys.
Define product guardrails and onboarding controls without creating unnecessary friction for low-risk, standardised consumption.
Ensure new and changed capabilities meet operational readiness criteria, including support ownership, monitoring, resilience, documentation, service levels and recovery requirements.
Manage product risks, dependencies, audit findings, technical debt and remediation actions through established governance forums.

Service Performance and Value Realisation

Define and track product OKRs and KPIs, including adoption, active users, self-service completion, fulfilment lead time, automation rate, customer satisfaction, abandonment, failure demand and cost-to-serve.
Monitor marketplace availability, performance, content quality and end-to-end journey health.
Use telemetry and value-stream data to identify bottlenecks and prioritise continuous improvement.
Demonstrate business value through faster provisioning, reduced manual effort, improved standardisation, stronger control and better developer productivity.
Provide transparent product reporting to leadership and governance stakeholders.

Financial, Commercial and Vendor Management

Contribute to and manage the product budget, forecasts and investment priorities for the marketplace and IDP capability.
Monitor product costs, licensing, vendor services, cloud consumption and delivery expenditure against approved budgets.
Assess total cost of ownership and expected value when prioritising platform enhancements and integrations.
Support commercial management of technology vendors, implementation partners, contracts, renewals and service performance.
Identify cost optimisation opportunities while protecting service quality, security, resilience and strategic outcomes.

Stakeholder and Delivery Leadership

Act as the primary product interface across I&O Product Owners, Platform Engineering, application development, architecture, cyber security, service management and business technology teams.
Lead product ceremonies including backlog refinement, sprint planning, reviews, showcases and quarterly planning.
Provide clear product decisions, resolve priority conflicts and manage cross-product dependencies.
Work with designers, engineers, architects and delivery leads to ensure solutions are valuable, feasible, usable, secure and supportable.
Provide product leadership, coaching and guidance to delivery teams and marketplace contributors.




Essential Qualifications - NQF Level

Matric / Grade 12 / National Senior Certificate




Preferred Qualification

Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification in Information Technology, Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Business Management or a related discipline.
Postgraduate qualification in technology management, business administration or product leadership would be advantageous.




Essential Certifications

Product management training covering discovery, product strategy, roadmaps, experimentation, product analytics and value measurement.
ITIL 4 Foundation or equivalent practical service-management training.




Preferred Training / Certifications

Platform engineering or Internal Developer Portal training, including practical exposure to products such as Backstage, Port, Humanitec, Cortex or equivalent enterprise IDP capabilities.
Cloud foundation or associate-level certification in Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services or Google Cloud.
DevOps, DevSecOps, SRE, Lean Portfolio Management or value-stream management training.
Automation platforms such as Ansible, Terraform, Chef or equivalent.
ServiceNow Certified System Administrator or relevant ServiceNow catalogue, portal, automation or workflow certification where ServiceNow underpins marketplace fulfilment.
User experience, design thinking, customer journey mapping or accessibility training would be advantageous.




Minimum Experience Level

Minimum 8 years of experience in technology, digital products, infrastructure, platform engineering, service management or related disciplines, including at least 4 years in Product Owner, Product Manager or comparable product leadership roles.
Demonstrated experience owning a digital platform, enterprise service marketplace, developer portal, service catalogue or self-service product from discovery through adoption and value realisation.
Experience working with infrastructure and platform products across cloud, compute, network, database, middleware, observability, automation or security domains.
Experience managing complex backlogs, cross-team dependencies and delivery across multiple product or engineering teams.
Experience in a large, regulated enterprise where cybersecurity, technology risk, auditability and operational resilience are critical.
Experience with Agile delivery, DevOps practices, APIs, workflow automation, service management and product analytics.
Ansible, Terraform, Chef




Technical / Professional Knowledge

Product strategy and lifecycle management: ability to define product vision, outcomes, roadmaps, investment priorities and retirement decisions.
Customer and developer experience: strong capability in discovery, personas, journey mapping, usability, information architecture and continuous feedback.
Internal Developer Portal concepts: software catalogues, golden paths, templates, plug-ins, self-service actions, documentation, scorecards and developer analytics.
Platform engineering and DevOps: understanding of CI/CD, source control, APIs, infrastructure as code, cloud platforms, container platforms, automation and policy as code.
Service management and catalogue design: understanding of request models, approvals, entitlements, fulfilment workflows, service levels, support models and CMDB/service relationships.
Backlog and value management: ability to prioritise using evidence, value, risk, cost, dependencies and product outcomes.
Data and analytics: ability to define meaningful KPIs, interpret telemetry, assess adoption funnels and quantify product value.
Governance and risk: ability to incorporate cybersecurity, privacy, access control, auditability, resilience and regulatory requirements into product design.
Commercial acumen: understanding of budgets, total cost of ownership, vendor management, licensing and value-based investment decisions.
Stakeholder leadership: ability to influence senior stakeholders, product teams and engineering communities without relying solely on positional authority.
Communication: strong written, verbal, facilitation, negotiation and executive presentation capability.
