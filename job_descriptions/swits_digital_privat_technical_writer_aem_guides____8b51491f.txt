Job Title: Technical Writer AEM Guides & DITA

Experience: 5 7 Years

Location : Bangalore Role Overview

We are looking for an experienced Technical Writer with hands-on expertise in Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) Guides and DITA XML. The ideal candidate should have experience creating, managing, reviewing, and publishing structured technical documentation using DITA standards and AEM Guides.

The candidate should be proficient in developing user guides, product documentation, technical manuals, and other structured content while collaborating with developers, product managers, and subject matter experts (SMEs).

Key Responsibilities


Create, edit, and maintain technical documentation using DITA XML and AEM Guides.
Develop structured content such as Concept, Task, and Reference topics.
Create and manage DITA maps, sub-maps, and content hierarchies.
Work with DITA elements such as conref, keyref, profiling, conditional content, and content reuse.
Use AEM Guides for content creation, version management, review workflows, and publishing.
Publish documentation into multiple output formats, including PDF, HTML5, and online help.
Collaborate with developers, product owners, and SMEs to understand complex technical concepts and convert them into clear documentation.
Maintain documentation standards, templates, style guides, and content quality.
Perform technical reviews, proofreading, editing, and validation of documentation.
Manage documentation updates and ensure consistency across product releases.
Work with XML authoring tools and DITA-OT for content validation and publishing, where applicable.


Mandatory Skills


Hands-on experience in Technical Writing and Documentation.
Strong knowledge of DITA XML and structured authoring.
Practical experience with Adobe Experience Manager (AEM) Guides.
Experience creating Concept, Task, and Reference topics.
Knowledge of DITA maps, conref, keyref, and conditional processing.
Experience with XML-based authoring tools such as Oxygen XML Editor.
Understanding of content reuse, version control, and documentation workflows.
Strong written and verbal communication skills.


Good-to-Have Skills


Experience with DITA Open Toolkit (DITA-OT).
Knowledge of HTML, CSS, XML, and basic JavaScript.
Experience with Git or other version control systems.
Familiarity with API documentation and software product documentation.
Experience publishing documentation in PDF, HTML5, and web-based formats.
Knowledge of Agile/Scrum development environments.


Required Candidate Profile


Strong attention to detail and documentation quality.
Ability to understand complex technical information and simplify it for end users.
Ability to work independently and collaborate with cross-functional teams.
Good analytical, editing, and problem-solving skills.
