Cluster Release Manager

Introduction

The Cluster Release Manager will play a critical role in enabling end-to-end coordination of

integrated software deliveries across 25 interdependent value chains (similar to projects)

within a major transformation programme at Julius Baer.

This individual will be part of the Release Management Team and ensures that all relevant

processes related to Release Management such as Release Planning, Dependency

Management, Deployment Management, Release Note Creation, Quality Gate Adherence

and Communication are correctly performed.

Operating in a highly matrixed environment, the Cluster Release Manager will act as a

central orchestrator between different stakeholders on different levels (senior management,

project leads, technical teams, testing groups, application leads, infrastructure providers, and

business stakeholders to deliver coordinated, traceable, and quality-assured releases.

Additionally, this role also requires the willingness to work extra hours and weekends

overwatching specific activities such as Data Refresh or Dress Rehearsal Activities.

Key Responsibilities

 Responsible for the Release Management discipline for up to 5 projects in parallel

(together with Release Coordinator)

 Develop and maintain a comprehensive release plan aligned with delivery timelines,

dependencies, risk profiles, and environment availability.

 Coordinate all aspects of release management execution across multiple projects and

suppliers, ensuring alignment with enterprise change management principles

 Chair regular release coordination meetings with stakeholders to track progress, resolve

blockers, and manage risks.

 Ensure full traceability from requirements → design → development → test cases →

defects → deployments, leveraging tools such as JIRA, Confluence, or similar ALM

platforms.
