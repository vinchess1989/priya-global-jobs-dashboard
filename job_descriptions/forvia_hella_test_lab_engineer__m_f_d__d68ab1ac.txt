Location Auckland, New Zealand

FORVIA HELLA is a listed international automotive supplier. As a company of the FORVIA Group, FORVIA HELLA stands for high-performance lighting technology and vehicle electronics and, with the Lifecycle Solutions Business Group, also covers a broad service and product portfolio for the spare parts and workshop business as well as for manufacturers of special vehicles. With currently around 34,000 employees at over 125 locations, the company is active worldwide and generated adjusted sales of €8.0 billion in the fiscal year 2025.

YOUR TASKS

Are you an engineer with a passion for testing, problem-solving and product development? Do you enjoy investigating how things work, analysing results and finding practical solutions to complex technical challenges?

HELLA New Zealand is looking for a motivated and technically capable Test Lab Engineer to join our Design & Development team.

This is an opportunity to play an important role in product development and validation, ensuring our products meet the highest standards of performance, quality, safety and reliability against defined requirements, while improving laboratory methods and processes.

About The Role

Reporting to the Head of Design & Development, you will be responsible for the day-to-day operation of our test laboratory. You will plan and conduct product testing, analyse results and maintain laboratory processes and compliance documentation.

Working closely with our Design & Development, Quality, Product Management and Production teams, you will help ensure products are thoroughly tested and validated before moving into production.

Key Responsibilities


Laboratory scheduling and equipment readiness: Coordinate laboratory activities and test schedules, maintain equipment, arrange servicing and calibration, and ensure testing resources and consumables are available.
Test planning and execution: Develop test plans and execute testing programmes for components, assemblies, devices and complete systems. Use product requirements, engineering standards and failure mode and effects analysis (FMEA) inputs to guide testing and minimise technical risks.
Results analysis and technical reporting: Investigate test outcomes, identify potential issues and recommend product improvements. Prepare clear, accurate and objective technical reports, and communicate findings to relevant stakeholders.
Laboratory compliance: Maintain laboratory processes and documentation in accordance with ISO/IEC 17025 requirements and applicable external certification standards.
Continuous improvement: Identify opportunities to improve laboratory processes, testing methodologies, equipment utilisation and overall efficiency.
Budget and investment planning support: Support laboratory capital expenditure planning and monitor operating expenditure needs.



Your Qualifications

You are a practical, analytical and self-motivated engineering professional who enjoys working hands-on with technical equipment and solving problems through a structured, scientific approach.

Essential Experience And Capabilities


An engineering background or qualification, preferably in electrical or mechanical engineering or a related science discipline.
Previous experience in a laboratory testing, product validation or engineering testing environment.
A sound understanding of engineering principles and technical problem-solving.
Experience developing test procedures, conducting tests and interpreting technical results.
Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to prepare technical reports.
Good planning and organisational skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities.
Confidence working collaboratively with cross-functional teams and external stakeholders.
A proactive approach to identifying issues, taking ownership and driving improvements.
A strong commitment to workplace health and safety and professional standards.



Useful Additional Experience


Experience working with ISO/IEC 17025 laboratory accreditation or quality management requirements.
Experience in the automotive, marine, mining or other regulated or safety-focused industries.
Knowledge of electrical and/or mechanical testing, test equipment and measurement systems.
Experience with hardware testing, test circuit development or automated testing systems.
