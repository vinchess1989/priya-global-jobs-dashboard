Job Description

DePuy Synthes is recruiting for a Commercial Quality Specialist - India, located in Mumbai, India.

The Commercial Quality Specialist – India plays a critical leadership role in ensuring the effectiveness, compliance, and continuous improvement of the Quality Management System (QMS) and Health, Safety & Environment (HSD) programs across DePuy Synthes India operations. This role partners closely with regional and global Quality, Regulatory, and business leaders to safeguard product quality, employee safety, and regulatory compliance while enabling operational excellence. This is an exciting opportunity to make a meaningful impact by strengthening quality culture, driving risk‑based decision making, and supporting business growth in a highly regulated medical device environment.

Key Responsibilities


Lead and maintain the India QMS in alignment with global DePuy Synthes and Johnson & Johnson Quality & Compliance standards, applicable regulations, and internal policies.
Oversee HSD programs to ensure compliance with local, regional, and company requirements, promoting a strong culture of safety and environmental responsibility.
Serve as the primary quality and HSD business partner for India leadership, providing strategic guidance, risk assessments, and decision support.
Lead internal and external audits, inspections, and assessments; ensure timely and effective remediation of observations and findings.
Drive continuous improvement initiatives, including CAPA, management review, and quality metrics to enhance system effectiveness and compliance performance.
Ensure effective change management, document control, and training programs across India operations.
Collaborate with Regional and Global QARA partners to align India practices with enterprise strategies and regulatory expectations.


Qualifications

Education


Bachelor’s degree required in Engineering, Life Sciences, Quality, Environmental Health & Safety, or a related discipline.
Master’s degree or advanced qualification preferred.


Required

Experience and Skills


Minimum 5–8 years of progressive experience in Quality Management Systems and/or Health, Safety & Environment within a regulated industry (medical devices, pharmaceuticals, or healthcare preferred).
Strong working knowledge of global and local quality and HSD regulations and standards (e.g., ISO 13485, ISO 14001, ISO 45001, and applicable Indian regulations).
Demonstrated experience leading audits, inspections, and regulatory interactions.
Proven ability to lead cross‑functional initiatives and influence stakeholders at multiple organizational levels.


Preferred


Prior experience supporting manufacturing, distribution, or commercial operations in India.
Experience working in a global or matrixed organization.
Demonstrated success driving culture change and continuous improvement.
Familiarity with digital quality systems and data‑driven quality metrics.
Strong problem‑solving, risk management, and decision‑making skills.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills.


Other


Language: Fluency in English required; proficiency in local languages preferred.
Travel: Limited domestic travel; limited international travel as needed.
Certifications: Lead Auditor, Quality, or HSE certifications (e.g., ISO Lead Auditor, NEBOSH) preferred.
