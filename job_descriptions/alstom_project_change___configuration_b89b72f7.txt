At Alstom, we understand transport networks and what moves people. From high-speed trains, metros, monorails, and trams, to turnkey systems, services, infrastructure, signalling and digital mobility, we offer our diverse customers the broadest portfolio in the industry. Every day, more than 80 000 colleagues lead the way to greener and smarter mobility worldwide, connecting cities as we reduce carbon and replace cars.

YOUR FUTURE ROLE :


Full accountability of the end to end CCM process and deliverables in a Low Criticality Project / No Criticality Project or Program (Level 3).
Ensure accuracy, consistency, and traceability of the delivered products and services to internal and external customers.
Keep the Project/ Program configuration under control and manage changes during the whole lifecycle of the Project or Program.


POSITION IN THE ORGANIZATION –

MAIN OFFICE LOCATION:


Located in Nigel (Ekurhuleni) at the production site
May also be required to visit suppliers and other Alstom sites/offices
Work with Cross Functional Teams to execute Sourcing Strategies i.e. Central Teams, Suppliers, Projects Team, Supplier Quality, Supplier Development, Supply-Chain, Quality, Engineering, Economic Development, Legal, Finance, etc.


WE’LL LOOK TO YOU TO:


Is leading the CCM activities in a Project/Program or is collaborating with the Advanced PrCCM or the Senior PrCCM of the Project when applicable.
Applies CCM procedures, instructions, templates etc. and ensures that project/ program team members are trained and apply them.
Establishes Configuration Management Plan with the CCM activities and the associated roles & responsibilities.
Ensures Configuration and Change Management applicable tools are applied and manages access rights.
Escalates any risks / issues CCM related within the Project / Program organization and reports on CCM Performance Indicators, derives and manages actions (PDCA).
Ensures the execution Development for Quality (DfQ) Gates for CCM deliverables.
Performs Configuration Management activities, such as:
Identifies and monitors the accuracy and maturity of items under configuration control.
Prepares a Configuration Strategy and publishes Release Notes accordingly (Configuration Baselines, Target Configurations), following review with technical stakeholders, ensuring the product consistency.
Ensures Supplier Configuration Management incl. CCM requirement negotiation and requested CCM documentation.
Supervises the correctness of Configuration Status Accounting Report (CSAR) for each unit (i.e. As Build/As Maintained/As Installed).
Performs Change Management activities, such as:



Organizes and leads the Change Boards, while recording decisions and actions.
Ensures all Change Requests are complete, clear, evaluated, assessed in terms of cost, schedule, quality and risk through all the stages, with the cooperation of the Change Boards participants.
Monitors Change Requests progress end-to-end within the Change Process and ensures all involved stakeholders are informed at all times.
Facilitates event management (e.g. electrical, software etc.) and transfer into the Change Management Process, when necessary.

All About You

We value passion and attitude over experience. That’s why we don’t expect you to have every single skill. Instead, we’ve listed some that we think will help you succeed and grow in this role :

GRADUATE OF UNIVERSITY DEGREE IN ANY OF ENGINEERING/PROJECT MANAGEMENT/BUSINESS MANAGEMENT OR EQUIVALENT.


Knowledge from Configuration & Change Management
Knowledge of Business Process Management, Project Management, Risk Management, Requirements Management Supply Chain, Engineering, Industrialization, Data Management and Document Control inter-related with CCM.


Work Experience In An International Environment


Knowledge of Alstom configuration & change processes and related processes of contributing Métiers/ Functions
Above 3 years of experience in a configuration and change management role.
Experience in the entire CCM lifecycle of a Project/Program.
Mandatory knowledge of railway products, railway solutions (signalling, telecom etc.), systems and components
Knowledge of CCM tools (ALM, Clear Quest, Clear Case SAP, PLM, AEC-tpPLM, ERP, etc.)
Ability to communicate with technical and non-technical project/program stakeholders
Systematic, rigorous, proactive and organized with good attention to details.
Is a team player and sets a good environment for team colleagues
Driven for results with s trong multi-tasking and influencing skills
Good knowledge of Microsoft Office
