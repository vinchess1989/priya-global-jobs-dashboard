Your Opportunity

The Technical Writer / Editor is responsible for transforming complex technical content into clear, accurate, and audience-appropriate documentation. This role ensures documentation is consistent, usable, and aligned with established standards, while supporting authors and subject matter experts in producing high-quality deliverables. Working within a quality-driven environment, the role also contributes to the continuous improvement of documentation practices, processes, and standards.

Working closely with Project Managers, Engineers, subject matter experts, and clients, the Technical Writer / Editor helps ensure technical information is effectively communicated, fit for purpose, and appropriate for its intended audience. Through writing, editing, review, and collaboration, the role strengthens the quality and effectiveness of project deliverables and supports clear communication throughout project delivery.

Your Key Responsibilities

Technical Editing and Writing


Edit and refine technical documents to ensure clarity, logical flow, and alignment with the intended audience (e.g., internal stakeholders, clients, regulatory bodies).
Perform multi-level editing including: - Proofreading: Grammar, punctuation, spelling, formatting, and typographical accuracy
Copy editing: Consistency, tone, terminology, and adherence to style guides
Substantive editing: Structural improvements, content organization, and message clarity
Rework and rewrite sections of documentation as required to improve readability and coherence without altering technical intent.


Standards and Style Compliance


Apply and enforce Stantec styles and style guides or client styles and style guides where project mandated.
Ensure strict consistency across terminology, formatting, headings, numbering, references, and document structure.
Identify and resolve inconsistencies in voice, tone, and technical nomenclature across deliverables.


Document Development and Templates


Design, develop, and maintain standardized templates for internal and external documentation.
Ensure templates are optimized for usability, accessibility, and efficiency across teams.
Support document lifecycle management, including version control, revision tracking, and archival practices.


Quality Assurance and Control


Implement quality assurance checks at each stage of the document lifecycle.
Perform final reviews to confirm completeness, accuracy, formatting integrity, and compliance with applicable standards.
Maintain traceability and documentation of revisions, approvals, and final outputs.


Collaboration and Author Support


Work directly with technical authors (primarily engineers and subject matter experts) to improve content clarity and structure.
Provide constructive, actionable feedback that enhances writing quality and technical communication skills.
Facilitate content refinement discussions, ensuring alignment between technical accuracy and audience comprehension.
Serve as a quality advisor by identifying gaps, ambiguities, or risks in documentation.


Document Management and Control


Assist with document control processes, including file, organization, metadata management, and controlled distribution.
Ensure proper handling of confidential and sensitive information in accordance with organizational and project policies.
Maintain document integrity through systematic tracking of revisions and approvals.


Multi-Channel Content Editing


Edit and adapt content for multiple formats and outputs, including:- Technical reports
Manuals and procedures
Training materials
Presentations and visual documentation
Apply visual communication principles to enhance document readability and engagement.


Your Capabilities and Credentials

Technical Expertise


Demonstrated experience in technical writing and/or editing within a professional, engineering, or technically complex environment.
Advanced proficiency in: - Microsoft Word (including styles, templates, and advanced formatting)
Microsoft Excel
Adobe Acrobat Pro (for markup, review, and PDF optimization)
Experience with document design tools (e.g., Visio or equivalent) considered an asset.


Communication and Editing Skills


Exceptional command of written English, with the ability to adapt tone and style for diverse audiences.
Strong ability to interpret technical content and translate it into clear, structured documentation.
Capacity to identify and resolve ambiguity, redundancy, and inconsistency in complex materials.
French language proficiency is an asset.


Analytical and Editorial Judgment


Ability to independently assess the appropriate level of editing required (proofreading vs. substantive editing).
Strong editorial judgment when balancing technical accuracy with readability and clarity.
Ability to prioritize edits based on audience needs, document purpose, and project constraints.


Collaboration and Influence


Proven ability to work constructively with subject matter experts, including providing feedback that may require content changes.
Strong interpersonal skills with the ability to negotiate revisions diplomatically while maintaining document quality standards.
Ability to receive and incorporate feedback professionally.


Organization and Execution


Excellent time management skills with the ability to handle multiple concurrent deliverables.
Strong attention to detail and commitment to accuracy and consistency.
Ability to manage competing priorities and meet tight deadlines, including occasional overtime if required.


Process and Continuous Improvement


Experience contributing to or developing documentation processes and procedural improvements.
Demonstrated interest in enhancing quality systems, workflows, and document lifecycle efficiency.
Ability to identify opportunities to standardize, streamline, and improve documentation practices.


Professional Attributes


High degree of professionalism and discretion, particularly when handling confidential materials.
Self-directed and capable of working independently with minimal supervision.
Adaptable and quick to learn new tools, systems, and processes


Education and Experience


Post-secondary education or certification in technical writing, editing, communications, education, or a related discipline.
Equivalent combination of education and relevant professional experience will be considered.
Experience in instructional design, engineering documentation, or technical publishing is an asset.
Minimum of two to three years of related work experience; or an equivalent combination of


education and related experience

Working Conditions


Primarily office-based with extended computer use.
Flexible in-office and remote work arrangement (Hybrid).


Additional Notes


This position requires the ability to operate in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.
Responsibilities may evolve based on project requirements and organizational needs.
