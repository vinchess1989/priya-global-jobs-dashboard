DevOps Engineer (Azure) x 2 - Up to £55k - New!




Location: Cardiff

Working pattern: Hybrid




We’re working with a large organisation that is looking for DevOps Engineers to support the development and continuous improvement of software delivery capabilities within a collaborative technology team.




Skills

Experience with CI/CD pipelines, source control, build automation, automated testing and deployment tooling.
Working knowledge of Azure DevOps and cloud-based technologies.
Understanding of on-premises and cloud infrastructure, including virtualisation, containerisation and microservices.
Experience with technologies such as SQL and .NET, alongside relevant automation and infrastructure tools.
Knowledge of non-functional testing, including security, scalability, availability and reliability.
Awareness of secure coding principles and testing practices.
Familiarity with Agile or hybrid delivery methodologies and continuous integration principles.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, numerical reasoning and technical documentation skills.




Responsibilities

Develop and enhance DevOps capabilities, CI/CD pipelines and automated deployment processes.
Build, configure and maintain technical environments, integrations and associated data throughout the software development lifecycle.
Improve build automation, source control, testing and release management processes.
Support integration and non-functional testing, including performance, security, availability and reliability.
Work across cloud and on-premises infrastructure, selecting appropriate technologies to meet delivery requirements.
Identify and implement tools and techniques that improve efficiency, reduce delivery complexity and maintain quality.
Collaborate with engineering and delivery teams to understand requirements, share technical knowledge and support the adoption of new capabilities.
Use DevOps metrics and performance measures to identify opportunities for continuous improvement.
Promote secure engineering practices and contribute to a strong, collaborative DevOps culture.
