About The Role

We are looking for an experienced Business Analyst to support the development and enhancement of an Enterprise Architecture (EA) Tool for a banking environment.

In this role, you will work closely with business stakeholders, Enterprise Architecture teams, developers, and testers to understand business requirements and translate them into clear functional specifications, user stories, UI prototypes, and test cases.

You will be involved throughout the software development lifecycle, from requirement gathering and design to testing, release coordination, and post-release verification.

Key Responsibilities


Gather business requirements and translate them into functional requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria
Participate in requirement workshops, design discussions, and backlog refinement sessions
Create process flows, wireframes, and UI prototypes using Figma or AI-assisted tools
Develop and maintain conceptual data models, class diagrams, and Entity Relationship Diagrams (ERDs)
Prepare test scenarios and test cases, support UAT, track defects, and validate fixes
Coordinate with development, testing, product, and support teams to ensure smooth releases
Support production verification and track post-release issues through closure
Maintain project documentation and provide regular progress updates to stakeholders


Requirements


3-5 years of experience in Business Analysis, Technical Business Analysis, product development, or technology delivery
Strong understanding of requirements gathering, user stories, acceptance criteria, and backlog management
Good knowledge of Object-Oriented Analysis and Design (OOAD), class diagrams, and ERDs
Experience in functional testing, UAT, test case preparation, and defect management
Understanding of release management and software delivery processes
Hands-on experience with Jira, Azure DevOps, or similar tools
Exposure to Figma, wireframing, or UI prototyping tools
Strong analytical, documentation, and stakeholder management skills


Preferred Skills


Experience in banking or financial services
Understanding of Enterprise Architecture concepts, governance, and EA tools
Familiarity with Zephyr, Cucumber/Gherkin, Productboard, or Aha!
Exposure to AI-assisted prototyping tools such as Gemini, Claude, or Figma Make


Educational Qualification

Bachelor's degree in Business, Information Systems, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.

Ideal Candidate

The ideal candidate is a hands-on Business Analyst who can bridge the gap between business requirements and technical delivery, with practical experience in data modelling, prototyping, testing, and release coordination.
