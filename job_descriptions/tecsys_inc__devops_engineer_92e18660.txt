Description

At Tecsys, the software we deliver helps hospitals, pharmacies and distributors get what they need, when they need it. Every role here contributes to that outcome.

Why this role matters

As a DevOps Engineer on the TecsysIQ Data & AI team, you will build and run the infrastructure, deployment and release automation behind our data platform on AWS, Databricks and Kubernetes.

What You'll Be Part Of

Tecsys is trusted by essential healthcare and distribution organizations to support resilient, efficient and secure supply chains. We are a global provider of AI-powered cloud software, listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange (TSX: TCS).

Three values guide how we work. We earn trust every day by putting the customer first and acting with integrity. We support each other by recognizing individual strengths and working as a team. And we explore boldly by staying curious and adapting to succeed.

You'll join a culture built on curiosity, continuous learning and people who push the limits of what's possible.

What You'll Do


Provision and maintain AWS and Databricks infrastructure across regions and environments with Terraform, keeping changes reviewable and free of drift
Build and maintain the Helm charts and Kubernetes Jobs that onboard, upgrade and offboard customer environments safely and repeatably
Design and improve CI/CD pipelines and own release management and versioning across interdependent repositories
Apply security and compliance best practices (secrets management, least privilege, vulnerability remediation, SOC 2 and HIPAA controls)
Implement logging, monitoring, alerting and backups, troubleshoot production issues and optimize cloud cost
Collaborate with developers, Product Owners and cloud operations in an Agile environment, and maintain clear runbooks and documentation



Requirements

What you bring


Proven production experience with Terraform on AWS, including remote state, modules and multi-environment setups
Proven production experience with Kubernetes, Helm and Docker
Proven experience building CI/CD pipelines and Git-based workflows across multiple repositories, including release management
Proficiency in Python, Bash, Java or a similar language
Working knowledge of secrets management and cloud security fundamentals
Strong troubleshooting, communication and documentation skills, and experience in Agile teams


Nice to have


Databricks administration, GitLab CI/CD or Maven
Experience in regulated environments (SOC 2, HIPAA)
AWS, Kubernetes or Terraform certifications


Fluency in written and spoken English is essential for communicating effectively with clients, suppliers, business partners, and colleagues outside of Quebec.

Benefits

Why You'll Love It Here


Remote, flexibility and the trust to manage your own time
Benefits and vacation from day one
Pension plan with competitive employer contribution
Employee events and community initiatives
Room to learn, stretch and take on new challenges
Collaboration with global teams


We foster an inclusive workplace where everyone feels valued, respected and equipped to thrive.
