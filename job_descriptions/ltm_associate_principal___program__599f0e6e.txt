Job Description







Role Release Manager Global IBP




Experience 12 years of experience




Location Hyderabad




Job Description




We are looking for an experienced o9 IBP Release Manager to lead endtoend release planning governance and execution across global and regional IBP implementations The role will coordinate Product Engineering Data Platform Testing Business and o9 teams to ensure releases are delivered in a controlled predictable and businessready manner




The successful candidate will own the release lifecycle from scope and dependency alignment through SITUATregression testing change governance production deployment and hypercare while proactively managing risks conflicts and stakeholder communication Role Highlights




Develop and manage the global release strategy roadmap release calendar and deployment plans for o9IBP solutions across multiple markets and regions




Own endtoend release governance including release scope milestones readiness criteria dependencies approvals




Coordinate with global and regional Product Owners o9 teams Engineering Data Platform Testing and Business teams to drive successful releases




Manage release activities across development SIT UATregression and Production environments ensuring environment and tenant readiness




Drive release readiness assessments covering codeconfiguration readiness testing status data dependencies defects business validation and deployment prerequisites




Drive the IBP Code Merge Process to maintain the unified code base across international region




Coordinate SIT UAT endtoend and regression testing ensuring critical issues are tracked and resolved before Production deployment




Govern Change Requests CRs and deployment activities ensuring required approvals and changerelease management controls are completed




Identify and proactively manage release risks crossproduct dependencies environment conflicts deployment constraints and schedule impacts




Facilitate GoNoGo discussions and provide clear release status risks decisions and escalations to programme and senior leadership




Manage Production deployment coordination rollbackcontingency planning postdeployment validation and hypercare




Drive continuous improvement of release management processes through automation standardisation release metrics lessons learned and retrospectives




Maintain comprehensive release documentation including release plans dependency trackers deployment plans risk logs readiness dashboards and release communications




Required Skills




11 years of overall experience with substantial experience in Release Management Product Management Programme Delivery or largescale technology implementations




Strong experience managing enterprise Supply Chain Planning Integrated Business Planning IBP releases




Handson functional or delivery exposure to o9 Solutions experience with SAP IBP andor JDA is highly desirable




Strong understanding of release lifecycle management including SIT UAT regression testing Production deployment and hypercare




Experience managing complex implementations involving multiple applications markets regions and interdependent teams




Good understanding of change management and release governance including change approvals deployment controls risk assessment and rollback planning




Strong working knowledge of AgileSAFe delivery practices and tools such as JIRA Confluence and Azure DevOps




Ability to manage multiple concurrent releases competing priorities dependencies and critical deployment timelines




Excellent stakeholder management communication negotiation and escalationmanagement skills




Ability to translate technical release information into concise business and leadershiplevel status reporting




Strong analytical organisational and problemsolving skills with a proactive approach to risk management




Familiarity with controlled release processes such as CABchange approval deployment freeze windows release readiness reviews and postproduction validation
