My Client is one of the world’s leading quantitative trading firms to hire a Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) / Platform Engineer in Singapore.




The role

You’ll own the platforms behind mission-critical trading systems that run at global scale, around the clock. You’ll have real technical ownership and work alongside exceptional engineers.




Responsibilities

Design, build and run scalable, resilient platform infrastructure
Own and optimize CI/CD pipelines to speed up software delivery
Strengthen observability: monitoring, logging, tracing and alerting
Build automation and self-service tooling that engineers genuinely rely on
Support containerized workloads and orchestration at large scale
Drive reliability, performance and scalability across distributed systems and middleware




Requirements

Background as a Platform Engineer, SRE or DevOps Engineer
Strong Linux and automation skills
Proven track record building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines
Hands-on Kubernetes and Docker
Solid grasp of monitoring, logging and observability platforms
Infrastructure as Code experience
Scripting or programming in Python, Go, Bash or similar
Real passion for reliable, highly available platform

Missing one or two of these? Apply anyway.




Why this role

Real ownership of large-scale, mission-critical infrastructure
Work with exceptional engineers in a high-performance environment
Complex engineering challenges using modern DevOps and SRE practices
Base salary up to SGD 250K, plus bonus and comprehensive benefits
