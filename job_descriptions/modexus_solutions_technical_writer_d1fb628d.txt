Position Description

An excellent opportunity has arisen for a Technical Writer to join the Product Development Department and take ownership of the creation, release and maintenance of technical literature across the Airedale by Modine product portfolio. Utilising Adobe Creative Suite, particularly InDesign, alongside other industry-standard tools, the role will ensure all customer-facing technical documentation is accurate, compliant, professionally presented and aligned with product, legislative and business requirements. Working closely with Engineering, Manufacturing, Service and Marketing teams, the Technical Writer will drive documentation standards and continuous improvement, delivering clear and effective information to support product selection, installation, commissioning, operation and maintenance.

Key Responsibilities


Produce all new technical literature in electronic format for all standard products in agreed language sets and required format for distribution and printing for customers, sales, and all technical staff as required.
Aid the generation of assembly and maintenance drawings in appropriate design package for integration into literature.
Maintain all existing technical literature keeping it up to date with design and legislation changes.
Work closely with the Marketing team to support the addition and maintenance of technical information on the Airedale by Modine website.
Manage translation of technical literature packages, liaising with external agency for translation, proofing and distributing to customer where required
Maintain a library of past technical literature for reference on an on-going basis and maintain a change record system.
Ensure the latest standards and legislature are accurately reflected and fully integrated into literature, across the full range of technical documentation (technical manuals, controls manuals, installation and maintenance documents, commissioning sheets, declarations of conformity, loose parts instructions, test sheets, etc).
Engage with the engineering and manufacturing teams throughout the literature development process to ensure the most useful, accurate and relevant information is presented.
Identify and implement initiatives to improve the technical literature library and processes, to streamline the technical literature creation and maintenance processes and the costs associated with its production.
Maintain the records and registers for the technical literature library.
Manage the document obsolescence process, including the retention and preservation of previous revisions and documentation associated with obsolete products.
Any other roles required by this position.
