Senior Salesforce Administrator (Solutions Engineer)




Up to £62,000 | Hybrid, Edinburgh (min. 2 days/week on-site) |




Our client, a major UK housebuilder with an ambitious technology transformation underway, is growing its Salesforce team and this is a brand-new role at the heart of it.




This isn't a "keep the lights on" admin job. You'll take full day-to-day ownership of Salesforce configuration and administration across Sales, Service and Field Service Cloud, working hand-in-hand with the Solutions Architect to turn approved designs into well-built, properly tested platform changes. You'll also take on line management of a small team of Salesforce administrators acting as their escalation point, quality gatekeeper and mentor.




If you're a senior Salesforce Administrator who's ready to step up, own the platform, and start building a team around you, this is that next move.




What you'll be doing




Own day-to-day Salesforce administration, configuration and maintenance across Sales, Service and Field Service cloudsBuild and maintain complex automation using Flow Builder, validation rules and other declarative tools; review Apex and Lightning Component changes where required
Manage and mentor a small team of Salesforce administrators/support analysts overseeing workload, quality of output and professional development
Triage and resolve complex user support escalations across Salesforce and integrated systems
Partner with the Solutions Architect to translate architecture and solution designs into configured, tested platform changes
Manage user access, the security model, profiles, permission sets and data integrity
Deploy platform changes through the development pipeline in line with change control protocols
Maintain up-to-date system documentation, configuration records and standard operating procedures
Support release management activities and assess the impact of new Salesforce releases on existing configuration
Engage with external partners and vendors on delivery standards and timelines
Identify opportunities for process improvement, automation and technical debt reduction




What you'll bring

Salesforce Certified Administrator (essential); Advanced Administrator or Platform App Builder certification desirable
Substantial, senior-level experience as a Salesforce Administrator comfortable owning the platform day-to-day rather than working under close direction
Strong hands-on configuration skills: Flow Builder, validation rules, page layouts, reports & dashboards, security model
Experience managing or mentoring more junior team members, formally or informally
Working knowledge of Apex and Lightning Components sufficient to review and QA changes hands-on development is not a requirement of this role
Familiarity with deployment tools such as Copado or Gearset
Comfortable working within Agile ways of working and structured change control processes
Excellent stakeholder engagement and communication skills across technical and non-technical audiences
Experience working with Salesforce managed packages is a plus




Join a Salesforce function investing seriously in modern tooling and process




Interested in finding out more? Get in touch to hear the full details.
