Job Description

As a Product Specialist, you will be part of a dynamic engineering team based in Bengaluru /Pune, supporting the development and maintenance of industrial automation configuration and selection tools. Your role will involve collaborating with Product Management, Engineering, Commercial Engineering, UX, Development, and QA teams to analyse product requirements, define business rules, validate tool functionality, and improve product accuracy and user experience. You will report to the Team Lead and work in a hybrid environment, following the 12:00 PM to 9:00 PM shift from Bengaluru/Pune, India.

Your Responsibilities


Provide product and technical expertise to support development and maintenance of industrial automation configuration and selection tools.
Collaborate with Product Management, Engineering, Commercial Engineering, UX, Development, and QA teams to define, document, and validate product requirements.
Analyse product, system, and business rules to ensure accurate functional and technical specifications.
Support discovery, requirement-gathering, UAT, and validation activities to improve tool accuracy, usability, and customer experience.
Maintain product data, configuration rules, and supporting documentation for implementation by development teams.
Identify gaps, inconsistencies, and improvement opportunities that impact tool functionality and user experience.
Monitor market and competitor tools to provide practical insights and recommendations to product stakeholders.


The Essentials - You Will Have


4–6 years of experience in pre-sales, post-sales engineering, technical support, product specialist, or product configuration roles within industrial automation.
Experience supporting product selection tools, configuration platforms, product data, or software-enabled engineering applications.
Ability to analyse product requirements, business rules, and system logic to prepare clear functional specifications, user stories, and validation scenarios.
Hands-on exposure to Rockwell Automation products and tools such as Integrated Architecture Builder, Motion Analyzer, Studio 5000, or Logix Designer is preferred.
Experience working with Agile/Scrum teams and tools such as Jira for backlog updates, issue tracking, testing support, and sprint execution.
Strong collaboration and communication skills to work effectively with Product Management, Engineering, Development, QA, Technical Support, and business stakeholders.
Ability to identify gaps, support process improvements, and contribute practical recommendations to improve tool accuracy, usability, and operational efficiency.


The Preferred - You Might Also Have


Bachelor's degree in electrical, Electronics, Instrumentation, Mechanical, Manufacturing, Computer Engineering, or a related engineering discipline.
Academic or practical exposure to industrial automation, control systems, software applications, and engineering workflows.
Knowledge of software development concepts, system design, product configuration, and selection tools is preferred.


Ability to apply engineering and software knowledge to support product accuracy, tool usability, and business process improvements.
