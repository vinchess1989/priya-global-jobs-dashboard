About The Company

EROAD is a global fleet management technology company delivering telematics, compliance and safety solutions through an integrated hardware and software platform, and it's growing its Devices Team to keep pace with demand for its connected hardware and services. We're hiring an Intermediate Test Automation QA Engineer to help us get there.

What You'll Do

You'll own quality across EROAD's connected devices, from physical hardware to the cloud services behind them.


Test physical devices such as telematics units, cameras and adapters alongside their firmware and hardware integrations
Build and maintain automated tests spanning component, integration, contract, performance and end-to-end coverage
Write supporting automation scripts to extend and strengthen test tooling
Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines so quality gates run with every build
Work hands-on in Linux environments to test and troubleshoot connected devices
Validate end-to-end workflows across hardware, mobile apps and cloud-integrated services
Investigate and diagnose defects across the hardware-software stack
Collaborate within a cross-functional agile team, reporting to the Chapter Lead


What You Need

We're after someone with hands-on depth across both embedded testing and modern automation practice.


Proven test automation experience in Java, with Kotlin experience a strong complement or substitute where framework depth is clear
Solid background in embedded systems, connected devices and hardware-software integration testing
Strong understanding of serial communication protocols such as UART, SPI, I2C, RS-232, RS-485, USB CDC and CAN
Comfortable working in Linux/Ubuntu environments for device testing and troubleshooting
Experience building BDD-based test automation with Cucumber, plus JUnit and TestNG
Practical knowledge of CI/CD tooling such as Jenkins and GitHub Actions
Skilled in Python and Bash scripting for test automation and tooling Working understanding of Azure and/or AWS for cloud-integrated device solutions
Exposure to AI-assisted development or testing tools to speed up automation and defect investigation
Able to read hardware schematics or familiar with observability tools like Datadog or Grafana is a bonus


WHY JOIN

This is a chance to own quality for hardware that runs at serious scale, across a genuinely global platform


Work on connected devices used by fleets across New Zealand, Australia and North America
Be part of a high-growth, publicly listed technology company making a real, tangible impact
Join a talented, collaborative and multicultural team
Access continuous learning, EAP support and AI tooling to help you grow
Competitive salary and benefits package
