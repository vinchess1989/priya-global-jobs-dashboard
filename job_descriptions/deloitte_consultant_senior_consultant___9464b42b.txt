About The Business

Cyber & Strategic Risk

We Help Clients Address Various Aspects Of Cyber And Other Strategic Risks To Their Organizations To Inform Risk-based Strategic Choices, Prepare To Respond To Disruption, Assess And Manage Full-lifecycle Enterprise Risks, As Well As Strategize And Respond To Risks Associated With The Reliability And Protection Of Data, Associated Processes And Technology. We Provide Advisory And Managed Services To Help Senior Executives Spot, Assess, Manage, And Respond To Risks And/or Catastrophic Unforeseen Incidents That Could Undermine Their Competitive Position Or Jeopardize Their Critical Assets, Reputation And/or Financial Standing. Offerings Include


Cyber;
Crisis & Resilience;
Strategy, Brand and Reputation;
Climate and Sustainability;
Extended Enterprise


Our objective is to help clients develop and implement strategies for IT risk management and aim to help clients find the appropriate balance between risk management and cost containment. Our integrated solutions covering: Cyber Strategy, Cyber Security, Cyber Vigilance, Cyber Resilience.

Work you'll do


Build and maintain streaming and batch log ingestion pipelines from network appliances, cloud platforms and servers (e.g. Juniper, Fortigate, Tencent Cloud, Alibaba Cloud, GCP) into Google SecOps — parsing, normalisation and data-quality validation.
Operate and upgrade the ingestion and cloud estate across zones — Kafka / ZooKeeper / Logstash, GKE, Cloud Composer, SecOps integrations: alerting, health checks, maintenance windows, version upgrades and runbooks.
Onboard new log sources end to end: requirements, collection design, implementation, data-quality validation and documentation.
Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code (Terraform, Azure DevOps, ArgoCD GitOps); automate recurring operational tasks.
Own change request management: documentation, approvals, execution and closure evidence, in line with enterprise ITSM processes.
Develop and tune detection content — MITRE ATT&CK-aligned rules and SOAR playbooks — and deliver security data reporting: propose architecture and solutions, review with the Technical Delivery Lead, deliver outputs for security monitoring and executive review.
Take L2 shift for platform incidents: triage, troubleshoot and resolve incidents and service requests to closure.
Write clean, tested, version-controlled code and documentation.


During your tenure with us, you will demonstrate and develop your leadership and professional capabilities in the following areas: Inspiring, Creating purpose, Driving agility, Building diverse capability, Influencing, Collaborating, Delivering value, Building the business, Analytical acumen, Effective communication, Engagement management/delivery excellence, Managing change, Managing quality & risk, Sales excellence, Strategic thinking and problem solving, and Tech savviness.

We are looking for someone with


Bachelor degree holder in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related disciplines
Linux operations and troubleshooting; shell or Python automation.
Hands-on streaming / log ingestion: Kafka, ZooKeeper, Logstash (or production-scale equivalents).
Kubernetes on a major cloud (GKE or equivalent); GitOps tooling such as ArgoCD.
CI/CD and IaC: Terraform and a major pipeline platform (Azure DevOps or equivalent).
A major cloud platform, GCP preferred: orchestration (Airflow / Cloud Composer), Kubernetes, networking, IAM, monitoring.
Production platform operations: monitoring and alerting, L2 incident support, change management discipline.
Fluent English; client-facing requirements discussion, design proposal and end-to-end delivery.
Google SecOps (Chronicle) — highly preferred: log ingestion, YARA-L rules, SOAR playbooks.
GKE-related certifications (CKA / CKAD / Professional Cloud DevOps).
Multi-cloud log collector experience (Tencent Cloud, Alibaba Cloud).
SecOps use-case development; MITRE ATT&CK TTP mapping.
Security audit / regulated environment experience.
