What is the role?

The Senior Supplier Quality Engineer is responsible for providing technical and quality engineering activities of external suppliers, ensuring that suppliers are capable of consistently delivering products, materials, and services that meet Aerogen’s requirements. The role will ensure that all Suppliers to Aerogen are providing the highest quality products and services with appropriate quality monitoring processes and inspections established for continued development and effective relationships. This role will be located in our Dangan office in Galway, or in our Shannon facility.




What are the key responsibilities?

• Appropriately train, develop and mentor resources within Supplier Quality Team to ensure objectives are communicated and delivered within agreed requirements.

• Build Strong relationships with suppliers focusing on strong collaboration, communications, and monitoring/planning activities whilst establishing agreements to appropriately address regulatory and quality obligations and responsibilities.

• Lead supplier approval and qualification activities for assigned suppliers, overseeing the Supplier Quality Assurance process to ensure compliance with Aerogen’s supplier approval requirements.

• Lead and ensure timely and effective closure of Supplier Quality System activities, including Audit Actions, SCARs, SCNs, PANs and associated corrective and preventive actions.

• Lead and manage supplier validation activities, including IQ, OQ and PQ, within key Aerogen projects such as design improvements, supplier transfers, cost improvement initiatives and dual sourcing, ensuring compliance with defined quality and validation requirements.

• Lead the implementation and continuous improvement of Supplier Quality processes, including the deployment of new electronic systems and digital solutions.

• Collaborate with cross functional internal teams to establish inspection plans, frequencies, and test methods for incoming material/parts.

• Partner with suppliers to monitor key process outputs and performance metrics, using data analysis to identify trends, mitigate risks, and implement sustainable process improvements.

• Champion continuous process improvement and, in conjunction with other functions, analyze feedback and identify and implement appropriate problem solving and statistical techniques to improve quality of manufactured product and overall effectiveness and efficiency of processes.

• Overall responsibility for GMP standards and compliance within Aerogen’s Project Teams and Supplier Engagement.




What education and experience are required?

• At least 8 years’ equivalent experience in Engineering, Process Development or Manufacturing Environment.

• Minimum of a primary degree in Engineering or related technical field.




What key skills will make you great at the role?

• Ability to work on own initiative.

• Strong team member with the ability to identify and drive implementation of innovative quality improvements (Six Sigma Green / Black Belt Certification an advantage).

• Ability to work within project teams, consistently meeting deadlines and ensuring that all actions are addressed appropriately.

• Demonstrate and actively promote high levels of professional quality engineering.

• Good communication skills. Must have the ability to communicate effectively at all levels of the company and with suppliers.

• Demonstrated knowledge and experience of leading and executing process validations.

• Demonstrated knowledge and experience of leading and executing measurement system analysis, including Gauge R&R.

• Working knowledge of thermoplastics Injection Moulding and Silicone Moulding.

• Proven experience in managing critical suppliers and 3PLs, driving resolution of quality issues and delivering supplier-related project activities.

• Good administrative/organizational ability with high level of attention to detail.

• Ability to liaise with suppliers, customers and regulatory contacts in an informed professional manner.

• Thorough understanding of ISO 13485 and FDA QSR standards.
