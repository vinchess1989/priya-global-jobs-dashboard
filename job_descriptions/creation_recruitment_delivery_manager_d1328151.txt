Morwenna Buck
Senior Consultant - Product & Data

Mobile App Product Delivery Manager

Permanent

Salary: £70k - £80k

Location: Edinburgh – hybrid (highly flexible)




An opportunity to manage the delivery of a significant programme of releases for an established global mobile app with 3 million users, as the product evolves and expands across international markets.




Working across Product, Engineering, Design and external development partners, you'll turn the product roadmap into structured delivery plans and ensure new features and releases are delivered successfully.




The role




Translate the product roadmap into clear delivery plans, milestones and release schedules across multiple markets.
Coordinate Engineering, Product, UX/UI, QA and external development partners to maintain delivery momentum.
Manage dependencies, risks and priorities, ensuring teams have the clarity needed to deliver against agreed timelines.
Oversee mobile app releases, coordinating testing, approvals, launches and post-launch support.
Improve delivery processes and support the transition from external development partners to an in-house delivery team.




Skills, Knowledge & Experience




Experience delivering B2B2C or B2C mobile apps or digital products
Proven experience managing major mobile app launches, relaunches and releases, ideally across multiple international markets.
Strong understanding of iOS and Android release processes, including App Store and Google Play.
Experience coordinating cross-functional teams, external development partners, QA and UAT.
Strong stakeholder management skills, including working with commercial partners and managing complex delivery dependencies.




Experience with Shape Up methodology would be an advantage.
