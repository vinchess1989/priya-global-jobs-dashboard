ABOUT THE POSITION

About the Role:

The role translates agreed product priorities into an integrated delivery plan, with clear milestones, dependencies, resource requirements and release commitments.

It manages execution from discovery and planning through development, integration, testing, market readiness, launch and post-launch stabilisation.

Working within the scaled agile framework (SAFe), you will perform all aspects of the Release Train Engineer role. This includes being accountable for, and facilitating the execution of, ART processes

Manage impediments, escalating, when necessary, to manage risk, to help ensure value delivery and help drive relentless improvement within your Agile Release Train and across your teams.

Contagious passion for, and commitment to, driving mobile money growth and innovation.
Excellent problem analysis and resolution skills to support work in reactive and proactive environments.
Strong understanding of local and global payment capabilities and technologies

Relevant certified Scrum Master qualification, for example, SAFe Scrum Master or equivalent

3 - 5 years' experience as a Delivery Manager and / or RTE

Agile Experience which includes international suppliers and / or markets - minimum 5 years
