About Us

We are a global mining software business bringing together the expertise of Micromine, Motion Metrics and Fast2Mine.

Our solutions help mining companies explore, design, plan and operate more effectively through industry-leading software, AI, machine vision and automation technologies. From geology and mine planning to fleet performance and operational optimisation, we help customers improve productivity, efficiency and sustainability across the mining value chain.

Backed by Weir's global reach and resources, we combine deep mining expertise with a strong innovation roadmap, delivering trusted technology solutions used by mining operations around the world.

The Opportunity

This role is part of Micromine, one of the specialist businesses within Weir Software Solutions. You'll transform complex technical concepts into clear, engaging documentation that supports customers throughout their software journey. Working alongside product and development teams, you'll influence how users experience our products while helping drive documentation excellence across a global software portfolio.

The Impact You'll Have

Develop and maintain release notes, installation guides, configuration manuals, online help systems and user documentation
Collaborate with product managers, developers, testers and support teams to understand product updates and documentation requirements
Translate technical concepts into clear, accessible content for a range of audiences
Review and update existing documentation to ensure accuracy, consistency and usability
Contribute to documentation standards, processes and best practices
Support the administration of documentation and localisation tools

What You Need To Succeed

Proven experience as a Technical Writer, ideally within software, technology or mining technology environments
Exceptional written and verbal communication skills
Ability to understand complex technical concepts and communicate them clearly to end users
Experience using technical authoring and content management tools, with MadCap Flare highly regarded
Experience working closely with software development teams and understanding the software development lifecycle
A degree in Computer Science, Geology, Engineering or a related discipline will be highly regarded

What You Can Expect From Us

Hybrid working model
Competitive remuneration
Career development within a growing global organisation
14 weeks paid parental leave (primary and secondary carers)
Health and wellbeing support, including EAP and gym membership
Opportunity to work with innovative technologies that support the global mining industry
Exposure to international projects and stakeholders
