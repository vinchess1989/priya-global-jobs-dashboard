Quality Engineer

Location: Hyderabad, India




Job Responsibilities:

Develop product quality control standards and inspection procedures.
Monitor production process quality and conduct data analysis (e.g., SPC, CPK).
Handle customer complaints and quality abnormalities, and drive corrective actions.
Maintain the ISO quality management system and organize internal audits.
Train employees on quality awareness and promote quality improvement projects.
Assist the company in completing product quality certifications and ensure a smooth production launch.




Job Requirements:

Major in Quality Management, Mechanical Engineering, Electronic Engineering or related fields is preferred.
1–3 years of experience in quality management, with familiarity with quality standards in the home appliance industry.
Proficiency in using quality tools, such as Minitab and QC 7 tools.
Familiarity with internal auditor requirements or ISO9001 is preferred.
Rigorous, detail-oriented and principled, with good communication and coordination skills.
Familiarity with the ISO13485 certification system.
