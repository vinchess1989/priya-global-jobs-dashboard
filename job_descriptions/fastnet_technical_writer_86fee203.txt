Summary:

We are currently recruiting for a Technical Writer to join a leading pharmaceutical manufacturing organisation in Cork on an 18 month contract. This is an on-site role.




Key Responsibilities:

Prepare, review and update GMP documentation, including SOPs, work instructions and manufacturing records.
Support the documentation of deviations, manufacturing events and CAPA activities.
Review Batch Production Records (BPRs), ensuring accuracy, completeness and compliance with GMP standards.
Collaborate with manufacturing, engineering and quality teams to maintain accurate and compliant documentation.




Key Requirements:

1–3 years' experience in a GMP-regulated environment, ideally within pharmaceutical manufacturing, quality administration or document control.
Good understanding of GMP, Good Documentation Practice (GDP) and controlled documentation processes.
Excellent attention to detail, organisational and communication skills, with the ability to work predominantly onsite in Cork.
