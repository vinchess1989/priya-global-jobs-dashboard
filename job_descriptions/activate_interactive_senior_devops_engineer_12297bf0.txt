Activate Interactive Pte Ltd (“Activate”) is a leading technology consultancy headquartered in Singapore with a presence in Malaysia and Indonesia. Our clients are empowered with quality, cost-effective, and impactful end-to-end application development, like mobile and web applications, and cloud technology that remove technology roadblocks and increase their business efficiency.

We believe in positively impacting the lives of people around us and the environment we live in through the use of technology. Hence, we are committed to providing a conducive environment for all employees to realise their full potential, who in turn have the opportunity to continuously drive innovation.

We are searching for our next team members to join our growing team.

If you love the idea of being part of a growing company with exciting prospects in mobile and web technologies that create positive impact on people’s lives, then we would love to hear from you.

What will you do?


Develop automation and processes to enable application teams to deploy, manage, scale and monitor their applications on the case management application platform (built on OutSystems)
Take ownership of platform-level solutions, and coordinate with the infrastructure team on hosting, network and infrastructure issues
Manage platform configuration, including user and role management, access control, integration endpoints (e.g. APIs, SSO) and platform settings
Own the CI/CD pipelines and release management process for applications hosted on the case management platform, including deployment plans, versioning and rollback, and approve production release readiness
Implement platform and application observability (metrics, logs, traces) with defined SLIs/SLOs, dashboards and alert tuning, and report availability and performance against service levels
Deploy and manage platform and application monitoring for performance, availability, errors and usage, and identify bottlenecks to maximise service availability
Conduct performance and load testing, capacity planning and performance tuning with application teams to keep the case management platform responsive under enterprise workloads
Lead system troubleshooting and problem solving across platform and application domains, run incident and problem management including root cause analysis and post-incident reviews, and lead on-call escalations for customer facing issues
Support business continuity at the platform level, including application backup, restore and disaster recovery testing, in coordination with the infrastructure team
Track and report OutSystems licence utilisation (e.g. application objects, users, environments) to support central governance and license cost optimisation
Implement and maintain platform and application security controls, such as secure configuration, secrets management and access policies
Perform threat and risk assessments at the platform and application level, and apply secure configuration profiles
Employ best practices when implementing security controls within the platform and applications
Implement security practices that comply with standards (e.g. IM8) to protect the organisation’s data and applications
Maintain security and compliance evidence for ICT&SS / IM8 and the System Security Plan (SSP), such as configuration baselines, access reviews, scan results and change records
Extend CI/CD pipelines and environment controls to promote agentic AI components (e.g. agent configurations, prompts, guardrail settings) consistently with the same approvals, scanning and rollback as application releases
Provide technical guidance and peer review to other DevSecOps engineers and relevant application teams.


What are we looking for?


Degree or Diploma in Computer Science, Computer or Electronics Engineering, Information Technology or related disciplines.
At least 4 years of experience relevant to DevSecOps engineering, preferably at the application platform level
Experience operating the OutSystems platform (O11 or OutSystems Developer Cloud), including LifeTime deployment, environment management and platform monitoring
Passion for automation, standardisation and best practices
Excellent understanding of Software Development Life Cycle, Test Driven Development, Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery
Experience supporting high availability, high performance and high security applications in cloud and hybrid environments
Demonstrable skills in scripting languages such as Bash, PowerShell or Python
Experience with version control systems such as Git
Experience with cloud platforms such as GCC (e.g. AWS, Azure, Google Cloud)
Ability to troubleshoot complex issues ranging from platform configuration to application stack traces
Experience with application performance monitoring and logging tools (e.g. OutSystems monitoring, Elastic, Grafana, CloudWatch)
Knowledge of application security testing tools (e.g. HP Fortify, SonarQube, Sonatype Nexus IQ) and secure coding practices
Knowledge of platform-level backup, restore and disaster recovery
Knowledge of container technologies such as Docker, Kubernetes
Experience with DevOps toolset like GitLab, JIRA, BitBucket, Confluence
Working knowledge of Government ICT&SS management policies (IM8) and SSP requirements, and has applied them to security controls, monitoring and release processes
Experience with observability practices (SLIs/SLOs, distributed tracing, alert design, e.g. OpenTelemetry) and performance testing tools (e.g. JMeter, k6)
Comfortable with Agile methodologies and working closely with product development teams
Strong on collaboration and communication including documentation
OutSystems certifications
Experience with security assessments pertaining to government projects
Worked for an organisation which succeeded DevSecOps transformation
Basic understanding of network infrastructure and Linux, to work effectively with the infrastructure team.
