Company Description IRCLASS Systems and Solutions Pvt. Ltd., now operating as ISSPL LIMITED, is a growing organization that shares updates, news, and official announcements through its dedicated LinkedIn page. The company is focused on delivering systems and solutions with an emphasis on quality, reliability, and compliance. ISSPL LIMITED offers opportunities for professionals to work on impactful projects in a structured and process-driven environment. Team members are encouraged to contribute to continuous improvement and help strengthen the company’s reputation in its industry.
Role Description The Senior Quality Engineer role is a full-time, on-site position based in Patna. This role involves leading and executing quality engineering activities across projects, including developing and implementing quality plans, standards, and procedures. The Senior Quality Engineer will monitor product and process performance, conduct inspections and audits, analyze test data, and drive corrective and preventive actions to resolve quality issues. The role also includes collaborating with cross-functional teams, mentoring junior quality staff, and preparing reports and documentation to support compliance and continuous improvement initiatives. The Senior Quality Engineer is expected to ensure that quality objectives are met consistently and that best practices are embedded throughout the organization.
Qualifications


Candidates should possess strong skills in Quality Engineering and Quality Management, with experience designing and maintaining robust quality frameworks.
Candidates should possess skills in Quality Assurance and Quality Control, including developing test plans, conducting inspections, and verifying compliance with standards.
Candidates should possess expertise in Product Quality, focusing on defect prevention, root cause analysis, and continuous improvement of product performance.
Candidates should possess the ability to use data-driven methods and quality tools (e.g., SPC, Pareto analysis, cause-and-effect diagrams) to identify trends and improve processes.
Relevant qualifications such as a bachelor’s degree in engineering or a related field, and experience in a senior or lead quality role.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with the ability to work collaboratively with technical and non-technical stakeholders.
Experience with industry standards and regulatory requirements relevant to the company’s domain; certifications in quality (e.g., Six Sigma, ISO-related) are an advantage.
