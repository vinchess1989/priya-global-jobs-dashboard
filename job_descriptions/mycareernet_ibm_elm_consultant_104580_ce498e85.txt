Key Skills: DOORS, RTC, Contract Lifecycle Management, Integration

Roles and Responsibilities:

Configure and administer IBM ELM capabilities supporting requirements, contracts, workflows, and engineering lifecycle processes.
Manage IBM DOORS repositories, requirement structures, traceability relationships, attributes, views, and access controls.
Support Contract Lifecycle Management workflows, approvals, baselines, change control, and audit requirements.
Administer Rational Team Concert projects, work items, permissions, release structures, and collaboration processes.
Analyze process issues, coordinate configuration changes, and support users through incident resolution, documentation, and training.

Skills Required:

5 - 7 years of relevant experience in IBM ELM consulting, requirements management, or application lifecycle management.
Hands-on experience with IBM DOORS in requirements management and engineering lifecycle environments.
Practical experience configuring or administering Contract Lifecycle Management processes and workflows.
Experience with IBM Rational Team Concert for work management, collaboration, and project administration.
Understanding of enterprise requirements traceability, lifecycle governance, configuration management, and controlled change processes.

Good to Have:

Experience integrating IBM ELM components with enterprise engineering, quality, or business systems.

Education: Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related discipline.
