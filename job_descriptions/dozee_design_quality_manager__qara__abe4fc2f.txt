Overview

Dozee Health AI is a pioneer in Contactless Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM), proven to drive transformation at scale. Headquartered in Bengaluru, India, Dozee has emerged as a leader in this category. We are seeking talented individuals to help us in this very exciting journey. Candidates will be working with top healthcare providers in the country and leverage RPM's innovation to tackle healthcare's biggest challenges - from resource constraints to quality outcomes, to new care models.

Role Overview

As our Design Quality Manager, you will be part of Dozee’s QARA – Quality Assurance & Regulatory Affairs team, leading Design Controls, Product Risk Management and Design Transfer activities in partnership with Product, Engineering and Manufacturing, and building and leading our Design Quality team. It is a high-ownership role for someone who brings structure, quality and pace to everything they deliver, and who wants to help improve patient care through connected health.


Champion design quality for Dozee’s software and hardware medical devices across the product lifecycle, from user needs through design transfer and design changes.
Lead design control and product risk management activities with Product, Engineering, Data Science and Manufacturing, in line with US FDA requirements (21 CFR 820 / ISO 13485) and applicable international standards.
Build and develop the Design Quality team, embedded with the Bengaluru design team.
Partner with Regulatory on the regulatory impact of design changes and on submission readiness.


Responsibilities


Drive the redesign of Dozee’s design control and software lifecycle processes, and oversee their consistent use in line with 21 CFR 820 / ISO 13485, IEC 62304 and ISO 14971.
Lead design quality activities across the product lifecycle, including design inputs, outputs, reviews, verification, validation, and design changes.
Drive Design History File (DHF) development, maintenance, and remediation, ensuring documentation supports FDA submissions, including 510(k).
Facilitate product risk management with the cross-functional team so risks are identified, assessed, controlled, and monitored throughout the product lifecycle including input from complaints and post-market data..
Partner with Engineering, Data Science and Product teams on software lifecycle quality, cybersecurity (FD&C Act Section 524B)and usability (IEC 62366)..
Oversee design transfer to contract manufacturers, ensuring product specifications, processes, and quality requirements are effectively transferred and verified.
Lead design quality reviews, audits, CAPAs, and remediation initiatives, ensuring timely resolution of quality gaps.
Collaborate with cross-functional stakeholders to build a strong design quality culture and ensure regulatory readiness.


Requirements


8-15 years of experience in Design Quality, Quality Assurance, or Medical Device Quality, preferably with at least 7 years of US medical device product experience.
Strong experience with FDA design control requirements and medical device quality systems (21 CFR 820 / ISO 13485)..
Hands-on experience with IEC 62304 software lifecycle and ISO 14971 risk management.
Experience leading design control remediation or building and maintaining Design History Files (DHF) supporting 510(k) submissions.
Experience managing design transfer activities with contract manufacturers.
Exposure to cybersecurity design requirements for connected medical devices, including FDA requirements under FD&C Act Section 524B.
Proven track building, leading, and developing a design quality team.
Familiarity with the software as a medical device (SaMD) or AI/algorithm-based devices is a plus.


Skills That Set You Up To Win


Strong understanding of US FDA regulations, medical device quality systems, and design assurance requirements.
Ability to translate regulatory requirements into practical design and engineering processes.
Strong risk management, problem-solving, and quality remediation capabilities.
Ability to influence and collaborate with senior leadership, Product, Engineering, Regulatory, and Manufacturing teams.
Strong documentation and audit readiness skills, including supporting FDA inspections and notified body audits..
High ownership, attention to detail, and ability to drive quality standards in a fast-paced product development environment.
