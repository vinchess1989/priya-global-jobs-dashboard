This position is listed on behalf of a partner company, which manages all applications and next steps. Our partner is looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer based in Ireland.

As the first dedicated DevOps Engineer within the organization, you will take ownership of the infrastructure powering an innovative, stablecoin-based payments platform. You will play a central role in designing, securing, scaling, and maintaining cloud infrastructure while working closely with software engineers and a broader infrastructure team. Your main focus will be strengthening platform reliability, automation, security, and observability across distributed systems. You will also lead key infrastructure initiatives related to PCI DSS certification, regulatory compliance, and the consolidation of cloud environments. This is an opportunity to shape infrastructure strategy, establish engineering best practices, and make critical technical decisions in a fast-evolving fintech environment. You will have significant autonomy and the opportunity to build scalable solutions with a strong impact on the platform's security and performance.

Accountabilities


Design, build, and maintain distributed cloud infrastructure and platform services in close collaboration with software engineering teams.
Improve the scalability, automation, reliability, performance, and observability of production services.
Manage and optimize Kubernetes-based infrastructure, ensuring secure deployments and efficient resource management.
Troubleshoot complex production issues across services and multiple layers of the technology stack.
Proactively identify security vulnerabilities and implement preventive controls, including network segmentation, egress restrictions, least-privilege access, secrets and key management, and audit logging.
Lead infrastructure-related activities for PCI DSS certification, addressing audit findings, maintaining compliance evidence, and embedding security controls into daily operations.
Drive the consolidation and migration of infrastructure toward Google Cloud, supporting the gradual retirement of legacy AWS environments.
Improve monitoring, alerting, on-call procedures, and incident response processes, ensuring clear service ownership and effective escalation paths.
Establish and enforce change-management processes for production environments and infrastructure code.
Review and strengthen secrets management, encryption keys, and production access policies.
Collaborate with security, infrastructure, and software engineering stakeholders to define technical priorities and contribute to roadmap planning.
Take ownership of infrastructure initiatives, identify improvement opportunities, and promote engineering best practices across the organization.


Requirements


Proven experience designing, building, and operating large-scale, cloud-based distributed systems.
Strong hands-on experience managing Kubernetes in production environments, including network policies, access controls, and deployment strategies.
Experience working in regulated or audited environments, with exposure to PCI DSS, SOC 2, ISO 27001, or similar compliance frameworks.
Practical experience preparing audit documentation, gathering compliance evidence, and implementing security controls.
Strong background in system administration and Infrastructure as Code (IaC), particularly Terraform, within business-critical production environments.
Demonstrated ability to identify infrastructure shortcomings, optimize performance, and make data-driven cost and architecture decisions.
Experience managing critical production incidents, troubleshooting complex technical issues, and improving incident management practices.
Ability to manage multiple projects and priorities simultaneously while maintaining high engineering standards.
Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a collaborative mindset and the ability to work effectively across technical and non-technical teams.
Strong ownership mentality and ability to navigate ambiguity, establish clear responsibilities, and drive initiatives forward independently.
A proactive approach to enabling developer self-service and improving operational efficiency.
Preferred qualifications: Experience in payments, fintech, or financial technology environments; familiarity with key management solutions such as HSM or KMS and secrets management; experience with GitOps tools such as Argo CD; software engineering experience; strong Linux system administration skills; and knowledge of platform hardening, malware detection, and endpoint management through configuration as code.
Ability to communicate technical concepts clearly to different audiences, including leadership, developers, and external stakeholders.
Commitment to engineering excellence, mentoring colleagues, and maintaining high standards for production systems.


Technical Environment


Cloud platforms: Google Cloud (GKE Autopilot, Cloud SQL) and AWS (RDS, ECS), with a strategic transition toward Google Cloud.
Container orchestration: Kubernetes.
CI/CD and delivery: GitHub Actions and Argo CD.
Observability: Grafana, Loki, Mimir, Tempo, and AWS CloudWatch.
Infrastructure as Code: Terraform.
Database: PostgreSQL.


Benefits


Fully remote working environment with a long-standing remote-first culture.
Flexible working hours, supporting a healthy work-life balance.
Personal development budget to support professional learning and career growth.
Conference allowances to encourage participation in industry events and knowledge sharing.
Access to modern hardware, equipment, and tools to support effective remote work.
Flat organizational structure with minimal hierarchy and direct access to decision-makers.
Significant autonomy and ownership, with the opportunity to shape infrastructure strategy and technical direction.
Opportunities for professional growth within a rapidly expanding fintech and blockchain ecosystem.
Collaborative and inclusive international team environment built around a shared mission.
Equal opportunity workplace that values diversity, different perspectives, and inclusive collaboration.
