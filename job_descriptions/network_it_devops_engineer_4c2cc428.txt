DevOps Engineer | Azure DevOps | Kubernetes | CI/CD | Cloud | Your Next Career Move Starts Here

Are you a skilled DevOps Engineer ready for your next career move?
I specialize in connecting talented DevOps Engineers with exciting opportunities across software development houses, consultancies, fintechs, start-ups, and large corporates.
I work with organisations that value automation, cloud technologies, modern software delivery practices, and strong technical talent, both within South Africa and internationally.
Whether your expertise lies in DevOps, infrastructure automation, CI/CD pipelines, cloud platforms, containerization, or platform engineering, I'd be interested in connecting with you to explore opportunities aligned with your career goals.
Duties & Responsibilities
Requirements:
- Relevant IT / Computer Science / Engineering qualification and experience
- Strong DevOps Engineering experience
- Experience implementing and maintaining CI/CD pipelines
- Experience with Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar DevOps tools
- Experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform, ARM Templates, or CloudFormation
- Strong experience with Microsoft Azure and/or AWS
- Experience with Docker and containerization technologies
- Experience with Kubernetes and container orchestration platforms
- Experience with Linux administration and scripting
- Experience with Git and version control systems
- Experience monitoring, troubleshooting, and optimizing production environments
- Experience with automation using PowerShell, Bash, Python, or similar scripting languages
- Experience with cloud networking and security principles
- Experience working within Agile / Scrum environments
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
- Effective communication and stakeholder engagement skills
- Self-motivated with excellent self-management capabilities
- Commitment to continuous improvement and lifelong learning

Advantageous:
- Azure Certifications or AWS Certifications
- Experience with Site Reliability Engineering (SRE) practices
- Experience with Prometheus, Grafana, ELK, or similar monitoring tools
- Experience with Serverless technologies
- Experience with FinOps and cloud cost optimization
- Experience with Kubernetes administration certifications
- Experience supporting high-availability enterprise environments
If you're ready for your next DevOps Engineering opportunity, I'd love to hear from you.

Send me your CV now to tmashiane@networkrecruitment.co.za

If you have not had any response in two weeks, please consider the vacancy application unsuccessful. Your profile will be kept on our database for any other suitable roles / positions.
