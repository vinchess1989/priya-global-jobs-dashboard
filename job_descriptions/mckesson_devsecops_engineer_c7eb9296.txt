About The Role

McKesson is seeking a DevSecOps Engineer to help modernize and scale enterprise digital commerce platforms through automation, cloud engineering, and platform reliability practices. This role partners with architects, product teams, developers, QA, cloud operations, and security teams to build secure, resilient, and highly automated software delivery platforms. You will drive CI/CD excellence, cloud-native architecture, infrastructure automation, and operational reliability while enabling faster, safer releases across commerce applications, APIs, and microservices.

What You'll Do


Design, build, and optimize CI/CD pipelines using GitHub Actions, or similar platforms.
Automate build, test, security validation, deployment, release, and rollback processes.
Implement Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform, Bicep, ARM Templates, or CloudFormation.
Develop and maintain cloud-native solutions across Azure and AWS environments.
Deploy and manage containerized workloads using Docker and Kubernetes.
Integrate DevSecOps controls including SAST, DAST, Software Composition Analysis (SCA), container security, and secrets scanning.
Support and improve reliability, observability, monitoring, logging, and alerting across digital commerce platforms.
Partner with product, architecture, QA, security, and operations teams to improve engineering efficiency, release quality, and platform stability.



Basic Requirements


4+ years of experience in DevOps, DevSecOps, Platform Engineering, Cloud Engineering, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), or related software engineering roles.
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Technology, or equivalent experience.
Experience building and supporting CI/CD pipelines using Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar tools.
Hands-on experience with Azure.
Experience implementing Infrastructure as Code using Terraform, Bicep, ARM, or CloudFormation.
Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, containers, and cloud-native application deployment.
Experience supporting APIs, microservices, production environments, and automated release processes.
Experience using source control systems, branching strategies, and software delivery lifecycle practices.



Preferred Skills / Experience


Custom digital commerce platforms.
Experience implementing enterprise DevSecOps controls and secure software delivery frameworks.
Knowledge of cloud-native architecture and platform engineering best practices.
Experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Splunk, Dynatrace, AppDynamics, Azure Monitor, or CloudWatch.
Experience with release orchestration across distributed systems and microservices.
Experience supporting audit, compliance, risk, and enterprise security requirements.
Experience leading technical initiatives, mentoring engineers, and driving engineering standards.
Familiarity with AI-assisted engineering and automation tools.



Travel / Work Arrangement / Physical Requirements


Ability to work remotely or in a hybrid environment based on business needs.
Occasional travel may be required for team meetings, planning sessions, or business initiatives.
Ability to perform standard computer-based work activities for extended periods
