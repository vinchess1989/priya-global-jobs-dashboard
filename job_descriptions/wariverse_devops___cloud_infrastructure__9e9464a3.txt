About The Role

We run several codebases in production across different environments, and this role owns deployment, monitoring, and reliability across all of them, not a single app. This is a high-stakes, high-autonomy role for someone who thinks in failure modes before they happen.

What You'll Need


3-4+ years of DevOps/infrastructure experience with production web applications
Personal infrastructure projects, a homelab, or open-source contributions are welcomed as proof of skill, since job titles alone don't tell us enough
Strong grasp of CI/CD pipelines, queue workers, and zero-downtime deploys
Experience operating more than one relational database engine in production
Experience with cloud/PaaS hosting for modern backend stacks, and webhook/queue reliability, including idempotency, retries, and dead-letter handling


Nice to Have


Python, Go, or Rust for infrastructure tooling and scripting
Observability tooling experience for a team without a dedicated SRE function
Experience hardening webhook endpoints against replay/signature attacks


Who We're Looking For

You fix things before anyone notices they're broken.

You'd rather automate your own job out of boredom than repeat a manual step twice.

You're comfortable being the only infrastructure person in the building, and you build just enough process, never more than that.
