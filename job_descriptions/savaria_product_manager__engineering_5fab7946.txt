BETTER MOBILITY FOR LIFE

Savaria is a global leader in accessibility and patient care products, designing and building wheelchair lifts, stairlifts, elevators for home and commercial applications, medical beds, therapeutic surfaces, ceiling track lifts and patient slings. Savaria employs 2,500 valued associates globally and has 11+ manufacturing facilities to reach markets in North America, Europe, Australia, and Asia.

Position Summary

As a Product Manager at Savaria, you will play a key role in ensuring product quality, managing lifecycle improvements, and driving collaboration between R&D, production, sales, customer service, and technical support. You will be the go-to expert for a designated set of products and act as a liaison across internal teams to champion continuous improvement, manage custom orders, and support product launches across Savaria’s suite of accessibility and mobility solutions.

Responsibilities


Serve as the primary point of contact for product-related quality issues; identify trends and coordinate root cause analysis and corrective actions
Track, log, and manage product improvements and field issues using Smartsheet or other internal tools
Collaborate with R&D to support and oversee product launches from concept to market
Liaise with various operations across all Savaria-owned facilities to align on quality control and inventory optimization
Recommend design and process enhancements based on internal performance data and customer feedback
Manage and review custom order requests to ensure feasibility and compliance with product standards
Work closely with Sales, Engineering, Technical Support, Production, and Customer Service to ensure consistent product performance and satisfaction
Maintain up-to-date knowledge of product specifications, improvements, and field applications
Contribute to the Engineering Change Order (ECO) process when applicable
Work directly with shopfloor staff to identify root causes of production issues and implement corrective actions; provide on-the-job training as needed
Support design reviews and product development using CAD tools such as SolidWorks and AutoCAD
Create, validate, and maintain Bills of Materials (BOMs) to ensure product accuracy and alignment with production capabilities, particularly in terms of proper allocation and consumption


Mindset, Skills & Education


Energetic, eager, tenacious!
Post-secondary education in Engineering, Product Management, or related technical discipline
Passionate about product quality and customer experience
Strong interpersonal and cross-functional communication skills
Experience in product lifecycle management, preferably in manufacturing or accessibility products
Proactive problem-solver with a continuous improvement mindset
Proficient in tools such as Smartsheet, Excel, and ERP systems (SAP, D365, etc.)
Hands-on experience with CAD tools, including SolidWorks and AutoCAD
3+ years of experience in product management, engineering support, or quality assurance


This is an excellent opportunity for an ambitious, career-oriented individual desirous of being part of a large, evolving, and successful global organization with the ability to impact future success. This role is a chance to shape your career and the future of Savaria! Join us!
