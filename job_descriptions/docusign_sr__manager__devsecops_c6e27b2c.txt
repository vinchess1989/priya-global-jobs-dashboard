Company Overview

Docusign brings agreements to life. Over 1.5 million customers and more than a billion people in over 180 countries use Docusign solutions to accelerate the process of doing business and simplify people’s lives. With intelligent agreement management, Docusign unleashes business-critical data that is trapped inside of documents. Until now, these were disconnected from business systems of record, costing businesses time, money, and opportunity. Using Docusign’s Intelligent Agreement Management platform, companies can create, commit, and manage agreements with solutions created by the #1 company in e-signature and contract lifecycle management (CLM).

What you'll do

Docusign is seeking a transformative, hands-on leader to redefine our DevSecOps landscape. As a Sr. Manager of DevSecOps, you will be the driving force behind modernizing our infrastructure, release philosophy, and CI/CD ecosystem. This is a high-visibility role for a thought leader who challenges the status quo and builds world-class engineering engines from the ground up. You will act as the vital bridge between development, security, and release management, ensuring our pipelines are both lightning-fast and inherently secure.

This position is a people manager role reporting to the VP of Strategy, Release & Operations.

Responsibility


Architect the Future: Move the organization beyond basic automation into the realm of self-healing infrastructure, internal developer platforms (IDP), and advanced GitOps workflows
Apply a data-driven approach to manage complex release cycles and infrastructure projects, ensuring precision in execution
Act as a subject matter expert on industry trends (e.g., AI-driven operations, FinOps, OpenTelemetry) and advocate for their adoption across the engineering org
Identify architectural bottlenecks and implement solutions proactively (You are expected to audit the current state and propose a 90-day transformation plan within your first month)
Mentor the India-based team to shift from a "support" mindset to an "engineering-first" mindset, fostering high ownership and innovation


Job Designation

Hybrid: Employee divides their time between in-office and remote work. Access to an office location is required. (Frequency: Minimum 2 days per week; may vary by team but will be weekly in-office expectation)

Positions at Docusign are assigned a job designation of either In Office, Hybrid or Remote and are specific to the role/job. Preferred job designations are not guaranteed when changing positions within Docusign. Docusign reserves the right to change a position's job designation depending on business needs and as permitted by local law.

What you bring

Basic


15+ years of experience leading large-scale migrations (e.g., Monolith to Microservices, on-prem to cloud, or manual release & deployment operations to continuous integration & continuous development (CI/CD) focusing on progressive rollouts)
Proven ability to manage multiple high-priority workstreams under an executive office while maintaining crystal-clear documentation and roadmaps
The gravitas to present to executive leadership and persuade cross-functional teams to adopt new DevSecOps and Security standards
Expertise in Infrastructure as Code (IaC) (Terraform, CloudFormation) with integrated security guardrails
Deep experience managing cloud/hybrid environments, including identity, networking, and secure service configurations
Hands-on experience with Secrets Management (Vault) and Observability (logging, monitoring, and incident response integration)


Preferred


Proven track record of migrating legacy pipelines to a modern DevSecOps model
Strong experience with Salesforce architecture, including Scratch Orgs, sandbox strategies, and CI/CD-driven releases
Deep expertise in GitHub Enterprise and GitHub Actions, architecting reusable workflows for enterprise-grade SaaS
Hands-on experience with CodeQL, secret scanning, dependency reviews, and Docker/Kubernetes hardening
