Introduction

The DevOps / Platform Engineer (Keycloak Specialist) is responsible for the deployment, automation, scalability, and operational stability of enterprise Keycloak platforms. The role focuses on managing highly available Keycloak environments, implementing infrastructure automation, GitOps-driven configuration management, database optimisation, supporting cluster operations, and ensuring secure, resilient identity services.

Working closely with Solution Architects, Security Teams, Developers, and Infrastructure Engineers, the successful candidate will drive platform reliability, lifecycle management, configuration automation, performance optimisation, and zero-downtime deployment strategies across cloud and on-premises environments. The role is accountable for ensuring the operational health of Keycloak platforms, including distributed caching performance, database efficiency, platform observability, and infrastructure scalability.

Duties & Responsibilities
Deploy, configure, and maintain Keycloak platforms in Kubernetes and OpenShift environments.
Manage Keycloak clustering, high availability, and platform performance.
Manage Keycloak deployment lifecycles using the official Kubernetes Keycloak Operator and custom Custom Resource Definitions (CRDs).
Implement and support Keycloak Operator-based deployments and lifecycle management.
Implement GitOps workflows (e.g., ArgoCD / Flux) using the Terraform Keycloak Provider or Keycloak Config CLI to automate realm, client, and role deployments without manual console intervention.
Configure and optimise Infinispan caching and PostgreSQL database clustering.
Tune JDBC connection pooling (Agroal/HikariCP), optimise read/write transaction limits, and manage Keycloak schema migration during minor and major platform upgrades.
Develop Infrastructure as Code (IaC) solutions using Terraform, including the Terraform Keycloak Provider.
Automate platform provisioning, configuration, monitoring, and operational processes.
Plan and execute zero-downtime upgrades, migrations, platform maintenance activities, and infrastructure lifecycle management.
Implement end-to-end observability, alerting, and health probes using Prometheus metrics (/metrics), Grafana dashboards, and synthetic transaction monitoring for authentication and authorisation endpoints.
Troubleshoot platform, infrastructure, database, caching, and authentication-related issues.
Collaborate with development, security, architecture, and infrastructure teams to deliver reliable IAM solutions.
Produce and maintain operational documentation, standards, runbooks, configuration baselines, and deployment procedures.
Desired Experience & Qualification
Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field.
Minimum of 5 years' experience in DevOps, Platform Engineering, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), or Infrastructure Engineering roles.
Proven experience deploying and managing Keycloak in enterprise environments.
Strong operational experience with the modern Quarkus-based Keycloak runtime (Keycloak 17+ / 2x+), including container build-time versus runtime optimisation strategies.
Strong experience with Kubernetes and/or OpenShift platforms.
Hands-on experience with the Keycloak Operator, Custom Resource Definitions (CRDs), and Keycloak lifecycle management.
Experience configuring and managing clustered Keycloak deployments.
Strong knowledge of Infinispan caching, distributed session management, and clustering technologies, including:
JGroups discovery protocols (DNS_PING, KUBE_PING).
Distributed cache replication tuning.
Split-brain prevention and recovery strategies.
Network partition handling.
Cross-node session consistency.
Experience with PostgreSQL administration, replication, database clustering, performance tuning, and schema lifecycle management.
Proficiency with Terraform and Infrastructure as Code (IaC) practices, including the Terraform Keycloak Provider.
Experience implementing GitOps operating models using ArgoCD, Flux, or similar GitOps platforms.
Experience implementing CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation.
Strong understanding of high availability, disaster recovery, backup, recovery, and business continuity strategies.
Experience planning and executing zero-downtime upgrades, migrations, and production deployments.
Familiarity with containerisation technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
Knowledge of authentication and federation technologies including:
OAuth 2.0
OpenID Connect (OIDC)
SAML
Strong experience with observability and monitoring platforms, including:
Prometheus
Grafana
OpenSearch / Elasticsearch
OpenTelemetry
Jaeger
Centralised logging and alerting platforms
Strong troubleshooting, analytical, and problem-solving skills.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management abilities.
Experience within Telecommunications, Financial Services, Consulting, or large-scale enterprise environments will be advantageous.
