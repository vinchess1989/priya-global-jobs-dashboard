Role - DevOps Engineer

Location: India - Noida

Type: Full-time

Department: R&D

About NeoXam

NeoXam is a leading B2B fintech software vendor serving buy-side investment managers and asset servicers globally. Our platform covers data management, reconciliation, fund accounting, reporting, and portfolio management. As we scale, we are investing in the reliability, automation, and security of the infrastructure and delivery pipelines that power our platform.

The Role

We are looking for a DevOps Engineer to own the design, automation, and reliability of our cloud infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines.

The problem space is clear: engineering teams need fast, safe, and repeatable ways to build, test, and ship software across multiple products and client environments. This is a hands-on individual contributor role with end-to-end ownership, from infrastructure design to production operations. As the initiative matures, you will have the opportunity to shape standards adopted across engineering.

What You Will Work On


Design, build, and maintain CI/CD pipelines that support multiple engineering teams and products
Provision and manage cloud infrastructure as code (Terraform, CloudFormation, or equivalent) across environments
Deploy, operate, and scale containerized workloads using Docker and Kubernetes
Build monitoring, logging, and alerting systems that give engineering and support teams real visibility into production health
Automate configuration management, release processes, and routine operational tasks to reduce manual toil
Define and enforce security, access control, and secrets management practices across the infrastructure (DevSecOps)
Continuously improve system reliability, deployment speed, and cost efficiency based on real usage and incidents


What We Are Looking For


3-7 years of DevOps, SRE, or infrastructure engineering experience
Must have hands-on experience with at least one major cloud platform (AWS, Azure, or GCP)
Strong scripting ability in Python, Bash, or Go for automation
Hands-on experience with containerization (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes)
Experience with Infrastructure as Code tools, Terraform preferred
Solid understanding of CI/CD tools and practices (Jenkins, GitLab CI, GitHub Actions, or equivalent)
Experience with configuration management tools (Ansible, Chef, or Puppet)
Working knowledge of monitoring and logging tools (Prometheus, Grafana, ELK Stack, Datadog, or equivalent)
Solid Linux system administration and networking fundamentals (DNS, load balancing, VPC,firewalls)
Comfortable working end-to-end autonomously, from design to production operations


Who You Are


A builder, energized by improving systems that other teams depend on
Pragmatic, you pick the right tool, not the flashiest one
Autonomous, you drive toward outcomes without waiting to be told what to do next
Detail-oriented, you take reliability and security seriously


Why Join


Ownership of infrastructure and delivery pipelines that power a global fintech platform
Direct visibility with engineering leadership on architecture decisions
Path to growing scope and influence as the initiative matures
