Job Description

About the role

As an IS Service Configuration & Change Lead, you’ll play a critical role in ensuring Northumbrian Water Group’s service and configuration management processes are effective, trusted and continuously improving. You’ll take ownership of the organisation’s Configuration Management Database (CMDB), leading the governance, quality and development of service configuration practices while overseeing Change Management and CAB activities. Through your work, teams across the business will have greater visibility of service relationships, dependencies and operational impacts, enabling informed decision-making, reduced risk and more reliable service delivery.

Working closely with service owners, product teams and business stakeholders, you’ll establish robust standards, controls and assurance processes across service configuration and change activities. You’ll drive improvements in data quality, reporting, automation and governance, helping to create a mature and sustainable service management capability that supports both colleagues and customers.

This is an opportunity to shape how services are understood and managed across the organisation, ensuring accurate service information is available when it matters most and supporting safe, well-controlled change across a complex technology landscape.

About You

You’ll bring significant experience in Configuration Management and CMDB governance, with a strong track record of improving data quality, ownership, controls and configuration relationships. You’ll also have experience leading Change Management activities and CAB governance, confidently assessing risk, challenging constructively and supporting the delivery of safe, controlled change.

You’ll have a strong understanding of service management principles and how business services, technical services, applications, configuration items and dependencies work together to support effective service delivery. Alongside your knowledge of ITIL-aligned practices, you’ll be comfortable using service management platforms and reporting tools to provide meaningful insight and assurance.

We’re looking for someone who combines analytical thinking with excellent stakeholder management skills. You’ll be confident working with both technical and non-technical colleagues, influencing decisions, driving improvements and promoting the value of accurate service information. Most importantly, you’ll have a continuous improvement mindset, a focus on operational excellence and a passion for helping teams deliver reliable services through effective governance, collaboration and control. Relevant professional qualifications in IT Service Management, Configuration Management or Change Management would be advantageous.

Please note this role will require a DBS check prior to employment.
