We are looking for a Senior DevOps Engineer for our client.




Work Location: Changi, Singapore




Responsibilities:

Lead DevSecOps work, run platform and operational reviews, and coach engineers
Administer Kubernetes platforms, including workloads, ingress, namespaces, RBAC, scaling, networking, storage, upgrades and recovery
Handle Linux operations, shell scripting, patching and performance troubleshooting
Provide production support covering monitoring, incident response, root cause analysis, capacity planning, backup and recovery
Debug and troubleshoot across applications, Linux, Kubernetes, containers, networks and storage
Design reusable GitLab CI/CD pipelines for VM and Kubernetes deployments using Helm, Terraform and GitOps
Set up observability with Prometheus, Grafana, Loki or OpenTelemetry
Apply security controls such as secrets management, IAM, mTLS and vulnerability scanning, and support DR testing




Requirements:

7+ years in DevOps, DevSecOps, platform engineering or production operations
Proven experience leading DevSecOps work and coaching engineers
Strong hands on experience in Kubernetes administration and Linux operations
Strong production support, debugging and troubleshooting skills
Experience with GitLab CI/CD or similar tools such as Jenkins, ArgoCD or FluxCD
Hands on experience with Helm, Terraform, GitOps and observability tools
Good knowledge of supply chain security, secrets management, IAM and mTLS
Experience with high availability, safety critical or mission critical platforms, and Agile or SAFe
