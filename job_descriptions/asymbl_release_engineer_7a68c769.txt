Release Engineer




Asymbl is The Workforce Orchestration Company™. We help staffing and recruiting firms bring people, AI, and software together as one hybrid workforce. We create the software, we put the AI to work, and we have the consulting expertise to orchestrate it all.




We believe the future of work isn't human or digital. It's human and digital, orchestrated as one.




Position Overview




You will own how Asymbl ships software: planning, coordinating, and running releases of Asymbl Intelligence, the platform behind our products, so that customers get new features quickly and reliably. Our customers run hybrid teams of people and digital workers on our products every day, so every release has to be predictable, well communicated, and safe to roll back.




You will work closely with our engineering, QA, infrastructure, and product teams in Jaipur, and with US-based product, support, and customer-facing teams. You'll work with Git, CI/CD pipelines, Jira, our cloud environments on Google Cloud Platform and AWS, and Salesforce release tooling. Success will be measured by releases shipped on schedule, fewer failed deployments and rollbacks, and stakeholders who always know what is shipping and when.




Responsibilities:




You will be responsible for:




Release Planning and Coordination

Owning the release calendar and planning releases with product, engineering, and QA
Running release readiness and go/no-go reviews, including scope, testing sign-off, and risks
Coordinating dependencies across teams, environments, and customer commitments




Release Execution

Managing deployments through development, QA, staging, and production environments
Owning branching, versioning, and merge strategy with the engineering leads
Running production releases and hotfixes, including rollback plans and post-release checks
Working with the Infrastructure Engineer to improve CI/CD pipelines and automate release steps




Quality and Risk

Making sure every release meets quality gates for testing, security checks, and approvals
Tracking release issues and leading post-release reviews to prevent repeats
Maintaining release documentation, runbooks, and an audit trail of what shipped




Communication

Writing release notes for internal teams and customers
Keeping leadership, support, and customer-facing teams informed of schedules, changes, and incidents
Reporting on release metrics such as frequency, lead time, and change failure rate




Qualifications:

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field
5 to 8 years in software delivery, with 3+ years as a Release Manager or in a similar release or build role
Hands-on experience with Git branching strategies and versioning
Experience with CI/CD tools such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, or Cloud Build
Working knowledge of cloud environments on GCP or AWS and containerized deployments
Experience with Agile delivery and tools such as Jira and Confluence
Strong organization and attention to detail, with the ability to manage several releases at once
Clear written and verbal communication with technical and non-technical stakeholders
Comfort working with US-based teams across time zones




Nice to have:

Salesforce release management experience (change sets, SFDX, Copado, Gearset, or managed packages)
Experience releasing AI or data-intensive products
Experience with feature flags and progressive rollouts
Scripting skills in Bash or Python to automate release tasks
ITIL or Scrum certifications
