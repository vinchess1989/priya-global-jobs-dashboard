The Enterprise IT Services Project Team supports Aptiv’s business through the delivery of IT projects across all business functions.

As a Senior IT Project Manager, you will manage projects to ensure delivery within the defined scope, on time, within budget, and to the customer’s satisfaction. You will be responsible for all phases of the Solution Development Life Cycle (SDLC), namely planning, requirements definition, design, development, testing, deployment, and transition to post-implementation support.

You will be the central point of contact for all project stakeholders, including internal customers, business relationship managers, architects, suppliers, operations, and infrastructure teams.

You will report directly to either an Aptiv Programme Manager within Enterprise IT Services or a Portfolio Manager.

What Your Role Will Look Like


Lead end-to-end delivery of infrastructure and application technology projects.
Plan, coordinate, and oversee project teams, timelines, and budgets.
Drive project execution while ensuring quality, cost, and schedule objectives are met.
Partner with IT and business stakeholders to define delivery approaches and minimize risks.
Manage project risks, issues, and dependencies, ensuring timely resolution.
Communicate project status and key decisions to stakeholders and leadership.
Ensure compliance with Aptiv PMO governance, release management, and change control processes.
Serve as the primary liaison for business partners, vendors, and technical teams throughout project delivery.


What You’ll Have


Experience delivering similar infrastructure and/or application projects.
Experience with Microsoft tools and licensing.
A graduate or postgraduate qualification in a STEAM subject.
A PMP, PRINCE2, APM, SAFe, or similar project management or software delivery qualification.
10+ years of relevant IT project management experience across the full project lifecycle.
Experience across the full Software Development Life Cycle and in large-scale systems implementation.
Experience delivering IT projects in Sales, Engineering, Manufacturing, Purchasing, or Supply Chain within the automotive, industrial, or electronics sectors is desirable.
Experience delivering any of the following capabilities is desirable: Business Intelligence, Data Warehousing, Data Governance, Teradata, Informatica, SAP or other ERP systems, CLM, Salesforce, or MAP.


What You’ll Bring


Be Strategic: Demonstrate strategic thinking and use forward-looking insights to break down barriers.
Be a Leader: Provide visionary leadership and understand the downstream impact of your decisions.
Be Curious: Explore new ideas and challenge the status quo.
Be Accountable: Hold yourself and the team accountable for critical deadlines and commitments.
Be Influential: Communicate a compelling and inspiring vision or purpose for the project to motivate and engage the team. Articulate and present effectively to all levels of management.
Be Proactive: Anticipate future consequences and trends, develop appropriate strategies, and mitigate risks to cost, scope, schedule, and customer satisfaction.
Be Results-Driven: Be dedicated to meeting the expectations and requirements of internal and external customers.
Be Adaptable: Respond effectively to change and work well without having the complete picture.
Be Organised: Know how to organise people and activities into an efficient workflow.
Be a Problem Solver: Solve difficult problems using rigorous logic and effective methods.
Be Transparent: Provide direct, complete, and actionable positive and corrective feedback, and conduct business openly and transparently.
