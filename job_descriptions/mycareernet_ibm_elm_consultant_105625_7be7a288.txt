Company:IT Services Organization

Key Skills: IBM Engineering Lifecycle Management (ELM), IBM DOORS Next, Requirements Management, Configuration Management, Workflow Configuration, Traceability, Lifecycle Governance, Java, SQL

Roles & Responsibilities:

Configure and administer IBM ELM environments in line with project lifecycle and governance requirements.
Design requirements, review, approval, traceability, and change workflows for engineering teams.
Support integrations and data flows across lifecycle management applications and enterprise tools.
Analyze platform issues, coordinate resolution, and maintain the reliability of ELM implementations.
Prepare solution documentation, configuration standards, user guidance, and knowledge-transfer materials.

Skills Required:

5-7 years of relevant experience with IBM ELM or enterprise lifecycle management implementations.
Hands-on experience with IBM Engineering Lifecycle Management (IBM ELM).
Strong understanding of enterprise requirements and engineering lifecycle processes.
Experience configuring workflows, roles, permissions, projects, and traceability structures.
Ability to support integrations, data migration, validation, and platform testing activities.

Good to Have:

Experience with IBM Engineering DOORS Next, engineering workflow management, requirements management, and configuration management.
Working knowledge of Java and SQL.

Education:

B.Tech or B.E. degree.
