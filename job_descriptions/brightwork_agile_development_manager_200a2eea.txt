Agile Development Manager

Galway, Ireland (Hybrid) or Remote within Ireland




About BrightWork 

At BrightWork, our mission is simple but critical: Project Management Success for Successful Business Outcomes. 




How do we do this? 

We help customers standardise project management practices, align project teams, improve visibility across portfolios, and give leaders the control and insight they need to make informed decisions – all within the security and familiarity of Microsoft 365.




We are also proud to be a Worker-Owned Cooperative, where our Co-Owners have an equal stake in the success and future direction of the company.




The Opportunity

BrightWork is looking for an experienced Agile Development Manager to work closely with our Head of Product Development as we scale our team. 

This is a leadership role with responsibility for people, product, and process outcomes. 

You will report directly to our Head of Product Development and lead part of our product delivery team, ensuring product development makes a clear, measurable contribution to our sustainable growth.




What You'll Do 

Agile Leadership and Scrum Facilitation

• Facilitate Scrum ceremonies including Sprint Planning, Daily Scrum, Sprint Reviews, Retrospectives, and Refinements.

• Work closely with Product Management through the Product Owner function.

• Monitor team health, velocity, and delivery predictability.

• Promote continuous improvement across development teams.




Team Leadership and Mentoring

• Act as a mentor and day-to-day leader for development team members.

• Support professional growth and development of engineers, QA specialists, and other product development staff.

• Coach teams in Agile and Scrum practices.

• Develop future technical and delivery leaders within the organisation.




Delivery Management

• Define, document, and improve software development processes.

• Implement best practices in planning, estimation, testing, and release management.

• Identify risks, dependencies, and schedule impacts.

• Provide visibility into delivery status and release outcomes.

• Coordinate regular product releases across multiple teams, tracking progress against goals and milestones.




Support to Product Development Leadership

• Take ownership of delegated operational and delivery management responsibilities.

• Prepare status updates, delivery reports, and planning information.

• Contribute to departmental planning and process initiatives.

• Facilitate organisational scaling and continuous improvement efforts.

• Coordinate R&D activity tracking and reporting.




What We're Looking For 

You may be a great fit if you have: 

• 5+ years’ experience in Agile software development environments.

• 3+ years’ experience as a Scrum Master, Agile Coach, Delivery Lead, Project Manager, or similar role.

• Strong facilitation, communication, and management skills.

• Experience coordinating cross-functional software development teams.

• Experience with Agile planning and delivery tools such as Azure DevOps, Jira, or equivalent.

• Strong organisational and problem-solving capabilities.

• Proven ability to mentor and coach technical teams.




Bonus Points For:

• Experience working in a software product company.

• Experience with release management and multi-team coordination.

• Scrum Master certification (CSM, PSM, or equivalent).

• Experience with product development metrics and reporting.

• Understanding of software engineering practices, quality assurance, and modern development lifecycles.




What Success Looks Like 

In your first year, success will look like: 

• An aligned Agile team contributing to our multi-year growth plan. 

• Ownership of Scrum processes and their implementation. 

• Ongoing contributions to a continuous improvement cycle.

• Strong working relationship with the product management team.

• Confidence giving constructive feedback as a mentor and coach.

• Measurable improvements in delivery predictability and release quality (for example, velocity and cycle-time trends).




Why Join BrightWork? 

• Shape the next stage of growth for our product development team.

• Contribute to how we develop software as we scale. 

• Have a voice that shapes decisions.

• Work with our close-knit team as we adapt and grow in the age of AI. 

• Work on products that our customers use to manage their business-critical projects. 

• Join a cooperative where your contribution directly shapes our shared success. 




Ready to Apply? 

If you're an experienced Agile Development Manager who enjoys software delivery, managing teams, improving processes, and hitting targets. 

Apply through LinkedIn – we'd love to hear from you.
