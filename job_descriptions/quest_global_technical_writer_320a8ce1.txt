Job Requirements

At Quest Global, it’s not just what we do but how and why we do it that makes us different. With over 25 years as an engineering services provider, we believe in the power of doing things differently to make the impossible possible. Our people are driven by the desire to make the world a better place—to make a positive difference that contributes to a brighter future. We bring together technologies and industries, alongside the contributions of diverse individuals who are empowered by an intentional workplace culture, to solve problems better and faster.

Key Responsibilities


Create product manuals/ instruction manuals/ How- To guides and other supporting documents to communicate technical information.
Gather requirements, analyse user needs, and translate them into effective documentation solutions.
Evaluate current content and develop innovative methods for improvement
Proofread and reauthor functional specifications and other engineering-authored technical documents to simplify overall functionality and product intricacies.
Present complex information to the target audience in a controlled language such as Simplified Technical English (STE) and Plain English.
Serve the role of an editor / proofreader by participating in peer reviews.
Establish and enforce quality benchmarks and review processes.
Contribute to the development and continuous improvement of technical communications business processes, competencies, tools, standards, and best practices (style guides, templates etc.)
Develop, maintain, and improve personal professional competencies, specialized knowledge, and skills in technical communications and relevant technical domains.
Lead initiatives to continuously improve documentation maturity.
Drive adoption best practices in modular, reusable and scalable documentation.


We are known for our extraordinary people who make the impossible possible every day. Questians are driven by hunger, humility, and aspiration. We believe that our company culture is the key to our ability to make a true difference in every industry we reach. Our teams regularly invest time and dedicated effort into internal culture work, ensuring that all voices are heard.

We wholeheartedly believe in the diversity of thought that comes with fostering a culture rooted in respect, where everyone belongs, is valued, and feels inspired to share their ideas. We know embracing our unique differences makes us better, and that solving the worlds hardest engineering problems requires diverse ideas, perspectives, and backgrounds. We shine the brightest when we tap into the many dimensions that thrive across over 21,000 difference-makers in our workplace.

Work Experience

Key Skill Sets:


Manufacturing / Engineering Product documentation experience
Excellent grammar and punctuation skills with attention to detail
Key interpersonal skills including a self-starter and ability to work independently with geographical distributed teams & SME’s
Tools such as MS Word, Adobe FrameMaker, RoboHelp, Advanced user of Content Management Systems (Windchill), XML authoring (Arbortext Editor) or equivalent
Experience creating multi-form at deliverables (Videos, infographics, guided flows
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills


Minimum Qualification

B.E. or B. Tech (Mechanical/ Automobile) / Masters in English or Equivalent Degree

5 to 8 years experience in Technical Writing.
