Requisition ID: 279332

Join a purpose driven winning team, committed to results, in an inclusive and high-performing culture.

Scotiabank continues to be an industry leader for Payments technology and innovation. We have a history of safely leveraging new tools to enable new experiences for our customers, focusing on ensuring we protect their interests and goals. The result of this leads to modernization programs which aim to offer enriched information and transparencies to support the rapidly evolving payments community.

The Role

We are looking for an enthusiastic individual who understands the end-to-end lifecycle of international payments and can collaborate effectively with banks, fintech partners, and internal technology teams also to innovate and collaborate on the value-driven goals in delivering customer value. You will engage in a fast-paced and challenging environment, with opportunities to build new relationships and learn how you can make an impact in the competitive, trillion-dollar, Payments industry. You will be exposed to a wide range of businesses both internally and externally, then leveraging Scotiabank’s strong technology base, build and develop on strategic, forward-looking infrastructure.

What You’ll Do


Develop business and system requirements, including user stories, acceptance criteria, business rules, process flows, story maps, and supporting functional documentation.
Document end-to-end payment journeys covering initiation, validation, routing, processing, clearing and settlement, reconciliation, rejects, returns, and exceptions.
Produce impact analyses identifying affected payment flows, integration points, data requirements, dependencies, operational impacts, and gaps.
Support Scrum Masters, Product Owners, and Engineering Leaders with initiative planning and scoping, providing impact assessments, dependencies, risks, and analysis needed to define and sequence the work.
Develop risk analyses that identify delivery, implementation, operational, and payment-processing risks, with appropriate controls and mitigations.
Develop test strategies and plans defining scope, scenarios, environments, test data, dependencies, entry and exit criteria, and expected coverage.
Create and execute functional, integration, end-to-end, regression, negative, exception, recovery, reconciliation, and certification test scenarios and cases.
Validate ISO 20022 messages, business rules, transaction outcomes, settlement and reconciliation, including reject, return, and exception flows.
Coordinate certification and interoperability testing with payment networks, financial institutions, and external partners, including test planning, execution, defect management, and evidence.
Maintain traceability across requirements, acceptance criteria, test coverage, defects, and release readiness.
Produce defect and root-cause analyses for complex test and production issues and work with delivery teams through resolution and validation.
Develop implementation and production validation plans covering deployment checks, critical payment flows, reconciliation, operational verification, and success criteria.
Facilitate working sessions with business, technology, and operations teams to develop standard operating procedures, runbooks, change-management plans, and support documentation required to operationalize new or changed capabilities.
Produce release-readiness assessments covering test results, defects, risks, dependencies, operational readiness, and outstanding items to support go/no-go decisions.
Contribute reusable standards, templates, and knowledge that improve requirements, testing, and production-readiness practices across the team.


What You'll Bring


Strong understanding of the end-to-end payment lifecycle, including processing, clearing and settlement, reconciliation, rejects, returns, and exception handling.
Experience with Low Value, domestic account-to-account, or real-time payment systems.
Experience with ISO 20022 and XML payment messages.
Strong business and systems analysis experience, with demonstrated ability to produce requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria, process flows, story maps, and impact analyses.
Hands-on experience developing test strategies and executing functional, integration, end-to-end, regression, and certification testing.
Experience designing test coverage for positive, negative, reject, return, exception, recovery, and reconciliation scenarios.
Experience producing risk assessments, implementation plans, production validation plans, operating procedures, or similar delivery artifacts.
Experience investigating complex defects and production issues across integrated systems.
Ability to understand complex system interactions and identify downstream impacts, dependencies, and gaps.
Ability to communicate clearly and work effectively across business, technology, operations, payment networks, financial institutions, and external partners.


Nice To Have


Experience with batch payments such as EFTs, ACH, or Real-Time Rail (RTR).
Knowledge of payment clearing and settlement processes.
Experience with ISO 20022 payment and payment-status messages.
Experience with industry certification, interoperability, or bilateral testing with financial institutions.
Working knowledge of SQL for transaction analysis, reconciliation, and test validation.
Experience testing APIs and working with XML or JSON payloads.
Experience with event-driven or message-based integrations.
Experience with test automation.
Experience supporting highly available or real-time transaction-processing platforms.
Experience delivering large-scale payment modernization initiatives.
