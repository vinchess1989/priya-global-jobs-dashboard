The Role

An opportunity has arisen for a Manufacturing Technical Author to join an established engineering and manufacturing business.




Working closely with Production, Manufacturing Engineering and Quality teams, you will be responsible for developing, writing and maintaining clear, accurate and practical work instructions for the manufacture and assembly of specialist electronic and electro-mechanical products.




You will spend time on the shop floor working alongside production operators and engineers to understand existing manufacturing processes, capture best practice and ensure documented procedures reflect the most efficient, safe and reliable methods of production.




Key Responsibilities

Create, update and maintain detailed manufacturing work instructions, assembly procedures and electronic production routes.
Work closely with production operators to understand current build methods, assembly sequences, tooling requirements and potential production challenges.
Liaise with Manufacturing Engineering, Design Engineering, NPI and Quality teams to establish and document the most effective manufacturing processes.
Translate technical information and shop-floor knowledge into clear, structured and easy-to-follow work instructions.
Ensure documentation includes relevant assembly stages, materials, tooling, quality checks, and health and safety requirements.
Trial and validate new and existing work instructions alongside production teams, making improvements where necessary.
Manage document updates and revisions, ensuring changes are correctly recorded, approved, and communicated to relevant departments.
Support the introduction of new products into production, contributing to design for manufacture (DFM) and process improvement activities.
Assist with investigations into production issues and non-conformances, identifying documentation gaps and implementing improvements to prevent repeat issues.
Identify opportunities to improve manufacturing processes, production efficiency, consistency and product quality.
Work with production teams to identify tooling, equipment and training requirements.
Produce regular updates on documentation progress, outstanding actions and improvement activities.
Ensure all manufacturing instructions comply with relevant quality, environmental, electrical safety and workmanship standards.
Contribute to continuous improvement projects and work collaboratively across multiple departments.




Skills & Experience

Technical background in Electronics, Electro-Mechanical or Manufacturing Engineering.

Experience writing manufacturing work instructions, assembly procedures, or technical documentation.

Ability to interpret engineering drawings, electrical schematics, and bills of materials.

Understanding of manufacturing processes, quality standards, ESD, and health & safety requirements.

Experience with ProPlanner, Autodesk Inventor, or similar software would be advantageous.

Familiarity with ERP systems and Lean Manufacturing principles would be beneficial.

Strong communication skills, with the ability to work closely with production and engineering teams.

Practical, hands-on approach with a willingness to work on the shop floor.

Organised, methodical and detail-oriented, with good problem-solving skills.




Salary & Benefits

Salary up to £40,000, depending on experience.

25 days annual leave plus 8 bank holidays.

Access to a company profit share scheme following successful completion of probation.

Life assurance worth 3x annual salary.

Annual discretionary Christmas payment.

Long-service awards at 10, 15, 20, 25 and 30 years.
