Summary

UST is looking for an experienced Regulatory Affairs professional to drive complex global regulatory submissions for IVD and Medical Device portfolios. The role involves defining regulatory pathways, executing submissions across multiple geographies, coordinating with health authorities and Notified Bodies, mentoring junior team members, and partnering with cross-functional teams to support regulatory compliance and successful market access.

The Opportunity

Role: Regulatory Affairs – IVD & Medical Devices

Experience: 3–8 years

Domain: IVD / Medical Devices

Focus: Global Regulatory Submissions & Compliance

Key Responsibilities


Lead the development and execution of global regulatory strategies for product registrations, renewals, and change controls across major markets.
Prepare, review, and submit regulatory dossiers in accordance with MDR, IVDR, and other regional regulatory frameworks across APAC, LATAM, EMEA, and other markets.
Manage the end-to-end regulatory submission lifecycle, including:
Gap analysis
Remediation activities
Application tracking
Regulatory query and response coordination
Partner with R&D, Quality, and Manufacturing teams to provide regulatory input into design and development documentation.
Coordinate directly with Notified Bodies and health authorities to address queries and facilitate timely regulatory approvals.
Maintain regulatory documentation and databases to support global portfolio maintenance.
Guide regional teams on regulatory requirements, submission formats, and timelines to ensure consistency in execution.
Support labeling reviews, product change assessments, and post-market compliance activities.
Identify opportunities to improve submission preparation and document management workflows.
Mentor regulatory specialists through on-the-job training and review of regulatory deliverables.
Maintain accurate, complete, and up-to-date regulatory documentation and records.
Ensure appropriate documentation practices are followed in accordance with applicable regulatory standards.

What You Need

Required Skills: IVDR, MDR, Compliance Management, Business Analysis

Qualification

Life Sciences, Regulatory Affairs, Biomedical Engineering, or related degree

Advanced Degree Preferred
