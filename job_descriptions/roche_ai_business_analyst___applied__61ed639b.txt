The Position

About The Role
At Roche Digital Technology (RDT), we are advancing the boundaries of Applied AI. As part of our strategic initiatives, we enable high-value AI solutions (with a strong focus on Generative AI and agentic systems) through fit-for-purpose platforms, services, and applications. The Applied AI Engineering Team is tasked with building innovative AI applications, GenAI agents, and agentic foundations.

In the 2026 tech landscape, the lines between traditional disciplines have blurred. We operate in small agile teams (typically ~9 members) powered by advanced coding agents (like Claude Code) to develop and ship solutions faster than ever before (Agentic SDLC).

To support our rapid-turnaround, high-velocity engineering teams, we are seeking a highly skilled, deeply experienced IT Business Analyst. Operating in a highly regulated pharmaceutical and clinical environment (GxP), you will serve as the strategic interface between complex business priorities, regulatory compliance frameworks, and cutting-edge engineering realities. You are not a traditional analyst who merely documents requests; you are a passionate, technically literate individual contributor equipped with advanced AI skills (including prompt engineering & modern agentic workflows) and the business acumen to collaborate closely with product leaders. Furthermore, you possess strong Product Owner (PO) skills and are fully prepared to confidently act as a Proxy PO for the squad if necessary—managing the product backlog, defining sprint goals, and steering delivery timelines to align with Roche’s strategic objectives.

Core Tech Stack & Scope

This role requires a deep understanding of three core domains: AI systems, the life sciences and pharmaceutical business context, and the complexities of operating within a highly regulated environment.

The IT Business Analyst serves as the strategic bridge between complex business requirements and cutting-edge technical execution. You will take ownership of the end-to-end requirements lifecycle for AI initiatives, ensuring that all architectural designs and engineering deliverables are scalable, high-performing, and strictly compliant with global regulatory standards. By aligning technical roadmaps with strategic priorities, you will drive the delivery of robust AI solutions that provide measurable business value and maintain the highest levels of data integrity and quality assurance.

Key Responsibilities

Agile Requirements & Product Owner Support (60% Focus):


Manage the full AI project lifecycle from opportunity identification through deployment and value realization
Translate business goals into prioritized product backlogs, Epics, and User Stories with clear acceptance criteria
Define MVP/MMR strategies to iteratively deliver business value while managing project risks
Act as a Proxy PO when necessary, making critical decisions on prioritization and sprint goals


LLM Prompt Engineering & Solution Prototyping:


Design and optimize LLM prompts to ensure accurate, secure, and relevant model outputs
Prototype agentic workflows and evaluate model responses using Python scripting and SQL data exploration
Develop specifications for dynamic Generative UI components and RAG pipeline context mappings


Requirements Engineering & Business Process Analysis:


Lead requirements workshops and JAD sessions to elicit business and functional specifications
Author standard-compliant artifacts including URS, FRS, and "as-is/to-be" process maps
Conduct gap analyses and re-engineering assessments to optimize and automate manual workflows


GxP Compliance, Computerized System Validation (CSV) & Software Assurance (CSA):


Guide compliance-by-design strategies for AI deployments following GAMP 5, 21 CFR Part 11, and Annex 11
Guide risk-based CSA/CSV activities, authoring risk assessments (FMEA), trace matrices, and validation reports
Enforce strict data integrity controls (ALCOA+) and system classifications to preserve validated states


AI Governance, Risk Management & Observability Specifications:


Ensure governance guardrails, escalation triggers, and observability requirements for agentic AI systems are applied
Assess AI-specific risks (drift, leakage, tool misuse) and formulate TCO/ROI business cases


Change Control, ITSM & Configuration Management:


Manage formal Change Requests (RFC) and present impact analyses to CAB/ECAB boards
Maintain system configuration documentation and audit-readiness for internal and regulatory inspections


Audit Readiness & Operational Support:


Act as IT SME during audits, presenting validation evidence and requirements engineering practices
Author SOPs, manuals, and training materials to support business readiness and operational transitions



Practical Skills Required
Experience:


8+ years of IT Business Analysis experience supporting enterprise-scale software solutions, system integrations, and business processes
5+ years of experience in highly regulated environments, with a strong preference for the pharmaceutical, biotechnology, medical devices, or clinical healthcare sectors
3+ years of proven experience in Agile/Scrum product delivery, with demonstrated success managing product backlogs, writing detailed User Stories, and supporting agile ceremonies
Hands-on AI/LLM Project Experience: Experience working directly on Generative AI or machine learning projects, supporting data preparation, prompt development, or AI application testing
Proven track record of delivering computerized system validation or software assurance under industry-standard quality guidelines and electronic records regulations
Demonstrated experience working with cross-functional, globally distributed engineering squads (including developers, architects, QA, and validation teams)
Agentic Workflow / aSDLC Experience: Proven experience leveraging AI productivity tools and writing/coding assistants (e.g., Claude Code, ChatGPT, Codex, GitHub Copilot) daily to accelerate elicitation, business process mapping, and requirements generation within an agentic software development lifecycle



Skills
Must-have:


Advanced Requirements Lifecycle Mastery: Expert-level proficiency in elicitation, specification, modeling, verification, and traceability
LLM Prompt Engineering & AI Prototyping: Proficient in designing, evaluating, and refining prompts for large language models. Ability to construct robust system prompts and templates that control model outputs and behavior
Technical & Data Literacy (Analysis & Logical Assessment): Advanced ability to analyze complex data structures, logically assess AI model outputs, and trace data lineage. Strong technical aptitude to interpret technical specifications and evaluate system behaviors, with a focus on data-driven decision-making rather than hands-on coding or scripting
Regulatory & Quality Expertise: Deep theoretical and practical knowledge of industry-standard regulatory and quality protocols, software validation and quality assurance standards, and global guidelines for electronic records and digital signatures
Process Modeling & Visualization: Advanced skills in process mapping, business workflow design, and data flow diagramming using tools like Microsoft Visio, Lucidchart, or BPMN engines
Business Case & Financial Analysis: Experience conducting financial analyses, cost-benefit modeling, and calculating the total cost of ownership for enterprise platforms
Stakeholder Management & Facilitation: Outstanding active listening, communication, and negotiation skills, with the ability to build consensus across conflicting viewpoints, push back on unrealistic business requests, and translate between "business speak" and "tech speak."


Should-have


Agile Product Ownership Capability: Strong product thinking and backlog management capabilities using JIRA and Confluence. Ability to confidently step in as a Proxy PO when necessary to define MVPs, prioritize backlogs, and drive sprint goals
AI/ML and Agentic Architecture Familiarity: Broad understanding of LLM architectures, RAG/GraphRAG concepts, multi-agent frameworks, and vector/graph databases to effectively analyze requirements for advanced AI projects
IT Service Management (ITSM): Solid understanding of ITIL-based change management, incident management, and CMDB architectures
Testing and QA Partnership: Experience partnering with QA to review test plans, coordinate User Acceptance Testing (UAT), and verify test coverage using Requirements Traceability Matrices
Agentic SDLC: Strong drive to apply agentic Software Development Lifecycle practices and tools


Could-have:


Professional Certifications: International Institute of Business Analysis (IIBA) credentials such as CBAP (Certified Business Analysis Professional) or CCBA; Project Management Institute (PMI-PBA) or Scrum Alliance (CSPO/PO) certifications
Multicloud Awareness: Basic familiarity with cloud service models (AWS/Azure) and cloud-native application deployments


Capabilities:


Problem-Solving Skills: Dissect complex, ambiguous, and abstract business challenges into manageable, structured, and verifiable functional requirements
Ownership & Decisiveness: High-ownership mindset with the confidence to make tough priority decisions, remove development roadblocks, and step in to lead product direction as a Proxy PO when necessary
Consulting & Trust: Ability to serve as a strategic, trusted advisor to senior business stakeholders, QA leads, and engineering teams, translating seamlessly between clinical business speak and technical developer speak
Ethics & Empathy: Deep commitment to patient safety, product quality, data security, and ethical AI deployment, backed by a strong user-centric design approach



Qualifications
The successful candidate should:


Have a strong understanding of both AI applications and traditional software development life cycle (SDLC) methodologies
Be passionate about AI, staying up-to-date with emerging digital trends, software architectures, prompt engineering, and validation practices
Be team-oriented, proactive, and collaborative, with a proven ability to thrive in a high-velocity, fluid, and multidisciplinary agile environment where roles are fluid
Be detail-oriented, highly organized, and an exceptional problem-solver who takes initiative in identifying and solving problems
Be willing to learn and expand their skill set in an environment where roles are fluid and multidisciplinary
Be able to communicate in English at the level of: C1+


Located in Hyderabad, India, with working hours structured to capture the "golden hours" of overlap with Central European Time (typically running through the IST evening).

Growth Opportunities: Bridge the gap between innovative technologies and critical, life-saving regulatory standards, expanding your skills into Product Ownership and Prompt Engineering
Collaborative Culture: Be a part of a diverse and inclusive team that values technical excellence, strategic thinking, and creative leadership
