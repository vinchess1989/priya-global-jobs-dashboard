The Company

Dexcom Corporation (NASDAQ DXCM) is a pioneer and global leader in continuous glucose monitoring (CGM). Dexcom began as a small company with a big dream: To forever change how diabetes is managed. To unlock information and insights that drive better health outcomes. Here we are 25 years later, having pioneered an industry. And we're just getting started. We are broadening our vision beyond diabetes to empower people to take control of health. That means personalized, actionable insights aimed at solving important health challenges. To continue what we've started: Improving human health.

We are driven by thousands of ambitious, passionate people worldwide who are willing to fight like warriors to earn the trust of our customers by listening, serving with integrity, thinking big, and being dependable. We've already changed millions of lives and we're ready to change millions more. Our future ambition is to become a leading consumer health technology company while continuing to develop solutions for serious health conditions. We'll get there by constantly reinventing unique biosensing-technology experiences. Though we've come a long way from our small company days, our dreams are bigger than ever. The opportunity to improve health on a global scale stands before us.

Meet The Team

As a Systems Engineer 2 at Dexcom, you will be an integral part of our dynamic and collaborative Systems Engineering team in India. You will work closely with experienced professionals who are passionate about leveraging technology to make a positive impact on patients. You will be working in a fast-paced cross-functional environment, demonstrating rigor and a sense of urgency as you contribute to cutting-edge medical device development.

Where You Come In


You will contribute to the development, documentation, and maintenance of Product Needs and System/Software Requirements, ensuring traceability between requirements and design elements.
You will proficiently utilize GenAI tools (e.g., Gemini) to generate drafts of system engineering documentation, refine requirements, and help manage traceability, ensuring the accuracy and completeness of AI-generated content.
You will participate in system integration testing for both software and hardware sub-systems, identifying, reporting, and troubleshooting issues collaboratively.
You will assist in the creation of test plans and test cases, and support the generation of verification trace matrices.
You will collaborate closely with software developers, hardware engineers, and quality assurance professionals to contribute to the overall success of project deliverables.
You will demonstrate proactive communication, representing the Systems Engineering team in discussions and contributing to adjustments as needed.


What Makes You Successful


You have a solid understanding of Systems Engineering principles with a focus on both hardware and software design elements, preferably in New Product Development.
You are skilled in using GenAI tools (e.g., Gemini) to efficiently generate and refine system engineering documentation and maintain traceability, critically reviewing and validating AI outputs.
You possess strong verbal and written communication skills and can communicate technical information effectively to both technical and non-technical team members.
You have the ability to adapt to a fast-paced and dynamic work environment and a willingness to learn and apply new skills as needed.
You bring a collaborative mindset and the ability to work effectively in a team-oriented setting.
You have experience with requirements management tools (e.g., Jama Connect) and are familiar with software development tools like Jira.
You have demonstrated willingness to solve problems and interface with other teams as needed for product delivery.
Typically requires a Bachelor’s degree in an engineering discipline and 2+ years of related experience, or an advanced degree without experience.


Nice To Haves


Experience in the medical device industry and familiarity with standards like IEC 62304 and ISO 14971.
Systems Engineering prior training, INCOSE certifications (e.g., ASEP).
Experience with mobile application development (Android/iOS) or BLE architecture.
