Overview

 

The Consortium for Clinical Research and Innovation Singapore (CRIS), a wholly owned subsidiary of MOH Holdings, was established in 2020 with the goal of strengthening synergies and promulgating strategies for national-level clinical research and translation programmes under the stewardship of the Singapore Ministry of Health. 
 
CRIS brings together seven national R&D, clinical translation and service programmes to advance clinical research and innovation for Singapore, and establish important capabilities for a future-ready healthcare system.
 
The Business Entities under CRIS include:
•    Singapore Clinical Research Institute (SCRI)
•    National Health Innovation Centre (NHIC)
•    Advanced Cell Therapy and Research Institute, Singapore (ACTRIS)
•    Precision Health Research, Singapore (PRECISE)
•    Singapore Translational Cancer Consortium (STCC)
•    Cardiovascular Disease National Collaborative Enterprise (CADENCE)
•    SIngapore Medical FOundation AI model (SIMFONI)
 
Together, CRIS makes a positive difference to Singapore patients and researchers by ensuring that these clinical research platforms and programmes are at the cutting edge of capability development and innovation. If you are as passionate as we are in clinical trials and research, we want you!

 

SIMFONI

 

SIMFONI is Singapore's national programme building healthcare AI models grounded in local clinical guidelines and Asian patient data. SIMFONI envisions a transformed health system that is patient-centric, data-driven and digitally-enabled to better empower health, prevent disease and provide excellent value-based care. To realise this vision, SIMFONI’s mission is to design and implement innovative solutions essential for the desired health system transformation.

 

About the Role

 

We are hiring a Senior Technical Project & Product Manager to lead the planning, delivery, and adoption of SIMFONI CONDUCTOR, a common platform for deploying, orchestrating, and integrating AI Clinical Decision Support System (AI-CDSS) solutions across Singapore's healthcare ecosystem.

 

This role is responsible for defining and driving the product roadmap, coordinating delivery across multiple stakeholders, and ensuring successful onboarding and operationalisation of AI use cases. The successful candidate will work closely with healthcare institutions, AI teams, platform engineers, vendors, and governance bodies to translate strategic objectives into tangible outcomes.

 

The role will partner closely with the Lead AI Architect and engineering team, combining strong product and delivery leadership with technical understanding to drive both platform evolution and ecosystem adoption.

 

Key Responsibilities

 

•    Product Strategy and Roadmap: Define and maintain the product vision, roadmap, and priorities. Translate stakeholder needs into platform capabilities and balance short-term delivery with long-term platform evolution.
•    Product Delivery and Execution: Lead end-to-end delivery of platform capabilities and Health AI use case onboarding. Manage plans, budgets, timelines, dependencies, resources, and risks.
•    Stakeholder Engagement and Adoption: Engage healthcare clusters, clinical teams, Synapxe, technology teams, and other stakeholders. Drive adoption of platform capabilities and alignment across stakeholders.
•    Governance and Risk Management: Manage programme risks, issues, dependencies, executive reporting, and funding approvals. Coordinate architecture, security, privacy, and governance reviews by working with tech lead and engineering team.
•    Technical and Operational Coordination: Work closely with tech lead and engineering teams on integration, testing, deployment, operational readiness, and production onboarding.
•    Resource Management: Coordinate budgets, contracts, and resource planning.
•    Performance Monitoring and Continuous Improvement: Define success measures, monitor adoption and delivery outcomes, gather stakeholder feedback, and improve product delivery processes.

 

Required Qualifications

 

•    Bachelor's Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Healthcare Informatics, Business Administration, Project Management, or a related discipline.
•    At least 8 years of experience in product management, project management, enterprise technology, healthcare IT, AI platforms, digital transformation, or related fields.
•    Proven experience leading complex technology products or platforms involving multiple stakeholders and delivery teams.
•    Experience managing product roadmaps, prioritisation, strategic planning, and end-to-end delivery.
•    Good understanding of enterprise platforms, cloud technologies, APIs, system integration, cybersecurity, and data governance.
•    Ability to engage effectively with architects, engineers, AI specialists, and infrastructure teams.
•    Strong stakeholder management, communication, facilitation, problem-solving, and organisational skills.

 

Preferred Qualifications

 

•    Experience in healthcare technology, healthcare AI, digital health, or healthcare data platforms.
•    Experience managing platform products serving multiple organisations or user groups.
•    Familiarity with AI governance, agentic systems, MLOps, or LLMOps concepts.
•    Experience working with public-sector or healthcare organisations.
•    Professional certifications such as PMP, Scrum, SAFe Product Manager/Product Owner, or AgilePM.
•    Familiarity with Singapore's healthcare ecosystem and national digital health initiatives.
