Job Title: Senior Device Engineer




Reports to: Director, Device Development

Department: Technical Development




This role is our client’s technical support lead for its drug delivery devices in clinical trials. It brings the knowledge and experience of a device development engineer to device use in the field: training, field-based support, troubleshooting, technical investigations, complaint handling, support for regulatory reporting obligations, sustaining engineering and continuous improvement.

The role works closely with Clinical Operations and Clinical Supply, Quality Assurance, Regulatory Affairs, device manufacturing partners and our client’s manufacturing oversight engineers. It feeds field learning back into current and future device designs. As our client’s products move towards commercialisation, the role is expected to evolve to include technical support for commercial use, such as customer and field technical support, service and post-market activities. The role will require some overseas travel to clinical sites, device development and manufacturing partners.




Key Duties and Responsibilities

Clinical trial support, training and field support

Provide device technical support to clinical trial execution, working alongside Clinical Operations and Clinical Supply.
Develop and maintain device training materials, and train clinical staff and end users on proper device operation, including train-the-trainer.
Provide field-based support at clinical sites as required (site set-up, refresher training, inspection of devices, follow-up on issues), and remote technical support when not in the field.
Support device logistics with Clinical Supply, including backup device strategy, device replacement, device accountability and the returns process.
Document support activities, including reports on site visits, issues, device replacements and user feedback.

Troubleshooting, investigations, complaints and reporting

Act as first-line technical contact for device issues from the field. Troubleshoot issues and escalate to engineering teams as required.
Lead or support technical root cause analysis and investigations of device issues and returned devices with manufacturing partners.
Support complaint handling in line with our client’s QMS (intake, triage, investigation, trending and closure) with Quality Assurance.
Support Regulatory Affairs and Pharmacovigilance in meeting their reporting obligations by providing timely technical assessments of device-related events and malfunctions.

Sustaining engineering, continuous improvement and manufacturing support

Provide sustaining engineering support for devices in use, including design and documentation changes, engineering change review, and component and supplier issues.
Identify and deliver continuous improvement projects across device design and manufacturing, using field, complaint and manufacturing data.
Support the manufacturing oversight engineers on manufacturing issues, NCRs and CAPAs, linking field performance to build quality.
Collect and analyse field performance data and feed learning into risk management and future device user needs.




Education and Experience

Bachelor’s or Master’s qualification in Science, Engineering or a related field.
5+ years’ related experience in medical device development, sustaining engineering or field / technical support.
Technical expertise in electromechanical medical devices.
Knowledge of EU MDR 2017/745, ISO 13485, 21 CFR Part 820 and ISO 14971 is desired.
Hands-on experience with complaint handling, technical investigations and root cause analysis.
Experience supporting devices in clinical trials or in the field, including user training, is strongly preferred.
Knowledge of the relevant regulations and guidelines for inhalation medical devices and combination products is desirable.
Experience working with third-party service providers is required.
Willingness to travel regularly to clinical sites and manufacturing partner facilities.




Knowledge and Abilities

Excellent written and verbal communication and training skills, and comfortable working directly with clinical site staff.
Acute attention to detail, with the ability to see the big picture.
Strong organisational, problem-solving, analytical and decision-making skills, focused on data-driven decisions.
An independent thinker who applies structured problem-solving to resolve issues on their own initiative, taking ownership from investigation through to resolution and knowing when to escalate.
A self-starter who brings energy and enthusiasm to the role and who is proactive in identifying opportunities for device design and operational process improvements.
Resilient, energetic and enthusiastic.
