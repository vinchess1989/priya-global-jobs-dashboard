Hae 
Tallenna
The Environments Manager is responsible for the strategic planning, coordination, and governance of all non-production and production environments across the software delivery lifecycle. This role ensures that environments are stable, scalable, secure, and fit for purpose, enabling seamless development, testing, and deployment activities.

Responsibilities


Own and manage the lifecycle of environments (Dev, QA, UAT, Staging, Production).
Collaborate with Cloud Engineering and DevOps teams to provision and maintain infrastructure across hybrid or multi-cloud platforms.
Oversee environment configuration, visioning, and access control.
Coordinate environment readiness for performance testing, release cycles, and quality assurance.
Implement monitoring and alerting strategies to ensure environment health and performance.
Drive automation of environment provisioning and tear down using Infrastructure as Code (IaC).
Manage environment-related incidents and root cause analysis.
Maintain environment documentation, topology diagrams, and runbooks.
Engage with Release Managers to align environment availability with deployment schedules.
Ensure compliance with security and governance policies.
Coordinates and governs DR testing to validate recoverability, RTO/RPO compliance, and resilience across environments, ensuring high availability and alignment with business continuity objectives.
Design monitoring and observability frameworks to accelerate root cause analysis and reduce Mean-Time-to-Recovery (MTTR)


Essential Skills


Proficiency in DevOps tools (Terraform, Ansible, Jenkins, GitHub Actions)
Solid understanding of CI/CD pipelines and release orchestration.
Familiarity with software development and QA processes.


Experience


Strong experience in cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, GCP)
Experience with performance testing tools and environment tuning.
Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills.
Proven ability to manage multiple environments across concurrent projects.
