Senior Cloud DevOps Engineer (AWS) | Johannesburg




Are you passionate about building scalable cloud infrastructure and ensuring high-performance systems run seamlessly at scale? We’re looking for a Senior Cloud DevOps Engineer to join a fast-growing technology business where you'll play a critical role in shaping and maintaining modern cloud environments that support innovative SaaS solutions.




This is an opportunity for a hands-on engineer who enjoys automating processes, improving reliability, and collaborating closely with development teams to deliver robust, secure, and scalable applications.




What You'll Be Doing

Design, implement, and maintain scalable, highly available cloud infrastructure on AWS.
Build and manage CI/CD pipelines to enable efficient and reliable software delivery.
Administer and support containerized applications using Docker and Kubernetes.
Deploy, monitor, and optimize applications across cloud environments.
Implement infrastructure automation to reduce manual processes and improve operational efficiency.
Strengthen platform security through best practices, hardening, monitoring, and access controls.
Monitor system health, availability, and performance, proactively identifying areas for improvement.
Support and manage databases, including PostgreSQL and emerging NoSQL solutions.
Establish and maintain backup, disaster recovery, and business continuity processes.
Collaborate with engineering and data teams to troubleshoot, resolve, and prevent production issues.
Identify cloud cost-saving opportunities and drive infrastructure optimisation initiatives.
Create and maintain clear technical documentation and operational procedures.




What We're Looking For

Education & Certifications

Degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, Mathematics, or a related field preferred.
AWS certifications advantageous.




Technical Skills

Strong experience with AWS, including services such as ECS, EC2, RDS, S3, CloudFront, and Lambda.
Solid understanding of Docker and container orchestration with Kubernetes.
Experience building CI/CD pipelines using tools such as GitHub Actions, Jenkins, or similar.
Familiarity with Infrastructure as Code tools including Terraform, CloudFormation, or Ansible.
Experience with monitoring and logging solutions such as Prometheus, Grafana, and CloudWatch.
Strong working knowledge of Git/GitHub workflows.
Scripting experience using Python, Bash, Ruby, or similar languages.
Experience integrating systems, APIs, and automation workflows.
Sound understanding of networking, security, cloud architecture, and infrastructure management.




Personal Attributes

Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting skills.
High attention to detail.
Ability to work independently while collaborating effectively across teams.
Passion for building reliable, scalable, and secure systems.
Comfortable working in a dynamic, fast-paced environment.




Bonus Points

Contributions to open-source projects or active involvement in the DevOps community.
Experience with hybrid cloud environments.
Previous experience in a SaaS or high-growth technology business.




Interested?




If you're excited about cloud infrastructure, automation, and enabling engineering teams to deliver exceptional products, we'd love to hear from you.
