JOB TITLE: Primary Labeling SME (Medical Devices)

Welcome to Natus

Natus Sensory develops and manufactures medical devices and software that support screening, diagnosis, and treatment of disorders of the sensory systems. Our mission is to improve patient outcomes through innovative solutions.

About The Role

The Primary Labeling SME, within R&D, owns and coordinates labeling activities for new and existing medical devices throughout the product lifecycle. The role develops, reviews, and maintains compliant device labels, Instructions for Use (IFUs), user manuals, quick guides, packaging artwork, symbols, translations, and electronic labeling. Working with Regulatory Affairs, Quality, Engineering, Manufacturing, Marketing, and Supply Chain, the SME ensures labeling is accurate, controlled, aligned with approved product and regulatory requirements, and implemented through the company’s change-control processes. The role also supports related product documentation, BOM/configuration updates, and engineering changes as needed.

Essential Duties And Responsibilities

Primary labeling and regulatory compliance


Develop, review, and maintain device labels, IFUs, user manuals, quick guides, packaging artwork, symbols, translations, and eIFU content.
Ensure labeling accurately reflects intended use, indications, contraindications, warnings, precautions, operating instructions, product specifications, and risk-management outputs.
Apply relevant global requirements and standards, including FDA labeling requirements, EU MDR, ISO 13485, ISO 15223-1, IEC 62366, applicable IEC 60601 requirements, and UDI requirements.
Coordinate market-specific labeling, translation/localization, artwork review, and approval activities with Regulatory Affairs and other stakeholders.
Monitor labeling regulatory changes and assess or communicate their impact on released and developing products.


Labeling change control and lifecycle support


Coordinate labeling changes from impact assessment and document updates through cross-functional review, approval, release, and implementation.
Support NPI, regulatory submissions, product updates, design control, risk management, post-market activities, audits, and regulatory inspections.
Maintain labeling version control, traceability, document integrity, and alignment with approved submissions and released product configurations.
Develop or maintain labeling procedures, work instructions, and best practices; provide guidance or training to teams on labeling processes.


Product documentation and engineering change support


Support controlled product documentation and related updates to BOMs, product structures, part information, and configuration records in Agile PLM or equivalent systems.
Support ECOs, DCOs, and MCOs involving labeling or related product documentation; coordinate with R&D, Manufacturing, Quality, Regulatory, and Supply Chain to ensure accurate implementation.
Assist with sustaining engineering, NPI, and design transfer documentation to ensure released records reflect the approved product configuration.
Track actions, risks, and deliverables using Jira or equivalent tools, and coordinate effectively with global teams.


Qualifications

Education & experience


Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Life Sciences, Regulatory Affairs, or a related discipline.
At least 5 years of experience in medical device labeling, regulatory affairs, quality, or controlled product documentation; medical electrical device experience is preferred.
Experience supporting labeling change control, UDI, global product launches, artwork approvals, and translations/localization.


Regulatory and technical expertise


Working knowledge of FDA labeling requirements, EU MDR, ISO 13485, ISO 15223-1, IEC 62366, ISO 14971, applicable IEC 60601 requirements, and UDI.
Proficiency with Microsoft Office and Adobe Acrobat; experience with Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop, and labeling software such as NiceLabel is desirable.
Experience with electronic document/QMS systems and, preferably, Agile PLM, BOM/configuration management, and engineering change control.


Communication and execution


Strong written communication, document-review, organization, and attention-to-detail skills.
Ability to manage multiple labeling activities, timelines, and priorities independently and collaborate across functions and global teams.
Experience with design controls, risk management, audits, root-cause investigations, or CAPA is desirable.


Working arrangement


Hybrid role based in India, with potential for office-based work in Bangalore as business needs evolve.
Regular working-hour overlap with U.S.-based teams, generally within approximately 7:00 AM–1:00 PM U.S. Central Time; schedules may vary with project needs.
Travel: less than 5%.
