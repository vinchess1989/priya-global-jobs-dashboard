Job Description


On behalf of our client, we are looking for a full-time experienced Quality Assurance Specialist The Quality Assurance Specialist is responsible for developing, implementing, and maintaining quality management systems to ensure products, services, and business processes meet regulatory, industry, and organisational standards. The daily duties and responsibilities include:

Develop, implement, and monitor quality assurance systems, policies, and procedures to ensure compliance with regulatory and organisational requirements.
Conduct audits, inspections, and quality reviews, and identify opportunities for continuous improvement and risk mitigation.
Investigate quality issues, non-conformances, and compliance risks, and implement corrective and preventive actions.
Liaise with internal and external stakeholders to ensure adherence to quality standards, regulatory requirements, and best practices.
Participate in quality, compliance, and risk management initiatives, and prepare reports and recommendations for management.
Carry out additional duties that may be assigned to you.

Education - Bachelor degree or higher qualification desirable or at least 5 years of relevant experience may substitute Qualification Requirements

Demonstrated relevant experience in quality assurance, quality management, compliance, auditing, or a related field.
Demonstrated experience implementing and maintaining quality management systems and compliance frameworks.
Knowledge of quality standards, auditing practices, risk management principles, and regulatory requirements.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and organisational skills with a high level of attention to detail.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced environment.
Relevant quality or ISO certifications will be highly desirable.
