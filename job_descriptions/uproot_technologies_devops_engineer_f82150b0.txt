🚀 We're Hiring | DevOps Engineer

📍 Milton Keynes, United Kingdom

💼 Full-time, Permanent

Uproot Technologies is seeking a talented DevOps Engineer passionate about cloud infrastructure, automation, scalable architectures, and AI-powered enterprise applications.

Key Responsibilities

Design, deploy, and maintain scalable cloud infrastructure.
Build and optimise CI/CD pipelines.
Automate infrastructure provisioning and application deployments.
Ensure system security, reliability, performance, and availability.
Support containerised applications and AI infrastructure.

What We're Looking For

Degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related discipline.
Experience in DevOps, cloud engineering or infrastructure automation.
Proficiency in Docker, Kubernetes, and Linux.
Experience with AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
Knowledge of Terraform, Git, CI/CD and scripting.

Experience with Infrastructure as Code (IaC), microservices, observability, DevSecOps and AI/ML infrastructure is advantageous.

Join us in building secure, scalable, and intelligent technology solutions alongside international engineering teams.
