The Quality Assurance Manager is responsible for leading the organization's quality management systems, quality assurance programs and continuous improvement initiatives across products, installation services and operational processes. The role ensures compliance with applicable standards and regulatory requirements while driving quality performance, risk management and operational excellence.

Benefits

Permanent full-time opportunity in the energy-efficiency sector.
Opportunity to lead quality systems across varied products and installation services.
Competitive remuneration commensurate with qualifications and experience.
Task & responsibilities


Quality Strategy, Leadership and Governance

Develop and implement the organization’s quality management strategy, quality objectives and annual quality plans aligned with business goals.
Lead and manage the organization’s quality assurance function across products, services, installations and operational processes.
Advise senior management on quality performance, quality risks, compliance obligations and improvement opportunities.
Lead management reviews and quality governance activities to evaluate system effectiveness and drive continual improvement.
Promote a culture of quality, accountability and continuous improvement throughout the organization.

Quality Management Systems

Develop, implement, maintain and continuously improve the organization’s Quality Management System (QMS) in accordance with ISO 9001 principles and industry best practice.
Establish and maintain quality policies, procedures, standards, inspection frameworks, control measures and reporting systems.
Ensure quality management systems remain effective, documented and consistently applied across all business operations.
Oversee document control, record management and quality assurance reporting processes.

Quality Assurance and Operational Performance

Establish organizational quality standards, key performance indicators and performance monitoring processes.
Analyze operational workflows and implement initiatives to improve quality, efficiency, consistency and customer outcomes.
Design and oversee quality control, inspection and verification programs for products, services and installation activities.
Monitor organizational performance against quality objectives and implement corrective measures where required.

Technical Compliance, Auditing and Risk Management

Ensure compliance with applicable Australian Standards, manufacturer specifications, regulatory requirements and energy-efficiency program obligations.
Plan, direct and oversee internal quality audit programs to assess compliance and quality system effectiveness.
Identify, assess and manage quality-related operational and compliance risks and implement appropriate controls.
Monitor regulatory, industry and program changes and update quality systems, procedures and controls accordingly.

Continuous Improvement and Corrective Actions

Lead investigations into quality incidents, non-conformances, recurring defects, warranty issues, customer complaints and service failures.
Conduct root-cause analysis and oversee corrective, preventive and continuous improvement initiatives.
Manage non-conformance reporting systems and ensure timely resolution of identified issues.
Evaluate quality assurance programs and implement improvements based on audit outcomes, performance data and industry best practice.

Supplier and Contractor Quality Management

Establish supplier and contractor quality management requirements, quality standards and performance assessment criteria.
Monitor and evaluate supplier and contractor quality performance and implement improvement initiatives where required.
Collaborate with operational, technical and installation teams to improve service quality and operational consistency.

Quality Performance Reporting and Analysis

Develop quality metrics and analyze audit findings, inspection outcomes, defect rates, complaints, rework and contractor performance trends.
Prepare and present quality performance reports, recommendations and strategic improvement proposals for senior management.
Monitor quality trends and identify opportunities to improve operational performance, customer satisfaction and business outcomes.

Training, Resources and Stakeholder Management

Develop and oversee quality training programs, competency frameworks and quality awareness initiatives for employees and contractors.
Allocate and coordinate resources required to achieve organiszational quality objectives and quality assurance outcomes.
Represent the organization in quality-related matters with regulators, accreditation bodies, suppliers, contractors and other stakeholders.
Ensure quality management systems support customer satisfaction, operational excellence and regulatory compliance objectives.
Qualification & experience

Bachelor's degree or higher qualification in Quality Management, Engineering, Operations Management, Business Management or a related field.
Experience developing and managing Quality Management Systems (QMS), audits, corrective actions and continuous improvement programs.
Strong leadership, analytical, stakeholder management and reporting skills.
Experience in energy efficiency, HVAC, solar, building services or technical installation operations is highly regarded.
Knowledge of ISO 9001 principles, Australian Standards and Victorian Energy Upgrades (VEU) requirements is desirable.
At least 1-2 years of experience in a relevant field
