Full job description

Sanderson are working with one of our market leading clients to help them recruit a DevOps Engineer to join a growing team as they continue to expand. This is a newly created position.

The ideal candidate will be looking for the next step in their DevOps career journey in the build, deployment and support of secure and reliable cloud solutions. Collaborating closely with developers, testers, architects and their customers they will help contribute at an early stage of projects to ensure solutions are ready for production. Training and development will be given to help their knowledge of the business, customers, frameworks and processes.

You will need to have a mix of the following knowledge base;

Analysis, design and understand business problems and develop appropriate solutions
Cloud Infrastructure with a strong understanding of Microsoft Azure
Scripting - PowerShell and Bash
Designing, building or maintaining CI/SD pipelines
Performance monitoring
Technical Documentation

This is an excellent opportunity to join a progressive and growing organisation. Based in Edinburgh the expectation is the selected candidate will work a hybrid model of two days a week in the office.
