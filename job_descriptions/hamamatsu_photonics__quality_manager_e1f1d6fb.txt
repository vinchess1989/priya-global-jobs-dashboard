Join us to Make an Impact

Innovation is in our DNA. We invented supercontinuum lasers and photonic crystal fibers and we constantly reimagine how our products can be used and improved. Our customers use our products in medical and life science research, quantum computing and space exploration.

We seek curious, creative and collaborative people to work in our cutting-edge company. Can you see yourself working in teams and providing solutions for innovators?

If you wish to apply, please contact us with your CV / Résumé. If you have a cover letter, please save this in one document with your CV / Résumé as you are only able to upload one document.

We look forward to hearing from you!

Responsible for ensuring services, processes, and facilities are in compliance with the organization’s Quality Assurance (QA) standards, international standards, and government regulations. Establishes quantitative measurements and techniques for measuring quality and drives improvements. Conducts process quality audit and documents inspection records. Requires understanding of external standards such as International Organization for Standardization (ISO), Lean Six Sigma for quality in their industry field. Will contribute in scaling the team overtime. Problems faced may be difficult but are not complex. Ensures policies, practices and procedures are understood and followed by direct reports, customers and stakeholders.

Specific requirements and experience for this position are listed below.


Implementation of required quality policies to meet AQAP2110 (and ISO9001)
Implementation of required quality policies to meet IEC61508 Implementation of required quality policies for establishing incoming quality inspection on goods inwards
Maintaining quality reporting in line with project requirements
EcoVadis assessment or equivalent ESG review
CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism) information provision where legally applicable
EUDR (EU Deforestation Regulation) compliance where applicable
ESG audits and sustainability documentation on request
Quality supplier assessments and management
CAPAs
Internal audits
Liaising with customer quality management team
