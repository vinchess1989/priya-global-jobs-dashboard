Role Highlights  
This is a rare opportunity for a detail-driven compliance and document control professional to strengthen and take ownership of the Field Services function at Powerfleet. Powerfleet is a global leader in the AIoT software-as-a-service mobile asset industry, with telematics at the heart of what it does. The role will suit someone who enjoys creating structure, owning the records that keep a technician network safe and compliant, and making sure technicians and contractors arrive on site prepared and ready to work.  
Role Overview  
Reporting to the Field Services Supervisor, the Field Services Officer will play a critical role in building a thriving, confident and efficient Field Services department that is aligned and moving in one direction. Sitting at the intersection of the technical, compliance, digital and organisational elements of the function, you will own the compliance records for internal technicians and the installer partner network, as well as the document control system for the department. Working closely with Implementation and the Master Technician, you will ensure only qualified, insured and compliant technicians are scheduled. Installation standards, induction material, work instructions and audit records will be controlled rather than sitting across disconnected sources.  
This is a hands-on role that goes well beyond administration. Alongside compliance and document control, you will prepare and coordinate client-specific job briefs. Before technicians attend site, you will bring together technical, compliance, operational and customer-specific information, so they arrive with everything they need. You will turn technical and project requirements into clear, practical information that technicians and external partners can use, and make sure current standards, work instructions and site information are readily accessible. Technician eligibility will be clear before work is dispatched, documentation will be current and accessible, and upcoming expiries, exceptions and corrective actions will be visible and proactively managed. Over time, you will be able to zoom out, spot the recurring backend problems that create extra work for technicians, and improve the process behind them rather than continually solving the same issues.  
Success in this role means fewer details falling through the cracks, less duplication and less time spent revisiting administrative issues. Information captured after completed field activity will flow back into the business correctly, and audit information will be retrievable and properly followed through, including for ISO 9001. Your monthly compliance reporting will give the Field Services Supervisor clear visibility of record currency, upcoming expiries, exceptions and partner audit status. The broader measure will be how smoothly the function operates, with technicians able to focus on the technical work, confident that the information, compliance and processes behind them are being properly managed.  
Responsibilities
Prepare and coordinate client-specific job briefs so technicians arrive on site with everything they need.
Bring together technical, compliance, operational and customer requirements before each job.
Translate technical and project requirements into clear, practical information for technicians and partners.
Maintain insurance, licence, certification, accreditation and induction records for all internal technicians and installer partners.
Flag expiries at least 30 days in advance and follow up until renewals are recorded.
Advise Implementation of technician eligibility so only compliant technicians are scheduled.
Log every approved exception and escalate unapproved exceptions to the Field Services Supervisor and Implementation Manager.
Verify documentation for new partners, and schedule and record inductions before any technician is scheduled for work.
Establish and maintain the Field Services document control system, keeping installation standards, work instructions and site information current, versioned and accessible.
Partner with the Master Technician (content owner) and the Field Services Supervisor (approver) to publish the installation standard, induction material, work instructions and audit templates.
Record partner and installation audit outcomes, track corrective actions to closure, and prepare evidence for internal and external audits, including ISO 9001.
Support the correct capture of information following completed field activity.
Deliver a monthly compliance report covering record currency, upcoming expiries, exceptions granted and partner audit status.
Identify recurring backend problems that create extra work for technicians and improve the supporting process.
Candidate Profile  
You are an experienced compliance administrator, document controller or contractor management professional with at least two years’ experience maintaining accurate records and keeping others accountable to a standard. You have genuine ownership experience building structured registers and version-controlled documents, and you are comfortable following up persistently until an issue is closed. Rather than simply processing paperwork, you take pride in creating order, spotting gaps early and making compliance easy for the people who rely on it. Technical curiosity will also be well regarded, particularly an interest in 12V systems, telematics, GPS, cameras, IoT or similar installation environments. You are methodical, disciplined and naturally curious about how processes can be improved.  
You bring exceptional attention to detail and the confidence to hold a compliance standard with partners and colleagues, even when it is inconvenient. You communicate clearly across technicians, contractors, operations teams and senior stakeholders, and can turn technical requirements into plain, practical language. Advanced Excel, Microsoft 365 and Salesforce skills are essential, and you are comfortable working across digital systems to keep information accurate, connected and accessible. ISO 9001 or subcontractor compliance experience is desirable, as is ISO 9001 Internal Auditor training (or a willingness to complete it). A Certificate IV in Business Administration, Quality or Compliance would also be well regarded.  
Most importantly, you are a hands-on and adaptable professional who enjoys building and improving rather than simply maintaining. You will be at your best in a culture built around Powerfleet's values: It’s All About People and Relationships, Have a Growth Mindset, Togetherness, We Deliver, Integrity Matters and Fully Inclusive.  
Powerfleet has grown to nearly 3 million subscribers and about 50,000 customers. It did this by combining Powerfleet, MiX Telematics and Fleet Complete, and its telematics, video and asset-tracking solutions run through its Unity platform. Its culture is built on strong relationships, a growth mindset and a commitment to delivering for customers and partners. The Operations team is focused on building a Field Services function that is aligned, efficient and confident, offering the successful candidate genuine influence over how it operates.
Shape the future - establish the document control and compliance framework for Field Services
Drive real impact - give technicians and partners a smoother, clearer way of working
Join a global leader - be part of a growing business in telematics and AIoT
