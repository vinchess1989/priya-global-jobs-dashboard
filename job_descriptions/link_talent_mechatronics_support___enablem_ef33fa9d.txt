Overall purpose:




On behalf of our well-established manufacturing client, we are looking for a Mechatronics Support & Enablement Engineer to provide Tier 2 technical support and remote installation and commissioning assistance for automated electromechanical systems.

The role also involves developing technical documentation, training and certifying external technicians, analysing recurring failures, and testing software releases before deployment.




Requirements:




3–6 years’ experience with electromechanical or embedded systems
Experience in remote diagnostics, field service, or technical support
Knowledge of Linux, networking protocols, and scripting
Familiarity with mechatronics and software testing
Strong organisational, documentation, and customer-facing communication skills
Fluent English; French is an advantage




Working hours: Monday to Friday, 9:00–18:00

Salary: €30,000–€35,000 gross per year plus medical insurance
