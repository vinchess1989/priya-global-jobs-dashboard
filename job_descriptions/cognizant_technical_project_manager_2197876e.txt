About The Role

We are looking for an experienced Technical Project Manager to lead end-to-end delivery of technology and customer-experience initiatives. The role requires strong expertise in Agile/Scrum delivery, ServiceNow Change Management, Release Management, stakeholder coordination, and delivery governance.

The ideal candidate will bridge business, customer, and engineering teams, ensuring projects and releases are delivered on schedule, within scope, and with effective risk and change management.

In This Role, You Will


Lead end-to-end planning and execution of technical and CX projects, from requirements through production release.
Drive Agile/Scrum ceremonies, including sprint planning, daily stand-ups, backlog refinement, sprint reviews, and retrospectives.
Partner with Product Owners, Scrum Masters, engineering teams, business stakeholders, and external partners to manage priorities and dependencies.
Develop and maintain project plans, milestones, RAID logs, delivery dashboards, and executive status reports.
Manage ServiceNow Change Management activities, including change requests, implementation plans, risk/impact assessments, approvals, CAB coordination, and closure.
Own and coordinate Release Management, including release calendars, readiness reviews, deployment planning, go/no-go decisions, rollback plans, and post-release validation.
Coordinate releases across development, QA, infrastructure, DevOps, support, and business teams.
Identify delivery risks, blockers, cross-team dependencies, and schedule conflicts, driving them through timely resolution.
Track Agile delivery metrics such as sprint commitments, velocity, burn-down/burn-up, defects, and release readiness.
Ensure adherence to established SDLC, Agile, ITIL, change, and release governance processes.
Facilitate communication between technical teams and business/customer stakeholders, translating technical issues into clear business impact.
Manage multiple concurrent initiatives while maintaining focus on delivery timelines, quality, customer experience, and operational stability.
Support production implementation, hypercare, incident follow-up, retrospectives, and continuous process improvement.


Work model

We strive to provide flexibility wherever possible. Based on this role's business requirements, this is a Remote position open to qualified applicants in the Canada. Regardless of your working arrangement, we are here to support a healthy work-life balance through our various wellbeing programs.

Note: The working arrangements for this role are accurate as of the date of posting. This may change based on the project you're engaged in, as well as business and client requirements. Rest assured; we will always be clear about role expectations.

What You Need To Have To Be Considered


Overall 10+ years in IT/project delivery, ideally including 4+ years leading technical projects for large organizations.
Strong hands-on experience with Scrum, Kanban, sprint planning, backlog management, and Agile delivery.
Proven background in release planning, deployment coordination, release calendars, go/no-go governance, and production readiness.
Experience with Change Management workflows, change tickets, approvals, CAB, and implementation tracking.
Experience managing customer-facing or customer-experience technology initiatives.
Strong understanding of application development, testing, deployment, production support, and DevOps processes
Ability to work with Product, Engineering, QA, DevOps, Operations, vendors, and senior leadership.
Experience driving RAID management, dependency tracking, status reporting, and delivery metrics while collaborating across Product, Engineering, QA, DevOps, vendors, and senior leadership.
Proficiency in ServiceNow, Jira and/or Azure DevOps, Confluence, and Microsoft Project (or similar planning tools).
