About The Role

The Product Lead – Configuration as Code (CaC) defines and executes the strategic technology vision for enterprise fleet management, infrastructure automation, and configuration governance across a high-volume, mission-critical ecosystem. Serving as a key bridge between strategy and engineering execution, this role converts complex enterprise architecture blueprints into standardised, production-ready Ansible playbooks, Terraform patterns, and GitOps workflows.

Key Responsibilities


Set and uphold configuration-as-code standards: version control, peer review, branching and promotion, and automated testing for configuration code. Provision and manage platforms through Terraform, CI/CD Pipelines and GitHub.
Ensure the platforms (Ansible/Ansible Automation Platform (AAP), Azure Key Vault, GCP Secret Manager, HashiCorp Vault) are secure, reliable and supportable, covering upgrades, incident and problem management, and runbooks.
Define the technical strategy and design direction for configuration management, server state automation and fleet-wide compliance across the enterprise infrastructure, aligned to the product roadmap and priorities set by the Platform Manager.
Lead technical delivery of the SOI project portfolio using agile methodologies, covering sprint execution, release readiness, dependency and risk management, and delivery status reporting to the Platform manager.
Work with business analysts and consuming teams to translate requirements and audit findings into well-defined technical solutions, stories and acceptance criteria for the backlog.
Lead and develop a team of 2-3 engineers and business analysts. Allocate work, coach, and maintain quality through design and code review.
Direct the GCC offshore team and external partner day to day, with clear scope, handover and acceptance criteria, and quality oversight against agreed outcomes and SLAs.


What You’ll Bring


Strong expertise in enterprise CaC platform, specifically Ansible, with the demonstrated ability to review, design, and guide complex playbooks, roles, and manifests for large scale fleet management.
Proven experience managing configuration drift, implementing secrets management architectures using native Vaults, and maintaining high availability across platform upgrades.
Demonstrable experience applying Policy-as-Code frameworks (e.g., Sentinel Policies) to enforce deployment governance, compliance, and automated risk controls.
Solid understanding of public cloud environments (Azure, GCP) integrated with IaC tools like Terraform / Terraform Cloud and GitOps principles.
Hands-on familiarity with GitHub and CI/CD pipelines to build, test, and release automated configuration workflows seamlessly.
Proven capability operating across all levels of product delivery—from high-level strategy and architectural blueprints to sprint-level execution and operational support.
Demonstrated success working as an Engineer or Product Lead, translating complex infrastructure needs into clear backlog items (features, technical debt, security requirements, and bug fixes).
