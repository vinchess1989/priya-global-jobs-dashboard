Co-headquartered in San Francisco and Singapore with offices in 14 markets worldwide, we are entering one of the most exciting chapters in our journey. In March 2026, we delivered the largest month in our 11-year history with record revenue, record volumes, and EBITDA profitability. Today, Nium moves nearly $60B in payments annually, almost entirely for enterprises, while continuing to strengthen an already healthy balance sheet.

It is an incredible time to join us, and we are only just getting started.

Our payout network spans 190+ countries and 100 currencies, with 100 + corridors in real time. We power seamless transfers to accounts, wallets, and cards, support local collections in 35 markets, and as a principal card issuer on Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and UATP, Nium issues over 50 million card tokens every year. Role Overview

As a DevOps Team Lead at Nium, you will lead a team of DevOps engineers responsible for the infrastructure, delivery pipelines, and cloud platforms that power our card issuance platform. You will set the technical direction for our migration from on-premises systems to AWS, including the monolithic architecture and microservices, and drive the adoption of GitOps across engineering.

In this role, you will combine hands-on technical leadership with people leadership. You will stay close to the code and infrastructure, reviewing designs and contributing to critical work, while growing your team, setting clear priorities, and ensuring they have the skills and support to deliver. You will be the go-to point of accountability for platform reliability and delivery readiness.

You will champion infrastructure-as-code and automation, improve tooling and processes, and build a culture of ownership, collaboration, and knowledge sharing. You will also be the bridge between the DevOps team and the wider engineering organisation, enabling teams to deliver their work end to end. Based in Malta, you will help shape how Nium builds and ships payment technology at scale.

Key Responsibilities

Team Leadership & Development


Lead, coach, and grow a team of DevOps engineers through regular one-to-ones, clear goals, and performance feedback
Plan and prioritise team capacity across migration, platform, and support work
Recruit, onboard, and retain engineers with the skills the platform needs



Cloud Migration & Platform Strategy


Own the plan and delivery of migrating workloads and CI/CD pipelines from on-premises infrastructure to AWS
Define the target platform architecture to support the move from a monolith to microservices
Make informed infrastructure and tooling decisions aligned with business objectives and cost, security, and reliability requirements



GitOps & Infrastructure Automation


Set standards for infrastructure provisioning with Terraform and application deployment to Kubernetes with ArgoCD
Drive the adoption of GitOps practices across teams and ensure consistent, auditable delivery
Set the benchmark for code quality, review practices, and reusable Kustomize configuration



Continuous Integration & Delivery


Establish and govern Continuous Integration practices using tools such as CircleCI
Ensure CI/CD pipelines are reliable, secure, and fast, and continuously improve them



Engineering Enablement


Lead the delivery of tools, templates, and self-service capabilities that let engineering teams handle their deliverables end to end
Ensure the team provides the infrastructure required to run and scale the card issuance platform



Operations & Reliability


Own the team's operational health, including monitoring, incident response, and post-incident reviews
Ensure timely support for customers and internal departments, and lead root cause analysis for recurring issues
Define and track reliability and delivery metrics for the platform



Stakeholder Management & Collaboration


Partner with Engineering leadership, Product, and Security to align roadmaps, manage risk, and communicate progress
Represent the DevOps function in planning and architecture discussions across the organisation
Will be the contact person for audit reporting



Knowledge Sharing


Build a culture of knowledge sharing through training sessions, technical talks, documentation, and mentoring



Ideal Candidate


Proven experience leading or managing a DevOps, Platform, or Infrastructure team, with a track record of delivering through others
Hands-on experience with cloud solutions, ideally AWS, at scale
Strong proficiency with Kubernetes, Docker, and Terraform. Experience with Kustomize is a big plus
Strong working knowledge of Linux and scripting in Bash, Groovy, and Python
Deep experience designing and maintaining CI/CD pipelines. Experience with CircleCI and GitOps tooling such as ArgoCD is highly desirable
Experience leading a cloud migration or a move from monolith to microservices is a strong advantage
Strong communication skills and the ability to balance technical depth with business priorities
