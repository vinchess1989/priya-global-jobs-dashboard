Position Overview

Job Title- Public Websites Requirements Engineer, AVP

Location- Pune, India

Corporate Title: Assistant Vice President

Role Description


We are seeking an experienced Requirements Engineer with at least 12 years of experience in the Banking and Financial Services industry to elicit, define, and document system requirements that support our digital content strategy for public websites built on Adobe Experience Manager.
This is an engineering role and any prior experience in analysis, coding, systems design and delivery will only become an advantage to excel in the role.

Your Key Responsibilities

We are looking for someone who is a team player and who has the following responsibilities:


Requirements elicitation:
Independently conduct interviews, workshops, and surveys
Align requirements with stakeholders
Facilitate and guide cross-functional requirements sessions with other Requirements Engineers
Requirements analysis
Analyse and decompose large-scale business requirements into implementable functional and non-functional solution requirements
Assess the complexity and dependencies of the identified requirements
Enhance and structure requirements by involving technical stakeholders and Solution/Enterprise Architects
Identify and resolve conflicts, ambiguities, and inconsistencies
Requirements documentation
Create and maintain structured documentation in accordance with common TDI PB standards
Familiarity with industry standards for documentation and commonly used modeling languages
Requirements management
Use suitable tools and frameworks to ensure traceability and version control.
Capture and update requirements
Perform impact analyses
Guide teams comprising multiple Requirements Engineers or working on cross-application initiatives


Your Skills And Experience


Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English and good to have experience in the German language.
Technical background or a strong technical understanding of IT systems, interfaces, and architecture
Extensive experience with modeling techniques using UML, SysML, BPMN and Diagramatic representations of solutions.
Familiarity with Documentation as Code and usage of Jira / Jama as a requirements repository tool.
Extensive experience with IDEs and CI/CD toolchains
Understanding of common IT architecture, principles, design patterns, and non-functional requirements (NFRs)
AI experience: Working knowledge in MS Copilot, Claude CoWork to help with documentation and project status reporting / planning if required.
Adobe Services Knowledge: Familiarity with Adobe Managed Services or Adobe Cloud Services would be an added advantage to excel in the role.
Agile Methodology: Strong understanding and promotion of Agile values familiar with tools such as JIRA and Confluence.



Proven ability to leverage AI tools to enhance productivity, optimise workflows to solve business problems, while applying critical judgment to ensure responsible and ethical use of data and AI outputs.
