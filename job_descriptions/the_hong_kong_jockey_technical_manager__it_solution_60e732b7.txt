The Hong Kong Jockey Club

Founded in 1884, The Hong Kong Jockey Club ("the Club") is a world-class horse sports and entertainment organisation that acts continuously for the betterment of our society. The Club has a unique value creation model, through which the Club generates sustainable economic and social value for the benefit of the community and supports the HKSAR Government in combatting illegal gambling. The Club is Hong Kong’s largest single taxpayer and one of the city’s major employers. Its Charities Trust is one of the world’s leading charity donors.

Who are we?

We are the IT Division of HKJC, a vibrant community of over 1,500 dedicated professionals working collaboratively across Hong Kong and Shenzhen.

Our team is a diverse mix of individuals from various backgrounds, from all across the world. We embrace our humanity, recognizing that each of us brings unique strengths and perspectives. This diversity not only enriches our work environment but also drives our innovation and creativity as we strive to achieve our collective goals.

What do we do?

We design, build, and operate the technology that powers the Club. Our primary focus is on delivering the service that supports our hospitality, racing and wagering operations, to ensure that our customers and members enjoy exceptional experiences.

We also deliver the changes necessary to drive business growth through new products and services. And, we are committed to safeguarding the Club by protecting it from external threats, providing a secure and resilient technological environment.

The Department

The IT Change Department is crucial for ensuring the successful delivery of all IT change initiatives, whether using agile or waterfall methodologies. It is supported by a robust IT Change Management Office (CMO), which oversees the entire process to ensure efficiency and effectiveness. Key responsibilities of the IT change department:


Change Initiative Delivery
Change Management Office
Quality Assurance
Release Management
Portfolio Management
PDLC Governance



The Job


Support the planning and execution of business analysis activities for assigned initiatives
Gather, document and validate business and user requirements through interviews, workshops, observation and document review
Map current and future-state processes, customer journeys, data flows and business rules
Maintain requirements documentation, traceability records, decision logs and change requests
Coordinate with business users, product teams, architects, developers, testers and vendors to clarify requirements
Coordinate vendor deliverable submission, review, comment resolution, acceptance evidence and document-control activities in accordance with agreed governance and quality requirements
Support SIT, SAT and UAT planning, test-scenario preparation, defect clarification, business readiness and training materials
Support data-migration and integration oversight through schedule tracking, dependency coordination, issue escalation and alignment of business, IT and vendor activities
Track analysis issues and actions, escalate conflicts or gaps, and prepare status information
Support implementation readiness, cutover planning, go/no-go governance, hyper-care tracking, knowledge transfer and transition to BAU support



About You


Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Information Technology, Computer Science, Information Systems or a related discipline
PMP, PRINCE2 Foundation, Scrum or PMO-related certification is advantageous
Minimum 5 years of relevant experience in project coordination, PMO, business analysis or IT project delivery
Experience supporting medium-to-large enterprise application, SaaS or transformation projects across the full delivery lifecycle
Experience coordinating system integrators, software vendors and cross-functional business and IT stakeholders
Knowledge of procurement, Source-to-Contract or ERP implementation is preferred
Experience supporting testing, data migration, system integration, cutover and production-readiness activities is preferred
Strong organisation, communication, documentation, facilitation and follow-up skills
Proficiency in Jira, Confluence, Microsoft Project or equivalent planning tools, Excel, PowerPoint and Microsoft 365
