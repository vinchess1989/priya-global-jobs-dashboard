About Accenture

Accenture is a global professional services company with leading capabilities in digital, cloud and security. Find out more about us at www.accenture.com.

Build the platforms that make innovation possible.

At Accenture, we believe great technology is built by diverse teams with different perspectives, experiences, and ideas. We're looking for a DevOps Engineer who is passionate about automation, cloud technologies, continuous improvement, and helping teams deliver their best work.

This is an opportunity to work at the intersection of cloud engineering, software delivery, security, and platform automation, contributing to large-scale transformation programs that make a real impact.

Whether your background is in DevOps, cloud engineering, platform engineering, infrastructure automation, or site reliability, we'd love to hear from you.

THE WORK

As a DevOps Engineer, you'll play an important role in creating modern, secure, and scalable cloud environments by:



Designing, building, and maintaining cloud infrastructure and environments.

Developing Infrastructure as Code and automation solutions to improve delivery efficiency.

Creating and enhancing CI/CD pipelines that enable reliable, high-quality software delivery.

Collaborating with development, testing, architecture, and security teams to embed best practices across the software development lifecycle.

Supporting performance, stress, and volume testing activities.

Implementing DevSecOps practices and integrating security controls into delivery pipelines.

Troubleshooting platform, deployment, and environment challenges.

Driving continuous improvement across tooling, processes, and cloud platforms.

Contributing to Agile delivery teams that value collaboration, learning, and innovation.



Here’s What You’ll Need

We're interested in talented people who enjoy solving problems, working collaboratively, and building high-quality technology solutions.

You may bring experience in:



Designing and implementing DevOps tooling, frameworks, and architecture.

CI/CD practices and modern software delivery approaches.

Containerised and microservices-based environments.

Configuration and environment management.

Supporting enterprise-scale cloud platforms.

Infrastructure automation and platform engineering.



Technical Experience

Experience across a number of the following areas will be highly regarded:



Azure Cloud technologies.

Terraform (Open Source and/or Terraform Enterprise).

Azure DevOps and pipeline configuration.

YAML pipelines and PowerShell automation.

Git, Azure Repos, and source control management.

Kubernetes and container orchestration.

SonarQube and automated code quality practices.

Artifact repository management.

Performance monitoring, tuning, and optimisation.

DevSecOps capabilities including:

Key Vaults and secrets management.

Managed Identity and RBAC.

Security testing tools such as OWASP ZAP or similar.
