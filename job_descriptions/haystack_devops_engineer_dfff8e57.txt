We're hiring on behalf of a Haystack partner!




The Role

• Continuously improve the resiliency and security of systems powering a leading e-commerce platform.

• Build tooling and infrastructure to enhance operational excellence for software engineering teams.

• Own and operate infrastructure across production, staging, and pre-production cloud environments.

• Drive security and compliance initiatives, including risk remediation and patching.

• Execute infrastructure cost optimization strategies.

• Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation.

• Participate in on-call rotations for incident response and resolution.

• Contribute to platform reliability through multi-AZ resilience, monitoring, and alerting.




What You'll Need

• Professional software development experience.

• Knowledge of systems engineering fundamentals (networking, storage, operating systems).

• Experience programming in at least one modern language (e.g., Python, Ruby, Golang, Java, C++, C#, Rust).

• Experience designing or architecting new and existing systems for reliability and scaling.

• Experience automating, deploying, and supporting large-scale infrastructure.

• Experience utilizing cloud solutions in a DevOps environment.




What's On Offer

• Competitive salary

• Opportunity to work with talented software engineers across multiple locations.

• Contribute to a fast-growing e-commerce fulfillment platform.

• A dynamic environment with end-to-end ownership of infrastructure.
