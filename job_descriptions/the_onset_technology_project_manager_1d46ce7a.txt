Megan Ngo
Megan Ngo
Associate Director | Revenue Operations (Operations, Finance + Sales)
North Sydney, NSW | $160,000 – $190,000 + Super

Own the technology. Drive digital delivery. Make a real impact.

Are you a technically savvy Project Manager who understands how digital platforms work, thrives on solving complex challenges and isn't afraid to roll up your sleeves?

We're partnering wiith an exciting to find an experienced Technical Project Manager who can take ownership of its e-commerce platform, drive technology initiatives and ensure seamless delivery across digital projects.

This isn't your typical Project Manager role.

We're looking for someone who brings genuine technical credibility, strong e-commerce experience and the confidence to challenge requirements, manage external development partners and translate business objectives into practical technology solutions.

If you've previously worked as a Developer, Technical Lead, Technical Product Owner or hands-on Technical Project Manager, this could be the opportunity you've been looking for.

The Opportunity Based in North Sydney, you'll play a pivotal role in managing and enhancing the digital platform, working closely with business stakeholders, external development agencies and technology partners.

You'll be responsible for ensuring projects are technically sound, commercially viable and delivered to a high standard.

With no internal development team, you'll become the critical link between business requirements and technical execution, taking genuine ownership of outcomes rather than simply managing timelines.

What You'll Be Doing


Own digital delivery: Take responsibility for the e-commerce website, its ongoing enhancements and technology delivery initiatives.
Provide technical leadership: Understand APIs, integrations, application architecture and technical dependencies to confidently challenge solutions and identify potential issues.
Manage external development partners: Coordinate agencies, oversee deliverables, challenge estimates and ensure quality, accountability and value.
Drive end-to-end project delivery: Manage requirements, scope, budgets, timelines, risks and stakeholder expectations.
Own release management: Coordinate internal releases, oversee testing, validate functionality and ensure changes meet business and operational requirements.
Translate business needs into solutions: Work with stakeholders to refine requirements, identify improvements and recommend smarter ways of delivering outcomes.
Strengthen delivery practices: Introduce practical structure, governance and processes to improve consistency and execution.
Maintain exceptional quality: Ensure digital releases are accurate, thoroughly tested and delivered with meticulous attention to detail.


What We're Looking For We're looking for a technically mature professional who combines strong delivery capabilities with a genuine understanding of e-commerce technology.

Essential Experience


Proven experience as a Technical Project Manager, Technical Product Owner, Technical Delivery Manager or similar.
Strong background delivering e-commerce platforms, websites or customer-facing digital products.
Solid understanding of software development lifecycles, APIs, integrations and application architecture.
Demonstrated experience managing external software development agencies or technology vendors.
Hands-on experience coordinating testing, UAT, release management and production deployments.
Strong capability in project scoping, budgeting, estimation and stakeholder management.
Confidence to question technical recommendations, challenge assumptions and propose better solutions.
Exceptional attention to detail and a strong sense of accountability.


Highly Regarded


Previous experience as a Software Developer, Technical Lead or Solutions-focused professional.
Experience working in environments with formal governance, compliance or regulatory requirements.
Exposure to both Agile and traditional project delivery methodologies.
Experience balancing technical delivery responsibilities with product ownership.


You don't need to be coding every day, but you do need to understand what good engineering and technical delivery look like.

What Will Make You Successful? You'll be someone who:


Takes ownership rather than waiting for direction.
Can confidently engage with developers and technical specialists.
Understands the bigger picture while never overlooking the finer details.
Is comfortable being hands-on with testing, releases and delivery activities.
Brings practical solutions, sound judgement and a continuous improvement mindset.
Enjoys working in a collaborative, established business environment where quality and reliability matter.


Why Consider This Opportunity?


Genuine ownership: Take responsibility for a customer-facing e-commerce platform and influence how it evolves.
Technical autonomy: Use your experience to shape solutions, challenge thinking and improve delivery outcomes.
Visible business impact: Work on digital initiatives where your contribution makes a tangible difference.
Collaborative environment: Join an established organisation that values maturity, accountability and attention to detail.
Competitive remuneration: Indicative salary of $160,000 – $190,000 + Super, depending on experience.


Ready to Take Ownership? If you're a technically strong Project Manager or Product Owner who wants more than simply tracking project milestones, we'd love to hear from you.

This is an opportunity to combine your technical expertise, commercial judgement and delivery experience in a role where you can genuinely make your mark.
