Key Responsibilities:

Drive Technical / System Requirements Lifecycle from abstract product vision till detailed system / technical requirements
Support Functional Business Analyst (FBA) in translating high level business requirements into tangible, detailed technical requirements for developers, in collaboration with platform architects.
Deliver Functional Specifications Document (FSD) and Master Design Specifications (MDS) that details system interactions, process flows, functional and non-functional specifications, and acceptance criteria for user stories
Support Development and Testing by collaborating with developers and testers by providing the first level of technical clarifications during development and/or testing, review and improve SIT test cases, and conduct Defects Triage together with relevant stakeholders
Document final technical artefacts and ensuring complete traceability to original technical and business requirements
Drive the implementation of agreed functional capability by collaborating with developers.
Refine / elaborate backend stories related to specific systems / platforms based on the agreed technical requirements, ensuring sufficient details and correct acceptance criteria so that developers can estimate and work with them.
Partner with Solution / Domain Architects to ensure detailed design specifications complies with enterprise design principles, security and control standards
Provide status update related to technical delivery to Technical Delivery Manager (TDM) and Project Manager (PM)
Partner with Test Manager to ensure completion of System Integration Testing (SIT), User Acceptance Testing (UAT), performance / load testing
Escalate issues that impact project schedule on a timely basis and propose workarounds/resolutions

Key Skills below –

Strong understanding of SDLC, DevOps practices, and release management processes.
Good understanding of system integration patterns, APIs, microservices, and event-driven architectures.
Familiarity with container platforms (RedHat OCP), Linux OS, Splunk
