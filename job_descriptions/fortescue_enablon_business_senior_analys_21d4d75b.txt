Our Opportunity

Work Location: Perth CBD. Fortescue’s Perth office is located on the traditional lands of the Whadjuk people.

Roster: Monday to Friday.

We’re seeking a Senior Business Analyst to lead analysis across business processes, modules and integrations on the Enablon platform.

Reporting to Manager Group Technology Products, you’ll work with business and delivery teams to understand complex needs, assess process and platform impacts, and shape clear requirements that support effective and sustainable solutions.

Key Responsibilities



Lead analysis for platform enhancements, bringing together business needs, process impacts, Enablon capabilities and delivery constraints.
Work with Process Owners, Functional Leads and users to understand end to end processes, pain points, controls, exceptions and desired outcomes.
Map current and future processes, including roles, hand offs, business rules, data and control points.
Facilitate discovery and document functional and non-functional requirements, user stories, assumptions, dependencies, risks and acceptance test criteria.
Assess impacts across Enablon modules, workflows, forms, roles, data, reports and integrations.
Partner with the Technical Lead and delivery specialists to assess feasibility, security, access, performance, supportability, upgrade impacts and technical dependencies.
Maintain traceability from business needs through requirements, acceptance criteria, testing and delivered outcomes; support backlog refinement, testing, defect triage and business acceptance.
Facilitate user feedback and use service data to identify adoption issues and opportunities to improve usability, data quality and service performance.
Contribute to release readiness, training and adoption activities, and support post release reviews to confirm outcomes and identify follow up actions.



Qualifications And Experience



Demonstrated experience leading business analysis for complex enterprise system changes.
Strong capability in requirements elicitation, process modelling, stakeholder facilitation, options analysis and writing clear, testable acceptance criteria.
Working technical knowledge of Enablon or a comparable enterprise workflow platform, including configuration concepts, data, roles, integrations and reporting.
Experience supporting testing and business acceptance, with the ability to trace needs through to delivered outcomes.
Understanding of Agile practices and experience working in a Scrum team, contributing to refinement, planning, reviews and other team activities alongside the Scrum Master.
Knowledge of health, safety, environmental or compliance processes, such as incidents, audits, risk, actions or obligations, is desirable.
Communicates clearly and works effectively with business stakeholders and technical teams to resolve problems and explain options and trade-offs.
