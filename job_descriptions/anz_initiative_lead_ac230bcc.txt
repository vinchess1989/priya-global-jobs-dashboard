About Us

At ANZ, we're shaping a world where people and communities thrive, driven by a common goal: to improve the financial wellbeing and sustainability of our millions of customers.

About The Role

The SCFE Go-to-Market Initiative Lead orchestrates the end-to-end delivery of Go-to-Market outcomes across SCFE releases. The role connects Value Streams, delivery squads, Product, Technology, PMO and GTM workstreams to ensure scope, sequencing, dependencies and decisions are sufficiently mature to enable customer, colleague and operational readiness. Working in close partnership with the GTM Workstream Leads and Business Readiness Manager, the role converts the SCFE delivery roadmap into an integrated, executable GTM plan and actively manages package-level risks, trade-offs and decisions through activation, stabilisation and transition to BAU.

Banking is changing and we’re changing with it, giving our people great opportunities to try new things, learn and grow. Whatever your role at ANZ, you’ll be building your future, while helping to build ours.

Role Type: Permanent

Role Location: 839 Collins Street, Docklands

Work Hours: Full Time (40 Hours/Weekly)

What will your day look like?

This job description emphasises leadership in agile delivery, stakeholder engagement, risk management, and continuous improvement within a collaborative and dynamic environment at ANZ.

Delivery and Coordination


Experienced in agile methodologies and principles, actively engaging with challenges and complex problems, bringing your value stream, squads and stakeholder with you
Drive and coordinate the timing and delivery pace of features built by multiple engineering squads, managing interdependencies across technology areas, value streams and/or other parties
Close collaboration with product owners and embedded risk, compliance, and legal leads managing end-to-end risk
Utilise data and insights to report performance and continuously improve delivery speed and quality
Promotes a culture of continuous improvement whilst partnering with Delivery Performance Lead and Agile Delivery Leads to strengthen standards and frameworks



Stakeholder Engagement and Team Leadership


Partner with Agile Delivery Leads, Product Owners, and Tech Leads to attain high performance and quality outcomes within squads
Partner with Value and/or Tech Platform Stream Leads to working together on planned outcomes foster unified engagement amongst stakeholders and squads
Inspire Agile Delivery Leads and works closely with Agile Delivery Practice Leads to support their continual upskilling and career development



What will you bring?

Initiative Management


Build, iterate, socialize and visualize the forward plan in line with initiative goals
Identify and manage risks, issues and dependencies with timely mitigation and escalation
Identify systemic initiative risks and issues, and be an active contributor in regular risk forums.



Safely grow, run and iterate our live proposition


Timely production deployments with minimal outage and impact
Timely response and repair of production defects in line with SRE budget & priority



Constantly uplift our delivery capability and team dynamic


Act as a trusted advisor on delivery health, risk and mitigant strategies
Regular review and appraisal of our delivery approach
Partner with Delivery Performance and Agile Practice Leads for continuous refinement.



Team Leadership & Collaboration


Act as a key contact point for initiative performance and escalation management
Provide leadership, guidance and mentorship to leads and other initiative team members
Promote collaboration, knowledge sharing and alignment across business and technology streams
