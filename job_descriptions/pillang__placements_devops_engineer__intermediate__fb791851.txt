DevOps Engineer (Intermediate)
Pillangó Placements
I'm interested
Reference: PTA003957-CB-1

Take your DevOps skills into the world of space technology, working on sophisticated software products used around the globe.

Join a specialist software development business operating in the space technology sector. The team develops sophisticated software products for international customers, working across complex systems and cloud environments.
Duties & Responsibilities
The role
You'll play an important part in managing infrastructure, improving deployment processes and ensuring software systems run reliably, securely and efficiently.

What you'll be doing

Manage cloud infrastructure, deployments and Infrastructure as Code.
Improve CI/CD pipelines, automate monitoring and identify performance bottlenecks.
Monitor vulnerabilities and contribute to system security.
Maintain automated testing pipelines, improve test coverage and support quality releases.
Conduct performance benchmarking and production-scale testing.
Configure and maintain customer deployments, troubleshoot issues and provide first- and second-line support.
Develop and test incident response plans.
Maintain technical documentation and assist with user training.
Collaborate with development teams to improve systems and processes.
What you'll need

Bachelor's degree in IT, Engineering or Computer Science.
At least 2 years' DevOps experience.
Strong analytical, troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
Ability to understand complex systems and translate problems into practical solutions.
Good communication skills and a collaborative approach.
Enthusiasm for technology and continuous learning.
Availability for scheduled after-hours support standby (no more than one week in every four).
Technology environment

Cloud and containers: MicroK8s, Azure, AWS, GCP, Kubernetes, Helm, Operators.
Automation and CI/CD: Jenkins, Git-based pipelines, Bash, Python.
Monitoring and integration: InfluxDB, Grafana, REST interfaces.
Systems and security: Linux, Windows, IAM, container security, vulnerability scanning.
Salary
If you're confident that you fit the role, let's chat! The salary is open for discussion. It will be market related based on qualifications, skills, and experience.
