Elaine Mifsud Western

Join Crane Authentication Malta as a Personalisation Expert and play a key role in supporting our Digital Services and Production operations. Working closely with Engineering, IT, Production and Operational teams, you will help maintain and optimize critical systems, support secure production processes, and drive continuous improvement initiatives across the business.




What you will be doing:

Maintaining variable printing software, applications and hardware interfaces.
Supporting Digital Services applications, databases and production systems.
Managing secure customer data and production workflows.
Creating and optimizing camera inspection models and validation processes.
Producing technical documentation, reports and traceability information.
Supporting new equipment validation, NPI activities and continuous improvement projects.
Providing technical support and training across the business




What we are looking for:

MQF Level 5 qualification in ICT or Electrical Engineering.
Experience with hardware systems and their associated software.
Familiarity with camera-based inspection systems.
Experience integrating hardware and software solutions.
Basic IT troubleshooting skills.
Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.




What's in it for you?

Build your career with us through internal career progression opportunities and annual development reviews.
Be rewarded for your contribution with annual salary reviews and recognition for your achievements.
Invest in your future with dedicated learning and development support to help you grow professionally and personally.
Enjoy flexible work arrangements with a hybrid office and remote working environment
Support your wellbeing through a range of employee wellbeing initiatives and benefits.
Be part of an inclusive culture where everyone is valued, respected and encouraged to succeed.
Connect, engage and have fun through an active Sports & Social Committee that organizes events and activities throughout the year.
Be part of a global organization while making a meaningful impact at a local level.
