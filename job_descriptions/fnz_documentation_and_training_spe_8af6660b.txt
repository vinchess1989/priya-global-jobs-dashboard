We are looking for a Documentation and Training Specialist to play a pivotal role in upskilling existing and incoming FNZers working on our exciting Syn~ Capital Markets platform.

This is a highly influential role at the heart of how we build capability, confidence and quality operators within Capital Markets. You will translate deep product, operational and domain knowledge into best‑in‑class training, documentation and AI‑enabled knowledge solutions that support both internal and external stakeholders around the world.

As FNZ continues to invest in and grow our Capital Markets capability over the coming years, this role will be central to ensuring our people are not just trained but truly enabled to operate at a high standard in complex capital markets environments.

What You’ll Be Doing


Design, author and maintain high‑quality training materials, user guides and operational documentation for the Syn~ Capital Markets platform
Own and continuously improve the Syn~Assist knowledge base, ensuring content is accurate, structured and up to date
Build and maintain AI agents and notebooks that support learning, onboarding and self‑service knowledge access
Work closely with product owners, business analysts, solution consultants and SMEs to capture, validate and curate critical knowledge
Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders globally, supporting clients and teams across multiple regions
Back‑test AI responses with subject matter experts to ensure accuracy, relevance and usability
Design and evolve AI‑enabled training experiences for both internal FNZ teams and external customers
Develop prompt guidance and best practices for effective use of tools such as Copilot and Rovo
Ensure documentation and training stays aligned with product releases and change initiatives
Play a key role in improving time‑to‑competency, helping FNZ scale high‑quality Capital Markets capability as the function grows


What We’re Looking For


5–10 years’ experience in documentation, training, knowledge management or enablement roles
Strong experience structuring complex information into clear, consumable learning artefacts
Understanding of financial services or capital markets concepts
Practical exposure to AI agents, notebooks and prompt design
Excellent written communication and instructional design skills
Strong stakeholder engagement skills across technical and non‑technical audiences
Highly organised, self‑directed and delivery‑focused
Experience with enterprise software platforms and operational workflows
Exposure to AI‑assisted learning or support tools
Familiarity with agile or product‑led delivery models


Why should you join us?


A hybrid 3+2 model combining working from the office and from home.
A competitive salary and excellent benefits, including full comprehensive health insurance, Life insurance, additional annual leave days after your first 12 months, sick days, and more.
Be part of a highly successful, rapidly growing, global business that is leading the delivery of financial services via cloud computing and partners with some of the world’s largest companies.
We provide global career opportunities for our employees at any of our offices in the UK, Czech Republic, Australia, New Zealand, China and more.


If this role appeals to you, please apply as soon as possible. We shortlist as we receive applications and encourage early applications as we may withdraw advertising at any time.

For more information, please reach out to us on apac_recruitment@fnz.com.

About FNZ

FNZ is committed to opening up wealth so that everyone, everywhere can invest in their future on their terms. We know the foundation to do that already exists in the wealth management industry, but complexity holds firms back.

We created wealth’s growth platform to help. We provide a global, end-to-end wealth management platform that integrates modern technology with business and investment operations. All in a regulated financial institution.

We partner with the world’s leading financial institutions, with over US$2.5 trillion in assets on platform (AoP).

Together with our clients, we empower nearly 30 million people across all wealth segments to invest in their future.
