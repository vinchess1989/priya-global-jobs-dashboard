Moog Commercial Aircraft Group is seeking a Senior Systems Engineer.

Moog Commercial Aircraft Group is seeking a Senior Systems Engineer to join our growing business unit supporting the design and development of complex commercial aircraft systems. In this role, you will support requirements management, engineering documentation, and systems integration activities for flight actuation and related commercial aircraft systems. You will report to the Commercial Aircraft Systems and Software Engineering Manager and be based at our Tewkesbury site.

As a Senior Systems Engineer, you will collaborate cross-functionally with software, electronics, test, product, and systems engineering teams to develop next-generation commercial aircraft solutions. You will support activities including requirements management, system architecture development, performance prediction, system analysis, and integration, verification, and validation planning while ensuring engineering solutions meet customer, regulatory, quality, and program requirements.

As a Senior Systems Engineer, you will make critical contributions by holding responsibility for:


Maintain system and subsystem requirements within requirements management tools, ensuring accuracy, completeness, and traceability throughout the product lifecycle.
Create, review, and update requirements while maintaining bidirectional traceability to design, verification, and test artifacts.
Develop and maintain system architectures supporting commercial aircraft flight actuation and related aircraft systems.
Prepare and update specifications, interface control documents (ICDs), design justification documents, and verification matrices in accordance with company configuration management standards.
Perform system analysis and support performance prediction activities to ensure products meet customer and regulatory requirements.
Develop test plans and procedures supporting requirements verification and system qualification activities.
Gather evidence for compliance and prepare verification reports and test summaries.
Support integration, verification, and validation planning throughout the development lifecycle.
Assist in planning, executing, and documenting integration and test activities conducted in laboratory and in-house test environments.
Create test scripts to support automated testing of systems and subsystems.
Record test results and support anomaly investigation, tracking, and closure activities.
Drive successful real-time system model integration to support a variety of system-level and unit-level test environments.
Collaborate across multidisciplinary engineering teams to develop robust and reliable aircraft system solutions.
Support occasional travel as required to customer locations, vendors, and other Moog sites.


To be considered for the Senior Systems Engineer position, here's what you'll typically bring with you:


Bachelor's degree in Systems Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Software Engineering, or a related engineering discipline.
Minimum four (4) years of relevant engineering experience, preferably within aerospace, defense, or another highly regulated industry.
Experience with requirements management, integration and test activities, modeling and simulation methodologies, and control system design and verification.
Proficiency with requirements management tools such as DOORS, DOORS Next, or Polarion.
Familiarity with ARP4754 concepts and safety-critical product development lifecycles.
Experience supporting systems integration, verification, and validation activities.
Familiarity with MATLAB/Simulink for signal analysis, data analysis, modeling, and simulation activities.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and technical decision-making skills.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills with the ability to work effectively across multidisciplinary engineering teams.
Exposure to Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) tools and methodologies is preferred.
Familiarity with SysML or similar system modeling tools is preferred.
Hands-on laboratory and test experience including bench integration, wiring, data acquisition, and instrumentation is preferred.
Experience with configuration management tools such as Teamcenter and issue tracking systems such as JIRA is preferred.
Willingness and ability to travel approximately 5-10% as required.
