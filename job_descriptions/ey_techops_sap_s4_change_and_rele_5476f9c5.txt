The Opportunity

We are seeking a proactive SAP AMS Application Change & Release Management Co-ordinator / Senior to coordinate S/4 Enterprise Landscape releases from planning through production readiness and post-release follow-up. The role brings together application support teams, delivery teams, change management and client stakeholders to ensure that release scope, testing, documentation, approvals, dependencies and risks are visible and managed before the release window.

This is a hands-on coordination and governance position with ITSM model. It is not primarily an SAP development role or a passive PMO/status-reporting role. The successful candidate will own the Change & release-readiness process, drive actions to closure and communicate clear go/no-go status while technical teams remain accountable for their technical deliverables and the client retains final approval authority.

This is a fantastic opportunity to be part of a leading firm whilst being instrumental in the growth as you get to work with high-quality team to support clients ensuring the stability of global 500 companies through best-in-class solutions, automation and innovation.

Your Key Responsibilities

To qualify for the role, you must have:


Bachelor’s degree in related technology field (Computer, Engineering, Science, etc.) or comparable job experiences.
6-7 Years of experience required in one or more areas with SAP Change & Release Management preferably with Global client.


End-to-end release coordination


SAP application Change & release Management Co-ordination for Support team in planned Maintenance cycles.
Coordinate planned, ad hoc and emergency releases across EY SAP application and support teams.
Maintain the integrated release calendar, key milestones, scope and deployment windows.
Drive release activities from initial planning through readiness, deployment coordination, validation and closure.
Responsible for decision-making, optimizing processes, resource management, and overseeing team management as needed for task execution.
Accountable for allocating personnel, supervising team members, assigning tasks, ensuring that the team has the necessary tools and support to succeed in their roles and optimizing and evaluating their performance to meet organizational goals.


Skills And Attributes For Success

Ideally, you’ll also have following skillset


Well versed with SAP Change and Release process in ITSM , with hands on Service Now and JIRA Ticketing tools .
With the criteria below:
7+ years of SAP experience with 5+ years in SAP Change and Release Management.
Strong expertise in commissCommunication and collaborating with team and onshore Client Leadership
Release planning and scope control
Reconcile planned release scope against Jira items, ITSM/change records and agreed delivery commitments.
Confirm owners, target dates, dependencies and readiness criteria for every release item.
Control additions, removals and deferrals so changes to scope are transparent and approved.
Readiness and compliance
Lead structured readiness checkpoints beginning sufficiently ahead of the release window.
Validate testing status and evidence, required documentation, change-record quality, implementation plans, approvals, deployment readiness and rollback or recovery plans where applicable.
Identify incomplete evidence, missing approvals and unresolved dependencies early and escalate through the agreed governance path.
Stakeholder and risk management
Facilitate concise, action-oriented release calls with application teams, L2/L3 support, change management and client stakeholders.
Follow up actively on open actions and challenge incomplete readiness constructively for SAP Maintenance Weekends.
Provide clear status using Ready, At Risk, Blocked, Pending Approval and Deferred classifications.
Governance and continuous improvement
Maintain release dashboards, action logs, decision records and post-release outcomes.
Capture lessons learned and improve checklists, runbooks, templates and release cadence.
Use release trends to identify recurring failure points and recommend practical improvements.

What will you do:


Practical experience in IT release management, release readiness, change enablement or deployment coordination within an enterprise application environment.
Demonstrated ownership of multi-team releases, including scope, milestones, dependencies, risks, approvals and production readiness.
Working experience with SNOW and Jira or a comparable Agile work-management platform.
Working experience with ServiceNow or another enterprise ITSM/change-management platform.
Understanding of release planning, testing governance, change approvals, deployment controls, incident/problem fixes and post-release validation.
Strong written and spoken English, with the ability to communicate clearly with technical teams, delivery leadership and client stakeholders.
Ability to manage multiple concurrent deliverables, follow through on commitments and escalate risks before they affect release dates.
Ability and willingness to provide meaningful overlap with U.S. Eastern Time, including additional coordination during critical release windows.
