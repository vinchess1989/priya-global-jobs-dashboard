For more than 30 years, TradeBeyond has been advancing a more efficient, responsible supply chain. Responding to retail sourcing’s need for smarter, automated workflows and data transparency, we developed the industry’s leading supply chain platform, CBX, which is relied on by many of the largest brands and retailers around the world.




As consumers, businesses, and governments alike have increased their commitment to sustainability, Fortune 500 companies such as The Home Depot, REI, Safeway, Lidl, and Lululemon have turned to TradeBeyond to help optimize product development, manage suppliers, reduce waste, and improve quality and compliance.




Learn more at TradeBeyond.com




About the Role




We are seeking a driven, analytical Product Manager (B2B SaaS) to join our core product team and help evolve our end-to-end enterprise platform. Working closely with senior Product Managers, fellow Product Owners, UX/UI designers and engineering teams, you will translate product direction and high-level requirements into detailed functional designs and development-ready specifications.




You will contribute across our ecosystem of solutions spanning Supplier Management, Supplier Compliance, PLM, Costing, Product Compliance, Order Management, Shipment ASNs, Quality Inspection and Traceability. This is a hands-on product role requiring strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, and the ability to turn complex multi-enterprise processes into intuitive, scalable software solutions.




You will also help shape practical AI-driven features and automated workflows that improve decision-making, identify supply chain risks and simplify work for brands, vendors and factories.




Responsibilities:




Product Direction to Functional Design: Collaborate with senior Product Managers to translate product strategy, roadmap priorities and high-level concepts into detailed solution designs and actionable requirements; constructively challenge assumptions and recommend improvements.
Solution Design & Workflow Modeling: Define end-to-end user journeys, screen behaviors, business processes, data requirements, validation rules, exceptions and dependencies, working closely with UX/UI designers to create coherent experiences across the platform.
Functional Specifications & Backlog: Produce clear functional specifications, Jira epics and user stories, acceptance criteria and supporting documentation; refine and clarify backlog items so engineering teams can implement them effectively.
Cross-Functional Delivery: Partner with Engineering, UX/UI and QA throughout development to explain requirements, resolve functional questions, assess trade-offs and maintain consistency with the intended solution and broader product architecture.
Functional Validation & Quality: Review delivered functionality against intended workflows and acceptance criteria; support QA and user acceptance testing, identify gaps and edge cases, and help resolve issues before release.
Collaborative Discovery: Work with senior Product Managers, enterprise clients and internal stakeholders to understand operational pain points, validate proposed solutions and incorporate practical feedback.
AI & Intelligent Workflows: Collaborate with product and technical teams to incorporate AI-driven insights, recommendations and automation into useful enterprise workflows, defining understandable behavior and functional requirements.
Compliance & Enterprise Context: Research and translate relevant sourcing, trade, quality and regulatory requirements into product rules and workflows, with support from domain experts where needed.
Iterative Improvement: Contribute to backlog refinement and scope decisions with senior Product Managers and engineering leads and use customer feedback and observed product behavior to propose improvements.




Requirements




Experience: 5+ years in Product Ownership, Product Management, Business Analysis or Functional Consulting within B2B SaaS or enterprise software, with demonstrable involvement in detailed feature design and agile delivery.
Domain Understanding: Familiarity with at least one of retail sourcing, supplier management, manufacturing operations, PLM, compliance, order execution or quality management; ability to learn adjacent domains quickly.
Functional Design & Analysis: Strong ability to decompose complex workflows, identify business rules, exceptions and data dependencies, and produce clear specifications, user stories and acceptance criteria.
Tools & Delivery: Practical experience working with Jira or comparable agile backlog tools and collaborating day to day with software engineering, UX/UI and QA teams.
Technical Acumen: Comfort discussing enterprise data models, APIs, integrations and technical constraints with engineers; no hands-on coding requirement.
Problem-Solving & Systems Thinking: Ability to simplify legacy or spreadsheet-heavy processes into intuitive, consistent and scalable solutions while considering wider platform impact.
Communication & Collaboration: Strong written and verbal communication skills, good judgement, attention to detail and confidence working with global stakeholders and cross-functional teams.
AI & Innovation: Interest in practical AI-enabled features and intelligent workflow automation; prior hands-on experience is an advantage, not a prerequisite.
Education: Bachelor’s degree in supply chain management, Computer Science, Engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
