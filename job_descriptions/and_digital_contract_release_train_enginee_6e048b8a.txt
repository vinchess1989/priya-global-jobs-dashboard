Release Train Engineer, 13 months, London/Hybrid

We help organisations navigate the future of technology, combining human expertise, emerging tech and AI to deliver better outcomes, faster.

Since 2014, we've worked side-by-side with clients to solve complex challenges, build high-performing teams, and create lasting capability.

About You


You care deeply about producing high-quality work that delivers real value
You're comfortable navigating ambiguity and solving complex problems collaboratively
You bring strong expertise in your craft, alongside a willingness to keep learning
You communicate clearly and build trust quickly with clients and teammates
You're pragmatic, adaptable and outcome-focused
You value low-ego collaboration and enjoy working as part of multidisciplinary teams


The Role

Agile Delivery and Release Train Leadership


Facilitate and coordinate the Agile Release Train (ART), ensuring alignment and consistent delivery of value.
Lead PI Planning, System Demos, Inspect and Adapt workshops and programme-level ceremonies.
Manage cross-team dependencies, priorities and delivery plans.
Coach teams, Product Owners, Scrum Masters and leaders in Agile and Lean practices.
Drive continuous improvement using delivery metrics and programme-level reporting.


Release Management and Deployment


Own and coordinate the end-to-end release lifecycle, from planning through to production deployment and post-release validation.
Maintain an integrated release calendar, coordinating deployment schedules and business priorities.
Lead release planning and execution meetings across DevOps, Engineering, QA, Architecture and operational teams.
Coordinate complex, integrated releases across multiple applications, platforms and environments.
Facilitate production readiness reviews and Go/No-Go decisions.
Ensure appropriate testing, approvals, security clearances and operational sign-offs are completed before deployment.


Governance, Risk and Environment Management


Identify and manage delivery risks, impediments, dependencies and deployment bottlenecks.
Coordinate the readiness and synchronisation of development, test, UAT and production environments.
Support effective CI/CD processes, automated testing and consistent deployment practices.
Ensure releases meet agreed Definition of Done, quality and governance standards.
Work with Architecture, Cyber Security and operational teams to maintain compliance and production stability.


Stakeholder Management and Continuous Improvement


Act as the central coordination point between delivery teams, business stakeholders, technology leaders and external partners.
Provide clear visibility of delivery progress, release readiness, risks and dependencies.
Influence and support decision-making across technical and business teams.
Promote consistent ways of working, engineering excellence and the adoption of value-driving technologies.
Drive improvements in delivery predictability, release efficiency and quality.


What You'll Bring To The Table


Strong understanding of Agile, Lean and SAFe principles and practices.
Proven experience coordinating large-scale, multi-team delivery and release activities.
Expertise in PI Planning, programme-level ceremonies and dependency management.
Strong understanding of software delivery lifecycles, release management and DevOps practices.
Working knowledge of CI/CD pipelines, automated testing and deployment processes.
Experience with tools such as Jira, Azure DevOps and Jenkins.
Understanding of release governance, production readiness, risk management and environment management.
Strong stakeholder management, communication and influencing skills.
Ability to interpret delivery metrics and drive continuous improvement.
