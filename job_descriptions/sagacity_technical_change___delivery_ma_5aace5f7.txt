Sagacity is in a major phase of growth and key to this is continuing to meet the change demands of the solutions we deliver for our clients. As Technical Change and Delivery Manager, you will own the processes that let us move at pace and with control across our IT landscape (change control, defect resolution and compliance) to deliver value for our clients. This will be underpinned by owning the governance and planning that makes all three tick. Alongside this you will lead the delivery of our internal change programme, from tooling rollouts to security and migration work.

This is a role with real ownership and visibility. You will work directly with our Head of IT, Head of Service Operations, PMO Manager and delivery leads across the business, with the mandate to shape how these processes run - not just administer them.




Responsibilities




Technical Change, Defects & Compliance

Own the end-to-end Change Control Notice (CCN) process for our clients' live solutions - from change design and estimation through to development, testing and deployment
Own defect resolution end-to-end, coordinating triage, prioritisation, routing and resolution within SLA, with root causes fixed so issues do not recur
Support ISO 27001 and Cyber Essentials Plus compliance activity with the Head of IT - tracking remediation actions to closure and capturing audit-ready evidence




Internal Change Delivery

Lead internal IT projects from initiation to closure, with the efficiency and control to deliver the benefits case
Run delivery cadences and ceremonies that keep teams focused and value flowing
Lead by unblocking issues, protect our teams' focus, manage the backlog and clear obstacles before they impact delivery




Resource Planning & Governance

Forecast demand and balance resource across change, defect and compliance work so commitments are consistently met
Maintain a clear portfolio view, with weekly reporting on status, risks and issues that feeds our wider business reporting
Improve our governance, processes and tooling, and coach the team on agile ways of working




What Success Looks Like In The Role

Change flows through the business predictably, with timely reporting into local and wider business governance to ensure the picture is always clear
Defects are resolved quickly, within SLA and with control to ensure first time resolution and recurring issues are the exception
Compliance actions are closed promptly and evidenced end-to-end, so we are consistently audit-ready
Our teams have a sustainable cadence, clarity on priorities and the support to do their best work
Internal projects are delivered on time, unlocking value as they go and providing long-term improvement to our teams' capability
Identify improvements to our processes that drive efficiency through practical, well-adopted changes to tooling and ways of working
Build strong relationships within the IT team and wider business, providing knowledge transfer and peer reviews to contribute to a culture of continuous improvement




Competencies & Behaviours




Experience And Knowledge

5+ years in project or delivery management, ideally in a consultancy, data or technology services business
Proven ownership of change control or equivalent commercial governance processes in a technical environment
Service management grounding (ITIL or equivalent)
Experience of internal IT or transformation projects - tooling rollouts, security programmes, migrations
Familiarity with ISO 27001, Cyber Essentials or SOC 2
A recognised delivery qualification - Prince2, APM PMQ, PMP, or a Scrum/agile equivalent




Delivery and Leadership

Strong resource and capacity planning across competing priorities
Strong governance and risk management with the judgement to apply proportionate controls without creating unnecessary bureaucracy
Excellent prioritisation and decision-making, able to balance competing client, operational, compliance and internal change demands
Commercially aware, with an understanding of how change control, scope, resource and delivery decisions impact clients
Credible with technical, commercial and executive stakeholders
Able to influence without authority and drive action across teams
Strong problem-solving and root-cause analysis, resolving underlying issues rather than repeatedly managing symptoms
Comfortable operating in ambiguity, able to bring structure and momentum to projects where requirements, priorities or dependencies are evolving
Continuous improvement mindset, with a track record of simplifying processes and embedding ways of working that teams actually adopt




Tools and Ways of Working

Hands-on experience with Scrum, Kanban and traditional project methodologies
Comfortable with project and agile tooling: Jira, ClickUp, MS Project, Smartsheet or equivalent
Confident with Microsoft 365 (Excel, PowerPoint, SharePoint, Teams) for project artefacts and reporting
Highly numerate, with strong attention to detail
Solutions-oriented, with natural problem-solving instincts
Organised and pro-active - you prioritise well and stay calm under pressure
An excellent communicator, written and verbal, comfortable influencing others
A team player: personable, adaptable and genuinely curious
Committed to your own development, and to helping others grow
Applicants must have the right to work in the UK. Some UK travel may be required.
