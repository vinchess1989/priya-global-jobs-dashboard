About The Role

The Configuration Manager - OCS is responsible for managing changes affecting Operational Control Systems, ensuring changes are planned, controlled, documented and implemented effectively while maintaining configuration integrity. The role ensures changes are appropriately assessed, documented, governed and implemented, while maintaining the integrity of configuration information and supporting ongoing improvements to change management practices.

Working with a broad range of stakeholders, you'll coordinate change activities, facilitate governance processes, support project delivery and help ensure business risks are effectively managed.

Key accountabilities include:


Lead the Change Control Advisory Board, driving effective governance and decision-making for Operational Control Systems.
Coordinate change activities across projects, engineering, maintenance and operational stakeholders.
Facilitate change governance, approval and compliance processes.
Maintain accurate configuration records, databases and asset information.
Support the administration and improvement of the ServiceNow change management process.
Monitor and support adherence to risk management and change control requirements.
Contribute to the successful delivery of projects by managing operational impacts and change-related activities.
Identify and implement opportunities to improve change management and data management practices.
Prepare and maintain documentation to support governance, traceability and decision-making.
Foster stakeholder engagement and support for change initiatives.



About You

You're an organised and collaborative professional who enjoys bringing people together to achieve shared outcomes. You understand the importance of effective governance and change management and can balance operational requirements, stakeholder needs and business objectives to deliver practical solutions.

Our ideal candidate will possess the following:


Demonstrated experience coordinating and embedding change initiatives, system improvements or projects within operational, technology, infrastructure or asset management environments.
Strong understanding of change control, governance, risk and compliance processes, with the ability to ensure changes are effectively documented, assessed and implemented.
Proven ability to build stakeholder engagement, facilitate discussions and influence outcomes across diverse stakeholder groups.
Experience maintaining configuration information, records and databases, with a high level of accuracy and attention to detail.
Strong planning, coordination and organisational skills, with the ability to manage competing priorities and support successful delivery outcomes.
Practical problem-solving skills and sound judgement, with the ability to balance operational impacts, risk and business requirements.
A continuous improvement mindset, with a proven ability to identify opportunities and implement more effective ways of working.
Demonstrated alignment to V/Line’s values of responsiveness, integrity, impartiality, accountability, respect, leadership and human rights.



Highly Regarded


Experience using ServiceNow or similar IT service management, workflow or ticketing systems; exposure to ITIL principles.
Experience within rail, transport, infrastructure, asset-intensive or technology environments.
Background in Operational Technology (OT) environments would be highly regarded.



If your skills or experience don’t exactly match our listed requirements, we still encourage you to apply. We value diverse perspectives and believe in the potential for growth and development.
