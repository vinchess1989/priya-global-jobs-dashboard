Designation – R&D Technical Documentation Specialist (Associate Manager)




Experience:

Technical Writing & Documentation Management

Master’s degree in quality assurance, Regulatory Affairs, or related field preferred.
9 to 11 years of experience in regulated medical device environments.
Experience supporting product development from concept through commercialization.
Experience with ISO 13485, ISO 14971, EU MDR 2017/745, Design Controls, Technical Documentation, CAPA Systems, and Change Control processes.
Experience supporting international regulatory submissions preferred.
