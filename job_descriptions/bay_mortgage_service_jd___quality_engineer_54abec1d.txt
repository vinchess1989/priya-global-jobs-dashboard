Support the continual development, improvement, implementation and maintenance of the organization’s Information Security Management System (ISMS) and Quality Management System (QMS).
Define, implement, review and maintain the organizational quality & information security policies, processes, procedures and controls in line with ISO 27001:2013, ISO 13485:2016 standards.
Responsible for document and record control, review, issue, retrieval & superseding of all SOP and other.
Collaborate with internal stakeholders for implementing controls and processes for respective functions and ensure continuous operating effectiveness of the defined procedures.
Awareness of Medical Device standards & regulations - ISO 13485, ISO 14971, IEC 62304, SaMD, IEC 82304, CE, 21 CFR 820 – Design Controls, 510(k), MDR, TGA ARGMD and others.
Provides expertise and guidance in interpreting policies, regulatory and/or governmental regulations, and internal regulations to assure compliance.
Conduct / participate in Internal and External Audits.
Coordinate and ensure timely closure of audit findings.
Initiate and moderate the root cause analysis and monitor effective and timely implementation of CAPA.
Analyze data to identify trends and opportunities for improvement on quality aspects.
Conduct Information Security / Quality Management System Awareness and Training programs for employees as a part of their induction and regular awareness.
Support on process improvement and continuous improvement activities.
Drive process compliance in the complete lifecycle process of the medical device, mainly Software.
Collaborate and consult product team members in implementing and realizing the processes.
Collaborate and coordinate with cross functional team to review technical documents.
Ensures product compliance with legal and normative requirements.
Drive best practices within the organization
