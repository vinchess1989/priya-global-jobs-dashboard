The opportunity

This position will fulfil the responsibilities of a Subject Matter Resource (SMR), entrusted with executing the implementation, enablement and adoption of Adobe capabilities within Global Talent, including Adobe Experience Manager (AEM), Workfront and Digital Asset Management (DAM).

The role will play a key part in end-to-end delivery, from requirements gathering and system configuration through to testing and implementation, partnering with Enterprise Technology, Adobe, Talent Functions and the Global Talent Content-as-a-Service (CaaS) programme team.

As part of this role, the consultant will ensure implementation designs are future-focused, scalable and incorporate the end-to-end processes necessary to facilitate the content lifecycle. The role will support content management, metadata, workflows, content migration and business readiness activities required to successfully deploy Adobe capabilities across Talent.

Your Key Responsibilities

Essential functions of the job


Support project workstreams, bringing input and experience from Adobe platform implementations.
Partner with stakeholders to gather, validate and refine business and functional requirements.
Support the configuration, development, testing and implementation of Adobe capabilities to deliver efficiencies aligned to functional requirements.
Monitor dependencies, risks, assumptions and decisions throughout implementation.
Work with vendors and internal teams to provide feedback on release issues, defects and incidents.
Work closely with project workstreams, Enterprise Technology and the PMO to ensure a successful transition from development to business as usual (BAU), including testing, hypercare and documentation activities.
Support implementation of content management processes, workflows, metadata and content lifecycle requirements across Workfront, AEM and DAM.
Support content migration activities and associated validation and readiness activities.
Lead and coordinate testing activities, including QA, SIT, UAT and release validation, ensuring solutions are delivered as defined.
Contribute to training, communications and enablement activities to support successful implementation and adoption.
Collaborate across Global Talent, Enterprise Technology, Talent teams and vendors to deliver solutions that improve the employee experience and drive business value.


Analytical / Decision Making Responsibilities


Provide expertise and knowledge in Adobe solution enablement and implementation, supporting decisions that drive effective delivery outcomes.
Understand the Adobe landscape in conjunction with Talent strategy, business requirements and operational needs.
Evaluate process and control gaps across the end-to-end content lifecycle (create, review, publish and retire content) and recommend practical improvements.
Support go/no-go decisions for releases by reviewing readiness criteria, including UAT results, open defects, training readiness, documentation completeness and support readiness.
Advise on governance requirements for content ownership, access, approvals and publishing workflows to support quality, compliance and efficient delivery.
Assess and prioritize enhancement requests by considering impact, effort, dependencies and risk, providing recommendations to project stakeholders and the PMO.
Evaluate opportunities to streamline processes, improve user experience and increase automation through Adobe platform capabilities.


Skills And Attributes For Success

To qualify for the role you must have


Experience with Adobe Experience Manager (AEM), Adobe Workfront, Digital Asset Management (DAM) or related Adobe capabilities.
Strong business acumen and ability to understand platform functionality, integrations and business requirements.
Experience with requirements gathering, testing and implementation activities.
Knowledge of content management practices and content lifecycle processes.
Knowledge of taxonomy, metadata and content architecture concepts.
Understanding of search optimisation and content discoverability principles.
Strong consulting skills with the ability to interpret business and technology requirements and develop pragmatic solutions.
Strong analytical, research and problem-solving skills.
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills.
Strong teaming skills and ability to collaborate effectively across global and virtual teams.
Strong organisational skills and attention to detail, with the ability to work within budget and effective timeframes.
Experience working in Agile delivery environments.
Significant ability to cope with ambiguity and drive outcomes in a complex and fast-paced environment.


What We Look For


Experience delivering Adobe Digital Experience Platform capabilities.
Expertise in content management strategy and best practices.
Demonstrable experience collaborating with stakeholders to understand business needs and requirements.
Experience supporting content migration, metadata and content management activities.
Experience participating in global, geographically dispersed teams to enhance services, processes and standards.
Experience conducting research and analysis to identify opportunities and drive improvements.
Demonstrable experience of working in fast-paced, ambiguous environments to deliver successful outcomes.
Demonstrable experience anticipating issues and challenges and proactively working to navigate them.
Demonstrable experience leading or providing subject matter expertise to support successful change outcomes.
Experience supporting implementation of content structures that enable reusable, searchable and AI-ready content.
