DevOps Engineer

I'm interested
Reference: CTI007282-TF-1

Keep systems running, deployments seamless, and innovation moving.
We're continuously partnering with innovative technology companies, fintechs, SaaS businesses, consultancies, and enterprise organisations seeking skilled DevOps Engineers to bridge the gap between development and operations. If you're passionate about automation, cloud technologies, CI/CD pipelines, and building reliable, scalable infrastructure, we'd love to connect with you.
Duties & Responsibilities

Key Responsibilities:

Design, implement, and maintain cloud infrastructure
Build and optimise CI/CD pipelines
Automate deployment, monitoring, and operational processes
Improve system reliability, scalability, and security
Collaborate with development teams to streamline software delivery
Monitor infrastructure performance and resolve production issues
Implement infrastructure and security best practices
 
Job Experience Required:

Minimum 3+ years’ of experience in DevOps, Cloud Engineering, Site Reliability Engineering, or Infrastructure Engineering
Degree or Diploma in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field
Hands-on experience with AWS, Azure, GCP, Kubernetes, Docker, Terraform, Ansible, Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, GitHub Actions, or ArgoCD
Strong knowledge of Linux administration, networking, monitoring, and troubleshooting
Experience building and maintaining CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
Proficiency with Infrastructure as Code (IaC) methodologies and tools
Experience working with Git, version control best practices, and Agile development teams
Exposure to monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, ELK Stack, Datadog, or New Relic
 
This is an opportunity to join our talent pool and be considered for future opportunities with leading organisations across South Africa.
