The Position

The Marketing Operations, Compliance, and Content Management team is dedicated to driving operational excellence and safeguarding content integrity across the enterprise. Working in close partnership across Marketing Compliance, Creative, Legal, Medical, and external agency partners, the department ensures digital assets and commercial materials are governed, fully compliant, and seamlessly accessible to power global marketing initiatives.

The Content Librarian Lead is responsible for the end-to-end administration, governance, and optimization of both the enterprise Digital Asset Management (DAM) platform and the Medical Legal Regulatory (MLR) / Content Approval system. Partnering closely with the marketing compliance manager, this operational role ensures the organization utilizes these platforms to their maximum potential by maintaining data integrity, managing user access, overseeing the content lifecycle, and ensuring all assets are compliant and discoverable.

The Opportunity


Work with the Marketing Operations Manager for Vault and Content Lab to establish and enforce global taxonomy, metadata standards, and naming conventions to ensure discoverability of assets and maximize metadata accuracy
Manage the integration points between the DAM and the content approval system, ensuring approved, compliant assets are available for use while maintaining a compliance rate of zero expired or unapproved assets in live campaigns
Act as the primary gatekeeper for system access—granting, revoking, and auditing user permissions and security groups in accordance with service-level agreements (SLAs)
Monitor asset expiration dates and usage rights to mitigate legal risks associated with expired or restricted content
Design and deliver comprehensive training programs for internal teams and external agencies to ensure proper system adoption, while driving active user growth and asset reuse across the enterprise
Identify bottlenecks in content upload and approval workflows, streamline processes where applicable, and audit the platform to ensure assets are reused efficiently to reduce production duplication and waste
Serve as the primary subject matter expert and first point of contact for troubleshooting, user support, and "how-to" queries related to the Digital Asset Management and Content Approval platforms within defined support SLAs


Who You Are


5+ years of martech operations experience managing a DAM and/or MLR systems
Strong expertise in metadata, taxonomy, information architecture, and digital asset lifecycle management
Rigorous attention to detail and commitment to data integrity, ensuring assets are correctly tagged and compliant with regulatory standards
Strong communication and training capabilities with the ability to translate technical system requirements into simple, actionable guidance for non-technical marketing users
Process-oriented mindset with a proven ability to manage complex workflows involving multiple cross-functional stakeholders, including Creative, Legal, Medical, and Marketing
