DevOps Engineer




We are hiring a DevOps Engineer to join a global investment firm in Singapore. This is a hands-on role focused on cloud infrastructure, automation and supporting scalable, reliable environments.




Key Responsibilities

Design, manage and optimise cloud infrastructure.
Build and maintain Infrastructure as Code (IaC) using Terraform or Pulumi.
Develop and improve CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation.
Work closely with development teams to improve infrastructure reliability and delivery processes.
Implement cloud security, monitoring and infrastructure best practices.




Requirements

5+ years of experience in DevOps, Cloud or Platform Engineering.
Hands-on cloud experience, preferably Microsoft Azure.
Strong experience with Terraform or Pulumi.
Proficiency in CI/CD tools, scripting and automation.
Good understanding of networking, Git and SQL/NoSQL databases.
Strong troubleshooting skills with the ability to work independently.




Salary: SGD 120,000 – 144,000 Base Per Annum + Bonus & Benefits
