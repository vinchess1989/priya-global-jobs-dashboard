About the company
Our client is a leading global medical technology company with a strong presence across international markets. Recognised for its innovative diagnostic solutions, the organisation is committed to advancing healthcare through technology, quality and regulatory excellence.

About the opportunity
An exciting opportunity is available for an experienced Regulatory Affairs Specialist to join a global medical technology organisation on an initial 12-month contract.
Based in Sydney, this role will support key regulatory projects across Australia, APAC and Europe, with a particular focus on the transition from IVDD to IVDR requirements. Working closely with regional Regulatory Affairs teams, you will play a hands-on role in managing regulatory changes, supporting product compliance and ensuring ongoing alignment with regulatory requirements across multiple markets. This opportunity would suit a proactive Regulatory Affairs professional with experience in IVDs who can confidently manage regulatory activities with minimal supervision.

Duties
• Support the transition of IVD products from IVDD to IVDR requirements
• Coordinate and execute regulatory change projects across APAC markets
• Support ongoing regulatory maintenance and product updates
• Prepare, review and maintain regulatory documentation and submissions
• Ensure compliance with applicable Australian and international regulatory requirements
• Work closely with regional and global Regulatory Affairs stakeholders
• Support Australia-specific regulatory projects and activities as required
• Monitor regulatory changes and assess their impact on existing product portfolios
• Contribute to regulatory project timelines, deliverables and reporting

Skills and Experience
• Tertiary qualifications in Science, Biomedical Science, Engineering or a related discipline
• Previous Regulatory Affairs experience within the medical device or IVD industry
• Strong understanding of Australian regulatory requirements, including TGA regulations
• Experience with IVD regulations, particularly IVDD and IVDR, highly regarded
• Experience managing regulatory changes, submissions and product maintenance activities
• Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple projects and deadlines
• Excellent communication skills and confidence engaging with international stakeholders
• Proactive, adaptable and capable of working independently in a fast-paced environment

Culture
Join a collaborative, internationally connected team that values innovation, accountability and continuous improvement.
