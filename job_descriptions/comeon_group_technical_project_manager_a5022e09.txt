Technical Project Manager

St. Julian’s, Malta | Hybrid | Indefinite




Ready to make your next move count?

At ComeOn Group, we’re all about creating great entertainment experiences. Powered by our own technology, driven by people who love what they do, and always looking for ways to make things better. With 600+ team members working across Europe, we’re big enough to make an impact and connected enough to make it together.




We're looking for a Technical Project Manager to join our Service Delivery team in Malta. In this role, you will help close the gap between business needs and technology delivery by owning the technical delivery of key projects—from cross-functional regulatory and compliance launches to tech-led automation, platform, and infrastructure changes.




What You’ll Do

Here is where you'll make your mark:




Drive Technical Ownership & Milestones: Own the plan, milestones, dependencies, and baselines for your workstreams and technology projects, keeping them current as things change.
Translate Requirements into Deliverables: Turn regulatory, business, and technical requirements into clear deliverables with owners, dates, and acceptance criteria, collaborating with Product, Engineering, Data, and QA.
Lead Cross-Functional Collaboration & Decision-Making: Lead project and working-group meetings with confidence, including with senior stakeholders, driving decisions and action items to conclusion.
Manage Risk & Mitigations: Maintain RAIDD logs, raise risks and issues early, and keep owners and mitigations visible in our in-house tracker.
Report Progress & Coordinate Releases: Track and report progress honestly with weekly status updates, explain slippage with clear proposals, and coordinate releases, migrations, audit, or regulatory submissions.
Guide & Mentor Teammates: Escalate blockers early with actionable options, while supporting and guiding junior project managers to elevate how we plan and deliver.




What You'll Bring to the Team

You know your craft, take pride in what you do and enjoy finding better ways to get things done. You bring experience, fresh thinking and the confidence to take ownership, while knowing that great results come from sharing ideas, challenging each other and working together.




Core Experience: At least 3 years of project management experience delivering technology projects or technical workstreams to dates, ideally in a software or digital setting.
Technical & Tool Expertise: Strong, meticulous planning skills with a track record of keeping plans, dependencies, and RAIDD logs up to date in project tracking software (experience in Agile environments, Jira, or project management certifications like PRINCE2, PMP, CAPM is a plus).
Domain Knowledge & Problem Solving: Solid understanding of how software is built, tested, released, and supported, with the confidence to run meetings, challenge constructively, and translate technical matters for non-technical colleagues (exposure to iGaming or another regulated industry like Fintech/Telecoms is a bonus).
AI & Emerging Tech (Bonus): Experience with artificial intelligence tools, machine learning workflows, or AI-assisted project management practices is a plus.
Run Together: You share knowledge openly, support teammates without ego, earn trust across multiple teams through presence and usefulness, and collaborate to win as one team.
Show the Way: You question routine ways of working, spot inefficiencies in processes, and bring fresh thinking and clear facts rather than settling for default solutions.
Make It Happen: You take clear ownership of delivery, unblock challenges pragmatically, make it clear who is doing what by when, and deliver dependable results.




Don’t tick every box? If the role excites you and you believe you could make a meaningful difference, we’d still love to hear from you.




Life at ComeOn

Great work, great people, and plenty to enjoy along the way. We take our work to heart, but we don't take ourselves too seriously. We’re a community of curious minds, problem-solvers, and people who genuinely enjoy working together.




The benefits of being part of ComeOn




Hybrid Flexibility: Enjoy a flexible split between home and our Spinola office, plus up to 40 days per year to work remotely from anywhere in the world.
Competitive Package: Attractive salary, eligibility for a bonus scheme, and a one-time workstation setup bonus.
Health & Well-being: Comprehensive international private health insurance and an annual wellbeing allowance.
Office Perks & Social Life: Fresh catered lunches, our very own barista station, drinks and nibbles, and regular team celebrations.
Team Activities & Events: Quarterly team outings and regular company culture events.
Culture & Growth: A collaborative, innovative environment with a modern tech stack, opportunities to grow, and an international community of employees representing 40+ nationalities across 6 countries.
