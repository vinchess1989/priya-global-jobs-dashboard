DevOps Engineer – Healthcare Claims Platform

Location: Singapore

Experience: 3+ Years

Job Description

We are seeking a hands-on DevOps Engineer to support a national healthcare claims platform in Singapore. The role involves managing secure and scalable AWS cloud infrastructure, CI/CD automation, Kubernetes environments, and monitoring systems to ensure high availability, reliability, and security of healthcare applications.

Key Responsibilities

Design and maintain AWS cloud infrastructure using Terraform/Terragrunt and EKS Fargate.
Build and manage GitLab CI/CD pipelines with automated security and vulnerability scanning.
Implement GitOps deployment workflows using Argo CD, Helm, and Canary/Blue-Green strategies.
Manage containerized microservices using Docker and Kubernetes.
Configure monitoring, logging, and distributed tracing using Prometheus, Grafana, CloudWatch, OpenTelemetry, and Jaeger.
Automate infrastructure and operational tasks using Shell, Python, and Ansible.
Troubleshoot production issues, manage incidents, and ensure infrastructure security and compliance.
Collaborate with development, QA, and operations teams in an Agile environment.

Mandatory Skills

3+ years of DevOps / Cloud Infrastructure experience.
Strong hands-on experience with AWS, Kubernetes (EKS Fargate), Docker, and Terraform/Terragrunt.
Experience with GitLab CI/CD, Argo CD, and Helm.
Knowledge of security scanning tools such as Trivy, GitLab SAST, and AWS Inspector.
Experience with monitoring and observability tools: Prometheus, Grafana, and CloudWatch.
Strong scripting skills in Shell/Bash and Python.
Mandatory: Prior experience in the healthcare domain, preferably healthcare claims processing or healthcare insurance platforms.
Experience supporting secure, regulated, or government healthcare environments is highly preferred.

Preferred

Singapore Government Cloud (GCC 2.0) and SHIP-HATS 2.0 experience.
Knowledge of healthcare claims workflows and backend microservices.
Familiarity with SRE practices, DORA metrics, and performance testing.
