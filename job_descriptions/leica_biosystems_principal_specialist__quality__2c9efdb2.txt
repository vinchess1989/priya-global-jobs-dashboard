The Principal Specialist, Quality Assurance (Design) is responsible for providing senior Design Quality Assurance leadership across complex in vitro diagnostic and medical device development and lifecycle programmes. The role partners with cross-functional teams to ensure design controls, quality management, risk management, verification and validation, design transfer and regulatory requirements are effectively integrated, with objective evidence supporting safe, effective and compliant products.

This position reports to the Manager, Quality Assurance (Design) and is part of the Design Quality Assurance team located in Newcastle upon Tyne, UK and will be an on-site role.

In This Role, You Will Have The Opportunity To


Provide independent, risk-based Design Quality Assurance leadership across complex IVD and medical device development and lifecycle programmes, ensuring appropriate application of design controls throughout the product lifecycle.
Partner with Research & Development, Regulatory Affairs, Clinical Affairs, New Product Introduction, Manufacturing and Quality teams to translate product and regulatory requirements into robust design, risk-management and quality deliverables.
Lead and challenge product risk-management activities, including the adequacy and traceability of hazards, risk controls, design outputs and verification and validation evidence.
Provide quality oversight of verification and validation strategies, protocols and reports, including acceptance criteria, methods, sample-size rationale, traceability and objective evidence.
Support design transfer, commercialisation and lifecycle changes by ensuring development outputs, design history documentation and quality evidence are complete, traceable and suitable for implementation and release.


The Essential Requirements Of The Job Include


Bachelor’s or master’s degree in a scientific, medical, engineering or other relevant technical discipline.
Long-standing experience with increasing responsibility in Quality Assurance, Design Assurance or Design Control within the in vitro diagnostic (IVD) or medical device industry, including support for product development throughout the lifecycle.
Demonstrated working knowledge of ISO 13485 quality management systems and medical device/IVD design-control requirements.
Demonstrated experience applying ISO 14971 risk-management principles, including traceability between hazards, risk controls, design outputs and verification and validation evidence.
Demonstrated knowledge and practical application of EU IVDR and FDA requirements for IVD or medical device development; familiarity with other applicable global regulations and standards is advantageous.


Travel, Motor Vehicle Record & Physical/Environment Requirements


Ability to travel up to 10%, including occasional international travel.


It would be a plus if you also possess previous experience in:


IVD assay and/or companion diagnostic development, including design transfer and commercialisation in collaboration with New Product Introduction and Manufacturing teams.
Experience with additional global regulations and standards applicable to IVD and medical devices, such as UK MDR, Health Canada requirements, or relevant international market requirements.
The use, assessment or oversight of artificial intelligence-enabled tools, software or products in a regulated medical device or IVD environment.
