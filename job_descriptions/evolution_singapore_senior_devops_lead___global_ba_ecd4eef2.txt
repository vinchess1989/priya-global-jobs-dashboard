DevOps Lead — Financial Markets | Global Bank




A global bank is seeking a hands-on DevOps Lead to lead a team supporting its Financial Markets business. This role suits someone who combines strong technical expertise with a clear bias for action—removing bottlenecks, making decisions and accelerating delivery.

The role

Lead and develop a DevOps team, setting clear priorities and driving execution.
Experience integrating AI into the DevOps process
Build and improve cloud infrastructure using Microsoft Azure, Kubernetes and Terraform.
Strengthen CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure as code and automation to make releases faster and more reliable.
Work closely with engineering and Financial Markets stakeholders to translate business priorities into delivery.
Maintain production resilience, security and operational controls while increasing the pace of change.




Your background

Strong hands-on experience with Azure, Kubernetes and Terraform.
Previous financial services experience, ideally supporting financial markets or trading technology.
Proven experience managing a team and taking ownership of delivery.
A strong understanding of CI/CD, cloud infrastructure, container orchestration and production operations.
A pragmatic, decisive approach, with evidence of shortening delivery cycles and improving engineering productivity.




We’re looking for a leader who stays close to the technology, builds momentum and turns priorities into working solutions quickly.
