Vantive is a vital organ therapy company on a mission to extend lives and expand possibilities for patients and care teams everywhere. For 70 years, our team has driven meaningful innovations in kidney care. As we build on our legacy, we are deepening our commitment to elevating the dialysis experience through digital solutions and advanced services, while looking beyond kidney care and investing in transforming vital organ therapies. Greater flexibility and efficiency in therapy administration for care teams, and longer, fuller lives for patients— that is what Vantive aspires to deliver.

We believe Vantive will not only build our leadership in the kidney care space, it will also offer meaningful work to those who join us. Join us in advancing our mission to extend lives and expand possibilities.

The Mgr, Data Enablement Platform is accountable for the governance, operating model, adoption, lifecycle, and value realization of assigned enterprise data, AI, automation, and workflow platforms. The role establishes platform direction, defines decision rights and standards, owns governance outcomes, and drives measurable business value. Working with business stakeholders, BRMs, Data Services, Architecture, Security, Privacy, Compliance, platform teams, and delivery teams, this role makes and influences platform decisions, resolves ownership and control gaps, and ensures each platform remains secure, compliant, scalable, well adopted, and aligned to business priorities.

Core Responsibilities


Own the strategy, governance, lifecycle, and business enablement outcomes for assigned enterprise platforms, such as Copilot, Pega, OneTrust, SprocketAI, Canon OCR, Data Access Studio, RAMSES, and similar capabilities.
Define and maintain the platform operating model, decision rights, ownership structure, governance standards, service expectations, platform inventory, and authoritative documentation.
Lead platform governance reviews and risk assessments; make recommendations, assign accountable owners, drive remediation decisions, and ensure actions are completed and evidenced.
Set the platform adoption and change strategy, including target user groups, enablement priorities, communications, training approach, and adoption interventions.
Define success measures and own performance reporting for adoption, governance maturity, ownership health, risk remediation, service effectiveness, and value realization; initiate corrective action when outcomes are off track.
Own the platform capability roadmap and modernization recommendations by evaluating vendor roadmaps, business demand, technical fit, risk, cost, and expected value.
Establish and enforce governance for citizen-developed solutions, including automations, workflows, agents, reports, dashboards, and other platform-based assets; define guardrails, approval criteria, lifecycle controls, and accountability requirements.
Drive cross-functional decisions with Data Services, Architecture, Security, Privacy, Compliance, and Delivery teams, resolving conflicts and ensuring platform changes meet enterprise standards and business objectives.
Own and continuously improve platform playbooks, standards, templates, scorecards, controls, and executive-ready reporting used to govern the platform portfolio.
Define and execute ownership transition plans, including readiness criteria, decision rights, knowledge transfer, control handoff, and acceptance by the long-term System Owner, Product Owner, or service organization.


Requirements


Bachelor's degree in Business, Information Systems, Information Management, or related field.
5 years of experience with direct accountability for platform governance, platform administration, product ownership, business enablement, process improvement, or related technology outcomes.
Experience working with enterprise platforms, SaaS applications, automation tools, analytics solutions, or low-code/no-code technologies.
Strong decision-making, analytical, documentation, and outcome-management skills, with the ability to translate business priorities into platform standards, roadmaps, controls, and measurable results.
Demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional work and drive decisions across business, IT, security, privacy, compliance, architecture, and delivery teams.
Excellent communication, facilitation, and stakeholder management skills.


Preferred Qualifications


Experience with Microsoft Copilot, Power Platform, Pega, Data Access Studio, OneTrust, SharePoint, Purview, or similar enterprise platforms.
Experience owning governance, compliance, risk management, citizen development, or platform adoption outcomes.
Experience with CMDB management, platform inventories, support models, or service management processes.
Familiarity with AI governance, automation governance, or application portfolio management concepts.
Experience defining performance measures and producing executive reporting, dashboards, enablement materials, communications, and governance documentation that drive decisions and accountability.
