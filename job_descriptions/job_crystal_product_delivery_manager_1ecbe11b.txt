Job Intro
Product Delivery Manager - Cape Town, WC Western Cape

Product Delivery Manager ABOUT THE ROLE
Own delivery governance for the Member App, including intake, triage, framework gates and release readiness.
Manage the app roadmap delivery in partnership with the Product Owner and development partner.
Act as the main internal contact for the app’s daily operation and the partner’s Level 1 escalation point.
Monitor app health, coordinate incidents and support problem management for a live consumer product.
Prepare the app for business events, member communications and planned releases.
Lead UAT, phased roll-outs, feature-flagging, rollback planning and operational readiness.
Review development estimates, technical decisions, quality standards and delivery progress.
Manage delivery through Agile Scrum and Kanban practices, supported by CI/CD, test automation and DORA metrics.
Track service performance, compliance, partner performance, budgets and delivery commitments.
Maintain clear documentation, training materials and operational knowledge.
Use product analytics, app-store ratings and member feedback to assess the impact of releases.
Ensure member data and communication consent are handled in line with POPIA requirements.
Work within technology governance, with daily priorities agreed alongside the Member Relations Operations Manager.
Job Specification
WHAT YOU'LL NEED
National Senior Certificate or equivalent.
Degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Business or a related field.
Agile delivery certification, such as Professional Scrum Master, Certified ScrumMaster or Kanban Management Professional.
At least 10 years’ experience in digital product and software delivery.
A minimum of five years’ experience delivering consumer-facing mobile and web applications in a competitive market.
Full product delivery experience, from discovery and design through build, release, support and continuous improvement.
Strong understanding of software development lifecycle management, Agile delivery and DevOps practices.
Practical experience with CI/CD, test automation, shift-left quality and security, and DORA metrics.
A proven record of delivering releases on committed dates within formal governance, PMO, CAB and ITSM frameworks.
Technical fluency across native and cross-platform mobile applications, web platforms, APIs, cloud environments, integrations and observability.
Experience working with development teams and external technology partners, with the confidence to challenge estimates, architecture decisions and quality concerns.
Strong UX and UI knowledge, including user research, usability testing, customer journeys, prototypes and design systems.
Experience using product analytics, funnels, cohorts, retention analysis, event tracking and experimentation.
Experience managing production support, incident coordination, service levels and severity classifications from P1 to P4.
Experience managing partner budgets, forecasts, contracts, warranties, purchase orders and commercial models.
Knowledge of app-store guidelines, push notifications, device fragmentation, low-end devices and poor-connectivity environments.
Familiarity with accessibility standards, including WCAG, Apple Human Interface Guidelines and Material Design.
Working knowledge of tools such as Azure DevOps, GitHub, Figma, product analytics platforms and observability dashboards.
NICE TO HAVE
Experience delivering a major app transformation or re-platforming project.
Experience with member, loyalty or communication platforms, e-commerce or fintech products.
Experience supporting multi-country or multi-language products.
Product management, UX, DevOps, project delivery or IT service management certification.
Certifications such as PMP, PRINCE2 or ITIL V4 Foundation.
Experience with New Relic or similar observability tools.
Familiarity with Odoo as a system of record.
WHY JOIN US
Help shape a digital platform that supports communication and engagement across a cosmetics and personal care network.
Work across product delivery, technology governance, member operations and external partner management.
Contribute to an app designed to make member interactions, ordering and communication more seamless.
Play a central role in improving the reliability, usability and performance of a live consumer product.
Work alongside product, operations, technology and development specialists throughout the full delivery lifecycle.
Apply with your CV and a brief summary of your experience delivering consumer mobile or web applications.

Monthly Salary: R80000 - R90000
