Your Responsibilities - Regulatory compliance management for UK-manufactured medical device portfolios


Creation and maintenance of technical documentation for global regulatory requirements
Regulatory support for new product development (NPD) and product lifecycle projects
Execution of EU MDR compliance and remediation activities
Management of product changes, including IFUs and labeling updates
Review and assessment of product and process change requests
Coordination of international product registrations and license renewals
Regulatory support for sustaining and compliance projects
Process optimization and continuous improvement of regulatory workflows
Cross-functional collaboration with Quality, R&D and Manufacturing teams
Maintenance of regulatory procedures and documentation systems
Adherence to quality, regulatory, health, safety and environmental standards
Support of external Regulatory audits such as Notified Body and FDA.


Your qualification - Degree in Biomedical Sciences, Pharmacy, Engineering or a related scientific discipline


Professional experience (2+ years) in Regulatory Affairs, Quality Assurance or R&D within the medical device industry
Knowledge of global medical device regulatory frameworks, including EU MDR, ISO13485, MDSAP and product specific Regulatory standards.
Understanding of medical device registration and market authorization processes
Ability to interpret regulatory and legislative requirements
Experience with technical documentation and regulatory submissions
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills
Negotiation and influencing capabilities across cross-functional environments
Structured organizational and project coordination skills
Collaborative and team-oriented working style
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel and PowerPoint
Willingness to travel internationally when required


Your competencies - Patient Focus - We put patients at the heart of everything


Integrity - We do the right thing
Innovation - We look for new ways to make things better
Impact - We take accountability and get things done
Empathy - We care for one another and work together


Your benefits - In addition to a competitive salary, you will receive a generous annual leave entitlement


You will be eligible to receive private medical cover
Comprehensive company pension scheme
You will receive an annual health check with BUPA, funded by us
Employee Assistance Program to support your health, mental and emotional-wellbeing
At our HQ, we have a subsidised staff restaurant and free parking
Other benefits include discounted gym and wellness memberships, cashback and discounts with major UK retailers


About Olympus At Olympus, we are committed to Our Purpose of making people’s lives healthier, safer and more fulfilling. As a global medical technology company, we partner with healthcare professionals to provide best-in-class solutions and services for early detection, diagnosis and minimally invasive treatment, aiming to improve patient outcomes by elevating the standard of care in targeted disease states.

For more than 100 years, Olympus has pursued a goal of contributing to society by producing products designed with the purpose of delivering optimal outcomes for its customers around the world.
