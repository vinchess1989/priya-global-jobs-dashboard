Palisades has partnered with Diversified for a large Australian managed service opportunity.  We’re seeking Perth applicants for the following permanent employment opportunities:

·       Account Director – Commercial, Contract and Reporting - Enterprise account leadership, contract performance and variations, financial forecasting, executive reporting.

·       Applications Support Lead - Production application support, major incident recovery, vendor escalation, release and enhancement planning.

·       Applications Transition Lead - Application portfolio handover, support entitlement reviews, knowledge transfer, production readiness testing.

·       Architecture and Recovery Lead - Disaster recovery design, application dependency mapping, recovery sequencing, restore and failover testing.

·       Change and Training Lead (mid-level) - Change impact assessment, role-based training design, workshop facilitation, adoption measurement.

·       Cloud and Infrastructure Operations Lead - Hybrid cloud operations, capacity forecasting, infrastructure incident recovery, workload resilience testing.

·       Cybersecurity Services Lead - Security operations leadership, SOC coordination, vulnerability prioritisation, incident containment and recovery.

·       Enterprise Architect – Transformation Requirements - Business requirements analysis, application modernisation, cloud migration roadmaps, architecture options assessment.

·       IT Service Management (ITSM) Process and Governance Lead - Change and release governance, configuration record quality, problem management, service-level reporting.

·       ITSM Process Lead (mid-level) - Incident and request workflow design, ticket routing, escalation rules, end-to-end workflow testing.

·       Network Operations Lead - LAN and WAN operations, routing and wireless troubleshooting, carrier management, multi-site outage recovery.

·       Network Transition Lead - Network and voice service handover, circuit and device mapping, carrier coordination, failover rehearsals.

·       Operational Support Systems (OSS) Delivery Lead – Project Management and Scheduling - Monitoring tool rollout, supplier coordination, integrated scheduling, cutover and rollback planning.

·       Operations and India Delivery Lead – Perth or Hebbal, Bangalore - Cross-site team mobilisation, 24/7 roster planning, skills and access verification, shift handover.

·       PMO Scheduler (mid-level) - Resource-loaded scheduling, critical path analysis, dependency tracking, modelling the impact of delays.

·       Security Transition Lead - Identity and privileged access management, access readiness reviews, control testing, security handover.

·       Senior Cyber and Privacy Architect - Secure cloud and integration design, identity and access architecture, data flow mapping, privacy risk assessment.

·       Senior Digital Employee Experience Architect and Acceptance Reviewer - Endpoint telemetry design, user experience measurement, pilot evaluation, technical acceptance criteria.

·       Senior Service Delivery Lead - Release readiness assessment, support model design, rollback rehearsals, operational handover.

·       Senior Solution Architect - ITSM and monitoring integration, interface and data flow design, workflow automation, service acceptance criteria.

·       Senior Transformation Manager - Multi-supplier program leadership, resource planning, release governance, benefits realisation.

·       Service Delivery Manager - Service-level management, major incident coordination, supplier performance reviews, continuity planning.

·       Service Integration and Management (SIAM) Lead - Multi-provider operating model design, ITSM record integration, resolver ownership, supplier performance management.

·       Service Integration Lead – Transition - Service mapping, supplier handover design, service catalogue setup, end-to-end escalation testing.

·       Technical Architecture Lead - Enterprise infrastructure design, architecture standards and patterns, technology lifecycle planning, resilience assessment.

·       Transition Manager - Managed service transitions, integrated scheduling, cutover and rollback leadership, stabilisation. A recognised project management qualification is required.

·       Transition Project and Test Coordinator - Project scheduling, test coordination, defect and retest tracking, cutover rehearsal planning.

·       Workforce Change Lead - Workforce mobilisation, skills gap analysis, induction and access coordination, training readiness.

·       Workforce Planning and Resource Coordinator - Shift rostering, demand and capacity planning, relief coverage, skills-based scheduling across time zones.

·       Workplace Services Lead - End-user computing operations, endpoint lifecycle management, software deployment, field support coordination.
