About The Role

As the DevOps Product Lead for Strategy & Delivery, you will serve as the pivotal bridge between strategic product vision and hands on engineering execution. You will drive the long term technology vision, transform complex architectural blueprints into scalable production systems, and foster engineering excellence through hands-on mentorship and vendor leadership.

Key Responsibilities


Define and drive the long-term technical strategy and roadmap for enterprise DevOps platforms, CI/CD pipelines, and core developer tooling to maximise developer productivity and operational resilience.
Lead the technical delivery of the Service Operations & Infrastructure (SOI) project portfolio and platform enhancements using agile methodologies to ensure predictable, high-quality outcomes.
Drive enterprise-wide standardisation of DevSecOps practices, enforcing Policy-as-Code to secure the software supply chain and leading audit responses for deployment governance and compliance.
Direct, mentor, and empower a high-performing team of 4–6 engineers and business analysts, fostering continuous learning and engineering excellence.
Oversee the administration and governance of core DevOps platforms, drawing on deep operational experience with GitHub Enterprise, Jfrog, Azure DevOps (ADO) just to name a few.
Architect and standardise enterprise deployment workflows, leveraging 5+ years of hands-on expertise with GitHub and CI/CD Pipelines to automate end-to-end software delivery.
Standardise pipeline templates and deployment patterns, embedding and enforcing enterprise security scanning platforms (such as Snyk and Orca) directly into automated build and release workflows.
Manage the rollout, governance, and adoption of AI-assisted developer tools like GitHub Copilot, alongside agile platforms (Jira, Confluence) and telemetry suites (Splunk, Dynatrace) to measure platform health and team efficiency


What You’ll Bring


Deep administration experience with GitHub Enterprise and CI/CD Pipelines, paired with 5+ years automating complex CI/CD workflows using GitHub Actions and ADO Pipelines.
Proven track record implementing standardised pipeline templates, integrating security scanners (e.g., Snyk, Orca), and managing the enterprise rollout and governance of AI-assisted tools like GitHub Copilot.
Deep understanding of IT processes within high-volume retail or enterprise environments requiring mission-critical availability, high stability, and robust SDLC quality control.
Comprehensive IT Service Management (ITSM) experience across strategy, design, and build, combined with telemetry monitoring (e.g., Splunk, Dynatrace) to ensure platform health.
Demonstrated background working as an Engineer or Product Lead, leading cross-functional teams and managing diverse backlog items (features, defects, non-functional requirements).
Ability to operate seamlessly across all delivery levels, from high-level strategic roadmap definition to granular sprint planning and execution.
Expert level knowledge of agile frameworks and collaboration platforms (Jira, Confluence) to measure team metrics and drive delivery efficiency.
