Job Details:

Job Summary:

The Operational Service Delivery Manager (OSDM) is responsible for the operational execution and coordination of services that are live, transitioning into service, or actively under development for assigned customer programs. The role ensures day‑to‑day service stability while coordinating delivery activity in a way that protects availability, reliability, and customer outcomes.

The OSDM operates as the operational execution lead within the Services core delivery model, providing direction to IT Project Managers, Release Managers, and core technical services teams to ensure work is sequenced, coordinated, and delivered with full awareness of operational risk and readiness.

The OSDM maintains end‑to‑end situational awareness across incidents, changes, and delivery activity—stepping in to provide direction, escalation, and prioritisation when required. The role bridges “run” and “change,” ensuring development activity translates into stable, supportable live services.

Key Responsibilities

Operational Service Performance


Own day‑to‑day operational oversight for live services, ensuring availability, incident consistency, and predictable execution.
Maintain visibility across operational health, risk, and trends, escalating where service performance or customer commitment is at risk.
Ensure operational processes (incident, problem, change, communications) are followed and continuously improved.



Delivery Execution & Coordination


Provide operational direction to IT Project Managers, Release Managers, and core technical services teams working on the program.
Coordinate in‑flight development, releases, and enhancements to ensure alignment with operational readiness and service constraints.
Ensure delivery activity is sequenced to minimize disruption and protect live service performance.
Surface dependencies, risks, and conflicts requiring prioritization or trade‑off decisions by the Service Delivery Manager.



Incident Awareness, Direction & Escalation


Establish and maintain effective project governance, including decision tracking and escalation paths. Maintain big‑picture awareness of incidents and service degradation across the program.
Guide cross‑team response when incidents span multiple domains or delivery streams.
Confirm severity alignment and escalation pathways per policy.
Determine when to invoke major incident processes and guide stakeholder communication cadence.



Operational Readiness & Release Support


Partner with IT Project Managers and Release Managers to validate operational readiness prior to go‑live, including support models, monitoring, documentation, and handover.
Confirm readiness gates are met before changes enter production.
Support post‑release stabilization and ensure corrective actions are tracked through to completion.



Customer‑Facing Operational Rhythm


Own preparation and execution of customer‑facing operational forums (MORs, service reviews, QBR inputs).
Present an integrated operational view covering live performance, active incidents, and delivery impacts.
Translate technical and operational activity into clear, outcome‑focused updates for customers and leadership.

Qualifications & Experience


Bachelor’s degree in IT, Engineering, Operations, or related discipline (or equivalent experience)
8+ years’ experience supporting or operating complex, mission‑critical systems
5+ years in service operations, service delivery, or operational leadership roles
Strong working knowledge of ITIL practices, particularly Incident, Problem, and Change Management
Experience coordinating work across engineering, infrastructure, operations, and vendors
Ability to understand technical delivery impacts without acting as a hands‑on engineer



Preferred Experience


Experience delivering cloud‑based or infrastructure‑focused initiatives (e.g., AWS, Azure). Experience in transit, payments, or regulated, high‑availability environments
Exposure to compliance frameworks such as PCI DSS or ISO 27001
Experience operating within large, matrixed or global service organizations



Personal Attributes


Strong operational judgment and calm decision‑making under pressure
Clear, confident communicator during incidents and high‑impact events
Highly organized with strong follow‑through and execution discipline
Customer‑centric, accountable, and outcome‑focused
Collaborative leader who builds alignment across run and change teams
