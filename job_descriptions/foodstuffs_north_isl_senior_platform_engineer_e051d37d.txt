Ko Wai Mātou – About Us

Kia ora. We’re Foodies. You know New World, PAK’nSAVE and Four Square right? That’s us!

With 300+ stores and over 27,000 Foodies across Te Ika-a-Māui, our North Island Co-op has the privilege of helping New Zealanders get more out of life. Locally owned, we reflect the communities we serve, and we celebrate our people, our taonga, every day for being uniquely them.

Our Support Centre is 1,500+ people strong, which includes 500+ IT employees, who all play a vital role in ensuring the cogs of our Co-op stay turning and our systems stay ‘byte’-sized and ‘bug’-free.

Te Tūranga – About The Role

We’re looking for a Senior Platform Engineer to help lead the operation, security, reliability and continuous improvement of our Salesforce platforms.

This is a senior, hands-on engineering role where you'll take ownership of some of our most critical Salesforce environments spanning Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing Cloud, Data Cloud, and emerging platform capabilities. You'll be responsible for solving compl ex technical challenges, leading major platform initiatives, strengthening security and reliability, and ensuring our Salesforce ecosystem continues to evolve in a sustainable, scalable and operationally healthy way.

You'll work closely with the Technical Lead, internal delivery teams and external partners, providing technical leadership and governance to ensure solutions delivered into our platforms meet the high standards required to support our business.

As a trusted technical expert, you'll mentor other engineers, drive engineering excellence, and help shape the future direction of our Salesforce capability.

This role will suit an engineer who enjoys owning the long-term health of business-critical platforms. While you'll help deliver new capabilities and improvements, you'll be equally motivated by platform reliability, security, lifecycle management, automation and operational excellence, ensuring our Salesforce ecosystem remains stable, supported and ready for the future. You'll take pride in preventing problems before they occur, improving observability and reducing operational risk through strong engineering practices

What You’ll Do


Lead the design and delivery of complex Salesforce platform changes, enhancements and technical improvements across multiple Salesforce products.
Own the resolution of complex, high-impact incidents, driving the fastest safe path to restoration and identifying root causes to prevent recurrence.
Champion platform reliability, observability and operational excellence across business-critical Salesforce environments.
Strengthen platform security through secure configuration, access management, vulnerability remediation, platform currency and secure development practices.
Build and improve automation, tooling and deployment processes that reduce manual effort and operational risk.
Provide technical governance across platform changes, reviewing solution designs, configuration and code to ensure quality, maintainability, security and alignment with platform standards.
Partner with internal teams and external vendors delivering into the Salesforce ecosystem, providing technical guidance and constructive challenge where required.
Lead major releases, upgrades and platform initiatives using strong testing, deployment and rollback practices.
Mentor Platform Engineers and Associate Platform Engineers through technical coaching, peer review and knowledge sharing.
Contribute to our 24/7 support model, acting as a senior escalation point for critical platform issues.
Continuously improve engineering practices across automation, security, reliability and platform governance.

He Kōrero Mōu – About You

You'll be an experienced Salesforce engineer with a passion for building, operating and continuously improving secure, reliable and scalable platforms. You'll enjoy balancing platform evolution and enhancement delivery with the operational disciplines required to keep business-critical services secure, resilient and available.

You'll bring strong technical capability, sound judgement and a platform mindset, balancing business outcomes with long-term platform health, security and maintainability.

You'll Bring With You


Significant hands-on experience supporting and engineering Salesforce platforms in complex enterprise environments.
Strong experience across Salesforce Sales Cloud and Service Cloud.
Experience with Salesforce Marketing Cloud and Data Cloud as well as a good understanding of how platforms integrate across the wider customer ecosystem.
Strong capability with Salesforce Flow, platform automation and release management practices.
Experience with Apex, Lightning Web Components (LWC), Salesforce CLI and integration technologies will be highly regarded.
Knowledge of emerging Salesforce capabilities such as Data Cloud and Agentforce.
Strong understanding of Salesforce security, including access controls, data security, secure configuration and secure development practices.
Experience leading complex production incidents and driving long-term corrective actions.
Experience reviewing and governing platform changes delivered by both internal teams and external partners.
The ability to mentor engineers and lift the capability of those around you.
Salesforce certifications relevant to the platform are highly regarded.
Excellent communication skills and the ability to build trusted relationships with technical and non-technical stakeholders alike.
