About the role and what you’ll do

The role involves deploying, configuring, and maintaining software packages for various high-profile IT systems, including the Public Key Infrastructure (PKI). This includes supporting and improving the underlying systems to ensure optimal operation across the entire solution.

Deployments & Change Management

· Lead the software deployment lifecycle, ensuring effective planning, implementation, maintenance, and retirement of software packages
· Provide technical leadership for system design, integration, and architecture, ensuring quality, security, scalability, and compliance with agreed standards
· Manage change processes, ensuring changes are planned, tested, documented, approved, and supported by appropriate rollback procedures
· Oversee testing and quality assurance throughout the project lifecycle to ensure reliable hardware, software, and documentation deliverables




Support & Maintenance

· Provide technical leadership for the support and maintenance of Windows Server environments, PKI infrastructure, backup, networking, and other critical systems
· Lead Incident and Problem Management activities, including root cause analysis, resolution of complex issues, and implementation of preventive measures in line with ITIL practices
· Collaborate with internal teams and third-party vendors to support system integrations, Level 3 escalations, and business-critical operations

Support PKI operations and Trusted Roles, ensuring compliance with ICAO, eIDAS, and other applicable security and regulatory standards

Stakeholder and Knowledge Management

· Lead technical meetings and communicate effectively with internal and external stakeholders
· Act as Technical Lead on project teams, providing updates on technical deliverables and work packages
· Maintain technical documentation, knowledge bases, and training materials, ensuring accuracy, version control, and compliance with IT standards
· Develop and maintain expert knowledge of end-to-end solutions to provide technical guidance and troubleshoot complex issues
· Promote best practices, support compliance requirements, and coach less experienced team members

About you

The successful candidate will be focused on integrating new services and improvements whilst ensuring stability and performance of the system is maintained, by troubleshooting issues to root cause resolutions. This includes improving the security posture to ensure compliance with current and recently introduced security standards. In addition, the successful candidate should be a mentor for fellow support teams personnel.

Experience and knowledge

· Higher level education in IT or a related degree
· Minimum of 5 years’ experience successfully supporting, maintenance and operations in windows enterprise environments
· Experience in audits and certifications such as ISO27001, EIDAS, ICAO




How you work




Focus Areas (at least 2 years of hands-on practical experience in 2 of the below areas)

DevOps & Cloud Engineering:
· Containerization & Orchestration (Docker, Kubernetes)
· CI/CD Pipeline Development (GitHub Actions, Jenkins)
· Persistent Storage for Containerized Environments (NFS and SMB)




Networking & Security:
· Configuration and troubleshooting of Cisco and Cloudflare devices
· DNS, CDN, and WAF management with Cloudflare
· Firewall configuration, VPN setup, and SSL/TLS certificate management
· Network diagnostics using tools like Wireshark for performance and threat analysis




Windows Domain Administration:
· Setup and configuration on physical and virtual machines
· General Windows Server Configurations with Server 2022/5 features, including Active Directory (AD), DNS, DHCP, Group Policy Objects (GPO), Hyper-V, and Failover Clustering
· Experience with Backup solutions (Preferred VEAM)
· Experience with the patch management process, specifically involving WSUS deployments




Database Administration:
· Design, setup, and configuration of MSSQL, MariaDB, and PostgreSQL for performance, reliability, and security
· Performance tuning and resource optimization across database environments.
· Implementation of security best practices, including access control and data protection
· Development of automation jobs for backups, monitoring, and maintenance to support business continuity
· Experience with high availability and disaster recovery setups




Certifications

· Docker Certified Associate, Certified Kubernetes Administrator, Azure Administrator Associate
· Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA), Cloudflare Fundamentals Certificate,
· Microsoft Certified: Windows Server Hybrid Administrator Associate and Microsoft Certified: Azure Solutions Architect Expert




Skills & Competencies

· Strong communication and customer-facing skills, with the ability to tailor messaging for diverse audiences and cultures
· Excellent troubleshooting, problem-solving, and critical thinking skills, with experience identifying root causes, sharing knowledge, and preventing recurring issues
· Ability to interpret technical documentation, including schematics, wiring diagrams, specifications, and international standards; additional European language is an advantage
· Experience with Incident and Change Management using service management tools
· Highly organized, proactive, and goal-oriented, with the ability to plan, prioritize, and perform effectively in a fast-paced environment
· Strong leadership and interpersonal skills, capable of inspiring teams, supporting development, and communicating confidently at all organizational levels
· Logical and analytical approach to decision-making, with the confidence to challenge ideas constructively, anticipate risks, and develop effective solutions
· Flexible regarding work location and hours to meet business needs




✨Location, travel and eligibility

· Some travel abroad may be required (implementation, training, FATs, UATs, SATs, CATs, occasional Level 3 support visits etc.) up to 30%. Therefore must possess, or be able to acquire, a valid passport and the required VISAs for international travel.
