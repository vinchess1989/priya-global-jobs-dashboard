About The Role

We are looking for an experienced DevOps Engineer with 6+ years of experience in managing Kubernetes infrastructure, AWS EKS clusters, and cloud-native deployment environments. The ideal candidate will have strong hands-on expertise in EKS upgrades, infrastructure as code, GitOps workflows, and Kubernetes workload management, with a focus on ensuring platform stability, availability, and operational efficiency.

Key Responsibilities


Manage and execute AWS EKS cluster upgrades, ensuring Kubernetes version compatibility, version skew compliance, and compatibility of cluster add-ons.
Review and validate Terraform plans through Spacelift, ensuring infrastructure changes are safe, reliable, and aligned with operational standards.
Manage GitOps-based deployments using Argo CD, including manual application synchronization, deployment validation, and troubleshooting synchronization issues.
Operate and maintain Karpenter for Kubernetes node provisioning, lifecycle management, scaling, and AMI updates.
Ensure Kubernetes workload availability during maintenance and upgrades by managing Pod Disruption Budgets (PDBs), node draining, workload rescheduling, and rollback procedures.
Work with Helm charts to manage Kubernetes application deployments, configurations, and release updates.
Troubleshoot cluster-level issues, deployment failures, and infrastructure-related incidents while maintaining platform reliability.
Follow established change management and operational best practices to minimize service disruption during infrastructure upgrades and deployments.


Requirements

Required Skills & Experience


6+ years of experience in DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), Platform Engineering, or Cloud Infrastructure Engineering.
Strong hands-on experience with AWS EKS and Kubernetes cluster upgrades, including version skew policies and add-on compatibility.
Strong experience with Terraform and reviewing infrastructure change plans using Spacelift.
Practical experience with Argo CD and GitOps workflows, including manual application syncs and deployment troubleshooting.
Hands-on experience with Karpenter, Kubernetes node lifecycle management, and AMI management.
Strong understanding of Kubernetes availability, Pod Disruption Budgets (PDBs), node draining, workload disruption management, and rollback procedures.
Working knowledge of Helm for managing and deploying Kubernetes applications.


Preferred Qualifications


Experience operating production-grade Kubernetes environments on AWS.
Understanding of infrastructure change management, deployment safety, and cluster maintenance best practices.
Strong troubleshooting, analytical, and incident-resolution skills.
Ability to collaborate with engineering and platform teams to maintain reliable and scalable infrastructure.
