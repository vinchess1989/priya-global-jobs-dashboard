QA Tester-AI, Automation and Power Platform

12 Months Contract

Johannesburg-Hybrid-3 days in the office




Role Purpose

The Quality Assurance (QA) Tester is responsible for ensuring the quality, stability, reliability, security, and performance of solutions delivered across the Digitisation & Automation portfolio.

The role covers RPA and Bot solutions, Power Apps, Power Automate workflows, AI Agents and Copilot solutions, Azure AI Foundry solutions, APIs and system integrations, and end-to-end automated business processes. The role implements quality engineering, test automation, and continuous testing approaches to support production-ready solutions that meet business, security, and regulatory requirements.




Key Responsibilities

Test Planning and Quality Engineering
Automation and Digital Solution Testing
RPA and Intelligent Automation
Power Platform Solutions
AI and Copilot Solutions
API and Integration Testing
AI Quality Assurance Responsibilities
Test Automation and Continuous Quality Engineering
Quality Management




﻿Technical Skills

Essential

Software testing, quality assurance, and test automation frameworks.
Functional, regression, integration, API, and end-to-end process testing.
Power Platform, RPA, AI Agent, and Copilot testing concepts.
Defect management, Agile delivery methodologies, and continuous quality engineering.




Advantageous

Microsoft Copilot Studio, Azure AI Foundry, Power Platform, Power Automate, UiPath, or Automation Anywhere.

Azure DevOps, PowerShell, SQL, REST APIs, performance testing, security testing, and CI/CD pipelines.




Experience

3-5 years of experience testing enterprise solutions.

Experience testing automation, workflow, Power Platform, APIs, and integrations.

Experience working in Agile delivery teams and with test automation frameworks.

Exposure to AI-enabled solutions, Generative AI agents, chatbots, or Copilot solutions is advantageous.




Qualifications

Degree or diploma in Information Technology, Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Systems, or a related technical discipline.

Certifications

Mandatory

ISTQB Foundation Certification.




Preferred

Microsoft Power Platform Certification: PL-100 and/or PL-200.

Microsoft Certified: Azure AI Engineer Associate (AI-102) or equivalent.

Test automation-related certification.

Microsoft Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900).

Microsoft Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900).
