Lead - Talent Acquisition & Employee Experience @ Magma General Insurance Limited | Driving People-Centric Solutions

Requirement gathering and Analysis:

Consults with business functions to identify, define and document business needs and objectives, current operational procedures, scenarios, problems and solutions.
Identifies opportunities for improving business processes. Liaison between departmental end-users, technical analysts/vendors, consultants and other stakeholders in the analysis, design, configuration, testing and maintenance of systems.
Analyses the feasibility and document requirements for new systems as well as enhancements to existing systems. Tracks and fully documents changes for functional and business specifications.
Assists in the preparation of Scope to develop new systems and/or operational changes.
Participate in vendor walkthrough sessions and perform vendor evaluation from functional perspective.
Conducts change impact analysis to assess the potential implications of changes and documents business rules, functions and requirements as part of the CR document.
Conduct Requirements walkthrough, Demo sessions with Vendors & Testers
Jointly review & evaluate efforts & WBS along with the Change & Project manager
Perform FSD/SRS/Design reviews & sign offs along with the Change & Project manager.




User Acceptance Testing and signoff

Design test scenarios and cases based on business requirement documents and processes.
Manage creation of the UAT test plan, identifying and mapping of appropriate end-users
Perform Test Cases Reviews, Provide UAT support until go live and resolve any vendor or tester queries during the SDLC phase.
Perform necessary System configurations as required for implementing a particular CR/Project.
Participates in analysis with the BAU team. Differentiate between bugs and potential change requests.
Ensure timely closure of open bugs with assistance of Technical Team.
Assist in securing final sign-off from stakeholders
