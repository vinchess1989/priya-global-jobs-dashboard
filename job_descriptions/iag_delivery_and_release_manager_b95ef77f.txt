ROLLiN’ is rethinking traditional insurance and doing it a lil differently. Part of Insurance Australia Group (known as IAG), we’re making insurance more accessible for the young and young-at-heart.

Step into a role where you’ll help shape how work moves from idea to reality across one of Australia’s most innovative insurance brands. As Delivery & Release Manager, you’ll bring people, priorities and delivery together, helping teams navigate complex change while keeping customer outcomes front of mind. Working across major transformation initiatives, business priorities and technology delivery, you’ll play a key leadership role in ensuring releases are well coordinated, risks are managed and teams stay aligned. If you enjoy bringing clarity to complexity and creating momentum across multiple stakeholders, this is an opportunity to make a real impact. This is a 12-month max-term full-time role based in Gadigal Country (Darling Park 2, Sydney) or Naarm (Melbourne).

What You'll Do


Lead delivery and release management activities across multiple technology, data and business workstreams, ensuring priorities remain aligned and progress stays on track.
Coordinate large scale transformation initiatives, including policy administration and data migration programs, while managing dependencies, risks and key milestones.
Partner with business, product, technology and vendor teams to support smooth and reliable releases across testing and production environments.
Guide and support a team of Technical Business Analysts, a Scrum Master and Production Support resources to achieve shared outcomes.
Drive continuous improvement in release processes, governance and delivery practices to create efficient, repeatable and scalable ways of working.
Provide clear reporting, delivery insights and recommendations, helping leaders make informed decisions and proactively address emerging issues.


What You'll Bring


Experience leading complex technology delivery programs and release activities in fast paced environments.
Strong stakeholder management skills and an ability to influence across business and technology teams.
Confidence balancing strategic thinking with hands on delivery oversight.
Experience working in Agile, Lean or iterative delivery environments.
A strong understanding of software delivery lifecycles, dependencies and risk management.
People leadership capability with a collaborative and supportive approach.
Experience in financial services, insurance or other regulated environments will be highly regarded.


What We Offer


Boosted superannuation with 13% as standard.
20 days annual leave + 5 days MyLeave (extra leave for what’s important to you).
Up to 50% off personal insurance, including home and motor insurance.
Partner discounts on private health insurance, tech & appliances, and many more.
Access to LinkedIn Learning, the award-winning IAG Academy, study assistance and secondment opportunities.
