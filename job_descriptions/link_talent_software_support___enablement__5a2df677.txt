Overall Purpose




On behalf of our well-established manufacturing client, we are seeking a Product Enablement Engineer to develop and manage a comprehensive technical support system for automated electromechanical products. The role involves creating technical documentation, troubleshooting guides and knowledge bases, managing spare parts, and training and certifying third-party technicians. The successful candidate will also help establish a network of external service providers and work closely with the engineering team to improve remote diagnostics and product reliability.




Requirements:

Experience with electromechanical, mechatronic or embedded systems.
Strong technical documentation, troubleshooting and support process development skills.
Experience training technicians and coordinating third-party service providers.
Knowledge of Linux, networking protocols and remote diagnostic tools.
Familiarity with spare parts management, software testing and failure analysis.
Excellent organisational, communication and training skills.
Fluent English; French is an advantage.




Salary up to €35,000 gross per year depending on the experience
