Role Summary




We are looking for an experienced Environment Manager to own and coordinate the end-to-end management of enterprise non-production environments. The role will ensure environments are available, stable, secure, correctly configured, and ready to support development, testing, release, migration, and recovery activities.

The Environment Manager will work across application, infrastructure, database, middleware, network, security, release, testing, and support teams to govern environment demand, resolve issues, maintain controls, and improve environment delivery through automation and standardisation.




Key Responsibilities:




Own the end-to-end Environment Management process, governance, inventory, controls, and readiness requirements.
Manage environment demand for new projects, testing cycles, releases, refreshes, migrations, disaster recovery activities, and urgent recovery requirements.
Validate environment requests, including environment type, source/target details, required dates, approvals, data requirements, and change dependencies.
Assess and coordinate infrastructure, application, database, middleware, network, security, capacity, monitoring, backup, and integration dependencies.
Develop and maintain environment plans covering provisioning, refresh, cloning, configuration, promotion, validation, rollback, and handover.
Ensure non-production environments maintain the correct application build levels, configurations, integrations, and testing baselines.
Prevent uncontrolled changes and ensure environment activities follow appropriate ITSM and Change Management processes.
Own environment issues end to end, coordinating support teams and leading cross-functional resolution or war-room activity where required.
Coordinate environment readiness validation, including infrastructure, database, middleware, application, monitoring, backup, batch jobs, and integration checks.
Partner with development, testing, release, and delivery teams to define environment strategy and routes to production for project changes, platform changes, production fixes, and concurrent releases.




Required Skills and Experience

Strong experience managing complex enterprise test and non-production environments across multiple applications and technology teams.
Proven track record of delivering environment provisioning, configuration, refresh, integration, and readiness activities within complex technology programmes.
Good understanding of software development, testing, release, deployment, and route-to-production processes.
Strong knowledge of IT service management disciplines, including Incident, Problem, Change, Configuration, and Release Management.
Experience coordinating infrastructure, cloud, database, middleware, networking, application support, and security teams.
Good understanding of cloud and hybrid infrastructure and automation/DevOps practices such as CI/CD and Infrastructure as Code.
Experience managing environment risks, dependencies, conflicts, schedules, and stakeholder expectations.
Understanding of security, access management, data privacy, masking, and compliance requirements for non-production environments.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, negotiation, and organisational skills.
Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to work across technical and non-technical teams.
Ability to work independently, take ownership, and drive issues through to resolution.
