Up to £55,000 p.a, flexible working hours, 1 day per week working from home and attractive pension contributions are a few of the benefits a Quality Manager will enjoy whilst working for this industry leading Engineering & Manufacturing business.




This employer is based in LEEDS, meaning the successful Quality Manager will easily be able to commute from surrounding towns & cities including Bradford, Huddersfield, Wakefield and Halifax.




Key responsibilities of the Quality Manager will include:

Lead and maintain the ISO 9001 Quality Management System, ensuring quality procedures, document control processes and records are effectively managed and consistently followed across the business.
Drive continuous improvement by setting quality objectives, developing improvement plans and monitoring progress against agreed targets, KPIs and business priorities.
Manage quality activities across engineering, manufacturing, procurement and service teams, providing leadership, coaching, training and support to improve quality performance.
Plan, conduct and coordinate internal, supplier and external audits, ensuring compliance with quality standards and the effective closure of audit findings and corrective actions.
Lead investigations into customer complaints, non-conformances and quality issues, applying root cause analysis and implementing corrective and preventive actions to prevent recurrence.
Monitor and report on quality metrics, supplier performance, defect trends, cost of poor quality and business risks, escalating significant issues and driving improvement initiatives.
Oversee inspection and quality control activities, review engineering drawings and specifications for compliance, and work closely with suppliers and internal teams to improve product quality, reduce waste and ensure customer requirements are met.




For the Quality Manager opportunity, we are keen to receive CV’s from candidates who possess:

Quality management experience within engineering and manufacturing environments, with a proven track record of driving continuous improvement and leading cross-functional quality initiatives.
Strong expertise in ISO 9001 Quality Management Systems, including internal and supplier auditing, compliance management and quality assurance best practices.
Experience interpreting engineering drawings and specifications, managing inspections, assessing product conformity and maintaining quality control standards.
NCR management, root cause analysis, CAPA implementation, customer complaint resolution and supplier quality improvement activities.
Effective communication with strong analytical and organisational skills, able to influence stakeholders at all levels, uphold SHEQ standards and manage priorities while travelling as required.
