Job: Project Manager - Technology & Customer Implementation

Location: County Donegal | Hybrid working

Salary: €50,000 – €60,000




The company

Kura is delighted to be partnering with a growing technology manufacturer serving regulated industries for a Project Manager to join the company who are experiencing rapid growth.




The role

You’ll own a portfolio of internal and customer-facing projects from start to finish. That includes product development, operational improvement, business change and customer rollouts. You’ll work across R&D, Quality, Operations, Sales, Technical Services and Customer Success, bringing structure, pace and visibility to delivery.




Your role includes:




Define scope, goals and success criteria, and build realistic plans covering milestones, resources and budgets.
Drive delivery: chair meetings, coordinate teams and third parties, clear blockers and escalate early.
Manage risks, issues, changes and costs, with clear logs and up-to-date forecasts.
Build quality, testing and stage gate approvals into plans where needed.
Lead customer projects from sale through installation, training, sign-off and handover.
Give leadership and customers clear, decision-ready status updates.
Close projects properly, run lessons-learned reviews and improve processes.




What you’ll bring




Proven experience running several cross-functional projects at once in tech, manufacturing, engineering, software or a similar environment.
Strong grip on budgets, governance, risk and change control.
Confidence working with engineers, senior leaders, customers and partners without formal authority.
Excellent organisation and communication, and the confidence to challenge unrealistic plans.
A degree or equivalent hands-on project delivery experience.
Nice to have: PRINCE2, PMP or Agile certification; experience with stage gate processes; projects combining hardware and software; regulated settings




This is an opportunity to work for a company who firstly genuinely care for their people and secondly have big ambitions including international growth.
