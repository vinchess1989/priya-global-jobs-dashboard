The Opportunity

Join BearingPoint's Dublin Technology team and help organisations modernise the way they build, deploy and operate software. BearingPoint is currently recruiting for an experienced Platform Engineer to join our successful global consulting firm in our Dublin office.

As part of BearingPoint's Technology Delivery team, you will work with clients to implement and enhance modern engineering platforms, DevOps practices and software delivery capabilities. You will support the development of self-service platforms and CI/CD solutions that enable development teams to deliver software more efficiently, reliably and securely.

Working alongside software engineers, architects, QA specialists and cloud engineers, you will contribute to the automation and optimisation of software delivery processes across a range of public sector and enterprise engagements. This role provides an excellent opportunity to deepen your expertise in Platform Engineering, Cloud and DevOps while working with leading organisations and modern technology stacks.

You will play an important role in building and improving engineering capabilities for our clients while contributing to BearingPoint's culture of innovation, collaboration and continuous improvement.

What you will do


You will contribute to the development and maintenance of platform engineering capabilities that enable self-service software delivery.
You will build, maintain and improve CI/CD pipelines and automation frameworks across client and internal development platforms.
You will work closely with software developers, QA engineers and architects to improve software delivery processes and overall development efficiency.
You will support cloud-based application deployments across development, test and production environments.
You will identify and help resolve bottlenecks within the software delivery lifecycle through automation and process improvement.
You will implement and maintain Infrastructure as Code, configuration management and deployment automation solutions.
You will troubleshoot and resolve platform, deployment and operational issues across multiple projects and environments.
You will contribute to the adoption of DevOps and Platform Engineering best practices across delivery teams.
You will share knowledge, lessons learned and engineering best practices within project teams and the wider technology community.



What we are looking for

Core Experience


Minimum of 4 years' experience in a Platform Engineering, DevOps Engineering or Systems Engineering role.
Experience working with CI/CD tools such as Azure DevOps, Jenkins, GitHub Actions or similar technologies.
Experience supporting software delivery and release processes across multiple environments.
Good understanding of source control systems and branching strategies.
Experience with scripting and automation technologies such as PowerShell, Bash or Python.
Experience working with cloud platforms, ideally Microsoft Azure.
Strong debugging, troubleshooting and problem-solving capabilities.
Good understanding of software development lifecycle (SDLC) and DevOps practices.
Strong communication and collaboration skills, including fluent English.
Passion for innovation, continuous improvement and learning new technologies.
3rd level degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering or a related discipline, or equivalent experience.



Desirable Experience


Experience with Infrastructure as Code technologies such as Terraform, Bicep or ARM templates.
Experience with container technologies such as Docker and Kubernetes.
Knowledge of monitoring and observability tools such as Azure Monitor, Grafana or Application Insights.
Exposure to Agile delivery methodologies.
Microsoft Azure or DevOps certifications.
Previous consulting or client-facing experience.

About BearingPoint

BearingPoint is an independent management and technology consultancy with European roots and a global reach. The company operates in four units: Consulting, Solutions, Business Services, and Ventures. Consulting covers the advisory business; Solutions provides the tools for successful digital transformation, advanced analytics and regulatory requirements; Business Services provides managed services beyond SaaS; Ventures drives the financing and development of start-ups. BearingPoint’s clients include many of the world’s leading companies and organizations. The firm has a global consulting network with more than 10,000 people and supports clients in over 75 countries, engaging with them to achieve measurable and sustainable success.
