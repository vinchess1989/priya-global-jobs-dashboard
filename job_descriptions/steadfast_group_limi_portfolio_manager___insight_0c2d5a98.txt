Introduction:

Steadfast was founded on the belief that a network of brokers would be stronger together, and this idea has been the backbone of our culture ever since. We believe that none of us is as good as all of us.

At Steadfast we are proud of our diverse mix of people and skills, coming together across multiple business and business units to not only support our broker network, but to create the next generation thinking, systems and technologies that will drive the insurance sector forward.

We are leaders of the general insurance broking and underwriting industry, and we lead from the front.

Description:

Join a high-impact technology leadership team responsible for governing and coordinating enterprise-wide delivery and release activities across a complex portfolio of IT and Insurtech initiatives. This role plays a critical part in ensuring technology changes are delivered smoothly, risks are managed effectively, and stakeholders remain informed and prepared throughout the delivery lifecycle.

Key Responsibilities:


Own and manage the integrated release calendar across Enterprise IT and Insurtech initiatives.
Coordinate release planning, governance forums, change approvals, and deployment activities.
Manage delivery risks, issues, dependencies, and competing priorities across multiple projects.
Prepare portfolio reporting, executive dashboards, and stakeholder communications.
Drive business readiness activities, including communications, training, and change coordination.
Identify and implement continuous improvement opportunities across delivery, governance, and release management practices.



Skills and Qualifications:


Experience in release management, delivery governance, portfolio management, or project leadership roles.
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills, with the ability to influence at all levels.
Proven ability to manage multiple concurrent initiatives, risks, dependencies, and delivery outcomes.
Experience with tools such as Jira, Confluence, and Freshworks or similar platforms.
Excellent planning, problem-solving, and organisational skills.
Insurance, broking, underwriting, or Insurtech experience will be highly regarded.
