He angitūtanga: The opportunity

Auckland Council is seeking a Technical Lead Platform Engineering to provide technical leadership across our enterprise Platform Engineering practice. You’ll be the technical authority for platform engineering, defining standards, architecture patterns and engineering guardrails while driving IaC, CI/CD, automation, DevOps, security, observability and resilience.

This is not a people management role. You’ll lead through deep engineering expertise, technical influence and hands-on problem solving, working across architecture, product and engineering teams to create scalable, production-ready platforms and uplift engineering capability.

Your focus will include:


Platform engineering strategy and technical direction
Secure, resilient and scalable platform architecture
IaC, CI/CD and engineering automation
DevOps standards and reusable engineering patterns
Observability, performance and operational resilience
Technical debt, security and scalability risk
Technical design assurance and complex problem solving
Engineering mentoring and capability uplift



He kōrero mōu: About you

You’re an experienced Technical Lead, Principal Engineer or Lead Platform Engineer who combines deep engineering expertise with pragmatic technical leadership.

You’ll bring:


10+ years’ hands-on engineering experience in complex enterprise environments
Strong Platform Engineering and DevOps expertise
Deep knowledge of Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD and automation
Experience designing secure, scalable and resilient platforms and integrations
Strong capability across observability, reliability, security and technical standards
Confidence influencing architecture, engineering and delivery decisions
A track record of mentoring engineers and lifting technical capability



Experience with Azure, Azure API Management, Azure DevOps, GitHub Actions, MuleSoft, SAP Integration Suite, Kafka, Kubernetes, Splunk or Azure Monitor would be highly valuable.

You don’t need to tick every box. If you bring strong platform engineering foundations and technical leadership at enterprise scale, we want to hear from you.

Why this opportunity?

Few platform engineering roles offer the opportunity to influence technology operating at the scale and complexity of Auckland Council. You'll be able to work on challenging enterprise problems while helping mature the engineering practices behind services used across Tāmaki Makaurau.

You'll have the scope to influence how platforms are designed, automated and operated, while helping create an engineering culture built around technical excellence, collaboration, continuous learning and ownership.
