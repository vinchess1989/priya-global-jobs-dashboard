About the Company

Founded in 2016 and headquartered in Ireland, with offices in London, New York and Lisbon, ID-Pal is a fast-growing B2B identity and business verification company delivering industry-leading KYC and KYB solutions to businesses worldwide.




Our award-winning, AI-powered platform enables organisations to verify individuals and businesses simply, securely and conveniently. Operating in a highly regulated environment, we help businesses prevent fraud, meet their compliance obligations and build trusted digital experiences that support global growth.




About the Role




***This is a 12-month fixed-term contract providing maternity leave cover.***




We are looking for an experienced Program Manager / Scrum Master to join ID-Pal on a 12-month fixed-term contract providing maternity leave cover. Reporting to the Tech Team Manager, you will coordinate technology delivery across our Product, Engineering, QA and DevOps teams, helping turn priorities into clear plans and successful releases.




This is a hands-on role combining programme management with Agile delivery support. You will bring structure to multiple concurrent initiatives, manage dependencies and delivery risks, coordinate releases, and keep stakeholders informed of progress and decisions required. You will also support incident follow-up, technology governance and audit readiness within our security-focused, regulated environment.

Working closely with technical leads and Product Owners, you will help teams remove blockers, strengthen delivery practices and continuously improve how we plan, deliver and report on our work. The role will suit someone who is organised, proactive and comfortable bringing clarity to complex situations while building strong relationships across teams.




Core Responsibilities

Lead the planning, coordination and delivery governance of multiple concurrent technology initiatives across cross-functional teams.
Coordinate programme milestones, delivery plans, dependencies, risks, issues, decisions and actions, ensuring clear ownership and timely escalation.
Own the operational coordination of web, mobile and hotfix releases, including scope confirmation, readiness reviews, prerequisites, approvals, communications and post-release follow-up.
Work closely with Product Owners and Engineering leads to support prioritisation, capacity planning and alignment between product commitments and technical delivery.
Monitor delivery progress and quality using agreed metrics, identifying trends and driving corrective or improvement actions.
Coordinate the programme-management aspects of major and security incidents, including stakeholder communications, action tracking, retrospectives and follow-up work.
Produce regular Technology, delivery and IMS reporting for senior stakeholders, translating detailed delivery data into clear status, risks, outcomes and decisions required.
Support audit-readiness activities, including the coordination and tracking of risks, controls, exceptions, access reviews, evidence and remediation actions.
Maintain effective delivery governance and accurate programme records across Monday.com, Jira, Confluence and other internal systems.
Facilitate Scrum and Agile ceremonies where required, remove impediments and coach teams on effective delivery practices.
Drive continuous improvement and automation across delivery, reporting, release and defect-management processes.
Build strong working relationships across Product, Engineering, QA, DevOps, Security, Compliance and senior leadership.




Key Requirements

6+ years’ experience in programme management, delivery management or a combined Agile delivery/programme role.
Demonstrable experience coordinating complex software delivery across multiple teams and disciplines.
Strong release-management experience, including readiness, risk assessment, stakeholder sign-off and dependency management.
Experience supporting incident management, retrospectives and corrective-action tracking.
Strong programme reporting skills, with the ability to present delivery status, metrics, risks and decisions to senior stakeholders.
Practical experience with Agile delivery methods and tools.
Excellent facilitation, communication, influencing and stakeholder-management skills.
Ability to work independently, impose structure on ambiguous situations and follow actions through to completion.




Nice to haves

Experience delivering identity-verification, KYC or KYB products.
Experience coordinating web and mobile releases.
Experience working in an ISO 27001 and 9001, security-focused or otherwise regulated technology environment.
Experience using automation or AI to improve delivery and reporting workflows.
Experience producing executive or board-level technology updates.
