The Opportunity

We are seeking an experienced Change Control Manager to lead the people and of major transformation initiatives, including the implementation of a new Enterprise Project & Portfolio Management (EPPM) capability and the embedding of new governance frameworks and ways of working across the CIO directorate.

This role sits at the heart of enterprise transformation — ensuring that change is not only delivered, but understood, adopted, and sustained.

This is a senior role that works closely with programme leadership, subject‑matter experts, system vendors, and senior leaders to ensure that new systems, processes, and operating models are successfully implemented and embedded into business‑as‑usual.

The Team

The Programme Management Office (PMO) team is responsible for overseeing delivery programme performance, governance, regulation and risk, investment portfolio reporting, and Network Capex-related Price Review. The team monitors performance against agreed targets, develops insights from portfolio data, and communicates performance to internal and external stakeholders key stakeholders such as DCEE and the CRU.

Key Responsibilities


Develop and embed change control procedures, workflows and templates across scope, schedule and cost.
Own the end-to-end change request and approval lifecycle, leading governance forums.
Ensure data integrity, baseline control and consistent application of data standards.
Partner with the EPPM project team, PMO leadership and system vendor to integrate change control into delivery plans.
Coordinate clear, consistent communications across CIO functions, programmes and governance forums.
Support the transition to EPPM-enabled ways of working, embedding and sustaining new behaviours and controls.
Drive adoption, compliance and continuous improvement with PMO and programme teams as Project Controls capability matures.


Knowledge, Skills And Experience


Relevant bachelor’s degree and at least 10 years’ experience.
Project Controls Manager experience delivering transformation governance in regulated infrastructure.
EPPM/PPM change control expertise, managing scope, schedule and cost baselines.
Strong planning systems, data and reporting skills, ensuring integrity and SSOT alignment.
Expertise in change governance and assurance, applying PRINCE2, APM or PMI principles.
Strong analytical, organisational and prioritisation skills to support decisions and manage risks.
Influential stakeholder leadership, communication and facilitation skills, including executive-level presentations.
