About Us

BIOTEK is a global medical device company with a strong focus on new product development in the most advanced orthopedic sector: SPORTS MEDICINE. With presence in all six continents around the world, the growth and demand for our products throughout the world is unprecedented.




Position Details:

Company Name : BIOTEK

Designation : Deputy Manager - Regulatory Affairs

Location : SG Highway, Ahmedabad

Report to : Sr. Manager - Regulatory Affairs




Company website : www.biotekortho.com




Role Objective :

To manage and ensure regulatory compliance for medical devices, orthopedic implants, surgical instruments, and related products across domestic and international markets. The role involves obtaining and maintaining product registrations, regulatory licenses, approvals, and certifications while ensuring compliance with applicable medical device regulations and quality management system requirements.




Roles & Responsibilities :

Manage regulatory compliance as per Indian Medical Device Rules, 2017, CDSCO, ISO 13485:2016, EU MDR, and other applicable regulations.
Prepare, submit, and follow up on product registration applications, manufacturing licenses, approvals, and renewals.
Handle domestic and international product registrations and coordinate with regulatory authorities, notified bodies, consultants, and overseas distributors.
Prepare and maintain regulatory dossiers, technical files, product specifications, risk management documents, labels, IFUs, and other technical documentation.
Coordinate CE marking, Free Sale Certificates, declarations, and other regulatory certifications, as applicable.
Monitor regulatory updates and assess their impact on existing products and business operations.
Coordinate with QA, QC, Manufacturing, and Sales teams for regulatory compliance and new product approvals.
Support regulatory audits, inspections, CAPA, change control, and closure of audit observations.
Review product labeling, packaging, and instructions for use to ensure regulatory compliance.
Maintain country-wise registration records, licenses, certificates, and renewal trackers.
Provide regulatory support for new product development, product launches, and international business expansion.
