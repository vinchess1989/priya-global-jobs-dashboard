Accelerate the adoption of modern DevSecOps practices across CommBank.
Shape engineering platforms that make software delivery simpler, safer and faster.
Connect technology teams and enterprise strategy to deliver measurable change.


Do Work That Matters

CommBank is investing in world-class engineering capability, including AI-powered engineering, platforms built through code and clear delivery guardrails. Our ambition is to help technology teams deliver safely and quickly, with less friction from idea to production.

As the Technology Product Owner, DevSecOps Transformation, you will connect our enterprise engineering platforms with technology teams across the Group. You will translate strategy into practical adoption, identify barriers in the software delivery lifecycle and bring teams together to create simpler, more automated and secure ways of working.

Your impact will extend beyond delivering platform features. You will help teams adopt and embed modern engineering practices, improve the developer experience and realise measurable benefits from CommBank’s strategic engineering capabilities.

See yourself in our team

You will join Engineering Platforms, working with Product Owners, engineers and technology leaders across CommBank to shape the capabilities that support modern software delivery.

Our technology teams enable the bank to innovate at scale, protect our technology and customer data, and provide reliable banking services. In this role, you will connect enterprise engineering capability with the priorities of teams across the organisation, helping create a faster, safer and more consistent path from code to production.

As a Technology Product Owner, you will:


Drive the adoption of modern DevSecOps practices, translating enterprise engineering strategy into practical transformation pathways.
Partner with technology leaders to understand their engineering environments, priorities and challenges, then develop pragmatic adoption plans.
Work with Product Owners and engineers to influence platform roadmaps and ensure capabilities address common enterprise needs.
Identify friction across the software delivery lifecycle and simplify, standardise and automate the path from code to production.
Champion secure-by-design practices and reusable automated controls that embed security and risk requirements earlier in delivery.
Use adoption data, engineering insights and team feedback to track outcomes and guide product and investment priorities.
Facilitate co-building between platform teams and business unit engineers so solutions are practical, reusable and scalable.
Explore AI-powered and agentic engineering capabilities that improve productivity, delivery flow and the developer experience.


We’re interested in hearing from people who:

You are a technology product leader who understands how engineering teams build, secure and deliver software. You are comfortable operating across product, engineering and transformation, and can create clarity and momentum in a complex technology environment.

You will bring:


Product ownership or product management experience across engineering platforms, developer tooling, DevSecOps or technology transformation.
Strong knowledge of modern software delivery, including continuous integration and delivery, automation, security controls and platform engineering.
Experience driving adoption and change across multiple technology teams, rather than focusing solely on backlog delivery.
Technical depth to engage credibly with engineers and translate complex needs into clear product priorities and outcomes.
Strong stakeholder engagement and influencing skills, with the ability to align senior leaders and engineering teams around shared priorities.
A data-led approach to measuring adoption, developer experience, engineering productivity, security and delivery performance.
The ability to navigate ambiguity, connect teams across organisational boundaries and turn enterprise strategy into practical action.
Curiosity about how AI-powered engineering can reshape the software development lifecycle.
