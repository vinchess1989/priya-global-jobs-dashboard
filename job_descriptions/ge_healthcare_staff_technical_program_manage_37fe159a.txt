Job Description

Job Summary


The Staff Technical Program Manager will execution of the Cardiovascular Ultrasound (CVUS) Digital Viewer program across on-premises, cloud and hybrid product environments. The role will convert product and technology roadmaps into integrated program plans and drive delivery across Software Engineering, Architecture, Systems, V&V, Cybersecurity, Quality, Regulatory, Service and other cross-functional teams.
This role requires strong technical program leadership, understanding of software product development and the ability to work with globally distributed stakeholders. The Staff TPM will drive cross-functional alignment, manage dependencies and risks, establish release governance, and ensure Digital Viewer releases are delivered with the required scope, quality, cybersecurity, regulatory readiness and execution predictability.


Roles and Responsibilities


Lead program execution for CVUS Digital Viewer releases from planning and architecture alignment through development, integration, verification, release, deployment readiness and Installed Base transition.
Create and own integrated multi-generational program and release plans aligned with the Digital Viewer product and technology roadmap.
Partner with Product Management, Software Architects and Engineering leadership to align product scope, architecture evolution, program sequencing and release outcomes.
Coordinate execution across Software Engineering, Systems Engineering, V&V, Automation, Cybersecurity, Quality, Regulatory, Service, DevOps and other contributing teams.
Drive execution across on-premises, cloud and hybrid deliverables, including key dependencies related to deployment, data integration, interoperability and platform services.
Establish program operating mechanisms covering milestones, scope, dependencies, risks, defects, quality, cybersecurity, release readiness and leadership decisions.
Identify and resolve cross-team roadblocks and interdependencies; escalate issues with clear impact, options, ownership and recommended actions.
Manage program risks, scope changes, technical dependencies and non-standard events throughout the product lifecycle.
Drive end-to-end release readiness with Engineering, Systems and V&V teams, ensuring required quality evidence and stakeholder deliverables are complete.
Partner with Quality and Regulatory teams to ensure program execution supports applicable medical-device software lifecycle and design-control requirements.
Maintain transparent stakeholder communication and progress reporting for engineering, product and senior leadership.
Drive metrics and data-based program management, including automated dashboards for execution, defects, quality, risk and release readiness.
Drive operational efficiency through workflow automation and appropriate use of digital and AI-enabled tools for program planning, reporting and execution.
Promote common program-management practices, Agile execution, continuous improvement and lessons learned across the CVUS Digital organisation.


Required Qualifications


Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering with 10+ years of relevant work experience and hands-on exposure to the software development lifecycle.
6+ years of technical program or project-management experience leading multiple teams within or across organisations.
Demonstrated experience leading complex software product releases involving globally distributed, cross-functional engineering teams.
Strong understanding of Agile product development and experience with frameworks such as SAFe, Scrum and Kanban.
Experience applying program-management methods to manage scope, schedule, risks, dependencies, resources, quality and release commitments.
Ability to understand software architecture and technical dependencies sufficiently to drive technical program decisions and escalation.
Strong leadership and influencing skills with demonstrated ability to organise teams around shared outcomes without direct authority.
Excellent written and verbal communication, including program reviews and communication with senior leadership.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with ability to convert complex program data into decisions and actionable plans.


Desired Qualifications


Experience delivering healthcare, medical-device or other regulated digital software products.
Experience in driving Agile programs under medical device QMS environments.
Knowledge of quality, regulatory, cybersecurity and software lifecycle practices applicable to medical-device software.
Experience with CI/CD, DevOps/DevSecOps, software release governance and automated system testing environments.
Experience building program dashboards and automating workflows across engineering data sources; Power BI experience is desirable.
Understanding of Generative AI and AI-enabled productivity tools such as GitHub Copilot, with an ability to identify practical program-management use cases.
PMP, PMI-ACP, SAFe, ScrumMaster or related project/program-management certification.
Demonstrated customer focus, ability to manage competing priorities and commitment to continuous improvement and change leadership.


Common Engineering Expectations


Cloud-native and hybrid cloud/on-premises product development using AWS services.
Containers, Kubernetes, CI/CD, Infrastructure as Code and production observability.
Secure engineering, identity and access management, encryption, secrets management and DevSecOps.
Performance, scalability, availability, reliability, fault tolerance and disaster recovery.
Automated unit, integration, API, system, performance and reliability testing.
Strong design, coding, debugging, root-cause analysis and technical documentation practices.
Integration challenges of third party and second party SaaS solutions.
Working with AI-DLC and software development acceleration.


Healthcare and Medical Device Context


Experience in regulated healthcare or medical-device development is required for the Verification Lead and strongly preferred for senior architecture, automation and program roles.
Strong knowledge on SDLC, PRD process, Risks, FMEAs.
Awareness of patient-data privacy, cybersecurity and secure handling of clinical information.
Knowledge of healthcare interoperability standards such as DICOM, HL7 and FHIR.
Experience with real-time clinical data, device connectivity, patient monitoring or high-availability clinical applications is advantageous.
