Design Quality Engineer

Heartstream is building on our 40+ year legacy of growth and innovation in emergency medical technology to continue passionately pursuing our mission of saving lives, lowering the cost of healthcare, and advancing the science of resuscitation while serving the public access AED, EMS, military, and hospital market segments. Heartstream includes leading brands and products like HeartStart AEDs, Intrepid and DFM100 monitor defibrillators, Tempus monitor and Tempus ALS systems, and Corsium and ECI informatics solutions.




Your Impact

Lead design quality activities across the product lifecycle, ensuring new products are successfully transitioned into production while meeting quality and regulatory requirements.
Partner closely with R&D, Manufacturing, Regulatory Affairs, and Product Quality teams to drive product reliability, quality improvements, and compliance with global regulations.
Influence the development of innovative life-saving medical technologies by guiding design controls, validation strategies, risk management, and post-market quality activities.
Utilize post-market surveillance insights and field performance data to continuously improve product quality, safety, and reliability.
Build strong relationships across cross-functional teams, providing quality leadership, guidance, and mentorship to support successful product launches and sustained product excellence.
Develop deep expertise in systems-level quality engineering, design controls, process validation, risk management, and regulatory compliance within the medical device industry.
This is an individual contributor role.




Our Commitment to Collaboration

Your work will directly improve the quality of emergency medical services and enhance patient safety and survival rates by making high-quality care accessible to more people around the world. As part of a number one provider of therapeutic care solutions, your contribution will help us continue to save more lives every year. With every project, you'll be making a world of difference.
We believe collaboration is better together than apart. For this office-based teams this means working in-person at least 3 days per week from our Bangalore office.




You'll thrive in the role if:

You've acquired 5+ years of experience in Quality Engineering, Design Quality, Product Quality, Safety Engineering, R&D Engineering, or a related discipline.
Preferably your experience will have been gained in medical device or health technology industry, however other regulated industry experience such as Aerospace or Automotive will also be considered.
Your skills include Design Controls, Process Validation, Design Verification & Validation, Risk Management, CAPA, Root Cause Analysis, Reliability Engineering, Design for Quality (DFQ), Statistical Analysis, and Regulatory Compliance.
Experience with Post Market Surveillance (PMS) and product reliability is highly desirable.
You have a Bachelor's or Master's Degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electronics Engineering, Science, or a related technical discipline.
A Six Sigma Green Belt certification is preferred.
You're a collaborative and influential professional with excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, capable of working effectively across multidisciplinary teams.
You are comfortable mentoring others, driving continuous improvement initiatives, and occasionally travelling as required to support business needs.
