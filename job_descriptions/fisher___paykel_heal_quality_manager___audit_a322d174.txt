Quality Manager – Audit

Care to make a difference

Join a team of good people doing good work with intent. People like you, who care to make a difference.

About The Opportunity

The Quality Manager – Audit is responsible for leading, managing and continuously improving the Quality Systems audit program. This role leads the audit programme for the New Zealand campus and supports other F&P locations as required. We are looking for an exceptional leader with a strategic focus and a confident communicator who loves interacting with regulators and mentoring and coaching internal customers.

You will lead a risk-based audit programme, work closely with internal stakeholders and external auditors, and use audit insights to support corrective action, Management Review and wider QMS improvement.

Your responsibilities will include:


Lead and continually improve the Quality Systems audit programme.
Develop and maintain a risk-based audit plan that reflects regulatory, business and QMS priorities.
Coordinate and support internal audits, external audits and regulatory inspections.
Ensure audit findings, actions and follow-up activities are clearly documented, monitored and progressed.
Analyse audit performance and trends and provide meaningful input into CAPA, Management Review and QMS improvement.
Build audit capability across the business through training, coaching and practical guidance.
Develop strong relationships with stakeholders across F&P and communicate confidently with auditors and regulators.
Lead, coach and support the people contributing to audit programme delivery.


About You


A strategic and systems-focused thinker who can identify risks and opportunities for improvement.
A clear and confident communicator who builds trusted relationships with a wide range of stakeholders.
Strong leadership, coaching and influencing capability.
Significant audit experience, ideally within medical devices or another highly regulated industry.
Sound knowledge of Quality Management Systems and risk-based auditing principles.
A Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Science or a related discipline.
