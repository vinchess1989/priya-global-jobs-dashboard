Company Overview

At Motorola Solutions, we believe that everything starts with our people. We’re a global close-knit community, united by the relentless pursuit to help keep people safer everywhere. We build and connect technologies to help protect people, property and places. Our solutions foster the collaboration that’s critical for safer communities, safer schools, safer hospitals, safer businesses, and ultimately, safer nations. Department Overview

Department Description

Innovation is the oxygen of our company, and in 2023, we invested more than US$850 million in research and development (R&D). We’re now opening a new Research & Development Centre in Cork, Ireland focused on designing software for the company’s comprehensive land mobile radio (LMR) portfolio, with plans for expansion across other technologies in the future.

Motorola Solutions’ ecosystem of technologies is centred on safety and security, with LMR representing a foundational core. The company has deployed more than 13,000 LMR networks worldwide, trusted by governments and enterprises for highly secure communications designed to work in the most extreme conditions. Modernised with broadband and advanced data applications, LMR remains at the forefront of providing public safety agencies with the communications they rely on and need.

You’ll be part of our diverse and experienced global team, developing meaningful solutions that support thousands of public safety agencies and enterprises worldwide in solving complex safety and security challenges. Come join our team to work on real-world projects with a real-world impact.

Job Description


We are looking for a DevOps Engineer to join our team of innovative engineers who are consistently raising the bar on creativity and user experiences. Responsibilities include but not limited to:
Design and implement improvements on current SCM/DevOps practices to stay on top of industry standard best practices with emphasis on automation.
Implement automation strategies, effective monitoring, deploy and maintain CI/CD pipelines.
Build and release engineer.
Inter-group and intra-group teamwork in a multi-site world-wide development environment.


Basic Requirements


Practical Knowledge of Git
Experience in Linux OS as a user
Knowledge of Continuous Integration tools, preferably Jenkins
Knowledge of Cloud Platforms (e.g. GCP)
Knowledge of Docker Containerization
Knowledge of JCasc or IAC
Scripting Languages: Bash, Perl, Python
Programming Languages: Groovy, Java
Tools: Git, Bitbucket, Artifactory, Jenkins, Sonarcube, Klocwork, Gradle, Maven, MySQL
Infrastructure: Linux, AWS, Azure, Dockers
A drive towards automating repetitive tasks (e.g. scripting via Python, Bash, Groovy)
AI Engineering Tools: Claude Code, Github Copilot, ChatGPT
Willingness to learn and apply new technologies through self-learning
Ability to work independently but also be a team player


Basic Requirements


Bachelor's Degree required; major in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or Electrical Engineering
1+ years experience desirable
