Requirements Engineer - Civil

Location: Bristol, Hybrid 3 days a week in the office

Salary: £50,000 + Career Progression + Training + Benefits

Are you an engineer with experience reviewing technical requirements and engineering documentation?

Would you like to play a key role in ensuring project requirements are clearly defined, reviewed and aligned with technical standards?

This is an opportunity to join a leading global engineering and technology organisation, where you will work with clients, contractors and technical teams to support the development and delivery of high-quality engineering documentation.

Here, you will have the opportunity to develop your career within an organisation that actively supports employee growth, whether you want to deepen your technical expertise or progress into a more senior position.

The Role

Review technical documentation and identify gaps.
Gather requirements and liaise with stakeholders.
Coordinate document updates and review comments.
Manage approvals and final document delivery.

What They're Looking For

Experience with technical requirements and documentation.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management.
Strong attention to detail and organisational skills.

Interested?

If you're looking for your next challenge and want to play a key role in technical requirements and engineering project delivery, get in touch to discuss the opportunity further.

Dairis Sprudzans, Engineering Recruitment Consultant | Elix Sourcing Solutions
