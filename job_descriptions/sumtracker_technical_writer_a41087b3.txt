Short Summary




Skills: Excellent written communication, strong SaaS product understanding, ability to simplify complex features

Experience: 2-5 years of experience at a SaaS company

Location: Remote (hybrid available for Delhi NCR)

Company website: https://www.sumtracker.com




About the company




Sumtracker is a leading SaaS solution for inventory management serving e-commerce businesses worldwide. Founded by IIT Delhi alumni Ankit and Kush, the company has a global client base across the US, the UK and Australia.




About the role




We’re looking for a Technical Writer to own Sumtracker’s help documentation and product content. You’ll work closely with the product and support teams to give customers and AI chatbots clear, accurate answers they can find on their own.




Responsibilities




Write and maintain help centre articles, setup guides, and how-to tutorials
Make the documentation AI bot ready and constantly work on it to increase the accuracy of responses
Document new features and product updates as they ship
Rewrite existing documentation to make it clearer and fill in missing details
Create onboarding content that helps new customers get started with Sumtracker




Qualifications




2–5 years of experience writing product documentation, help content, or technical content for a SaaS or tech product
You can explain complex features in plain language that merchants can understand
You’re comfortable exploring the product, testing workflows, and asking questions to get the details right




Work Policy

Remote role. 5-day work week. Hybrid option available.




10 am to 7 pm (IST): Remote




Office location for hybrid work: WeWork Platina Tower, near Sikanderpur metro station, DLF Phase 1, Gurgaon.
