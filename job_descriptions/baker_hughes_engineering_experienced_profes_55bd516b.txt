Built on over a century of innovation, the company provides advanced products, services, and digital solutions that support efficient, safe, and lower carbon energy operations. Baker Hughes continues to help customers meet the world’s evolving energy needs through technology leadership, operational excellence, and a strong commitment to sustainability

 As an Engineering Experienced Professional – Firmware and Software, your key responsibilities will include the following:  

 Application Support & Maintenance 



Demonstrate a willingness to work across multiple disciplines, contributing to development in Embedded C, C#, Golang, and REST APIs.
Support and maintain existing firmware and software including Hardware-in-the-loop testing and performance enhancements.
Proficient in leveraging AI tools to support daily tasks, problem-solving, and workflow optimization
Perform bug fixes, enhancements, and environment support across DEV, QA, and PROD 
Analyzing logs, scripts, and utilities to identify inconsistencies, performance issues, and system risks 
Understand end-to-end PLM lifecycle.



 Development & Customization 



Implementing server-side customizations including handlers and business logic 
Aligning deployed configurations with GitHub repositories and ensure consistency across environments 
Working on integration logic and backend processing for enterprise systems interacting with applications
Basic proficiency in SQL and database querying.



System Governance & Standardization 



Identifying and follow best DevOps practices such as logging, configuration, and environment alignment 



Documentation & Process Compliance 



Creating and maintaining complete technical documentation for all deliverables.
Ensuring all changes comply with defined PLM processes and are audit-ready 
Collaborating with stakeholders to translate business requirements into robust technical solutions 
Developing and completing all required project documentation, including the Root Cause Analysis (RCA), Functional Specification Document (FSD), Technical Design Document (TDD), and deployment documentation.



Fuel your passion!  

To be successful in this role you will have: 



Have great problem-solving and analytical thinking 
Have Ownership, accountability, and cross-functional collaboration 
Have Focus on standardization, governance, and continuous improvement 
Have Strong communication skills and a willingness to learn and develop expertise in PLM & License Utilization Optimization.
