Position: Engineering Manager

Location: Melbourne, Australia

Start Date: Immediate




Role Overview

We are looking for an Engineering Manager to lead the Platform Engineering function, providing people leadership, delivery oversight, strong team performance and strategic alignment across platform capabilities on Azure.

This role is responsible for ensuring the team delivers reliable, scalable, and well-adopted platform services that support application delivery, platform operations and developer productivity.

Key Responsibilities

Lead and manage a team of platform engineers supporting their growth, performance, and engagement.
Be accountable for platform stability during incidents and outages, contributing to effective incident response and communication when required.
Ensure effective delivery of platform initiatives by providing clear priorities, removing blockers, and maintaining focus on outcomes.
Support and guide the platform roadmap in collaboration with product, architecture, and engineering stakeholders to align platform work with business and technology goals.
Drive a strong engineering culture focused on accountability, quality, and continuous improvement.
Ensure appropriate resourcing and capacity planning to support both project delivery and operational needs.
Manage risks related to delivery, platform stability, and team capacity.
Support hiring, onboarding, and retention of engineering talent.
Promote effective collaboration across teams to ensure platform adoption and alignment.

Required Skills & Experience

10+ years of experience in DevOps, software engineering, platform engineering, or related fields.
Proven experience managing and leading engineering teams.
Strong understanding of Kubernetes, CI/CD, and cloud platforms (Azure preferred).
Experience with internal developer platforms (IDP) and developer experience initiatives.
Experience working in Agile environments and delivering complex technical initiatives.
Ability to balance delivery, operational stability, and team wellbeing.
Strong stakeholder management and communication skills.
Experience working with cross-functional teams including product, security, and architecture.

Nice to Have

Experience applying SRE practices, including SLO design, alerting strategies, and error budget management.
Exposure to agentic systems and AI-assisted engineering workflows in a platform context.
Familiarity with engineering metrics frameworks (e.g., DX Core 4 / DORA) to measure and improve developer productivity.
Experience driving developer experience (DevEx) improvements through measurable outcomes.
Exposure to FinOps practices and cost optimisation in Azure, using tools such as Azure Advisor, Cost Management, or platforms like Harness.




Key Attributes

Strong people leader with a focus on team growth and performance
Delivery-focused with the ability to drive outcomes in complex environments
Pragmatic and able to balance competing priorities
Comfortable operating across both technical and business contexts
Calm under pressure and able to manage risk effectively
