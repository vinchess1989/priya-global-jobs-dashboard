Responsibilities

Own end-to-end QA for consumer-facing products, including test strategy, execution, and quality assessment.
Build API, UI automation, and performance testing solutions integrated with CI/CD.
Leverage AI/LLM Agents for test generation, automation, defect analysis, and code review to improve efficiency.

Requirements

3+ years of QA experience, including 1+ year in consumer-facing products, with independent project ownership.
Proficient in API/performance testing (Postman, Apifox, JMeter), MySQL, Redis, and SQL.
Skilled in Python, Go, JavaScript, or Bash, with hands-on automation and CI/CD experience.
Familiar with mobile testing (Appium, ADB, network debugging, compatibility, and weak-network scenarios).
Practical experience using AI tools for testing, with measurable productivity improvements.
Understanding of LLM/Agent workflows, prompt engineering, and AI output validation.
Strong problem-solving and communication skills.
