Do the best work of your career by making our systems and processes work as one.

Behind every renewal, invoice and support ticket is a chain of handovers between teams and systems. Each link matters. You will find the ones that slow us down, design something better and see it through to everyday use.

Please note this is a 12-month maternity cover

The role

You will join our Business Systems team, covering a colleague's maternity leave. You will sit between the people who run our business and the systems that support them, including Salesforce, Maxio, NetSuite and Freshdesk, turning what teams need into changes that are well designed, properly tested and actually used.

Day to day, you will:


Map current and future-state processes end to end, using Miro and Lucid
Gather requirements from stakeholders and turn them into clear system specifications
Run work-in-progress sessions and design workshops to validate requirements and shape solutions
Write test cases and support system, integration and user acceptance testing
Help configure business logic and user interfaces alongside the development team
Track changes through to release in Jira
Spot gaps, manual workarounds and opportunities to simplify or automate
Apply service management (ITIL) practices to keep processes consistent and reliable


What Success Looks Like

By month three, you understand how our core processes flow from CRM through billing, finance and support. You have built strong relationships with key stakeholders and mapped the processes that matter most.

By month six, you have led workshops that shaped real changes and taken improvements through testing and into everyday use. Teams can point to steps that are simpler or faster because of your work.

By the end of your contract, you leave clear, documented processes the team can keep building on.

What You Will Bring

Must-haves:


Experience as a business process analyst, business analyst or systems analyst, ideally in a SaaS or technology business
A continuous improvement mindset, with a track record of analysing and improving business processes
Strong process mapping and documentation skills, with hands-on use of tools like Miro or Lucid
Solid working knowledge of Salesforce, ideally including administration
Experience gathering requirements and supporting testing, including UAT
Confidence working with stakeholders at every level and balancing several priorities at once


Nice-to-haves:


Experience with billing or ERP processes, especially Maxio or NetSuite
Familiarity with ITIL or service management practices
Using Jira to document requirements and manage releases
A degree in IT, Computer Science, Business Administration or a related field, or equivalent experience


If you do not tick every box, apply anyway. We care more about how you think than a perfect checklist.

Why CyberSentriq

CyberSentriq helps MSPs protect their customers through an integrated cybersecurity and data protection platform. Every Sentrian, in every role, is part of the product we deliver. When our processes run cleanly, our partners feel it.

We invest in our people through structured learning, AI upskilling, mentoring and leaders who close the loop on what you raise. Twelve months is a real chance to make lasting change, grow your skills and add meaningful work to your portfolio.

Flexible hybrid working

We will agree a hybrid pattern with you, depending on where you are based. Our office is in Galway, and in-person time is intentional: focused on building relationships, not ticking a box.

CULTURE CODE - HOW WE EXPECT YOU TO SHOW UP

Our Culture Code shapes how we hire, work and win together. Here is what it looks like in this role:


Fortress: Strength through consistency and integrity. You hold the same standard on documentation, testing and sign-off every time, and you work across Sales, Finance, Support and Business Systems so the right people shape every change.
Shield: Protection is every Sentrian's responsibility. You spot process gaps and data issues before they reach customers or billing, flag risks early and keep stakeholders informed at every stage.
Disruptor: Clarity and accountability are disruptive. You question manual workarounds and "how we have always done it", and push for simpler, automated ways of working. You challenge internally and align externally.
Champion: You champion outcomes, not activity. You make sure changes are adopted, not just delivered, close the loop with the people who raised them, and celebrate the colleagues who helped make them happen.


AI-native at CyberSentriq

We are curious about how AI can make our work better. In this role, you will use approved AI tools to speed up process documentation, requirements drafting and test case writing. You will keep the human in the loop by checking outputs and applying your own judgement, protect company and customer data, and share what works so the whole team improves.
