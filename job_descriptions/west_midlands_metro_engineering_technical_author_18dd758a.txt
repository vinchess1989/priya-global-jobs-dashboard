Help Keep the West Midlands Moving

Are you an experienced Technical Author, Document Controller, or Information Management professional looking to play a key role in one of the UK's most exciting transport infrastructure programmes

West Midlands Metro is seeking a Technical Documentation Author to support the successful integration of new network extensions into operational service.

Working across multiple engineering disciplines, you'll be responsible for creating, managing, and coordinating critical technical documentation that enables the safe and effective handover of infrastructure into day-to-day operations.

This is a fantastic opportunity to work at the heart of a growing light rail network, collaborating with engineering, project delivery, operations, asset management, and safety teams on complex infrastructure projects.

The Opportunity

As Technical Documentation Author, you'll act as the central point for technical information management, ensuring documentation is accurate, compliant, controlled, and readily available to support operational readiness and asset handover activities.

You'll be involved in the development and management of: Technical standards, procedures and work instructions, Maintenance manuals and operational documentation, Safety Management System (SMS) documentation, Asset Integration Procedures, Health & Safety files, Engineering and project documentation and Interface documentation between project delivery and operational teams

You'll also play a key role in document control, version management, stakeholder engagement, and continuous improvement initiatives across the business.

Key Responsibilities


Produce, review, edit and maintain high-quality technical documentation
Gather and interpret information from engineers, contractors and subject matter experts
Develop clear and user-friendly maintenance, operational and engineering procedures
Coordinate document reviews, approvals and distribution processes
Manage document control and version control activities within SharePoint and approved management systems
Support infrastructure handover, commissioning and operational readiness activities
Review contractor documentation for quality, compliance and accuracy
Facilitate stakeholder workshops and documentation reviews
Help improve documentation standards, templates and information management processes


About You

We're looking for someone with strong technical writing and document management experience within a regulated engineering environment.

Essential Experience


Minimum 3 years' experience in a Technical Author, Document Controller, Engineering Support, Technical Administrator or Information Management role
Experience producing technical documentation, procedures, maintenance manuals and operational guidance
Strong understanding of document control, version control and information governance
Experience working within engineering, rail, light rail, infrastructure, construction, utilities or asset management sectors
Excellent written communication and technical writing skills
Experience working with multiple stakeholders across engineering and operational environments
Strong Microsoft Office and SharePoint skills


Desirable Experience


Rail, light rail, tramway or infrastructure project experience
Asset handover, commissioning or operational readiness experience
Knowledge of Safety Management Systems (SMS)
Familiarity with CDM Regulations and Health & Safety Files
Experience using ProjectWise or similar document management systems
Knowledge of West Midlands Metro infrastructure and operations
What You'll Bring
Exceptional attention to detail
Strong organisational and planning skills
The ability to manage multiple deadlines and priorities
A collaborative and proactive approach
Confidence engaging with both technical and non-technical stakeholders
A passion for high-quality documentation and process improvement


Why Join West Midlands Metro

This is a unique opportunity to contribute to the expansion of a major transport network and play a critical role in ensuring new infrastructure is safely integrated into operational service.

You'll work alongside experienced engineering and project teams while helping shape the documentation and information management processes that support the future of the Metro network.

Interested

If you're an experienced Technical Author or Document Management professional looking to make an impact on a nationally significant infrastructure programme, we'd love to hear from you.
